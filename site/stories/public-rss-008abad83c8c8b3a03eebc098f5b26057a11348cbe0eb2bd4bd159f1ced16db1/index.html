    勒克莱尔拉斯维加斯与汉密尔顿碰撞后称“为车队感到难堪”

    法拉利在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛仅收获七分，勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿在第38圈7至8号弯发生碰撞，两人各被罚10秒。

    勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯与汉密尔顿代价惨重的碰撞后称“为车队感到难堪”

    对法拉利车手来说，拉斯维加斯的这场比赛很漫长

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地方的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    法拉利在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛仅带走七分、两个10秒罚时，以及一团需要理清的队内新乱局。在经历Q2双双被淘汰的痛苦后，勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿从第10和第11位起步，周六晚间大部分时间都在同一段赛道上争夺位置——直到第38圈在7至8号弯的减速弯，两人的缠斗彻底爆发。

    勒克莱尔从内线切入队友的行驶路线，撞上了汉密尔顿的SF-26赛车，导致这位七届世界冠军打转并跌至第10位。国际汽联干事随即因引发碰撞对勒克莱尔处以10秒罚时。

    尽管勒克莱尔在赛道上以第6名冲过终点线，但这一处罚将他降至第10位，仅获得1个积分；而汉密尔顿则回升至第7名——此前他因在与兰多·诺里斯的缠斗中驶离赛道并获得优势，已经先接受了10秒罚时。

    车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔立即介入，在冷却圈通过无线电命令勒克莱尔：“查尔斯，比赛结束后、处理其他事情之前，我要先见到你。”赛后，勒克莱尔在接受天空体育F1记者克雷格·斯莱特采访时神情明显低落，并未对判罚提出异议。

    “我受到了处罚，所以结果就是，我想大多数人和干事都认为这是我的失误，我接受这一点，”勒克莱尔说。“但是，是的，我不会再多说什么了。归根结底，我受到了处罚，所以这必须是我的失误。”

    比赛早些时候，法拉利维修区墙上已经爆发出不满情绪，汉密尔顿在试图追赶前方赛车时，要求比赛工程师卡洛·桑蒂“告诉他给我滚远点”。尽管如此，当桑蒂后来告知汉密尔顿，勒克莱尔因第38圈的接触被处以10秒罚时，汉密尔顿回答说：“我不想要那个！”

    当斯莱特问勒克莱尔如何看待两人之间的缠斗，以及法拉利现在是否需要为车手制定更严格的规则时，勒克莱尔回答得很直白。

    “嗯，我只是为车队整体感到相当尴尬，”勒克莱尔说。“是的，我会复盘这件事。”

    法拉利紧张赛季中的第三次冲突爆发点

    拉斯维加斯是2026年法拉利队友之间第三次重大赛道冲突。在蒙扎的意大利大奖赛起步时，勒克莱尔将汉密尔顿逼出赛道，随后一圈后自己退赛；在墨西哥城大奖赛争夺领先时，两人一同冲出赛道——这给了马克斯·维斯塔潘取胜的机会，并促使两人在下一站圣保罗与瓦塞尔进行澄清会谈。

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    在积分榜上，法拉利的损失仍然有限。这支车队以517分位居车队锦标赛第二，仍领先红牛车队（440分）77分，领先迈凯伦（431分）86分，剩余卡塔尔和阿布扎比两站。个人方面，汉密尔顿从第七位恢复，升至第三，积267分，而勒克莱尔滑落至第六，积250分——这让瓦塞尔在赛季结束前再次寻求休战。

    Charles Leclerc “embarrassed for the team” after costly Las Vegas clash with Lewis Hamilton

    It was a long race for the Scuderia drivers in Vegas

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari left the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix with seven points, two 10-second time penalties, and a fresh intra-team mess to untangle. Starting 10th and 11th after a painful double Q2 elimination, Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton spent much of Saturday night fighting over the same stretch of road - until their battle boiled over on Lap 38 at the Turns 7–8 chicane.

    Diving up the inside of his team-mate, Leclerc clipped Hamilton’s SF-26 and pitched the seven-time world champion into a spin down to 10th. FIA stewards promptly handed Leclerc a 10-second time penalty for causing a collision.

    Although Leclerc crossed the chequered flag sixth on the road, the sanction demoted him to 10th for a single point, while Hamilton recovered to seventh - having already served an earlier 10-second penalty of his own for leaving the track and gaining an advantage while duelling Lando Norris.

    Team principal Fred Vasseur wasted no time stepping in, ordering Leclerc over cool-down lap radio, "Charles, I want to see you just after the race, before everything." Speaking afterwards to Sky Sports F1’s Craig Slater, a visibly subdued Leclerc offered no pushback against the verdict.

    "I’ve been penalised, so as a result, I guess the majority and the stewards thought that it was my mistake, which I’ll accept that," Leclerc said. "But, yeah, I’m not going to say much more. At the end of the day, I’m penalised, so it has to be my mistake."

    Frustration had already flared at Ferrari’s pitwall earlier in the race, with Hamilton asking race engineer Carlo Santi to "tell him to back the f*** off" while trying to chase the cars ahead. Even so, when Santi later informed Hamilton that Leclerc had been handed a 10-second penalty for the Lap 38 contact, Hamilton replied, "I don’t want that!"

    Asked by Slater how he viewed the racing between the pair and whether Ferrari now needs stricter rules for its drivers, Leclerc kept his answer blunt.

    "Well, I just feel quite embarrassed for the team in general," Leclerc said. "Yeah, I will review it."

    Third flashpoint in a tense Ferrari campaign

    Las Vegas marks the third major on-track clash between Ferrari’s team-mates in 2026. Leclerc ran Hamilton wide at the start of the Italian Grand Prix in Monza before crashing out a lap later, and the pair went off track together fighting for the lead at the Mexico City Grand Prix - handing Max Verstappen an opening to win and prompting clear-the-air talks with Vasseur at the following round in São Paulo.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    In the standings, Ferrari’s damage remained limited. The Scuderia holds second in the constructors’ championship on 517 points, still 77 clear of Red Bull Racing (440) and 86 ahead of McLaren (431) with Qatar and Abu Dhabi left to run. Individually, Hamilton’s seventh-place recovery lifted him to third on 267 points, while Leclerc slipped to sixth on 250 - leaving Vasseur once again searching for a truce before the season closes.
