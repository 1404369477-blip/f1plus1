    法拉利面临关键会议，汉密尔顿与勒克莱尔紧张关系升级至巴西站

    法拉利车队在墨西哥大奖赛出现的紧张关系尚未平息，计划举行一场澄清会议。车队在赛季末面临的问题，对2027年赛季也具有重要意义。

    法拉利车队在墨西哥大奖赛出现的紧张关系尚未平息，计划举行一场澄清会议，涉及汉密尔顿、勒克莱尔和车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔。

    这场重要的会议不仅关系到赛季的结束，更重要的是为2027年的赛季做准备。

    法拉利车队在Interlagos面对的问题，在几周前还难以想象。经过一个漫长的赛季，车队似乎终于找到了许多之前SF-26赛车所缺失的答案。

    最近几场大奖赛的结果证实了这种进步，但就在车队重新获得争夺胜利的能力时，一个微妙的问题出现了：汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔在赛道上的关系。

    墨西哥大奖赛的开局发生了什么，仍然占据着人们的讨论。两位法拉利车手之间的冲突破坏了一个本可能为车队带来截然不同结果周日，更重要的是，暴露了一种在比赛后似乎并未消散的紧张关系。

    在昨天Interlagos的媒体采访中，汉密尔顿重申了他在墨西哥城周日晚上所表达的观点。没有回头，也没有试图淡化所发生的事情。这表明问题仍未解决。

    自墨西哥以来，两位车手和车队老板弗雷德·瓦瑟尔没有机会坐下来一起讨论。瓦瑟尔回到了马拉内洛，而勒克莱尔则去陪伴他的妻子亚历山德拉，准备迎接他们孩子的出生。因此，讨论被推迟到今天，当这位摩纳哥人抵达Interlagos赛道维修区时。

    这将是一次重要的会议。与其说是为了确定在墨西哥谁对谁错，不如说是为了定义一个原则，两位车手都必须遵守：当法拉利处于获胜的位置时，车队利益不能因内部斗争而受到威胁。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    但为什么两位已经不再有世界冠军争夺机会的车手会达到如此高的紧张程度？答案并不简单，不可避免地涉及一定程度的主观猜测。汉密尔顿的大部分职业生涯都处于一个非常特殊的地位。在2016年尼科·罗斯伯格退役后，这位英国车手在梅赛德斯车队度过了许多年，车队是以他为中心建立的。这是一个他的位置从未真正受到质疑的情况。

    他的转会到法拉利让他进入了一个完全不同的环境。汉密尔顿发现自己与一位不仅对组织了如指掌，而且多年来与车队建立了非常牢固关系的队友并肩作战。对于一个习惯于成为团队中参考点的车手来说，分享这个角色可能是一种不熟悉的经验。

    另一方面是勒克莱尔，自2019年以来一直身穿法拉利的战袍。这位摩纳哥车手经历了艰难的赛季，接受了经常未能达到预期目标的车辆的技术限制，并继续成为车队的中坚力量。他现在感到自己已经赢得了应有的位置，即使面对七次世界冠军，他也没有打算放弃。

    这是两条不同的旅程，两种强烈的个性，以及两位拥有足够动力不轻易让步的车手。世界冠军头衔并不一定是激发竞争的必要条件。有时，正是缺乏立即的共同目标，才使得找到平衡更加困难。

    矛盾的是，就在法拉利似乎克服了困扰其整个赛季的许多问题时，这种局面出现了。在过去的三场比赛中，斯库德里亚在车队 constructors' championship 中获得了比任何其他车队更多的积分，与赛季前半段相比，这是一个显著的转变。

    SF-26 再次成为汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔可以瞄准 pole positions 和胜利的赛车。然而，正是在这个时候，车队必须应对内部斗争可能损害集体结果的风险。墨西哥发生的事情仅仅展示了在有助于推动性能的竞争和可能导致潜在胜利，甚至 1-2 完美收官的竞争之间，界限是多么的微妙。

    法国车队负责人面临着他任期中最微妙的一项挑战。到目前为止，他一直坚持捍卫车手在赛道上相互竞争的自由，相信两位冠军应该在没有过度限制的情况下竞争。这是一个有道理的哲学，但它需要一项基本条件：两位车手都必须理解和尊重个人竞争对团队有害的界限。

    这正是今天在Interlagos会议必须解决的问题。汉密尔顿已经明确表示，他没有改变对事件解释的意图。勒克莱尔的位置还有待听取，因为他缺席了昨天的媒体承诺。瓦瑟尔的任务是倾听两位车手，最重要的是制定没有解释余地的规则。

    然而，为了实现这一点，车队经理必须得到法拉利领导层的全力支持。这不仅仅是给他提供他需要的支持来做出决定，即使是不受欢迎的决定，而是确保在处理车手时有一个统一的意见——一个每个人都认可的最高权威。

    这是管理一个团队的基本原则，尤其是拥有汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔这样水平的车手。如果指令来自不同的人，或者甚至有可能在其他地方寻求确认和支持，那么负责领导团队的人的权威不可避免地会受到削弱。法拉利的领导层必须支持瓦瑟尔，但更重要的是，它必须允许他——只有他——代表车队向车手传达立场。这是唯一给予决定充分权威并确保它们得到尊重的方法。

    管理两位这样水平的车手不能受到他们各自声誉的影响，也不能受到决策潜在的外部影响。如果目标是让法拉利回到可以持续争夺世界锦标赛的位置，那么车队的需求必须优先于任何个人的野心。这并不意味着引入预定的车手等级制度。而是确保在斯库德利亚的最佳利益下做出的决定被两位车手认可和尊重，无一例外。

    有一个最终的因素使得Interlagos的会面更加重要。2026赛季现在正进入尾声，但法拉利已经在为下一年的冠军赛做准备，目标是带着争夺最大奖杯的位置进入赛季首场比赛。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、查尔斯·勒克莱尔、法拉利

    这个前景不仅需要一辆有竞争力的赛车，还需要一个和谐的环境，以及一个每个人都能理解自己角色的组织。一场潜在的世界冠军争夺战不可避免地会使管理竞争变得更加困难——尽管本赛季没有冠军可争夺，但已经出现了紧张迹象。

    因此，瓦瑟尔、汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔之间的会面将不仅仅是澄清在墨西哥发生的事情。这可能会标志着法拉利确立未来其车手如何合作的基本规则。

    SF-26终于似乎找回了竞争力的道路。现在，球队需要确保，当赢得比赛的机会来临时，自己的车手不会再次错失机会。

    Ferrari set for crunch meeting as Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc tension reaches Brazil

    The tensions that emerged in the Mexico Grand Prix have not yet subsided and a clear-the-air meeting is planned between Lewis Hamilton, Charles Leclerc and team principal Fred Vasseur. An important meeting not only for the end of the season, but above all with a view to 2027

    Ferrari arrived at Interlagos facing a problem that, until just a few weeks ago, would have been difficult to imagine in quite this form. After a long season spent chasing competitiveness, the team finally appears to have found many of the answers that had been missing with its SF-26 Formula 1 car.

    The results from the last few grands prix confirm that progress, but just as the Scuderia has regained the ability to fight for victories, a delicate issue has emerged: the relationship between Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc on track.

    What happened in the opening corners of the Mexico GP continues to dominate the conversation. The flashpoint between the two Ferrari drivers compromised a Sunday that could have delivered a very different result for the team and, more importantly, exposed a tension that does not appear to have dissipated after the race.

    Speaking to the media at Interlagos yesterday, Hamilton reaffirmed the position he had expressed on Sunday evening in Mexico City. There was no backtracking and no attempt to play down what happened. It is a sign that the issue remains unresolved.

    There has been no opportunity for the two drivers and team boss Fred Vasseur to sit down together since Mexico. Vasseur returned to Maranello, while Leclerc went to be with his family ahead of the birth of his child with his wife, Alexandra. The discussion has therefore been postponed until today, when the Monegasque arrives in the Interlagos paddock.

    It will be an important meeting. Not so much to establish who was right or wrong in Mexico, but to define a principle that both drivers must respect: when a Ferrari is in a position to win, the team's interests cannot be put at risk by an internal battle.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    But why have two drivers who are no longer in contention for the world championship reached such a high level of tension? The answer is not straightforward and inevitably involves a degree of speculation. Hamilton has spent much of his career in a very particular position. Following Nico Rosberg's retirement at the end of 2016, the British driver enjoyed many years at Mercedes with a team built around him. It was a situation in which his position was never genuinely in question.

    His move to Ferrari brought him into a very different environment. Hamilton found himself alongside a team-mate who not only knows the organisation inside out, but has also built a very strong relationship with the Scuderia over the years. For a driver accustomed to being the reference point within his team, sharing that role can be an unfamiliar experience.

    On the other side is Leclerc, who has worn Ferrari colours since 2019. The Monegasque has endured difficult seasons, accepted the technical limitations of cars that have often fallen short of expectations and continued to be one of the team's cornerstones. It is understandable that he now feels he has earned a position he has no intention of surrendering, even in the face of a seven-time world champion.

    These are two different journeys, two strong personalities and two drivers with more than enough motivation to not give ground. A world championship does not necessarily have to be at stake to fuel a rivalry. Sometimes, it is precisely the absence of an immediate shared objective that makes finding a balance more difficult.

    The paradox is that this situation has emerged just as Ferrari appears to have overcome many of the problems that have hampered its season. Over the last three races, the Scuderia has scored more points than any other team in the constructors' championship, marking a significant turnaround compared with the first part of the campaign.

    The SF-26 has once again become a car with which Hamilton and Leclerc can target pole positions and wins. Yet, at precisely this moment, the team must contend with the risk that an internal battle could compromise the collective result. What happened in Mexico showed just how fine the line can be between a rivalry that helps drive performance and one capable of turning a potential victory, or even a 1-2 finish, into a missed opportunity and what ended up being second and fourth.

    The French team principal faces one of the most delicate challenges of his tenure. Until now, he has consistently defended his drivers' freedom to race each other on track, believing that two champions should be allowed to compete without excessive restrictions. It is a philosophy that makes sense, but it requires one fundamental condition: both drivers must understand and respect the point beyond which individual rivalry becomes detrimental to the team.

    That is precisely what today's meeting at Interlagos must address. Hamilton has already made it clear that he has no intention of changing his interpretation of events. Leclerc's position remains to be heard, as he was absent from yesterday's media commitments. Vasseur's task will be to listen to both drivers and, above all, establish rules that leave no room for interpretation.

    To achieve that, however, the team principal must have the full backing of Ferrari's leadership. This is not simply about giving him the support he needs to make decisions, even unpopular ones, but ensuring that there is a single voice when dealing with the drivers – one that everyone recognises as the ultimate authority.

    This is a fundamental principle in running a team, particularly one with drivers of Hamilton and Leclerc's calibre. If instructions come from different people, or if there is even the possibility of seeking confirmation and support elsewhere, the authority of the person responsible for leading the team inevitably risks being undermined. Ferrari's leadership must support Vasseur, but, more importantly, it must allow him – and him alone – to represent the team's position to its drivers. That is the only way to give decisions full authority and ensure they are respected.

    Managing two drivers of this calibre cannot be influenced by the weight of their respective reputations or by the potential external repercussions of a decision. If the objective is to return Ferrari to a position where it can consistently fight for world championships, the team's needs must take precedence over any individual ambition. That does not necessarily mean introducing predetermined driver hierarchies. Rather, it means ensuring that decisions taken in the Scuderia's best interests are recognised and respected by both drivers, without exception.

    There is one final aspect that makes the Interlagos meeting even more important. The 2026 season is now entering its closing stages, but Ferrari is already working towards next year's championship, with the ambition of arriving at the season opener in a position to fight for the biggest prizes.

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari

    That prospect requires not only a competitive car, but also a harmonious environment and an organisation in which everyone understands their role. A potential battle for the world championship would inevitably make managing a rivalry even more difficult – one that has already shown signs of tension despite there being no title at stake this season.

    For that reason, the meeting between Vasseur, Hamilton and Leclerc will be about more than simply clearing the air over what happened in Mexico. It could mark the moment when Ferrari establishes the ground rules for how its drivers will work together in the future.

    The SF-26 finally appears to have rediscovered the path to competitiveness. Now it is up to the team to ensure that, when the opportunity to win comes, its own drivers do not turn it into another missed opportunity.
