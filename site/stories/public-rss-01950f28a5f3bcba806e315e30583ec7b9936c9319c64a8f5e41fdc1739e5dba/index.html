    罗斯伯格：拉塞尔已重回最佳状态

    2016年F1世界冠军尼科·罗斯伯格认为，乔治·拉塞尔在经历2026赛季艰难开局后终于重回最佳状态。拉塞尔在墨尔本站和中国站冲刺赛中获胜，但随后遭遇时机不佳的安全车等周期问题，影响了策略和赛车表现。

    尼科·罗斯伯格表示，乔治·拉塞尔在经历了艰难的F1开局后已经"重回最佳状态"

    尼科·罗斯伯格认为，在2026年F1赛季艰难的上半程之后，乔治·拉塞尔已经恢复到了他的最佳状态

    2016年F1冠军尼科·罗斯伯格认为，乔治·拉塞尔终于回到了他的最佳状态。

    拉塞尔在2026赛季的开局相当坎坷。这位英国车手以夺冠热门的身份来到揭幕战澳大利亚大奖赛，但在墨尔本站和中国站冲刺赛获胜之后，他遭遇了诸多问题，包括打乱他策略的不合时宜的安全车以及赛车故障。

    与此同时，他十几岁的队友安东内利在日本大奖赛登上车手积分榜榜首，并在赛季上半段总共赢得了六场大奖赛。

    但这位28岁的车手在夏休期后焕然一新地回归。他从杆位出发赢得了荷兰冲刺赛，并在荷兰大奖赛排位赛中获得第二。

    在接受天空体育 F1采访时，Rosberg表示，围场里的人对Russell在赛季早些阶段缺乏纯粹速度这件事感到措手不及。

    "他在赛季上半段的表现让我们所有人都非常惊讶，"Rosberg解释道，"你提到了运气不好，但不仅仅是运气不好，他的速度也不够快。Antonelli就是更快的那位车手。

    "所以这周末看到他恢复到最佳状态真是太棒了，他看起来状态非常在线。也许他的订婚让他大脑里的某个开关被打开了。现在事情尘埃落定，他终于放松下来了，又变回了从前那个拉塞尔。

    "这就是我们所知道的、他有能力达到的水平。"

    Russell向天空体育 F1解释说，他现在对赛车的感觉更加直觉化了。"我希望这种状态（我的好状态）能继续保持。这个周末感觉更像一个正常的周末。赛车的手感更直觉化了，我对赛车更有信心，一切也就来得更自然了。

    "看看今天会带来什么吧。这将会是一场有趣的比赛。我认为法拉利会很快，Lando也会很快，所以这将是一场精彩的较量。"

    Nico Rosberg says George Russell is "back at his best" after difficult F1 start

    Nico Rosberg believes George Russell has returned to his best form after a difficult first half of the 2026 Formula 1 season

    2016 Formula 1 champion Nico Rosberg believes George Russell is finally back to his best form.

    Russell has endured a tricky start to the 2026 season. The Briton arrived at the season-opening Australian Grand Prix as a title favourite, but after winning in Melbourne and the Chinese sprint race, he faced multiple issues, including ill-timed safety cars that hampered his strategy and car problems.

    Meanwhile, his teenage team-mate Kimi Antonelli took the lead of the standings at the Japanese Grand Prix and went on to win a total of six grands prix in the opening half of the season.

    But the 28-year-old has returned after the summer break refreshed. He won the Dutch sprint race from pole position and qualified second for the Dutch Grand Prix.

    Speaking to Sky Sports F1, Rosberg said the paddock had been caught off guard by Russell's lack of raw speed in some of the earlier stages of the season.

    "It was a big surprise to all of us his performance in the first half of the season," Rosberg explained. "You mentioned bad luck but it wasn't just bad luck, he's also not been quick enough. Antonelli was just the quicker driver.

    "So it's great to see him back at his best here this weekend, he seems really on it. Maybe his engagement has flipped a switch in his mind. He's finally relaxed now because that's done, and it's like the George Russell of old.

    "This is the level we know he's capable of."

    Russell explained to Sky Sports F1 that he was feeling more intuitive with the car. "I hope it [my form] continues. It feels more like a normal weekend. The car’s feeling more intuitive, I’ve got more confidence in the car, and then it just comes more naturally.

    "Let’s see what today brings. It’s going to be an interesting race. I think the Ferraris are going to be quick, Lando will be fast, so it’s going to be a good fight."
