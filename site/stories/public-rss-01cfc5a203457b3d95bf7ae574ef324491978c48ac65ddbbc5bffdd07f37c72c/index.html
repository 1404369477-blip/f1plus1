    维斯塔潘分享对法拉利F1 2026速度不足的理论

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中第二快的时间并不满意，指出小红牛22存在神秘问题。同时，他分析了法拉利速度不足的原因。

    维斯塔潘分享对法拉利F1 2026速度不足的理论

    除了较小的涡轮，维斯塔潘还指出法拉利的"排气吹拂"问题。在拉斯维加斯这样的赛道上，速度不足尤为明显。

    尽管维斯塔潘对他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中第二快的时间并不满意，指出小红牛22存在神秘问题，但观察法拉利的情况显示情况可能更加糟糕。

    自2025年伊莫拉以来，法拉利车队首次在Q2阶段两辆赛车都被淘汰，之后刘易斯·汉密尔顿解释说地板损坏和部署问题阻碍了他。他估计缺失的地板部分让他失去了三到四十分之一秒，而部署问题又让他失去了另外六十分之一秒。

    尽管如此，法拉利事先就已经知道拉斯维加斯赛道对SF-26来说将是一个相当大的挑战。这部分原因与有意的工程设计选择有关。法拉利使用的涡轮比大多数其他动力单元制造商都要小，这使得位于马拉内洛的法拉利车队在起步时具有优势，尤其是在今年年初，但也对整体动力产生了影响。

    法拉利获得了两个ADUO代币，这意味着根据国际汽联的测量，这家意大利制造商在最佳内燃机引擎上落后了2%到4%，而红牛的引擎就是最佳内燃机引擎。

    在拉斯维加斯，这一点尤其痛苦，因为当导流板在长直道上启动时，F1车手必须完全依赖内燃机的动力在一定时间内行驶。

    这解释了为什么法拉利在最高速度上如此缺乏动力，尽管维斯塔潘看到了另一个影响因素。

    “他们显然缺乏顶级速度，但这与他们的排气吹拂设计方式有关。因为如果你看看哈斯，直道上这个问题要小得多，”当Motorsport.com问他关于法拉利灾难性的排位赛时，维斯塔潘说。

    “这始终是你要多少下压力和愿意为此牺牲多少动力的结合。你把排气前面的那个东西做得越大，你失去的动力就越多。所以从某种程度上说，这显然也在他们自己的掌控之中。”

    维斯塔潘指的是法拉利的排气翼，这是斯库德里亚今年开创的概念。它有助于产生额外的下压力，但维斯塔潘认为它也可能成为拉斯维加斯等赛道长直道上的额外障碍。

    “在这样的赛道上，这确实可能是一个大障碍。所以你可能需要稍微适应一下，但这显然是他们自己必须解决的问题！”

    红牛车队在能源匮乏的赛道上取得了显著的进步。在今年年初，这类赛场的效率仍然是我们的弱点，尤其是与梅赛德斯相比，但红牛福特动力系统在整个赛季中在该领域获得了大量的知识。

    "一开始，我们的赛车显然有很多问题，而且在动力单元方面也没有完全掌握。我认为这自动意味着我们有一些更容易的步骤要走，但我们仍然完成了这些步骤。这是我们值得骄傲的事情。"

    除了纯粹的性能之外，执行也是拉斯维加斯的一个主要因素。尾流产生了极端的影响，许多车手表示，这甚至比他们之前经历过的任何事情都要大，这使得法拉利车手在排位赛中大部分时间没有尾流显得更加令人惊讶。

    考虑到他们缺乏最高速度，这似乎是一种奇怪的方法，尽管维斯塔潘不愿意在这方面对法拉利过于苛刻。

    “嗯，这是从外面看的。我只专注于我们自己的车队。当然，我们的目标始终是尽可能少犯错误，尽可能多地掌握一切。我认为我们在今天的执行方面做得相当不错。”

    Max Verstappen shares theory on Ferrari's lack of top speed in F1 2026

    Besides the smaller turbo, Verstappen also points to Ferrari's "exhaust blowing". The lack of top speed is particularly painful at a circuit like Las Vegas

    Although Max Verstappen was not entirely satisfied with his second-fastest time in qualifying for the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix – pointing to a mysterious issue with the RB22 – a glance at Ferrari shows that things could have been considerably worse.

    For the first time since Imola 2025, the Scuderia saw both cars eliminated in Q2, after which Lewis Hamilton explained that floor damage and deployment issues had hampered him. He estimated that the missing section of the floor had cost him three to four tenths, while the deployment issue had cost him another six tenths.

    That said, Ferrari already knew beforehand that the Las Vegas circuit would pose a considerable challenge for the SF-26. This is partly related to deliberate design choices. Ferrari runs a smaller turbo than most other power unit manufacturers, which gave the Maranello-based team an advantage off the line, particularly at the start of this year, but also has implications for outright power.

    Ferrari has received two ADUO tokens, meaning that, according to FIA measurements, the Italian manufacturer was between 2% and 4% down on the best internal combustion engine on the grid, Red Bull's unit.

    In Las Vegas, that is particularly painful, as clipping kicks in on the long straight and F1 drivers then have to rely solely on the internal combustion engine's power for a certain period.

    This partly explains why Ferrari is lacking so much top speed, although Verstappen sees another contributing factor.

    "They are obviously lacking top speed, but that has to do with the way they have designed their exhaust blowing as well. Because if you look at Haas, it's much less of an issue on the straight," Verstappen said when Motorsport.com asked him about Ferrari's disastrous qualifying session.

    "It's always a combination of how much downforce you want to have and how much power you're willing to sacrifice for it. The bigger you make that thing in front of the exhaust, the more power you lose. So to some extent, it's obviously in their own hands as well."

    Verstappen is referring to Ferrari's exhaust wing, a concept the Scuderia has pioneered this year. It helps generate additional downforce, but Verstappen believes it can also become an extra handicap on the long straights of tracks such as Las Vegas.

    "At a circuit like this, it can indeed be a major handicap. So perhaps you have to adapt a little to that, but that's obviously something they have to figure out for themselves!"

    Red Bull, meanwhile, has made significant strides on energy-poor circuits. At the start of this year, efficiency at such venues was still a weakness, particularly compared to Mercedes, but Red Bull Ford Powertrains has gained a great deal of knowledge in that area over the course of the season.

    "At the beginning, we obviously had a lot of problems with our car and we weren't quite on top of things with the power unit either. I think that automatically meant we had some easier steps to make, but we still made those steps. And that's something we should be proud of."

    Aside from outright performance, execution is also a major factor in Las Vegas. The slipstream had an extreme effect, with many drivers saying it was even greater than anything they had experienced before, making it all the more surprising that the Ferrari drivers were mostly without a slipstream during qualifying.

    Particularly given their lack of top speed, that seems a strange approach, although Verstappen preferred not to be too critical of Ferrari in that respect.

    "Well, that's judging from the outside. I just focus on our own team. Of course, our goal is always to make as few mistakes as possible and to stay on top of everything as much as we can. I think we did a pretty good job in terms of execution today."
