    汉密尔顿2026年失去‘最具市场价值运动员’称号

    汉密尔顿不再是体育界最具市场价值的运动员，甚至不再是最具市场价值的F1车手，因为他在最新由媒体和活动公司SportsPro发布的排名中落后于法拉利队友勒克莱尔。汉密尔顿在2025年SportsPro市场价值排名中位居第一，总市场价值得分为96.03分。2026年，汉密尔顿排名22位，市场价值得分为66.63分，与足球运动员内马尔和维尼修斯·儒尼奥尔相当。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿不再是体育界最具市场价值的运动员——甚至不再是最具市场价值的F1车手，因为在由媒体和活动公司SportsPro发布的最新排名中，他落后于法拉利车队队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔。

    汉密尔顿在2025年SportsPro运动员市场价值排名中位居第一，总市场价值得分为96.03分（满分100分）。

    2026年，汉密尔顿排名22位。他的市场价值得分为66.63分，与足球运动员内马尔和维尼修斯·若纳尔并列。

    汉密尔顿的队友勒克莱尔在榜单上领先，排名第9，市场价值得分为71.12分，比2025年榜单上的第25位，得分为75.03分有所上升。

    下一位最高的车手是马克斯·维斯塔潘（第48位，去年排名第81位），而2025年冠军兰多·诺里斯（第78位）现在排名在麦凯轮车队队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂（第56位）之后，去年他领先23位排在第41位。

    新冠军基米·安东内利是排名最低的F1车手，他在第126位进入前150名，比2025年的第78位下降了48位。

    MotoGP骑手马克·马奎兹是唯一一个未与F1挂钩的赛车运动运动员，排名第86位，比阿尔卑斯F1车手皮埃尔·加斯利高5位。

    白俄罗斯网球运动员阿莉娜·萨巴伦卡位居榜首，领先中国滑雪运动员谷爱凌、希腊篮球运动员吉安尼斯·安特托昆博、挪威足球运动员埃尔林·哈兰德和西班牙网球运动员卡洛斯·阿尔卡拉斯。

    美国运动员占据了前150名的32%，而英国和印度运动员各占7.3%的份额。

    足球（足球）有33名运动员上榜，占据了22%的份额，而赛车运动的8位代表（5.3%）将其排在第六位，排在网球和板球之后，但领先于高尔夫和拳击。

    NASCAR女车手托尼·布赖丁格和F1车手利亚姆·劳森从榜单上退下，此前他们在2025年曾上榜。

    市场推广评分是如何计算的——为什么汉密尔顿的评分低于勒克莱尔？

    市场可及性评分是品牌价值、势头和商业轨迹三个指标的组合，这三个指标是通过结合“数百个数据点”以及截至2025年7月1日和2026年6月30日超过3000个品牌合作记录来计算的。

    向下滚动查看赛车运动运动员在排名前150位中的位置。

    品牌价值是运动员品牌的强度和信任度，势头是运动员价值增长的速率，商业轨迹是运动员未来商业增长的潜力。

    在这三个指标中，最后一个指标（商业轨迹）的权重最小，分数是相对的而不是绝对的；萨巴连卡（Sabalenka）的排名是在考虑其他运动员的情况下确定的。

    勒克莱尔，29岁，品牌价值评分高于汉密尔顿，但动力评分较低。这位摩纳哥人的商业轨迹排名也提升了他的排名，与汉密尔顿相比有所提升。

    年龄和运动生涯的持久性对轨迹评分有影响：41岁的汉密尔顿与克里斯蒂亚诺·罗纳尔多和勒布朗·詹姆斯并列成为名单上最年长的运动员。

    此外，勒克莱尔在商业轨迹指标上得分很高，这意味着他的品牌力量尚未转化为与汉密尔顿相比的商业回报。

    勒克莱尔在品牌价值上得分高，这得益于他个人的风格和公众形象，以及他受众的规模和受众如何积极地与他品牌互动。

    克里斯·柯林斯，排名系统提供方的总裁对BlackBook Motorsport表示，勒克莱尔拥有世界上任何运动员中最强大的品牌之一。

    他在我们评估的247名运动员中品牌价值排名第二，这建立在如何展示自己、引人入胜的个人故事以及持续选择与他合作的优质合作伙伴的基础上。

    作为赛车场上最受关注的驾驶员之一，他的观众评分表现良好并不令人意外，这也强调了他的粉丝积极与他互动。他还受益于高观众覆盖度评分，这对于所有拥有全球形象的F1车手来说是预期结果。

    他还补充说，汉密尔顿排名不在前十的原因不是他的品牌，而是时间。41岁的他自然在职业生涯的前景上有所减少，前瞻性的评估应该会在他职业生涯的后期章节中看到一段伟大的职业生涯。

    英文原文

    Lewis Hamilton is no longer sport’s most marketable athlete - or even the most marketable Formula 1 driver, having fallen behind Ferrari team-mate Charles Leclerc - in the latest rankings created by media and events business SportsPro.

    Hamilton was top of the 2025 SportsPro marketability rankings for athletes, with a total marketability score of 96.03 out of 100.

    In 2026, Hamilton is ranked 22nd. His marketability score is 66.63, on par with footballers Neymar and Vinicius Junior.

    Hamilton’s team-mate Leclerc is ahead in ninth on the list with a marketability score of 71.12, up from 25th in the 2025 list with a score of 75.03.

    The next-highest driver is Max Verstappen (48th, up from 81st last year), while 2025 champion Lando Norris (78th) is now ranked behind McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri (56th) having been 23 places ahead in 41st last year.

    New champion Kimi Antonelli is the lowest placed F1 driver who made the top 150 in 126th, a 48-place drop from 78th in 2025.

    MotoGP rider Marc Marquez is the only motorsport athlete not tied to F1 on the list, ranking 86th, five places above Alpine F1 driver Pierre Gasly.

    Belarusian tennis player Aryna Sabalenka is top of the list with, ahead of Chinese skier Eileen Gu, Greek basketball player Giannis Antetokounmpo, Norwegian footballer Erling Haaland, and Spanish tennis player Carlos Alcaraz.

    American athletes make up 32% of the top 150 while British and Indian athletes are next with 7.3% of the pie apiece.

    Football (soccer) has 33 athletes on the list and a 22% share of the list while motorsport’s eight representatives (5.3%) put it sixth on the list of sports, behind tennis and cricket but ahead of golf and boxing.

    Female NASCAR driver Toni Breidinger and F1 driver Liam Lawson dropped off the list having appeared in 2025.

    How the marketability scores are calculated - why is Hamilton lower than Leclerc?

    The marketability score is a combination of brand equity, momentum, and commercial trajectory, three metrics that are calculated by combining "several hundred data points" and more than 3000 brand partnership records over July 1, 2025, and June 30, 2026.

    Scroll down to see how the motorsport athletes rank within the top 150.

    Brand equity is the strength and trust of the athlete’s brand, momentum is the rate at which the athlete’s value is growing, and commercial trajectory is the room for an athlete’s future commercial growth.

    The latter metric is given the smallest weighting out of the three, and the scores are relative rather than absolute; Sabalenka’s ranking puts her top with the other athletes in mind.

    Leclerc, 29, has a higher brand equity score than Hamilton but a lower momentum score. The Monegasque’s ranking of commercial trajectory also boosts his ranking compared to Hamilton.

    Age and longevity in the sport has an impact on the trajectory score: at 41 Hamilton is the joint-oldest athlete on the list with Cristiano Ronaldo and LeBron James.

    Furthermore, Leclerc scores highly on the commercial trajectory metric, implying that there is strength in his brand that hasn’t been converted into commercial returns compared to Hamilton.

    Leclerc scores high in brand equity due to his personal style and public presentation, plus the size of his audience and how actively that audience engages with his brand.

    Chris Collins, president of NorthStar (which powers the rankings) said to BlackBook Motorsport that Leclerc has "one of the strongest established brands of any athlete in the world.

    "He ranks second of all 247 athletes we assessed on brand equity, built on how he presents himself, a compelling personal story and premium partners who keep choosing to work with him.

    "As one of the most-followed drivers on the grid, it’s little surprise to see his audience scores performing well, but it also emphasises that his followers actively interact with his profile. He also benefits from a high audience breadth score, an expected outcome for all Formula 1 drivers thanks to their global profile."

    He added of Hamilton: "What keeps him below the top 10 isn’t his brand, it’s time. At 41, there is naturally less career runway ahead, and a forward-looking assessment should read a great career in its later chapters exactly that way."
