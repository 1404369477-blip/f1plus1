    克里斯蒂安·霍纳承认红牛车队过早地将潜力股推向维斯塔潘

    红牛车队第二车手位置一直是F1中最具压力的环境之一。尽管车队频繁更换高评价的潜力股以寻找与维斯塔潘的搭档，但前车队经理克里斯蒂安·霍纳现在公开质疑这种无情的车手培养逻辑。

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳承认红牛车队在对抗马克斯·维斯塔潘时过早地投入了年轻车手

    前红牛车队经理克里斯蒂安·霍纳表示，车队在2019年的"残酷"晋升体系下，让皮埃尔·加斯利在心理上崩溃了

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析

    红牛车队的第二个席位长期以来一直被视为一级方程式中最具压力的环境之一。当米尔顿凯恩斯的运营频繁地更换被高度评价的年轻车手，试图为马克斯·维斯塔潘找到合适的搭档时，前车队经理克里斯蒂安·霍纳现在公开质疑这种无情的车手培养体系的逻辑。

    在《高性能播客》中，霍纳回顾了他管理车队的日子，并承认他经常不同意将车手迅速投入到与一代天才对抗的深水区。

    “有些车手来得太早了，我并不完全同意他们进入红牛车队的速度，但红牛青训车队中有他们的决心，”霍纳透露。

    霍纳将皮埃尔·加斯利、亚历克斯·阿隆和利亚姆·劳森列为过早晋升到高级别座位的潜在例子。

    这种发展失败的例子之一是皮埃尔·加斯利在2019年与高级车队短暂的任职。在仅仅在姐妹车队托罗罗索（现更名为小红牛）度过一个完整赛季后，加斯利难以适应高度敏感的红牛底盘和维斯塔潘原始速度带来的巨大压力。

    Horner回忆起球队观看法国车手信心崩溃的准确时刻。在那年的奥地利大奖赛和匈牙利大奖赛中，维斯塔潘的速度如此之快，以至于他在赛道上连续两次将加斯利甩在身后。

    “你可以明显地看到他们感受到了那种压力，无论是皮埃尔，我记得他在奥地利和匈牙利大奖赛中连续两场比赛被超越，他在那些比赛中已经崩溃了，”Horner表示。

    匈牙利大奖赛之后，红牛队突然将加斯利降级回小红牛队，直到赛季结束，并用阿隆索取代了他。

    尽管Horner指出红牛体系通常通过允许车手返回青训车队而不是完全解雇他们来提供“降落伞”，但他并没有回避批评环境的残酷现实。

    “一方面，红牛所做的是非常慷慨的，但就其执行方式而言，你曾是当月的宠儿，直到你不再是，”霍纳总结道。

    Christian Horner admits Red Bull prospects were thrown in against Max Verstappen too soon

    Former Red Bull boss Christian Horner says the team's "brutal" promotion system mentally broke Pierre Gasly in 2019

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    The second seat at Red Bull Racing has long been viewed as one of the most high-pressure environments in Formula 1. While the Milton Keynes operation frequently cycled through highly rated prospects trying to find a match for Max Verstappen, former team principal Christian Horner is now openly questioning the logic of that ruthless driver pipeline.

    Speaking on the High Performance podcast, Horner reflected on his tenure managing the team and admitted that he frequently disagreed with how quickly drivers were thrown into the deep end against a generational talent.

    "Some of these drivers were arriving too soon, and I didn't completely agree with the speed at which they were arriving into Red Bull Racing, but there was a determination to have them from the Red Bull Junior pool," Horner revealed.

    Horner named Pierre Gasly, Alex Albon, and Liam Lawson as prime examples of prospects who were rushed into the senior seat before they were properly prepared.

    One example of this developmental failure was Pierre Gasly's short-lived stint with the senior team in 2019. Promoted after just one full season at sister outfit Toro Rosso (now Racing Bulls), Gasly struggled to adapt to the highly sensitive Red Bull chassis and the immense pressure of Verstappen's raw pace.

    Horner recalled the exact moment the team watched the French driver's confidence collapse. During both the Austrian and Hungarian Grand Prix that year, Verstappen's pace was so blisteringly fast that he lapped Gasly on track.

    "You could visibly see that they were feeling that pressure, whether it was Pierre, I remember [him] getting lapped in Austria and at the Hungarian Grand Prix two races in a row, and he was broken in those races," Horner stated.

    Immediately following the Hungarian Grand Prix, Red Bull abruptly demoted Gasly back to Toro Rosso for the remainder of the season, replacing him with Albon.

    While Horner noted that the Red Bull system usually provided a "parachute" by allowing drivers to return to the junior team rather than firing them completely, he did not shy away from criticising the harsh reality of the environment.

    "It was, on the one hand, hugely generous what Red Bull were doing, but it was quite brutal in terms of its execution that you were the flavour of the month until you weren't," Horner concluded.
