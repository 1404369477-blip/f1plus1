    安东内利有望超越维特尔获得四届F1冠军

    前哈斯车队负责人古恩特·施泰纳认为，安东内利将比四届冠军维特尔在F1的职业生涯更加成功。如果他在墨西哥大奖赛中获得前九名，他将打破维特尔创下的最年轻冠军记录。

    基米·安东内利得到了冈瑟·施泰纳的支持，有望超越塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔获得四个F1世界冠军

    前哈斯车队负责人冈瑟·施泰纳支持基米·安东内利超越塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔获得四个一级方程式世界冠军

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里有最新的银箭动态，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析

    前哈斯一级方程式车队负责人冈瑟·施泰纳认为，基米·安东内利将比四次世界冠军塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔在F1生涯中取得更大的成功。

    安东内利正站在赢得他首个F1世界冠军的边缘，如果他在本周末的墨西哥大奖赛中排名前九，他将被加冕。20岁的他将打破维特尔在2010年创下的F1最年轻世界冠军记录。

    维特尔在2010年至2013年间连续四年为红牛车队赢得世界冠军。

    施泰纳在《红旗播客》节目中讨论了安东内利的未来。

    当被问及他认为安东内利是否会比维特尔更成功时，施泰纳说：“绝对会。”，“我的意思是，维特尔很出色，但我的意思是，维特尔也处于正确的时间、正确的地点。但如果你看他后来去法拉利的时候，已经没有太多剩余了。塞巴斯蒂安非常出色，但他并不是像马克斯[维斯塔潘]那样非凡的天才，或者我认为基米也会是。”

    询问他是否认为安东内利将赢得超过四个冠军，斯特纳确认说：“是的。”

    在F1的第二赛季，安东内利在连续赢得中国和日本大奖赛之后，领跑车手积分榜。从那时起，他总共赢得了九场大奖赛。

    他目前以365分领跑积分榜，领先他的梅赛德斯队友乔治·拉塞尔，后者以242分排在第二。法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿以241分排在第三。

    “冠军越来越近，但我们的心态没有改变。思考结果并不能帮助我们实现它，”梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫在墨西哥大奖赛预览中说道。

    “我们将保持纪律，对待每一节练习都认真对待，专注于做好基础工作。”

    虽然安东内利本周末可能赢得车手总冠军，但梅赛德斯也有机会赢得车队总冠军。在美国大奖赛之后，梅赛德斯以607分领先，比法拉利多133分，法拉利积474分。

    Kimi Antonelli backed to surpass Sebastian Vettel's four F1 titles by Guenther Steiner

    Former Haas team principal Guenther Steiner has backed Kimi Antonelli to surpass Sebastian Vettel's four Formula 1 world titles

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Former Haas Formula 1 team principal Guenther Steiner believes Kimi Antonelli will go on to have a more successful F1 career than four-time champion Sebastian Vettel.

    Antonelli is on the verge of winning his maiden F1 championship, and will be crowned at this weekend's Mexican Grand Prix if he finishes in the top nine. At 20, he would break Vettel's record as F1's youngest champion, set in 2010.

    Vettel went on to win four consecutive titles with Red Bull between 2010 and 2013.

    Steiner discussed Antonelli's future during an appearance on The Red Flags Podcast.

    "Absolutely," Steiner said when asked whether he thinks Antonelli will become more successful than Vettel. "I mean Vettel was good, but I mean Vettel was also at the right time, at the right place. But if you see when he went to Ferrari, there was not a lot left. Sebastian was very good, but he was not an exceptional talent like Max [Verstappen], or I think Kimi will be."

    Asked to clarify if he thinks Antonelli will win more than four championships, Steiner confirmed: "Yes."

    In just his second season in F1, Antonelli took the lead of the drivers' championship after back-to-back wins at the Chinese and Japanese Grands Prix. Since then, he has gone on to claim nine grand prix wins in total.

    He currently leads the standings with 365 points, ahead of his Mercedes team-mate George Russell in second on 242 points. Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton follows in third on 241 points.

    "The championships are moving closer, but our mindset does not change. Thinking about the outcome won’t help us achieve it," Mercedes team chief Toto Wolff said in the Brackley outfit's Mexican Grand Prix preview.

    "We will stay disciplined, take each session as it comes, and concentrate on doing the fundamentals well."

    While Antonelli could claim the drivers' championship this weekend, Mercedes also has the opportunity to secure the constructors' title. Mercedes leads on 607 points after the United States Grand Prix, 133 points clear of Ferrari on 474.
