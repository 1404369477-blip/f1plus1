    拉塞尔批评维斯塔潘：他自视高人一等

    在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛第三节自由练习中，拉塞尔与维斯塔潘发生碰撞，赛后拉塞尔公开批评维斯塔潘。

    乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯碰撞后猛烈抨击马克斯·维斯塔潘：“他走路的样子好像自己比所有人都更大、更好。”

    在第三节自由练习中发生该事件后，拉塞尔对维斯塔潘极为不满。

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯；马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队；乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯。

    乔治·拉塞尔在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛第三节自由练习中与马克斯·维斯塔潘发生碰撞后，对后者进行了抨击。

    维斯塔潘在第14号弯前超越了前方的梅赛德斯赛车，以求获得干净空气，但拉塞尔随即反超，荷兰人试图关门未果。拉塞尔最终强行将对手挤开，红牛车手随后向他竖了中指。

    拉塞尔对维斯塔潘的驾驶方式感到愤怒，这也让后者收到了赛会的警告。“我认为警告是合理的，但你知道……规则里并没有说不能超越正在开始飞驰圈的人，但当你这么做时，你就是在坑害这个人，”这位英国车手对意大利天空体育表示。

    “他每周都和许多车手这样干，大家都接受了，但我不会接受有人这样坑我，还装作自己比所有人都高人一等。总得有人站出来对抗那家伙。”

    与此同时，维斯塔潘在排位赛后的新闻发布会上对情况的评价很直接：“什么都没发生。”

    警告基本上无关紧要，因为与训诫不同，如果再次犯规，警告不会导致更重的处罚。

    尽管如此，维斯塔潘仍被认定对这起事件负有责任，以下是干事们的看法。

    干事报告写道：“两辆车都在接近14号弯，3号车在63号车前方。3号车比预期更早刹车，并在刹车时改变方向。从后方接近的63号车向左移动，试图从内线超车。在这一操作过程中，63号车的前轴位于3号车后视镜的前方。随后两车发生了接触。”

    “在听证会上，车手们还讨论了关于飞驰圈准备的非正式君子协定，以及在这种情况下车手之间应有的行为。干事们注意到了这些解释，但认为此类非正式谅解对事件评估的相关性有限。决定性的考量是两辆车的实际动作及其相对位置。

    “因此，干事们审查了3号车的刹车和变向、63号车的避让动作和位置，并考虑了适用的驾驶标准指南。

    “干事们认为，3号车在刹车下变向是不可预测的，并导致了63号车不得不向内移动以避免接触的情况。虽然干事们认为该动作不够严重，不足以给予更严厉的处罚，但他们得出结论，在这些情况下刹车下变向低于预期的驾驶标准。

    “考虑到事件的情况，干事们确定车手警告是适当的。”

    维斯塔潘和拉塞尔将在周六的拉斯维加斯大奖赛中共享头排，他们分别以第二和第三名排位，而最快圈速的安东内利则被罚退五位发车。

    George Russell slams Max Verstappen after Las Vegas clash: ‘He walks around like he’s bigger and better than everyone’

    Russell was highly frustrated with Verstappen following the incident that occurred in FP3

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    George Russell has hit out at Max Verstappen after they collided in Free Practice 3 at Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Verstappen overtook the Mercedes ahead of Turn 14 in a quest for free air, but Russell moved to get back ahead, with the Dutchman unsuccessfully closing the door. Russell eventually muscled his rival out of the way, with the Red Bull racer later giving him the middle finger.

    Russell was indignant at Verstappen’s driving, which earned him a warning from the stewards. “I think a warning was fine but, you know... There's nothing in the rules to say you cannot overtake someone who is starting the lap, but when you do this, you are screwing this person over,” the Briton told Sky Sport Italy.

    “He does this week in week out with many drivers and everybody accepts it, and I'm not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they're bigger and better than everyone else. Somebody's got to stand up to that guy.”

    Meanwhile, Verstappen’s assessment of the situation in the post-qualifying press conference was straightforward: “Nothing happened.”

    A warning is largely inconsequential as, unlike a reprimand, it cannot lead to a bigger penalty in the event of a repeat offence.

    Still, Verstappen was found responsible for the incident, and here is how the stewards viewed it.

    “Both cars were approaching Turn 14, with Car 3 ahead of Car 63,” the stewards’ report read. “Car 3 braked earlier than expected and changed direction under braking. Car 63, approaching from behind, moved to the left and attempted to overtake on the inside. During the manoeuvre, the front axle of Car 63 was ahead of the mirror of Car 3. The two cars subsequently made contact.

    “During the hearing, the drivers also discussed the informal gentlemen’s agreements concerning the preparation for a flying lap and the conduct expected between drivers in such situations. The Stewards noted these explanations but considered that such informal understandings were of limited relevance to the assessment of the incident. The decisive considerations were the actual movements of both cars and their relative positions.

    “The Stewards therefore examined the braking and change of direction of Car 3, the evasive manoeuvre and positioning of Car 63 and taking into account the applicable Driving Standards Guidelines.

    “The Stewards considered that Car 3’s change of direction under braking was unpredictable and contributed to the situation in which Car 63 had to move to the inside to avoid contact. While the Stewards did not consider the manoeuvre sufficiently serious to warrant a more severe penalty, they concluded that changing direction under braking in these circumstances fell below the standard of driving expected.

    “Taking into account the circumstances of the incident, the Stewards determined that a Driver Warning was appropriate.”

    Verstappen and Russell will share the front row in Saturday’s Las Vegas GP, having qualified respectively second and third with pacesetter Kimi Antonelli taking a five-place grid penalty.
