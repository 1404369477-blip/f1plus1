    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括一级方程式新闻、车手更新、赛车开发和从布拉克利和布里克斯顿的赛事分析

    在20岁68天大的时候赢得2026年世界车手锦标赛是一项巨大的、打破历史记录的成就。但对于安德烈亚·基米·安东内利来说，墨西哥城大奖赛最令人震撼的部分甚至没有发生在赛道上。它发生在终点线后。

    与赛车冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘和亚军刘易斯·汉密尔顿共享领奖台已经足够令人敬畏。然后，在一场精彩的即兴体育精神时刻，这两位重量级人物将新晋梅赛德斯冠军扛在肩上。这是对那个刚刚改写F1记录簿的孩子的终极‘欢迎加入俱乐部’的举动。

    在Nu Silver Arrows广播节目中，安东内利终于道出了那一刻的情感负担。

    从那些在赛车运动绝对巅峰较量了十年的家伙那里得到公众的认可，让这位20岁的年轻人完全不知所措。

    ‘领奖台上的这一刻是我永远珍藏的记忆之一，’安东内利坦白道。‘首先，看到两位为这项运动创造历史的两位车手所给予的如此多的尊重和欣赏……而且他们仍在这样做，因为他们仍在顶级赛场上竞争。’

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队、安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯

    突如其来的重活儿让这位新冠军完全措手不及，瞬间消融了他整个赛季所保持的冰冷镇定。

    “他们所做的一切，在领奖台上扶我起来，这是我永远都会感激的时刻，”他承认。“这对我来说也是意外的，一旦他们扶我起来，我就非常高兴，我也非常激动。”

    领奖台上的庆祝活动完美地捕捉了F1火炬的传递。通过在赫尔曼诺斯·罗德里格斯赛道锁定冠军，安东内利正式打破了塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔长期保持的记录，成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。为了锦上添花，他还打破了长达73年的干旱，成为自1953年传奇人物阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来第一位赢得世界冠军的意大利车手。

    尽管那些巨大的统计数据变化，赛后仪式中的人性化元素显然给这位年轻的天才留下了最深刻的印象。安东内利知道何时抓住一个标志性的拍照机会，他完全没有打算让这段记忆被埋藏在相机胶卷里。

    “那张照片，我会把它装进相框，挂在墙上，因为这是一段如此特别的记忆，一个如此特别的时刻，”他微笑着总结道。

    Kimi Antonelli reacts to Max Verstappen and Lewiw Hamilton on Mexico podium: “I will frame it”

    Kimi Antonelli reacts to Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton hoisting him on the podium after his historic 2026 title win

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Securing the 2026 world drivers' championship at just 20 years and 68 days old is a massive, history-book-shattering achievement. But for Andrea Kimi Antonelli, the most overwhelming part of the Mexico City Grand Prix did not even happen on the asphalt. It happened after the chequered flag.

    Sharing the podium with race winner Max Verstappen and runner-up Lewis Hamilton is intimidating enough. Then, in a brilliant moment of unscripted sportsmanship, the two heavyweights hoisted the newly minted Mercedes champion on their shoulders. It was the ultimate 'welcome to the club' gesture for the kid who just rewrote the F1 record books.

    Jumping on the Nu Silver Arrows Radio Show, Antonelli finally broke down the emotional weight of that exact moment.

    Getting the public seal of approval from guys who have spent a decade trading blows at the absolute pinnacle of motorsport left the 20-year-old completely floored.

    "This moment on the podium is one of those memories that will live forever," Antonelli confessed. "First of all, to see so much respect and appreciation from the two drivers that have made the history of the sport... and they're still doing so because they're still competing at the top."

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    The impromptu heavy-lifting session caught the new champion entirely off guard, instantly melting away that icy composure he carried all season.

    "What they did, picking me up on the podium, is a moment that I will always be grateful for," he admitted. "It was also unexpected for me, and once they picked me up, I was just so happy, and I got so emotional as well."

    That podium celebration perfectly captured the passing of the torch in Formula 1. By locking down the crown at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, Antonelli officially obliterated Sebastian Vettel's long-standing record to become the youngest world champion in F1 history. Just for good measure, he also snapped a brutal 73-year drought, becoming the first driver from Italy to win the World Championship since the legendary Alberto Ascari did it way back in 1953.

    Despite those massive statistical flexes, the human element of the post-race ceremony clearly left the biggest mark on the young prodigy. Antonelli knows an iconic photo op when he sees one, and he has zero intention of letting this memory get buried in the camera roll.

    "That picture, I will frame it and put it on the wall because it's such a special memory and such a special moment," he concluded with a smile.
