    维斯塔潘：我真的以为今年我们不会赢得任何比赛

    在连续八次登上领奖台进入拉斯维加斯大奖赛之际，维斯塔潘看起来像是本 decade 早期统治 F1 的力量。然而，在 3 月时，这位四次世界冠军曾准备好迎接自 2015 年以来的首次无冠赛季。

    维斯塔潘：我真的以为今年我们不会赢得任何比赛

    维斯塔潘承认他在 红牛 的糟糕开局后预计 2026 年将无冠，并称赞车队在赛季末期的强劲反弹

    对于 红牛 的粉丝来说，关注 红牛 Racing，获取 F1 新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和赛事分析

    在拉斯维加斯媒体日上发言时，维斯塔潘回顾了红牛车队在2026年一级方程式赛车中进行的全面底盘和引擎大改后，即车队首次运行其内部红牛福特动力单元的中期复苏规模。

    “我在前几轮比赛后是否个人期望会是这样的？当然不是，”维斯塔潘说。“我真心以为我们今年不会赢得任何一场比赛，而现在我们一直在为胜利或接近胜利而战。”

    在澳大利亚、中国和日本的头三场比赛中，维斯塔潘仅获得12分——在墨尔本排名第六，在上海退赛，在铃鹿排名第八——同时公开表示小红牛22在中国由于急转弯平衡问题而“完全无法驾驶”。他连续四场比赛没有登上领奖台，在15场大奖赛中没有获得第一名，在中国、摩纳哥、英国和荷兰共遭遇四次退赛。

    当被问及红牛车队的2026年复苏是否反映了其一年前的调整时，维斯塔潘立即关闭了这种比较。

    “不，完全不相关。因为我们今年面临的问题和去年非常不同，”他说。 “所以，这是在提高汽车性能的同时，也解决了一些我们的问题的组合，这自然会给我们带来更多的性能。”

    “这种逐步进步不仅限于空气动力学。 “在发动机方面，我们也更清楚地了解了我们想要从发动机中得到什么，”维斯塔潘指出。 “尽管如此，总是有改进的空间；我们知道这一点。这就是我们一直在努力的事情。”

    “回报在秋天到来。维斯塔潘在10月4日在马来西亚打破了P1的干旱，一周后在新加坡的冲刺赛和大奖赛中也取得了胜利，并在11月1日在墨西哥城又赢得了第三个胜利。加上在圣保罗的第二名，这一波势头使他以279分排名车手锦标赛第二，比新晋冠军基米·安东内利落后111分，但比乔治·拉塞尔高出17分，同时使红牛车队在车队锦标赛中保持第三的位置。”

    “向整个团队致敬；他们做得真的很棒，”维斯塔潘说。 “但现在，我们也向前看，是的，我们还在寻找我们的一些弱点和我们想要为明年改变的事情，希望使汽车变得更好。”

    Max Verstappen: “I really thought that we would not win a race this year”

    Max Verstappen admits he expected a winless 2026 after Red Bull's rough start, crediting the team for a late surge

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Heading into the Las Vegas Grand Prix on a run of eight consecutive podiums, Max Verstappen looks every bit the force who dominated Formula 1 earlier this decade. Back in March, however, the four-time world champion was braced for his first winless campaign since 2015.

    Speaking during Las Vegas media day, Verstappen reflected on the scale of Red Bull Racing’s mid-season recovery during Formula 1’s sweeping 2026 chassis and engine overhaul - the team’s first year running its in-house Red Bull Ford power unit.

    "Did I personally expect it to be like this after the first few rounds? For sure not," Verstappen said. "I really thought that we would not win a race this year and now we are constantly fighting for a win or close to it."

    Across the first three rounds in Australia, China, and Japan, Verstappen managed just 12 points - finishing sixth in Melbourne, retiring in Shanghai, and crossing eighth at Suzuka - while publicly describing the RB22 as "completely undriveable" in China due to acute cornering balance issues. He went four races without a podium and endured 15 grands prix without P1, absorbing four retirements across China, Monaco, Great Britain, and the Netherlands.

    Asked whether Red Bull’s 2026 recovery mirrored its adjustments from a year earlier, Verstappen shut down the comparison immediately.

    "No, no, not related at all. Because we, this year, have very, very different problems to last year," he said. "So, it was a combination of putting more performance on the car, but at the same time also just fixing some of our problems, which automatically would give us more performance."

    That step-by-step progress extended beyond aerodynamics. "Also, engine side, we understood a lot more what we wanted from the engine," Verstappen noted. "Still, there’s always room for improvement; we know that. That’s what we are constantly working on."

    The payoff arrived in autumn. Verstappen broke his P1 drought on 4 October in Malaysia, swept the sprint and grand prix in Singapore a week later, and added a third victory in Mexico City on 1 November. Coupled with second place in São Paulo, the surge lifted him to second in the drivers’ championship on 279 points - 111 behind newly crowned champion Kimi Antonelli, yet 17 clear of George Russell - while keeping Red Bull third in the constructors’ fight.

    "Hats off to the whole team; they’ve done a really good job," Verstappen said. "But now, we also look forward, and yeah, still look into some of the weaknesses that we have and things that we want to change for next year to hopefully make the car even better."
