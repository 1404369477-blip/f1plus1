    诺里斯新青训车队LN4 Fusion签下前小红牛青训车手奥利弗·戈特

    诺里斯的新青训车队LN4 Fusion签下了前小红牛青训车手奥利弗·戈特，他将加入F2赛事，这是他F2生涯的第三个赛季。

    兰多·诺里斯的LN4融合车队签下了前红牛青训车队车手奥利弗·戈特，作为其首个F2车手

    戈特将从MP车队加入，开始他的第三个F2赛季，今年已经获得了两次领奖台位置

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    兰多·诺里斯的新青训车队LN4融合签下了前红牛青训车队车手奥利弗·戈特，作为2027年F2赛季的首位车手

    22岁的车手将从MP Motorsport转会至F2，这是他第三个赛季参加F2，此前他已经在本年度获得了两次领奖台的成绩。

    LN4 Fusion车队在上个月获得了F2的参赛资格，成为锦标赛下三个赛季周期中唯一的新车队。该车队由麦凯轮一级方程式车队的车手在九月创立，并将同时运营F3、意大利F4和英国F4的比赛车辆。

    戈特海姆曾是红牛青训项目的成员两年，于2025年底离开。在晋升至F2之前，他在2022年赢得了11场胜利，获得了欧罗巴公式开放赛冠军，并在FIA F3中赢得了两场比赛。

    在宣布这笔交易时，LN4 Fusion首席执行官丹·理查兹表示，德国车手的经验使他成为车队第一个赛季的最佳选择。

    "奥利弗是我们队的绝佳选择。他对赛车、赛道和锦标赛都非常了解，他今年的领奖台表现也显示他已经采取了必要的步骤来争夺领奖台，”理查兹说。

    "我们希望为他提供他所需要的环境和支持，以继续朝着他的最终目标迈进，并且我们相信，我们一起可以有一个强劲的赛季，”戈特说。

    戈特表示，他认为2027年可能是他争夺冠军的最佳机会。

    "我非常激动能够加入LN4 Fusion车队参加2027赛季！这是一个非常激动人心的项目，我迫不及待地想要成为其中的一员。我真心觉得明年可能会是我的年份，我决心充分利用这次机会，”他说。

    “作为德国赛车运动团队与奥迪合作的一部分，我已经在奥迪的F1模拟器中完成了几节练习，这让我在即将到来的赛季之前获得了有用的经验和赛道洞察。”

    LN4 Fusion的欧洲运营将由Prema车队的前成员领导，前Prema老板Rene Rosin担任顾问和车队负责人，前Prema技术总监Guillaume Capietto担任赛车总监。

    Lando Norris's LN4 Fusion signs former Red Bull junior Oliver Goethe as its first F2 driver

    Goethe will join from MP Motorsport for his third F2 season after two podiums this year

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Lando Norris's new junior team LN4 Fusion has signed former Red Bull junior Oliver Goethe as its first driver for the 2027 Formula 2 season.

    The 22-year-old will make the move from MP Motorsport for his third season in F2, after celebrating two podium finishes so far this year.

    LN4 Fusion was granted an entry to F2 last month, as the only new team for the championship's next three-year cycle. The McLaren Formula 1 driver launched the team in September, and it will also run cars in Formula 3, Italian Formula 4 and British F4.

    Goethe was part of Red Bull's junior programme for two years before leaving at the end of 2025. Before stepping up to F2, he won the Euroformula Open title in 2022 with 11 victories, and took two wins in FIA Formula 3.

    Announcing the deal, LN4 Fusion chief executive Dan Richards said the German's experience made him the right choice for the team's first season.

    "Oliver is a great choice for our team. He knows the car, the tracks and the championship well, and his podiums this year show that he has taken the steps needed to fight at the front," Richards said.

    "We want to give him the environment and support he needs to continue making progress towards his ultimate goal, and we are confident that together we can have a strong season."

    Goethe said he believes 2027 could be his best chance yet to fight for the title.

    "I'm absolutely thrilled to be joining LN4 Fusion for the 2027 season! It's a really exciting project, and I can't wait to be part of it. I genuinely feel that next year could be my year, and I'm determined to make the most of this opportunity," he said.

    "As part of the cooperation between Motorsport Team Germany and Audi, I have already completed several sessions in Audi's F1 simulator, gaining useful experience and insight into the tracks ahead of the upcoming season."

    LN4 Fusion's European operation will be led by former members of the Prema team, with ex-Prema boss Rene Rosin on board as a consultant and team principal, and former Prema technical chief Guillaume Capietto as racing director.
