    安东内利包揽卡塔尔周五两节练习最快

    安东内利在2026年F1卡塔尔大奖赛周五的两节练习中均领先于梅赛德斯队友拉塞尔，第二节练习两度出示红旗。勒克莱尔表示法拉利速度“非常非常差”。

    安东内利在2026年F1卡塔尔大奖赛周五的两节练习中都领先于梅赛德斯队友拉塞尔，其中第二节练习两度因红旗中断。

    有些车手比其他车手更挣扎——少数车手在开局几节练习中损失了大量行驶时间——但谁才是卡塔尔周五的赢家与输家？

    如果你问勒克莱尔，汉密尔顿在第二节练习中因动力问题把车停到路边，很可能是法拉利最不担心的事！

    汉密尔顿在练习中使用的是较旧的引擎，因此勒克莱尔似乎并未因这一问题而不安。更大的问题在于，勒克莱尔认为“速度就是非常非常差”。

    从今天早上第一圈到第二节练习赛最后一圈，我们都非常慢，很难搞清楚问题到底出在哪里。

    勒克莱尔打滑后更换底板浪费了时间，但这只是分散了对法拉利真正问题的注意力。无论从哪个角度看，这辆车都很慢，他们有一个漫长的夜晚来尝试解决。 - 杰克·贝尼恩

    倍耐力的轮胎在卡塔尔赛道上备受折磨——这已使其成为本赛季对轮胎最具挑战性的一站。

    卢赛尔赛道因为高速弯角会带来颗粒化、高磨损、高胎体温度以及疲劳风险，加上碎石带来的额外危险，而今年情况比赛前预测的更糟。

    风化后的赛道表面变得更加粗糙，再加上2026年的下压力与性能水平，两者共同作用产生了高程度的局部颗粒化，导致某些轮胎组在仅仅15圈后就在局部区域达到100%的磨损。

    尽管使用了本赛季最高的胎压，并且规定每组轮胎的最长使用段为27圈，倍耐力今晚仍在进行分析，以确定是否需要在排位赛日之前采取更多措施，以避免正赛中出现任何风险。——乔恩·诺布尔

    在连续两个周末未能登上领奖台、此前已锁定两项世界冠军之后，梅赛德斯看起来处于非常有利的位置，有望在这里终结这一小段低谷。

    很难指望比这更好的周五了。赛车看起来非常平衡，在所有弯道类型中都表现出色，单圈优势巨大，长距离表现也同样强劲。

    两位车手似乎对赛车的感觉相当满意，而所有竞争对手都感到担忧，认为梅赛德斯自成一档。

    因此，这对梅赛德斯来说，是长久以来表现最好的一个周末了。——斯科特·米切尔-马尔姆

    尽管梅赛德斯认为红牛可能在排位赛中成为其最接近的对手，但目前这与红牛和马克斯·维斯塔潘在最近几场比赛中设定的标准相差甚远。

    周五晚上，红牛阵营明显弥漫着不悦的气氛，维斯塔潘抱怨赛车感觉脱节，存在严重的转向问题需要连夜解决，而且总体而言，红牛在赛车平衡方面看起来远未理顺。

    技术总监皮埃尔·瓦什并不乐观地认为差距能够显著缩小，因为差距实在太大了。所以目前看来，红牛在周五遭遇了一个令人警醒的日子，这使得他们对本周末剩余时间的期望低于其近期强劲表现本会让其在进入本周末时所抱有的信心。——SMM

    你可能会看看小红牛的表现然后想，嗯，像往常一样领跑中游车队，没什么可看的。

    但深入剖析一下，这里其实还有更多情况。

    它最大的问题是直线速度，但正如利亚姆·劳森所说，那都是“我们能看到也应该能解决的问题”。

    他表示，车队在练习赛之间对赛车设置进行了调整，调整幅度在F1中极为罕见；车队领队艾伦·珀曼证实，这次调整主要是为了增加赛车的旋转能力，因为大多数车队都在与转向不足作斗争。

    劳森认为：“我们在第一节练习中就已经有一辆相当不错的赛车，现在我们的赛车更快了”，并且车队在长距离跑动中与下一支只使用中性胎的车队（奥迪最接近，但使用的是硬胎）之间有着很大的差距。——JB

    加斯利的打滑以及随后赛车陷入沙坑，让他在第二节练习中损失了宝贵的时间，而这一节练习是最能代表排位赛条件的。

    这位法国人表示，他“可能本不该冒险”，因为轮胎温度低于工作温度，他压到了路肩，随后失去了对赛车的控制——在此过程中损坏了赛车的底板。

    此外，他对赛车调校“并不完全满意”——阿尔派 董事总经理 Steve Nielsen 表示，平衡性“远未达到所需水平”。

    考虑到高速性能是 阿尔派 的最大弱点，这条赛道本来就不太可能适合他们，但如今加斯利在进入周六前处境更加被动。——Samarth Kanal

    卡马拉周五顺利无碍，这对这位新秀来说是个宽慰，他在主场比赛中经历了 F1 生涯的艰难开局。

    在卡塔尔，卡马拉排在哈斯队友奥利·贝尔曼之前，但他的长距离节奏更为重要。

    这位巴西车手在第二次练习赛中与贝尔曼不相上下，在他的那一段（stint）里比这位英国人慢了不到十分之一秒。

    这看起来是他这次客串出场以来最有用的测试，为他的全赛季F1首秀做准备。——SK

    对阿斯顿·马丁来说，这个周末看起来比拉斯维加斯稍好一些，但必须加上一个极大的星号：情况本来就不可能更糟了。

    就目前而言，所谓更好也许只是意味着不用和凯迪拉克缠斗。没有长距离跑（long run）可以用来评判这支车队，部分原因是当天最大的问题——这辆车在这样一条容易损坏零件的赛道上似乎并不特别结实。

    阿斯顿·马丁选择在诸多干扰下结束当天的工作，以便尽可能修复问题，因为他们在各节练习与排位之间以及期间有大量停摆时间用来尝试修复损伤。

    迈克·克拉克预计晚上要用胶水把碳纤维碎片粘回去，兰斯·斯特罗尔有背部问题，这看起来是一个棘手的周末开局，而相对而言，阿斯顿·马丁原本对这个周末抱有略高一些的期望。——SMM

    威廉姆斯周五徘徊在第15名左右，两位车手都没能开个好头，卡洛斯·塞恩斯和亚历克斯·阿尔本在第一节练习中都有失误。

    塞恩斯的长距离平均速度比阿尔本更快，但两人都远远落后于中游其他车队——我们的分析认为威廉姆斯比次好的车队奥迪慢了将近一秒，也落后于哈斯。

    这位西班牙车手表示他未能“完成”自己的单圈。“总是有突然的失控，总是有转向不足，总是有一种可能不适合整圈的平衡，很难把它收尾。”

    这对排位赛来说不是好兆头。——天空体育

    英文原文

    Kimi Antonelli led Mercedes team-mate George Russell in both Friday Formula 1 practice sessions for the 2026 Qatar Grand Prix, including an FP2 that was punctuated by two red flags.

    Some drivers struggled more than others - and a few lost considerable running over the opening practice sessions - but who are the winners and losers from Friday in Qatar?

    If you ask Charles Leclerc, Lewis Hamilton pulling over to the side of the road with a power issue in FP2 was likely the least of Ferrari's worries!

    Hamilton was running an older engine for practice, so Leclerc seemed unperturbed by the issue. The bigger problem is that the "pace has just been very, very poor", reckons Leclerc.

    "We were just very slow from the first lap this morning to the last lap in FP2 and it's very difficult to understand what is the outlier."

    It didn't help that time was wasted changing Leclerc's floor after a spin, but that just distracted from what is Ferrari's real issue. It's slow by any measure and has a big night to try and fix it. - Jack Benyon

    Pirelli’s rubber is taking a battering around the Qatar track - which has sealed its status as the most challenging of the season for tyres.

    The Lusail venue delivers graining, high wear, high carcass temperatures because of the fast corners and fatigue risks, plus extra jeopardy caused by gravel stones - and this year things are worse than predicted pre-event.

    A combination of the weathered track surface that has got rougher allied to 2026 downforce and performance levels have come together to produce high levels of localised graining that is resulting in some sets reaching 100% wear in localised areas after just 15 laps.

    Despite the highest tyre pressures of the season, and a 27-lap maximum stint length for each set having been laid down, Pirelli is conducting analysis tonight to see if even more action needs to be taken ahead of qualifying day to avoid any risk of trouble in the race. - Jon Noble

    After two consecutive weekends without a podium finish since wrapping up both its world championships, Mercedes looks in a mega place to end that mini streak here.

    It could hardly have hoped for a better Friday. It looks very well balanced, strong in all corner types, and there's a massive one-lap advantage and in the long run too.

    Both drivers seem pretty happy with how the car feels, and all of its rivals are worried and feel that Mercedes is in a league of its own.

    So this is about as good as any weekend has looked for Mercedes in a long time. - Scott Mitchell-Malm

    Though Mercedes thinks Red Bull could emerge as its nearest rival come qualifying, right now this is miles off the standard that Red Bull and Max Verstappen have been setting in recent races.

    There was a distinct air of unhappiness from the Red Bull camp on Friday evening, with Verstappen bemoaning a car that feels disconnected, a heavy steering problem needing resolving overnight, and just generally Red Bull not looking nearly as sorted with its car balance.

    Technical director Pierre Wache is not optimistic that the gap can be closed significantly because it is so big. So this is looking like at the moment Red Bull suffering a chastening Friday that leaves it with lower hopes for the rest of the weekend than its recent mega form would have had it believing in coming into the weekend. - SMM

    You might look at Racing Bulls' performance and think, hmm, leading the midfield as is pretty much usual, nothing to see here.

    Peel back the layers and there's quite a lot more going on here.

    Its biggest issue is straightline speed, but, as Liam Lawson puts it, that's all "stuff we can see and should be able to fix".

    He says the team made a set-up change to an extent that is rarely seen between practice sessions in F1, which team boss Alan Permane confirmed was about adding more rotation into the car with most teams struggling with understeer.

    "We already had quite a good car in P1 and now we have an even faster car," reckons Lawson, and the team has a big gap to the next team that only ran the mediums (Audi was closest but on the hards) on long runs. - JB

    Gasly’s spin and subsequent beaching cost him valuable time in FP2, the session most representative of qualifying conditions.

    The Frenchman said he “should have probably not gone for it” as the tyres were below temperature, he hit the kerbs, and then lost control of the car - damaging the car’s floor in the process.

    Furthermore he was “not fully pleased” with the set-up - and Alpine’s managing director Steve Nielsen said the balance is “nowhere near where it needs to be”.

    This was never likely to be a great Alpine track given high-speed performance is its biggest weakness but now Gasly is even more on the back foot heading into Saturday. - Samarth Kanal

    Camara’s trouble-free Friday will come as a relief to the rookie who endured a rocky start to F1 in his home race.

    In Qatar, Camara finished ahead of Haas team-mate Ollie Bearman but his long-run pace is of greater importance.

    The Brazilian was right on par with Bearman in FP2, under a tenth slower than the Briton over his stint.

    It’s looking like the most useful running yet in this cameo ahead of his full-season F1 debut. - SK

    This is looking like a slightly better weekend for Aston Martin than in Las Vegas, but that comes with the extremely big asterisk that it couldn't really be worse.

    As it stands, better might just mean not fighting with Cadillac. There are no long runs to be able to judge the team on partly because of the biggest issue of the day, which is that the car didn't seem particularly robust around the challenges of such a parts-breaking circuit.

    Aston Martin opted to call it a day with all the disruptions in order to fix what it could, having had a lot of downtime between and during the sessions to try and repair damage.

    Mike Krack predicted an evening bonding bits of carbon back together with glue, Lance Stroll has a back problem, and this looks like a tricky start to a weekend that Aston Martin had, in relative terms, slightly higher hopes for. - SMM

    Williams hovered around P15 on Friday and neither driver started on the front foot, as both Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon had offs in FP1.

    Sainz’s long run was faster than Albon’s on average, but the pair are well behind the rest of the midfield - our analysis putting Williams almost a second behind next-best team Audi and behind Haas.

    The Spaniard said he failed to “close” his laps. “There's always snaps, always understeer, there's always a balance that maybe doesn't suit the whole lap and it's difficult to close it.”

    That doesn’t bode well for qualifying. - SK
