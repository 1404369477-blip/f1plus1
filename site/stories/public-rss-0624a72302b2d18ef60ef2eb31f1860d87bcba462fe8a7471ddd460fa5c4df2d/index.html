    前F1车手丹尼尔·里卡多在美国德克萨斯州奥斯汀的现场表演中与美国歌手兼词曲作者罗布·巴伊德共同登台。

    丹尼尔·里卡多在美国大奖赛前夕的现场表演令F1粉丝感到惊喜

    前F1车手丹尼尔·里卡多与音乐家罗布·贝尔德一起吹奏口琴，令粉丝感到惊喜

    丹尼尔·里卡多，Drive to Survive第七季

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    前一级方程式车手丹尼尔·里卡多在美国德克萨斯州奥斯汀的舞台上出现了一个惊喜。

    这位澳大利亚人与歌手兼词曲作者罗布·贝尔德一起加入了福特赛车和Enchanté快闪活动，在周五晚上一起演奏他与美国大奖赛旅途中共创作的原创歌曲。

    一位粉丝在Instagram上发布的表演视频，截至写作时已经获得了超过7000个赞。一位粉丝评论说：“这治愈了我心中的某一部分，”另一位粉丝补充说：“我敢打赌他上辈子要么是鲍勃·迪伦的兄弟，要么是个牛仔哈哈。我们爱你，丹尼尔。”

    “在这个时候，没有人关心比赛。很高兴看到丹尼尔回来了，即使他不再赛车，”另一位粉丝写道，另一位粉丝写道：“作为一个音乐家和蜜獾粉丝，和丹尼·里卡多一起演奏是我的梦想。”

    “我是说真的很长的一段路。沙漠、乡村小道、游泳洞，所有这些。说实话，我迫不及待了。”

    这次长途旅行是推广新系列 Enchanté Collection 的活动之一，名为《长途》。这些新产品在奥斯汀的 Distribution Hall 的临时展位上出售，展位上还有食品、饮料、闪粉纹身和赛车模拟器。

    里卡多也被看到在支持 小红牛 车队的车手利亚姆·劳森，作为新西兰人，他在 Esses Bazaar ATX 活动中担任主角，与利亚姆·劳森和朋友们一起在东第六街的 Inn Cahoots 举办车库音乐会，此次活动是为本周末的美国大奖赛做准备。

    Daniel Ricciardo surprises F1 fans with on-stage performance ahead of US GP

    Former Formula 1 driver Daniel Ricciardo surprised fans with a harmonica performance alongside musician Rob Baird

    Daniel Ricciardo , Drive to Survive Season 7

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Former Formula 1 driver Daniel Ricciardo made a surprise appearance on stage in Austin, Texas, with American singer-songwriter Rob Baird.

    The Australian joined Baird at the Ford Racing and Enchanté pop-up on Friday night to play harmonica on an original song he co-wrote with Baird during a road trip to the United States Grand Prix.

    A fan video of the performance, posted on Instagram, had picked up more than 7000 likes by the time of writing. "This healed something in me," one fan commented, while another added: "I'm sure he was either Bob Dylan's brother or a cowboy in a previous life lol. We love you, Daniel."

    "At this point, no one cares about the race. Glad to see Daniel back, even if he’s not racing," someone else posted, and another fan wrote: "As a musician and a honey badger fan, it would be a dream to play with Danny Ric."

    "And I mean the lonnnng way. Desert, backroads, swimming holes, all of it. Not gonna lie, I can't wait."

    The road trip was part of the promotion of the new Enchanté collection, The Long Road. The new products are being sold at a pop-up at Distribution Hall in Austin alongside food, drinks, flash tattoos and racing simulators.

    Ricciardo was also spotted supporting Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson as the New Zealander headlined Garage Sessions with Liam Lawson and Friends as part of Esses Bazaar ATX, held at Inn Cahoots on East Sixth Street ahead of this weekend's United States Grand Prix.
