    弗雷德·瓦瑟尔拒绝成为查尔斯·勒克莱尔孩子的教父：'我有四个孩子'

    法拉利车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔开玩笑说将成为查尔斯·勒克莱尔新生女儿的教父

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，弗雷德里克·瓦瑟尔，法拉利

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    法拉利一级方程式车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔自己排除了成为勒克莱尔女儿教父的可能性，开玩笑说他已经有很多孩子要照顾了。

    勒克莱尔和他的妻子亚历山德拉于周三宣布了他们第一个孩子比安卡的出生。

    在回答关于上周周末在墨西哥的碰撞后，他的车手之间可能存在的紧张关系的系列问题时，瓦瑟尔在周五的新闻发布会上被问及摩纳哥车手是否请求他成为婴儿的教父。

    “我家里有四个孩子，我不想成为我所有车手孩子的教父。”他回答说。

    法拉利车队主管在本周处理了很多事情，因为勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿在墨西哥大奖赛的首个回合发生了碰撞。勒克莱尔试图在发车时超越从杆位起步的七冠王汉密尔顿，但两位车手都跑出了赛道。

    勒克莱尔取得了领先，但随后因为这次事件被处以五秒罚时。与此同时，汉密尔顿跌至第三，落后于红牛的马克斯·维斯塔潘。

    瓦瑟尔在新闻发布会上承认，拥有两位竞争激烈的驾驶员会带来问题，但他表示这是他乐于接受的选择。

    “我们有两位经验丰富的车手，两位竞争激烈的车手。现在我们已经连续四到五场比赛处于领先位置，在蒙扎和马德里之后获得了最多的积分。”他说。

    “然后，当然，你们都想要赢得比赛，所以会有一种升级的趋势。我认为这可能是系统的缺点，因为每个周末我们都有两位竞争激烈的选手并肩作战。”

    “但我想如果我们今天排在第二，也是因为我们有两个非常接近的选手，我们必须处理好这一点。但这是一个选择。这是阵容中的选择，我有正面和负面，但我会处理负面。”

    “法拉利目前在车手积分榜上以504分排在第二位，落后积分榜领跑者梅赛德斯126分。”

    Fred Vasseur turns down being godfather to Charles Leclerc’s baby: “I have four children”

    Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur joked about becoming godfather to Charles Leclerc's newborn daughter

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Frederic Vasseur, Ferrari

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Ferrari Formula 1 team principal Fred Vasseur has ruled himself out of becoming godfather to Charles Leclerc's daughter, joking that he already has enough children to look after.

    Leclerc and his wife, Alexandra, announced the birth of their first child, Bianca, on Wednesday.

    After a series of questions about the potential tension between his drivers following last weekend's clash in Mexico, Vasseur was asked in Friday's press conference whether the Monegasque driver had asked him to be the baby's godfather.

    "I have four children at home and I don't want to be the godfather of all the children of my drivers," he replied.

    The Ferrari team chief has had plenty to deal with this week after Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton's clash on the opening lap of the Mexican Grand Prix. Leclerc attempted to overtake the seven-time champion, who started on pole position, at the race start, but both drivers ran wide.

    Leclerc took the lead but was subsequently handed a five-second penalty for the incident. Meanwhile, Hamilton dropped to third behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

    Vasseur admitted earlier in the press conference that having two competitive drivers comes with problems, but said it was a choice he was happy with.

    "We have two experienced guys, two competitive guys. There are now four or five races in a row that we are at the front, scoring the highest number of points after Monza and Madrid," he said.

    "Then, for sure, you have a kind of escalation because you all want to win races. I think it's probably the downside of the system, that we have two competitive guys starting each weekend next to each other.

    "But I think if we are P2 today, it's also because we have two guys who are very, very close, and we have to manage it. But it's a choice. It's a choice in the line-up, and I have the positive and the negative, but I will deal with the negative.

    Ferrari currently sits second in the constructors' standings with 504 points, 126 behind championship leader Mercedes.
