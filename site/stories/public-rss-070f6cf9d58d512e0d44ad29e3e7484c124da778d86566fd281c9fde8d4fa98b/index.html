    拉塞尔指责维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛练习中“盛气凌人”

    乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛练习中与维斯塔潘发生碰撞后，指责后者在赛道上表现得“盛气凌人”。

    乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛练习中指责马克斯·维斯塔潘在赛道上表现得“盛气凌人”。

    在FP3练习中，拉塞尔和维斯塔潘在14号弯发生碰撞，裁判对维斯塔潘发出警告，原因是他在刹车中移动。

    在排位赛中，尽管维斯塔潘因基米·安东内利的引擎处罚而获得杆位，但两人都对事件表示不满，并将在正赛中从第一弯开始对决。

    拉塞尔表示，他不接受被这样对待，并认为维斯塔潘经常以这种方式对待许多车手。

    “我认为警告是恰当的。规则中没有规定你不能超越正在开始一圈的车手，但当你这么做的时候，你就是在坑这个人，而且他每周都这样对很多车手做，而每个人都接受了这一点，”拉塞尔告诉天空体育意大利。”

    “我不会接受有人这样坑我，然后像他们比所有人都大或更好一样四处走动。”

    “所以，是的，有人必须站出来对抗那个人。”

    拉塞尔和维斯塔潘有历史，因为拉塞尔声称维斯塔潘在2024年卡塔尔大奖赛之前，因为维斯塔潘因为阻挡拉塞尔而被罚退后，曾说过要把他‘他妈的’撞到墙上。”

    两位车手在显然寒冷的排位赛前三名赛后新闻发布会上被问及最新的FP3事故，基米·安东内利将他们区分开来。

    当被问及此事时，维斯塔潘简单地回答道：“没有发生什么。”，而罗素说：“没有混淆，我认为很清楚。”

    国际汽联在拉斯维加斯周末宣布，从2027年开始，一级方程式将首次使用全职专业裁判员。

    罗素称这一举措为“太棒了”，并表示“一级方程式可能是唯一一个只有志愿者裁判或裁判员的高水平运动……有很多裁判员在他们的工作中做得很好，但如果没有一致性，每个人都会略微不同地解释情况。

    “如果你每周都有同样的三周和四周，我们都会知道他们在想什么。我很高兴听到这已经实现了。”

    英文原文

    George Russell has accused Max Verstappen of “walking around like they’re bigger and better than everyone else” after they clashed in practice at Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Russell and Verstappen banged wheels at Turn 14 during FP3 with the stewards issuing Verstappen with a warning for moving under braking.

    Neither was impressed and both were clearly still frustrated with the incident after a qualifying session that ensured they’ll share the front row together in Las Vegas, with Verstappen moving up to pole thanks to Kimi Antonelli's engine penalty.

    With Russell second, the duo will likely face off into Turn 1 in the grand prix proper.

    “I think a warning was fine. There's nothing in the rules to say you cannot overtake someone who is starting a lap, but when you do this, you are screwing this person over, and he does this week in, week out with many drivers, and everybody accepts it,” Russell told Sky Italia.

    “I'm not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this, and walk around like they're bigger and better than everyone else.

    “So, yeah, somebody's got to stand up to that guy.”

    Russell and Verstappen have history as Russell claimed Verstappen said he was going to “put me on my f***ing head in the wall" before the 2024 Qatar Grand Prix after Verstappen lost pole position to a grid penalty for impeding Russell.

    Both drivers were asked about the latest incident in FP3 in a clearly frosty post-qualifying top-three press conference, with Kimi Antonelli dividing them.

    “Nothing happened” Verstappen simply said when asked about it, while Russell said “no confusion, it was pretty clear I think”.

    The FIA announced earlier in the Vegas weekend that F1 would use full-time professional stewards for the first time from 2027.

    Russell called that move “fantastic”, saying “Formula 1 was probably the only high-level sport that would have volunteer referees or stewards…there's a lot of stewards that are great at what they do but when there isn't the consistency everybody interprets a situation very slightly differently.

    “If you have the same three same four week in week out we'll all know what what they're thinking. I'm very glad to hear that's come to fruition.”
