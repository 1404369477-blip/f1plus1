    维斯塔潘与汉密尔顿父亲意外重逢获F1粉丝称赞

    维斯塔潘与汉密尔顿的父亲安东尼·汉密尔顿在Portimao的GT世界挑战赛欧洲周末期间意外重逢，这一幕赢得了F1粉丝的广泛赞誉。

    粉丝们对维斯塔潘与汉密尔顿的父亲安东尼·汉密尔顿在GT世界挑战赛欧洲周末的意外会面反应热烈。

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车运动不仅仅是运动的故事。

    荷兰人在F1赛历中的空档期参加了GT世界挑战赛欧洲总决赛，与克里斯·卢尔汉和本·巴尼科特一起参赛。三人最终在比赛中排名第9。

    汉密尔顿先生在庆祝他在赛车运动中度过35周年之际，参加了本周末的GT世界赛事的第一场GT世界赛事。

    汉密尔顿先生在Instagram上写道：“很高兴与我的朋友们马克斯和瓦伦蒂诺·罗西见面，谈论赛车运动、生活和家庭。附上与维斯塔潘和瓦伦蒂诺·罗西的合影，他们也在这次赛事中参赛。”

    “在赛车运动中度过35年，这是我第一次参加GT世界赛事。多么美好的一天！61辆赛车在起跑线上。多么壮观，多么震撼。向斯蒂芬·拉特和惊人的SRO团队表示衷心的感谢。”

    “我很少拍照，但昨天我破例了……三位绝对绅士。”

    “粉丝们迅速在社交媒体上对此刻做出反应，将其描述为‘随意’和‘纯真’。”,

    纯真。难道安东尼不是一般的大赛车迷吗？老一辈的赛车迷似乎很尊重马克斯。他可能很容易相处，并且有相似的喜好。记得杰克·伊克斯说过，马克斯认出他并过来聊天，这在年轻车手中间并不常见，

    一位粉丝在Reddit上评论道。

    “这个人真是太好了。为了给刘易斯实现他梦寐以求的生活，他做了所有的事情，尽管他们当时没什么钱。所有你听说的小事。在2021年立即祝贺马克斯。在哈吉亚出事故后安慰他。现在又是这样。他看起来就像一个真正的好人，”另一位粉丝评论道，而另一个人写道：“他也在维斯塔潘的维修区里。太随机了，也太酷了。”

    Max Verstappen and Anthony Hamilton's "wholesome" reunion earns F1 fan praise

    Fans react to Max Verstappen's surprise paddock meeting with Lewis Hamilton's father, Anthony Hamilton, at the GT World Challenge Europe weekend

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton's father, Anthony Hamilton, shared a surprise paddock meeting during the GT World Challenge Europe weekend in Portimao, which has drawn widespread praise from Formula 1 fans.

    The Dutchman used a gap in the F1 calendar to compete in the GT World Challenge Europe finale alongside Chris Lulham and Ben Barnicoat. The trio finished ninth in the race.

    Hamilton Sr., who was celebrating 35 years in motorsport, attended his first GT World event during the race weekend.

    "Great to catch up with my friends Max and Valentino Rossi, talking all things motorsport, life and family," Hamilton Sr. wrote on Instagram alongside photos of him with Verstappen and Valentino Rossi, who was also racing in the event.

    "35 years in motorsport and my first ever GT World event. What a day! 61 cars on the grid. What a sight, what a sound. Huge thanks to Stéphane Ratel and the amazing SRO team.

    "I rarely take photos, but yesterday I made an exception… three absolute gentlemen."

    Fans were quick to react to the moment on social media, describing it as both "random" and "wholesome".

    "Wholesome. Isn't Anthony a big motorsport fan in general? Older motorsport people seem to respect Max quite a bit. He's probably pretty easy to talk to and has similar preferences. Remember Jacky Ickx saying that Max recognises him and comes to chat, which isn't common amongst young drivers," one fan commented on Reddit.

    Someone else posted: "I don't know much about Lewis's dad, other than he's a stand-up guy. There were a few times when he was a spare dad to one of the new rookies who just needed a kind human being, and Anthony was it. Btw, it seems that Max is cut from the same fabric."

    "Man, the guy is so wholesome. Everything he did to get Lewis the life he dreamed of, even though they had little money. All the small things you hear. Congratulating Max in 2021 right away. Consoling Hadjar after his crash. Now this. He seems like just a genuinely good person," another fan commented, while someone else wrote: "He was in Verstappen’s garage as well. So random and cool."
