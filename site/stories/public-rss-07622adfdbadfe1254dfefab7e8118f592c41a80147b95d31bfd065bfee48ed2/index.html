    维斯塔潘在卡塔尔大奖赛练习赛中感到“脱节”，红牛寻求解决“巨大局限”

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯夺冠一周后，本周面临全新的挑战。尽管拉斯维加斯的街道赛道以长直道和慢弯著称，但卢塞尔国际赛道几乎完全相反。除了6号弯——赛道上唯一的慢弯外，赛道主要特点是高速弯道。

    维斯塔潘感到与红牛车队之间有一种“脱节”的感觉，车队正在努力解决在卡塔尔大奖赛练习赛中存在的“巨大局限”

    维斯塔潘表示，在卡塔尔，梅赛德斯车队领先优势明显，但他补充说，其中一半的差距可能已经是因为红牛车队没有达到它想要的车辆平衡状态

    在维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯的胜利一周后，这个周末将面临一个完全不同的挑战。虽然沿着大道的街道赛道以长直道和慢速弯道为特点，但卢塞尔国际赛道几乎完全相反。

    除了6号弯——赛道上唯一的慢速弯道之外，赛道主要特点是高速弯道，根据维斯塔潘的说法，这并不是红牛车队在本赛季擅长的领域。因此，他在媒体日预测这个周末将会更加困难，而且这种感觉在第一和第二次练习赛中得到了加强。

    维斯塔潘在周五的比赛中以第五名的成绩结束，落后于基米·安东内利半秒，附加条件是梅赛德斯车手在倍耐力中胎上设置了最快圈速，部分原因是红旗的影响。

    “这是一条非常不同的赛道，相当独特。这是一条很难做对事情的赛道，所以我希望我们能做得更好，”维斯塔潘在卡塔尔的第二次练习赛后说。

    “我认为与梅赛德斯之间的差距非常大，所以我们需要有很大的进步。但我想，如果我们不能从我们这边得到更好的平衡，那可能已经是我们失去的半圈时间了。”

    在周五的比赛中，维斯塔潘再次遇到了几个问题。他在车队无线电中抱怨说方向盘感觉“非常沉重”，而小红牛22的整体平衡也不够好。

    “我们还没有达到预期的目标，”他说。“目前只是稍微有些脱节，所以我们需要尝试调整平衡，看看我们是否能够调整正确。让我们看看我们是否可以为明天解决这个问题。”

    红牛技术总监皮埃尔·瓦切表示，在卡塔尔解决这些问题是一个困难的平衡行为。车队需要解决他所说的‘巨大限制’——高速弯角的前左轮胎，在卡塔尔承受特别高的负荷——同时不使赛车在弯道入口处不稳定。

    “我认为通过在高速和中速弯角处实施巨大的前限制来解决这个问题，而不影响弯道入口，这是关键。他们[梅赛德斯]能够做到这一点，而我们还有工作要做才能实现这一点，”瓦切说。

    对于红牛来说，好消息是车队之前已经多次扭转了困难的星期五，但瓦切表示，他并不一定对在卡塔尔有类似的转变有信心。

    “不，但我认为这就是我的感觉。这并不意味着我们会实现[它]，”他说。“我希望我们能像往常一样实现同样的转变，但我感觉我们……差距很大。差距非常大，这使得我们很难实现这一点。”

    这意味着，根据周五的测试，红牛的战斗似乎主要是与麦克拉伦队，这也是他们在 constructors' championship 中争夺第三名的对手。

    “我们能做的最好的就是排在麦克拉伦队前面，或者希望更好。很难说，但根据我们所了解的，我同意梅赛德斯在前面，而他们后面，我们与麦克拉伦队在一起，我们后面可能是法拉利。”

    梅赛德斯在直线和高速度弯道上的表现都很强劲，这些弯道是卢塞尔赛道的特征，但维斯塔潘最后强调，红牛需要首先关注自己，只有当它改善了自身的赛车平衡后，才能关注竞争。”

    “他们可能在直线上的速度很快，也许他们的平衡性也比我们目前的好一些。我们自己并不满意，当你自己不满意的时候，很难达到同样的水平，”这位四次世界冠军说。

    Max Verstappen feels a “disconnect” as Red Bull looks to solve “massive limitation” in Qatar GP practice

    Verstappen says Mercedes is particularly far ahead in Qatar, but adds that half of that gap may already be down to Red Bull not having its own car balance where it wants it to be

    A week after Max Verstappen's victory in Las Vegas, a completely different challenge awaits this weekend. While the street circuit along the Strip was characterised by long straights interspersed with slow corners, the Lusail International Circuit is almost the exact opposite.

    Apart from Turn 6 – the only slow corner on the circuit – the track predominantly features high-speed corners, and according to Verstappen, that is not exactly an area in which Red Bull excels this season. The Dutchman therefore predicted during media day that this weekend would be more difficult, and that impression was reinforced during the first two practice sessions.

    Verstappen ended Friday's running in fifth place, half a second behind Kimi Antonelli, with the added caveat that the Mercedes drivers set their fastest lap times on Pirelli’s medium tyres, partly due to the red flags.

    “It's a very different track, quite a unique track. It's not an easy track to get things right, so I hope we can do a little bit better,” Verstappen said after the second practice session in Qatar.

    “I think the gap to Mercedes is very big, so we need a big jump for that. But I think already from our side, if we can't get that balance more together, that's probably already half of the lap time that we're losing.”

    During Friday's sessions, Verstappen once again encountered several problems. He complained over the team radio that the steering felt “very heavy”, while the overall balance of the RB22 is not good enough either.

    “It's not where we want it to be yet,” he said. “It's just a bit too disconnected at the moment, so we need to try and bring the balance together and see if we can get it right. Let’s see if we can fix that for tomorrow.”

    Red Bull technical director Pierre Wache says that solving these issues is a difficult balancing act in Qatar. The team needs to address what he calls a ‘massive limitation’ in high-speed corners – the front-left tyre, which is subjected to particularly high loads in Qatar – without making the car unstable on corner entry.

    “I think it’s key to fix the balance issue that we can have here by having a massive front limitation in the high-speed and medium-speed corners without compromising the corner entry. They [Mercedes] are able to do that and we have work to do to achieve that,” Wache said.

    The good news for Red Bull is that the team has turned difficult Fridays around on several occasions before, but Wache indicated that he is not necessarily confident of a similar turnaround in Qatar.

    “No, but I think that’s just what I feel. It doesn't mean what we will achieve [it],” he said. “I hope that we will achieve the same turnaround as normal, but I don't feel that we... The gap is big. The gap is very big and that makes it difficult for us to achieve that.”

    It means that, judging by Friday's running, Red Bull's battle appears to be primarily with McLaren, the team it is also fighting for third place in the constructors' championship.

    “The best we can have is to be in front of McLaren or hopefully even better. It's difficult to say, but from what we read, I agree that Mercedes is in front and that behind them, we are together with McLaren, and after us maybe Ferrari.”

    Mercedes appears strong both on the straights and through the many high-speed corners that characterise the Lusail circuit, but Verstappen concluded by stressing that Red Bull needs to focus primarily on itself and can only look at the competition once it has improved its own car balance.

    “They're probably quick on the straights, but maybe they also have a bit of a nicer balance than what we have at the moment. We are not happy ourselves, so when you're not happy yourself, it's very hard to get the same number,” the four-time world champion said.
