    金·卡戴珊与马丁·布兰德利就尴尬的F1事件打破沉默

    金·卡戴珊和马丁·布兰德利在2026年新加坡大奖赛前夕接受了采访，卡戴珊终于将她在摩纳哥的尴尬时刻抛诸脑后。

    金·卡戴珊终于打破沉默，与马丁·布兰德利就尴尬的F1事件进行了讨论。

    金·卡戴珊在新加坡大奖赛期间出现在法拉利维修区。

    Motorsport文化超越了赛道，包括车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和赛车故事。

    布伦德尔走向了这位明星：“你好，金。你怎么样？”他问道。

    “我不知道规则吗？我们是不是一开始就搞错了？”卡戴珊开玩笑地说，两人都笑了起来，布伦德尔回应道：“不，完全没问题。金，很高兴你能来这次大奖赛。你对这个激动人心的F1世界有什么看法？”

    在今年早些时候的摩纳哥大奖赛中，布伦德尔试图采访卡戴珊，当时她与这位七届世界冠军的关系正在升温。她选择保持沉默，因为她享受着F1如何将公国变成赛道。

    “我认为这太神奇了，很有趣，很刺激，是我以前从未见过的东西，”她兴奋地对布伦德尔说，“太神奇了。”

    Brundle将话题转向了法拉利车手，提到‘她的男人刘易斯’正处于‘极佳的状态’。他在周六的新加坡短道赛中已经获得了第二名，因为他的法拉利赛车在马克斯·维斯塔潘的雷诺红牛赛车之后冲过终点。

    “他状态极佳，”她回答道。“我只希望今晚能一切顺利。”

    Brundle询问汉密尔顿是否对她自己的看法有信心。

    “是的，非常有信心。我也是。我们只需要祈祷今晚一切顺利。”然后简短的对话就结束了。

    法拉利车手在周日的新加坡大奖赛中获得了第三名的成绩，排在队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔第二和维斯塔潘第一之后。维斯塔潘在成功的冲刺赛后，在赛道上取得了他的第一个胜利，并希望周日能再次做到这一点。

    汉密尔顿和卡戴珊现在已经在公共场合露面多次，包括在摩纳哥大奖赛期间。‘看到她这个周末来并得到支持真是太棒了，’车手在排在梅赛德斯车手基米·安东内利之后获得第二名后说。

    Kim Kardashian finally breaks silence with Martin Brundle after awkward F1 snub

    Kim Kardashian made a surprise appearance in the Ferrari garage at the Singapore Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Kim Kardashian and Martin Brundle have finally shared an interview in the build up to the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix, with the reality star finally putting her awkward Monaco moment behind her. The two finally caught up during the former driver's grid walk on the Sunday at the Marina Bay Circuit, where Kardashian was there to support her partner Lewis Hamilton.

    Brundle approached the star: "Hello, Kim. How are you?" he asked.

    "Did I not know the rules? Did we get off on the wrong foot?" Kardashian joked as they both laughed with each other, with Brundle responding: "No, it’s absolutely fine. It's great to have you here, Kim, at this grand prix. What are your thoughts on the exciting world of Formula 1?"

    During the Monaco Grand Prix earlier this year, Brundle attempted to interview Kardashian as the news of her relationship with the seven-time world champion was growing. She chose to stay silent as she enjoyed how Formula 1 had transformed the principality into a race track.

    "I think it’s amazing, and fun, and wild, and something I’ve never seen before," she told Brundle eagerly. "It’s amazing."

    Brundle moved the conversation to the Ferrari driver, referring to 'her man Lewis' being in 'great form'. He had already finished second in Saturday's Singapore sprint race as his Ferrari crossed the line after Max Verstappen's Red Bull.

    "He is [in great form]," she answered. "I’m just praying for the best tonight."

    Brundle asked if Hamilton seemed confident in her view.

    "Yes, very confident. And so am I. Just got to pray for the best." And that's where the brief conversation ended.

    The Ferrari driver qualified third for Sunday's Singapore Grand Prix behind his team-mate Charles Leclerc in second and Verstappen in first. Verstappen took his first win at the circuit after a successful sprint campaign, and is hoping to repeat that on Sunday.

    Hamilton and Kardashian have appeared publicly several times now, including during the Monaco race. “It’s amazing to have her come this weekend and have the support,” the driver said after he finished second behind Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli.
