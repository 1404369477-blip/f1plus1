    三届F1冠军塞纳使用的罕见本田NSX以六位数价格打破拍卖估价记录

    塞纳曾使用过的本田NSX在拍卖会上打破了该型号的拍卖记录。这辆车原本预计售价在50万至80万英镑之间，但在伦敦RM Sotheby's拍卖行于10月31日以87.125万英镑的价格成交。

    艾尔顿·塞纳罕见的本田NSX在拍卖中以创纪录的六位数成交价打破估价

    曾由三届一级方程式冠军艾尔顿·塞纳使用过的本田NSX为该车型创下了新的拍卖纪录

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    由三届一级方程式冠军艾尔顿·塞纳使用过的本田NSX为该车型创下了拍卖纪录。

    这辆车的预估售价在拍卖前在50万至80万英镑之间，于10月31日在伦敦的RM Sotheby's拍卖会上以87.125万英镑的价格成交。拍卖还包括一辆2002年的法拉利550 Maranello、一辆由Scaglietti设计的1964年法拉利250 GT/L Berlinetta Lusso以及一辆1903年的克莱蒙特AC4R 12/16 HP后置引擎敞篷车。

    在塞纳加入迈凯轮-本田F1车队期间，作为与塞纳的一项协议，本田公司允许他使用三辆NSX作为个人车辆，其中两辆是黑色的。第三辆，底盘号为T000233，是红色配黑色车顶和黑色内饰，于1991年在葡萄牙注册为SX-25-59。它一直停在里斯本，并在塞纳访问该国时被带去，他在Quinta do Lago购买了房产。

    塞纳在1994年圣马力诺大奖赛中的不幸去世后，这辆NSX被卖给了罗伯特·麦卡甘，并搬到了英国。这位英国人在2024年卖掉了NSX，当时的新主人将其送回日本，但后来又将其运回英国进行这次最新的拍卖。

    这辆传奇车型配备了一台六缸三升发动机，产生271马力，可在5.8秒内从0加速到60英里/小时，最高时速可达165英里。

    塞纳参与了本田NSX的测试，并且著名地在这辆赛车在铃鹿赛道上进行了驾驶，这是日本大奖赛的举办地。

    Sholto Gilbertson，RM Sotheby's UK的销售总监表示："塞纳的遗产远不止他的三个世界冠军头衔。他的风格和优雅是永恒的，当你想到塞纳——不拘小节地穿着牛仔裤、白色袜子以及棕色乐福鞋，坐在一辆道路赛车的驾驶座上——你会想到NSX。这是拥有一个真正的体育和文化历史的宝贵机会。"

    Ayrton Senna's rare Honda NSX smashes auction estimate with record six-figure sale

    A Honda NSX once used by three-time Formula 1 champion Ayrton Senna has set a new auction record for the model

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    A Honda NSX used by three-time Formula 1 champion Ayrton Senna has set an auction record for the model.

    The car, which had been estimated to sell for between £500,000 and £800,000 prior to the auction, sold for £871,250 at RM Sotheby's in London on 31 October. The auction also included a 2002 Ferrari 550 Maranello, a 1964 Ferrari 250 GT/L Berlinetta Lusso by Scaglietti and a 1903 Clement AC4R 12/16 HP Rear-Entrance Tonneau.

    As part of an agreement with Senna during his time with the McLaren-Honda Formula 1 team, Honda gave him access to three NSXs for personal use, two of which were black. The third, chassis T000233, was Formula Red with a black roof and black leather interior, and was registered in Portugal as SX-25-59 in 1991. It remained in Lisbon and was brought to the Brazilian driver on his visits to the country, where he had bought a property at Quinta do Lago.

    After Senna's tragic passing at the 1994 San Marino Grand Prix, the NSX was sold to Robert McKagan and moved to the UK. The Briton later sold the NSX in 2024, when its new owners returned it to Japan, but it has since been moved back to the UK for this latest sale.

    The legendary car boasts a six-cylinder three-litre engine producing 271bhp, can accelerate from 0-60mph in 5.8 seconds and reach a top speed of 165mph.

    Senna was involved in testing the Honda NSX and famously drove it at the Suzuka Circuit, home of the Japanese Grand Prix.

    Sholto Gilbertson, RM Sotheby's UK director of sales, said: "Senna’s legacy runs far deeper than his three world championships. His style and elegance are everlasting, and when you think of Senna—nonchalantly dressed in jeans, white socks and brown loafers behind the wheel of a road car—you think of an NSX. This is a wonderful opportunity to own a genuine piece of sporting and cultural history."
