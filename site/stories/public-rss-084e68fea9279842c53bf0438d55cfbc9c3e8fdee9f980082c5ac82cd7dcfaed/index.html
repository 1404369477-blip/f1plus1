    F1车队展示美国大奖赛特别系列：牛仔帽、牛仔裤和哥斯拉元素

    美国大奖赛是少数几个车队会特别庆祝的赛事之一。本周五，F1赛事将抵达德克萨斯州，让我们一起看看车队带来的牛仔帽、牛仔裤和西部风格的商品。

    F1车队展示了特别为美国大奖赛准备的丹宁牛仔装、牛仔帽和哥斯拉系列

    一级方程式车队和车手们正在拥抱德克萨斯州的氛围，为2026年美国大奖赛做准备

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    美国大奖赛是少数几个几乎每个车队都会有所庆祝的赛事之一。一级方程式巡回赛本周末抵达德克萨斯州，让我们来盘点一下车队带来的丹宁牛仔装、牛仔帽和西部风格的商品。

    奥斯汀自豪地展示着来自红牛、凯迪拉克、威廉姆斯、维斯塔潘和兰多·诺里斯本人的收藏，甚至连F1也带来了自己的装备。

    红牛全力以赴，因为它的引擎合作伙伴福特在美国赛车场为其主场赛事而疯狂地推出了Raptor系列。车队将穿着由Raptor和美国西部启发的Castore系列赛车服、靴子、夹克和衬衫。车手维斯塔潘和伊萨克·哈德贾本周末将身穿白色赛车服，以庆祝这一场合。

    “奥斯汀总是个充满乐趣的周末，”红牛商业总监保罗·甘多菲说。“今年，我们将比以往任何时候都要更大规模，无论是在赛道上还是在整个城市。”

    福特赛车总经理威尔·福特补充说：“我们非常喜欢ORBR车队对Raptor精神的拥抱，从赛车服到维修区。”

    红牛和新时代公司还推出了一款深红色带有奶油色帽檐的奥斯汀帽子，上面印有维斯塔潘的3号和哈吉尔的6号。

    维斯塔潘的狮子头戴牛仔帽，出现在他独一无二的奥斯汀头盔上，同样的主题也出现在他自己的‘释放狮子奥斯汀’系列中，其中包括一件上面印有牛仔狮子和‘奥斯汀，德克萨斯州’字样的87美元连帽衫。头盔的复制品从93美元的1:4比例模型开始销售。

    哈斯车队将奥斯汀视为其最重要的主场赛事，转而选择了哥斯拉。作为其与日本工作室东宝长期合作的一部分，车队于周一公布了以哥斯拉为主题的涂装，埃斯蒂班·奥康和奥利弗·贝尔曼将身穿配套的赛车服。19日，车队系列帽衫和车手T恤衫开始销售，同时还有海报和拼图。从10月23日至25日，粉丝可以在巴顿克里克广场购物中心看到哥斯拉展示车并购买该系列商品。还有一款55美元的新时代奥斯汀帽子。

    ‘通过推出续集涂装，我们在一级方程式赛场上开辟了新的领域，这是我们非常自豪的，’哈斯车队负责人小松英昭说。

    凯迪拉克的Austin胶囊系列源自其与Tommy Hilfiger的合作，并于10月13日开始销售。该系列包括售价321美元的浅色水洗牛仔夹克，一件带有银色箔面涂层的女士牛仔背心，一件T恤，一件背心，以及售价74美元的牛仔帽，紧随迈阿密和墨西哥胶囊系列。拉斯维加斯版预计将在11月上市。

    ‘我们从Tommy衣橱的标志性单品开始设计’，Tommy Hilfiger首席设计官Lee Holman说道。

    威廉姆斯车队今年将装备供应商改为New Era，推出了Austin赛车系列，包括售价180美元的牛仔夹克，售价85美元的牛仔帽，牛仔司机帽和印有‘Austin, TX’和‘Est. 1977’的西部风格T恤。

    梅赛德斯-奔驰和奥迪则保持了简洁的风格，推出了特别版的adidas Austin帽，每顶售价61美元。

    诺里斯的LN4品牌走了一条不同的路线。兰多的原始服务部门是一个由12件套组成的系列，灵感来源于70年代的赛车车库，从奥斯汀市中心的一个改造后的加油站出售，直到周日。

    F1的自己的系列包括一个如你所见带有NAHMIAS的收藏。轮胎供应商倍耐力还提供一款售价94美元的奥斯汀领奖台帽子。

    美国大奖赛将于10月23日至25日在美国赛车场举行。

    F1 teams reveal special United States GP collections with denim, cowboy hats and Godzilla

    Formula 1 teams and drivers are embracing the spirit of Texas for the 2026 United States Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    The United States Grand Prix is one of the few races where almost every team will do something to celebrate. The Formula 1 circus arrives in Texas this weekend, so let's do a roundup of the denim, cowboy hats and Western-styled merchandise the teams have brought with them.

    Austin is proudly showing off collections from Red Bull, Cadillac, Williams, Max Verstappen and Lando Norris themselves, and even F1 has brought its own kit.

    Red Bull has gone all out as its engine partner, Ford, goes Raptor crazy at the Circuit of the Americas for its home race. The team will be wearing a Castore collection of race suits, boots, jackets and shirts inspired by the Raptor and the American West. Drivers Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar will be wearing white race suits this weekend to celebrate the occasion.

    “Austin is always a weekend where we have a lot of fun,” said Red Bull commercial director Paul Gandolfi. “This year, we’re going bigger than ever, both at the circuit and across the city.”

    Ford Racing general manager Will Ford added: "We love how the ORBR team has embraced the spirit of Raptor, from the race suits to the pit garages."

    Red Bull and New Era have also produced an Austin cap in maroon with a cream peak, carrying Verstappen’s No. 3 and Hadjar’s No. 6.

    Verstappen’s lion wears a cowboy hat on his one-off Austin helmet, and the same theme features in his own Unleash the Lion Austin collection, which includes an $87 hoodie with the cowboy lion above the words “Austin, Texas”. Replicas of the helmet start at $93 for a 1:4 scale model.

    Haas, which considers Austin its biggest home race, has turned to Godzilla. As part of its season-long partnership with Japanese studio TOHO, the team revealed a Godzilla-themed livery on Monday, and Esteban Ocon and Oliver Bearman will wear matching race suits. A collection of team hoodies and driver T-shirts went on sale on 19 October, alongside posters and jigsaw puzzles. Fans can see a Godzilla show car and buy the range at Barton Creek Square mall from 23 to 25 October. There is also a $55 New Era Austin cap.

    “We’re breaking new ground in Formula 1 by launching a sequel livery, and it’s something we’re very proud of,” said Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu.

    Cadillac’s Austin capsule stems from its partnership with Tommy Hilfiger, and went on sale on 13 October. It includes a light-wash denim jacket at $321, a women’s denim vest with a silver foil coating, a T-shirt, a tank top and a $74 denim cap, and follows capsules for Miami and Mexico. A Las Vegas edition is due in November.

    “We started with icons of the Tommy wardrobe,” said Tommy Hilfiger chief design officer Lee Holman.

    Williams, which changed to New Era as its kit supplier this year, has an Austin race collection with a $180 denim jacket, an $85 cowboy hat, denim trucker caps and T-shirts with Western-style lettering reading “Austin, TX” and “Est. 1977”.

    Mercedes and Audi have kept it easy, with special edition adidas Austin caps at $61 each.

    Norris’s LN4 brand has gone down a different route. Lando’s Original Service Department is a 12-piece collection inspired by 1970s racing garages, sold from a converted petrol station in downtown Austin until Sunday.

    F1’s own range includes a collection with NAHMIAS as you can see below. Tyre supplier Pirelli also has a $94 Austin podium cap.

    The United States Grand Prix takes place at the Circuit of the Americas from 23 to 25 October.
