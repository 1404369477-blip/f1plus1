    哈贾尔因推搡围场访客获正式警告

    红牛车手伊萨克·哈贾尔因在墨西哥大奖赛第一次练习赛前推搡一名围场访客，被F1赛会干事给予正式警告。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔因在墨西哥大奖赛第一次练习赛前推搡一名围场访客，被给予正式警告。

    社交媒体周五流传的视频显示，哈贾尔推搡了一名试图与他自拍的人。

    这名红牛车手随后于周六上午被叫去见F1赛会干事，传唤文件将此事描述为“涉嫌不当行为”。

    哈贾尔在赛会干事面前表示，他当时正赶着去参加第一次练习赛，在停下来与一名年轻车迷合影后，被一名围场访客以带有攻击性的方式接近。

    干事文件详细记录了该事件的经过，并写道“一名安保人员当时走在哈贾尔身后，未能

    介入并阻止这一等同于侵犯车手私人空间的行为”。

    哈贾尔起初对这一举动进行了抵抗，随后“用张开的右手将他推到一边”——这违反了国际汽联国际体育法典第12.2.1.l条关于不当行为的规定。

    然而，在仅给予警告的处罚时，干事依据国际体育法典附录B中的指导原则，认可了五项对哈贾尔有利的减轻情节。

    这些情况是：哈贾尔当时“在回应另一人未经邀请且相当激进的举动”；他正试图穿过拥挤区域前往红牛车库参加第一节练习；现场没有“足够”的安保人员；那名嘉宾未经同意侵入了他的个人空间；以及车手们在围场内通行时面临的“普遍问题”。

    英文原文

    Isack Hadjar has been given a formal warning for pushing a guest in the paddock ahead of the first Formula 1 practice session at the Mexican Grand Prix.

    Footage circulated on social media on Friday that appeared to show Hadjar pushing someone who had tried to take a selfie with him.

    The Red Bull driver was subsequently called to see the F1 stewards on Saturday morning for what the summons document described as 'alleged misconduct'.

    Hadjar stated in his appearance in front of the stewards that he was running late for FP1 and, having stopped for a photograph with a young fan, was "approached by a paddock guest in an aggressive manner".

    The stewards' document detailing that account also said "one security person was walking behind him [Hadjar] and was unable to

    intervene and prevent what was tantamount to an invasion of the driver's private space".

    Having initially resisted that move, Hadjar subsequently "pushed him aside with his open right hand" - a breach of article 12.2.1.l of the FIA international sporting code relating to misconduct.

    However, in awarding only a warning the stewards acknowledge five mitigating circumstances in Hadjar's favour, in line with the guidelines in appendix B of the ISC.

    These were that Hadjar was "reacting to an uninvited and rather aggressive action by another person"; that he was trying to make his way to the Red Bull garage for FP1 through a congested area; that he did not have "sufficient" security present; that the guest invaded his personal space without consent; and the "general issue" drivers have moving through the paddock.
