    维斯塔潘排除参加印第安纳波利斯500赛事

    四次F1世界冠军维斯塔潘因纳尔逊·皮克特的赛车事故而排除参加印第安纳波利斯500赛事。

    为什么维斯塔潘排除了参加印第安纳波利斯500赛的可能性

    维斯塔潘表示参加印第安纳波利斯500赛的风险太高

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解一级方程式新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析。

    四次一级方程式冠军维斯塔潘已经排除了在印第安纳波利斯500赛中参赛的可能性，他提到了纳尔逊·皮克特在赛道上的事故对赛车手的长期影响。

    皮奎特，维斯塔潘搭档凯莉·皮奎特的父亲，在1992年印第安纳波利斯500赛事的练习赛中发生撞车，腿部和脚部受到严重伤害，需要数月时间进行康复。

    维斯塔潘在上周末赢得拉斯维加斯大奖赛之后，在卡塔尔大奖赛周末之前接受媒体采访时，被问及他是否有可能参加这场标志性的赛事。这座罪恶之城街道上的比赛被比作是印第安纳赛车系列赛的比赛。

    但红牛车手表示这并没有改变他的想法。

    “不，但也不是我不会擅长它。只是风险管理的考虑，这就是为什么我不想参加。我喜欢观看比赛，但我也知道发生碰撞的风险非常高，我有足够的例子。”维斯塔潘说。

    “即使如此，我的岳父在那儿也发生过严重的车祸，看到他在那次车祸后余生是如何度过的，这是我不愿意在已经参加F1和GT3赛车的同时承担的风险。”

    “在某个时刻，你已经承担了很多风险，所以有时候有几个周末你不需要一直处于极限状态是很好的。”

    “这位荷兰人透露了他的计划，在未来参加更多耐力赛，尽管他说这取决于能否组建正确的团队。”

    “好吧，首先，总是从预算开始的。我不喜欢全部用我自己的钱包支付。不管你有多少钱，每个人都不喜欢这样，”他说。

    “我喜欢参加所有的耐力赛，但同时也喜欢以正确的方式参加，做好充分的准备，与正确的车队一起参加。我不是去那里只是参加比赛的，我是去那里赢的。”

    维斯塔潘以304分进入赛季最后两场比赛，在车手积分榜上排名第二。法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿以267分排名第三，梅赛德斯的乔治·罗素和麦克拉伦的兰多·诺里斯分别排名第四和第五，两人都是264分。

    Why Max Verstappen has ruled out Indy 500 entry

    Verstappen says the risk is too high to enter the Indianapolis 500

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen has ruled out racing in the Indianapolis 500, citing the lasting effects of Nelson Piquet's crash at the Speedway.

    Piquet, the father of Verstappen's partner Kelly Piquet, suffered serious injuries to his legs and feet when he crashed in practice for the 1992 Indy 500, and needed months of rehabilitation.

    Verstappen was asked about the possibility of him entering the iconic race while speaking to the media ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix weekend, after he won the Las Vegas Grand Prix last weekend. The race around the streets of Sin City featured racing that has been likened to that of the IndyCar Series.

    But the Red Bull driver said that had not changed his mind.

    "No, but it's also not that I won't be good at it. It's just the risk management, that's why I don't want to do it. I like watching it, but I also know there is a very high risk involved by crashing, and I've had plenty of examples," Verstappen said.

    "Even, of course, my father-in-law had a big crash there, and seeing how he had to live for the rest of his life after that crash is some risk that I'm not willing to take while already doing F1 and racing in GT3.

    "At one point you're taking already a lot of risk, so sometimes it's good to have a few weekends where you don't need to be on the limit all the time."

    The Dutchman did reveal his plans to race more endurance events in the future, although he said it would depend on getting the right package together.

    "Well, it always starts with budget, first of all. I don't like to fully pay it out of my own wallet. Everyone doesn't like that, it doesn't matter how much you have," he said.

    "I like to do all the endurance races, but at the same time I also like to do it in the proper way, be well prepared, doing it with the right team. I'm not there to just participate, I'm there to win."

    Verstappen heads into the final two races of the season second in the drivers' championship on 304 points. Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton sits third with 267 points, and Mercedes' George Russell and McLaren's Lando Norris are fourth and fifth, both on 264 points.
