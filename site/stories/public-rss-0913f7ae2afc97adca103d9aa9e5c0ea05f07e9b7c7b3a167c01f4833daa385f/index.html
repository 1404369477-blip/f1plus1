    安东内利若在奥斯汀赢得F1冠军将打破纪录

    如果安东内利在本月美国大奖赛中获得F1世界冠军，他将打破塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔保持的最年轻F1世界冠军纪录，并结束意大利73年的冠军荒。

    如果基米·安东内利在奥斯汀赢得F1冠军，他将打破所有记录

    夺冠将使安东内利成为最年轻的冠军，也是自阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来意大利的第一个冠军

    对于梅赛德斯车迷来说，这里有最新的银箭消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和从布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯的车队分析

    如果基米·安东内利在本月晚些时候的美国大奖赛中获得F1世界冠军，他将打破塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔保持的成为有史以来最年轻F1世界冠军的记录。他还将结束意大利冠军73年的干旱期。他将在锦标赛中第二年，也就是20岁时打破这两项记录。

    这些记录他如果在美国赛车场赢得冠军就能打破。

    这位年轻的意大利人目前以340分领先。他的队友乔治·拉塞尔以238分紧随其后，法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿以229分排名第三。虽然理论上锦标赛的竞争仍然存在，还有150分可供争夺，但安东内利确实有可能在COTA的比赛中赢得他的奖杯。

    安东内利需要获胜，拉塞尔必须排名第九或更低，汉密尔顿必须排名第五或更低，才能给安东内利一个不可动摇的领先优势。

    由于拉塞尔在美国大奖赛上面临引擎处罚，而不是在拉斯维加斯，所以他可能会排名如此之低，但这可能性不大。

    领奖台：比赛获胜者塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔，红牛车队，第二名刘易斯·汉密尔顿，麦克拉伦车队，第三名简森·巴顿，麦克拉伦车队

    什么会随着冠军头衔而来，什么不会？

    当奥斯汀赛事举行时，20岁零61天的梅赛德斯车手将打破维特尔的三年多记录。这位德国人在2010年阿布扎比以23岁零134天的年龄赢得了他的第一个世界冠军头衔。安东内利也将成为首位在21世纪出生的赢得世界冠军的车手。最近的世界冠军兰多·诺里斯出生于1999年。

    安东内利也将成为自阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里73年前赢得冠军以来，意大利的第一个世界冠军。他将成为第三位赢得一级方程式世界冠军的意大利人。他将追随1950年的尼诺·法里纳和1952年及1953年的阿斯卡里的脚步。他将成为第一位为意大利车队以外的车队赢得世界冠军的意大利人。

    在奥斯汀赢得比赛将使他职业生涯的胜利次数达到九次，距离阿斯卡里的意大利纪录13次还差四次。

    他也不是第一个在第二个赛季赢得比赛的人。汉密尔顿在2008年做到了，雅克·维伦纽夫在1997年做到了。法里纳在1950年的第一个赛季就做到了。

    安东内利今年也已经创造了多项纪录。他成为中国最年轻的杆位得主，打破了维特尔之前的纪录。他还在日本大奖赛上成为最年轻的冠军领跑者，当时年仅19岁216天。在蒙扎获胜后，他成为六十年内第一位在那里获胜的意大利人。

    尽管可能失去自己的纪录，维特尔对这位车手表示兴奋：‘我非常为他感到高兴。他今年表现得非常出色，有句老话说纪录就是为了被打破的。他还有很多年可以打破纪录。

    “我喜欢这项运动，所以作为一个观众，这非常令人兴奋。我希望其他人也能回来，更多地参与到比赛中，但我有点独立，为他们感到高兴，因为我知道要实现这个目标背后有多少工作。而且无论谁获胜，都应得到它。”

    Every Record Kimi Antonelli breaks if he wins the F1 title in Austin

    A title win would make Antonelli the youngest champion and Italy’s first since Alberto Ascari

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    If Kimi Antonelli becomes Formula 1 world champion at the United States Grand Prix later this month, he will break Sebastian Vettel's record of being the youngest F1 world champion ever to be crowned. He will also end a 73-year drought of Italian champions. Both of these records he will break in just his second year driving in the championship, and both at just 20 years old.

    These are all the records he could break if he achieves the championship win at Circuit of the Americas.

    The young Italian currently leads with 340 points. His team-mate George Russell follows with 238 and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton boasts 229. The championship battle is still - technically - alive with 150 points still available, but Antonelli can indeed collect his trophy if things go his way at COTA.

    Antonelli will need to win, Russell has to finish ninth or lower and Hamilton has to finish fifth or lower to give Antonelli an unassailable lead.

    With Russell facing an engine penalty not at the United States Grand Prix but instead likely in Las Vegas instead, it's possible but unlikely that he will finish so far back.

    Podium: Race winner Sebastian Vettel, Red Bull Racing, second place Lewis Hamilton, McLaren, third place Jenson Button, McLaren

    What falls with the title and what doesn't?

    At 20 years and 61 days old when the Austin race takes place, the Mercedes driver will beat Vettel's record by over three years. The German secured his first championship title in 2010 in Abu Dhabi at 23 years and 134 days old. Antonelli would also be the first driver born in the 2000s to win the championship. Lando Norris, the most recent champion, was born in 1999.

    Antonelli would also become Italy's first championship winner since Alberto Ascari, who won his championship 73 years ago. He would also be only the third Italian to win an F1 championship. He would follow in the footsteps of Nino Farina in 1950 and Ascari in 1952 and 1953. He would be the first Italian champion not driving for an Italian team.

    A win in Austin would take him to nine career wins, four short of Ascari’s Italian record of 13.

    He also isn't the first to win in his second season. Hamilton did it in 2008 and Jacques Villeneuve in 1997. Farina managed it in his first season, 1950.

    Antonelli has also already collected several records this year. He became the youngest polesitter in China taking over Vettel's former record. He also became the youngest championship leader at the Japanese Grand Prix at just 19 years and 216 days old. On winning at Monza, he became the first Italian to win there in six decades.

    Vettel, despite potentially losing his record, is excited for the driver: “I’d be very happy for him. He’s had an incredible year, and there’s the old phrase that records are there to be broken. He still has a lot of years ahead of him to do so.”

    “I love the sport, so as a spectator, it’s very exciting. I’d wish for the others to come back and be more in the mix, but I’m sort of independent and happy for them because I know how much work there is behind it to achieve it. And whoever will win will deserve it.”
