    维斯塔潘解释巴西雨战为何如此出色

    维斯塔潘在巴西大奖赛再次展现出色驾驶后，解释了自己在圣保罗雨战中的比赛能力来源。英特拉格斯是他职业生涯登台率最高的赛道之一，11场比赛8次进入前三。

    维斯塔潘解释为何自己在巴西雨战中如此出色

    英特拉格斯是维斯塔潘最擅长的赛道之一，尤其是当天空下起雨时

    维斯塔潘在巴西大奖赛再次奉献一场精彩驾驶后，深入剖析了是什么让他在圣保罗的雨战中拥有如此出色的比赛能力。

    在维斯塔潘职业生涯初期就已在赛历上的赛道中，他在英特拉格斯和奥斯汀的登台率最高，11场比赛中有8次进入前三。

    其中三场是在若泽·卡洛斯·帕塞赛道（Autodromo Jose Carlos Pace）上演的雨中精彩驾驶。2016年，年仅18岁、二年级生的维斯塔潘在安全车带领七圈后绿旗终于挥动时，早在1号弯就找到了超越基米·莱科宁法拉利的方法；随后他在太阳弯（Curva do Sol）外侧对最终冠军尼科·罗斯伯格完成了一次精彩的外道超越。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘对尼科·罗斯伯格的难忘超越

    2024年，这位红牛车手从第15位起步，第一圈就提升了四个位置，一路劈开赛道上的车流，最终以19秒的优势领先埃斯特班·奥康夺冠。

    最后，上周末，维斯塔潘发现自己排在发车格最后一排，位列第21位，但他在五圈之内一路拼杀升至第七；他最终获得第二名，落后比赛冠军兰多·诺里斯15秒——而且他实际上相信自己本可以做得更好。

    “在前往发车格的几圈之后，我感觉相当有信心，”这位四届世界冠军解释道。“我出去跑了一下，心想‘哦，抓地力很低’。但后来我身边有几辆车，我就想‘不，我觉得我们实际上有很多抓地力’。

    “在两圈之内，我几乎在每个弯道都飞速超过车辆。我想我们比中场大多数车手拥有更多的抓地力。我不知道我在第几圈进入了前十名，但这一切发生得非常快。

    “我知道我们拥有的平衡就在那里，这在前几圈也体现出来了。不幸的是，在第一段 比赛阶段之后，那完全消失了，但幸运的是，至少在第一段 stint 中赛车运转良好，这让我能够处于这个位置。

    “我们必须理解为什么或什么出了问题，当我们一换上干地轮胎时，因为即使我在最后换回半雨胎时，赛车与我的第一段 stint 相比天差地别，这表明有问题。”

    因此，当被问及如何在湿滑赛道上相对其他车手找到机会时，维斯塔潘指出，英特拉格斯赛道的地势起伏和多条实用赛车线让这变得更容易——这在干燥条件下确实如此，但被雨水放大了。

    “我对赛车感到舒适、有信心，只是努力在不同位置寻找抓地力，”他解释道。“然后赛车就在我前面，所以我试着超越他们。

    “当然，这条赛道也允许你走不同的线路，我认为这很棒，因为它确实有助于比赛。无论干地还是湿地，这都是一条好赛道，这确实创造了更多超车机会。

    “你需要在赛车中感觉良好。你需要感到舒适，才能可能多冒一点风险，而在这里这对我来说效果相当不错。”

    维斯塔潘在最近三届巴西大奖赛中都是从第15位或更靠后的位置发车；尽管这让他完成了多达23次超车，并最终三次登上领奖台，包括前述的那场胜利，但这位荷兰车手还是希望在2027年能有一场更顺利的比赛。

    “也许明年我更愿意从稍微靠前的位置发车，而不是从后排或维修区起步，但明年我们就知道了，”维斯塔潘说道，他如今已在2026年车手锦标赛中锁定亚军席位。

    Max Verstappen explains why he’s so good in the rain in Brazil

    Interlagos is among Verstappen’s best tracks – especially when the skies open up

    Max Verstappen has delved into what gives him such racecraft when it rains in Sao Paulo, after another remarkable drive in Formula 1’s Brazilian Grand Prix.

    Among tracks that have been on the calendar since the beginning of his career, Verstappen has his highest podium-finishing rate at Interlagos – and Austin – with eight top-three results in 11 races.

    Three of these were starring wet-weather drives at Autodromo Jose Carlos Pace. Back in 2016, as an 18-year-old sophomore, Verstappen found a way past Kimi Raikkonen’s Ferrari as early as Turn 1 when the green flag was finally waved after seven laps behind the safety car; he went on to perform a superb around-the-outside move on eventual champion Nico Rosberg in Curva do Sol.

    Max Verstappen's memorable move on Nico Rosberg

    In 2024, starting from 15th, the Red Bull driver gained four spots on lap one, scythed through the field and romped to victory, 19 seconds ahead of Esteban Ocon.

    Finally, last weekend, Verstappen found himself on the back row on the grid, down in 21st, but he fought his way up to seventh in the space of five laps; he eventually finished second, 15 seconds adrift of race winner Lando Norris – and he actually believes he could have done even better.

    “After the laps to the grid, I felt pretty confident,” the four-time world champion explained. “I went out and I was like, ‘Oh, low grip’. But then I actually had some cars around me and I was like, ‘No, I think we actually have a lot of grip’.

    “Within two laps, I was just flying by cars, almost every corner. We had a lot more grip, I guess, than most in the midfield. I don't know what lap I was in the top 10, but it all happened very quickly.

    “I knew that the balance we had was there, and that also showed in the first few laps. Unfortunately, that then completely disappeared after the first stint, but luckily at least in the first stint the car was working, and that allowed me to be in this position.

    “We have to understand why or what went wrong with the car as soon as we went to the slick tyres, because even when I switched back to the Inter tyres at the end, the car was night and day different to my first stint, which shows that there is something wrong.”

    So, when asked how he manages to find opportunities relative to other drivers on a wet track, Verstappen points out the Interlagos track makes it easier, with its elevation changes and various practical racing lines – which is true in dry conditions but magnified by the rain.

    “I felt comfortable with the car, confident, just trying to find the grip in different places,” he explained. “Then the car is ahead of me, so I try to pass them.

    “This track, of course, also allows you to do different lines, which I think is great because it really helps the racing. It's a good track in the dry and wet, and that really sets up more overtaking opportunities.

    “You need to feel good in the car. You need to feel comfortable to be able to risk probably a little bit more, and it's been working for me quite well here.”

    Verstappen has now started from 15th or lower in the last three Brazilian grands prix; even though those yielded a whopping 23 overtakes on his way to three podium finishes, including the aforementioned victory, the Dutchman would appreciate a more straightforward drive in 2027.

    “Maybe next year I'd love to start a bit further up front instead of starting from the back or the pit lane, but we'll find out next year,” said Verstappen, who has now grabbed the runner-up spot in the 2026 drivers’ championship.
