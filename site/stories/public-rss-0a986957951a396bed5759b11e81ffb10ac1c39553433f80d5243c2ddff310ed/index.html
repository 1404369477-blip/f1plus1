    F1荷兰大奖赛红旗：维斯塔潘重大事故引发中断

    F1荷兰大奖赛在首圈后因红旗中断，主场英雄马克斯·维斯塔潘发生重大事故。在潮湿赛道上，维斯塔潘在赞德福特最后一个弯道（倾斜的全油门卢伊恩迪克弯）内侧压上白线，导致红牛赛车失控撞墙。

    荷兰F1大奖赛因马克斯·维斯塔潘严重撞车而出示红旗

    维斯塔潘的最后一场F1主场比赛在一圈后结束，这位红牛车手遭遇了巨大的事故

    红牛车队荷兰车手马克斯·维斯塔潘撞毁的赛车

    F1荷兰大奖赛在主场英雄马克斯·维斯塔潘遭遇严重事故一圈后出示红旗。

    在湿滑的赛道上，第1圈结束时，维斯塔潘的红牛赛车失控，因为他压到了赞德沃特最后一个弯道内侧的白线——那是一个倾斜的、全油门通过的吕延迪克弯，通向主直道。

    维斯塔潘的车头直接撞上了外侧护墙，随后他的赛车横穿赛道，停在了内侧。这位荷兰车手很快报告自己“没事”，并为提前结束比赛向车队道歉。

    维斯塔潘很幸运没有撞上其他赛车，阿尔卑斯车队的皮埃尔·加斯利采取了避让动作，而在背景中，奥迪车队的尼科·霍肯伯格也险险躲过了一场严重事故，因为他的队友加布里埃尔·博托莱托在他前方打滑。

    红旗期间，所有车手都聚集在维修通道等待重新起跑。他们必须在进入维修区之前穿过一片散落的碎片，但规则允许车队在红旗期间、重新起跑前更换轮胎。预计重新起跑时间为当地时间15:33。

    当时，维斯塔潘在令人失望的排位赛后位居第七。杆位起步的兰多·诺里斯在混乱的发车阶段保持领先，积分榜领跑者安东内利超越拉塞尔升至第二。

    法拉利车队的夏尔·勒克莱尔上升两位排在第四，领先于迈凯伦车队的奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里以及队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿。

    对维斯塔潘而言，这场事故为他在家乡赛道六年的征战画上了不幸的句号——他曾在2021年、2022年和2023年赢下荷兰大奖赛复兴后的前三届比赛。由于主办方决定不再与一级方程式管理公司续约，这场比赛将从赛历上消失。

    本周早些时候，这位四届世界冠军宣布他已将红牛车队的合同延长至2030赛季结束。

    Dutch F1 grand prix red flagged after huge Max Verstappen crash

    Verstappen's final Formula 1 home race ended after one lap as the Red Bull driver suffered a colossal accident

    crashed car of Red Bull Racing's Dutch driver Max Verstappen

    Formula 1's Dutch Grand Prix was red-flagged after one lap following an enormous accident for home hero Max Verstappen.

    At the end of lap 1 on a damp circuit, Verstappen lost control of his Red Bull as he tagged the white line on the inside of Zandvoort's final turn - the banked, flat out Luyendyk corner leading onto the main straight.

    Verstappen veered head on into the outside wall, before his car crossed the track and came to a stand still on the inside. The Dutchman soon reported he was "OK" and apologised to his team for ending his race early.

    Verstappen was fortunate not to be collected by any other cars, with Alpine's Pierre Gasly taking avoiding action, while in the background Audi's Nico Hulkenberg also narrowly avoided a huge accident as his team-mate Gabriel Bortoleto spun in front of him.

    Under the red flag, all drivers gathered in the pitlane awaiting the restart. They had to navigate a field of debris before pit entry, but teams are allowed to change tyres under a red flag ahead of the restart. That restart is expected at 15:33 local time.

    At the time, Verstappen was running seventh after a disappointing qualifying session. Polesitter Lando Norris retained his lead during a chaotic start sequence, with championship leader Kimi Antonelli passing George Russell for second.

    Ferrari's Charles Leclerc gaining two positions to hold down fourth ahead of the second McLaren of Oscar Piastri and team-mate Lewis Hamilton.

    For Verstappen, the accident marks an unfortunate end to a six-year spell racing on home soil, winning the first three editions of the Dutch Grand prix revival in 2021, 2022 and 2023. The race will disappear from the calendar after organisers decided not to pursue a contract renewal with F1 management.

    Earlier in the week, the four-time world champion announced he had extended his Red Bull contract until the end of the 2030 season.
