    西班牙大奖赛周五练习赛集锦

    世界锦标赛领先者基米·安东内利在F1西班牙大奖赛马德里新赛道的首日运行中做出最快圈速，而马德林赛道极具挑战性的特点也因英国新秀阿尔维德·林德布拉德的严重撞车而显露无遗。

    在当天第一节练习赛中被梅赛德斯队友乔治·拉塞尔压制后，安东内利在二练中做出1:33.662的更快圈速，以略多于0.1秒的优势力压法拉利双雄查尔斯·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿。

    成绩表进一步证明了安东内利极高的自信心，上周末他在意大利大奖赛上从第19位发车赢得主场胜利，将他在积分榜上对拉塞尔的领先优势扩大到66分，2026赛季还剩10站比赛。

    从周五练习赛的情况来看，梅赛德斯和法拉利实力接近，可能在周六排位赛中上演激动人心的杆位争夺，西班牙首都充满巨大兴奋，因为F1自1981年以来首次重返该城市。

    阿尔维德·林德布拉德在二练13号弯严重撞车，引发马德林赛道首次红旗

    许多人此前预测周五——以及整个周末剩余时间——会出现大量红旗，因为这条赛道狭窄且极具惩罚性，所有车手都从未在此比赛过。

    虽然周五的F2和F3比赛中出现了多次撞车和红旗，但F1车手展现了他们的高超水平，在一练中避免了任何重大事故，然而林德布拉德在二练刚过半程后便遭遇了麻烦。

    这位19岁车手表现令人印象深刻，刚刚升至第四位，但在下一圈飞驰圈中推得太猛，在13号弯失控，高速旋转撞向护栏。

    阿尔维德·林德布拉德在周五练习赛撞车后形容马德林赛道“不是一条容易的赛道”。

    林德布拉德表示：“这不是一条容易的赛道。我们都在努力寻找极限。这对我们所有人来说都是新的。只是犯了个小错误，在13号弯做得过头了。很遗憾。我向机械师和车队道歉。今晚他们要多干活了。”

    “但从大局来看，这是不错的一天。我们非常快。我凭实力跑到第四，距离第一只有0.2秒，这非常强。”

    “对车队进入明天充满信心。我只专注于重置，试着把它抛在脑后，吸取所有好的经验。”

    F2的塔萨纳波尔·因特拉普瓦萨克在赛车起火后安然走开！

    超过10分钟的中断结束了当时正在进行的排位赛模拟，确保了安东内利在成绩表榜首的位置。

    拉塞尔在似乎难以优化赛车调校后排名第五，但仍力压红牛的马克斯·维斯塔潘和迈凯伦的奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里。

    “第一次驾驶这条赛道很有趣，”拉塞尔说。“驾驶新赛道总是很棒的体验。”

    “一练是强劲的一节。二练我们做了一些调校改动，效果不太好，所以我们可能会改回去。到目前为止一切顺利。”

    车手们在周五练习赛后分享对马德林赛道的第一印象。

    卫冕世界冠军兰多·诺里斯因变速箱故障仅跑两圈便退出本节，未能做出时间。

    鉴于诺里斯在安东内利蒙扎夺冠前取得背靠背胜利，迈凯伦本被预期在马德里具有竞争力，但周五几乎没有证据表明这一点。

    虽然诺里斯近期总体比皮亚斯特里更快，但在新赛道损失整个一节练习赛将使这位英国车手在进入周六时处于不利位置。

    Spanish GP: Kimi Antonelli edges Ferrari in Madrid practice as Arvid Lindblad suffers first F1 crash at challenging Madring circuit

    Kimi Antonelli fastest from Ferrari's Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton in Practice Two; Arvid Lindblad first F1 driver to crash at new Madrid track; watch Spanish GP Qualifying from the all-new Madring circuit live on Sky Sports F1 at 3pm on Saturday

    Highlights from Friday's practice sessions at the Spanish Grand Prix

    World championship leader Kimi Antonelli was fastest on Formula 1's first day of running at the Spanish Grand Prix's new Madrid circuit as the hugely challenging nature of the Madring was exhibited by a big crash for British rookie Arvid Lindblad.

    After being outpaced by his Mercedes team-mate George Russell in the day's first session, Antonelli produced a quicker time of 1:33.662 in Practice Two to edge out Ferrari duo Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton by little more than a tenth of a second.

    The timesheet was further evidence of Antonelli's sky-high confidence, which was elevated by a stunning victory at the Italian Grand Prix last weekend as he won his home race from 19th on the grid to move 66 points clear of Russell at the top of the standings with 10 rounds of the 2026 season remaining.

    On the evidence of Friday's sessions, Mercedes and Ferrari are closely matched and could deliver an exciting pole contest in qualifying on Saturday, with huge excitement in the Spanish capital as F1 returns to the city for the first time since 1981.

    Arvid Lindblad brought out the first red flag at the Madring track after this heavy crash on turn 13 during second practice

    Many had predicted a high number of red flags on Friday - and throughout the rest of the weekend - due to the tight and punishing nature of a circuit that the field have never raced at before.

    While there were several crashes and red flags in Friday's F2 and F3 sessions, the F1 drivers showed their excellence to avoid any major incidents in first practice, but it was Lindblad who came unstuck just beyond the halfway point of Practice Two.

    The 19-year-old was driving impressively and had just elevated himself to fourth when he pushed too hard on his next flying lap, losing control at Turn 13 and spinning into the barrier at high speed.

    Arvid Lindblad describes the Madring circuit as 'not an easy track' following his crash during Friday's practice.

    Lindblad said: "It's not an easy track. We are all pushing to find the limits. It's new for all of us. Just made a bit of a mistake and overdid it in Turn 13. A shame. I apologise to the mechanics and the team. It's extra work for them tonight.

    "But looking at the big picture, it's been a good day. We are very fast. I was running P4 on merit and was only two tenths from P1 which is really strong.

    "A lot of confidence in the team going into tomorrow. I'm just focused on resetting and trying to block it out and taking all the good learnings."

    F2's Tasanapol Inthraphuvasak walks away after his car went up in flames!

    A delay of just over 10 minutes put an end to the qualifying simulations that had been taking place at that stage, securing Antonelli's position at the top of the timesheet.

    Russell was fifth after appearing to struggle to optimise his setup, but still managed to edge out Red Bull's Max Verstappen and McLaren's Oscar Piastri.

    "It was fun to drive the track for the first time," Russell said. "It's always a great experience driving a new circuit.

    "FP1 was a strong session. FP2 we made some setup changes which weren't quite as good, so we will probably revert back on that. So far so good."

    Drivers share their first thoughts of the Madring circuit after friday's practice.

    Reigning world champion Lando Norris was unable to set a time after a gearbox failure took him out of the session after just two laps.

    McLaren were expected to be competitive in Madrid given Norris had claimed back-to-back victories before Antonelli's win in Monza, but there was little evidence of that on Friday.

    While Norris has generally been quicker than Piastri recently, losing an entire session at a new circuit will put the Brit on the back foot going into Saturday.
