    皮亚斯蒂回应与马克·韦伯分道扬镳后的管理计划

    麦克拉伦一级方程式车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂表示，他与经理安和马克·韦伯的合作关系终止后，他未来的管理结构尚未确定。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂在马克·韦伯分手后讨论管理计划

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂在首次公开讲话中表示，他与韦伯家族的合作关系已经结束

    迈凯轮一级方程式车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂表示，他与经理安和马克·韦伯的合作关系终止后，他未来的管理结构尚未确定

    皮亚斯蒂于11月9日发布了一份联合新闻稿，表明他与韦伯家族“共同结束了协议”

    他们的七年合作见证了这位年轻车手在青训方程式赛车中的飞速进步，连续获得了Formula Renault Eurocup、Formula 3和Formula 2的冠军头衔；随后，他的经理们在皮亚斯特里职业生涯的决定性转折中发挥了关键作用，他惊人地离开了他的阿尔派车队，签约加盟迈凯伦成为一级方程式赛车手。

    然而，韦伯夫妇在2026赛季初期采取了退步的策略，马克不再在赛道旁协助皮亚斯特里。这位澳大利亚人得到了他在Formula 2的Prema车队赛车工程师佩德罗·马托斯和心理咨询师艾玛·默里的支持，但截至目前，他无法透露任何关于他未来管理的线索。

    皮亚斯特里在与韦伯夫妇分手后首次公开讲话说：“他们显然在我的职业生涯中扮演了巨大的角色，并帮助我达到了这个阶段，尤其是在我的青训生涯结束和我的F1生涯开始时。无论是在赛道上的支持还是在幕后的支持都极其宝贵。我认为现在是我们各自走上不同道路并尝试新事物的时候了。”

    “目前我没有任何确定的事情。我还在观察我周围以及我所在团队的未来看起来会是什么样子，但是的，他们在我的职业生涯中扮演了极其重要的角色。”

    关于他未来将由谁代表的问题，皮亚斯特里再次强调：“目前还没有任何人确认。我显然有一个临时的安排，但未来没有任何确定的事情，我将在适当的时候确定这一点。”

    皮亚斯特里于2025年3月与迈凯轮车队签订了多年合同，理论上合同将持续到2028年底。

    2026赛季并没有达到他的预期，作为去年夺冠热门，他很难适应F1的新赛车。他目前正处于16场比赛无领奖台的成绩，这是他职业生涯中最长的无领奖台记录。

    当被问及他希望在赛季最后三场大奖赛中实现什么目标，以增强信心并为2027年做准备时，皮亚斯特里表示：“只是试图继续学习，这是最重要的事情。我知道这并不是一个令人兴奋的答案，但只是试图继续建立理解。”

    “今年我们一开始赛季早期试图弄清楚我需要做哪些不同的尝试，然后逐渐对需要达到的目标有了相当好的理解，但实际上去实现它却是一个挑战。”

    “在过去的五到六场比赛中，确实有更多令人鼓舞的迹象。这并不是线性的，有些比赛表现更好，有些则不尽如人意。巴西站，即使结果没有显示出来[他最终排名第六，落后于队友兰多·诺里斯56秒]，这可能是我在很长时间内最好的比赛节奏。”

    “所以，对我来说，就是努力保持这种进步。显然，作为一支车队，我们想在车队 constructors' championship 中击败红牛，对我来说，我很想重返领奖台或者尝试赢得一场胜利。但最重要的是，尝试尽可能地为明年做好安排，希望我们能在接下来的几周内实现这一点。”

    Oscar Piastri addresses management plans after Mark Webber split

    Oscar Piastri has spoken publicly for the first time since he and the Webbers parted ways

    McLaren Formula 1 driver Oscar Piastri says his upcoming management structure is yet to be defined, after his partnership with managers Ann and Mark Webber was terminated.

    Piastri put out a joint press release on 9 November, indicating he and the Webbers had “mutually ended their agreement”.

    Their seven-year partnership saw the youngster’s meteoric rise in junior formulae, with back-to-back Formula Renault Eurocup, Formula 3 and Formula 2 titles; his managers then played a key role in Piastri’s career-defining move as he stunningly walked away from his Alpine team and signed for McLaren as an F1 race driver.

    However, the Webbers took a step back early in the 2026 season, with Mark no longer assisting Piastri trackside. The Australian has been getting support from Pedro Matos, his race engineer at Prema in Formula 2, and mental coach Emma Murray, but as of now he can’t provide any clues as to his future management.

    Speaking publicly for the first time since his split with the Webbers, Piastri said: “They played a massive role in my career obviously and getting to this point, particularly the end of my junior career and the start of my F1 career. Both the support at the track and behind the scenes was incredibly valuable. And I think now is the appropriate time to go on our separate paths and do new things.

    “I don't have anything set in stone at the moment. I'm still kind of looking at what the future looks like necessarily around me and the team around me, but yeah, they played a massively important role in my career.”

    Pressed on who he was going to be represented by going forward, Piastri reiterated: “I don't have anyone confirmed. I've obviously got a kind of interim set-up at the moment, but no, nothing set in stone for the future, and I'll define that in due course.”

    Piastri signed a multi-year contract with McLaren in March 2025, with the deal theoretically running to the end of 2028.

    The 2026 season hasn’t matched his expectations, as last year’s title favourite has struggled to adapt to F1’s new machinery. He is on a 16-race podiumless run, now the longest streak in his career.

    Asked what he would like to achieve in the last three grands prix of the campaign to build on confidence and performance ahead of 2027, Piastri said: “Just trying to keep learning, that's the biggest thing. It's not a very exciting answer, I get it, but just trying to keep building understanding.

    “This year we've gone from in the earlier part of the season trying to get our head around what I need to do differently to then having a pretty good understanding of what it needs to look like, but then actually going and achieving it has been challenging.

    “There's definitely been more encouraging signs in the last five or six races. It's not been linear, there's been some better races, there's been some worse ones in amongst those. Brazil, even if the result didn't show [he finished sixth, 56 seconds adrift of race-winning team-mate Lando Norris], it was probably the best race pace that I've had for a long time.

    “So, for me, just trying to keep building on that. Obviously as a team we want to beat Red Bull in the constructors' championship, and for myself I'd love to get back on the podium or try and snag a win. But the most important thing is to try and set up next year as best as I can, and hopefully we can achieve that in these next few weeks.”
