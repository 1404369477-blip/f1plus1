    哈贾尔称美国站周末开局灾难 排位赛仍实现逆转

    哈贾尔表示，尽管美国大奖赛周末开局堪称“灾难”，但排位赛实现逆转后，红牛可以凭借两辆赛车在前排的数量优势挑战杆位得主安东内利。

    哈贾尔称美国站周末开局灾难 排位赛仍实现逆转

    哈贾尔表示，红牛将利用两辆赛车在前排的优势，在美国大奖赛上挑战安东内利

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，伊萨克·哈贾尔，红牛车队

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔表示，红牛可以利用其数量优势，在美国大奖赛上击败杆位得主、当前领跑积分榜的基米·安东内利，此前他刚从周末“灾难性”的开局中恢复过来。

    这位法国-阿尔及利亚车手最终仅落后队友马克斯·维斯塔潘0.036秒，在美洲赛道落后安东内利的杆位成绩0.215秒，并表示对赛车设置的大幅调整彻底改变了赛车。

    哈贾尔将在发车格上排在第三位，位于安东内利和维斯塔潘之后，与法拉利的夏尔·勒克莱尔并列第四。

    “对我来说，这个周末简直是一场灾难，”哈贾尔在排位赛后的新闻发布会上解释道。“我的圈速一直都被删除。今天早上，我在软胎上连一圈都没能完成。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，伊萨克·哈贾尔，红牛车队

    “我知道车里有些东西，因为有时候我能跑半圈并且感觉很舒服，但我始终无法完成整圈。但在那次排位赛中，赛车对我来说真的更有活力了一些。我们在调校上真的下了很大功夫。这个周末我们尝试了很多东西，而且真的得到了回报。

    “所以，从这一点来说，真的非常非常令人满意。但我有点遗憾，不是太多关于最后一圈，而是更多关于Q3的第一次尝试，因为是的，最后一圈赛道状况更差，那不是一圈好圈，但每个人都是一样的。

    “在第一圈，我真的觉得因为那巨大的顺风我损失了很多时间，我觉得今天还有更多潜力。但是的，我对这次回归感到高兴，可以说。”

    当被问及他是否认为自己能在明天的大奖赛中挑战安东内利争夺胜利时，哈贾尔指出，拥有两辆红牛赛车分列第二和第三位是一种力量。

    “是我们两个人，所以这算是一点优势。所以我们会尝试利用这一点，”哈贾尔说。

    美国大奖赛将于10月25日星期日举行。

    Isack Hadjar rues "disaster of a weekend" despite US GP qualifying turnaround

    Isack Hadjar says Red Bull will use its two cars at the front to challenge Kimi Antonelli in the United States Grand Prix

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Isack Hadjar says Red Bull can try to use its numbers advantage to beat polesitter and current championship leader Kimi Antonelli at the United States Grand Prix, after recovering from a "disaster" start to the weekend.

    The French-Algerian driver ended up just 0.036 seconds behind team-mate Max Verstappen and 0.215s off Antonelli's pole time at the Circuit of the Americas, and said a big push on set-up had transformed the car.

    Hadjar will line up third on the starting grid behind Antonelli and Verstappen, and alongside Ferrari's Charles Leclerc in fourth.

    "It's been a disaster of a weekend for me," Hadjar explained in the post-qualifying press conference. "I've had all my lap times deleted all the time. This morning, I could not complete a single lap on the soft tyres.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    "I knew there was something in the car because there were times when I could do half a lap and I felt comfortable, but I could never finish it. But in that qualifying, the car really came alive a bit more for me. We really dug deep on the set-up. We tried so many things this weekend, and it really paid off.

    "So, on that note, it's really, really satisfying. But I'm a bit gutted, not too much about the last lap, but more about the first attempt in Q3, because yes, the track was worse on that final lap and it wasn’t a great lap, but it was the same for everyone.

    "On that first lap, I really felt like I lost so much time with that massive tailwind I got, and I felt like there was a bit more today. But yeah, I'm happy with the comeback, let's say."

    When asked if he thinks he can challenge Antonelli for the win in the grand prix tomorrow, Hadjar pointed to the power of having two Red Bull cars in second and third.

    "It's the two of us, so it's a bit of an advantage. So we'll try and use that," Hadjar said.

    The United States Grand Prix will take place on Sunday 25 October.
