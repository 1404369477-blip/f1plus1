    传BBC筹拍汉密尔顿与阿隆索2007年迈凯伦内斗纪录片

    前西班牙F1评论员安东尼奥·洛瓦托称，BBC正在制作一部关于刘易斯·汉密尔顿与费尔南多·阿隆索2007年在迈凯伦队内激烈竞争的纪录片，BBC尚未确认该项目。

    关于刘易斯·汉密尔顿与费尔南多·阿隆索F1竞争的纪录片据称正在制作中

    一名前西班牙F1评论员声称，BBC正在制作一部关于刘易斯·汉密尔顿与费尔南多·阿隆索2007年在迈凯伦那段臭名昭著的内斗的纪录片

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，迈凯伦，费尔南多·阿隆索，迈凯伦

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车不止于运动的故事。

    据前西班牙F1评论员安东尼奥·洛瓦托透露，BBC正在制作一部关于刘易斯·汉密尔顿和费尔南多·阿隆索2007年在迈凯伦共度的混乱赛季的纪录片。

    洛瓦托目前是西班牙网站SoyMotor.com的联合总监，本周早些时候他在该媒体的Twitch频道上讨论了这一据称的项目。需要指出的是，BBC尚未确认这部纪录片。

    “这件事肯定会被讲述出来。问题是……会讲述什么？因为我知道BBC正在准备一部纪录片，”他说。

    洛瓦托曾是西班牙F1转播的解说员，见证了阿隆索最辉煌的赛季——2005年和2006年他随雷诺争夺两届世界冠军，以及2007年他在迈凯伦的那一年。

    “他们邀请我参与，我拒绝了邀请，”他谈到这部纪录片时说。“我不想卷入那些故事。我知道我经历过什么，我知道发生了什么，也许从我的角度来看，这也可能有些失真，当纪录片出来时，我想看看。”

    他补充说，其他报道那个赛季的记者也将参与。

    “我知道还有其他记者同事，那一年他们也在场，他们会参与，会在那里讲述部分发生的事情。我们看看他们如何对待它，如何制作，以及那个故事如何被讲述。”

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，迈凯伦，费尔南多·阿隆索，迈凯伦

    2007赛季仍然是锦标赛历史上最具戏剧性的赛季之一。阿隆索以两届卫冕冠军的身份来到迈凯伦，而汉密尔顿在他身旁完成首秀，希望一上来就能迅速进入状态。

    随着赛季的推进，紧张气氛不断加剧，最终彻底爆发：两人在赛季结束时同积109分，落后于夺得冠军的基米·莱科宁一分。迈凯伦还因“间谍门”事件被排除在车队锦标赛之外，而阿隆索在赛季结束后离开，重返雷诺。

    将近20年后，两位车手仍在参赛。汉密尔顿代表法拉利，以229分在2026年车手积分榜上排名第三。阿隆索如今效力于阿斯顿·马丁，处境艰难，仅积7分。

    两人都将出现在10月23日至25日于奥斯汀举行的美国大奖赛发车格上。

    Documentary on Lewis Hamilton and Fernando Alonso's F1 rivalry reportedly in the works

    A former Spanish F1 commentator claims the BBC is working on a documentary about Lewis Hamilton and Fernando Alonso's infamous 2007 McLaren rivalry

    Lewis Hamilton, McLaren, Fernando Alonso, McLaren

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    According to former Spanish F1 commentator Antonio Lobato, the BBC is making a documentary about Lewis Hamilton and Fernando Alonso's chaotic 2007 season together at McLaren.

    Lobato, who is now the co-director of Spanish website SoyMotor.com, discussed the supposed project on the outlet's Twitch channel earlier this week. It should be noted that the BBC is yet to confirm the documentary.

    "It will definitely be told. The question is... what will be told? Because I know the BBC is preparing a documentary," he said.

    Lobato was the voice of F1 coverage in Spain during Alonso's greatest seasons as he fought for two championship wins with Renault in 2005 and 2006 and during his year with McLaren in 2007.

    "They invited me to take part and I turned the invitation down," he said of the documentary. "I don't want to get involved in those stories. I know what I lived through, I know what happened, perhaps from my point of view, which may also be a bit distorted, and when the documentary comes out, I'd like to watch it."

    He added that other journalists who covered that season will be taking part.

    "I know there are other journalist colleagues who were there that year who are going to do it, who are going to be there and will tell part of what happened. We'll see how they treat it, how it is made, and how that story is told."

    Lewis Hamilton, McLaren, Fernando Alonso, McLaren

    The 2007 season remains one of the most dramatic in the history of the championship. Alonso arrived at McLaren as the two-time reigning champion, and Hamilton made his debut alongside him hoping to hit the ground running.

    Tension grew as the season continued, and eventually it boiled over as the pair ended on a level 109 points at the end of the season, one point behind Kimi Raikkonen, who won the championship. McLaren was also excluded from the constructors' championship over Spygate, and Alonso left at the end of the season to return to Renault.

    Almost 20 years later, both drivers are still racing. Hamilton is third in the 2026 drivers' standings with Ferrari on 229 points. Alonso, now at Aston Martin and struggling, has seven points.

    Both will be on the grid at the United States Grand Prix in Austin, which takes place from 23 to 25 October.
