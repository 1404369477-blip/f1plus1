    汉密尔顿在新加坡大奖赛激烈角逐后送医

    经过62圈的激烈角逐，法拉利车手汉密尔顿在极端炎热和潮湿的滨海湾街道赛道上，赛后并未出现在强制性的媒体区。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿在新加坡大奖赛艰难的62圈比赛中后，被送往国际汽联医疗团队

    法拉利确认刘易斯·汉密尔顿在新加坡极端高温下，使用旧轮胎进行的残酷62圈比赛中感到不适

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔、法拉利、刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利

    为法拉利车迷。最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，车手更新，技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应。

    在极端炎热潮湿的滨海湾街道赛道上经历了一场折磨人的62圈比赛后，法拉利车队车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿在赛后强制媒体区完全缺席。

    法拉利发言人已正式确认，这位七届世界冠军在走出SF-26赛车驾驶舱后需要立即的医疗关注。

    “法拉利发言人表示，汉密尔顿在比赛后感到不适，并被国际汽联医疗团队接诊，”卢克·史密斯报道。由于他的身体状况，国际汽联明确允许他免于履行所有标准的赛后媒体义务。

    在线赛车新闻杂志RacingNews365也证实了法拉利车队的声明，指出艰苦的街道赛道的剧烈体力考验迫使这位经验丰富的车手接受了赛道医生的全面检查。

    新加坡的炎热气候对汉密尔顿造成了巨大的身体负担，这在他比赛中犯下的一个关键战略失误下被大大加剧。在一场虚拟安全车晚期的维修区呼叫中，汉密尔顿积极忽视法拉利维修区的呼叫，被迫在磨损严重的旧轮胎上艰难地在要求苛刻的赛道上驾驶。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、兰多·诺里斯、麦克拉伦

    这种严重的抓地力不足使他完全无法在大奖赛的最后阶段捍卫自己的赛道位置。汉密尔顿在车手排名中猛烈下跌，被梅赛德斯车手、青训车队车手基米·安东内利以及两辆麦克拉伦车手兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里压倒。起初他在领奖台上轻松驾驶，但最终他以令人失望的第六名冲过终点。

    有趣的是，这并不是第一次新加坡令人窒息的滨海湾气候让这位英国车手身体不适。在2024赛季中，汉密尔顿和他的当时队友乔治·拉塞尔在新加坡的检旗后也感到非常不适。

    Lewis Hamilton sent to FIA medical team after grueling Singapore Grand Prix

    Ferrari confirms Lewis Hamilton felt unwell following a brutal 62-lap battle on older tires in the extreme Singapore heat

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Following a punishing 62-lap battle under the extreme heat and humidity of the Marina Bay Street Circuit, Scuderia Ferrari driver Lewis Hamilton was completely absent from the mandatory post-race media pen.

    A Ferrari spokesperson has officially confirmed that the seven-time world champion required immediate medical attention after stepping out of the SF-26 cockpit.

    "A Ferrari spokesperson says that Hamilton felt unwell after the race and was seen by the FIA medical team," Luke Smith reported. As a direct result of his physical condition, Hamilton was explicitly granted permission by the governing body to skip all of his standard post-race media obligations.

    Online racing publication RacingNews365 also verified the Ferrari garage statement, noting that the intense physical rigours of the gruelling street circuit forced the veteran driver to be thoroughly checked over by track doctors.

    The intense physical toll of the Singapore climate was massively compounded by a critical strategic blunder made directly by Hamilton mid-race. After actively ignoring a pit call from the Ferrari pitwall during a late virtual safety car period, Hamilton was forced to wrestle his car around the demanding layout on heavily degraded, older tyres.

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Lando Norris, McLaren

    That severe lack of grip left him completely powerless to defend his track position in the closing stages of the grand prix. Hamilton violently plummeted down the running order, getting steamrolled by Mercedes rookie Kimi Antonelli and both McLaren drivers, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri. After initially running comfortably in the podium places, he ultimately crossed the finish line in a highly disappointing sixth place.

    Interestingly, this is not the first time the suffocating Marina Bay climate has physically broken the British driver. During the 2024 campaign, both Hamilton and his then-Mercedes team-mate George Russell also felt severely unwell following the chequered flag in Singapore.
