    小红牛车手伊萨克·哈德贾最贵交易卡以五位数价格售出震惊众人

    小红牛F1车手伊萨克·哈德贾在Topps公司公布他最贵交易卡售价时感到震惊。这张带有三个小红牛标志的签名卡被展示给他，他立刻认出，但最初的估价却远低于实际售价。

    哈贾尔 惊讶地发现他最昂贵的交易卡以五位数的价格售出

    红牛车手在 Topps 展示他签名的卡片时猜测了 3,000 美元，并在 35,000 美元时放弃

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    当 Topps 揭示了 哈贾尔 最有价值的交易卡售出价格时，红牛一级方程式车手 哈贾尔 愣住了。

    卡片公司拍摄了这位法国人被展示一张带有三个红牛标志的签名卡，并要求他为这张卡定价。他立刻就认出来了，但他的第一个猜测有些偏离。

    "我记得签过这张卡。我不知道，我不擅长这个，但可能是3千," 哈吉说。

    被告知要定更高的价格，他逐渐将价格提高到1万美元、1.5万美元和2.5万美元，每一步都越来越不相信。

    "好吧，我停在3.5万," 他说，然后尝试了5万美元，这太高了。他的最终猜测是4.3万美元，但还是比实际价格4.4万美元少了。

    问他会对买家说什么，哈吉尔指向卡片上他签名旁边的信息：‘你真幸运！’

    ‘他自己在考虑签售一些自己的东西，’一位粉丝开玩笑说，另一位评论道：‘作为一个哈吉尔的收藏家，这太棒了！’

    ‘伊萨克，你不应该这么惊讶。相信我，世界上有很多（可能太多）人真的（可能太过于）喜欢你，’另一位粉丝写道，还有人也发帖说：‘他正在考虑把下一个请求他签名的卡片留给自己，然后自己卖掉。’

    这段视频是在Fanatics Collectibles公司办公室拍摄的，Fanatics Collectibles是Topps的母公司，自2020年以来一直持有官方一级方程式交易卡许可。

    自从那时起，一级方程式卡牌市场蓬勃发展，销量最大的仍然是那些知名明星。今年早些时候，Topps NOW卡牌上标记着刘易斯·汉密尔顿在巴塞罗那-加泰罗尼亚大奖赛上为法拉利赢得的第一场比赛，成为有史以来销量最高的F1 Topps NOW卡牌，印刷量超过50,000份。

    Isack Hadjar stunned as his most expensive trading card sells for five-figure sum

    The Red Bull driver guessed $3,000 when Topps showed him the signed card, and gave up at $35,000

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Red Bull Formula 1 driver Isack Hadjar was left speechless when Topps revealed how much the most valuable of his trading cards had sold for.

    The card company filmed the Frenchman being shown an autographed card carrying three Red Bull patches and asked him to put a price on it. He recognised it straight away, but his first guess was some way off.

    "I remember signing this one. I don't know, I'm not good at this, but probably 3k," Hadjar said.

    Told to aim higher, he worked his way up through $10,000, $15,000 and $25,000, growing more disbelieving with every step.

    "OK, I stop at 35," he said, before trying $50,000, which was too high. His final guess of $43,000 left him just short of the real figure: $44,000.

    Asked what he would say to the buyer, Hadjar pointed to the message he had written next to his signature on the card itself: "Lucky you!"

    "He’s thinking about signing and selling some himself," one fan joked, while another commented: "As a Hadjar collector, this is awesome!"

    "Isack, you shouldn’t be so surprised. Believe me, there are plenty of people in the world who really (perhaps too much) like you," another fan wrote, and someone else also posted: "He's thinking about pocketing the next one they ask him to sign and selling it himself."

    The video was shot at the offices of Fanatics Collectibles, the parent company of Topps, which has held the official Formula 1 trading card licence since 2020.

    F1 cards have boomed since then, and the biggest sellers are still the established stars. Earlier this year, the Topps NOW card marking Lewis Hamilton's first win for Ferrari, at the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix, became the highest-selling F1 Topps NOW card ever, with more than 50,000 copies printed.
