    布朗解释为何在2027年拍卖前就出售麦凯轮F1赛车

    麦凯轮赛车首席执行官扎克·布朗解释了为何在赛车比赛前就将其F1赛车出售，称否则只能将它们闲置。

    Zak Brown解释了为什么在2027年拍卖会之前，麦凯轮车队会出售其F1赛车

    麦凯轮车队首席执行官表示，车队宁愿将赛车卖给收藏家，也不愿让它们像堆在架子上一样闲置

    对于麦凯轮车队的粉丝来说，来自沃金克的最新消息，包括车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个周末的赛事

    麦凯轮赛车首席执行官Zak Brown解释了为什么在赛车甚至还没转一圈的情况下，沃金克车队就要将其F1赛车拍卖出去，他说另一种选择是将它们藏起来

    麦凯轮车队将在12月9日阿布扎比的RM Sotheby's拍卖会上拍卖其2027年的赛车MCL41，拍卖会将在赛季决赛三天后举行。一辆由兰多·诺里斯或奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里使用的底盘将与2027年规格的梅赛德斯动力单元一同出售。

    这紧随去年对本赛季赛车MCL40A的出售，该车在去年12月的阿布扎比RM Sotheby's拍卖会上以惊人的1148万美元成交。这是F1赛车首次在比赛前被出售。

    由于旨在阻止竞争对手获取车队知识产权的规则，买家将不会在2028年之前收到那辆车，并且在此期间被提供一辆2025年的展示车。

    在拉斯维加斯的团队负责人新闻发布会上被问及销售背后的想法时，布朗表示，这比将赛车锁起来更有意义。

    “是的，另一种选择是把它们都包起来放在货架上，这真是太可惜了。我们有一个很棒的汽车业务，我们有一个非常热情的粉丝群，我们粉丝群中有一部分人对收集汽车有着浓厚的兴趣，”布朗说。

    布朗本人也是一个热衷的汽车收藏家，他说去年的买家能够跟随他们的赛车整个赛季。

    “因此，去年与苏富比合作，我们卖出了26辆车，有人有机会参与这次旅程，观看他们的赛车比赛，赢得一些比赛。所以我们至少一年要做一次这样的事情。这是我们打算做的事情，”他说。

    麦克拉伦的2026款赛车在本赛季赢得了三场比赛，全部由诺里斯驾驶，分别在匈牙利、荷兰和巴西。车队目前在车手积分榜上排名第四，积分为398分，落后第三名的红牛17分。

    今年的中标者将在2027赛季开始前参观麦凯轮的沃金总部，有机会见到布朗、诺里斯和皮亚斯特里，并受邀参加车队2027年的发布会。

    布朗表示，这次销售让一小部分粉丝体验到了原本不存在的体验。

    “我们有一个很好的生意。我们已经为我们的勒芒赛车制作了35辆赛道车，所以对我们来说，这是为我们那些处于特权地位、能够拥有一级方程式赛车的粉丝提供体验机会。否则，我们每年只制作四到五辆车，最后它们都堆在架子上，这对任何人都没有好处，”他说。

    Zak Brown explains why McLaren keeps selling F1 cars before they race ahead of 2027 auction

    The McLaren CEO says the team would rather sell its cars to collectors than leave them "stacking up" on a shelf

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    McLaren Racing CEO Zak Brown has explained why the Woking outfit is selling its Formula 1 cars at auction before they have even turned a wheel, saying the alternative is leaving them hidden away.

    McLaren will auction its 2027 car, the MCL41, with RM Sotheby's in Abu Dhabi on 9 December, three days after the season finale. A chassis used by either Lando Norris or Oscar Piastri will be offered with a 2027-specification Mercedes power unit.

    It follows last year's sale of this season's car, the MCL40A, which went for a staggering $11.48million at RM Sotheby's Abu Dhabi sale last December. It was the first time an F1 car had been sold before it had raced.

    The buyer will not receive that car until 2028, because of rules designed to stop rivals getting access to a team's intellectual property, and was offered a 2025 show car to display in the meantime.

    Asked in the team principals' press conference in Las Vegas about the thinking behind the sales, Brown said it made more sense than locking the cars away.

    "Yeah, the other alternative is you leave them all on shelves under wraps, which is a shame. We have a great automotive business, we have a very passionate fan base, and we have a certain part of our fan base that has a passion to collect cars," Brown said.

    Brown, a keen car collector himself, said last year's buyer had been able to follow their car all season.

    "And so last year with Sotheby's, we sold '26 cars and someone had the ability to come on the journey, watch their car race, win some races. And so we're going to do that once a year at least. That's our intention," he said.

    McLaren's 2026 car has won three races this season, all with Norris, in Hungary, the Netherlands and Brazil. The team currently sits fourth in the constructors' championship with 398 points, 17 points behind Red Bull in third.

    This year's winning bidder will get a tour of McLaren's Woking headquarters before the 2027 season, the chance to meet Brown, Norris and Piastri, and an invitation to the team's 2027 launch.

    Brown said the sales gave a small group of fans an experience that would otherwise not exist.

    "We have a nice business. We've done 35 track cars of our Le Mans car, so for us it's about experiential opportunities for those fans of ours that are in a privileged position to be able to own Formula 1 cars. Otherwise, we make four or five cars a year and they end up stacking up on a shelf, and that's no good for anybody," he said.
