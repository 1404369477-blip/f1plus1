    瓦尔特里·博塔斯击败科尔顿·赫塔获得2027年凯迪拉克F1席位

    现任车手瓦尔特里·博塔斯将继续与凯迪拉克F1车队合作至2027年，车队决定不将美国测试车手科尔顿·赫塔快速提升为正式车手。

    瓦尔特里·博塔斯击败科尔顿·赫塔获得2027年凯迪拉克F1车手席位

    凯迪拉克决定与瓦尔特里·博塔斯和塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯一起参加2027年F1赛事

    瓦尔特里·博塔斯，凯迪拉克车队，回答Autosport读者的提问

    现任车手瓦尔特里·博塔斯将在美国车队决定不加速测试车手科尔顿·赫塔晋升至赛车席位后，继续留在凯迪拉克一级方程式车队至2027年。

    博塔斯将按照原计划继续与凯迪拉克车队并肩作战，与塞戈维亚·佩雷斯一起，两位赢得过比赛的资深车手在最初加入F1的第11支车队时都签署了多年合约。

    但在与墨西哥人竞争速度的过程中，博塔斯的座位在车队和组织内部以及由丹·托里斯斯领导的多数股东TWG Motorsports公司受到了更多的审视，该公司权衡是否在2027年就给赫塔一个机会。

    毕竟，车队不惜一切代价为这位前IndyCar明星准备参加F1的比赛，包括艰苦的F2赛季和越来越多的FP1练习，以确保这位26岁的加利福尼亚人能够达到所需的40个FIA超级驾照积分。

    博塔斯的运气并不好，他与新车队的一系列性能、可靠性和零部件一致性问题的困扰使得这位芬兰人的表现更难公平地评判。

    随着凯迪拉克仍远离一线F1运营，托里斯和车队负责人Marcin Budkowski现在似乎已经达成一致，认为博塔斯的丰富F1经验——目前涵盖265场大奖赛——将在2027年为车队带来最大益处。

    凯迪拉克周三表示："这一宣布提供了连续性和稳定性，因为凯迪拉克在构建其首个赛季的赛车网格上继续前进。它继续其长期目标，即在全球赛车运动的顶峰建立一个有竞争力的车队。

    这现在完成了明年的22车车阵，而凯迪拉克尚未发布关于赫塔下一步计划的更新，表示"预备和测试车手将在适当的时候宣布"。

    拥有10场胜利的37岁博塔斯表示："致相关人士，我将留下！我期待着与凯迪拉克一起继续这段旅程进入2027年。我们知道从头开始建立一个全新的F1车队不会容易，但正是这一点让这个挑战如此特别。

    “我们已经打下了基础，我们在学习和进步，还有很多更多的事情要做，我渴望更多，我相信我们一起正在建造的东西。这个故事才刚刚开始。”

    托里斯补充说：“瓦尔特里的经验、视角和承诺对我们团队来说是非常重要的优势。连续性为我们提供了一个强大的平台，随着我们继续发展团队并朝着一级方程式的目标努力，我们期待着我们共同旅程的下一阶段。”

    “在拉斯维加斯大奖赛前夕，团队中的每个人都很高兴瓦尔特里与切科一起锁定2027年的位置，并期待着我们共同旅程的下一阶段。”

    Valtteri Bottas beats Colton Herta to 2027 Cadillac F1 seat

    Cadillac has decided to stick with Valtteri Bottas alongside Sergio Perez for F1 2027

    Valtteri Bottas, Cadillac Racing, answers Autosport readers' questions

    Incumbent Valtteri Bottas will remain with the Cadillac Formula 1 team through 2027 after the US-owned team decided against fast-tracking American test driver Colton Herta to a race seat.

    Bottas will stay on board with Cadillac alongside Sergio Perez as initially planned, with both race-winning veterans having signed a multi-year deal when they initially joined F1's 11th team.

    But after struggling for pace against the Mexican, Bottas’ seat came under increased scrutiny within the organisation and majority owner TWG Motorsports, led by CEO Dan Towriss, weighed up whether to give Herta a chance as early as 2027.

    After all, the team had spared no expense readying the former IndyCar star for racing in F1, including a tough F2 campaign and an increasing number of FP1 outings to ensure the 26-year-old Californian would reach the required threshold of 40 FIA superlicence points.

    Bottas' fortunes were not helped by a series of performance, reliability and parts consistency issues with the rookie team, which has made the Finn's form harder to judge fairly.

    And with Cadillac still far removed from being a frontline F1 operation, Towriss and team principal Marcin Budkowski now appear to have agreed that Bottas' vast F1 experience - which currently spans 265 grand prix starts - is what will benefit the team the most in 2027.

    Cadillac said on Wednesday: "The announcement provides continuity and stability as Cadillac builds on its first season on the grid. It continues its long-term ambition to establish a competitive team at the pinnacle of global motorsport.”

    This now completes the 22-car grid next year with Cadillac yet to issue an update on what's next for Herta, saying "reserve and test drivers will be announced in due course”.

    Bottas, 37, who has 10 race wins, said: "To whom it may concern, I’m staying! I’m excited to continue this journey with Cadillac into 2027. We knew building a new Formula 1 team from the ground up wasn’t going to be easy, but that’s exactly what makes this challenge so special.

    "We’ve laid the foundations, we’re learning and improving, and there’s still plenty more to come, I’m hungry for more, and I believe in what we’re building together. The story is only just getting started."

    Towriss added: "Valtteri’s experience, perspective and commitment are important strengths for our team. Continuity gives us a strong platform as we continue developing the team and working toward our ambitions in Formula 1.

    “On the eve of the Las Vegas GP, everyone in the team is pleased to have Valtteri locked in for 2027 alongside Checo and look forward to the next stage of our journey together."
