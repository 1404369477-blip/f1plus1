    法拉利发布汉密尔顿与勒克莱尔接管社交媒体幕后视频

    法拉利发布了刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔在新加坡大奖赛周末接管车队社交媒体账号的幕后视频。两位车手在20分钟窗口内随意使用账号，场面十分混乱。

    法拉利发布汉密尔顿与勒克莱尔接管社交媒体账号的混乱视频

    法拉利分享了刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔在新加坡大奖赛周末接管车队社交媒体账号的幕后视频

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地区的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    法拉利发布了刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔接管车队社交媒体频道的幕后花絮。

    这两位车手在新加坡大奖赛周末期间接管了马拉内罗车队的账号，场面一片混乱。

    在拥有20分钟随意使用这些频道的窗口期内，汉密尔顿很快承认自己近期缺乏社交媒体练习，并解释道：“我很久没有发布自己的内容了，我其实甚至不知道怎么用这个应用。”

    勒克莱尔立即接手指挥，指示这位七届冠军摆姿势拍一张极度特写的自拍，用作新的头像。

    这次接管活动包括汉密尔顿简要参观法拉利接待区、勒克莱尔骑汉密尔顿的滑板车、两人分别打断会议、两位车手都进行直播，以及其他帖子。

    法拉利随后发布了一份半开玩笑的“非官方声明”。

    “法拉利惠普车队确认，在过去两个小时内，组织内部有两名个人未经授权控制了我们的社交媒体频道，”车队写道。

    “在此期间，做出了一系列非常可疑的编辑决定，包括更改我们的个人资料图片以及发布未经我们标准审批程序的内容。

    "我们可以确认，两名嫌疑人已被确认身份，目前局势已得到控制。我们账户的完全控制权已经恢复。"

    "我们感谢粉丝们在这起前所未有的事件中给予的理解。"

    截至撰写本文时，这段社交媒体营销噱头的幕后花絮视频已被观看超过4万次。"我们需要更多这样的内容，"一位粉丝在YouTube视频下评论道，另一位则补充说："请每个月都来一次。"

    "我爱你们俩在一起，我原以为在社交媒体和赛道上都很难取代查理/奇利的组合，但我错了，"另一个人发帖说。"

    Ferrari releases chaotic footage of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc's social media takeover

    Ferrari has shared behind-the-scenes footage of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc taking over the team's social media channels during the Singapore GP weekend

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari has released behind-the-scenes footage of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc hijacking the team's social media channels.

    The two drivers took over the Maranello outfit's accounts during the Singapore Grand Prix weekend, and it was pure chaos.

    With a 20-minute window to do whatever they wanted with the channels, Hamilton quickly admitted his lack of recent social media practice, explaining: "I haven't posted my own content for so long I actually don't even know how to use the app."

    Leclerc immediately took charge, directing the seven-time champion to pose for an extreme close-up selfie to be used as a new profile picture.

    The takeover featured Hamilton giving a brief tour of the Ferrari hospitality unit, Leclerc riding Hamilton's scooter, the pair separately interrupting meetings and both drivers going live, among other posts.

    Ferrari later issued a tongue-in-cheek "unofficial statement".

    "Scuderia Ferrari HP confirms that, over the past two hours, two individuals within the organisation gained unauthorised control of our social media channels," the team wrote.

    "During this period, a series of highly questionable editorial decisions were made, including changes to our profile picture and the publication of content that did not undergo our standard approval procedures.

    "We can confirm that the two suspects have been identified and the situation has now been contained. Full control of our accounts has been restored.

    "We thank our fans for their understanding during this unprecedented incident."

    At the time of writing, the behind-the-scenes footage of the social media stunt had already been viewed more than 40,000 times. "WE NEED MORE CONTENT LIKE THISS," one fan commented on the YouTube video, while another added: "We needed every month please."

    "I love you two together, I thought it was going to be hard to replace the Charlie/Chilli combo on both socials and the track but I was wrong," someone else posted.
