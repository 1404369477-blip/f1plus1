    四次一级方程式冠军维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛资格赛后，用两字回应了他与乔治·拉塞尔的最终练习赛碰撞：'没有发生。'

    这对车手在FP3的最后一圈在14号弯道发生了接触，当时拉塞尔在内线试图重新超越红牛车队。 stewards在发现维斯塔潘在刹车时的方向改变不可预测后，对他进行了警告。

    stewards认为3号车的刹车时方向改变不可预测，并导致了63号车为了避免碰撞而不得不内移的情况。官方FIA文件解释说。

    虽然 stewards认为这个动作并不严重到需要更严厉的处罚，但他们得出结论，在这种情况下刹车时的方向改变低于预期的驾驶标准。

    在拉斯维加斯大道的排位赛后，拉塞尔在接受天空体育采访时谈到了FP3的事故。他说：“我不会就这样接受有人这样对我，然后像他们比所有人都大或更好一样四处走动。有人必须站出来对抗那个人。”

    Max Verstappen dismisses George Russell Las Vegas clash with two-word response

    Verstappen and Russell will start first and second after Kimi Antonelli’s engine penalty

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen dismissed his final practice clash with George Russell in two words after qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix: "Nothing happened."

    The pair made contact at Turn 14 at the end of FP3, when Russell went down the inside to re-pass the Red Bull. The stewards gave Verstappen a warning for erratic driving after finding that his change of direction under braking was "unpredictable".

    "The Stewards considered that Car 3's change of direction under braking was unpredictable and contributed to the situation in which Car 63 had to move to the inside to avoid contact," the official FIA document explained.

    "While the Stewards did not consider the manoeuvre sufficiently serious to warrant a more severe penalty, they concluded that changing direction under braking in these circumstances fell below the standard of driving expected."

    While speaking to Sky Sports after qualifying on the Las Vegas Strip, Russell commented on the FP3 incident. "I'm not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they're bigger and better than everyone else," he said. "Somebody's got to stand up to that guy."

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    Russell qualified third for the Las Vegas Grand Prix behind his Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli and Verstappen, but an engine penalty for the Italian driver means Verstappen and Russell will start first and second.

    Verstappen was asked about the FP3 clash in the post-qualifying press conference following Russell's comments. "Nothing happened," he answered simply.

    Both Verstappen and Russell are currently fighting for second in the drivers' championship. With three rounds remaining in 2026, Verstappen is second with 279 points and Russell is third with 262 points. Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton is just one point behind Russell in fourth.

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix kicks off the season-ending triple-header, which includes the Qatar and Abu Dhabi Grands Prix.
