    基米·安东内利的对手们在墨西哥城大奖赛上向他夺得一级方程式世界冠军致敬。

    这位意大利车手以第三名完赛，落后于马克斯·维斯塔潘和刘易斯·汉密尔顿，在还剩四场比赛的情况下锁定总冠军。年仅20岁零68天的他成为这项运动历史上最年轻的冠军，也是自1953年阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来首位赢得车手总冠军的意大利人。

    以下是他的同行车手们在赛后所说的话。

    “当然，他这个赛季的表现令人难以置信。而且，你知道，在如此年纪做到这一点，我觉得真的非常、非常令人印象深刻。”

    "当然，看到整个团队和他一起庆祝真是太棒了。他的家人也在这里。所以我确信今天对他来说是非常激动的一天，我为他感到非常骄傲。"

    "向基米和梅赛德斯车队致以最热烈的祝贺。看到他们所有人在这里庆祝他们的成功真是太棒了。史上最年轻的世界冠军，这真是非凡的成就。我非常感激能在这场比赛中见证这一切。"

    "显然我为他感到非常高兴。我认为自己很年轻，但当我看着他时，他更年轻，看到他的父母在领奖台下方如此激动，这是一幅美丽的画面，所以我为他感到非常非常高兴。

    "我试图在方格旗后等他，但我想他开得很慢来庆祝，这是应该的，我稍后会去看他，但这完全是他应得的。我的意思是，从年初开始他没有犯过一步错误。"

    "我认为他所做的就是让所有人都大为震撼。我希望大家能充分认可他今年取得的成就，因为他让其他所有人都显得黯然失色。他开得那么好，而且能够与他的赛车完美契合。

    "他努力去理解如何驾驶这辆赛车。他只是比其他人更好地发挥了它的潜力，所以他配得上每一份成功。他度过了不可思议的一年。更令人印象深刻的是，这只是他在一级方程式的第二个赛季，而且他才20岁。

    "我很高兴看到我的一号车号转到他名下，因为他配得上，所以从某种意义上说，我很乐意看到它易主。

    "我认为，这是他的第二年，他有一位非常强大的队友，但他自始至终都击败了队友，整个赛季都是如此。数数他本赛季犯了多少错误。说实话，我觉得你一只手都数不过来。

    “他从队尾发车，也从前排赢过。他做到了需要做的一切，而且每一场比赛都在那里，这是本赛季其他任何车手都无法说自己做到的事情。你知道，他碾压了所有人。碾压了和他驾驶同一辆车的队友，这很难做到，所以这非常令人印象深刻。”

    “我不知道这到底算不算一场冠军争夺战。这只是一次冠军胜利。早些时候还稍微艰难一些。

    “他做得非常出色。我认为在F1的第二个赛季就能取得今年这样的成就，非常非常令人印象深刻。显然梅赛德斯在他周围做得很好，给了他能够交付成绩的赛车，支持他，而他也完成了任务，所以是非常令人印象深刻的赛季。”

    “没有比这更好的了。世界冠军，在F1上一个时代两位最优秀冠军之间产生，所以他非常年轻。他需要享受这一切。我认为他今年所拥有的，是我们所有人的梦想。

    速度方面，我们早就知道他有这个能力，但我认为他今年展现出的稳定性——一场接一场地登上领奖台、拿分并且不犯错——令人印象极其深刻。

    How F1 drivers reacted to Kimi Antonelli's world title: "He's made everyone else look silly"

    Lewis Hamilton, Max Verstappen, Lando Norris and more pay tribute to the Mercedes driver after he became the youngest champion in Formula 1 history

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Kimi Antonelli's rivals have paid tribute to the Mercedes driver after he clinched the Formula 1 world championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix.

    The Italian finished third behind Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton to secure the title with four races to spare. At just 20 years and 68 days old, he is the youngest champion in the sport's history, and the first Italian to win the drivers' title since Alberto Ascari in 1953.

    Here is what his fellow drivers had to say after the race.

    "It's incredible of course what a season he had. And, you know, to do that also at such an age, I think it's just really, really impressive.

    "It's amazing to see the whole team of course celebrating with him. His family is here as well. So I'm sure this is a very emotional day for him and I'm very proud of him."

    "A huge congratulations to Kimi and the Mercedes team. It's great to see them all up here celebrating their success. Youngest world champion ever, it's pretty phenomenal. And I'm so grateful to be in the race to witness it."

    "I'm obviously very happy for him. I consider myself young, but when I look at him he's even younger, and to see his parents underneath the podium being so emotional is a beautiful image, so I'm very, very happy for him.

    "I was trying to wait for him after the chequered flag, but I guess he was going very slow to celebrate as he should, and I'll go and see him later, but it's fully deserved. I mean, he didn't put a step wrong since the beginning of the year."

    "I think what he's done is he's blown everyone away. I hope everyone gives him enough credit for what he's achieved this year because he's made everyone else look silly. He's driven that well and he's been able to click with his car.

    "He's worked hard to understand how to drive this car. He just exploited it better than anyone else, so he deserves every single bit of success. He's had an unbelievable year. And it's even more impressive that it's only his second season in Formula 1 and he's 20 years old.

    "I'm very happy to see my number one go over to him because he deserves it, so in a way happy to see it go.

    "I think the fact it's his second year, he has a very strong team-mate, but he's beaten him the whole time, the whole season. Count how many mistakes he's made this season. I don't even think you could put it on one hand, honestly.

    "He's come from the back of the grid. He's won from the front. He's done everything he needs to do and he's been there every single race, and that's something that not one other driver can say that they've done this season. You know, he's smashed everyone. Smashed the guy in the same car as him, and that's difficult to do, so that's been very impressive."

    "I don't know if it's necessarily been a championship battle. It's just been a championship win. Earlier on it was a bit tougher.

    "He's done an incredible job. I think in his second season in F1 to accomplish what he's accomplished this year is very, very impressive. Clearly Mercedes have done a good job around him, giving him the car to deliver, supporting him, and he's done the business as well, so very impressive season."

    "Can't get any better than that. World champion between the two best champions in the last era of F1, so he's extremely young. He needs to enjoy. I think it's the dream of all of us what he had this year.

    "The speed, we all knew that he had it, but I think the consistency that he showed this year, race after race being on the podium and scoring the points and not making mistakes, it's been extremely impressive.

    "To have that weight on your shoulders of being P1 in the championship for so long and also from start to the end is, wow. So very well done to him. Very happy for him. He's a great champion, so he needs to enjoy."

    "Great, fast learner, amazing guy. I'm very happy for him. He's a great guy, amazing driver and no mistakes all year long, good car, good teamwork, solid performance.

    "Sealing the championship already with four races to go just shows how he's performed this year."

    "I think completely deserved and honestly he's done a really, really good year. He's known how to maximise his chance of becoming world champion in his second year in Formula 1. A lot of us would have wished to have such a competitive car in our first years in Formula 1.

    "The year that he was given it to him, he maximised everything to be perfect. First half of the year dominated and then controlled the second part of the year, which is what a world champion has to do, and I think he did it really, really well."

    "Very impressive. He's been very good all year. So full credit to him."
