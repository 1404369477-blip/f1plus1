    拉塞尔与泰勒吉他合作推出签名吉他，将在奥斯汀的现场音乐会上展示。下周的F1美国大奖赛前夕，他将作为主角参加一场现场音乐会。

    李安·劳森在奥斯汀现场表演前揭幕限量版吉他

    小红牛一级方程式车手李安·劳森与泰勒吉他合作推出了一款限量版签名吉他

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手的生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    李安·劳森在奥斯汀现场表演前，与泰勒吉他合作揭幕了一款签名吉他。

    小红牛车队的车手将在下周的美国大奖赛 Formula 1 前主持一场现场音乐活动。活动定于10月22日星期四在奥斯汀的 Inn Cahoots 举行，劳森 将与他的童年乐队成员们重聚，包括他的兄弟和两位亲密朋友，进行一场完整的现场表演。

    为了庆祝这一时刻，这位新西兰人已与 Taylor Guitars 合作推出了一款签名乐器：定制的 LL30 GS Mini 特别版。

    这款定制款原声吉他具有粉到白色的渐变效果，在他的护板上有他的赛车号码 30，吉他体内还装饰有他 F1 头盔上的卡通图形，通过音孔可见。

    “拥有我自己的 Taylor 吉他感觉太疯狂了，” 劳森 在 Instagram 上发布。‘我已经为此努力了很长时间，很高兴看到它变成现实。LL30 GS Mini 是我想要的吉他的一切，也展现了我赛场内外的一些个性。我喜欢它，希望你们也喜欢。”

    LL30 GS Mini特别版将于今年晚些时候在美国上市，据泰勒吉他网站确认，将仅限量生产。

    粉丝们迅速分享了他们对限量版吉他的兴奋之情。'看起来太棒了，粉色真是太适合你了！'一位评论者在他的Instagram帖子中写道，截至写作时，该帖子已有超过82,000个赞。

    '这把吉他太棒了，真是太适合你了！'另一个人写道，而另一位粉丝补充说：'迫不及待想看到你给它一个一、二、滑行三！'

    与劳森一起登台的是乡村歌手/词曲作者乔尼·卡佩奇、斯科特·特斯利，以及杰斯·法伦和新兴人才艾莉森·尼科尔斯和德雷克·米利根。

    美国大奖赛将于10月23日至25日在美国赛车场举行。

    Liam Lawson unveils limited-edition guitar ahead of Austin live performance

    Racing Bulls Formula 1 driver Liam Lawson has unveiled a limited-edition signature guitar in collaboration with Taylor Guitars

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Liam Lawson has unveiled a signature guitar in collaboration with Taylor Guitars ahead of his live performance in Austin.

    The Racing Bulls driver will headline a live music event ahead of the Formula 1 United States Grand Prix next week. Scheduled for Thursday 22 October at Inn Cahoots in Austin, the event will see Lawson reunite with his childhood bandmates, including his brother and two close friends, for a full live performance.

    To mark the occasion, the New Zealander has collaborated with Taylor Guitars to launch a signature instrument: the custom LL30 GS Mini Special Edition.

    The bespoke acoustic guitar features a pink-to-white ombré finish, with his racing number 30 on the pickguard alongside a cartoon graphic from his F1 helmet inside the body, visible through the soundhole.

    "My very own Taylor Guitar feels crazy to say," Lawson posted on Instagram. "Been working on this one for a very long time, so stoked to see it come to life. The LL30 GS Mini is everything I’d want in my own guitar and shows a bit of my personality on and off the track. I dig it, and I hope you do too."

    The LL30 GS Mini Special Edition will be available in the United States later this year, according to the Taylor Guitars website, which also confirms that only a limited number will be made.

    Fans were quick to share their excitement for the limited-edition guitar. "It looks so good and the pink is very you!!!" one commented on his Instagram post, which had reached over 82,000 likes at the time of writing.

    "This guitar is amazing, and it’s very you!" someone else wrote, while another fan added: "Can't wait to see you give it a one, two, skiddily do."

    Joining Lawson on the bill are country singer/songwriters Jonny Capeci, Scoot Teasley, alongside Jess Farren and up-and-coming talents Alison Nichols and Drake Milligan.

    The United States Grand Prix will take place at the Circuit of the Americas from 23-25 October.
