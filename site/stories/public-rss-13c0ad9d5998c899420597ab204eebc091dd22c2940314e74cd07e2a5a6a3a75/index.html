    阿隆索将代表阿斯顿·马丁出战2027年达喀尔拉力赛

    阿隆索将在2027年F1赛季开始前代表阿斯顿·马丁参加达喀尔拉力赛，这将是他继2020年代表丰田获得第13名后再次出战这项赛事。

    费尔南多·阿隆索将在2027年F1赛季开始前代表阿斯顿·马丁参加达喀尔拉力赛。

    阿隆索曾在2020年离开F1期间参加这项传奇的沙漠马拉松赛事，代表丰田获得第13名。

    他长期以来公开表示自己很想再次挑战达喀尔，但就在上个月他还淡化了即将回归的说法，称“很难安排，尤其是在同一年兼顾F1和达喀尔这两项挑战”。

    但他现在已决定，在同一赛季同时参加这两项赛事正是他想迎接的挑战，并表示这一决定在他确认2027年继续留在阿斯顿·马丁征战F1后变得明确。两年前与阿斯顿·马丁达成上一份合同时，他就曾把达喀尔列为F1之外的重要目标。

    阿隆索表示：“最终，这个决定变得很简单。”

    “我想完全拥抱这项挑战，在同一年同时征战一级方程式和达喀尔拉力赛。”

    “2020年，达喀尔是我职业生涯中最具挑战性也最令人愉快的经历之一。我对这项赛事以及精通它的专家们怀有极大的敬意。”

    由于中东持续的安全局势，明年的达喀尔拉力赛已从原计划的沙特阿拉伯路线改至葡萄牙和摩洛哥。

    比赛于1月9日至23日举行，比2月24日至27日的一级方程式季前测试早一个月。

    阿隆索的队友将是九届世界拉力锦标赛冠军、如今常驻达喀尔领奖台的塞巴斯蒂安·勒布，以及现任世界拉力突袭锦标赛冠军卢卡斯·莫赖斯。

    五届达喀尔摩托车组冠军马克·科马将担任阿隆索的领航员，正如他们在2020年的尝试中一样。

    阿斯顿·马丁的达喀尔项目将使用“无畏”品牌，该品牌迄今仅以数字形式存在，是为游戏《使命召唤：现代战争4》创作的军用概念车。

    它将由传奇的Prodrive团队运营，该团队今年与之前的合作伙伴Dacia一起赢得了达喀尔拉力赛。

    阿隆索在2010年代末期在F1之外的冒险包括三次印第安纳波利斯500大赛的尝试——结果从争夺胜利到未能获得参赛资格不等——两次勒芒24小时耐力赛胜利、一个世界耐力锦标赛冠军和一次戴通纳24小时耐力赛胜利。

    他最近表示，达喀尔是所有这些中最艰难的任务，因为它与F1所需的技能和风格如此不同。

    “对我来说，对一名F1车手来说，这是最困难的挑战，”阿隆索上个月说，“因为在驾驶技术和如何解读地形方面，以及用双脚驾驶赛车的方式——始终同时踩刹车和油门以稳定赛车——它与F1的差异不可能更大了。”

    “这与F1完全相反，所以这正是它吸引人的地方。”

    阿隆索首次尝试达喀尔的表现如何

    阿隆索在2020年首次尝试达喀尔时整体上颇具竞争力。

    他受益于一套强大的装备——由Gazoo车队准备的丰田Hilux，该车曾与纳赛尔·阿提亚和吉尼尔·德·维利尔斯一起长期位居前列，达喀尔摩托车传奇科马则担任领航员——最终他以第13名完赛，落后冠军4小时43分钟。

    其中仅一小时多一点的损失可以追溯到两个赛段，他在那里因撞上岩石受损和翻车而被迫停车维修。

    除此之外，他始终稳定地处于前列附近，在一半的赛段中跻身前七——其中亮点是在第八赛段借助赛道位置优势获得第二名。

    阿提亚后来表示，阿隆索的即时速度非常突出，但他可能需要积累更多达喀尔经验，才能考虑尝试夺冠。

    “他非常非常有天赋，”阿提亚在2022年说道。

    “而他在达喀尔首次做到的是——他展现出了速度。对于这类比赛，你真的需要经验。

    “因为，如果你看看达喀尔的历史，你需要有大约四、五、六年的经验，然后才能获胜。”

    英文原文

    Fernando Alonso will contest the Dakar Rally with Aston Martin ahead of the 2027 Formula 1 season starting.

    Alonso competed on the legendary desert marathon in 2020 while on his break from F1, finishing 13th with Toyota.

    He has long openly admitted that he would love to tackle the Dakar again, though as recently as last month he played down talk of an imminent return, saying it would be "difficult to fit, especially in the same year, both challenges - Formula 1 and Dakar".

    But he has now decided that competing in both in the same season is exactly the challenge he wants to take on, saying that was a decision that crystallised once he'd committed to staying in F1 with Aston Martin for 2027. He had highlighted Dakar as a key non-F1 goal when agreeing his previous deal with Aston Martin two years ago.

    "In the end, the decision became simple," said Alonso.

    "I wanted to embrace the challenge completely and take on both Formula 1 and the Dakar Rally in the same year.

    "In 2020, Dakar was one of the most challenging and enjoyable experiences of my career. I have enormous respect for this race and the specialists who have mastered it."

    Next year's Dakar has moved from its planned Saudi Arabian route to Portugal and Morocco due to the ongoing security situation in the Middle East.

    It takes place from January 9-23, a month before F1 pre-season testing on February 24-27.

    Alonso's team-mates will be nine-time World Rally champion turned regular Dakar podium finisher Sebastien Loeb and reigning World Rally Raid champion Lucas Moraes.

    Marc Coma, a five-time Dakar winner in the motorcycle class, will be Alonso's co-driver just as he was for their 2020 attempt.

    Aston Martin's Dakar project will use the Dreadnought brand that has so far only existed in digital form, created as a military concept vehicle for the game Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4.

    It will be run by the legendary Prodrive operation that won this year's Dakar with its previous partner Dacia.

    Alonso's adventures outside F1 in the late-2010s included three Indianapolis 500 attempts - in which the outcomes ranged from fighting for victory to failing to quality - two Le Mans 24 Hours victories, a World Endurance Championship title and a Daytona 24 Hours win.

    He recently said Dakar had been the hardest mission of them all because it is so different to the skillset and style required for F1.

    "That's the most difficult challenge for me, for a Formula 1 driver," Alonso said last month, "because it cannot be more different than F1 in terms of driving techniques and how to read the terrain, and the way you drive the car also with both feet - on brake and throttle all the time to stabilise the car.

    "It's completely the opposite of F1, so that's why it's appealing."

    How Alonso fared in his first Dakar attempt

    Alonso was broadly competitive when trying out the Dakar for the first time in 2020.

    He benefitted from a strong package - a Toyota Hilux prepared by the Gazoo team that had been a perennial frontrunner with Nasser Al-Attiyah and Giniel de Villiers, with Dakar bikes legend Coma stepping in as co-driver - and ultimately reached the finish in 13th, 4h43m down on the winner.

    Just over an hour of that time loss could be tracked to a pair of stages in which he was halted by repairs for a damaging rock impact and a roll.

    He was otherwise reliably there or thereabouts, a top-seven finisher on half the stages - with the highlight a track position-aided second place on stage eight.

    Al-Attiyah later said that Alonso's immediate speed had stood out, but that he might need a lot more Dakar experience before he could think about trying to win it.

    "He's very, very talented," said Al-Attiyah in 2022.

    "And what he did for the first time in the Dakar - he showed the speed. For this kind of race you need to really have experience.

    "Because, if you see the history of Dakar, you need to have like four, five, six years' experience, and then you win."
