    Mercedes took what Wolff admitted was the "unusual" step of running two different car specifications at the last race after troubles with the big car upgrade introduced onto both W17s at the previous round in Malaysia, the world championship leader's first major upgrade since May.

    Kimi Antonelli ran the newer spec in Singapore, while George Russell reverted to the older package on a weekend he already knew he would start at the back of the Grand Prix grid owing to engine penalties.
