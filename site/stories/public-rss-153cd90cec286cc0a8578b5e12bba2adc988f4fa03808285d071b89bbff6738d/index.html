    安东内利分享赢得F1冠军后的难忘塞纳时刻

    2026年F1冠军安东内利在赢得首冠后两天内，便坐在了他英雄的车中。上周在墨西哥大奖赛夺冠的梅赛德斯车手，在巴西大奖赛前夕访问了塞纳家族的家。

    基米·安东内利在赢得一级方程式冠军后分享了“难忘”的阿yrton Senna时刻

    新晋一级方程式冠军基米·安东内利访问了阿yrton Senna在巴西的家庭庄园

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    2026年一级方程式冠军基米·安东内利从赢得他的首个冠军到坐在他英雄的赛车中，仅用了两天时间。

    梅赛德斯车手，上周末在墨西哥大奖赛中夺得冠军，周二在巴西大奖赛之前参观了塞纳家族的家。他分享了自己在传奇的黑白Lotus 98T赛车驾驶舱中的照片，这位三届冠军在1986年驾驶这辆车。

    “难忘的一天。感谢整个塞纳家族。这些记忆需要一段时间才能沉淀，但我将永远带着它们。”安东内利在Instagram上发文。

    这位意大利人在他的父亲马可、塞纳的侄子布鲁诺和侄女们布鲁娜和拉拉利陪同下参观了位于塔图伊的Fazenda Dois Lagos庄园。这个地产有自己的卡丁车赛道，建于1991年，安东内利似乎充分利用了它。前F1车手布鲁诺·塞纳在安东内利的帖子下评论道：“感谢你的卡丁车课程，冠军！”

    安东内利并不是第一个访问这个庄园的F1冠军，七届冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿也在2022年因特拉戈斯站的比赛之前访问过这个地产。

    安东内利之前曾谈到他对塞纳的钦佩。在2025年巴西大奖赛之前，他被发现在莫伦比公墓巴西人的墓前坐着。

    “那是一次真的很酷的经历，”安东内利当时对媒体解释道。‘阿yrton是我的赛车英雄。昨天我有很多空闲时间，所以我想，为什么不呢？那离我的酒店很近。’

    “所以我决定去一次。我决定早上去，但后来我也下午去了，因为我觉得那是一个很安静的地方，非常平和。所以这次下午，我决定和我的教练一起去那里，我们读了一本书。我们在一个安静的地方读书，只是在那里度过了一些平和的时光，真的很棒。但是的，我真的很想去看他。”

    Kimi Antonelli shares "unforgettable" Ayrton Senna moment after F1 title win

    Newly crowned Formula 1 champion Kimi Antonelli visited Ayrton Senna's family estate in Brazil

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    2026 Formula 1 champion Kimi Antonelli went from winning his maiden title to sitting in his hero's car in the space of two days.

    The Mercedes driver, who clinched the title at the Mexican Grand Prix last weekend, visited Ayrton Senna's family home on Tuesday ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix. He shared a photo of himself in the cockpit of the iconic black and gold Lotus 98T that the three-time champion raced in 1986.

    "Unforgettable day. Obrigado to the whole Senna family. It will take some time to sink in, but I will carry these memories with me forever," Antonelli posted on Instagram.

    The Italian was joined by his father, Marco, and Senna's nephew and nieces, Bruno, Bianca and Lalalli Senna, when he visited the Fazenda Dois Lagos estate in Tatuí. The property has its own kart track, opened in 1991, and Antonelli seems to have made the most of it. Bruno Senna, a former F1 driver himself, commented on Antonelli's post: "Thank you for the karting lesson, Champ!"

    Antonelli is not the first F1 champion to make the trip to the estate, with seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton also visiting the property before the race at Interlagos in 2022.

    Antonelli has previously spoken about his admiration for Senna. He was spotted sitting by the Brazilian's grave in the Morumbi Cemetery ahead of the 2025 Brazilian Grand Prix.

    "It was a really cool experience," Antonelli explained to the media at the time. "Ayrton is my racing hero. And yesterday I had a lot of time on hand, so I said, you know, why not? It was very close to my hotel.

    "So I decided to go once. I decided to go in the morning, but then I also went in the afternoon because I found it a really quiet place, very peaceful. So in the afternoon, I decided to go there this time with my coach, and we read a book. We were reading a book and just spent some time in peace in a quiet place and it was really nice. But yeah, I really wanted to go and say hi to him."
