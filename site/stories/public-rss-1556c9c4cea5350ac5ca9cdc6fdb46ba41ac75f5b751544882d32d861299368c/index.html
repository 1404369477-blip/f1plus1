    汉密尔顿确认与勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯碰撞后进行积极对话

    七冠王汉密尔顿表示，自拉斯维加斯大奖赛以来，法拉利的对话一直很积极，并相信他和队友勒克莱尔可以在最近的碰撞后一起前进。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿确认在拉斯维加斯碰撞后与查尔斯·勒克莱尔进行了“积极”的谈话

    法拉利车手表示，在拉斯维加斯的碰撞之后，他和查尔斯·勒克莱尔坐下来进行了交谈

    七届一级方程式冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿表示，自拉斯维加斯大奖赛以来，法拉利的谈话一直“积极”，并且相信他和他的队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔可以在最近的碰撞之后一起前进。

    勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯将汉密尔顿旋转了一圈，而在墨西哥大奖赛三周后的另一场碰撞中，队友们争夺第六名。勒克莱尔被处以10秒罚时，最终排名第10，而汉密尔顿恢复到第七名。

    车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔将这次事件称为"对团队的缺乏尊重"，并表示墨西哥之后法拉利的消息已经非常明确。

    在卡塔尔接受媒体采访时，汉密尔顿没有详细说明之后说了什么，但表示讨论进行得很顺利。

    "一切都在内部。但讨论得很好，"他说。"积极的，是的。一直都很积极。查尔斯和我之前也坐在这里进行了很好的讨论。"

    问及这对搭档是否现在可以和谐地前进，汉密尔顿表示明确。

    "是的，是的。我们会找到解决办法的。你知道，毕竟我们是队友，我们总是从这些经历中学习，虽然可能看起来不是这样，"他说。

    "但我们会从这个困难时期学到一些东西，我仍然相信我们在幕后建立和努力追求的一切。

    汉密尔顿表示他和勒克莱尔已经一起经历了每一场比赛的每一个"弯道"，但他承认没有计划可以覆盖赛道上的每一个情况。

    “你永远无法覆盖一切，因为每个场景都是不同的，”他说。

    “总会有一些新情况是你可能没有预料到的。但你也必须运用你的直觉，并且始终要有一个团队在核心。”

    他认为，艰难的时刻会让法拉利从长远来看变得更强大。

    “这对弗雷德、查尔斯和我来说无疑是极具挑战性的时期。但这些正是你真正学习和更紧密团结在一起的时刻，通过交谈和沟通，以及那些坐下来讨论的时刻，”汉密尔顿说。

    这位英国人也期待着在冬季与勒克莱尔保持联系，那时两人都"没有比赛"，勒克莱尔也正在适应作为新父亲的生活。

    "我对明年我们能够共同努力并取得更好的成绩没有疑问。我们希望能够建立更好的流程，以便真正提升车队," 他说。

    法拉利在最后两场比赛中，以车队锦标赛第二的位置进入，领先红牛77分。

    Lewis Hamilton confirms "positive" talks with Charles Leclerc after Las Vegas clash

    The Ferrari driver says he and Charles Leclerc have sat down together following their collision in Las Vegas

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton says Ferrari's talks since the Las Vegas Grand Prix have been "positive", and believes he and his team-mate Charles Leclerc can move forward together after their latest clash.

    Leclerc spun Hamilton around while the team-mates fought over sixth place in Las Vegas, three weeks after another clash at the Mexican Grand Prix. Leclerc was given a 10-second penalty and finished 10th, while Hamilton recovered to seventh.

    Team principal Fred Vasseur called the incident "a lack of respect for the team", and said Ferrari's message after Mexico had been clear.

    Speaking to the media in Qatar, Hamilton would not go into detail about what had been said since, but made it clear the discussions had gone well.

    "Everything stays internal. But good discussions," he said. "Positive, yeah. It's been positive. Charles and I sat here earlier on and had a good discussion as well."

    Asked whether the pair could now move forward in harmony, Hamilton was clear.

    "I do. I do. We're going to figure it out. You know, we're team-mates at the end of the day, and we do always learn from these experiences, while maybe it doesn't necessarily look like it," he said.

    "But there will be learnings that we take from this difficult period, and I still believe in everything we're building and working towards in the background."

    Hamilton said he and Leclerc had already been through every race "corner by corner", but admitted no plan could cover every situation on track.

    "You can never cover everything, because every scenario is different," he said.

    "There's always going to be something new that you maybe haven't foreseen. But also you have to use your instinct, and at the core of it, always have to have the team."

    He believes the difficult moments will make Ferrari stronger in the long run.

    "These are definitely very, very challenging times for Fred and Charles and I. But these are the moments that you really learn and grow closer together, through conversation and communicating and having those sit-downs," Hamilton said.

    The Briton also expects to stay in touch with Leclerc over the winter, when the pair are "out of competition" and Leclerc is settling into life as a new father.

    "I have no doubts that next year we'll come back working hard together and in a better place. We'll hopefully put better processes in place so that we can really elevate the team," he said.

    Ferrari goes into the final two races second in the constructors' championship, 77 points ahead of Red Bull.
