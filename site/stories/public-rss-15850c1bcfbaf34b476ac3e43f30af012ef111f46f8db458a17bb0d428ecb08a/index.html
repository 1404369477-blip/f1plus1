    诺里斯在搞笑的UNO对决中败给F1对手

    F1车手皮埃尔·加斯利、兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在新加坡进行了一场巨型UNO游戏对决。

    兰多·诺里斯在搞笑的UNO对决中败给F1对手

    兰多·诺里斯、奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里和皮埃尔·加斯利在新加坡进行了一场巨型UNO游戏对决。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里、麦克拉伦、兰多·诺里斯、麦克拉伦。

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    一级方程式车手皮埃尔·加斯利、兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在一段视频中展开了一场超级大型的UNO游戏，该视频在不到一天的时间内迅速获得了超过32万的观看次数。

    一级方程式在YouTube上发布了这段视频，描述了“麦克拉伦组合”与皮埃尔·加斯利一起在维修区进行了一场超级大型的UNO游戏。

    牌的大小大约和标牌一样，游戏开始时要求车手们洗牌。

    “哦我的天，它们实际上也很重，”皮亚斯特里在队友观看时说道。‘我不希望伤到自己，’诺里斯开玩笑地说，同时他看着澳大利亚人试图将牌分成几堆来洗牌。规则是标准的UNO，只是牌更大，抽到最大数字的人先手。轮到皮埃尔了。

    很快就开始了垃圾话。“别看我的牌了，伙计，”加斯利笑着，车手们在整个游戏过程中互相盯着对方的大手。

    加斯利在得到诺里斯的+4之后，首先用完了他的牌，而那些像木瓜一样颜色的车手们开始联合起来对抗他们的阿尔卑斯竞争对手。他很快就结束了游戏，留下两位麦凯轮车手去争夺胜利。

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂，麦凯轮，兰多·诺里斯，麦凯轮

    “好吧，现在是我对奥斯卡了，”这位现任冠军说。“我们都输了，所以我并不在乎，”皮亚斯蒂补充道。

    诺里斯拿着一大堆牌，挣扎着玩，甚至不小心用一张牌打到了自己的脸上。‘他手里有一半的牌，却还是不会玩，’加斯利在皮亚斯特里被迫拿走几张牌后开玩笑说，随着麦凯轮车手再次被迫拿走更多牌，他最终开玩笑地将自己的牌摔到了地上。

    然而，游戏很快发生了变化。皮亚斯特里与加斯利合作，成功减少了手中的牌，最终游戏结束时，诺里斯手里拿着牌。

    “这是我一生中第一次输的UNO游戏，”他说。

    比赛结束后，问车手们对游戏的看法。‘我认为在某些时候，由于牌太多，这是一项两个人的工作，’皮亚斯特里说。‘你需要扔几个……’

    “没有太多争议。通常在我家里，圣诞节过得并不太好，”加斯利承认。

    本周末的美国大奖赛，诺里斯和皮亚斯特里占据了上风。诺里斯在车手积分榜上以201分排名第六，皮亚斯特里以138分排名第七，而加斯利以41分排名第十，代表阿尔派车队。

    Lando Norris suffers defeat to F1 rivals in hilarious UNO showdown

    Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri and Pierre Gasly took on a giant game of UNO in Singapore

    Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Formula 1 drivers Pierre Gasly, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri went head to head at a super-sized game of UNO in a video that has quickly racked up more than 320,000 views in less than a day.

    Formula 1 posted the clip on YouTube, describing it as “the McLaren duo” being “joined by Pierre Gasly for a game of super-sized UNO in the paddock”.

    The cards were roughly the size of a sign board, and the game started with the drivers being asked to shuffle the deck.

    “Oh my god, they’re actually heavy as well," Piastri said as his team-mate watched on. "I don’t want to injure myself,” Norris joked as he laughed at the Australian's attempt at separating the cards into piles to shuffle. The rules were standard UNO, just with “extra big cards”, and whoever drew the highest number went first. Enter Pierre.

    It did not take long for the trash talk to start. “Stop looking at my cards, man,” Gasly laughed, with the drivers peering at each other’s oversized hands throughout the game.

    Gasly got down to his last card first after he got Norris with a +4 and the papaya-coloured drivers were starting to team up against their Alpine competitor. He finished it off soon after, leaving the two McLaren drivers to fight it out.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    “OK, so it’s me against Oscar now,” the reigning champion said. “We’ve both lost, so I don’t really care," Piastri added.

    Norris, holding a huge pile of cards, struggled to play, with him even managing to hit himself in the face with one. “He’s got half the deck in his hand and still can’t play,” Gasly joked after Piastri was forced to pick up several cards, with the McLaren driver eventually jokingly slamming his cards to the floor as he was once again forced to pick up more.

    The game quickly changed, however. Piastri, teaming up with Gasly, was able to whittle his hand down and eventually Norris was the one holding cards as the game ended.

    “That’s the first ever UNO game I’ve lost in my life,” he said.

    The drivers were asked what they thought following the game. “I think it’s a two-person job at some points with so many cards,” Piastri said. “You need to Frisbee a few..."

    “Not much argument. Usually with my family it goes down pretty badly over Christmas,” Gasly admitted.

    This weekend at the United States Grand Prix, it is Norris and Piastri who hold the upper hand. Norris is sixth in the championship on 201 points and Piastri seventh on 138, while Gasly is 10th on 41 for Alpine.
