    沃尔夫回应安东内利夺冠后薪资问题

    托托·沃尔夫回应了有关2026年世界冠军基米·安东内利在梅赛德斯仅拿400万欧元基础年薪的报道。

    托托·沃尔夫回应安东内利夺冠后薪资问题

    托托·沃尔夫回应了有关2026年世界冠军基米·安东内利在梅赛德斯仅拿400万欧元基础年薪的报道

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的比赛分析。

    当一支F1车队把一名青少年车手从F2直接提拔到顶级厂商车队席位时，最初的合同很少会按照超级明星的大合同来设计。但当这名年轻人在第二个赛季就赢得车手世界冠军时，最初的财务条款很快就变得严重过时。

    这正是目前梅赛德斯车队与20岁车手安东内利之间正在上演的情形。

    在最近接受德国媒体《图片报》独家专访时，车队领队托托·沃尔夫被追问了这位年轻冠军的财务现实。

    该媒体向沃尔夫出示了一份报道，称安东内利目前的基本年薪约为400万欧元（约合460万美元）。虽然围场内的估算各不相同——一些消息来源如F1Salaries将他的基本薪资定在接近340万美元——但共识依然一致：安东内利的基本薪资被认为严格属于顶级F1车手的“平均水平”，完全被他队友乔治·拉塞尔超过3400万美元的巨额底薪所碾压。

    当被直接问及安东内利是否会获得一份反映其夺冠地位的新合同时，沃尔夫给出了一个多层次的回应。

    “基米明年有合同在身，”沃尔夫直截了当地确认道，从而打消了任何关于他立即成为自由身的猜测。

    安东内利即将迎来一笔巨额收入？

    尽管沃尔夫迅速指出，现有法律框架将安东内利与布拉克利车队绑定在一起，但他并未回避一个基本前提：这位年轻的意大利车手目前的表现超出了他的薪酬水平。

    “我不会对具体数字发表评论，但我可以这么说：经济上没人需要为他担心，”沃尔夫表示。“基米和他的家人尤其关心的是他能坐进一辆有竞争力的赛车。与此同时，我们知道我们有一位世界冠军，他也应该得到相应的回报。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    虽然基本工资只是F1车手薪酬的一个组成部分——据报道，安东内利因分站胜利和最终的总冠军胜利触发了巨额、数百万美元的绩效奖金——但沃尔夫的言论是一个强烈的意图信号。

    通过公开承认世界冠军理应得到相应的回报，沃尔夫可能是在确认梅赛德斯正准备在财务上锁定其招牌明星，并将他的基本薪酬提升到与他刚刚在赛道上赢得的精英地位相匹配的水平。

    Toto Wolff responds to Kimi Antonelli salary questions after F1 title success

    Toto Wolff addresses reports that 2026 World Champion Kimi Antonelli earns a base salary of just €4 million at Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    When a Formula 1 team elevates a teenager from Formula 2 directly into a top-tier factory seat, the initial contract is rarely structured like a blockbuster superstar deal. But when that kid wins the drivers' world championship in his sophomore season, the original financial terms quickly become drastically outdated.

    That is exactly the scenario currently playing out at Mercedes with 20-year-old Kimi Antonelli.

    In a recent exclusive interview with the German publication BILD, team principal Toto Wolff was pressed on the financial reality of his young champion.

    The publication confronted Wolff with the report that Antonelli is currently operating on a base salary of around €4million (roughly $4.6million USD). While estimates across the paddock vary - with some sources such as F1Salaries placing his base closer to $3.4million USD - the consensus remains the same: Antonelli's base pay is considered strictly "average" for a top-field F1 driver, completely dwarfed by the massive $34million-plus retainer of his team-mate, George Russell.

    When asked directly if Antonelli would be rewarded with a new contract to reflect his title-winning status, Wolff offered a layered response.

    "Kimi has a contract for next year," Wolff bluntly confirmed, shutting down any speculation of immediate free agency.

    A big payday coming for Antonelli?

    While Wolff was quick to point out the existing legal framework tying Antonelli to Brackley, he did not shy away from the underlying premise that the young Italian is currently outperforming his paycheque.

    "I won't comment on the figures, but I can say this much: no one needs to worry about him financially," Wolff stated. "Kimi and his family especially care about him sitting in a competitive car. At the same time, we know that we have a world champion who should also be rewarded."

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    While base salary is only one component of an F1 driver's compensation - with Antonelli reportedly triggering massive, multi-million-dollar performance bonuses for race wins and the ultimate championship victory - Wolff's comments are a massive signal of intent.

    By publicly acknowledging that a world champion deserves to be rewarded accordingly, Wolff might be confirming that Mercedes is preparing to financially secure its franchise star and elevate his baseline compensation to match the elite status he just earned on the track.
