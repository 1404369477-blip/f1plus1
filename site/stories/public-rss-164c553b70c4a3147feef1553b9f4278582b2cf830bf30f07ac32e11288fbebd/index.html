    在马德里赛道对他而言令人失望的周末，这一差距扩大到赛季最高的81分，这个周末因排位赛Q3的困难以及正赛中早早出现的虚拟安全车而受到影响。这一结果导致拉塞尔在周日晚上几乎将自己排除在争夺之外。

    在评估自己对即将到来的比赛（从9月24日至26日的阿塞拜疆大奖赛开始）的策略时，拉塞尔告诉F1：“继续推进。尽管今天的成绩远非理想，但最近几场比赛更为积极。

    “但这差不多就是本赛季的写照。我只需要专注于表现，继续努力帮助车队。

    “我们显然处于赢得车队冠军的有利位置，实际上我已不在车手冠军的争夺中，这让我能够只专注于一场一场地比赛，努力专注于取得一些好成绩。”

    George Russell: Mercedes driver's losses to Kimi Antonelli will feel 'painful' says Nico Rosberg as driver backed for breakthrough

    George Russell fell 81 points behind Kimi Antonelli at the Madring; Russell conceded that "realistically I'm not in the fight for the drivers'" after the race; watch every session of the Azerbaijan Grand Prix from September 24-26 with a Saturday race, live on Sky Sports F1

    George Russell on how the Virtual Safety Car compromised his race at the Spanish Grand Prix

    Nico Rosberg believes George Russell will currently be driven by a similar level of "insane motivation" to turn the tables on Kimi Antonelli as he was to get the better of Lewis Hamilton after recurring defeats.

    Russell's hopes of becoming world champion for the first time this year are increasingly slim with in-form Mercedes team-mate Antonelli opening an 81-point lead over him at the top of the standings.

    Since winning March's season-opening Australian Grand Prix, pre-season title favourite Russell has only twice finished ahead of Antonelli in the last 10 races they have both completed with the Briton seeing the young Italian take his seventh and eighth wins of the season across the Monza-Madrid double header.

    Rosberg was asked after Sunday's race, in which Russell finished fifth, on Sky Sports F1 about his experience of rebounding to win his world title in 2016 after being soundly beaten to the crown by Mercedes team-mate Hamilton the year before.

    "I was driven a lot by the pain," recalled Rosberg.

    "Subjectively, it was painful to keep losing against Lewis and that just drove an insane motivation.

    "George will be in a similar situation. He will be so damn motivated at the moment to try and find improvements.

    "He'll be working so hard because I know George doesn't give up and he's hating the situation of getting beaten over and over by Kimi.

    "He'll be working super hard but it's a bit of a difficult one because it's hard to understand where the pace has gone for him."

    Highlights from the 2026 Spanish Grand Prix

    Russell has been Mercedes' top-scoring driver in three of his previous four seasons at the team, twice in three years against Hamilton (2022 and 2024) and once alongside a rookie Antonelli (2025), but he requires a major turnaround for that to be the case again this year with nine scheduled races left.

    Rosberg believes the diligent Russell will keep working away to find a breakthrough with the W17 car, although says it is inevitable that the 28-year-old will be questioning himself too.

    "I'm very impressed with George's approach because he's really a perfectionist and he tries to cover every aspect of his human performance," added the Sky Sports F1 pundit.

    "I know he's doing everything he can, and I admire that.

    "But, all the more, it's tough for him because he doesn't understand himself. He's always been fast; he's always been at least level with his team-mate and suddenly it's gone away from him there.

    "It's really painful because you start to doubt yourself. It doesn't matter how confident you are, it then comes [to questioning] 'am I not good enough? Was I better in the past and I'm just doing something wrong now?' It's really difficult."

    'Realistically I'm not in the fight' - Russell all-but rules out drivers' title win

    Fresh from being beaten to victory by Antonelli the weekend before at Monza despite his team-mate starting a penalised 19th on the grid to fall 66 points off the pace, Russell conceded going into the Spanish Grand Prix weekend that he would "need some luck" to realise his title dreams this year.

    That deficit grew to a season-high 81 points in a disappointing weekend for him at the Madring, which was compromised by a difficult Q3 in qualifying and then an early Virtual Safety Car in the race. That result led to Russell all but ruling himself out of contention on Sunday evening.

    Assessing his approach for the forthcoming races, starting with the Azerbaijan Grand Prix on September 24-26, Russell told F1: "Just keep pushing along. It has been a more positive couple of races even though the result was far from great today.

    "But it's kind of story of the season. I just need to focus on the performance, keep trying to help the team.

    "We're obviously in a great place to win the constructors', realistically I'm not in the fight for the drivers', and that just allows me just to focus on race-by-race and trying to focus on getting some good results."

    'We just have to unlock it' - Wolff backing 'top driver' Russell to find breakthrough

    His Mercedes team remain convinced Russell will rediscover his best form.

    Asked what Russell was fighting hardest at the moment, Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff said: "I don't know, because you can see the pace he had towards the end when he was catching Leclerc. He was fastest out on track.

    "But after the tyre changes, the pace wasn't there.

    "I just don't know where it is and we have to analyse it, because clearly it's in him, it's in the car, and we just have to unlock it."
