    诺里斯认为乔治·罗素是F1界下一位詹姆斯·邦德

    诺里斯认为乔治·罗素是最适合扮演F1界詹姆斯·邦德的赛车手，并表示这位梅赛德斯车手甚至愿意为了这个角色放弃赛车。

    兰多·诺里斯将乔治·罗素称为F1的下一个詹姆斯·邦德

    这位麦凯轮车手认为他的梅赛德斯对手"会为了扮演007角色而停止F1比赛",而瓦尔特里·博塔斯选择了费尔南多·阿隆索。

    乔治·罗素、梅赛德斯、兰多·诺里斯、麦凯轮

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    兰多·诺里斯认为乔治·罗素是适合扮演詹姆斯·邦德的F1车手，并表示这位梅赛德斯车手甚至愿意为了这个角色放弃赛车运动。

    这个问题是在亚马逊MGM工作室确认罗伯特·帕丁森将接替丹尼尔·克雷格出演传奇英国间谍，并完成五部电影之后提出的。

    在拉斯维加斯的驾驶员新闻发布会上，诺里斯被问及哪位F1车手未来能胜任这个角色，他回答道："乔治。我的意思是，他通常穿着西装之类的。我认为乔治会为了成为詹姆斯·邦德而放弃F1。所以我会说乔治是你的最佳选择，"他笑着说。

    我不知道还有谁会想这么做。就像我不知道谁我认为最适合这个角色，但他可能也想这么做。所以我把赌注压在乔治身上。

    诺里斯将自己排除在主演之外，但并未排除客串的可能。

    "我？不，我会在电影中扮演另一个角色，"他开玩笑说。"我不知道会是什么角色，但我会出现在其他地方。我不会说我是一个反派，但是的，我会在其他地方扮演另一个角色。

    坐在诺里斯旁边的凯迪拉克车手瓦尔特里·博塔斯认为这将是他的经验丰富的对手，阿斯顿·马丁的车队，这个品牌现在与这个系列同名。

    "我从没想过这个问题。所以这是一个好问题。第一个出现在我脑海的是费尔南多，你知道，他穿着西装走进来，他很有魅力。所以我会说费尔南多，"博塔斯说，然后补充道："我可以看到乔治。"

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    梅赛德斯车手因其强大的装备和手表搭配而闻名，这得益于梅赛德斯赞助商瑞士IWC手表，并且最近他推出了自己的卡丁车学院，GR63。

    然而，这位英国人在本周末还有一些更紧迫的事情要考虑。拉塞尔在车手锦标赛中排名第三，落后于马克斯·维斯塔潘17分，梅赛德斯表示正在考虑给他"全部的力量"以帮助他排在队友基米·安东内利之后。

    F1车手之前也曾进入好莱坞的世界。刘易斯·汉密尔顿是苹果公司F1电影的制片人，这部电影由布拉德·皮特主演，于2025年上映，并且几位车手在其中客串出演。

    邦德与赛车运动有着悠久的联系。这个角色的阿斯顿·马丁DB5是电影史上最著名的汽车之一，现在这个品牌正在参加一级方程式赛车，甚至之前曾提供其Vantage和DBX车型用于安全车和医疗车角色。

    诺里斯和拉塞尔将回归赛道，参加周六晚上的拉斯维加斯大奖赛。

    Lando Norris names George Russell as F1's next James Bond

    The McLaren driver reckons his Mercedes rival "would stop Formula 1" to take on the role of 007, while Valtteri Bottas chose Fernando Alonso.

    George Russell, Mercedes, Lando Norris, McLaren

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Lando Norris thinks George Russell would be the Formula 1 driver best suited to play James Bond, and says the Mercedes driver would even give up racing for the role.

    The question came days after Amazon MGM Studios confirmed Robert Pattinson as the next actor to play the legendary British spy following a five-movie run from Daniel Craig.

    Asked in the drivers' press conference in Las Vegas which F1 driver could take on the role in future, Norris answered: "George. I mean, he normally dresses in suits and stuff. I reckon George would stop Formula 1 to become James Bond. So I would say George would be your best bet," he laughed.

    "I don't know who else would want to do it. Like, I don't know who I think he would suit it best, but he would also probably want to do it. So my money's on George."

    Norris ruled himself out of the lead role, but failed to rule out a cameo.

    "Me? No, I'll play another part in the movie," he joked. "I don't know what it would be, but I'll be somewhere else. I wouldn't say I'm a villain either, but yeah, I'll play another role somewhere else."

    Cadillac driver Valtteri Bottas, who sat alongside Norris, believed it would instead be his experienced rival at Aston Martin - the car brand now synonymous with the franchise.

    "I never thought of this. So it's a good question. First one came to my mind is Fernando, you know, some of the walk-ins he's walked in with a suit and he's got the charisma for it. So I'm going to say Fernando," Bottas said, before adding: "I can see George."

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    The Mercedes driver is known for his strong outfit and watch combinations thanks to Mercedes sponsor IWC Schaffhausen, and recently launched his own karting academy, GR63.

    The Briton has more pressing matters on his mind this weekend, however. Russell is third in the drivers' championship, 17 points behind Max Verstappen, and Mercedes has said it is considering giving him "all the force" to help him finish second behind team-mate Kimi Antonelli.

    F1 drivers have previously stepped into the world of Hollywood. Lewis Hamilton was a producer on Apple’s F1 movie starring Brad Pitt, released in 2025, and several drivers made cameo appearances in it.

    Bond, meanwhile, has a long link with motorsport. The character's Aston Martin DB5 is one of the most famous cars in film history, with the brand now racing in F1 and even previously offering its Vantage and DBX for safety and medical car roles.

    Norris and Russell will be back on track for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, which takes place on Saturday night.
