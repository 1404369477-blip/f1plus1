    詹姆斯·阿隆索揭秘基米·安东内利F1世界冠军背后的冒险技巧

    詹姆斯·阿隆索说安东内利在锦标赛赛季中从刹车到弯顶的控制给人留下深刻印象

    梅赛德斯为粉丝提供最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析

    意大利车手在20岁时就赢得了2026年的冠军，这是他在F1的第二赛季，今年已经赢得了九场大奖赛。

    在天空体育银箭电台节目中，艾莉森表示安东内利在如何使用刹车迁移方面非常非常灵活，这种技术会改变车辆在转弯时前后刹车力的分配，以及差速器的工作方式。

    他非常非常敢于冒险：如果他的车在转向时出现他不喜欢的转向不足，他会通过差速器和刹车迁移来释放它，即使它并不特别想转向。相反，当它感觉过于活跃时，他会关闭它，使其不那么活跃。

    现在，每个人都会这样做，但基米在这方面非常敢于冒险。他愿意在极端设置上尝试，并依靠自己的力量去做，相信他能够控制车辆，使其指向弯道的顶点。这已经是他今年从车辆中获得速度的一大原因。

    “从他踩下刹车到进入弯道的极限，大部分时间他都在控制圈速，而且他做得非常轻松。这使得他在不同条件下对车辆的控制非常灵活，观看他的表现真的非常令人印象深刻。”

    “这种进入弯道的速度也使得安东内利能够更好地照顾他的轮胎。”

    “如果你想看到基米今年最出色的表现，那就是在其他人已经进站换胎的时候，他的圈速依然保持稳定，”他补充道。“他一遍又一遍地做到这一点，而且由于他在进站时的所有工作，他并没有因为工作而损坏后胎。”

    “艾莉森还透露了安东内利和他的赛车工程师彼得‘Bono’博宁顿之间的信任程度，说这位车手会直接告诉他：‘好吧，我信任你。你认为怎么做就怎么做，我会尽力利用我所得到的一切。’”

    “波诺可能会告诉你，这是他经历过的最愉快的赛季之一，”艾莉森总结道。

    James Allison reveals the "very adventurous" trick behind Kimi Antonelli's F1 title

    James Allison says Antonelli’s control from braking to the apex has been impressive throughout his championship season

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes technical director James Allison has explained how Kimi Antonelli's bold use of the tools on his steering wheel helped him become Formula 1's youngest world champion.

    The Italian driver secured the 2026 title at the Mexican Grand Prix at the age of 20, in only his second season in F1, and has won nine grands prix so far this year.

    Speaking on the NU Silver Arrows Radio Show, Allison said Antonelli was "very, very agile" in how he uses brake migration, which changes how the braking force is shared between the front and rear of the car as a corner unfolds, and the differential.

    "He is very, very adventurous: if he has a car that's understeering in a way that he doesn't like, he will free it up with the diff and the brake migration to allow it to turn even if it doesn't particularly want to. And the contrary, when it feels too lively, he will close it down so that it is less so," Allison explained.

    "Now, everyone does this, but Kimi is very adventurous with it. He's willing to go to quite extreme settings on it and do it under his own steam, trusting that he will have the control to keep the car pointed nicely to the apex. And that has been a large part of the pace that he has got from the car this year.

    "From the moment he hits the brakes to the apex is mostly where he is taking the lap time, and he does all that with a minimum of fuss. It makes him very flexible with the car from condition to condition, and it's been really impressive to watch."

    That speed into the corner also allows Antonelli to look after his tyres.

    "If you want to see the standout performances of Kimi this year, it's 20 laps in when everyone else has peeled into the pits and his lap times just go level," he added. "He does it over and over and over, and he just does not damage the rear tyres because of all the work he's doing on the entry."

    Allison also revealed the level of trust between Antonelli and his race engineer, Peter 'Bono' Bonnington, saying the driver will simply tell him: "Well, I trust you. You do what you think is right, and I'll try and work with what I get."

    "Bono would probably tell you that it's been one of the most pleasurable seasons he's had," Allison concluded.
