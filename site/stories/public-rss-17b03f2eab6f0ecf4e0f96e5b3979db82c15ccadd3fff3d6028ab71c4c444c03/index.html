    维斯塔潘拉斯维加斯夺冠 法拉利车手相撞

    维斯塔潘凭借出色的防守驾驶赢下拉斯维加斯大奖赛，主要对手拉塞尔撞车退赛，法拉利两位车手在比赛中相撞。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘凭借一场防守驾驶的大师级表现，为红牛赢下了呈集团军式缠斗的拉斯维加斯大奖赛，而他的主要挑战者乔治·拉塞尔撞车退赛，法拉利两位车手在近期的挣扎中相撞。

    前15圈场面极为疯狂，尾流效应强到位置不断变化，拉塞尔在14号弯三次取得领先，但每次都在1号弯前或进入1号弯时被维斯塔潘反超。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔也曾位居第二并进攻维斯塔潘，他在无线电中恳请红牛指示维斯塔潘不要防守，以便两人互相超越、试图拉开差距，但他预判自己即将被拉塞尔超越，结果确实如此。

    这种来回拉锯在第15圈发生变化，因为当时的前五名——维斯塔潘、拉塞尔、哈贾尔、皮埃尔·加斯利和基米·安东内利——停止缠斗足够久，甩开了阿尔维德·林德布拉德，与后方车阵之间形成缓冲，让这几辆车得以脱身；随后塞尔吉奥·佩雷兹与拉斐尔·卡马拉在14号弯相撞引发安全车，佩雷兹撞上护墙。

    在因修复护栏而延迟七圈、期间还完成了一整套进站之后，比赛刚重启，哈贾尔的前翼就发生故障，使他退出争夺；维斯塔潘和拉塞尔又展开了三次领先位置交替，而比赛中段曾排在第11位的兰多·诺里斯，以及采用相反轮胎策略、用软胎跑完比赛的安东内利，轮流取得领先，随后又相继被超越。

    尤其是对拉塞尔，维斯塔潘两次在2号弯从外线完成不可思议的超越，又在5号弯一次内切夺回领先，他的大多数超车都在弯道完成，而许多其他车手则主要在直道上完成超车。

    安东内利在比赛最后阶段成为维斯塔潘的主要威胁，尤其是在拉塞尔锁死轮胎、在比赛还剩三圈时于5号弯撞上护栏之后；拉塞尔竟还能重新起步，最终拿到积分。

    随后安东内利似乎遇到动力问题，在最后一圈进入1号弯时被两辆迈凯伦先后超越。

    这个缓冲让维斯塔潘——他在撞车后还在无线电里笑着嘲讽拉塞尔（两人在周五的第三次练习赛中发生过冲突），说了句“干得好”——获得了一秒的关键领先优势，他得以将这一优势保持到终点，转化为胜利。

    在安全车期间，迈凯伦首席执行官扎克·布朗在接受天空体育F1采访时听起来颇为沮丧，当时诺里斯已经跌至第11位，但第一段 stint（ stint）中的最快圈速展现了这位迈凯伦车手的速度，他和皮亚斯特里——上升了14个位置——的出色恢复为他们赢得了双领奖台。“谁能想到呢？”诺里斯在谈到这一结果时说道。

    安东内利在动力问题后勉强守住了第四名，在最后一圈于减速弯重新超越加斯利，保住了这个位置。

    尽管如此，第五名仍是加斯利本赛季的最好成绩，而阿尔派队友弗朗哥·科拉平托在最终排名中升至第六，车队在对阵小红牛的比赛中追回了18分。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿最终成为法拉利排名最高的车手，位列第七，这对法拉利车队而言是一场苦涩的表现，因为他们的两位车手在比赛中都丢失了大量位置，还互相撞车，团队配合问题仍在继续。

    法拉利两位车手在第一段 stint 中就发生争执，汉密尔顿爆粗口抱怨勒克莱尔需要配合他完成超车，而勒克莱尔则指责汉密尔顿“挤压”他，并表示如果继续这样“结局会很糟”。

    汉密尔顿在与诺里斯的争夺中驶出赛道，被罚10秒，他在安全车期间执行了处罚，随后奋力追赶并领先勒克莱尔，之后勒克莱尔在第7弯将汉密尔顿撞得打转——赛会因此对勒克莱尔处以10秒罚时。

    处罚前勒克莱尔在赛道上的实际排名是第六。

    他跌至第10名，帮助卡洛斯·塞恩斯为威廉姆斯车队取得了本赛季并列最佳的成绩，而这条赛道正是车队在赛季末少数没有放弃的赛道之一，与此同时他的队友亚历克斯·阿尔本未能发车。

    拉塞尔紧随其后位列第九，勒克莱尔在受罚后以第10名完赛，排在奥迪车手尼科·霍肯伯格和加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托之前。

    林德布拉德在安全车下曾排在队列第五位，但在试图为轮胎升温时打滑，最终退赛，这意味着小红牛车队未能获得任何积分——利亚姆·劳森发车时落后一圈，追至第15名，比哈贾尔落后一位。

    英文原文

    Max Verstappen delivered a masterclass in defensive driving to win a peloton-style Las Vegas Grand Prix for Red Bull, while main challenger George Russell crashed and the Ferrari drivers collided amid their recent struggles.

    The first 15 laps were manic with the slipstream proving so powerful that constant changes of position took place, as Russell took the lead three times at the Turn 14 chicane only to be repassed before or into Turn 1 by Verstappen each time.

    Isack Hadjar had also ran second and attacked Verstappen, and begged Red Bull to instruct Verstappen not to defend so that they could pass and repass each other to try to break away, but he predicted on the radio he was about to be passed by Russell and so it proved.

    The yo-yoing changed on lap 15 because the top five at that point - Verstappen, Russell, Hadjar, Pierre Gasly and Kimi Antonelli - stopped fighting long enough to break clear of Arvid Lindblad, creating a buffer to the rest of the field and allowing these cars to escape before a safety car for Sergio Perez and Rafael Camara's collision at Turn 14, which left Perez in the barrier.

    After a seven-lap delay for barrier repairs that included a full set of pitstops, immediately after the restart Hadjar's front wing failed taking him out of contention, and Verstappen and Russell went at it with another three lead changes, as Lando Norris - 11th earlier in the race - and Antonelli, on an opposite tyre strategy on the softs to finish the race, took turns in the lead before being dispatched again.

    On Russell in particular, Verstappen pulled off two incredible instances of going around the outside at Turn 2 and one dive at Turn 5 to retake the lead, completing most of his overtaking in the corners versus many others doing most of their work on the straights.

    Antonelli established himself as Verstappen's main threat in the closing stages, especially after Russell locked up and went into the barrier at Turn 5 with three laps to go, remarkably getting going again to finish in the points.

    And then Antonelli appeared to have a power issue as he was passed by both of the McLarens going into Turn 1 on the final lap.

    That buffer gave Verstappen - who laughed and mocked Russell (with whom he'd clashed in FP3 on Friday) on the radio with a "well done" after his crash - a crucial one-second lead that he was able to eke out into victory across the line.

    During the safety car McLaren CEO Zak Brown sounded defeated when speaking to Sky Sports F1 after Norris had dropped as low as 11th, but the fastest lap in the first stint showed the McLaren driver's pace and an excellent recovery from him and Piastri - up 14 places - was rewarded with a double-podium. "Who would have thought?" said Norris at that result.

    Antonelli just held on for fourth after his power issues, repassing Gasly into the chicane on the final lap to secure the position.

    Fifth was nevertheless Gasly's best result of the season, while Alpine team-mate Franco Colapinto was elevated to sixth in the final classification as the team clawed back 18 points against Racing Bulls.

    Lewis Hamilton ended as the top Ferrari driver in seventh in a bitter display for the Scuderia as its drivers both lost a sack full of positions and crashed into each other during this race as their teamwork issues continue.

    The Ferraris had bickered in the first stint as Hamilton had an expletive-led rant about how Leclerc needed to work with him to overtake, while Leclerc accused Hamilton of "squeezing" him and said that would "end badly" if it carried on.

    Hamilton was given a 10-second penalty for going off track in a battle with Norris, which he served under the safety car, and fought back to lead Leclerc afterwards, before Leclerc spun Hamilton out at Turn 7 - which the stewards gave Leclerc a 10-second penalty for.

    Leclerc had been sixth on the road before the penalty.

    His dropping down to 10th helped Carlos Sainz give Williams its joint-best result of the year at one of the few tracks it hadn't written off at the end of the season, while his team-mate Alex Albon was not able to start the race.

    Russell was just behind him in ninth and Leclerc rounded out the top 10 after his penalty, ahead of the Audis of Nico Hulkenberg and Gabriel Bortoleto.

    Lindblad had been fifth in the queue under the safety car but spun while trying to generate heat in his tyres and ultimately retired from the race, meaning Racing Bulls failed to score any points - Liam Lawson starting the race a lap down and recovering to 15th, one place behind Hadjar.
