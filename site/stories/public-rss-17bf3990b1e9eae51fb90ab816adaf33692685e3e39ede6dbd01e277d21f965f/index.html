    安东内利自嘲“开车像奶奶”承认在巴库缺乏信心

    梅赛德斯车手、世界冠军领跑者安东内利表示，阿塞拜疆大奖赛中因在巴库城市赛道缺乏信心，自己“开车像奶奶一样”。他从第十六位发车最终获得第五。

    基米·安东内利：这位一级方程式锦标赛领跑者表示，在阿塞拜疆大奖赛上由于缺乏信心，他“开得像自己的奶奶一样”

    在阿塞拜疆大奖赛之后，基米·安东内利对乔治·拉塞尔的世锦赛冠军积分领先优势缩小到了66分；安东内利从第16位发车最终获得第五名，而拉塞尔则从杆位起步赢得冠军

    梅赛德斯车手基米·安东内利表示，阿塞拜疆大奖赛是“那种你必须对第五名感到满意的日子之一”，但他还补充说，在马来西亚大奖赛上他会“恢复到我的正常水平”。

    世锦赛领跑者基米·安东内利表示，由于在巴库城市赛道缺乏信心，他在阿塞拜疆大奖赛期间“开得像自己的奶奶一样”。

    安东内利在周六的比赛中上演了一次不错的翻盘：由于排位赛发生撞车，他从第16位发车，最终以第五名完赛。不过，随着英国车手拉塞尔夺冠，他在车手积分榜上对梅赛德斯队友拉塞尔的领先优势缩小到了66分。

    这位20岁的意大利车手一直表现出极强的稳定性，从而在积分榜榜首建立起巨大领先优势，但在巴库，由于赛车出现技术问题、错过了大量练习时间，他遇到了困难。

    鉴于安东内利在九月初的意大利蒙扎站曾从第19位发车并惊人地赢得了自己的主场之战，外界对他的期望很高，但这一次他的前进过程要平稳得多。

    安东内利说：“如果我有和蒙扎或之前几场比赛相同的速度，结果就会不一样。”

    “本来会是全力进攻的打法。但今天，我很快就意识到，首先，当一长串赛车全都处于超车模式时，超车要难得多，然后我的速度也不如之前，所以心态很快就变了。”

    安东内利在阿塞拜疆大奖赛排位赛中撞上护墙。

    安东内利在起步时实际上掉了一个名次，他用了17圈才一路追回到前10名。

    随后他受益于前方车手的失误，继续提升了名次。在两次安全车动态起步中的第一次，他也险险躲过一劫——避开了一场导致三辆赛车退赛的事故，并且尽管与奥利弗·比尔曼驾驶的哈斯赛车发生了碰撞，仍然得以继续比赛。

    安东内利说：“重新起步之后，我的赛车底板受损严重。发生碰撞后，我左侧整个都报废了。

    阿尔派车队两队友之间发生碰撞，导致兰多·诺里斯撞车退赛，安全车重新起步时一片混乱——这正是阿塞拜疆大奖赛的场面。

    “那之后，这辆车开起来非常不稳定，但我还是努力去争取第四名。总的来说，这就是那种你必须对第五名感到满意的日子之一。”

    "同时看到这么多人犯错，你会有点收敛一些，因为我只是利用了其他所有人的失误，然后不断向前。"

    安东内利不用等太久就能重返赛道，三连赛的第二站将于下周在巴林大奖赛举行，由于中东持续的冲突，这场比赛改由马来西亚承办。

    拉塞尔打出了自赛季揭幕战澳大利亚站以来最完整的一个比赛周末，拿下了一场胜利，若非安全车的多次出动，这本会是一场统治级的胜利。

    阿塞拜疆大奖赛迎来了戏剧性的结局：瓦尔特利·博塔斯在最后一圈撞车，而乔治·拉塞尔以十分之一秒的优势险胜马克斯·维斯塔潘，夺得冠军。

    安东内利仍然是总冠军的最大热门，尤其是考虑到拉塞尔必须在现在到赛季结束之间更换引擎而接受发车罚退。

    因此，这位积分榜领先者在下一场比赛前保持积极心态也就不足为奇了。

    "这很令人兴奋，"安东内利对天空体育F1说。"乔治度过了一个难以置信的周末，他开得非常出色。他有很多信心。"

    "而这个周末到目前为止是我今年表现最差的一站。我不在状态，我并没有真正投入其中，尤其是发生了那么早的事情。"

    Kimi Antonelli: F1 championship leader says he was 'driving like his grandma' due to lack of confidence at Azerbaijan GP

    Kimi Antonelli's world champions lead over George Russell was reduced to 66 points following the Azerbaijan Grand Prix; Antonelli recovered from 16th on the grid to finish fifth, while Russel won from pole position

    Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli says that the Azerbaijan GP was 'one of those days you have to be happy with P5' but adds that he will be 'back at my level' at the Malaysian Grand Prix.

    World championship leader Kimi Antonelli says he was driving "like his grandmother" during the Azerbaijan Grand Prix because of a lack of confidence at the Baku City Circuit.

    Antonelli staged a decent recovery in Saturday's race to come from 16th on the grid, following a crash in qualifying, to finish fifth, but his championship lead over his Mercedes team-mate George Russell was reduced to 66 points as the Brit claimed victory.

    The 20-year-old Italian has shown remarkable consistency to build a commanding lead at the top of the standings, but struggled in Baku after missing a significant chunk of practice due to technical issues with his car.

    "I was driving a bit like my grandmother today, just damage save mode," Antonelli said. "I didn't take risks. Before making a move, I was making sure that I could go for it."

    Highlights from the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    Expectations were high given Antonelli had remarkably come from 19th on the grid to win his home race at Monza earlier in September, but his progress was far more steady this time around.

    "If I had the same pace as at Monza or in previous races, it would have been different," Antonelli said.

    "It would have been like just full attack. But today, I realised very early that first of all, it was a lot harder to overtake with a train of cars all in Overtake Mode, then I didn't have the same pace as I had previously, so the mindset changed very quickly."

    Kimi Antonelli hits the wall during qualifying at the Azerbaijan GP.

    Antonelli actually dropped a place at the start, and it took him 17 laps to work his way up into the top 10.

    He then benefitted from errors made by drivers ahead as he made further progress. He also survived a near-miss at the first of two rolling restarts following Safety Cars as he avoided a crash that took three cars out of the race, and was able to continue despite contact with the Haas of Oliver Bearman.

    Antonelli said: "After the restart, I had big floor damage. My whole left side was gone with the contact.

    There was carnage on the safety car restart after contact between Alpine team-mates resulted in Lando Norris crashing out of the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    "After that, the car was very sketchy to drive, and I still tried to get to P4, but all in all, it's one of those days where you have to be happy with P5.

    "Also seeing so many people doing mistakes, you kind of pull back a little bit because I just took advantage of everyone else's mistakes and just move forward."

    Antonelli doesn't have to wait long to return to the track with the second of a triple-header of races coming next weekend with the Bahrain Grand Prix, which is being hosted by Malaysia amid the ongoing conflict in the Middle East.

    Russell produced his most complete weekend since the opening round of the season in Australia, taking a victory that would have been dominant without the Safety Car interruptions.

    The Azerbaijan GP saw a dramatic ending as Valtteri Bottas crashed on the final lap as George Russell just beat Max Verstappen to victory by one tenth of a second.

    Antonelli remains a heavy favourite for the title, especially given Russell must take a grid penalty for changing his engine between now and the end of the season.

    It was therefore unsurprising that the championship leader was retaining a positive outlook ahead of the next race.

    "It's exciting," Antonelli told Sky Sports F1. "George has done an incredible weekend, he's driven incredibly. He had a lot of confidence.

    "And this weekend was my worst of the year by far in terms of performance. I wasn't there, I wasn't really present, especially with what happened so early on."
