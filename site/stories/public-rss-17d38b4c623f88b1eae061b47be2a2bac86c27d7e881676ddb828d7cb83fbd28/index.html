    经典F1赛车在尼斯港成为电影《Ocean's》前传的街道赛道

    经典F1赛车在尼斯港进行比赛，Instagram上的视频展示了布拉德利·库珀的《Ocean's》前传如何将赛车运动呈现在大银幕上。港口被改造成了20世纪60年代的欧洲赛道。

    经典一级方程式赛车将尼斯港口变成布拉德利·库珀《海洋》前传的街道赛道

    画面显示复古赛车在水面旁的干草堆轨道上挑战

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    经典一级方程式赛车正在尼斯港口周围竞速，Instagram上流传的这段视频是目前为止最清晰的一次，展示了布拉德利·库珀的《海洋》前传计划如何再次将赛车运动搬上银幕。正如你所见，这个工作港口已经被改造成了一个20世纪60年代初的欧洲公路赛道。如果你想要证明这部电影对赛车是认真的，这就是证据。

    画面从一辆配备起重机的日产GT-R摄像机车辆开始，它位于几辆复古开放式赛车前方，这些赛车并排行驶在水边。制作临时结构和防护屏障沿着赛道两侧排列。领头的赛车是深红色带有白色条纹，其尾部似乎粘着一个像布拉汉姆F1赛车的东西，后面跟着几辆其他的赛车。

    随着车队前进，摄像机横扫港口设施。港口管理局大楼，上面有“Service Plaisance Yachting”的标志，与电影拖车和工作人员区域并排而立。引导车辆绕过该区域的屏障和赛车都穿着来自马蒂尼、BP和壳牌等品牌的复古标志。

    最佳时刻是发夹弯。赛车挤过一条由干草堆围成的狭窄弯道，正好在水面边缘，红色赛车在蓝色赛车挤占其变速箱后被超越。

    这个项目是一部未命名的，作为1962年摩纳哥大奖赛的序曲，描绘了丹尼·奥申的父母在比赛周末参与抢劫。由于与电影方向的分歧，布拉德利·库珀在2026年3月接替李·艾萨克·乔恩的职务，负责写作、导演和表演。制作地点包括巴黎、摩纳哥和尼斯。

    上映日期是2027年6月25日。

    主演并制作该片的玛戈特·罗比表示："在丹尼·奥申踏入拉斯维加斯之前，两位智谋者教会了他所有的一切：他的父母。",

    据悉，演员阵容包括瓦格纳·莫拉、莫妮卡·巴巴拉、维基·克里普斯、乔治·麦凯和奥马尔·西。

    Classic F1 turns Port of Nice into street circuit for Bradley Cooper's "Ocean's" Prequel

    Footage shows vintage racers tackling a hay bale-lined track beside the water

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Classic Formula 1 cars are racing around the Port of Nice, and the footage doing the rounds on Instagram is the clearest look yet at how Bradley Cooper's Ocean's prequel plans to put motorsport on screen once again. As you can see, the working harbour has been transformed into an early-1960s European road course. If you wanted proof that this film is treating its racing seriously, this is it.

    The footage begins with a Nissan GT-R camera vehicle fitted with a crane, positioned ahead of several vintage open-wheel racers driving parallel to the water. Temporary structures for production and protective barriers are positioned along both sides of the track. The lead car is dark red with a white stripe, with something like a Brabham F1 car glued to its tail with a number of others following.

    As the pack moves on, the camera pans right across the harbour furniture. The port authority building, with its "Service Plaisance Yachting" signage, sits alongside film trailers and crew staging areas. The barriers steering the cars around the area and the cars wear retro branding from the likes of Martini, BP and Shell.

    The best moment is the hairpin. The cars squeeze through a tight corner lined with hay bales right at the water's edge, and the red car gets overtaken after the blue car crowds its gearbox.

    The project, an untitled prequel to, follows the 1962 Monaco Grand Prix and depicts Danny Ocean's parents taking part in a heist over the race weekend. Bradley Cooper has assumed writing, directing, and acting duties following Lee Isaac Chung's departure in March 2026 due to disagreements over the film's direction. The production includes Paris, Monaco and Nice as locations.

    The release date is 25 June, 2027.

    Margot Robbie, who stars and produces, said: "Before Danny Ocean ever stepped foot in Vegas, two masterminds taught him everything he knows: his parents."

    The cast roster is understood to feature Wagner Moura, Monica Barbaro, Vicky Krieps, George MacKay, and Omar Sy.
