    墨西哥大奖赛致敬F1传奇，汉密尔顿的爱犬Roscoe成焦点

    墨西哥大奖赛组织者在维修区设立了亡灵节展览，纪念已故的F1人物，其中甚至包括了刘易斯·汉密尔顿的爱犬Roscoe。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿的已故爱犬Roscoe在墨西哥大奖赛的亡灵节纪念活动中受到尊敬。

    Motorsport文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车运动不仅仅是运动的故事。

    七次世界冠军的獒犬与F1的一些最伟大的名字一起受到了荣誉。他的照片位于一个展示的中心，位于埃尔顿·塞纳之下。与他们并列的是恩佐·费拉利、杰克·布拉汉姆、胡安·曼努埃尔·方吉奥、詹姆斯·亨特、帕特里克·德帕伊勒、佩德罗·罗德里格斯和让-玛丽·巴列斯特罗。

    其他几个展示也展示了F1的一些伟大人物，包括阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里、格雷厄姆·希尔、罗兰·拉岑贝格、亚历克斯·赞德利、科林·查普曼、马克斯·莫斯利、埃迪·乔丹、斯图尔特·莫斯、何塞·阿贝德、里卡多·罗德里格斯和约亨·林特等人。

    这些展示分布在几面墙上，每面墙都由橙色金盏花拱门装饰，并排着蜡烛和黑白肖像。

    亡灵节（亡灵节）是墨西哥最重要的传统之一，今年它与墨西哥大奖赛在同个周末举行。传统上，家庭会建造祭坛，称为ofrendas，上面装饰着照片、蜡烛和金盏花来纪念已故的亲人，宠物通常也会被包括在内。

    粉丝们迅速在社交媒体上对此致敬做出了反应。

    "Roscoe从未完成过一圈比赛，但不知怎的成为了车库里最受欢迎的面孔之一。墨西哥人绝对把这个致敬做得很好，"有人在Reddit上写道，另一位补充说："他们真是太贴心了，Lewis会喜欢的。"

    "Roscoe在里面真是太感人了，"另一个人写道，另一位粉丝评论道："做得好，这是真正的风范。"

    墨西哥大奖赛是本赛季最后一轮三连赛的第二轮。它紧随美国大奖赛之后，由冠军领跑者安东内利获胜，并引领着下一周的巴西大奖赛。

    赛季将在计划顺利进行的情况下，以拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔和阿布扎比的最终三连赛结束。

    Mexican GP includes Lewis Hamilton's beloved Roscoe in touching tribute to F1 legends

    Lewis Hamilton's late bulldog Roscoe has been honoured alongside Formula 1 legends in a Day of the Dead tribute at the Mexican GP

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    The Mexican Grand Prix organisers have set up a Day of the Dead display in the paddock in memory of late Formula 1 figures, and they have even included Lewis Hamilton's bulldog, Roscoe.

    The seven-time champion's bulldog has been honoured alongside some of F1's greatest names. His photo sits in the centre of one display, underneath Ayrton Senna. Alongside them are Enzo Ferrari, Jack Brabham, Juan Manuel Fangio, James Hunt, Patrick Depailler, Pedro Rodríguez and Jean-Marie Balestre.

    Several other displays feature F1 greats, including Alberto Ascari, Graham Hill, Roland Ratzenberger, Alex Zanardi, Colin Chapman, Max Mosley, Eddie Jordan, Stirling Moss, José Abed, Ricardo Rodríguez and Jochen Rindt, among others.

    The displays were spread across several walls, each framed by an arch of orange marigolds and lined with candles and black-and-white portraits.

    Día de Muertos (Day of the Dead) is one of Mexico's most important traditions, and this year it falls on the same weekend as the Mexican Grand Prix. Traditionally, families build altars, known as ofrendas, decorated with photographs, candles and marigolds to remember loved ones who have died, and it is common for pets to be included.

    Fans were quick to react to the tribute on social media.

    "Roscoe never raced a single lap, but somehow became one of the most loved faces in the paddock. Mexico absolutely nailed this tribute," one wrote on Reddit, while another added: "This is so sweet of them, Lewis will love it."

    "Very touching that Roscoe was in there," someone else wrote, and another fan commented: "Fair play, that’s a class act."

    The Mexican Grand Prix is the second round of the penultimate triple-header of the season. It follows the United States Grand Prix, won by championship leader Kimi Antonelli, and leads into the Brazilian Grand Prix next week.

    The season then closes with a final triple-header in Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi, if all goes to plan.
