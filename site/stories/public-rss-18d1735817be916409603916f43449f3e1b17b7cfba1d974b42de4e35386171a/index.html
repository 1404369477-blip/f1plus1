    墨西哥大奖赛期间，汉密尔顿向前梅赛德斯比赛工程师彼得·“博诺”·邦宁顿致意的视频走红，F1车迷对此表示赞赏。

    F1车迷称赞汉密尔顿向前比赛工程师彼得·邦宁顿的真挚致意

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更广范围的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析与比赛反应。

    七届冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿在墨西哥大奖赛上向前梅赛德斯比赛工程师彼得·“博诺”·邦宁顿致意的视频走红后，F1车迷对此表示赞赏。

    汉密尔顿在效力于布拉克利车队期间一直与邦宁顿合作，并在那里赢得了七个总冠军中的六个。在41岁的汉密尔顿于2025年离开梅赛德斯转投法拉利后，邦宁顿开始与汉密尔顿的继任者基米·安东内利合作。

    这位意大利车手在F1的第二个赛季就在墨西哥大奖赛上赢得了他的首个总冠军头衔。汉密尔顿被拍到拥抱邦宁顿，祝贺他取得这一成就。

    “这个周末的照片太棒了。领奖台上的三人组非常出色，”一位车迷在Reddit上评论道，指的是墨西哥大奖赛的领奖台。红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道夺冠。从杆位发车的汉密尔顿和安东内利分别获得第二和第三名。

    “刘易斯真是个非常有风度的人。很明显他仍然对那起事件感到非常难过，但他把这件事放在一边，去庆祝他那些值得庆祝的朋友们，”另一个人发帖说。

    “说实话，汉密尔顿感觉还是更像梅赛德斯车手，只是换了身赛车服，”另一位车迷写道，还有人评论说：“博诺今天一定百感交集。为安东内利高兴，但他心里有一部分还是希望这是汉密尔顿的第八个冠军，不是吗……”

    更多评论包括：“博诺本赛季也是大赢家。想象一下，你曾是有史以来最伟大的F1车手之一的工程师，然后又要回去当一名新秀的工程师。最终结果对他来说真的非常好。而且我认为，这种搭档关系正是基米能做到这一点的关键。如此年轻、初来乍到，我觉得幕后有一位经验丰富的人支持你，真的会有天壤之别。”

    Lewis Hamilton goes viral after emotional moment with Peter Bonnington in Mexico

    Formula 1 fans have praised Lewis Hamilton for his heartfelt congratulations to former race engineer Peter Bonnington

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Formula 1 fans have praised seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton after footage of him congratulating his former Mercedes race engineer Peter 'Bono' Bonnington at the Mexican Grand Prix went viral.

    Hamilton worked with Bonnington throughout his time at the Brackley outfit, where he secured six of his seven championships. After the 41-year-old left Mercedes to move to Ferrari in 2025, Bonnington started working with Hamilton's successor, Kimi Antonelli.

    The Italian driver secured his maiden championship title at the Mexican Grand Prix in only his second season in F1. Hamilton was captured hugging Bonnington as he congratulated him on the achievement.

    "The pictures from this weekend have been class. The podium trio was fantastic," one fan commented on Reddit, referring to the Mexican Grand Prix podium. Red Bull's Max Verstappen won at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez. Hamilton, who started from pole position, and Antonelli finished second and third, respectively.

    "Lewis is just a very classy dude. It's obvious he's still very upset about the incident, but he's putting that aside to celebrate his friends who deserve the celebration," someone else posted.

    "Hamilton still feels more like a Merc driver honestly, just in a different suit," another fan wrote, while someone else commented: "Bono must have felt bittersweet today. Happy for Antonelli but part of him did want it to be Hamilton's 8th, didn't he..."

    Further comments included: "Bono has also been a big winner this season. Imagine being the engineer for perhaps the greatest F1 driver of all time, then having to go back to being the engineer for a rookie. It really turned out really well for him in the end. And I think this partnership was really what Kimi needed to pull this off. Being so young and new to the scene, I think having a seasoned head behind the scenes supporting you really makes a world of difference."
