    汉密尔顿巴西大奖赛后向法拉利车迷发声

    汉密尔顿在巴西大奖赛从第20位发车一路追赶，却在最后几圈打滑，最终以第九名完赛；他随后在社交媒体上向车迷表示自己对此感到“崩溃”。

    汉密尔顿巴西大奖赛后向法拉利车迷发声

    汉密尔顿在令人失望的巴西大奖赛后向车迷分享了一则信息

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地方的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    汉密尔顿告诉车迷，他对巴西大奖赛的结果感到“崩溃”；这场比赛他以从队尾发车的精彩追赶开局，却在最后几圈打滑。

    这位法拉利车手在周日于英特拉格斯赛道获得第九名后，于周一在Instagram上分享了他的想法。

    汉密尔顿因引擎罚退不得不从第20位发车，但他在使用半雨胎的早期阶段提升了11个位置，随后他的后轮开始衰退。他推动马拉内罗车队换上干胎，并成为第一位做出切换的车手。

    比赛在第62圈雨水回归时发生转折。这位英国车手在干胎上多坚持了一圈，并在到达维修区前打滑，最终在他的第400场大奖赛中获得第九名，落后一圈。

    “对昨天的比赛感到极度沮丧，尤其是它的结束方式。开局非常好，但我们在轮胎和严重的转向过度上挣扎，”这位七届冠军发帖写道。

    “在那种条件下，比赛变得极其困难。我尽了最大努力去推进，但本周末我们就是缺乏速度。我们会努力弄清楚原因，并在下一场比赛中重新奋起反击。”

    “整个周末车队都在赛车上付出了巨大努力，我对此心怀感激。最终，这并不是我们想要交付的比赛。尤其是对这群观众而言。”

    “巴西，感谢你一如既往的能量与支持。这个地方在我心中占据着如此特殊的位置，你们给予我的爱，我原样奉还。祝贺梅赛德斯赢得制造商冠军，也祝贺他们整体上不可思议的一年。实至名归。”

    汉密尔顿还向他本人和法拉利的车迷补充了一段话，解释他并没有把他们的支持视为理所当然。

    “感谢每一位支持我们的人，无论是艰难的日子还是美好的时光，谢谢你们一直与我们同在，”他总结道。“我感受到了这份支持，我并不会将其视为理所当然。”

    汉密尔顿目前在车手积分榜上排名第四，本赛季还剩三站比赛。

    Lewis Hamilton sends message to Ferrari fans after 'devastating' Brazil GP

    Lewis Hamilton shared a message to fans after disappointing Brazilian GP

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton has told fans he is "devastated" by the outcome of his Brazilian Grand Prix, which began with an impressive charge from the back of the grid and finished with a spin in the closing laps.

    The Ferrari driver shared his thoughts on Instagram on Monday after finishing ninth at Interlagos on Sunday.

    Hamilton had to start from 20th on the grid after an engine penalty, but climbed 11 places in the early running on intermediate tyres before his rears began to fade. He pushed the Maranello outfit to change to slicks and was the first driver to make the switch.

    The race turned when the rain returned on lap 62. The Briton stayed out for one more lap on slicks and spun just before reaching the pits, eventually finishing ninth and a lap down in his 400th grand prix.

    "Devastated by yesterday’s race, especially with how it ended. It started so well, but we struggled with the tyres and massive oversteer," the seven-time champion posted.

    "In those conditions, it made things incredibly difficult. I pushed as hard as I could, but we just didn’t have the pace this weekend. We’ll work to understand why and come back fighting at the next race.

    "The team worked so hard on the car all weekend and I’m grateful for every bit of that effort. Ultimately, this isn’t the race we wanted to deliver. Especially to this crowd.

    "Brasil, thank you always for the energy and support. This place holds such a special place in my heart, the love you give to me I send right back to you. Well done to Mercedes for winning the constructors' championship and the incredible year they’ve had overall. Truly deserved."

    Hamilton added a message to his and Ferrari's fans, explaining that he does not take their support for granted.

    "To everyone supporting us, thank you for sticking with us through the difficult days as well as the good ones," he concluded. "I feel that support, and I don’t take it for granted."

    Hamilton currently sits fourth in the drivers' standings with three rounds remaining in the season.
