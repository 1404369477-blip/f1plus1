    安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛锁定2026年F1世界冠军，成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。他透露计划买一辆新车犒赏自己，并称父母一直密切关注他的花钱方式。

    F1世界冠军安东内利在历史性夺冠后计划进行特别采购

    新加冕的F1世界冠军安东内利透露了他庆祝夺冠的计划

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    安东内利透露了他打算如何犒赏自己赢得2026年F1世界冠军。

    尽管父母一直密切关注他的花钱方式，这位意大利车手告诉《米兰体育报》，他打算犒劳自己一辆新车。

    这位20岁的车手上周末在墨西哥大奖赛上锁定总冠军，成为F1历史上最年轻的冠军。

    安东内利对这家意大利媒体表示：“我父母一直试图教我金钱的价值，现在我开始赚到可观的收入，他们想确保知道我怎么花、花在哪里。但可以说，跟我打交道这事挺容易的，因为我不是那种有昂贵爱好的人。”

    他说自己的大部分钱都花在日常事物上，而他最大的享受就是他的赛车模拟器。

    “我家里有模拟器，所以我就靠它找乐子，”这位梅赛德斯车手解释道。“我不需要去购物。我把钱花在食物、家里用的东西、汽油上……然后给自己买几条新赛道在模拟器上开。”

    尽管他尽量在花钱上保持谨慎，但他补充说，汽车是他的例外。

    “这么说吧，我父母得盯着我的地方就是车，因为有几辆我真的很想买。所以我想，我会为了赢得总冠军犒劳自己一辆，”他补充道。

    安东内利还有一种稍微不那么传统的庆祝夺冠的方式，他透露自己仍打算兑现染发的承诺。

    “我已经拿到喷雾罐了！现在我只得决定是这周末就动手，还是等到下周我去布拉克利的工厂、得在所有员工面前讲话的时候再说，”他说，“染着头发出现在那里会很有趣。”

    F1 champion Kimi Antonelli plans special purchase after historic title win

    Newly crowned Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli has revealed his plans to celebrate his title victory

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Kimi Antonelli has revealed how he plans to reward himself for winning the 2026 Formula 1 world championship.

    Despite his parents keeping a close eye on how he spends his money, the Italian driver told La Gazzetta dello Sport that he is going to treat himself to a new car.

    The 20-year-old clinched the title at the Mexican Grand Prix last weekend, becoming the youngest champion in F1 history.

    "My parents have always tried to teach me the value of money, and now that I’m earning significant amounts, they want to make sure they know how I spend it and where," Antonelli told the Italian publication. "But let’s say it’s a fairly easy job with me, because I’m not someone who has expensive hobbies."

    He said most of his money goes on everyday things, and that his main indulgence is his racing simulator.

    "I have my simulator at home, so I have fun with that," the Mercedes driver explained. "I don’t need to go shopping. I spend money on food, things for the house, petrol... and then I buy myself some new tracks to drive on the simulator."

    While he tries to be careful with money, he added that cars are his exception.

    "Let’s say that what my parents have to keep an eye on me about is cars, because there are a couple I’d really like to buy. So I think I’ll treat myself to one for winning the title," he added.

    Antonelli also has a slightly more unconventional way to celebrate his title, revealing that he still plans to keep his promise to dye his hair.

    "I’ve got the cans! Now I just have to decide whether to do it this weekend or maybe wait until next week when I go to the factory in Brackley and have to speak in front of all the employees," he said. "It would be fun to turn up there with dyed hair."
