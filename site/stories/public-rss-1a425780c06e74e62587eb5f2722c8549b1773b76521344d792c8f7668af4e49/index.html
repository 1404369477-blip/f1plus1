    前F1车手丹尼尔·里卡多将参加从亚利桑那沙漠的福特Raptor rally到奥斯汀的美国大奖赛的公路之旅

    赛车文化之外，丹尼尔·里卡多的生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和赛车故事

    前F1车手丹尼尔·里卡多在美国亚利桑那沙漠参加福特Raptor rally后，将前往德克萨斯州奥斯汀参加美国大奖赛

    福特赛车和里卡多的生活方式品牌Enchanté在联合Instagram帖子中宣布了他参加活动的消息。"Raptor Rally，你准备好了吗？我们将与丹尼尔·里卡多一起踏上征程。在那里见！"帖子的标题写道。

    根据福特公司的消息，1000名嘉宾和450多辆卡车聚集在亚利桑那州的拉赫萨湖，参加了第三届Raptor Rally。在两天的时间里，有沙漠小径、巴哈环线、专业车手的赛道热身以及首届Raptor巴哈短距离赛。

    阵容中包括洛伦·希利、布拉德·洛维尔、沃恩·吉廷小弟和米奇·格思里小弟。

    Enchanté和福特赛车将在10月22日至25日在奥斯汀的1500 East 4th Street的Distribution Hall开设一个临时展位。

    跟随他在2024年退出F1后，里卡多成为了福特赛车全球大使。在他的任职期间，他曾在福特全球设施幕后工作，成为了Raptor品牌的代表，参与了各种福特Raptor T1+活动，并参与了红牛F1赛车揭幕仪式，这是其与福特新动力单元合作的一部分。

    Daniel Ricciardo hints at F1 paddock return amid Ford Raptor road trip to Austin

    Former F1 driver Daniel Ricciardo is embarking on a road trip from Ford's Raptor Rally in Arizona to Austin for the United States Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Former Formula 1 driver Daniel Ricciardo is taking on an intense road trip across America, starting in the Arizona desert for Ford's Raptor Rally this weekend and ending in Austin, Texas, for the United States Grand Prix.

    Ford Racing and Ricciardo's lifestyle brand Enchanté announced his participation in the event in a joint Instagram post. "Raptor Rally, you ready? We’re rolling out with Daniel Ricciardo to hit the trails. See you out there!" the caption read.

    According to Ford, 1000 guests and more than 450 trucks gathered in Lake Havasu, Arizona, for the third edition of the Raptor Rally. Over two days, there are desert trails, Baja loops, hot laps with professional drivers and the first Raptor Baja Sprint competition.

    Included in the line-up are Loren Healy, Brad Lovell, Vaughn Gittin Jr and Mitch Guthrie Jr.

    Enchanté and Ford Racing are opening a pop-up at Distribution Hall, 1500 East 4th Street, Austin, from 22-25 October.

    Following his F1 exit in 2024, Ricciardo became a global ambassador for Ford Racing. During his time in the role, he has spent time behind the scenes at Ford's global facilities, become the face of the Raptor brand, taken part in various events with the Ford Raptor T1+ and been involved with the Red Bull F1 car reveal event as part of its new power unit collaboration with Ford.
