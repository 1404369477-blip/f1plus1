    F1 Mexico GP: Sunday schedule, weather forecast and how to watch

    What time the 2026 Formula 1 Mexico Grand Prix starts, plus the full Mexico City schedule, weather forecast and how to watch live

    Formula 1 heads into Sunday in Mexico City with Lewis Hamilton on pole and Kimi Antonelli only one result away from becoming world champion.

    Hamilton leads George Russell and Ferrari team-mate Charles Leclerc on the grid, while Antonelli will start fourth after Max Verstappen one-place grid penalty.

    Antonelli needs only to finish ninth or higher to clinch his first Formula 1 world championship regardless of what his rivals do. Even if he finishes outside the points, the title will be his unless Russell or Hamilton wins the race.

    That puts both of Antonelli’s remaining championship rivals ahead of him on the grid, setting up a decisive 71-lap Mexico Grand Prix.

    Here is everything you need to know ahead of Sunday’s F1 Mexico GP.
