    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队

    法拉利车迷们。最新的法拉利F1新闻、车手更新、技术分析和来自马拉内洛及以外的比赛反应。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿和马克斯·维斯塔潘都预计在英特拉戈斯赛道超越对手将比以往赛季更加困难，因为他们准备从发车线的最后位置开始周日举行的巴西大奖赛。

    两位车手都在巴西更换了新的动力单元部件，超出了本赛季的分配。因此，汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘将分别从第20位和第21位发车，只有凯迪拉克的塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯在他们后面，排在第22位。

    英特拉戈斯赛道一直是一个超越相对容易的赛道。维斯塔潘和汉密尔顿都曾在赛道上从很后的位置赢得比赛：汉密尔顿在2021年从第10位起步，维斯塔潘在2024年从第17位起步。但随着2026年规则的改变，驾驶者辅助系统被取消，新的超越模式出现，他们都认为这可能不再是事实。

    ‘超越将会更加困难一些，’维斯塔潘在雨后的排位赛结束后对天空体育F1频道说。

    “这里的超车模式实际上并没有做什么。每个人都从最后一个弯道到1号弯道在消耗电池。每个人都打开了他们的翅膀。”

    “所以效果将比正常情况下要小。这会让你在到达与你有相似圈速的车辆时超车变得更难。前几辆车没问题，但其他的就会有点难了。”

    汉密尔顿在接受媒体采访时补充道：“我认为会更难。因为那时候我们显然有一个非常长的直道。如果你接近，你就有DRS，也许对方没有。”

    “因为现在每个人都只是打开了他们的翅膀，我认为超车将会非常非常难。我不确定你是否会看到过去看到的那种比赛。但也许湿滑的天气会改变这一点。”

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen warn of difficult F1 Brazilian GP comebacks

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen expect overtaking to be more difficult at the Brazilian Grand Prix

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen both expect overtaking to be much harder at Interlagos than in previous seasons, as they prepare to start Sunday's Brazilian Grand Prix from the back of the grid.

    Both drivers have taken new power unit parts in Brazil, exceeding their allocation for the season. As a result, Hamilton and Verstappen will start from 20th and 21st on the grid, respectively, with only Cadillac's Sergio Perez behind them in 22nd.

    Interlagos has long been a track where overtaking has been relatively easy. Both Verstappen and Hamilton have won at the track from well down the order before: Hamilton from 10th in 2021 and Verstappen from 17th in 2024. But with DRS gone under the 2026 regulations and the new overtake mode, both believe this may no longer be the case.

    "It's going to be a bit harder to overtake," Verstappen told Sky Sports F1 after the rain-soaked qualifying session.

    "The overtake mode doesn't really do a lot around here. Everyone is draining their battery from the last corner to Turn 1. Everyone has their wings open.

    "So the effect is going to be less than normal. And that will make it a bit harder to overtake once you get to the cars that are similar lap time. The first few cars is not a problem, but then the other ones will be a bit harder."

    Hamilton added while speaking to the media: "I think [it will be] harder. Because back then we had, obviously, a very long straight. And if you got close, you had the DRS and maybe a car wouldn't have it.

    "Because now everyone is just opening up their wings, I think overtaking will be very, very, very hard. I'm not sure you're going to see the same sort of races that you've seen in the past. But maybe the wet conditions will change that."
