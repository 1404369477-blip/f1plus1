    乔治·拉塞尔暗示托托·沃尔夫对维斯塔潘私下看法

    乔治·拉塞尔暗示，梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫在麦克风关闭后对维斯塔潘的看法有所不同。

    乔治·拉塞尔对托托·沃尔夫的看法进行了暗示

    乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯赛后飞行中与托托·沃尔夫开怀大笑，之后暗示了沃尔夫对维斯塔潘的私下看法

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析

    托托·沃尔夫在过去的一周里对F1最激烈的车手纷争显得不以为意，然而乔治·拉塞尔暗示，沃尔夫在麦克风关闭后看法有所不同

    独家接受天空体育F1的马丁·布兰德利采访——在周日卡塔尔大奖赛完整播出之前，采访于周五提前预告——拉塞尔被问及在拉斯维加斯事件后沃尔夫对维斯塔潘的外交性公开立场。拉塞尔没有重复私下对话的内容，但几乎没有疑问，沃尔夫镜头外的评价更加尖锐。

    “嗯，我知道他在场外的立场，所以可能不会重复那些话，”拉塞尔说。“托托和我比赛后在拉斯维加斯飞回，我们确实对赛后的一些评论笑了出来。但我们只是知道某些人是如何处理他们的业务的。”

    沃尔夫在拉斯维加斯的语气为何引起侧目

    这番话直接切入 车队区最受审视的动态之一。自从梅赛德斯在2025年追求红牛车手以来，沃尔夫对维斯塔潘的公开友好态度一直受到密切关注——拉塞尔本人在2025年6月接受天空体育F1采访时确认的谈判推迟了他的合同续签。

    即使在2026年3月沃尔夫公开否认了新的谣言后——坚称“目前Max与梅赛德斯的事情还没有定论”，并支持拉塞尔与布拉克利“一直合作到2037年”——沃尔夫和维斯塔潘一起度假的照片在2026年7月再次出现，再次激起了猜测。

    上周末在内华达州，这种动态再次受到显微镜般的审视。在最后练习中在14号弯道发生接触后，拉塞尔公开批评了维斯塔潘，而维斯塔潘则通过赢得周六晚上的大奖赛来回应，而拉塞尔在与队友基米·安东内利战斗时撞上护栏，最终排名第九。事后，维斯塔潘宣称拉塞尔“到目前为止水平还远低于我”，并公开表示对对手晚上的错误感到满意。

    梅赛德斯AMG Petronas F1车队执行董事沃尔夫、梅赛德斯AMG Petronas F1车队拉塞尔

    与沃尔夫的公开反应形成对比的是，他的反应出奇地冷静。沃尔夫称维斯塔潘是一个“当之无愧的赢家”，他“没有犯错误”，并且“总是有回应”，沃尔夫轻描淡写地处理了两人之间的紧张关系。沃尔夫对记者说：“这可能是一点点猫捉老鼠的游戏，”他还补充说，“双方的挑衅是娱乐的另一个好因素”，同时指出在 stewards’ 审判长，里确实发生了一些“交流”。

    计时解释了其中一部分限制：沃尔夫最初的赛道旁言论是在旗杆落下后不久发表的，在维斯塔潘最尖锐的新闻发布会挖苦尚未完全传开之前。然而，一位车队负责人赞扬刚刚公开嘲讽他车手的对手，这一行为立即引发了争议——这使得拉塞尔回家的揭示成为了一个尖锐的提醒，表明梅赛德斯内部的团结与公开公关看起来不同。

    沃尔夫的私下笑声是否会在最后两轮比赛中转化为赛道上的报复，现在取决于拉塞尔。进入卡塔尔，维斯塔潘以九场领奖台 streak，其中包括四次胜利，在车手积分榜上以304分排名第二 - 比拉塞尔（264分）高出40分，还剩下50分未决。

    至少在周五的洛杉矶赛道首节练习中，梅赛德斯以鼓舞人心的速度开始了周末的比赛，冠军候选人安东内利带领银箭车队以1-2的成绩领先，领先于拉塞尔半秒，而维斯塔潘在最后一小时结束时排名第六。将这种早期的速度转化为周日比红牛更出色的结果，将比在回家的航班上所说的任何话更能决定性地解决拉斯维加斯的争议。

    George Russell drops cryptic hint over where Toto Wolff “stands off the record” on Max Verstappen

    George Russell teases Toto Wolff's "off the record" view of Max Verstappen after their post-Las Vegas flight laugh

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Toto Wolff has spent the past week sounding remarkably unfazed by Formula 1’s fiercest driver feud. George Russell, however, just hinted that Mercedes’ team principal sounds rather different once the microphones are switched off.

    Speaking exclusively to Martin Brundle for Sky Sports F1 - in an interview previewed Friday ahead of its full broadcast before Sunday’s Qatar Grand Prix - Russell was pressed on Wolff’s diplomatic public stance toward Max Verstappen following their Las Vegas flashpoint. Russell stopped short of repeating private conversations, but left little doubt that Wolff’s off-camera assessment carries a sharper edge.

    "Well, I know where he [Wolff] stands off the record, so I won't probably repeat that," Russell said. "Toto and I were flying back after the race in Vegas, and we did have quite a laugh about the comments that were said after the race. But we just know how certain people go about their business."

    Why Wolff’s Las Vegas tone raised eyebrows

    That remark cuts straight into one of the paddock’s most scrutinised dynamics. Wolff’s public warmth toward Verstappen has been tracked closely ever since Mercedes pursued the Red Bull driver in 2025 - talks Russell himself confirmed to Sky Sports F1 in June 2025 had delayed his own contract renewal.

    Even after Wolff publicly shut down fresh rumours in March 2026 - insisting "The Max to Mercedes thing for now is not on" and backing Russell to stay with Brackley "until 2037" - photographs of Wolff and Verstappen holidaying together surfaced again in July 2026, keeping the speculation alive.

    Last weekend in Nevada put that dynamic back under the microscope. After final-practice contact at Turn 14 triggered public criticism from Russell, Verstappen answered by winning Saturday night’s Grand Prix while Russell clipped the barrier late while battling team-mate Kimi Antonelli and dropped to ninth. In the aftermath, Verstappen declared Russell "so far below my level" and took open satisfaction in his rival’s late error.

    Toto Wolff, Executive Director of Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team, George Russell, Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team

    Wolff’s public reaction, by contrast, stayed conspicuously calm. Calling Verstappen "a deserving winner" who "made no mistakes" and "always had a response," Wolff brushed off the tension between the pair. "It's probably been a bit of cat and mouse," Wolff told reporters, adding that "these provocations in either side is another good factor of entertainment" while noting only that there had been "a bit of an exchange" in the stewards’ room.

    Timing explains part of that restraint: Wolff’s initial paddock remarks came shortly after the chequered flag, before Verstappen’s sharpest press-conference digs had fully made the rounds. Still, the optics of a team principal praising the rival who had just publicly mocked his driver sparked immediate debate—making Russell’s flight-home reveal a pointed reminder that internal alignment inside Mercedes looks different from public PR.

    Whether Wolff’s private laughs translate into on-track payback is now down to Russell over the final two rounds. Entering Qatar, Verstappen rides a nine-race podium streak featuring four wins and sits second in the Drivers’ standings on 304 points - 40 clear of Russell (264) with only 50 points left on the table.

    Mercedes at least opened the weekend with encouraging pace in Friday’s opening practice at Losail, where champion-elect Antonelli led a Silver Arrows 1-2 half a second clear of Russell, while Verstappen ended the hour down in sixth. Turning that early speed into a Sunday result ahead of Red Bull would settle the Vegas fallout far more decisively than anything said on a flight home.
