    汉密尔顿支持V8回归，批评2026引擎直道乏力

    在拉斯维加斯大奖赛媒体日上，汉密尔顿对国际汽联推动V8引擎回归表示欢迎，同时直言批评2026款动力单元在直道中段就失去动力。

    汉密尔顿支持V8回归，批评2026引擎在直道“乏力”

    汉密尔顿支持F1拟议中的V8引擎回归，同时抨击2026款动力单元在直道中段就失去动力

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地方的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    汉密尔顿经历了F1 V6涡轮混合动力时代的每一个赛季，他对最新规则的问题并不讳言。在拉斯维加斯大奖赛媒体日上，这位七届世界冠军对国际汽联推动V8引擎回归表示欢迎，同时直言批评当前2026款动力单元在直道上逐渐失去动力。

    国际汽联主席穆罕默德·本·苏拉耶姆周二在Instagram上发布了一段带有V8引擎声浪的预告视频，配文“你们说了，我们听了。V8即将到来”，此后V8话题便占据了内华达围场。在管理机构、一级方程式管理公司和引擎制造商会议之后，国际汽联官方渠道曾短暂发布又删除了一些图表，内容涉及2030年达成原则性协议。

    在拉斯维加斯被问及对V8引擎可能在2030年或2031年回归有何感想时，汉密尔顿直接指出了自F1在2013年后退役自然吸气2.4升V8引擎以来声浪的下降。

    “这取决于他们在规则层面如何设计，以及能否回到那种好声音，因为我认为自从我们进入V6时代以来，这一直是缺失的，”汉密尔顿说。“是的，带回小排气管，找回那种声音，但你可以拥有那个，只要我们的引擎不会在直道中途熄火，那我认为那样会更好。”

    汉密尔顿关于引擎在直道中途断力的抱怨，直指2026年规则最大的痛点。当前1.6升V6混合动力单元严重依赖350千瓦的MGU-K，车手经常触及能量部署限制，在刹车区之前就早早失去电力推力——这一问题在拉斯维加斯1.9公里的长直道上将被放大。

    即便V8回归，它们也不会复刻汉密尔顿2007年首秀、2008年首夺世界冠军时所驾驶的自然吸气发动机。目前讨论中的框架采用2.1升涡轮增压V8发动机，使用可持续燃料，并搭配一台功率小得多的100千瓦混合动力电机，以降低成本并减轻动力单元重量。

    目前尚无任何定论。各制造商——梅赛德斯、法拉利、红牛福特、本田、奥迪和凯迪拉克——将于周五在拉斯维加斯再次会晤，并将在卡塔尔再度聚首，以就统一方案达成一致，然后在12月10日于上海提交给国际汽联世界汽车运动理事会。鉴于当前发动机周期计划持续至2030年，若要在2030年而非2031年推出V8，就需要将现有规则提前一年终止。

    Lewis Hamilton backs V8 return, hits out at 2026 engines “dying” on straights

    Lewis Hamilton backs F1's proposed V8 engine return while ripping 2026 power units for dying halfway down straights

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton has raced through every season of Formula 1's V6 turbo-hybrid era, and he is not shy about what the latest rules are getting wrong. Speaking on Las Vegas Grand Prix media day, the seven-time world champion welcomed the FIA's push to bring back V8 engines - while taking a blunt jab at how the current 2026 power units fade down the straights.

    V8 talk took over the Nevada paddock after FIA President Mohammed Ben Sulayem posted an Instagram teaser on Tuesday featuring V8 audio and the caption, "You spoke, we listened. V8s coming soon." Official FIA channels briefly published and deleted graphics referencing an agreement in principle for 2030, following a meeting between the governing body, Formula One Management, and engine manufacturers.

    Asked in Las Vegas how he felt about a potential V8 comeback in 2030 or 2031, Hamilton pointed straight to the acoustic drop-off since F1 retired its naturally aspirated 2.4-litre V8s after 2013.

    "It depends on how they design it, regulation-wise, and going back to the good sound, because that's, I think, been lacking since we've been in the V6 era," Hamilton said. "Yeah, bring back the small exhaust and get that sound back, but you could have that, and as long as we don't have the engine dying halfway down the straight, then I think that'd be better."

    Hamilton's complaint about engines cutting out mid-straight targets the biggest pain point of the 2026 regulations. With the current 1.6-litre V6 hybrids leaning heavily on a 350kW MGU-K, drivers frequently hit energy-deployment limits and lose electrical punch well before braking zones - an issue that will be magnified on Las Vegas's 1.9-kilometre Strip straight.

    Even if V8s return, they will not mirror the naturally aspirated engines Hamilton raced when he debuted in 2007 and won his first title in 2008. The framework under discussion features a 2.1-litre turbocharged V8 running on sustainable fuels, paired with a much smaller 100kW hybrid motor to cut costs and trim power unit weight.

    Nothing is finalised yet. Manufacturers - Mercedes, Ferrari, Red Bull Ford, Honda, Audi, and Cadillac - are set to reconvene in Las Vegas on Friday and again in Qatar to align on a unified proposal before presenting it to the FIA World Motor Sport Council in Shanghai on 10 December. With the current engine cycle scheduled through 2030, launching V8s in 2030 rather than 2031 would require cutting the existing rules short by a year.
