    维斯塔潘回击拉塞尔批评：他远低于我的水平

    维斯塔潘回应拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯的批评，称对方“远低于我的水平”，并表示看到拉塞尔撞墙感觉“太棒了”。

    维斯塔潘表示，拉塞尔的批评并未困扰他，因为对方“远低于我的水平”，他还说看到拉塞尔在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛中撞上护墙感觉“太棒了”。

    拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯三练两人发生离奇冲突后公开批评维斯塔潘，称维斯塔潘“周复一周地坑害车手”，是那种“走路的样子好像比别人都高人一等”的人。

    两人在拉斯维加斯大奖赛正赛同排头排发车，比赛中多次交换领先，随后拉塞尔在尝试超越队友安东内利失败后车头撞上护墙，退出冠军争夺。

    拉塞尔虽能继续比赛，但最终仅列第九，维斯塔潘则拿下近六场比赛的第四场胜利。

    当在车队无线电中得知拉塞尔的事件，并在赛后新闻发布会上被问及拉塞尔的言论是否在比赛中萦绕在维斯塔潘脑海中时，维斯塔潘对此表示觉得好笑，他说：“嗯，这又不是什么成功。”

    “问题是，他在赛道外话很多。老实说，对我来说，这并不真的困扰我，因为他远低于我的水平，所以对我来说，试图去挑战那件事根本没有任何意义，所以它不困扰我。”

    “今天，因果报应真是个贱货。老实说，当我看向后视镜，看到正在发生的事情时，那真是太棒了。”

    “所以我就想，‘太棒了，我的一天只会越来越好’，当然，当我随后第一个冲过终点线时，我的一天就完整了。”

    这个周末让人想起2024赛季末两人之间的口水战，当时拉塞尔声称维斯塔潘在当年的卡塔尔大奖赛上对他说，会“把我他妈的头按到墙上”。

    那是在维斯塔潘因阻挡拉塞尔而失去该站杆位之后发生的，并导致拉塞尔在接下来一周的阿布扎比发表了如今广为人知的言论，称维斯塔潘“带着不必要的愤怒和近乎暴力的情绪发作”。

    英文原文

    Max Verstappen said George Russell's criticism has not bothered him because he's "so far below my level", and said it felt "fantastic" to see Russell hit the barriers in Formula 1's Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Russell called out Verstappen after their bizarre clash during FP3 in Las Vegas, claiming Verstappen screwed over drivers "week-in, week-out" and is someone who "walk[s] around like they're bigger and better than everyone else".

    The duo shared the front row for the start of the Las Vegas GP and swapped the lead on multiple occasions throughout the race, before Russell took himself out of victory contention by nosing into the barriers after a failed overtake on team-mate Kimi Antonelli.

    Russell was able to continue but ended up down in ninth place while Verstappen took his fourth victory in six races.

    Verstappen expressed amusement at Russell's incident when told about it over the team radio and in the post-race press conference, when asked whether Russell's comments were in Verstappen's mind during the race, said: "Well, it's not like it's like a success.

    "The thing is, he speaks a lot next to the track. And honestly, for me, it doesn't really bother me because he's so far below my level that it doesn't really make any sense for me anyway to try and challenge that, so it doesn't bother me.

    "Today, karma is a bitch. It was honestly fantastic when I looked in my mirror and I saw what was happening.

    "So I was like, 'Fantastic, my day is only getting better', and of course, when I then crossed the line in first, my day was complete."

    This weekend is reminiscent of a war of words between the pair at the end of the 2024 season when Russell claimed Verstappen told him he'd "put me on my f***ing head in the wall" in the Qatar Grand Prix that year.

    That followed Verstappen losing pole position there for impeding Russell and led to Russell's now-famous claim that Verstappen "lashes out with unnecessary anger and borderline violence" in Abu Dhabi the following week.
