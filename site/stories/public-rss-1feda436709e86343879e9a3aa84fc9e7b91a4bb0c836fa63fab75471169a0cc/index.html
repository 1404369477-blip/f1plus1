    在本周末于马德里举行的西班牙大奖赛之前，他目前在车手积分榜上以66分的优势领先队友拉塞尔，并有望在20岁时成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。

    他的父亲马尔科·安东内利（Marco Antonelli）此前透露，他曾与法拉利进行过洽谈，但这支车队不想签下如此年轻的车手，而梅赛德斯则愿意冒这个风险。

    在最新一期《The F1 Show》节目中，Chandhok说：“我真的认为，不让他成为法拉利青训车手，不把他推到法拉利面前，不设法让他成为法拉利年轻车手，而是走梅赛德斯的路线，实际上是Marco Antonelli为他儿子所做的最好的事情。这从很多方面减轻了压力。

    “赛后我在梅赛德斯的房车旁看到了Kimi的父母和他的妹妹。看到他显然在主场大奖赛夺冠后，他们看起来非常开心。我不禁想到，没有让他成为法拉利车手，确实帮他们卸下了一部分压力。

    上周，Antonelli 淡化了有关他未来可能为法拉利车队的猜测，他声称自己可能在梅赛德斯度过整个职业生涯。

    他说：“尽管法拉利是一个庞大而了不起的品牌，但我对自己现在的处境非常满意”，不过 Chandhok 希望他有一天能穿上红色的战袍出战。

    Antonelli 今年以他的速度和表现震惊了 F1 围场中的大多数人，但他在蒙扎最近一场从第 19 位发车并夺冠的比赛更是登上了世界各地的头条。

    天空体育 F1 的 David Croft 表示，Antonelli 是一位“划时代的天才”，但他仍然“脚踏实地”。

    Croft 说：“那个周末开始时，围场里大家在捉弄他，他的车队为了给他一个生日惊喜回报他，向他扔奶油派。我不确定发车线上大多数 F1 车手是否能容忍这种事。”

    “基米（Kimi）对此完全乐在其中，有一位车手在他所在的车队里如此放松、如此自在。愿这种状态在赛道内外都能长久保持下去。对于F1来说，他简直是一股清新的空气。”

    基米·安东内利（安东内利）回顾了他从第19位发车到夺冠的蒙扎惊艳胜利，追上队友乔治·拉塞尔（拉塞尔），夺得一座具有里程碑意义的主场冠军，并扩大了他的积分榜领先优势

    “现在这些孩子在10岁、11岁、12岁时就从初级卡丁车阶段被签入车手培养计划，他们从更小的年纪就开始被塑造。”

    “对比一下上一代，比如费尔南多·阿隆索或迈克尔·舒马赫，他们直到已经进入F3时才签下职业合同。”

    “所以我认为，从某种角度来说，他以一个更加成熟的姿态来到这里并不令人意外，因为他已经在职业体系中待了10年，而上一代车手并没有这样的经历。”

    Kimi Antonelli driving for Mercedes rather than Ferrari has taken pressure off Italian says Karun Chandhok

    On the latest episode of Sky Sports F1's The F1 Show, Simon Lazenby, Karun Chandhok and David Croft discuss Kimi Antonelli's stunning win at Monza; watch every session of the Spanish Grand Prix at a new track in Madrid from this Friday, live on Sky Sports F1

    Watch how Kimi Antonelli converted 19th to first to take a sensational victory at the Italian Grand Prix

    Sky Sports F1's Karun Chandhok believes Kimi Antonelli driving for Mercedes, rather than Ferrari, has taken some pressure off the Italian.

    Antonelli joined the Mercedes junior programme as an 11-year-old after some remarkable results in karting and quickly rose up the motorsport ranks to Formula 1 and replaced Lewis Hamilton at the Silver Arrows only last year.

    He currently leads the Drivers' Championship by 66 points from team-mate George Russell ahead of this weekend's Spanish Grand Prix in Madrid and is on course to become the youngest world champion in F1 history at 20 years old.

    His father, Marco Antonelli, has previously revealed he held discussions with Ferrari but the Scuderia did not want to sign someone so young whereas Mercedes were willing to take the risk.

    On the latest episode of The F1 Show, Chandhok said: "I actually think by not making him a Ferrari junior, not putting him in front of Ferrari, not trying to make him a Ferrari young driver, and going down the Mercedes path, is actually the best thing that Marco Antonelli has done for his son. It's taken the pressure off in so many ways.

    "Could you imagine if he was there as a Ferrari Italian driver? The pressure would be unbelievable. And Mercedes have done a fantastic job of nurturing him and bringing him here.

    "I saw Kimi's parents and his sister afterwards by the Mercedes motorhome. They looked so delighted with the fact that obviously he'd won the home Grand Prix. I couldn't help but think they've managed to take away some pressure by not having him as a Ferrari driver."

    Kimi Antonelli wins the Italian Grand Prix from 19th on the grid

    Last week, Antonelli played down suggestions that he could drive for Ferrari in the future as he claimed he could spend his entire career with Mercedes.

    He said "as much as Ferrari is massive, incredible brand, I am very happy where I am" but Chandhok hopes he does drive in red one day.

    "For the sake of the sport and the sake of Ferrari, I hope, a bit like Lewis, in 10, 15 years' time when Kimi's achieved a whole lot of things, maybe he will become a Ferrari driver. It'll be great for Ferrari to have him one day," he said.

    Antonelli has stunned most of the F1 paddock this year with his speed and performances so far, but his most recent in Monza where he won from 19th on the grid made headlines around the world.

    Sky Sports F1's David Croft says Antonelli is a "generational talent" but is still "keeping his feet on the ground".

    "At the start of the weekend he was getting pranked in the paddock, getting cream pies flung at him from his team as they got him back with a birthday surprise. I'm not sure the majority of F1 drivers on the grid would have stood for that," said Croft.

    "Kimi absolutely revelled in it and there is a driver who is so at ease and so comfortable in the team that he's in. Long may that continue on track and off it as well. He is a total breath of fresh air for Formula 1."

    Kimi Antonelli reflects on his sensational 19th to first Monza victory, catching teammate George Russell to claim a landmark home win and strengthen his championship lead

    Chandhok added: "He's been prepared from a much younger age as a professional, all-rounded driver - how to work with a team, how to work with a bigger structure.

    "These kids now who are being signed to driver programmes from the ages of 10, 11, 12 in junior karting, they're being moulded from a younger age.

    "You compare and contrast to the generation above, a Fernando Alonso or a Michael Schumacher, they only signed to professional contracts by the time they were already in F3.

    "So I think, in some ways, it's not a surprise, I guess, that he is arriving as a more finished package because he's had 10 years of being as part of a professional structure, which the generation above wasn't."
