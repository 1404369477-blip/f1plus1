    Speaking after Sunday's race in Austin, Verstappen said: "It's not the second time. It's already, I don't know how many times this year.

    Verstappen and his team-mate Isack Hadjar were both also frustrated with another race where they both made poor starts.

    The Dutchman was overtaken by Ferrari's Charles Leclerc on the opening lap after being left vulnerable from a poor start, while Hadjar was cleared by both Leclerc and his team-mate Lewis Hamilton.

    Verstappen told Sky Sports F1: "Just the same always at the start, the problem we have."

    Hadjar went into more detail, telling Sky Sports F1: "My race start was… I do everything perfect in terms of procedure and I have clutch over delivery. These things are out of my control.

    "I have poor radio coverage, can't hear what's happening, can't hear what's happening in the pits. I leave the pits with the wrong map, then I lose a second every lap on that hard tyre.

    "It needs a redesign. That's it. The clutch. It's too fragile. It's not coping well with temperatures. It's a real struggle."

    Mekies: It's making everyone's lives difficult

    Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies said the radio issue was "making everyone's lives very difficult" within the Milton Keynes squad.

    "We are all frustrated to struggle for the second race in a row with the radio issues," Mekies said. "It's making everyone's lives very difficult.

    "So, it is true that it's something that we would like to fix quite quickly, because it's an item that should be working without using too much of our energy."

    Mekies also admitted that while he feels Red Bull's starts are improving, they can't become "the benchmark" before the end of this season due to the presence of issues that will only be possible to fix during the winter.

    "The start is a slightly more complex issue to solve, meaning that the start performance is going across pretty much all the departments of the company, probably except aerodynamics," Mekies said.

    "On one side, I'm confident that we will continue to reduce the gap to the competition from now to the end of the year, because every race we learn, every race we come up with small ways to close the gap.
