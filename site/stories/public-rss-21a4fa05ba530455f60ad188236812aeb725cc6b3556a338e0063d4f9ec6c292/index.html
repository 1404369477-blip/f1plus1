    安东内利：夺冠最后一圈泪流满面看不清赛道

    安东内利表示，在墨西哥城大奖赛最后一圈他因情绪激动而泪流满面，几乎看不清赛道，最终他以第三名完赛并首次加冕F1世界冠军。

    安东内利成为F1世界冠军：这位梅赛德斯车手表示，在墨西哥城大奖赛最后一圈他泪流满面，看不清赛道。

    安东内利透露，在墨西哥城大奖赛的最后阶段他情绪难以自持，他在这场比赛中以第三名完赛，锁定2026年F1车手世界冠军；天空体育F1将从本周五起直播圣保罗大奖赛的各节练习与排位。

    安东内利回看了一段视频集锦，回顾他从卡丁车到成为F1最年轻冠军的历程。

    安东内利表示，在墨西哥城大奖赛最后一圈他“看不清”赛道，因为他在首次成为F1世界冠军之前情绪激动、泪流满面。

    安东内利以第三名完赛，排在分站冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘和法拉利车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿之后，将塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔作为F1最年轻世界冠军的纪录提前了三年多，以20岁68天的年龄锁定总冠军。

    这位意大利车手在车队无线电和赛后多次采访中情绪激动，并透露他在比赛最后阶段就已经哭了。

    “我带着泪水，不知怎么地还能看到两辆法拉利。所以，那非常特别。那一刻，很多回忆涌上心头，从卡丁车一直到这里的全部记忆。”

    “那是一个非常特别的最后一圈，而且毫无疑问，当我冲过终点线时，我彻底释放了情绪，因为我刚刚实现了自己最大的梦想之一。那非常特别。”

    基米·安东内利在墨西哥城大奖赛获得第三名后，成为有史以来最年轻的一级方程式世界冠军。

    安东内利只是第三位赢得F1车手总冠军的意大利车手，也是自1953年阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来的第一位。

    这位梅赛德斯车手在领奖台上被维斯塔潘和汉密尔顿举起，背景中播放着标志性的意大利歌曲《今夜无人入睡》和《Sarà, perché ti amo》，安东内利后来透露，这让他想起了已故的祖父。

    “第一首歌对我来说非常感人，因为那是祖父过去常听的歌，所以听到它非常特别。我想他会非常骄傲，”安东内利在赛后新闻发布会上泪流满面，稍作平复后说道。

    “天哪，我今天哭得太多了，但这是好事。我只是因为我的祖父而非常激动。”

    基米·安东内利在成为有史以来最年轻的一级方程式世界冠军后心情大好。

    安东内利：我会把与维斯塔潘和汉密尔顿同登领奖台的照片挂在家里

    安东内利紧随F1最伟大的两位车手维斯塔潘和汉密尔顿之后完赛，从而成为世界冠军，这多少有些恰如其分。

    安东内利在汉密尔顿于2025年令人震惊地转投法拉利后，接替了后者在梅赛德斯的位置，这位20岁的车手表示，能与两位多次获得世界冠军的车手一同登上领奖台，是一个“不可思议”的时刻。

    “今年早些时候在加拿大也发生过同样的事，但显然这次很特别。我一定会把这张照片装裱起来挂在家里，因为这实在是一个不可思议的时刻，”他说。

    “能看到两位书写了这项运动历史的了不起的车手展现出如此多的尊重，真的很特别，这是一个将永存、将永远被铭记的时刻。所以我一定会把这张照片装裱起来，挂在我家的墙上，这样每次进屋都能看得清清楚楚。”

    前世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘和刘易斯·汉密尔顿在墨西哥将F1最新冠军基米·安东内利扛在肩上

    安东内利从12岁起就是梅赛德斯的一员，自从他在2025年初完成F1首秀以来，他的父亲马尔科·安东内利几乎每场比赛都陪伴在他身边。

    马尔科拥有一支拉力车队，但他在周六上午与基米的妹妹一起飞抵墨西哥，以确保他们能见证基米成为冠军。

    安东内利说：“他们能来这里付出了很大努力，我非常高兴能够一起分享并见证这一刻，因为我的家人为我做出了、并且仍在做出难以置信的巨大牺牲。”

    “尤其是我的父亲，尽管他自己的工作非常繁忙，他今年基本上每场比赛都来了。所以，你知道，拥有这样一位人物一直至关重要。

    “他是那个让我保持脚踏实地的人，尤其是在非常高峰的时刻之后，但同样，他也是在我面临困难局面时、或者在一节练习或比赛前感觉不在最佳状态时始终陪伴在我身边的人。”

    梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫在基米·安东内利成为一级方程式历史上最年轻的冠军后欣喜若狂。

    “然后，当然，梅赛德斯，我怎么感谢他们都不为过，因为首先，他们是在我11岁时冒险押注我的人，他们一直相信我，现在依然如此。”

    “首先，能为这支车队比赛，是一种巨大的荣幸。希望我们在未来几年能取得更多成功。”

    "但同样，能跟博诺（安东内利的比赛工程师彼得·博宁顿）以及所有其他人、所有那些总是来到赛道并竭尽全力的男孩女孩们共事，也是一大荣幸，因为这是一个不可思议的团队，一个非常独特、非常团结的团队。他们每一个人都在实现这一目标的过程中发挥了巨大的作用。"

    Kimi Antonelli becomes F1 world champion: Mercedes driver says he couldn't see through his tears on final lap of Mexico City GP

    Kimi Antonelli has revealed he was overcome with emotion during the closing stages of the Mexico City Grand Prix, which he finished third in to seal the 2026 F1 Drivers' Championship; watch every session of the Sao Paulo Grand Prix from this Friday, live on Sky Sports F1

    Kimi Antonelli watches back a montage of videos from his journey from go-karting, to F1's youngest champion

    Kimi Antonelli says he "couldn't see" through his tears on the final lap of the Mexico City Grand Prix as he was overcome with emotion before becoming Formula 1 world champion for the first time.

    Antonelli finished third behind race-winner Max Verstappen and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton to lower Sebastian Vettel's record of being F1's youngest world champion by over three years, sealing the title at 20 years and 68 days old.

    The Italian was overcome with emotion on team radio and in several post-race interviews, and revealed he was already crying during the closing stages of the race.

    "Already the lap before, I think I lost probably two seconds because I was already crying, basically. I couldn't see clearly ahead," said Antonelli.

    "I could see two Ferraris somehow with the tears. So, it was very special. A lot of memories went through my mind in that moment, all the memories since karting all the way to here.

    "It was a very special final lap, and definitely, when I crossed the line, I let it all out because it was just achieving one of my biggest dreams. It was very special."

    Kimi Antonelli became the youngest ever Formula 1 world champion after finishing third at the Mexico City Grand Prix

    Antonelli is just the third Italian driver to win the F1 Drivers' Championship and the first since Alberto Ascari in 1953.

    The Mercedes driver was lifted up by Verstappen and Hamilton on the podium with iconic Italian songs 'Nessun Dorma' and 'Sarà, perché ti amo' playing in the background, which Antonelli later revealed made him think about his late grandfather.

    "The first song was very emotional for me because it was a song that my grandfather used to listen to always, so it was very special to hear that. I think he would be very proud," said Antonelli as he took a moment to compose himself through the tears in the post-race press conference.

    "Oh my God, I've cried way too much today, but it's good. I just got very emotional because of my grandfather."

    Kimi Antonelli was in buoyant mood after becoming the youngest ever Formula 1 world champion.

    Antonelli: I will put podium picture with Verstappen-Hamilton in my house

    It was somewhat fitting that Antonelli followed home two of F1's greatest drivers in Verstappen and Hamilton to become world champion.

    Antonelli replaced Hamilton at Mercedes following the latter's shock move to Ferrari in 2025 and the 20-year-old says it was an "incredible" moment to share the podium with the two multiple world champions.

    "It happened in Canada as well this year, earlier in the year, but obviously this is special. I will definitely frame the picture and put it in my house because it's just an incredible moment," he said.

    "To see so much respect from two incredible drivers who have done the history of the sport is truly special, and a moment that will live and will always be remembered. So definitely I will frame the picture and put it on the wall of my house so I can see it very well whenever I go in."

    Former World champions Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton lift F1's newest champion, Kimi Antonelli, onto their shoulders in Mexico

    Antonelli has been part of Mercedes since he was 12 years old and his father, Marco Antonelli, has been by his side at nearly every race since he made his F1 debut at the start of 2025.

    Marco has a rallying team but flew into Mexico on Saturday morning with Kimi's sister to ensure they could watch Kimi become champion.

    "It was a big effort for them to come here, and I'm very happy to be able to share and witness this moment altogether because my family has made, and they still do, an incredible amount of sacrifices for me," said Antonelli.

    "Especially my dad, he has come to basically all the races this year, despite being very busy with his own work, his own job. So, you know, that has been crucial, to have such a figure.

    "He was the one keeping me grounded, especially after very high moments, but as well he was the one that was always there when I was facing a difficult situation, or I wasn't feeling the best ahead of a session or ahead of the race.

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff was ecstatic after Kimi Antonelli became the youngest ever champion in Formula 1 history.

    "Then, of course, Mercedes, I couldn't thank them enough because, first of all, they were the ones who took the gamble when I was 11, and they were the ones who believed in me all the way, and they still do.

    "t's been a massive privilege to be able to, first of all, race for this team. Hopefully we'll have much more success in the upcoming years.

    "But as well, it's been a big privilege to work with Bono (Antonelli's race engineer Peter Bonnington) and with all the other guys, all the other boys and girls who always come to the track and do their best, because it's an incredible group, a very unique one and a very united one. And every single one of them has played a massive role in achieving this."
