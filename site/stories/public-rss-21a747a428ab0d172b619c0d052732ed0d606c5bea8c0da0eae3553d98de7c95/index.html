    The Qatar Grand Prix starts at 8 am PT on Sunday, November 29, which is 11 am ET. Formula 1 returns to the Lusail International Circuit for the penultimate race of the 2026 season, with every session live on Apple TV. The full schedule and viewing information are below.

    In Alaska every session starts one hour earlier than in the PT column, and in Hawaii two hours earlier. This is not a sprint weekend.

    What time does the Qatar Grand Prix start?

    The Qatar Grand Prix starts on Sunday, November 29, at 8 am PT, 9 am MT, 10 am CT and 11 am ET. In Alaska it starts at 7 am, and in Hawaii at 6 am.

    The race runs over 57 laps of the 3.367-mile Lusail International Circuit, covering 191.8 miles. Without interruptions it should finish at around 9:30 am PT, 12:30 pm ET.

    Qualifying starts on Saturday, November 28, at 10 am PT, 11 am MT, 12 pm CT and 1 pm ET. The slowest five drivers are eliminated in Q1 and another five in Q2, leaving 10 to fight for pole position in Q3.
