    沃尔夫：拉塞尔目睹安东内利夺冠后情绪崩溃

    梅赛德斯车队经理托托·沃尔夫透露，英国车手乔治·拉塞尔在目睹队友基米·安东内利夺得2026年F1车手总冠军后情绪崩溃，因此车队免除了他参加墨西哥城大奖赛后的媒体活动。

    乔治·拉塞尔：梅赛德斯车队经理托托·沃尔夫表示，英国车手乔治·拉塞尔在目睹队友基米·安东内利夺得2026年F1车手总冠军后情绪崩溃。

    乔治·拉塞尔在墨西哥城大奖赛中排名第六，而他的队友基米·安东内利夺得了车手总冠军；梅赛德斯车队因免除拉塞尔参加赛后媒体活动而受到罚款；托托·沃尔夫表示，拉塞尔在目睹队友夺冠后情绪崩溃。

    乔治·拉塞尔在墨西哥城大奖赛发车时从第二位起降了几个位置。

    托托·沃尔夫透露，梅赛德斯车队免除了乔治·拉塞尔参加墨西哥城大奖赛后的媒体活动，因为英国车手在目睹队友基米·安东内利夺得车手总冠军后情绪崩溃。

    拉塞尔开始赛季时是明显的夺冠热门，但被安东内利全面超越，安东内利在周日的比赛中获得第三名，成为有史以来最年轻的20岁F1世界冠军，尽管还有四轮比赛尚未进行。

    拉塞尔从第二位发车，但由于无法控制的技术问题导致的糟糕起步，他在第一圈就跌至第六位，在此期间，他被判定与红牛的伊萨克·哈吉亚发生碰撞，这将使他获得10秒的罚时。

    “我在FIA称重台上看到了乔治赛后，很明显他对这种情况感到非常沮丧，”沃尔夫说。

    “去看基米真是太棒了，展现了运动精神。”

    “所以我此刻非常支持他。我曾经处于非常、非常难以应对的情况中。”

    George Russell: Mercedes boss Toto Wolff says British driver was 'distraught' after watching team-mate Kimi Antonelli seal 2026 F1 title

    George Russell finished sixth in the Mexico City GP as his Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli sealed the Drivers' Championship; Mercedes were fined after excusing Russell from carrying out his post-race media duties; Toto Wolff says Russell was "distraught" after the "catastrophic moment"

    George Russell dropped several places from second on the grid at the start of the Mexico City Grand Prix.

    Toto Wolff has revealed that Mercedes excused George Russell from carrying out his post Mexico City Grand Prix media duties because the British driver was "distraught" after seeing his team-mate Kimi Antonelli clinch the Drivers' Championship.

    Russell began the season as the clear title favourite but has been comprehensively outperformed by Antonelli, who finished third in Sunday's race to become Formula 1's youngest ever world champion at the age of 20, with four rounds still remaining.

    Russell started second on the grid, but a poor getaway - caused by a technical issue out of his control - saw him drop back to sixth on the opening lap, during which he was adjudged to have caused a collision with Red Bull's Isack Hadjar that would earn him a 10-second time penalty.

    Mercedes initially announced Russell wouldn't be doing any post-race interviews without providing a reason, but Wolff later explained the situation.

    "I saw George after the race on the FIA scales, and obviously he's distraught about the situation," Wolff said.

    "It was a great show of sportsmanship to go and see Kimi.

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff was ecstatic after Kimi Antonelli became the youngest ever champion in Formula One history.

    "I have known George for such a long time. So, I saw him and thought, for a racing driver, this is a catastrophic moment.

    "And maybe today the race was a little bit a picture of his season. This was a race for him to win. We had the pace in the car. And we let him down on the first straight. The energy delivery is not what we thought it should be.

    "So, him not doing the media work, or leaving the track, is absolutely understandable. Him staying would have actually surprised me."
