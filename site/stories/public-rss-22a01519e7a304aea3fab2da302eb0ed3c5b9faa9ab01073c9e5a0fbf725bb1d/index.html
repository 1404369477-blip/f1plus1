    麦凯轮赛车首席执行官扎克·布朗描述了他2016年加入的团队为一个“非常有毒的环境”，并指责领导层的频繁变动导致了多年赛道内外的不良表现。

    麦凯轮赛车首席执行官Zak Brown将2016年加入的团队描述为“一个非常恶劣的环境”，并将多年来的赛道内外不佳成绩归咎于领导层的频繁更迭。

    这位美国人十年前加入迈凯轮车队，当时车队股东与罗恩·邓恩分道扬镳，之后他带领车队从车队末尾的排名恢复到2024年和2025年的车队制造商锦标赛。

    在接受RACER采访时，布朗表示，当他最初走进车队时，并没有意识到问题的严重性。

    “那是一个非常有毒的环境。问题始于领导层的真正不协调以及领导力的缺乏。在六年的时间里，你有了五位不同的老板，”布朗说。

    “这在任何商业中都是灾难的配方。如果你在领导层有旋转门，那么团队不知道该跟随谁。这会滋生信任的缺失，并在组织中产生毒性。”

    他补充说，对车队的影响是显而易见的。

    每个人都处于保护自己、指责他人的模式。这是一个糟糕的环境。这种糟糕的环境体现在糟糕的赛道成绩、缺乏赞助、缺乏粉丝参与和非常不满意的员工队伍。

    布朗承认他最初相信车队自己的解释，即其发动机合作伙伴是主要问题。当沃金团队将本田换成雷诺后，从2017年的车队 constructors' championship 第9位跃升至2018年的第6位，但他表示，这仅仅表明问题有多深。

    “我知道在我们当时拥有的1,200人中，我们可能只需要修复其中大约20人。在我心中，毫无疑问，我们有1,100人在那里赢得了许多比赛和冠军。我们需要领导团队的重置，”他说。

    在2024年和2025年，麦凯轮车队赢得了车队冠军，兰多·诺里斯在2025年赢得了车手冠军之后，本赛季对布朗领导的车队来说更加艰难。麦凯轮车队在车队积分榜上排名第四，落后红牛17分，尽管诺里斯赢得了三场比赛。

    “我们在2024年和2025年赢得了比赛，今年也赢得了比赛，但这并不赋予我们在2027年任何东西。我们必须赢得它，”布朗说。

    Zak Brown recalls McLaren's "very toxic environment" after joining team in 2016

    The McLaren CEO says most of the team's 1,200 staff were not the problem

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    McLaren Racing CEO Zak Brown has described the team he joined in 2016 as "a very toxic environment", blaming a revolving door of leaders for years of poor results on and off the track.

    The American arrived at McLaren a decade ago, after the team's shareholders parted ways with Ron Dennis, and went on to lead its recovery from the back of the grid to the 2024 and 2025 constructors' championships.

    Speaking to RACER, Brown said he had not realised the scale of the problems when he first walked in.

    "It was a very toxic environment. It started with a real lack of alignment of the leadership as well as a lack of leadership. You had five different bosses in six years," Brown said.

    "That's a recipe for disaster in any business. If you have a revolving door at the leadership level, then the team don't know who to follow. That breeds a lack of trust and breeds toxicity inside an organisation."

    He added that the effect on the team was clear to see.

    "Everyone was in cover-their-ass mode and blame mode. It was a terrible environment. That manifested itself in terrible on-track results, lack of sponsorship, lack of fan engagement and a very unsatisfied workforce."

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    Brown admitted he had initially believed the team's own explanation that its engine partner was the main problem. When the Woking outfit replaced Honda with Renault, it moved from ninth in the constructors' championship in 2017 to sixth in 2018, but he said that only showed how much deeper the issues ran.

    "I knew out of the 1,200 people that we had at the time that we probably only needed to fix about 20 of them. There was no doubt in my mind that we had 1,100 people there who had won a lot of races and championships. It needed the leadership team reset," he said.

    After McLaren won the constructors' title in both 2024 and 2025 and Lando Norris secured the drivers' championship in 2025, this season has been tougher for the Brown-led team. McLaren is fourth in the constructors' standings, 17 points behind Red Bull, although Norris has won three races.

    "The fact that we won in 2024 and 2025 and that we have won races this year, that doesn't entitle us to anything in 2027. We have got to earn it," Brown said.
