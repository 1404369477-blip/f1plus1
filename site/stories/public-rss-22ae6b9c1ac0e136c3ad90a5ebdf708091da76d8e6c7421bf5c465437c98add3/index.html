    Frederic Vasseur: Ferrari state 'full confidence' in F1 team principal after Christian Horner comments and speculation

    "Ferrari has made its choices and has full confidence in the leadership of Fred Vasseur and his team and we will continue to do so"; watch Formula 1's return to Malaysia live on Sky Sports from Friday morning F1 as Sepang hosts the Bahrain Grand Prix

    Ferrari have reiterated their "full confidence" in Frederic Vasseur as team principal after speculation around his future was fuelled by Christian Horner suggesting interest in the role, with the Italian marque making clear it "has made its choices" around who leads the F1 team.

    Vasseur admitted after last Saturday's Azerbaijan Grand Prix that the team were "not working in perfect serenity" amid persistent and "frustrating" rumours around his position at the Scuderia.

    That was amplified last week by an interview with Horner published in The Times in which the former Red Bull team principal said Ferrari were the only outfit he would consider returning to the pit-wall for without the ownership stake that he has been prioritising for any comeback to the sport.

    In the unusual step of a hastily arranged media briefing on Tuesday morning ahead of this weekend's Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia, a Ferrari spokeswoman made clear the company's senior leadership had full confidence in Vasseur to continue running their F1 operation.

    "Ferrari's position is clear and unchanged," said the spokesperson.

    "Ferrari has made its choices and has full confidence in the leadership of Fred Vasseur and his team and we will continue to do so.

    "Ferrari has always attracted interest, it's a reflection of what Ferrari represents. We say this today because we want to avoid gossip and speculation in Malaysia and focus on the weekend."

    The spokesperson said was responding to "not only the piece with Mr Horner in The Times but subsequent stories that have been increasing, not dying down".

    Vasseur is in his fourth season in charge since joining from Sauber from the start of the 2023 season and signed a multi-year contract extension last year.

    Asked if Vasseur retained the full support of Ferrari chair John Elkann and chief executive Benedetto Vigna and would stay in post for the remainder of his contract, the spokesperson reiterated: "Ferrari's position is clear and unchanged.

    "Ferrari has made its choices, it has confidence in Fred Vasseur and we continue to do so."
