    沃尔夫解释拉塞尔为何无缘拉斯维加斯新涡轮

    梅赛德斯在巴西已锁定2026年车队和车手世界冠军，来到拉斯维加斯站既要测试2027年研发部件，也要为拉塞尔争取车手积分榜第二名。沃尔夫解释了拉塞尔为何不会在拉斯维加斯获得车队新的ADUO涡轮升级。

    沃尔夫解释拉塞尔为何无缘梅赛德斯在拉斯维加斯的新涡轮

    梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫解释了拉塞尔为何不会在拉斯维加斯大奖赛获得车队新的ADUO涡轮升级

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    梅赛德斯在巴西已正式锁定2026年车队和车手两项世界冠军，来到拉斯维加斯大奖赛时肩负双重任务：测试面向2027年的研发部件，并为拉塞尔锁定车手积分榜第二名。

    为了实现第一个目标，银箭车队终于推出了在F1的额外开发与升级机会框架下开发的新涡轮规格。但只有一半的车库能用到它。

    在车队的官方拉斯维加斯前瞻中，领队托托·沃尔夫解释了拒绝给拉塞尔升级的决定，确认新涡轮根本不值得为此承受发车格罚退。

    “从本周末起，我们将拥有符合ADUO的涡轮规格，”沃尔夫表示。“这主要是为了在比赛周末条件下验证开发成果，预期的性能提升相对较小。

    “我们预计让基米使用它，而对于乔治，我们的优先事项是保护他的发车位置，性能提升可能太有限，不足以证明接受发车格罚退是合理的，”沃尔夫补充道。

    安东内利可以在不受罚的情况下测试新的涡轮，因为他仍在赛季配额之内，而拉塞尔已经超出了他的限额。

    在拉斯维加斯故意接受发车位罚退，对拉塞尔的争冠形势可能是灾难性的。他目前以262分位列积分榜第三，仅落后红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘17分，同时还要守住对法拉利车队刘易斯·汉密尔顿仅一分的微弱优势。

    “赢得两个世界冠军是一项了不起的成就，我们也花时间进行了庆祝，”沃尔夫说。“我们的目标很明确：帮助乔治在车手锦标赛中尽可能取得最高名次，带着势头结束这一年，并继续从赛车中挖掘更多性能。”

    尽管拉塞尔错过了新硬件，梅赛德斯仍对它在1.9公里长的拉斯维加斯大道赛道上的整体速度感到乐观。

    “因此，拉斯维加斯是下一个很好的挑战，”沃尔夫指出。“它很快就确立了自己作为一级方程式赛车最大赛事之一的地位……以拉斯维加斯大道为背景，赛历上再也没有其他能与之媲美。我们知道在那里获胜是什么感觉，我们当然想再次体验那种感觉。”

    在内华达州的高速、严寒条件之后，围场将以中东背靠背赛事结束本赛季。沃尔夫赞扬了国际汽联（FIA）应对最后阶段后勤障碍的工作，并指出车队“很兴奋能在卡塔尔和阿布扎比的车迷面前为这一年收官”。

    Toto Wolff explains why George Russell won't get Mercedes’ new turbo in Las Vegas

    Mercedes boss Toto Wolff explains why George Russell won't receive the team's new ADUO turbo upgrade at the Las Vegas GP

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Having officially wrapped up both the 2026 constructors' and drivers' world championships in Brazil, Mercedes arrives at the Las Vegas Grand Prix with a dual mandate: test development parts for 2027 and secure the runner-up spot in the drivers' standings for George Russell.

    To accomplish the first goal, the Silver Arrows are finally introducing a new turbo specification developed under Formula 1's ADUO (Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities) framework. But only half the garage will get to use it.

    In the team's official Las Vegas preview, team principal Toto Wolff explained the decision to deny Russell the upgrade, confirming that the new turbo simply is not worth the grid penalty it would incur.

    "We will have the ADUO-enabled turbo specification available from this weekend," Wolff stated. "This is primarily about proving out the development in race-weekend conditions, with the expected performance benefit relatively small.

    "We anticipate running it with Kimi, while with George our priority is protecting his grid position, with the performance benefit likely too limited to justify taking a grid penalty," Wolff added.

    While Antonelli can test the new turbo without a penalty because he remains within his season allocation, Russell has already exceeded his limit.

    Taking an intentional grid drop in Las Vegas could be catastrophic for Russell's championship positioning. He currently sits third in the standings on 262 points, trailing Red Bull's Max Verstappen by just 17 points while defending a razor-thin one-point advantage over Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton.

    "Winning both world championships was an incredible achievement, and we have taken the time to celebrate that," Wolff said. "Our targets are clear: helping George finish as high as possible in the drivers’ championship, ending the year with momentum, and continuing to unlock more performance from the car."

    Despite Russell missing out on the new hardware, Mercedes is optimistic about its overall pace on the 1.9-kilometre Las Vegas Strip.

    "Las Vegas is therefore a good next challenge," Wolff noted. "It has established itself remarkably quickly as one of Formula 1's biggest events... With the Strip as the backdrop, there is nothing else quite like it on the calendar. We know what it feels like to win there and we would certainly like to experience that again."

    Following the high-speed, freezing conditions of Nevada, the paddock will conclude the season with a Middle Eastern double-header. Wolff praised the FIA for dealing with the logistical hurdles of the final stretch, noting the team is "excited to finish the year" in front of the fans in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
