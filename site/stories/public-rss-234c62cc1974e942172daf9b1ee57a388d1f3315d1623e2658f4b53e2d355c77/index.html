    法拉利预计在美国大奖赛推出重大升级套件

    据Motorsport.com了解，法拉利预计将在美国大奖赛推出新底板，作为2026赛季第四套升级套件的一部分。

    法拉利预计将在美国大奖赛推出新底板，作为2026赛季第四套升级套件的一部分

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利，兰多·诺里斯，迈凯伦

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地方的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    据Motorsport.com了解，法拉利很可能在美国大奖赛上引入一款新底板，作为其2026赛季最后一次重大升级的一部分。

    尽管马拉内罗车队已将重心转向2027年，但预计车队将在下周于德克萨斯州奥斯汀的美洲赛道举行的比赛中带来新底板。

    新底板的目标是在产生相同或更多下压力的同时，让车队能够采用稍软一些的调校，从而拓宽赛车的操作窗口。修改后的侧箱和车身也有望很快推出，但它们可能会在10月30日至11月1日墨西哥大奖赛的SF-26上亮相。

    法拉利目前以444分位居车队积分榜第二，落后领跑积分榜的梅赛德斯578分。迈凯伦以339分排名第三，红牛以332分排名第四。

    但在红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘统治了新加坡大奖赛周末——包揽冲刺赛和正赛的杆位并双双夺冠——之后，法拉利车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔讨论了这支米尔顿凯恩斯车队如今是否已成为众矢之的。

    法拉利车队的夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利车队的刘易斯·汉密尔顿

    “不确定，”这位法国人告诉 Motorsport.com 及其他媒体。“老实说，他们表现得非常好，但他们并不是众矢之的。如果你看看迈凯伦和梅赛德斯，如果你回顾赛季初，梅赛德斯曾一路飞驰，然后我们赢了两场比赛，接着迈凯伦赢了两场，然后红牛赢了两三场。

    “我认为局势在不断变化，因为经常会有四支车队，我觉得昨天就有四支车队在不到两场比赛的差距之内。

    这意味着如果下周有人带来升级，排名可能会发生变化。但最重要的是专注于我们自己，而不是去想别人。

    美国大奖赛将于10月23日至25日在美洲赛道举行。

    Ferrari expected to introduce major upgrade package at United States GP

    Ferrari is expected to introduce a new floor at the United States Grand Prix as part of its fourth 2026 upgrade package

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Lando Norris, McLaren

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari will likely introduce a new floor at the United States Grand Prix as part of its final major upgrade of the 2026 season, Motorsport.com understands.

    While the Maranello outfit has already shifted its focus to 2027, the team is expected to bring a new floor to the race at the Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas, next week.

    The aim of the new floor is to produce the same or more downforce while letting the team run a slightly less stiff set-up, widening the car's operating window. Revised sidepods and bodywork are also expected imminently, but they might appear on the SF-26 at the Mexican Grand Prix on 30 October - 1 November.

    Ferrari currently sits second in the constructors' standings with 444 points, behind championship leader Mercedes with 578 points. McLaren is third with 339 points and Red Bull is fourth with 332 points.

    But after Red Bull's Max Verstappen dominated the Singapore Grand Prix weekend, claiming pole position for both the sprint and the main race, and winning both, Ferrari team chief Fred Vasseur discussed whether the Milton Keynes outfit was now the team to beat.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton Ferrari

    "Not sure," the Frenchman told Motorsport.com and other media. "Honestly, they are doing very well, but it's not the team to beat. If you look at the McLaren and Mercedes, if you have a look from the beginning of the season, Mercedes was flying, then we won two races, then McLaren won two races, then Red Bull won two or three.

    "I think it's changing, because quite often you have four teams, I think yesterday you had four teams in less than two races.

    "It means that if someone is bringing upgrades next week, the classification can change. But the most important thing is to be focused on ourselves, not to think about the others."

    The United States Grand Prix will take place from 23-25 October at the Circuit of the Americas.
