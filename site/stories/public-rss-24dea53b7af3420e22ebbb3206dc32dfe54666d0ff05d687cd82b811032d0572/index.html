    红牛举办极限冰上赛 F1赛车对决MotoGP摩托

    红牛在威斯康星州伊格尔里弗的冰冻湖面赛道上举办首届Ultimate Winter冰上赛，F1赛车、MotoGP摩托、拉力赛车、Pro 4卡车和雪地摩托同场竞技，前MotoGP车手丹尼·佩德罗萨驾驶KTM MotoGP摩托夺冠。

    红牛F1赛车在极限冰上赛中迎战MotoGP摩托、拉力赛车和雪地摩托

    五台机器均使用定制钉胎，并在冰冻赛道上走各自独立的行车线

    塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔，红牛车队小红牛7雷诺

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    红牛让一辆一级方程式赛车、一辆MotoGP摩托车、一辆拉力赛车、一辆Pro 4卡车和一辆雪地摩托在首届终极冬季赛中相互对决。

    前MotoGP车手丹尼·佩德罗萨驾驶KTM MotoGP摩托车在定制的冰湖赛道上获胜。

    这场在威斯康星州伊格尔河举行的活动将五个赛车项目汇聚在同一发车阵容中。每台机器都使用定制的钉胎，红牛为每辆车设计了不同的赛车线路，让五者都有平等的获胜机会。

    前F1车手帕特里克·弗里萨赫驾驶的是小红牛7，这辆赛车曾由四届冠军塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔驾驶夺得2011年世界冠军。他与佩德罗萨、驾驶斯巴鲁WRX的拉力车手兼前F1车手斯科特·斯皮德、Pro 4卡车车手安德鲁·卡尔森以及雪地摩托车手贡纳尔·斯特恩同场竞技。

    “那是小红牛7，塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔的一辆世界冠军赛车。2011年赢得了总冠军，”弗里萨赫在红牛发布到YouTube的视频中说道。“是的，它被抬高了一点。其余部分有更多冷却，但就温度而言，我们是安全的。天气很冷，零下15度。”

    弗里萨赫驾驶这辆F1赛车跑出了五辆车中最快的校准圈，1分11秒。斯特恩的雪地摩托和佩德罗萨的摩托车都跑出了1分12秒，而卡尔森的卡车和斯皮德的拉力赛车各自以1分13秒完成了他们的圈。

    佩德罗萨的摩托车搭载了全场最大的防滑钉，并做了一系列改动以应对这些条件。

    “前轮有207个防滑钉，后轮有300个，”这位西班牙人解释道。“我们修改了行程悬挂使其更短。我们用的是钢制刹车盘，不是碳纤维的，然后我们还必须修改发动机映射，以便在这些寒冷的条件下比赛。”

    由于校准圈之间相隔约两秒，五辆车排成一列，准备进行决定性的比赛。斯特恩的雪地摩托在早期就拉开了距离，但它需要完成最长的一圈，佩德罗萨逐渐追了上来，并在两车通过最后一个弯道时取得领先，赢得了胜利。

    雪地摩托获得第二名，F1赛车第三名，Pro 4卡车第四名，拉力赛车第五名。

    Red Bull F1 car takes on MotoGP bike, rally car and snowmobile in extreme ice race

    All five machines used custom studded tyres and separate racing lines on the frozen course

    Sebastian Vettel, Red Bull Racing RB7 Renault

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Red Bull pitted a Formula 1 car, MotoGP bike, rally car, Pro 4 truck and a snowmobile against each other in the first Ultimate Winter race.

    Former MotoGP rider Dani Pedrosa won, riding a KTM MotoGP bike on the custom frozen lake course.

    The event, held in Eagle River, Wisconsin, brought five motorsport disciplines together on the same grid. Each machine ran on custom studded tyres, and Red Bull laid out a different racing line for each vehicle to give all five an equal shot at victory.

    Former F1 driver Patrick Friesacher was behind the wheel of the RB7, the machinery that four-time champion Sebastian Vettel drove to the 2011 world title. He lined up against Pedrosa, rally driver and ex-F1 racer Scott Speed in a Subaru WRX, Pro 4 truck racer Andrew Carlson and snowmobile racer Gunnar Sterne.

    "That's the RB7, a world championship-winning car of Sebastian Vettel. Won the championship in 2011," Friesacher said in the video, released by Red Bull on YouTube. "Yeah, it's lifted a little bit higher. All the rest has more cooling, but temperature-wise, we are safe. It's pretty cold, minus 15 degrees."

    Friesacher set the fastest calibration lap of the five, a 1 minute 11 seconds, in the F1 car. Sterne's snowmobile and Pedrosa's bike both posted a 1m12s, while Carlson's truck and Speed's rally car each completed their laps in 1m13s.

    Pedrosa's bike carried the biggest studs in the field, with a series of changes made to cope with the conditions.

    "It's 207 spikes on the front and 300 at the rear," the Spaniard explained. "We modified the travel suspension to make it shorter. We have a steel disc, not carbon, and then we had to modify as well the mapping engine in order to race in these cold conditions."

    With the calibration laps separated by around two seconds, all five lined up for the deciding race. Sterne's snowmobile pulled clear early on, but it had the longest lap to complete, and Pedrosa reeled it in to take the win as the pair went through the final corner.

    The snowmobile finished second, the F1 car third, Pro 4 Truck fourth and rally car fifth.
