    梅基斯回击霍纳称红牛失去魔力说法

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯驳斥了克里斯蒂安·霍纳关于车队在他离任后“失去魔力”的说法。

    红牛F1车队负责人洛朗·梅基斯回击克里斯蒂安·霍纳“失去魔力”的说法

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯驳斥了克里斯蒂安·霍纳关于车队“失去魔力”的说法

    洛朗·梅基斯，红牛车队领队

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    红牛车队首席执行官兼领队洛朗·梅基斯驳斥了克里斯蒂安·霍纳的说法，即这支位于米尔顿凯恩斯的车队自他离任以来已经“失去了魔力”，并坚称正是车队的精神推动了一次“非常了不起”的逆转。

    霍纳在执掌车队二十年后于2025年7月离开，他一直在宣传自己的回忆录《Drive》，该书于上周出版。在接受《泰晤士报》采访时，这位英国人声称，自他离开后，车队已经“失去了魔力、失去了存在感、失去了氛围”。

    在周五于墨西哥城举行的领队新闻发布会上被问及这些言论时，梅基斯表示：“听着，首先，克里斯蒂安有权发表自己的看法。他显然在这支车队度过了很长时间，我尊重他的观点。

    “我们对车队氛围的感受显然截然不同。过去几个月里我们一直享有的一个强项，就是那种不可思议的团队精神，它让我们从一个客观上非常艰难的时刻中走了出来。

    "当你赢得比赛时，保持积极的精神总是容易的；但当你面临压力时，团队成员之间仍能保持同样的战斗精神和团结，那就是另一回事了。今年上半年我们确实面临了挑战，无论是底盘方面还是动力单元方面。

    "团队团结一致的方式，团队让米尔顿凯恩斯部门全力以赴试图反击的方式，以及他们做到这一点的方式，这本身就是一件极具挑战性的事情，从我们任何一位团队成员到车手们直言不讳的评论都是如此。这就是红牛精神，这也正是我们觉得需要去比赛的方式。

    "伙计们得到了他们应得的回报，因为他们所实现的逆转非常了不起，但最终我们很清楚，我们的成败取决于成绩。

    "目标不是赢一两场比赛；目标是重返世界冠军，直到我们达到那里之前，没有人会觉得我们取得了任何成就。"

    红牛目前位列车队积分榜第三，与第四名迈凯伦同分。

    Red Bull F1 boss Laurent Mekies hits back at Christian Horner's "lost its mojo" claim

    Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies has dismissed Christian Horner's claim that the team has "lost its mojo"

    Laurent Mekies, Red Bull Racing Team Principal

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Red Bull Racing CEO and team principal Laurent Mekies has rejected Christian Horner's claim that the Milton Keynes outfit has "lost its mojo" since his exit, insisting its spirit has driven a "very remarkable" turnaround.

    Horner, who left the team in July 2025 after two decades in charge, has been promoting his memoir Drive, which was published last week. During an interview with The Times, the Briton claimed the team had "lost its mojo, its presence, its vibe" since he left.

    Asked about the comments in Friday's team principals' press conference in Mexico City, Mekies said: "Look, first of all, Christian is entitled to his opinion. He obviously has spent a lot of time in this team and I respect his point of view.

    "We certainly feel extremely differently about the vibe in the team. One of the strong points we have been enjoying certainly in the last few months is the incredible team spirit that has allowed us to come back from an objectively very difficult moment.

    "It's always one thing to have a positive spirit when you are winning races, it's something else to have that same fighting spirit and unity between the team members when you are put under pressure, and we certainly had a challenging first part of the year, on the chassis side and the PU side.

    "The way the team stuck together, the way the team set the Milton Keynes department on fire to try to come back, and the way they have done that, it's such a challenging matter, from any of our team members to the brutally honest comments of our drivers. That's the Red Bull spirit and that's certainly how we feel we need to go racing.

    "The guys got the reward they deserved with the turnaround that they have produced, which is very remarkable, but ultimately we are well aware that we live and die with our results.

    "The target is not to win one race or two races; the target is to be back to world titles, and until we are there, nobody will feel we have achieved anything."

    Red Bull currently sits third in the constructors' standings, level on points with fourth-placed McLaren.
