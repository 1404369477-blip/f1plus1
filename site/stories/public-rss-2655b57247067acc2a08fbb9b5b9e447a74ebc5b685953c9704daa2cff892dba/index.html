    LN4 Fusion已收购三级方程式车队AIX 比赛的部分资产，将于明年参加该锦标赛，并正在办理相关申请程序，以在未来参加二级方程式比赛。

    "我非常兴奋。这是我参与过的最重要的事情之一，而且不仅仅是局限于我自己的F1领域之内。"诺里斯在本周末的意大利大奖赛之前告诉天空体育 F1。

    “这是我自己参与联合创办的车队。所以我将在所有初级系列赛中拥有自己的车队。”

    “显然，我们希望它成为最好的。我们希望它成为领先的单座方程式车队。但能够参与一个全新的事业、去创造一些东西，而且对我来说还能在这么长的一段时间里参与其中，这真的非常酷。”

    除了LN4 Fusion之外，Norris还将启动LN4 Legacy计划——这是一个独立的、资金充足的青训车手发展计划，由ADD Management运营，并由经验丰富的项目总监Julian Rouse领导。

    该计划旨在发现和支持那些没有足够资金在赛车运动阶梯中晋升的未来人才。

    诺里斯说：“这么做的首要目标其实很简单，就是帮助那些有天赋、有能力进入F1，但可能在经济上没有能力的年轻车手，并在其他各个方面帮助他们实现最终目标。”

    “基本上，这是我个人尝试回馈的一种方式，再次借助我的车队、我身边的人以及我的合作伙伴，给其他人、给年轻车手一个机会。这就是我更具个人色彩的一面，这也挺酷的。”

    兰多·诺里斯与天空体育的罗布·琼斯坐下畅谈，讨论他与迈凯伦续签多年合同的事宜

    LN4 Fusion 的欧洲主要总部将设在意大利的威尼托大区，那里正在新建一座设施，同时在剑桥郡设有一处英国运营基地。

    诺里斯补充道：“我成长过程中非常幸运，我爸爸能够一路支持我直到二级方程式（Formula 2）。

    “但我知道，外面还有很多车手，他们其实有能力进入一级方程式（Formula 1），却没有资金支持，也不知道该如何走完整个流程，这正是车队要发挥作用的另一个方面。”

    “这就是我的方式——我们要说的是数十万，实际上说的是数百万——我要把我的整个体系、我的支持、我的知识整合起来，努力让尽可能多的其他人，尤其是某些车手，也有机会实现他们的目标。”

    Lando Norris: F1 world champion launches new junior single-seater team LN4 Fusion ahead of 2027 racing debut

    Reigning F1 world champion Lando Norris has launched and co-founded LN4 Fusion - a new single-seater racing team; watch Friday's practice sessions for the Italian GP at 11.30am and 3pm, live on Sky Sports F1

    Speaking on The Fast And The Curious podcast, Betty Glover, Christian Hewgill and Greg James discuss whether Lando Norris is capable of winning the 2026 World Championship

    Lando Norris has launched a new junior single-seater team, LN4 Fusion, which will race in multiple championships in 2027.

    LN4 Fusion has acquired selected assets of Formula 3 team AIX Racing to race in the championship next year and is undertaking the relevant application procedure to compete in Formula 2 in the future.

    The group, developed by a high-profile investment group led by Dan Richards, will also have teams in Italian F4 and Formula Regional in 2027 with plans to enter British F4 in the future

    "I'm very excited. This is one of my biggest things I've ever been part of that's not just within my own stuff of Formula 1. Norris told Sky Sports F1 ahead of this weekend's Italian Grand Prix.

    "[It's] my very own race team that I co-founded. So I'll be having my own race team in all the junior series.

    "Obviously, we want it to be the best. We want it to be the leading single-seater race team. But it's just very cool to be part of something new, create something, and for me to be part of something for such a long period of time as well."

    Alongside LN4 Fusion, Norris is also launching the LN4 Legacy Programme - an independent, fully funded driver development programme, operated by ADD Management and led by experienced programme director Julian Rouse.

    The programme aims to identify and support future talent for those who do not have the finances to move up the motorsport ladder.

    "The main objective of this is simply to help younger drivers who have talent and have the ability to get to Formula 1, but probably not financially [the ability], and help them in all those other areas reach their end goal," said Norris.

    "Basically my little way of trying to give back, again, using my team, using the people that I'm with, my partners, and give other people, give young drivers a chance. So that's my more personal side of things, which is pretty cool too."

    LN4 Fusion's principal European headquarters will be based in Veneto in Italy where a new facility is being built with a British operation in Cambridgeshire.

    Norris added: "I was very lucky growing up that my dad could support me all the way through to Formula 2.

    "But I know that there are a lot of other drivers out there who probably can get to Formula 1, but don't have the financial backing or the ability to know how to go through the whole programme, which is where the team comes into place too.

    "This is my way, and we're talking hundreds of thousands, we're talking millions, in fact, of how I can bring my whole infrastructure together, my support, my knowledge, and try to give as many other people and certain other drivers opportunities to reach their goal too."
