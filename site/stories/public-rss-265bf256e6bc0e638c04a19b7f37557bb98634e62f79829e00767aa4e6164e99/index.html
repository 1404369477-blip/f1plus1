    梅赛德斯-奔驰和奥迪发布F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛adidas系列

    梅赛德斯-奔驰和奥迪推出了即将到来的F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛的特别adidas粉丝系列。梅赛德斯-奔驰系列名为“霓虹灯”，奥迪系列名为“深夜”。这些系列现在已在adidas官网上市，价格从30美元到200美元不等。

    梅赛德斯-奔驰和奥迪在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛上推出阿迪达斯系列

    梅赛德斯-奔驰霓虹灯和奥迪夜场系列现已通过adidas.com购买

    拉斯维加斯欢迎标志上空出现彩虹

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    梅赛德斯-奔驰和奥迪为即将到来的Formula 1拉斯维加斯大奖赛推出了特别的阿迪达斯粉丝系列。

    梅赛德斯-奔驰的系列名为"霓虹灯"，而奥迪的系列名为"深夜"。这两个系列现在已在adidas.com上上市，品牌美国店内的单品价格从30美元到200美元不等。

    最贵的产品是外套。梅赛德斯-奔驰的拉斯维加斯夹克和奥迪的拉斯维加斯轰炸机夹克都售价200美元。

    梅赛德斯-奔驰的系列比奥迪的大。除了夹克，它还包括120美元的卫衣、95美元的长袖T恤、60美元的短裤、55美元的车手帽、50美元的比赛帽和40美元及45美元的T恤。

    奥迪车队的阵容有55美元的车手上限，两件各45美元的图形T恤和一件30美元的包。

    两支车队抵达拉斯维加斯时的位置截然不同。梅赛德斯在巴西大奖赛上锁定了2026年 constructors' championship。奥迪在接管萨博后作为一支工厂车队的第一赛季，目前排名第八，积28分。这家德国车队落后哈斯三分。

    梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛中赢得了车手冠军，成为F1历史上最年轻的冠军。他的队友乔治·拉塞尔目前正在争夺亚军，目前排名第三，落后红牛的马克斯·维斯塔潘。

    奥迪的加布里埃尔·博尔特洛托和尼科·胡肯伯格目前在车手积分榜上分别排名第14和第15，两人都积14分。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛将于11月19日至21日举行，是赛季最后三轮比赛中的第一轮，紧接着是卡塔尔和阿布扎比。

    Mercedes and Audi unveil adidas collections for F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix

    The Mercedes Neon Lights and Audi After Hours collections are available now through adidas.com

    A rainbow above the Welcome to Las Vegas sign

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Mercedes and Audi have unveiled special adidas fan collections for the upcoming Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Mercedes' range is called "Neon Lights", while Audi's is named "After Hours". Both collections are available now through adidas.com, and the pieces listed on the brand's US store range in price from $30 to $200.

    The most expensive items are the outerwear. Mercedes' Las Vegas jacket and Audi's Las Vegas bomber jacket both cost $200.

    The Mercedes collection is the bigger of the two. Alongside the jacket, it includes a $120 hoodie, a $95 long-sleeve T-shirt, $60 shorts, a $55 driver cap, a $50 race cap and T-shirts at $40 and $45.

    Audi's line-up has a $55 driver cap, two graphic T-shirts at $45 each and a $30 bag.

    The two teams arrive in Las Vegas in very different positions. Mercedes secured the 2026 constructors' championship at the Brazilian Grand Prix. Audi, in its first season as a works team after taking over Sauber, currently sits eighth in the standings with 28 points. The German outfit is three points behind Haas.

    Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli claimed the drivers' title at the Mexican Grand Prix, becoming the youngest champion in the history of F1. His team-mate George Russell is currently in the fight for second in the championship, sitting third behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

    Audi's Gabriel Bortoleto and Nico Hulkenberg currently sit 14th and 15th in the drivers' standings, both on 14 points.

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix takes place from 19 to 21 November and is the first of the final three rounds of the season, ahead of Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
