    贝尔曼终于确认2027年加盟哈斯车队

    奥利·贝尔曼与哈斯车队的2027赛季续约终于尘埃落定。作为一名法拉利的青训车手，贝尔曼将在下赛季为哈斯车队出战第三个完整赛季。

    Ollie 贝尔曼在2027赛季F1中留在哈斯车队的事情终于得到了确认，经过一段漫长的续约过程。

    贝尔曼是法拉利的青训车队成员，明年他将代表哈斯参加第三个完整的赛季。

    他在2024年首次亮相F1，作为沙特阿拉伯站时为卡洛斯·塞恩斯顶替的法拉利车手，然后在同赛季晚些时候为哈斯参加了两次比赛。

    贝尔曼在哈斯的续约一直被视为板上钉钉的事情，因为哈斯拥有他第三个赛季的合同选项，在宣布他的续约时，车队负责人小川豊雄将其称为‘对我们来说是一个自然的决定’。

    尽管如此，鉴于协议的三方性质，贝尔曼的情况出人意料地复杂——法拉利对他的整体、长期F1未来至关重要，而哈斯车队在短期内雇佣了贝尔曼。

    不同来源对延误的原因意见不一，有传言称贝尔曼的团队无法与哈斯车队就第三年的财务安排达成满意协议。

    但也有人认为，与法拉利的谈判也很棘手，因为法拉利已经与查尔斯·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿签订了下一年的合同，这阻碍了贝尔曼为他的长期赞助商参赛的可能性。

    有一种说法是，虽然贝尔曼本人对2027年感到放松，并不是续约的障碍，但由于法拉利无法保证他所需的那种灵活性，因此谈判已经陷入僵局。

    它甚至导致贝尔曼反复提及他对上周在拉斯维加斯的失望，他甚至暗示这对他未来的法拉利生涯构成了挑战。

    虽然要求法拉利向贝尔曼保证2028年的赛车座位是不合理的，但似乎这与他本人有选择去追求其他机会有关，以防长期法拉利的僵局让他被困在中游太多年。

    无论延迟了多少，这个问题最终得到了解决，贝尔曼表示“这并不是什么秘密，我很高兴”。

    “这个车队在我的成长中扮演了至关重要的角色，我感受到了来自这里每个人的巨大信任和支持，”贝尔曼说。

    “我们有着宏伟的目标，继续向前推进，我很兴奋能领导这项工作，基于我们所取得的成就，共同努力追求更多——我们还有未完成的事业。”

    在哈斯车队度过的两年里，他一直优于经验丰富的队友奥康，奥康在今年墨西哥周末的比赛中因内部冲突意外离开车队。奥康原本明年就会被Rafa Camara取代。

    小松表示，在他看来，没有比贝尔曼更好的人来领导这个车队向前发展，并将与Camara的组合称为“赛场上最激动人心的车手搭档之一”。

    英文原文

    Ollie Bearman’s stay at Haas for the 2027 Formula 1 season has finally been confirmed after a drawn-out renewal.

    Bearman, a Ferrari junior, will contest a third full season with Haas next year.

    He made his F1 debut in 2024 standing in for Carlos Sainz at Ferrari in Saudi Arabia at short notice, then raced twice for Haas as a reserve later that same season.

    Bearman’s renewal at Haas was considered a sure thing for a long time as Haas held a contractual option on him for a third season, and in announcing his continuity team principal Ayao Komatsu called it a “natural decision for us”.

    Despite this, Bearman’s situation was unexpectedly complicated given the tripartite nature of the agreement - with Ferrari crucial to his overall, long-term F1 future and Haas employing Bearman in the short-term.

    Different sources disagreed over what the hold-up was, with speculation Bearman’s camp could not reach a satisfactory financial arrangement with Haas for a third year.

    But it was also believed that negotiations had been tricky with Ferrari, which has Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton under contract for next year and blocking a path for Bearman to race for his long-time backer any time soon.

    One suggestion was that while Bearman himself was relaxed over 2027 and not an obstacle to a renewal, there had been a stall in progress because Ferrari could not guarantee him the flexibility that his management felt he needed.

    It even led to Bearman repeatedly referencing his disappointment with the situation last week in Las Vegas where he went as far as implying it posed challenges for his potential Ferrari future.

    While it would have been unreasonable to expect Ferrari to assure Bearman of a 2028 race seat it seems likely this related to giving Bearman the option to pursue alternatives himself in case a longer-term Ferrari impasse left him stuck in the midfield for too many years.

    Whatever the delay, the matter has finally been resolved, and Bearman said “it’s no secret that I’m delighted”.

    “This team has played an integral role in my development, and I’ve felt a huge amount of belief and support from everyone here,” Bearman said.

    “We’ve got big ambitions to keep pushing forward, and I’m excited to lead that work, building on what we’ve achieved, and striving for even more together - we have unfinished business.”

    In his two years at Haas he consistently outperformed experienced team-mate Esteban Ocon, who left the team unexpectedly in the middle of this year’s Mexico weekend following an internal fallout. Ocon was due to be replaced by Rafa Camara next year anyway.

    Komatsu said there “is no better person, in my opinion, to lead this team forward” than Bearman and called the combination with Camara “one of the most exciting driver pairings on the grid”.
