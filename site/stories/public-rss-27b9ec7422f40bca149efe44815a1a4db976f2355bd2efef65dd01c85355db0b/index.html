    韩泰启动F1轮胎测试并接触车队，准备竞标2029年供应合同

    韩泰已启动F1轮胎测试计划并与车队展开谈判，准备竞标从2029年起成为F1官方轮胎供应商。F1当前与倍耐力的合同持续到2028年底。

    据The Race了解，韩泰已启动一项F1轮胎测试计划，并与车队展开谈判，为竞标从2029年起成为大奖赛官方供应商做准备。

    F1目前与倍耐力的合同持续到2028年底。此前，这家意大利公司击败普利司通的竞争性报价，赢得了F1最近一次招标合同，覆盖2025至2027年。

    今年6月，在国际汽联行使选择权后，其合同又延长了一个赛季。

    然而，从2029年起，国际汽联将启动新的招标程序，届时似乎将出现新的竞争，以争夺F1官方供应商资格。

    据了解，倍耐力希望先等待看到招标细节，然后再决定是否参与竞标，以延长其自2011年以来的当前合约期，而普利司通长期以来一直在评估重返F1的可能性，其代表已出席过多场比赛。

    然而，现在有消息显示，韩泰也准备加入竞争，并且一直在暗中加大自身的努力。

    该公司的代表被发现出现在最近的拉斯维加斯大奖赛上，据了解，这是他们继9月前往马德里之后，今年第二次造访F1比赛。

    据The Race了解，韩泰利用这些行程会见了大多数车队，并与老板、首席执行官和其他高级管理人员讨论了计划。

    然而，前往各地观看比赛——其中还包括2025年在中国、日本和新加坡举行的赛事——只是其为2029年做准备的努力的一方面。

    据了解，韩泰已在公司内部组建了一支44人的专项工作组，为进入F1做准备。

    除了投入研发能力和模拟器运行之外，该公司今年还使用一辆改装的F2同级测试车完成了两次F1轮胎开发测试，以评估潜在的轮胎结构以及不同配方。

    第三次测试已安排用于研发其雨地轮胎，该公司希望这也能帮助其开发出一款具有更窄水雾羽流的轮胎，以减少湿地条件下造成的能见度问题。

    韩泰也在努力进行生产和制造规划，以满足任何轮胎招标合同下供应F1、F2和F3轮胎所需。

    韩泰对国际汽联的赛事当然并不陌生，这家韩国公司已经是世界拉力锦标赛的官方轮胎供应商，并且刚刚结束了对电动方程式锦标赛的供应，而普利司通将在即将到来的赛季接替。今年早些时候，韩泰还被指定为该管理机构的全球合作伙伴。

    2029年之后的新F1轮胎招标期几乎肯定会涵盖向下一个规则时代的过渡，F1正着眼于更轻、更灵活的新赛车，以配合从2030年开始改用V8动力单元。

    这些计划还将包括一种新的轮胎设计，该设计将比目前的18英寸设计略微减小车轮尺寸。

    英文原文

    Hankook has begun a Formula 1 tyre testing programme and opened talks with teams as it prepares to bid on becoming grand prix racing’s official supplier from 2029, The Race has learned.

    F1’s current contract with Pirelli runs until the end of 2028, after the Italian company won F1’s most recent tender deal, which covered the period from 2025-2027, against a rival bid from Bridgestone.

    Its contract was extended by a further season in June this year after an option was taken up by the FIA.

    From 2029, however, a new tender process is set to be launched by the FIA and there looks set to be fresh competition to secure the right to be the official F1 supplier.

    It is understood that Pirelli wants to wait to see details of the tender before deciding if it enters the fray to extend its current spell which stretches back to 2011, while Bridgestone has long evaluated an F1 return with representatives having attended races.

    However, it has now emerged that Hankook is ready to throw its hat into the ring too and has been secretly ramping up its own efforts.

    Representatives of the company were spotted at the recent Las Vegas Grand Prix, which was understood to have been their second visit to an F1 race this year following a trip to Madrid in September.

    The Race understands that Hankook has used these trips to meet a majority of teams and discuss plans with bosses, CEOs and other senior executives.

    The trips to races, which also included visits to events in China, Japan and Singapore in 2025, are only one side of its efforts to get ready for 2029, however.

    It is understood that Hankook has already committed a 44-person task force within the company to prepare for an F1 entry.

    Beyond committing research and development capability plus simulator running, it has this year completed two development tests for F1 rubber using a modified F2-equivalent-mule car to evaluate potential construction as well as different compounds.

    A third test has been scheduled to work on its wet-weather rubber, which it hopes can also help it develop a tyre that features a narrower spray plume to reduce visibility problems caused in wet conditions.

    Hankook is also working hard on production and manufacturing planning that would be required to supply F1, F2 and F3 tyres that would come under the umbrella of any tyre tender contract.

    Hankook is certainly no stranger to FIA competition, with the South Korean company already the official tyre supplier to the World Rally Championship and has just ended its supply to Formula E where Bridgestone is taking over for the upcoming season. Earlier this year Hankook was also named as a global partner of the governing body.

    The period of the new F1 tyre tender beyond 2029 will almost certainly cover the transition to the next rules era, with F1 setting sights on new lighter and nimbler cars to coincide with the switch to V8 power units from 2030.

    The plans are also set to include a new tyre design, which is set to reduce wheel size slightly from the current 18-inch design.
