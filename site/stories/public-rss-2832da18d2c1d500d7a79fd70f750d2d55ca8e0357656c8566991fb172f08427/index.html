    维斯塔潘以巴西主题头盔亮相圣保罗大奖赛

    维斯塔潘将在本周末的圣保罗大奖赛中身着巴西国旗颜色参赛，并展示了一款特别的头盔。

    维斯塔潘带来了一些桑巴舞的气氛到圣保罗，他在Instagram上分享了自己为这场比赛设计的特别头盔。

    这位红牛车手在本周末的Interlagos赛道比赛中，将换上巴西国旗的颜色。

    对于红牛车迷来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和比赛分析。

    维斯塔潘将在本周末的圣保罗大奖赛中身着巴西国旗颜色参赛，并在Instagram上展示了这款特别头盔。

    红牛车手将他的常用红、白、蓝涂装换成了巴西国旗上的绿、黄、蓝，并在Instagram上分享了这一设计，并配文：“为圣保罗带来一些桑巴。”

    这并不是他第一次用新的头盔设计来庆祝这场比赛。去年圣保罗的头盔在他的常用狮子图案上增加了金色和绿色的点缀，当时维斯塔潘将这场比赛描述为“特别”的。

    这有很多原因。他的伴侣凯莉·皮奎特是三届世界冠军内尔森·皮奎特的女儿。再加上，他还把奥迪的巴西车手加布里埃尔·博尔特洛托视为亲密的朋友。

    这条赛道也给他职业生涯中留下了许多难忘的时刻。

    在2016年，他只在F1的第二赛季，尽管在比赛还剩16圈时排名第16，但在一场雨战中他最终获得了第三名，这是他能力的一次展示。

    自那以后，他在Interlagos赢得了三次比赛，分别是2019年、2023年和2024年。其中最后一次被认为是他职业生涯中最伟大的驾驶之一。他在大雨中从第17位发车，数月来首次获胜，并且离他的第四个世界冠军更近了一步。

    甚至在去年，他从维修区起步，最终攀升至第三名，仅落后于第二名的小车手安东内利不到半秒。

    今年，随着维斯塔潘在墨西哥上周末获胜，他取得了在该赛事中的第六次胜利，来到这里。安东内利已经锁定了冠军，但如果在维斯塔潘经常表现出色的赛道上获胜，这将是一个强劲的结果，可以带入赛季的最后几轮。

    他不是唯一一个在本周末改变赛车服设计的车手。安东内利也将佩戴一款特别的头盔，以纪念埃尔顿·塞纳，而他本人和乔治·拉塞尔在本周末将身穿紫色赛车服代表梅赛德斯。

    Max Verstappen brings “some samba” to Sao Paulo with Brazil-themed F1 helmet

    The Red Bull driver has swapped his usual colours for those of the Brazilian flag ahead of this weekend’s race at Interlagos

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Max Verstappen will race in the colours of Brazil at this weekend’s Sao Paulo Grand Prix after revealing a special helmet for the race.

    The Red Bull driver has replaced his usual red, white and blue with the green, yellow and blue of the Brazilian flag, sharing the design on Instagram alongside the following caption: “Bringing some samba to São Paulo."

    It is not the first time he's celebrated this race with a new helmet design. Last year’s Sao Paulo helmet added gold and green accents to his usual lion motif, with Verstappen describing the race as “special” at the time.

    There are plenty of reasons for that. His partner, Kelly Piquet, is the daughter of three-time world champion Nelson Piquet. To add to this, he counts Audi’s Brazilian driver Gabriel Bortoleto as a close friend.

    The circuit has also provided some of the most memorable moments of his career.

    In 2016, in only his second season in F1, he finished third in the wet despite being 16th with 16 laps to go, in one of the first races to show what he was capable of.

    He has since won at Interlagos three times, in 2019, 2023 and 2024. The last of those is often regarded as one of the greatest drives of his career. He started 17th in heavy rain, won for the first time in months and moved much closer to his fourth world title.

    Even last year, when he started from the pitlane, he climbed to third, finishing less than half a second behind a young Kimi Antonelli in second.

    This year’s visit comes with Verstappen in good form, having won in Mexico last weekend to take his sixth victory in that race. Antonelli has already clinched the title, but a win at a track where Verstappen has so often excelled would be another strong result to take into the final rounds of the season.

    He is not the only driver changing the design of his racing suit for this coming weekend. Antonelli will also wear a special helmet as a tribute to Ayrton Senna, while he and George Russell will wear purple suits for Mercedes across the weekend.
