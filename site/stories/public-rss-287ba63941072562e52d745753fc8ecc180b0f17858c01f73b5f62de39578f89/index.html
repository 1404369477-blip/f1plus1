    维斯塔潘拉斯维加斯夺冠回击拉塞尔

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛夺冠，赛后以尖锐言论回击拉塞尔周五的批评。两人在第三次练习赛中因飞驰圈前的超车发生小碰撞。

    维斯塔潘对拉塞尔的复仇：“因果报应”

    拉塞尔周五严厉批评了维斯塔潘；这位红牛车手周六以胜利和赛后尖锐言论作出回应

    维斯塔潘用拉斯维加斯大奖赛的胜利回击了拉塞尔周五的言论，并在赛后发表了尖锐评论。

    两人在第三次练习赛中发生事件：维斯塔潘在飞驰圈前超越拉塞尔，拉塞尔认为这违反了君子协定。这位英国车手试图重新抢回位置，但维斯塔潘封堵未果，两人最终发生小碰撞。

    这些把戏导致赛会干事正式警告维斯塔潘在刹车时移动，而拉塞尔则在天空体育意大利台毫不掩饰地指责对手：“他每周都和许多车手这样干，所有人都接受了，而我不会就这样接受有人这样坑我，还像他们比所有人都更大更好一样四处走动。总得有人站出来对抗那家伙。”

    二十四小时后，维斯塔潘在一场多事的比赛中顶住了两辆梅赛德斯的进攻，夺得胜利，双方在整场比赛中多次交换领先。拉塞尔在第48圈（共50圈）为争夺第二名向队友安东内利内线俯冲时锁死轮胎并撞车，最终名列第九——在维斯塔潘看来，这很说明问题。

    当被问及两人争夺领先时是否想到拉塞尔的话时，维斯塔潘回击道：“嗯，我是说，今天这算不上什么成功。

    “我是说，问题是，他在赛道边话很多。老实说，对我来说这真的无所谓，因为他远低于我的水平，所以对我来说，尝试去挑战那件事根本没有任何意义，所以这并不困扰我。

    “今天因果报应真是残酷。老实说，当我看向后视镜，看到正在发生的情况时，感觉太棒了。所以我想，‘太棒了！我这一天只会越来越好。’

    “当然，当我随后第一个冲过终点线时，我的一天圆满了。”

    维斯塔潘或许不在乎年度亚军的位置，但他的胜利和拉塞尔的失利意味着他现在对这位梅赛德斯车手拥有40分的优势，而赛季仅剩两站——只有汉密尔顿更接近，尽管仍落后37分。

    Max Verstappen’s revenge on George Russell: “Karma is a bitch”

    Russell was highly critical of Verstappen on Friday; the Red Bull racer responded with victory – and acerbic post-race remarks – on Saturday

    Max Verstappen has hit back at George Russell’s Friday comments by winning Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix – and delivering scathing remarks afterwards.

    The pair were involved in an incident in Free Practice 3, with Verstappen breaching what Russell deemed to be a gentlemen’s agreement by overtaking him ahead of a flying lap. The Briton moved to get back ahead but Verstappen unsuccessfully attempted to close the door and the duo eventually had a small collision.

    The shenanigans led stewards to officially warn Verstappen for moving under braking, while Russell unabashedly blamed his rival on Sky Sport Italy: “He does this week in week out with many drivers and everybody accepts it, and I'm not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they're bigger and better than everyone else. Somebody's got to stand up to that guy.”

    Twenty-four hours later, Verstappen held off both Mercedes for victory in an eventful race as they traded the lead throughout the contest. Russell crashed as he locked up when diving down the inside of team-mate Kimi Antonelli for second place on lap 48 of 50, ending up ninth – and in Verstappen’s eyes, this speaks volumes.

    Asked if Russell’s words had been on his mind when they were racing one another for the lead, Verstappen retorted: “Well, I mean, it's not like it's a success today.

    “I mean, the thing is, he speaks a lot next to the track. And honestly, for me, it doesn't really matter because he's so far below my level that it doesn't really make any sense for me anyway, to try and challenge that, so it doesn't bother me.

    “And today karma is a bitch. It was honestly fantastic when I looked in my mirror and I saw what was happening. So I was like, ‘Fantastic! My day is only getting better.’

    “And of course, when I then crossed the line in first, my day was complete.”

    Verstappen may not care about the runner-up spot in the championship, but his victory and Russell’s downfall mean he now has a 40-point advantage over the Mercedes driver, with just two rounds remaining – only Lewis Hamilton is closer, albeit still 37 points adrift.
