    佩雷兹与凯迪拉克续约至2029年

    凯迪拉克与塞尔吉奥·佩雷兹达成多年续约协议，佩雷兹至少将为这支美国车队效力至2029年。

    佩雷兹与凯迪拉克F1车队续约至2029年

    凯迪拉克与塞尔吉奥·佩雷兹在这位墨西哥车手的主场比赛前达成了一份多年续约协议

    塞尔吉奥·佩雷兹已承诺至少为凯迪拉克F1项目效力至2029年，这位墨西哥车手与这支美国车队签下了新合同。

    佩雷兹在离开红牛后于去年一度无车可开，随后加入这支F1新军，随队迎来2026年的首秀。

    尽管在这支美国车队经历了起伏不定的首个赛季，这位36岁的车手在本赛季找回了最佳状态，与同为老将的队友瓦尔特利·博塔斯相比也不落下风。

    从第一天起就渴望在凯迪拉克车队亲力亲为的佩雷兹，已与车队首席执行官丹·托里斯和通用汽车总裁马克·罗伊斯建立了牢固的关系，并被该项目未来的规划所说服，决定签下新合同，至少再留队三个赛季。

    “从第一天起加入凯迪拉克一级方程式车队就是我真正相信的项目，延长我的承诺感觉是这一篇章的自然延续，”佩雷兹说道。

    “从零开始打造一支一级方程式车队需要时间、不懈的努力和耐心，但我们共同取得的进展令人无比欣慰。前方仍有大量工作要做，我完全承诺投入接下来的三年。”

    “我想感谢丹、马克，以及TWG赛车运动和通用汽车的所有人，感谢你们给我机会，让我们在已经共同开启的事业上继续建设。对于这支特殊团队的每一位成员，无论是在赛道现场、在美国、在英国，还是我们的车迷，感谢你们的支持与信任。我不会让你们失望。让我们继续努力。”

    托里斯补充道：“在F1打造一支有竞争力的车队是一段长期旅程，锁定佩雷兹的才华和领导力，为我们提供了延续性和承诺，让我们能够在第一年打下的坚实基础上继续建设。”

    “我们正共同专注于旅程的下一阶段，推动车队前进，在发车格上不断提升位置。”

    佩雷兹续约的消息传出之际，他正备战本周末在墨西哥城主场迎来的F1第300场大奖赛。

    为纪念这一时刻，曾效力于赛点车队和红牛车队、六次赢得大奖赛冠军的佩雷兹将在墨西哥首都街头进行一场表演驾驶。

    佩雷兹2026年的队友人选则不那么确定。博塔斯此前与车队签下了一份据信为期多年的合同，但令人失望的赛季表现让凯迪拉克重新考虑其选择。

    由于车队仍处于建设阶段，而博塔斯的一些挣扎又被凯迪拉克不稳定的赛车和不均衡的生产质量所掩盖，博塔斯仍很有可能再留队一个赛季。

    但车队也投入了大量资源，帮助美国测试车手科尔顿·赫塔为F1首秀做好准备。而作为2027年市场上最后一个可用席位，凯迪拉克体系之外对博塔斯席位感兴趣者也不在少数。

    Sergio Perez signs fresh Cadillac F1 deal until 2029

    Cadillac and Sergio Perez have agreed a multi-year contract extension ahead of the Mexican's Formula 1 home race

    Sergio Perez has committed to the Cadillac F1 project until at least 2029 as the Mexican signed fresh terms with the US-owned outfit.

    Perez joined F1's expansion team for its 2026 debut after his Red Bull exit left him on the sidelines last year.

    Despite going through the ups and downs of an inconsistent debut season for the American team, the 36-year-old has found his best form this season, comparing favourably to fellow veteran team-mate Valtteri Bottas.

    Having been keen to play a hands-on role at the Cadillac team from day one, Perez has forged a strong relationship with both team CEO Dan Towriss and General Motors chief Mark Reuss, and has been convinced by the project's future plans to commit to fresh terms, extending his stay for another three seasons at least.

    “Joining Cadillac Formula 1 Team from day one was a project I truly believed in, and extending my commitment feels like a natural continuation of this chapter,” said Perez.

    “Building a Formula 1 team from the ground up takes time, relentless work and patience, but the progress we are making together is incredibly rewarding. There is still a lot of work ahead of us and I am fully committed for the next three years.

    “I want to thank Dan, Mark, and everyone at TWG Motorsports and General Motors for the opportunity to build on what we have started together. To every member of this special team, at the track, in the US, the UK, and our fans, thank you for your support and trust. I won’t let you down. Let’s keep pushing."

    Towriss added: "Building a competitive team in F1 is a long-term journey, and securing Checo’s talent and leadership gives us the continuity and commitment we need to build on the strong foundation established in our first year.

    "Together, we are focused on the next phase of our journey, pushing the team forward and making progress up the grid.”

    The news of Perez's contract extension lands as he gears up for his 300th grand prix in F1 at this weekend's home race in Mexico City.

    To mark the occasion Perez, a six-time grand prix winner with Racing Point and Red Bull, will take to the streets of the Mexican capital for a show run.

    The identity of Perez's team-mate for 2026 is less certain. Bottas had signed what was believed to be a multi-year deal with the team, but a disappointing campaign has made Cadillac reconsider its options.

    With the team very much still in the build-up phase and some of Bottas' struggles clouded by Cadillac's inconsistent car and uneven production quality, there is still every chance Bottas will remain on board for another season.

    But the team has also invested heavily in getting American test driver Colton Herta ready for an F1 debut. And as the last available seat on the 2027 market, there has been no shortage of suitors for Bottas' seat from outside Cadillac's orbit.
