    Netflix续订《Formula 1: Drive to Survive》第九季

    Netflix已确认《Formula 1: Drive to Survive》将回归第九季

    安东内利，梅赛德斯，《Drive to Survive》第八季

    Netflix已正式确认，其幕后纪录片系列《Formula 1: Drive to Survive》（《极速求生》）将回归第九季，内容涵盖正在进行中的2026赛季。

    这部纪录片剧集于2019年在该流媒体平台首播，让观众前所未有地深入围场，并得以了解车手们在赛道之外的个人生活。

    制作该节目的Box to Box Films公司执行制片人詹姆斯·盖-里斯和保罗·马丁说：

    我们非常高兴能制作这部标志性节目的第九季。今年我们将比以往任何时候都更深入地探索这项精彩运动的隐秘角落。

    ,"尽管该剧因采用创造性的剪辑手法来营造真人秀式的戏剧效果而受到一些批评，但这部系列赛对锦标赛人气提升的影响是不可否认的。它在扩大美国车迷群体、吸引更多年轻观众以及提高女性观众比例方面发挥了重要作用。","盖-里斯在2023年接受F1官网采访时谈到了这部纪录片剧集的影响。"说实话，我们为能参与其中感到无比自豪。能率先做出这样的东西也带来了极大的满足感，我认为这在某种程度上确实是第一部产生如此深远影响的主要深度探访节目——至少在英国范围内肯定是。

    “我们都听过那些关于受众结构在变化、越来越年轻化的统计数据，如今对任何人、对任何大型组织来说，想要守住自己的阵地都很艰难，因为消费者的选择实在太多了。把如此庞大的新观众群体带进一项运动——这显然是理所当然应得的，只是需要有人把它打开——让人感到非常满足。”

    “事实证明这并非偶然，事实证明它在（每个赛季）不断成长……这是一种真正的荣誉，我们热爱它。做这样一档节目非常困难，但我们热爱这个过程，热爱这个世界。希望它能长长久久地继续下去！”

    这部纪录剧集的第九季将聚焦2026赛季的F1。

    车迷们已经纷纷表达了对2027年播出这一季的期待。一位车迷在Netflix英国官方Instagram账号发布的公告下评论道：“阿斯顿·马丁那一集绝对会是电视上的神作。”

    “期待《求生之路》（DTS）马拉松，”另一个人发帖说，而另一位车迷补充道：“好，我等不及看《求生之路》（DTS）了。我其实挺喜欢的。”

    Netflix renews "Formula 1: Drive to Survive" for season 9

    Netflix has confirmed "Formula 1: Drive to Survive" will return for a ninth season

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Drive to Survive Season 8

    Netflix has officially confirmed that its behind-the-scenes docuseries Formula 1: Drive to Survive will return for a ninth season, covering the ongoing 2026 season.

    The docuseries, which premiered on the streaming service in 2019, has given viewers unprecedented access to the paddock and an insight into the drivers' personal lives beyond the track.

    "We’re so excited to be making Season 9 of this iconic show," James Gay-Rees and Paul Martin, executive producers at Box to Box, which produces the show, said. "This year we’re going to go deeper than ever before into the hidden spaces of this fantastic sport."

    While it has been met with some criticism for its creative editing to bring the drama of a reality television show, the impact the series has had on the popularity of the championship cannot be denied. It has been instrumental in the growth of the fanbase in the United States and in attracting a younger audience with a higher proportion of women to the racing series.

    Gay-Rees spoke about the impact of the docuseries during an interview with F1.com in 2023. "Genuinely, we are enormously proud to be a part of it. There’s also a lot of satisfaction at being first with these things, and I think that it kind of does feel like the first main access show – certainly out of the UK anyway – that has had this profound effect.

    "We’ve all had the statistics about the changing, younger demographic, and it’s tough out there for anybody, for any big organisation, to hold its ground given the amount of choices that are out there for the consumer. Bringing such a big new audience to a sport – which obviously was deserved, it just needed somebody to open it up – is massively satisfying.

    "The fact that it wasn’t a fluke, the fact that it’s grown [with every season]… It’s a real honour and we love it. It’s a very difficult show to make, but we love the process, we love the world. Long may it continue!"

    The ninth season of the docuseries will centre on the 2026 F1 season.

    Fans have already shared their excitement about the 2027 release. "The Aston Martin episode will be Absolute Television," one fan commented on the announcement shared on the official Netflix UK Instagram account.

    "Looking forward to the DTS marathon," someone else posted, while another fan added: "OK I can’t wait for DTS. I actually like it."
