    卡马拉将代表哈斯完成2026赛季F1

    拉斐尔·卡马拉将代表哈斯车队完成2026赛季F1剩余比赛，这意味着他将放弃争夺F2年度冠军的机会。

    拉斐尔·卡马拉将代表哈斯车队完成2026赛季F1剩余比赛，这实际上意味着他将放弃争夺F2年度冠军的机会，以便继续提前建立与2027年F1车队的合作关系。

    尽管在墨西哥大奖赛周末中途哈斯与埃斯特班·奥康突然分手后，卡马拉代表哈斯参加了巴西大奖赛和拉斯维加斯大奖赛，但他此前一直是逐站安排。

    他目前以7分优势领跑F2积分榜，领先红牛储备车手尼科拉·措洛夫，但最后两轮比赛与F1卡塔尔站和阿布扎比站同期举行，车手被禁止在同一赛事中同时参加两个系列赛（即便允许，从时间表来看也不切实际）。

    尽管哈斯本可再次启用平川亮——正如奥康离队后墨西哥周末剩余时间所做的那样——但车队周日宣布卡马拉获得卡塔尔站和阿布扎比站的席位。

    车队领队小松礼雄表示：“我们很高兴能够以拉法（Rafa）驾驶赛车完成本赛季，并在他在巴西和美国代表车队的前两场比赛中继续积累。”

    “我很高兴拉法把每一节练习与排位都当作学习机会，并进一步融入了车队。我们现在可以一起完成本赛季，这对我们所有人都有利，尤其是在相对较短的休赛期之前，以及我们2027年的准备工作正在进行之际。”

    卡马拉（Camara）在巴西一场充满波折的F1首秀中排名第20位、位列最后，但本周末在拉斯维加斯提升至第16位。

    奥迪F1培养车手弗雷迪·斯莱特（Freddie Slater）很可能在最后几轮比赛中接替卡马拉在Invicta F2车队的席位，因为他也能提前启动自己的2027年计划。

    尽管卡马拉仍有可能成为F2冠军，但这将取决于措洛沃在卡塔尔和阿布扎比的四场比赛中得分少于七分，并且后面没有人能同时超越他们两人。

    阿尔派 F1青训车手亚历克斯·邓恩——Racer曾强烈暗示他将在2027年转投戴尔·科因车队参加IndyCar——目前以仅落后措洛沃11分的成绩排名第三。

    英文原文

    Rafael Camara will complete the 2026 Formula 1 season with Haas, effectively sacrificing his Formula 2 championship shot to continue getting a headstart on his relationship with his 2027 F1 team.

    Though Camara contested the Brazilian and Las Vegas Grands Prix for Haas after its sudden split with Esteban Ocon midway through the Mexican GP weekend, Camara had been on a race-by-race arrangement.

    He currently leads the F2 standings by seven points over Red Bull reserve Nikola Tsolov, but the last two rounds are on the F1 bill in Qatar and Abu Dhabi and drivers are barred from trying to compete in both series at the same event (which would in any case be impractical with the timetables even if it was permitted).

    Though Haas could have again called on Ryo Hirakawa, as it did for the remainder of the Mexico weekend after Ocon’s exit, it announced on Sunday that Camara had got the nod for Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

    Team principal Ayao Komatsu said: “We’re excited to be able to finish the season with Rafa driving and build on his first two race starts with the team in Brazil and America.

    “I’m delighted that Rafa’s embraced every track session as a learning opportunity and embedded himself further within the team. We now get to finish the season together which is advantageous to us all ahead of a relatively short off-season and as our 2027 preparations get underway.”

    Camara finished 20th and last in a fraught F1 debut in Brazil but improved to 16th in Vegas this weekend.

    Audi F1 protege Freddie Slater is likely to step into Camara’s Invicta F2 seat for the final rounds, as he also gets a headstart on his 2027 programme.

    Though it’s not impossible that Camara could still be F2 champion, that would rely on both Tsolov scoring fewer than seven points from the four races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, and no one further behind leapfrogging them both.

    Alpine F1 junior Alex Dunne - who has been strongly tipped by Racer for a 2027 IndyCar move with Dale Coyne Racing - is just 11 points behind Tsolov in third.
