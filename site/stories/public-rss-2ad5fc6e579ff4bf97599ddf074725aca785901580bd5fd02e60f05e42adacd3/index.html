    汉密尔顿：若得到一号车手的支持，本可击败安东内利夺F1冠军

    七届F1冠军汉密尔顿认为，如果他在赛季早期得到法拉利的更多支持，本可以击败梅赛德斯的安东内利。他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛后的采访中表达了这一观点。

    法拉利车手表示，他从未要求成为一号车手，但他认为在他成为安东内利最接近的挑战者时，他需要支持。

    对于法拉利车迷来说，这是最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，包括车手更新、技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应。

    七届F1冠军汉密尔顿相信，如果他在赛季早期得到法拉利的更多支持，本可以击败梅赛德斯的安东内利。

    这位英国车手是在拉斯维加斯大奖赛之后发表上述言论的，在那场比赛中，他与队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔又发生了碰撞，导致他车辆失控。他最终恢复状态，以第七名的成绩完赛，而勒克莱尔则受到了处罚，最终排名第10。

    当被问及1996年F1冠军达蒙·希尔建议法拉利应该让他成为车队一号车手时，汉密尔顿表示他从未要求过这样的地位，但他觉得在今年年初的时候他确实需要这样的地位。

    “我从未要求成为一号车手。我真的是从未要求过，”汉密尔顿说。

    他指的是赛季中某个阶段，当时他领先勒克莱尔很多，并且是距离安东内利争夺冠军最近的车手。

    “我在这一年里长时间保持第二的位置，那时我需要支持来确保我们能击败基米。在赛季中有几次，有了支持，我们本可以击败基米，”他说。

    41岁的他在6月份在巴塞罗那以法拉利车队赢得了他的第一个大奖赛胜利后，在车手积分榜上排名第二，落后安东内利41分，领先勒克莱尔40分。在摩纳哥、比利时和匈牙利之后，他也排名第二。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、查尔斯·勒克莱尔、法拉利

    安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛上赢得了冠军，这是在梅赛德斯赢得车队冠军之前的一周，现在他有402分，而汉密尔顿有267分。

    “我不知道，但这不是我想卷入的事情，”当被问及他与队友的关系将如何发展时，他说，“这些问题是人们都在问的。最后，我尽了最大的努力，我给了我能给的一切来争取今天的分数。”

    “我们一直在面对挑战，我只是低下头继续前进。”

    “在比赛早期，汉密尔顿因为离开赛道并在麦克拉伦的兰多·诺里斯身上获得优势而被处以10秒罚时。”

    “10秒的罚时是疯狂的，非常非常严厉，”他继续说，“我是在避免与他们相撞，他们本可以让我把位置让回去。10秒的[罚时]简直是胡闹。但我回来了，我重新回到了比赛中，我看到前面的马克斯，我想，我们重新回到了比赛中。”

    “我和麦凯轮车队在战斗，所以我真心认为我可能能争取到领奖台，我实际上只想着和麦凯轮车队战斗，因为这是我们需要的，才能获得第二名。”

    Lewis Hamilton: With No. 1 driver backing, we could have beaten Kimi Antonelli to F1 title

    The Ferrari driver says he never asked to be number one, but believes he "needed backing" when he was Kimi Antonelli's closest challenger

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton believes he could have beaten Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli in their battle earlier in the season had he been given more support from Ferrari.

    The Briton was speaking after the Las Vegas Grand Prix, where he had another clash with his team-mate Charles Leclerc, which sent him into a spin. He recovered to finish seventh, while Leclerc was penalised and finished 10th.

    Asked about 1996 F1 champion Damon Hill's suggestion that Ferrari should make him its number one driver, Hamilton said he had never asked for that status, but felt he had needed it earlier in the year.

    "I've never asked to be number one. I genuinely haven't ever asked," Hamilton said.

    He pointed to a stage of the season when he was well clear of Leclerc and the nearest driver to challenge Antonelli for the title.

    "I was second for a long time in the year, and at that point I needed backing to make sure we beat Kimi. There were a few circumstances in the season where, with the support, we could have beaten Kimi," he said.

    After the 41-year-old claimed his first grand prix win with Ferrari in Barcelona in June, he was second in the drivers' standings, 41 points behind Antonelli and 40 points ahead of Leclerc. He was also second after Monaco, Belgium and Hungary.

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari

    Antonelli went on to secure the championship at the Mexican Grand Prix, one week before Mercedes won the constructors' title, and now has 402 points to Hamilton's 267.

    "I don't know, but it's not something I want to get into," he said when asked where his relationship with his team-mate goes from here. "These are questions that people are asking. At the end of the day, I performed as hard as I could, and I've been giving everything I could to get those points today.

    "We were just constantly hit with challenges, and I just kept my head down."

    Earlier in the race, Hamilton had been handed a 10-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage over McLaren's Lando Norris.

    "10 seconds was insane and very, very harsh," he continued. "I was avoiding colliding with them, and they could have just told me to give the place back. A 10-second [penalty] is nuts. But I came back and I got back into the fight, and I could see Max up ahead and I was like, we're back in the fight.

    "I was fighting with McLaren, so I honestly thought that I could maybe fight for a podium, and I'm literally only thinking about fighting McLaren, because that's where we need to be to get the second."
