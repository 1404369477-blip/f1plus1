    诺里斯调侃巴西领奖台香槟瓶意外

    这位迈凯伦车手在英特拉格斯从杆位夺冠后，香槟瓶碎裂，随后他走入领奖台火花中

    致迈凯伦车迷。来自沃金的木瓜色新闻，包含车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个比赛周末的最新消息。

    2025年F1世界冠军诺里斯在巴西大奖赛混乱的领奖台庆祝中调侃自己职业生涯首次“找到了香槟瓶的极限”。

    这位迈凯伦车手在英特拉格斯从杆位发车夺冠，但他标志性的庆祝动作出了问题。当他将香槟瓶猛砸在地上时，瓶子在他手中碎裂。更糟的是，他随后又走进了领奖台的火花中。

    在拉斯维加斯大奖赛前的车手新闻发布会上接受媒体采访时，诺里斯被问及此次事件后是否会改变赛后庆祝方式。

    “是的，那是这个周末最难的部分，”他说。“我想周日你们就会知道我是否站在最高领奖台上。我的意思是，如果我不是第一名，我从不那样做。所以我需要努力再赢一场比赛，你们才能知道。”

    “但在我职业生涯中第一次，我找到了香槟瓶的极限。所以我很高兴我终于找到了它。”

    这位英国车手淡化了火花带来的影响。

    “我确实也走进了火花里，这没什么帮助。但还好。我几乎没注意到。我只是感觉耳朵挺热的，但没有烫伤我或怎么样，”他说。

    “所以是的，那不是我最好的几秒钟，但也是以后值得回忆的时刻。所以我留了一点那瓶酒。巴西领奖台上可能还有一些，但是的，等我下次夺冠你们就会知道。”

    这起事件发生在诺里斯所说的“一个超棒的周末”结束时。他在湿地条件下夺得杆位，随后赢得比赛，这是他本赛季的第三场胜利。

    不过，诺里斯承认，在巴西的纯干地速度上，迈凯伦并未完全追上梅赛德斯，而复杂的天气条件帮了忙。

    2026赛季还剩三场比赛，诺里斯目前位列车手积分榜第六。

    Lando Norris jokes he "found the limit of a champagne bottle" in Brazil podium mishap

    The McLaren driver shattered his bottle before walking into the podium sparks after winning from pole at Interlagos

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    2025 Formula 1 champion Lando Norris has joked about how he "found the limit of a champagne bottle" for the first time in his career during his chaotic podium celebrations at the Brazilian Grand Prix.

    The McLaren driver won from pole at Interlagos, but his trademark celebration went wrong. As he slammed the champagne bottle down on the floor, it shattered in his hands. To make matters worse, he then walked into the podium sparks.

    While speaking to the media during the drivers' press conference ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, Norris was asked whether he would be changing his post-race celebrations after the incident.

    "Yeah, that was the hardest part of the weekend," he said. "I guess you'll find out on Sunday if I'm on the top step. I mean, I never do that if I'm not first. So I need to try to win another race for you guys to find out.

    "But for the first time in my career, I found the limit of a champagne bottle. So I'm happy I finally managed to find it."

    The Briton played down the effect of the sparks.

    "I did also walk into the sparks, which didn't help. But that was fine. I barely noticed it. I just kind of felt my ear was pretty hot, but it didn't burn me or anything," he said.

    "So yeah, not my finest couple of seconds, but also one for the memory books down the road. So I saved a little bit of the bottle. There's probably still some up on the podium in Brazil, but yeah, you'll find out when I next win."

    The incident came at the end of what Norris called "a mega weekend". He took pole position in the wet and went on to win the race, his third victory of the season.

    Norris admitted, however, that McLaren had not quite matched Mercedes for outright dry pace in Brazil, and that the tricky conditions had helped.

    With three races remaining in the 2026 season, Norris currently sits sixth in the drivers' standings.
