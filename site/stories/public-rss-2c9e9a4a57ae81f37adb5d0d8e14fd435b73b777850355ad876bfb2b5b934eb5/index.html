    安东内利履行承诺，以意大利国旗发色庆祝梅赛德斯F1世界冠军

    新世界冠军安东内利以意大利国旗发色出现在梅赛德斯布拉克利工厂，庆祝车队成功赛季。

    安东内利履行了他的承诺。梅赛德斯的新世界冠军本周以意大利国旗的颜色——绿色、白色和红色染发，出现在车队位于布拉克利的工厂，随着车队在总部庆祝其成功的赛季。

    梅赛德斯在周三通过社交媒体发布了一段视频，展示了安东内利与车队成员见面时的场景，他和Toto Wolff以及拉塞尔都笑着欣赏他的新造型。

    梅赛德斯在帖文中写道：“基米做到了。他真的做到了。”

    安东内利在赛季初就承诺要染发。在圣保罗大奖赛之前，他告诉《La Gazzetta dello Sport》他肯定会这么做，只是不确定什么时候。

    “我有了染发剂！现在我只是要决定是这周末做还是等到下周我前往布拉克利工厂并在所有员工面前发言时再做。带着染了色的头发去那里会很有趣。”

    他选择了Brackley，在那里他和车队享受了一个没有比赛的星期，有很多值得庆祝的事情。安东内利在墨西哥赢得了车手冠军，当时还有四场比赛尚未进行，成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军，也是自1953年阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来意大利的第一个世界冠军。本赛季他总共赢得了九场大奖赛——这是他在锦标赛中的第二次夺冠。

    梅赛德斯在周日于Interlagos锁定了制造商冠军，这是自2021年以来的第一次，尽管安东内利的比赛颇具挑战性。他在切换到干胎后发生打滑，最终排名第五。这是本赛季第一次梅赛德斯车队没有在领奖台上结束的比赛。

    Kimi Antonelli keeps F1 title promise with Italian flag hair at Mercedes factory

    The new world champion turned up at Brackley with green, white and red hair as Mercedes celebrated its title double with staff

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Kimi Antonelli has kept his word. Mercedes' new world champion arrived at the team's Brackley factory this week with his hair dyed green, white and red, the colours of the Italian flag, as the team celebrated its successful season at its headquarters.

    Mercedes posted a video of the moment on social media on Wednesday, showing Antonelli meeting members of the team to laughter, with Toto Wolff and George Russell alongside him also laughing at his new look.

    "Kimi did it. He actually did it," Mercedes wrote on the post.

    Antonelli had promised to dye his hair earlier in the season. Before the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, he told La Gazzetta dello Sport that he was definitely going to do it, just not sure about when.

    "I've got the cans! Now I just have to decide whether to do it this weekend or maybe wait until next week when I go to the factory in Brackley and have to speak in front of all the employees," he said. "It would be fun to turn up there with dyed hair."

    He chose Brackley, where he and the team were enjoying a week without a race with plenty to celebrate. Antonelli clinched the drivers' championship in Mexico with four races still to go, becoming F1's youngest world champion at 20, and Italy's first since Alberto Ascari in 1953. He has won a total of nine grands prix this season - his second in the championship.

    Mercedes then sealed the constructors' title at Interlagos on Sunday, its first since 2021, though Antonelli's own race was a challenging one. He spun out of the lead after the switch to slick tyres and finished fifth. This was the first race of the season where a Mercedes didn't finish on the podium.
