    迈凯伦正在出售其经典车收藏中的五辆历史性F1赛车，包括两辆塞纳时代的赛车，以及布拉德·皮特拍摄电影《F1》时驾驶的赛车。

    迈凯伦从其丰富的经典车收藏中出售五辆F1赛车，包括两辆来自塞纳最后一个夺冠赛季的赛车，以及布拉德·皮特拍摄这部成功F1电影时驾驶的赛车。

    这些赛车将通过专业经销商Tom Hartley Jnr出售——正是这家机构此前售出了伯尼·埃克莱斯顿的一级方程式赛车收藏——而在此之前，这些赛车一直归迈凯伦所有。

    两辆1991年的迈凯伦MP4/6A（底盘04和07），来自塞纳赢得他第三个也是最后一个世界冠军的那一年。两辆车都曾由塞纳测试，并由队友格哈德·伯格驾驶参赛。

    2012年的MP4/27A（底盘01），来自刘易斯·汉密尔顿效力该车队的最后一个赛季。2012年，简森·巴顿赢了三场比赛，而汉密尔顿赢了四场。

    2021年的MCL35M（底盘01），兰多·诺里斯曾驾驶它在摩纳哥以一次性海湾石油涂装获得第三名。同年晚些时候，这辆车参与了迈凯伦在蒙扎的1-2名完赛，当时丹尼尔·里卡多击败诺里斯，为车队赢得了自2012年以来的首场胜利。

    2023 年的 MCL37A（底盘 03），由诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里驾驶参赛。影片拍摄期间，皮特驾驶它绕行美洲赛道。

    据哈特利介绍，2023 年的 MCL37A 是迈凯伦有史以来向私人买家提供的最新一款曾参加比赛的 F1 赛车。

    迈凯伦赛车首席执行官扎克·布朗表示：“我们决定出售这些标志性的迈凯伦一级方程式赛车，因为我们认为让它们仅仅闲置在仓库里，是对它们所代表意义的一种辜负。”

    “这些赛车生来就是为了被驾驶和竞速的，我们希望看到它们被使用、被享受。我们相信，这是让我们的历史保持鲜活、让我们的经典车业务保持活力的最佳方式。”

    目前还没有价格消息，但有报道估计总价值在2500万至5000万美元之间。与埃克莱斯顿收藏被单一买家整体收购不同，这些赛车可能被单独出售。

    哈特利在一份声明中表示：“当然，这里也完全有可能发生同样的情况。不过，这五辆车将单独出售，而且没有任何时间压力。我们的首要任务，也是迈凯伦的首要任务，是为它们找到合适的归宿。”

    这些赛车将由迈凯伦进行整备，然后才交付给新车主。

    McLaren opens its vault to sell five extraordinary Formula 1 cars

    McLaren is selling five historic Formula 1 cars from its heritage collection, including two Ayrton Senna-era machines and the car driven by Brad Pitt during filming of "F1"

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    From its vast heritage collection, McLaren is selling five Formula 1 cars, including two from Ayrton Senna's final title-winning season and the car that Brad Pitt drove during the filming of the successful F1 movie.

    The cars will be sold through specialist dealer Tom Hartley Jnr - the same organisation that sold Bernie Ecclestone’s grand prix collection of cars - with them being under the ownership of McLaren up until this point.

    Two 1991 McLaren MP4/6As (chassis 04 and 07), from the year Senna won his third and final world title. Both were tested by Senna and raced by team-mate Gerhard Berger.

    The 2012 MP4/27A (chassis 01), from Lewis Hamilton’s final season with the team. Jenson Button won three races in 2012, while Hamilton won four.

    The 2021 MCL35M (chassis 01), which Lando Norris drove to third in Monaco in a one-off Gulf livery. The car was part of McLaren’s 1-2 at Monza later that year, when Daniel Ricciardo beat Norris to the team’s first win since 2012.

    The 2023 MCL37A (chassis 03), raced by Norris and Oscar Piastri. Pitt took it round the Circuit of the Americas while the film was being shot.

    The 2023 MCL37A is the newest race-driven McLaren F1 car ever offered to a private buyer, according to Hartley.

    “We have decided to part with these iconic McLaren Formula 1 cars because we feel that having them simply sitting in storage is a disservice to what they represent,” McLaren Racing CEO Zak Brown said.

    “These cars were built to be driven and raced, and we want to see them being used and enjoyed. We believe that is the best way to keep our history alive and our heritage business active.”

    There's no word of a price yet, but there are reports that estimate a combined value of somewhere between £25 to $50million. Unlike the Ecclestone collection, which was sold to just one buyer, these cars could be sold individually.

    “There is of course every possibility that the same could happen here. However, these five cars will be offered individually and there is no time pressure whatsoever,” Hartley said in a statement. “Our priority, and McLaren’s, is to find the right homes for them.”

    The cars will be prepared by McLaren before they are handed over to their new owners.
