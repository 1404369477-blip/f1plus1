    沃尔夫称不担心安东内利转投法拉利

    梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫表示，他“0.0%”不担心基米·安东内利有朝一日转投法拉利，并称安东内利及其家人忠诚。

    托托·沃尔夫不担心失去基米·安东内利转投法拉利

    托托·沃尔夫谈到基米·安东内利在其祖国意大利的地位，并解释了他为何不担心这位意大利车手在不久的将来转投法拉利

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯

    梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫“0.0%”不担心基米·安东内利可能在F1中转投法拉利。

    许多意大利人希望有朝一日能看到这位新科世界冠军坐进红色座舱，但据沃尔夫所说，这在可预见的未来不会发生。

    “因为基米和他的家人都是忠诚的人，”沃尔夫向《图片报》解释道，并明确表示他“确信”安东内利不会突然背弃梅赛德斯。“但我们并非只依赖忠诚。

    “本赛季已经表明，我们能够赢得比赛和冠军。这对任何车手来说都是最重要的。对基米也是如此。他属于梅赛德斯，就像车轮属于汽车一样。这就是为什么在赢得冠军后，他说他无法想象为梅赛德斯以外的任何车队效力。”

    梅赛德斯自安东内利卡丁车时期起就一直支持他，陪伴他走过每一个青训系列赛并进入F1——以及在今年夺得冠军。据沃尔夫所说，这在安东内利与梅赛德斯之间，以及与他个人之间，建立了紧密的纽带。

    “两三年前，他每次来布拉克利工厂时都住在我们家。他当时还是未成年人，所以我们不能随便把他安排到酒店。正因如此，他几乎成了我和[妻子]苏茜家里的常客，”这位奥地利人透露。

    “晚上我们经常一起看电影——不过实际上只有我一个人在看，因为苏茜看一部电影撑不过10分钟就会睡着，而基米在那些高强度的日子过后已经筋疲力尽。然后他们俩就肩并肩睡着了，我一个人把电影看完。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    比迈克尔·舒马赫更受欢迎？

    沃尔夫表示，安东内利不为法拉利效力这一事实，并不会削弱他在祖国的人气。这位奥地利人认为，这位出生于博洛尼亚的车手在意大利“绝对”比迈克尔·舒马赫在法拉利时期更受欢迎。

    “毕竟他是意大利人，”沃尔夫说，并补充道：“基米凭借他天生的举止和成功所取得的成就是，意大利仍然自然而然地支持自己的国家队法拉利，但也向这个小小的意大利人敞开了心扉和灵魂。”

    “铁杆车迷（Tifosi）现在穿着法拉利衬衫，戴着梅赛德斯帽子。蒙扎就是完美的例子。观众们实际上对两辆红色赛车都没有获得第一名感到难过，但他们很高兴他们自己人之一，基米，站在了领奖台的最高处。”

    如果沃尔夫如愿以偿，意大利人将需要他们的梅赛德斯帽子很多年。

    Toto Wolff is not afraid of losing Kimi Antonelli to Ferrari

    Toto Wolff discusses Kimi Antonelli’s status in his home country and explains why he is not afraid that the Italian could move to Ferrari in the near future

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff is “0.0%” afraid that Kimi Antonelli could switch to Ferrari in Formula 1.

    Many Italians would love to see the new world champion in a red cockpit one day, but according to Wolff, that will not happen in the foreseeable future.

    “Because Kimi and his family are loyal people,” Wolff explained to Bild, making clear that he is “convinced” Antonelli will not suddenly turn his back on Mercedes. “But we are not relying solely on loyalty.

    “This season has shown that we can win races and titles. That is the most important thing for any driver. For Kimi too. He belongs at Mercedes like wheels belong on a car. That is why, after winning the title, he said that he cannot imagine driving for any team other than Mercedes.”

    Mercedes has supported Antonelli since his karting days and accompanied him through every junior series and into F1 – and, this year, to the title. According to Wolff, this has created a close bond between Antonelli and Mercedes, as well as with him personally.

    “Two or three years ago, he always stayed at our home when he was at the factory in Brackley. He was still a minor, so we could not simply put him in a hotel. That is why he was practically a permanent guest at [wife] Susie’s and my place,” the Austrian revealed.

    “In the evenings, we often watched films together – although it was really only me, because Susie cannot stay awake through a film for more than 10 minutes and Kimi was completely exhausted after the intense days. Then the two of them fell asleep shoulder to shoulder, and I watched the film on my own.”

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    More popular than Michael Schumacher?

    According to Wolff, the fact that Antonelli does not drive for Ferrari does not diminish his popularity in his home country. The Austrian believes the Bologna-born racer is “definitely” more popular there than Michael Schumacher was during his Ferrari years.

    “He is Italian, after all,” Wolff said, adding: “What Kimi has achieved through his natural demeanour and success is that Italy still naturally roots for its national team, Ferrari, but has also opened its heart and soul to this little Italian.

    “The Tifosi now wear Ferrari shirts with Mercedes caps. Monza was the perfect example. The spectators were actually sad that neither of the red cars finished first, but they were happy that one of their own, Kimi, stood at the very top of the podium.”

    And if Wolff has his way, Italians will need their Mercedes caps for many more years.
