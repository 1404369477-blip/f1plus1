    勒克莱尔严重撞车引发意大利大奖赛提前红旗

    夏尔·勒克莱尔在意大利大奖赛第二圈末段于最后一个弯出弯时发生严重撞车，比赛随即被红旗中断。

    在第二圈结束时，夏尔·勒克莱尔（勒克莱尔）在最后一个弯道出弯处发生严重撞车后，F1意大利大奖赛被红旗中止。

    首圈法拉利两车并排驶入一号弯，汉密尔顿从内侧切入勒克莱尔——在汉密尔顿看来，勒克莱尔随后将他挤出了赛道，使其在二号弯陷入砂石区。

    勒克莱尔继续保持在第三位，然后在第二圈开始时短暂超越了杆位起步的皮埃尔·加斯利（加斯利），但随即被反超，后来又跌至第四位，落后于马克斯·维斯塔潘（维斯塔潘）的红牛赛车。

    随后勒克莱尔的比赛宣告结束：他在通过Ascari减速弯时出现晃动，在Parabolica（Alboreto）弯失去了对法拉利赛车的控制，高速撞上了护栏。

    皮亚斯特里 的迈凯伦当时正寻找机会切入弯角，而 Leclerc 似乎稍稍跑宽，压到了赛道旁的绿色柏油路面，他的法拉利随即发生了甩尾打转。

    Leclerc 很快从他撞毁的法拉利中爬了出来，安全车随即出动，随后红旗也迅速亮出。

    这次事故与 Leclerc 在 2020 年意大利大奖赛上于 Parabolica 弯撞车的情形非常相似。

    法拉利在比赛即将重启前——当地时间下午 3 点 39 分——表示，Leclerc 已经离开了医疗中心。

    红旗出示时，乔治·拉塞尔（拉塞尔）处于领先位置，他在第二圈起步时超过了加斯利（Gasly）的阿尔派赛车。

    加斯利位列第二，身后依次是维斯塔潘（Verdictappen）、阿尔派车队的第二位车手弗朗哥·科拉平托（科拉平托）、皮亚斯特里（Piastri）、他的迈凯伦队友兰多·诺里斯（诺里斯）、阿维德·林德布拉德（Arvid Lindblad，小红牛车队）以及汉密尔顿（Hamilton）。

    英文原文

    Formula 1's Italian Grand Prix has been red-flagged following a huge crash for Charles Leclerc on the exit of the final corner at the end of the second lap.

    The Ferraris ran side-by-side into the first turn on the opening lap with Hamilton diving down the inside of Leclerc - who, in Hamilton's view, proceeded to push him off the track and into the gravel at Turn 2.

    Leclerc continued in third, then briefly overtook polesitter Pierre Gasly at the start of lap two before being repassed, and later dropped to fourth behind Max Verstappen's Red Bull.

    And Leclerc's race then came to an end when he had a wobble through the Ascari chicane and lost control of his Ferrari at the Parabolica (Alboreto) and speared into the barriers at high speed.

    Oscar Piastri's McLaren had been looking for a move into the corner and Leclerc appeared to run slightly wide onto the green Tarmac just off the track, with his Ferrari swapping ends on him.

    Leclerc quickly emerged from his wrecked Ferrari and the safety car, then the red flag, were issued in quick succession.

    The incident was similar to Leclerc's crash at Parabolica in the 2020 Italian GP.

    Ferrari said shortly before the race was due to restart - at 3.39pm local time - that Leclerc had left the medical centre.

    When the red flag was called George Russell led, having overtaken Gasly's Alpine at the start of lap two.

    Gasly was second ahead of Verstappen, the second Alpine of Franco Colapinto, Piastri, his McLaren team-mate Lando Norris, Arvid Lindblad (Racing Bulls) and Hamilton.
