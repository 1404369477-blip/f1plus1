    拉塞尔携手银石推出卡丁车学院

    梅赛德斯F1车手乔治·拉塞尔与银石合作推出GR63卡丁车学院，面向8至9岁男孩和女孩开放，旨在让赛车运动更易参与并发掘年轻新星。

    这位梅赛德斯F1车手希望让赛车运动在低龄阶段更易参与。

    F1明星乔治·拉塞尔与银石合作推出了一所卡丁车学院，旨在让赛车运动更易参与并发掘下一位年轻超级新星。

    这位八次大奖赛冠军于周四推出GR63卡丁车学院，面向8至9岁的男孩和女孩开放，设在银石的卡丁车赛道。

    学院设有“四阶段通往顶峰之路”，将于12月启动，首阶段为开放报名形式，邀请所有符合条件者前往银石，随后将选出80人进入第二阶段。

    这意味着80名车手将于1月31日参加“GR63选拔赛”，以决出25个名额，参加2027年3月至11月在银石赛道举行的八轮锦标赛。

    每位车手都将获得全额资助，前五名将在明年12月的第四阶段进行正面交锋，获胜者将获得2028年全额资助的卡丁车赛事机会。

    “卡丁车是我一切开始的地方，”拉塞尔说道，他曾在国家和国际级别赢得冠军，之后晋级到单座赛车比赛。

    “它教会了我整个职业生涯中所依赖的一切。我知道有天赋的年轻车手获得第一次机会有多难，尤其是在预算紧张的时候。”

    “通过GR63卡丁车学院，我想回馈社会，发掘那些隐藏的天才，并为他们提供所需的支持和平台，帮助他们发挥潜力。”

    “我非常感谢银石赛道和英国赛车手俱乐部帮助促成此事。”

    该学院面向那些“可能没有足够预算在赛车运动中晋级”的人，因为无论是基层还是顶级赛车运动的成本都在增加。

    因此，进入学院初始阶段的费用为25英镑，参加“GR63选拔赛”的费用则升至75英镑，而从第三阶段开始，所有费用将得到全额资助。

    BRDC 主席大卫·库特哈德表示：“作为银石赛道的所有者以及英国赛车运动的守护者，BRDC 始终致力于对这项运动进行再投资，并培养本土人才。

    “这一承诺在英国赛车运动长期的成功记录中发挥了重要作用，也是俱乐部及其会员深感自豪的源泉。

    “GR63 学院完美地体现了这一精神，我们很高兴与乔治合作，在银石赛道支持下一代车手。”

    所有车手都必须是英联邦国家的公民，如需预约第一阶段（即到场驾驶环节），请点击此处银石赛道网站的链接。

    The Mercedes F1 driver is hoping to make motorsport more accessible at a young age

    Formula 1 star George Russell has launched a karting academy in partnership with Silverstone to make motorsport more accessible and discover the next young superstar.

    The eight-time grand prix winner launched his GR63 Karting Academy on Thursday with it open to boys and girls aged eight to nine, based at the kart track in Silverstone.

    It features a “four stage path to the top” and will start in December with an open-access format inviting every eligible driver to Silverstone, where 80 will be selected for stage two.

    That will see the 80 drivers compete in the “GR63 Shootout” on 31 January to determine the 25 slots for an eight-round championship at Silverstone from March to November in 2027.

    The championship is fully-funded for each driver and the top five will go head-to-head in stage four next December, where the winner will receive a fully-funded karting campaign in 2028.

    “Karting is where it all started for me,” said Russell, who won titles on a national and international level before progressing into single-seater competition.

    “It taught me everything I’ve relied on throughout my career. I know how hard it is for talented young drivers to get that first opportunity, especially when budgets are tight.

    “With the GR63 Karting Academy, I want to give something back by finding those hidden talents and giving them the support and platform they need to reach their potential.

    “I'm really grateful to Silverstone and the British Racing Drivers’ Club for helping make this happen."

    The academy is designed for those who “may not have the budget required to progress through the sport” with the costs in motorsport, both at grassroots level and at the top, increasing.

    So costs to enter the academy’s opening stage are £25 with that rising to £75 for the “GR63 Shootout”, before everything is fully-funded from stage three onwards.

    BRDC president David Coulthard said: “As owners of Silverstone and guardians of British motorsport, the BRDC has always been committed to reinvesting in the sport and nurturing home-grown talent.

    “That commitment has played a big part in Britain’s long record of success in motorsport, and it is a great source of pride for the Club and its Members.

    “The GR63 Academy captures that ethos perfectly, and we are delighted to work with George to support the next generation of drivers here at Silverstone."
