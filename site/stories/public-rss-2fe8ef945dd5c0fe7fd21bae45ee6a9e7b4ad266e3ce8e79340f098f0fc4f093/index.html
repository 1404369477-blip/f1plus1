    安东内利有望加冕仍坚持自己洗车

    安东内利本周末有望在美国大奖赛提前锁定车手总冠军，他透露自己仍坚持亲手洗车。

    安东内利有望加冕仍坚持自己洗车

    梅赛德斯车手安东内利透露，他仍坚持亲手洗车。

    致梅赛德斯车迷：来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    安东内利本周末有望加冕F1车手总冠军，但在讲述自己赛道之外如何度过时间时，他透露自己仍坚持亲手洗车。

    这位梅赛德斯车手抵达奥斯汀时，领先队友乔治·拉塞尔102分，并有机会在本周末的美国大奖赛上锁定车手总冠军。这将使他成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军，也是自1953年阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来首位夺得该头衔的意大利人。

    在梅赛德斯美国大奖赛特别涂装发布活动上，这位20岁的车手表示，他在家的日常安排包括清洗自己的梅赛德斯-AMG GT63公路跑车。

    “我喜欢洗我的车，”安东内利说。“我从来不用自动洗车机，你知道的，那种机器。我总是喜欢自己动手洗。因为我热爱汽车，我真的很喜欢打理我的车和一切。

    “我喜欢看到它干干净净的，所以这是我热爱做的一件事。这是一种日常习惯。”

    在德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的美国大奖赛，拉开了2026赛季倒数第二个三连赛的序幕。梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫认为，在美洲赛道举行的这个周末，将给这支位于布拉克利的车队一个机会，在经历了马来西亚和新加坡两个艰难的周末后做出“回应”。

    “马来西亚和新加坡对我们来说是艰难的周末，我们已经努力去理解我们在哪里做得不够好，”沃尔夫在车队的美国大奖赛前瞻中表示。“奥斯汀给了我们一个做出回应的机会。”

    “我们将在两辆车上运行最新的升级套件，同时继续研发赛车。竞争格局非常接近，所以如果我们想在赛季最后阶段持续在前方竞争，就需要不断向前推进。”

    “美国大奖赛开启了又一个要求很高的三连赛。有很多利害攸关的事情，但我们不会好高骛远。优先事项是眼前的这个周末：执行到位，最大化我们的表现，一场一场地应对每场比赛。”

    “奥斯汀对我们来说也将是一个重要的场外周末，全市将举办多项商业活动。其中的核心将是AMG和MANUFAKTUR的接管，在整个周末展现更广泛的梅赛德斯家族的卓越性能、精湛工艺和个性。我们期待展示这一点，并最终在最重要的地方——赛道上——交出答卷。”

    Kimi Antonelli reveals down-to-earth habit ahead of potential F1 title win

    Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli has revealed that he still washes his own car by hand

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Kimi Antonelli could be crowned Formula 1 champion this weekend, but while explaining how he spends his time away from the track, he revealed that he still washes his own car by hand.

    The Mercedes driver arrived in Austin with a 102-point lead over his team-mate George Russell and has the opportunity to claim the drivers' championship at this weekend's United States Grand Prix. That would make him F1's youngest champion and the first Italian to win the title since Alberto Ascari in 1953.

    While speaking during the launch event of the Mercedes United States Grand Prix livery, the 20-year-old explained that his at-home routine includes cleaning his Mercedes-AMG GT63 road car.

    "I like to clean my car," Antonelli said. "I never do automatic, you know, with the machine. I always like to wash by hand, myself. Because I love cars, I really like to take care of my car and everything.

    "I like to see it clean, so it’s an activity I love to do. It’s a routine."

    The United States Grand Prix in Austin, Texas, kicks off the penultimate triple-header of the 2026 season. Mercedes team chief Toto Wolff believes the weekend at the Circuit of the Americas will give the Brackley outfit an opportunity to "respond" after two difficult weekends in Malaysia and Singapore.

    "Malaysia and Singapore were difficult weekends for us and we have worked hard to understand where we fell short," Wolff said in the team's United States Grand Prix preview. "Austin gives us an opportunity to respond.

    "We will run our latest update package on both cars as we continue to develop the car. The field is incredibly close, so we need to keep moving forward if we want to fight consistently at the front in the closing stages of the season.

    "The United States Grand Prix begins another demanding triple-header. There is plenty at stake, but we won’t get ahead of ourselves. The priority is the weekend in front of us: execute well, maximise our performance and take each race as it comes.

    "Austin will also be an important weekend for us off track, with a number of commercial activities taking place across the city. At the heart of that will be AMG and the MANUFAKTUR takeover, bringing to life the performance, craftsmanship and individuality of the wider Mercedes family throughout the weekend. We are looking forward to showcasing that and, ultimately, delivering where it matters most: on track."
