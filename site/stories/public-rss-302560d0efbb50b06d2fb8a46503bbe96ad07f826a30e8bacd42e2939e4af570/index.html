    Lando Norris reacts to the news that the Monaco Grand Prix will be a Sprint event in 2027

    World champion Lando Norris believes Formula 1 drivers will face a greater challenge at next year's Monaco Grand Prix than normal due to the addition of the Sprint format.

    The 24-round 2027 calendar, published on Wednesday, includes 10 Sprint weekends - four more than this season - with Monaco's street circuit to host the alternative format for the first time.

    The Principaliy's narrow track is not known for overtaking, where the Sprint format has hitherto tended to be used, but is considered arguably the greatest challenge in the sport for an F1 driver when it comes to qualifying.
