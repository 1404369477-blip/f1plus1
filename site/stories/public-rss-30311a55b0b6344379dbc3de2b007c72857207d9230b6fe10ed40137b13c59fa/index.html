    红牛为F1 2027赛季保留伊萨克·哈贾尔，与马克斯·维斯塔潘搭档

    伊萨克·哈贾尔赢得了与四届世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘并肩作战的第二个红牛F1赛季

    红牛已确认2027赛季F1车手阵容保持不变，伊萨克·哈贾尔将继续与马克斯·维斯塔潘搭档。

    哈贾尔在姊妹车队小红牛度过新秀赛季后，于2026年升入红牛，并在这一历史上颇具挑战性的红牛二号席位上展现出速度。

    这位法国车手因伤缺席三场比赛后，将于本周末在阿塞拜疆站回归赛场，并将在2027赛季继续与维斯塔潘并肩作战，后者近期已续约至2030年。

    “我们非常兴奋能继续与伊萨克同行，”红牛车队负责人洛朗·梅基斯表示。“自今年年初加入我们以来，他在场上和场下都表现出色，尤其是考虑到这只是他第二个F1赛季，这让续约成为轻而易举的决定。”

    “从一开始，伊萨克就全身心融入车队，在工厂与工程师们分秒必争地研发赛车，始终渴望学习和进步。从墨尔本的第一场比赛就能看出，他以非凡的沉着应对了这次跃升，并赢得了这个席位。”

    尽管缺席了赞德沃特、蒙扎和马德里三站比赛，哈贾尔仍位居积分榜第八，这得益于他在摩纳哥为红牛拿下首个领奖台，加上2025年的首个领奖台，以及在红牛这辆大体上是F1第三或第四快赛车上连续七次进入前六名的表现。

    “我非常兴奋能继续与Oracle红牛车队合作，”这位21岁的车手说道。“工厂里有太多才华横溢的人，你清楚能为这样一支不可思议的车队效力，与像马克斯这样的四届世界冠军并肩作战是一种特权。”

    “从我签约的那一刻起，我就立志为车队奉献一切，在洛朗和米尔顿凯恩斯所有人的支持下，我得以证明他们对我的信任是正确的。我不断寻求倾听、学习，并每天推动自己更进一步，这只会让我对未来的前景更加兴奋。”

    小红牛尚未确认其2027赛季车手阵容，现任车手利亚姆·劳森和阿尔维德·林德布拉德本赛季均有亮眼表现，车队还有青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫待命。

    Red Bull retains Isack Hadjar alongside Max Verstappen for F1 2027

    Isack Hadjar has earned a second Red Bull Formula 1 campaign alongside four-time world champion Max Verstappen

    Red Bull has confirmed it will field an unchanged Formula 1 driver line-up in 2027, with Isack Hadjar staying put alongside Max Verstappen.

    Hadjar stepped up in 2026 after a rookie season at sister team Racing Bulls, and has impressed with his speed in what has historically been a difficult second Red Bull seat.

    The Frenchman, who is making his return in Azerbaijan this weekend after missing three races through injury, will stay on board for another campaign in 2027 alongside Verstappen, who recently signed a contract extension to 2030.

    "We’re very excited to continue our journey with Isack," said Red Bull team boss Laurent Mekies. "Since joining us at the start of the year, he has been superb on and off track, especially considering this is only his second Formula 1 season, which made extending his contract an easy decision.

    "From the very beginning, Isack immersed himself in the team, spending every minute at the factory with his engineers to develop the car, always eager to learn and improve. You could see from that first weekend in Melbourne that he handled the step up with remarkable composure and had earned his place in this seat."

    Despite sitting out Zandvoort, Monza and Madrid, Hadjar still holds eighth in the standings, boosted by a first Red Bull podium in Monaco to complement his maiden podium in 2025, as well as a streak of seven consecutive top-six finishes in what has largely been F1's third or fourth quickest car.

    "I am really excited to continue with Oracle Red Bull Racing," the 21-year-old said. "There are so many talented people at the factory, and you’re aware of the privilege of driving for such an incredible team alongside a four-time world champion like Max.

    "From the moment I signed, I set out to dedicate myself to the team, and with the support of Laurent and everyone in Milton Keynes, I’ve been able to justify the faith they’ve had in me. I’m constantly looking to listen, learn and push myself further every single day, which only makes me more excited about what lies ahead."

    Racing Bulls is yet to confirm its own driver line-up for 2027, with incumbents Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad having both impressed this season while the squad also has junior driver Nikola Tsolov waiting in the wings.
