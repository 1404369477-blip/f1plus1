    罗斯伯格认为乔治·罗素无法在2027年F1夺冠：他已经陷入心理困境

    乔治·罗素在2026赛季初期被视为梅赛德斯车队的不二领袖，但前世界冠军尼科·罗斯伯格认为28岁的罗素在下赛季的冠军争夺战中无望。

    尼科·罗斯伯格将乔治·罗素排除在2027年F1冠军争夺之外：“自己挖了一个心理陷阱”

    尼科·罗斯伯格将乔治·罗素排除在他的2027年F1冠军热门之外，警告这位梅赛德斯车手挖了一个"心理陷阱"

    对于梅赛德斯车迷来说。这里有来自银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析，来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯。

    乔治·罗素进入2026年Formula 1赛季时，看起来像是梅赛德斯无可争议的领导者。九个月之后，前银箭世界冠军尼科·罗斯伯格甚至不认为这位28岁的车手是下赛季的冠军争夺者。

    德国媒体Sport Bild采访中，罗斯伯格预测2027年拉塞尔不会出现快速反弹——将卫冕冠军安东内利、维斯塔潘、诺里斯，甚至法拉利的困顿搭档汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔排在拉塞尔之前。

    将拉塞尔排除在这个名单之外，罗斯伯格承认这在纸面上听起来“有些疯狂”。然而，对于41岁的罗斯伯格来说，看着一个青少年掌控车队的心理冲击留下了深刻的印记。

    罗斯伯格说，拉塞尔“自己挖了一个心理的坑”。“他想着：‘我现在是F1的国王，我正在统治这个赛季。’然后这个年轻的车手出现了，去年他几乎要离开F1，但他统治得如此之好，以至于乔治再也没有机会了。从那么高的地方跌落到地下室，心理上非常困难。”

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·安东内利，梅赛德斯

    这场逆转在任何标准下都十分剧烈。在2025年，拉塞尔轻松地处理了他的新晋队友，在排位赛和大奖赛中都以21-3的成绩击败安东内利，总积分以319分对150分的优势领先。当拉塞尔在2026年从杆位起步，在墨尔本赢得比赛时，这看起来像是冠军争夺战的自然开端。

    然而，整个赛季完全逆转。经过21轮比赛，安东内利以402分，9个胜利和16个领奖台成绩结束赛季——在墨西哥城已经锁定了车手冠军——而拉塞尔落后138分，以264分结束赛季，有3个胜利（澳大利亚、奥地利和阿塞拜疆）和8个领奖台成绩。

    六场比赛没有领奖台——以及代价高昂的拉斯维加斯失误。

    拉塞尔赛季末期的下滑使得对他的审视更加严格。自从9月底在巴库从杆位赢得比赛以来，他已经连续六场大奖赛没有领奖台——这是一段艰难的时期，包括在马来西亚退赛、在新加坡排名第九、在奥斯汀排名第八、在墨西哥城排名第六和在圣保罗排名第四。

    拉斯维加斯带来了又一次痛苦的挫折。在拉斯维加斯大道上从第二位起步，并坚定地保持在领先集团中，拉塞尔在48圈时在5号弯道锁死在护栏上，与安东内利搏斗——在终点线前跌落到第九位，而维斯塔潘夺得了胜利。

    在拉斯维加斯之前，拉塞尔就坦率地谈到了这一年对他有多大的压力。在安东内利在墨西哥夺冠后，这位英国人承认2026年是"我职业生涯中第一个坏日子比好日子多的完整赛季"。

    "这很难处理吗？是的，非常难，"拉塞尔在11月写道。"每周都要振作起来很难吗？是的，非常难。有时会感到困惑？经常。我所知道的是，我已经全力以赴，2026年本不属于我。

    车队负责人托托·沃尔夫将问题归咎于机械而不是心态，在奥斯汀之后指责梅赛德斯W17的"操作窗口"很窄，并指出车队必须扩大这个甜点区域，以便拉塞尔能够提取出稳定的速度。在赛季末段之前，拉塞尔语气更加坚定，坚持自己"没有遗憾"，并准备"比任何人都要多"来解决汽车的怪癖。

    与安东内利一起在2027年已经锁定梅赛德斯车队的席位，拉塞尔目前并不面临立即的合同风险。然而，在11月27日至29日的卡塔尔（Qatar）和12月4日至6日的阿布扎比（Abu Dhabi）的比赛中，他与诺里斯（诺里斯）积分相同，均为264分，并且仅比汉密尔顿（汉密尔顿）少3分，位居第三。在最后两个周末取得强劲的收官表现，将是他首次有机会证明罗斯伯格（Rosberg）的悲观预测是错误的。

    Nico Rosberg rules George Russell out of 2027 F1 title fight: “Dug himself into a mental hole”

    Nico Rosberg leaves George Russell out of his 2027 F1 title favorites, warning the Mercedes star dug a "mental hole"

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell entered the 2026 Formula 1 campaign looking every bit like Mercedes’ undisputed leader. Nine months later, former Silver Arrows world champion Nico Rosberg does not even consider the 28-year-old a championship contender for next season.

    Speaking to German outlet Sport Bild, Rosberg predicted no quick rebound for Russell in 2027 - placing defending champion Kimi Antonelli, Max Verstappen, Lando Norris, and even Ferrari’s embattled pairing of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc ahead of him in the title pecking order.

    Leaving Russell off that list was something Rosberg admitted sounds "somehow crazy" on paper. Yet to the 41-year-old, the psychological whiplash of watching a teenager seize control of the garage has left a deep mark.

    Russell has "dug himself into a mental hole," Rosberg said. "He thought: 'I am now the king of Formula 1, I'm dominating the season.' And then this young driver comes along, who was almost on his way out of Formula 1 last year, and dominates so much that George doesn't stand a chance anymore. Going from so high up down into the basement like that is mentally extremely hard."

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    That reversal was sharp by any measure. In 2025, Russell handled his rookie team-mate with ease, beating Antonelli 21–3 in both qualifying and grands prix while outscoring him 319 points to 150. When Russell opened 2026 from pole with a Melbourne win, it looked like the natural start of a title charge.

    Instead, the campaign flipped completely. Through 21 rounds, Antonelli sits on 402 points with nine victories and 16 podiums - having already sealed the drivers’ title in Mexico City - while Russell trails by 138 points on 264 with three wins (Australia, Austria, and Azerbaijan) and eight podiums.

    Six races without a podium - and a costly Las Vegas lapse

    Russell’s late-season slide has only sharpened the scrutiny. Since winning from pole in Baku in late September, he has gone six consecutive grands prix without a podium - a rough stretch featuring a retirement in Malaysia, ninth in Singapore, eighth in Austin, sixth in Mexico City, and fourth in Sao Paulo.

    Las Vegas brought another painful setback. Starting second on the Strip and running firmly in the lead pack, Russell locked up into the Turn 5 barrier on Lap 48 while fighting Antonelli - tumbling to ninth at the chequered flag as Verstappen claimed victory.

    Even before Las Vegas, Russell was candid about how heavily the year had weighed on him. Reflecting after Antonelli clinched the crown in Mexico, the Briton admitted 2026 marked "the first proper season of my career where I've had more bad days than good".

    "Has it been tough to deal with? Yes, very," Russell wrote in November. "Has it been tough to pick myself up week in, week out? Yes, very. Confusion at times? Often. All I know is that I've given it my all and 2026 was not meant to be my year."

    Team principal Toto Wolff has pointed to machinery rather than mindset, blaming the Mercedes W17’s narrow "operational window" after Austin and noting the squad must widen that sweet spot so Russell can extract consistent pace. Russell himself struck a firmer tone ahead of the run-in, insisting he carries "no regrets" and prepared "more than anybody" to solve the car’s quirks.

    With his Mercedes seat already secured for 2027 alongside Antonelli, Russell does not face immediate contract jeopardy. Still, entering Qatar (27-29 November) and Abu Dhabi (4-6 December) tied with Norris on 264 points - and just three shy of Hamilton for third overall - a strong finish over the final two weekends gives him his first chance to prove Rosberg’s bleak forecast wrong.
