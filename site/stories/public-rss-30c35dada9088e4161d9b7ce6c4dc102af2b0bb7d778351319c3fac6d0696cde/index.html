    皮亚斯特里认为，迈凯伦在巴西大奖赛排位赛中换上半雨胎的时机太晚。

    这位澳大利亚车手在因雨推迟的英特拉格斯排位赛中位列第12，不过在法拉利的夏尔·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿因动力单元更换被罚后，他将从第10位起步。

    皮亚斯特里使用全雨胎通过了第一节排位赛，但赛道条件已改善到足以让各车队在第二节排位赛中换上半雨胎。

    “我刚完成一圈，但我觉得我们换上干胎可能稍晚了一些，需要再多一两圈才能真正让轮胎达到温度，”皮亚斯特里在排位赛后向媒体解释道。

    “我在全雨胎上使用的一些走线在半雨胎上肯定行不通，所以当然有一些东西需要复盘，但我认为再多一圈、或许做出稍好一些的决定，我们本可以跻身其中。”

    兰多·诺里斯，迈凯伦，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，迈凯伦

    当被要求澄清他是否在指责红旗导致他损失了那一圈时，这位迈凯伦车手补充道：“我肯定本可以再多跑一圈，但这对每个人来说都是一样的。”

    “每个人都会多跑一圈，所以我不确定。我认为肯定还有更多时间可以争取，但你只需要在正确的时间用上正确的轮胎。”

    展望正赛，皮亚斯特里解释说，如果雨水再次降临，比赛将会很有挑战性。

    他说：“我们先看看天气如何，再作打算。我认为雨战会非常具有挑战性，所以看看情况再说。”

    皮亚斯特里的队友兰多·诺里斯锁定了巴西大奖赛的杆位。诺里斯和皮亚斯特里目前分别以221分和144分位列车手积分榜第六和第七。

    Oscar Piastri explains what led to Brazilian GP qualifying disappointment

    Oscar Piastri believes McLaren's late switch to intermediate tyres cost him a place in Q3 during a rain-affected Brazilian Grand Prix qualifying session

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Oscar Piastri believes McLaren left it too late to switch to intermediate tyres in qualifying for the Brazilian Grand Prix.

    The Australian driver qualified 12th in a rain-delayed session at Interlagos, although he will start from 10th after penalties are applied to Ferrari's Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton for power unit changes.

    Piastri made it through Q1 on full wet tyres, but conditions had improved enough for teams to switch to intermediates in Q2.

    "I'd just finished a lap, but I think we probably went onto the inters a little bit too late and needed that extra lap or two to really get some temperature in them," Piastri explained to the media after the session.

    "Some of the lines I was using on the wets definitely didn't work on the inters, so some things to review, of course, but I think with another lap and maybe slightly better decisions we could have been there."

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Oscar Piastri, McLaren

    When asked to clarify if he was blaming the red flag for costing him the lap, the McLaren driver added: "I definitely would have got one more lap in, but it's the same for everybody.

    "Everyone would have got another lap, so I'm not sure. I think there was definitely a lot more time out there, but you just need to be on the right tyre at the right time."

    Looking ahead to the grand prix, Piastri explained the race will be challenging if the rain returns.

    "We'll see what weather we get first and go from there. I think it's going to be very challenging in the rain, so let's see what we get," he said.

    Piastri's team-mate Lando Norris secured pole position for the Brazilian Grand Prix. Norris and Piastri currently sit sixth and seventh in the drivers' standings with 221 and 144 points, respectively.
