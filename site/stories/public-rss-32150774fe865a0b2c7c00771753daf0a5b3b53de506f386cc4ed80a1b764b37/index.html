    梅基斯称巴西站是哈贾尔在红牛的最佳比赛

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯认为，伊萨克·哈贾尔在2026年圣保罗大奖赛获得季军的表现为其加盟红牛以来的“最佳比赛”。

    洛朗·梅基斯：巴西站是伊萨克·哈贾尔在红牛的“最佳比赛”

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯称赞伊萨克·哈贾尔在巴西站的登台表现是他在车队中的“最佳比赛”

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    2026年圣保罗大奖赛对红牛车队而言是一个意义重大的周末。马克斯·维斯塔潘从第21位发车、以不可思议的追击最终获得第二名，抢走了头条；而他的队友伊萨克·哈贾尔则悄然交出了车队领队洛朗·梅基斯眼中他迄今最令人印象深刻的一场驾驶。

    哈贾尔为米尔顿凯恩斯的车队锁定了双领奖台，他从第五位发车，在英特拉格斯获得第三名，巩固了他2026赛季第三次登上领奖台的成绩。但据梅基斯所说，真正引人注目的不仅仅是最终结果，而是这位年轻法国车手在快速变化、险恶的条件下展现出的纯粹速度和比赛技巧。

    当被问及哈贾尔是否即将在合适的环境下挑战分站冠军时，梅基斯毫不吝惜赞美之词。

    “我认为，如果你现在问我，我觉得这是他与我们一起跑得最好的一场比赛，”梅基斯在赛后对记者说。“就纯粹速度而言，这正是我们所关注的，你知道，在干地上、在半雨胎条件下、在极其困难的条件下展现出的纯粹速度，以及他在比赛中的聪明才智。”

    哈贾尔在英特拉格斯的表现进一步证明了红牛确认他参加2027赛季的决定是正确的。梅基斯指出，这种稳步提升的轨迹正是车队在锁定他未来时所寄望的。

    梅基斯解释道：“如你所知，他今年跑了很多场非常强劲的比赛……但我认为他又一次提高了标杆，你知道这就是我们想继续看到的。”“你知道，这就是我们在决定确认他2027年席位时印象最深刻的地方，就是他一直在不断提高标杆，我们只是希望能为他提供继续做到这一点的环境。”

    哈贾尔在比赛中相当长的一段时间里，在相同轮胎策略下追平或超过了维斯塔潘的速度，证明他具备在发车格最前方竞速的速度。

    比赛中红牛车库的主要话题之一涉及车队指令。第38圈时，哈贾尔正跑在第二位，他接到指令让维斯塔潘——当时正在从后方一路追赶——超过他。在无线电中，哈贾尔询问如果维斯塔潘追不上领先的诺里斯，位置是否会换回来。

    梅基斯直接回应了无线电通话，确认换回位置绝对在考虑之中，但这场混乱比赛的实际情况最终阻止了这一点。

    “是的，我认为如果赛车一直保持接近，我们本来会这么做的；我们本来会做的，”梅基斯透露。“因为伊萨克的速度非常强劲，而且他一直紧跟着马克斯，正如我们在换位之后看到的那样。”

    兰多·诺里斯，迈凯伦，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，伊萨克·哈贾尔，红牛车队

    但随着圈数逐渐减少，圣保罗的天气又抛出了新的变数。

    “但后来，随着比赛推进、雨又下起来，赛车之间距离非常非常远，那样做就没有意义了，再加上最后雨势加大，那样做也完全没有意义，”梅基斯解释道。

    尽管没有进行反向交换，梅基斯仍称赞了两位车手展现出的团队合作。

    “但我必须说，马克斯和伊萨克非常、非常紧密地合作，才在这条赛道上取得了最佳结果，”这位红牛车队负责人总结道。

    2026赛季（希望）还剩三站比赛，哈贾尔日益增长的自信和原始速度表明，红牛已经为其未来锁定了一位能力出众的车手。

    Laurent Mekies: Brazil was Isack Hadjar’s “best race” for Red Bull

    Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies praises Isack Hadjar's podium drive in Brazil as his "best race" with the team

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    The 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix was a monumental weekend for Red Bull Racing. While Max Verstappen stole the headlines with a logic-defying charge from 21st to finish second, his team-mate Isack Hadjar quietly delivered what team principal Laurent Mekies believes is his most impressive drive yet.

    Securing a double podium for the Milton Keynes squad, Hadjar finished third at Interlagos after starting fifth, cementing his third podium appearance of the 2026 season. But according to Mekies, it wasn't just the final result that stood out, it was the raw pace and racecraft the young Frenchman displayed in rapidly changing, treacherous conditions.

    When asked if Hadjar is on the verge of challenging for outright wins in the right circumstances, Mekies didn't hold back his praise.

    "I think ultimately if you ask me right now, I think it’s the best race he has done with us," Mekies told reporters following the race. "In terms of pure pace, and that’s what we look at, you know, pure pace on the dry, on the inters, super difficult conditions, in how clever he has been racing."

    Hadjar's performance at Interlagos adds further justification to Red Bull's decision to confirm him for the 2027 season. Mekies noted that this steady trajectory of improvement is exactly what the team was banking on when it secured his future.

    "He has done a lot of very strong racing this year as you know... but I think he has raised the bar another time, so you know that’s what we want to keep seeing," Mekies explained. "You know that’s what we said we were most impressed by when we took the decisions to confirm him for '27 is the way he has been keeping raising the bar, and we were simply hoping to [provide] the environment for him to keep doing that."

    Hadjar spent a significant portion of the race matching or exceeding Verstappen's pace on the same tyre strategy, proving he has the speed to run at the very front of the grid.

    One of the major talking points from the Red Bull garage during the race involved a team order. On lap 38, Hadjar was running in second place when he was instructed to let Verstappen - who was charging through the field on a recovery drive - pass him. Over the radio, Hadjar asked if the positions would be swapped back if Verstappen couldn't catch race leader Lando Norris.

    Mekies addressed the radio traffic directly, confirming that a swap-back was absolutely on the table, but the circumstances of the chaotic race ultimately prevented it.

    "Yeah, I think we would have done it if the car had stayed close together; we would have done it," Mekies revealed. "Because Isack had a very strong pace and he stayed with Max, as we have seen after the swap."

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    But as the laps ticked down, the weather in São Paulo threw another curve ball.

    "But then, as the race panned out and the rain came again, the cars were, you know, very, very distant, and it would not have made sense, plus the increase rain intensity at the end, like it would not have made any sense," Mekies clarified.

    Despite the lack of a reverse swap, Mekies praised the teamwork displayed by both drivers.

    "But I have to say Max and Isack worked very, very strongly together to get the best of the circuit," the Red Bull boss concluded.

    With three rounds (hopefully) remaining in the 2026 season, Hadjar's growing confidence and raw speed suggest that Red Bull has secured a highly capable driver for its future.
