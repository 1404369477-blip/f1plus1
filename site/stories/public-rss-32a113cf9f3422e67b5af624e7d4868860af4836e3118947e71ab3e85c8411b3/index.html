    詹姆斯·沃尔斯称威廉姆斯F1重建为“人生之旅”

    威廉姆斯车队领队詹姆斯·沃尔斯表示，带领车队重返F1顶级竞争水平将成为他的“人生之旅”。

    James Vowles称威廉姆斯 F1的重建是他的“人生之旅”

    James Vowles表示，将威廉姆斯重建为具有竞争力的F1力量将成为他的“人生之旅”

    威廉姆斯车队领队James Vowles宣称，让这支位于Grove的车队重新达到F1所需的竞争水平，将成为他的“人生之旅”。

    Vowles于2023年加入威廉姆斯，面临着将过时的基础设施提升到标准的巨大挑战，并重建车队使其重返昔日夺冠时的辉煌。

    在车队官方播客 Team Torque 中，Vowles 与 阿尔本 和 塞恩斯 一起，回顾了 威廉姆斯 正在进行的长期建设过程。

    在回答一位车迷关于如何在 威廉姆斯 这样规模的团队中培养韧性和心理承受力的问题时，Vowles 说：“生活中有很多艰难的经历，但只要你以正确的方式去面对，你就能以一个更好的人从另一端走出来。”

    “第二点是我固执且有毅力，我们一定会达到所需的水平。实现这一目标将成为我毕生的追求，但这同样也是你们两位（Albon 和 Sainz）的追求，而其背后的现实是，这会造就一种无人能摧毁的力量。”

    威廉姆斯 的 2026 赛季开局艰难，他们带着一辆超重的赛车抵达巴林参加季前测试，并且错过了巴塞罗那的私人测试。但 Albon 和 Sainz 都在荷兰大奖赛之前签署了续约合同。

    沃尔斯在播客中谈到了两位车手的承诺。他说：“感谢你们两位，明年能让你在我身边、成为威廉姆斯的一员，并希望未来许多许多年都如此，将是我的莫大荣幸。”

    阿尔本已签约延长一年至2027年底，塞恩斯则签署了一份新的多年期合同。

    James Vowles calls Williams F1 rebuild his "life's journey"

    James Vowles says rebuilding Williams into a competitive Formula 1 force will become his "life's journey"

    Williams team principal James Vowles has declared that returning the Grove outfit to the required competitive level in Formula 1 will become his "life's journey".

    Vowles joined Williams in 2023 and was faced with the immense challenge of bringing the outdated infrastructure up to scratch and rebuilding the team to return it to its championship-winning form.

    Speaking on the team's official Team Torque podcast alongside Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz, Vowles reflected on the long-term building process underway at Williams.

    Responding to a fan question about how to build resilience and mental toughness in a team the size of Williams, Vowles said: "There are so many life experiences that are hard that as long as you have the right approach to it, you come out the other side a better person.

    "The second is that I am stubborn and persistent and we will be at the level we need to be. This will become my life's journey to achieve it, but as it is the both of yours [Albon and Sainz] as well, but the reality behind that is that it creates a strength that no one can break as a result of it."

    Williams has had a tough start to its 2026 campaign, arriving at pre-season testing in Bahrain with an overweight car and having missed the private test at Barcelona. But both Albon and Sainz signed contract extensions ahead of the Dutch Grand Prix.

    Vowles touched on the commitment from both drivers during the podcast. "Thank you to both of you, but it'll be an absolute honour to have you by my side and as part of Williams for next year and hopefully many, many years beyond," he said.

    Albon has signed a one-year extension until the end of 2027 and Sainz has signed a new multi-year deal.
