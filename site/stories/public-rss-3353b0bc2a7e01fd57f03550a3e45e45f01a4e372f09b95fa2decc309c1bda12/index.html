    F1卡塔尔大奖赛：安东内利领跑两次红旗中断的二练

    安东内利在两次红旗中断的卡塔尔大奖赛第二节练习中排名第一，梅赛德斯包揽前二。二练在更凉爽的夜间进行，赛道温度比一练低约10摄氏度，更接近周六排位赛的条件。

    F1卡塔尔大奖赛：安东内利领跑两次红旗中断的二练

    安东内利和拉塞尔让梅赛德斯在F1卡塔尔大奖赛第二节练习中保持领先

    安东内利在两次红旗中断的第二节练习中排名第一，横扫周五在多哈进行的卡塔尔大奖赛练习。

    二练在更凉爽的夜间条件下进行，为周六同一时段的排位赛提供了更具参考价值的条件，赛道温度比下午酷热的一练低约10摄氏度。

    不过，正如之前的情况一样，梅赛德斯从一开始就占据了圈速榜的领先位置，乔治·拉塞尔的早期成绩为1分23秒715，随后被基米·安东内利的1分23秒077超越，几乎整个车阵在开场阶段都使用了中性胎。

    赛道温度的下降似乎并没有让卢赛尔这条高下压力的摩托车赛道变得更容易驾驭，查尔斯·勒克莱尔驾驶法拉利在7号弯出弯时发生了严重的打转。勒克莱尔并不是唯一遇到操控困难的车手，刘易斯·汉密尔顿报告说他的车尾非常松散、非常不稳定，至少这一点两位法拉利王牌车手能达成一致。

    偏离赛道的次数之多甚至在半程左右引发了一面红旗，让赛道工作人员得以清理勒克莱尔和其他人抛洒到赛道表面的碎石。

    这次短暂的中断为更多车队切换至软胎运行形成了一个自然的交叉点。阿尔派的皮埃尔·加斯利未能就软胎做出反馈，因为他在14号弯滑入了碎石区，最终搁浅在缓冲区。

    当练习赛再次恢复时，计时器上还剩15分钟，赛事控制中心决定将本次练习赛延长13分钟。这让各车队得以弥补失去的时间，大多数车队转而使用中性胎进行更多长距离测试，而不是用软胎去冲击单圈成绩。

    在这个过程中，汉密尔顿和马克斯·维斯塔潘在主直道上相遇，维斯塔潘在两人准备做飞驰圈时超过了汉密尔顿，这种动作正是乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯极为不满的那类行为。汉密尔顿迅速反超了这位荷兰车手，并在接近刹车区时向他靠拢，这让红牛车手颇为不满。赛事控制中心记录了这一事件，认定为“驾驶行为不稳定”，但并未需要进一步调查。

    对长距离测试的侧重意味着安东内利此前的标杆成绩——在第一次红旗前几乎被拉塞尔追平——此后从未真正受到威胁。对情况没有帮助的是，汉密尔顿在晚些时候引发了一次虚拟安全车，他的法拉利似乎遭遇了动力单元熄火。

    与第一次练习赛的情况一样，安东内利和拉塞尔让梅赛德斯占据榜首，领先迈凯伦的兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里0.4秒，后者周五仍在使用迈凯伦旧规格的底板，预计周六才会换上新版本。

    维斯塔潘和哈贾尔为红牛车队位列第五和第六，勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿则排名第七和第八，落后一秒。利亚姆·劳森和阿尔维德·林德布拉德继续两两成对，为小红牛车队包揽前十名的最后两个席位。

    F1 Qatar GP: Kimi Antonelli leads Mercedes charge in twice red-flagged FP2

    Kimi Antonelli and George Russell keep Mercedes on top in second practice at Formula 1's Qatar Grand Prix

    Kimi Antonelli has swept Friday's running at F1's Qatar GP in Doha after topping a second practice session that was interrupted by two red flags.

    The cooler evening conditions for FP2 presented more relevant conditions ahead of Saturday's qualifying session around the same time, with the track temperatures around 10 degrees lower than the afternoon's sweltering FP1 session.

    As was the case earlier, though, Mercedes still took charge of the timesheets from the start, with George Russell's early effort of 1m23.715s bested by Kimi Antonelli's 1m23.077s as nearly the entire field donned medium tyres for the opening stages.

    The drop in track temperatures didn't appear to make Lusail's high-downforce motorcycle circuit much easier to tame though, with Charles Leclerc suffering a huge spin in the Ferrari coming out of Turn 7. Leclerc wasn't alone in his handling difficulties, with Lewis Hamilton reporting his rear was very loose, very unstable, at least one thing the two Ferrari aces can agree on.

    The number of off-track excursions even prompted a red flag around the halfway mark, allowing marshals to clear the gravel that Leclerc and others had thrown onto the racing surface.

    The brief stoppage formed a natural crossover point for more teams to switch to soft tyre running. Alpine's Pierre Gasly was unable to report back on those as he shuffled into the gravel trap at Turn 14, ended up beached in the runoff area.

    With 15 more minutes on the clock when the session resumed one more time, race control decided to extend the session by 13 minutes. That allowed teams to make up for lost time, the majority pivoting to more long running on mediums rather than chasing a time on soft tyres.

    In doing so, Hamilton and Max Verstappen found each other's company on the main straight, with Verstappen passing Hamilton as they were preparing a lap, the kind of move that drew George Russell's ire so much in Las Vegas. Hamilton swiftly repassed the Dutchman and drifted towards him approaching the braking zone, much to the displeasure of the Red Bull driver. The incident was noted by race control for "driving erratically", but it didn't warrant further investigation.

    The emphasis on longer runs meant Antonelli's early benchmark, which was almost matched by Russell before the first red flag, was never really threatened after that. What didn't help was a late virtual safety car for Hamilton as he appeared to suffer a power unit shutdown on his Ferrari.

    As was the case in FP1, Antonelli and Russell put Mercedes on top with a four-tenth gap to McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri, who were still running McLaren's older floor specification on Friday before being expected to move to the newer version on Saturday.

    Verstappen and Hadjar were fifth and sixth for Red Bull, with Leclerc and Hamilton seventh and eighth, one second in arrears. Continuing to run two by two, Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad completed the top 10 for Racing Bulls.
