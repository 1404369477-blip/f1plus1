    F2发布改进版赛车，外观向F1 2026新规靠拢

    F2在意大利大奖赛期间发布了一款大幅修改的赛车，外观更接近F1现役赛车，以适应F1 2026年的规则变化。

    Formula 2 在意大利大奖赛上展示了为接下来几个赛季大幅修改的赛车，使其外观更接近 Formula 1 目前的赛车。

    这款经修改的赛车将保留现有底盘，周六在蒙扎的 F1 维修区亮相，将从 2027 年到 2029 年用于 F1 的主要预备系列赛。

    F2 上一次推出新赛车是在 2024 年，目的是更贴近 F1 在 2022 至 2025 年使用的地面效应时代赛车的外观。

    这款新的 2027 年赛车更好地反映了 F1 全新 2026 年赛车的视觉方向，但并未进行彻底翻新，从而将成本控制在较低水平。

    它的空气动力学套件同样旨在让赛车能够更贴近地跟随前车行驶。

    新车将搭载与2024-26世代相同的3.4升涡轮增压V6 Mecachrome发动机。

    它将再次使用18英寸轮毂，早在2020年的赛车上就率先采用了这一规格，比F1采纳18英寸轮毂早了两年。

    这款车将使用由阿斯顿·马丁F1车队合作伙伴沙特阿美提供的可持续燃料。

    这辆赛车将首次配备车载启动器，这意味着熄火的赛车不太可能被困在赛道上。

    F2表示，这是通过与供应商Hewland合作开发“现有变速箱的进化版”实现的。

    它应该就是兰多·诺里斯（诺里斯）全新LN4 Fusion车队明年将使用的赛车，该车队由前Prema领队雷内·罗辛（Rene Rosin）领导。

    该车队的参赛资格尚未正式获批，但这被认为只是走个形式。

    英文原文

    Formula 2 has revealed a heavily modified car for the coming seasons at the Italian Grand Prix, bringing it closer to the look of Formula 1's current machine.

    The revised car, which will retain the existing chassis, was revealed in the F1 paddock at Monza on Saturday and will be used in F1's primary feeder series from 2027 to 2029.

    F2 last introduced a new car in 2024 to more closely mirror the look of F1's ground-effect-era cars used from 2022-25.

    This new 2027 car better reflects the visual direction of F1's new 2026 cars but without being a complete overhaul, keeping costs lower.

    Its aero package is also designed to allow cars to follow each other more closely.

    The new car will be powered by the same 3.4-litre turbocharged V6 Mecachrome engine used in the 2024-26 generation.

    And it will run on 18-inch wheelrims once more, having pioneered them on its 2020 car, two years before F1 adopted them.

    The car will run on sustainable fuel, supplied by Aramco, partner of Aston Martin's F1 team.

    For the first time, the car will feature an on-board starter, meaning stalled cars are less likely to be stranded.

    F2 said this had been accomplished through the development of "an evolution of the current gearbox" from supplier Hewland.

    It should be the car used by Lando Norris's brand-new LN4 Fusion team next year, led by former Prema leader Rene Rosin.

    The team is yet to be officially granted an entry, but this is believed to be a formality.
