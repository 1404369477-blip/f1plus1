    斯特拉赞扬诺里斯新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛冲突反应

    麦拉伦F1车队经理斯特拉赞扬诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中与队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里发生碰撞后的反应。

    麦拉伦F1车队经理斯特拉对诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中的表现给予赞扬

    麦拉伦车队经理斯特拉对诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中的表现给予赞扬

    这迫使皮亚斯特里退赛，诺里斯降至第八位，尽管现任世界冠军避免了惩罚，因为裁判声称“两位车手都有贡献。”

    尽管如此，麦克拉伦的管理层仍然愤怒，但周日诺里斯获得第四名，并在成功反击法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿，获得第五名时提出帮助皮亚斯特里，士气更高了。

    “虽然从这个角度来看，对话还没有结束，但我们还可以做更多的事情，成为一个更加团结和强大的团队，”斯特拉说，她的车队现在已经连续三轮没有登上领奖台了。

    “我很高兴，兰多在某个阶段比赛中采取了这种思维方式。这就是作为车队经理我所期望的。这也是在麦克拉伦工作的1000多人期望我们的车手所做到的。”

    “所以，我们有这个例子是好事，我肯定未来还会看到更多这样的情况，不管他们是在广播中被听到，还是在每个会议的闭门会议中发生，或者是在我们制定赛车计划的方式中。”

    诺里斯对这一事件的公开反应随着周末的进行而发生了变化，最初在周六声称“这是一个很大的差距”，所以他“有权利”发起攻击。

    然而，在周日的赛后，这位英国人承认他“本不应该尝试”超车，皮亚斯蒂补充说‘这本来就不需要发生’，但他已经从这件事中走出来了。

    这使斯泰拉在麦凯轮车队为保持 constructors’ standings 第三名的位置而战斗时感到高兴，因为复苏的红牛车队在最后一轮的比赛中落后只有七分。

    “我们昨天所发生的事情，我将其归类为不可接受，这是一个来自车队主管的声明，”斯特拉补充道。

    “但我对这一点感到非常鼓舞，因为这也是我们的车手们迅速做出的声明。

    兰多·诺里斯，麦克拉伦，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，麦克拉伦

    “我们正在进行的对话还没有结束。但这并不是因为我们需要回去讨论Lando是否像以前那样和Oscar并排，那不是有趣的部分。”

    Andrea Stella praises Lando Norris for reaction to Singapore GP sprint clash

    Norris collided into McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri on the last lap of the Singapore sprint race at Marina Bay

    McLaren Formula 1 boss Andrea Stella has praised Lando Norris for the way he responded to his clash with team-mate Oscar Piastri in the Singapore Grand Prix sprint race.

    The McLaren pair were squabbling over fourth on Saturday until Norris made an opportunistic lunge down the inside of Turn 5 during the final lap, causing both to collide at the apex.

    It forced Piastri into a retirement with Norris dropping to eighth, though the reigning world champion avoided punishment as stewards claimed “both drivers had contributed”.

    McLaren’s hierarchy was nevertheless furious, but spirits were higher on Sunday when Norris finished fourth and offered to help Piastri in his successful late attack on Ferrari’s Lewis Hamilton for fifth.

    “While the conversations from this point of view are not concluded, there's more we can do to become a more united and even stronger team,” said Stella, whose squad is now without a podium in three rounds.

    “I'm pleased that Lando, at some stage in the race, adopted this way of thinking. This is what I expect as a team principal. This is what the 1,000 people that work at McLaren expect from our drivers.

    “So, it's good that we have had this example and I expect definitely more of that in the future, independently of the fact that they will be heard on the radio or they will happen behind the closed door in every meeting or in the way we approach our race plan.”

    The way Norris publicly reacted to the incident certainly changed as the weekend progressed, initially claiming on Saturday that “it was a big gap” so he had the “right” to attack.

    Yet post-race on Sunday the Briton accepted that he “should never have attempted” the overtake, with Piastri adding it ‘didn’t need to happen’ but he’s already moved on from it.

    This left Stella pleased amid McLaren’s fight to hold onto third in the constructors’ standings, as a resurgent Red Bull is just seven points behind after Max Verstappen won the last two rounds.

    “What we had yesterday I classified it as unacceptable, which is a statement that comes from the team principal,” added Stella.

    “But I was very encouraged by the fact that it's a statement that very rapidly came from our drivers as well.

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Oscar Piastri, McLaren

    “So, we are aligned as to how we want to lead this team forward in a joint effort between the team and the drivers.

    “The conversations that we are having, they are not finished. But it's not because we need to go back discussing if Lando was alongside Oscar like with...that's not the interesting bit.

    “For me, the interesting bit is a reflection that all the stakeholders, the team, the drivers will have to make around living together the team.

    “Embracing constantly from inside the cockpit the interest of the team as something that is superior to any particular singular individual interest.”
