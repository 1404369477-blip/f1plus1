    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里：红牛现在是F1中最快的车队

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里认为，经过最近升级后，红牛车队现在在F1中是最快的车队。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里：红牛现在是F1的“最快车队”

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里认为，红牛车队在最近升级后已经成为了F1中最具竞争力的车队

    对于麦凯轮车队的粉丝来说，来自沃金的消息，有关车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个周末的比赛的最新消息。

    在马克斯·维斯塔潘连续几个周末取得成功之后，麦凯轮车队的奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里认为，红牛车队在最近升级后已经成为了F1中最快的车队

    米尔顿凯恩斯车队现在在美国大奖赛上以仅落后于那辆橙黄色的赛车七分，加入了车队冠军争夺战。得益于在马来西亚和新加坡的连续两站胜利，维斯塔潘突然迎来了金光闪闪的时刻，现在压力来到了沃金车队身上。

    在美洲赛车场举行的赛前新闻发布会上，皮亚斯特里表示，红牛车队现在是所有车队中最强劲的对手。

    “我认为红牛在最近的三站分站赛中确实迈出了很大的一步，”他说，“他们在巴库对赛车进行的升级非常强大。在那里他们获得了双料领奖台，并且由马克斯赢得了接下来的两站比赛。”

    这位澳大利亚人原本预计法拉利车队将在许多赛道上成为标杆，但他惊讶地看到红牛车队在这个时候已经处于如此强劲的位置。

    “我认为显然红牛非常强大，确实比我们强大。所以过去几周，红牛无疑成为了，我可以说，最稳定的快速赛车。但我认为每个周末，挑战都在轮流出现。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，麦克拉伦，兰多·诺里斯，麦克拉伦

    麦克拉伦去年赢得了车队冠军，但今年面临着由现任冠军兰多·诺里斯定义为“精神上耗尽”的赛车。

    这位澳大利亚人表示，他所在车队对赛季末更强表现的希望被其他顶级车队快速进步所摧毁。

    “在赛季中途，我们感觉我们已经获得了一些动力，并且有可能重新回到能够争夺胜利和常规领奖台的位置，”他说。 “但在一级方程式赛车中，这往往是这种情况。你认为你再次做到了，然后你周围有一些车队想要把你拉下来几个档次。”

    不是每个人都同意这个观点。在新闻发布会上也出席的伊萨克·哈德贾表示：“我认为我们并没有最快的赛车，”并暗示法拉利在新加坡更快。

    维斯塔潘将在本周末奥斯汀再次追逐另一个胜利，但麦克拉伦将努力保持他们在积分榜上的位置。

    Red Bull is now F1's “most consistently quick car” says Oscar Piastri

    Oscar Piastri believes Red Bull has become Formula 1's most consistently competitive team following its recent upgrades

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    After what was a very successful couple of weekends from Max Verstappen, McLaren's Oscar Piastri believes that Red Bull is now the most consistently fast team on the Formula 1 grid after its recent upgrades rocketed it to the top of the timesheets.

    The Milton Keynes outfit now arrives in Austin for the United States Grand Prix just seven points behind the papaya-coloured cars in the battle for the constructors' championship. After a sudden gold rush for Verstappen thanks to back-to-back wins across the races in Malaysia and Singapore, the pressure is now on the Woking team.

    Speaking in the press conference ahead of the race at the Circuit of the Americas, Piastri believes the Red Bull cars are now the ones to beat across the board.

    “I think Red Bull definitely took a pretty big step forwards over the last triple-header,” he said. “Whatever upgrades they put on the car in Baku were very strong. They had a double podium there and won the next two races with Max."

    The Australian expected the Ferrari team to be the benchmark at a lot of circuits, and was surprised to see Red Bull be in such a strong position at this point.

    “I think clearly the Red Bull was very strong, both stronger than us, for sure. So over the last few weeks, Red Bull’s definitely become, I would say, the most consistently quick car. But I think each weekend, the challenges are kind of rotating.”

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    McLaren won the constructors' championship last year, but this year has been confronted with a car that reigning champion Lando Norris defines as "mentally exhausting".

    The Australian suggested that his team's hopes of a stronger end to the season have been decimated by the rapid improvements made by the other top teams.

    “In the middle of the season we felt like we’d gained some momentum, and potentially we were back to being able to fight for wins and regular podiums,” he said. “But that’s often the way in Formula 1. You think you’re there again, and you’ve got a few teams around you to pull you down a few pegs.”

    Not everyone at Red Bull agrees with this. Isack Hadjar, also available in the press conference, argued: “I don’t think we have the fastest car,” and suggested Ferrari had been quicker in Singapore.

    Verstappen will be chasing another win in Austin this weekend, but McLaren will be fighting hard to maintain their place in the standings.
