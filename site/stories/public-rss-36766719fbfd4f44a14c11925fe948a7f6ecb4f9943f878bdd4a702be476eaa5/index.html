    2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛揭晓灵感源自霓虹灯的奖杯

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛为2026年的F1赛事揭晓了一款新奖杯，其设计灵感来源于城市的标志性霓虹灯牌和复古照明招牌。奖杯采用独特的V形轮廓，代表拉斯维加斯和胜利。社交媒体上的粉丝迅速提出了V形设计的第三层含义：'V代表维斯塔潘'，开玩笑说这个奖杯可能是为小红牛的维斯塔潘量身定做的。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛为2026年的F1赛事揭晓了一款新奖杯，其设计灵感来源于城市标志性的霓虹灯牌和复古照明招牌。

    这款奖杯是自该赛事于2023年加入F1赛历以来使用的第二个设计，其独特的V型轮廓代表着拉斯维加斯和胜利。

    然而，社交媒体上的粉丝迅速提出了V型设计的第三种含义：‘V代表维斯塔潘’，开玩笑说这个奖杯可能是为红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘量身定做的。

    其设计借鉴了与著名弗里蒙特街传统上相关的霓虹灯和白炽灯招牌，这个概念旨在让赛事获胜者拥有自己的照明拉斯维加斯标志。

    冠军奖杯高22英寸，宽8¼英寸，重8磅（3.6千克），由实心复合材料制成，并镀有铜和铬。它还配备了122个独立灯光，并有一个五英尺的周界灯光。

    该设计由拉斯维加斯大奖赛与Bowen Studios和Ore Designs合作开发。Bowen Studios负责奖杯的3D模型、渲染和动画，而Ore Designs，由Shane Larson领导，负责设计和制造实体奖杯。

    Bowen Studios和Ore Designs之前曾合作开发了2023年至2025年的拉斯维加斯大奖赛奖杯，以及过去两年的美国大奖赛奖杯。

    英文原文

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix has unveiled a new trophy for its 2026 Formula 1 race, with a design inspired by the city's iconic neon signs and vintage illuminated marquees.

    The trophy is the second design used since the event joined the F1 calendar in 2023 and features a distinctive V-shaped silhouette, representing both Vegas and victory.

    However, fans on social media were quick to suggest a third meaning behind the V-shaped design: 'V for Verstappen', joking that the trophy could be tailor-made for Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

    Its design draws on the neon and incandescent-bulb signage traditionally associated with the famous Fremont Street, with the concept intended to give the race winner their own illuminated Las Vegas sign.

    The winner's trophy measures 22 inches high and 8¼ inches wide, weighs 8lbs (3.6kg) and is made from solid composite with copper and chrome plating. Alongside its 122 individual lights, it features a five-foot perimeter light.

    The design was developed by the Las Vegas Grand Prix in collaboration with Bowen Studios and Ore Designs. Bowen Studios was responsible for the trophy's 3D model, renderings and animations, while Ore Designs, led by Shane Larson, engineered and fabricated the physical trophies.

    Bowen Studios and Ore Designs previously worked together on the Las Vegas Grand Prix trophies from 2023 to 2025, as well as the past two United States Grand Prix trophies.
