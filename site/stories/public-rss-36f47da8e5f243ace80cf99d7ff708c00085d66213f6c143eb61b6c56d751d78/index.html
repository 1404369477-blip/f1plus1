    汉密尔顿：勒克莱尔的心态让我们今天损失了很多积分

    汉密尔顿暗示勒克莱尔的心态是两人在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛相撞的原因。

    汉密尔顿：勒克莱尔的心态让我们今天损失了很多积分

    汉密尔顿没有公开抨击队友，但在拉斯维加斯再次发生事故后，他的沮丧情绪仍然流露出来

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿暗示，夏尔·勒克莱尔的心态是两人在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛相撞的原因。

    在第37圈的内华达赛事中，汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔分别位列第六和第七，仅落后领跑者马克斯·维斯塔潘约两秒，此时摩纳哥车手在第7弯内线俯冲。他的前轮几乎与英国车手并排，但他似乎看到门被关上而退出；结果，他的前轴在弯心处紧挨着汉密尔顿的侧箱，最终擦到了队友的左后轮，导致同队赛车打转。

    这为勒克莱尔招致了10秒罚时，而汉密尔顿则失去了四个位置和大约八秒的时间。因此，他们在格子旗挥动下分别位列第10和第7，错失了可能的前五名和潜在的领奖台完赛。

    这只是本赛季法拉利车手涉及的众多事件之一，包括他们在墨西哥大奖赛第一圈的显著冲突，当时他们正以1-2名行驶，却将胜利输给了马克斯·维斯塔潘——这促使汉密尔顿要求进行“严肃会谈”。

    会谈确实进行了，但三周过去，情况依旧存在。

    在拉斯维加斯事件之后，当被问及接下来会发生什么——在他有机会与队友讨论这个问题之前——汉密尔顿含糊地回应道：“我不知道，但这不是我想深入讨论的事情。这些是法拉利该回答的问题。”

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利，夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利

    然而，当被追问在碰撞发生的那一刻，考虑到他们之前的冲突，他脑海中在想什么时，汉密尔顿巧妙地将责任引向了队友。

    “我经历过很多次这种情况。我想就在那一刻，我知道我更快。我刚刚从10秒罚时中恢复过来，”这位法拉利车手指出，他在四圈内从第14位追至第8位，随后超越勒克莱尔，两人又一同超过了皮埃尔·加斯利。

    “我实际上只想着击败迈凯伦，我们必须击败迈凯伦，才能避免在后面缠斗。每次我们缠斗，就只会失去位置。让我们齐心协力，保持尾流，继续推进，追上前面那些人。”

    “我想，我们当时的心态不一致。最终，这让我们今天损失了很多积分。”

    有趣的是，1996年世界冠军达蒙·希尔最近建议法拉利应将汉密尔顿定为2027年的头号车手。

    “让刘易斯成为法拉利头号车手。解决这件事。明确下来。终结所有争论。刘易斯会为法拉利带来回报。如果他们100%支持他，”希尔于11月6日在推特上写道。

    当汉密尔顿随后被问及法拉利是否应该这样做，以及他本人是否会要求获得这种地位时，他毫不犹豫地回答道：“我从未要求过成为一号车手，我真的从来没有要求过。”

    “赛季中有一段时间我领先40多分。那个时候，竞争是在我和基米（安东内利）之间，我是最接近他的人。在那一年的很长一段时间里我都排在第二，”他补充道，此前他在6月的摩纳哥和巴塞罗那站比赛后，以及7月的比利时和匈牙利站比赛后都占据了亚军位置。

    “那个时候，我们需要支持来确保击败基米。赛季中有几次，如果有支持，我认为我们本可以击败基米。

    “那不是……我所要求的是我们不应该撞在一起。那根本就不应该发生。关于这件事我已经说了我能说的一切，这个问题应该去问法拉利。”

    Lewis Hamilton: ‘Charles Leclerc’s frame of mind cost us a lot of points today’

    Hamilton refrained from hitting out at his team-mate but his frustration still transpired following yet another incident in Las Vegas

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    Lewis Hamilton subtly implied Charles Leclerc’s “frame of mind” was to blame for their collision in Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Hamilton and Leclerc were sixth and seventh on lap 37 of the Nevadan race, only some two seconds adrift of race leader Max Verstappen, when the Monegasque dived down the inside in Turn 7. His front wheels were nearly alongside the Briton’s, but he seemingly saw the door closing and backed out; as a result, his front axle was next to Hamilton’s sidepod at the apex and he ended up clipping his team-mate’s rear-left wheel, sending the sister car into a spin.

    This netted Leclerc a 10-second penalty, while Hamilton lost four positions and about eight seconds. As a consequence, they ended up respectively 10th and seventh under the chequered flag, missing out on a likely top five and a potential podium finish.

    This was just one of many incidents involving the Ferrari drivers this season, including their prominent clash on lap one of the Mexican Grand Prix, as they were running 1-2 and lost victory to Max Verstappen – which had prompted Hamilton to demand “serious talks”.

    Talks did occur, but three weeks on, the situation persists.

    Asked what was next following the Las Vegas incident – before he got a chance to discuss the issue with his team-mate – Hamilton elusively responded: “I don't know, but that's not something I want to get into. These are questions for Ferrari.”

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari

    However, pressed on what went through his mind when the collision occurred, in the context of their previous clashes, Hamilton subtly steered the blame towards his team-mate.

    “I've been there many times. I think just in that point, I knew I was quicker. I've just come back from a 10-second penalty,” the Ferrari driver pointed out, having recovered from 14th to eighth in four laps, before he overtook Leclerc and they both passed Pierre Gasly.

    “I'm literally only thinking about beating McLaren, we have to beat McLaren to stop us battling behind. Every time we battle, we just lose ground. Let's just work together and stay in the tow, let's keep pushing and catch those guys up ahead.

    “We weren't in the same frame of mind, I guess. Ultimately, it cost us a lot of points today.”

    Interestingly, 1996 world champion Damon Hill recently suggested Ferrari should make Hamilton its number-one driver for 2027.

    “Make Lewis #1 Ferrari driver. Sort it out. Make it clear. Ends all debate. Lewis will deliver for Ferrari. If they back him 100%,” Hill tweeted on 6 November.

    When Hamilton was consequently asked if Ferrari should do so and whether he would request that status himself, he replied without a second’s hesitation: “I've never asked to be number one, I genuinely haven't ever asked.

    “There was a point in the season where I was 40-odd points ahead. At that point, the fight was with me and Kimi [Antonelli], I was the closest one to him. I was second for a long time in the year,” he added, having held the runner-up spot after the Monaco and Barcelona races in June, as well as the Belgian and Hungarian rounds in July.

    “At that point, we needed backing to make sure we beat Kimi. There were a few instances in the season where, with the support, I think we could have beat Kimi.

    “That's not… What I've asked for is we shouldn't have to come together. That should just never happen. I've said everything I can on it, and the question should be for Ferrari.”
