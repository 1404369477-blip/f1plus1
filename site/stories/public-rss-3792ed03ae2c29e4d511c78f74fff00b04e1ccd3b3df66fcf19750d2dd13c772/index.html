    新加坡大奖赛胜利后，粉丝对维斯塔潘的花卉赛车靴爱不释手

    维斯塔潘在新加坡大奖赛上穿着新设计的赛车靴，并最终赢得了他多年追求的胜利。

    粉丝对维斯塔潘在新加坡大奖赛上穿着的新款赛车靴爱不释手。

    这位四次世界冠军在周末前展示了这双特别的靴子，并穿着它们打破了在新加坡的魔咒。

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛赛车新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和比赛分析是他们的日常。

    维斯塔潘带着新的一双赛车靴来到新加坡大奖赛，并带着他多年追求的胜利离开。

    维斯塔潘.com Instagram账号在周末之前分享了一张靴子的图片。这款设计以海军蓝为基础，配有粉色花卉图案，同时融入了他常规装备中的红牛和Oracle品牌元素。这条帖子获得了接近令人惊讶的5万点赞。

    粉丝们迅速注意到了细节的丰富。几位粉丝质疑花朵是否像马来西亚的国花扶桑花，而不是新加坡的国花兰花。F1在上一个周末在雪邦赛道进行了比赛，那里举办了重新定位的巴林大奖赛，维斯塔潘赢得了比赛。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    虽然设计引人注目，但赛车靴是车手装备的功能性部分。它们必须满足FIA的防火安全标准，并采用薄底设计，以便车手能够精确地感觉到踏板。这意味着他们只能通过不同的颜色和设计来个性化它们，但这通常不会阻止维斯塔潘。正如一位粉丝在帖子下问的那样：‘他什么时候没有特殊设计的靴子？’

    无论灵感的来源是什么，他们都证明了自己的幸运。

    新加坡是少数几个未能击败维斯塔潘的赛道之一。在2023年，他在滨海湾国际赛道上获得了第11个发车位，最终以第五名的成绩结束比赛，而卡洛斯·塞恩斯代表法拉利车队获胜——这是红牛车队本赛季唯一一次全赛季未获胜的比赛。去年，他输给了乔治·拉塞尔。

    这次，一切似乎都恰到好处。维斯塔潘在周六的短道赛中获胜，这场比赛因大雨而推迟，乔治·拉塞尔发生事故，两辆麦拉伦赛车发生碰撞。然后他在滨海湾国际赛道上获得了他的第一个大奖赛分站赛杆位，并在周日的炎热天气中保持了冷静，以一场统治性的表现赢得了比赛。周日，他从发车到结束一直领先，以7.335秒的优势击败了法拉利的查尔斯·勒克莱尔，梅赛德斯车手基米·安东内利获得第三名。

    这次胜利也是维斯塔潘第250次大奖赛发车，是他继在雪邦之后的第二次连续获胜，也是红牛车队自2022年以来在新加坡的第一次获胜。

    “经过了一段时间，但我认为这场比赛非常好。我的意思是，是的，我们在软胎和中等胎上都需要速度，尽管我认为对于每个人来说，中等胎都有些挑战，但最终它还是成功了，所以当然非常高兴在这里获胜。”

    Fans can't get enough of Max Verstappen's floral race boots after Singapore win

    The four-time world champion revealed a special pair of boots ahead of the Marina Bay weekend, and went on to break his Singapore curse in them

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Max Verstappen arrived at the Singapore Grand Prix with a new pair of race boots and left with a victory he had been chasing for years.

    The Verstappen.com Instagram account shared an image of the boots before the weekend. The design features a navy base with a pink floral pattern, alongside the Red Bull and Oracle branding of his regular kit. The post drew close to an astonishing 50,000 likes.

    Fans were quick to take in the immense detail. Several fans questioned whether the flower resembled a hibiscus, the national flower of Malaysia, rather than an orchid, Singapore's national flower. F1 raced at Sepang the weekend before, where the relocated Bahrain Grand Prix was held, and Verstappen won it.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    While the design is eye-catching, race boots are a functional part of a driver's kit. They must meet FIA safety standards for fire resistance, and are built with thin soles so drivers can feel the pedals precisely. That means they can only really personalise them with different colours and designs, but that does not usually stop Verstappen. As one fan asked under the post: 'does he ever not have special boots?'

    Whatever the inspiration, they proved lucky.

    Singapore had been one of the few venues to elude Verstappen. In 2023, he qualified 11th and finished fifth at Marina Bay as Carlos Sainz won for Ferrari - the only race Red Bull lost all season. Last year, he finished second to George Russell.

    This time, everything came together. Verstappen won a sprint on Saturday that was delayed by heavy rain and featured a crash for George Russell and a collision between the two McLarens. He then took his first grand prix pole at Marina Bay, and kept his composure in the heat on Sunday with a commanding show of dominance. On Sunday he led from start to finish, beating Ferrari's Charles Leclerc by 7.335 seconds, with Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli third.

    The win came on Verstappen's 250th grand prix start, too. It was the 73rd victory of his career, his second in a row after Sepang, and Red Bull's first in Singapore since 2022.

    "It's taken a while, but I think this race was very good. I mean yeah, we had pace when we needed to have it on the soft tyre and on the medium, even though I think the medium tire for everyone was a bit more of a struggle, but it worked out, so of course very happy to win here."
