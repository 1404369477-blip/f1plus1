    安东内利在排位赛中速度最快，但将降五位发车

    维斯塔潘将在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛中占据杆位，尽管安东内利以0.258秒的优势领先

    2026年世界冠军以0.258秒的优势击败了红牛车手，但由于他的梅赛德斯新内燃机，他将从五号位起跑

    在维特斯坦普以1分34秒151的成绩获得临时杆位，而安东内利排名第六的情况下，但荷兰人在最后一圈未能提升成绩，给了其他人超越他的机会。

    然而，只有安东内利成功击败了维特斯坦普，在终点线处以1分33秒893的成绩完成比赛——这是他今年第八次在排位赛中取得最快成绩，也是自奥斯汀以来首次。

    但由于赛前的罚分，维特斯坦普将在发车线上与梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔并肩，拉塞尔原本希望得到队友的推车，但交通状况阻止了这一机会。

    拉塞尔的成绩为1分34秒224，比他的首圈快了两十分之一，仅比麦凯轮车队的兰多·诺里斯快0.020秒，因此诺里斯将在周六晚上的发车线上完成前三名。

    维斯塔潘的队友伊萨克·哈德贾在Q3中排名第五，比最快的慢了0.699秒，第六名是令人印象深刻的菜鸟车手阿里夫德·林德布拉德，就在小红牛确认他将于2027年加入车队一周后。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂排名第七，但他是有五位将要接受发车位处罚的车手之一，他的处罚是10个发车位，因为更换了引擎，第八名是林德布拉德的队友利亚姆·劳森。

    阿尔派恩车队组合皮埃尔·加斯利和弗朗哥·科拉平托分别完成了前十名，分别比安东内利慢了1.212秒和1.421秒，但最大的话题发生在第二节的练习中，因为法拉利遭遇了自去年伊莫拉以来首次双Q2退出的情况。

    意大利车队在混乱的Q2后占据了第六排，勒克莱尔在第七弯中锁死轮胎后，中途放弃了这一圈。

    摩纳哥车手随后未能进入赛道开始本回合，最终排名11位，领先排名12位的汉密尔顿，汉密尔顿在最后一圈未能提升自己的成绩。

    “难以置信，”勒克莱尔表示，他的队友如果在旗杆处有所改进，本可以将安东内利淘汰出局——这位意大利车手仅以0.111秒的差距幸存下来。

    汉密尔顿身后是奥迪车手尼克·胡肯伯格，排名第13位，第14位是威廉姆斯车手卡洛斯·塞恩斯，他因更换新涡轮增压器将被处以10位降级。

    胡肯伯格的队友加布里埃尔·博特洛托将从第15位跃升至更高位置，而第16位是亚历克斯·阿隆博，这是威廉姆斯车队在2026赛季首次在第二阶段练习中出现双车手。

    拉斐尔·卡马拉在比赛初期在第一个弯道发生打滑，原本看起来将从队友奥利弗·贝尔曼的显著拖车中获益，但博尔特洛在终点线前将他降入Q1淘汰区。

    这位新的哈斯车队招募者仅以0.071秒的差距未能进入Q2，位列第17，与第18位的凯迪拉克车手塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯相差1.163秒，比第19位的费尔南多·阿隆索领先1.180秒。

    阿隆索的阿斯顿·马丁车队队友兰斯·斯特罗尔排名第20，但因为他AMR26赛车上的新能量存储和新控制电子设备安装，将面临10个位置的处罚。

    因此，瓦尔特里·博塔斯将从第21位上升，他正是在凯迪拉克宣布2027年10次大奖赛冠军得主后的两天内，在Q1代表时间内速度最慢的。

    贝尔曼因此是唯一一个没有完成一个完整圈的车手，因为他的赛车需要更换整个引擎，导致他在Q1的主要角色是协助Camara。

    F1 Las Vegas GP: Kimi Antonelli quickest but Max Verstappen on pole, Ferraris out in Q2

    Antonelli was fastest in qualifying at Las Vegas but takes a five-place grid drop

    Max Verstappen will start on pole at the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix despite Kimi Antonelli going fastest - while Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton both failed to make Q3.

    The 2026 world champion beat the Red Bull driver by 0.258s, but will take a five-place grid drop due to a new internal combustion engine on his Mercedes.

    It came after Verstappen took provisional pole with a 1m34.151s while Antonelli was down in sixth, but the Dutchman failed to improve on his final lap, allowing others the chance to beat him.

    Yet only Antonelli managed to topple Verstappen, setting a 1m33.893s at the chequered flag - the eighth time he has gone quickest in qualifying this year and the first since Austin.

    But because of the grid penalty, Verstappen will be joined on the front row by Mercedes’ George Russell, who hoped for a tow from his team-mate but traffic denied that opportunity.

    Russell set a 1m34.224s, two-tenths quicker than his opening lap, and was just 0.020s ahead of McLaren’s Lando Norris, who will therefore complete the top three on Saturday night’s grid.

    Verstappen’s team-mate Isack Hadjar was fifth quickest in Q3, 0.699s off the pace, with sixth going to impressive rookie Arivd Lindblad a week after Racing Bulls confirmed him for 2027.

    Oscar Piastri took seventh, but is one of five drivers set for a grid penalty, his being 10 places for an engine change, while eighth went to Lindblad’s team-mate Liam Lawson.

    Alpine duo Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto respectively completed the top 10, 1.212s and 1.421s off Antonelli, but the biggest talking point came in the second session as Ferrari suffered its first double Q2 exit since Imola last year.

    The Italian outfit occupied row six after what was a messy Q2, highlighted by Leclerc aborting a lap midway through due to a Turn 7 lock-up.

    The Monegasque subsequently failed to make the line to start a lap at the end, eventually dropping to 11th ahead of 12th-placed Hamilton, who failed to improve on his final tour.

    “Unbelievable,” reacted Leclerc, whose team-mate would have knocked out Antonelli had he improved at the chequered flag - the Italian surviving the drop by 0.111s.

    Three-tenths behind Hamilton was Audi’s Nico Hulkenberg in 13th, with 14th going to Williams man Carlos Sainz, who is set to serve a 10-place drop for a new turbocharger.

    Hulkenberg’s team-mate Gabriel Bortoleto will jump up from 15th, while 16th went to Alex Albon for Williams’ first double Q2 appearance of the 2026 season.

    Rafael Camara, who suffered a spin at the opening corner early on, looked set to profit from a significant tow by team-mate Oliver Bearman until Bortoleto dropped him into the Q1 elimination zone at the chequered flag.

    The new Haas recruit missed out on Q2 by just 0.071s to take 17th, with a 1.163s gap to Cadillac’s Sergio Perez in 18th and a 1.180s advantage over 19th-placed Fernando Alonso.

    Alonso’s Aston Martin stablemate Lance Stroll took 20th, but is set to serve a 10-place penalty for a new energy store and new control electronics being fitted on his AMR26.

    That will therefore bump up Valtteri Bottas from 21st, who was slowest in the Q1 representative times just two days after Cadillac announced the 10-time grand prix winner for 2027.

    Bearman was therefore the only driver not to complete a proper lap, as his 35-place grid drop for a complete engine change meant his main role in Q1 was to assist Camara.
