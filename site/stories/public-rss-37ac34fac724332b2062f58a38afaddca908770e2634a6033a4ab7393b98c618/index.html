    巴西大奖赛：博托莱托伴侣Isabella Bernardini展示定制奥迪礼服

    博托莱托的伴侣Isabella Bernardini身着由设计师Fiona Roerig设计的独特奥迪礼服亮相巴西大奖赛。

    加布里埃尔·博托莱托的伴侣伊莎贝拉·贝尔纳尔迪尼在巴西大奖赛上展示定制的奥迪礼服

    加布里埃尔·博托莱托的伴侣伊莎贝拉·贝尔纳尔迪尼在巴西大奖赛上吸引了众人的目光

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    加布里埃尔·博托莱托的伴侣伊莎贝拉·贝尔纳尔迪尼身着一件由奥迪为比赛特别定制的阿迪达斯系列独一无二的礼服抵达了英特拉戈斯赛道，准备参加巴西大奖赛。

    这件礼服是由设计师和改造师Fiona Roerig制作的，她与车队和Bernardini合作，将他们的想法融入设计之中。这件礼服是从adidas Sampa Soul系列中改造而来，这是德国品牌为博托莱托的家乡赛事推出的系列。

    颜色直接来源于这个系列，森林绿与金属金色相映成趣，而刺绣则借鉴了圣保罗的钩针和刺绣历史。

    Bernardini还在设计中融入了对博托莱托的致敬，他将博托莱托的比赛号码五融入了设计中。

    粉丝们很快就分享了他们对独特设计的看法。一位在X上评论说：“Isabella做的商品真美！对我们这位巴西男孩来说，没有比这更好的第一支持者和粉丝了！”另一位补充道：“哎呀，我喜欢它！！她看起来很美。我们需要这样的商品！！”

    “一个每个人都受到平等对待的团队。令人印象深刻，”有人评论道，而另一条评论则写道：“这件连衣裙很漂亮。”

    博托莱托将在雨中进行的排位赛中滑入障碍物后，从第19位发车开始他的主场大奖赛。

    “在排位赛早期，我试图与Ollie [贝尔曼]保持一定的距离，因为我正在追上他，”博托莱托在排位赛后对媒体说。

    “我最终锁定了前轮，在撞上墙壁之前没能及时停车。这不是一次硬碰撞，但它改变了翼片，我们无法继续这样行驶。他们试图更换它，但时间不够。”

    “在排位赛开始时，天气非常湿滑。从4号和5号弯道出来时，你无法全油门加速。说实话，我在发生事故的弯道那里，很容易锁死轮胎并撞上墙壁。”

    “他还补充说：“现在，我只想回家休息；这已经是一个非常漫长的一天了。直到我在排位赛中犯了一个错误，一切都在进行得非常顺利。我需要冷静一下，明天我们会制定计划。我可能要从后面或接近后面的位置出发。我只想尽力进行一场强劲的反击赛。”"

    Gabriel Bortoleto's partner Isabella Bernardini shows off custom Audi dress at Brazil GP

    Gabriel Bortoleto's partner Isabella Bernardini turned heads at the Brazilian Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Gabriel Bortoleto's partner Isabella Bernardini arrived at Interlagos ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix in a one-off dress made from Audi's special adidas range for the race.

    The dress was made by designer and upcycler Fiona Roerig, who worked with the team and Bernardini to incorporate their ideas into the design. The dress has been upcycled from the adidas Sampa Soul collection, which the German outfit launched for Bortoleto's home race.

    The colours come straight from that range, with forest green set against metallic gold, while the embroidery draws on São Paulo's history of crochet and needlework.

    Bernardini also included references to Bortoleto by incorporating his race number, five, into the design.

    Fans have been quick to share their thoughts on the unique design. "Beautiful merchandising by Isabella! And there couldn't be a better number one supporter and fan for our Brazilian boy!" one commented on X, while another added: "Ahh I love it!! She looks gorgeous. We need merch like this!!"

    "A team where everyone gets treated equally. Impressive," someone else posted, while another comment read: "That dress is gorgeous."

    Bortoleto will start his home grand prix from 19th on the grid after sliding into the barriers during a rain-soaked qualifying session.

    "Early in the qualifying, I was trying to keep some distance from Ollie [Bearman], as I was catching up to him," Bortoleto told the media after qualifying.

    "I ended up locking the front wheel and couldn’t stop the car in time before hitting the wall. It wasn’t a hard impact, but it shifted the wing, and we couldn’t continue like that. They tried to replace it, but there wasn’t enough time.

    "At the start of qualifying, it was very wet. Coming out of turns 4 and 5, you couldn’t go full throttle. Honestly, at the corner where I crashed, it was very easy to lock a wheel and end up in the wall."

    He added: "For now, I just want to go home and rest; it’s been a very long day. Everything had been going incredibly well until I made a mistake in qualifying. I need to clear my head, and tomorrow we’ll figure out the plan. I’ll likely be starting from the back, or close to it. I just want to try and put together a strong recovery race."
