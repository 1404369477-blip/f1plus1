    一级方程式CEO斯蒂法诺·多梅尼卡利表示，在基米·安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛成为该系列赛最年轻冠军后，'整个国家都将为之庆祝'。

    这位20岁的年轻人也成为73年来首位意大利世界冠军，上一位是1953年的阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里。梅赛德斯车手在阿维莫罗·赫尔曼诺斯·罗德里格斯赛道上获得第三名，在获胜者马克斯·维斯塔潘和法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿之后，获得了足够的积分来赢得冠军。

    多梅尼卡利在一份声明中表示，'今天，基米·安东内利创造了历史。成为最年轻的一级方程式世界冠军是一项非凡的成就，这是通过非凡的才能、勇气和决心获得的。'

    '看到基米成为自1953年阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来首位意大利一级方程式世界冠军，我感到无比自豪。73年来，我们的国家一直在等待这一刻，今天整个国家都将庆祝自己的同胞达到我们这项运动的顶峰。

    “伟大不仅仅由记录衡量，还在于它激励的世代。基米吸引了全世界数百万粉丝的想象力，并向全世界年轻人证明，通过奉献、信念和努力工作，没有什么是不可能的。今天全世界观看比赛的男女孩子们将会看到，基米证明了最大的梦想可以成为现实。”

    “刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队、安东内利、梅赛德斯”

    “安东内利在本赛季已经赢得了九个大奖赛，这是他加入F1的第二年，包括他在蒙扎的家乡赛事，他在2025年取代了汉密尔顿在梅赛德斯的位置。”

    “今天，他的名字加入了我们运动中的传奇人物，”意大利人继续说。“F1历史的一个新篇章已经写下，我坚信这只是一个非凡遗产的开始。”

    F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali releases touching statement after Kimi Antonelli's title win

    Formula 1 CEO Stefano Domenicali has celebrated Kimi Antonelli's historic championship victory at the Mexican Grand Prix

    Stefano Domenicali, CEO of the Formula One Group

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Formula 1 CEO Stefano Domenicali says "an entire nation will celebrate" after Kimi Antonelli became the series' youngest champion at the Mexican Grand Prix.

    The 20-year-old also became the first Italian world champion in 73 years, since Alberto Ascari in 1953. The Mercedes driver finished third at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez behind race-winner Max Verstappen and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, securing enough points to claim the title.

    "Today, Kimi Antonelli has made history. Becoming the youngest Formula 1 world champion is an extraordinary achievement, earned through exceptional talent, courage and determination," Domenicali said in a statement.

    "It fills me with immense pride to see Kimi become the first Italian Formula 1 world champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953. For 73 years, our country has waited for this moment, and today an entire nation will celebrate one of its own reaching the very pinnacle of our sport.

    "Greatness is measured not only by records, but by the generations it inspires. Kimi has captured the imagination of millions of fans around the world and shown young people everywhere that there are no limits to what can be achieved through dedication, belief and hard work. Across the world, young boys and girls watching today will see in Kimi someone who proves that the biggest dreams can become reality."

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    Antonelli has won nine grands prix so far this season in only his second year in F1, including his home race at Monza, having replaced Hamilton at Mercedes in 2025.

    "Today, his name joins the legends of our sport," the Italian continued. "A new chapter in Formula 1 history has been written, and I firmly believe this is only the beginning of an extraordinary legacy.

    "Congratulations to Kimi, Mercedes and his family on this incredible achievement. The entire Formula 1 community is proud to celebrate this special moment with them."
