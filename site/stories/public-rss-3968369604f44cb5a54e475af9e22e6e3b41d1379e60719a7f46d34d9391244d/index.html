    Ferrari team principal Frederic Vasseur blamed Charles Leclerc for a "completely unacceptable" collision with team-mate Lewis Hamilton in the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    The Ferrari drivers made contact when battling over sixth place with 12 laps remaining in a breathless, frenzied race in which the team were attempting to recover from a poor qualifying when both drivers were knocked out in Q2.

    Hamilton was spun around in the collision, dropping to 10th in the order. Leclerc was found to be at fault by race stewards, who imposed a 10-second penalty on him.
