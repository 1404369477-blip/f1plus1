    凯迪拉克确认博塔斯留队至2027赛季

    尽管外界盛传科尔顿·赫塔可能升入F1，凯迪拉克仍决定2027赛季继续由瓦尔特利·博塔斯出任车手。

    尽管凯迪拉克为培养科尔顿·赫塔付出了一切努力，外界也盛传他可能升入F1，但凯迪拉克最终还是选择在2027赛季继续留用瓦尔特利·博塔斯。

    前印地赛车（IndyCar）车手赫塔目前征战F2，排名第19位；他在10月底成为接替博塔斯的热门人选，就在塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯宣布与车队续约至2029年之前不久。

    尽管超级驾照问题再度引发混乱——这几乎是他职业生涯的标志性插曲——赫塔在上一站巴西终于拿到了参加F1所需的超级驾照，靠的是参加第一节自由练习（FP1）所积累的积分。

    不过，凯迪拉克将保留这位10次大奖赛分站冠军得主博塔斯继续驾驶赛车。

    其新闻稿称，博塔斯“为车队带来了丰富的F1经验、技术洞察力和获胜血统”，并且“他的反馈和领导力将继续支持车队在各个运营环节的发展”。

    “我很兴奋能继续与凯迪拉克一起走到2027年，”博塔斯说道。

    “我们知道从零开始组建一支新的F1车队并不容易，但这正是这项挑战如此特别的原因。

    “我们已经打下了基础，我们正在学习和进步，还有更多的东西即将到来，我渴望更多，我相信我们共同建设的事业。这个故事才刚刚开始。”

    车队首席执行官丹·托里斯补充道：“瓦尔特里的经验、视角和投入是我们车队的重要优势。”

    “延续性为我们提供了一个坚实的平台，让我们得以继续发展车队，并朝着我们在一级方程式中的目标努力。”

    佩雷兹在两人的排位对决中以11比8略微领先，但在正赛中占据上风，以13比4击败博塔斯，这不包括两人都退赛的奥地利站和匈牙利站。

    博塔斯和佩雷兹在凯迪拉克的首个赛季中都尚未获得积分。

    英文原文

    Despite all its efforts to prepare Colton Herta - and the rumours surrounding his possible graduation to Formula 1 - Cadillac has stuck with Valtteri Bottas for the 2027 season after all.

    Ex-IndyCar driver Herta - currently 19th in F2 - emerged as a frontrunning candidate to replace Bottas at the end of October, just before Sergio Perez announced a contract extension through 2029 with the team.

    Despite more superlicence confusion - a hallmark of his career - Herta finally secured the superlicence he needs to be F1-eligible in Brazil last time out thanks to the points gathered for participating in FP1 sessions.

    However, Cadillac will keep 10-time grand prix winner Bottas in the car.

    Its release said Bottas "brings extensive Formula 1 experience, technical insight and race-winning pedigree to the team" and "his feedback and leadership will continue to support the team’s development across its operations".

    "I’m excited to continue this journey with Cadillac into 2027," said Bottas.

    "We knew building a new Formula 1 team from the ground up wasn’t going to be easy, but that’s exactly what makes this challenge so special.

    “We’ve laid the foundations, we’re learning and improving, and there’s still plenty more to come, I’m hungry for more, and I believe in what we’re building together. The story is only just getting started.”

    The team's CEO Dan Towriss added: “Valtteri’s experience, perspective and commitment are important strengths for our team.

    “Continuity gives us a strong platform as we continue developing the team and working toward our ambitions in Formula 1."

    Perez narrowly leads the duo's qualifying battle 11-8, but has the upper hand in races, beating Bottas 13-4, excluding the Austrian and Hungarian races they both retired from.

    Neither Bottas nor Perez has scored points in Cadillac's first season.
