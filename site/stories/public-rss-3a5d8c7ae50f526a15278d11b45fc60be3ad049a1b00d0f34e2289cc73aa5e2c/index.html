    李安森职业生涯挽救性F1赛季的惊人数据

    小红牛车队宣布李安森和阿尔维德·林德布拉德将留队至2027赛季，结束了关于是否让李安森继续留队或提拔F2头号种子尼古拉·茨洛夫的长期争论。

    支撑着利亚姆·劳森职业生涯挽救性F1赛季的令人印象深刻的统计数据

    利亚姆·劳森的稳定性使他成为小红牛车队在2026年一级方程式赛季中如此宝贵的资产

    小红牛车队终于宣布利亚姆·劳森和阿尔维德·林德布拉德将留队参加2027赛季，结束了长期的不确定，因为车队和更广泛的红牛公司权衡是否让劳森再留队一年，或者提升下一个潜力股，F2领跑者尼古拉·索洛夫

    劳森今年获得了89分，其中75分来自小红牛车队，14分是在受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔缺席三场比赛时获得的，他在小红牛车队确保其最佳 Constructors' championship 结果中发挥了关键作用，仅靠阿尔卑斯山的奇迹才让车队未能锁定第五名

    但是，原本看似简单的向前一步决策，由于小红牛的双重动机而变得越来越复杂。一方面，它的主要存在理由仍然是寻找下一个马克斯·维斯塔潘或基米·安东内利。安东内利展示了早些时候晋升到一级方程式的价值——20岁时的艰难学习年最终导致了毁灭性的冠军争夺战——一些高管渴望看到Tsolov能够快速晋升以最大化他的成长。

    但小红牛还肩负着在商业和赛道表现上独立自主的双重任务。因此，获得第五名的奖金对这支英意车队来说是一个巨大的提升，该队在近年来对其基础设施进行了大量投资。

    这就是劳森发挥作用的地方。他的2026年表现对车队至关重要，他新发现的稳定性——尽管在赛季中有一段时间在红牛车队代表哈贾尔参赛——为车队提供了一个稳定的基准来评估新秀Lindblad的进步。现在，这一趋势预计将在2027年继续，Tsolov将担任预备车手角色，包括增加的私人测试、新秀FP1练习和模拟器任务。

    以下数据真实地展示了为什么劳森对车队来说太出色、太有价值，以至于不能被忽视。

    *75分属于小红牛车队，不包括劳森为红牛车队获得的14分

    劳森在中场位置上以20场比赛中获得15个前十名成绩和仅一次DSQ的成绩领先

    *哈达尔因手腕骨折缺席了三场比赛。

    如果小红牛车队只有劳森参赛，车队积分榜的排名情况。

    *仅剩一辆赛车参赛的排名

    The impressive stats underpinning Liam Lawson's career-saving F1 season

    How Liam Lawson's consistency made him such a valuable asset to Racing Bulls in the 2026 Formula 1 season

    Racing Bulls has finally announced Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad will stay put for the 2027 season, ending a long-running saga as the team and the wider Red Bull company weighed up keeping Lawson on board for another year or promoting the next prospect in the pipeline, F2 frontrunner Nikola Tsolov.

    Lawson scored 89 points this year, 75 for Racing Bulls and 14 standing in for the injured Isack Hadjar for three races, as he played a pivotal role in Racing Bulls securing its best-ever constructors' championship result, with only an Alpine miracle keeping the team from locking up fifth.

    But what felt like a straightforward forward decision became increasingly complex given Racing Bulls' dual motives. On one hand, its main raison d'etre is still to find the next Max Verstappen or Kimi Antonelli, too. With Antonelli demonstrating the value of an early promotion to F1 - a tough learning year leading to a devastating title campaign aged 20 - some executives were keen to see Tsolov fast-tracked to maximise his growth.

    But Racing Bulls is also tasked to stand on its own two feet both commercially and in terms of on-track performance. Therefore, taking the prize money attached to fifth place is a huge boost for the Anglo-Italian team, which has significantly invested in its infrastructure in recent years.

    That's where Lawson comes in. His 2026 performances have been so crucial to the team, and his newfound consistency - despite a mid-season three-race run at Red Bull to deputise for Hadjar - has given the team a solid benchmark to evaluate the progress of rookie Lindblad. That is now set to continue in 2027, with Tsolov set for a reserve role that includes an increased workload of private testing, rookie FP1 sessions and simulator duties.

    The following stats really demonstrate why Lawson has simply been too good and too valuable for the team to overlook.

    *75 points for Racing Bulls, not counting the 14 points Lawson scored for Red Bull

    Lawson also leads the midfield in number of grand prix points finishes with 15 top 10 results in 20 races and just a single DNF.

    *Hadjar missed three races due to a wrist fracture.
