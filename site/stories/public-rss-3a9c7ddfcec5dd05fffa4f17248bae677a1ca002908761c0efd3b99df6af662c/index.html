    布里亚托雷谈阿尔派如何借鉴迈凯伦复苏之路

    阿尔派车队负责人布里亚托雷表示，车队可以从迈凯伦近几个赛季的复苏中汲取灵感，走出低谷。

    布里亚托雷在阿隆索“灾难”之后从迈凯伦身上学到什么

    阿尔派车队负责人弗拉维奥·布里亚托雷认为，他的F1车队可以从极低的起点出发，走上与迈凯伦近几个赛季相似的复苏之路。

    失望、内斗以及高层人员的频繁更替，构成了阿尔派“失去的十年”的特征——雷诺于2015年底收购了这支位于恩斯通的车队。

    现任负责人弗拉维奥·布里亚托雷表示，阿尔派可以从迈凯伦身上获得一些启发：迈凯伦在2024年和2025年夺得车队总冠军，并在去年帮助兰多·诺里斯拿下车手总冠军。迈凯伦在2010年代经历了漫长的沉寂期，连续八个赛季未能取胜，并两次在车队积分榜上排名第九。

    阿尔派在经历了几个赛季在中游徘徊后，于2025年排名垫底。目前它位列第六。

    “这是‘ni’，”布里亚托雷在拉斯维加斯的国际汽联新闻发布会上被问及是否对该排名满意时说道。

    “这意味着不，也意味着是，意味着中游，而且我们在赛季最后阶段丢了很多分。车队在拼搏，车手在拼搏，无论在哪里，我们都在丢很多分，因为通常我们会以第五名完赛，而这样一来，我认为我们预算的是第六名完赛。这是我们的预算，但实际上我们非常接近第五名。

    “无论如何，相对于我在2024年初所拥有的，这是一个巨大的飞跃，现在车队团结在一起。工程方面、技术方面或多或少都在一起了。这又是一支赛车队了。这才是更重要的。”

    “而且我们下个赛季有一个很棒的赞助商，说实话，如果你问我是否开心，是的。我不是特别满意，但车队好多了。这就是我每天看到的。”

    恩斯通车队以阿尔派名义取得的唯一一场胜利是在2021年，当时埃斯特班·奥康利用第一弯的混乱抢到了赛道位置。

    布里亚托利此前曾以不同名称两次执掌阿尔派车队，他于2024年6月被任命为“执行顾问”，并自去年前车队领队奥利弗·奥克斯辞职以来一直是事实上的老板。在他此前的任期内，车队三次赢得制造商冠军，并带回四个车手冠军，但一直饱受争议困扰。

    自雷诺在2016赛季前收购该车队、并于2021年将其更名为阿尔派以来，管理层和工程团队的更迭速度惊人。除了埃斯特班·奥康的一次幸运胜利和2022年制造商锦标赛第四名之外，车队尚未真正挑战领先者，尽管布里亚托利的人脉为下赛季带来了古驰这一高知名度冠名赞助商。

    尽管在本赛季结束前，即便是要追上排名第五的小红牛也需要一次惊人的逆转，但布里亚托雷相信领先的车队是可以被追上的。

    “这是可能的，”他说。“我们在1994年做到过，在2006年、2008年、2007年也做到过。扎克[布朗，迈凯伦首席执行官]也做到过。

    “我记得费尔南多[阿隆索]时期的迈凯伦，那支车队也是一团糟。他们把一切整合在了一起。你需要一点时间。当然，你需要朝着正确的方向前进，但一级方程式实际上关乎时机和车手组织。

    “这不仅仅是车手的事，也不仅仅是车队的事。一切——维修区停站必须准确，策略必须正确，工程技术必须到位。赛车可以很好，车手需要完成好自己的工作，这就是我们需要具备的。”

    “如果一切运转良好，我不认为这很容易，但明年落后迈凯伦两到三成（十分之一秒）已经是巨大的成功……我们用的是同一款引擎。”

    迈凯伦和阿隆索在2010年代经历了一段艰难时期，其特点是组织混乱以及赛车和动力单元缺乏竞争力

    很少有人会质疑用“灾难”一词来定义费尔南多·阿隆索2015年至2018年第二次效力迈凯伦的那段时期。这位两届F1世界冠军曾寄予厚望，因为本田作为引擎合作伙伴回归，但这段关系最终在相互指责中破裂。

    虽然本田明显低估了打造涡轮增压混合动力单元的挑战，但当迈凯伦先后改用雷诺和梅赛德斯动力后，底盘方面的缺陷便暴露了出来。2018年臭名昭著的“Freddo-gate”事件则暴露了一种有毒的管理文化。

    扎克·布朗于2016年以执行董事身份加入，随后启动了数次重组中的第一次，这些重组最终使迈凯伦重返前列竞争力。

    布里亚托雷提及阿尔派此前的复苏颇为耐人寻味。1994年，作为贝纳通车队，它凭借迈克尔·舒马赫夺得车手世界冠军，但那个赛季因围绕非法牵引力控制系统的技术争议而蒙上阴影——该系统可通过车队软件中的隐藏菜单访问——此外还有一次维修区火灾，起因是有人拆除了加油系统中的过滤器以加快流速。

    贝纳通家族于1997年将布里亚托雷逐出，当时车队在舒马赫离开后陷入竞争力低谷，随后雷诺在2001年收购该组织后又将他请回。那个时代带来了两个车手世界冠军和一个车队总冠军（2006年），但次年又再度从巅峰跌落。

    那次低谷恰逢阿隆索离队以及轮胎供应商米其林退出F1。改用普利司通轮胎暴露了风洞中的缺陷，而一套平庸的车手阵容随后在一辆性能妥协的赛车中苦苦挣扎。

    史蒂文·蒂拍摄的1994年霍根海姆赛道上贝纳通车队技师保罗·西比被火焰吞没的照片已成为经典画面。

    阿隆索在2008年回归，但赛季初赛车仍相对缺乏竞争力。这引来了上层的压力，促使布里亚托雷策划了臭名昭著的新加坡大奖赛操纵事件，小尼尔森·皮奎特被指示故意撞车，引发安全车出动，这在赛道位置上让阿隆索受益。

    讽刺的是，阿隆索随后凭借实力赢得了下一场比赛——在日本站——使得在新加坡操纵比赛结果变得毫无必要。当此事在次年曝光后，布里亚托雷被短暂禁止参与一级方程式。

    What Flavio Briatore takes from McLaren after Fernando Alonso "disaster"

    Alpine boss Flavio Briatore believes his F1 team can chart a similar path to recovery from a very low bar as McLaren has in recent seasons

    Disappointment, infighting, and a revolving door of senior personnel have characterised Alpine's ‘lost decade' after Renault acquired the Enstone-based team at the end of 2015.

    Current boss Flavio Briatore says Alpine can take some inspiration from McLaren, which claimed the constructors' championship in 2024 and '25, and helped Lando Norris to the drivers' title last year. McLaren endured a long fallow period in the 2010s, going eight seasons without a win and twice finishing ninth in the constructors' standings.

    Alpine finished at the bottom of the table in 2025 after several seasons of bumping around in the midfield. It is currently sixth.

    "It's 'ni'," said Briatore when asked during the FIA press conference in Las Vegas whether he was satisfied with that position.

    "That means no, it means yes, means in the middle, and we're losing a lot of points in the last part of the season. The team fought, the driver fought, wherever it is, we're losing a lot of points because normally we'd be finishing P5, and in this way I think it's about we budgeted into finishing P6. This was our budget, but really we are very close to P5.

    "Anyway, with respect to what I have in the beginning of 2024, it's a big jump, and now the team is together. The engineering, the technical side is plus or minus together. It's a racing team again. This is what is more important.

    "And we have a great sponsor for the next season, and honestly, if you ask me if I am happy, yes. I'm not fantastic, but the team is much better. This is what I see every day."

    The Enstone team's only win as Alpine came in 2021, when Esteban Ocon parlayed first-corner chaos into track position

    Briatore, who has run the Alpine team twice before under different incarnations, was installed as ‘Executive Advisor' in June 2024 and has been de facto boss since the resignation of former team principal Oliver Oakes last year. Under his previous tenures the team won the constructors' title three times and returned four drivers' championships, but was dogged by controversies.

    Since Renault bought the team ahead of the 2016 season, rebranding it as Alpine in 2021, the rate of management and engineering churn has been remarkable. Aside from a fortuitous win for Esteban Ocon and a fourth-place finish in the constructors' championship in 2022, it has yet to seriously challenge the frontrunners although Briatore's connections have brought high-profile title sponsorship in the form of Gucci for next season.

    Although it would take a remarkable turnaround even to catch fifth-placed Racing Bulls before the end of this season, Briatore believes the frontrunners can be caught.

    "It's possible," he said. "We've done it in 1994, we've done it in 2006, 2008, 2007. Zak [Brown, McLaren CEO] has done it.

    "I remember McLaren at the time of Fernando [Alonso], the team was a disaster as well. They put everything together. You need a little bit of time. Sure, you need to go in the right direction, but Formula 1, in effect, is timing and driver organisation.

    "It's not only the driver and not only the team. Everything – the pitstops need to be correct, the strategy needs to be correct, the engineering needs to be correct. The car can be good, the driver needs to do the job, this is what we need to have.

    "If everything is working well, I don't think it's easy, but next year to be two tenths, three tenths behind McLaren is already a big success… we have the same engine."

    McLaren and Alonso had a torrid time in the 2010s, marked by organisational chaos and uncompetitive cars and power units

    Few would argue with the choice of "disaster" to define McLaren during Fernando Alonso's second stint there from 2015 to 2018. The double F1 world champion had great expectations, given the return of Honda as engine partner, but the relationship foundered in mutual recrimination.

    While Honda had clearly underestimated the challenges of creating a turbocharged hybrid power unit, when McLaren switched to Renault and then Mercedes power it exposed deficiencies on the chassis side. A toxic management culture was exposed during the infamous ‘Freddo-gate' incident of 2018.

    Zak Brown, who joined as executive director in 2016, then initiated the first of several restructurings which ultimately led to McLaren returning to frontrunning form.

    Briatore's references to Alpine's previous recoveries are intriguing. In 1994, as Benetton, it claimed the drivers' championship with Michael Schumacher, but the season was marred by technical controversies around illegal traction control systems accessible via a hidden menu in the team's software, and a pitlane fire caused by the removal of a filter in the refuelling system to speed up the flow.

    The Benetton family ejected Briatore in 1997 as the team fell into a competitive slump after Schumacher's departure, then Renault brought him back after it acquired the organisation in 2001. That era returned two drivers' championships and a constructors' title (in 2006) before another competitive fall from grace the following year.

    That slump coincided with Alonso leaving and tyre supplier Michelin quitting F1. The swap to Bridgestone tyres exposed shortcomings in the wind tunnel, and a middling driver-line-up then struggled with a compromised car.

    Steven Tee's shot of Benetton mechanic Paul Seaby enveloped in flames at Hockenheim in 1994 has become iconic

    Alonso returned in 2008 but the car was still relatively uncompetitive at the start of the season. This brought pressure from above, triggering Briatore to orchestrate the notorious fixing of the Singapore Grand Prix where Nelson Piquet Jr was directed to crash deliberately, bringing out a safety car which benefitted Alonso in terms of track position.

    Ironically, Alonso then won the next race – in Japan – on merit, rendering the manipulation of the outcome in Singapore unnecessary. When that was exposed the following year, Briatore was briefly banned from Formula 1.
