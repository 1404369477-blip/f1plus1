    霍肯伯格解释如何避免与队友博托莱托相撞的“惊险”瞬间

    在荷兰大奖赛中，尼科·霍肯伯格描述了他如何在最后一刻做出“本能”的转向，以避免与队友博托莱托发生侧面碰撞。他称这次险情为“惊险”的近距离接触。

    尼科·霍肯伯格描述了他如何在荷兰大奖赛中做出“本能”的最后一秒转向，以避免直接撞上队友的侧面。

    加布里埃尔·博托莱托在荷兰大奖赛第一圈超越霍肯伯格后不久，就在他面前打转。

    “太惊险了，太悬了，”霍肯伯格说。“你某种程度上是个乘客。”

    博托莱托在最后一个弯道失控，此前不久马克斯·维斯塔潘也在同一位置失控并撞上外侧护栏。博托莱托将赛车留在赛道上，但霍肯伯格必须立即决定是向左还是向右转向以避开另一辆奥迪。

    “我尽量晚地反应，然后本能地选择了方向，”霍肯伯格解释道。“显然我在等着看他会上滑还是下滑。在湿滑赛道上这很悬也很困难，但幸运的是我们成功避开了。”

    博托莱托怀疑自己失控的部分原因是看到前方的维斯塔潘冲出赛道。

    “我只看到大量烟雾冒出，”他说。“我想是马克斯撞车了，然后我可能对方向盘做出了一个小反应。

    “但我只是感觉到什么，然后我也失控了。我转了360度，又回来了。”

    两位奥迪车手都完成了比赛，霍肯伯格以第八名获得积分，而博托莱托起步不佳和打转使他排名第13。博托莱托说他在比赛中还有其他“问题”，但不愿详细说明。

    “我不想深入细节，因为我不知道我能不能说，之类的。但我们只需要理解今天发生的一些事情，然后继续前进。

    “我今天有一些起步问题，几乎让我可能无法驶出起步格，但我感谢车队的努力。他们真的全力以赴让一切发生并修复了一切。

    “很遗憾今天的结果不是我们想要的，但尼科拿到了一些积分，所以这很好。我们为主场车队带来了积分，所以很好。”

    英文原文

    Nico Hulkenberg described how he made an “instinctive” last-second swerve to avoid T-boning his team mate in the Dutch Grand Prix.

    Gabriel Bortoleto overtook Hulkenberg moments before spinning in front of him on the first lap of the Dutch Grand Prix.

    “It was scary, sketchy,” said Hulkenberg. “You’re a passenger to some [extent].”

    Bortoleto lost control of his car in the final corner, moments after Max Verstappen did the same and hit the barrier on the outside. Bortoleto kept his car on track but Hulkenberg had to make an immediate call whether to swerve to the left or right of the other Audi.

    “I tried to react as late as possible and then just instinctively chose the side,” Hulkenberg explained. “Obviously I was waiting to see if he’s going to slide up or down. On a wet track it’s sketchy and difficult, but luckily we managed to avoid that.”

    Bortoleto suspected he lost control of his car partly because he saw Verstappen going off ahead of him.

    “I just saw big smoke coming out,” he said. “I think it was Max that crashed and then I maybe made a small reaction on the steering.

    “But I just felt something and then I also lost the car. I did a 360, came back.”

    Both Audi drivers finished the race, Hulkenberg claiming points for eighth while Bortoleto’s poor start and spin left him 13th. Bortoleto said he had other “issues” in his race but was unwilling to elaborate on them.

    “I don’t want to go too much into details, because I don’t know if I can say, or whatever. But we just need to understand some stuff that happened today, and we’ll move on.

    “I had a few start problems today that almost made me not probably manage to run out of the grid box, but I appreciate the work of the team. They were really on it to make it happen and to fix everything.

    “It’s a shame the result was not what we wanted today, but Nico scored some points, so that’s good. We brought some points for the team at home, so good.”
