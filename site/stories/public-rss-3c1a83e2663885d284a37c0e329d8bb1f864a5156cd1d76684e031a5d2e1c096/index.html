    Leclerc led FP2 by 0.103s over Lando Norris, with George Russell just 0.003s further back in third. Championship leader Kimi Antonelli was fourth ahead of Max Verstappen, leaving three teams closely matched heading into qualifying.

    Ferrari’s Friday was not entirely clean, however, as Lewis Hamilton lost power and stopped midway through FP2.

    Antonelli remains on the verge of securing his first Formula 1 world championship, but Saturday’s qualifying session will determine whether he starts Sunday in a position to finish the job.
