    维斯塔潘调侃拉塞尔的无线电对话走红

    在荷兰大奖赛排位赛后，马克斯·维斯塔潘与红牛工程师詹皮耶罗·兰比亚斯的一段无线电对话在网络上走红，他预判了乔治·拉塞尔可能会抱怨维修区放行，但这一抱怨并未发生。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘（维斯塔潘）关于乔治·拉塞尔（拉塞尔）的无线电玩笑在荷兰大奖赛排位赛后走红

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在荷兰大奖赛排位赛期间关于乔治·拉塞尔的无线电玩笑已经走红

    四届F1世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘发给他的红牛比赛工程师詹皮耶罗·兰比亚斯（Gianpiero Lambiase）的一条车队无线电信息，在赞德沃特的荷兰大奖赛排位赛后走红。

    在排位赛期间，维斯塔潘预料到了梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔会提出投诉，但投诉实际上并没有出现。当车手们驶出维修区通道时，这位荷兰车手说：“我们可能会听到乔治在无线电里为维修区放行哭诉，但那其实一点也不危险。”

    就转播画面所展示的内容来看，这位梅赛德斯车手并没有像维斯塔潘开玩笑预测的那样抱怨放车问题。

    "什么都没做。被讨厌了。学学乔治吧，"一位车迷在Reddit上评论道，而另一位则补充说："大概是开玩笑吧，但我觉得如果乔治这么说的话，应该就不会被认为好笑了……"

    "当拉塞尔的无线电打开时，维斯塔潘有点尴尬，因为他问的其实是天气。那些他过去用来挑衅的人已经比他更成熟了，不再上他那些把戏的当，"另一位车迷发帖说。

    还有人评论道："抱怨对手可能会抱怨刚刚发生的情况，结果那位对手根本没抱怨。这是26年我最喜欢的一条信息，干得好GR。"

    进一步的反应包括：“我确实喜欢Max的车队无线电，因为我喜欢GP一直保持冷静的样子，Max太搞笑了哈哈”，以及“我喜欢Max的俏皮劲儿”。另一条评论写道：“Max实在太逗了。”

    迈凯伦的诺里斯拿下荷兰大奖赛杆位，Russell将与他共同排在头排。Verstappen排位第七，他的队友劳森——他顶替受伤的哈贾尔出战——将在第八位与他并肩发车。

    荷兰大奖赛将于8月23日星期日下午3点（英国时间下午2点）举行。

    Max Verstappen's George Russell radio joke goes viral after Dutch GP qualifying

    Max Verstappen's radio joke about George Russell during Dutch Grand Prix qualifying has gone viral

    A team radio message from four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen to his Red Bull race engineer Gianpiero Lambiase has gone viral after qualifying for the Dutch Grand Prix at Zandvoort.

    During the qualifying session, Verstappen anticipated a complaint from Mercedes rival George Russell, which never actually came. As the drivers made their way out of the pitlane, the Dutchman said, "We'll probably have George crying on the radio about the pit release, but it was nothing unsafe."

    As far as what was shown on the broadcast, the Mercedes driver did not complain about the pit release as Verstappen had jokingly predicted.

    "Does nothing. Got hated. Be like George," one fan commented on Reddit, while another added: "Banter ig, but I bet it wouldn't be considered as funny if George said it..."

    "It was a bit embarrassing for Verstappen when Russell's radio turned on, and he asked about the weather instead. The guys he used to bait just matured more than him and don't fall for his games anymore," another fan posted.

    Someone else commented: "Complaining that a rival will probably complain about a scenario that just occurred, only for that rival not to complain. My favourite message of 26, well done GR."

    Further reactions included: "I do like a Max radio just because I like how calm GP is all the time, very funny from Max lol," and "I love sassy Max." Another comment read: "Max is too funny."

    McLaren's Lando Norris secured pole position for the Dutch Grand Prix and will be joined by Russell on the front row. Verstappen qualified seventh and his team-mate Liam Lawson, who has stepped in for an injured Isack Hadjar, will line up beside him in eighth.

    The Dutch Grand Prix will take place at 3pm local time (2pm UK time) on Sunday 23 August.
