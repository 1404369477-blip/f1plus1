    F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛特别涂装汇总

    多支F1车队为本周的拉斯维加斯大奖赛带来了特别涂装，包括阿斯顿·马丁、凯迪拉克和威廉姆斯等。

    如同惯例，多个一级方程式车队将在本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛上展示特别的涂装。

    以下是目前已公布的全部涂装总结。

    阿斯顿·马丁为了庆祝其人工智能云合作伙伴CoreWeave，为其特别的拉斯维加斯涂装使用了大量的白色和黑色。

    阿斯顿·马丁将这种设计描述为‘数据、技术和性能如何结合以推动进步的视觉表达，从银石赛道上的AMR技术园区到拉斯维加斯大道的明亮灯光’。

    这些颜色将遍布整个车库，并出现在拉塞尔·斯特罗尔的头盔上。

    这辆涂装不仅展示了引擎合作伙伴本田的标志，还展示了制造商（主要位于美国）的豪华和性能部门Acura的标志。

    凯迪拉克为其首赛季的第三场和最后一场主场比赛准备了另一款特别涂装。

    车队表示："灵感来源于拉斯维加斯大道的灯光，这种半透明的、科技感十足的设计邀请粉丝深入了解。这是对凯迪拉克从零开始打造一级方程式车队的巨大努力背后的人们、决心和美国创新精神更深层次的致敬。"

    凯迪拉克也为迈阿密、银石和最近在奥斯汀的比赛准备了特殊的涂装。

    威廉姆斯车队为拉斯维加斯设计的引人注目的蓝色铬色图案是对其赞助商Atlassian的致敬。

    威廉姆斯车队表示，其“蓝色铬色FW48”将成为清晰和认知的移动象征，通过反射和放大拉斯维加斯大道的灯光，亚历克斯·阿隆索和卡洛斯·塞恩斯在内华达之夜疾驰而过。在铬色之下，裸露的碳纤维展现了每一圈都依赖的工程学科。

    阿隆索和塞恩兹还将拥有定制的赛车服和手套，以及在整个比赛周中特别定制的品牌赛车。

    英文原文

    As has become tradition, multiple Formula 1 teams are bringing special liveries for the Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend.

    Below you'll find a summary of all of them revealed so far.

    Aston Martin is celebrating its AI cloud partner, CoreWeave, with a big splash of white and black for its special Las Vegas livery.

    The design has been described by Aston Martin as a "visual expression of how data, technology and performance combine to drive progress, from the AMR Technology Campus in Silverstone to the bright lights of the Las Vegas Strip".

    The colours will be featured across the garage and Lance Stroll's helmet.

    Rather than the logos of engine partner Honda, this livery also sports those of the manufacturer's (primarily US-based) luxury and performance division Acura.

    Cadillac has another special livery for its third and final home race of its maiden F1 season.

    The team said: "Inspired by the lights of the Las Vegas Strip, the translucent, tech-forward design invites fans to look beneath the surface. It's a deeper tribute to the people, determination, and American ingenuity behind Cadillac's monumental effort of building an F1 team from the ground up."

    Cadillac also had special liveries for Miami, Silverstone and most recently in Austin.

    Williams has a striking blue chrome design for Las Vegas in tribute to its title sponsor Atlassian.

    Williams said its "blue chrome FW48 will become a moving symbol of clarity and knowing by reflecting and amplifying the Strip's glow as Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz speed through the Nevada night. Beneath the chrome, exposed carbon shows the engineering discipline that every lap depends on".

    Both Albon and Sainz will also have custom race suits and gloves, "plus a special, branded hot-laps car across the race week".
