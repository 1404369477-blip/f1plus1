    拉塞尔解释拉斯维加斯大奖赛末段撞车

    拉塞尔承认在拉斯维加斯大奖赛最后三圈为争胜做出“高风险动作”后撞车，名次跌至第九。

    拉塞尔解释拉斯维加斯大奖赛争胜途中的末段撞车

    拉塞尔整场都是维斯塔潘在内华达站争冠的主要对手，但撞车后名次跌至第九。

    拉塞尔承认在拉斯维加斯大奖赛最后三圈为争胜做出“高风险动作”后撞车。

    随着“拉锯式”比赛回归F1，杆位发车的维斯塔潘与拉塞尔在整场比赛中多次交换领先位置，上半程哈贾尔、后段安东内利也曾对领先集团构成挑战。

    在第48圈（共50圈）时，拉塞尔已跌至第三位，落后于维斯塔潘和安东内利，他插入队友银色赛车的内侧，但锁死轮胎并撞墙。轻微损伤使他得以完成比赛，尽管名次下滑至第九。

    “我知道如果我想赢得比赛，在最后几圈时必须处于第二位，”这位英国车手解释道。“基米没有超车模式，我只是尝试了一次高风险动作并付出了代价，但我不后悔。当然我对此感到失望，但我本可以保守一些，那样我会以第三名完赛。”

    由于F1新动力单元——尤其是其电池和能量部署——在这条高速街道赛道上的工作方式，拉塞尔至少六次从维斯塔潘手中夺走领先位置，却始终未能保持住。

    “这很有趣，”这位梅赛德斯车手评论道。“我觉得比赛前半程还挺好玩的。然后某一刻开始有点累人，因为绝对不可能拉开差距。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    “我觉得自己当时处于非常好的能量部署模式，可以超越维斯塔潘并在起跑线前领先。基米当时在超车，但马克斯在过线前已经反超了回来。但这让我在中段非常被动，这也是我丢掉一些位置的原因。我掉到了第五位，然后不得不改变我的策略模式，这帮助我重新提升了排名。

    “我觉得到了最后一圈，我可以回到我必须尝试去赢下比赛的状态。那很艰难。”

    这种‘悠悠球’式的比赛让人想起电动方程式，自本赛季开局几站以来，还没有出现过如此程度的场面。

    “真的，就只是在实践中边跑边学，”拉塞尔补充道。“是啊，边跑边学，感觉非常诡异。直道上的速度差异极其巨大。我在直道上对马克斯每圈能追回1.3秒，太疯狂了。一场奇怪的比赛。”

    拉塞尔的低名次意味着他在车手积分榜上跌至第四——与第五名兰多·诺里斯积分相同，落后刘易斯·汉密尔顿三分，与第二名维斯塔潘相差高达40分。

    George Russell explains late-race crash in Las Vegas F1 victory bid

    Russell was Verstappen’s main rival for victory throughout the Nevadan race but crashed and collapsed to ninth

    George Russell has admitted to making “a high-risk move” after crashing with three laps remaining in Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix in an attempt to clinch victory.

    As ‘yo-yo’ racing returned to F1, polesitter Max Verstappen and Russell frequently traded the lead throughout the race, occasionally challenged by the sister cars of Isack Hadjar in the first half of the race and Kimi Antonelli later on.

    On lap 48 of 50, Russell had dropped down to third behind Verstappen and Antonelli and dived down the inside of his team-mate’s Silver Arrow, but locked up and hit the wall. Minimal damage meant he was able to finish the race, albeit down in ninth.

    “I knew if I wanted to win the race I needed to be in second going into the last couple of laps,” the Briton explained. “Kimi didn't have the overtake mode and I just went for a high-risk move and paid the price, but I don't regret it. Of course I'm disappointed with it, but I could have been conservative and I would have finished in third.”

    Russell took the lead of the race away from Verstappen on at least six occasions without ever being able to hold on to it, due to the way F1’s new power units – and particularly their batteries and energy deployment – work on this high-speed street circuit.

    “It was interesting,” the Mercedes driver commented. “I think it was quite fun for maybe half the race. Then it started to get a bit tiring at one point because it was absolutely impossible to get away.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    “I felt I was in a very good deployment mode to overtake Verstappen and be ahead of the start line. Kimi was overtaking but Max was already re-overtaking before the line. But it left me very vulnerable in the middle sector, and that's why I lost some positions. I went down to P5, and then I had to change my strat mode, and it helped me get back up the order.

    “I felt into the last lap I could go back to what I had to try and win the race. It was tough.”

    This ‘yo-yo’ type of racing, which is reminiscent of Formula E, hadn’t been seen to this extent since the opening races of the season.

    “It was just learning on the fly, really,” Russell added. “Yeah, learning on the fly, it was very bizarre. The speed differences in the straight were monumental. I was gaining 1.3s on the straight on Max, it was wild. Strange race.”

    Russell’s lowly result means he is down to fourth in the drivers’ championship – on equal points with fifth-placed Lando Norris, three points away from Lewis Hamilton and a whopping 40 points adrift of runner-up Verstappen.
