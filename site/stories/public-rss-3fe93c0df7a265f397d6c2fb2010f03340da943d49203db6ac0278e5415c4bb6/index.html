    塞纳1994年威廉姆斯FW16赛车将拍卖，估价600万美元

    三届F1世界冠军艾尔顿·塞纳在1994赛季驾驶过的威廉姆斯FW16赛车将在纽约拍卖，估价400万至600万美元。

    艾尔顿·塞纳1994年的威廉姆斯F1赛车将拍卖，估价600万美元

    一辆艾尔顿·塞纳在1994年F1赛季驾驶过的威廉姆斯FW16即将在纽约拍卖

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    三届F1世界冠军艾尔顿·塞纳在个人最后一个赛季驾驶过的威廉姆斯FW16赛车将在纽约拍卖。

    RM苏富比将在苏富比“现在与当代晚间拍卖”上提供底盘编号FW16B-01，估价为400万至600万美元（295万至445万英镑）。这是该车首次公开出售。

    1994年，这位巴西车手从迈凯伦转投威廉姆斯时，格罗夫车队同意，如果赢得当年的车队总冠军，他将获得其中一台底盘。

    塞纳不幸在1994年圣马力诺大奖赛中驾驶FW16-02底盘身亡。威廉姆斯随后击败贝纳通夺得车队总冠军，而达蒙·希尔以一分之差将车手总冠军输给了迈克尔·舒马赫。年底，车队信守承诺，将FW16B-01交给了塞纳的家人。

    据RM苏富比称，这是塞纳驾驶过的仅存两台FW16之一，尽管他本人从未驾驶该底盘参加比赛。它是赛季揭幕战巴西大奖赛的备用车，但塞纳在英特拉格斯驾驶它参加了正赛日的暖胎练习，并做出了比后来夺冠的舒马赫更快的圈速。

    几天后，他和希尔在赫雷斯的测试中共同驾驶了这辆车，在三节测试中总共跑了842公里。

    塞纳家族从1994年到2024年拥有FW16B-01，这辆车一直由威廉姆斯车队保管。现任车主通过苏富比拍卖行私下购得。这辆车从未经过修复。

    拍卖清单上写道：“FW16B-01底盘在接下来的30年里完全保持原样，唯一的显著例外是加装了塞纳的个人座椅，如今它呈现出完全保存完好、未经修复的状态。”

    “现任车主于2024年从塞纳家族通过苏富比拍卖行购得此车，此后它一直保持保存状态，未被使用。”

    Ayrton Senna's 1994 Williams F1 car heads to auction with $6million estimate

    An Ayrton Senna-driven Williams FW16 from the 1994 Formula 1 season is set to go up for auction in New York

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    A Williams FW16 driven by three-time Formula 1 champion Ayrton Senna in his final season in the series is going under the hammer in New York.

    RM Sotheby's is offering chassis FW16B-01 in Sotheby's 'The Now & Contemporary Evening Auction', with an estimate of $4million to $6million (£2.95million to £4.45million). It is the first time the car has been put up for public sale.

    When the Brazilian driver joined Williams from McLaren in 1994, the Grove outfit agreed that he would receive one of its chassis if it won that year's constructors' championship.

    Senna tragically died at the 1994 San Marino Grand Prix, in chassis FW16-02. Williams went on to beat Benetton to the constructors' crown, while Damon Hill lost the drivers' title to Michael Schumacher by a single point. At the end of the year, the team kept its word and gave FW16B-01 to Senna's family.

    According to RM Sotheby's, it is one of only two surviving FW16s that Senna drove, although he never raced the chassis himself. It was the spare car at the season-opening Brazilian Grand Prix, but Senna took it out for the race-day warm-up at Interlagos and set a quicker time than Schumacher, who went on to win.

    Days later, he and Hill shared it in testing at Jerez, covering 842km across three sessions.

    The Senna family owned the FW16B-01 from 1994 until 2024, with the car kept in Williams's care. The current owner bought it privately through RM Sotheby's. It has never been restored.

    "Chassis FW16B-01 remained completely untouched for the following 30 years, with a notable exception being the addition of Senna’s personal seat, and is today presented in totally preserved, unrestored condition," the auction listing states.

    "Acquired by the current owner in 2024 having been purchased from the Senna family via RM Sotheby’s, it has remained preserved and unused since."
