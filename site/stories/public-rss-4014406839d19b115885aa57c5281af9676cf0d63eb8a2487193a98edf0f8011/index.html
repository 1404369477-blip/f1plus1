    哈斯确认卡马拉出战拉斯维加斯站

    哈斯车队宣布，新签下的拉斐尔·卡马拉将在拉斯维加斯大奖赛继续驾驶赛车，此前他在巴西完成了混乱的F1首秀。

    哈斯新签下的拉斐尔·卡马拉将很快获得机会，摆脱他在巴西混乱的F1首秀，因为他将继续驾驶赛车出战本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛。

    卡马拉的首秀因哈斯与埃斯特班·奥康戏剧性且突然的分手而从2027年提前，但最初的公告只确认卡马拉出战英特拉格斯。

    如今哈斯宣布他将继续留在拉斯维加斯站，而曾在墨西哥大奖赛周末中途顶替奥康的平川亮，将回到阵容中，在第一次自由练习中驾驶奥利·贝尔曼的赛车。

    不过拉斯维加斯之后的情况可能更复杂，因为F1最后几站卡塔尔和阿布扎比同期也有F2比赛，而卡马拉目前以仅7分的优势领跑F2积分榜，领先红牛储备车手尼科拉·措洛夫。鉴于赛程安排，同一周末同时参加F2和F1实际上是不可能的。

    卡马拉表示，他“期待在拉斯维加斯重新坐进赛车”，因为巴西站是一次“很好的学习经历”。

    车队领队小松礼雄表示，卡马拉的首秀是在“极具挑战性的条件下”完成的，但他仍然“向我们和全世界展示了他为什么值得我们签下他，直到2027年”。

    卡马拉在那场首秀周末中排名第20位，也就是最后一名，落后两圈。但考虑到在那场比赛之前他从未驾驶过2026年的哈斯赛车，再加上不断变化的天气条件，他面临着许多可以理解的不利因素。

    就首秀周末而言，很难想象还有比拉斐尔·卡马拉所面对的更为艰难的处境了。

    他直到本周初才被临时召入，此前从未驾驶过哈斯赛车或任何2026款F1赛车。这压力巨大且充满挑战，而天气预报也从未显示出对他有利的迹象。事实证明果然如此。

    同样令人沮丧和鼓舞的是，卡马拉展现出了许多潜力的闪光点。他在排位赛中非常快，并让自己处于一个非常有利的位置，即便排位赛期间下了雨。如果他在艰难条件下首秀就闯入Q3，那也不会令人意外，那将是非常令人印象深刻的表现。

    在大奖赛的干胎阶段，他与队友奥利·贝尔曼相比实际上表现不错：在两段软胎 阶段中平均每圈慢约十分之一秒。但需要注意的是，贝尔曼的比赛受到了影响，他有一段时间陷入了车流。卡马拉在比赛开始和结束时使用半雨胎的阶段特别弱：与软胎相比，这两段与贝尔曼的差距都大幅扩大。

    他在排位赛中的两次失误无疑让他的信心受到了一些打击。第一次是水滑和车轮打滑的混合作用，导致赛车在雷塔奥波斯塔直道上失控甩尾。第二次是在12号弯洪考弯锁死轮胎——许多车手在那里都遇到了麻烦——随后相当猛烈地撞上了护栏。

    他在比赛开始时没有过度推进是可以理解的。事实上，哈斯车队很可能鼓励他不要冒任何愚蠢的风险。因此他花了一段时间才慢慢进入状态并找到节奏，但当他找到节奏时，他的速度非常非常快。

    如果他在最后阶段能有一段更强的中途跑（inter stint）会更好。但到那时已经没有什么可争取的了，他只需要把车安全带回，所以这一点可以理解。

    在获得第20名、落后两圈完赛后，他的F1生涯只会从这里变得更好。这不仅仅是因为成绩在纸面上如此糟糕，还因为他在排位赛和大奖赛中展现出的真正潜力也时有闪现。

    英文原文

    Haas's new signing Rafael Camara will get a quick chance to move on from his messy Formula 1 debut in Brazil as he will stay in the car for this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Camara's debut was brought forward from 2027 following Haas's dramatic and abrupt split with Esteban Ocon, but the original announcement only committed to Camara racing at Interlagos.

    Now Haas has announced he will stay on for Vegas, with Ryo Hirakawa - who initially jumped in for Ocon mid-way through the Mexican GP weekend - back in the line-up for FP1 in Ollie Bearman's car.

    The situation beyond Vegas may be more complicated though, as the final F1 rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi also have Formula 2 on the bill, and Camara currently leads the F2 standings by just seven points over Red Bull reserve Nikola Tsolov. It is effectively impossible to race in both F2 and F1 over the same weekend given the schedules.

    Camara said he was "looking forward to getting back in the car in Las Vegas" as Brazil had been a "good learning experience".

    Team principal Ayao Komatsu said Camara's debut had been in "extremely challenging conditions" but that he's still "showed us and the world why we signed him for 2027".

    Camara ended that debut weekend 20th and last, two laps down. But having never driven the 2026 Haas before that event, and given the changing weather conditions, he faced plenty of mitigating factors.

    As debut weekends go, it's hard to imagine tougher circumstances than the ones Rafael Camara faced.

    He only got called up at the start of the week and hadn't driven the Haas or any 2026 F1 car before. This was high pressure and challenging, and the forecast never looked like it was going to be on his side. And so it proved.

    What's probably equally frustrating and encouraging is that there were so many flashes of potential from Camara. He was very quick in qualifying and got himself into a really nice place, even with the rain that fell during the session. It would not have been a surprise if he'd snuck into Q3 on debut in tough conditions, which would have been very impressive.

    In the slick running in the grand prix, he actually compared well to team-mate Ollie Bearman: about a tenth of a second a lap slower on average across two stints on softs. The caveat is that Bearman's race was compromised and he spent some time in traffic. Camara was particularly weak in the intermediate running at the start of the race and again at the end: the gap to Bearman ballooned in both of those stints compared with the softs.

    His confidence was surely battered a little by his two offs in qualifying. The first was a mix of aquaplaning and wheelspin that sent the car fishtailing out of control down the Reta Oposta straight. The second was a lock-up at the Juncao Turn 12, where a lot of drivers had trouble, followed by a fairly heavy hit into the barrier.

    You can forgive him for not over-pushing at the start of the race. In fact, Haas probably encouraged him not to take any silly risks. So it took a while for him to ease his way in and find his rhythm but, when he did, he was very, very quick.

    It would have been better if he could have had a stronger inter stint at the end. But by that point there was nothing to play for and he just needed to bring it home, so you can understand that.

    After finishing 20th and two laps down, his F1 career will only get better from here. That's not just because the result was so weak on paper but because the glimpses of his true potential throughout qualifying and the grand prix were there too.
