    Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu has dismissed speculation suggesting Oliver Bearman will be leaving the team

    Haas boss Ayao Komatsu has furiously dismissed reports claiming that contract extension talks with Oliver Bearman are being held up by the British driver demanding a £5m annual salary.

    Both Bearman and Komatsu have expressed confidence in recent weeks that a deal will be done to extend the 21-year-old's current contract, which expires at the end of the 2026 season.

    With Haas having already announced the signing of Bearman's fellow Ferrari junior Rafael Camara to replace Esteban Ocon next season, the lack of confirmation on his own future has led to speculation over what the issues are that are holding up the deal.

    Asked on Friday at the Mexico City Grand Prix about claims that Bearman's financial demands are the main sticking point, Komatsu said: "I have no idea where that comes from.

    "I've experienced this sometimes this season as well. People say there's no smoke without fire but really some people will invent a story.

    "What happened in Miami? Apparently, I had an argument with (Bearman's team-mate) Esteban Ocon. There's just absolutely zero truth in it and even those people claiming that they had some insider information etc, absolute total nonsense.

    "This one again I don't know who smoked up this six-million euros number. The first time I saw it on Instagram, or Stu [Morrison, Haas press officer] forwarded it to me, and I was just like, 'oh another great… somebody has come up and stirred up whatever'.
