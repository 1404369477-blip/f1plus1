    凯迪拉克选择博塔斯留队，赫塔获2027年储备车手邀约

    凯迪拉克F1车队确认博塔斯将在下赛季继续与佩雷兹搭档，并向感到失望的赫塔提供了2027年储备车手角色。赫塔此前已在巴西大奖赛完成足够里程以获得F1超级驾照，但下赛季无缘正式车手席位。

    凯迪拉克F1车队已向感到“失望”的科尔顿·赫塔提供了2027年储备车手角色，此前车队选择让瓦尔特利·博塔斯下赛季继续为车队比赛。

    凯迪拉克在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛前确认博塔斯将至少再留一个赛季、继续担任塞尔吉奥·佩雷兹的队友，从而结束了赫塔可能取代博塔斯的猜测。

    博塔斯原本已有合同在身，但在博塔斯经历了一个常常难以与佩雷兹匹敌的艰难赛季后，凯迪拉克此前拒绝平息有关博塔斯可能被买断并由赫塔接替的猜测。

    赫塔此前一直在积累足够里程以取得F1超级驾照，这一任务最终在11月早些时候的巴西大奖赛上完成，但如今这一努力不会在下赛季转化为F1正式车手席位。

    在拉斯维加斯大奖赛期间，凯迪拉克车队所有者TWG Motorsports的首席执行官丹·托里斯表示，赫塔对车队不得不做出的这个“艰难决定”感到“失望”。

    “这绝对是一个艰难的决定，与科尔顿进行了一次艰难的讨论，”托里斯说，“我认为他很失望。为了走到这一步，付出了很多努力，也有很多积累。”

    “很明显，今年科尔顿在F2中经历了一个非常艰难的赛季，但他在第一节自由练习和旧车测试（TPC）环境中所做的工作相当扎实，表明他确实有能力驾驶这辆赛车。

    “但当我们审视车队目前的状况以及车队所需时，博塔斯是面向未来的最佳选择。

    “但我们会继续和科尔顿讨论目前摆在桌面上的各种选择，并再给一点时间，因为我认为他对没能得到那个机会感到失望。”

    赫塔在F2的成绩并不理想——最好排位赛成绩为第三，在巴塞罗那获得第五名，但除此之外大多在排位赛和正赛中远离前十——不过这些成绩应当放在一位来自印地赛车的车手必须迅速适应一个完全陌生且水平极高的欧洲赛车环境的背景下来看待。他为凯迪拉克出战的六次第一节自由练习（FP1）表现也并不惊艳。

    如今一些车手在F1测试中的表现远比他们在F2中能展现出来的更令人印象深刻，这并不罕见；而像凯迪拉克这样一支全新的车队虽然没有自己的测试旧车项目，却能够通过其他车队（包括阿尔派）借出的旧车来评估赫塔，看起来赫塔在那样的环境中表现得更令人印象深刻。

    但还不足以令人印象深刻到把博塔斯挤出比赛席位，因此赫塔将需要选择是否接受凯迪拉克的最新报价，并耐心等待到2028年。

    “我们已经给了他一个储备车手的位置。2028年的席位是开放的。所以对科尔顿来说，桌面上有多个选择，”托里斯确认道。“其他（摩托）运动领域也有选择。”

    “我认为他已经决定了一个方向，但我们仍在就此进行沟通，并期待看到科尔顿想做什么。”

    托里斯基本上还确认了车队正在认真评估在2027年替换博塔斯，但认定他在2026年与佩雷斯的赛车表现差距有足够部分“不在他的控制范围内”，因此不足以构成终止合同的理由。

    “我们确实想审视一下，”托里斯在谈到对博塔斯位置的疑虑时说。“当我们纵观各场比赛时，虽然练习赛和排位赛与佩雷斯相似，但正赛速度下降了。

    其中一些是瓦尔特里无法控制的幕后因素。我们确实想研究一下这一点。

    我们也在努力让科尔顿·赫塔做好准备。我们车队有一位美国车手参赛很重要。但最终，当我们审视之后，瓦尔特里才是合适的人选。

    我们希望让佩雷兹和瓦尔特里继续搭档，他们的经验、连续性以及他们为车队带来的东西。

    最终，这对车队来说是正确的决定，我们很高兴与瓦尔特里续约。他对我们来说绝对非常出色。

    英文原文

    The Cadillac Formula 1 team has offered a "disappointed" Colton Herta a reserve driver role for 2027, after choosing Valtteri Bottas to continue racing for the team next season.

    Cadillac ended speculation that Herta could replace Bottas by confirming ahead of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix that Bottas would remain Sergio Perez's team-mate for at least one more season.

    Bottas was already under contract, but Cadillac refused to dampen speculation Bottas could be paid off and replaced by Herta after a tough season in which Bottas has often struggled to match Perez.

    Herta was building up enough mileage to secure an F1 superlicence, a mission that was finally accomplished at the Brazilian Grand Prix, earlier in November, but now won't see that quest turn into an F1 race seat next season.

    Speaking at the Las Vegas GP, Dan Towriss, CEO of Cadillac team owner TWG Motorsports, said Herta was "disappointed" with what had been a "tough decision" for the team to make.

    "It was definitely a tough decision, a tough discussion with Colton," Towriss said "I think he was disappointed. There was a lot of work, a lot of build-up going to this.

    "It was obvious it was a very tough season for Colton in F2 this year, but then work that he did in FP1s and the TPC [Testing of Previous Cars] setting were quite strong and showed that he really is capable of driving this car.

    "But when we look at where the team is right now and what's needed for the team, Valtteri was the best decision going forward.

    "But we're going to continue to talk to Colton about the options that are on the table and give it a little bit of time because I think he is disappointed to not have that opportunity."

    Herta's F2 results have been poor - a best qualifying of third and a fifth placed finish at Barcelona but otherwise mostly qualifying and racing well outside the top 10 - but those results should be taken in the context of a driver from IndyCar having to rapidly adapt to an unfamiliar European racing scene at a very high level. His six FP1 outings for Cadillac haven't been stunning either.

    It's not uncommon now for some drivers to be much more impressive in F1 test running than they can show in F2, and although a brand new team like Cadillac doesn't have its own TPC programme, it has been able to evaluate Herta in old cars loaned by other teams, including Alpine, and it would seem Herta has been more impressive in that setting.

    But not impressive enough to force Bottas out of a race seat, and so Herta will need to choose whether to accept Cadillac's latest offer and bide his time until 2028.

    "We've offered him a reserve position. The seat's open for 2028. So there are options on the table for Colton," Towriss confirmed. "There are options in other [motor]sports as well.

    "I think he's decided on a direction, but we're still in conversations on that and look forward to seeing what Colton wants to do."

    Towriss essentially also confirmed the team was seriously evaluating replacing Bottas for 2027, but determined that enough of his race performance discrepancy to Perez in 2026 had been "out of his control" to not warrant a contract termination.

    "We did want to take a look at it," said Towriss of the doubt over Bottas's position. "As we looked across the races, while practice and qualifying was similar to Checo, race pace was down.

    "Some of that is things behind the scenes that are out of Valtteri's control. We did want to take a look at that.

    "We were working hard to get Colton Herta ready as well. It's important to have an American driver with our team racing. But ultimately, when we took a look at it, Valtteri's the right guy.

    "We want to keep Checo and Valtteri together, their experience, the continuity, what they bring to the team.

    "Ultimately, it was the right decision for the team and we were happy to extend Valtteri. He's been absolutely fantastic for us."
