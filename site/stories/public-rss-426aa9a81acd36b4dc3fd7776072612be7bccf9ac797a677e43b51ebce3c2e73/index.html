    拉塞尔在湿滑的拉斯维加斯大奖赛第二场自由练习中险些被阿尔派和加斯利超越

    梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛第二场自由练习中险些被阿尔派和加斯利超越。尽管赛道湿滑，但比赛条件比第一场练习要好得多。

    梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛第二场自由练习中险些被阿尔派和加斯利超越。

    尽管赛道湿滑，但比赛条件比第一场练习要好得多。

    兰多·诺里斯在练习三分之一的时间内从湿胎切换到中间胎，随着赛道逐渐干燥，时间也在不断下降。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔在大部分时间里领跑法拉利，但最终被拉塞尔在最后几秒以1m47.272s的圈速超越。

    加斯利在飞驰过前两个赛段时看起来有机会击败梅赛德斯，但一次大的转向失误让他落后了0.071秒，未能获得第一名。

    勒克莱尔保持第三名，红牛车队的伊萨克·哈德贾排名第四，他在最后一圈遇到了他所说的“完全迷失”的奥利·贝尔曼的哈斯赛车，这让他失去了位置。

    早先的领跑者诺里斯最终排名第七，领先冠军安东内利一名。

    维斯塔潘的练习非常奇特。他在红牛车队的维修区里坐了大部分时间，只完成了九圈，这是所有人中最少的。

    当他终于在最后几分钟出来进行第一次正式的跑圈时，他抱怨说没有"动力"。工程师Gianpiero Lambiase建议红牛的引擎只需要升温，但维斯塔潘坚决反驳说"这不是正常的"。

    他在结束一小时的比赛后仅排名第13，通过车队无线电抱怨说赛车"不好"，并且有和他两周前在巴西大奖赛练习中报告的"相同"问题。

    尽管天气预报显示整个赛事的天气将保持干燥，但周四的条件从未干燥到足以使用光头胎。

    勒克莱尔提出了尝试光头胎的想法，但被法拉利劝阻，而拉塞尔在梅赛德斯询问他对赛道条件的看法时回应说"如果你在问光头胎，没门，约瑟夫"。

    尽管路面湿滑，但唯一的事故只是轻微的跑偏或打滑，唯一的一次红旗是因为一块大的塑料包装物被风吹到赛道上，被拉塞尔·斯特罗尔的阿斯顿·马丁车队收集。

    加斯利是唯一一个最终在成绩单上占据引人注目位置的黑马，但阿斯顿·马丁车队当天排名第10和第11，以及塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯驾驶的凯迪拉克排名第14，都是相当引人注目的成绩。

    英文原文

    Mercedes' George Russell narrowly denied Pierre Gasly and Alpine an upset result in second practice for Formula 1's Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Conditions remained wet for Thursday night's second session but were much more amenable than they had been in the puddle-strewn practice one in which no one even tried a flying lap and the most laps any driver completed was two.

    Lando Norris led a move from wet to intermediate tyres a third of the way into the session, and times kept falling as the track kept drying through to the end.

    Charles Leclerc topped much of the running for Ferrari, but was usurped in the final seconds by Russell's 1m47.272s lap.

    Gasly looked like he had a shot at toppling the Mercedes as he flew through the first two sectors, before a big oversteer slide cost him enough that he fell 0.071 seconds short of first place.

    Leclerc remained third, with Isack Hadjar fourth for Red Bull having lost a late lap when he came across what he described as the "completely lost" Ollie Bearman's Haas running slowly in front of him.

    Early pacesetter Norris fell to seventh for McLaren by the end, one place ahead of champion Kimi Antonelli.

    Max Verstappen had a curious session. He sat out much of the hour in the Red Bull garage, completing only nine laps - the least of anyone.

    When he did come out for his first proper run in the closing minutes, he complained of having "no power". Engineer Gianpiero Lambiase suggested the Red Bull's engine just need to come up to temperature, but Verstappen refuted that with a firm "this is not normal".

    Having ended the hour only 13th, he complained over team radio that the car was "not good" and had the "same" problems as he'd reported in Brazilian GP practice a fortnight ago.

    Though the weather is forecast to be dry for the rest of the event, conditions never dried enough for slicks on Thursday.

    Leclerc floated the idea of trying them but was discouraged by Ferrari, while Russell responded "if you're asking about slicks, no way Jose" when Mercedes asked for his thoughts on track conditions.

    Despite the slippery surface, the only incidents were minor runoff area visits or spins, and the only red flag was caused when a large piece of plastic packaging blew onto the track and was collected by Lance Stroll's Aston Martin.

    Gasly was the only underdog to end up in a truly eye-catching place on the timesheets, but Aston Martin finishing the day 10th and 11th and Sergio Perez's Cadillac 14th were reasonably notable results.
