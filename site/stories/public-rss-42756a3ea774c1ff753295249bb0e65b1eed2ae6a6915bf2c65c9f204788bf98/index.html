    加斯利摩纳哥领奖台听证会将闭门进行

    国际汽联国际上诉法院关于皮埃尔·加斯利在F1摩纳哥大奖赛领奖台位置恢复的听证会将于下周闭门举行。

    FIA国际上诉法庭关于恢复加斯利在F1摩纳哥大奖赛领奖台成绩的听证会将于下周举行——但不寻常的是，这次听证会将闭门进行。

    Gasly被判获得第三名，此前阿尔派车队成功推翻了他因在维修通道超速而收到的赛后时间处罚。

    FIA的这一态度转变是因为Formula One Management承认其维修通道测量存在误差，导致Gasly被错误地认定违反了限速规定。

    FIA行使复审权将第三名还给Gasly的决定并未获得其他车队的认可，各车队随后对该决定提出了抗议。

    红牛车队和迈凯伦车队都决定将此事上诉至国际汽联国际上诉法院。红牛车队的车手伊斯克·哈贾尔（哈贾尔）原本被判定为第三名，之后加斯利才被恢复名次；而迈凯伦车队的车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里（皮亚斯特里）则在比赛中因超速接受了处罚。

    听证会现已定于8月25日在巴黎的国际汽联总部举行。

    然而，与常规程序不同的是，此次听证会将闭门进行，公众和媒体均被拒之门外。

    根据国际汽联的司法规则，除成本上限违规案件外，国际上诉法院的听证会均'对媒体和公众开放'。

    不过有一项条款规定：“听证会主席可以出于公共秩序的原因或为了保护数据的保密性，决定以非公开形式进行全部或部分听证。”

    国际汽联（FIA）发布的一份声明称，评审团之所以决定不允许媒体和公众参加此次听证会，是因为案件相关方提出了请求。

    声明称：“根据评审团的决定，并应本案相关方的请求，出于保密原因，上述听证会不对公众、媒体以及任何未获授权出席的人员开放。”

    是什么促成了这一保密请求尚未披露，但很可能与维修区测量如何进行以及车队如何计算车速的相关细节有关。

    国际上诉法院任何听证会的裁决通常需要长达三周时间才能作出，但国际汽联（FIA）表示，这次将“尽快”作出裁决。

    英文原文

    The FIA International Court of Appeal hearing into Pierre Gasly's podium reinstatement in Formula 1's Monaco Grand Prix will take place next week - but unusually behind closed doors.

    Gasly was awarded third place after Alpine successfully overturned a post-race time penalty he was given for speeding in the pitlane.

    This change of heart from the FIA was the result of Formula One Management admitting that there had been errors with its pitlane measurements that meant Gasly was wrongly deemed to have broken the speed limit.

    The FIA's right of review decision to give Gasly his third place back did not go down well with other teams, which subsequently protested the decision.

    Both Red Bull, whose driver Isack Hadjar had originally been classified third before Gasly was reinstated, and McLaren, whose driver Oscar Piastri had served a speeding penalty in the race, elected to take the matter to the FIA's International Court of Appeal.

    A hearing has now been set for the FIA's headquarters in Paris on August 25.

    However, in a step away from normal process, the session will take place behind closed doors with the public and media barred.

    Under the FIA's judicial rules, International Court of Appeal hearings for anything other than cost cap breaches are "open to the media and to the public".

    There is a clause though that states: "The president of the hearing may however decide to conduct all or part of the hearings in closed sessions for public order reasons or in order to preserve the confidentiality of data."

    A statement issued by the FIA said that the judging panel had elected not to allow the media and public access to this hearing because of a request from the parties involved in the case.

    "Upon decision of the judging panel and as requested by the parties involved in this case, for confidentiality reasons the above hearing will be closed to public, media and any person who is not authorised to attend it," it said.

    What has prompted the request for confidentiality has not been revealed, but it most likely relates to the details surrounding how pitlane measurements are made and how teams calculate speeds.

    A verdict on any International Court of Appeal hearing typically takes up to three weeks to be made, but the FIA has said that on this occasion a ruling will be made "as soon as possible".
