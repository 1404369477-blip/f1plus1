    安东内利墨西哥加冕F1世界冠军，成为史上最年轻冠军

    安东内利在墨西哥城大奖赛以第三名完赛，锁定个人首个F1世界冠军，并以20岁之龄成为F1史上最年轻的世界冠军，将维特尔的纪录提前三年。

    情绪激动的基米·安东内利在墨西哥夺得首个F1世界冠军后“说不出话”

    基米·安东内利在墨西哥城大奖赛锁定F1世界冠军后流下眼泪

    新加冕的F1世界冠军基米·安东内利含泪向梅赛德斯致敬，他在墨西哥成为大奖赛赛车史上最年轻的世界冠军。

    安东内利在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道以第三名完赛，凭借一场稳健的表现拿下个人首个世界冠军，以20岁之龄成为最年轻的世界冠军，将塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔此前的纪录提前了三年。

    由于乔治·拉塞尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿都没有赢得比赛，安东内利甚至不需要完赛就能锁定总冠军，但他用一场沉稳的比赛彰显了自己统治性的赛季，在座无虚席的太阳广场超越夏尔·勒克莱尔获得第三名，为自己献上了一场领奖台庆祝。

    "非常感谢，我们做到了，"安东内利在冲过终点线时泪流满面，哽咽着说道。

    他的比赛工程师彼得·邦宁顿回应道："干得漂亮，基米。能与你同行是我的荣幸，但这只是第一章。"

    从梅赛德斯W17赛车中爬出后，他与父亲、母亲和妹妹拥抱，随后被他的机械师们团团围住，并得到了车队老板托托·沃尔夫的拥抱。

    安东内利说：“没有梅赛德斯，没有我的家人，我就不会站在这里。我非常感激这一刻，它将永远留存，我简直说不出话来。我正在努力消化刚刚发生的一切。”

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利；安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯

    “这个周末我确实感到更紧张了一些，但我非常高兴，我们做到了，现在我们可以享受赛季剩余的比赛了。”

    四届冠军维斯塔潘评论道：“当然，这太不可思议了，他度过了一个怎样的赛季，而且在如此年纪做到这一点，我认为这真的非常非常令人印象深刻。看到整个车队与他一起庆祝，真是太棒了。他的家人也在这里，所以我确信今天对他来说是非常激动的一天，我为他感到非常骄傲。”

    七届世界冠军汉密尔顿补充道：“史上最年轻的世界冠军。这相当惊人，我非常感激能在这场比赛中亲眼见证。”

    安东内利成为73年来首位意大利世界冠军，上一位是1953年的阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里。他仅是第三位达成这一成就的意大利人，前两位是朱塞佩·法里纳（1950年）和阿斯卡里（1952年、1953年）。

    他的夺冠之路建立在梅赛德斯在新规下重返状态，以及他自己新获得的稳定性和成熟度之上，驾驶W17赛车拿下九场大奖赛胜利。其中包括在蒙扎主场从第19位发车后令人难忘的逆转夺冠。

    Emotional Kimi Antonelli left "speechless" by maiden F1 world title in Mexico

    Kimi Antonelli was left in tears after clinching the Formula 1 world championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix

    Newly-crowned Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli paid a tearful tribute to Mercedes as he became grand prix racing's youngest-ever title winner in Mexico.

    Antonelli took third at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez after a controlled driver to his maiden title, beating Sebastian Vettel's previous record by three years as he became the youngest driver to do so aged 20.

    With neither George Russell nor Lewis Hamilton winning the race, Antonelli didn't even have to finish to seal the crown, but he underlined his dominant campaign with a composed race, treating himself to a podium celebration at a packed Foro Sol by picking off Charles Leclerc for third.

    "Thank you so much, we did it," Antonelli blurted out in between his copious tears as he crossed the line.

    His race engineer Peter Bonnington responded: "Beautiful work, Kimi. It's been a pleasure being part of the journey with you but this is just the first chapter."

    Climbing from his Mercedes W17, he shared an embrace with father, mother and sister before being mobbed by his mechanics and receiving a hug from team boss Toto Wolff.

    "I wouldn't be here without Mercedes, without my family," Antonelli said. "I'm very grateful for this moment, and it's one that will live forever, and I'm just speechless. I'm trying to process what has just happened.

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    "This weekend I did feel a bit more tense, but I'm just so happy and happy that we got it done, and then we can now we can enjoy the rest of the season."

    Four-time champion Verstappen commented: "It's incredible, of course, what a season he had, and to do that also at such an age, I think it's just really, really impressive. It's amazing to see the whole team celebrating with him. His family is here as well, so I'm sure this is a very emotional day for him, and I'm very proud of him."

    Seven-time world champion Hamilton added: "Youngest world champion ever. It's pretty phenomenal, and I'm so grateful to be in the race to witness it."

    Antonelli became the first Italian world champion in 73 years, since Alberto Ascari in 1953. He is only the third Italian to achieve that accomplishment behind Giuseppe Farina (1950) and Ascari (1952, 1953).

    His title romp was built on a return to form from Mercedes under the new regulations as well as his own new-found consistency and maturity, taking nine grand prix wins aboard the W17. That included a memorable comeback win at home in Monza from 19th on the grid.
