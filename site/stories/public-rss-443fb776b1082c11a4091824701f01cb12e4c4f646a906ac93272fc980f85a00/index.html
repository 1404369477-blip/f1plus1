    阿尔派 2024年F1赛车部件变身终极收藏品展示

    阿尔派 2024年F1赛车的一个部件即将拍卖，它被改造成可以放在客厅的收藏品。F1官方纪念品平台F1 Authentics将开始拍卖一个2024年赛事使用的变速箱载体，配以带有工作DRS襟翼的赛事规格后翼，安装在定制的展示架上。

    阿尔派的2024年F1赛车部件即将变身终极收藏品展示。F1 Authentics，F1的官方纪念品平台，将开始拍卖一个2024年赛事使用的变速箱载体，配以带有工作DRS襟翼的赛事规格后翼，安装在定制的展示架上。

    从阿尔派 2024年Formula 1赛车中使用的变速箱载体被配对了一个带有工作DRS襟翼的赛事规格后翼，形成了一个不同寻常的收藏品展示。

    阿尔派 2024年Formula 1赛车的一部分即将进行拍卖，并且已经被改造成可以放在你客厅的东西。

    F1 Authentics，F1官方纪念品平台，正在开启对一款非常酷的2024年赛事使用的变速箱载体以及与其配套的赛车规格后翼的竞拍。变速箱载体上仍保留着其原始的FIA封印，而该翼保留了可工作的DRS襟翼。

    Alpine's 2024 F1 car has been turned into the ultimate collector's display

    A race-used gearbox carrier from Alpine's 2024 Formula 1 car has been paired with a race-spec rear wing featuring a working DRS flap for an unusual collector's display

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    A piece of Alpine's 2024 Formula 1 car is about to go up for auction, and it has been turned into something you could actually put in your living room.

    F1 Authentics, the official memorabilia platform of F1, is opening bidding on a very cool 2024 race-used gearbox carrier paired with a race-spec rear wing, mounted together on a custom-built display stand. The gearbox carrier still carries its original FIA seal, and the wing keeps a working DRS flap.

    It could make for an incredible thing to lean on while you look soulfully out of the window drinking your morning coffee.

    The lot, that makes up part of 'The Austin Auction,' is titled the BWT Alpine Formula 1 Team 2024 Race-Used Gearbox Carrier & Rear Wing Assembly. Only the gearbox carrier is described as race-used; the rear wing is a race-specification part.

    Made from carbon fibre and metal, the gearbox carrier forms part of the car's rear structure while the wing above it, still in Alpine's pink-and-blue BWT livery with Castrol branding on the endplates, manages airflow and creates downforce at the rear of the car.

    The DRS flap can still be opened and closed. And what an incredible showing that would be for when you have visitors.

    Esteban Ocon, Alpine, Pierre Gasly, Alpine

    While 2024 wasn't the perfect season for Alpine, it did result in a double podium for the then-all-French lineup.

    After a difficult start, the team finished sixth in the constructors' standings, helped by a wet race at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, where Esteban Ocon finished second and Pierre Gasly third.
