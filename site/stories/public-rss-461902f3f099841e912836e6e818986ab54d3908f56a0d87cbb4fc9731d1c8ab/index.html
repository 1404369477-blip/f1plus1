    诺里斯对迈凯伦拉斯维加斯排位赛直道速度感到沮丧

    诺里斯在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛无缘杆位后，批评迈凯伦赛车直道速度“糟糕透顶”。他在周五夜间位列第四，落后杆位得主安东内利0.351秒，维斯塔潘和拉塞尔也比他更快。

    诺里斯对迈凯伦在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中“糟糕透顶”的直道速度感到沮丧

    这位迈凯伦车手在最后一个计时段比全场最快者慢了近0.8秒

    诺里斯在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛无缘杆位后，批评迈凯伦赛车“糟糕透顶”的直道速度。

    这位2025年世界冠军在周五夜间于内华达州位列第四，落后杆位得主安东内利0.351秒，维斯塔潘和拉塞尔也比他更快。

    诺里斯的差距主要出现在最后一个计时段，他在那里比接替其世界冠军头衔的维斯塔潘慢了0.776秒，尽管他在前两个计时段更快——甚至在第二计时段全场最快。

    但最后一个计时段通常决定拉斯维加斯这条低低下压力街道赛道的单圈成绩，因为其中包含1.2英里全油门的大直道赛段，以及最后一个弯角前0.5英里的直道。

    “我们在直道上简直糟透了，”诺里斯说道，由于安东内利因更换新的内燃机被罚退五位，他将从第三位发车。

    “就像，在直道上太可怕了。我的单圈非常好。那很容易是我最好的一圈。但我们就是，是的，跟基米相比，我们在最后一个计时段慢了0.8秒。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，迈凯伦；兰多·诺里斯，迈凯伦

    “他获得了完美的尾流，做得非常出色。但与大多数其他人相比，我们落后太多了。

    “我必须在距离前车1.5秒的位置开始我的飞驰圈，这意味着在每个弯道我都会损失至少0.05到0.1秒，因为我损失了太多的下压力。

    “最终，拥有良好的尾流显然能带来更大的收益，但我们不得不弥补直线速度的严重损失——因此我们不得不牺牲我们的过弯速度。

    “所以总的来说，在空气下压力和阻力方面，我们就是没有找到正确的平衡。是的，这让我们根本无法去争夺杆位，这是肯定的。”

    由于迈凯伦在2026年新规下难以优化梅赛德斯引擎，直线速度今年一直是他们持续存在的问题。

    由于今年动力单元的复杂性，厂商车队一直占据着先发优势，因此迈凯伦在其他低下压力赛道上的挣扎在2026年一直很突出。

    在蒙扎，他们排位赛获得第三和第九后，正赛以第四和第五完赛；在巴库，他们排位赛获得第三和第五，却未能拿到积分；而在奥地利，他们从第六和第七位发车后，正赛以第四和第七完赛。

    诺里斯补充道：“你不能怪直道，只是我们的车不够好。我们必须在这方面大幅改进。在蒙扎、这里、巴库，你都无法竞争。”

    队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在拉斯维加斯比诺里斯经历了更大的困难，这位澳大利亚车手仅排名第七快，比安东内利的梅赛德斯慢了多达1.006秒。

    皮亚斯特里在最后一个计时段也很挣扎，比这位意大利车手慢了0.837秒，他的问题源于缺乏尾流，而尾流在排位赛中如此抢手。

    “第三阶段排位赛由于几个原因有点混乱，”皮亚斯特里说，他将因更换新引擎而接受发车位后退10位的处罚。

    “想要获得合适的尾流，如果你没处理好，那还不如干脆别出去跑。

    “即便是在这里开启一圈，我觉得赛车之间也有点差异，它们在进入1号弯时消耗多少能量，如果那有半秒之差，到了这一圈后面的直道上就可能变成三四成的差距。

    “所以这很难处理好，我觉得有几件事我们大概会复盘一下，看看能不能做得稍微好一点。但我觉得我的圈速有点乱。”

    Lando Norris frustrated by McLaren's "shocking" top speed at Las Vegas GP qualifying

    The McLaren driver was close to eight tenths off the pace in the final sector

    Lando Norris lamented McLaren’s “shocking” straightline speed after missing out on pole at the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    The 2025 world champion was fourth fastest on Friday night in Nevada and 0.351s off pacesetter Kimi Antonelli, with Max Verstappen and George Russell also quicker.

    Norris’s deficit largely came in the final sector where he was 0.776s slower than his world champion successor, despite being faster in the first two - even quickest of all in the second.

    But the final sector is usually what defines a lap around the low-downforce Vegas street track due to its 1.2-mile flat-out Strip section, plus the 0.5-mile straight ahead of the final corner.

    “We're just shocking in the straights,” said Norris, who will start from third due to Antonelli’s five-place grid drop for a new internal combustion engine.

    “Like, horrendous in the straights. My lap was very good. It was easily my best lap. But we're just, yeah, to Kimi we're eight-tenths off in the last sector.

    Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    “He has a perfect tow and he does a very good job. But comparing to most other people, we're too far off.

    “I have to start my lap 1.5 seconds off the car ahead, which means in every corner I'm losing at least half a tenth to a tenth because of how much downforce I'm losing.

    “There's obviously a bigger gain at the end of the day in having a good slipstream, but we're having to make up for a really terrible loss of straightline speed - and therefore we have to harm our cornering speed.

    “So we just haven't got the right balance, all in all, in terms of downforce, drag. Yeah, so it's made our life impossible to fight for pole, that's for sure.”

    Straightline speed has been a continuous problem for McLaren this year due to its struggles optimising the Mercedes engine under the new-for-2026 regulations.

    Factory teams have held a pre-standing advantage due to the complexity of this year’s power units and so McLaren’s struggles at other low-downforce circuits has been prevalent in 2026.

    In Monza it finished fourth and fifth after qualifying third and ninth, Baku it qualified third and fifth but failed to score a point, while in Austria it finished fourth and seventh after starting sixth and seventh.

    “You can't blame the straights, it's just our car is not good,” added Norris. “We have to improve that a lot. You cannot fight at Monza, here, Baku."

    Team-mate Oscar Piastri endured bigger struggles than Norris in Las Vegas as the Australian was only seventh fastest and a significant 1.006s slower than Antonelli’s Mercedes.

    Piastri also struggled in the final sector, 0.837s slower than the Italian, with his problems stemming from a lack of a tow, which was so sought after in qualifying.

    “Q3 was just a bit messy for a few reasons,” said Piastri, who will take a 10-place grid penalty for a new engine.

    “Trying to get the right slipstream is, you might as well not even go out if you don't get that right.

    “Even opening the lap here, I think there's a bit of a difference between cars, how much energy they use to Turn 1 and if that's half a second, that can be the difference of three or four tenths down the straight later in the lap.

    “So it's difficult to get right and I think there are a few things that we probably will review and see if we can do a slightly better job. But I think my laps were a little bit messy.”
