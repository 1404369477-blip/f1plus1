    贝尔曼坦言法拉利合同状况奇怪，对2027年席位未定感到失望

    贝尔曼表示，由于他的管理团队与法拉利就长期未来的谈判迟迟未能取得成果，他2027年的F1合同仍未正式敲定，他对此感到相当失望。

    奥利·贝尔曼表示，由于他2027年的F1合同仍未确认，他“相当失望”，而他承认自己的法拉利前景因一个“奇怪”的局面而变得不明朗。

    这位法拉利青训车手在2024年沙特阿拉伯大奖赛上顶替卡洛斯·塞恩斯，在令人震惊的情况下代表法拉利完成F1首秀，但此后他的职业生涯其余时间都在哈斯度过：贝尔曼2024年作为储备车手为哈斯出战两次，随后于2025年全职加入。

    贝尔曼预计2027年将继续留在哈斯，迎来第三个赛季；他希望留在该车队，并表示明年为其他车队效力的可能性为“0%”。

    但官方宣布意外推迟，原因是他的管理团队与法拉利就他的长期未来进行了旷日持久且未获成功的谈判；据信，谈判涉及贝尔曼在一支仍与刘易斯·汉密尔顿和夏尔·勒克莱尔有合同的车队能获得怎样的机会等问题。

    贝尔曼表示，他不会评论自己不满的对象是谁，只会说：“我相当失望，在第20几轮（23轮中的第21轮）坐在这里，却还没有签下明年的合同。”

    “这是一个非常非常奇怪的情况，但我们正在取得进展，我只能说这有点令人失望，”他说。

    贝尔曼补充说，他“清楚自己想要什么”，但对奥斯汀/墨西哥/巴西三连赛期间一次看似突破的进展未能取得成果感到失望。

    “很明显，我正在努力留在哈斯，我也值得留在哈斯，”贝尔曼说。

    我真的以为我们在上一个三连赛周末中取得了不错的进展，但看起来可能并非如此，所以这有点令人失望。

    但我希望我们能撑到这个周末结束，我有一些好消息要告诉你。更多的是为了在进入最后几场比赛时，知道明年情况良好。

    当被问及这是否影响了他为2027年做的准备时，他说仍然没有合同"感觉不好"，而且"老实说，没有签合同就待在这里没有任何借口"。

    除了2027年围场里没有其他空缺这一事实之外，贝尔曼"哈斯或什么都没有"的立场源于一种理解，即哈斯渴望确保贝尔曼以某种方式留下来。

    这很可能意味着哈斯要么行使选择权，让与法拉利有合同在身的贝尔曼留队第三年，要么，如果出于某种原因没有法拉利的总体协议，则另行达成一项单独协议。

    但预期是这件事会得到解决，最好是在这最后三连赛期间，贝尔曼与法拉利续约，同时他在哈斯的位置也一并敲定。

    不过，即使这真的发生，而且贝尔曼重申他的愿望仍然是有朝一日为法拉利比赛，但已经有人暗示，那段关系已经受到了一些损害，即使贝尔曼本人对此并不负责。

    “我与法拉利有合同，法拉利给了我这次不可思议的机会，让我第一次随他们进入F1，”贝尔曼说。

    当然，我仍然是法拉利的青训车手，我的终极梦想是为法拉利出赛，因为首先他们是最具标志性的车队，而且他们是最早在我还是个孩子时就相信我的车队。所以，这仍然是我的终极梦想。

    但毫无疑问，展望未来，那里可能会有一些挑战。这一点不可否认。这非常清楚。

    在这件事上我其实没有太多发言权，我们只需要看看事情随着时间如何发展。

    Ollie Bearman is "quite disappointed" he is still without a confirmed 2027 Formula 1 deal as his Ferrari future has become clouded by what he admits is a "strange" situation.

    The Ferrari protege made his F1 debut with the team in shock circumstances at the 2024 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix, substituting for Carlos Sainz, but the rest of his career since has been spent at Haas, with which Bearman raced twice in 2024 as a reserve, then joined full-time in 2025.

    Bearman is expected to continue with Haas for a third season in 2027, wants to stay with the team, and said there is "0%" chance he races for anyone else next year.

    But an official announcement has been unexpectedly delayed due to protracted, unsuccessful negotiations between his management and Ferrari over his longer-term future, believed to relate to matters such as what opportunities Bearman will get at a team that still has Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc under contract.

    Bearman said he would not comment on who was the subject of his frustration, only that "I'm quite disappointed that I'm sitting here at round 20-whatever [round 21 of 23], without a contract signed for next year".

    "It's been a strange, strange situation, but we're getting there, and all I can say is that it's a bit disappointing," he said.

    Bearman added he was "certain what I want" but was disappointed by an apparent breakthrough during the Austin/Mexico/Brazil triple-header not bearing fruit.

    "It's very clear that I'm trying to stay at Haas, and I deserve to stay at Haas," Bearman said.

    "I really thought that we had made decent progress in the last triple-header, and it doesn't maybe seem that way, so it's just a little disappointing.

    "But I hope that we can get to the end of this weekend, and I have some good news to tell you. It's more to go into the last few races knowing that the next year is good."

    Asked whether it was affecting his preparations for 2027, he said it was "not a good feeling" to still be without a contract and that "there's no excuse for being here without a contract signed quite honestly".

    Apart from the fact that there is no other vacancy on the grid for 2027, Bearman's 'Haas or nothing' position comes from an understanding that Haas is keen to ensure Bearman stays on one way or another.

    That likely means Haas either taking up the option it has to keep a Ferrari-contracted Bearman for a third year or, if for some reason there is no overarching Ferrari deal, striking a separate agreement.

    But the expectation is that it will be resolved, ideally during this final triple-header, with Bearman re-signing with Ferrari and having his Haas position sealed at the same time.

    Even if that happens, though, and Bearman reiterated his desire is still to race for Ferrari one day, it has been implied that some damage has been done to that relationship even if Bearman himself is not responsible for that.

    "I'm under contract with Ferrari, and Ferrari have given me this incredible opportunity to step into F1 with them for the first time," said Bearman.

    "And of course I’m still a Ferrari junior driver, and my ultimate dream is to race with Ferrari because first of all they're the most iconic team, and they're the first team that believed in me as a kid. So, it's still my ultimate dream.

    "But there's no doubt that looking into the future, there may be some challenges there. There's no denying that. It's very clear.

    "I don't really have much say in the matter, and we just need to see how things develop over time."
