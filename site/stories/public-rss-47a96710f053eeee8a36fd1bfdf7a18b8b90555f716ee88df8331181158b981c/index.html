    马丁·布伦德尔与女儿查理共同揭晓860马力保时捷911改装车

    前F1车手马丁·布伦德尔与女儿查理和加州公司Gunther Werks联手，打造了一款独家860马力的保时捷911改装车

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    前F1车手和天空体育解说员马丁·布伦德尔与女儿查理合作，联手加州公司Gunther Werks打造了一款860马力的空冷运动跑车——这是一款非常特别的机器

    The Brundle Edition Turbo 是基于 Gunther Werks 改装的保时捷 911 Turbo，总共只会建造五辆。

    查理·布伦德尔，一位车手，他的简历包括在迈凯轮、阿斯顿·马丁和捷豹路虎等制造商的工作经历，领导了这个项目的造型设计。马丁凭借他在各种赛车驾驶方面的几乎无限经验，帮助开发了它的驾驶性能，包括其运动和赛道设置。

    “与 Gunther Werks 合作，并且让我的女儿领导设计，意味着这个项目对我来说非常个人化，”布伦德尔在公告中表示。“我们将我们从驾驶座、维修区和设计工作室的集体经验都倾注到了这辆车上。”

    后置的 4.0 升双涡轮增压水平六缸发动机产生了惊人的 860 马力和 610 磅英尺的扭矩。考虑到汽车纯粹主义者，它仅配备六速手动变速箱，将动力传递到后轮。

    尽管有这些数据，布伦德尔坚持认为这仍然是一辆你可以在公共道路上享受的车。

    “对我来说， Gunther Werks 车辆最独特的地方之一是，尽管它拥有极端的赛车性能，但它却非常文明且易于使用，”他说。

    除了其惊人的性能外，它还配备了可调节的后翼、前翼尖、碳陶瓷布雷博刹车和带有布伦德尔标志的锻造镁合金轮毂。它还采用了赛车手头盔启发的双色珍珠漆，引擎盖上带有“Brundle”徽标。

    车内，头枕上带有由查理本人设计的“MB”纹章，仪表板的末端由他自己签名。仪表盘还带有英国车迷会认出的标语：“汽车最重要的部分是固定方向盘的螺母。”

    买家还将收到一顶与赛车同色系的Bell HP77头盔，由布伦德尔签名并经过赛车使用认证。

    这辆车将在拉斯维加斯的温尼康克赛会上向公众展示。希望我们能在接近时间时得知价格和其他性能细节。

    Martin Brundle unveils incredible 860hp Porsche 911 creation with daughter Charlie

    Former F1 driver Martin Brundle has joined forces with his daughter Charlie and Gunther Werks to create an exclusive 860hp Porsche 911 restomod

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Former F1 driver and Sky Sports commentator Martin Brundle has teamed up with his daughter Charlie on an 860hp air-cooled sports car, built by Californian company Gunther Werks - and it's a pretty special machine.

    The Brundle Edition Turbo is based on Gunther Werks’ reworked Porsche 911 Turbo, and only five will ever be built.

    Charlie Brundle, a car designer whose CV includes stints at manufacturers such as McLaren, Aston Martin and Jaguar Land Rover, led the design aspect of the project. Martin, using his almost infinite experience behind the wheel of all sorts of motor vehicles, helped develop how it drives, including its Sport and Track settings.

    “To collaborate with Gunther Werks and to have my daughter lead the design means this project is deeply personal,” Brundle said in the announcement. “We’ve poured our collective experience from the driver’s seat, the paddock and the design studios into this car.”

    A rear-mounted 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged flat-six produces an astonishing 860hp and 610lb-ft of torque. And with automotive purists in mind, it only comes with a six-speed manual gearbox that sends power to the rear wheels.

    Despite those figures, Brundle insists it's still a car you can enjoy on the public road.

    “For me, one of the most unique aspects of a Gunther Werks vehicle is that, despite having extreme race car performance, it is remarkably civilised and usable too,” he said.

    On top of its blistering performance, it also gets an adjustable rear wing, front canards, carbon ceramic Brembo brakes and forged magnesium wheels with the Brundle branding. It also gets two-tone pearl paint inspired by the racing driver's helmet, with "Brundle" badging on the engine cover.

    Inside, the headrests carry an "MB" monogram designed by Charlie herself, and the endplates of the dash are signed by the man himself. The dashboard also carries a line that fans of the Briton will recognise: “The most important bit of the car is the nut holding the steering wheel.”

    Buyers will also receive a Bell HP77 helmet painted in the same colour scheme, signed by Brundle and certified for race use.

    The car will be shown to the public at the Concours at Wynn Las Vegas. Hopefully we find out the price and other performance specifics closer to the time.
