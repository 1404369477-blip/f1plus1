    红牛确认哈贾尔2027赛季留队

    红牛车队已确认，伊萨克·哈贾尔将在2027年F1赛季继续留队，担任马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔确认成为马克斯·维斯塔潘在2027年F1赛季的红牛队友，消息在阿塞拜疆大奖赛前公布。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔已与红牛续约一年，将在2027年作为马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友迎来第二个赛季；阿塞拜疆大奖赛周四的练习赛将于上午9:30和下午1点在天空体育 F1直播。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔承认他不确定新合同续约的细节，也不确定2027年后是否还会留在红牛，但他希望用驾驶表现来说话。

    红牛已确认伊萨克·哈贾尔将在2027年F1赛季继续担任马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友。

    哈贾尔在今年年初升入红牛，此前他在2025年代表小红牛完成了令人印象深刻的首个赛季，但由于在上个月夏休期间手腕受伤，他缺席了最近三站比赛。

    但这位21岁的车手现在已经恢复到可以驾驶的状态，并将在本周末的阿塞拜疆大奖赛回归，正赛于周六举行——在天空体育 F1直播。

    红牛上个月已经宣布，四届世界冠军维斯塔潘签署了一份新合同，至少留在车队至2030年底。

    “我们非常兴奋能继续与伊萨克同行。自从今年年初加入我们以来，他在赛道上和赛道外都表现出色，尤其是考虑到这只是他第二个F1赛季，这让续约成为一个容易的决定，”红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯说道。

    “从一开始，伊萨克就全身心融入车队，在工厂里与工程师们分秒必争地研发赛车，总是渴望学习和进步。

    “从墨尔本的第一场比赛周末就能看出，他以非凡的沉着应对了这次跃升，并赢得了这个席位。”

    不是双关……续约了！红牛用一段搞笑视频宣布哈贾尔合同！

    维斯塔潘身边的第二个红牛席位被广泛认为是F1发车格上最难的位置，此前包括皮埃尔·加斯利、亚历克斯·阿尔本、利亚姆·劳森和角田裕毅在内的多位车手都在与这位荷兰人的较量中陷入困境。

    但哈贾尔比这些车手更接近维斯塔潘，本赛季甚至有时胜过队友，因此他的席位并未受到质疑。

    尽管缺席了最近三个比赛周末，哈贾尔仍在车手积分榜上排名第八，并在红牛和迈凯伦成功上诉加斯利被推翻的超速处罚后，重新获得了摩纳哥大奖赛的领奖台席位。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔在红牛上诉后重新回到摩纳哥领奖台，阿尔派对皮埃尔·加斯利在维修区超速的处罚被恢复表示不满。

    “他受伤以来的这段时间对伊萨克来说很艰难，但这次续约证明了我们对他有多么信任，我们迫不及待想看到他在赛道上回归，”梅基斯继续说道。

    “本赛季我们还有很多目标要实现，但知道车手、工程师和工厂里的每个人都在朝着同一个方向前进，专注且团结，这种感觉很棒。”

    “哈贾尔因为手臂和手腕受伤缺席了最近三站。据我了解，那是他在训练中打重沙袋造成的，因为他在训练中会打拳击。

    “但他已经康复，本周末将参赛，并且他将在2027年成为马克斯·维斯塔潘的搭档。

    “哈贾尔将继续留在红牛。利亚姆·劳森在他缺席期间替补表现得非常好，但没有人对哈贾尔获得继续留队的认可感到意外。

    “他可能是自丹尼尔·里卡多多年前离开以来，维斯塔潘最具竞争力的队友。”

    哈贾尔补充道：“我非常兴奋能继续与Oracle红牛车队合作。我在八个月前来到这支车队，立刻明白了这个组织的规模和雄心。

    “工厂里有那么多才华横溢的人，你会意识到能为这样一支不可思议的车队效力，与像马克斯这样的四届世界冠军并肩作战，是一种特权。

    “从我签约的那一刻起，我就决心为车队奉献自己，在洛朗和米尔顿凯恩斯所有人的支持下，我得以证明他们对我的信任是值得的。我不断寻求倾听、学习，并每天进一步推动自己，思考未来的一切。”

    皮埃尔·加斯利和伊萨克·哈贾尔参观太阳马戏团，尝试了一些搞笑的特技！

    “如果你足够优秀，就能留下”——哈贾尔谈续约和伤愈复出

    周三晚些时候在巴库出席车手新闻发布会时，哈贾尔对媒体表示，他对合同续约谈判完全放松——甚至声称自己不知道合同期限有多长。

    “我甚至没有谈过这件事。你知道，我让我的人替我谈，”他说。

    “我在赛道上谈[判]，就这样。所以是的，非常容易。”

    当被问及是否是多年合同时，哈贾尔回答说：“我其实不知道。我不在乎。是的，但是，你知道，如果你足够优秀，就能留下，就这样。”

    这位法国人还谈到了他手腕伤势的性质以及康复过程。

    “我的手腕外侧有一处小骨折，这是一个相当漫长的过程，”他说。

    Isack Hadjar confirmed as Max Verstappen's Red Bull team-mate for 2027 Formula 1 season ahead of Azerbaijan Grand Prix

    Isack Hadjar has extended his contract at Red Bull for another year and will be Max Verstappen's team-mate for a second season in 2027; watch Thursday's practice sessions for the Azerbaijan Grand Prix at 9.30am and 1pm, live on Sky Sports F1

    Isack Hadjar admits he's unsure of the new contract extension details and if he'll remain at Red Bull beyond 2027, but hopes his driving will do the talking

    Red Bull have confirmed Isack Hadjar will remain as Max Verstappen's team-mate for the 2027 Formula 1 season.

    Hadjar stepped up to Red Bull at the start of this year after an impressive debut campaign with Racing Bulls in 2025 but has missed the last three rounds due to a wrist injury he suffered during last month's summer break.

    But the 21-year-old is now fit enough to drive and is back at this weekend's Azerbaijan Grand Prix, which takes place on Saturday - live on Sky Sports F1.

    Red Bull had already announced last month that four-time world champion Verstappen signed a new contract to stay at the team until at least the end of 2030.

    "We're very excited to continue our journey with Isack. Since joining us at the start of the year, he has been superb on and off track, especially considering this is only his second Formula 1 season, which made extending his contract an easy decision," said Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies.

    "From the very beginning, Isack immersed himself in the team, spending every minute at the factory with his engineers to develop the car, always eager to learn and improve.

    "You could see from that first weekend in Melbourne that he handled the step up with remarkable composure and had earned his place in this seat."

    The second Red Bull seat alongside Verstappen is widely regarded as the toughest on the F1 grid after several drivers including Pierre Gasly, Alex Albon, Liam Lawson and Yuki Tsunoda all struggled against the Dutchman.

    But, Hadjar has been closer to Verstappen than those drivers and has even got the better of his team-mate at times this season, so his seat has not come under scrutiny.

    Despite missing the last three race weekends, Hadjar is still eighth in the Drivers' Championship and reclaimed his Monaco Grand Prix podium after Red Bull and McLaren successfully appealed Gasly's overturned speeding penalties.

    Isack Hadjar has been reinstated to the Monaco podium after a Red Bull appeal with Alpine expressing frustration as Pierre Gasly's penalty for speeding in pit lane is restored

    "The period since his injury has been a difficult one for Isack, but this extension is a testament to how much we believe in him and we can't wait to see him back out on track," continued Mekies.

    "We still have a lot we want to achieve this season, but it's a great feeling to know that the drivers, engineers and everyone back at the factory are all heading in the same direction, committed and together."

    "Hadjar has been out for the last three rounds because he damaged his arm and wrist. I understand that was done by hitting a heavy bag in training because he does boxing in training.

    "But he has recovered, he is taking part this weekend, and he will be Max Verstappen's partner in 2027.

    "Hadjar will be continuing with Red Bull. Liam Lawson deputised very well in his absence but nobody was surprised that Hadjar gets the nod to carry on.

    "He has probably been Verstappen's most competitive teammate since Daniel Ricciardo left a few years ago."

    Hadjar added: "I am really excited to continue with Oracle Red Bull Racing. I came to this team eight months ago and immediately understood the scale and ambition of this organisation.

    "There are so many talented people at the factory, and you're aware of the privilege of driving for such an incredible team alongside a four-time world champion like Max.

    "From the moment I signed, I set out to dedicate myself to the team, and with the support of Laurent and everyone in Milton Keynes, I've been able to justify the faith they've had in me. I'm constantly looking to listen, learn and push myself further every single day about what lies ahead."

    'If you're good, you stay' - Hadjar on extended deal and injury return

    Speaking to the media later on Wednesday in Baku when appearing in the Drivers' Press Conference, Hadjar said he had been completely relaxed about the contract renewal discussions - even claiming he did not know how long the deal was for.

    "I didn't even talk about it. You know, I let my people do the talking for me," he said.

    "I do it [the talking] on the track and that's about it. So yeah, it was very easy."

    And asked whether it was a mufti-year term, Hadjar replied: "I actually don't know. I don't care. Yeah, but, you know, if you're good, you stay, and that's it."

    The Frenchman also spoke about the nature of his wrist injury and how his rehabilitation had gone.

    "I have a small fracture outside of the wrist and it's just quite a long process," he said.
