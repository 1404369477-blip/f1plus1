    奥迪谈巴西站霍肯伯格争议罚时：科拉平托从未切到弯心

    霍肯伯格在英特拉格斯因在一号弯离开赛道并获得优势被罚10秒，奥迪方面对此判罚给出看法，认为科拉平托从未切到弯心。

    “科拉平托从未切到弯心”——奥迪对巴西站霍肯伯格争议罚时的看法

    霍肯伯格在英特拉格斯因在试图超越弗朗哥·科拉平托时离开赛道并获得优势而被罚10秒

    奥迪在车队积分榜上排名第八，这与赛车的竞争力并不太相符，但种种情况叠加，让车队在2026年始终未能进入积分区。尼科·霍肯伯格和加布里埃尔·博托莱托两人合计已有十次第11名完赛。

    问题往往出在起步表现不佳，这让车手在离开发车格后损失了赛道位置。但英特拉格斯的混合条件让霍肯伯格得以发挥，尽管他在第48圈与阿尔派的弗朗哥·科拉平托争夺最后一个积分席位时，因在一号弯离开赛道并获得优势而被罚10秒。

    霍肯伯格在周六受雨水影响的排位赛中闯入前十名争夺战，并做出第六快的时间——由于乔治·拉塞尔和夏尔·勒克莱尔被罚，他在发车格上升至第四。他在开场圈跌至第六，但随后一直坚守，直到赛道开始变干时马克斯·维斯塔潘和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里将他超越。

    换上干地轮胎后，霍肯伯格也落在了刘易斯·汉密尔顿和夏尔·勒克莱尔驾驶的两辆法拉利之后，但他仍有望拿分，直到第44圈的第二次进站让他落到了科拉平托身后，后者自第17圈结束以来就再未进站。尽管轮胎老化，这位阿尔派车手仍顽强防守，霍肯伯格花了四圈才在第1弯完成超越——但他在外线绕行时驶过了沥青缓冲区。

    霍肯伯格随后因离开赛道并获得优势而被处以10秒罚时。奥迪车队赛事总监艾伦·麦克尼什解释了车队为何没有指示霍肯伯格交还位置。

    尼科·霍肯伯格，奥迪F1车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    麦克尼什表示：“相对于法拉利车队，我们的竞争力让我感到相当惊喜。”

    “在湿滑条件下，与他们相比我们看起来相当不错。尼科（霍肯伯格）能够稳住并守住位置。在干地换上光头胎后，我们的表现没有达到预期。我们在那里有些挣扎。”

    “也许是条件原因，我不确定。但相对于竞争对手，我们确实没有达到同样的速度水平。不过仍然在前十名之内。”

    “当我们进站时，他（霍肯伯格）出来时正好遇到科拉平托。科拉平托当时处于使用旧胎的赛段尾声，勉强支撑并在1号弯跑宽了。他又做了一次，因为尼科在外线——就是把他挤宽了，错过了弯心。但因为尼科驶出赛道，随后被认为获得了持久优势，所以我们被罚了10秒。”

    “对我们来说，情况并不明确。通常如果你在弯心处于领先位置，那么你就有权获得该位置。由于1号弯向下进入‘S’弯的方式，弯心的位置非常主观。科拉平托根本没有碰到弯心。他离弯心差得远。他离弯心还有很长一段距离。

    “但国际汽联定义了弯心线在哪里。而在那个点上，科拉平托已经跑宽了。所以，对我们来说，这是一个非常有争议的情况。我们老实说不知道该怎么处理。如果我们把位置还回去，那么我们就会陷入同样的位置。

    “另一个因素是，等到我们能够回看的时候，尼科已经领先太多了。他在那一圈拉开了两秒。

    “你必须遵守国际汽联的裁决。这毫无疑问。但对我们来说，并不清楚那不是尼科的弯角——这是最好的说法。”

    国际汽联的驾驶指南规定，试图在弯道外侧超车的车手，只有在弯心处其前轴领先于另一辆车的前轴时，才有权获得“赛车空间”。虽然1号弯的地形使得精确判断弯心位置变得困难，但干事们确实必须在某处划定界线，而霍肯伯格被判定在那一刻未达到该标准。

    此外，驾驶指南规定，尝试超车的车辆必须“从入弯、到弯心、再到出弯都处于受控驾驶状态”，并且“能够在赛道限制内完成该弯道”。

    后几条条款近年来经过了细化，因为一些车手被认为利用了早期版本指南中规定不那么具体的规定。但关于进攻车手是否发动了神风式突击，或者防守车手是否也通过‘意外’跑宽而占了便宜，仍然经常存在争论。

    在他最后一次进站时执行10秒罚时——当时正值比赛中所有车手因雨带扫过赛道而换回半雨胎的阶段——这让霍肯伯格再次跌出前十。但他在第65圈开始时从阿维德·林德布拉德手中夺回了第10名。

    麦克尼什说：“对林德布拉德的那次超车，我认为绝对是一次漂亮且富有侵略性的超车。这也是一种宣言：你们可以给我们处罚，但我们依然能把它追回来。”

    "Colapinto never hit the apex" – Audi's take on contentious Nico Hulkenberg penalty in Brazilian GP

    Hulkenberg was hit with a 10-second penalty at Interlagos for leaving the track and gaining an advantage while trying to pass Franco Colapinto

    Audi's eighth place in the constructors' championship doesn't quite tally with the competitiveness of its car, but circumstances have contrived to keep the team falling short of the points in 2026. Between them, Nico Hulkenberg and Gabriel Bortoleto have notched up ten 11th-place finishes.

    Often the issue has been poor starting performance which costs the drivers track position away from the grid. But the mixed conditions at Interlagos enabled Hulkenberg to shine, despite a 10-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage at Turn 1 while battling Alpine's Franco Colapinto for the final points-paying spot on lap 48.

    Hulkenberg had made it through to the top-10 shootout in the rain-affected qualifying session on Saturday, and set the sixth-fastest time – which became fourth on the grid thanks to penalties for George Russell and Charles Leclerc. He dropped back to sixth on the opening lap but then held on until Max Verstappen and Oscar Piastri demoted him as the track began to dry out.

    On the slick tyres Hulkenberg also fell behind the Ferraris of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc but he was still on course for points until his second pitstop on lap 44 dropped him behind Colapinto, who hadn't stopped since the end of lap 17. Despite his ageing tyres, the Alpine driver put up a stubborn defence and it took Hulkenberg four laps to make a pass stick at Turn 1 – but in going around the outside he strayed through the asphalt run-off.

    Hulkenberg was subsequently hit with a 10-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage. Audi's racing director Allan McNish explained why the team didn't instruct Hulkenberg to give the position back.

    Nico Hulkenberg, Audi F1 Team, George Russell, Mercedes

    "I was quite positively surprised how competitive we were relative to the Ferraris," said McNish.

    "We looked pretty good in the wet conditions in comparison to them. And Nico was able to hold on and keep his position. In the dry, when we switched to the slicks, we didn't quite have the same performance as what we had hoped. We struggled a little bit there.

    "Maybe the conditions, I'm not sure. But we didn't quite have that same level of speed relative to the competition. But still in the top 10.

    "When we did the pitstop, he [Hulkenberg] was coming out with Colapinto. And Colapinto was at the end of his stint on old tyres, hanging on and running wide into Turn 1. He did it again because Nico was on the outside – just pushed him wide, missed the apex. But because Nico went off and then was deemed to have gained the long and lasting advantage, then we had a 10-second penalty.

    "For us it wasn't clear. Normally if you're ahead at the apex, then you have the right to be given position. Because of how it [Turn 1] drops down into the ‘S', then it's very subjective where the apex is. Now Colapinto never hit the apex. He was nowhere near the apex. He was a long way from the apex.

    "But the FIA defined where the apex line was. And at that point Colapinto had ran wide. So, for us, it was a very debatable situation. And we honestly didn't know which way to go with it. If we had given it back, then we were stuck in the same position.

    "And the other factor was that by the time we had been able to review, Nico had gone so far ahead. He pulled two seconds on the lap.

    "You have to follow the FIA ruling. There's no question. But it wasn't clear for us that it wasn't Nico's corner – that's the best way to put it."

    The FIA's driving guidelines state that a driver trying to pass around the outside of a corner is entitled to ‘racing room' only if their front axle is ahead of the other car's front axle at the apex. While the topography of Turn 1 makes identifying precisely where the apex is difficult, the stewards literally have to draw the line somewhere, and Hulkenberg was adjudged not to have met the threshold by that point.

    Additionally, the driving guidelines state that the car attempting the overtake must "be driven in a controlled manner from entry, to apex, and to exit" and "be able to make the corner within track limits".

    These latter clauses have been finessed in recent years because some drivers were deemed to have exploited less prescriptive earlier versions of the guidelines. But there are still often debates about whether an attacking driver has launched a kamikaze-style assault, or whether the defender has also taken liberties by ‘accidentally' running wide.

    Serving the 10-second penalty at his final pitstop, during a phase of the race in which the entire field switched back to intermediate tyres as a band of rain swept across the circuit, dropped Hulkenberg out of the top 10 again. But he relieved Arvid Lindblad of 10th place at the start of lap 65.

    "The overtake [on Lindblad] was one that definitely I think was good and aggressive," said McNish. "And it was also a delivery of a statement: give us a penalty, but we can still get it back."
