    F1发布限量版莱卡相机，售价高达9950美元

    一级方程式赛车锦标赛与莱卡相机公司达成合作协议，推出一款碳纤维装饰的限量版相机，售价高达9950美元。

    F1推出限量版莱卡相机，售价高达9950美元

    一级方程式赛车与豪华相机制造商莱卡合作，推出限量版Q3 43，该款相机采用碳纤维细节设计，并配备更多功能

    一级方程式赛车最近几天非常忙碌。锦标赛与莱卡相机公司最终确定了许可合作协议，推出一款以碳纤维为特色的限量版模型，售价高达9950美元。这是一款奢侈品，对于那些既喜欢F1又喜欢摄影的人来说，几乎是一笔五位数的大额购买

    该款相机是Q3 43系列限量生产版本，配备60兆像素的全画幅成像传感器和永久安装的43mm APO-Summicron f/2镜头。莱卡表示，它"能够实现极其真实的摄影"。

    该型号现在正在销售中，购买可通过莱卡零售店、其电子商务平台和授权经销商进行。美国的价格是上述的9,950美元，揭幕仪式在莱卡洛杉矶的商店和画廊举行，由主持人Ariana Bravo和2009年世界冠军Jenson Button主持。

    赛车元素对我来说非常明显，即使像我这样的非相机狂热者也能感受到。握把采用了防滑Microvelour材质，灵感来源于赛车方向盘，F1标志出现在上壳和镜头盖上，一条织物带子上印有这两个组织的标志。

    它由真正的碳纤维制成，这是莱卡传统上不使用的材料，碳纤维部件在德国制造和测试。包装包括带有无线充电的碳壳握把、结合碳细节的软释放按钮和拇指支撑。

    软件方面是一款专用的F1图像外观，具有更高的对比度和更丰富的饱和度，旨在捕捉赛道上的 维修区氛围和11支参赛车队在赛道上的颜色。它通过莱卡FOTOS应用程序工作，该应用程序还允许快速编辑并将照片和视频传输到手机。莱卡表示，将会有更多限量版和特别版紧随其后。

    一辆价值近一万美元的相机并不是我会作为粉丝带到一级方程式赛车比赛中的东西，但包括刘易斯·汉密尔顿和兰多·诺里斯在内的几位车手，被看到手持它们拍摄比赛的瞬间。作为行业领导者，莱卡的出现对于热爱汽车和赛车摄影的人来说，这是一个非常激动人心的时刻。

    F1 unveils limited-edition Leica camera with eye-watering $9,950 price tag

    Formula 1 has partnered with luxury camera manufacturer Leica to launch a limited-edition Q3 43 featuring carbon fibre detailing and more

    Formula 1 had a busy few days. The championship finalised licensing partnership agreement with Leica Camera, resulting in a carbon-accented model priced at an eye-watering $9,950. It's a luxury toy for people who like F1 and photography enough to warrant an almost-five-figure purchase.

    The model is a limited production variant of the Q3 43 line, pairing a 60-megapixel full-frame imaging sensor with a permanently mounted 43mm APO-Summicron f/2 optic. Leica says it "enables exceptionally authentic photography".

    The model is on sale now, with purchases available through Leica retail locations, its e-commerce platform and authorised dealers. The US price is the aforementioned $9,950, and the unveiling happened at Leica's Los Angeles store and gallery with presenter Ariana Bravo and 2009 world champion Jenson Button.

    The motorsport touches are fairly obvious, even to non-camera nerds like me. The grip features a non-slip Microvelour texture inspired by a racing steering wheel, the Formula 1 marking appears on both the upper housing and lens cover, and a fabric strap boasts the logos of both organisations.

    It is built from genuine carbon fibre, a material Leica traditionally doesn't use, and the carbon parts are manufactured and tested in Germany. The package features a carbon-shell grip with wireless charging, a soft-release button incorporating carbon detailing and a thumb support.

    The software angle is a dedicated Formula 1 image look with higher contrast and richer saturation, meant to capture the atmosphere of the paddock and the colours of the 11 teams racing on the circuit. It works through the Leica FOTOS app, which also allows for quick edits and transfers photos and videos to a phone. Leica says more limited and special editions will follow this one.

    A camera worth almost 10 grand is not something I would personally take to an F1 race as a fan, but several drivers, including Lewis Hamilton and Lando Norris, have been spotted wielding them to take their own snaps of the action. With Leica being an industry leader, this is a really exciting moment for those who love automotive and motorsport photography.
