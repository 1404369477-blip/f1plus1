    哈斯车队决定在拉斯维加斯站继续留用拉斐尔·卡马拉

    新手车手卡马拉将获得另一个机会为2027年的完整F1首秀做准备

    拉斐尔·卡马拉将在即将到来的拉斯维加斯大奖赛中再次为哈斯车队参赛，而平田翔太将取代奥利·贝尔曼在首场练习赛中的位置

    在墨西哥城大奖赛周末期间被解雇的埃斯特班·奥康被解雇后，平田翔太和卡马拉都已为车队参赛

    Hirakawa在摩纳哥参加世界耐力锦标赛决赛——后来赢得了他的第三个WEC冠军——因此无法参加之前在巴西的F1赛事，这给了哈斯车队一个机会，让Camara提前亮相。

    Camara将留在赛车中参加拉斯维加斯的比赛，为他为车队在下一赛季的全职F1首秀积累宝贵经验。与此同时，贝尔曼今年至今只为一个FP1赛段让出了座位，因此他将最后一次让出座位，以便给Hirakawa在VF-26赛车中获得更多赛道时间。

    Hirakawa在墨西哥城的F1首秀中获得了积分，尽管在奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里和两位阿尔卑斯车手被处以赛后罚分之后，他给车队总监小松洋雄留下了“非常好的心理素质——[他]非常干净。他做到了我们期望的。”的印象。

    Camara的处子秀更加艰难，因为圣保罗赛道（Interlagos）的天气条件极其多变。这位巴西人在周末逐渐提升状态，FP3结束时展现出了良好的速度，但雨在排位赛之前到来，使他的生活变得更加困难。

    在Q1从打滑中挽救了自己的赛车后，卡马拉进入了Q2，但在12号弯道由于在低抓地力条件下无法正确调整后轴制动，导致赛车撞上了墙壁。

    因此，他获得了第13个发车位，但由于其他车手受到发车位处罚，他实际上从第11位起步，但在中雨轮胎的艰难比赛中，卡马拉几乎掉到了车队的最后。

    鉴于卡马拉在F2车手积分榜上的位置，他是否会留在车队参加剩余赛季还不确定；他可能希望完成本赛季，但这将很大程度上取决于哈斯车队和法拉利的决定。

    如果卡马拉决定参加卡塔尔和阿布扎比的赛事，那么伊拉卡瓦可以在这些比赛中重返赛车。

    Haas sticks with Rafael Camara for Las Vegas, Ryo Hirakawa in for FP1

    Rookie driver Camara gets another chance to prepare for his full F1 debut in 2027

    Rafael Camara will race again for Haas at Formula 1's upcoming Las Vegas Grand Prix, as Ryo Hirakawa takes Ollie Bearman's seat for the opening practice session.

    Both Hirakawa and Camara have now raced for the team following the dismissal of Esteban Ocon, who was stood down during the Mexico City Grand Prix weekend.

    Hirakawa, contesting the World Endurance Championship finale in Monza - later winning his third WEC title - was unavailable for the previous F1 round in Brazil and this presented Haas an opportunity to bring Camara in for an early debut.

    Camara will stay in the car for Las Vegas, as he gathers valuable experience for his full F1 debut next season with the team. Bearman meanwhile has only given up his seat for one FP1 session so far this year, so will do so for the final time to present Hirakawa with more track time in the VF-26.

    Hirakawa scored a point in his Mexico City F1 debut, albeit after Oscar Piastri and the two Alpine drivers were handed post-race penalties, and impressed team principal Ayao Komatsu with "very good mental conviction - [he was] so clean. He delivered what we expected."

    Camara endured a tougher debut run, as conditions at Interlagos were incredibly variable. The Brazilian built up through the weekend and showed good pace by the end of FP3, but the rain arrived ahead of qualifying and made his life more difficult.

    After rescuing his car from a spin in Q1, Camara got as far as Q2 - but put his car into the wall at Turn 12 having been unable to get the correct rear-axle braking in low-grip conditions.

    He thus qualified 13th and started 11th with other drivers taking grid penalties, but a tough stint on the intermediates put Camara towards the back of the field.

    It is uncertain whether Camara will stay in the car for the rest of the season, given his position at the top of the F2 drivers' standings; he may wish to complete the season, but this will largely be at the behest of Haas and Ferrari.

    Hirakawa could feasibly return to the car in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, should Camara return to F2 for those rounds.
