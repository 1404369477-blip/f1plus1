    法拉利车手汉密尔顿表示，他相信自己和队友勒克莱尔会“解决”上周拉斯维加斯大奖赛上的最新赛道摩擦。

    汉密尔顿谈与勒克莱尔最新摩擦：“我们会解决”

    在拉斯维加斯最新一次赛道冲突后，汉密尔顿与法拉利的勒克莱尔试图以并列头号车手的身份共处，汉密尔顿对此语气缓和

    法拉利车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿表示，他相信自己和队友夏尔·勒克莱尔会“解决”上周拉斯维加斯大奖赛上的最新赛道摩擦。

    两周前在墨西哥争夺领先时发生的一起代价高昂的赛道事故曾招致车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔的不满，而历史在拉斯维加斯重演：勒克莱尔被认定对与汉密尔顿的接触负有责任，使两位车手都错失了良好成绩。

    这次冲突让瓦塞尔更加愤怒，并就如何让他两位头号车手守规矩展开了新的讨论。“这是不可接受的，”瓦塞尔在赛后气愤地说。“这是对车队、对赞助商的不尊重，和墨西哥站之后说的话一样。我认为我们在圣保罗进行了明确的讨论。今天这很不专业。”

    周四在多哈发表讲话时，勒克莱尔表示他“非常想与车队一起努力寻找解决方案，以确保我们最大化车队积分，并确保像上周那样的事件不再发生”，但他没有提出任何具体建议。

    汉密尔顿也采取了和解的语气，并表示他仍然相信自己能和勒克莱尔作为并列头号车手在车队中和谐共处。

    “是的，是的。我们会解决的，”汉密尔顿说。“归根结底我们是队友，我认为我们总是从这些经历中学习，尽管也许看起来不一定如此。但我们会从这段困难时期中吸取教训。

    “我依然相信我们在幕后所建设和努力的一切。我们来这里的目标是带领法拉利重返巅峰。”

    他补充道：“你确实是在最艰难的时刻学到东西。对弗雷德、查尔斯和我来说，这绝对是极其、极其具有挑战性的时期。但我认为，正是这些时刻，你才能真正学到东西，并通过对话、沟通和坐下来谈心而彼此更加亲近。”

    汉密尔顿透露，他和勒克莱尔已经逐一复盘了今年两人之间每一个弯角和每一次事故，其中也包括在蒙扎的一次冲突。但这位七届世界冠军也承认，每一次事故都各不相同、情况动态变化，所以并不像施加严格的比赛规则那么简单。相反，他建议他和勒克莱尔必须凭借本能行事，同时把车队利益放在心上。

    “你永远无法覆盖所有情况，因为每个场景都不一样，”汉密尔顿解释道。

    “但你确实可以逐场比赛、逐个弯角地复盘讨论，查尔斯和我就这么做过。

    “但总会有一些你或许未曾预见的新情况。你还得依靠直觉，而归根结底你始终需要团队。就我而言，当我驾驶时，当我在赛道上进攻任何人时，我总在想：‘好吧，从车队角度考虑，这会如何影响我身后的所有人？’所以，我真的尽力做到尽可能负责，并在进攻时做出最佳决定，我想。

    Lewis Hamilton on latest Charles Leclerc flashpoint: "We're going to figure it out"

    Lewis Hamilton struck a conciliatory tone as he and Ferrari Charles Leclerc try to co-exist as joint-number one drivers after their latest on-track clash in Las Vegas

    Ferrari driver Lewis Hamilton says he is confident he and team-mate Charles Leclerc will "figure it out" following their latest on-track skirmish at last week's Las Vegas GP.

    Two weeks on from a costly on-track incident fighting for the lead in Mexico, which drew the ire of team boss Fred Vasseur, history repeated itself when Leclerc was found responsible for contact with Hamilton on the streets of Las Vegas, robbing both drivers of a solid result.

    The clash prompted more anger from Vasseur and fresh talks over how he will get his two number one drivers in line. "It's unacceptable," Vasseur fumed after the race. "It's a lack of respect for the team, for the sponsors, the same speech as after Mexico. I think we had a clear discussion in Sao Paulo. Today it wasn't professional."

    Speaking in Doha on Thursday, Leclerc said he was "very much thinking about trying to find a solution together with the team in order to make sure that we maximise the team points and that episodes like last week don't happen again", stopping short of offering any suggestions.

    Hamilton also struck a conciliatory tone and said he was still convinced he and Leclerc could co-exist in harmony at the team as joint number one drivers.

    "I do, I do. We're going to figure it out," Hamilton said. "We're team-mates at the end of the day, and I think we always learn from these experiences, whilst maybe it doesn't necessarily look like it. But there will be learnings that we take from this difficult period.

    "I still believe in everything we're building and working towards in the background. We came here with the goal of taking Ferrari back to the top."

    He added: "You learn really through the hardest times. And these are definitely very, very challenging times for Fred and Chares and I. But think these are the moments that you really learn and grow closer together through conversation and communicating and having those sit-downs."

    Hamilton revealed he and Leclerc have gone through every corner and every incident between the pair this year, which also included a clash at Monza. But the seven-time world champion also acknowledged every incident is different and dynamic, so it's not as simple as imposing strict racing rules. Instead, he suggested that he and Leclerc have to use their instinct while keeping the team's interests in the back of their minds.

    "You can never cover everything, because every scenario is different," Hamilton explained.

    "But you can definitely go through every single race, corner by corner, and discuss things, which Charles and I have.

    "But there's always going to be something new that you maybe haven't foreseen. You also have to use your instinct, and at the core you always have to have the team. Speaking for myself, when I'm driving, when I'm attacking, whoever it is on track, I'm always thinking: 'OK, how is this going to impact everybody behind me, team-wise?' So, I really do try to be as responsible as I can, and make the best decision when I am attacking, I think."
