    阿隆索因病毒感染缺席卡塔尔大奖赛媒体日

    阿隆索因病毒感染将缺席卡塔尔大奖赛媒体日，目标是周五首次练习赛前恢复健康。

    阿隆索因病毒感染缺席卡塔尔大奖赛媒体日

    阿隆索的目标是周五自由练习赛前恢复健康以便驾驶

    阿隆索因病毒感染康复中，将缺席卡塔尔大奖赛前的F1媒体日。

    这位两届冠军得主将缺席在卢赛尔国际赛道的传统媒体义务，目标是周五首次练习赛前恢复健康。

    他的阿斯顿·马丁车队在一份声明中解释道：“费尔南多将缺席今天的媒体日，因为他仍在从拉斯维加斯大奖赛周末临近结束时感染的病毒感染中恢复。”

    “他恢复得很好，计划于周五返回围场。”

    阿隆索在拉斯维加斯的这场压轴大戏中未能完赛，原因是他所说的比赛后段引擎故障；由于这条拉斯维加斯大道街道赛道对动力要求极高，搭载本田动力单元的阿斯顿·马丁经历了本赛季最艰难的周末之一。

    这意味着这位西班牙车手未能增加自己七个积分的进账，而凭借这些积分，阿斯顿·马丁在车队积分榜上领先于新加入的凯迪拉克。

    F1 在经历了一场旷日持久的争论后抵达卡塔尔，争论的焦点是在该地区因美国/以色列与伊朗之间的战争而引发紧张局势的情况下，在中东参赛是否安全。

    巴林大奖赛和沙特阿拉伯大奖赛都在年初被取消，不过前者后来重返赛历，尽管是在马来西亚的雪邦赛道举行。

    在安全保证到位的情况下——而且由 Liberty 拥有的 MotoGP 几周前已在卢赛尔赛道参赛——F1 确认卡塔尔站比赛以及下周末在阿布扎比举行的赛季收官战将按计划进行，从而完成 23 站赛历。

    Fernando Alonso to miss Qatar F1 media day with viral illness

    Alonso is aiming to be fit to drive in Friday's free practice sessions

    Fernando Alonso will be absent from Formula 1's media day ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix as he recovers from a "viral infection".

    The two-time champion will miss the traditional media obligations at the Lusail International Circuit as he aims to return to fitness for first practice on Friday.

    A statement from his Aston Martin team explained: "Fernando will miss media day today as he continues to recover from a viral infection contracted towards the end of the Las Vegas GP weekend.

    "He is recovering well, and is planned to return to the paddock on Friday."

    Alonso failed to finish the race in Las Vegas due to what he described as a broken engine late on at the Nevada showpiece, with the Honda-powered Aston Martin enduring one of its most difficult weekends of the season, given the power-hungry nature of the Strip street circuit.

    It meant that the Spaniard was unable to add to his tally of seven points, which has seen Aston Martin take its place ahead of newcomer Cadillac in the constructors' standings.

    F1 arrives in Qatar after a drawn-out saga over whether it would be safe to race in the Middle East amid tension in the region, triggered by war between the United States/Israel and Iran.

    Both the Bahrain and Saudi Arabian Grands Prix were cancelled at the start of the year, though the former later returned to the schedule, albeit racing at Malaysia's Sepang circuit.

    With safety guarantees in place - and with Liberty-owned MotoGP having raced at Lusail a few weeks prior - F1 confirmed that the Qatar race and next weekend's season finale in Abu Dhabi would run as scheduled to complete a 23-race calendar.
