    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里承认：麦拉伦车队问题难以解决

    麦拉伦一级方程式车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里表示，车队可能无法在2026赛季结束前解决其问题。他认为，随着仅剩六场比赛，赛车已达到其极限。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里对剩余六场比赛的麦克拉伦表示担忧

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里认为，麦克拉伦不太可能在2026赛季结束前解决MCL40的性能问题

    麦克拉伦一级方程式车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里承认，他的车队可能无法在2026赛季结束前找到解决问题的方法。在只剩下六场比赛的情况下，这位澳大利亚人认为赛车已经达到了其极限。

    “我认为在短期内，这可能是它最好的样子了，”他告诉媒体。"马来西亚站是另一个向前迈出的好步子，让我更清楚地知道我需要做什么，从根本上让赛车更接近我想要的状态。

    “我认为今年我们不太可能找到任何神奇的答案。”

    2026年的规定带来了巨大的变化，导致赛车网格中的几位车手都难以完全理解这些变化。

    “汽车本身非常不同，”皮亚斯特里说。“轮胎也相当不同，所以这种情况不会很快改变。我确实在进步，[但]仍然没有达到我希望的舒适度。”

    皮亚斯特里将迈凯伦的表现描述为要么全有要么全无。

    “要么你离得很远，要么你真的很快。实际上没有中间地带，”他说。当被问及性能发生巨大变化的原因时，他开玩笑说： “如果我知道，我会是一个更快乐的人。”

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂，麦克拉伦，兰多·诺里斯，麦克拉伦

    他还承认，他的队友和现任冠军兰多·诺里斯有时过分夸大了汽车的性能，展现了汽车实际上无法达到的表现。

    “兰多在排位赛中完成的某些圈数，使汽车占据杆位，比汽车应得的要好，”他说，始终是一个团队玩家。诺里斯描述这辆车驾驶起来“精神上非常疲惫”，车队负责人安德烈亚·斯特拉也同意皮亚斯蒂的看法，确认MCL40的潜在问题直到2027年的汽车才能解决。"

    皮亚斯特里在本赛季的锦标赛中排名第七，积分为138分，但本赛季尚未赢得大奖赛。作为上届车队冠军，梅赛德斯车队以339分排名第三，但在这个周末的美国大奖赛之前，仅领先重振旗鼓的红牛车队7分。

    Oscar Piastri delivers worrying McLaren verdict with six races remaining

    Oscar Piastri believes McLaren is unlikely to resolve its MCL40 performance problems before the end of the 2026 Formula 1 season

    McLaren Formula 1 driver Oscar Piastri has admitted that his team will likely not find a fix for its problems before the close of the 2026 season. With only six races remaining, the Australian believes the car has reached its limit.

    “I think in the short term, it’s probably as good as it’s going to get,” he told the media. "Malaysia was another good step forward in terms of understanding what I need to do to fundamentally bring the car a bit closer to where I would like it to be.

    “I don’t think we’re going to have any magic answers this year.”

    The 2026 regulations have brought drastic changes that several drivers across the grid are struggling to fully understand.

    “The cars themselves are very different,” Piastri said. “The tyres are quite different as well, so that’s not going to change anytime soon. I’m definitely making progress, [but] it’s still not as comfortable as I would like.”

    Piastri described McLaren’s performance as all or nothing.

    “Either you’re quite a way off or you’re really fast. There’s not really an in-between,” he said. Asked what is behind the drastic changes in performance, he joked: “If I knew, I’d be a much happier person.”

    Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    He also admitted that his team-mate and reigning champion Lando Norris has at times flattered the car, exhibiting more performance than the car is actually capable of.

    “Some of the laps that Lando’s done in qualifying to put the car on pole have been better than what the car deserves,” he said, ever a team player. Norris has described the car as "mentally exhausting" to drive, and team principal Andrea Stella has also agreed with Piastri, confirming that the MCL40's underlying problems won't be fixed until the 2027 car.

    Piastri is seventh in the championship on 138 points without a grand prix win this season. McLaren, the reigning constructors’ champion, is third on 339 points, but only seven ahead of a resurgent Red Bull going into this weekend’s United States Grand Prix.
