    尽管两位梅赛德斯车手在最后三圈跌出领奖台，车队领队托托·沃尔夫在离开2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛时仍对这场比赛的观赏性感到兴奋。

    尽管批评者质疑F1的2026年能量管理规则是否让周六晚间那场35次领先易主的战斗变得有些人为，沃尔夫却认为，这项赛事不应轻视车迷刚刚目睹的一切。

    “这些规则带来了极佳的娱乐性，我们见证了一场超级精彩的比赛，尤其是对美国观众而言，”沃尔夫在赛后媒体采访中表示。“我觉得这就像是印地500、纳斯卡和F1的混合体！”

    当被直接问及频繁的尾流交换是否过于偏向人为制造的比赛时，沃尔夫指向了维修区墙外的反应——包括他的妻子、F1学院董事总经理苏西·沃尔夫，以及F1首席执行官斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利。

    “是的，我的意思是，我猜会有很多人说，‘好吧，这不算赛车。’就我个人而言，我们需要保持开放的心态，”沃尔夫说。“苏西当时在围场俱乐部，她说整整90分钟里，人们都在为超车尖叫。你都不太确定自己是否还占着位置。我从观看比赛的朋友、我们的赞助商那里得到的即时反馈是，太棒了。斯特凡诺（多梅尼卡利）非常喜欢。”

    “这种情况不会在所有地方发生，”他补充道。“从某种意义上说，这很适合拉斯维加斯的比赛——纳斯卡、印地500，就像我说的，全都聚在一起。说实话，我不知道比赛持续了多久，90分钟。对我来说，感觉就像20分钟，因为比赛如此不同，观看如此有趣。”

    Toto Wolff: Las Vegas was “Indy 500, NASCAR and Formula 1 mixed”

    Toto Wolff compares the 2026 Las Vegas GP to "Indy 500 and NASCAR and Formula 1 mixed" and urges critics to stay open

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Even after watching both Mercedes drivers slip off the podium in the final three laps, team principal Toto Wolff left the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix thrilled by the spectacle.

    While critics questioned whether Formula 1’s 2026 energy-management rules turned Saturday night’s 35-lead-change battle into something artificial, Wolff argued the series should not dismiss what fans had just witnessed.

    "These regs are great entertainment, and we saw a mega race, particularly for the Americans," Wolff said in his post-race media session. "I guess it was Indy 500 and NASCAR and Formula 1 mixed!"

    Asked directly if the constant slipstream swaps leaned too far into artificial racing, Wolff pointed to the reaction away from the pitwall - including from his wife, F1 Academy managing director Susie Wolff, and Formula 1 CEO Stefano Domenicali.

    "Yeah, I mean, I guess you will have many people that will say, 'Well, that’s not racing.' Personally, we need to be open-minded," Wolff said. "Susie was in Paddock Club, and she said that for 90 minutes, people [were] screaming with the overtakes. You were not quite sure whether you had the position or not. The instant feedback I got from friends watching, our sponsors, was that it was awesome. Stefano [Domenicali] liked it very much.

    "That’s not going to happen everywhere," he added. "In a way, it’s fitting that that was the Las Vegas race - NASCAR, Indy 500, like I said, all together. To be honest, I don’t know how long the race was 90 minutes. For me, it was like 20 minutes, because it was so different and so entertaining to watch."

    Praise for Verstappen despite Mercedes’ late collapse

    Max Verstappen won the 50-lap race in 1h27m39.624s - just under 90 minutes - to claim his third Las Vegas victory ahead of McLaren’s Lando Norris (+0.639 seconds) and Oscar Piastri (+1.529s).

    Mercedes’ own podium charge vanished in quick succession. On lap 48, George Russell locked up on the dirty line at Turn 5 while attacking team-mate Kimi Antonelli, sliding into the barrier and recovering to ninth - a move Wolff called "a misjudgement". Moments later, Antonelli lifted and reapplied throttle down the back straight trying to hold overtake mode behind Verstappen, draining his battery and tumbling from second to fourth.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    Wolff revealed Mercedes had saved "a last weapon up our sleeve for the final 400 metres over the left kink" for Antonelli before the error, yet gave full credit to Red Bull’s winner. "Max is a deserving winner," Wolff said. "He made no mistakes, and that's why he won the race."

    With Mercedes already crowned 2026 constructors’ champions in Brazil and holding a 149-point lead over Ferrari (666 to 517) entering next weekend’s Qatar Grand Prix, Wolff could afford to enjoy the chaos - calling the Strip’s slipstream duel "very authentic" for Las Vegas.
