    如何基米·安东内利在墨西哥赢得他的首个F1冠军

    基米·安东内利在赢得美国大奖赛胜利后，将前往墨西哥大奖赛，再次争取赢得他的首个一级方程式冠军

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析，来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯的消息。

    基米·安东内利将在下周末的墨西哥大奖赛中再次争取赢得他的首个一级方程式冠军，因为在奥斯汀的比赛中他险些赢得冠军。

    20岁的车手在美国大奖赛中将杆位转化为本赛季的第九场胜利，但刘易斯·汉密尔顿的第四名和乔治·拉塞尔的第八名至少让冠军争夺战持续到下周。

    基米·安东内利在墨西哥如何赢得F1冠军？

    如果安东内利下周日在墨西哥城的比赛中排名前十，他将成为冠军。在这种情况下，拉塞尔和汉密尔顿的表现将无关紧要。

    如果巴西、拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔和阿布扎比的比赛中一切顺利，墨西哥之后还剩下四场比赛，最多可以获得100分。安东内利需要在墨西哥大奖赛中至少比两位对手领先100分。

    即使拉塞尔在墨西哥获胜而安东内利排名第九，他也只能将差距缩小到正好100分，然后他必须赢得剩下的四场比赛，同时安东内利不得得分。但即便如此，安东内利仍将通过积分反超赢得冠军。他本赛季已经赢得了九场大奖赛，而拉塞尔赢得了三场，最多只能达到八场。

    如果基米·安东内利在墨西哥的比赛中未能进入前十名，他还能赢得冠军吗？

    是的。即使安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛中未能得分，只要拉塞尔和汉密尔顿都没有赢得比赛，他仍然会是冠军。

    只有当拉塞尔获胜而安东内利排名第十或更低，或者汉密尔顿获胜而安东内利未能进入积分榜时，冠军争夺战才会移至巴西。

    How Kimi Antonelli can clinch his maiden F1 championship in Mexico

    Kimi Antonelli heads to the Mexican GP with another chance to secure his maiden Formula 1 championship after his United States GP victory

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Kimi Antonelli will have another chance to claim his maiden Formula 1 championship at next weekend's Mexican Grand Prix after narrowly missing out on securing the title in Austin.

    The 20-year-old converted pole position into his ninth win of the season at the United States Grand Prix, but Lewis Hamilton's fourth place and George Russell's eighth kept the title fight alive for at least another week.

    What does Kimi Antonelli need to do to win the F1 title in Mexico?

    If Antonelli finishes in the top nine in Mexico City next Sunday, he will be champion. It would not matter what Russell and Hamilton do in that scenario.

    After Mexico, four races remain if all goes as planned in Brazil, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi, with a maximum of 100 points available. Antonelli needs to finish the Mexican Grand Prix at least 100 points clear of both rivals.

    Even if Russell won in Mexico and Antonelli finished ninth, he would only cut the gap to exactly 100, and he would then have to win all four remaining races while Antonelli failed to score. But even then, Antonelli would win the title on countback. He already has nine grand prix wins this season, while Russell has three and could only reach a maximum of eight.

    Can Kimi Antonelli still win the title if he finishes outside the top nine in Mexico?

    Yes. Even if Antonelli fails to score at the Mexican Grand Prix, he will still be champion as long as neither Russell nor Hamilton wins the race.

    The only way the title fight goes to Brazil is if Russell wins and Antonelli finishes 10th or lower, or Hamilton wins and Antonelli finishes outside the points.
