    安东内利谈打破维特尔最年轻世界冠军纪录

    安东内利在美国大奖赛前谈到有望打破维特尔的最年轻F1世界冠军纪录，并坦言自己对维特尔2010年夺冠没有记忆。

    安东内利谈打破维特尔F1纪录的可能性

    安东内利承认，在接近可能打破纪录之际，他不记得维特尔成为F1最年轻世界冠军的时刻

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的比赛分析。

    安东内利在美国大奖赛前谈到本周末有望打破德国车手维特尔的纪录时承认，他对维特尔成为F1最年轻世界冠军一事没有记忆。

    维特尔在2010年阿布扎比大奖赛上以23岁的年龄赢得了他的首个总冠军，在四人争冠决战中击败了费尔南多·阿隆索、马克·韦伯和刘易斯·汉密尔顿，成为最年轻的F1世界冠军。这一纪录可能在本周末被打破，20岁的安东内利有望在奥斯汀锁定总冠军。

    安东内利带着102分的积分榜领先优势进入美洲赛道的比赛周末，领先于他的队友乔治·拉塞尔。如果这位意大利车手赢得美国大奖赛，同时拉塞尔排名在前八之外、刘易斯·汉密尔顿排名在前四之外，他就将赢得自己的首个总冠军。

    在新闻发布会上被问及是否记得维特尔成为最年轻冠军的那一刻，以及他何时首次意识到自己可能打破这一纪录时，安东内利说：“我不记得那个。我当时四岁。抱歉。我记得我在玩风火轮玩具车，但我不记得他锁定（总冠军）。”

    “后来，等我长大后，我看了那场比赛，那场比赛也很疯狂，因为那是你们所有人之间一场非常接近的总冠军争夺战。”

    他补充道：“我处于一个不可思议的位置，但今年确实，考虑到事情的发展、我在周末驾驶赛车时的感受以及势头，我以为今年有机会去争夺。幸运的是，我现在处于非常有利的位置。”

    “但我仍然必须继续前进，心态始终如一，那就是每当我上赛道，都要处于顶尖位置，并尽可能多地去赢。无论是我赢得总冠军，无论是这个周末还是下个周末，还是在巴西，让我们拭目以待。”

    “比赛没结束就不算结束，这就是为什么心态将始终如一：每当我上赛道就要赢。你也希望以高昂的姿态结束赛季，然后带着高期望进入明年。”

    “今年，凭借我们拥有的赛车，我们有一辆如此强大的赛车，车队在开发和理解这个新套件方面做得非常出色。车队已尽其所能为我们提供一辆好车和争夺的机会。”

    Kimi Antonelli reacts to prospect of breaking Sebastian Vettel's F1 record

    Kimi Antonelli admits he cannot remember Sebastian Vettel becoming F1's youngest champion as he approaches the possibility of breaking the record

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Kimi Antonelli admitted he has no memory of Sebastian Vettel becoming Formula 1's youngest world champion while discussing the prospect of breaking the German driver's record this weekend at the United States Grand Prix.

    Vettel won his first title at the 2010 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, aged 23, beating Fernando Alonso, Mark Webber and Lewis Hamilton in a four-way decider to become the youngest F1 champion. That record could be broken this weekend, with 20-year-old Antonelli able to clinch the championship in Austin.

    Antonelli heads into the race weekend at the Circuit of the Americas with a 102-point lead in the standings over his team-mate George Russell. The Italian will win his maiden title if he wins the United States Grand Prix, Russell finishes outside the top eight and Lewis Hamilton finishes outside the top four.

    Asked in the press conference whether he remembered the moment Vettel became the youngest champion, and when he first realised he could break the record, Antonelli said: "I don't remember that. I was four years old. I'm sorry. I remember I was playing with Hot Wheels, but I don't remember him clinching [the title].

    "Later on, when I grew up, I saw the race, which was crazy as well because it was a very close title fight between all of you guys."

    He added: "I'm in an incredible position, but definitely this year, with how things went and how I felt in the car during the weekends and also with the momentum, I thought that I could have had a chance this year to fight. Luckily, I'm in a very good position right now.

    "But I've still got to keep going, and the mindset is always the same, which is whenever I go on track to be at the top and to try and win as much as possible. Whether I win the championship, whether I win it this weekend or next weekend, or in Brazil, let's see.

    "It's not over until it's over, and that's why the mindset is going to always be the same: to win whenever I go on track. You want to finish the season on a high as well to then go with high hopes into next year.

    "This year, with the car we've got, we've had such a strong car and the team is doing an incredible job on developing and understanding this new package. The team has done everything in their power to give us a great car and the opportunity to fight."
