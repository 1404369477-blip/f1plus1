    Netflix确认舒马赫新纪录片2026年10月上映

    Netflix宣布关于迈克尔·舒马赫首次夺冠赛季的F1纪录片《舒马赫'94 - 传奇诞生》将于2026年10月2日全球上映。该片是继2021年纪录片《舒马赫》之后的又一作品。

    Netflix确认了新的迈克尔·舒马赫F1纪录片的上映日期

    Netflix将于十月推出《舒马赫 '94 - 一个传奇的诞生》

    迈克尔·舒马赫与他的车队一起庆祝夺冠

    Netflix宣布，这部聚焦迈克尔·舒马赫首个冠军赛季的全新F1纪录片将于2026年10月2日在全球上线。

    这部名为《舒马赫 '94——传奇的诞生》的长片电影，最初于今年早些时候在 Netflix 德国的一次发布会上进行了预览，并且是继 2021 年纪录片《舒马赫》上映之后的又一作品。它也进一步扩充了 Netflix 不断扩大的赛车运动内容版图，其中包括广受欢迎的纪录片剧集《极速求生》。

    这部纪录片将是一部德语非虚构长片，将讲述 25 岁的舒马赫在 1994 赛季从黑马到世界冠军的转变过程。预计影片还将涉及 1994 年伊莫拉那个悲惨的比赛周末，该周末夺走了埃尔顿·塞纳和罗兰·拉岑贝格的生命。

    这部纪录片由 Christin Freitag 执导，LEONINE Documentaries 制作，将呈现舒马赫的妻子科琳娜，以及与前法拉利车手关系密切的其他人的讲述。

    Netflix 对这部电影的简介写道："1994 年是一级方程式历史的一个转折点：25 岁的迈克尔·舒马赫在巴西赢得赛季首场大奖赛，为一项前所未有的职业生涯奠定了基础。"

    “1994年是F1历史上最具戏剧性和最激动人心的赛季之一，发生了许多影响深远的事件，例如埃尔顿·塞纳（Ayrton Senna）的致命事故，以及有关操纵比赛和不公平驾驶行为的指控。与此同时，一场真正的‘舒马赫狂热’（Schumi Mania）也随之兴起。”

    简介中继续写道：“迈克尔·舒马赫（Michael Schumacher）的妻子科琳娜·舒马赫（Corinna Schumacher）以及他身边的其他人回忆起他当年的样子。他们记得他是赛道上一位毫不妥协的斗士，也是赛道下一个敏感、富有同理心的人。”

    “一直是他情感支柱的科琳娜·舒马赫（Corinna Schumacher）还讲述了一段非凡爱情故事的开端——这段爱情至今依然坚固。”

    Netflix confirms release date for new Michael Schumacher F1 documentary

    Netflix will release "Schumacher '94 - The Birth of a Legend" in October

    Michael Schumacher celebrates championship victory with his team

    Netflix has announced that its new Formula 1 documentary focusing on Michael Schumacher's maiden title-winning campaign will be released globally on 2 October 2026.

    Titled Schumacher '94 - The Birth of a Legend, the feature-length film was initially previewed earlier this year during a Netflix Germany presentation and follows the release of the 2021 documentary Schumacher. It also adds to Netflix's expanding motorsport portfolio, including the popular docuseries Drive to Survive.

    The documentary, which will be a German-language non-fiction feature, will follow the 25-year-old Schumacher's transformation from underdog to world champion during the 1994 season. It is also expected to cover the tragic 1994 Imola race weekend that claimed the lives of Ayrton Senna and Roland Ratzenberger.

    Directed by Christin Freitag and produced by LEONINE Documentaries, the documentary will feature Schumacher's wife, Corinna, as well as others who are close to the former Ferrari driver.

    "1994 marks a turning point in Formula 1 history: 25-year-old Michael Schumacher wins the first grand prix of the racing season in Brazil, setting the stage for an unprecedented career," the synopsis of the feature film from Netflix reads.

    "The year 1994, which is among the most dramatic and exciting racing seasons in Formula 1 history, featured impactful events such as Ayrton Senna's lethal accident, as well as accusations of manipulation and unfair driving manoeuvres. At the same time, a true 'Schumi Mania' emerged."

    The synopsis continued: "Michael Schumacher's wife, Corinna Schumacher, and other people close to him reminisce about him at that time. They remember an uncompromising fighter on the race track and a sensitive, empathic man off the course.

    "Corinna Schumacher, who has always been his emotional support, also offers insight into the beginnings of an extraordinary love story - one that is still going strong today."
