    阿隆索排除留队等V8回归：纯赛车16年已失去

    阿隆索表示，他对即将到来的V8引擎回归感到“沮丧”，并称F1自2014年以来因规则方向错误浪费了多年时间。

    阿隆索排除留队等V8回归：“纯赛车16年已失去”

    阿隆索承认，他将错过即将到来的V8引擎回归，这令人“沮丧”；他还表示，F1自2014年以来因规则方向错误浪费了多年时间。

    尽管国际汽联周二的正式确认只在网上停留了几分钟便被暂时撤下，但围场内所有人都清楚，V8引擎将重返F1。

    该原则性协议涉及2.1升涡轮增压V8——涡轮对奥迪和本田尤为重要——以及大幅缩小的电力部件。目标引入时间为2030年，比最初计划提前一年。

    这在很大程度上正是车手们一直推动的事情，这也解释了拉斯维加斯围场在媒体日期间为何反应积极。

    然而，费尔南多·阿隆索向包括 Motorsport.com 在内的媒体强调，V8 回归对他来说来得太晚了。这位西班牙人目前 45 岁，或许并不令人意外的是，他无意再在赛车运动的最高殿堂待上四年：“不，我不会等到新 V8 的时候。百分之百。”

    当有人指出阿隆索此前曾在围场让人惊讶，例如他 2021 年重返 F1 时，他笑了：“是啊，我过去也让自己惊讶过！但这一次我认为这是正确的那个。当我停下来时，那就会是那个时刻，也是尝试不同事物的时刻。”

    “（V8 回归）还有很长的路要走，但我认为（在那之前）对我来说已经足够了。”

    从某种意义上说，阿隆索确实感到沮丧，因为他将不得不在一套他和其他许多车手一样并不喜欢的规则下告别：“是的，令人沮丧的是我们在2014年还没有这套规则。从2014年到2030年或2031年，这意味着16年或17年的纯粹赛车时光被浪费了。”

    虽然届时阿隆索将不再参加F1比赛，但他仍然把目光投向其他赛车类别。即使从F1退役后，阿隆索也打算继续长期参赛。

    2027年1月，他将亮相达喀尔拉力赛，但一旦他的F1生涯结束，他也为其他标志性赛事留有余地。

    “达喀尔一直在我心中。勒芒一直在我心中，印地500一直在我心中，”这位阿斯顿·马丁车手说道。

    “我喜欢竞争，我喜欢驾驶赛车竞争，那种踩下油门就有动力、踩下刹车就有减速的感觉。这些正是我们现在在一级方程式中所缺失的。”

    “显然，如果我继续留在一级方程式，那永远会是我的优先事项，要把不同项目凑在一起或结合起来很困难。达喀尔拉力赛因为在一月举行，稍微更合适一些，但即便是那个也得靠一些巧合才能成行。”

    阿隆索想重返勒芒等耐力赛并不令人意外——他甚至愿意与马克斯·维斯塔潘并肩作战——但他提到印第安纳波利斯500大赛则有些出人意料。

    在2020年最后一次尝试之后，这位西班牙人似乎并不特别热衷于再次出现在砖厂赛道的“赛车界最伟大奇观”上，但随着新的印地赛车底盘即将到来，他的想法可能会改变。

    “是啊，我不知道。他们很快就会换车。我记得2028年会有新车到来，所以，是啊，我不知道，”他说。“我可能有一天会考虑。这不在我的短期计划中，现在也不在我心上，但我不能百分之百地拒绝。”

    费尔南多·阿隆索，Arrow 迈凯伦 SP 雪佛兰

    Fernando Alonso rules out staying in F1 for V8 return: "16 years of pure racing lost"

    Fernando Alonso admits it is "frustrating" that he will miss the upcoming return of V8 engines, adding that F1 has wasted many years since 2014 by taking the wrong direction with its regulations

    Although the FIA's formal confirmation on Tuesday was only online for a few minutes before being temporarily taken down again, it is clear to everyone in the paddock that V8 engines will return to Formula 1.

    The agreement in principle involves a 2.1-litre turbocharged V8 – with the turbo being particularly important for Audi and Honda – and a significantly smaller electrical component. The target for its introduction is 2030, which would be a year earlier than initially planned.

    It is largely what the drivers have been pushing for, which explains the positive reactions in the Las Vegas paddock during the media day.

    Fernando Alonso, however, stressed to media including Motorsport.com that the V8 return will come too late for him. The Spaniard is currently 45 and, perhaps unsurprisingly, has no intention of remaining in the pinnacle of motorsport for another four years: "No, I will not be around for the new V8. 100%."

    When it was pointed out that Alonso has surprised people in the paddock before, for example with his F1 comeback in 2021, he laughed: "Yeah, I have surprised myself in the past as well! But this time I think it's the good one. When I stop, it will be the moment, also the moment to try different things.

    "It's still a long way ahead, [the V8 return], but I think it will be enough [for me before that]."

    Alonso does find it frustrating, in a sense, that he will have to bow out under a set of regulations that he, like many other drivers, is far from fond of: "Yes, it's frustrating that we didn't have it in 2014 already. From 2014 to 2030 or 2031, so that means 16 years or 17 years of pure racing are lost."

    While Alonso will no longer be competing in F1 by then, he still has his sights set on other racing categories. Even after retiring from F1, Alonso intends to continue racing for a long time.

    In January 2027, he will make an appearance at the Dakar Rally, but he is also leaving the door open to other iconic races once his F1 career comes to an end.

    "Dakar was on my mind. Le Mans is on my mind, the Indy 500 is on my mind," the Aston Martin driver said.

    "I love competing and I love competing in race cars in which you have power when you go on throttle, and in which you have deceleration when you brake. Those are the things that we miss now in Formula 1.

    "Obviously, if I continue in Formula 1, that will always be my priority and it's difficult to put things together or to combine different programs. Dakar, because it's in January, it fits a little bit better, but even that had to come with a few coincidences."

    That Alonso would like to return to endurance races such as Le Mans is no surprise – he would even like to do so alongside Max Verstappen – but his mention of the Indy 500 is somewhat more unexpected.

    After his last attempt in 2020, the Spaniard did not seem particularly keen on another appearance at The Greatest Spectacle in Racing at the Brickyard, but with a new IndyCar chassis on the way, his opinion could change.

    "Yeah, I don't know. They will change cars soon. There's a new car coming for 2028, I think, so yeah, I don't know," he said. "I may consider it one day. It’s not in my short-term plans and not on my mind now, but I cannot say no to it 100%."

    Fernando Alonso, Arrow McLaren SP Chevrolet
