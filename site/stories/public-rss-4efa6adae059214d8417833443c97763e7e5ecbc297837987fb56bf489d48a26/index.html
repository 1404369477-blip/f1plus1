    维斯塔潘在卡塔尔大奖赛将无常驻助手，汤姆·哈特接替

    维斯塔潘在卡塔尔大奖赛将没有他的常驻助手吉安皮耶罗·兰比亚斯，红牛车队已选择轮换制度，让汤姆·哈特提前适应角色，兰比亚斯仍将留在车队。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在卡塔尔大奖赛上没有吉安皮耶罗·兰比亚塞，汤姆·哈特临时顶替

    兰比亚塞原本计划在2026年最后两场F1比赛中担任维斯塔潘的工程师，但由于个人原因将缺席卡塔尔站

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在本周末的卢塞尔国际赛道上将没有他的常任副手吉安皮耶罗·兰比亚塞。红牛队已经为本赛季后半段选择了轮换制度，主要是为了让汤姆·哈特能够适应这个角色，以便于他预期的永久任命，同时兰比亚塞仍然留在车队中

    最初的计划是让兰比亚塞作为维斯塔潘的赛事工程师，覆盖本赛季最后两场大奖赛，同时他还担任红牛车队赛车部门负责人，但由于个人原因他将缺席卡塔尔站。车队告诉Motorsport.com，如果兰比亚塞的个人情况允许，他预计将在阿布扎比回归

    Lambiase预计将在2027年转会至迈凯轮之前被安排休假。尽管红牛尚未正式宣布，但这使得哈特很可能会在2027赛季开始时成为维斯塔潘的永久赛车工程师。

    哈特自2013年以来一直为红牛效力，原本他正前往威廉姆斯车队，但维斯塔潘内部表示更倾向于哈特作为Lambiase的替代者。红牛随后设法阻止了哈特前往位于格罗夫的团队的计划，他原本将成为亚历山大·阿隆索的赛车工程师。

    哈特与这位四次世界冠军合作的首场比赛就遇到了一些挑战。在奥斯汀，无线电连接出现了技术问题，而在巴西，维斯塔潘在开场阶段后反复抱怨小红牛22赛车有一种奇怪的感觉。在拉斯维加斯，虽然维斯塔潘和哈特之间的沟通已经显得相当好，但比赛仍然非常混乱。

    维斯塔潘对哈特评价积极，他表示尽管面临这些挑战，哈特还是留下了“良好的印象”。

    如何适应F1的能量管理要求

    维斯塔潘、红牛车队、乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯

    在拉斯维加斯，维斯塔潘因为‘智胜’梅赛德斯车队而受到赞誉，正如红牛车队负责人劳伦特·梅基斯所说。尽管这位荷兰人仍然不是当前规则的粉丝，但他在与乔治·拉塞尔的较量中展现了他对能量管理的理解，他多次超越拉塞尔。

    维斯塔潘的表现突出了红牛车队在能量匮乏赛道上DM01动力单元的进步，同时也展现了维斯塔潘如何运用他手中的工具——这与他赛季初在墨尔本的表现相比，似乎是一个进步。

    “我认为我们所有人，包括车手和车队，在理解能量管理方面都变得更擅长了，但这是正常的，因为这是一项新的规定，”当被Motorsport.com在卡塔尔问到他在幕后采取了哪些步骤时，维斯塔潘说。

    “当你回顾第一个周末的比赛时，我认为如果我们能回到过去，我们会做不同的事情。如果你回去却不知道该怎么做不同的事情，那也是不好的，所以这只是正常的进步，我会这么说。”

    虽然维斯塔潘觉得拉斯维加斯的赛道布局非常适合小红牛22，但他预计卡塔尔将带来不同的挑战。卢塞尔国际赛道以高速弯角为主，这是维斯塔潘表示红牛车队今年在这一领域并不总是特别有竞争力的地方。

    然而，在巴库红牛车队成功升级后，队友伊萨克·哈德贾认为车队现在应该在任何地方都具有竞争力。

    Max Verstappen without Gianpiero Lambiase at Qatar GP as Tom Hart steps in

    Lambiase was originally scheduled to engineer Verstappen for the final two F1 races of 2026 but will miss Qatar due to personal circumstances

    Max Verstappen will be without his regular right-hand man Gianpiero Lambiase this weekend at the Lusail International Circuit. Red Bull had already opted for a rotation system for the latter part of this Formula 1 season, primarily to allow Tom Hart to settle into the role ahead of his expected permanent appointment while Lambiase is still with the team.

    The initial plan was for Lambiase to cover the final two grands prix of the season as Verstappen's race engineer, besides his duties as Red Bull’s head of racing, but he will be absent in Qatar due to personal circumstances. The team told Motorsport.com that Lambiase is expected to return in Abu Dhabi, provided his personal circumstances allow.

    Lambiase is expected to be placed on gardening leave in 2027 ahead of his move to McLaren. While Red Bull has yet to formally announce it, this makes it very likely that Hart will become Verstappen's permanent race engineer as early as the start of the 2027 season.

    Hart, who has worked for Red Bull since 2013, was on his way to Williams until Verstappen internally expressed his preference for Hart as Lambiase's replacement. Red Bull subsequently managed to reverse Hart's planned move to the Grove-based team, where he was due to become Alexander Albon's race engineer.

    Hart’s first races working with the four-time world champion have presented several challenges. In Austin, there were technical problems with the radio connection, while in Brazil Verstappen repeatedly complained about a strange feeling in the RB22 after his opening stint. A particularly chaotic race unfolded in Las Vegas, although communication between Verstappen and Hart already appeared to be considerably better.

    Verstappen has spoken positively about Hart, who he says has made a “good impression” despite those challenges.

    How Verstappen has adapted to F1’s energy management demands

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    In Las Vegas, Verstappen was praised for  "outsmarting" Mercedes, as Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies put it. Although the Dutchman is still not a fan of the current regulations, he demonstrated his understanding of energy management in his battle with George Russell, whom he repeatedly repassed.

    Verstappen’s performance underlined the progress Red Bull has made with its DM01 power unit on energy-poor circuits, but also the extent to which Verstappen played with the tools at his disposal – something that also appeared to represent a step forward compared with the season opener in Melbourne.

    “I think we all got better at it, drivers and teams, in understanding the energy management, but that's normal when it's a new regulation,” Verstappen said when asked by Motorsport.com in Qatar which steps he has taken behind the scenes.

    “When you look at the first race weekend, I think if we go back, you would do things differently. It would also be bad if you go back and you wouldn't know what to do differently, so it's just normal progression, I would say.”

    While Verstappen felt the layout in Las Vegas suited the RB22 well, he expects Qatar to present a different challenge. The Lusail International Circuit is dominated by high-speed corners, an area in which Verstappen says Red Bull has not always been particularly competitive this year.

    However, following Red Bull's successful upgrade in Baku, team-mate Isack Hadjar believes the team should now be competitive everywhere.
