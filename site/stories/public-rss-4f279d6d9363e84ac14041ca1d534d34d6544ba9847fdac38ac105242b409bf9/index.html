    Haas have confirmed that Rafael Camara will continue as Oliver Bearman's team-mate for this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix after making his Formula 1 debut last time out in Brazil.

    The American-owned squad have been left with a vacant seat for the final five rounds of the season following a fall out between French driver Esteban Ocon and team boss Ayao Komatsu last month in Mexico.

    Japanese driver Ryo Hirakawa, who had already featured in first practice in Mexico, drove in the Mexico City Grand Prix, before Camara, who had earlier in October been confirmed as Ocon's replacement at Haas for the 2027 season, was given the opportunity to make his debut at his home race in Sao Paulo.
