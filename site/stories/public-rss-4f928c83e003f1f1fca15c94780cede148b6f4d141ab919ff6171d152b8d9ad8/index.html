    麦凯轮一级方程式车手兰多·诺里斯表示，在拉斯维加斯大奖赛中，他花更多的时间关注电池而不是前方的道路，因为他从积分榜外恢复到第二名。

    2025年的冠军起步排在第三位，但在第12圈时已经落后到了第11位，MCL40在拉斯维加斯街道赛道的长直道上失去了优势。

    诺里斯在赛后已经表示这辆车在直线上的表现‘糟糕’，比赛的开局几圈也证明了这一点。

    ‘在比赛一开始，我只是在尝试了解电池的情况。我总是感觉处于不利的一方。每次被超越，我的电池电量不足，而别人后面有更多的电池电量，我就一直掉队，’他在赛后新闻发布会上解释道。

    ‘整个比赛都在判断电池电量。我观察电池的时间比观察我在赛道上的行驶时间还要多。所以这是最重要的事情。’

    安全车被叫出，这是由于塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯和拉斐尔·卡马拉发生碰撞，帮助诺里斯重新回到竞争行列。

    第一节的节奏和第二节一样好。只是我们在直道上的防御太脆弱了。大部分时间都在尝试找出在哪里使用电池，在哪里不要使用。

    我必须每圈按九个按钮，试图做正确的事情，让电池按照应有的方式工作，给我们自己一点机会。因为我们直线上的速度比其他人慢，我们不得不做很多事情来尝试给自己任何希望。

    赛季还剩下两轮，在卡塔尔和阿布扎比，诺里斯目前在车手积分榜上排名第五，积264分，与梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔积分相同，他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛之后下滑到第四。

    Lando Norris: "Looking at the battery more than where I'm going" in Las Vegas

    The result leaves Norris fifth in the championship, level on points with George Russell

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    McLaren Formula 1 driver Lando Norris says he spent more of the Las Vegas Grand Prix watching his battery than the road ahead, as he recovered from outside the points to second place.

    The 2025 champion started third but was running 11th by lap 12, with the MCL40 losing out on the Las Vegas street circuit's long straights.

    Norris had already called the car "awful in the straight" after qualifying, and the opening laps showed why.

    "It was just, at the very beginning of the race, I was just trying to figure out the battery. I always just seemed to be on the bad side. Every time I got overtaken, I had no battery, someone else had more battery behind, and I just kept dropping back," he explained during the post-race press conference.

    "The whole race was judging battery the whole time. I'm looking at the battery more than I'm looking where I'm going around this track. So it's the most important thing."

    The safety car, called when Sergio Perez and Rafael Camara collided, helped Norris get back into contention.

    "The pace was just as good in the first stint as it was in the second. It was just we were so vulnerable down the straight. A lot of it was just trying to figure out where to use the battery, where not to.

    "I must be pressing like nine buttons a lap, trying to just get the correct things and make the battery work as it should and give ourselves a bit of a chance. Because we were so slow in the straights compared to others, we just had to do a lot to try and give ourselves any hope."

    With two rounds remaining of the season, in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, Norris currently sits fifth in the drivers' championship with 264 points, level on points with Mercedes' George Russell, who slipped to fourth after the Las Vegas Grand Prix.
