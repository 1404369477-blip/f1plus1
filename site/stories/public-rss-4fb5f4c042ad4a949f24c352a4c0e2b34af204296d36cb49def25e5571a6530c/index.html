    詹姆斯·阿隆索表示乔治·拉塞尔面对基米·安东内利的速度感到压力

    乔治·拉塞尔在2026赛季被20岁的队友基米·安东内利超越，詹姆斯·阿隆索认为这给拉塞尔带来了高压情境。

    乔治·拉塞尔表示正在面临基米·安东内利的强大速度，詹姆斯·阿隆索如是说

    詹姆斯·阿隆索赞扬乔治·拉塞尔的诚实，面对基米·安东内利的驾驶优势，英国车手直面自己的驾驶不足

    对于梅赛德斯车迷来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯顿的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析

    对于一位资深的F1车手来说，被20岁的队友击败有时会导致维修区政治和借口。对于乔治·拉塞尔来说，2026赛季正是这样一场高压场景，基米·安东内利在姐妹车W17上勇夺世界冠军。

    然而，在经历了机械故障和驾驶错误而备受破坏的一个赛季之后，而不是推卸责任，拉塞尔直面了安东内利原始速度的现实。

    在Nu Silver Arrows广播节目中发表讲话，梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森透露了布拉克利车队内部的动态，赞扬了拉塞尔在失败面前的残酷自我意识。

    “他从不掩饰自己的不足。他是我有幸共事过的最诚实车手之一，”阿利森透露。“他可以看到基米今年在某些方面比他更快，他知道这些都是他需要带到桌面上来与这位早熟年轻天才平等或更好竞争的东西。”

    安东内利的驾驶风格——特别是他在弯道中极端使用刹车迁移调整——使这位意大利人能够保持他的后胎，并在各节练习与排位中后期提取出惊人的速度。拉塞尔的传统优势在于在弯道出口使用更传统、更具侵略性的油门应用，但在几个轮胎磨损的场景中，他发现自己落后了。

    但是，根据阿隆索的说法，拉塞尔愿意承认这一具体技术差距正是他成为冠军级车手的原因。

    "我非常钦佩他直面这一问题的态度，" 阿隆索解释道。"[我们]将会看到他努力构建一个平台，这将使他能够在下一年与一位极具天赋的队友并肩作战。",

    随着2026年 车队对梅赛德斯的成功锁定，拉塞尔的焦点已经完全转向调整他的设置参数，以匹配安东内利为即将到来的2027赛季设定的基准。

    George Russell is “looking down the barrel” of Kimi Antonelli’s raw pace, says James Allison

    James Allison praises George Russell's honesty as the Briton confronts his own driving deficiencies against Kimi Antonelli

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    For an established Formula 1 driver, being beaten by a 20-year-old team-mate can sometimes lead to garage politics and excuses. For George Russell, the 2026 season delivered exactly that high-pressure scenario as Kimi Antonelli stormed to the world championship in the sister Mercedes W17.

    But rather than deflecting blame after a season marred by mechanical misfortune and driving errors, Russell is confronting the reality of Antonelli's raw speed head-on.

    Speaking on the Nu Silver Arrows Radio Show, Mercedes technical director James Allison provided a glimpse into the intra-team dynamic at Brackley, praising Russell for his brutal self-awareness in the face of defeat.

    "He does not hide from his own deficiencies. He's one of the most honest drivers I've ever had the good fortune to work with," Allison revealed. "He can see the areas where Kimi has been quicker than him this year, and he knows that those are things that he needs to bring to the table to compete on equal or better terms with this precocious young talent."

    Antonelli's driving style - specifically his extreme use of mid-corner brake migration adjustments - allowed the Italian to preserve his rear tyres and extract blistering pace late in stints. Russell, whose strength traditionally lies in a more conventional, aggressive throttle application on corner exit, found himself outpaced in several tyre-degrading scenarios.

    But, according to Allison, Russell's willingness to acknowledge that specific technical gap is exactly what makes him a championship-calibre driver.

    "I do very much admire the fact that he looks straight down the barrel of that," Allison explained. "[We're] gonna see him looking to build the platform that is going to let him have a run at it next year alongside an amazingly talented team-mate."

    With the 2026 constructors' championship successfully secured for Mercedes, Russell's focus has completely shifted toward evolving his set-up parameters to match Antonelli's benchmark for the upcoming 2027 season.
