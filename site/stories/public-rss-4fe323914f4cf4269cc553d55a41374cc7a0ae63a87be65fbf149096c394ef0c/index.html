    汉密尔顿分享与新宠物狗Halo的首批照片

    七届F1世界冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿在夏休期分享了家中新成员——金毛寻回犬幼犬Halo的首批照片和视频。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿（汉密尔顿）分享与新小狗Halo的首批照片

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿（汉密尔顿）在F1夏休期期间分享了他的新小狗Halo的首批照片和视频

    七届F1冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿（汉密尔顿）分享了他与家庭新成员——一只名为Halo的金毛寻回犬幼犬的首批照片。

    在2026赛季上半程的激烈比赛之后——汉密尔顿在巴塞罗那-加泰罗尼亚大奖赛上为法拉利取得了他的首个分站冠军，并连续多次登上领奖台——这位英国车手正借此机会与一位新的四条腿伙伴一起休息和充电。

    汉密尔顿从美国繁殖者Goldiva Goldens处获得了这只幼犬。繁殖者在社交媒体上确认了交接，并附上一张这只狗戴着赛车头盔摆拍的照片。

    “我们很高兴将Halo小姐空运到洛杉矶，交给法拉利F1车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿，”Goldiva Goldens发帖称。“驯犬师Kirstin McMillian是一位三代动物训练师，她昨天已飞往缅因州。”

    McMillian是一位常驻洛杉矶的动物训练师，此前曾长期与汉密尔顿的其他爱犬合作，其中包括深受粉丝喜爱的英国斗牛犬Roscoe，遗憾的是Roscoe已于2025年离世。

    目前尚未确认Halo最终是否会随汉密尔顿参加大奖赛周末，但如果这只金毛寻回犬前往比赛，她将与汉密尔顿的队友夏尔·勒克莱尔那条著名的长毛腊肠犬Leo为伴。

    虽然Halo到来的消息早已通过Goldiva Goldens的帖子以及这只小狗出现在Kim Kardashian的一则帖子中在社交媒体上传开，但Hamilton直到现在才在自己的账号上分享了她的照片。

    这位法拉利车手分享了一组迄今为止暑假期间的照片和视频，其中包括一段他和Halo玩耍的视频，以及几张他与她合影的照片。

    截至撰写本文时，该帖子在Instagram上已获得170万个赞和超过2万次转发。Goldiva Goldens在帖子下评论道：“很高兴你能和你的小狗度过一段美好的时光。小狗的口臭味也很可爱。Halo的姐姐Danica下周开始训练。她将成为Goldiva的Grand Prix。”

    Hamilton目前在车手积分榜上排名第二，落后总冠军领跑者安东内利 50分。

    Lewis Hamilton shares first photos with new puppy Halo

    Lewis Hamilton has shared the first photos and videos of his new puppy, Halo, during his Formula 1 summer break

    Seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton has shared his first photos with the newest member of his family, a golden retriever puppy named Halo.

    After a demanding first half of the 2026 season, in which Hamilton secured his maiden grand prix victory with Ferrari at the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix and a string of podiums, the Briton is taking the opportunity to rest and recharge with a new four-legged companion.

    Hamilton acquired the puppy from Goldiva Goldens, a US-based breeder. The handover was confirmed by the breeder on social media, alongside a photograph of the dog posing in a racing helmet.

    "We’re excited to fly Ms Halo to Los Angeles to Formula 1 Ferrari driver Lewis Hamilton," Goldiva Goldens posted. "Dog trainer Kirstin McMillian, who is a three-generation animal trainer, flew to Maine yesterday."

    McMillian, a Los Angeles-based animal trainer, previously worked extensively with Hamilton's other dogs, including his fan-favourite English bulldog, Roscoe, who sadly passed away in 2025.

    It remains unconfirmed whether Halo will eventually join Hamilton at grand prix weekends, but should the golden retriever travel to races, she would join Hamilton's team-mate Charles Leclerc's famous long-haired dachshund, Leo.

    While the news of Halo's arrival had already made its way across social media through the post from Goldiva Goldens and the puppy's appearance in one of Kim Kardashian's posts, Hamilton has only just shared photos of her on his own feed.

    The Ferrari driver shared a carousel of photos and videos from his summer break so far, which included a video of him playing with Halo and a couple of photos of him posing with her.

    At the time of writing, the post has already received 1.7 million likes and over 20,000 shares on Instagram. Goldiva Goldens commented on the post, writing: "Glad you’re getting some quality time with your pup. Stinky puppy breath is wonderful. Halo’s sister Danica starts training next week. She will be Goldiva’s Grand Prix."

    Hamilton currently sits second in the drivers' standings, 50 points behind championship leader Kimi Antonelli.
