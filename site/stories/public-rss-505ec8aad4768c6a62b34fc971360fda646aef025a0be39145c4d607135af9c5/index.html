    新加坡大奖赛：维斯塔潘首次在F1职业生涯中赢得比赛，领先查尔斯·勒克莱尔夺冠，勒克莱尔表示对策略失误道歉。

    红牛车队的维斯塔潘在新加坡大奖赛中以灯灭到旗落的全场胜利统治了比赛；查尔斯·勒克莱尔获得第二名，基米·安东内利获得第三名；汉密尔顿因策略失误，最终跌出领奖台。

    维斯塔潘首次赢得新加坡大奖赛

    维斯塔潘在F1职业生涯中首次以一场统治性的胜利赢得新加坡大奖赛，而法拉利的勒克莱尔则因策略失误，在比赛后期跌出领奖台。

    维斯塔潘尽管因为无线电问题无法与车队沟通，但他在比赛中领跑每一圈，连续第二次获胜，尽管法拉利的查尔斯·勒克莱尔在早期发起了挑战，最终以七秒之差排在第二。

    基米·安东内利利用了汉密尔顿和迈凯轮的兰多·诺里斯在后期展开的激烈对抗的机会，在后者后期的轮胎磨损严重的情况下，获得了第三名，并在车手积分榜上以102分的优势领先乔治·拉塞尔。

    Singapore GP: Max Verstappen wins event for first time in F1 career ahead of Charles Leclerc as Lewis Hamilton apologises for strategy

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen dominated the Singapore Grand Prix with a lights-to-flag victory; Charles Leclerc was second and Kimi Antonelli third; Lewis Hamilton apologised for a messy race strategically that saw him finish down in sixth

    Max Verstappen wins the Singapore Grand Prix for the first time

    Max Verstappen won the Singapore Grand Prix for the first time in his Formula 1 career with a dominant victory, while Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton endured a messy strategic race to tumble off the podium late on.

    Verstappen led every lap on his way to a second consecutive win despite being unable to communicate with the team due to a radio problem, but it did not affect him as he held off an early challenge from Ferrari's Charles Leclerc who eventually finished seven seconds behind in second.

    Kimi Antonelli took advantage of an elbows-out battle late on between Hamilton and McLaren's Lando Norris, with the seven-time world champion on old tyres in the latter stages, to take third and extend his lead over George Russell to 102 points in the Drivers' Championship.
