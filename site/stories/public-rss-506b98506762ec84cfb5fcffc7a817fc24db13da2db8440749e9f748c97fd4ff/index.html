    Azerbaijan GP: Max Verstappen fastest in challenging final practice to usurp George Russell and Mercedes ahead of Qualifying

    Max Verstappen's pace suggests he could vie for Red Bull's first pole position of 2026; George Russell second, Lewis Hamilton third; watch Qualifying later on Friday at 1pm with the Azerbaijan Grand Prix itself on Saturday at 12pm, live on Sky Sports F1 and Main Event

    George Russell narrowly avoids hitting wall during the final practice session at the Azerbaijan GP.

    Max Verstappen denied George Russell a clean sweep of fastest practice times as Red Bull led a renewed challenge to the Mercedes drivers ahead of Qualifying later on Friday at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    The arrival of more characteristic gusty conditions on the streets of Baku for final practice increased the challenge around the wall-lined circuit, with numerous drivers running off track after errors throughout the hour.

    Mercedes' dominance from Thursday's calmer running initially continued, but Verstappen found increasing pace on the soft tyres and eventually usurped Russell at the top of the timesheet with a best lap of 1:43.922.

    Russell, who had a near-miss with the barriers at Turn Seven after running straight on, finished 0.099s behind in second.

    Ferrari had been well behind the leading pace for most of the session but Lewis Hamilton moved into third with his final lap to finish only 0.111s adrift of Verstappen.

    Like his Red Bull rival earlier in the session, Hamilton's final-lap time was boosted by a tow from other cars down Baku's mammoth pit straight, giving him extra straight-line speed.

    "The weather has brought us the possibility of an absolutely fascinating and dramatic qualifying to come," said Sky Sports F1's David Croft.

    Kimi Antonelli, who leads Russell by 81 points in the championship, was fourth fastest ahead of the second Ferrari of Charles Leclerc. Both drivers were among those to have off-course moments.

    Three weeks on from the stunning lap that took him to a maiden F1 pole at similarly high-speed Monza, Pierre Gasly put his Alpine up in sixth to outpace the McLarens.
