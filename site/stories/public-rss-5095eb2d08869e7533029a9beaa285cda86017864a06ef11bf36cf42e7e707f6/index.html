    诺里斯盛赞安东内利：他让所有人显得可笑

    2026年F1新科世界冠军安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛以第三名完赛加冕，卫冕冠军诺里斯盛赞他让所有人显得可笑，并认为他完全配得上这份成功。

    据即将卸任的世界冠军兰多·诺里斯所说，2026年F1新科世界冠军基米·安东内利“让所有人显得可笑”。

    安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛以第三名完赛，从而成为2026年世界冠军，他的夺冠庆祝画面在车手们进行赛后媒体采访的地点旁的屏幕上播放。

    他的F1对手们纷纷送上赞誉，并对他今年所取得的成就表达了真诚的欣赏。

    “他所做到的，令人难以置信；他让所有人震惊。我希望每个人都给予他今年成就足够的认可，”诺里斯说。

    “他让其他所有人都显得很愚蠢。他开得那么好，而且他能与赛车完美契合。他努力去理解如何驾驶这辆赛车，并且比任何人都更好地发挥了它的潜力。他配得上每一份成功。他配得上他现在所做的这些时刻和庆祝。”

    “更令人印象深刻的是，这仅仅是他参加一级方程式的第二个赛季。他才20岁。这太不可思议了。我为他感到非常高兴。我很高兴看到我的一号车手位置转移给他，因为他配得上。从某种意义上说，我很高兴看到它易主。”

    诺里斯补充道：“他有一个非常强大的队友[乔治·拉塞尔]，但他从头到尾都击败了他，整个赛季都是如此。”

    “[你可以]数数他本赛季犯了多少错误——老实说，我觉得你甚至用一只手[五根手指]都数不完。他从发车区后方起步。他从前方获胜。他做了他需要做的一切。他每一场比赛都在那里。这是本赛季其他任何车手都无法说自己做到的事情。”

    “他的稳定性，他的表现。他把所有人都打败了。把和他开同一辆车的人也打败了。这很难做到。这非常令人印象深刻。”

    完美的领奖台？

    弗朗哥·科拉平托正在回顾自己的比赛时，安东内利正站在领奖台上，身旁是他在梅赛德斯取代的车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿，以及他长期以来被比较的对象马克斯·维斯塔潘。

    “他就在那里，在刘易斯和马克斯之间，”科拉平托说。“没有比这更好的了——世界冠军在F1近年两位最佳冠军之间。”

    “他配得上这个冠军；他非常年轻，所以需要享受这一刻。这是我们所有人的梦想，他今年所经历的一切正是如此。

    “速度方面，我们早就知道他有这个能力，但我认为他今年展现出的稳定性——赢下比赛、登上领奖台、拿到积分并且不犯错误——令人印象极其深刻。

    “几乎从赛季初到赛季末都背负着积分榜第一的重担，哇，他处理得非常好。

    “为他感到非常高兴，他是世界冠军，所以他是当之无愧的冠军。”

    汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘都迅速向安东内利致敬，在围场封闭区拥抱了他，维斯塔潘说他“为他感到非常骄傲”，而汉密尔顿说他“非常感激能在这场比赛中见证”F1史上最年轻冠军的“相当非凡”加冕。

    安东内利已经赢下九场比赛，今年在他完赛的大奖赛中仅有一次未能登上领奖台。

    “他的稳定性，”当《The Race》问及安东内利本赛季最令人印象深刻的部分时，加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托说道。

    “我在F2和F3为冠军而战过，我知道你在争夺时有多紧张，但想象一下在F1锦标赛中与拉塞尔、马克斯、刘易斯以及所有这些世界冠军争夺。那么多经验，而这家伙没有崩溃。”

    “他总是保持稳定，砰，位居榜首。我的意思是，他[只]有一两场糟糕的比赛，你可以说他没有速度或者撞车了。

    “所以，他的表现令人印象深刻，我希望未来能和他一起争夺冠军，因为那将是一个美好的故事，一个我从10岁、11岁就认识的人，进入F1，现在成为冠军。我正在努力达到那个[目标]，希望有一天能实现。”

    安东内利在第二个赛季就拥有一辆有争冠实力的赛车，这令人羡慕——他的对手们会渴望如此——但他们中没有人怀疑安东内利已经最大化了这个机会。

    “完全应得的，”卡洛斯·塞恩斯说。“老实说，他度过了一个非常非常好的年份。他知道如何在F1的第二个赛季最大化成为世界冠军的机会。”

    “我们很多人都会希望在进入一级方程式的头几年就拥有如此有竞争力的赛车，而他恰好拥有这样的赛车。他获得了赢得世界冠军的机会，而在机会降临的那一年，他把一切都发挥到了极致。

    “他在上半年表现完美，占据统治地位，随后在下半年控制住了局面，这正是世界冠军必须做到的，我认为他真的做得非常出色。”

    塞恩斯的威廉姆斯队友阿尔本开玩笑说：“进入这一年时，我不确定他的夺冠赔率是多少”，并补充道：“这真的令人印象深刻。

    “还有他的心态。至少从外界来看，他显得非常放松。

    “我在排位赛中听到一些评论，说他有点紧张。除了墨西哥站的排位赛之外，他的表现一直很好。”

    “要说的话，他的正赛速度确实非常令人印象深刻。这正是今年他与其他车手拉开差距的地方。”

    “当然，这大概也有一部分是赛车的原因，但看到他的表现确实非常令人印象深刻。”

    英文原文

    Formula 1’s new world champion Kimi Antonelli has “made everyone look silly” in 2026, according to outgoing champion Lando Norris.

    Antonelli became the 2026 champion by finishing third in the Mexican Grand Prix and his title celebrations were playing out on screens next to where drivers were conducting their post-race media debriefs.

    There was plenty of praise from his F1 rivals and genuine appreciation for what he’s been able to achieve this year.

    “Unbelievable, what he’s done; he’s blown everyone away. I hope everyone gives him enough credit for what he’s achieved this year,” Norris said.

    “He’s made everyone else look silly. He’s driven that well, and he’s been able to click with his car. He’s worked hard to understand how to drive this car, and he’s exploited it better than anyone else. He deserves every single bit of success. He deserves these moments he’s doing now and celebrating.

    “It’s even more impressive that it’s only his second season in Formula 1. He’s 20 years old. It’s incredible. I’m very happy for him. I’m very happy to see my number one go over to him, because he deserves it. I’m, in a way, happy to see it go.”

    Norris added: “He has a very strong team-mate [George Russell], but he’s beaten him the whole time, the whole season.

    “[You can] count how many mistakes he’s made this season - I don’t even think you could put it on [all five fingers of] one hand, honestly. He’s come from the back of the grid. He’s won from the front. He’s done everything he needs to do. He’s been there every single race. That’s something that no other driver can say they’ve done this season.

    “His consistency, his performance. He’s smashed everyone. Smashed the guy in the same car as him. That’s difficult to do. That’s been very impressive.”

    The perfect podium?

    Franco Colapinto was debriefing his race when Antonelli was standing on the podium alongside the driver he replaced at Mercedes, Lewis Hamilton, and the driver he’s long been compared to, Max Verstappen.

    “There he is, between Lewis and Max,” Colapinto said. “It can’t get any better than that - the world champion between the two best champions in the last years of F1.

    “He deserves it; he’s extremely young, so he needs to enjoy it. It’s the dream of all of us, what he had this year.

    “The speed, we all knew that he had it, but I think the consistency he showed this year, winning the races, being on the podium and scoring the points and not making mistakes, has been extremely impressive.

    “To have that weight on your shoulders of being P1 in the championship for so long, from almost the start to the end - it’s wow, [he dealt with it] so very well.

    “Very happy for him and he’s a world champion, so he’s a deserved champion.”

    Hamilton and Verstappen were both quick to pay tribute to Antonelli, embracing him in parc ferme, with Verstappen saying he was “very proud of him” while Hamilton said he was “so grateful to be in the race to witness” the “pretty phenomenal” crowning of F1’s youngest-ever champion.

    Antonelli has won nine races and has only been off the podium in one grand prix he’s finished this year.

    “His consistency,” Gabriel Bortoleto said when The Race asked what’s been the most impressive part of Antonelli’s season.

    “I fought for championships in F2 and F3, and I know how nervous you are when you are fighting, but imagine fighting for a Formula 1 championship against Russell, Max, Lewis, all these world champions. So much experience, and the guy didn’t break.

    “He was always there, consistent, boom, on top. I mean, he [only] had one or two bad races where you can say he was out of pace or he crashed.

    “So, impressive job from him and I hope to be fighting for titles with him in the future because that would be a beautiful story, a guy I’ve known since I was, like, 10 years old, 11 years old, to F1 and now he’s champion. I’m trying to reach that [goal] so hopefully one day.”

    Antonelli’s been in an enviable position of having a title-challenging car in his second season - his rivals would relish that - but there’s zero doubt among them that Antonelli has maximised the opportunity.

    “Completely deserved,” Carlos Sainz said. “Honestly, he's done a really, really good year. He's known how to maximise his chance of becoming world champion in his second year in Formula 1.

    “A lot of us would have wished to have such a competitive car in our first years in Formula 1, and he had exactly that. He was given the opportunity to win a championship, and the year that it was given it to him, he maximised everything.

    “He did a perfect first half of the year, dominated and then controlled the second part of the year, which is what a world champion has to do, and I think he did it really, really well.”

    Sainz's Williams team-mate Alex Albon joked that “coming into the year, I'm not sure what his betting odds were”, adding: “It's really impressive.

    “Also his mentality. It seemed, at least from the outside, that he looked very relaxed.

    “I heard some comments in qualifying that he was a bit tense. Apart from [Mexico] qualifying, he's been good.

    “If anything, his race pace has been really impressive. That's what's separated him from the rest of the grid this year.

    “Of course, it's probably partly car as well, but he's been really impressive to see.”
