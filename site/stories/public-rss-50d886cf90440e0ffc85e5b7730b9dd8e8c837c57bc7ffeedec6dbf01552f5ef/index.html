    汉密尔顿巴西大奖赛帽子揭晓，发售日期确认

    法拉利揭晓了汉密尔顿将在巴西大奖赛上佩戴的巴西主题帽子，并确认了发售日期。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿的巴西大奖赛帽子揭幕，发布日期确认

    法拉利为刘易斯·汉密尔顿揭幕了一款特别的巴西主题帽子，他将佩戴这款帽子参加2026年的巴西大奖赛

    法拉利揭幕了一款巴西主题帽子，刘易斯·汉密尔顿将在巴西大奖赛中佩戴，粉丝们可以在比赛前几天购买自己的帽子。

    Maranello车队与Puma在10月16日通过Instagram联合发布了这款帽子。"莱维斯将在Interlagos赛道上戴上带着一小块巴西元素的帽子。而且它很快也将属于你。将于11月3日开始发售，"车队在展示七届冠军戴着新设计的帽子的照片时写道。

    这款帽子以大胆的黄色为主题，细节处有磨损效果，露出下面的绿色，反映了巴西国旗的颜色。法拉利标志以银色突出地放置在前面，与银色的Puma标志相匹配，缝制在帽檐右侧。

    "给我来一顶吧，莱维斯先生，"一位粉丝评论道，另一位粉丝补充说："我甚至不是法拉利的粉丝，但这真的很酷。我也想戴。"

    其他人质疑这款帽子是否是法拉利车手将在Interlagos赛事中引入类似特殊头盔的信号。

    汉密尔顿于2022年11月正式成为巴西的荣誉公民。

    "我想对在这里的每一个人说声大大的谢谢，对于那些为了让这一天成为可能而搬山填海的人，" 41岁的汉密尔顿当时说。"这对我来说真的是一种巨大的荣誉，能够在这里接受和接受这个公民身份。我真的感觉现在我是你们中的一员，终于。

    "当我2007年第一次来到这里[汉密尔顿的第一个F1赛季]，这里的爱是如何增长的，我们共同的经历，尤其是2021年[他向胜利发起冲击]，知道你们一直在我身后为我加油，这是我一生中最特别的时刻之一。我迫不及待地想要继续努力向前。

    "我真的很想将今天，这个荣誉，献给埃尔顿·塞纳——献给埃尔顿的家庭，他的朋友和他的粉丝。对我来说，当我五岁的时候，我第一次看到埃尔顿赛车，那就是我知道我想要成为世界冠军，就像他一样。

    “那时我才知道并了解了巴西。那时我开始以巴西队的身份玩FIFA！但实际上，通过他的眼睛，我看到了巴西人有多么热情，这种热爱和激情有多深，你对国家的热爱有多么美好。我非常兴奋能在这里多待一些时间。”

    Lewis Hamilton's Brazilian GP cap unveiled with release date confirmed

    Ferrari has unveiled a special Brazil-themed cap for Lewis Hamilton to wear at the 2026 Brazilian Grand Prix

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari has revealed a Brazil-themed cap that Lewis Hamilton will wear at the Brazilian Grand Prix, and fans will be able to buy their own a few days before the race.

    The Maranello outfit announced the cap in a joint Instagram post with Puma on 16 October. "Lewis will be wearing a small piece of Brazil with him at Interlagos. And it can also be yours soon. Available to buy on November 3rd," the team wrote alongside photos of the seven-time champion wearing the new design.

    The cap features a bold yellow theme with distressed detailing that reveals green underneath, reflecting the colours of the Brazilian flag. The Ferrari logo is prominently placed on the front in silver, with a matching silver Puma logo embroidered on the front right of the bill.

    "Give me one right now, Sir Lewis Hamilton," one fan commented, while another added: "I'm not even a Ferrari fan, but that is slick. I'd wear it."

    Others questioned whether the cap could be a sign that the Ferrari driver will introduce a similar special helmet for the race at Interlagos.

    Hamilton officially became an honorary citizen of Brazil in November 2022.

    "I’d like to say a big, big thank you to everyone here at the house, for all those who’ve moved mountains to make this day possible," the 41-year-old said at the time. "It is honestly the greatest honour for me to be here receiving and accepting this citizenship. I really do feel like now I’m one of you, finally.

    "When I arrived here in 2007 [Hamilton’s first F1 season], the love that’s grown, the experiences we’ve had and, particularly 2021 [his charge to victory], knowing you guys were cheering me on the way, it was one of the most special moments of my entire life. I can’t wait for us to continue to strive ahead.

    "I really want to dedicate today, this honour, to Ayrton Senna – to Ayrton’s family, to his friends and to his fans. For me, when I was five years old, I saw Ayrton race for the first time, and that was the moment I knew that I wanted to be a world champion, just like him.

    "It was then that I knew and learnt about Brazil. It was then that I started to play FIFA as Brazil! But really, through his eyes, I was able to see how passionate Brazilians were, and how deep it goes, the love and passion you have for your country, and how beautiful it is. I’m so excited to spend some more time here."

    The 2026 Brazilian Grand Prix is scheduled for 6-8 November and will round out a triple-header starting with the United States Grand Prix from 23-25 October followed by the Mexican Grand Prix from 30 October to 1 November.
