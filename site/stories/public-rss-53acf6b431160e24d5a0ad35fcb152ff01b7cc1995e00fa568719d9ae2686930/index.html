    天空体育的克雷格·斯莱特回顾了克里斯蒂安·霍纳担任红牛首席执行官兼车队领队的20年历程。

    “我不会说这对现任者来说很可怕，但你必须时刻保持警惕。”

    Asked after Sunday's Azerbaijan Grand Prix, in which his drivers finished fourth and sixth, whether the external noise around him and the team recently was affecting them, Vasseur conceded it was frustrating.

    "We are not working in perfect serenity," said Vasseur.

    "For me, first, but for everybody in the team, for the media, for the 50 per cent of the questions about this. I think it's a bit of a frustrating situation.

    "We are fighting for P2 in the championship. At the end of the day, we are all speaking a lot about this, and even for the guys in the garage to discuss about this, it's not the best discussion that we can have.

    "But it is what it is, it's not in my hands."

    Christian Horner spoke to Craig Slater when he returned to the paddock at this year's British Grand Prix.

    Referencing his former Red Bull counterpart, Vasseur added: "Mr Horner is looking for a job. Everybody knows, everywhere.

    "What can I do? I just try to do my best to push the team, to ask the team to be motivated, focus on what is better today, and not to spend time with gossips.

    "In each interview, it will be 25 times a day."

    "Christian Horner is someone you have to take seriously and under consideration because he built up the winning Red Bull team," the 1997 world champion told Sky Sports News in Baku.

    "He has a lot of very good and positive baggage, full of successes so you have to take that into consideration.
