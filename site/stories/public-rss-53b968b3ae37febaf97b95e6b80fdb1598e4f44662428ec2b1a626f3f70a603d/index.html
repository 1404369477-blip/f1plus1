    安东内利坦言夺冠后过于自信，付出失误代价

    安东内利承认在锁定F1年度冠军后自己“过于自信”，并因此犯下至少一次在争冠压力下不会出现的代价高昂的失误。自一个月前在墨西哥夺冠以来，他已连续两场比赛未能登上领奖台。

    安东内利承认，在锁定F1年度冠军后自己“过于自信”，并因此犯下至少一次在争冠压力下不会出现的代价高昂的失误。

    自一个月前在墨西哥夺冠以来，安东内利因两次不同的失误连续两场比赛未能登上领奖台；这是他在 otherwise 出色的2026赛季中首次连续两场无缘领奖台。

    安东内利此前表示，在奥斯汀站他驾驶得异常紧张，因为他正处于夺冠边缘，而那个周末他还在病后恢复，这也对他不利。而那个周日，他仍从令人失望的排位赛中恢复过来并夺冠。

    但在卡塔尔站，他透露夺冠也影响了他的驾驶，他认为这导致了他在巴西站的失误——当时他在领先时受到诺里斯压力后打滑退赛。

    安东内利表示：“我一直很享受，但与此同时也没有那么享受，因为显然过去的几场比赛并不顺利，尽管我认为速度是有的。”

    “知道冠军争夺已经结束，感觉有点奇怪，当然比赛时压力会小一些，但感觉并不完全一样。”

    “所以我完全没有抱怨；我很高兴能处在这个位置。但确实，当你提前赢得冠军后，很容易在回到车里时降低一点强度和专注度。”

    “这就是为什么我觉得，尤其是在巴西，如果我当时还在为冠军而战，比赛中犯的错误可能就不会发生。但当时我过于自信，同时那一刻也缺乏一点专注，然后错误就发生了。”

    所以我觉得，在压力之下我确实能够更加专注，但这种情况对我来说也是全新的，所以我一直在努力从中学习，希望这两场比赛我能以非常高的[注]完赛。

    安东内利并非第一位在夺冠后遭遇“冠军后遗症”的车手。他的梅赛德斯前任刘易斯·汉密尔顿在2015年强势锁定总冠军后，接下来三场比赛的胜利全部被梅赛德斯队友尼科·罗斯伯格夺走。

    两年后，他在击败塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔和法拉利提前锁定总冠军后，在巴西站遭遇了一次不符合他水准的排位赛撞车。

    迈克尔·舒马赫在2004年最具统治力的总冠军征程收官阶段尤为混乱，在中国站的排位赛和正赛中均发生打转，还遭遇了爆胎并与克里斯蒂安·克莱恩相撞。

    他在英特拉格斯排位赛中撞车，又在蒙扎开场圈打转，把胜利拱手让给了整个赛季都被他压制的对手们。

    安东内利在与马克斯·维斯塔潘争夺胜利的战斗中掉队，最终在拉斯维加斯获得第四名——他战术性地收油以留在维斯塔潘身后获取超车模式，结果却触发了极端的能量回收，导致他后来在直道上电池耗尽。

    “我们进行了分析，并从中得出结论，在这方面我们应该让系统更加稳健，”安东内利在卡塔尔解释道。

    “这一切都始于5号弯，因为在5号弯我也收了一次油，基本上收油让系统回收了大量能量，所以基本上第二次收油时我的回收额度已经归零，因此我在减速弯无法回收任何能量，这就是为什么我最后电池没电了。”

    "车队已经在着手处理这个问题，试图让系统在这类场景下更加稳健，并且告诉动力单元‘看，如果我要在这里抬油门，那就什么都别做’。

    "我没有深入了解细节，因为非常复杂，但据他们告诉我，他们加了一个安全功能，应该不会再让那种情况发生了。

    "所以系统应该会稳健得多，那应该能让我正常比赛，因为归根结底我想做的就是留在后面保住超车机会，并在直道上获得尾流，从而给自己创造最好的机会。

    "但因为那两次抬油门，就足以触发系统，把我置于非常不利的位置。"

    队友乔治·拉塞尔因为对安东内利的一次超车失败而失去了获胜机会，当时他正试图跟上领跑者维斯塔潘。

    他在卡塔尔承认：“我现在的风险指数可能有点太高了，因为我就是想要去争取这些大成绩，在赛季末段留下一些高光时刻。”

    “如果那是赛季第一场比赛，我绝对不会在还剩两圈时去尝试那个动作。但赛季还剩两场比赛，我有点想去拼一下。”

    他解释说，他原本把5号弯入口处的一个凸起当作那个弯道刹车点的参照物，但关键的是，当他走到弯道内侧时，就失去了那个参照物。

    拉塞尔解释道：“我会撞到颠簸处然后刹车，接着我切入那个弯道的内侧，那里没有颠簸。当我刹车时，我心想：‘天哪，我刚刚刹车太晚了。’”

    “事后看来总是很容易，但那次事故完全是我的责任。”

    英文原文

    Kimi Antonelli admitted he was "overconfident" after sealing his Formula 1 title, and has since made at least one costly error he wouldn't have made had he still been in a championship fight.

    Antonelli hasn't been on the podium since he won the title in Mexico a month ago due to two different errors; the first time he's gone two consecutive races without a podium in an otherwise sublime 2026.

    Antonelli previously said that at Austin he was driving unusually tense because he was on the cusp of the title, not helped by the fact he was also recovering from an illness that weekend. And on that Sunday he still managed to recover from a disappointing qualifying to win.

    But in Qatar, he revealed that winning the title has also had an impact on his driving, believing it contributed to the error he made in Brazil, where he spun out of the race lead while under pressure from Lando Norris.

    "I've been enjoying but I've not been enjoying at the same time, because obviously the past races haven't gone well, I would say, despite having the pace," Antonelli said.

    "It kind of feels a little bit weird knowing that the championship is already over, and of course you race with less pressure, but it's not quite the same.

    "So I'm not complaining at all; I'm happy to be in this position. But definitely when you win the championship in advance, it's really easy to drop a bit of intensity and a bit of focus as well, then when you go back in the car.

    "And that's why I feel like, mainly in Brazil, the mistake that I did in the race probably wouldn't have happened if I was still fighting for the championship. But there I was overconfident, but as well I had a bit of a lack of focus in the moment and then the mistake happened.

    "So I feel like definitely under pressure I'm able to have a higher focus, but also it's something new for me, this scenario, so I've been trying to learn from it, and hopefully these two races I can finish on a very high [note]."

    Antonelli isn't the first champion to suffer a post-title hangover. His Mercedes predecessor, Lewis Hamilton, was beaten to victory in all three races by Mercedes team-mate Nico Rosberg after his dominant 2015 title victory was sealed.

    Two years later, he had an uncharacteristic qualifying crash in Brazil after beating Sebastian Vettel and Ferrari to an early crown.

    Michael Schumacher had a particularly messy end to his most dominant title victory in 2004, spinning in both qualifying and the race in China, as well as picking up a puncture and colliding with Christian Klien.

    He crashed in qualifying at Interlagos and spun at Monza on the opening lap, leaving victories on the table for rivals he'd crushed all season.

    Antonelli dropped out of a fight with Max Verstappen for the win and finished fourth in Las Vegas after he tactically lifted to stay behind Verstappen to get overtake mode, only for that to trigger extreme harvesting that later left him with no battery on the straight.

    "We analysed and we came away [from] that [and concluded] on that regard we should make the system a bit more robust," Antonelli explained in Qatar.

    "It all started from Turn 5 because also in Turn 5 I had a lift and basically by lifting the system harvested loads of energy, so basically by doing the second lift I ended up with zero harvest left so I couldn't harvest any energy in the chicane and then that's why I ended up with no battery.

    "The team has been already working on it on trying to make the system more robust in those kinds of scenarios and in order to say to the power unit 'look if [I'm] going to lift at this point just don't do anything'.

    "I didn't get into details because it's very complicated, but from what they told me, they put a safety feature that shouldn't make that happen anymore.

    "So should be [that] the system should be much more robust, and that should allow me to race normally, because at the end what I was trying to do is staying behind to keep the overtake and have the tow for the straight in order to give myself the best opportunity.

    "But because of the two lifts, that was enough to trigger the system and put me in a very unfavourable position."

    Team-mate George Russell lost his victory chance because of a failed overtake on Antonelli, while trying to keep touch with race leader Verstappen.

    He admitted in Qatar "My risk dial is probably a bit too high at the moment because I'm just wanting to go for these big results and get some highlights on the board towards the end of the season.

    "If that was race one of the season, there's no way I would have gone for that move with two laps remaining. But two races to go in the season, I kind of wanted to go for it."

    He explained he was using a bump on the entry to Turn 5 as a reference for his braking point for that corner, but he crucially lost that when he went to the inside of the corner.

    "I'd hit the bump and then I would brake, and I went on the inside of that corner, and there was no bump. When I braked, I was like, "Jeez, I've just braked way too late here," Russell explained.

    "It's always easy in hindsight, but that incident was all on me."
