    Max Verstappen goes fastest in the Sao Paulo Grand Prix final practice

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen was fastest in final practice at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix but will serve grid penalties along with Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc.

    Verstappen pipped new Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli by 0.025s but will start at the back of the grid for a complete power unit change, while Hamilton and Leclerc have 10-grid place penalties for exceeding their internal combustion engine allocation.

    Mercedes had already announced on Friday that George Russell will have a five-place penalty for a new internal combustion engine, the second time he has exceeded that part's limit, while the Ferrari drivers are being penalised for the first time, so have a bigger penalty.
