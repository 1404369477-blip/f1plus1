    维斯塔潘与拉塞尔争执升级，称2026赛季“彻底灾难”

    维斯塔潘与拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛第三节自由练习中的冲突在媒体上进一步升级，维斯塔潘称2026赛季是“彻底灾难”。

    维斯塔潘与拉塞尔的争执持续发酵，他称2026赛季是“彻底灾难”

    维斯塔潘表示他很享受看到拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯“每个弯角都被击败”。红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯补充说，两人的竞争不会分散车队注意力

    维斯塔潘与拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛第三节自由练习中的冲突，原本似乎以对这位红牛车手的一次警告告一段落，但这场争执此后在媒体上进一步升级。

    拉塞尔在排位赛后对天空体育意大利表示，他不再愿意接受“有人这样算计我，还表现得比别人都高人一等”。

    维斯塔潘周五没有回应那些评论，但在赢得拉斯维加斯大奖赛之后他做出了回应。拉塞尔直行冲出赛道、眼看胜利希望破灭之后，维斯塔潘已经在车队无线电中表达了自己的喜悦，赛后他在围场里又更进一步。

    “我确实很享受今天某个人完全没有任何机会这件事，”他指的是拉塞尔。

    “他显然整场比赛每个弯角都在被击败，但这也是他应得的。不过，对我个人来说，这场比赛跟一级方程式毫无关系。”

    维斯塔潘用“每个弯角都在被击败”这句话，指的是他故意在拉斯维加斯大道上让自己被超越了好几次，以便给电池充电，随后又在1号弯入口或3号弯反击回来。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队；乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    这位四届世界冠军表示，他对那些动作感到满意，因为涉及的是拉塞尔，不过他补充说，梅赛德斯车手周五的言论并没有提供任何额外的动力。

    “不，你知道为什么吗？因为他远不及我的水平，所以那样的评论根本不会激励我。

    “但这就是他的典型作风：他在赛道外大放厥词，但在赛道上，整个赛季对他来说都是一场彻底的灾难，尤其是在那辆车里。所以这完全吻合，今天再次如此，这简直是完美的因果报应，”维斯塔潘对Viaplay说。

    “我就说到这里，因为我不需要再在他身上浪费时间了。”

    整件事多少让人想起两年前阿布扎比大奖赛前的媒体日，当时局面同样演变成激烈交锋，维斯塔潘称其对手为“背后捅刀的人”，还有其他一些说法。

    那也是拉塞尔说荷兰人一旦事情不顺他意就“带着不必要的愤怒和近乎暴力的方式发飙”的那一天。

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯在拉斯维加斯补充说，在他看来，这种对立既不会分散维斯塔潘的注意力，也不会分散整个位于米尔顿凯恩斯的车队的注意力。

    “我既没听到马克斯说了什么，显然也没听到托托说了什么。所以我不确定那是什么，但这对我们来说是一种干扰吗？今天在发车格上已经有人根据昨天说的那些话问过我了，但不，干扰为零。”

    “说实话，我们的做法是专注于比赛。我们可以自豪地说，我们不在乎周围的噪音。我们就是这么看待它的，周围的噪音。所以不，对我们来说零干扰。”

    Max Verstappen continues George Russell fallout, labels 2026 F1 season a "complete disaster"

    Verstappen says he enjoyed watching Russell get "beaten at every corner" in Las Vegas. Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies adds that the rivalry is not a distraction

    While the clash between Max Verstappen and George Russell during the third free practice session for the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix appeared to have been settled with a warning for the Red Bull driver, the dispute has since escalated in the media.

    Speaking to Sky Sports Italy after qualifying, Russell made it clear that he was no longer willing to accept "somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they’re bigger and better than everyone else."

    Verstappen did not respond to those comments on Friday, but he did so after winning the Las Vegas Grand Prix. After Russell went straight on and saw his chance of victory disappear, Verstappen had already expressed his delight over the team radio, and he went a step further in the paddock afterwards.

    "I did enjoy the fact that one particular person had absolutely no chance at all today," he said referring to Russell.

    "He was obviously getting beaten at every corner the whole time, but he deserves that too. But yeah, for me personally, this race had nothing to do with Formula 1."

    With the words "beaten at every corner", Verstappen was referring to the fact that he deliberately allowed himself to be overtaken several times on the Strip in order to recharge his battery, before fighting back either on the approach to Turn 1 or at Turn 3.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    The four-time world champion said he took satisfaction from those moves because Russell was involved, although he added that the Mercedes driver's comments on Friday had not provided any extra motivation.

    "No, and do you know why? Because he's nowhere near my level, so comments like that don't motivate me at all.

    "But this is typical of him: he has a big mouth off the track, but on the track this whole season has been a complete disaster for him, especially in that car. So it fits perfectly, and today again, it's just perfect karma," Verstappen told Viaplay.

    "I'll leave it at that, because I don't need to waste any more time on him."

    The whole affair is somewhat reminiscent of the media day ahead of the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix two years ago, when the situation also descended into a heated exchange and Verstappen called his rival a "backstabber", among other things.

    That was also the day Russell said the Dutchman "lashes out with unnecessary anger and borderline violence" whenever things are not going his way.

    Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies added in Las Vegas that, in his view, the rivalry is not a distraction, either for Verstappen or for the Milton Keynes-based team as a whole.

    "I didn't hear neither what Max said, nor what Toto said apparently. So I'm not sure what it is, but is it a distraction for us? Somebody already asked me this on the grid today based on whatever was said yesterday, but no, the distraction is zero.

    "Honestly, our approach is that we concentrate on the racing. We take some pride to say that we don't care about the noise around us. That's how we consider it, noise around us. So no, zero distraction for us."
