    F1与WHOOP合作展示车手健康数据

    F1与可穿戴设备公司WHOOP签署合作协议，部分车手将佩戴其健康追踪设备，相关数据将于2027年在电视转播中展示。

    F1与WHOOP合作展示车手健康数据

    F1已与WHOOP合作，以展示赛车运动对身体的体能要求

    WHOOP首席执行官威尔·艾哈迈德与F1总裁兼首席执行官斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利

    F1已与可穿戴设备公司WHOOP签署协议，部分车手将佩戴该公司的健康追踪设备，相关数据将于2027年在电视转播中展示。

    WHOOP 将成为 F1 的官方合作伙伴，根据协议，越来越多的车手将在车内佩戴该公司提供的科技手环。按照 F1 的计划，心率等数据随后将被纳入其叙事内容，从社交媒体上的图表到最早 2027 年可在电视直播中使用的叠加信息。

    F1 未透露下赛季将有多少车手使用这些可穿戴设备，据了解，相关协议正在逐案敲定中，但至少已有少数车手签约加入该计划。

    今年早些时候，WHOOP 与法拉利签署协议，让车队成员可以使用这些设备，在繁忙的赛季中监测他们的健康状况。这些可穿戴设备能够记录心率和心率变异性、睡眠质量以及最大摄氧量等指标。F1 希望该协议能更好地展示赛车运动对体能的要求。

    其公告写道：“通过这一开创性合作，利用 WHOOP 行业领先的可穿戴技术，并将其与这项运动屡获殊荣的转播相结合，车迷将更深入地了解他们在大奖赛周末所见证的关键时刻对身心提出的要求。

    “合作的一个基石将是开发新的转播叙事体验，精选洞察数据将以恰当方式呈现，并与车手协作，为车迷提供一扇实时窗口，了解赛车运动中的身体真实状况。这些创新将以前所未有的方式展现这项运动人性化的一面。”

    法拉利车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔已经在使用WHOOP的可穿戴设备。

    WHOOP首席执行官威尔·艾哈迈德补充道：“WHOOP正在为一级方程式带来一个全新的维度，揭示方向盘背后的人类表现。”

    “几十年来，车迷们对赛车有着惊人的洞察，但对比赛对车手的影响却了解较少。现在，我们首次能够展示车手在比赛最激烈时刻体内发生的变化。这将改变车迷体验一级方程式的方式，并最终加深我们对人类表现的理解。”

    F1首席执行官兼总裁斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利表示：“有机会挖掘新的洞察、讲述新的故事，并展示造就F1成为世界上最极端、最激动人心的运动之一的世界级竞技能力，这 truly 具有开创性。”

    “我迫不及待想看到这些洞察通过我们的转播变为现实，为车迷提供一种全新的方式来体验一级方程式，同时凸显在赛车运动最高水平竞争所需的非凡力量、韧性和决心。”

    F1 to showcase driver health data as it partners with WHOOP

    Formula 1 has partnered with WHOOP to showcase the physical demands of racing

    Will Ahmed, WHOOP CEO, and Stefano Domenicali, F! president and CEO

    Formula 1 has signed a deal with wearables company WHOOP which will see a number of drivers wear the company's health trackers and showcase their data on the TV broadcasts in 2027.

    WHOOP will become an Official Partner of F1 in a deal that will see an increasing number of drivers start wearing tech bracelets provided by the company while inside the car. Per F1's plans, that data such as heart rate, will then be fed to into its storytelling, ranging from graphics on social media to overlays that can be used during the live TV broadcast as early as 2027.

    F1 did not disclose how many drivers will use the wearables from next season, as it is understood to be in the process of finalising deals on a case-by-case basis, but at least a handful of drivers have already signed up to the programme.

    Earlier this year, WHOOP signed a deal with Ferrari, making the devices available to team members to monitor their wellbeing throughout a demanding season. The wearables are able to chart metrics like heart rate and heart rate variability, sleep quality, and VO2 Max. F1 hopes the deal will better be able to showcase the physical demands of racing.

    Its announcement read: "Through this groundbreaking partnership, utilising WHOOP's industry-leading wearable technology and combining it with the sport’s award-winning broadcast, fans will gain a deeper understanding into what the critical moments they witness over a grand prix weekend require both physically and mentally.

    "A cornerstone of the partnership will be the development of new broadcast storytelling experiences, featuring select insights delivered in the right way in collaboration with the drivers to provide fans with a real-time window into the physical realities of racing. These innovations will help showcase the human side of the sport in ways never seen before."

    Ferrari drivers Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc are already using WHOOP's wearables.

    WHOOP CEO Will Ahmed added: "WHOOP is bringing an entirely new dimension to Formula 1 by revealing the human performance behind the wheel.

    "For decades, fans have had incredible insight into the cars, but less insight into the impact that races have on the drivers. Now, for the first time, we can show what happens inside a driver's body during the most intense moments of a race. This will change how fans experience Formula 1 and ultimately deepen our understanding of human performance."

    F1 CEO and president Stefano Domenicali said: "The opportunity to uncover new insights, tell new stories and showcase the world-class athleticism that goes into making F1 one of the most extreme and exciting sports in the world, is truly groundbreaking.

    "I can't wait to see these insights come to life through our broadcasts, giving fans a completely new way to experience Formula 1 while highlighting the incredible strength, resilience, and determination required to compete at the highest level of motorsport."
