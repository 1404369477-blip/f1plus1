    瓦塞尔：法拉利尽管升级了引擎，仍缺乏纯粹的动力

    法拉利推出了他们本赛季第二次也是最后一次引擎升级，但在直道上仍然比梅赛德斯动力的赛车慢。

    直线速度一直是法拉利最大的弱点，而在新的多弯马德里赛道上这将不成问题，瓦塞尔表示他“没有指望”能弥补与梅赛德斯在动力单元上的差距。

    “当你缺乏纯粹的动力时，你就会开始研究能量输出策略，试图在直道上进行弥补，”他说。

    "但如果你在某些地方增加部署，肯定就会在其他地方减少部署。那么在战斗的时候，你就是在走向错误的螺旋，试图玩弄这个。"

    "老实说，这不是部署的问题。即使是迈凯伦，他们在周五和周六早上也稍微玩了一些手段。归根结底，我们缺的是纯粹的动力。"

    罗斯伯格表示，在这个赛季阶段，法拉利偏袒汉密尔顿是"不可行的"

    在今年的第一次额外研发与升级机会（ADUO）审查之后，法拉利被判落后于基准发动机制造商红牛超过百分之四。

    这意味着法拉利可以在今年进行两次引擎升级，并且在2027年也可以，届时这支车队（Scuderia）将拥有新引擎。

    “引擎的研发过程非常漫长。底盘的话，你可以从一天到另一天很快地完成升级并追回节奏，”瓦塞尔（Vasseur）说。

    “至于引擎，这个过程非常漫长，因为零部件的交付周期很长，而且成本上限也不容易应对——你必须为整个赛季生产引擎。”

    汉密尔顿表示车队要实施任何形式的“法拉利优先”都“太晚了”

    “而如果你想要开始进行研发，你就必须生产一批新的引擎。我们并不指望通过 ADUO 1 或 ADUO 2 来实现追赶。我们正在一小步一小步地前进。”

    “如果你在引擎上迈出一小步，请记住，我们身后（的对手）只相差百分之几秒。就排位而言，即使只多出几匹马力，也是在发车格上迈出的好一步。明年我们将迎来一台新引擎。当然，目标就是尽快追上（对手）。”

    Ferrari boss Frederic Vasseur says his team will head to this weekend's Spanish Grand Prix in Madrid with "all the energy" to bounce back from their "failure" at Monza.

    There were big expectations for Ferrari at their home event after Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc were quick in Friday practice, only to miss out on the front row in qualifying before a dismal race on Sunday.

    Hamilton dropped from fourth to 10th after going side by side with Leclerc at the first chicane, going through the gravel at Turn 2, which he was unhappy with.

    Leclerc crashed out the of the Italian Grand Prix

    Vasseur: Ferrari missing pure power despite engine upgrade

    Ferrari introduced their second and final engine upgrade of the season but were still slower than the Mercedes-powered cars on the straights.

    Straight-line speed has been Ferrari's biggest weakness, something which will not be an issue around the new twisty Madrid circuit, and Vasseur says he was "not expecting" to make up the power-unit deficit to Mercedes.

    "When you have a lack of pure power, then you start to play with deployment, to try to compensate in the straight line," he said.

    "But if you deploy more somewhere, for sure you will deploy less somewhere else. Then you are going in the wrong spiral to try to play with this when you are fighting.

    "Honestly, it's not about deployment. Even McLaren, they were playing a little bit Friday and Saturday morning. In the end, we are missing pure power."

    Rosberg says it is 'not feasible' for Ferrari to favour Hamilton at this stage of the season

    After the first Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities (ADUO) review earlier this year, Ferrari were deemed to be more than four per cent behind benchmark engine manufacturer Red Bull.

    It meant Ferrari could make two engine upgrades this year, and also for 2027, when the Scuderia will have a new engine.

    "The process of the engine is mega long. The chassis you can make an upgrade from one day to the other very quickly and come back into the pace," said Vasseur.

    "On the engine, the process is mega long because the lead time of the parts is long and the cost cap is not easy - you have to produce the engine for the season.

    Hamilton says the team are 'too late' to implement any sort of 'Ferrari priority'

    "And if you want to start to do the development, you have to produce a new batch of engines. We are not expecting to recover with ADUO 1 or ADUO 2. We are doing small steps.

    "If you make a small step on the engine, keep in mind that behind us, it was hundredths of a second. In terms of position, even if you make a couple of horsepower, it's a good step on the grid. Now we will have a new engine next year. For sure, the target is to catch up as soon as possible."
