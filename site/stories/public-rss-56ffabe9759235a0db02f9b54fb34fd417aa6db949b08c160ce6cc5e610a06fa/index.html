    阿斯顿·马丁宣布与美国大奖赛前的首创F1合作

    阿斯顿·马丁与Padeltek合作推出一系列品牌拍板和配件，以迎接即将在美国德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的美国大奖赛。

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    阿斯顿·马丁成为首个与拍板和壁球公司合作的一级方程式车队，与Padeltek合作推出一系列拍板，将于本周上市。

    银石车队宣布了与Padeltek的新合作，这项合作将在本周末在美国德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的美国大奖赛之前启动，届时车队和Padeltek将在一个全新的帕代尔场地Padel Society举办一场独家I / AM粉丝活动。

    阿斯顿·马丁Padeltek系列将包括三支帕代尔球拍和三个球袋。

    阿斯顿·马丁许可和商品部门负责人马特·查普曼解释说：“一级方程式赛车是一个跨越更广泛体育和生活方式领域的合作平台，我们正在看到粉丝对将世界融合在一起的需求不断增长。”

    “作为第一家正式与帕代尔和壁球公司合作的一级方程式车队，我们很高兴通过一个真实且性能驱动的实体产品来结合我们的品牌DNA。”

    Ron Saslow，Thirty-5 Capital的创始人和管理合伙人补充道：“壁球和一级方程式赛车很快就找到了彼此。这两种运动都奖励精准、速度和表现，并且都在美国吸引着越来越多的粉丝。”

    对于Padeltek来说，与阿斯顿·马丁阿美科一级方程式车队合作是一个将这种共同能量带到球场的机会，创造出的设备可以让赛车迷以新的方式与他们的车队建立联系，同时让更多人了解壁球。

    这项合作将在2027年继续扩大，计划推出壁球拍和配件。

    壁球在一级方程式车手中越来越受欢迎，许多车手现在都将它纳入了他们的训练计划。

    阿斯顿·马丁车队将参加10月23日至25日举行的美国大奖赛，目前以7分位列车手积分榜第10位。两届冠军车手费尔南多·阿隆索在车手积分榜上排名第17位，他赢得了车队所有的7分，而他的队友兰斯·斯特罗尔排名第21位，没有得分。

    Aston Martin announces first-of-its-kind F1 partnership ahead of US GP

    Aston Martin has partnered with Padeltek to launch a range of branded padel rackets and accessories ahead of the United States Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Aston Martin has become the first Formula 1 team to partner with a padel and pickleball company, teaming up with Padeltek for a range of rackets that will go on sale this week.

    The Silverstone outfit announced the new collaboration ahead of this weekend's United States Grand Prix in Austin, Texas, where the team and Padeltek will host an exclusive I / AM fan event at a brand-new padel venue, Padel Society.

    The Aston Martin Padeltek collection will include three padel rackets and three cover bags.

    "Formula 1 is a platform for collaboration across wider sport and lifestyle spaces, and we're continuing to see the growing demand from fans to bring the worlds together," Aston Martin head of licensing and merchandise Matt Chapman explained.

    "As the first Formula 1 team to formally partner with a padel and pickleball company, we're excited to combine our brand DNA via a tangible product that feels authentic and performance-driven."

    Ron Saslow, founding and managing partner of Thirty-5 Capital, added: "It didn't take long for padel and Formula 1 to find each other. Both sports reward precision, speed and performance, and both are attracting a growing fan base here in the United States.

    "For Padeltek, collaborating with the Aston Martin Aramco Formula 1 Team is an opportunity to bring that shared energy to the court, creating equipment that gives racing fans a new way to connect with their team while introducing more people to padel."

    The partnership will continue to expand in 2027, with pickleball rackets and accessories planned for release.

    Padel has become increasingly popular among F1 drivers, with many now incorporating it into their training routines.

    Aston Martin heads into the United States Grand Prix, which is scheduled for 23-25 October, 10th in the constructors' standings with seven points. Two-time champion Fernando Alonso is 17th in the drivers' championship, having earned all seven of the team's points, and his team-mate Lance Stroll is 21st with no points.
