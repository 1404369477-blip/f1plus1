    诺里斯在2025年赢得了他的首个车手总冠军，这是在与他的麦凯轮车队队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里以及红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘在赛季末的威胁中艰难战斗后取得的。

    “是七个。我不知道你怎么总结，”诺里斯说。"我的意思是，他能够长时间做到这一点，甚至到现在，看到他与查尔斯的竞争，这真的很令人难以置信。"

    “我认为查尔斯无疑是车阵中最出色的之一。他速度惊人，而且刘易斯仍然在给他很好的对抗，尤其是在今年。他似乎在争夺前茅的比赛中恢复到了正常状态，证明了他确实是那里最出色的之一。”

    The 2025年的冠军补充说，汉密尔顿的职业生涯对车阵中的许多人来说都是鼓舞人心的，但他解释说他不太可能继续赛车到40多岁。

    “他为了这么长时间地竞争而战，这是一项相当了不起的成就。他是我们许多人的好榜样。我当然不认为我会走那么远，但这是我职业生涯中值得期待的好事。"

    “我知道我对赢得一个冠军感到满意，但当我看到刘易斯时，他让我对只赢得一个冠军感到不太满意。”

    “但这是好事，我可以看着他，看到他这个年纪的状态，这让我比以往任何时候都更加相信我可以再战斗很多很多年，这是我期待做的事情。”

    Lando Norris reveals how Lewis Hamilton has changed his F1 championship ambitions

    Lando Norris has admitted that Lewis Hamilton's seven F1 world titles have motivated him to chase more championships after securing his maiden title in 2025

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Lando Norris, McLaren

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Lando Norris says Lewis Hamilton's seven Formula 1 world titles have left him dissatisfied with winning just one.

    Norris won his maiden championship in 2025 after a hard-fought battle against his McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri and a late-season threat from Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

    Hamilton will make his 400th grand prix start this weekend in Brazil and Norris was asked to sum up his fellow countryman's career while speaking to the media at Interlagos.

    "It’s seven. So, I don’t know how you want to sum it up," Norris said. "I mean, it’s pretty unbelievable what he’s been able to do for such a long time, even to now, to see him fighting against Charles.

    "I think Charles is easily one of the best on the grid. Insanely quick, and the fact that Lewis is still giving him a good fight, especially this year. He seemed to kind of look like he’s back to normal in terms of fighting at the front and proving that he’s one of the best out there."

    The 2025 champion added that Hamilton's career is inspirational for many on the grid, but explained he was unlikely to continue racing into his 40s.

    "It’s a pretty incredible achievement for him to fight for this long. He’s a good inspiration for many of us. I certainly don’t think I’m going that far, but it’s a good thing to look forward to in terms of my career.

    "I know I was happy with one championship, but when I look at Lewis, he makes me not very happy to have only won one championship.

    "But that’s in a good way, that I can look at him and see him at his age, in his form, and it makes me believe more than ever that I can fight for many, many more years, and that’s something I look forward to doing."
