    阿尔派双车赛后受罚 平川亮F1首秀得分

    加斯利和科拉平托因在墨西哥大奖赛黄旗下未减速被赛后处罚，双双跌出积分区，哈斯车手平川亮因此在F1首秀中收获积分。

    阿尔派车手赛后受罚，平川亮在墨西哥大奖赛F1首秀中得分

    阿尔派车手加斯利和科拉平托双双在赛后受到处罚并跌出积分区，哈斯车手平川亮因此在墨西哥大奖赛的F1首秀中收获积分

    皮埃尔·加斯利，阿尔派车队；弗兰科·科拉平托，阿尔派车队

    皮埃尔·加斯利和弗兰科·科拉平托均被处以赛后处罚，跌出积分区，哈斯新秀平川亮因此在墨西哥大奖赛的F1首秀中收获积分。

    两位阿尔派车手在墨西哥城正赛后都因在黄旗状态下未减速而面临国际汽联干事的调查。在临时成绩中，科拉平托以第八名完赛，而他的队友加斯利获得第十名。

    加斯利因在双黄旗挥动下未充分减速而被处以停站再出发处罚——该处罚在赛后转换为30秒罚时——而科拉平托则在赛后因在单黄旗挥动下未充分减速而被处以10秒罚时。

    这位阿根廷车手曾面临与加斯利相同的处罚风险，但根据国际汽联干事报告，他在双黄旗路段被认定无违规，因为“有充分理由怀疑该车手是否获得了足够的警告以遵守要求，因此未认定该路段存在任何违规行为”。

    在墨西哥大奖赛最终成绩中，加斯利被降至第17位，科拉平托被降至第15位。

    再加上迈凯伦车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里因与阿尔维德·林德布拉德发生碰撞而在赛后受到10秒罚时，这三位车手因此跌出了积分区。

    利亚姆·劳森升至第七位，这一位置原本属于皮亚斯特里；而奥迪车手加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托则从积分区外直接跃升至第八位。

    红牛车手伊萨克·哈贾尔也出现同样情况，从第12位升至第九位；而F1新秀平川亮则为哈斯车队拿下第十名，完成积分区的收尾。

    这位日本车手是在周五晚间美国车队因未说明的原因决定让埃斯特班·奥康坐冷板凳后临时被征召的，这给了平川亮完成F1正赛首秀的机会。

    Alpine drivers penalised post-race, Ryo Hirakawa scores on F1 debut at Mexico GP

    Alpine pair Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto have picked up post-race penalties and drop out of the points, resulting in Ryo Hirakawa scoring on his F1 debut at the Mexico GP

    Pierre Gasly, Alpine, Franco Colapinto, Alpine

    Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto have both been given post-race penalties which have dropped them out of the points and resulted in Haas debutant Ryo Hirakawa scoring on his Formula 1 debut at the Mexico Grand Prix.

    Both Alpine drivers faced post-race investigations with the FIA stewards for failing to slow under yellow flags during the Mexico City race. In the provisional results Colapinto finished in eighth place while his team-mate Gasly took 10th.

    Gasly was given a stop-and-go penalty for failing to slow sufficiently under double waved yellows – which was converted into a 30-second penalty post-race – while Colapinto was given a 10s penalty after the race, having failed to slow sufficiently under single waved yellows.

    The Argentinean driver was at risk of the same punishment as Gasly, but was cleared of wrongdoing in a double yellow sector because “there was sufficient doubt that the driver had sufficient warning in order to comply with the requirements, so did not determine that there was any breach in relation to that sector,” according to the FIA stewards report.

    In the final Mexico GP classification it dropped Gasly down to 17th place with Colapinto demoted to 15th place.

    Coupled with a post-race 10s penalty for McLaren’s Oscar Piastri for clashing with Arvid Lindblad, it sees the trio of drivers drop out of the points-paying positions.

    Liam Lawson has moved up to seventh place, originally taken by Piastri, while Audi’s Gabriel Bortoleto has jumped from outside of the points entirely into eighth place.

    The same situation occurs for Red Bull’s Isack Hadjar who moves from 12th to ninth, while F1 debutant Hirakawa completes the points in 10th place for Haas.

    The Japanese driver was a late call-up for the US team after it opted to bench Esteban Ocon for unspecified reasons on Friday night, giving the opportunity to Hirakawa to make his F1 race bow.
