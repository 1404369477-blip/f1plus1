    Isack Hadjar: Red Bull driver's injury recovery going to plan as team provide update on next steps ahead of Azerbaijan GP decision

    Red Bull's Isack Hadjar has missed three races since the summer break with an injured wrist; team to assess whether Hadjar is ready to return ahead of Baku; watch every session of the Azerbaijan Grand Prix from September 24-26 with a Saturday race, live on Sky Sports F1

    Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies says they will continue to prioritise Isack Hadjar's recovery over rushing his return from injury, with evaluations planned before a call on whether he races at next week's Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    Hadjar has missed the last three races after sustaining a wrist injury during routine training ahead of the sport's return to action from its summer break. Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson has stood in for him.

    Speaking after last Sunday's Spanish Grand Prix, Mekies said Hadjar's recovery was going to plan, with a simulator session planned to help assess his readiness for any return to action in Baku.

    Red Bull pleased with Lawson's 'very good' stand-in performances

    Lawson has stepped up from sister Racing Bulls to cover for Hadjar, with the New Zealander returning to a seat he briefly held in an ill-fated two-race stint at the start of last season. Yuki Tsunoda has been driving for Racing Bulls.

    Lawson has twice qualified eighth and scored points at both Zandvoort and Madrid, where he was sixth.

    "I think he's done a very good job," said Mekies.

    "I think we all know how difficult it is to jump to a different team, to a different car, and I think he has done that to the best of what could have been our expectations.

    "He has taken some very important points [in Madrid] with the P6. He did the same in Zandvoort, and in Monza he would have done something similar without the contact with Nico [Hulkenberg] and the damage.

    "But I think that, of course, is very, very strong."
