    法院裁决后加斯利再次被剥夺摩纳哥领奖台

    阿尔派车手皮埃尔·加斯利的两项五秒处罚被重新执行，他因此再度失去2026年摩纳哥大奖赛的领奖台成绩。

    阿尔派车队F1车手加斯利的两项五秒处罚被重新恢复，因此他再次失去了2026年摩纳哥大奖赛的领奖台名次。

    这一决定是在迈凯伦和红牛就FIA撤销Gasly在摩纳哥因维修区超速而受到的两项处罚的裁决提出上诉之后作出的。

    撤销Gasly处罚的决定是在阿尔派申请复审之后作出的。结果显示，Formula One Management在用于计算速度的维修区距离测量上出现了错误。

    红牛和迈凯伦对这一改判提出上诉，认为事后更改测量基准损害了体育公平，因为各车队都是根据原始测量数据来校准其维修区限速器的。

    红牛车队的伊斯克·哈贾尔（哈贾尔）——本周末因手腕受伤缺席蒙扎站——因此递补获得2026年摩纳哥大奖赛的最后一个领奖台席位。

    迈凯伦车队的奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里（皮亚斯特里）在摩纳哥原成绩为第四名，当加斯利被恢复至第三名后，他被降到了第五名。

    关键的是，皮亚斯特里是多名因在维修区超速而受罚的车手之一——但他不可能让处罚被撤销，因为他已经在比赛中执行了处罚。

    不过，这位澳大利亚车手在摩纳哥站排名中将回升一位至第四名，排在小红牛车队的两位车手之前——而加斯利则被降至第七名。

    阿尔派 F1车队发布的一份声明开头写道：“车队对国际汽联国际上诉法庭就2026年摩纳哥大奖赛结果所作出的裁决表示失望和沮丧。”

    “尽管我们不认同这一决定，并且仍然完全坚持10号车在比赛期间的任何时候都没有超出维修区限速，但车队接受近日在巴黎举行的听证会上评审团的裁决。”

    “虽然这一结果并非我们所期望的，因为我们认为自己受到了不公正的处罚，但车队将坚定地专注于继续提升赛道上的表现，并继续投身于一场激烈而势均力敌的锦标赛争夺之中。”

    “这一结果不会动摇车队在赛季结束前实现其目标的努力和动力。”

    英文原文

    Alpine Formula 1 driver Pierre Gasly has had his two five-second penalties reimposed and therefore lost his 2026 Monaco Grand Prix podium finish, again.

    The decision comes after appeals were brought by McLaren and Red Bull against the FIA’s decision to rescind Gasly’s pair of penalties for pitlane speeding in Monaco.

    That decision to rescind Gasly’s penalties was made after Alpine petitioned for a right of review. It emerged that Formula One Management had made a measurement error relating to the pitlane distance used to calculate speed.

    Red Bull and McLaren appealed the reversal, arguing that changing the measurement benchmark retrospectively undermined sporting fairness as competitors had calibrated their pitlane speed limiters using the original measurements.

    Red Bull’s Isack Hadjar - out this weekend in Monza with a wrist injury - therefore receives the final podium place from the 2026 Monaco GP.

    McLaren’s Oscar Piastri was classified fourth in Monaco and demoted to fifth when Gasly was reinstated to third.

    Piastri, crucially, was one of several drivers who picked up pitlane speeding penalties - but could not have had his punishment rescinded as he served the penalties during the race.

    The Australian will however move back up one place in the Monaco standings to fourth, ahead of the Racing Bulls pair - with Gasly now demoted to seventh.

    “The team expresses its disappointment and frustration with the outcome reached by the FIA International Court of Appeal relating to the result of the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix,” began a statement released by the Alpine F1 team.

    “While we disagree with the decision, and still fully maintain that Car #10 did not exceed the Pit Lane speed limit at any point during the race, the team acknowledges the decision of the judging panel from the recent hearing in Paris.

    “While the result is not what we had hoped for, as we feel that we have been unjustly punished, the team is firmly focused on continuing to improve its performance on track and remains in a close and very competitive Championship fight.

    “This outcome will not deter the team’s efforts and motivation in achieving its objectives by the end of the season.”
