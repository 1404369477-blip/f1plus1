    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯凭能量管理智取梅赛德斯

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯赢得近六场F1大奖赛中的第四胜，车队领队洛朗·梅基斯认为这场胜利更多体现维斯塔潘在能量管理和尾流战术上的智慧，而非红牛在能量匮乏赛道的进步。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘“智取”梅赛德斯的一个方面让红牛哑口无言

    洛朗·梅基斯表示，拉斯维加斯的胜利更多说明马克斯·维斯塔潘如何智取对手，而非红牛在能量匮乏赛道上的进步

    马克斯·维斯塔潘赢得近六场F1大奖赛中的第四场胜利。这一次，这位荷兰车手在一条被称为能量匮乏的赛道上获胜，考虑到红牛福特动力总成今年初在类似赛道仍表现挣扎，这一点值得注意。

    拉斯维加斯的胜利似乎凸显了红牛在该领域的进步幅度，尽管车队领队洛朗·梅基斯认为，这一结果主要讲述了另一个故事。据他所说，维斯塔潘尤其出色地应对了能量管理和极强尾流带来的战术挑战。

    “我认为维斯塔潘今天显然用他成功防守50圈的方式智胜了对手，”当被Motorsport.com问及这场胜利有多少归功于维斯塔潘智胜梅赛德斯、多少归功于红牛优化动力单元时，梅基斯说道。

    “是的，维斯塔潘被超越过几次，但我有点觉得他可能故意这么做了几次。他大概已经探索了倒数第二个弯道到终点线之间可能发生的所有情况。”

    梅基斯尤其对维斯塔潘的前瞻性思维印象深刻。还剩十圈时，他已经在问车队最后一圈在能量部署方面能做些什么，以避免在终点线前被超越。

    “甚至到了这样的程度，正如你们所见，他要求我们重新审视最后一圈的情景，”梅基斯继续说道。

    “显然，大家对此已经考虑了很久，但我们回到他那里得出的结论是，他已经掌握了它，并且已经探索了所有选项。所以我们觉得他当时完全知道该怎么做，而事实证明正是如此。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，洛朗·梅基斯，红牛车队领队

    不过应当指出，梅赛德斯两位车手在最后阶段掉队了。乔治·拉塞尔因直行冲出赛道而退出争夺，而基米·安东内利本处于极佳位置，但据这位意大利车手所说，他松开油门时动力单元出现了混乱。

    尽管如此，这丝毫不减维斯塔潘这场胜利的成色，梅基斯甚至将其描述为他本赛季最好的比赛之一。

    “我认为他又跑出了一场出色的比赛，一场非凡的比赛。我不知道这场比赛是否会比其他比赛更让他铭记，但我觉得他长时间顶住压力的方式令人叹为观止。”

    据梅基斯说，有一点让红牛无言以对，那就是维斯塔潘自己优化能量管理的方式。

    在拉斯维加斯大道赛段，他故意让自己被超越几次，以便为电池充电，然后在通往1号弯的直道上反击。一旦梅赛德斯意识到如何应对这一点，维斯塔潘就调整了战术，转而多次在3号弯发动反击。

    “看看今天的比赛，看看巴西的比赛，再看看他相对于其他车手摆放赛车的方式。他总是在完美的时刻出现在完美的位置，”梅基斯说。

    "是的，他每天仍然让我们感到惊讶。每场比赛在维修区指挥台上，我们互相看着对方说，刚才那是什么？因为这确实就是我们做的事。

    "我不知道你是否想称之为被低估，因为我不认为马克斯被低估了，但他来自另一个星球。"

    因为这是一场在能量管理方面极具战术性的比赛，而且太多环节都围绕着尾流展开，梅基斯承认，他并没有更清楚地了解红牛福特动力总成在能量匮乏的赛道上究竟处于什么位置。

    "当涉及能量匮乏和能量充沛的赛道时，我认为鉴于比赛在极高尾流效应下展开的方式，我觉得很难得出结论。

    “我本希望能带着对赛车在正赛节奏中相对对手速度的清晰认识离开这里，但我认为今天没有人有那种清晰的画面。因此，对此没有真正的结论。”

    The one aspect of Max Verstappen "outsmarting" Mercedes that left Red Bull speechless

    According to Laurent Mekies, the victory in Las Vegas says more about how Max Verstappen outsmarted the opposition than about Red Bull's progress on energy-poor circuits

    Max Verstappen claimed his fourth victory in the last six Formula 1 Grands Prix. This time, the Dutchman won at what is known as an energy-poor circuit, which is noteworthy given that Red Bull Ford Powertrains was still struggling at such venues at the start of this year.

    The victory in Las Vegas appears to underline just how much progress Red Bull has made in that area, although team principal Laurent Mekies believes the result primarily tells a different story. According to him, Verstappen handled the tactical challenges of energy management and the extremely powerful slipstream particularly well.

    "I think Max clearly outsmarted the opposition today in the way he has managed to defend for 50 laps," Mekies said when asked by Motorsport.com how much of the win was down to Verstappen outsmarting Mercedes and how much was down to Red Bull optimising the power unit.

    "And yes, Max has been overtaken a few times, but I kind of think that he probably did that on purpose a few times. He has probably explored every possible way of what's happening between the second-to-last corner and the line."

    Mekies was particularly impressed by the way Verstappen thought ahead. With ten laps remaining, he was already asking the team what could be done in terms of energy deployment during the last lap to avoid being overtaken before the finish line.

    "It even went to the extent where, as you have seen, that he has asked us to have another look at the scenario for the final lap," Mekies continued.

    "Obviously the guys were thinking about that for quite a while, but the conclusion when we came back to him was that he was already mastering it and that he had explored all the options. So we felt that he knew exactly what to do at that moment and it proved to be that case."

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Laurent Mekies, Red Bull Racing Team Principal

    It should be noted, however, that the Mercedes duo dropped away in the closing stages. George Russell took himself out of contention by going straight on, while Kimi Antonelli was in an excellent position but, according to the Italian, his power unit became confused when he lifted off the throttle.

    Nevertheless, that takes nothing away from Verstappen's victory, with Mekies even describing it as one of his best races this season.

    "I think he's done another outstanding race, an exceptional race. I don't know if it's one he will remember more than the other ones, but I think it was breathtaking the way he resisted the pressure for so long."

    One aspect left Red Bull speechless, according to Mekies, and that was the way Verstappen managed to optimise energy management himself.

    On the Strip, he deliberately allowed himself to be overtaken a few times to recharge the battery before fighting back on the straight towards Turn 1. Once Mercedes realised how to deal with that, Verstappen adjusted his tactics and managed to counterattack several times at Turn 3 instead.

    "Look at the race today, look at the race in Brazil, and look at the way he positions his car relative to the others. He is always in the perfect place at the perfect moment," Mekies said.

    "And yes, he still surprises us every day. Every race on the pit wall, we look at each other and we say, what was that? Because that's really what we do.

    "I don't know if you want to call it underrated, because I don't think Max is underrated, but he is from a different planet."

    Because it was such a tactical race in terms of energy management and so much revolved around the slipstream, Mekies admitted that he had not gained much clarity about exactly where Red Bull Ford Powertrains stands on energy-poor circuits.

    "When it comes to energy-poor and energy-rich tracks, I think given how the race panned out with the extremely high slipstream effect, I think it's very difficult to have a conclusion.

    "I would have loved to leave here with clear pictures of the pace of the car in the race pace against the opposition, but I think nobody has that clear picture today. And therefore, no real conclusion on that."
