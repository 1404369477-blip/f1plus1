    拉塞尔称汉密尔顿与维斯塔潘风格不同

    拉塞尔在天空体育F1专访中对比了汉密尔顿的“风度与尊严”和维斯塔潘的场外风格，并谈及他与维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯再起的争执。

    乔治·拉塞尔称刘易斯·汉密尔顿提供了与马克斯·维斯塔潘不同的榜样。

    乔治·拉塞尔在卡塔尔大奖赛前将刘易斯·汉密尔顿的“风度与尊严”与马克斯·维斯塔潘的场外风格进行了对比。

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    在天空体育F1对马丁·布伦德尔的独家专访中——该专访于周五先行预告，完整版在周日卡塔尔大奖赛前播出——乔治·拉塞尔谈及他与四届世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯再起的争执，并在两位F1当代巨人之间悄然划出了一道清晰可辨的对比。

    拉塞尔说：“他（维斯塔潘）是一个冷酷无情的竞争者，我非常尊重这一点，但还有汉密尔顿，我和他做了三年队友，也看着他比赛了一辈子。而且显然，托托（沃尔夫，梅赛德斯车队领队）和（汉密尔顿）共事了12年，他同样是一个冷酷无情的竞争者，在方向盘后面绝对是猛兽，但显然也有着令人难以置信的价值观、风度和尊严。”

    “而刘易斯是我真正敬仰的人，对于正在成长的孩子们来说，他是一个不可思议的榜样。但没有什么对与错，只有观点。每个人都有不同的观点，有些人喜欢车手A，有些人喜欢车手B，随着我长大，我不再让它对我产生那么大的影响。”

    为什么汉密尔顿这个标杆具有真正的分量

    这个比较之所以更有冲击力，是因为拉塞尔亲身经历过。从2022年到2024年，作为梅赛德斯队友，两人共同起步68次，拉塞尔和汉密尔顿在极其微小的差距上较量：比赛完赛次数34比34持平，总积分仅相差两分（汉密尔顿以697比695占优），而拉塞尔在排位赛中以39比29占优。即使在赛道上短暂出现紧张——比如2023年卢赛尔首圈的纠缠——这对组合也从未演变成公开的人身攻击。

    上周末在拉斯维加斯，维斯塔潘的回应几乎截然不同。此前在第十四号弯的第三次练习中，两人发生轻微接触，拉塞尔公开批评了维斯塔潘的驾驶方式——这一事件导致这位红牛车手因驾驶行为不稳定而收到国际汽联的正式警告——随后维斯塔潘赢下了周六晚上的正赛，而拉塞尔则在比赛后段锁死轮胎，在與队友基米·安东内利的缠斗中撞上五号弯护栏，最终跌至第九名。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利；乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    赛后，维斯塔潘称拉塞尔“远低于我的水平”，并将他后段的失误称为“因果报应”。在接受荷兰广播公司Viaplay采访时，他更进一步表示：“他的言论激励不了我，因为他根本和我不是一个级别。整个赛季场下全是空谈，场上完全是个笑话。完美的因果报应。”

    在与布伦德尔回顾这场风波的后续时，拉塞尔承认胜利让维斯塔潘掌握了话语权——同时强调了两人的性格分歧所在。

    拉塞尔说：“事后我觉得这一切挺有意思的。但显然他赢了比赛，我跑了一场糟糕的比赛，而他能笑到最后，所以我确信他挺享受这一点的。”

    布伦德尔将塞纳与舒马赫相提并论，以及一场激烈的第三名争夺战

    布伦德尔在天空体育单独发表看法时表示，拉塞尔其实应该把维斯塔潘的敌意解读为一种尊重的信号。“某种程度上，我认为乔治应该把这当作一种赞美，因为马克斯甚至愿意对他发表评论，这说明他认为他足够快，”布伦德尔说。“他们两个人完全不是一路人，而这正是让世界继续运转的原因，不是吗？”

    布伦德尔将维斯塔潘的威慑气质与自己赛车生涯中遇到的偶像车手相比较，补充道：“马克斯让我想起我职业生涯中经历过的其他车手，他们想稍微统治一下赛场。塞纳是我在三级方程式时遇到的一位，但他们采取的方式有微妙的不同。迈克尔·舒马赫，当我们在贝纳通车队做队友时；他想压制我，在赛道上相当有攻击性。”

    尽管拉塞尔称赞汉密尔顿的标准，但这两位前队友在2026年的积分榜上仍然是直接竞争对手。继六月在巴塞罗那为法拉利赢得首胜——这是他职业生涯第106场胜利，拉塞尔紧随其后获得第二——汉密尔顿以267分进入卡塔尔站，在车手积分榜上排名第三，仅领先拉塞尔和兰多·诺里斯（264分）三分，落后第二名维斯塔潘（304分）37分。

    周五在卢赛尔的首节练习至少给了拉塞尔一个早期提振：新加冕的冠军安东内利领跑梅赛德斯一二名，拉塞尔获得第二，维斯塔潘第六，汉密尔顿第八。2026赛季还剩两场比赛，拉塞尔已经明确了他选择的榜样——现在他需要周日的成绩来证明这一点。

    George Russell says Lewis Hamilton offers a different example from Max Verstappen

    George Russell contrasts Lewis Hamilton's "grace and dignity" with Max Verstappen's off-track style before the Qatar GP

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    In an exclusive Sky Sports F1 interview with Martin Brundle - previewed Friday ahead of its full broadcast before Sunday’s Qatar Grand Prix - George Russell addressed his reignited Las Vegas feud with the four-time world champion Max Verstappen by drawing a quiet, unmistakable contrast between two of Formula 1’s modern giants.

    "He [Verstappen] is a ruthless competitor, and I massively respect that, but there's also Lewis Hamilton, who I've spent three years team-mates with and watched my whole life," Russell said. "And obviously Toto [Wolff, Mercedes' team principal] spent 12 years with [Hamilton], who is equally a ruthless competitor and absolute animal behind the wheel, but obviously has incredible values and grace and dignity.

    "And Lewis is somebody who I really look up to and who is an incredible role model for young kids coming through. But there is no right or wrong; there's just opinions. Everyone has different opinions, and some people like driver A and some people like driver B, and as I've grown older, I don't really let it affect me as much."

    Why the Hamilton benchmark carries real weight

    The comparison hits harder because Russell lived it firsthand. Across 68 shared starts as Mercedes team-mates from 2022 through 2024, Russell and Hamilton fought on razor-thin margins: deadlocked 34–34 in race finishes, separated by just two total points (697 to 695 in Hamilton’s favour), with Russell holding a 39–29 edge in qualifying. Even when tensions briefly flared on track - such as their opening-lap tangle at Losail in 2023 - the pairing never descended into public character attacks.

    Verstappen’s Las Vegas response last weekend could hardly have been more different. After Russell called out Verstappen’s driving following their Turn 14 final-practice brush - an incident that earned the Red Bull driver a formal FIA warning for erratic driving - Verstappen won Saturday night’s race while Russell locked up late and clipped the Turn 5 barrier battling team-mate Kimi Antonelli, falling to ninth.

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, George Russell, Mercedes

    After the flag, Verstappen dismissed Russell as "so far below my level" and called his late off "karma". Speaking to Dutch broadcaster Viaplay, he went even further: "His comments don't motivate me, because he's nowhere near me. All talk off the track and a complete joke on it this season. Perfect karma."

    Reflecting on the fallout with Brundle, Russell accepted that winning gives Verstappen the microphone - while emphasising where their personalities diverge.

    "I found it all quite amusing afterwards. But obviously he won the race, I had a c**p race, and he's able to have the last laugh, so I'm sure he quite enjoyed that," Russell said.

    Brundle’s Senna–Schumacher parallel, and a tight P3 fight

    Weighing in separately on Sky Sports, Brundle suggested Russell should actually read Verstappen’s hostility as a sign of respect. "In a way, I think George should take it as a compliment that Max even cares to say something about him, because that means he thinks he's fast enough," Brundle said. "They're cut from a completely different cloth, those two, and that's what keeps the world spinning, isn't it?"

    Comparing Verstappen’s intimidating streak to icons from his own racing career, Brundle added: "Max reminds me of other drivers I experienced in my career who wanted to try to dominate the field a little bit. Senna was one for me in Formula 3, but they did it in a subtly different way. Michael Schumacher, when I was team-mates at Benetton; he wanted to dominate me and was quite aggressive out on the race track".

    Even as Russell praises Hamilton’s standards, the former team-mates remain direct rivals on the 2026 scoreboard. Following his maiden Ferrari victory in Barcelona in June - his 106th career win, with Russell standing right beside him in second - Hamilton enters Qatar third in the Drivers’ Championship on 267 points, just three clear of both Russell and Lando Norris (264), and 37 adrift of second-placed Verstappen (304).

    Friday’s opening practice at Losail at least gave Russell an early boost: while newly crowned champion Antonelli paced a Mercedes 1-2, Russell finished second ahead of Verstappen in sixth and Hamilton in eighth. With two races left in 2026, Russell has made his choice of role model clear - now he needs Sunday’s result to back it up.
