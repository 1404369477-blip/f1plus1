    凯迪拉克F1携手汤米·希尔费格推出得州主题系列

    凯迪拉克F1车队与合作伙伴汤米·希尔费格在美国大奖赛前发布得克萨斯主题服装系列。该系列本周二已开售。

    凯迪拉克F1以全新汤米·希尔费格系列在美国大奖赛上尽显得克萨斯风情

    凯迪拉克与汤米·希尔费格发布了一个以得克萨斯为灵感的五件式牛仔（denim）系列

    瓦尔特利·博塔斯，凯迪拉克F1车队——图片由汤米·希尔费格提供

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    凯迪拉克一级方程式车队及其合作伙伴汤米·希尔费格在奥斯汀美国大奖赛前发布了一个德克萨斯主题的服装系列，这让我们所有人都兴奋不已。

    汤米·希尔费格与凯迪拉克一级方程式车队的奥斯汀系列于本周二早些时候开售。但这并不是这两个品牌第一次推出与比赛相关的产品。他们已经在同一城市系列中推出了迈阿密和墨西哥系列，并且还计划在11月推出拉斯维加斯系列。

    不出意外的话，牛仔布是这些单品的核心，它们带有镀铬细节和汤米·希尔费格红白蓝配色的V形美国国旗。

    男士可以购买基于经典卡车司机风格的浅色水洗牛仔夹克。女士则有一件带银色箔涂层的牛仔背心。一件T恤、一件背心和一顶牛仔帽让这个系列更加让人难以拒绝。你知道还有什么同样让人难以拒绝吗？可以看到凯迪拉克车手瓦尔特利·博塔斯在日落时分向镜头举起一顶牛仔帽，背上印有V形旗帜图案。

    你还需要什么来说服你？

    瓦尔特利·博塔斯，凯迪拉克F1车队——由汤米·希尔费格提供

    “在我们的美国根源上增添一点乐趣”

    汤米·希尔费格首席设计官李·霍尔曼表示，设计师们从该品牌最受欢迎的两件单品入手。

    他说：“我们从 Tommy 衣橱中的标志性单品——牛仔夹克和白 T 恤——入手，并研究了如何以奥斯汀的城市气质为灵感对它们进行重新演绎。”

    “关键在于把熟悉的单品赋予全新的视角，同时坚守我们经典美式酷感的品牌基因。”

    凯迪拉克一级方程式车队首席品牌顾问卡西迪·托里斯表示，在得克萨斯州举行的这场比赛让两个品牌都有了发挥空间。

    “奥斯汀让我们有机会在美国根源上玩得更尽兴一些，”托里斯说，“这是为比赛周末打造的粉丝服饰，但设计上力求在围场之外也能长久穿着。”

    汤米·希尔费格是凯迪拉克的官方服装和车迷服饰合作伙伴，车手塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯——博塔斯的队友——也是该品牌的全球男装大使。奥斯汀站是美国车队本赛季继五月初迈阿密大奖赛之后的第二个主场比赛，佩雷斯期待能在离家更近的地方驾驶。

    “我们距离能够与前车竞争还差大约一秒到一秒半，我认为我们现在真正需要的是一辆更强的赛车，”佩雷斯在新加坡站后表示。“其他一切都在按预期推进，我相信我们作为一个团队会达到目标。我非常期待奥斯汀和墨西哥，我们的两场主场比赛。

    “奥斯汀是车队的主场，墨西哥对我而言将格外特别，在家乡观众面前比赛并迎来第300场大奖赛。那将是一个非常特别的周末。”

    凯迪拉克在首个赛季仍在等待它的首个积分。

    Cadillac F1 goes full Texas with new Tommy Hilfiger collection for US Grand Prix

    Cadillac and Tommy Hilfiger have unveiled a five-piece denim collection inspired by Texas

    Valtteri Bottas, Cadillac F1 team - courtesy of Tommy Hilfiger

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    The Cadillac Formula 1 Team and its partner Tommy Hilfiger have released a Texas-themed clothing collection ahead of the United States Grand Prix in Austin, and it's making us all kinds of excited.

    The Tommy Hilfiger x Cadillac Formula 1 Team Austin collection went on sale on Tuesday earlier this week. But this isn't the first time the two brands have released something in connection with a race. They have already released Miami and Mexico collections in the same city series, and a Las Vegas drop is also planned for November.

    Denim, maybe unsurprisingly, is the heart of these pieces, which carry chrome detailing and a chevron-shaped American flag in Tommy Hilfiger's red, white and blue.

    Men can buy a light-wash denim jacket based on the classic trucker style. For women, there's a denim vest with a silver foil coating. A T-shirt, a tank top and a denim cap make the range even harder to say no to. And do you know what else does the same? Cadillac driver Valtteri Bottas can be seen tipping a cowboy hat to the camera at sunset, with the chevron flag design across his back.

    What more do you need to persuade you?

    Valtteri Bottas, Cadillac F1 team - courtesy of Tommy Hilfiger

    "A little more fun with our American roots"

    Lee Holman, Tommy Hilfiger's chief design officer, said the designers started with two of the brand's most popular pieces.

    "We started with icons of the Tommy wardrobe – the denim jacket and the white T-shirt – and looked at how we could rework them inspired by the character of Austin," he said.

    "It's about taking familiar pieces and giving them a fresh point of view, while staying grounded in our classic American cool DNA."

    Cassidy Towriss, chief brand adviser for the Cadillac Formula 1 Team, said the race in Texas gave both brands room to play.

    "Austin gives us a chance to have a little more fun with our American roots," Towriss said. "It's fanwear made for race weekend, but designed to live well beyond the paddock."

    Tommy Hilfiger is Cadillac's official apparel and fanwear partner, with driver Sergio Perez, Bottas's team-mate, also the brand's global menswear ambassador. With the Austin race being the American team's second home race of the season after the Miami Grand Prix earlier this season in early May, Perez is looking forward to driving a little closer to home.

    “We are still lacking around one to one-and-a-half seconds to be able to compete with the cars ahead, and I think what we really need now is a stronger car underneath us," Perez said following his Singapore campaign. "Everything else has been progressing as expected, and I’m confident we will get there as a team. I’m really looking forward to Austin and Mexico, our two home races.

    "Austin for the team, and Mexico will be particularly special for me, racing in front of my home crowd and reaching 300 grands prix. It’s going to be a very special weekend.”

    Cadillac is still waiting for its first points in its debut season.
