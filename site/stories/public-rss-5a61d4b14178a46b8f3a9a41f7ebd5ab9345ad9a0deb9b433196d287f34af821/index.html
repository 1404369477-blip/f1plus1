    威廉姆斯双雄为荷兰站撞车道歉，塞恩斯被罚10秒

    荷兰大奖赛期间，威廉姆斯车队的塞恩斯与阿尔本发生碰撞，导致阿尔本退赛。双方均为此道歉，赛会干事认定塞恩斯负全责并对其处以10秒罚时。

    两位威廉姆斯车手都为他们在荷兰大奖赛期间彼此碰撞中所扮演的角色道歉。

    然而赛会干事裁定小卡洛斯·塞恩斯应对此次碰撞负责，该事故导致他的队友亚历山大·阿尔本退赛。

    碰撞发生时，阿尔本正试图在进入Tarzan弯时从外侧超越塞恩斯。塞恩斯锁死了轮胎，并与另一辆威廉姆斯赛车发生接触。

    “当时的情况是比赛让我们处于阿尔本用更新的中性胎在我身后的局面，”塞恩斯解释道。“比赛接近尾声时，我的硬胎已经非常挣扎，而突然当我试图防守时，一号弯刹车区的一个大颠簸让我措手不及。”

    “我引发了严重的前轮锁死，显然并非有意，我带上了阿尔本，这是你最不想对队友做的事。但老实说，我在刹车区那个大颠簸让我措手不及，引发了严重的前轮锁死，之后我无法挽救。”

    “所以为此道歉，我们会作为一个团队继续前进。我认为这绝不是我们想要处于的位置。”

    赛会干事对塞恩斯处以10秒罚时，裁定他“锁死轮胎并导致两车相撞”，且“负有全部责任”。

    然而阿尔本也为他在碰撞中的角色道歉。他表示，这不像队友之间其他冲突那么严重，尤其是2018年阿塞拜疆大奖赛中红牛车手马克斯·维斯塔潘和丹尼尔·里卡多的事故，那次事故导致两人双双退赛。

    “这对车队不利，”阿尔本说。“显然我们向车队道歉，但这不是维斯塔潘-里卡多巴库的情况。”

    “我们肯定会复盘，但我认为他可能只是锁死了，我也试图避开，但我们受损了，所以不得不退赛。”

    英文原文

    Both Williams drivers apologised for their roles in the collision between the pair during the Dutch Grand Prix.

    However the stewards ruled Carlos Sainz Jnr was responsible for the collision which ended his team mate Alexander Albon’s race.

    The collision occured as Albon tried to overtake Sainz on the outside entering Tarzan corner. Sainz locked up and made contact with the other Williams.

    “It was a situation of the race put us with Alex on a fresher medium [tyre] behind,” Sainz explained. “I was struggling already a lot with my hard tyres there towards the end of the race and all of a sudden when I went to defend, a big bump in the braking area into turn one surprised me.

    “I triggered a massive front lock and without wanting, obviously, I took Alex with me, which is the last thing you want to do with a team mate. But honestly I was caught off guard with that big bump there in the braking and triggered a massive front lock that I couldn’t recover later.

    “So apologies for that and we will move on as a team. I think it’s just not the position we want to be in.”

    The stewards handed Sainz a 10-second time penalty, ruling he “locked up and caused a collision between both cars” and was “wholly at fault”.

    However Albon also apologised for his role in the collision. He said it was not as serious as other clashes between team mates, notably the 2018 collision between Red Bull duo Max Verstappen and Daniel Ricciardo at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix which took both drivers out.

    “It’s not good for the team,” said Albon. “Obviously we apologise to the team, but it’s not a Verstappen-Ricciardo Baku situation.

    “We’ll review it for sure but I think he maybe just locked up and I tried to avoid it as well but we had damage so we had to retire.”
