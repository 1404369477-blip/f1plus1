    安东内利卡塔尔周五练习赛长距离领先维斯塔潘近一秒

    安东内利在卡塔尔大奖赛周五练习赛中单圈和长距离均表现强势，长距离平均每圈比最接近的对手维斯塔潘快0.83秒。梅赛德斯统治了周五的练习。

    F1卡塔尔大奖赛练习赛分析：安东内利长距离比维斯塔潘快近一秒

    梅赛德斯统治了卡塔尔周五的练习，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利在比赛模拟中平均每圈比最接近的对手快0.83秒

    安东内利带着早期热门身份进入2026年F1赛季倒数第二站卡塔尔。这位卫冕世界冠军统治了卢赛尔国际赛道的周五练习，无论单圈还是比赛模拟都表现出色。他在长距离上的优势尤其令人印象深刻：在调整后的长距离分析中，他最接近的挑战者马克斯·维斯塔潘平均每圈慢0.83秒。

    安东内利在第一节自由练习中已经做出最快圈，比队友乔治·拉塞尔快0.507秒。梅赛德斯的统治在第二节练习中延续，尽管多次事故和红旗限制了排位模拟的参考价值。

    在领先的车队中，只有红牛和迈凯伦成功用软胎完成了飞驰圈。尽管如此，两支车队都未能击败梅赛德斯用中性胎做出的圈速。安东内利以1:23.077的成绩位居榜首，拉塞尔落后略超过十分之一秒半。作为最接近梅赛德斯双雄的挑战者，兰多·诺里斯已经落后近半秒。

    在比赛模拟中，竞争格局更加明显。在计入轮胎配方和 连续圈数长度的差异后，安东内利成为场上轻松最快的车手。即便是拉塞尔，平均每圈也比队友慢0.90秒。

    为什么梅赛德斯在卡塔尔如此强势？

    梅赛德斯优势之大尤其值得注意，因为这支车队传统上并不以在周五练习中释放赛车全部潜力而闻名。厂队通常采取相当保守的策略，尤其是在引擎性能方面。

    虽然不能完全排除练习数据中存在燃油量和引擎设置的差异，但梅赛德斯一贯的做法让人有理由相信，其优势反映了真实的基础速度，尽管这一优势的幅度仍不确定。

    相反，仔细观察各计时段的时间，可以为梅赛德斯的优势所在提供一些线索。这支世界冠军车队在第一和第三计时段明显快于对手，这两个计时段都以中高速弯道为特点。

    在开场的第一计时段，梅赛德斯对其最接近的挑战者红牛拥有约0.3秒的优势。在最后的第三计时段，梅赛德斯对迈凯伦取得约0.25秒的优势，对红牛则多达0.4秒——尽管对手使用的是更软的轮胎配方。

    只有在第二计时段，红牛和迈凯伦才能与梅赛德斯匹敌。然而，软胎很可能在赛道的这一区段带来特别优势，因为这里更紧、更慢的弯道对牵引力提出了更高的要求。

    红牛还是迈凯伦：谁将成为梅赛德斯最接近的挑战者？

    在梅赛德斯身后，红牛和迈凯伦之间几乎没有差距，至少在单圈速度上是如此。兰多·诺里斯、奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里、马克斯·维斯塔潘和伊萨克·哈贾尔在排位赛模拟中的差距约为0.06秒。

    然而在长距离跑中，力量对比向红牛倾斜。这支位于米尔顿凯恩斯的车队在轮胎衰减管理方面相比迈凯伦尤为高效。维斯塔潘平均每圈比安东内利慢0.83秒，而他的队友哈贾尔则落后1.02秒。与此同时，诺里斯每圈落后1.24秒，皮亚斯特里则落后多达1.40秒。

    就目前情况来看，红牛因此在比赛条件下似乎是梅赛德斯最有希望的挑战者。然而，鉴于与领先的梅赛德斯之间存在相当大的差距，数据显示对维斯塔潘而言，拉塞尔可能比安东内利是更现实的目标。

    法拉利在卡塔尔是否面临中游集团的竞争？

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利，阿尔维德·林德布拉德，小红牛

    评估法拉利的表现要困难得多。这支车队在第二节自由练习中执行了不同的计划，在排位赛模拟上花费了相对较长的时间，随后在练习赛末段才切换到正赛模拟。

    然而，法拉利的单圈速度并不令人信服。单圈下来，法拉利落后梅赛德斯超过一秒。这让法拉利更接近小红牛，尽管对红牛的姊妹车队略有优势。

    在长距离测试中，刘易斯·汉密尔顿仅完成了三圈，而夏尔·勒克莱尔只跑了两圈。因此，计算出的汉密尔顿每圈1.28秒和勒克莱尔2.18秒的差距几乎没有代表性。

    特别是，在如此少的圈数基础上，无法可靠评估更长一段 连续驾驶时段中的实际轮胎退化情况。因此，汉密尔顿相对于迈凯伦车手较小的差距应谨慎对待。

    小红牛 统治中游集团，哈斯苦苦挣扎

    与前方阵营类似，中游集团的格局也相当清晰。无论是在排位赛模拟还是长距离测试中，小红牛 都是传统中游车队中速度最快的。

    在单圈方面，该车队对奥迪保持着约零点二秒的优势。在比赛模拟中，阿尔维德·林德布拉德每圈比安东内利慢1.59秒，而利亚姆·劳森则落后1.79秒。

    小红牛之后存在巨大差距：奥迪作为次快的中游车队，每圈已落后领先节奏2.77秒。加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托是唯一在比赛模拟中测试硬胎的车手。

    威廉姆斯（+2.97秒）、阿尔派（+3.28秒）和阿斯顿·马丁（+3.32秒）组成下一集团。哈斯表现尤为糟糕，每圈落后3.80秒。只有凯迪拉克更慢，落后4.71秒。

    这些差距的规模是周五练习的一个显著特征——无论是安东内利与其他领先车手之间，还是顶级车队与中游车队之间。

    为什么卡塔尔强制两停策略可能本来就是必要的

    轮胎策略在卡塔尔将再次扮演特别重要的角色。出于安全考虑，单段最多27圈的里程限制已被强制实施。在57圈的比赛距离下，至少需要两次进站。

    去年，到比赛周末结束时轮胎退化程度如此之低，以至于如果没有限制，一停策略本来是可行的。然而，今年周五的练习呈现出了相当不同的竞争图景。

    在排位模拟中，一些车手仍能在其单段（stint）的第九或第十圈做出个人最快圈。相比之下，在长距离（long run）中，众多车手都苦于严重的轮胎退化。在某些情况下，圈速在十圈内恶化了约两秒。

    有趣的是，一些车手在这一关键阶段之后轮胎状况有所恢复，随后圈速得到提升。软胎表现尤其糟糕，奥利弗·贝尔曼和费尔南多·阿隆索等人在赛车模拟中使用了软胎。然而，中性胎也表现出相对较高的退化，平均每圈损失0.18秒。

    在博尔托莱托手中，硬胎被证明要稳定得多，退化仅为每圈0.065秒。然而，博尔托莱托是唯一在硬胎配方上提供长距离数据的车手。

    随着抓地力水平提高，轮胎退化在周末剩余时间里可能会减少。然而，鉴于周五观察到的高退化水平，即使没有强制进站次数限制，两停策略似乎也很可能出现。就目前情况而言，中性胎和硬胎预计将是首选的正赛轮胎。

    F1 Qatar GP practice analysis: Kimi Antonelli nearly a second clear of Max Verstappen on long runs

    Mercedes dominated Friday’s running in Qatar, with Andrea Kimi Antonelli averaging 0.83s per lap faster than his closest rival in the race simulations

    Kimi Antonelli heads into the penultimate race weekend of the 2026 Formula 1 season in Qatar as the early favourite. The reigning world champion dominated Friday practice at the Lusail International Circuit, both over a single lap and in the race simulations. His advantage on the long runs was particularly impressive: his closest challenger, Max Verstappen, was on average 0.83s per lap slower in the adjusted long-run analysis.

    Antonelli had already set the fastest time in the opening free practice session, outpacing his team-mate George Russell by 0.507 seconds. Mercedes' dominance continued in the second session, although several incidents and red flags limited the usefulness of the qualifying simulations.

    Of the leading teams, only Red Bull and McLaren managed to complete flying laps on the soft tyres. Nevertheless, neither team was able to beat the times set by Mercedes on the mediums. Antonelli topped the timesheets with a 1:23.077, with Russell just over a tenth and a half behind. Lando Norris, the closest challenger to the Mercedes duo, was already nearly half a second adrift.

    The competitive picture was even more pronounced in the race simulations. Once differences in tyre compounds and stint lengths were accounted for, Antonelli emerged as comfortably the fastest driver in the field. Even Russell was, on average, 0.90 seconds per lap slower than his team-mate.

    Why is Mercedes so dominant in Qatar?

    The size of Mercedes' advantage is particularly noteworthy given that the team has not traditionally been known for unleashing the full potential of its car during Friday practice. The works team typically takes a rather conservative approach, particularly when it comes to engine performance.

    While differences in fuel loads and engine settings cannot be entirely ruled out on the basis of the practice data, Mercedes’ usual approach provides some grounds for believing its advantage reflects genuine underlying pace, although the extent of that advantage remains uncertain.

    A closer look at the sector times instead offers some clues as to where Mercedes' strengths lie. The world championship-winning team is significantly faster to its rivals in the first and third sectors, both of which are characterised by medium- and high-speed corners.

    In the opening sector, Mercedes holds an advantage of around three-tenths of a second over Red Bull, its closest challenger. In the final sector, Mercedes gains approximately 0.25s on McLaren and as much as four tenths on Red Bull – despite its rivals running on the softer tyre compound.

    Only in the second sector are Red Bull and McLaren able to match Mercedes. However, the soft tyre is likely to offer a particular advantage in this section of the circuit, where tighter, slower corners place greater demands on traction.

    Red Bull or McLaren: Who will emerge as Mercedes' closest challenger?

    Behind Mercedes, little separates Red Bull and McLaren, at least over a single lap. Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri, Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar were separated by around 0.06s in the qualifying simulations.

    Over the long runs, however, the balance of power shifted in Red Bull's favour. The Milton Keynes-based team was particularly effective at managing tyre degradation compared to McLaren. While Verstappen was an average of 0.83 seconds per lap slower than Antonelli, his team-mate Hadjar was 1.02 seconds adrift. Norris, meanwhile, was 1.24 seconds per lap off the pace, with Piastri trailing by as much as 1.40 seconds.

    As things stand, Red Bull therefore appears to be Mercedes' most promising challenger in race conditions. Given the considerable deficit to the lead Mercedes, however, the data suggests Russell may be a more realistic target for Verstappen than Antonelli.

    Is Ferrari facing a midfield battle in Qatar?

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Arvid Lindblad, Racing Bulls

    Assessing Ferrari's performance is considerably more difficult. The Scuderia pursued a different programme during the second free practice session, spending a relatively long time on qualifying simulations before switching to race simulations late in the session.

    Ferrari’s single-lap pace, however, was unconvincing. Over a lap, Ferrari was more than a second off Mercedes' pace. That placed the Scuderia closer to Racing Bulls, albeit with a slight advantage over Red Bull's sister team.

    In the long runs, Lewis Hamilton completed just three laps, while Charles Leclerc managed only two. Consequently, their calculated deficits of 1.28s per lap for Hamilton and 2.18s for Leclerc are hardly representative.

    In particular, actual tyre degradation over a longer stint cannot be reliably assessed on the basis of so few laps. Hamilton's relatively small deficit to the McLaren drivers should therefore be treated with caution.

    Racing Bulls dominates the midfield as Haas struggles

    A similarly clear-cut picture to that at the front of the field emerged in the midfield. Racing Bulls was comfortably the fastest of the traditional midfield teams, both in the qualifying simulations and over the long runs.

    Over a single lap, the team held an advantage of around two tenths of a second over Audi. In the race simulations, Arvid Lindblad was 1.59 seconds per lap slower than Antonelli, while Liam Lawson was 1.79 seconds adrift.

    Behind Racing Bulls lies an enormous gap: Audi, the next-fastest midfield team, was already 2.77 seconds per lap off the leading pace. Gabriel Bortoleto was the only driver to trial the hard tyre in the race simulations.

    Williams (+2.97s), Alpine (+3.28s) and Aston Martin (+3.32s) form the next group. Haas endured a particularly poor showing, with a deficit of 3.80s per lap. Only Cadillac was slower, trailing by 4.71s.

    The sizes of these gaps was a notable feature of Friday's running – both between Antonelli and the other leading drivers, and between the top teams and the midfield.

    Why Qatar's mandatory two-stop strategy may have been necessary anyway

    Tyre strategy will once again play a particularly important role in Qatar. Due to safety concerns, a maximum stint length of 27 laps has been imposed. With a race distance of 57 laps, at least two pit stops will be required.

    Last year, tyre degradation was so low by the end of the race weekend that, a one-stop strategy would have been feasible without the restriction. This year's Friday practice, however, painted a rather different competitive picture.

    During the qualifying simulations, some drivers were still able to set personal bests on the ninth or tenth lap of their stints. In the long runs, by contrast, numerous drivers struggled with significant tyre degradation. In some cases, lap times deteriorated by around two seconds within ten laps.

    Interestingly, some drivers saw their tyres recover after this critical phase, with lap times subsequently improving. The soft tyre performed particularly poorly and was used in the race simulations by, among others, Oliver Bearman and Fernando Alonso. However, the medium also exhibited relatively high degradation, with an average time loss of 0.18 seconds per lap.

    The hard tyre proved considerably more consistent in Bortoleto's hands, with degradation of just 0.065 seconds per lap. However, Bortoleto was the only driver to provide long-run data on the hard compound.

    As grip levels improve, tyre degradation is likely to decrease over the remainder of the weekend. Given the high levels of degradation observed on Friday, however, a two-stop strategy appears likely even without the mandatory stint limit. As things stand, the medium and hard compounds are expected to be the preferred race tyres.
