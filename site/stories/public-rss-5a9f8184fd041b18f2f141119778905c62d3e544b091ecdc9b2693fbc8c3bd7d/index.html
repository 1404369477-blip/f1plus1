    奥康永久离开哈斯车队，卡马拉巴西站首秀——我们的看法

    奥康因在墨西哥站的周末中途被替换而提前离开哈斯车队，2027年车手拉斐尔·卡马拉将在本周巴西站迎来F1首秀。哈斯车队为何选择卡马拉在家乡站参赛而非在墨西哥站的得分新秀永井大翔？

    奥康在墨西哥站周末比赛中被突然换下后，提前被解约，他将不再为哈斯车队参赛，2027年的车手Rafael Camara将在本周的巴西大奖赛中首次亮相一级方程式。

    我们对奥康的困境、哈斯车队的事态以及决定让Camara在本届巴西大奖赛中而不是墨西哥站的得分新秀Ryo Hirakawa驾驶赛车的决定有何看法。

    我相信哈斯车队在给Camara一个早期的F1首秀方面做出了正确的选择——但这将是一场地狱般的洗礼。

    Camara还没有参加过FP1，这将是他F1周末的首秀。

    他曾在私人测试中为哈斯和法拉利车队驾驶，所以他并不是完全陌生的，但周末比赛的压 力非常不同——这将是他在现实生活中第一次接触2026年机械的真实体验，因为那些测试是在旧车上进行的。

    除此之外：这是卡马拉的主场赛事。

    去年，正处于一个稳健的新秀赛季中的加布里埃尔·博尔特奥托，在那种压力下真的很挣扎。

    哦，而且目前预报周末将有大量降雨。

    所以，在这样的环境下首次亮相是非常困难的。

    但哈斯选择卡玛拉参加2027赛季，有非常好的理由。他的适应能力和对赛道的自然感觉使他能够立即提供速度——正如他在波尔蒂马奥的‘对决’中所做的那样，这场对决为他赢得了这个席位——这可以帮助他在这周末克服所有这些困难。

    尽管在个人层面上我喜欢奥康，我觉得他现在的情况非常令人难过，但我从未觉得他的困境在公众舆论中引发了多数人的支持。

    现在我看到越来越多的极端和不准确的假设或声明被用来支持他，以及批评哈斯，尽管如此，但我感到有必要挑战这些，即使这可能让我看起来像是哈斯的同情者。

    它没有在奥康加入车队的前两年为他做得足够好，但我并不认为哈斯车队将他置于困境。因为数据上并没有显示车辆的不一致或性能差异，所以车队驳回了奥康关于这些问题的投诉。今年出现的问题不同了，哈斯车队可以看到它未能解决的问题。

    然而，我真的无法支持的是，哈斯车队方便地操纵了墨西哥发生的事情，以便提前从付费车手中获利。这对哈斯车队来说非常不方便。

    如果金钱是这里的优先事项，那么通过支付奥康的费用或让车队面临违约的法律风险来将他排除在外，同时通过更换第二辆车的车手来损害车队在锦标赛中锁定数百万美元位置的机会，这是没有意义的。

    Hirakawa在墨西哥表现不错，但他得到的分数非常幸运。Camara可能成为一名非常优秀的F1车手——我认为他是明年一个出色的意外之选——但他严重缺乏所需的准备和了解哈斯F1赛车的知识，以便在本赛季立即成为奥康的升级。

    我简直无法想象除了哈斯车队和车队总监小川明夫感到他们的手被逼得无处可去之外，还有其他任何原因会导致这种情况发生，对他们来说，在竞争方面最好的短期和长期策略就是尽快摆脱奥康，重新开始。

    给年轻车手一个机会

    我一直都是年轻车手的粉丝，并支持给他们机会。奥康已经得到了他的机会，是的，他赢得了一场比赛（有点运气），但总体来说，他的表现并不令人印象深刻。他与任何队友的赛道关系都值得怀疑，而他被告知将被替换后的态度更是证实了这一点。

    说‘我应属于F1’是一个很大的声明——没有人‘应属于’F1；表现和潜力应该让你在那里，并让你保持在那里，但无论他的情况如何，他在墨西哥练习中与车队关于轮胎分配的口头争执只是压垮棺材的最后一根钉子。

    在过去的几个赛季中，我们很高兴地看到了许多新车手的加入：奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂、奥利·贝尔曼、博托莱托、阿维德·林德布拉德和基米·安东内利，仅举几个例子。是的，不出所料，一些人在第一个赛季中遇到了困难，安东内利就是一个典型的例子，但他今年通过有说服力地赢得世界冠军证明了自己的潜力。

    卡马拉是下一个要加入这个名单的人。给他一个早些时候的开始来准备他2027赛季的到来是一个积极的决定。他可以了解F1周末的复杂性，然后有整个冬天的时间来消化这个情况，我相信这将使他在准备方面处于更好的位置。

    他需要从其他新秀车手和他们的第一个赛季中学习，并以此为鉴，因为罗马不是一天建成的。

    更重要的是，尽量不要像安东内利在他在蒙扎的第一场新秀FP1比赛中撞上护栏时那样做！要逐步适应，如果哈斯能提供所需的赛车，那么天空才是极限——正如基米在2026年所证明的那样。

    我们缺少一些关键细节，但从已经公开的信息来看，哈斯在整个过程中都表现出了极高的专业性。

    对于发布这样消息的团队来说，通过这种方式获得道德制高点很容易，但从哈斯发布的声明和其中小松的话中可以看出，他们不得不长时间深思熟虑才能决定让奥康提前离队的结果。

    如果将比较（非常）业余的体育运动，有一件事让我印象深刻，那就是F1车手在战斗激烈和战斗后的即时情况下能够保持冷静。

    想象一下，在足球比赛中，你向罚球区前进，却被对方拉倒，而且是一对一的情况下对方球员没有被出示黄牌；你接下来最想做的就是给对方一个回击——或者至少对这种不公正进行抱怨。

    这种比较并不是为了在这个场景中提供一个完美的报复性一对一比较，而是用来展示在这样的高风险情况下，驾驶舱内所发生的司机们设法不做出反应的挫败感。通常，为了他们所代表的那些人的更大利益，司机们往往能够做到这一点。

    奥康在墨西哥周五的无线电信息听起来非常不舒服。是的，这是一个为了自己的生计而战斗的车手。但他仍然是他所受雇的团队的代表。被迫失业，或者想要离开，你需要给任何潜在的雇主留下最好的印象。

    奥康的最后一周并没有足够的内容来成为他职业生涯的救命稻草，以防止他的一级方程式职业生涯可能结束的情况。这看起来更可能是最终的结束——以一种非常悲伤的方式，考虑到他所取得的成就。

    哈斯需要非常小心地管理这种动态。

    卡马拉在F1的黄金时期加入，刘易斯·汉密尔顿已经让法拉利的车手晋升计划陷入僵局，并表示他觉得自己可以一直赛车到2030年代。

    如果他真的做到了，那将会是一个很长的等待，因为比阿曼在2024年首次亮相时，看起来就像是法拉利的未来之星。

    哈斯需要做的是阻止哈斯F1车队陷入‘谁将取代刘易斯·汉密尔顿’的困境。

    卡马拉将对自己有信心，并相信他足够优秀，可以代表法拉利赛车，正如他应该的那样。

    贝尔曼会觉得自己在法拉利的座位旁边是应得的，并且不太可能对有人试图抓住这个机会感到高兴。

    也许他们会为了共同的目标而和谐地工作，为了团队的利益。但不可否认，从纸面上看，这是一套风险极高的阵容，具有重大影响。车队必须控制住绝望、决心和自我，以支持哈斯的成功，就像支持这些年轻车手一样。

    英文原文

    Esteban Ocon will not race for Haas again after being axed prematurely following his shock mid-weekend benching in Mexico, with 2027 driver Rafael Camara making his Formula 1 debut in Brazil this week.

    This is what we make of Ocon's plight, the fallout at Haas, and the decision to put Camara in the car at his home race instead of Mexico's point-scoring debutant Ryo Hirakawa again.

    I believe Haas has made the right choice in giving Camara an early F1 debut - but it's going to be one hell of a baptism of fire.

    Camara hasn't done an FP1 yet so this will be his F1 weekend debut.

    He has driven for Haas and Ferrari in private tests so he's not coming in completely cold but the pressure of a race weekend is very different - and this will be his first taste of 2026 machinery in real-life given those tests were in old cars.

    Then on top of all of that: this is Camara's home race.

    Gabriel Bortoleto, who was deep into a solid rookie season, really struggled under the weight of that pressure last year.

    Oh, and there's currently plenty of rain forecast for the weekend.

    So it's an enormously difficult set of circumstances to be making your debut in.

    But Haas picked Camara for 2027 for very good reasons. His adaptability and natural feel for the conditions that enables him to instantly deliver speed - as he did in the Portimao 'shootout' that won him the seat - could help him overcome all of that this weekend.

    Much as on a personal level I like Ocon and I find it very sad that it's come to this, I've never felt that his plight has triggered majority support in the court of public opinion.

    I have now seen more and more extreme and inaccurate assumptions or declarations being made in support of him and as criticism of Haas, though, which I feel compelled to challenge even at the risk of being seen as a Haas sympathiser.

    It has not done a good enough job for Ocon in his two years with the team, but I don't believe Haas hung him out to dry. It rebuffed Ocon's complaints about car inconsistencies or performance discrepancies because they didn't exist on the data - this year the problems are different, and Haas can see issues it has failed to resolve.

    The narrative I really can't get behind, though, is that Haas is conveniently manipulating whatever happened in Mexico, to cash in early on pay drivers. This is thoroughly inconvenient for Haas.

    If money's the priority here, it doesn't make sense to park Ocon by paying him off or leaving the team vulnerable to legal action for breach of contract - AND at the same time hurt its chances of sealing a multi-million dollar position in the championship by chopping and changing who is in the second car.

    Hirakawa did well in Mexico but the point he scored was very fortunate. And Camara could be a very good F1 driver - I think he's an excellent wildcard option for next year - but is sorely lacking the kind of preparation needed and the knowledge of Haas's F1 car needed to come in and immediately be an upgrade on Ocon this season.

    I just can't imagine a scenario in which this plays out for any reason other than Haas and team principal Ayao Komatsu feeling their hand's been forced, and that the best short-term and long-term play for the team competitively is to just get rid of Ocon and start fresh as soon as possible.

    Better to give a young driver the chance

    I have always been a fan of young drivers and giving them a chance. Ocon has had his and yes he won a race (with a bit of luck), but in general his performances have been unimpressive. His on-track relationship with any of his team-mates has been questionable and his attitude after being told he was being replaced just confirms this.

    To say 'I deserve to be in F1' is a big statement - nobody 'deserves' to be in F1; performance and potential should get you and keep you there, but no matter what his situation his verbal altercation with his team about his tyre allocation in practice in Mexico was just the nail in the coffin.

    In the last few seasons thankfully we have seen lots of new drivers: Oscar Piastri, Ollie Bearman, Bortoleto, Arvid Lindblad and Kimi Antonelli to name but a few. Yes, some unsurprisingly have struggled in the first season and Antonelli is a prime example, but he has proved his potential by convincingly winning the world championship this year.

    Camara is the next to add to that list. Giving him an early start to prepare him for the 2027 season is a positive decision. He can learn about the intricacies of an F1 race weekend and then have the winter to digest the situation and I am sure it will put him in a better place preparation-wise.

    He needs to learn from the other debut drivers and their first seasons and use this as an example that Rome wasn't built in a day.

    More importantly, try your best to not do an Antonelli when he threw it into the barrier in Monza in his first rookie FP1 appearance! Just build into it and if Haas can supply the car required then the sky's the limit - as Kimi has just proven in 2026.

    We are short of some crucial details but, from those that have been afforded, Haas has acted with the utmost professionalism throughout all of this.

    It's very easy for a team releasing such news to gain the moral high ground by extension but it is evident from Haas's statement, and Komatsu's words in it, that it had to deliberate long and hard over this outcome of Ocon being released early.

    If the comparison to (very) amateur sports allows, one thing that has always impressed me beyond comprehension is how composed F1 drivers are able to remain in the heat of battle and in the immediate aftermath of it.

    Imagine, if you will, advancing on the penalty box in a football game, just to be dragged down, while one-on-one, and not have your opposing player carded; the very next thing you want to do is have a pop at said opponent - or at the very least rant about the injustice.

    The comparison is not meant to serve as a perfect retaliatory like-for-like in this scenario, but it's drawn to illustrate the level of frustration, in such high-stakes situations, that goes on in the cockpit that drivers somehow manage to resist without reaction. Often, for the greater good of those they are representing.

    Ocon's radio message on Friday in Mexico made for a deeply uncomfortable listen. Yes, this is a driver fighting for his individual livelihood. But he is still a representative of the team he's employed by. Forced out of a job, or looking to leave, you need to give off the best impression possible to any prospective employers out there.

    What's turned out to be Ocon's final few weeks have not had enough of that about them to be a career lifeline and prevent what could be the end of his F1 career. This seems much more likely to be a definitive end - in a really sad way, considering what's been accomplished.

    Haas needs to manage this dynamic very, very carefully.

    Camara comes into an F1 at an absolutely pivotal stage. Lewis Hamilton has caused a log-jam in the Ferrari driver graduation scheme and has said he feels he could race into the 2030s.

    If he does, that will feel like a very long time since Bearman made his debut in 2024 and looked like Ferrari's bright future.

    What Haas has to do is to stop the Haas F1 team descending into the 'who will replace Lewis Hamilton' team.

    Camara will be confident in his own ability and believe he's good enough to race for Ferrari, as he should.

    Bearman will feel he's earned the seat next to Leclerc at Ferrari, and likely won't take kindly to someone trying to take that opportunity.

    Maybe they take this shared goal and work harmoniously for the good of the team. But you can't deny, on paper, this is a risky line-up with massive implications. The team has to contain the desperation, determination and egos involved to support Haas's success as much as these young drivers.
