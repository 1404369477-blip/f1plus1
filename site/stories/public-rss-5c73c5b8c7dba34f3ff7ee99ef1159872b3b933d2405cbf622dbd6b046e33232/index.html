    梅赛德斯在巴西大奖赛加冕2026赛季F1车队总冠军

    梅赛德斯在英特拉格斯举行的巴西大奖赛上锁定2026赛季F1车队总冠军，这是车队第九次夺得该头衔。安东内利与拉塞尔分别从第二和第七位发车，拉塞尔因罚退五位发车。

    梅赛德斯在巴西大奖赛加冕2026赛季F1车队总冠军

    梅赛德斯在巴西大奖赛上锁定其第九次F1车队总冠军

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发与比赛分析。

    梅赛德斯赢得了2026年一级方程式锦标赛的车队总冠军，在英特拉格斯举行的巴西大奖赛上锁定了这一头衔。

    基米·安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔分别从第二位和第七位发车，此前这位英国车手被罚退后五位发车。虽然安东内利在比赛起步时从杆位得主兰多·诺里斯手中夺得领先，并在前七圈将优势扩大到五秒，但他在第20圈的第一弯打滑，给了诺里斯重新夺回位置的机会。

    在比赛最后几圈的大雨后，拉塞尔和安东内利分别以第四和第五名完赛，这足以让车队领先于法拉利，后者的车手夏尔·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿分别获得第七和第九名。

    “我们锁定了总冠军。我认为这是最重要的事情。感谢你们挺了过来！”梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫在方格旗挥动后通过无线电对拉塞尔说道。

    这位英国人回应道：“干得好，各位。很高兴看到我们今年取得的成就。”

    兰多·诺里斯赢得了巴西大奖赛，迈凯伦车手与红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘和伊萨克·哈贾尔一同登上领奖台。

    梅赛德斯抵达圣保罗时领先法拉利126分，需要带着129分的领先优势离开。

    这个冠军为车队完成了年度双冠，一周前安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛上锁定车手总冠军，成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。

    2026年的总冠军是这支位于布拉克利的车队第九次获得制造商冠军，此前他们已在2014、2015、2016、2017、2018、2019、2020和2021年夺冠。

    沃尔夫随后与印地赛车系列赛前车手、F1 TV评论员詹姆斯·欣奇克利夫讨论了这一成就。

    “首先，英特拉格斯和圣保罗的每一个人，这真是一场盛宴。感谢你们的款待。太棒了，”他说。

    “赛季开局非常强劲，这让我们冲过了终点线，但最近几场比赛有时也颇具挑战。今天很艰难，但总体而言，我为这个组织感到无比自豪。

    “我们花了几年时间才重返榜首。没有多少车队能够从那样的处境中恢复过来，这恰恰说明了我们组织内部的出色工作和优秀人才，能成为他们中的一员真是太棒了。”

    Mercedes crowned 2026 F1 constructors' champion at Brazilian GP

    Mercedes secured its ninth Formula 1 constructors' championship at the Brazilian Grand Prix

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes has won the 2026 Formula 1 constructors' championship, sealing the title at the Brazilian Grand Prix at Interlagos.

    Kimi Antonelli and George Russell started second and seventh on the grid, after the Briton took a five-place grid penalty. While Antonelli took the lead from polesitter Lando Norris at the start of the race and extended his advantage to five seconds over the first seven laps, he later spun into Turn 1 on lap 20, giving Norris the opportunity to retake the position.

    After heavy rainfall in the closing laps of the race, Russell and Antonelli finished fourth and fifth, which was enough to put the team out of reach of Ferrari, whose drivers Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton finished seventh and ninth, respectively.

    "We secured the championship. I think this is the most important [thing]. Thank you for surviving!" Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff told Russell over the radio after the chequered flag.

    The Briton responded: "Well done, everybody. It's great to see what we have achieved this year."

    Lando Norris won the Brazilian Grand Prix, and the McLaren driver was joined on the podium by Red Bull's Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar.

    Mercedes arrived in São Paulo 126 points clear of Ferrari and needed to leave with a lead of 129 points.

    The title completes a championship double for the team, one week after Antonelli clinched the drivers' crown at the Mexican Grand Prix to become the youngest world champion in the history of F1.

    The 2026 title is the Brackley outfit's ninth constructors' championship, after already winning in 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2021.

    Wolff later discussed the achievement with former IndyCar Series driver and F1 TV pundit James Hinchcliffe.

    "First of all, everybody here at Interlagos and in São Paulo, it has been a real blast. Thank you for having us. It's amazing," he said.

    "It was a very strong beginning of the season and that got us over the line, but also these last few races have been challenging at times. Today, it was difficult, but overall, I'm so proud of the organisation.

    "We've taken a few years to be back on the top spot. Not many teams have been able to come back from such a situation, and it just shows the great work, the great people that we have in our organisation, and it's fantastic to be among them."
