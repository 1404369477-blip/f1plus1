    安东内利将在巴西大奖赛上佩戴特别头盔纪念塞纳

    新晋F1世界冠军安东内利将在巴西大奖赛上佩戴特别头盔，以纪念他的赛车英雄塞纳。

    安东内利将在巴西大奖赛上佩戴特别头盔纪念塞纳

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析。

    安东内利将在本周末作为2026年世界冠军抵达Interlagos赛道，并计划利用这个机会向他的赛车英雄塞纳致敬。

    梅赛德斯车手确认他将在巴西大奖赛上佩戴一顶特别的头盔，以纪念塞纳，纪念这位巴西车手首次在家乡赢得比赛以来的35年。

    "我将为巴西准备一顶特别头盔，为了埃伊尔顿，" 安东内利在墨西哥对媒体说。

    "也因为这将是他第一次在第六档赢得家乡比赛的35周年。所以，是的，这将是一顶为巴西、为埃伊尔顿的纪念头盔，我认为这是我制作过的最好的头盔之一。

    ““所以，希望你们大家，希望你们会喜欢它。但是，是的，这将是一个非常特别的周末。我非常期待它，因为巴西的支持一直很棒，不仅今年如此，去年也是如此。”"

    “是的，我期待着回到那里，希望这将是一个美好的周末。”

    安东内利在父亲的陪伴下，通过DVD观看旧F1赛事长大，去年他对媒体表示，塞纳是所有车手中最突出的一个。他也以12号车手身份在莲花车队和1988年他在麦克拉伦车队赢得第一个冠军赛季中参赛。

    成为自那时以来首位以那个车号赢得冠军的车手后，他将保持12号车号至2027年，而不是换成1号。

    “我认为我会继续使用12号，”安东内利在墨西哥对媒体说，“我会继续使用12号，因为显然，这个号码对我来说非常特别。”

    他补充说：“这也是我保持谦逊、脚踏实地的一种方式。我觉得保持12号让我有一个新的起点，再次从头开始，并努力做得更好。”

    Kimi Antonelli to honour Ayrton Senna with special helmet at Brazilian GP

    Newly crowned Formula 1 champion Kimi Antonelli will honour Ayrton Senna with a special helmet at the Brazilian Grand Prix

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Kimi Antonelli will arrive at Interlagos as the 2026 world champion this weekend, and he plans to use the occasion to pay tribute to his racing hero, Ayrton Senna.

    The Mercedes driver has confirmed he will wear a special helmet at the Brazilian Grand Prix in honour of Senna, marking 35 years since the Brazilian driver won his home race for the first time.

    "I will have a special helmet for Brazil, for Ayrton," Antonelli told the media in Mexico.

    "Also because it’s going to be 35 years since he won in sixth gear, his home race. So, yeah, it’s going to be a tribute helmet for Brazil, for Ayrton, and I think it’s one of my best helmets that I’ve made.

    “So hopefully you guys, everyone, hopefully you will like it. But, yeah, it’s going to be a very special weekend. I’m very much looking forward to it because the support from Brazil in general has been incredible, not only this year but already from last year.

    “So, yeah, looking forward to going back there, and hopefully it’s going to be a great weekend."

    Antonelli grew up watching old F1 races on DVD with his father and told the media last year that Senna "was the driver who stood out among them all". He also races with the number 12 that Senna used at Lotus and in his first title-winning season at McLaren in 1988.

    Having become the first driver to win the championship with that race number since then, he will keep it for 2027 instead of switching to number 1.

    "I think I'll stay with number 12," Antonelli told the media in Mexico. "I'll stay with number 12 because, obviously, it's a very special number for me."

    He added: "It's also a way for me to stay grounded, to stay humble. I feel like keeping number 12 allows me to have a new point where to start and to build on everything again and try to improve more."
