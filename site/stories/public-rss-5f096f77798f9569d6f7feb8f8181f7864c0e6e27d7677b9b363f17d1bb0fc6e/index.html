    安东内利与梅赛德斯包揽卡塔尔大奖赛练习赛

    基米·安东内利在卡塔尔大奖赛FP3练习赛中连续三次夺冠，梅赛德斯竞争对手似乎在争夺最佳非梅赛德斯车队，红牛在第三次练习赛中取得第三名。

    在排位赛前的20分钟内，练习赛中的圈数非常少。似乎很少有车手能在他们的第一次排位赛中取得更好的成绩。

    安东内利的队友乔治·拉塞尔在第二阶段的表现中保持了领先，尽管他在著名的14号弯角发生了一次打滑，那里的地板损坏总是令人担忧，这使安东内利得以缩小到落后0.017秒。

    但在第三次尝试中，安东内利全速前进，逆转了排名，领先拉塞尔0.249秒，这个成绩无人能及。

    梅赛德斯的车手在任何时刻都落后了半秒左右，红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘在练习结束时排名第三，落后安东内利0.510秒。

    维斯塔潘的队友伊萨克·哈德贾排名第四，领先略有提升的法拉利车手查尔斯·勒克莱尔，勒克莱尔在周五的比赛中表现不佳，非常悲观。

    勒克莱尔的队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿排名第九，抱怨赛车转向不足，并要求增加前翼角度以避免提前进站。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂在最后一次性能测试跑之前排名第三，但在第六和第七位时被麦凯轮车队的兰多·诺里斯超越，最终排名下降。

    皮亚斯特里是众多抱怨转向不足的车手之一。

    小红牛在周五表现得非常乐观，并在FP3练习中领跑中游，Arvid Lindblad排名第八，劳森排名第十，领先其中游对手两十分。

    阿尔派恩落后小红牛很多，小红牛在车队积分榜上领先Enstone车队27分，争夺第五名。加斯利在FP3练习中排名第14，队友科拉平托排名第16。

    科拉平托在最后阶段幸运地避开了高速移动的斯特罗尔，这一事件将在练习结束后进行调查。

    在劳森和加斯利之间是奥迪和哈斯车队的奥利·贝尔曼，他的队友拉斐尔·卡马拉排名第15。

    费尔南多·阿隆索超越了两辆威廉姆斯赛车，为阿斯顿·马丁赢得了第17名。

    凯迪拉克车队以瓦尔特里·博塔斯领先近0.7秒的成绩，排在最后一名塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯之前。

    第17名 费尔南多·阿隆索（阿斯顿·马丁）+2.696秒

    英文原文

    Kimi Antonelli completed a hat-trick of practice sweeps in FP3 for the Qatar Grand Prix, as Mercedes' rivals appear to be fighting for the best of the rest with Red Bull taking that honour in third practice.

    Very few laps were turned in the first 20 minutes or so, before qualifying runs. It appeared that few drivers could improve on their first qualifying efforts.

    Antonelli's team-mate George Russell set the early pace and maintained that lead after the second performance runs despite a wide moment through the notorious Turn 14 - where floor damage is always a worry - and that allowed Antonelli to close in to just 0.017s behind.

    But on the third attempts, Antonelli turned on the afterburners to flip the order and lead Russell by 0.249s, which would not be beaten.

    Mercedes' challengers were regularly around half a second down at any given point, with Red Bull's Max Verstappen taking third at the end of the session, 0.510s adrift of Antonelli.

    Verstappen's team-mate Isack Hadjar took fourth ahead of the slightly improved Ferrari of Charles Leclerc, who was very pessimistic after Friday's running.

    Leclerc's team-mate Lewis Hamilton was down in ninth, complaining that the car was not turning in sufficiently and asking for more front wing angle to avoid understeer.

    Oscar Piastri had been third before the last performance run attempt, but was jumped by McLaren team-mate Lando Norris as they ran sixth and seventh, shuffled down the order by the end.

    Piastri was another of the many drivers complaining of understeer.

    Racing Bulls was extremely bullish on Friday and did head the midfield in FP3 with Arvid Lindblad in eighth and Liam Lawson in 10th, two tenths clear of its midfield rivals.

    Alpine was well behind Racing Bulls, which is 27 points ahead of the Enstone team in the chase for fifth in the constructors' standings. Pierre Gasly finished 14th and team-mate Franco Colapinto 16th in FP3.

    Colapinto was lucky to avoid a slow-moving Lance Stroll at high speed in the closing stages and that incident will be investigated after the session.

    Between Lawson and Gasly were the Audis and Haas driver Ollie Bearman, whose team-mate Rafael Camara finished 15th.

    Fernando Alonso cleared both Williams to take 17th for Aston Martin.

    Cadillac anchored the order with Valtteri Bottas nearly 0.7s ahead of last-place Sergio Perez.
