    安东内利谈访问塞纳家族庄园：非常感人

    2026年F1冠军安东内利在巴西大奖赛前夕，分享了他近期访问塞纳家族庄园的经历。

    基米·安东内利在参观阿yrton Senna家族庄园时情绪激动

    2026年一级方程式冠军基米·安东内利在巴西大奖赛之前回顾了其对阿yrton Senna家族住宅的一次情感之旅

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    2026年一级方程式冠军基米·安东内利在周末的巴西大奖赛之前，对其最近访问阿yrton Senna家族住宅的经历进行了分享。

    梅赛德斯车手在墨西哥赢得F1冠军后的第二天，于周二前往了塞纳家族的Fazenda Dois Lagos庄园。他分享了一张自己坐在1986年塞纳驾驶过的Lotus 98T赛车中的照片，并对这个“难忘的一天”表示感谢。

    在Interlagos赛道接受媒体采访时，安东内利表示，这次经历比他预期的还要感人。

    “我真的很感动，”意大利人说道。“一个非常特别的日子，一个我会永远记住的日子。这就是为什么我会永远感激和感谢塞纳家族，因为他们让我看到了这一切，甚至让我坐进了那辆车，这非常特别。

    “那是一个美妙的日子。我实际上并不想离开。我尽量在那里待得尽可能久，因为这是一次如此美妙的体验。塞纳家族非常开放地展示了Ayrton曾经大部分时间都在那里度过的地方。想到这里真是太疯狂了。”

    “当我身处那里时，我简直不敢相信，阿yrton 真的来过这里。他曾在房间里度过时光。”这是一段非常感人的、非常特别的一天。

    安东内利在这次参观中得到了他的父亲 Marco、塞纳的侄子布鲁诺以及侄女比安卡和拉拉利的陪伴。布鲁诺后来在 Instagram 上感谢这位 20 岁的年轻人“赛道教学课”，因为地产自己就有卡丁车赛道。

    他本周末也将驾驶一辆特别致敬塞纳的头盔赛车，纪念巴西人在 1991 年首次在 Interlagos 赢得胜利的 35 周年。

    “这对我的意义很大，”安东内利在社交媒体上与特别头盔设计一同发布。 “我本周末的特别头盔！向我的偶像和巴西致敬。希望你们喜欢它，就像我喜欢它一样。”

    Kimi Antonelli opens up on "very emotional" visit to Ayrton Senna's family estate

    2026 Formula 1 champion Kimi Antonelli has reflected on an emotional visit to Ayrton Senna's family home ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    2026 Formula 1 champion Kimi Antonelli has opened up about his recent visit to Ayrton Senna's family home ahead of this weekend's Brazilian Grand Prix.

    The Mercedes driver went to Fazenda Dois Lagos, the Senna family estate, on Tuesday, two days after clinching the F1 title in Mexico. He shared a photo of himself sitting in the Lotus 98T that Senna raced in 1986 and thanked the family for an "unforgettable day".

    Speaking to the media at Interlagos, Antonelli said the experience had been even more emotional than he expected it to be.

    "I was truly emotional," the Italian said. "A very, very special day, a day that I will remember forever. That's why I will always be thankful and grateful towards the Senna family for allowing me to see that, and to even sit in the car, which was very special.

    "It was an amazing day. I actually didn't want to leave. I tried to stay as long as I could there, because it was such a nice experience. The Senna family was very open about showing places where Ayrton used to spend most of his time. It was crazy to think.

    "When I was there, I was like, 'Wow, Ayrton was actually here. He was spending time in his room.' It was very emotional and a very, very special day."

    Antonelli was joined for the visit by his father, Marco, as well as Senna's nephew Bruno and nieces Bianca and Lalalli. Bruno later thanked the 20-year-old on Instagram for "the karting lesson", with the estate having its own kart track.

    He will also race this weekend with a special tribute helmet to Senna, 35 years since the Brazilian's first home win at Interlagos in 1991.

    "This one means a lot to me," Antonelli posted on social media alongside the special helmet design. "My special helmet for the weekend! A tribute to my idol and Brazil. I hope you guys like it as much as I do."
