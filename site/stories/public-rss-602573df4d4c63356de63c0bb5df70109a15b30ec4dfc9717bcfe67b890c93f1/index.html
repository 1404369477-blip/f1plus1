    安东内利获塞纳家族赠礼

    安东内利透露，他在巴西大奖赛前拜访塞纳家族庄园时收到了塞纳家族赠送的特别礼物。

    安东内利在F1巴西大奖赛前透露了塞纳家族赠予的特别礼物

    安东内利透露了他在拜访塞纳家族巴西庄园期间收到的特别礼物

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    安东内利透露，他在本周末巴西大奖赛前拜访塞纳家族庄园时，收到了塞纳家族赠送的礼物。

    这位梅赛德斯车手于周二前往塔图伊的Fazenda Dois Lagos，两天前他刚刚锁定自己的首个一级方程式锦标赛冠军，并被拍到驾驶塞纳在1986年参赛的Lotus 98T。

    在接受意大利媒体《米兰体育报》采访时，这位20岁的车手表示，他F1偶像的家人让他自由参观庄园，还送了他几件带回家的礼物。

    “那非常令人动容，”安东内利解释道，“塞纳一家张开双臂欢迎我，让我参观整个庄园，在那里的赛道上开卡丁车，还乘坐了他1986年的Lotus。

    “他们送了我很多礼物：T恤、赛道上那座纪念他的雕像的复制品，甚至还有一件棕色皮夹克，是艾尔顿一直穿的那件的复制品。”

    安东内利在本周末向这位巴西车手致敬，因为距离他在1991年首次赢得主场大奖赛已经过去了35年。

    “我想在他的主场大奖赛上向他致敬，我必须说结果令人难以置信。实物看起来比我之前看到的照片要好得多，”他说。“所以，当我第一次拿起它时，我也很激动。我迫不及待想在赛道上使用它。”

    安东内利在英特拉格斯的第一节练习赛中获得了第二名。虽然他已经锁定了车手冠军，但梅赛德斯尚未锁定车队冠军。这支位于布拉克利的车队目前以630分领跑，有机会在本周末的巴西大奖赛上夺得冠军。

    法拉利目前以504分排名第二，红牛以382分排名第三。

    Kimi Antonelli reveals special gifts from Ayrton Senna's family ahead of F1 Brazilian GP

    Kimi Antonelli has revealed the special gifts he received from Ayrton Senna's family during a visit to their Brazilian estate

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Kimi Antonelli has revealed the gifts he received from Ayrton Senna's family during his visit to their estate ahead of this weekend's Brazilian Grand Prix.

    The Mercedes driver went to Fazenda Dois Lagos in Tatuí on Tuesday, two days after securing his maiden Formula 1 championship, and was photographed in the Lotus 98T that Senna raced in 1986.

    During an interview with Italian publication La Gazzetta dello Sport, the 20-year-old said the family of his F1 idol had given him the run of the estate and several gifts to take home.

    "It was very emotional," Antonelli explained. "The Senna family welcomed me with open arms, letting me tour the entire estate, go karting on the track there, and ride in his 1986 Lotus.

    "They gave me so many gifts: T-shirts, a replica of a statue in his honour that's on the circuit and even a brown leather jacket that's a replica of the one Ayrton always wore."

    Antonelli is paying homage to the Brazilian driver this weekend, as it is 35 years since he won his first home grand prix in 1991.

    "I wanted something to pay homage to him at his home grand prix, and I have to say the result is incredible. It looks much better in person than in the photographs I'd seen," he said. "So, when I first picked it up, I was thrilled too. I can't wait to use it on the track."

    Antonelli finished second in the first practice session at Interlagos. While he has already secured the drivers' title, Mercedes is yet to clinch the constructors' championship. The Brackley outfit currently leads with 630 points and has the opportunity to claim the title at this weekend's Brazilian Grand Prix.

    Ferrari is currently second with 504 points and Red Bull is third with 382 points.
