    汉密尔顿专属看台回归2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛

    汉密尔顿的专属看台将回归2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛，为粉丝提供独家商品和更广泛的赛事区域访问权限。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿的看台将回归拉斯维加斯大奖赛，为粉丝提供更广泛的访问权限

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿的专属看台将回归2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛

    法拉利车迷们。最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，车手更新，技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远处的赛事反应

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿的看台将回归2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛，该套餐提供独家商品和更广泛的赛事粉丝区访问权限。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿的看台门票包括在西和谐区的指定座位，包含啤酒在内的全套服务，在喜力舞台参加现场娱乐和车手采访，观看所有赛道上的各节练习，包括一级方程式和F1学院，在西和谐区、科瓦尔区和东和谐区的粉丝活动，以及进入F1学院的维修区。

    门票可供选择单日通行证或包含限量版汉密尔顿商品的三日套餐。

    单日门票起价99美元，三日套餐起价983美元。

    拉斯维加斯街道赛道于2023年加入赛历。汉密尔顿在第一年获得第七名，2024年获得第二名，2025年获得第八名。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛，定于11月19日至21日举行，标志着本赛季最后一场三连赛的开始。如果最后两场比赛按计划进行，那么拉斯维加斯之后将是卡塔尔大奖赛，赛季将在阿布扎比大奖赛中结束。

    今年早些时候，在著名的内华达城市举办的赛事签署了一份为期10年的合同延期，以确保至少到2037年都能出现在F1赛历上。

    “我们非常激动地宣布，一级方程式将继续在拉斯维加斯举办多年，”F1首席执行官斯蒂法诺·多梅尼卡利在宣布时表示。

    “自2023年首次亮相以来，该赛事就非常出色，迅速成为顶级赛车、世界级娱乐、全球商业领袖、一线明星和影响者首选的目的地。”

    “我们一直相信拉斯维加斯将成为我们在美国市场的重要基石，这次扩展加上近年来的成功，进一步强化了我们对这个重要市场的长期承诺。”

    Lewis Hamilton grandstand returns to Las Vegas GP with expanded access for fans

    Lewis Hamilton's dedicated grandstand will return for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton's own grandstand is returning for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, with the package offering exclusive merchandise and wider access to the event's fan zones.

    Lewis Hamilton Grandstand tickets include an assigned seat in the West Harmon Zone, all-inclusive beer, access to live entertainment and driver interviews at the Heineken Stage, viewing of all on-track sessions including Formula 1 and F1 Academy, access to fan activations in the West Harmon Zone, Koval Zone and East Harmon Zone, and access to the F1 Academy paddock.

    The tickets are available as single-day passes or a three-day package that also includes a limited-edition Hamilton merchandise item.

    Single-day tickets start from $99, and the three-day package starts at $983.

    The Las Vegas street circuit joined the calendar in 2023. Hamilton finished seventh in the first year, second in 2024 and eighth in 2025.

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix, scheduled for 19-21 November, kicks off the final triple-header of the season. Assuming the final two races go ahead as planned, Vegas will be followed by the Qatar Grand Prix before the season concludes at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix.

    The event in the famous Nevada city signed a 10-year contract extension to remain on the F1 calendar until at least 2037 earlier this year.

    "We are thrilled that Formula 1 will continue racing in Las Vegas for many years ​to come," F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali said in a statement at the time of the announcement.

    "Since its debut in ​2023, the event has been extraordinary, rapidly establishing itself as ⁠a premier destination for great racing, ⁠world-class entertainment, global business leaders, A-list celebrities and influencers.

    "We always believed that Las Vegas would become a cornerstone ‌of our presence in the United States, and this extension, together with the success of recent years, reinforces our long-term commitment to this important market."
