    劳森确信2027年留在F1，小红牛官宣车手阵容

    利亚姆·劳森表示，在与小红牛续约后，他几乎没有怀疑过自己会留在2027年F1发车格上。上周四，小红牛宣布劳森与新秀队友阿尔维德·林德布拉德将组成下赛季的车手阵容。

    劳森确信2027年留在F1，尽管有尼古拉·措洛夫的传闻

    尽管红牛青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫作为F2新秀表现出色，利亚姆·劳森上周仍被宣布为小红牛2027年F1车手。

    利亚姆·劳森确认，在最近与小红牛续约后，他几乎没有怀疑过自己会留在2027年F1发车格上。

    这位24岁的车手和新秀队友阿尔维德·林德布拉德上周四被宣布为这支意大利车队下赛季的车手阵容，此前他们在2026年表现非常强劲。

    劳森在车手积分榜上排名第九，以职业生涯最佳的89分领先于排名第11的林德布拉德的38分，这意味着小红牛有望在还剩三站的情况下获得第五名。

    尽管林德布拉德的二年级赛季几乎板上钉钉，但考虑到小红牛培养红牛下一位青训车手的宗旨，人们对劳森的未来仍存疑虑。

    那个人就是尼古拉·措洛夫，他在二级方程式（F2）中排名第二，而随着劳森转投他处，他本可以获得比明年已获得的储备车手角色更多的机会。

    但当在本周末拉斯维加斯大奖赛前被问及是否怀疑自己在2027年发车阵容中的位置时，劳森回答说：“并没有。如果回顾去年，我觉得我当时想了很多。”

    “你总是会有很多疑虑，尤其是在事情不顺利的时候。但我觉得对我们来说，今年各方面都相当强劲。

    “作为一支车队，我们的情况真的非常非常好。我们在所有赛道上都拥有一辆强劲的赛车，而且我觉得我们大多数周末都执行得很好。

    “所以我不觉得自己难以找到原因，但显然你会去想它，而且它也被谈论得很多。

    “但我觉得最重要的是，尽量把那种思绪从脑海中清除出去，尽量专注于并享受驾驶赛车。我觉得我们做到了这一点。”

    劳森确实指出，虽然红牛的姊妹车队传统上只启用新秀或二年级车手，但这位新西兰人自己仍然相对缺乏经验，只参加过55场大奖赛。

    尽管他在2023年完成F1首秀后先后为红牛和小红牛效力，但2026年只是他第二个完整赛季，所以这支车队并没有完全违背自己的信条。

    但这并不意味着他打算长期留队——措洛夫很可能在2028年获得提拔——只是说重点在于保持势头，让车队取得史上最佳成绩——此前曾四次获得第六名。

    “至少今年，重点显然一直放在这个第五名以及进入明年上，”劳森补充道。

    利亚姆·劳森，小红牛（小红牛），伊萨克·哈贾尔，红牛车队

    “对我们所有人来说，我们都在努力在这项运动中获胜。我显然对未来一无所知，但这就是我们所有人努力的方向。”

    “从技术上讲，今年是我在F1的第二个完整赛季，所以我觉得自己作为一名F1车手并没有非常丰富的经验，但显然，在今年下半年我感觉好多了，并且随着经验的积累越来越好。”

    “但未来无论如何，当你在这支车队时，无论是走向红牛还是其他地方，最终转会到别处显然是自然的进程。”

    “可以这么说，我并不打算在VCARB结束我的职业生涯，但目前我们有一辆很棒的赛车，而且我们度过了一个非常出色的赛季。

    “所以我会努力从每个比赛周末中榨取出一切，并保持这种势头，显然现在我有机会在明年继续这样做，我会努力保持下去。”

    Liam Lawson had little doubt he would remain for F1 2027 amid Nikola Tsolov talk

    Liam Lawson was announced last week as a Racing Bulls driver for F1 2027 despite Red Bull protege Nikola Tsolov impressing as an F2 rookie

    Liam Lawson has confirmed he had little doubt that he would remain on the Formula 1 grid for 2027 after recently extending his Racing Bulls contract.

    The 24-year-old and rookie team-mate Arvid Lindblad were announced last Thursday as the Italian outfit’s driver pairing for next season following a very strong 2026.

    Lawson is ninth in the drivers’ standings with a career-best 89 points compared to 38 for Lindblad in 11th, meaning Racing Bulls is on track to finish fifth with three rounds left.

    Although Lindblad’s sophomore year was a near certainty, doubt lingered over Lawson’s future considering Racing Bulls’ mantra of training Red Bull’s next junior driver.

    That is Nikola Tsolov, who lies second in Formula 2, and he could have been given more than the reserve driver role he has earned for next year with Lawson heading elsewhere.

    But when asked ahead of this weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix whether he doubted his position on the 2027 grid, Lawson replied: “Not really. If I look back to last year, I feel like I was thinking about it a lot.

    “You have a lot of doubts, always, especially when things aren't going well. But I feel like for us this year, things have been just quite strong across the board.

    “Things have been really, really good for us as a team. We've had a strong car across all the tracks, and I feel like we've executed mostly each weekend.

    “So I didn't feel like I struggled to find a reason why, but obviously you think about it and it's talked about a lot.

    “But I feel like more than anything, just tried to remove that sort of thought process from my mind as much as I could and just try to focus and enjoy driving the car.  I felt like we were able to do that.”

    Lawson did point out that while Red Bull’s sister squad traditionally employs only rookies or sophomores, the Kiwi is still relatively inexperienced himself with 55 grand prix starts.

    Although he has driven for both Red Bull and Racing Bulls after making his F1 debut in 2023, 2026 was only his second, full season so the squad isn’t exactly contradicting its mantra.

    But that doesn’t mean he is looking to stay put for the long-term - Tsolov will likely be promoted for 2028 - just that the focus was carrying momentum to give the team its best-ever finish - sixth on four occasions.

    “This year at least, the focus has obviously been heavily on just this P5 and going into next year,” added Lawson.

    Liam Lawson, Racing Bulls, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    “For all of us, we're all trying to win in the sport. I obviously have no idea in the future, but that's what we're all working towards.

    “This year technically, it's my second full season in Formula 1, so I feel like I'm not hugely experienced as a Formula 1 driver, but I feel a lot better, obviously, towards the second half of this year and getting better with experience.

    “But in the future, in any case, when you're in this team, whether it's towards Red Bull or somewhere else, it is the natural progression to obviously eventually move on somewhere else.

    “It's fair to say that I'm not looking to end my career at VCARB, but at the moment we have a great car and we've had a really good season.

    “So I'm trying to extract everything out of every weekend and keep that going and obviously now I have the opportunity to do that next year, and I'll try and keep doing that.”
