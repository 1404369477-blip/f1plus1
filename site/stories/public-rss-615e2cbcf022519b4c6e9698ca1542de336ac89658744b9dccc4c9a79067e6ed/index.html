    最后一辆梅赛德斯-Benz SLR 迈凯伦 Stirling Moss以超过600万美元的价格拍卖

    最后一辆出厂的梅赛德斯-Benz SLR 迈凯伦 Stirling Moss再次上市，RM Sotheby's将其估值超过600万美元。

    最终版的奔驰SLR麦拉伦斯特林·莫斯将拍卖超过600万美元

    最后一辆生产的奔驰SLR麦拉伦斯特林·莫斯即将由RM Sotheby's进行拍卖

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    最后一辆离开工厂的奔驰SLR麦拉伦斯特林·莫斯重返市场，RM Sotheby's将其无顶跑车估值超过600万美元（450万英镑）。

    这辆底盘编号为75的赛车正在拍卖行的在线密封12月拍卖中，拍卖从11月25日开始，至12月2日结束。

    梅赛德斯-奔驰和迈凯轮从2009年6月到2009年12月底共制造了75辆斯特林·莫斯版SLR，作为SLR的最终版本。它没有车顶、挡风玻璃或侧窗，其650马力的涡轮增压V8发动机使其最高时速达到350公里/小时。当时它的价格为75万欧元，仅向现有的SLR车主提供。

    根据RM Sotheby's的说法，这辆车是科威特王室订购的新车，于2009年12月完成，并于2010年3月在瑞士首次注册。没有其他梅赛德斯-奔驰SLR 迈凯伦斯特林·莫斯车型采用火蛋白红漆。它还配有红色皮革内饰和黄色缝线、金色刹车卡钳和立体声音响，据信只有另一辆同型号的车辆从新时就拥有这些配置。

    它最后一次易手是在2024年10月6日于比利时Knokke-Heist的Bonhams Zoute拍卖会上，当时以无底价322万欧元成交。

    新车主将其运往美国，在加利福尼亚的G&K Automotive公司根据展示或展示规则将其变为合法上路车辆，该规则允许少量具有历史意义的汽车进口。

    "该车辆于2009年12月由工厂完成，按照欧洲标准制造，并全新交付至瑞士，首次注册于2010年3月16日，在沃州注册。" 列表中总结道。"自那时以来，新车仅行驶了32.9英里。"

    RM Sotheby's 拍卖会上还有其他有趣的汽车，包括2009年法拉利F430 Spider、2014年奔驰SLS AMG Black Series和2018年保时捷911 Turbo Classic Series "Project Gold"。

    Final Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren Stirling Moss heads to auction for over $6million

    The final Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren Stirling Moss ever built is heading to auction with RM Sotheby's

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    The final Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren Stirling Moss to leave the factory is back on the market, with RM Sotheby's valuing the roofless speedster at more than $6million (£4.5million).

    The car, chassis number 75, is in the auction house's online Sealed December sale, with bidding open from 25 November until 2 December.

    Mercedes and McLaren built 75 Stirling Moss models from June 2009 until the end of December 2009 as the final version of the SLR. It has no roof, windscreen or side windows, and its 650-horsepower supercharged V8 gives a top speed of 350km/h. It was priced at €750,000 at the time and was only offered to existing SLR owners.

    According to RM Sotheby's, this car was ordered new by Kuwait's royal family, finished in December 2009 and first registered in Switzerland in March 2010. No other Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren Stirling Moss features Fire Opal Red paint. It also has a red leather interior with yellow stitching, gold brake callipers and a stereo, which only one other example of the model is believed to have had from new.

    It last changed hands at Bonhams' Zoute sale in Knokke-Heist, Belgium, on 6 October 2024, when it sold for €3.22million without reserve.

    The new owner shipped it to the United States, where it has just been made road-legal by G&K Automotive in California under the Show or Display rule, which lets small numbers of historically significant cars be imported.

    "Completed by the factory in December of 2009, the car was built to European specifications and delivered new to Switzerland, where it was first registered on 16 March 2010 in the canton of Vaud," the listing concluded. "Since then, it has been driven just 32.9 miles from new."

    Other interesting cars up for auction with RM Sotheby's include a 2009 Ferrari F430 Spider, a 2014 Mercedes-Benz SLS AMG Black Series and a 2018 Porsche 911 Turbo Classic Series "Project Gold".
