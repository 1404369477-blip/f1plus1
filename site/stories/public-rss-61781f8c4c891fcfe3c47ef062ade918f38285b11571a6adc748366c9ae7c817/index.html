    安东内利首张卡牌拍卖价高达87.84万美元，F1冠军在望

    一张独一无二的安东内利首张卡牌在拍卖中以87.84万美元的价格成交，成为有史以来第二贵的F1卡牌，就在这位梅赛德斯车手有望成为世界冠军的前夕。

    基米·安东内利的新秀卡以惊人的878,400美元的价格售出，在F1冠军机会之前

    独一无二的基米·安东内利新秀卡在拍卖中以878,400美元的价格成交，成为有史以来第二贵的F1交易卡

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    独一无二的基米·安东内利新秀卡在拍卖中以878,400美元的价格成交，成为有史以来第二贵的F1卡片，就在梅赛德斯车手可能成为世界冠军的前几天。

    这张卡片，2025年Topps Chrome F1 Chrome Autographs SuperFractor编号1/1，由PSA评定为GEM MINT 10级，在经过34次竞价后，通过Goldin在10月17日成交。

    这张卡片仅此一张。它拥有安东内利的蓝色墨水签名和Topps的黄金SuperFractor工艺，Goldin将其描述为他一级方程式生涯中的‘圣杯’。

    根据《体育画报》报道的Card Ladder数据，这张卡片是迄今为止唯一一张售价更高的F1卡片：2020年Topps Chrome F1 SuperFractor 1/1的刘易斯·汉密尔顿卡片，PSA评分为7，2022年5月通过Goldin以90万美元的价格成交。安东内利的卡片仅比这一记录少了21,600美元。

    这次拍卖是该周任何体育卡片的最高成交价，是之前安东内利卡片最高成交价（2019年Topps Dynasty青训车队卡，通过Goldin成交价为201,910美元）的四倍以上。

    同一场Goldin拍卖还展示了一张2025年Topps Chrome F1红折射安东内利卡片，编号5/5，PSA评分9。收藏账户Strictly Turn One报告称，这张卡片以12.81万美元的价格售出，此前在2月份在eBay上以5000美元的价格易手。

    需求的激增发生在安东内利在仅第二个赛季就接近冠军的时候。这位意大利人在奥斯汀抵达时领先梅赛德斯队友乔治·拉塞尔102分，如果他在周日获胜而拉塞尔未能进入前八名，他就能锁定冠军头衔，而刘易斯·汉密尔顿则未能进入前四名。

    20岁的他将成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军，打破2010年塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔创下的记录。

    Kimi Antonelli rookie card sells for staggering $878,400 ahead of F1 title opportunity

    A one-of-a-kind Kimi Antonelli rookie card has sold for $878,400 at auction, becoming the second most expensive Formula 1 trading card ever sold

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    A one-of-a-kind Kimi Antonelli rookie card has sold for $878,400 at auction, making it the second most expensive Formula 1 card ever sold, just days before the Mercedes driver could become world champion.

    The card, a 2025 Topps Chrome F1 Chrome Autographs SuperFractor numbered 1/1 and graded GEM MINT 10 by PSA, sold through Goldin after 34 bids, with the auction closing on 17 October.

    Only one copy of the card exists. It boasts Antonelli’s autograph in blue ink and Topps’ gold SuperFractor finish, and Goldin described it as the “Holy Grail” of his Formula 1 career.

    According to Card Ladder data reported by Sports Illustrated, only one F1 card has ever sold for more: a 2020 Topps Chrome F1 SuperFractor 1/1 of Lewis Hamilton, graded PSA 7, which fetched $900,000 through Goldin in May 2022. Antonelli’s card fell just $21,600 short of that record.

    The sale was the biggest of any sports card that week, and more than four times the previous high for an Antonelli card, a Topps Dynasty patch rookie card that sold for $201,910, also through Goldin.

    The same Goldin auction also featured a 2025 Topps Chrome F1 Red Refractor of Antonelli, numbered 5/5 and graded PSA 9. Card-collecting account Strictly Turn One reports that it sold for $128,100, having changed hands for $5,000 on eBay in February.

    The surge in demand comes as Antonelli closes in on the championship in only his second season. The Italian arrives in Austin with a 102-point lead over Mercedes team-mate George Russell, and can clinch the title on Sunday if he wins and Russell finishes outside the top eight, with Lewis Hamilton outside the top four.

    At 20, he would become the youngest world champion in F1 history, beating the record Sebastian Vettel set in 2010.
