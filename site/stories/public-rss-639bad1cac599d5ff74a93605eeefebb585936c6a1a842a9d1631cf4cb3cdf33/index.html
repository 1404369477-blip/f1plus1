    国际汽联与AWS合作，用AI协助制定F1未来规则

    国际汽联与亚马逊云科技（AWS）签署全球合作伙伴协议，将利用其人工智能技术协助制定F1下一代赛车的规则。

    国际汽联将利用亚马逊云科技（AWS）的人工智能协助制定F1未来规则，此前已与这家科技巨头签署新的全球合作伙伴协议。

    国际汽联已与亚马逊云科技合作，在制定未来F1规则时使用人工智能。

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    国际汽车联合会（FIA）在与这家科技巨头签署新的全球合作伙伴协议后，将使用亚马逊云科技（AWS）的人工智能来帮助制定F1下一代赛车的规则。

    根据该协议，AWS 成为该管理机构的官方人工智能与云计算合作伙伴，其技术将应用于包括 F1 在内的各项赛车运动。

    最大的变化将体现在未来规则的制定方式上。国际汽联已经使用 AWS 的计算能力来运行其空气动力学模拟。如今，AWS 还将让国际汽联的技术与工程人员使用人工智能工具，以帮助他们起草规则。

    “技术正在改变我们这项运动的每一个环节，国际汽联必须引领这一变革，”国际汽联主席穆罕默德·本·苏拉耶姆解释道。

    “我们与 AWS 的新合作将支持从未来赛车到基层俱乐部的一切，许多职业生涯正是从这些基层俱乐部起步。通过将世界一流的云与人工智能技术引入国际汽联的核心，我们可以让车手、官员和志愿者更简便地参与其中，帮助我们的会员俱乐部更高效地运作，并更接近我们让全球赛车运动参与人数翻倍的雄心。我很高兴欢迎 AWS 加入国际汽联大家庭。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，穆罕默德·本·苏拉耶姆，国际汽联主席

    AWS 全球赛事、体育与娱乐总监克里斯汀·沙夫补充道：“赛车运动是世界上要求最高、数据最丰富的环境之一，而国际汽联正处在其中心——管理这项运动、制定规则，并为人们在各个层面参与创造机会。”

    “我们很自豪成为国际汽联的官方人工智能合作伙伴，并带来 AWS 的云与人工智能能力，在赛事的各个层面支持他们的使命。”

    AWS 还将成为国际汽联颁奖典礼以及国际汽联可持续创新系列的官方合作伙伴。

    FIA to use AI to help shape future F1 regulations in new AWS partnership

    The FIA has partnered with Amazon Web Services to use artificial intelligence in developing future Formula 1 regulations

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    The Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) will use artificial intelligence from Amazon Web Services (AWS) to help draw up the rules for Formula 1's next era of cars, after signing a new global partnership with the tech giant.

    Under the deal, AWS becomes the governing body's official AI and cloud computing partner, with its technology to be used across motorsport, including F1.

    The biggest change will be felt in how future rules are written. The FIA already uses AWS computing power to run its aerodynamic simulations. Now, AWS will also give the FIA's technical and engineering staff access to AI tools to help them draw up regulations.

    "Technology is changing every part of our sport, and the FIA must lead that change," FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem explained.

    "Our new partnership with AWS will support everything from the cars of the future to the grassroots clubs where so many careers begin. By bringing world-class cloud and AI technology into the heart of the FIA, we can make it simpler for drivers, officials and volunteers to take part, help our Member Clubs work more efficiently, and move closer to our ambition of doubling global motor sport participation. I am delighted to welcome AWS to the FIA family."

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Mohammed Ben Sulayem, FIA President

    Global director of events, sports and entertainment at AWS, Kristin Shaff, added: "Motor sport is one of the most demanding and data-rich environments in the world, and the FIA sits at the center of it — governing the sport, shaping the regulations, and creating opportunities for people to participate at every level.

    "We're proud to become the FIA's official AI partner and bring AWS's cloud and AI capabilities to support their mission, across every level of the sport."

    AWS will also become an official partner of the FIA Awards and of the FIA Sustainable Innovation Series.
