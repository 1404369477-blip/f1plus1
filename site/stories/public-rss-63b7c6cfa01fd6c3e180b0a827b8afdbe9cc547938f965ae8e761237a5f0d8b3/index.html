    汉密尔顿谈其卡牌收藏："我41岁了，我还是喜欢宝可梦"

    汉密尔顿承认他对收集宝可梦卡牌的热爱延续到了成年后，一次为家人购买卡包的经历让他满载而归。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿关于他的卡牌收藏："我41岁了，我喜欢宝可梦"

    法拉利车手在拉斯维加斯告诉粉丝，他为侄子和侄女购物之旅以他的母亲开车带全家人去宝可梦商店结束。

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿承认，在为家庭成员中最年轻的成员购买宝可梦卡包的旅行后，他对收集宝可梦卡片的热爱已经持续到成年之后，整个车都装满了收藏者。

    在周二晚上，在拉斯维加斯的PUMA旗舰店举行的“Under The Lights”粉丝活动上，这位七次世界冠军在周末大奖赛之前解释了这一切是如何发生的。

    "我想给我的侄子和侄女买一些宝可梦卡，让他们开始收集。所以，我姐姐在开车，前排是我的妈妈，我和孩子们坐在后排，"汉密尔顿说。

    "我说，'妈妈，您要带我们去宝可梦商店。我41岁了，但我喜欢宝可梦。我只是——我喜欢收集卡片！"

    当被问及他最喜欢的卡片是哪一张时，他犹豫了一下，然后选择了该系列中最著名的三个角色："可能是喷火龙。我喜欢那个紫色的……皮卡丘……皮卡丘。"

    这两张卡片都是收藏者的安全选择。Charizard，这只来自90年代原始游戏的喷火龙，是收藏家最珍视的卡片之一，早期完美的全息复制品可以卖到六位数的价格。Mewtwo，第一部宝可梦电影的反派，也是粉丝的最爱。

    在一月份，汉密尔顿在接受《Vogue France》采访时表示，卡片已经成为他最新的痴迷，将宝可梦和星球大战列在其他体育卡片一起。他说：‘我有一个收藏，而且它还在增长。我每周都在买卡片。’

    他还说，这一切始于童年时期，当时他收集足球卡片。在三月的日本大奖赛之前，一位粉丝拿出了一张Charizard卡片和一支笔请他签名。‘哦，我不能这么做，不，我不能，很遗憾我不能这么做，’汉密尔顿回答说，然后继续前行。

    今年四月，他推出了由刘易斯·汉密尔顿创立的Card Culture，这是一家由美国零售商Dave & Adam's经营的交易卡片连锁店，第一家店于七月开业。

    他也是Topps官方F1卡片系列中的一个大名鼎鼎的人物。今年早些时候，为庆祝他在6月份的巴塞罗那大奖赛上首次身穿法拉利赛车服获胜而发行的Topps NOW卡片，成为有史以来销量最高的F1 Topps NOW卡片，印刷量超过50,000份。

    他的侄子和侄女似乎在专家的指导下开始收集卡片。但哈梅林姆本周末的注意力无疑将集中在赛道上，因为他在法拉利的第二个赛季只剩下三场比赛。

    Lewis Hamilton on his card collection: "I'm 41 years old and I love Pokémon"

    The Ferrari driver told fans in Las Vegas that a shopping trip for his niece and nephews ended with his mother driving the whole family to the Pokémon store.

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Lewis Hamilton has admitted that his love of collecting Pokémon cards has survived well into his adult life after a trip to buy packs for the youngest members of his family turned into a car full of collectors.

    Speaking on stage at PUMA's Under The Lights fan event at its Las Vegas flagship store on Tuesday night ahead of this weekend's grand prix, the seven-time world champion explained how it came about.

    "I wanted to get Pokémon cards for my niece and nephews so they can start collecting. So, there's my sister driving, that's my mum in the front seat, and I'm in the back seat with the kids," Hamilton said.

    "And I was like, 'mum you're driving us to the Pokémon store'. I'm 41 years old, and I love Pokémon. I just - I love collecting cards!"

    Asked which card was his favourite, he hesitated before settling on three of the franchise's best-known characters: "Probably Charizard. I like the purple one... Mew... Mewtwo."

    Both are safe picks for a collector. Charizard, the fire-breathing dragon from the original 1990s games, is one of the cards most prized by hobbyists, and early holographic copies in perfect condition sell for six-figure sums. Mewtwo, the villain of the first Pokémon film, is also a fan favourite.

    In January, Hamilton told Vogue France that cards had become his latest obsession, listing Pokémon and Star Wars alongside other sports cards. "I have a collection, and it's just growing. I'm buying cards every week," he said.

    He also said it began in childhood, when he collected football cards. And before the Japanese Grand Prix in March, a fan held out a Charizard card and a marker for him to sign. "Oh I can't do that, no I can't, unfortunately I can't do that," Hamilton replied, before walking on.

    In April this year he launched Card Culture by Lewis Hamilton, a chain of trading card shops run with the American retailer Dave & Adam's, and the first store opened in Frankfurt in July.

    He is also a big name in Topps' official F1 card range. Earlier this year, the Topps NOW card celebrating his first win wearing Ferrari colours at the Barcelona Grand Prix in June became the highest-selling F1 Topps NOW card ever, with more than 50,000 copies printed.

    His niece and nephews are, it seems, starting their collections under expert supervision. But Hamilton's attention will certainly be on the track this weekend with just three races of his second season at Ferrari remaining.
