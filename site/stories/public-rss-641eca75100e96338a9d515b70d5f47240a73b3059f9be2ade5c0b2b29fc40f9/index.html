    2026年最有价值的F1赞助商：Anthropic排名如何？

    威廉姆斯 F1赞助商Anthropic在纳斯达克股票市场指数的首次公开募股使其市值达到1.5万亿美元，成为2026年最有价值的F1赞助商之一。以下是2026年11月19日市场开盘前，按估计市值排列的七个最有价值的F1赞助商。

    威廉姆斯一级方程式车队赞助商Anthropic在纳斯达克股票市场指数的首次公开募股(IPO)使其市值达到1500亿美元，使其成为2026年赛场上最有价值的F1赞助商之一。

    但是，与其他F1赞助商相比，它排名如何呢？

    以下是2026年11月19日市场开盘前，根据估计市值排名的七个最有价值的F1赞助商列表。该列表排除了私有公司，估值以美元为单位。

    微软在2026年1月宣布与梅赛德斯F1车队达成多年合作协议，科技公司不仅装饰了银箭赛车的车身，还在其队服上也占据了显眼的位置。微软产品最近出现的故障对其价值影响甚微。

    谷歌的母公司Alphabet拥有庞大的估值。这家位于加州的科技公司向迈凯轮提供了众多标志，包括AI助手Gemini、Google Cloud、Android以及其更多产品在迈凯轮的品牌中有所体现。Alphabet在2026年6月达到了4.8万亿美元的高点，之后开始下跌——它在一天之内（10月29日）损失了5000亿美元。

    沙特阿拉伯的石油和天然气巨头阿美石油公司自2024年开始成为阿斯顿·马丁的独家冠名赞助商，此前两年它一直是与Cognizant一起作为银石车队联合冠名赞助商。阿美石油公司也是F1、F2和F3的主要合作伙伴，与阿斯顿·马丁的合同签到2028年。

    Meta赞助了梅赛德斯，使用Meta AI和消息应用WhatsApp。Meta AI于2025年底加入，WhatsApp和梅赛德斯于2023年底联手，这是一项多年的协议。

    Anthropic从2026年开始赞助威廉姆斯车队。这家科技公司以其AI产品Claude最为知名，其IPO发生在对AI公司审查比以往任何时候都更严格的时期。英伟达、Alphabet和微软是其所有者之一。

    梅赛德斯与电脑组件制造商高级微设备公司自2020年以来签订了为期多年的合作协议。当时，AMD的市值刚刚超过1000亿。自那时起，硅谷公司的估值大幅上升，人工智能是其主要推动力。

    总部位于旧金山的信用卡支付服务公司Visa与金融科技公司CashApp共同赞助小红牛车队，但两家公司并无关联。Visa自2024年起成为AlphaTauri的冠名合作伙伴，并在2026年通过与青训车队和高级车队签订小红牛合作协议，将合作延长至2030年。

    与这些赞助商相比，每支F1车队的平均估值约为36亿美元，但在法拉利的案例中高达65亿美元（福布斯，2025年）——而F1本身的估值约为280亿美元。

    F1本身拥有阿美石油公司作为全球合作伙伴，同时也是亚马逊网络服务（AWS）的全球人工智能合作伙伴。亚马逊的市值为2.78万亿美元，位列榜单第三。

    全球市值最大的公司是苹果公司（市值51万亿美元），其Apple TV服务在美国提供一级方程式赛事的直播。

    英文原文

    Williams Formula 1 sponsor Anthropic’s Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the NASDAQ stock market index gives it a market capitalisation of $1.5 trillion USD - making it one of the most valuable F1 sponsors on the grid in 2026.

    But how does it rank compared to other F1 sponsors?

    Here are the seven most valuable F1 sponsors in terms of estimated market cap, before markets opened on November 19, 2026 - when Anthropic filed for its IPO. The list omits privately held companies, and valuations are in USD.

    Microsoft announced a multi-year partnership with the Mercedes F1 team in January 2026, with the technology company adorning the Silver Arrows’ cars and taking a prominent spot on its teamwear too. Recent outages across Microsoft's products have done little to dent its value.

    Google’s parent company Alphabet carries a towering valuation. The California-based tech company lends numerous logos to McLaren with AI assistant Gemini, Google Cloud, Android, and more of its products featuring on McLaren’s branding. Alphabet reached a peak of $4.8tn in June 2026 before falling - it lost $500bn in one day (October 29).

    Saudi Arabian oil and gas giant Aramco has been Aston Martin’s sole title sponsor since the start of 2024, after two years of being the Silverstone’s team joint title sponsor alongside Cognizant. Aramco - which is also a major partner of F1, F2, and F3 - is signed with Aston Martin until 2028.

    Meta sponsors Mercedes, using Meta AI and messaging app WhatsApp. Meta AI joined in late 2025 while WhatsApp and Mercedes joined forces in late 2023, in a multi-year deal.

    Anthropic began sponsoring Williams in 2026. The tech company is best known for its AI product Claude and its IPO came at a time when scrutiny over AI companies is sharper than ever. Nvidia, Alphabet and Microsoft are among its owners.

    Mercedes and computer component maker AMD (Advanced Micro Devices) have partnered in a multi-year deal since 2020. Back then, AMD’s market cap was just over $100bn. The Silicon Valley company’s valuation has shot up since then, with AI being its primary catalyst.

    San Francisco-based card payment services company Visa shares title sponsorship of Racing Bulls with financial tech company CashApp, but the two companies are unrelated. Visa began as title partner of AlphaTauri in 2024 and, in 2026, it renewed its deals with the junior and senior F1 teams with the Racing Bulls partnership to continue “through to 2030”.

    Compared to those sponsors, each F1 team is valued at an average of $3.6bn but up to $6.5bn in Ferrari’s case (Forbes, 2025) - while F1 itself is valued at around $28bn USD.

    F1 itself boasts Aramco as a global partner, but also AWS - Amazon Web Services - as its global AI partner. Amazon's market cap of $2.78tn would put it third on this list.

    The biggest company in the world by market cap is Apple ($5.1tn) whose Apple TV service carries F1 streaming in the United States.
