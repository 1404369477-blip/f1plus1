    维斯塔潘宁愿待在家里，但信任中东赛事的安全保障

    一级方程式赛车宣布伊莫拉不再作为备选方案后，F1全力确保2026赛季在中东结束。尽管F1一直表示如果安全形势恶化，会做正确的事，但它将卡塔尔MotoGP周末作为最后两场大奖赛按计划进行的例子。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘"宁愿待在家里"但相信中东赛事的F1安全保证

    维斯塔潘表示，尽管从情感上讲他更愿意待在家里，但F1和FIA拥有的信息远比新闻上报道的多

    在F1通知伊莫拉不再需要作为B计划后，F1现在已完全致力于在中东结束2026赛季。尽管F1一直表示，如果安全形势恶化，它将做"正确的事情"，但它将卡塔尔摩托车大奖赛周末视为最后两场大奖赛按计划进行的例子

    马克斯·维斯塔潘之前已经明确表示，他总是喜欢访问中东，但鉴于该地区的冲突，他个人更愿意在其他地方比赛。

    这是四次世界冠军仍然持有的观点，这一点在Motorsport.com在拉斯维加斯赛道询问他关于即将到来的卡塔尔和阿布扎比的比赛时变得清晰。

    "他们给我们提供的保证，包括在私下谈话中，你只能假设所有信息都是正确的。我可以对此提出异议，但这样做也没有意义。我只希望一切顺利。"

    当有人指出，尽管有这些保证，他的想法和感受之间仍然存在差异时，范斯特appen在荷兰媒体采访中回应说："我对它感觉好吗？不。我宁愿待在家里。但如果我们得到每个保证说它是安全的，那么我也没有太多负面的话可以说。"

    "但你的左边、右边或下面的国家还在飞弹横飞，这难道好吗？当然不好。"

    维斯塔潘确实打算前往该地区，部分原因是红牛车队在车队 constructors' championship 中还存有争夺第三名的机会。在 MotoGP 赛事中，雅马哈车队已经没有东西可以争夺，这家日本制造商决定抵制卡塔尔的比赛。

    当被问及如果雇主没有东西可以争夺，他是否会考虑留在家里时，维斯塔潘回答说：“这总是很难说。当然，MotoGP 也需要在稍微不确定的一周提前一点去那里，”这位荷兰人说，他指的是美国的中期选举。

    伊莫拉是维斯塔潘的首选，正如几位其他 F1 车手的情况一样，但红牛车手最后强调了他必须信任 F1 提供的安全保障。

    “有可能发生什么吗？当然有。但在这里拉斯维加斯也有可能发生什么。你总是可以这样看。”

    “只是那里的风险可能离你更近一些，但我对这件事没有更多可以说的。我们只希望一切顺利。”

    Max Verstappen "would rather stay home" but trusts F1's safety guarantees for Middle East races

    Verstappen says F1 and the FIA have far more information than what makes the news, although feeling-wise he would still rather be at home

    After Formula 1 informed Imola that it would not be needed as a plan B, F1 is now fully committed to concluding the 2026 season in the Middle East. Although F1 has always maintained that it would do "the right thing" if the security situation deteriorated, it views the MotoGP weekend in Qatar as an example of how the final two Grands Prix can go ahead as planned.

    Max Verstappen has previously made it clear that he always enjoys visiting the Middle East, but that, given the conflicts in the region, he would personally prefer to race elsewhere.

    It is a view the four-time world champion still holds, as became clear when Motorsport.com asked him in the Las Vegas paddock about the upcoming races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

    "With the guarantees they give us, also in private conversations, you just have to assume that all the information is correct. I could push back against it, but there's no point in doing that either. I just hope everything goes well."

    When it was pointed out that there is still a difference between what his head tells him – given those guarantees – and how he feels, Verstappen replied during the Dutch media session: "Do I feel good about it? No. I'd rather stay at home. But if we're given every guarantee that it's safe, then there's not much negative I can say about it.

    "But is it nice that missiles are still flying around in the countries to your left, right or below you? No, that's certainly not nice."

    Verstappen does intend to travel to the region, partly because there is still something at stake for Red Bull with third place in the constructors' championship. In MotoGP, Yamaha had nothing left to fight for and the Japanese manufacturer decided to boycott the race in Qatar.

    Asked whether he would actually have considered staying at home if there had been nothing left at stake for his employer, Verstappen replied: "That's always difficult to say. And of course, MotoGP also had to go there a little earlier, during a slightly more uncertain week," the Dutchman said, referring to the midterm elections in the United States.

    Imola would have been Verstappen's preference – as was the case for several other F1 drivers – but the Red Bull driver concluded by stressing that he has to trust the safety guarantees provided by F1.

    "Is there a chance that something could happen? Yes, of course. But there's also a chance that something could happen here in Las Vegas. You can always look at it that way.

    "It's just that the risk might be a little closer to you over there, but there's not much more I can say about it. We just hope everything goes well."
