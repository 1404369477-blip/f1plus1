    小红牛延长拉森F1合同至2027赛季，打破马克奥时代

    小红牛延长了利亚姆·拉森的F1合同至2027赛季，避免了前所未有的车手解雇。拉森的席位近期面临巨大风险，因为F2赛事冠军尼古拉·索洛夫在等待机会，而小红牛需要决定哪两位车手将在下赛季为车队出战。

    小红牛延长了利亚姆·拉森的F1合同至2027赛季 - 拉森可以感谢他最近的表现，这说服了小红牛避免前所未有的车手解雇。

    拉森在小红牛第二车队的席位在最近几轮比赛中面临真实的风险，因为F2赛事冠军尼古拉·索洛夫在等待机会，而小红牛需要决定哪两位车手将在下赛季为车队出战。

    但如果拉森失去了席位——在其他车队没有2027赛季的赛车席位——这将是一项史无前例的举动。

    在过去10个赛季的F1中，只有两位车手的表现与拉森目前的表现相当，被解雇后却没有下赛季的替代席位。

    只有塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯——他在2024年锦标赛中排名第八——和亚历克斯·阿隆索——他在2020年锦标赛中排名第七——在现在的拉森（第九名）之前排名更高，但第二年却发现自己没有车可开。

    当然，他们都因为红牛车队而遭遇了这样的命运，但最大的不同是佩雷斯和阿隆索都是为红牛车队驾驶，并在过程中被马克斯·维斯塔潘彻底击败。

    拉森的情况完全不同，他是2026年世界锦标赛所有中游车手中排名最高的，并且轻松领先他的新秀队友林德布拉德。

    对拉森位置的威胁主要来自F2冠军候选人Tsolov。这位保加利亚人在他的新秀F2赛季中赢得了六场比赛，仅落后于2027年哈斯F1车手和法拉利青训车队成员拉斐尔·卡马拉——他在巴西取代埃斯特班·奥康首次亮相F1。

    Tsolov凭借其在F2赛事中的出色表现，以及他在墨西哥强制性的新人FP1练习中替代劳森时所展现出的出色表现，已经为晋升做出了强有力的证明。这不仅是因为他在F2赛事中的成绩，还因为他在墨西哥替代劳森参加强制新人FP1练习时，表现出了对F1赛事的从容不迫。

    在Tsolov在墨西哥的出色表现之上，劳森后来在FP2练习中与红牛车队的主将维斯塔潘发生了冲突——这使劳森遭到了四次世界冠军的严厉斥责，其中维斯塔潘暗示了劳森2027年合同的不确定性。

    《赛车》杂志理解，最近的连续三场赛事基本上是红牛车队和小红牛进行最终分析的关键时刻，以确定下一年谁将驾驶赛车——因此，在这段时间内，劳森承受着压力，真的有失去座位的风险。

    这也可能解释了维斯塔潘在墨西哥碰撞之后尖锐的评论：“当你处于他的位置上，选择与我发生冲突，这根本不是一种明智的行为。”

    但是劳森从那个低谷中反弹回来，从资格赛的技战术排除和维修区发车到最终第七名完成比赛，他熟悉的领先中游位置展现出了真正的毅力。

    再加上劳森在巴西的雨天表现——资格赛第五名和正赛第七名——在红牛体系中，任何人都很难提出他应该被肯定淘汰的理由。

    只有劳森真正处于危险之中——因为林德布拉德作为新秀与他并肩作战，出色地完成了工作，贡献了35%的积分，这让小红牛几乎肯定能以第五名的成绩结束车队历史上最高的位置。

    劳森在车队中的工作本质上是给红牛提供一个衡量林德布拉德表现的基准。劳森再次成为红牛赛车座位的竞争者几乎是不可能的，就像林德布拉德或最终茨洛夫可能还有机会一样。

    问题是，劳森在本赛季他的角色中做得如此出色——甚至在他为红牛车队在三次比赛中替换伊萨克·哈迪亚尔之后，作为车手又迈出了进一步的一步——以至于即使他的存在不再真正符合小红牛应该拥有的'红牛输送带'功能，他也变得难以被淘汰。

    在巴西的周五，小红牛车队老板艾伦·帕尔曼表示，小红牛正在尝试对这种情况“应用一点科学”，这是对过去赫尔穆特·马克奥似乎几乎随心所欲地雇佣和解雇青训车手的那些日子的明确反驳。

    “红牛有一个非常成功的年轻车手项目，而且我不想批评它，但是我认为如果你们批评它，也许有时候车手们没有足够的时间来适应，或者也许有些仓促的决定已经被做出。”帕尔曼说。

    劳森在排位赛中相对于林德布拉德的平均优势只有七百分之一秒，但在比赛中——那里才是积分被发放的地方——劳森有明显的优势。

    他在今年为小红牛参加的17场大奖赛中，有13场得分，还在6场冲刺赛中获得了积分。在14场比赛中，他和林德布拉德都完成了比赛，他11次领先林德布拉德，在77%的比赛中领先他。

    这使得他成为了中场的无可争议的王者。在今年举行的52场资格赛和比赛中，包括冲刺赛，劳森有18次被归类为该组的第一名。

    为了将这个数字放在正确的背景下，请记住，阿尔卑斯和奥迪在该组的前端竞争中非常接近，而且劳森在其中的8场比赛中没有进入中场，因为他当时在哈吉尔的小红牛车队。

    他的方法也引起了人们的注意。在2026年之前，他的战斗决策有时可能是自我毁灭的。这导致了比必要的更多赛道事故。

    今年他在这方面做得很好，尽管仍然努力争取结果。

    他也很好地适应了2026年的赛车，意识到他需要控制超车欲望，以充分利用它们 - 对于一个在弯道入口时可能天生具有侵略性的车手来说，这并不容易做到。

    他还能连续很好地完成周末的比赛，即使周五开始得不好，也能扭转局势。在为红牛的三次出赛中，他也表现得很好，尽管2025年发生在他身上的事情留下了伤痕。这显示了他的心理韧性，这也反映在他对未来不确定的心态中。他能够专注于自己的工作，没有被这些事情分心 - 这比说起来容易做起来。

    劳森解释说：“一年前我考虑了很多。这非常占据我的头脑，当你过度思考时，你的驾驶就会变差。”

    劳森并没有突然将自己变成F1中最好的车手。但他已经迈出了成为全面、稳定和可靠的中间集团选手的一大步——而且速度也非常快。

    这就是他最终成为2027年不可替代的原因。

    在宣布最终结果之前，佩尔马内已经暗示“每个人都会很高兴”，这表明现在劳森已经确认，Tsolov将会有替代安排——这很可能是成为官方储备车手，并在2027年进行更多F1测试和自由练习。

    英文原文

    Racing Bulls has extended Liam Lawson’s Formula 1 contract into the 2027 season - and Lawson can thank his recent performances for convincing Red Bull to avoid an unprecedented driver sacking.

    Lawson’s seat in Red Bull’s second team has been at very real risk in recent rounds, with Formula 2 race winner Nikola Tsolov waiting in the wings and Red Bull needing to decide which two of Lawson, Tsolov and Arvid Lindblad race for Racing Bulls next year.

    But had Lawson lost out - with zero options elsewhere on the grid to race in 2027 - it would have been an unprecedented move.

    In the past 10 seasons of F1, there have only been two comparable cases of a driver doing as well as Lawson is currently doing, getting dropped and then not having an alternative seat for the following season.

    Only Sergio Perez - who was eighth in the 2024 championship - and Alex Albon - who was seventh in the 2020 championship - have been higher placed than Lawson is right now (ninth) and yet found themselves without a drive the year after.

    Both, of course, met that fate thanks to Red Bull, but the big difference is both Perez and Albon were driving for Red Bull Racing - and getting absolutely destroyed by Max Verstappen in the process.

    Lawson is in a completely different situation here, in that he’s the highest-placed of all midfield drivers in the 2026 world championship and comfortably ahead of his rookie team-mate Lindblad.

    The main threat to Lawson’s position comes from F2 title contender Tsolov. The Bulgarian has won six races in his rookie F2 season and sits only seven points behind 2027 Haas F1 driver and Ferrari junior Rafael Camara - who made his F1 debut in place of Esteban Ocon in Brazil.

    Tsolov has made a strong case for promotion, not only because of those F2 results but also because of how well he did subbing for Lawson in a mandated rookie FP1 outing in Mexico, where Tsolov lapped within two tenths of Lindblad and generally looked very at ease with the whole business of being in F1.

    On top of Tsolov’s impressive showing in Mexico, Lawson later had a run-in with Red Bull’s kingpin driver Verstappen during FP2 - which earned Lawson a strong rebuke from the four-time champion, in which Verstappen alluded to Lawson’s 2027 deal uncertainty.

    The Race understands the recent triple-header has basically been the crunch moment for Red Bull and Racing Bulls to do their final analysis on who should drive next year - so Lawson has been under pressure during this period and at genuine risk of losing his seat.

    Which perhaps explains Verstappen’s pointed remark in the wake of their collision in Mexico, “It’s just not very smart behaviour when you’re in his position to pick a fight with me.”

    But the way Lawson rebounded from that low point, recovering from a technical exclusion in qualifying and a pitlane start to finish seventh that weekend, in his familiar position as the leading midfield runner, showed true grit.

    Add that to the way Lawson excelled in the wet in Brazil - fifth in qualifying and seventh in the race - and it’s difficult for anyone inside the Red Bull system to make the case he should definitely be dropped.

    It has only been Lawson at real risk - because Lindblad has done an excellent job alongside him as a rookie and contributed a meaningful 35% of the points that leave Racing Bulls almost certain to finish fifth in the constructors’ championship - the highest place in the team’s history.

    Lawson’s job within that team is essentially to give Red Bull a benchmark of performance to measure Lindblad against. There’s no realistic possibility of Lawson becoming a contender for a Red Bull Racing seat again, in the way there might still be for Lindblad or, eventually, Tsolov.

    The trouble is that Lawson has done such an excellent job in his role this season - and has even taken a further step as a driver since subbing for Isack Hadjar at Red Bull for three races - that he made himself impossible to drop even though his presence no longer really fits the 'Red Bull conveyor belt' function Racing Bulls is supposed to have.

    On Friday in Brazil, Racing Bulls team boss Alan Permane said Racing Bulls is trying to “apply a little bit of science” to the situation, a clear rebuke of the days when Helmut Marko would hire and fire junior drivers seemingly almost on a whim.

    “Red Bull's had a very successful young driver programme, and if I want to be... and I don't want to be critical of it, but I think if you guys are critical of it, maybe sometimes the drivers haven't been able to have enough time to settle, or maybe some snap decisions have been made,” Permane said.

    Lawson’s average qualifying advantage over Lindblad is only seven hundredths of a second, but in the races - where the points are paid out - Lawson has a clear advantage.

    He’s scored in 13 of the 17 grands prix he’s started for Racing Bulls this year, as well as taking points in three of the six sprint races staged. He’s also finished ahead of Lindblad 11 times out of the 14 races they have both finished and run ahead of him on 77% of the racing laps both completed.

    That’s made him the undisputed king of the midfield. Of the 52 qualifying sessions and races, including sprints, held this year, Lawson has been classified at the top of that group 18 times.

    To put that number in context, remember Alpine and Audi have made the fight at the front of that group a close one, and that Lawson wasn’t part of the midfield for eight of those because he was in Hadjar’s Red Bull.

    His approach has also caught the eye. Prior to 2026, there were times when his decision-making in battle could be self-defeating. That led to more on-track incidents than necessary.

    That’s something he’s largely cut out this year, while still fighting hard enough to get the results.

    He’s also adapted well to the 2026 cars, recognising he needed to contain the desire to over-drive to get the most out of them - not easy to do for a driver who can be naturally aggressive on corner entries.

    He’s also able to piece together weekends consistently well, often turning things round even if they start badly on Friday.He also did a good job in those three outings for Red Bull, despite the scars of what happened to him in 2025. That shows a mental robustness, which is also reflected in his mindset amid uncertainty about his future. He’s been able to focus on doing his job and not been distracted by this - something that is easier said than done.

    Lawson explained: “A year ago I thought about it a lot. It was very much in my head and you just drive worse when you overthink things.”

    Lawson hasn’t suddenly transformed himself into the best driver in F1. But he’s taken a big step to become a rounded, consistent, and dependable midfield performer - and a very quick one too.

    That’s what eventually made him undroppable for 2027.

    Permane had already suggested “everyone will be happy” when the announcement finally came, so that suggests that now Lawson has been confirmed alternative arrangements will be made for Tsolov - which will most likely mean an official reserve driver role and an expanded programme of F1 testing and free practice running in 2027.
