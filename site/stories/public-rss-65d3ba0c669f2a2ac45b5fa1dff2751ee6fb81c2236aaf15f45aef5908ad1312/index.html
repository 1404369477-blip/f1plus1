    哈达尔因涉嫌赛道不当行为被传唤

    哈达尔在墨西哥大奖赛期间因涉嫌赛道不当行为被 stewards 传唤，具体原因未明确。社交媒体上流传的视频显示哈达尔似乎推搡了试图与他合影的人。

    伊萨克·哈迪尔因在墨西哥大奖赛Formula 1维修区涉嫌不当行为而被 stewards 召唤。

    国际汽联 stewards 召唤了红牛车手哈迪尔，要求其就"Formula 1维修区事件中的不当行为"进行说明。

    召唤并未具体说明哈迪尔被召唤的原因。

    社交媒体上流传的一段视频似乎显示哈迪尔推了试图与他合影的人。然而，这是否是哈迪尔于当地时间周六上午10:15被召唤见 stewards 的原因尚不清楚。

    根据国际汽联的国际体育规则，"任何对国际汽联、其机构、其成员或其执行官员造成道德伤害或损失的字词、行为或书写，以及更普遍的关于赛车运动利益和国际汽联所捍卫的价值"可能被处以最高5000欧元的罚款。

    在其他情况下，可以处以最高20000欧元的罚款，国际汽联的体育规则指出："对于非常严重的违规行为，根据国际体育规则[ISC]可以实施更严厉的处罚。"

    然而，加重或减轻的情况可能会改变所施加的处罚。

    英文原文

    Isack Hadjar has been summoned by the stewards after alleged misconduct in the Formula 1 paddock at the Mexican Grand Prix.

    The FIA stewards called the Red Bull driver to see them for "misconduct relating to an incident in the Formula 1 paddock".

    The summons does not specify why Hadjar has been summoned.

    A video has circulated on social media appearing to show Hadjar pushing someone who tried to take a selfie with him. It is however unclear whether this is the reason Hadjar has been called to see the stewards at 10.15am local time on Saturday.

    As per the FIA's International Sporting Code, "any words, deeds or writings that have caused moral injury or loss to the FIA, its bodies, its members or its executive officers, and more generally on the interest of motorsport and on the values defended by the FIA" can carry a fine of up to €5000.

    Fines of up to €20,000 can be applied in other cases and the FIA's sporting code says: "For very serious offences, more severe penalties under the ISC [international sporting code] may be imposed."

    Aggravating or mitigating circumstances can however change the penalty imposed.
