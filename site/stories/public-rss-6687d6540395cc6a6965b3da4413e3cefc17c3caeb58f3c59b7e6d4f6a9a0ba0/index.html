    法拉利确认，查尔斯·勒克莱尔将因首个孩子的出生而缺席周四的巴西大奖赛前媒体日

    摩纳哥车手和他的妻子亚历山德拉本周宣布，他们迎来了他们的女儿比安卡，法拉利已经解释了这对他在圣保罗的赛车周末意味着什么。

    车手们在周四的媒体活动通常是强制性的，但这样的缺席将被视为批准的豁免，而不是违反规定。

    车队在社交媒体上发布称，查尔斯因女儿出生而免除今天在圣保罗的媒体活动。

    马拉内洛车队补充说，勒克莱尔将及时到达赛道，参加剩余的赛车周末。法拉利补充说：“他将于明天早上恢复正常的赛道活动。”

    勒克莱尔将在第一场练习赛中由迪诺·贝加诺维奇取代，这是强制性新秀出场的部分，因此勒克莱尔本周末的首个赛道练习将是FP2。

    这将是在2026年一级方程式赛季中贝加诺维奇的第三个FP1练习。车队最初计划让拉斐尔·卡马拉参加练习。但当哈斯车队宣布他将取代埃斯特班·奥康完成本赛季剩余的比赛时，他的练习首秀计划发生了变化。

    2026赛季还剩下四轮比赛。当梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛中赢得了他的首个冠军头衔时，排名第二的争夺战仍在继续。勒克莱尔目前在锦标赛中排名第五，但29岁的他和红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘在第二名的差距只有16分。

    巴西大奖赛将于11月8日在英特拉戈斯举行，当地时间下午2点（英国时间下午5点）。

    Ferrari confirms Charles Leclerc's Brazilian GP schedule after birth of first child

    Ferrari has confirmed Charles Leclerc will miss Thursday's media commitments at the Brazilian Grand Prix following the birth of his daughter

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari has confirmed that Charles Leclerc will miss Thursday's media day ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix following the birth of his first child.

    The Monegasque driver and his wife, Alexandra, announced earlier this week that they had welcomed their daughter, Bianca, and Ferrari has now explained what this means for his race weekend in São Paulo.

    Drivers' Thursday media commitments are normally mandatory, but an absence like this would be treated as an approved exemption rather than a breach of the regulations.

    "Charles has been exempt of attending media activities today in São Paulo, following the birth of his daughter," the team posted on social media.

    The Maranello outfit added that Leclerc will arrive at the track in time to take part in the remainder of the race weekend. "He will resume regular track activities from tomorrow morning," Ferrari added.

    Leclerc is already due to be replaced by Dino Beganovic in the first practice session as part of the mandatory rookie outings, so Leclerc's first on-track session this weekend will be FP2.

    This will be Beganovic's third FP1 session of the 2026 Formula 1 season. The team had initially planned to run Rafael Camara for the practice session. But plans for his practice debut changed when Haas announced he would replace Esteban Ocon for the remainder of the season.

    There are just four rounds remaining in the 2026 season. While Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli claimed his maiden championship title at the Mexican Grand Prix, the fight for second in the standings continues. Leclerc currently sits fifth in the championship, but only 16 points separate the 29-year-old and Red Bull's Max Verstappen in second.

    The Brazilian Grand Prix will take place at Interlagos on Sunday 8 November at 2pm local time (5pm UK time).
