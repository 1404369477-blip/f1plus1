    拉塞尔称沃尔夫用人不疑助梅赛德斯重夺F1车队总冠军

    拉塞尔表示，沃尔夫在地效时代困难时期坚持不换人，是梅赛德斯重回F1巅峰的重要原因。梅赛德斯在圣保罗大奖赛锁定第九个车队总冠军，这是车队自2021年八连冠终结后的首个冠军。

    拉塞尔称沃尔夫用人不疑助梅赛德斯重夺F1车队总冠军：“没有人被聘用或解雇”

    这位梅赛德斯车手表示，赢得车队总冠军的这批人，正是经历过车队四年低谷期的那批人

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    乔治·拉塞尔表示，托托·沃尔夫在地面效应时代的艰难岁月里拒绝改变梅赛德斯，这是车队重返一级方程式巅峰的重要原因。

    这支位于布拉克利的车队在周日的圣保罗大奖赛上锁定了第九个车队总冠军，这是自2021年结束八连冠以来首次夺冠。从2022年到2025年，他们经历了四个赛季的鲜有成功，一直在努力克服地面效应赛车的挑战。

    拉塞尔在接受媒体采访时表示，尽管性能突然下滑，车队始终信任自己的人员。

    “这些年我们经历了很多。那是一段极其艰难的时期，”他说。“但我认为托托领导风格中我很钦佩的一点是，我们今天拥有的人和辉煌岁月里的人是同一批人，也是经历过2022到2025年困难时期的同一批人。没有人被聘用或解雇。

    “感谢团队中的每一个人，但尤其要大大感谢托托，感谢他的忠诚和对大家的信任，即使在艰难时期也是如此。”

    在英特拉格斯赛后，沃尔夫本人被问及车队的稳定性，这位奥地利负责人也指出了员工流动率低的问题。

    他说，稳定性“当然是一个因素”，“但我们最近也有许多人加入，我想我选择用的词是安全感。你知道，我们不是一个随意雇人又随意解雇的组织。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    我们提供一种文化和环境，让你能够茁壮成长，允许你犯错，允许你直言不讳。从长远来看，我相信如果你想取得成功，不是几年，而是几十年，这就是你需要灌输给车队的东西。

    他也承认，那些年过起来比成绩所显示的更艰难。看成绩，你会看到车队在其中两个赛季获得第二名，但这对车队来说是一段充满挑战的时期。

    沃尔夫说：“单看数字，情况根本没有那么糟，但感觉并不好。我认为，车队的韧性，那些在艰难时期团结在一起的人，是一个因素。”

    拉塞尔于2022年加入车队，经历了这一切。他的2026赛季非常艰难，因为他看着队友基米·安东内利赢得冠军。他现在在积分榜上排名第三，落后马克斯·维斯塔潘17分，还剩三场比赛。

    沃尔夫在周日表示，车队总冠军的荣誉同样属于拉塞尔，和其他人一样：“我们欠他一份情。”

    George Russell credits Toto Wolff's loyalty for Mercedes' F1 title return: "Nobody was hired or fired"

    The Mercedes driver says the people who won the constructors' title are the same ones who went through the team's four lean years

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell says Toto Wolff's refusal to change Mercedes during the difficult years of the ground effect era is a big reason the team is back on top of Formula 1.

    The Brackley outfit sealed its ninth constructors' title at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix on Sunday, its first since the end of its run of eight in a row in 2021. From 2022 to 2025, it went four seasons with little success as it struggled to overcome the challenges of the ground effect cars.

    Speaking to media, Russell said the team had remained trusting in its people despite teh sudden drop in performance.

    "We have been through a lot over those years. It's been extremely tough times," he said. "But I think something I admire a lot about Toto's leadership is that the people we have today are the same people who were there during the glory years, and they're the same people who went through the difficulties during 2022 to 2025. Nobody was hired or fired.

    "Credit to everybody in the team, but huge credit to Toto for that loyalty and belief in the people, even when times were tough."

    Wolff himself was asked about stability of the team after the race at Interlagos, with the Austrian chief also pointing to the lack of staff turnover.

    Stability was "certainly a factor", he said, "but we have also many people that have joined us recently, and I think what I would like to choose as a word is safety. You know, we are not a hire and fire organisation.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    "We provide a culture and an environment where you can thrive, where you're allowed to make mistakes, where you can speak up. And over the long term, I believe if you want to be successful, not over a period of a few years, but over many, many decades, this is what you need to be able to kind of ingrain in the team."

    He also admitted those years had been harder to live through than the results suggest. Look at the results and you'll see the team finishing second in two of those seasons, but it was a challenging time for the squad.

    "It wasn't that bad at all when you look at the sheer numbers, but it felt not good," Wolff said. "And the resilience of the team, the people that held together in those tough times, is a factor, I think."

    Russell, who joined the team in 2022, has been through all of it, His 2026 campaign has been very difficult as he watched team-mate Kimi Antonelli win the championship. He is now third in the championship, 17 points behind Max Verstappen with three races left.

    Wolff said on Sunday that the constructors' title belonged to Russell as much as anyone: "we owe it to him".
