    梅基斯：阿德贾尔控制情绪为赛车带来圈速

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯表示，伊萨克·阿德贾尔在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛中控制住情绪，为赛车带来了圈速。阿德贾尔在第26圈前翼故障，赛后又被罚10秒安全车违规，最终仅列第15名，但梅基斯从中看到了更令人鼓舞的东西。

    洛朗·梅基斯：保持情绪稳定正为伊萨克·哈贾尔带来“圈速”

    洛朗·梅基斯表示，伊萨克·哈贾尔在拉斯维加斯一鸣惊人之后，保持情绪稳定正在释放“圈速”潜力

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与赛事分析。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛上空手而归——第26圈前翼故障，加上赛后10秒安全车处罚，使他跌至第15名——然而红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯在拉斯维加斯大道上看到了远为令人鼓舞的东西。

    这位22岁的车手在排位赛中取得第四名，并在第9圈与最终夺冠的马克斯·维斯塔潘形成早期的一二名领跑时短暂领先，赢得了梅基斯的盛赞——梅基斯认为哈贾尔日益增强的情绪稳定性如今正直接转化为速度。

    在赛后媒体采访中被问及哈贾尔在周四宣称在最后三站追逐胜利“没什么可失去的”之后所展现出的无畏态度时，梅基斯同时称赞了他的速度和战术纪律。

    “说实话，他做得非常非常好，”梅基斯说。“他靠实力站在那里；他站在那里是因为他有速度；他站在那里是因为他非常理解防守和进攻的动作，这对我们来说是最好的情况。他也很好地理解了当我们要求他把战斗留到后面、专注于不被身后的人追上的时候。”

    梅基斯指出连续两个突破性的周末——包括两周前的圣保罗大奖赛，哈贾尔在那里让位于强势追赶的维斯塔潘获得第二名，随后又拿下他2026年的第三个领奖台——作为稳步成长的证明。“他在发车格上是第四名，在赛道上是一、二名，”梅基斯指出。“上周末，我们取得了第二、第三名，而且上周末我们不得不交换了赛车。所以这是好消息。”

    这份冷静标志着这位车手的明显转变——在他随高级车队征战的首个赛季早些时候，他火爆的脾气曾引起关注：从六月摩纳哥站登上领奖台过程中麻烦不断的无线电交锋，到上个月在奥斯汀练习赛中与亚历克斯·阿尔本令人沮丧的碰撞后被国际汽联警告。据梅基斯所说，保持更冷静的头脑正在维修区墙两侧释放出性能。

    “伊萨克非常聪明，对我们这些非车手的人来说，很难评估车手在车内必须应对的情绪管理问题，”梅基斯解释道。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，伊萨克·哈贾尔，红牛车队

    “他认识到了这一点，他非常清楚，更好地管理这些情绪能让他在车内获得圈速，也能在车外通过与工程师沟通的质量获得圈速。

    正如你所指出的，他在过去两三场比赛中取得了非常非常大的进步。而且我们看到未来还有更多潜力。因此，他与工程团队之间的交流越来越好。当他能够控制住自己的情绪时，他的执行也更强了。

    红牛方面则另行承认，就尾翼突然断裂一事应向哈贾尔道歉——而赛会干事因他在安全车后减速约100公里/小时、为维斯塔潘制造双车进站间隔而对他处以10秒罚时——拉斯维加斯站对他第八名、122分的积分几乎没有帮助。

    尽管如此，在进入卡塔尔站和阿布扎比站之际，红牛正以440分对431分、领先迈凯伦9分捍卫车队积分榜第三的位置，哈贾尔更犀利的执行可能成为决定性因素。

    Laurent Mekies: Keeping his emotions in check is giving Isack Hadjar “lap time”

    Laurent Mekies says Isack Hadjar keeping his emotions in check is unlocking "lap time" after a breakout Las Vegas run

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Isack Hadjar left the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix empty-handed after a Lap 26 front wing failure and a post-race 10-second safety car penalty relegated him to 15th, yet Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies saw something far more encouraging on the Strip.

    Having qualified fourth and briefly led on Lap 9 during an early one–two run alongside eventual winner Max Verstappen, the 22-year-old earned glowing praise from Mekies - who believes Hadjar’s growing emotional composure is now translating directly into speed.

    Asked in his post-race media session about the fearless approach Hadjar showed after declaring on Thursday he had "nothing to lose" in chasing a win across the final three rounds, Mekies credited both his pace and tactical discipline.

    "He did very, very good, honestly," Mekies said. "He was there on merit; he was there because he had the pace; he was there because he was understanding very well the defensive, offensive manoeuvres, and that’s the best situation possible for us. And he also understood well when we asked him to keep the battles for later and to concentrate on not getting caught by the guys behind."

    Mekies pointed to consecutive breakthrough weekends - including the Sao Paulo Grand Prix two weeks earlier, where Hadjar yielded second to a charging Verstappen before securing his third podium of 2026 - as proof of steady growth. "He was P4 on the grid, P1, P2 on the road," Mekies noted. "Last weekend, we did P2, P3, and we had to swap the cars last weekend. So it’s good news."

    That calm marks a noticeable shift for a driver whose fiery temperament drew attention earlier in his first season with the senior team - from heated radio exchanges during a troubled Monaco podium run in June to an FIA warning in Austin last month after a frustrating clash with Alex Albon in practice. According to Mekies, keeping a cooler head is unlocking performance on both sides of the pitwall.

    "Isack is a very intelligent individual, and it’s very difficult for us non-drivers to assess what drivers have to deal with in terms of management of their emotions when they are in the car," Mekies explained.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    "He recognised that, he knows very well that managing these emotions better gives him lap time in the car and lap time outside of the car in terms of the quality of the communication with his engineers.

    "And as you pointed out, he made very, very big steps in the last two, three races. And we see even more potential moving forward. So he’s exchanging better and better with his engineering crew. And he’s executing stronger when he’s able to keep his emotions under control."

    With Red Bull separately admitting it owed Hadjar an apology over the sudden wing collapse - while stewards handed him a 10-second penalty for slowing roughly 100 km/h behind the safety car to create a double-stack gap for Verstappen - Las Vegas did little for his eighth-place tally of 122 points.

    Still, with Red Bull defending a nine-point edge over McLaren for third in the constructors’ standings (440 to 431) entering Qatar and Abu Dhabi, Hadjar’s sharper execution could prove decisive.
