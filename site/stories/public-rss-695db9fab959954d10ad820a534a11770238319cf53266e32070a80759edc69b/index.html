    拉斯维加斯大奖赛揭晓灵感源自霓虹灯牌的奖杯设计

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛公布了下周F1赛事的新奖杯设计，灵感来源于城市及其霓虹灯。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛揭晓灵感源自霓虹灯牌的奖杯设计

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛为下周的F1赛事准备了新的奖杯

    这个3.6公斤重，56厘米高的获胜者奖杯历时六个月开发制作，并模仿了拉斯维加斯大道上标志性的霓虹灯牌，该赛道就是围绕这些霓虹灯牌建造的。

    奖杯内有122个灯，为获胜者带来了独特的‘看到你的名字在灯光中’的意义，这是内华达市独有的设计。

    它还特别采用了V字形的轮廓，灯泡汇聚于V字的尖端，类似于拉斯维加斯的老式帐篷如何将注意力引向城市弗里蒙特街上的著名表演者名字，使其闻名。

    赛车组织者再次与鲍恩工作室和奥雷设计公司合作——他们曾设计了2023-25年拉斯维加斯大奖赛的奖杯——在开发成品之前，使用了3D模型和渲染。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛将于11月19日至21日举行，在F1赛季结束前，接下来的周末将进行卡塔尔和阿布扎比的比赛轮次。

    Las Vegas GP reveals neon sign-inspired trophy design

    Las Vegas GP has new trophies for next weekend’s F1 round

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix has unveiled a new trophy design for next weekend’s Formula 1 round, inspired by the city and its neon lights.

    The 3.6kg, 56cm-tall winners’ trophy took six months to develop and build and emulates the neon signs synonymous with the Las Vegas strip that the circuit is based around.

    The trophy contains 122 lights puts a new meaning to ‘seeing your name in lights’ for the winner in a design unique to the Nevada city.

    It also features a distinctive V-shaped silhouette, with the bulbs converging toward the point of the V, similar to the way vintage marquees in Las Vegas directed attention toward the featured performer's name made famous on the city’s Freemont Street.

    Race organisers teamed up with Bowen Studios and Ore Designs again – having designed the 2023-25 Las Vegas GP trophies – using a 3D model and rendering before developing the finished product.

    The Las Vegas GP takes place on 19-21 November before the F1 season concludes with the Qatar and Abu Dhabi rounds on the subsequent weekends.
