    维斯塔潘继承拉斯维加斯大奖赛杆位

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛Q3跑出1分34秒151，因安东内利赛车更换第六套内燃机被罚退，从而继承杆位。他周五晚间一直在与红牛小红牛22的“奇怪问题”作斗争。

    维斯塔潘拿下拉斯维加斯杆位，尽管自巴西站以来存在“奇怪问题”

    维斯塔潘继承拉斯维加斯大奖赛杆位，尽管自巴西站以来一直在与红牛小红牛22的“奇怪问题”作斗争

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    维斯塔潘将为周六晚间拉斯维加斯大奖赛的绿灯领跑，然而这位四届世界冠军周五晚间与自家赛车作斗争的程度不亚于与对手的较量。

    维斯塔潘在Q3跑出1分34.151秒，落后新加冕的世界冠军基米·安东内利的1分33.893秒0.258秒，在梅赛德斯为安东内利更换本赛季第六台内燃机、将这位意大利车手降至第六位后，继承了杆位。尽管如此，在经历波折的备战之后，排位赛仍被证明是一次意外的转机。

    “大概是这个周末最好的一节，因为真的从巴西站以来，我在车里基本一直有些奇怪的问题，这个周末也延续了，”维斯塔潘在排位赛后的新闻发布会上说。

    “我只是在努力想清楚，在周末剩余时间里到底该怎么应对这个问题。这让三练变得不容易，但在排位赛中我们设法让它运转起来了。”

    这些毛病在周五的最后一节练习中就能听出来，当时维斯塔潘最终排在第六，同时指出了转向机方面的担忧，并在车队无线电中报告他的小红牛22感觉“在直道上超级尖锐”且“很奇怪”。他的这节练习在赛会干事的审视下变得更加混乱：他在阿尔派车手弗朗哥·科拉平托前方变线后被认定没有危险驾驶，但因在第14弯与乔治·拉塞尔的冲突中刹车时改变方向而收到正式警告。

    拯救Q2的尾流——以及周六的策略

    即便在排位赛中，维斯塔潘也如履薄冰，随后在Q2跃居榜首，领先皮埃尔·加斯利，而两辆法拉利均被淘汰。

    “这里的尾流效应太疯狂了，因为我在Q2实际上差点被淘汰，”维斯塔潘解释道。“所以后来我在最后一圈出场，我大概落后前车四秒或四秒半，单圈我大约赚了七到八分之一秒，而实际上并没有赚到那么多。”

    维斯塔潘正追逐拉斯维加斯第三胜——他曾在2023年和2025年从第二位发车夺冠——队友伊萨克·哈贾尔从第四位发车，加强了红牛对迈凯伦17分优势的防守，以争夺车队积分榜第三。被问及从杆位如何打破拉斯维加斯大道强劲的尾流效应、且拉塞尔在他身旁时，维斯塔潘保持了对预期的务实态度。

    维斯塔潘说：“这也在一定程度上取决于基础速度，取决于我们是否真的能那样参与竞争，以及轮胎能否撑住。说实话，其余的事情都会自行理清——潜在的比赛走势、尾流效应之类的。”

    Max Verstappen takes Las Vegas pole despite “weird issues” since Brazil

    Max Verstappen inherits Las Vegas GP pole despite battling "weird issues" in his Red Bull RB22 since Brazil

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Max Verstappen will lead the field to the green light for Saturday night’s Las Vegas Grand Prix, yet the four-time world champion spent Friday evening fighting his own car as much as his rivals.

    Verstappen clocked a 1m34.151s in Q3 - 0.258 seconds behind newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli’s 1m33.893s - to inherit pole position after Mercedes fitted Antonelli’s sixth internal combustion engine of the year, dropping the Italian to sixth. Still, qualifying proved an unexpected reprieve after a rocky build-up.

    "Probably the best one of the weekend, because really since Brazil I have some weird issues basically in the car, and that continued also this weekend," Verstappen said in the post-qualifying press conference.

    "I'm just trying to get my head around how to deal with that basically for the remainder of the weekend. And that didn't make it easy in FP3, but in qualifying we managed to make it work."

    Those gremlins were audible during Friday’s final practice, where Verstappen wound up sixth while flagging steering-rack concerns and reporting over team radio that his RB22 felt "super sharp on the straights" and "weird". His session grew even messier under stewards' scrutiny: he was cleared of erratic driving after weaving ahead of Alpine’s Franco Colapinto, but received a formal warning for changing direction under braking in a Turn 14 clash with George Russell.

    The slipstream that saved Q2 - and Saturday’s strategy

    Even in qualifying, Verstappen walked a razor’s edge before jumping to the top of Q2 ahead of Pierre Gasly as both Ferraris were knocked out.

    "The tow around here is crazy because I was actually close to getting knocked out in Q2," Verstappen explained. "So then I went out on my final lap, and I was like four seconds behind the car or four and a half, and I gained like seven or eight tenths on the lap while not actually gaining that much."

    With Verstappen pursuing a third Las Vegas victory - having won both the 2023 and 2025 editions from second on the grid - team-mate Isack Hadjar starts fourth, strengthening Red Bull’s defence of its 17-point lead over McLaren for third in the constructors’ standings. Asked how he plans to break the Las Vegas Strip’s powerful draft from P1 with Russell alongside him, Verstappen kept expectations grounded.

    "It also just depends a bit on the base pace, if we can actually be in that fight like that, and then how the tyres hold up," Verstappen said. "The rest will all sort itself out, to be honest - the potential racing or drafting or whatever."
