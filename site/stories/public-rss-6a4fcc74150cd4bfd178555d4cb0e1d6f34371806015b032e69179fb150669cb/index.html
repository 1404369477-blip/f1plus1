    贝尔曼对2027年F1合同迟迟未定感到沮丧

    在劳森和博塔斯确认留队后，2027赛季F1只剩哈斯车队一个正式空缺席位，目前由贝尔曼占据。贝尔曼在拉斯维加斯表示，赛季仅剩三站却仍对未来没有确定答案，让他感到沮丧。

    “失望”与“没有借口”——奥利弗·贝尔曼因迟迟未拿到2027年F1合同而感到沮丧

    奥利弗·贝尔曼表示，本赛季仅剩三站，自己的F1未来却仍无定论，这种感觉“不好受”。

    在确认利亚姆·劳森和瓦尔特利·博塔斯将继续留在发车格后，2027赛季F1只剩一个正式空缺席位，即奥利弗·贝尔曼目前占据的哈斯席位。

    围场内几乎无人怀疑贝尔曼明年也会出现在F1发车格上，但他对迟迟没有定论感到极其沮丧，他在拉斯维加斯接受包括Motorsport.com在内的媒体采访时明确表达了这一点。

    贝尔曼表示：“没有什么新消息可说。说实话，我很失望，在第20站坐在这里，明年的合同还没签。情况很奇怪，但我们正在推进。”

    “我只能说这有点令人失望，显然这对车手的信心之类的事情并不理想。当然，我知道我今年做得不错，但我不知道明年要做什么，这并不理想。我就说这么多。”

    在拉斯维加斯的媒体日上，贝尔曼不愿回答他究竟对谁失望的问题：“我不会对此发表评论。”

    让贝尔曼尤其沮丧的是，他感觉最近几周取得了不错的进展，但这仍不足以宣布达成协议，而发车区上的所有其他车手都已经知道自己的处境。

    “很明显，我正在努力留在哈斯车队，而且我理应留在哈斯车队。至少我是这么觉得的。这只是一个奇怪的处境，”他说道。

    “我真的以为我们在上一个三连赛周末中取得了不错的进展，但也许看起来并非如此。这有点令人失望。我希望我们能撑到这个周末结束，并且我能有一些好消息告诉你们。”

    贝尔曼完全排除了在2027年为哈斯以外的任何F1车队效力的可能性，鉴于所有其他席位都已填满，这似乎已经不可能了。

    当被Motorsport.com问及是否存在最终不得不接受妥协的风险——即签下2027年的合同，但得不到他想要的之后几年的长期前景——他继续说道：“我对2027年的目标非常明确。我认为我们在这里还有未完成的事业。”

    “然后再往更远的未来看，那其实并不真正掌握在我手里，这么说吧。那不是我的路子。我在开车，但据我理解，这件事一直挺有挑战性的。”

    这一延迟似乎主要与他的长期前景有关。在墨西哥，哈斯车队领队小松礼雄驳斥了关于过高薪资要求的猜测，称其为“完全无稽之谈”，并暗示讨论更可能围绕贝尔曼2027年之后的未来展开。

    这位21岁的英国人仍隶属于法拉利，但他也承认，很难就未来能否效力法拉利车队获得任何保证。

    “我和法拉利有合同。法拉利给了我这次不可思议的机会，让我第一次随他们进入F1，”他说，“我仍然是法拉利的青训车手，我的终极梦想是为法拉利比赛。他们是最具标志性的车队，也是我小时候第一个相信我的车队。”

    “这仍然是我最终的梦想，但毫无疑问，展望未来，那里可能会有一些挑战。这一点不可否认，非常清楚。在这件事上我其实没有太多发言权。我们只能看看事情随着时间如何发展。”

    无论确切原因是什么、该怪谁，贝尔曼对在距离冬歇期仅剩几周时仍面临不确定性感到特别不安。

    “就像我说的，我们正在接近目标，但在还剩两场比赛、事情还没解决的情况下坐在这里，感觉并不好，”他说。

    “尽管我知道我们差不多就要成了，但事实是我们还没有。老实说，在没有签合同的情况下坐在这里，没有任何借口。”

    "Disappointed" and "no excuses" – Oliver Bearman frustrated by lack of 2027 F1 contract

    Oliver Bearman says it is "not a good feeling" to still have no certainty over his F1 future with just three races remaining this season

    Following confirmation that Liam Lawson and Valtteri Bottas will remain on the grid, only one seat formally remains vacant for the 2027 Formula 1 season. It is the Haas seat currently occupied by Oliver Bearman.

    There is virtually no doubt in the paddock that Bearman will also be on the F1 grid next year, but he finds the delay extremely frustrating, as he made clear in Las Vegas when speaking to media including Motorsport.com.

    "There is nothing new to say. If I'm honest, I'm quite disappointed that I'm sitting here at round 20 without a contract signed for next year. It's been a strange situation, but we're getting there," Bearman said.

    "All I can say is that it's a bit disappointing and obviously it's not ideal for a driver's confidence and things like that. Of course, I know that I've done a good job this year, but the fact that I don't know what I'm doing next year is not ideal. I'll tell you that much."

    Bearman preferred to avoid the question of who exactly he is disappointed with during the media day in Sin City: "I'm not going to comment on that."

    It is particularly frustrating for Bearman that he feels good progress has been made in recent weeks, yet it has still not been enough to announce an agreement, while all his fellow drivers on the grid already know where they stand.

    "It's very clear that I'm trying to stay at Haas and that I deserve to stay at Haas. At least I feel that way. It's just a strange situation," he said.

    "I really thought that we had made decent progress in the last triple header, but maybe it doesn't seem that way. It's a little disappointing. I hope that we can get to the end of this weekend and that I have some good news to tell you."

    Bearman completely ruled out racing for any F1 team other than Haas in 2027, something that already appears impossible given that all the other seats have been filled.

    Asked by Motorsport.com whether there is a risk that he will ultimately have to settle for a compromise – signing a deal for 2027 but not getting the long-term prospects he wants for the years beyond – he continued: "My goal for 2027 is very clear. I think we have unfinished business here.

    "Then looking further into the future, it's not really in my hands, let's say it like that. It's not my avenue. I'm driving the car, but from what I've understood, it's been a challenging one."

    The delay appears to be mainly related to his longer-term prospects. In Mexico, Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu dismissed speculation about excessive salary demands as “complete nonsense”, suggesting that the discussions are more likely to revolve around Bearman's future beyond 2027.

    The 21-year-old Briton is still affiliated with Ferrari, but acknowledges that it is difficult to get any guarantees about a potential future with the Scuderia.

    "I'm under contract with Ferrari. Ferrari have given me this incredible opportunity to step into F1 with them for the first time," he said. "I'm still a Ferrari junior driver and my ultimate dream is to race for Ferrari. They're the most iconic team and they're the first team that believed in me as a kid.

    "It's still my ultimate dream, but there's no doubt that looking into the future, there may be some challenges there. There's no denying that, it's very clear. I don't really have much say in the matter. We just need to see how things develop over time."

    Regardless of the exact cause and who is to blame, Bearman is particularly uncomfortable with still facing uncertainty with only a few weeks remaining until the winter break.

    "Like I said, we are getting there, but it's not a good feeling to be sitting here with two races to go without things sorted out," he said.

    "Even though I know that we're pretty much there, the fact is that we're not. There's no excuse for being here without a contract signed, quite honestly."
