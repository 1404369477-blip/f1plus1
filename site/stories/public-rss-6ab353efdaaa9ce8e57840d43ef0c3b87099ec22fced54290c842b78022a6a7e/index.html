    加斯利谈弗朗哥·科拉平托粉丝压力：一旦你处理了赫尔穆特·马克奥，其他压力都不算什么

    加斯利表示，与处理赫尔穆特·马克奥相比，其他压力都不算什么。

    皮埃尔·加斯利关于弗朗哥·科拉平托粉丝压力的看法：“一旦你处理了赫尔穆特·马克，其他任何压力都不算什么”

    阿尔卑斯车手皮埃尔·加斯利在网上遭到了一些弗朗哥·科拉平托粉丝的辱骂，但他表示欢迎数千名阿根廷粉丝在英特拉戈斯创造的气氛

    英特拉戈斯是弗朗哥·科拉平托距离主场大奖赛最近的地方，皮埃尔·加斯利知道这周末在圣保罗的阿尔卑斯车库里会营造出怎样的气氛

    自从科拉平托在2024年结束了该国23年的等待，让一名车手重返赛道以来，阿根廷国旗在一级方程式大奖赛的看台上已成为一种常见的景象。这种速度和规模的反应超出了预期，即使在联系这项运动的历史可以追溯到首届世界锦标赛的国家也是如此。尽管努力将大奖赛带回阿根廷仍在继续，但成千上万的粉丝已经习惯于越过边境来观看科拉平托在英特拉戈斯的比赛。

    这种支持也带来了一些社交媒体上的丑陋一面，一些自称为科拉平托支持者的账户对其他车手以及阿尔卑斯车队进行了侮辱。自从科拉平托加入车队以来，加斯利时不时地成为这种攻击的目标，最近一次是在西班牙大奖赛之后。

    加斯利认为在圣保罗面对如此多的阿根廷粉丝是一种积极的体验，尤其是对他队友来说。

    “我认为我们全球都有阿根廷人。我可以说可能更多在巴西，因为那里更近。你可以感受到这种支持，这就是Franco所说的。对他来说，这感觉就像在家乡的比赛一样，”加斯利说。

    当被问及在Interlagos的阿根廷粉丝数量是否会在本周末给他带来压力时，加斯利笑着回答：“我在F1已经10年了，一旦你处理过Helmut Marko，其他任何事都不会有压力！”

    “我知道我作为工作的任务是什么，我专注于那一点。我认为这通常是F1的美丽之处，我在这些时刻实际上能获得很多能量。”

    “我喜欢被逗弄，成为一个能够回归的人。当我处于赛道上时，我总是全力以赴。巴西是一个我得到很多支持的地方，所以对我来说这也是很好的能量。”

    科拉平托针对与他粉丝群体相关的网络虐待发表了评论，他在上个月发布在社交媒体上的视频中讨论了这个问题。阿根廷人在周四表示，自那以后情况有所改善，尽管他强调这是整个社区需要继续努力的事情。

    “是的，情况有所改善。我认为我们都看到了这一点，所以这是一个集体努力，而不仅仅是我的努力，”他说。“但是，是的，我已经谈过了这个话题，我认为显然，作为一个社区，我们必须继续改进，但我认为一切都在正轨上。”

    Pierre Gasly on Franco Colapinto fan pressure: “Once you deal with Helmut Marko, anything else is no pressure”

    The Alpine driver has faced online abuse from some Franco Colapinto supporters but says he welcomes the atmosphere created by thousands of Argentine fans at Interlagos

    Interlagos is the closest Franco Colapinto gets to a home grand prix, and Pierre Gasly  knows the atmosphere that creates around the Alpine garage in Sao Paulo this weekend.

    Argentina's flag has become a familiar sight in Formula 1 grandstands since Colapinto ended the country's 23-year wait for a driver on the grid in 2024. The speed and scale of the response exceeded expectations, even in a country whose ties to the sport stretch back to the inaugural world championship. And while efforts to bring a grand prix back to Argentina continue, thousands of fans have made a habit of crossing the border to watch Colapinto race at Interlagos.

    That support has also had an uglier side on social media, where some accounts identifying as Colapinto supporters have directed abuse at other drivers and Alpine itself. Gasly has been on the receiving end of it from time to time since Colapinto joined the team, most recently after the Spanish Grand Prix.

    Gasly sees the prospect of racing in front of so many Argentine fans in Sao Paulo as a positive, especially for his team-mate.

    "I think we have Argentinians all over the world. I would say probably more in Brazil because it's closer. You can definitely feel the support and that's what Franco was saying. For him, it feels the closest to the home race," Gasly said.

    Asked whether the number of Argentine fans at Interlagos might put him under pressure this weekend, Gasly laughed: "I've been 10 years in F1 and once you deal with Helmut Marko, anything else is no pressure!"

    "I know what I've got to do as my job and I focus on that. I think generally it's the beauty of F1 and I get actually quite good energy in these types of moments.

    "I like to be teased and be someone that comes back. I always give my best when I'm on track. Brazil is a place where I get a lot of support, so it's very good energy for me as well."

    Colapinto has spoken out against online abuse linked to his fan base, addressing the issue in a video he posted on social media last month. The Argentine said on Thursday that he had seen an improvement since then, though he stressed that it was something the wider community needed to keep working on.

    "Yes, it improved. I think we all saw that it improved, so it's a collective effort, not just mine," he said. "But yeah, I already spoke about the topic, and I think that obviously, as a community, we have to keep improving, but I think everything is a bit more on track."
