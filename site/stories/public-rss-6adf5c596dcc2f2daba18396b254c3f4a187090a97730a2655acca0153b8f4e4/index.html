    拉塞尔承认，在经历了充满反复挫折与不顺的2026年F1赛季后，他感到精神上已经疲惫不堪。

    拉塞尔承认，在一个他不得不反复克服挑战的赛季结束后，他感到精神上已经疲惫不堪。

    这位梅赛德斯车手在2026赛季初本是夺冠热门，但在经历了一连串超出他掌控的问题以及队友基米·安东内利的惊人表现之后，他只能眼睁睁看着这位意大利人于墨西哥加冕世界冠军。

    周四在英特拉格斯接受媒体采访时，拉塞尔表示，即便是来到巴西——他曾在2022年于此庆祝自己的首场一级方程式胜利——也未能让他振作起来。

    “正如我所说，这个赛季我大概只是感到有些筋疲力尽，不断地把自己重新扶起来，”他说。“当你被击倒在地，你把自己扶起来，然后你又被击倒，你再把自己扶起来，我原本希望一个赛季里也许经历两次这样的情况，而不是每隔一个周末就来一次，真的。

    “当赛季进行到这个阶段，你连续三周远离家乡，然后又遇到像墨西哥那样的比赛，一次获胜的机会、一次登上领奖台的机会就这样完全在你掌控之外消失了，这很难应对。真的非常难应对。

    “所以巴西是我喜欢的地方。我有很棒的回忆。我是否带着很高的期望来？说实话，没有。但肯定期待明年。”

    这位英国车手透露，在墨西哥站之后，他与工程师一起回顾了整个赛季，发现只有六场比赛没有出现问题。

    “在其中的14场比赛中，我们遇到了一些我们认为是坏运气的事情，”他说。“但当这种情况如此频繁地发生时，我们需要开始更深入地质疑，尤其是在我们这边。”

    拉塞尔目前在车手积分榜上以250分排名第四，落后红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘11分，后者以261分排名第二，落后法拉利车队的刘易斯·汉密尔顿9分，后者以259分排名第三。

    George Russell opens up on mental exhaustion after frustrating 2026 F1 season

    George Russell has admitted he is mentally exhausted after a frustrating 2026 Formula 1 season marked by repeated setbacks and bad luck

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell has admitted he is mentally exhausted after a season in which he has repeatedly had to overcome challenges.

    The Mercedes driver started 2026 as the favourite for the title, but after a string of issues outside of his control and incredible performances from his team-mate Kimi Antonelli, he watched on as the Italian became world champion in Mexico.

    Speaking to the media at Interlagos on Thursday, Russell said that even reaching Brazil, where he celebrated his first Formula 1 win in 2022, had not lifted him.

    "As I said, I probably just feel a bit exhausted this season of constantly picking myself back up," he said. "When you get kicked to the ground, you pick yourself back up, then you get kicked down again, you pick yourself back up and I was hoping to do that maybe twice in the season not every other weekend, really.

    "When you get to a point of this season, you're away from home for three weeks in a row and you have another race like what happened in Mexico where a chance of victory, a chance at the podium just disappears totally outside of your control, is tough to deal with. It's very tough to deal with.

    "So Brazil was a place that I love. I've got great memories. Do I come in with high expectations? Honestly, no. But looking forward to next year, for sure."

    The Briton revealed he had gone back through the whole season with his engineers after Mexico and found that only six races had been free of problems.

    "14 of those races, we’ve had things that have been what we thought was bad luck," he said. "But we need to start questioning things more deeply when it’s happening so often, and especially on our side."

    Russell is currently fourth in the drivers' standings on 250 points, 11 points behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen, who sits second on 261 points, and nine points behind Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, who sits third on 259 points.
