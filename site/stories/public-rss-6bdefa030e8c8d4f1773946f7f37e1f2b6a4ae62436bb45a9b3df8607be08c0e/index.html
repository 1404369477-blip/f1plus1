    粉丝激动：里卡多在奥斯汀现场支持利亚姆·劳森

    利亚姆·劳森在奥斯汀的Garage 各节练习与排位 with 劳森 and Friends音乐会上，得到了丹尼尔·里卡多的现场支持。

    粉丝们在奥斯汀现场表演中看到丹尼尔·里卡多支持利亚姆·劳森时情绪失控

    小红牛车手利亚姆·劳森在美国大奖赛前夕在奥斯汀登台进行罕见的现场音乐表演

    丹尼尔·里卡多，Visa Cash App 小红牛 F1车队

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    利亚姆·劳森在周四晚上在奥斯汀的舞台上看到了一些熟悉的面孔。

    这位新西兰人在Esses Bazaar ATX的Garage 各节练习与排位 with 劳森 and Friends上担任主角，这是在美国大奖赛周末之前在东第六街的Inn Cahoots举行的。

    劳森在2024年取代了丹尼尔·里卡多在小红牛的位置，里卡多在那里观看，还有他现在的队友阿维德·林德布拉德和前队友伊萨克·哈吉尔也在场。

    劳森与他的兄弟马库斯以及两个童年好友托马斯和哈里森一起表演，这是他自11岁以来第一次公开表演。

    “我有很多感受。我的意思是，我对……能和我的兄弟还有几个从小一起玩的朋友一起做这件事感到很兴奋，”这位24岁的年轻人告诉在奥斯汀表演前的媒体。

    “说实话，我们大概有10年没做过这样的事情了。显然，我们从未真正表演过，但我们很小的时候就一起长大了。所以这是一个很酷的机会。但当然，我紧张得要命。”

    当被问及现场表演的压力是否比大奖赛更大时，劳森补充说：“哦我的天，比这多10倍的压力。我宁愿坐进一辆一级方程式赛车去Q3，也不愿做这件事。”

    小红牛在社交媒体上分享了劳森和里卡多在活动中的照片，以及表演的几个视频。一位粉丝评论道：“小红牛给了人们他们想要的东西，”另一位补充说：“澳大利亚和新西兰喜欢看到这一切。”

    “哦我的天哪，我对这件事（我100%在尖叫）完全冷静和沉着，”另一位粉丝发布了一条帖子。

    美国大奖赛将于10月25日在美洲赛道举行。

    Fans lose it as Daniel Ricciardo spotted supporting Liam Lawson at Austin live performance

    Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson took to the stage for a rare live music performance in Austin ahead of the United States Grand Prix

    Daniel Ricciardo, Visa Cash App RB F1 Team

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Liam Lawson had some familiar faces in the crowd as he took to the stage in Austin on Thursday night.

    The New Zealander headlined Garage Sessions with Liam Lawson and Friends at part of Esses Bazaar ATX, held at Inn Cahoots on East Sixth Street ahead of this weekend's United States Grand Prix.

    Daniel Ricciardo, who Lawson replaced at Racing Bulls in 2024, was there to watch along with current team-mate Arvid Lindblad and former team-mate Isack Hadjar.

    Lawson performed alongside his brother Marcus and two childhood friends, Thomas and Harrison, in what was his first public performance since he was 11.

    "I am feeling a lot of things. I mean, I'm excited for... It's a cool opportunity to do this with my brother and a couple of friends that I've grown up playing with," the 24-year-old told the media in Austin ahead of the performance.

    "To be honest, we haven't done this for like 10 years. And obviously, we never really performed, but we grew up playing together when we were very young. So it's a cool opportunity. But no, I'm sh***ing myself, for sure."

    When asked if there was more pressure for the live performance than for a grand prix, Lawson added: "Oh my God, like 10 times more pressure for this. I would happily get in a Formula 1 car and go into Q3 over doing this."

    Racing Bulls shared a photo of Lawson and Ricciardo at the event, along with several videos from the performance. "Racing Bulls giving the people what they want," one fan commented, while another added: "Australasia loves to see it."

    "OH MY GOD, I'M TOTALLY CALM AND COLLECTED ABOUT THIS (I’m 110% screaming)," another fan posted.

    The United States Grand Prix will take place on 25 October at the Circuit of the Americas.
