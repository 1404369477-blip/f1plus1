    拉塞尔五站内被维斯塔潘追回90分

    2026赛季车手亚军争夺中，拉塞尔在最近五站被维斯塔潘追回90分，梅赛德斯正考虑全力支持拉塞尔以确保车手积分榜前二。

    拉塞尔在2026赛季F1亚军争夺中五站内被维斯塔潘追回90分

    在还剩最多三站的情况下，拉塞尔在与维斯塔潘的副冠军之争中处于下风

    马克斯·维斯塔潘（红牛车队）、乔治·拉塞尔（梅赛德斯）

    梅赛德斯正考虑给予乔治·拉塞尔“全部力量”，以在经历一段噩梦般的状态后确保车手积分榜前二。

    2026赛季还剩三站——假设卡塔尔和阿布扎比大奖赛按计划进行——在基米·安东内利于墨西哥锁定个人首个F1世界冠军后，至少有五名车手仍在争夺年度亚军：马克斯·维斯塔潘（279分）、拉塞尔（262分）、刘易斯·汉密尔顿（261分）、夏尔·勒克莱尔（249分）和兰多·诺里斯（246分）。

    拉塞尔在过去五场大奖赛中状态灾难性下滑，仅得26分，而维斯塔潘同期得到116分。这位英国车手在马来西亚遭遇动力单元故障，在新加坡冲刺赛中撞车退赛并接受引擎罚退，在奥斯汀经历了一场令人费解的低迷比赛，在墨西哥遇到部署问题，在圣保罗表现良好时于维修区出口打转。

    “我还没有考虑车手世界冠军，”车队领队托托·沃尔夫在巴西大奖赛后表示，梅赛德斯在那里锁定了制造商世界冠军。

    “我们绝对希望他拿到最高分，并希望他能获得第二名，我们需要考虑为此做些什么，但有可能我们会全力以赴。”

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    梅赛德斯在英特拉格斯的辉煌时刻是其F1生涯中的第九次，也是与拉塞尔合作的第二次；拉塞尔在萨基尔大奖赛顶替汉密尔顿出战，为银箭2020年的总冠军贡献了三分。

    当被问及如何在2027年前为拉塞尔找到鼓舞时，沃尔夫表示：“你知道，今天这是拉塞尔的总冠军，这是车队总冠军，他是车队的一部分——我真的很高兴他能珍惜这一刻，因为我们欠他的。”

    “你知道，他有时处境非常艰难，昨天就是一个很好的例子。他在湿地排位赛中绝对占据主导，本应轻松拿下杆位，但最后一圈夺走了他的杆位。”

    “现在我们还剩三场比赛。就像我说的，我们会全力以赴，而他一定会恢复的，对此我毫不怀疑，明年他就能做到。所有最顶尖的运动员都经历过困难时期，而这将成为他持续成长的一部分。”

    George Russell loses 90 points to Max Verstappen in five GPs in F1 2026 runner-up battle

    Russell is losing out to Verstappen in the race to be vice-champion, with up to three rounds remaining

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    Mercedes is considering giving George Russell “all the force” to secure a 1-2 in the drivers’ championship after a nightmare run of form.

    Three rounds remain in the 2026 season - assuming the Qatar and Abu Dhabi grands prix proceed as planned - with no fewer than five drivers in contention for the runner-up spot: Max Verstappen (279 points), Russell (262), Lewis Hamilton (261), Charles Leclerc (249) and Lando Norris (246) after Kimi Antonelli clinched his maiden Formula 1 world title in Mexico.

    Russell has been on a catastrophic streak over the last five grands prix, scoring just 26 points to Verstappen’s 116. The Briton suffered a power unit failure in Malaysia, crashed out of the sprint and took an engine penalty in Singapore, had a bafflingly poor Austin race, encountered a deployment issue in Mexico and spun at pit exit when running well in Sao Paulo.

    “I haven't thought about the drivers' championship yet,” team principal Toto Wolff commented following the Brazilian Grand Prix, where Mercedes wrapped up the constructors' championship.

    “We absolutely want him to score the maximum and hopefully finish second, and we need to think about what we're going to do about it, but it could be a possibility that we're giving it all the force.”

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    Mercedes' triumphant moment at Interlagos was its ninth in F1 and second with Russell, who contributed three points to the Silver Arrows’ 2020 crown as he stood in for Hamilton at the Sakhir Grand Prix.

    Asked how to find encouragement for Russell ahead of 2027, Wolff said: “You know, it's George's championship today, it's the constructors’ championship, and he's part of the team – and I'm really happy that he can cherish this moment, because we owe it to him.

    “You know, he had it so tough at times, and yesterday was a good example. He was absolutely dominating the wet qualifying, he should have been on pole easily, and then this last lap took away his pole position.

    “Now we have three races to go. We're going to, like I said, go full blast, and he will recover, I have no doubt about that for next year. All the very best sportspeople have gone through difficult periods, and that's going to be part of his continued development.”

    Motorsport.com · 2026年11月9日 21:46（北京时间）
