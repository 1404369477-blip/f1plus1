    F1世界冠军的年龄极限是多少？

    安东内利在2026年以20岁68天的年龄成为F1世界冠军，打破了塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔之前的年龄记录，但安东内利是否是史上最年轻的世界冠军还有待观察。

    一级方程式世界冠军的年龄可以有多小？

    一名车手能否在17岁时成为一级方程式世界冠军？这听起来可能不可能，但在某些情况下，国际汽联的规定实际上允许这样做

    基米·安东内利在2026年以20岁68天成为一级方程式世界冠军，打破了塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔之前保持的年龄记录，领先超过三年。但这就意味着安东内利是史上最年轻的一级方程式世界冠军吗？到目前为止是的，但总有人有机会来夺取这个意大利人的记录

    那么一级方程式世界冠军的年龄可以有多小，安东内利失去年龄记录被更年轻的车手打破的可能性有多大？

    部分答案可以在国际汽联运动规则附录L中找到。第13.1节（"完整超级驾照的资格"），第13.1.2条指出："车手必须在首次F1赛事开始时至少18岁。"

    17岁驾驶F1：理论上可行

    然而，这并不意味着只有18岁或以上的车手才能参加F1比赛。第13.1.2条还包含一个例外，该例外于2024年引入，内容如下：

    "在国际汽联的单独决定下，如果一个车手被判定在单人座赛车比赛中最近和持续地展现出卓越的能力和成熟度，他可以在17岁时获得超级驾照。"

    理论上，一个17岁的少年有可能成为一级方程式世界冠军，并且比安东内利的年龄记录提前两年多——前提是他们能在他们的第一个F1赛季中赢得冠军。

    已经有过17岁的一级方程式车手：马克斯·维斯塔潘在2015年澳大利亚的首次亮相时年仅17岁零166天，使他成为一级方程式历史上最年轻的车手。

    维斯塔潘在2014年就已经完成了他的第一个一级方程式测试，当时他16岁。同年，在他17岁生日不久后，他以周五练习车手的身份参加了他的第一个一级方程式周末赛。

    弗朗茨·托斯特，托罗罗索车队负责人，马克斯·维斯塔潘，托罗罗索车队

    但是，维斯塔潘令人惊讶的早期F1首秀产生了后果：国际汽联加强了超级驾照的要求，并将最低年龄从2016年起提高到18岁。年轻的车手还必须至少在单座赛车锦标赛中参赛两年，并积累40个超级驾照积分——维斯塔潘不会满足这些标准。

    在引入年龄豁免后，安东内利（2024年）和阿维德·林德布拉德（2025年）都在17岁时获得了超级驾照。然而，当他们首次参加大奖赛时都已经超过了18岁——同样，2017年首次亮相的拉塞尔·斯特罗尔也是18岁148天，使他成为仅次于维斯塔潘的第二年轻的一级方程式车手。

    从理论上讲，安东内利的年龄记录有可能会被打破。但要实现这一点，车手可能需要在他们的第一个或第二个赛季就成为世界冠军。这可能性有多大呢？

    作为新手的赛车世界冠军：这曾经发生过吗？

    F1历史记录中只有一位车手在首个世界锦标赛赛季就赢得了冠军——而且那是一个特殊情况：Giuseppe Farina在1950年赢得了首届世界车手锦标赛。

    从那时起，最早赢得冠军的车手是在他们的第二个世界锦标赛赛季。这仅发生过四次：年、年、汉密尔顿（2008年）和安东内利（2026年）。

    这四位车手中的三位在他们的新秀赛季已经非常接近冠军：Fangio在1950年只以三分之差输给了他的Alfa Romeo队友Farina；Villeneuve在1996年的冠军争夺战中输给了他的威廉姆斯队友Damon Hill，这是在最后的Grand Prix上；汉密尔顿在2007年获得了亚军，只比Kimi Raikkonen少了一分。

    安东内利打破纪录并非不可能。

    这些例子表明，这是可能的。但条件必须恰到好处——首先，一辆有竞争力的赛车，正如方吉奥（阿尔法·罗密欧）、维伦纽夫（威廉姆斯）、汉密尔顿（麦克拉伦）和安东内利（梅赛德斯）所拥有的。然而，很少有年轻车手一开始就能加入一支领先的F1车队，就像安东内利加入梅赛德斯车队那样。

    因此，一个20岁以下的驾驶员成为F1世界冠军需要极其特殊的条件。但这样的情景确实可能发生。

    How young can a Formula 1 world champion actually be?

    Could a driver become Formula 1 world champion at just 17 years old? It may sound impossible, but under certain circumstances, the FIA regulations actually allow it

    Kimi Antonelli became Formula 1 world champion in 2026 at 20 years and 68 days old, breaking Sebastian Vettel's previous age record by more than three years. But does that make Antonelli the youngest Formula 1 world champion of all time? So far yes, but there is room for someone to come and usurp the Italian.

    But how young can a Formula 1 world champion actually be, and what are the chances of Antonelli losing his age record to an even younger driver?

    Part of the answer can be found in Appendix L of the FIA's International Sporting Code. Section 13.1 ("Qualifications for the full Super Licence"), Article 13.1.2, states: "The driver must be at least 18 years old at the start of the event of his first F1 competition."

    Driving in Formula 1 at 17: Theoretically possible

    However, that does not mean only drivers aged 18 or over are allowed to compete in Formula 1. Article 13.1.2 also includes an exception, introduced in 2024, which reads:

    "At the sole discretion of the FIA, a driver judged to have recently and consistently demonstrated outstanding ability and maturity in single-seater formula car competition may be granted a Super Licence at the age of 17 years old."

    In theory, then, a 17-year-old could become Formula 1 world champion and beat Antonelli's age record by more than two years – provided they managed to win the title in their very first F1 season.

    There has already been a 17-year-old Formula 1 driver: Max Verstappen was just 17 years and 166 days old when he made his debut in Australia in 2015, making him the youngest driver in Formula 1 history.

    Verstappen had already completed his first Formula 1 test runs in 2014 at the age of 16. Later that year, shortly after his 17th birthday, he made his first appearance at a Formula 1 race weekend as a Friday practice driver.

    Franz Tost, Team Principal, Toro Rosso, and Max Verstappen, Toro Rosso

    But Verstappen's remarkably early F1 debut had consequences: The FIA tightened its Super Licence requirements and raised the minimum age to 18 from 2016. Young drivers were also required to have competed in single-seater championships for at least two years and accumulated 40 Super Licence points - Verstappen would not have met those criteria.

    Following the introduction of the age exemption, Antonelli (2024) and Arvid Lindblad (2025) both received their Super Licences at the age of 17. However, both were over 18 by the time they made their Grand Prix debuts – as was Lance Stroll, who debuted in 2017 at the age of 18 years and 148 days, making him the second-youngest Formula 1 driver after Verstappen.

    In theory, Antonelli's age record could therefore be broken one day. But to achieve that, a driver would probably have to become world champion in their first or second season. How likely is that?

    Winning the world championship as a rookie: Has it ever happened?

    F1 history records only one driver who won the title in his very first world championship season – and that was a special case: Giuseppe Farina won the inaugural world drivers' championship in 1950.

    Since then, the earliest any driver has won the title has been in their second world championship season. This has happened just four times: Juan Manuel Fangio (1951), Jacques Villeneuve (1997), Lewis Hamilton (2008) and Antonelli (2026).

    Three of those four drivers had already come remarkably close in their rookie seasons: Fangio lost the 1950 championship to his Alfa Romeo team-mate Farina by just three points; Villeneuve lost the 1996 title to his Williams team-mate Damon Hill at the final Grand Prix and Hamilton finished runner-up in 2007, just one point behind Kimi Raikkonen.

    Not impossible for Antonelli record to be broken

    These examples show that it is not impossible. But the circumstances have to be right – above all, a competitive car, as Fangio (Alfa Romeo), Villeneuve (Williams), Hamilton (McLaren) and Antonelli (Mercedes) all had at their disposal. Very few young drivers, however, enter Formula 1 with a frontrunning team straight away, as Antonelli did with Mercedes.

    It would therefore take an exceptional set of circumstances for a driver under 20 to become F1 world champion. But such a scenario could certainly happen.
