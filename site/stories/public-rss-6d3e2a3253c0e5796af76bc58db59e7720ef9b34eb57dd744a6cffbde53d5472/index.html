    1980年F1世界冠军艾伦·琼斯逝世

    1980年F1世界冠军艾伦·琼斯因短期疾病去世，享年80岁。他是威廉姆斯车队的第一位世界冠军车手。

    艾伦·琼斯，1980年F1世界冠军，威廉姆斯车队首位世界冠军车手，因短期疾病去世。

    本周早些时候，琼斯度过了80岁生日，他曾12次赢得F1赛事。

    周四凌晨，琼斯去世的消息传出。

    威廉姆斯车队发表声明，表示对琼斯去世深感悲痛。

    “艾伦对威廉姆斯车队今天的成就至关重要，他与弗兰克·威廉姆斯和帕特里克·海德建立了非凡的纽带，成为我们参加F1第四个赛季就获得首个车手世界冠军的第一人。”

    “他树立了威廉姆斯车队冠军的标准：一个快速、不妥协的赛车手，具有挑战者心态，热爱赛车，并将一生奉献给了这项运动。”

    “他是一位真正的澳大利亚偶像，也是威廉姆斯车队的传奇人物，我们将深切地怀念他。”

    “我们向琼斯家族和他的所有朋友致以我们的爱和思念。”

    英文原文

    Alan Jones, the 1980 Formula 1 world champion and first title winner for the Williams F1 team, has passed away after a short illness.

    Jones, who turned 80 earlier this week, was a 12-time F1 race winner and the second Australian driver to win the world championship, after Jack Brabham.

    News of Jones’s passing emerged during the early hours of Thursday morning.

    His former team Williams released a statement saying: “Everyone at Williams is deeply saddened to learn of Alan Jones’ passing.

    “Alan was instrumental in making Williams what it is today, forming an extraordinary bond with Frank Williams and Patrick Head to become our first drivers’ world champion in just our fourth season in Formula 1.

    “He set the standard for Williams champions: a fast, uncompromising racer with a challenger mentality who loved motor racing and dedicated his life to it.

    “He is a true Australian icon and a Williams legend, and he will be dearly missed.

    “We send our love and thoughts to the Jones family and all his friends.”
