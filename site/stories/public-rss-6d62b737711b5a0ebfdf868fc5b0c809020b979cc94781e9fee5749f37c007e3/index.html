    查尔斯·勒克莱尔与妻子亚历山德拉宣布迎来第一个孩子，女儿取名比安卡。F1各界纷纷送上祝贺。

    F1世界如何回应查尔斯·勒克莱尔成为父亲

    这位摩纳哥车手于周三迎来了女儿的降生

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔与妻子亚历山德拉宣布迎来第一个孩子、女儿取名比安卡后，收到了来自F1各界的祝贺信息。

    这对夫妇周三在Instagram联合发布消息，配上一张婴儿双脚的照片，并附文字“Bianca 3.11.26”。这一消息公布前仅数天，墨西哥城大奖赛刚刚结束，勒克莱尔在法拉利队内争斗的阴云下获得第四名；而本周末圣保罗大奖赛即将到来，迪诺·贝加诺维奇将在第一次自由练习中驾驶他的法拉利。

    以下是F1界在帖子回复中的反应。

    勒克莱尔的车手同行们迅速回应，带头的是他前法拉利队友塞恩斯，他直接以名字欢迎比安卡。迈凯伦的皮亚斯特里和阿尔派的加斯利也送上了祝贺。

    卡洛斯·塞恩斯：“真为你高兴。欢迎来到这个世界，比安卡”

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里：“祝贺你们俩！”

    皮埃尔·加斯利：“恭喜！！！”

    祝福还来自前车手，四届世界冠军普罗斯特和阿莱西都曾为法拉利车队效力。前DTM车手坦贝则送上了一位父亲般的寄语。

    阿兰·普罗斯特：“祝贺你们两位！欢迎比安卡。祝你们幸福美满。”

    让·阿莱西：“向超级妈妈和超级爸爸致敬”

    阿德里安·坦贝：“祝贺你们俩，夏洛。对一位父亲来说，世上最美好的事就是有一个小女儿……好好享受吧”

    法拉利领衔各车队的祝贺，梅赛德斯等竞争对手以及这项运动的管理机构也发来消息。与此同时，F1 学院已经在展望未来。

    F1：“恭喜！！”

    法拉利：“向你们两位致以最热烈的祝贺！！”

    国际汽联：“祝贺！”

    国际汽联主席穆罕默德·本·苏拉耶姆：“祝贺你们两位！”

    二级方程式：“恭喜你们两位”

    银石赛道：“恭喜！”

    更接近围场的人也加入了祝贺，包括马克斯·维斯塔潘的妹妹维多利亚和他的母亲索菲·库姆彭。塞恩斯的伴侣丽贝卡·唐纳森和前天空体育F1主持人雷切尔·布鲁克斯也回复了。

    维多利亚·维斯塔潘：“恭喜！”

    How the F1 world reacted to Charles Leclerc becoming a father

    The Monegasque welcomed his daughter to the world on Wednesday

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Charles Leclerc has received messages of congratulations from across the Formula 1 sphere after he and his wife Alexandra announced the birth of their first child, a daughter named Bianca.

    The couple shared the news in a joint Instagram post on Wednesday, with a photo of the baby's feet and the caption "Bianca 3.11.26". The announcement comes just days after the Mexico City Grand Prix, where Leclerc finished fourth amid a cloud of Ferrari battles, and ahead of this weekend's Sao Paulo Grand Prix, where Dino Beganovic will drive his Ferrari in FP1.

    Here is how the F1 world responded in the replies to the post.

    Leclerc's fellow drivers were quick to respond, led by his former Ferrari team-mate Sainz, who welcomed Bianca by name. McLaren's Piastri and Alpine's Gasly also sent their congratulations.

    Carlos Sainz: "So happy for you. Welcome to the world Bianca"

    Oscar Piastri: "Congrats to you both!"

    Pierre Gasly: "Congrats!!!"

    This continued to former drivers, with both four-time world champion Prost and Alesi having raced for the Scuderia. Tambay, a former DTM driver, offered a fatherly message.

    Alain Prost: "Congratulations to both of you ! Welcome Bianca. Wish you all the happiness"

    Jean Alesi: "Bravo à super maman et super papa"

    Adrien Tambay: "félicitation à vous deux Charlot. Best thing on earth for a dad to have a little daughter... enjoy"

    Ferrari led the tributes from the teams, with messages also coming from rivals Mercedes and the sport's governing body. F1 Academy, meanwhile, was already looking to the future.

    F1: "Congratulations!!"

    Ferrari: "Huge congratulations to you both!!"

    Mercedes: "Welcome to the world, Bianca! Huge congratulations to you all"

    FIA: "Congratulations!"

    FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem: "Congratulations to you both!"

    F1 Academy: "Congratulations! Maybe we'll see her on the grid in the future..."

    Formula 2: "Congratulations to the both of you"

    Silverstone: "Congratulations!"

    Those closer to the grid also joined in, including Max Verstappen's sister Victoria and his mother Sophie Kumpen. Sainz's partner Rebecca Donaldson and former Sky F1 presenter Rachel Brookes also replied.

    Victoria Verstappen: "Congrats!"

    Rachel Brookes: "Wonderful news. Huge Congratulations!"

    The news reached well beyond the race circuit as celebrities also flooded his replies, with actress Eva Longoria, model Nara Smith, Natalia Bryant and singer Charlie Puth among the famous names.

    Eva Longoria: "Ahhhhhh congratulations!"

    Natalia Bryant: "Congratulations!!"

    Charlie Puth: "Congratulations to you both!!!"
