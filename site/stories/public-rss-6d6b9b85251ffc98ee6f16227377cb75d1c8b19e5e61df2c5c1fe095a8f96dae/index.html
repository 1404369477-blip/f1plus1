    本田再次未能在阿隆索引擎上找到问题

    阿隆索在拉斯维加斯大奖赛最后一圈前突然退赛并称引擎“坏了”，但本田从数据和维护检查中均未发现引擎存在任何问题。

    本田在费尔南多·阿隆索上周拉斯维加斯大奖赛最后一圈开始前突然放弃完赛后，未能在其引擎上发现任何问题。

    阿隆索在拉斯维加斯倒数第二圈末段排名第18位时锁死轮胎，这对本田提供动力的阿斯顿·马丁来说又是在动力敏感赛道上一场非常糟糕的比赛，随后他在无线电中报告“引擎坏了”并宣布进站。

    似乎是在表达沮丧，他在进入维修区时猛踩刹车又出现了一次严重锁死，赛后他表示引擎在排位赛和比赛开始时“动力下降”，并且“最后两圈引擎彻底放弃了”。

    不过本田一直未能找到此类问题的任何证据。

    本田赛道现场首席代表折原慎太郎表示：“我们从数据和维护记录中都没有发现发动机存在任何问题。”

    “但同样，我们会在周五检查发动机在这条赛道上的运行情况。”

    这是阿隆索近期报告发动机问题的几个例子之一，他甚至像在阿塞拜疆那样让一辆看似健康的赛车退赛，而本田随后无论是通过数据还是物理检查都无法证实该问题。

    折原表示：“我们仔细分析了他的说法，同时也尊重他的意见。”

    这就是为什么我们检查了所有数据，也检查了引擎状况。

    "但从目前的数据来看，我们还没有发现任何问题。"

    阿隆索周四在卡塔尔将无法进一步评论这个问题，因为他将缺席媒体日，同时正在从阿斯顿·马丁所说的他在拉斯维加斯周末尾声感染的病毒中恢复。

    但他赛后的言论包括宣称，就直线性能而言"这可能是今年最糟糕的一场比赛"，而24小时前他在排位赛中曾称这"对我们来说就像一次演示圈"。

    “大概今年，我们的引擎性能不足以给车手带来满足感或信心，”折原说道。

    “然后，大概他们会感觉到引擎性能不太出色。”

    阿斯顿·马丁车队领队迈克·克拉克表示，2026款引擎的特性可能在一定程度上导致了车手们所感知到的异常行为。

    他举例说明了MGU-K在峰值输出时如今有多强大——350千瓦，几乎占引擎总功率的一半——以及它在赛道不同路段的不同部署方式。因此，如果这里的表现与车手的预期存在差异，感觉就会非常不同。

    奥里哈拉过去也曾提出过类似的缓和说法，例如提到当涡轮迟滞特别严重时车手会感到的不适。

    尽管如此，这至少是最近七场比赛中的第三次，阿隆索报告了一个问题，而本田表示在数据上找不到或无法确认该问题。

    在新加坡，阿隆索在排位赛后声称涡轮坏了——事实并非如此，但本田根据他的反馈决定更换部件，而到周日晚上仍未在数据上看到任何大问题。奥里哈拉在下一站美国比赛中再次强调了这一点，确认引擎已运回樱木，并且一次“良好的检查”也没有发现重大问题。

    阿隆索报告在巴库这条对动力极为敏感的赛道上与瓦尔特利·博塔斯的凯迪拉克争夺时出现了动力损失。本田检查了所有数据，赛后没有发现任何问题。随后进行了更多维护工作和数据审查，没有任何引擎问题的迹象。

    在巴库之后，实际上在拉斯维加斯之后也是如此，折原还表示，本田引擎在动力输出方面也达到了预期表现。

    折原一再强调诸如“车手是最好的传感器，而费尔南多经验丰富”这样的观点，因此他一直小心翼翼，不愿直接否定阿隆索所报告的担忧，甚至在有证据反驳这些担忧时，也不暗示这些问题是被捏造的。

    不过，考虑到被报告和强调的所谓非问题出现的频率，确实让人感觉本田正被逼得坐立不安。

    阿隆索倾向于用戏剧化的方式来强调自己的观点，这一点众所周知，而这些例子出现在一个极其令人失望的赛季深处，与一位受够了的车手有意将聚光灯对准本田的做法相符——正如他在迈凯伦-本田时代所做的那样。

    毕竟，本田在这段时间里的实际问题一直很严重：阿塞拜疆的机油系统异常、新加坡的驾驶性问题、墨西哥反复出现的冷却故障以及巴西的电池故障。

    考虑到预计本田的引擎在2027年开局时不会有明显提升，之后才会按计划在赛季初进行升级，那么现在这种雪上加霜的处境，或许正是阿隆索在漫长而最终徒劳的赛季后的挫败感，再加上对明年究竟能好多少的早期忧虑。

    英文原文

    Honda has not found any issue with Fernando Alonso's engine after he abruptly abandoned finishing last week's Las Vegas Grand Prix before starting his final lap.

    Alonso locked up late in the penultimate lap when running 18th in Vegas, which was another very poor race on a power-sensitive circuit for Honda-powered Aston Martin, then reported "broken engine" over the radio and declared he was coming into the pits.

    Seemingly in a display of frustration he had another big lock-up as he jumped on the brakes in the pit entry then after the race said the engine was "down" in qualifying and the beginning of the race, and "in the last two laps the engine completely gave up".

    Honda has been unable to find any evidence of such a problem, though.

    "We haven't found any issue on the engine from the data and also from the maintenance," said Honda's lead trackside representative Shintaro Orihara.

    "But again, we will check on Friday how the engine works at this track."

    It is one of several recent examples of Alonso reporting engine issues, even going as far as retiring a seemingly healthy car as he did in Azerbaijan, and Honda subsequently being unable to verify it either on the data or in physical inspections.

    "We carefully analysed his comment and also we respect his comment," said Orihara.

    "That's why we checked all data and also we checked the engine condition.

    "But from the data, at this moment, we haven't seen any issue."

    Alonso will not be able to comment on the issue further on Thursday in Qatar as he is missing media day while overcoming a viral infection Aston Martin said he contracted towards the end of the Vegas week.

    But his post-race remarks included declaring "this was probably the worst race of the year" for straightline performance, 24 hours after he referred to it as "a demo lap for us" in qualifying.

    "Probably, this year, our engine performance is not enough to give satisfaction or confidence to the driver," Orihara said.

    "And then, probably, they feel some feeling that the engine performance is not great."

    Aston Martin team principal Mike Krack said that the nature of the 2026 engines could be playing a part in what the drivers perceive to be abnormal behaviour.

    He gave the example of how powerful the MGU-K is at peak output now - 350kW, almost half the engine's total power - and how it is deployed differently in different parts of the circuit. So if there is variation here compared to what the driver expects, it can feel very different.

    Orihara has also offered some mitigation like this in the past, such as referencing the discomfort drivers feel when turbo lag is especially poor.

    Still, this is at least the third instance in the last seven races where Alonso reported a problem and Honda said it could not find or confirm it on the data.

    In Singapore, Alonso claimed after qualifying the turbo was broken – it was not but Honda decided to change the components based on his feedback, and by Sunday night still had not seen any big issues on the data. Orihara doubled down on this at the next race in the United States, confirming the engine had gone back to Sakura and a "good inspection" had not identified a major issue either.

    Alonso reported a loss of power while fighting Valtteri Bottas's Cadillac at the very power-sensitive Baku circuit. Honda checked all the data and after the race did not find any issue. More maintenance work and data review was carried out afterwards with no indication of an engine problem.

    After Baku, and indeed after Las Vegas, Orihara also said that Honda's engine had performed as expected in terms of power delivery, too.

    Orihara has repeatedly stressed sentiments like "the driver is the best sensor and Fernando has a lot of experience" so has been at pains not to just dismiss Alonso's reported concerns out of hand or, even when the evidence disputes them, imply that the issues were fictionalised.

    It does feel, though, that Honda is being made to squirm given the rate of apparent non-issues being reported and highlighted.

    Alonso's tendency to engage in theatrics to stress his point are well known and the proximity of the examples deep into an immensely disappointing season is consistent with a fed-up driver intentionally putting the spotlight on Honda - as he has done before back in the McLaren-Honda days.

    After all, Honda's actual problems in this time have been significant: an oil system irregularity in Azerbaijan, driveability issues in Singapore, chronic cooling failures in Mexico and battery faults in Brazil.

    With some expectation that Honda's engine will not be a significant improvement at the very start of 2027 before a planned early in-season upgrade, perhaps the twisting of the knife now is Alonso's frustration after a long and ultimately wasted season mixed with early trepidation about how much better next year is really likely to be.
