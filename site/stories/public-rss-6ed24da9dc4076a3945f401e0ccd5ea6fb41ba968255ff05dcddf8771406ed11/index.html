    哈贾尔：拉斯维加斯排位赛“我们看起来很蠢”，我不喜欢尾流

    哈贾尔不喜欢尾流，但这在拉斯维加斯排位赛中让他付出了代价

    哈贾尔承认自己在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中表现不佳，原因是没意识到这条高速街道赛道上尾流的威力。

    哈贾尔在Q2比红牛队友维斯塔潘慢0.67秒，在Q3慢0.44秒，最终位列第五；这一差距可能部分源于他的个人偏好，他告诉天空体育F1，他“只是没有真正专注于利用尾流”。

    “我们没有尾流，”这位二年级车手随后对文字媒体说。“我们在最后一轮跑得有点蠢，但其他车队玩得非常聪明，我们在出维修区时他们把车停在1号弯。最后，我发现自己孤零零的。[我的最后一圈]不错，但你在直道上就是没用，所以我宁愿直接放弃。”

    当被Motorsport.com问及这是否关乎下次更好地玩这些把戏时，哈贾尔回答说：“反正我不太喜欢那些把戏。我喜欢把车开得更快。只是你无法真正控制的事情，有点像掷骰子。

    “我不在乎。只要我知道我在弯道里更快，我就对此感到高兴。”

    然而，维斯塔潘的经历清楚地表明，尾流可以多么关键。

    “这里的尾流效应非常夸张，因为我在Q2时差点被淘汰，”这位荷兰车手说道。“然后我出去跑最后一圈，当时我落后前车四到四点五秒，单圈却赚了大约零点七到零点八秒，而在弯道里其实并没有获得那么多。这恰恰说明，如果你有不错的尾流，单圈能赚到多少时间。”

    尽管哈贾尔在Q3第二次尝试中未能提升成绩，这意味着他从第四掉到第五，但他仍将从第四位发车，而维斯塔潘则因速度标杆安东内利因超出引擎配额被罚退五位而继承杆位。

    “最终，我们明天从第四位发车，所以我们的位置不错，有机会登上领奖台，”这位法国车手乐观地评论道。“我认为在这条赛道上，以当前的规则，凭借这些超长直道，你可以轻松超车。所以第四、第五、第二……最终其实并不重要，所以我很开心。”

    Isack Hadjar: “We looked stupid” in Las Vegas as I don’t like slipstreaming

    Hadjar is no fan of tows, but that cost him dear in Vegas qualifying

    Isack Hadjar has admitted to underperforming in qualifying for Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix due to not realizing how powerful the tow would be on this high-speed street circuit.

    Hadjar was 0.67s slower than Red Bull team-mate Max Verstappen in Q2 and 0.44s adrift in Q3 as he ended up down in fifth, and the gap may be partly down to his personal preferences as he told Sky Sports F1 he “just didn’t really focus on having a tow”.

    “We had no tow,” the sophomore then told written media. “We looked a bit stupid out there for the final run, but the other teams played it very smart, parking the car in Turn 1 while we were exiting the pits. In the end, I saw I was alone. [My final] lap was good, but you're just useless down the straight, so I preferred to just abort.”

    Asked by Motorsport.com if it was about playing the games a bit better next time, Hadjar replied: “I don't really like those games anyway. I like driving the car faster. It's just things you can't really control, it's a bit like rolling the dice.

    “I don't really care. As long as I know I was quicker around the corners, I'm happy about that.”

    Yet, Verstappen’s experience made it clear how crucial slipstreaming could be.

    “The tow around here is crazy, because I was actually close to getting knocked out in Q2,” the Dutchman said. “Then I went out on my final lap and I was four or four-and-a-half seconds behind the car, and I gained like seven or eight tenths on the lap while not actually gaining that much in the corners. That just tells you how much you actually gain around the lap if you have a decent tow.”

    Despite failing to improve on his second Q3 attempt, meaning he dropped from fourth to fifth, Hadjar will start fourth with Verstappen inheriting pole position, courtesy of pacesetter Kimi Antonelli’s five-place grid penalty for exceeding his engine allowance.

    “In the end, we're starting fourth tomorrow, so we're in a good position for a podium,” the Frenchman optimistically commented. “I think it's a track where you can easily overtake with these massive straights, with the current regulations. So P4, P5, P2… It doesn't really matter in the end, so I'm happy.”
