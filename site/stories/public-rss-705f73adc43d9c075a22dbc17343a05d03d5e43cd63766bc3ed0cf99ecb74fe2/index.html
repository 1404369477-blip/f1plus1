    勒克莱尔透露墨西哥站担忧无法超车

    勒克莱尔对墨西哥大奖赛中的超车表示怀疑，警告比赛顺序可能在前几个弯道就确定。他在周五的练习赛中排名第一，但只能排在第三位发车。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔透露墨西哥站担心无法超车，在错过杆位后

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔警告在墨西哥大奖赛上超车可能几乎不可能

    对于法拉利车迷来说。这是最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，车手更新，技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔怀疑墨西哥大奖赛将没有超车，警告在赫尔曼诺斯·罗德里格斯赛道的前几个弯道，比赛顺序可能就已经确定。

    法拉利车手在周五的两次练习赛中都位居第一，但最终只能排在发车阵容的第三位。他的队友、七届冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿驾驶着马拉内洛车队获得了他的首个杆位，将与梅赛德斯车队的乔治·罗素一起占据前排。

    勒克莱尔对法拉利的比赛速度充满信心，但他认为墨西哥城赛道的低抓地力条件将使得超车变得极其困难。

    “我认为速度很好，我认为赛车感觉很好，自从第一节练习以来就感觉很好。所以我认为我们在这一点上已经拥有了比赛速度，但我怀疑明天不会有任何超车发生，”勒克莱尔在赛后新闻发布会上解释道。

    “我在第二节练习中尝试得更多，但非常困难，因为超车模式并没有起到太大作用。所以，我认为在3号弯之后，超车将会非常困难。所以，最终在关键时刻没有完成这项工作，这有点遗憾。”

    问他是否能利用队友的牵引，他说：“是的，我的意思是我会尽可能地保持冷静，看看能做什么，但是，是的，一号弯道总是很刺激，我们将看看结果如何。”

    勒克莱尔目前在车手积分榜上排名第五，积分为233分，但P2和P5之间的差距只有9分，争夺P2的竞争变得更加激烈。梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利目前领跑积分榜，并有机会在本周末的墨西哥大奖赛上夺得冠军。拉塞尔排名第二，汉密尔顿第三，马克斯·维斯塔潘排名第四。

    墨西哥大奖赛将于11月1日下午2点（英国时间晚上8点）举行。

    Charles Leclerc reveals Mexican GP overtaking fears after missing out on pole

    Charles Leclerc has warned that overtaking could prove almost impossible at the Mexican Grand Prix

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Charles Leclerc doubts there will be any overtaking in the Mexican Grand Prix, warning the race order will likely be set in the first few corners at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez.

    The Ferrari driver topped both of Friday's practice sessions but had to settle for third on the grid. His team-mate and seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton claimed his first pole position with the Maranello outfit and will be joined on the front row by Mercedes' George Russell.

    Leclerc is confident in Ferrari's race pace but believes the low-grip conditions at the Mexico City circuit will make passing extremely difficult.

    "I think the pace is good. I think the car feels good and felt good since FP1. So I think on that we've got the race pace, but I doubt there will be any overtakes tomorrow," Leclerc explained during the post-qualifying press conference.

    "I've tried more in FP2, and it's just very difficult because the overtake mode doesn't do much. So, I think whatever the standing is after Turn 3, it's going to be very difficult then to overtake. So, it's a bit of a shame that I didn't do the job eventually when it mattered."

    Asked whether he could take advantage of a tow from his team-mate, he said: "Yeah, I mean I’ll keep my head down as much as possible and see what’s possible, but yeah, it’s always an exciting Turn 1 there, and we’ll see how it goes."

    Leclerc currently sits fifth in the drivers' standings with 233 points, but the fight for P2 is becoming more tense as only nine points separate P2 and P5. Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli currently leads the championship and has the opportunity to claim the title at this weekend's Mexican Grand Prix. Russell sits second, Hamilton third and Max Verstappen fourth.

    The Mexican Grand Prix will take place on 1 November at 2pm local time (8pm UK time).
