    F1与圣保罗达成新协议，巴西大奖赛至2035年保留

    巴西大奖赛将在F1赛历上保留至至少2035年，双方达成了一份为期五年的合同延期。该赛事正式更名为圣保罗大奖赛，以反映市政府的财政贡献。

    F1与圣保罗达成新协议，巴西大奖赛保留至2035年

    一级方程式高层、圣保罗市和Interlagos推广者已达成一项新协议，将巴西保留在赛历上直至2035年

    在利益相关者同意五年合同延期后，巴西大奖赛将至少保留在一级方程式赛历上至2035年

    正式更名为圣保罗大奖赛，以反映市政府的财务贡献，巴西赛事上次签订合同延期是在2023年赛事前夕，这是一项将赛事延长至2030年的五年协议。

    但是，在F1管理层希望长期锁定其关键市场的愿望中，巴西作为下一个要得到这种待遇的比赛，誓言继续升级其老化的基础设施。长期安排为当局提供了抵消这些投资稳定性的能力。

    锦标赛于1990年重返José Carlos Pace赛道，从里约热内卢迁至此地。

    F1 retains Brazilian GP until 2035 with fresh Sao Paulo deal

    Formula 1 chiefs, the city of Sao Paulo and Interlagos promoters have agreed to a new deal keeping Brazil on the calendar until 2035

    The Brazilian Grand Prix will remain on the Formula 1 calendar until at least 2035 after stakeholders agreed a five-year contract extension.

    Officially dubbed the Sao Paulo Grand Prix to reflect the financial contribution of the city council, the Brazil race last signed a contract extension on the eve of the 2023 event, a five-year deal that took the race to 2030.

    But amid a desire for F1 management to lock up its races in key market for the long term, Brazil is the next race in line to get that treatment as it vows to continue upgrading its ageing infrastructure. The long-term arrangement gives the authorities the stability to write off those investments.

    The championship returned to the Autodromo Jose Carlos Pace in 1990, switching from Rio de Janeiro.
