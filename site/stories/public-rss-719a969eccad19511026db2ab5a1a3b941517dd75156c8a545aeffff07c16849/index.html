    诺里斯卫冕赛季恐创尴尬纪录

    诺里斯在2025年夺得F1世界冠军后，本赛季卫冕表现糟糕，目前以201分位列车手积分榜第六，还剩六站比赛。若最终排名第六或更低，他将成为1997年达蒙·希尔之后首位卫冕赛季跌出前五的卫冕冠军。

    诺里斯卫冕赛季表现糟糕，恐创下不受欢迎的F1纪录

    诺里斯可能成为自1997年达蒙·希尔以来，首位在卫冕赛季跌出前五的F1世界冠军

    在赢得2025年F1世界冠军并永远将自己的名字写入史册之后，诺里斯正在经历这项锦标赛历史上最糟糕的卫冕赛季之一。

    这位英国车手目前仅积201分，位列车手积分榜第六，本赛季还剩六站比赛。如果他最终排名第六或更低，他将成为自1997年达蒙·希尔以来首位在夺冠后的下一个赛季跌出前五的卫冕冠军。

    希尔在1996年随威廉姆斯车队赢得总冠军，但随后被车队放弃，转投飞箭车队，在1997年获得第12名。自那以后，卫冕冠军最差的年终排名是第五名。

    1998年：雅克·维伦纽夫，威廉姆斯车队，第五名

    可以说，尼科·罗斯伯格在2016年成为总冠军后因退役而未能卫冕，但我不确定这算不算数。

    诺里斯目前仅落后排名第五的法拉利车手查尔斯·勒克莱尔14分，落后排名第四的马克斯·维斯塔潘20分。因此，尽管本赛季已无冲刺赛周末，诺里斯仍有可能摆脱这一低迷状态。但前提是迈凯伦能在为时已晚之前改进赛车。

    “赛车速度不够快，而且太难驾驶了。我们差得很远，”他在新加坡大奖赛获得第四名后对媒体表示。

    “你必须始终着眼于整体套件，你知道吗？”车队领队安德烈亚·斯特拉说。“我想我们知道，在长直道赛道上，我们表现并不太好。这套套件在这些赛道上不够强。

    “所以像巴库、蒙扎、拉斯维加斯这样的赛道会很困难。赛车在低速、非常小且紧的弯角里，当你必须踩刹车时……这类情况下表现很强。我觉得它相当不错，但还不是最好的，所以我们需要在这方面努力。你知道它有优势，也有弱点，在赛季剩余时间里会有起伏。”

    Lando Norris risks unwanted F1 record as disastrous title defence continues

    Lando Norris risks becoming the first reigning Formula 1 world champion since Damon Hill in 1997 to finish his title defence outside the top five

    After winning the 2025 Formula 1 championship and forever solidifying his name in the history books, Lando Norris is putting together one of the worst title defences the championship has ever seen.

    The Briton currently sits sixth in the drivers' standings on just 201 points with six races to go. If he finishes sixth or lower, he will be the first reigning champion to finish outside the top five the following season since Damon Hill in 1997.

    Hill won the title with Williams in 1996, but was dropped by the team and moved to Arrows, finishing 12th in 1997. Since then, the worst a reigning champion has finished is fifth.

    1998: Jacques Villeneuve, Williams, fifth

    Arguably, Nico Rosberg failed to defend his title after becoming champion in 2016 due to his retirement, but I'm not sure that counts.

    Norris is only 14 points behind Ferrari's Charles Leclerc, who sits in fifth, and 20 points behind Max Verstappen in fourth. So even though there are no sprint race weekends left this season, it's still possible for Norris to pull himself out of this slump. But that's if McLaren can improve the car before it's too late.

    "The car's not quick enough and it's too difficult to drive. We're a long way off," he told the media after he finished fourth at the Singapore Grand Prix.

    "You have to always look at the package, you know?" team principal Andrea Stella said. "I think we know that tracks with long straights, we are not that great. The package is not strong in these circuits.

    "So tracks like Baku, like Monza, like Las Vegas will be difficult. The the car is strong in low speed, in very small tight, little corners when you have to be on brakes... these kind of things. I think it's quite good, but it's not the best so we need to work on that. You know it has strength, it has weaknesses and it will go up and down for the rest of the season."
