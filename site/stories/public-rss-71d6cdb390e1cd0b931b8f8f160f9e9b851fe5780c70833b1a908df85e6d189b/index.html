    劳森分享复古视频，十年后首次现场演出

    劳森不仅在奥斯汀驾驶小红牛赛车参赛，还在周四进行了他十年来的首次现场音乐演出。演出前，他发布了一段与兄弟和童年朋友的复古视频。

    劳森在首次超过十年的现场表演前分享了怀旧视频

    小红牛车手劳森与他的兄弟和童年朋友们重聚，为他首次现场音乐表演助阵

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    劳森不仅仅是在奥斯汀驾驶小红牛赛车来参加比赛，你知道吗？在周四，他还进行了十多年来的首次现场音乐表演。在演出前，他发布了一段自己与兄弟和童年朋友们一起演奏的怀旧视频。

    这篇帖子在撰写时已经获得了87,000个赞和超过370条评论，粉丝们兴奋地期待着在美洲赛车场的比赛周末看到这位新西兰车手。

    劳森在周四晚上美国大奖赛之前来到了Esses Bazaar的舞台，与他的兄弟马库斯和童年好友托马斯和哈里森重逢。这是他自11岁以来第一次在人群中表演。

    “我11岁时曾在人群中表演，那也是我最后一次这样做。已经很久了，”他在表演前对《人物》杂志说。

    劳森和他的兄弟在父亲引导下一起在新西兰长大，一起弹奏音乐。

    “音乐一直伴随着我的生活和家人的生活，自从我非常小的时候起，”劳森继续说道。 “他让我和我的兄弟从小就接触吉他。我们基本上是一起长大的。”

    他补充说，他为了吉他考试学习了七年，甚至在孩提时代就写了自己的歌曲。尽管如此，他现在并没有打算开始写歌。

    “和我的兄弟还有我从小一起玩的朋友们一起做这件事是个很酷的机会，”劳森对媒体说。 “说实话，我们大概有十年没做过这样的事情了。”

    这次表演吸引了许多一级方程式明星，丹尼尔·里卡多、伊萨克·哈贾尔和阿维德·林德布拉德都出现在东第六街的Inn Cahoots。”

    劳森在定制的泰勒吉他上演奏了一首乡村风格的经典曲目。

    小红牛后来称这是一场“充满音乐和朋友的难忘之夜”。

    劳森在即将到来的美洲赛车场的比赛中，以75分位列车手锦标赛第九位。

    Liam Lawson shares throwback video before first live performance in over a decade

    Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson reunited with his brother and childhood friends for his first live music performance

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Liam Lawson isn't just in Austin to race behind the wheel of a Racing Bulls car, you know? On Thursday, he also played his first live music gig in over a decade. Before the show, he posted a throwback clip of himself playing beside his brother and childhood friends.

    The post had passed 87,000 likes and more than 370 comments at the time of writing, with fans excited to see the Kiwi over the race weekend at Circuit of the Americas.

    Lawson took to the stage at the Esses Bazaar on Thursday night ahead of the United States Grand Prix, reuniting with his brother Marcus and childhood friends Thomas and Harrison. It was the first time he had played in front of a crowd since he was 11.

    “I played in front of a crowd when I was 11, and that was the last time I did it. It’s been a long time,” he told People before the show.

    The group grew up playing music together in New Zealand, after Lawson’s father got both of his sons into playing the guitar.

    “Music’s been in my life and my family’s life since I was very, very young,” Lawson continued. “He got me and my brother into guitar when we were very young. We grew up basically playing together.”

    He added that he had studied for guitar exams for seven years, and even wrote his own songs as a child. He doesn't have plans to start writing now, though.

    “It’s a cool opportunity to do this with my brother and a couple of friends that I’ve grown up playing with,” Lawson told the media. “To be honest, we haven’t done this for like 10 years.”

    The performance drew a strong turnout of Formula 1 stars, with Daniel Ricciardo, Isack Hadjar and Arvid Lindblad all in the crowd at Inn Cahoots on East Sixth Street.

    Lawson played a mostly country set of covers on a signature Taylor guitar made for him.

    Racing Bulls later called it “an unforgettable evening filled with music and friends”.

    Lawson is ninth in the drivers’ championship on 75 points heading into this weekend’s race at the Circuit of the Americas.
