    据《每日邮报》报道，七届F1世界冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿希望在洛杉矶安家，以便在冬歇期有更多时间陪伴伴侣金·卡戴珊及其孩子。

    报道称，这位法拉利车手曾就马里布、太平洋帕利塞德和好莱坞山的房产与房产中介接触，并正在权衡是租还是买。他的主要住所位于摩纳哥，而卡戴珊住在隐山，那是洛杉矶市中心西北部的一个封闭式社区。

    两人自一月起被传出恋情，汉密尔顿后来在社交媒体上分享了他们的合照。此后卡戴珊成为维修区的熟悉面孔，六月在摩纳哥、上个月在新加坡观看他比赛。

    汉密尔顿也温情地谈到她与说唱歌手坎耶·韦斯特婚姻所生的孩子诺斯、圣、普萨姆和芝加哥。

    在上个月美国大奖赛前于奥斯汀举行的一场车迷活动上，他说：“我女朋友的孩子们，我很喜欢和他们相处。”

    “她有四个孩子，所以我能和这些孩子们一起玩。今年我给他们买了一只狗，所以小Halo是这个家庭的新成员。”

    这发生在汉密尔顿起伏不定的赛季之中。在马拉内罗车队经历艰难的第一年后，随着规则的变化，他恢复了昔日夺冠的状态。他在中国大奖赛上为法拉利首次登上领奖台，随后在巴塞罗那庆祝了他身穿红色赛车服的首个大奖赛胜利。

    尽管汉密尔顿仍在争夺车手锦标赛第二名，但动力单元罚退以及与队友夏尔·勒克莱尔的赛道冲突影响了最近几场比赛。

    本赛季还剩三场比赛，汉密尔顿以261分排名第四，落后第三名梅赛德斯的乔治·拉塞尔一分，落后第二名红牛的马克斯·维斯塔潘18分。基米·安东内利已在墨西哥大奖赛上锁定了车手冠军。

    Lewis Hamilon reportedly house-hunting in LA amid Kim Kardashian relationship

    The Ferrari driver has reportedly approached estate agents about renting or buying a property

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton wants a Los Angeles base so he can spend more of the winter break with his partner Kim Kardashian and her children, according to a report from the Daily Mail.

    The report says the Ferrari driver approached estate agents about houses in Malibu, Pacific Palisades and the Hollywood Hills, and is weighing up whether to rent or buy. His main home is in Monaco, while Kardashian lives in Hidden Hills, a gated community north-west of central Los Angeles.

    The couple have been linked since January, and Hamilton later shared photos of them together on social media. Kardashian has since become a familiar face in the paddock, watching him race in Monaco in June and in Singapore last month.

    Hamilton has also spoken warmly about her children, North, Saint, Psalm and Chicago, from her marriage to rapper Kanye West.

    At a fan event in Austin before last month's United States Grand Prix, he said: "My girlfriend's kids, I love spending time with them.

    "She’s got four, so I’m getting to play with these kids. I got them a dog this year, so little Halo is a new member of that family."

    This comes amid an up-and-down season for Hamilton. After a rocky first year with the Maranello outfit, he bounced back to his old race-winning form with the turn of the regulations. He secured his first podium with Ferrari at the Chinese Grand Prix and later celebrated his first grand prix win in red in Barcelona.

    While Hamilton remains in the fight for second in the drivers' championship, power unit penalties and on-track clashes with his team-mate Charles Leclerc have marred the last few races.

    With three races remaining in the season, Hamilton is fourth with 261 points, one point behind Mercedes' George Russell in third and 18 points behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen in second. Kimi Antonelli already secured the drivers' title at the Mexican Grand Prix.
