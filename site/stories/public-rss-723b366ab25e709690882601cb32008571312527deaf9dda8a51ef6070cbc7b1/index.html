    梅赛德斯因乔治·拉塞尔而只能冒险启用安东内利，托托·沃尔夫对团队表示

    梅赛德斯车队表示，如果没有乔治·拉塞尔，他们不会冒险提升安东内利。安东内利在2025年加入梅赛德斯，成为继七届F1世界冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿加盟法拉利后的新成员。

    梅赛德斯只能赌一把金米·安东内利，因为乔治·拉塞尔，托托·沃尔夫告诉了工作人员

    托托·沃尔夫告诉梅赛德斯的工作人员，拉塞尔的水平使得提拔一名新手成为一场计算过的风险

    对于梅赛德斯车迷来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析

    根据托托·沃尔夫发给整个团队的信息，如果没有乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯不可能冒险提拔金米·安东内利

    安东内利在2025年加入了梅赛德斯车队，此前七次F1冠军车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿转投法拉利。在F1赛车的第二个赛季，这位意大利车手在墨西哥大奖赛上成为了F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。另一方面，尽管拉塞尔帮助车队在巴西赢得了车队 constructors' title，但他今年经历了一个令人沮丧的赛季。

    在天空体育的NU银箭车队广播节目中，副车队经理布拉德利·劳德透露，沃尔夫通过一封发给工厂的电子邮件强调了拉塞尔在车队成功中的贡献。

    “托托上周提出了一个精彩的观点：他给整个工厂发了一封电子邮件，并指出如果没有乔治这样级别的车手，我们作为一支车队在战略上就不可能像我们对待基米那样冒险引入一名新人，尽管这仍然是一种风险，”劳德说。

    “这是因为我们知道，在乔治身上，我们有一位能够为和赢得世界锦标赛而竞争的车手。在基米的故事和他作为梅赛德斯青训项目培养的车手的发展的兴奋中，我们绝对不能忽视这一点。”

    拉塞尔作为2026赛季夺冠热门进入赛季。但在赢得赛季揭幕战澳大利亚大奖赛和中国短道赛之后，他遇到了许多问题，其中大部分是他无法控制的。

    老板将2026年描述为拉塞尔F1生涯中最好和最艰难的一年。

    他说：“这既是他在一级方程式中最成功的赛季，同时也是他最具有挑战性和令人失望的赛季。正如你所说，他在遭遇不幸时，也展现了自己从挫折中恢复的能力。”

    乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯、安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯

    Lord补充说，在圣保罗大奖赛之后，他在工程办公室提醒了拉塞尔，构造商冠军头衔仍然是'你的第一个冠军'。拉塞尔在萨基尔大奖赛中作为汉密尔顿的替代者为梅赛德斯贡献了三个积分，但这是他作为全职梅赛德斯车手的第一个冠军。

    技术总监James Allison赞扬了拉塞尔关于安东内利在本赛季哪里有优势的诚实态度。

    '他是我在有幸与他共事的所有车手中最诚实的一个。他可以看到今年基米比他更快的地方，他知道这些都是他需要带到桌面上来与这位早熟年轻天才平等或更好竞争的东西，'Allison说。

    '而且我非常钦佩他直面这个问题的态度。'

    拉塞尔在车手积分榜上排名第三，落后于马克斯·维斯塔潘17分，争夺第二名。

    Mercedes could only gamble on Kimi Antonelli because of George Russell, Toto Wolff told staff

    Toto Wolff told Mercedes staff that Russell’s calibre made promoting a rookie a calculated risk

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes would not have been able to take the risk of promoting Kimi Antonelli without already having George Russell, according to a message Toto Wolff sent to the entire team.

    Antonelli joined Mercedes in 2025 after seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton moved to Ferrari. In only his second season in the series, the Italian driver became the youngest world champion in the history of F1 at the Mexican Grand Prix. Russell, on the other hand, has endured a frustrating year, despite helping the team to the constructors' title in Brazil.

    Speaking on the NU Silver Arrows Radio Show, deputy team principal Bradley Lord revealed that Wolff had used an email to the factory to underline Russell's part in the team's success.

    "Toto made a brilliant point last week: he sent an email to the whole factory, and he made the point that without a driver of George's calibre, we as a team strategically would not have been able to take the risk of bringing a rookie in as we did with Kimi and take that calculated risk, but a risk nonetheless," Lord said.

    "And that's because we know in George we have a driver who is capable of racing for and winning world championships. It's really important that we don't lose sight of that in the excitement around Kimi's story and his development as a Mercedes-grown driver from the junior programme."

    Russell entered the 2026 season as a clear favourite for the title. But after winning the season-opening Australian Grand Prix and the Chinese sprint race, he encountered numerous issues, most of which were outside of his control.

    Lord described 2026 as both the best and the hardest year of Russell's F1 career.

    "It's been both the most successful season of his career in Formula 1 and the most challenging and disappointing season for him at the same time," he said. "As you say, he's absolutely been through the wringer in terms of some of the misfortune that he's suffered. Each time he's had a disappointment, he's also shown himself capable of bouncing back from it."

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    Lord added that he had reminded Russell in the engineering office after the Sao Paulo Grand Prix that the constructors' title was still "your first championship". Russell did contribute three points to Mercedes' 2020 crown as Lewis Hamilton's stand-in at the Sakhir Grand Prix, but this is his first title as a full-time Mercedes driver.

    Technical director James Allison praised Russell's honesty about where Antonelli has had the edge this season.

    "He's one of the most honest drivers I've ever had the good fortune to work with. He can see the areas where Kimi has been quicker than him this year, and he knows that those are things he needs to bring to the table to compete on equal or better terms with this precocious young talent," Allison said.

    "And I do very much admire the fact that he looks straight down the barrel of that."

    Russell is third in the championship, 17 points behind Max Verstappen in the battle for second.
