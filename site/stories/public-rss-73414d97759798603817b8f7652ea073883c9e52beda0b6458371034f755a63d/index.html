    谁将赢得今年的终极F1梦想门票？您来决定...

    经过数百份大奖赛终极奖品的申请，喜力x F1赛季门票已缩减至六位决赛者，F1粉丝现在可以投票决定获胜者。

    谁将赢得今年终极F1梦想门票？由你决定……

    今年喜力F1赛季门票的决赛选手名单已经确定——现在F1粉丝可以选出他们想要追随的人

    在数百份大奖赛终极奖品的申请之后，喜力x F1赛季门票已经缩减至六位决赛选手——现在F1粉丝可以投票决定谁将获胜。

    这些候选人来自英国、新西兰、新加坡、捷克和美国——他们都已经提出了他们一生中冒险的提案，其中一位将获得免费旅行、住宿以及下赛季每场F1比赛的通行证。

    全球的F1粉丝被挑战分享他们对这项运动的热情，并解释为什么他们比任何人都值得获得一个难忘的赛季，体验独特的事件，与粉丝建立联系并亲眼目睹比赛。

    这项活动由喜力设计，旨在展示通过共同的运动热情可以形成的联系，证明了‘粉丝拥有更多朋友’的理念。

    F1粉丝布兰登·伯格是首位获得金色门票的人，他在本赛季已经将他的一些故事分享给了他社交媒体上的近15万名粉丝。他的旅程现在即将结束——现在是时候选择下一个‘明星’来接替他的位置了。

    参赛名单展示了这项运动的全球粉丝基础和比赛的巨大影响力——最后六位都有独特的故事要讲述，都有不同的理由让他们值得那个特别的奖项。

    Callum，来自苏格兰，小时候曾在电视前假装自己是迈克尔·舒马赫。他是一位热衷于电影摄影的人，并表示：“我希望能带你看看F1赛季票背后的场景，用电影记录F1的人和地方。”

    新西兰人Dale表示，他想要探索每一场比赛背后的“F1世界”，了解独特的人物、文化和粉丝。“我想遇见那些让它变得特别的人，并带大家一起体验这份乐趣，”他说。

    住在捷克共和国的Lukas表示，赢得奖品将“终于给我的沙发带来一些宁静”并承诺粉丝，他们可以通过为他选择每个周末要参加的挑战来“成为车队老板”，随着赛季的进行分享结果。

    来自英国的India称自己是一个“完全普通的粉丝”，希望明年能变得“非凡”，并要求其他粉丝为每场比赛给她设定有趣的挑战，表示赢得比赛将“将我对F1和环游世界的两个激情结合起来。”

    Joy，她的家乡新加坡的比赛在本月早些时候举行，她希望利用这次机会体验“全球F1的激动和体育精神”，并有机会与周围的人“分享同样的热情”。

    Jose来自美国，他不仅是赛车迷，还迷上了F1背后的策略、技术和团队合作。他去年参观了他在拉斯维加斯的家乡比赛，并表示：“如果能在2027年每场比赛都去旅行，那将真的是梦想成真。”

    前往每个大奖赛的机会为这项运动及其粉丝打开了前所未有的接触途径。当前的票持有者Burgess已经展示了如何在这个赛季充分利用这次经历，并且他曾是挑选候选人名单的评审团成员。

    在比赛开始前，他说：“我将关注的重点是F1的热情，因为只有真正的粉丝才能充分利用这次机会。放弃它将会很困难，而且选择下一个人的压力相当大。”

    比赛面向25岁及以上的粉丝开放，参赛者被要求说出他们最喜欢的车队和车手，并从F1和喜力提供的选项中选择他们愿意带去未来比赛的选手。

    然后随机选取了50名参赛者，邀请他们提交一个90秒的视频，说明为什么他们应该成为下一个票证持有者——现在有六个最终视频可供观看，所以粉丝可以决定谁应该赢得这一终身冒险的机会。

    Who will win this year’s ultimate F1 dream ticket? You decide…

    The shortlist of finalists for this year’s Heineken F1 Season Ticket has been set – now F1 fans can choose who they want to follow

    After hundreds of applications for the ultimate prize in grand prix racing, the Heineken x F1 Season Ticket is down to six finalists – and F1 fans can now vote to decide who wins.

    The candidates – from the UK, New Zealand, Singapore, Czechia and the USA – have all put their pitches forward for the adventure of a lifetime, which will see one of them get free travel, accommodation and passes for every single F1 race next season.

    The competition challenged F1 fans around the world to share their passion for the sport and explain why they, more than anyone, deserve to be gifted an unforgettable season of racing, experiencing unique events, connecting with fans and watching the action first hand.

    The initiative was designed by Heineken to showcase the connections that can be formed through a shared passion for the sport, demonstrating that ‘Fans Have More Friends.’

    F1 fan Brandon Burgess was the first recipient of the golden ticket and has been posting his stories to almost 150,000 followers on his social channels this season. His journey has now almost run its course – and now it is time to choose the next ‘star’ to take over his mantle.

    The list of entries demonstrates the sport’s global fanbase and the massive reach of the competition – and the final six all have a unique story to tell, with a different reason why they deserve that special prize.

    Callum, from Scotland, used to pretend to be Michael Schumacher in front of the TV when he was a toddler. He’s a keen film photographer and says: “I’d love to take you behind the scenes of the F1 season ticket and show you the people and places of F1 on film.”

    New Zealander, Dale, says he wants to discover the “F1 world” behind every race, exploring the unique people, cultures and fans. “I want to meet the people who make it special and bring everyone along for the ride,” he says.

    Lukas, who lives in Czechia, says winning the prize would “finally be some peace for my sofa” and promises fans they can “be the team boss” by choosing challenges for him to take part in each race weekend, sharing the results as the season goes on.

    India, from the UK, describes herself as a “completely ordinary fan” looking to make next year “extraordinary” – and also asked other fans to set her fun challenges for each race, saying that winning would “bring together my two passions of F1 and seeing the world.”

    Joy, whose home race in Singapore took place earlier this month, wants to use the opportunity to experience “the excitement and sportsmanship” of F1 around the world and to have a chance to “share the same passion” with those around her.

    Jose, from the USA, is a fan not just of the racing but also of the strategy, technology and teamwork behind F1. He visited his home race in Las Vegas last year and said: “Travelling to every race in the 2027 would honestly be a dream come true.”

    The opportunity to travel to every grand prix opens up unprecedented access to the sport and its fans. The current ticket holder Burgess has shown just how to get the best out of the experience this season and he was on the judging panel that picked the shortlist.

    Speaking before the competition began, he said: “The key thing I'll be looking for is the passion for F1, because you have to be a true fan to make the most of this opportunity. It’s going to be hard to give it up and there’s quite a lot of pressure choosing who’s next.”

    The competition was open to fans aged 25 or over, with entrants asked to name their favourite team and driver and select who they would take to a future race from a list of options provided by F1 and Heineken.

    Fifty entrants were then selected at random and invited to submit a 90-second video on why they should become the next ticket holder – and the final six videos are now available to watch, so fans can determine who should win the adventure of a lifetime.
