    法拉利发布美国大奖赛蓝图风格涂装

    法拉利发布了一款由HP设计的美国大奖赛一次性涂装

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更广范围的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析与比赛反应。

    法拉利为本周在美洲赛道举行的美国大奖赛发布了一款特别的一次性涂装。

    这款设计由冠名合作伙伴惠普共同打造，在引擎盖的白色背景和进气口周围，呈现了草图与蓝图风格的插图。

    法拉利色彩与装饰负责人西尔维娅·卡瓦拉罗表示：“这款涂装致敬了从理念到现实的旅程。每一辆法拉利在驶上赛道之前，都始于创意、草图和工程技术专长。”

    “得益于我们与惠普的合作，我们得以在视觉上让这一过程栩栩如生，讲述一个揭示性能背后工艺与创新的故事。”

    这些图案采用惠普Latex打印技术复制，概念由法拉利设计工作室开发。法拉利新车概念专家斯特凡诺·卢帕里亚解释说，最难的部分是让这些图案在赛车上呈现得当。

    他说：“为奥斯汀站开发这款特别涂装面临诸多挑战，涉及项目的创意与技术两个层面。”

    “一方面，我们必须将与法拉利设计工作室共同构思的概念转化为可实际应用的方案，使其适配赛车复杂的表面与空气动力学要求。”

    “另一方面，我们与惠普紧密合作，引入此前从未在该特定应用中使用的创新打印技术。我的职责是协调并验证项目的所有技术环节，确保我们的性能目标得以实现，并确保涂装在美国大奖赛的赛车上正确呈现。”

    虽然部分车迷担心新设计在赛道上难以辨认，但也有人对改动表示赞赏。一位车迷在红迪网上评论道：“说真的，在我看来这是一个华丽的设计。想象一下合金模型……”

    “我知道在时速200英里时你其实看不清它，但就它本身而言，我还挺喜欢的，”另一个人写道，而另一位车迷发帖说：“我挺喜欢这个。好主意，酷概念。”

    Ferrari reveals blueprint-style livery for United States GP

    Ferrari has unveiled a one-off HP-designed livery for the United States Grand Prix

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari has unveiled a special one-off livery for this weekend's United States Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas.

    The design, created with title partner HP, features sketches and blueprint-style illustrations against the white background of the engine cover and around the intake.

    "The livery celebrates the journey from idea to reality," Ferrari's head of colour and trim Silvia Cavallaro said. "Every Ferrari starts with creativity, sketches and engineering expertise long before the car reaches the track.

    "Thanks to our collaboration with HP, we were able to bring that process to life visually and tell a story that reveals the craftsmanship and innovation behind the performance."

    The graphics were reproduced using HP Latex printing technology, and the concept was developed with the Ferrari Design Studio. Ferrari's new car concept specialist Stefano Luparia explained that the hardest part was making the artwork work on a racing car.

    "There were numerous challenges in developing the special livery for Austin, spanning both the creative and technical aspects of the project," he said.

    "On the one hand, we had to transform the concept developed together with Ferrari Design Studio into a solution that could be practically applied, adapting it to the car’s complex surfaces and aerodynamic requirements.

    "On the other, we worked closely with HP to introduce innovative printing technologies that had never before been used in this specific application. My role was to coordinate and validate all the technical aspects of the project, ensuring that our performance objectives were met and that the livery was correctly implemented on the cars for the United States Grand Prix."

    While some fans worried the new design would be hard to see on track, others praised the changes. "Seriously, it's a gorgeous design in my opinion. Imagine the diecast..." one fan commented on Reddit.

    "I know you won’t really be able to see it going at 200 mph, but for what it is, I kinda like it," someone else wrote, while another fan posted: "I quite like this. Nice idea, cool concept."
