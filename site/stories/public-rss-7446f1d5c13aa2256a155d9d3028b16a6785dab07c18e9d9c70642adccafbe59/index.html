    劳森将在美国大奖赛前夕在奥斯汀进行现场音乐表演

    新西兰车手劳森将在美国大奖赛前夕在奥斯汀的VCARB Garage 各节练习与排位中担任头牌，他将在10月22日于Inn Cahoots进行现场表演。

    劳森将在奥斯汀美国大奖赛之前进行现场音乐表演

    劳森将与其兄弟和童年朋友们一起在奥斯汀的VCARB Garage 各节练习与排位进行现场表演

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    劳森将在下周的美国大奖赛之前成为奥斯汀VCARB Garage 各节练习与排位的头条现场表演者

    新西兰人将在10月22日离开驾驶舱，在Inn Cahoots现场表演。这场名为“劳森 & Friends”的表演是Esses Bazaar活动的一部分，届时劳森将和他的兄弟Marcus以及童年好友Thomas和Harrison一同登台。

    名单上还有乡村歌手/词曲作者Jonny Capeci、Scoot Teasley和Jess Farren，以及新兴人才Alison Nichols和Drake Milligan。

    “这是一种你永远感觉不到完全准备好的情况。你总是可以做更多，但我有一个非常具体的工作，这很难融入我的日常中，”当被问及为现场表演做准备时的挑战时，劳森对小红牛说。

    他还补充说，能够和他的兄弟以及童年好友一起表演“非常酷”。“我很兴奋，但也很紧张。有我的兄弟和童年好友陪伴我，自从我八岁起我们就一起玩耍，这真的很酷。”

    24岁的车手也分享了他的音乐灵感："我从小就听我爸的音乐。主要是80年代的摇滚乐。我第一次去音乐会看的是Def Leppard，当时我八九岁。"

    "我爸告诉我，如果我通过吉他考试并获得优异成绩，他就让我去，我做到了。成长过程中，John Mayer也是我的灵感来源。他非常出色，他的音乐知识和歌曲创作都很有意思。我喜欢那些老歌。"

    这发生在劳森一个令人印象深刻的赛季中。他目前在车手积分榜上排名第九，积分为75分。小红牛以100分排名第五。

    美国大奖赛将于10月23日至25日在美国赛车场举行。

    Liam Lawson to perform live music set ahead of F1 United States Grand Prix in Austin

    Liam Lawson is set to perform live alongside his brother and childhood friends at the VCARB Garage Sessions in Austin

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Liam Lawson will headline the live VCARB Garage Sessions in Austin ahead of the United States Grand Prix next weekend.

    The New Zealander will step away from the cockpit to perform live at Inn Cahoots on 22 October. Billed as "Liam Lawson & Friends", the performance forms part of the Esses Bazaar event and will see Lawson joined on stage by his brother, Marcus, alongside childhood friends Thomas and Harrison.

    Also on the line-up are country singer/songwriters Jonny Capeci, Scoot Teasley and Jess Farren, alongside up-and-coming talents Alison Nichols and Drake Milligan.

    "It’s one of those situations where you never feel fully prepared. You can always do more, but I have such a specific job that this can be difficult to fit into my routine," Lawson told Racing Bulls when asked about the challenges of preparing for a live performance.

    He added that it is "pretty cool" to be able to perform alongside his brother and childhood friends. "Excited, but also very, very nervous. To have my brother and childhood friends with me, who I’ve played with since I was eight years old, is pretty cool."

    The 24-year-old also shared some of his musical inspirations: "I grew up listening to my dad’s music. 80s rock, mainly. The first concert I went to was Def Leppard when I was eight or nine.

    "My dad told me that if I passed my guitar exam with a distinction, he’d let me go, and I did. Growing up, John Mayer was an inspiration too. He’s very good and his music knowledge and songwriting are very cool. I like the older stuff."

    This comes during an impressive season for Lawson. He currently sits ninth in the drivers' standings with 75 points. Racing Bulls is fifth with 100 points.

    The United States Grand Prix will take place at the Circuit of the Americas from 23-25 October.
