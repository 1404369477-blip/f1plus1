    马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛周末的戏剧性比赛之后怒斥乔治·拉塞尔，称这位车手"远远低于我的水平"，以至于他不需要回应他。这发生在梅赛德斯车手在他身后冲出第二名之后。

    这对车手在周五的最终练习中发生了冲突，当时 steward 对 维斯塔潘 的不稳定驾驶发出了警告。随后 拉塞尔 表示 维斯塔潘 行事像“他们比所有人都更优越”，并且“有人必须站出来对抗这个人”。

    周六晚上，他有了完美的机会去做这件事，因为 维斯塔潘 和 拉塞尔 从第一排起步。他们在一场由 yo-yo 赛车主导的比赛中多次交换领先，但在第 48 圈，拉塞尔 在试图超越队友 安东内利 以获得第二名时锁定了车辆，撞上了墙壁。他跌到了第九位，但仍然能够继续比赛。维斯塔潘 继续领先，赢得了比赛，领先于 迈凯伦 的 诺里斯 和 皮亚斯特里。

    在霓虹灯下的比赛中，维斯塔潘 在新闻发布会上并没有回避他与英国人的竞争。

    “问题是，他在赛道旁边说得多，老实说，对我来说，这并不真的困扰我，因为他离我的水平太远了，所以对我来说尝试挑战他也没有什么意义。所以这并不困扰我，”维斯塔潘 说。

    他随后转向了拉塞尔比赛结束的那一刻。

    今天， karma 真是个婊子。当我看着镜子，看到发生的一切时，我觉得真的很棒。所以我心想，'太棒了！我的日子只会越来越好！'他说。

    当然，当我后来第一个冲过终点线时，我的日子就完美了。

    这是维斯塔潘本赛季的第四次胜利，在巴林、新加坡和墨西哥之后，并且是他连续第九次登上领奖台。

    这也加强了他对车手积分榜第二名的掌控。维斯塔潘现在有304分，比排名第三的刘易斯·汉密尔顿高出37分，而罗素则下滑至第四，积分为264分，与诺里斯持平，还有两场比赛要进行。

    罗素在赛后表示，他对导致他撞车的队友的举动并不后悔，解释说他是"采取了一个高风险的举动并付出了代价"，并且他本可以满足于第三名。

    梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫则不太宽容，将这次撞车描述为"一个错误"。

    Max Verstappen lashes out at George Russell: "He's so far below my level"

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix winner hit back at Russell's criticism from earlier in the weekend, saying "karma" struck when the Mercedes driver crashed out of second place

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Max Verstappen has lashed out at George Russell following the dramatic Las Vegas Grand Prix race weekend, saying the driver is "so far below my level" that he does not feel the need to respond to him. This came after the Mercedes driver crashed out of second place behind him.

    The pair clashed in final practice on Friday, when Verstappen was given a warning by the stewards for erratic driving. Russell then said Verstappen walks around "like they're bigger and better than everyone else", and that "somebody's got to stand up to that guy".

    On the Saturday night he had the perfect opportunity to do just that as Verstappen and Russell started side by side on the front row. They swapped the lead several times in a race dominated by yo-yo racing, but on lap 48, Russell locked up while trying to pass team-mate Kimi Antonelli for second and hit the wall. He dropped to ninth and was able to keep going. Verstappen went on to win ahead of McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri.

    Speaking in the press conference following his campaign under the neon lights, Verstappen didn't shy away from his rivalry with the Briton.

    "The thing is, he speaks a lot next to the track, and honestly, for me, it doesn't really bother me because he's so far below my level that it doesn't really make any sense for me anyway to try and challenge that. So it doesn't bother me," Verstappen said.

    He then turned to the moment Russell's race ended.

    "Today, karma is a b***h. It was honestly fantastic when I looked in my mirror and I saw what was happening. So I was like, 'Fantastic! My day is only getting better!'" he said.

    "And of course, when I then crossed the line in first, my day was complete."

    It was Verstappen's fourth win of the season, after Bahrain, Singapore and Mexico, and his ninth consecutive podium.

    It also strengthened his grip on second place in the drivers' standings. Verstappen now has 304 points, 37 clear of Lewis Hamilton, who moved up to third, while Russell dropped to fourth on 264 points, level with Norris, with two races to go.

    Russell said after the race that he didn't regret his move on his team-mate that led to his crash, explaining that he "went for a high-risk move and paid the price", and that he could have settled for third.

    Mercedes boss Toto Wolff was less forgiving, describing the crash as "an error".
