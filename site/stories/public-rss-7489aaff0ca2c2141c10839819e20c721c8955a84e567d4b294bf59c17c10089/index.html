    汉密尔顿回应法拉利信任问题传闻

    汉密尔顿坚称法拉利内部没有信任问题，尽管他在最近几站F1大奖赛中对战略决策的否决成为常态。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿针对法拉利“信任”问题进行回应

    这位七届F1冠军在最近几轮比赛中多次无视维修区叫停

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿坚称在法拉利没有“信任”问题，尽管他在最近的几场F1大奖赛中对战略决策的否决已成为常态

    这位七届世界冠军在最近四轮中的两轮比赛中——新加坡和巴西——忽视了进站维修的叫停，但这两次都给斯库德利亚车手带来了负面影响。

    在新加坡，他选择在虚拟安全车下保持轨道位置，但由于轮胎磨损，他在比赛后期从领奖台争夺者降至第六位——对此他在赛后表示了歉意。

    在上次巴西的比赛中，尽管雨势加大，汉密尔顿仍然坚持使用干胎，这导致他在最后一圈发生打滑，并在下一圈进站维修，最终排名第九。

    这些事件发生在法拉利内部关系紧张的话题中，但在本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛之前，汉密尔顿确认一切顺利——尤其是他与2026年新任赛车工程师卡洛·桑蒂的关系。

    ‘这绝对不是信任问题，我们一直在努力改善沟通，’这位41岁的车手说。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    “今年情况好多了。卡洛做得非常出色，尤其是那两次，尤其是最后一次，只是我没有得到我需要的信息。”

    “我在最后一刻接到了电话。我们将继续完善流程。我们在正在有效运作、正确方向的事情上非常团结。”

    “今年对汉密尔顿来说有了很大的提升，他在积分榜上排名第四，还有三轮比赛剩余，目前积261分，比2025年的总和156分多了105分。”

    他的第一个法拉利车队赛季确实令人失望，但尽管与上个赛季相比有显著提升，2026年仍然有一些改进的迹象。

    这不仅是指战略决策，还包括他与队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔的关系，他们在几次比赛中发生冲突，最值得注意的是三周前的墨西哥站。

    这导致法拉利失去了胜利，之后汉密尔顿批评了勒克莱尔的“孤注一掷”态度，并在圣保罗表示摩纳哥人对他比对其他车手的压力更大。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    这导致了双方Scuderia车手以及车队老板Fred Vasseur和汉密尔顿、勒克莱尔之间长达一个小时的会议，现在他们相信问题已经得到解决。

    ‘查尔斯是一位伟大的车手，’汉密尔顿说。‘关于像关闭挡风玻璃罩这样的细节，我们的讨论非常明确，我们彼此理解，我们理解彼此的界限和最终目标，那就是为车队取得最佳成绩。’

    “所以我不觉得那会是个问题。我对与他比赛没有担忧或顾虑，我也不认为他会对我有顾虑。”

    “关于墨西哥碰撞的讨论自然是热烈的，但勒克莱尔认为那是为法拉利驾驶的结果，现在所有的焦点都集中在确保车队在制造商排名中取得第二名。”

    “一切都被夸大了，”勒克莱尔说。 “我已经习惯了，因为这么多年来一直在为法拉利赛车。但事情往往被夸大了。

    “目标并不总是要达成一致，这永远不会发生。我们可以有不同的观点，比如在墨西哥就是这样。"

    “但仅仅是说话就缓解了所有的紧张，我们对此完全没问题。并不是因为我们不得不聊一个小时，尽管最后聊了一个小时，只是因为我们过得愉快。"

    “我们开始谈论许多其他事情，试图帮助团队前进，帮助车队前进。"

    “但是关于紧张的讨论可能只有五到十分钟，然后气氛就变得很轻松，就这样了。”

    法拉利以95分的优势领先第三名的红牛，落后142分于在Interlagos赢得第九个冠军的梅赛德斯。

    Lewis Hamilton addresses speculation around Ferrari "trust" issues

    The seven-time F1 champion has defied pitstop calls on multiple occasions across recent rounds

    Lewis Hamilton has insisted there are zero “trust” problems at Ferrari despite him overruling strategic calls becoming a repeated occurrence across recent Formula 1 grands prix.

    The seven-time world champion has ignored calls to pit in two of the last four rounds - Singapore and Brazil - with both backfiring on the Scuderia driver.

    In Singapore he elected to keep track position under a virtual safety car, but on worn tyres he dropped from podium contention to sixth late on - for which he apologised afterwards.

    Then in Brazil last time out, Hamilton stayed on slicks despite the rain intensifying and this led to a late spin at the final corner before pitting the following lap to eventually finish ninth.

    Those instances came amid talk of fractious relations down at Ferrari but ahead of this weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix, Hamilton confirmed all is well - particularly with his new-for-2026 race engineer Carlo Santi.

    “It’s definitely not a trust thing, we’re constantly working on our communication,” said the 41-year-old.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    "It's been a lot better this year. Carlo has done an amazing job and those particular two times, particularly the last time, was just I didn't have the information I needed.

    “I got the call right at the last minute. We'll just continue to work on the processes. We're super united in what's working, in the right direction."

    This year has been much improved for Hamilton, who is fourth in the standings with three rounds remaining, currently on 261 points compared to his total of 156 in 2025.

    His first year in Ferrari colours was certainly underwhelming, but there have still been signs of improvement in 2026 despite it being a significant jump from last season.

    Not only is that in reference to the strategic calls, but also his connection with team-mate Charles Leclerc, clashing on a few occasions and most notably in Mexico three weeks ago.

    It cost Ferrari the win and Hamilton subsequently criticised Leclerc’s “do or die” attitude, before suggesting in Sao Paulo that the Monegasque races him harder than he does with other drivers.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    That led to an hour-long meeting between both Scuderia drivers plus team boss Fred Vasseur and Hamilton and Leclerc are now confident that the issue has been resolved.

    “Charles is a great driver,” said Hamilton. “The detail we went into about stuff like that, in terms of when we close the visor, it's very clear, we understand each other, we understand each other's boundaries and the ultimate goal, which is to get the best result for the team.

    “So I don't feel like that will be a problem moving forward. I have no concerns or worries about racing him, and I don't think he does for me either.”

    The talk surrounding the Mexico clash was naturally high, but Leclerc reckons that is a consequence of driving for Ferrari and all focus is now on securing runner-up in the constructors’ standings.

    “Everything has been taken out of proportion,” said Leclerc. “I'm kind of used to it now, driving for Ferrari for so many years. But things are very often taken out of proportion.

    “The goal is not always to agree and that will never happen. We can have different opinions and that was the case, for example, in Mexico.

    “But just the fact of speaking eased all tensions and we were completely fine with it. It's not that we had to speak for an hour, even though it ended up lasting an hour, just because we had a good time.

    “We started speaking about many other things in trying to bring the team and to help the team go forward.

    “But the discussion about the tension was like maybe five or 10 minutes and then the air was clean and that's it.”

    Ferrari heads into the Vegas race 95 points ahead of third-placed Red Bull and 142 behind Mercedes, who clinched its ninth title at Interlagos.
