    汉密尔顿称底板损伤影响拉斯维加斯排位赛

    法拉利车手汉密尔顿表示，底板受损和能量部署不足导致他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中无缘Q3，最终位列第12名。

    汉密尔顿称底板损伤影响拉斯维加斯排位赛

    这位法拉利车手表示，碎片造成的损伤和能量部署不足导致他无缘Q3，两辆法拉利均在Q2被淘汰。

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地方的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    七届F1世界冠军刘易斯·汉密尔顿表示，底板损伤和能量部署不足导致他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中无缘Q3。

    这位法拉利车手排位赛位列第12名，距离进入前十仅差0.167秒，比队友夏尔·勒克莱尔落后一位。这是法拉利自去年伊莫拉站以来首次双车止步Q2。

    汉密尔顿目前正在争夺车手锦标赛第二名，他告诉天空体育F1，尽管马拉内罗车队预计在拉斯维加斯的长直道上会陷入挣扎，但他对进入排位赛一直持乐观态度。

    “在第一圈或第二圈，我们的底板被什么东西撞了一下，一些碎片之类的，损坏了底板，我们因此损失了大量下压力，大约零点几秒，”汉密尔顿说。

    随后他又因为电量部署不足损失了更多时间，赛车在他飞驰圈结束时电量偏低。

    “我的最后一圈已经尽可能好了，但我前面没有车，”他说。“然后我们在最后阶段出现了部署不足，仅部署问题就让我损失了大约零点六秒。所以真是祸不单行，这就是那种倒霉的日子。”

    最后一圈没有前车为他提供尾流，汉密尔顿未能提升自己的最后一圈成绩。尽管他觉得进入第三节排位赛是可能的，但汉密尔顿承认，要想在发车格上排得更高会非常困难。

    “嗯，我们当时只是在勉强争取进入第三节排位赛，”他说。他认为如果没有部署损失、没有下压力损失，他本可以晋级，但他补充说“那仍然会很艰难”。

    “但我的圈速足以让我排在那里，”汉密尔顿说。“事情就是这样。对整个车队来说，这绝对不是什么好日子，但我们会努力在明天向前迈进。”

    Lewis Hamilton reveals floor damage cost him in F1 Las Vegas qualifying

    The Ferrari driver says debris damage and a loss of energy deployment cost him a place in Q3, as both Ferraris were knocked out in Q2

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton says floor damage and a lack of energy deployment cost him a place in Q3 in the Las Vegas Grand Prix qualifying session.

    The Ferrari driver qualified 12th, just 0.167 seconds away from making the top 10, one place behind team-mate Charles Leclerc. It was Ferrari's first double Q2 exit since Imola last year.

    Hamilton, who is currently fighting for second in the drivers' championship, told Sky Sports F1 he had been optimistic going into qualifying, even though the Maranello outfit expected to struggle on Las Vegas's long straights.

    "On the first or second lap, we took a hit on something on the floor, some debris or something, damaged the floor, we lost a bunch of downforce with it, a few tenths," Hamilton said.

    He then lost more time to a lack of deployment, as the car ran low on electrical energy at the end of his lap.

    "My lap was as good as it could be at the end, but I didn't have anyone ahead of me," he said. "And then we had a lack of deployment at the end, and I lost like six-tenths just on deployment. So when it rains, it pours, and that's one of those days."

    With nobody ahead of him for a tow on his final run, Hamilton failed to improve on his last lap. While he felt a Q3 appearance was possible, Hamilton admitted it would have been difficult to qualify much higher up the grid.

    "Well, we were scrapping to just get into Q3," he said. He believed he could have made it through "without the deployment loss, without the downforce loss", but added that "it would have still been tough".

    "But my lap was good enough in order to put me there," Hamilton said. "It is what it is. It's definitely not a nice day for all the team, but we'll try and move forward tomorrow."
