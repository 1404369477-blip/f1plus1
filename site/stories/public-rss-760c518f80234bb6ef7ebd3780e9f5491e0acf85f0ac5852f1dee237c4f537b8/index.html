    法拉利对汉密尔顿与勒克莱尔问题的解决方案保密

    勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿在卡塔尔讨论了拉斯维加斯大奖赛的碰撞事件，但法拉利不会透露解决车手问题的方案细节。勒克莱尔因碰撞被罚10秒，车队领队瓦塞尔称该碰撞“不可接受”。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿已在卡塔尔讨论了他们在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上的碰撞，但法拉利一级方程式车队不会透露解决其持续存在的车手问题的任何“方案”细节。

    勒克莱尔因在上周末比赛中与汉密尔顿的碰撞被罚10秒，车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔称该碰撞“不可接受”。

    鉴于勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿今年早些时候已发生一系列有争议的冲突，例如在蒙扎和墨西哥，两位车手都面临了媒体关于此次碰撞以及可能发生什么变化的大量提问。

    “我们两个人，每人一辆车，我们需要确保事情能够顺利进行。目前，它行不通，”勒克莱尔承认道。

    "当然，我可以把某一次事件的责任揽下来，那都无所谓。

    "重要的是我们找到一个双方都能接受的折中方案。我们可以讨论拉斯维加斯或其他地方的具体情况，或者之前或之后的圈数，但那并不重要。

    "归根结底，在那一刻，只有解决方案才是重要的。"

    当被 The Race 问及他是否有关于解决方案的想法时，勒克莱尔回答说："是的，我有，而且我认为我已经表达得很清楚了，但我更愿意在内部进行这些讨论。

    “我确实有看法，但最终重要的是我们集体认为什么是正确的决定，这将在内部进行。”

    瓦塞尔与两位车手都谈了，勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿周四在卡塔尔媒体会前讨论了该事件，汉密尔顿称会谈“积极”。

    但他不愿透露细节。

    “一切留在内部，”汉密尔顿说，呼应了勒克莱尔在被问及最后两站是否会实施新比赛规则时“我不能透露”的回答。

    法拉利不愿披露任何潜在的比赛规则变更，很可能是因为如果本周末这些规则未被严格遵守，就会招致批评。

    汉密尔顿最初在九月初的蒙扎比赛期间呼吁在车手之间引入比赛规则，但他表示这些规则的实施“最终由弗雷德决定”。

    “我当时已经把我的观点说清楚了。关于这件事真的没什么更多可说的了。我之前已经表明了我的立场，我的观点并没有真正改变。”

    勒克莱尔不愿被拉回重提拉斯维加斯事件的细节，因为当有人向他指出他的赛车与汉密尔顿并排但未被留出空间时，他说：“我真的不想再回到那次冲突上。”

    “我只想向前看。我理解你的观点，也认为它很有道理。但与此同时，我不想再回到那次冲突上。”

    汉密尔顿表示，在与勒克莱尔缠斗时，他始终把车队放在心上。

    当被问及通过沟通能提前规避多少问题时，他说：“你永远无法覆盖所有情况，因为每个场景都不同。但你可以逐弯复盘每一场比赛并讨论各种情况，查尔斯和我已经这么做了。

    “总会出现一些你可能没有预见到的新情况。但你也必须依靠直觉，而且归根结底，你始终要把车队放在心上。”

    “仅就我个人而言，当我在驾驶时，当我在赛道上攻击任何人时，我总是会想‘好吧，这会如何影响我身后的每一个人？’从车队角度考虑。所以我确实努力尽可能负责任，并在进攻时做出最好的决定，我想。”

    当勒克莱尔被问及瓦塞尔的“不可接受”言论时，他回答说：“我的意思是，这当然不可接受。作为一支车队，我们承受不起队友之间的冲突。

    “这对每个人来说都非常清楚。特别是拉斯维加斯的那次冲突，正如我所说，我承担了责任。我认为只是存在一些紧张情绪，我们只需要消散它，并确保我们着眼于未来更积极的日子。

    “这就是我所寻求的全部。但我确实同意。法拉利两次冲突加在一起，当然是不可接受的。”

    汉密尔顿确信他和勒克莱尔会解决这个问题。

    “我们会弄清楚的，你知道吗？我们归根结底是队友。而且我认为我们总能学到东西，”汉密尔顿说。

    “我们会从这段艰难时期中汲取经验教训。我仍然相信我们在幕后所做的一切建设和努力。我来这里的目标是带领法拉利重返巅峰。这是一个极具挑战性的赛季。人们很容易看到消极的一面，但我更愿意看到今年的积极面：我们正在争夺车队锦标赛的第二名；我们赢得过比赛；我认为今年我们拥有最好的赛车，尤其是在底盘方面。

    “所以，我只是努力去看积极的一面，而不是……我觉得大家似乎总是关注消极的东西，这显然是生活中最容易做的事。但我努力记住积极的一面，今年我们确实一起做出了了不起的、一些惊人的事情。”

    勒克莱尔也非常希望平息拉斯维加斯站后出现的猜测，即他的合同中存在某种“一号”优先待遇条款。

    “我通常从不透露合同内容，但我觉得这一条值得说明，”勒克莱尔说。

    “我的合同里绝对没有任何这样的条款。这从来不是我感兴趣的东西，也从来不是我个人想要的。所以没有，我没有。”

    “归根结底，这是我们工作的一部分。我认为我唯一不喜欢看到的是人们质疑我对车队的诚信、我对车队的热爱。我从2019年就在法拉利了。我认为我对车队的热爱再清楚不过了。

    “为了把法拉利带回巅峰，我会竭尽全力。当然，过程中难免会犯错，而且永远都会。我们只是尽量把错误降到最低，尤其是在队友之间。”

    “但我觉得，更让我受伤的是我看到四处流传的那个问号本身。”

    “其余的都只是很多噪音，现在并不是最愉快的时期，但这是比赛的一部分，我必须接受。”

    英文原文

    Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton have discussed their Las Vegas Grand Prix clash in Qatar, but the Ferrari Formula 1 team will not reveal details of any "solution" to its ongoing driver problem.

    Leclerc was penalised 10 seconds for what team principal Fred Vasseur labelled an "unacceptable" collision between the two drivers during last weekend's race.

    Both drivers faced a barrage of questions from the media about the clash and what might change, given Leclerc and Hamilton have had a series of controversial run-ins earlier in the year, such as at Monza and in Mexico.

    "We are two, one in each car, and we need to make sure that things just work out. At the moment, it's not working," Leclerc admitted.

    "Then, of course, I can take the blame from one episode and whatever. That doesn't really matter.

    "It's just important that we find a compromise and a solution that works for both. We can discuss about the specific of Las Vegas or somewhere else, or the laps before or laps after, but that doesn't really matter.

    "At the end of the day, it's only the solution that matters in that moment."

    Asked by The Race if he had ideas of what the solution could be, Leclerc replied: "Yeah, I do have and I think I've made it very clear, but I would rather have these discussions internally.

    "I do have an opinion, but at the end, what matters is what we think is the right decision collectively, and this will take it internally."

    Vasseur spoke to both drivers and Leclerc and Hamilton discussed the incident in Qatar on Thursday prior to their media sessions, with Hamilton describing the talks as "positive".

    But he was unwilling to disclose the details.

    "Everything stays internal," said Hamilton, echoing Leclerc's "I cannot disclose that" answers when asked if there would be new racing rules implemented for the final two rounds.

    Ferrari's unwillingness to disclose any potential racing rule changes is likely because it would open it up to criticism if those rules are not strictly followed this weekend.

    Hamilton first called for the introduction of racing rules between the drivers at the Monza race in early September, but he said the implementation of them is "for Fred to decide [on] ultimately".

    "I made my point clear already back then. There's not really much more to say on it, really. I said my piece before and my point hasn't really changed."

    Leclerc did not want to be drawn into revisiting the specifics of the Las Vegas incident as, when it was put to him that he had his car alongside Hamilton's but was not left room, he said: "I don't really want to go back to that clash.

    "I just want to look forward. I understand your point and I think it's a valid one. But at the same time, I don't want to go back to that clash."

    Hamilton said he has the team in mind when he has been battling Leclerc.

    Asked about how much could be preempted with conversations, he said: "You can never cover everything because every scenario is different. But you can go through every single race corner-by-corner and discuss things, which Charles and I have.

    "There's always going to be something new that you maybe haven't foreseen. But also you have to use your instinct and you also always, at the core of it, have to have the team [in mind].

    "Just speaking for myself, when I'm driving, when I'm attacking whoever it is on track, I'm always thinking 'OK, how is this going to impact everybody behind me?' team-wise. So I really do try to be as responsible as I can and make the best decision when I am attacking, I think."

    When Leclerc was asked about Vasseur's "unacceptable" comments, he replied: "I mean that it is unacceptable, of course. As a team, we cannot afford to clash between team-mates.

    "That is very clear for everyone. For the clash in particular in Las Vegas, as I said, I took responsibility. I think there's just tension around that we just need to dissipate and make sure that we look at more positive days in the future.

    "That's all I'm looking for. But I do agree. It's unacceptable that both Ferrari clashes together, of course."

    Hamilton was convinced he and Leclerc would resolve the matter.

    "We're going to figure it out, you know? We're team-mates at the end of the day. And I think we always learn," Hamilton said.

    "There will be learnings that we take from this difficult period. And I still believe in everything we're building and working towards in the background. I came here with the goal of taking Ferrari back to the top. And it's been a massively challenging season. It's so easy to look at the negatives, but I like to look at the positives this year: we're fighting for second in the constructors' championship; we have had wins; I think we've had the best car, chassis-wise particularly, this year.

    “So, I'm just trying to look at the positives and just not...I think everyone seems to be focusing always on the negatives, which is obviously the easiest thing to do in life. But I try to remember the positives and we've really done amazing, some amazing things this year together."

    Leclerc was also very keen to squash speculation that emerged after Vegas that he has any kind of '#1' preferential treatment clause in his contract.

    "I normally never disclose what I have in my contract, but I think this one is an important one to speak about," Leclerc said.

    "And I absolutely have zero clause that says that in my contract. It's never something that I was interested in and never something that I personally wanted. So no, I don't.

    "At the end of the day, it's part of our job. I think the only thing that I don't like seeing is people questioning my integrity to the team, my love for the team. I've been in Ferrari since 2019. I think there's nothing clearer than the love I have for the team.

    "I'll do absolutely everything in order to bring Ferrari back to the top. Then, of course, mistakes happen along the way and always will. We just try to have to minimise them and particularly between team-mates.

    "But I think it's more the question mark of that that I have seen going around that hurts me more than anything else.

    "The rest is a lot of noise and it's not the most enjoyable time, but it's part of the game and I have to accept that."
