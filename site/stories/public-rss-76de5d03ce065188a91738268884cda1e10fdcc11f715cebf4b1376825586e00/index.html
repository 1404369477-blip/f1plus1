    安东内利加冕最年轻世界冠军，盼与汉密尔顿维斯塔潘合影留念

    安东内利在2026年墨西哥城大奖赛加冕F1史上最年轻世界冠军，他表示想将与汉密尔顿、维斯塔潘同台领奖的瞬间留作墙上照片。

    安东内利希望在夺得F1世界冠军后，把与刘易斯·汉密尔顿和马克斯·维斯塔潘的特别合影挂在自己墙上。

    安东内利担心在墨西哥大奖赛最后一圈撞车，因为他几乎看不清赛道。与汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘同台领奖，是他想要留存的实体记忆。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利；马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队；安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯。

    2026年墨西哥城大奖赛足以载入史册：基米·安东内利加冕F1史上最年轻世界冠军，而部分由于超级驾照规则，这一纪录显然不会轻易被打破。

    在奥斯汀展现出极其惊艳的表现后，安东内利已经知道，锁定总冠军基本上只是走个形式，但他拒绝以这种心态对待。事实上，这位年轻的意大利车手在赛后承认，这个周末紧张情绪对他的影响比在奥斯汀时更大。

    “在奥斯汀，我更有侵略性，更专注于当下，而在墨西哥这里的排位赛中，我在某种程度上更紧张一些。而且我驾驶的方式也没有那么激烈。在那方面确实不容易。但现在我们终于锁定了冠军，现在我们可以享受赛季剩余的比赛了。”

    安东内利表示，他也把这些紧张情绪带入了正赛，尽管他逐渐学会了放下它们。

    “起步时我非常谨慎，给了很多空间。我可能过于保守了。但后来我看到自己有这样的速度，尤其是在第一段 stint（ stint ）里。我知道我们会参与竞争，因为使用旧胎时速度非常好。但后来，显然，因为红旗，一切又归零了。”

    那正是安东内利改变心态、决定抛开所有顾虑的时刻，随后他对夏尔·勒克莱尔完成了一次尤为精彩的超车。

    “那一刻我想，‘你知道吗？我干脆多冒点险。’我本有机会提升几个名次，而对夏尔的那次超车对登上领奖台至关重要。

    “之后我努力保持贴近直到最后，那最后一段 stint 非常有趣。我觉得我们三个人都在两秒之内，大家都在全力推进。只是超车很难，但强度很高，也很有趣。”

    安东内利最终落后分站冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘四秒完赛，并在回答 Motorsport.com 提问时透露，这完全归因于情绪极为激动的最后一圈。

    这位新科世界冠军表示：“最后一圈，我松了油门，因为我已经看不清赛道了。我情绪上来了，对自己说：‘我得收着点，不然就要撞车了！’能把车带回终点就已经很好了。”

    “我已经哭了，前方看不清楚。透过泪水，我不知怎么还能看到两辆法拉利，那一刻非常特别。很多回忆涌上心头，从卡丁车一路到这里的全部记忆。”

    “最后一圈非常特别，而且毫无疑问，当我冲过终点线时，我彻底释放了，因为我实现了我最大的梦想之一。那真的非常特别。”

    “特别”这个词同样适用于随后发生的事。在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道的体育场路段——太阳广场，安东内利见到了专程为周日飞来的父母。随后，他与F1历史上两位最伟大的车手——刘易斯·汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘——一同登上领奖台。

    在安东内利看来，几乎没有比这更好的方式来锁定他的首个世界冠军头衔了，他尤其珍视两位多次世界冠军将他扛上肩头的那一刻。

    “我一定会把这张照片装裱起来挂在家里，因为那简直是一个不可思议的瞬间，”这位梅赛德斯车手说道。

    “而且，看到两位在这项运动中创造历史的非凡车手对我如此尊重，这真的很特别。这是一个会[在我脑海中]永存、永远被铭记的时刻。我一定会把照片装裱起来挂在自家墙上，这样每次走进房间都能看得清清楚楚。”

    颁奖仪式结束时DJ接管了现场，随后又出现了另一个令新加冕的世界冠军动情的时刻。现场播放了《今夜无人入睡》和《Sarà, perché ti amo》两首意大利歌曲，让安东内利眼中涌出更多泪水。

    “是的，我的意思是，第一首歌对我来说非常动情，因为那是我的祖父以前常听的一首歌，所以听到它非常特别。而且我觉得他……我觉得他会非常自豪，”安东内利说道。

    当他说出这些话时，泪水再次涌出，赢得了在场媒体的一片掌声。

    “天哪，我今天哭得太多了，但这很好！”安东内利微笑着继续说道。“所以，是的，我只是因为我的祖父而非常动情，我也为他感到非常高兴。”

    Kimi Antonelli wants special photo with Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen on his wall after F1 title

    Antonelli feared crashing on the last lap of the Mexico Grand Prix because he could barely see the track. Sharing the podium with Hamilton and Verstappen is a moment he wants to preserve as a tangible memory

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    The 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix is one for the history books: Kimi Antonelli has been crowned the youngest world champion of all time, and partly because of the superlicence rules, that record will certainly not be an easy one to break.

    After his hugely impressive performance in Austin, Antonelli already knew that clinching the title would essentially be a formality, but he refused to treat it that way. In fact, the young Italian admitted after the race that nerves had affected him more this weekend than in Austin.

    “At Austin, I was much more aggressive, much more present in the moment, while in qualifying here in Mexico I was a bit more tense in a way. And also, I wasn't as intense in the way I was driving. It definitely hasn't been easy on that side. But now finally we clinched it, and now we can enjoy the rest of the season.”

    Antonelli said he also carried those nerves into the race, although he gradually learned to let them go.

    “At the start I was very cautious and I gave a lot of room. I was probably playing it very much on the safe side. But then I saw that I had the pace, especially during that first stint. I knew we would be in the fight because the pace was very good on used tyres. But then, obviously, with the red flag, it kind of all went back to zero.”

    That was precisely the moment when Antonelli changed his mindset and decided to cast aside all his caution, leading to a particularly impressive overtaking move on Charles Leclerc.

    “In that moment I was like, "You know what? I'm just going to take more risks." I had the opportunity to gain a few places and that move on Charles was crucial to get on the podium.

    “Then I tried to stay close until the end, and that last stint was very fun. I think the three of us were within two seconds and we were all pushing. It was just difficult to overtake, but it was high intensity and it was fun.”

    Antonelli eventually finished four seconds behind race winner Max Verstappen and revealed, in response to a question from Motorsport.com, that this was entirely down to a highly emotional final lap.

    “On the last lap, I just backed off because I just couldn't see the track anymore. I was getting emotional, and I said, "I'm just going to back off because otherwise I’ll crash here!" It was just good to bring it home”, the new world champion said.

    “I was already crying. I couldn't see clearly ahead. I could see two Ferraris somehow with the tears, so it was very special. A lot of memories went through my mind in that moment, all the memories since karting all the way to here.

    “It was a very special final lap, and definitely, when I crossed the line, I let it all out because I was just achieving one of my biggest dreams. It was very special.”

    The word special also applies to what followed. In the Foro Sol, the stadium section of the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez, Antonelli was greeted by his parents, who had flown in especially for Sunday. He then shared the podium with two of F1's all-time greats: Lewis Hamilton and Verstappen.

    In Antonelli's view, there could hardly have been a better way to clinch his first world title, and he particularly cherishes the moment when the two multiple world champions lifted him onto their shoulders.

    “I will definitely frame the picture and put it in my house because it's just an incredible moment”, the Mercedes driver said.

    “And as well, to see so much respect from two incredible drivers who have made history in the sport, it’s truly special. It is a moment that will live [in my mind] and will always be remembered. I will definitely frame the picture and put it on the wall of my house, so I can see it very well whenever I go in.”

    When the DJ took over at the end of the podium ceremony, another emotional moment followed for the newly crowned world champion. With Nessun Dorma and Sarà, perché ti amo, two Italian songs were played that brought even more tears to Antonelli's eyes.

    “Yeah, I mean, the first song was very emotional for me because it was a song that my grandfather always used to listen to, so it was very special to hear that. And I think he's... I think he would be very proud”, Antonelli said.

    As he spoke those words, tears began to flow once again, earning him a round of applause from the assembled media.

    “Oh my God, I’ve cried way too much today, but it's good!”, Antonelli continued with a smile. “So, yeah, I just got very emotional because of my grandfather, and I was very happy for him as well.”
