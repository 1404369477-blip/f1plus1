    阿尔派在杰里米·克拉克森于天空体育F1直播中开玩笑称弗兰科·科拉平托因“是阿根廷人”而被罚退后，公开表态支持自家车手。

    杰里米·克拉克森进入围场。

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计与那些让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    阿尔派 已发表声明，支持其车手弗朗哥·科拉平托，此前前《Top Gear》主持人杰里米·克拉克森在天空体育 F1 节目中开玩笑称，这位车手因“身为阿根廷人”而被处以发车位罚退。

    克拉克森在美国大奖赛周末与恩斯通车队共度时光，多年来他一直支持这支家乡车队，并在排位赛前于美洲赛道现身天空体育 F1 的直播节目。

    这位《克拉克森的农场》主持人讨论了多个话题，包括科拉平托的发车位罚退、马丁·布伦德尔的发车区巡游以及刘易斯·汉密尔顿的伴侣金·卡戴珊。

    当被问及 阿尔派 在美国大奖赛的机会时，克拉克森开玩笑说：“弗朗哥因为身为阿根廷人而拿到了发车位罚退。大概吧。好吧，我会因为这个给他一个罚退。”

    “但不管怎样，那只是因为我们当年去那里时发生的事情，”他补充道，指的是他连同联合主持人詹姆斯·梅和理查德·哈蒙德于2014年造访巴塔哥尼亚时那期最具争议的《Top Gear》节目。

    抗议爆发后，三人逃离了该国，当时克拉克森驾驶一辆车牌号为H982 FKL的保时捷928，剧组遭到石块投掷，当地人认为该车牌是在影射1982年的马岛战争。克拉克森和《Top Gear》剧组坚称车牌纯属巧合，并否认是故意选择的。

    克拉克森的言论发表后，阿尔派发布声明，支持科拉平托。

    “我们阿尔派注意到一位车队客人在奥斯汀最近一次电视采访中所作的评论，”车队表示。“弗兰科是我们的车手，我们全力支持他。”

    虽然声明中没有点名克拉克森，但紧随这位66岁人士的言论之后发布。

    科拉平托因在2026赛季第四次接受新的控制电子单元，被处以本周末美国大奖赛正赛发车位后退10位的处罚。

    Alpine issues statement after Jeremy Clarkson's controversial Franco Colapinto comments

    Alpine has publicly backed Franco Colapinto after Jeremy Clarkson joked on live TV that the driver had received a grid penalty "for being Argentinian"

    Jeremy Clarkson enters the paddock.

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Alpine has released a statement in support of its driver Franco Colapinto after former Top Gear host Jeremy Clarkson joked on Sky Sports F1 that the driver had been given a grid penalty "for being Argentinian".

    Clarkson is spending the United States Grand Prix weekend with the Enstone outfit, which he has supported for several years as his local team, and he appeared on the live Sky Sports F1 broadcast at the Circuit of the Americas ahead of qualifying.

    The Clarkson's Farm presenter discussed several topics, including Colapinto's grid penalty, Martin Brundle's grid walk and Lewis Hamilton's partner, Kim Kardashian.

    "Franco’s got a grid penalty for being Argentinian. Probably. Well, I’d give him one for that," Clarkson joked when asked about Alpine's chances at the United States Grand Prix.

    "But anyway, that’s just because of what happened when we went there," he added, referencing one of the most controversial Top Gear episodes from when he, along with co-hosts James May and Richard Hammond, visited Patagonia in 2014.

    The trio fled the country when protests broke out and the crew was pelted with stones after Clarkson drove a Porsche 928 carrying the number plate H982 FKL, which locals believed was a reference to the 1982 Falklands War. Clarkson and the Top Gear crew have insisted the number plate was a coincidence and have denied it was chosen on purpose.

    Alpine released a statement following Clarkson's comments, backing Colapinto.

    "We at Alpine are aware of the comments made by a team guest in a recent television interview in Austin," the team said. "Franco is our driver and has our full support."

    While the statement does not name Clarkson, it closely followed the 66-year-old's comments.

    Colapinto was handed a 10-place grid penalty for this weekend's United States Grand Prix for receiving his fourth new control electronics unit of the 2026 season.
