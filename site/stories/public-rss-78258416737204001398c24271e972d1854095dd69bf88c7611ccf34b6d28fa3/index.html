    红牛解释AI如何影响其F1赛事策略

    红牛透露，与Oracle合作开发的AI驱动的赛事策略工具正在帮助车队在大奖赛期间做出决策。

    红牛解释了AI如何影响其F1赛事策略

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔、法拉利、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队

    对于红牛车迷来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的赛事分析。

    红牛车队正在使用一套人工智能系统，在大奖赛期间为车队提供行动建议。

    在CNBC的一次采访中，红牛技术及性能合作伙伴负责人杰克·哈林顿解释说，该工具是与头号合作伙伴甲骨文共同开发的。

    这个工具最初是一个搜索工具，帮助工程师解读F1的规则和过去的裁决，现在已经发展成为一个"代理"人工智能，它利用更多信息并在比赛中提供行动建议。

    哈林顿解释说："它实际上在为我们做决策。"

    Oracle和红牛车队在2026年2月宣布了他们之间多年冠军合作伙伴关系的延长。米尔顿凯恩斯的车队在当时发布的新闻稿中解释说："延长的合作伙伴关系建立在车队传统的基础上，Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)和Oracle AI支撑着红牛福特动力系统下一代混合动力单元的2026年首秀，先进的赛车策略模拟和开创性的AI驱动策略代理。"

    哈灵顿以2025年意大利大奖赛为例，展示了AI代理的工作方式。在蒙扎赛道比赛的第一个弯道，维斯塔潘在与麦克拉伦车队的兰多·诺里斯争夺领先时冲出了赛道。根据哈灵顿的说法，代理建议红牛车队放弃位置以避免处罚。

    尽管这个建议与车队对局势的看法相悖，但红牛车队还是遵循了指示。维斯塔潘避免了处罚，很快从麦克拉伦车手那里夺回了领先，并以19.2秒的优势赢得了比赛。

    哈灵顿补充说，"一级方程式赛车始终是人类的运动"，但他解释说，AI代理可以帮助提升车队。

    红牛并非唯一使用人工智能的车队。谷歌正在与迈凯轮合作引入其Gemini人工智能工具，梅赛德斯与微软合作，法拉利则与IBM合作，为粉丝提供人工智能驱动的体验。

    Red Bull explains how AI is influencing its F1 race strategy

    Red Bull has revealed how an AI-powered race strategy tool developed with Oracle is helping the F1 team make decisions during grands prix

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Red Bull is using an artificial intelligence system that recommends actions to the team during a grand prix.

    During an interview with CNBC, Red Bull head of technical and performance partnerships Jack Harington explained that the team built the tool with its title partner, Oracle.

    What began as a search tool to help engineers interpret F1's regulations and past rulings has since become an "agentic" AI that draws on more information and recommends actions during a race.

    "It’s actually making decisions for us," Harington explained.

    Oracle and Red Bull announced a multi-year extension of their title partnership in February 2026. The Milton Keynes outfit explained in the press release at the time: "The extended partnership builds on the team’s legacy, with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) and Oracle AI underpinning the 2026 debut of Red Bull Ford Powertrains’ next-generation hybrid power unit, advanced race-strategy simulations, and a pioneering AI-powered strategy agent."

    Harington used the example of the 2025 Italian Grand Prix to show how the AI agent works. On the opening lap of the race at Monza, Verstappen went off at the first chicane while fighting McLaren's Lando Norris for the lead. According to Harington, the agent advised Red Bull to hand the place back to avoid a penalty.

    While that recommendation went against the team's view of the situation, Red Bull followed the instruction. Verstappen escaped a penalty, soon retook the lead from the McLaren driver and won the race by 19.2 seconds.

    Harington added that "Formula 1 is and will always be a human sport", but explained that the AI agent can help enhance the team.

    Red Bull is not the only team using AI. Google is working with McLaren to bring its Gemini AI tools, Mercedes has partnered with Microsoft and Ferrari is working with IBM on AI-powered experiences for fans.
