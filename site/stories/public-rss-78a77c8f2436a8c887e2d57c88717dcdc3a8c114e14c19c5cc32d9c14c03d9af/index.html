    科拉平托呼吁球迷停止网络辱骂

    弗朗哥·科拉平托公开呼吁阿根廷球迷停止网络辱骂，此前赞德沃特、马德里和巴库站后，国际汽联裁判、皮埃尔·加斯利及其家人、兰多·诺里斯等人遭到仇恨评论。阿尔派车队为此定期发布声明以试图遏制此类辱骂。

    弗朗哥·科拉平托呼吁球迷停止辱骂：“你们对我或我的未来没有帮助”

    在一些阿根廷球迷表现出仇恨行为之际，弗朗哥·科拉平托发表了迄今为止最强烈的声明

    弗朗哥·科拉平托公开呼吁阿根廷球迷停止网络辱骂，此前几个月内发生了多起事件。

    在赞德沃特、马德里和巴库站之后，仇恨评论指向了国际汽联裁判、皮埃尔·加斯利及其家人以及兰多·诺里斯等人。这一现象在2026年F1赛季反复出现，促使阿尔派车队定期发布声明，试图遏制这些辱骂。

    科拉平托此前在就此事表明立场时一直讳莫如深；在巴库，他怯生生地将这种行为称为“看着不舒服”，而在阿塞拜疆造成1号弯连环相撞后，他又对情况做了细致区分。

    “我觉得我们忽略了一点，那就是这不仅仅来自阿根廷人，”科拉平托当时说。“这很大程度上来自他们，我完全同意，[但]这并不只是来自他们。他们极其热情，也非常支持我。这很糟糕。他们很多次都很糟糕。

    “但这来自全世界，来自许多其他车迷群体。我不知道是不是我们没有看到。我收到大量这样的东西。从来没有人谈论它。”

    周四，这位阿根廷人进而发表了他迄今为止最强硬的声明，以试图遏制其车迷群体的狂热——他直接告诉自己的车迷，他们“有时做得太过分了”，并且“没有帮助到他或他的未来”。

    “大家好，有件非常重要的事，我早就需要和你们谈谈了，”科拉平托在Instagram上发布的一段西班牙语视频中说道。“我或许应该更早对你们所有人说，但这是必须解决的问题，是我们需要改变和改进的事情。

    “我将永远心怀感激，并且真心感到无比幸运，能有你们所有人从一开始就支持我。

    “我感激你们给予我的所有支持，感激你们帮助我取得成绩，也感激你们不断推动我追求更高目标。日复一日，一场接一场比赛，在我们训练并努力提升的过程中，你们帮助我和车队取得成绩。我真心感激这一切。

    “我知道，当我一切顺利、当我为自己和车队赢得积分时，你们会感到无比自豪。我一直非常感激这份支持。

    “但有时候，我们做得太过分了。你们非但没有支持我，反而最终去批评别人，攻击其他车手、其他同事、我的队友、我的机械师、我的工程师——那些我每天都与之共事的人。

    “我知道你们认为自己在维护我，但实际上你们做的恰恰相反。你们并没有帮助我，也没有帮助我的未来。”

    科拉平托的近期未来是稳固的，这位23岁的车手早在8月就锁定了一份2027赛季的新阿尔派合同。他目前在2026赛季积分榜上以27分位列第12，而队友加斯利以41分排在第10。

    Franco Colapinto urges fans to stop the abuse: “You’re not helping me or my future”

    As some Argentinian fans demonstrate hateful behaviour, Franco Colapinto makes his strongest statement to date

    Franco Colapinto has vocally asked Argentinian fans to stop online abuse after several incidents in the last couple of months.

    Following the Zandvoort, Madrid and Baku races, hateful comments were directed at FIA stewards, Pierre Gasly and his family, and Lando Norris, among others. This has been a recurring theme in the 2026 Formula 1 season, prompting Alpine to regularly release statements to try and combat the vituperation.

    Colapinto had previously been coy when it came to taking a stand on this matter; in Baku he meekly labelled the behaviour as “not nice to see”, and after causing the Turn 1 pile-up in Azerbaijan, he nuanced the situation.

    “I think we miss that it doesn't come only from Argentinians,” Colapinto then said. “It comes a lot from them, I fully agree, [but] it doesn't just come from them. They are extremely passionate and they support me a lot. It's bad. They are bad many times.

    “But it comes from all the world and from many other fanbases. I don't know if we are not seeing it. I get loads of it. No one ever talks about it.”

    On Thursday, the Argentine went on to make his strongest statement to date in a bid to curb his fanbase’s frenzy – he directly told his fans that they ‘sometimes went too far’ and ‘were not helping him or his future’.

    “Hello everyone, there’s something very important that I’ve needed to talk to you about for some time,” Colapinto said in a Spanish-language video posted on Instagram. “I probably should have spoken to all of you sooner, but this is something that needs to be addressed, something we need to change and improve.

    “I will always be grateful, and I genuinely feel incredibly fortunate, to have had you all supporting me from the very beginning.

    “I’m grateful for all the support you give me, for how you help me achieve results, and for how you constantly push me to aim higher. You help me and the team achieve our results, race after race, day after day, as we train and try to improve. And I’m genuinely grateful for all of that.

    “I know it makes you incredibly proud when things go well for me, when I score points for myself and the team. And I have always been very grateful for that support.

    “But sometimes, we go too far. Instead of supporting me, we end up criticizing people and attacking other drivers, other colleagues, my team-mates, my mechanics, my engineers – people I work with every single day.

    “I know you think you’re defending me, but you’re actually doing the opposite. You’re not helping me or my future.”

    Colapinto’s immediate future is secure, with the 23-year-old having clinched a new Alpine deal for the 2027 season back in August. He currently lies 12th in the 2026 standings with 27 points to his name, while team-mate Gasly is 10th on 41 points.
