    在拉斯维加斯观看F1赛车无需门票

    本周在拉斯维加斯，您无需购买看台票也能近距离接触F1赛车。车队、合作伙伴和度假村在拉斯维加斯大奖赛期间在拉斯维加斯大道上停放展示车和复制品，大部分都可以免费参观。

    在哪里可以看到拉斯维加斯的F1赛车而不需要比赛门票

    从贝拉吉奥的梅赛德斯到街头服饰店里的麦凯轮，这里是拉斯维加斯大奖赛周期间在拉斯维加斯大道上找到一级方程式赛车的地方

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    在这个星期，你不需要看台座位就能在拉斯维加斯接近一级方程式赛车。

    车队、合作伙伴和度假村在拉斯维加斯大奖赛的赛道上停放展示了赛车和复制品，其中大部分对游客免费开放。这里是它们的所在地。

    世界冠军车队拥有最大的展区。贝拉吉奥主入口处的梅赛德斯粉丝中心有一个2026年的展示赛车、赛车模拟器和在真实F1赛车上的维修区挑战，由车队技术人员提供。它从下午2点开放到晚上10点，直到周六。

    梅赛德斯，凯撒宫论坛购物中心

    第二个梅赛德斯展区位于论坛购物中心内，那里停放着一辆官方W17复制品，直到周日它都停放在IWC沙夫豪森精品店外。

    麦凯轮，文化王者在论坛购物中心

    在同一商场，一辆麦凯轮赛车在街头服饰店Culture Kings展出，该店曾与麦凯轮合作举办赛车周活动。

    在大道北端，红牛将一辆Oracle 红牛 Racing复制品赛车带到了Resorts World，并配备了两个从下午2点到晚上8点开放的模拟器。在威尼拉斯，粉丝还可以在威尼拉斯赛车汽车休息室挑战红牛维修区。

    纽约-纽约桥上的威廉姆斯粉丝区有一个全尺寸的FW48展示赛车，以及模拟器。它从上午11点到晚上7点开放，直到周六，粉丝需要通过威廉姆斯应用程序进行注册。

    威尼斯人酒店是阿尔法罗密欧车队酒店，一辆阿尔法罗密欧一级方程式赛车在酒店大柱廊展出，靠近大堂。

    哈斯赛车和车队纪念品在科斯莫波利坦酒店的切尔西塔2层全天展出，直到周日。

    Fontainebleau酒店在其北大堂有自己的定制赛车，展出至周六。

    为了有所不同，乐高将为周六比赛前的每支车队提供一辆可驾驶的乐高一级方程式迷你赛车，在赛道上绕一圈。乐高还装饰了一辆拉斯维加斯单轨列车，并接管了 flamingo/Caesars Palace车站。

    法拉利车迷们将不得不满足于贝拉吉奥的法拉利精品店，那里正在出售赛车周服装，以及威尼斯的Cafe 勒克莱尔。

    Grand Prix Plaza，这个全年都有的F1景点，通常展示包括埃尔顿·塞纳的Lotus 99T在内的历史赛车，在赛车周期间关闭，并于1月8日重新开放。

    Where to see F1 cars in Las Vegas without a race ticket

    From Mercedes at Bellagio to a McLaren in a streetwear store, here is where to find Formula 1 cars around the Strip during Las Vegas Grand Prix week

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    You do not need a grandstand seat to get close to a Formula 1 car in Las Vegas this week.

    Teams, partners and resorts have parked show cars and replicas all over the Strip for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, and most of them are free to visit. Here is where to find them.

    The world champions have the biggest set-up. The Mercedes Fan Hub in Bellagio's main porte cochere has a 2026 show car, racing simulators and a pit stop challenge on a real F1 car with team mechanics. It is open from 2pm to 10pm until Saturday.

    Mercedes, The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace

    A second Mercedes stop sits inside The Forum Shops, where an official replica of the W17 is parked outside the IWC Schaffhausen boutique until Sunday.

    McLaren, Culture Kings at The Forum Shops

    In the same mall, a McLaren car is on display at streetwear store Culture Kings, which has partnered with McLaren for race week in previous years.

    At the north end of the Strip, Red Bull has brought an Oracle Red Bull Racing replica car to Resorts World, along with two simulators that are open from 2pm to 8pm until Saturday. Over at Wynn, fans can also take on a Red Bull pit stop challenge in the Wynn Racing Motors Lounge.

    The Williams Fan Zone on New York-New York's Brooklyn Bridge has a full-size FW48 show car, plus simulators. It is open from 11am to 7pm until Saturday, and fans need to register through the Williams app.

    The Venetian is Alpine's team hotel, and an Alpine F1 car is on display in the Grand Colonnade, near the lobby.

    A Haas race car and team memorabilia are on display around the clock on Level 2 of The Cosmopolitan's Chelsea Tower until Sunday.

    Fontainebleau has its own custom race car in its North Lobby until Saturday.

    For something different, LEGO will send a drivable LEGO F1 minicar for each of the 11 teams on a lap of the circuit ahead of Saturday's race. LEGO has also wrapped a Las Vegas Monorail train and taken over the Flamingo/Caesars Palace station.

    Ferrari fans will have to make do with the Ferrari Boutique at Bellagio, which is selling race-week clothing, and Cafe Leclerc at Wynn.

    Grand Prix Plaza, the year-round F1 attraction that usually displays historic cars including Ayrton Senna's Lotus 99T, is closed for race week and reopens on 8 January.
