    拉塞尔称迈凯伦速度极快，诺里斯底板测试因雨推迟

    拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯第二节练习赛末段做出最快圈速，但他认为迈凯伦才是真正需要击败的车队；加斯利仅以0.071秒之差落后。

    拉塞尔对“速度极快”的迈凯伦印象深刻，诺里斯的底板测试因雨推迟

    这位梅赛德斯车手在拉斯维加斯第二节F1练习赛中做出最快圈速，但他认为迈凯伦实际上才是需要击败的车队

    拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯第二节一级方程式练习赛末段突然做出最快圈速，不过阿尔派车手加斯利险些爆出大冷门击败他。

    这位法国车手最终仅差0.071秒，梅赛德斯厂队因此占据计时榜首位。

    然而，拉塞尔认为，这一结果并不能反映真实情况。在第二节练习的大部分时间里，无论是使用全雨胎还是半雨胎，迈凯伦都占据了速度优势，而梅赛德斯两位车手则更为挣扎。

    “我觉得现实地说，我们今天可能并不是最快的，”拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯围场承认。“迈凯伦看起来异常快；勒克莱尔在某些时段看起来也非常快。我们只是把那一圈跑出来了。当时赛道处于最干的状态，而诺里斯没有跑。”

    目前位列车手积分榜第三的这位车手认为，梅赛德斯仍有功课要做，尤其是雨地感觉不如两周前在圣保罗时那么好。

    “我们有一些东西需要弄明白，因为上周在雨地里我们非常有竞争力，而[今天]基米和我都跟不上节奏，”他说。

    好消息是，天气预报显示接下来两天应该是干燥的，尽管这也给车队带来了巨大的复杂性。这实际上把拉斯维加斯大奖赛变成了一个冲刺赛周末，在排位赛之前只有一节有代表性的练习。

    “是的，会是这样。我们会在第三节练习一开始就出场，努力完成一次长距离跑。我认为没人知道周六的颗粒化会是什么样，”拉塞尔说。

    “去年是一次轻松的一停，但前年有很多颗粒化，是两停。所以，有些问题我们想要得到答案，但可能要到比赛日才能得到答案。”

    然而，当被 Motorsport.com 问询时，轮胎供应商倍耐力表示，它预计不会出现过度颗粒化，目前预计会是一场相对简单的一停比赛。

    虽然拉塞尔把矛头指向了迈凯伦，但车队本身仍然谨慎得多。赛事高级总监兰迪·辛格表示，车队之间的实力排序仍是一个很大的问号，迈凯伦在头两节练习赛中的速度并不能让他看出太多东西。

    “显然不可能就我们的速度或可能的位置得出结论：像往常一样，我们可以预期前四支车队彼此会非常接近，而今天有限的运行让预测发车顺位变得更加困难，”他说。

    迈凯伦为拉斯维加斯带来了底板升级，诺里斯原本计划在周四进行测试。然而，条件不允许这样做。

    迈凯伦不想冒任何风险，正如红牛选择不对其更新后的前翼冒险一样。

    “这样的条件让赛车很难得到恰当评估。在湿地情况下，我们不得不把一些传感器用胶带封起来，这意味着我们并不总能获得想要的数据证据，”辛格说道。

    “所以我们决定不安装新的底板，既是出于风险考虑，也是因为我们认为在这种条件下无法从中了解到太多东西。我们希望明天能装上它并进行更有效的评估。”

    George Russell impressed by "exceptionally fast" McLaren as rain delays Lando Norris' floor test

    The Mercedes driver topped the timesheets in the second F1 practice session in Las Vegas, but believes McLaren was actually the team to beat

    George Russell came out of nowhere to set the fastest time in the closing stages of the second Formula 1 practice session in Las Vegas, although Alpine driver Pierre Gasly almost caused a major surprise by beating him.

    The Frenchman ultimately fell just 0.071 seconds short, leaving the Mercedes works team at the top of the timesheets.

    According to Russell, however, that result does not reflect the true picture. For most of the second practice session, on both full wets and intermediates, McLaren set the pace, while the Mercedes duo struggled more.

    "I think realistically we were probably not the fastest today," Russell admitted in the Vegas paddock. "McLaren looked exceptionally fast; Charles looked very fast at points. We just got the lap in. The track was at its driest and Norris didn't run."

    The driver currently third in the world championship standings believes Mercedes still has homework to do, particularly as the feeling in the wet was not as good as it had been two weeks ago in Sao Paulo.

    "We've got some things to understand because we were very competitive in the wet last week and [today] both Kimi and I were off the pace," he said.

    The good news is that the weather forecasts suggest the next two days should be dry, although that also presents enormous complexity for the teams. It effectively turns the Las Vegas Grand Prix into a sprint weekend with just one representative session ahead of qualifying.

    "Yeah, it will be. We'll be out from the beginning in FP3, trying to get a long run in. I think nobody knows how the graining is going to be for Saturday," Russell said.

    "Last year it was an easy one-stop, but the year before there was lots of graining and it was a two-stop. So, there are questions that we want answered, but we probably won't get an answer until race day."

    However, when asked by Motorsport.com, tyre supplier Pirelli indicated that it does not expect excessive graining and is currently anticipating a relatively straightforward one-stop race.

    While Russell pointed to McLaren, the team itself remains much more cautious. Senior director of racing Randy Singh says the pecking order is still a major question mark, with McLaren's pace in the opening two practice sessions telling him very little.

    "It is clearly impossible to draw conclusions about our pace or likely positions: as usual, we can expect that the top four teams will be very close to each other, and the limited running of today makes it even more difficult to predict what will be the starting grid," he said.

    McLaren has brought a floor update to Las Vegas, which Norris was originally scheduled to test on Thursday. However, the conditions have prevented that from happening.

    McLaren did not want to take any risks, just as Red Bull opted not to do with its updated front wing.

    "The conditions made it difficult to evaluate the car properly. In the wet, we had to tape up some of the sensors, which meant we could not always get the evidence we wanted," Singh said.

    "So we decided not to fit the new floor because of the risk and because we did not think we would learn much from it in these conditions. We hope to run it tomorrow and assess it more effectively."
