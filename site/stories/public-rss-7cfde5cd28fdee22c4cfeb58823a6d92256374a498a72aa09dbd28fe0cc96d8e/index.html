    Rafael Camara: Haas confirm Brazilian will race in final two rounds of F1 season rather than chase F2 championship

    Rafael Camara will race again for Haas at the Qatar Grand Prix and Abu Dhabi Grand Prix ahead of joining full-time for the 2027 season; Camara leads the Formula 2 standings but will not feature in the final rounds

    Haas have confirmed that Rafael Camara will continue to feature for the team in the final two races of the season, rather than challenge for the Formula 2 championship.

    Camara made his debut at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix earlier this month, following Esteban Ocon's departure ahead of the Mexico City Grand Prix and reserve driver Ryo Hirakawa stepping in for that race.

    He also raced alongside team-mate Ollie Bearman at the Las Vegas GP, finishing 16th, with the Brazilian now retaining his place for the remainder of the season.
