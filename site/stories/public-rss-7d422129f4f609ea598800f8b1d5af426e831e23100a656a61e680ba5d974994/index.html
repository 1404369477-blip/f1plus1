    诺里斯为要求科拉平托禁赛的言论道歉

    兰多·诺里斯表示，他已就赛后要求科拉平托禁赛并称其不该出现在F1的言论向其道歉。

    兰多·诺里斯表示，在说出科拉平托应该因为把诺里斯和皮埃尔·加斯利撞出阿塞拜疆大奖赛而被禁赛的言论之后，他已经向科拉平托道歉。

    科拉平托在巴库第一辆安全车重启时误判了一号弯的刹车点，撞掉了诺里斯。赛后诺里斯立刻对媒体表示，制造这起连环相撞的人理应被禁赛。

    他还提到了“有些车手不应该出现在F1中”。

    但在周一上午，诺里斯发布了Instagram快拍，透露自己已就这番言论向科拉平托道歉。

    诺里斯说：“总的来说，巴库的这个周末很艰难，当然比赛也以失望的方式早早结束了。”

    “赛后，我给弗朗哥发了一条消息，为我说的那些话道歉，那些话我根本不应该说。”

    “我本应更清楚，当时情绪很激动，但这不是借口，我错了。这些年我自己也犯过错，我知道这在竞争中、在极限边缘的争夺中是很常见的一部分。”

    “我和弗朗哥已经达成了很好的谅解，我们仍然是好朋友，我们都很期待这个周末重新回到赛场上比赛。”

    这一事件在社交媒体上引发了巨大讨论，阿尔派 车队于周日发表声明，谴责针对各方所有相关者的网络辱骂。

    声明中写道：“作为一支车队，我们感到有必要再次就阿塞拜疆大奖赛之后网络上的仇恨与辱骂发声。”

    “这些攻击不仅针对我们的一位车手，还针对 F1（一级方程式）社区的其他成员，包括媒体，以及我们在赛车运动巅峰舞台上所尊重、钦佩并乐于与之竞争的车队同行。”

    “在极限状态下比赛时难免会出现失误，无论结果或结局如何，任何人都不应该遭受充满仇恨或辱骂性的言论。谴责社交媒体上的辱骂并非针对某一个车迷群体，也不是针对某支车队或某位车手的支持者，这是一个普遍性的问题，应该向这项运动的所有车迷呼吁并让大家共同认识到。”

    “正如我们在以往场合所说过的那样，我们强烈谴责部分粉丝在网上的反应，并与一级方程式、国际汽联以及其他车队站在一起，希望彻底杜绝这种行为。在最近的几场比赛中，这种情况已成常态，绝不能被纵容。我们知道，作为这项运动中自豪的参与者和利益相关方，我们有责任公开发声，并协助支持寻找解决方案。

    “我们将继续与赛事推广方和版权方以及管理机构密切合作，探讨应对网络辱骂的方法，倡导健康良性的体育竞争——这正是二级方程式所享誉盛名、并使其如此特别的原因。”

    英文原文

    Lando Norris says he has apologised to Franco Colapinto after saying he should receive a race ban for taking Norris and Pierre Gasly out of Formula 1's Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    Fresh from being wiped out in Baku by Colapinto on the first safety car restart, when the Alpine driver misjudged his braking point for the first corner, Norris told media right after the race that whoever caused the pile-up deserved a race ban.

    He also referred to "some drivers that shouldn't be in Formula 1".

    But on Monday morning Norris posted an Instagram story revealing he'd apologised to Colapinto for his comments.

    "Tough weekend overall in Baku and of course a disappointing and early end to the race," said Norris.

    "After the race, I sent Franco a message apologising for the comments I made that I simply should not have said.

    "I know better and emotions were running high but that is not an excuse, it was wrong of me. I've made mistakes of my own over the years and I know this is very much part of racing and fighting on the limit.

    "Me and Franco came to a good understanding, and are still good friends and are both excited to get back racing this weekend."

    The incident prompted huge social media discourse, with Alpine releasing a statement on Sunday calling out the online abuse directed towards all parties.

    "As a team, we feel it's necessary to speak out once more about the hate and abuse online following the Azerbaijan Grand Prix," the statement read.

    "Not only aimed at one of our own drivers, but also members of the Formula 1 community, including media, and to our racing counterparts who we respect, admire, and revel competing against in the pinnacle of motorsport.

    "Mistakes happen when racing at the limit and no matter the result or outcome, nobody should be subject to hateful or abusive comments. Condemning abuse on social media is not aimed at any one fan base or the supporters of one team or driver, this is universal and should be called out and recognised to all fans of the sport.

    "As we have said in previous instances, we strongly condemn the reaction of some fans online and stand in solidarity with Formula 1, the FIA, and other teams in wanting to stamp it out. It has become a regular occurrence in recent races and cannot be condoned. We know as a proud participant and stakeholder in the sport, we hold a responsibility to speak out and also help support in finding a solution.

    "We will continue to work closely with the championship promoter and rights-holder, and the governing body to discuss ways in which to tackle online abuse and encourage healthy sporting rivalry, which Formula 1 is renowned for and what makes it so special."
