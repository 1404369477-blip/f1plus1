    本周奥斯汀免费F1粉丝区位置

    没有票的粉丝仍可在奥斯汀的美国大奖赛期间享受免费粉丝区活动。

    本周在奥斯汀哪里可以找到免费的F1粉丝区

    威廉姆斯、梅赛德斯和红牛等车队将在奥斯汀提供免费体验

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    没有本周末美国大奖赛门票的粉丝仍然有很多事情可以做，因为车队和赞助商将在奥斯汀的赛车周期间设立免费的粉丝区，即使没有赛道通行证，你也可以享受其中。

    威廉姆斯车队率先开放，其粉丝区位于国会大道800号，从10月19日（星期一）至10月24日（星期六）开放。入场免费，包括展示赛车、赛车模拟器、风洞、威廉姆斯与漫威漫画合作的作品（在这里看看它揭晓的涂装），以及与亚历克斯·阿隆索和车队经理詹姆斯·沃尔斯的问答环节。周四至周六开放时间为上午10点至晚上7点。

    阿隆索今天（10月21日，星期三）也将出现在位于东4街的Brazos Hall的杜拉セル动力赛道上。这里由亚马逊运营，设有模拟牛骑、马蹄铁投掷、遥控赛车赛道和现场音乐表演。

    今晚，阿斯顿·马丁也与粉红赛道俱乐部合作，在Rainey Street的Bungalow举办了一场免费的女性聚焦F1聚会，包括现场播客录制。

    梅赛德斯车队，其车手基米·安东内利可能在周日赢得冠军，将从周四至周日开放其粉丝中心，位于国会大道400号。这里免费提供如维修区挑战、模拟器、每日抽奖和美味的德克萨斯州食品摊贩等服务。

    红牛车队本周最为忙碌。该车队及其动力单元合作伙伴福特将今天举办The Pull Up活动，活动内容包括福特Raptor车辆、一辆一级方程式展示车、烧烤和现场音乐，随后从周四到周日，红牛奥斯汀赛车之夜将在东第七街举行。活动包括维修区挑战、模拟器和甚至包括马克斯·维斯塔潘和伊萨克·哈德贾的现场表演。

    奥迪在周四于东塞萨尔·查韦斯街的中央机械工厂举办的黑白启动派对免费入场，需提前预约，活动内容包括奥迪展示、出售的艺术品和DJ表演。客人被要求穿着黑白服装……出于某种原因。

    车手的个人品牌也来到了这里。兰多·诺里斯的LN4将圣何塞街1500号的加油站变成了一个1970年代风格的车库，用于展示其从今天到周日的新收藏，而丹尼尔·里卡多德的Enchante品牌在周四于分销大厅与福特赛车合作举办了一个快闪店。他甚至可能也会在那里。

    小红牛车手利亚姆·劳森将在周四晚上在Inn Cahoots的Esses Bazaar举行他的音乐首秀，尽管位置有限，且需要提前预约。

    对于那些愿意付费的人来说，今晚在Long Center举办的Road to the Race超级跑车展览需要支付10美元，而Guenther Steiner将于周六晚上在ACL Live参加《红旗播客》的现场直播。

    10月25日星期日下午3点（当地时间）在美洲赛车场举行的比赛。

    Where to find free F1 fan zones across Austin this week

    Williams, Mercedes and Red Bull are among those hosting free experiences across Austin

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Fans without a ticket for this weekend’s United States Grand Prix still have plenty to do, with teams and sponsors setting up free fan zones across Austin during race week for you to enjoy even if you don't have access to the track.

    Williams was first to open, with its fan zone at 800 Congress Avenue running from Monday 19 October to Saturday 24 October. Entry is free and includes a show car, racing simulators, a wind tunnel, a Williams x Marvel comic (take a look at the livery it revealed here) and Q&As with Alex Albon and team principal James Vowles. It is open 10am to 7pm Thursday to Saturday.

    Albon will also pop up at Duracell’s Power Paddock at Brazos Hall on East 4th Street today (Wednesday 21 October). Run with Amazon, it features a simulated bull ride, a horseshoe toss, an RC race track and live music.

    Tonight, Aston Martin also teams up with the Pink Paddock Club for a free women-focused F1 meetup at The Bungalow on Rainey Street, including a live podcast recording.

    Mercedes, whose driver Kimi Antonelli could clinch the title on Sunday, opens its fan hub at 400 Congress Avenue from Thursday to Sunday. It is free and offers things such as pitstop challenges, simulators, daily giveaways and tasty Texas food vendors.

    Red Bull has the busiest week. The team, along with its power unit partner Ford, will host The Pull Up today, with Ford Raptor vehicles, an F1 show car, a barbecue and live music, before Red Bull Austin Race Nights run from Thursday to Sunday on East Seventh Street. Those include pitstop challenges, simulators and even appearances from Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar.

    Audi’s Black & White Kickoff Party on Thursday at Central Machine Works on East Cesar Chavez Street is free with an RSVP, mixing Audi displays, art for sale and DJ sets. Guests are asked to dress in black and white... for some reason.

    Drivers’ own brands are in town too. Lando Norris’s LN4 has turned a petrol station at 1500 San Jacinto Boulevard into a 1970s-style garage for its new collection from today until Sunday, while Daniel Ricciardo’s Enchante has a pop-up with Ford Racing at Distribution Hall from Thursday. He might even be there, too.

    Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson will make his musical debut at the Esses Bazaar at Inn Cahoots on Thursday evening, although places are limited and RSVPs are necessary.

    For those willing to pay, the Road to the Race supercar showcase at the Long Center costs $10 tonight, while Guenther Steiner joins The Red Flags Podcast for a live show at ACL Live on Saturday night.

    The race at the Circuit of the Americas starts at 3pm local time on Sunday 25 October.
