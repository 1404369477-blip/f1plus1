    奥迪对角田裕毅未受罚提出上诉，听证会定于11月25日举行

    奥迪对角田裕毅在意大利大奖赛上未受到处罚提出上诉，FIA宣布将于11月25日审理此案。

    国际汽联宣布，奥迪对土屋圭市在意大利大奖赛上未受到处罚的申诉将于11月25日听取。

    奥迪在蒙扎赛事后对规则对土屋圭市在发车时的所谓越位违规的适用感到不公，提出了投诉。

    土屋圭市原本应该排在第14位发车，但在最初错误地驶向第15位发车位后，他在最后一刻转向了右侧。

    虽然他能够在预定发车位内停车，因此没有越过前方发车位，但他的小红牛赛车最终以一个角度停在了那里。

    那足以让赛事总监鲁伊·马奎斯决定进行另一圈发车圈，因为根据后来的 stewards 文件，"他表示他不舒服以那样的位置开始比赛"。

    土佐的行动触发了一场 FIA stewards 的调查，因为造成额外发车圈的车手被认为违反了规则。

    任何造成额外发车圈但能够完成该圈的车手必须"在圈尾进入维修区，并从维修区末端开始[练习]"。

    由于土佐没有完成这个动作，F1 的体育规则规定，任何未执行的停站和出发处罚将被转换为30秒的赛后时间处罚。

    小红牛 然而，认为车手小岛没有造成额外的起步圈数——因为他仍然在自己的格子内，并且没有影响到其他车手。

    裁判组接受了这一观点，并认为小岛本人并非额外起步圈数的“原因”——这实际上是马奎斯自己选择的结果。

    不过，奥迪提出了抗议，因为它认为小岛确实是额外起步圈数的催化剂。

    它在蒙扎之后发表了一份简短的声明，称其“对B5.9.4条款的应用提出了关切。”

    如果曾田被处罚，奥迪将从中受益，因为30秒的处罚将使他降至第15位，而加布里埃尔·博尔特罗托将被提升至第10位。

    现在，这个问题将在11月25日在巴黎的FIA国际法庭上审理。应FIA的要求，听证将在闭门进行，因此公众和媒体将不允许参加。

    英文原文

    The FIA has announced that Audi’s appeal against Yuki Tsunoda not getting a penalty at Formula 1’s Italian Grand Prix will be heard on November 25.

    Audi complained after the Monza race for what it felt was an incorrect application of the rules against Tsunoda for an alleged grid infringement at the restart.

    Tsunoda had been due to line up 14th but, after mistakenly initially heading towards the 15th-place grid slot, he veered at the last-minute to the right-hand side.

    While he was able to come to a stop within his intended grid slot, so therefore was not out of position over the front, his Racing Bulls car had ended up at an angle.

    That was enough for race director Rui Marques to decide to go for another formation lap because, according to a later stewards' document, "he stated he was not comfortable starting the race with the car in that position".

    Tsunoda’s actions had triggered an FIA stewards’ investigation because drivers that cause an extra formation lap are deemed to be in breach of the rules.

    Any driver who causes an additional formation lap but is able to start that lap is required to "enter the pitlane at the end of the lap and must start the [session] from the end of the pitlane".

    With Tsunoda not serving that, F1's sporting regulations state that any unserved stop-and-go penalty is converted to a 30-second post-race time penalty.

    Racing Bulls argued, however, that Tsunoda had not caused the extra formation lap - as he was still within his grid box and was not impacting any other drivers.

    The stewards accepted that position and felt that Tsunoda himself had not been the “cause” of the extra formation lap – which was instead down to Marques’ own choice.

    Audi complained, though, as it felt that Tsunoda had indeed been the catalyst for the extra formation lap.

    It said in a short statement after Monza that it had "raised a concern regarding the application of Article B5.9.4".

    Audi would stand to benefit were Tsunoda to be penalised, because a 30-second sanction would drop him to 15th - with Gabriel Bortoleto being promoted to 10th.

    The matter will now be heard at the FIA’s International Court of Appeal in Paris on November 25. The hearing will take place behind closed doors at the request of the FIA, so no public nor media will be allowed to attend.
