    F1维修区墙数据传输提速一倍，延迟降至4秒内

    Globant高管透露，F1车队现在能在录制后4秒内收到数据和视频信号，速度至少是2024年的两倍。

    F1车队现在能在录制后4秒内收到数据和视频信号，速度至少是2024年的两倍。

    这一说法来自Globant媒体体育娱乐首席执行官Nicolas Avila，他在拉斯维加斯举行的The Race x Axios“Beyond the Race：创新与领导力投资”活动上对The Race首席执行官Fabricio Drumond作出上述表示。

    他说：“车队内容传输系统帮助把全部视频信号连同分析数据和遥测信息，传送给工厂里的人和坐在维修区墙上的人，让他们能够作出反应并向车手发出指令……过去的延迟超过10秒，现在降到了5秒以内。”

    数字服务提供商Globant于2024年5月成为F1官方合作伙伴，其职责之一就是维修区墙使用的内容传输系统。

    这套内容分发系统整合了来自摄像机、国际转播信号和赛事计时的30多路数据流，供维修区墙上的工程师使用。发车格上的每一支F1车队都在使用它。

    阿维拉承认，比赛的胜负可能就在“一次进站”之间决定，并表示他对F1允许车队有4秒延迟感到惊讶。

    “把延迟减半不仅提升了车队做出决策的能力，而且说实话，我很惊讶他们竟然允许我们延迟4秒，因为这差不多是能决定比赛胜负的时间的40倍，对吧？所以这始终是在挑战极限。”

    他补充说，Globant在推动F1车队能以多快速度接收数据方面所做的工作，可以迁移到该公司所服务的其他需要高效数据交付的行业。

    “但我们不只做体育，我们也与生命科学领域合作。我举一个非常好的例子：我们曾为一家制药公司做个性化医疗，你没法在沃尔格林买到你的药。他们得上门抽你的血，然后为你配制药物并送到你手上。

    “而治疗每延迟一天，就意味着生存机会少一分。所以我们以非常类似的方式与一家肿瘤公司合作，努力获取数据、努力加快进度，我们成功将周期缩短了30%以上，并将某类特定癌症的生存率提高到90%以上。

    “所以，一级方程式在数据和速度上推动的这些事，对这项运动有效，对所有行业也同样有效。”

    英文原文

    Formula 1 teams receive data and video feeds within four seconds of recording - at least twice as fast as they would have in 2024.

    That’s according to Nicolas Avila, Globant Media Sports and Entertainment CEO, who spoke to The Race CEO Fabricio Drumond at The Race x Axios's Beyond the Race: Investments in Innovation and Leadership event in Las Vegas.

    He said: “The team content delivery system helps bring all the video feeds with the analytics and telemetry for people that are in the factory, for people that are sitting in the pitwall, to be able to react and to give commands to the driver… And there was a delay [of] over 10 seconds; it went under five.”

    Digital services provider Globant joined F1 as an official partner in May 2024 and part of its remit has been the content delivery system used on the pitwall.

    This content delivery system combines more than 30 feeds from cameras, the international broadcast, and race timing, for engineers on the pitwall. It is used by every F1 team on the grid.

    Avila acknowledges that races can be won or lost within “a pitstop” and says that he’s surprised F1 is allowing a delay of four seconds to teams.

    “Not only was [halving the delay] an improvement in the ability for [teams] to decide, but I think I'm surprised that they're allowing us to delay 4 seconds because it's like 40 times what can win or lose a race, right? So that is pushing the limits all the time.”

    He adds that Globant’s work in pushing how fast F1 teams can receive data is transferable to other industries in which it works to provide efficient data delivery.

    “But we don't only do sports, we work with life sciences. And a really good example that I have is we worked on personalized medicine for a pharmaceutical company and you cannot go buy your medicine in Walgreens. They got to come, take your blood and go build the medicine for you and deliver it to you.

    “And every day that the treatment is delayed means less chance of survival. So we work with an oncology company in a very similar fashion, trying to get the data, trying to move things faster and we were able to improve the cycle by over 30% and improve the survival rate over 90% for a particular type of cancer.

    “So these things that Formula 1 is pushing on data and speed are valid for the sport, but are valid for all industries as well.”
