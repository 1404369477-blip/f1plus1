    沃尔夫要求解释拉塞尔为何错失拉斯维加斯杆位

    安东内利在拉斯维加斯排位赛Q3做出最快圈速1分33秒893，但因更换升级版ADUO涡轮增压器被罚退五位，正赛从第六位起步。拉塞尔以1分34秒224位列第二，落后维斯塔潘0.073秒，错失杆位，沃尔夫对车队表现仍有疑问。

    沃尔夫要求解释拉塞尔为何在“理想”的最后一圈位置下错失拉斯维加斯杆位

    沃尔夫要求解释拉塞尔为何在拥有理想赛道位置的情况下，仍以0.073秒之差负于维斯塔潘、错失拉斯维加斯站杆位

    致梅赛德斯车迷：来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    梅赛德斯在周五晚的拉斯维加斯街道赛道排位赛结束后拥有全场最快的赛车，但车队领队沃尔夫仍有疑问需要解答。世界冠军安东内利在Q3做出1分33秒893的最快圈速，却因更换梅赛德斯升级版ADUO涡轮增压器被罚退五位，正赛从第六位起步。队友拉塞尔未能因此继承杆位，红牛车手维斯塔潘以1分34秒151抢下头名，仅以0.073秒的优势压过拉塞尔的1分34秒224。

    这个结果令人刺痛，因为拉塞尔在当晚早些时候看起来不可撼动，他在最后一次练习赛中以1分34秒041的成绩领先安东内利0.911秒。Q3中情况更加复杂，拉塞尔曾要求梅赛德斯让已被罚退的队友为他提供尾流。

    然而，混乱的最后一圈出场圈让安东内利落在车阵后方，使这位意大利车手在直道上从前方车辆——包括拉塞尔——获得了价值高达16公里/小时的牵引力。

    排位赛后，当被问及梅赛德斯是否本应安排更好的尾流来帮助拉塞尔争夺车手锦标赛第二名时，沃尔夫驳斥了赛道位置是罪魁祸首的说法。

    “实际上，乔治对我来说拥有完美的位置。他落后前车四秒，”沃尔夫说。“我们需要查明情况，因为我认为在7号、8号、9号弯附近——是出现了失误还是发生了其他事情，说实话我不知道，但那看起来像是争夺杆位的理想位置，因为他一直是最快的。”

    与维斯塔潘一同在前排上演“大戏”

    即便没有拿到杆位，拉塞尔周六晚上仍将与维斯塔潘并排从第二位发车，而总冠军争夺正处于关键节点。拉塞尔目前排名积分榜第三，落后维斯塔潘17分，在两辆法拉利——勒克莱尔（第11）和汉密尔顿（第12）——于Q2被淘汰后，他迎来了大幅追分的绝佳机会。

    前排对手之间的紧张气氛已经升温。三练尾声，维斯塔潘在超越梅赛德斯后于14号弯减速，两人轮对轮缠斗在一起，荷兰人在车队无线电中抱怨拉塞尔“把我挤到了护栏上”。赛后审查后，国际汽联赛事干事就这次冲突向维斯塔潘发出警告。

    当被问及两人并排冲向1号弯时他预期会发生什么，沃尔夫并没有淡化火药味十足的可能性。

    “嗯，就电影而言，如果他们试图一起通过前几个弯道，我们可就要好好看着了，”沃尔夫说道。

    Toto Wolff wants answers after George Russell misses Vegas pole despite “ideal” final-lap position

    Toto Wolff wants answers after George Russell misses Las Vegas GP pole by 0.073s to Max Verstappen despite ideal track position

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes left Friday night qualifying at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit with the fastest car on track, yet team principal Toto Wolff still had questions to answer. World champion Kimi Antonelli topped Q3 with a 1m33.893s, only to drop to sixth on the grid due to a five-place penalty for taking Mercedes’ updated ADUO turbocharger. Instead of team-mate George Russell inheriting pole, Red Bull’s Max Verstappen snatched top spot with a 1m34.151s - edging Russell’s 1m34.224s by just 0.073 seconds.

    The result stung after Russell had looked untouchable earlier in the evening, topping final practice with a 1m34.041s that put him 0.911s clear of Antonelli. Complicating matters in Q3, Russell had asked Mercedes for a slipstream from his already-penalised team-mate.

    Instead, a messy final out-lap left Antonelli trailing the pack, allowing the Italian to pull a tow worth up to 16 km/h down the Strip straight from cars ahead—including Russell.

    Asked after qualifying whether Mercedes should have orchestrated a better tow to aid Russell’s fight for second in the Drivers’ Championship, Wolff dismissed track positioning as the culprit.

    "Actually, George for me had the perfect position. He was four seconds behind the car in front," Wolff said. "We need to find out because I think there was some kind of Turn 7, 8, 9 - whether there was a mistake or something else happened, I don’t know, to be honest, but it looked like the ideal position for a pole lap because he was quickest all the time."

    Setting up front-row “cinema” with Verstappen

    Even without pole, Russell lines up second alongside Verstappen on Saturday night with major championship stakes on the line. Sitting third in the standings and 17 points behind Verstappen, Russell has a prime chance to make serious ground after both Ferraris - Charles Leclerc (11th) and Lewis Hamilton (12th) - were knocked out in Q2.

    Tension between the front-row rivals is already running hot. Late in FP3, Verstappen and Russell tangled wheel-to-wheel at Turn 14 after Verstappen passed the Mercedes and slowed into the corner, prompting the Dutchman to complain over team radio that Russell had "pushed me into the barrier". Following a post-session review, the FIA stewards handed Verstappen a warning for the skirmish.

    Asked what he expected when the pair launch side by side toward Turn 1, Wolff didn't downplay the prospect of fireworks.

    "Well, with cinema, if they try to go through the first corners together, we shall be watching," Wolff said.
