    巴西大奖赛最终练习赛情况

    维斯塔潘在巴西大奖赛最终练习赛中领跑，但将因引擎部件更换而获得后发位置处罚。阿隆索和拉塞尔等车手因处罚已无缘杆位。

    维斯塔潘在巴西大奖赛最终练习赛中领跑，但将因引擎部件更换而获得后发位置处罚。

    维斯塔潘是四位受处罚的领跑者之一——包括法拉利的赛车（每人10个位置）和拉塞尔（5个位置处罚）——他们已经在巴西的杆位争夺战中出局。

    维斯塔潘在FP3中以1m11.622s的成绩领跑，比新世界冠军基米·安东内利快了0.025s。

    红牛在夜间对维斯塔潘的赛车进行了重大改动，但这位四次世界冠军在FP3中仍对“糟糕”的转向表示不满。

    兰多·诺里斯以0.131秒的差距成为麦凯轮车队第三快的车手，排在罗素驾驶的梅赛德斯之后位居第四。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔以0.274秒的差距成为第五快的车手，比维斯塔潘的基准成绩慢。

    队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿没有能够进行排位赛模拟，因为他在比赛还剩大约25分钟时，不得不将他的法拉利车拖入维修区，原因是车尾冒烟。

    这使他成为第12快的车手，他的最快圈速是在中胎上完成的。

    法拉利刚刚为汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔的两辆赛车更换了新的内燃机。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂排在第六位，领先于两位来自小红牛的阿维德·林德布拉德和利亚姆·劳森，他们的速度都比红牛的伊萨克·哈德贾快，比维斯塔潘慢0.628秒。

    前十名中，本土英雄加布里埃尔·博托莱托的奥迪排在最后，他的同胞、新秀拉斐尔·卡马拉在第十五位超过了奥利·贝尔曼超过十分之一。卡马拉的最快理论圈速实际上是所有车手中第十二快的，比贝尔曼的理论最佳圈速快近三十分之一。

    兰斯·斯特罗尔的阿斯顿·马丁只完成了七圈，随后因为一个问题被迫返回维修区，这个问题让他失去了剩余的练习时间。

    塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯将在第八弯道因将某物从驾驶舱扔出而面临 stewards 的赛后调查。

    即使是他的凯迪拉克车队也感到困惑，因为他的赛车工程师询问佩雷斯他扔出了什么。

    “我不知道，嗯...有什么东西掉了，”佩雷斯解释道，“我想是无线电，你放无线电电缆的地方，一个小盒子。”

    英文原文

    Max Verstappen topped final practice ahead of Formula 1 qualifying at the Brazilian Grand Prix, knowing he’ll take a back-of-the-grid penalty for engine component changes.

    Verstappen is one of four penalised frontrunners - along with the Ferraris (10 places each) and Russell (five-place penalty) - who are already out of the pole position fight in Brazil.

    Verstappen topped FP3 on a 1m11.622s, just 0.025s faster than the new world champion Kimi Antonelli.

    Red Bull made major changes to Verstappen’s car overnight but the four-time world champion was still complaining about his “terrible” steering in FP3.

    Lando Norris was third-quickest for McLaren, 0.131s slower with Russell’s Mercedes rounding out the top four.

    Charles Leclerc was fifth-fastest, 0.274s slower than Verstappen’s benchmark.

    Team-mate Lewis Hamilton didn’t get to conduct a qualifying simulation because he had to pull his Ferrari into the pits with around 25 minutes remaining, due to smoke billowing from his Ferrari.

    That left him 12th-fastest, with his fastest lap set on the mediums.

    Ferrari had just fitted a new internal combustion engine to the cars of both Hamilton and Leclerc.

    Oscar Piastri was sixth ahead of the two Racing Bulls of Arvid Lindblad and Liam Lawson, who were both faster than Red Bull’s Isack Hadjar, 0.628s adrift of Verstappen.

    The top 10 was rounded out by home hero Gabriel Bortoleto’s Audi, with his fellow countryman, rookie Rafael Camara, outpacing Ollie Bearman by over a tenth in 15th place. Camara’s fastest theoretical lap was actually the 12th-best of all the drivers, almost three tenths up on Bearman’s theoretical best.

    Lance Stroll’s Aston Martin managed just seven laps before he was forced to return his car to the garage with an issue that lost him the rest of the session.

    Sergio Perez will face a post-session investigation by the stewards for throwing something out of the cockpit at Turn 8.

    Even his Cadillac team were confused as his race engineer asked Perez what he threw out of his cockpit.

    "I don't know it was, uh..something came off," Perez explained. "I think the radio, where you put the radio cables, a little box."
