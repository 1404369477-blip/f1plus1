    科尔顿·赫塔与Hitech达成“双方协议”，将不会参加2026年F2赛季最后两轮比赛，提前结束这段艰难征程。这位九次IndyCar分站冠军今年转战欧洲单座赛事，目标是取得F1所需的FIA超级驾照。

    赫塔在Hitech被替换，提前结束表现不佳的F2赛季

    赫塔将不会参加2026年F2赛季最后两轮比赛

    科尔顿·赫塔与Hitech达成“双方协议”，将不会参加2026年F2赛季最后两轮比赛，提前结束这段艰难征程。

    这位九次IndyCar分站冠军今年转战欧洲单座赛事，目标是取得参加F1所需的FIA超级驾照。

    但对赫塔来说，F2 的进展并不如计划，他在积分榜上排名第 19，12 轮比赛仅获得 26 分，最好成绩是在巴塞罗那从倒序发车第三位起步获得第五名。

    这位 26 岁的美国车手不会参加卡塔尔和阿布扎比的最后两个比赛周末，将由罗曼·比利宾斯基顶替——后者原本就计划在 2027 年接替赫塔的席位。

    Hitech F2 赛事工程主管扬·苏曼表示：“我们很高兴欢迎罗曼加入 Hitech，参加本赛季最后两轮比赛。

    “这两个周末将给我们机会，在冬季之前开始建立重要的关系。”

    “我还要感谢科尔顿在效力 Hitech 期间所展现的投入与决心，尽管这个赛季充满挑战。我们祝愿他未来一切顺利。”

    车队声明中没有引用赫塔的话，目前也尚不清楚他明年的计划，因为尽管赛季表现不佳，他仍曾有望晋升 F1。

    这是通过他担任凯迪拉克储备车手的角色实现的，并且得益于 2026 年的六次第一节练习赛出场，他现在已经获得了超级驾照，但就在获得之后几天，瓦尔特利·博塔斯被确认将于明年与塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯搭档。

    至于他在 Hitech 的替代者，比利恩斯基今年也参加了他的 F2 新秀赛季，但表现也好不了多少，以 36 分位列车手积分榜第 16 位，效力于 DAMS。

    “我非常期待在赛季最后两站加入Hitech。能在2027年之前有机会与车队合作，真是额外的收获，”这位22岁的波兰裔英国车手说道，他在2025年F3中获得第11名。

    “这意味着我们可以立即开始合作，相互了解，并在明年之前打下坚实的基础。当然，我希望能在本赛季收官阶段取得一些强劲的成绩。”

    Colton Herta replaced at Hitech to end poor F2 season early

    Herta will not contest the final two rounds of the 2026 Formula 2 campaign

    Colton Herta will end his torrid time in Formula 2 early by not returning for the final two rounds of the 2026 season following a “mutual agreement” with Hitech.

    The nine-time IndyCar race winner made the transition to European single-seaters this year as part of his bid to gain an FIA Superlicence that is required to compare in Formula 1.

    But F2 has not gone as planned for Herta, who is 19th in the standings with only 26 points from 12 rounds, achieving a best result of fifth at Barcelona from third on a reversed grid.

    The 26-year-old American is not returning for the final race weekends in Qatar and Abu Dhabi and will be replaced by Roman Bilinski - who was already set to take Herta’s seat in 2027.

    Jan Sumann, Hitech's F2 head of race engineering said: “We're delighted to welcome Roman to Hitech for the final two rounds of the season.

    “These two weekends will give us an opportunity to start building important relationships before the winter.

    “I would also like to thank Colton for his commitment and determination throughout his time with Hitech, despite a challenging season. We wish him the very best for the future.”

    Herta was not quoted in the team statement and it is not yet known what his plans for next year are because, despite a poor season, he was still in the frame for an F1 promotion.

    That was via his role as Cadillac’s reserve driver and thanks to six FP1 appearances in 2026, he has now obtained a superlicence but days after doing so, Valtteri Bottas was confirmed for next year alongside Sergio Perez.

    As for his replacement at Hitech, Bilinski also contested his rookie F2 campaign this year but didn’t fare much better, sitting 16th in the standings with 36 points for DAMS.

    “I'm really excited to be joining Hitech for the final two rounds of the season. Having the opportunity to work with the team ahead of 2027 is a real bonus,” said the 22-year-old Polish-Briton, who finished 11th in F3 in 2025.

    “It means we can start working together straight away, get to know each other and build a strong foundation ahead of next year. Of course, I’ll be hoping for some strong results to close out the current season.”
