    马尔克斯就手腕伤势向哈贾尔提出建议，哈贾尔缺席荷兰大奖赛

    伊萨克·哈贾尔因上周健身房训练中手腕受伤，将缺席本周末的荷兰大奖赛。红牛和MotoGP冠军马克·马尔克斯都建议他不要冒险，角田裕毅被召入替补。

    MotoGP冠军马克·马尔克斯就手腕伤势给伊萨克·哈贾尔提出的建议

    这位法国F1车手原本急于立刻回到赛车中参加荷兰大奖赛，但红牛车队和这位MotoGP明星都敦促他不要冒任何风险

    伊萨克·哈贾尔因上周在健身房训练中手腕受伤，将错过本周末的荷兰大奖赛。据小红牛首席执行官彼得·拜耳透露，红牛的姊妹车队在周五就被告知可能会有车手变动，情况在周二进一步发展。

    正是在那时，角田裕毅接到召唤——当时他正在希腊的海滩上，被要求替补出场——而利亚姆·劳森在2025年中国大奖赛后被降级，如今在红牛又获得了另一次机会。

    虽然这种情况自然为两位车手都带来了机会，但对哈贾尔来说却极其令人沮丧，不过他周五上午还是出现在了赞德沃特的车队围场。

    哈贾尔去年在荷兰站拿到了自己的首个一级方程式领奖台，他原本计划在今年用一款特制的头盔设计，以俏皮的方式致敬那一成就。

    红牛与马尔克斯达成一致：慢慢恢复，不要着急

    哈贾尔——正如人们在一项运动员身上所能预期的那样——本想立刻重返赛场，但两方都建议他不要冒任何风险，缺席荷兰大奖赛这个周末。

    首先，这条消息来自红牛。劳朗·梅基斯确认，车队不想在维斯塔潘的队友身上冒任何风险，这位车手今年表现惊人地出色。

    "伊萨克没有问题，"梅基斯对F1官方网站表示。

    "我们没有任何理由在他身上冒险。他这个赛季的开局非常出色，每次把这台车开出车库时，他都在不断变强，一个赛段接一个赛段，一个周末接一个周末。

    "我们零冒险，让他这个周末好好休养恢复，希望我们能让他非常、非常快地回到车里。"

    哈贾尔原本为本周末计划了一款特别的头盔设计，灵感来自代尔夫特蓝陶和他去年摔碎的奖杯。

    跳过赞德沃特的建议还来自另一个更令人意外的来源：七届MotoGP冠军马克·马尔克斯。

    这位杜卡迪车手与哈贾尔共用同一位理疗师卡洛斯·J·加西亚。哈贾尔在手腕受伤后给马尔克斯打了电话，这位西班牙人建议他不要急于复出。

    这位目前在MotoGP积分榜上排名第四的车手还敦促哈贾尔不要急于进行某些医疗程序，因为这位法国人还很年轻，F1职业生涯还很漫长。

    因此，马奎兹认为，对哈德贾尔来说，慢慢来、好好恢复才是最明智的做法，而这正是红牛早已经告诉这位年轻车手的话。

    马奎兹本人在伤病方面有着丰富的亲身经历。这位MotoGP传奇人物曾摔断过右上臂和右手拇指，双肩都曾脱臼，还遭受过复视的困扰。

    马奎兹以伤后快速复出而闻名，但他却建议哈德贾尔反其道而行之

    当然，关键问题在于哈德贾尔何时能够重新坐进小红牛22的驾驶舱。

    当Motorsport.com进行询问时，红牛表示，现在就此提供官方消息还为时过早，尽管计划是让他在两周后的蒙扎重回赛车。

    “现在肯定下结论还为时过早，但我们的目标就是让他在蒙扎回归，”梅基斯对德国天空体育表示。

    意大利大奖赛将于9月4日至6日的周末举行。虽然蒙扎公园里这座标志性赛道绝对不是赛历上对身体要求最高的赛道，但一周之后紧接着就是在新马德林赛道举办的马德里大奖赛。

    The advice MotoGP champion Marc Marquez gave Isack Hadjar over his wrist injury

    The French F1 driver was eager to get straight back in the car for the Dutch GP, but both Red Bull and the MotoGP star urged him not to take any risks

    Isack Hadjar misses this weekend's Dutch Grand Prix after sustaining a wrist injury during a gym session last week. According to Racing Bulls CEO Peter Bayer, Red Bull’s sister team was informed on Friday of a potential driver change, with the situation developing further on Tuesday.

    That was when Yuki Tsunoda was called up – having been on a beach in Greece when he was asked to step in – while Liam Lawson was handed another opportunity at Red Bull following his demotion after the 2025 Chinese Grand Prix.

    While the situation naturally presents opportunities for both drivers, it is highly frustrating for Hadjar, who nevertheless made an appearance in the Zandvoort paddock on Friday morning.

    Hadjar claimed his maiden F1 podium in the Netherlands last year and had planned to reference that achievement with a playful nod in a special helmet design this year.

    Red Bull and Marquez in agreement: Take your time to recover

    Hadjar – as you might expect from an athlete – wanted to go racing again straight away, but he has been advised by two different parties not to take any risks and to sit out the Dutch GP weekend.

    First of all, that message came from Red Bull. Laurent Mekies confirmed that his team did not want to take any risks with Max Verstappen's team-mate, who has performed surprisingly well this year.

    "Isack is alright," Mekies told the official F1 website.

    "We have no reason to take any risk with him. He has done a fantastic start of the season, he has been stronger and stronger every time he has been driving this car out of the garage, session after session, weekend after weekend.

    "We take zero risk, we let him sort out the recovery this weekend, and hopefully we’ll have him very, very quickly [back] in the car."

    Hadjar had planned a special helmet design for this weekend, inspired by Delft blue pottery and his broken trophy from last year

    The advice to skip Zandvoort also came from another, more surprising source: seven-time MotoGP champion Marc Marquez.

    The Ducati rider and Hadjar share the same physiotherapist, Carlos J. García. Hadjar called Marquez after sustaining the wrist injury, with the Spaniard advising him not to rush his return.

    The rider currently fourth in the MotoGP standings also urged Hadjar not to rush into certain medical procedures, given that the Frenchman is still young and has a long F1 career ahead of him.

    Marquez therefore felt that taking his time and recovering properly was the most sensible approach for Hadjar, which is exactly what Red Bull had already told the youngster.

    Marquez himself has plenty of first-hand experience when it comes to injuries. The MotoGP icon has, among other things, broken his right upper arm and right thumb, dislocated both shoulders and suffered from diplopia.

    Marquez is known for returning quickly after injury, but advised Hadjar to do the opposite

    The key question, of course, is when Hadjar will be able to return behind the wheel of the RB22.

    When asked by Motorsport.com, Red Bull said it is still too early to provide an official update on that, although the plan is to have him back in the car at Monza in two weeks' time.

    "It's too early to say that definitely, but it’s our target to have him back in Monza," Mekies told Sky Sports Germany.

    The Italian Grand Prix will take place on the weekend of 4-6 September. Although the iconic circuit in Monza's park is certainly not the most physically demanding track on the calendar, the Madrid Grand Prix at the new Madring follows just one week later.
