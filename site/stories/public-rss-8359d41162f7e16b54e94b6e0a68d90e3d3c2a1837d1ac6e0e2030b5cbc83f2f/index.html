    阿隆索荷兰大奖赛积分完赛 被车迷誉为“真正的GOAT”

    两届F1世界冠军费尔南多·阿隆索在荷兰大奖赛从第18位冲至第9位积分完赛，被车迷誉为“史上最伟大车手”（GOAT）。

    F1荷兰大奖赛后，费尔南多·阿隆索被誉为"真正的GOAT"："简直就是一台机器"

    费尔南多·阿隆索在荷兰大奖赛上从第18位冲到第9名后，被车迷誉为GOAT

    两届F1世界冠军费尔南多·阿隆索在荷兰大奖赛上获得积分后，被车迷誉为历史最伟大车手。

    在2026赛季开局艰难的情况下，阿隆索在上半赛季四次退赛，他的队友兰斯·斯特罗尔七次退赛，这位效力于银石车队的车手在匈牙利大奖赛和荷兰大奖赛上进行了大幅升级。

    阿隆索在夏休期前唯一一次拿到积分是在摩纳哥大奖赛，当时多名车手因在维修通道超速被罚。现在，在八月停摆期后的首场比赛中，这位西班牙车手以第九名完赛，再添两个积分。

    车迷们很快就为这位45岁的老将喝彩。一位车迷在Reddit上评论道：“授予阿隆索荣誉荷兰公民身份。”另一位则补充说：“只要给他一辆稍微像样的车，他就能交出成绩。”

    还有人发帖说：“纯粹靠速度从P18追到P9（再加上维斯塔潘撞车退赛），阿斯顿·马丁终于像一辆真正的赛车了。”

    好几个人称阿隆索为史上最伟大车手（GOAT）。有人发帖说：“真正的GOAT。”还有人写道：“阿隆索这个名字里本来就有G.O.A和T这几个字母。”

    进一步的反应包括：“他本来有一半的机会拿到1分，结果他拿到了2分”，以及“天哪，我们一次又一次地说过。阿隆索简直就是一台机器。想象一下他开迈凯伦或奔驰会怎样。太疯狂了！我希望他再留两年，等纽维-本田把事情搞定，给他一辆能争冠军的车。”

    阿隆索目前在车手积分榜上以3分排名第19位，与排名第18位的埃斯特班·奥康积分相同。他排在角田裕毅、兰斯·斯托尔、瓦尔特利·博塔斯和塞尔吉奥·佩雷兹之前，这几位都是0分。角田在赞德沃特代替利亚姆·劳森为小红牛车队出战。

    Fernando Alonso hailed as "actual GOAT" after F1 Dutch GP: "Just a machine"

    Fernando Alonso was branded the GOAT by fans after charging from 18th to ninth at the Dutch Grand Prix

    Two-time Formula 1 champion Fernando Alonso has been branded the GOAT (greatest of all time) by fans following a points-paying finish at the Dutch Grand Prix.

    After a challenging start to 2026 for Aston Martin, which resulted in four grand prix retirements for Alonso and seven for his team-mate Lance Stroll in the first half of the season, the Silverstone outfit introduced extensive upgrades at the Hungarian and Dutch Grands Prix.

    Alonso picked up his only point before the summer break at the Monaco Grand Prix when several drivers were penalised for speeding in the pitlane. Now, at the first race back after the August shutdown, the Spaniard has collected two points with a ninth-place finish.

    Fans were quick to applaud the 45-year-old. "Honorary Dutch citizenship for Alonso," one fan commented on Reddit, while another added: "As soon as he is given even a slightly competent car, he delivers."

    Someone else posted: "P18 to P9 on pure pace (and Verstappen crashing out), finally the AM is something resembling a real car."

    Several referred to Alonso as the GOAT. "Actual GOAT," one posted, and someone else wrote: "You can’t spell Fernando Alonso without G.O.A and T."

    Further reactions included: "He had half a chance of getting 1 point, so he got 2," and "Man, we say it time after time. Alonso is just a machine. Imagine him in a Macca or a Merc. Absolutely insane! I hope he stays for 2 more years and Newey-Honda get their s**t together and give a championship-winning car."

    Alonso now sits 19th in the drivers' championship with three points, level on points with 18th-placed Esteban Ocon. He sits ahead of Yuki Tsunoda, who stepped in for Liam Lawson at Racing Bulls at Zandvoort, Lance Stroll, Valtteri Bottas and Sergio Perez, who are all on zero points.
