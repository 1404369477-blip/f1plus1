    法拉利在阿特尔多米罗·赫曼诺斯·罗德里格斯赛道的第一圈争议性发车为他们的对手提供了终极礼物。查尔斯·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿在4号弯的绝望的 车轮对车轮碰撞从根本上改变了墨西哥城大奖赛的轨迹，但随后的国际汽联 翻译为裁判的决定让一些维修区的人感到非常失望。

    观看那辆红色赛车完全脱离赛道，立即引起了车队负责人劳伦特·梅基斯的警觉。这位法国高管密切监控了第一圈的混乱情况，立刻意识到摩纳哥车手离开沥青路面并获得持久优势的行为不可避免地会受到体育处罚。

    “你的第一反应就是应该受到处罚，'梅基斯在赛后媒体任务中透露。'作为当时的一名竞争者，这是你首先想到的事情。

    最终，赛事控制部门对法拉利明星车手处以五秒时间罚分，但决定的延迟发布严重削弱了其对赛程的影响。因为勒克莱尔已经利用干净空气拉大了与队友的巨大差距，因此时间扣除部分被抵消了。

    “我们确实对确认处罚所需的时间感到有些惊讶，'梅基斯承认，并指出国际汽联延迟裁决的明显缺陷。'我们也对只罚五秒感到惊讶，因为在他们给出那个处罚的时候，我认为查尔斯已经比刘易斯领先五秒了。”

    与其完全依赖裁判来中和法拉利的威胁，策略小组采取了主动。当勒克莱尔为更换新胎而驶入维修区时，梅基斯和他的车队墙执行了一次完美的掩护策略，同时将自己的赛车驶入维修区，以否认马拉内洛车队获得任何干净的空气下压力优势。

    Laurent Mekies: Charles Leclerc’s penalty “felt a bit light”

    Laurent Mekies beleives Charles Leclerc's five-second Mexico City GP penalty was a bit light after Ferrari's lap one clash

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Ferrari’s divisive opening lap at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez provided the ultimate gift to their rivals. Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton's desperate wheel-to-wheel clash at Turn 4 fundamentally altered the trajectory of the Mexico City Grand Prix, but the subsequent ruling from the FIA stewards left some on the pitwall severely underwhelmed.

    Watching the scarlet cars run completely off the track immediately triggered alarms for team principal Laurent Mekies. The French executive closely monitored the lap 1 chaos, instantly recognising that a sporting sanction was inevitable for the Monegasque driver leaving the asphalt and gaining a lasting advantage.

    "The first thing that comes to your mind is that should be a penalty," Mekies revealed during his post-race media duties. "That's the first thing that come to your mind as a competitor at that moment."

    Race control ultimately handed the Ferrari star a five-second time penalty, but the delayed timing of the decision severely blunted its actual impact on the running order. Because Leclerc had already used the clean air to pull a massive gap over his team-mate, the time deduction was partly negated because of this.

    "We were certainly a bit surprised by how long it took to see the penalty confirmed," Mekies admitted, noting the glaring flaw in the FIA's delayed ruling. "We were also surprised that it was only five seconds because probably at the time they had given that penalty, I think Charles was already clear five seconds from Lewis anyway."

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    The team boss did not hold back his frustration with the regulatory leniency, stating the punishment "felt a bit light" but acknowledging "it's what it is".

    Rather than relying solely on the stewards to neutralise the Ferrari threat, the strategy group took matters into their own hands. When Leclerc dived into the pits for fresh tyres, Mekies and his pitwall executed a flawless cover strategy, bringing their own car in simultaneously to deny the Maranello squad any clean-air undercut advantage.

    "It was once again an extremely strong execution," Mekies praised. "The usual suspects with the strategy group and on the pitwall that came up with that when we heard Charles was coming in, and that was the best we had just about the gap to him considering the penalty, and took the shot, and it worked."

    The tactical masterclass, combined with a fierce defensive drive from race winner Max Verstappen, ultimately kept the Ferraris at bay. Mekies specifically highlighted Verstappen's clinical driving during the opening stint, stating he did "undoubtedly a very good job at keeping Lewis and Charles behind for the moment they were racing with him".
