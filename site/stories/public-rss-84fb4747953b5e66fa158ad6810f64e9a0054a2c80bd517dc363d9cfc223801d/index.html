    拉塞尔启动全新资助卡丁车学院

    梅赛德斯一级方程式车手乔治·拉塞尔在银石赛道启动了一所新的卡丁车学院，旨在培养下一代赛车运动人才，25名年轻车手将在2027年获得全额资助的锦标赛机会。

    GR63卡丁车学院将对八岁和九岁的男孩和女孩开放，拉塞尔希望为有才华的年轻车手提供一条进入赛车运动的途径，否则他们可能会因为运动的财务需求而受阻。

    该计划位于银石赛道的卡丁车赛道，将分为四个阶段进行，第一阶段将在2026年12月开始，以开放通道的资格赛形式进行。

    第一阶段将看到符合条件的车手在12月6日至31日之间参加即来即走的练习赛，80名最快的车手将晋级到2027年1月31日的淘汰赛。

    从那里，将有25名车手被选入GR63锦标赛，这是一场从2027年3月持续到11月的八轮系列赛。每一轮比赛将在银石赛道不同的赛道配置上进行，所有参赛费用将由学院承担。

    被选出的25名车手还将获得一套完整的学院装备，包括赛车服、头盔、手套和靴子。

    该计划将在2027年12月达到高潮，届时锦标赛的前五名完成者在银石赛道进行最后的决赛。车手们将根据他们的才能、圈速、体能、赛车技巧和媒体技能进行评估，整体获胜者将在2028年获得全额资助的卡丁车赛季。

    拉塞尔将帮助在整个计划中指导参与者。

    “卡丁车赛是我的起点，它教会了我职业生涯中依赖的一切，”拉塞尔说。

    我知道有才华的年轻车手获得第一次机会有多难，尤其是在预算紧张的时候。

    通过GR63卡丁车赛车院，我想通过发现那些隐藏的天赋并为他们提供他们需要的支持和平台来回报社会。

    该项目的财务可及性是其吸引力的一部分。虽然最初的资格赛将花费25英镑，而1月份的淘汰赛将花费75英镑，但从锦标赛阶段开始的所有费用都将由完全资助。

    该计划得到了银石赛道和英国赛车驾驶俱乐部（BRDC）的支持，BRDC主席大卫·库尔特哈德表示欢迎投资于英国赛车运动的未来。

    "GR63学院完美地捕捉了这种精神，我们很高兴能与乔治合作，在这里支持下一代的赛车手," 库尔特哈德说。

    最初的到达并驾驶的资格赛将于2026年12月6日至31日在银石卡丁车赛道举行，费用为25英镑。

    所有申请者必须是英联邦国家的公民。

    更多信息及预订可通过银石赛道GR63学院网站获得。

    英文原文

    Mercedes Formula 1 driver George Russell has launched a new karting academy at Silverstone aimed at developing the next generation of motorsport talent, with 25 young drivers set to receive a fully funded championship opportunity in 2027.

    The GR63 Karting Academy will be open to boys and girls aged eight and nine, with Russell hoping to provide a route into motorsport for talented youngsters who might otherwise be held back by the financial demands of the sport.

    Based at Silverstone's karting circuit, the initiative will run across four stages, beginning with open-access qualifying sessions in December 2026.

    The first stage will see eligible drivers compete in arrive-and-drive sessions between December 6 and 31, with the 80 fastest progressing to a shootout on January 31, 2027.

    From there, 25 drivers will be selected for the GR63 Championship, an eight-round series running from March to November 2027. Each round will take place on a different circuit configuration at Silverstone, with all participation costs covered by the academy.

    The 25 selected drivers will also receive a full set of academy equipment, including racing overalls, a helmet, gloves and boots.

    The programme will culminate in December 2027, when the championship's top five finishers compete in a final race at Silverstone. Drivers will be evaluated on their talent, lap times, fitness, racecraft and media skills, with the overall winner receiving a fully-funded karting season in 2028.

    Russell will help mentor and guide participants throughout the programme.

    "Karting is where it all started for me, and it taught me everything I've relied on throughout my career," said Russell.

    "I know how hard it is for talented young drivers to get that first opportunity, especially when budgets are tight.

    "With the GR63 Karting Academy, I want to give something back by finding those hidden talents and giving them the support and platform they need to reach their potential."

    The financial accessibility of the programme is a central part of its appeal. While the initial qualifying sessions will cost £25 and entry to the January shootout will cost £75, everything from the championship stage onwards will be fully funded.

    The initiative is being supported by Silverstone and the British Racing Drivers' Club (BRDC), with BRDC president David Coulthard welcoming the opportunity to invest in Britain's motorsport future.

    "The GR63 Academy captures that ethos perfectly, and we are delighted to work with George to support the next generation of drivers here at Silverstone," said Coulthard.

    The initial arrive-and-drive qualifying sessions will take place at Silverstone Kart Track between December 6 and 31, 2026, at a cost of £25.

    All applicants must be citizens of a Commonwealth country.

    Further information and bookings are available through Silverstone's GR63 Academy website.
