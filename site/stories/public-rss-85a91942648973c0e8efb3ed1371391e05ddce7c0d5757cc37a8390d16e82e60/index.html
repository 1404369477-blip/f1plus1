    哈斯车队保留卡马拉席位，结束其2026年F2冠军争夺战

    哈斯车队确认罗伯托·卡马拉将随车队参加卡塔尔和阿布扎比的赛季剩余比赛，这实际上结束了他在F2的冠军争夺。

    哈斯车队决定保留拉斐尔·卡马拉在F1的席位，结束了他在2026年F2冠军争夺战中的努力

    这位巴西车手将在卡塔尔和阿布扎比参加比赛，以积累2027年的经验，尽管他在F2积分榜上领先尼古拉·索洛夫七分

    哈斯车队的新人拉斐尔·卡马拉将在卡塔尔和阿布扎比赛季结束前继续为哈斯车队效力，实际上结束了他在F2锦标赛的挑战

    卡马拉在10月被确认将在2027年成为埃斯特班·奥康的全职替代者，并在奥康与美国车队关系恶化后迅速获得了这个席位。

    在测试车手Ryo Hirakawa在墨西哥大奖赛周末中途接管了奥康的赛车后，卡马拉在巴西的本土赛道上完成了他的F1首秀，并且在本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛中也参加了VF-26车队。

    最后的两轮比赛提出了更大的决定，因为这位21岁的年轻人以七分的优势领先红牛青训车队车手Nikola Tsolov的F2锦标赛。车手在同一场周末无法参加F1和F2的比赛。

    哈斯车队现在已经决定保留巴西人在座位上完成本赛季，而不是召回Hirakawa，以便在卡马拉2027年的新秀赛季之前尽可能多地给他积累经验。

    ‘我们很高兴能够在本赛季结束时有Rafa驾驶，并在他在巴西和美国的首次两场赛事中为车队建立基础，’哈斯F1车队老板小林章说。

    “我很高兴拉法将每一次赛道练习都视为学习的机会，并进一步融入了团队。我们现在可以一起结束本赛季，这对我们所有人来说都是有益的，因为接下来的休赛期相对较短，而且我们的2027年准备工作也已经开始。”

    卡马拉在雨中艰难地完成了在英特拉戈斯的处子秀，排名第20，而在拉斯维加斯排名第16。

    现在，Tsolov只需要再得到七个积分就能与卡马拉持平，而卡马拉的缺席也提升了第三名Alex Dunne的夺冠机会，他落后巴西人18分。

    Haas keeps Rafael Camara in F1, ending his 2026 F2 title bid

    The Brazilian will race in Qatar and Abu Dhabi to build experience for 2027, despite leading Nikola Tsolov by seven points in the Formula 2 standings

    Haas rookie Rafael Camara will see out the season with Haas in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, effectively ending his F2 championship challenge.

    Camara was confirmed in October as a full-time replacement for Esteban Ocon in 2027, and was fast-tracked into the seat after Ocon's relationship with the US-owned team deteriorated.

    After test driver Ryo Hirakawa took over Ocon's car midway through the Mexico Grand Prix weekend, Camara made his F1 debut on home soil in Brazil and was also in the VF-26 at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    The final two rounds presented a bigger decision to make, though, because the 21-year-old is leading the F2 championship by seven points over Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov. Drivers are unable to take part in both F1 and F2 on the same weekend.

    Haas has now decided to keep the Brazilian in the seat to finish the season, rather than bringing back Hirakawa, to give Camara as much experience as possible ahead of his 2027 rookie campaign.

    "We’re excited to be able to finish the season with Rafa driving and build on his first two race starts with the team in Brazil and America," said Haas F1 team boss Ayao Komatsu.

    "I’m delighted that Rafa’s embraced every track session as a learning opportunity and embedded himself further within the team. We now get to finish the season together which is advantageous to us all ahead of a relatively short off-season and as our 2027 preparations get underway."

    Camara finished 20th after a difficult maiden race in Interlagos in the wet, and took 16th in Las Vegas.

    While Tsolov now just needs seven points to match Camara, Camara’s absence also boosts third-placed Alex Dunne’s title chances, who trails the Brazilian by 18 points.
