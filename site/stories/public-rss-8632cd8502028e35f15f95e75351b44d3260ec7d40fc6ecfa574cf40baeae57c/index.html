    梅赛德斯解释安东内利在英特拉格斯打滑原因

    梅赛德斯副车队领队布拉德利·洛德解释了车队在F1巴西大奖赛中多次打滑的原因。安东内利在墨西哥确认首个年度冠军后的首场比赛中，原本有望利用杆位得主诺里斯的糟糕起步，从第二位发车抢得领先。

    梅赛德斯解释安东内利在英特拉格斯代价高昂的打滑背后的“异常”赛车表现

    梅赛德斯F1车队说明了安东内利在巴西大奖赛中操控困难的原因

    梅赛德斯副车队领队布拉德利·洛德解释了车队在F1巴西大奖赛中多次打滑的原因。

    在墨西哥确认首个年度冠军后的首场比赛中，安东内利原本有望利用杆位得主兰多·诺里斯的糟糕起步，从第二位发车抢得领先，并在湿干湿交替的比赛中前15圈建立了舒适的领先优势。

    但在第18圈，由于诺里斯在前一圈进站，安东内利从磨损的中性胎换成干地胎后，开始报告令人困惑的悬挂问题，感觉他的W17赛车向一侧跑偏。两圈后，安东内利在1号弯冲出赛道，赛车尾部在刹车时突然失控，将他甩入缓冲区。

    这位意大利车手重新回到比赛，但将领先位置输给了诺里斯，并在排名中进一步下滑，最终以第五名完赛，落后诺里斯41秒。安东内利的打滑并非孤立事件，他此前已在湿滑的12号弯冲出赛道。与此同时，队友乔治·拉塞尔在狭窄的维修区出口打滑后幸运地逃过一劫，他奋力追赶，最终以第四名完赛，排在安东内利之前。

    “打滑这件事我需要仔细看看，因为我也不知道为什么车尾会这么快失控，”安东内利在梅赛德斯锁定车队总冠军后，对自己糟糕比赛的愤怒有所平息，他说道。“毫无预警，什么都没有。但当我换上软胎时，我就感觉赛车有些奇怪。所以，我们需要检查发生了什么，但另一方面，很遗憾，因为我们就是在那里输掉了比赛。”

    梅赛德斯锁定了自2021年以来的首个车队总冠军

    在调查了两位车手所遇到的操控问题后，梅赛德斯认为，他们报告的操控异常主要归因于在周日较冷条件下让轮胎进入正确工作窗口的困难。

    “基米显然打滑了，四个轮胎都出现了平斑，所以他的速度受到了一定限制，”洛德在车队的社交媒体复盘中说。“当我们换上干地软胎时确实非常挣扎，在最后一段进站间隔也是如此，赛车非常非常难开，转向过度严重，尤其是在软胎阶段，轮胎温度非常低。

    “这意味着两位车手，实际上还有场上的其他车手，都以为赛车出了什么故障。但仔细看，看起来我们只是低于轮胎温度窗口，有时会短暂进入窗口，但在潮湿条件下又会掉出来，这就是导致赛车行为非常奇怪和异常的原因。”

    洛德补充说：“这是一场真正跌宕起伏的比赛。很少会出现这样的情况：你在编队圈打滑并几乎撞上护栏，另一辆车在维修区打滑，赛道上还发生了几起事故，尽管如此还是设法以第四和第五名完赛。

    “在巴西站的比赛中，有几个阶段——起步时使用第一套半雨胎，以及随后在干地上使用软胎的长时间阶段——我们实际上是赛道上最快的赛车。”

    Mercedes explains "unusual" car behaviour behind Kimi Antonelli's costly Interlagos spin

    The Mercedes Formula 1 team offers reasons behind Kimi Antonelli's handling difficulties in the Brazilian Grand Prix

    Mercedes deputy team principal Bradley Lord has provided an explanation for several spins that occurred for the Silver Arrows in Formula 1's Brazilian Grand Prix.

    In his first event since his maiden title was confirmed in Mexico, Kimi Antonelli looked set to capitalise on a poor start by polesitter Lando Norris to snatch the lead from second on the grid, building up a comfortable buffer over the first 15 laps of a wet-dry-wet race.

    But after changing from worn intermediates to slicks on lap 18, in response to Norris' stop one lap prior, Antonelli began reporting puzzling suspension issues, feeling like his W17 was pulling to one side. Two laps later Antonelli went off in Turn 1 as his car's rear end suddenly snapped under braking, spitting Antonelli out into the run-off area.

    The Italian recovered but lost the lead to Norris and dropped further down the order to finish fifth, 41 seconds behind Norris. Antonelli's spin didn't come in isolation, as he had already gone off at the slippery Turn 12. Meanwhile, team-mate George Russell was fortunate to survive a spin at the narrow pit exit as he rallied to finish ahead of Antonelli in fourth.

    "The spin is something I need to look at because I don't know why I lost the rear so quickly as well," Antonelli said after his anger over his poor race had subsided thanks to Mercedes clinching the constructors' title. "Like no warning, nothing. But already when I put on the softs, I felt something weird with the car. So, we need to check what happened but on the other hand yeah, shame because we lost the race there."

    Mercedes clinched its first constructors' title since 2021

    After investigating the handling issues both of its drivers experienced, Mercedes believes the handling anomalies they reported were largely down to the difficulties getting the tyres into the right operating window in Sunday's cooler conditions.

    "Kimi obviously had had a spin, flat-spotted all four tyres and so his pace was somewhat limited," said Lord in the team's social media debrief. "We did really struggle as we went onto that dry soft tyre and then in the final inter-stint as well, with a car that was very, very tricky to drive, very oversteery and in the soft tyre stint in particular, with very low tyre temperatures.

    "That meant both of the drivers, and indeed other drivers in the field, actually thought there was something broken with the car. But looking at it, it looked like we were just below the tyre temperature window, sometimes popping into it, but then dropping back out of it in the damp conditions, and that's what was causing the very strange and unusual car behaviour."

    Lord added: "It was a really topsy-turvy race. It's not very often that you spin off on a formation lap and almost make contact with a barrier, that you spin in the pitlane with the other car, that you have several incidents on track as well and nonetheless manage to come home fourth and fifth.

    "There were periods of the race in Brazil, the first inter tyre at the start and then during a large part of that dry soft-tyre stint, that we actually were the fastest car on track."
