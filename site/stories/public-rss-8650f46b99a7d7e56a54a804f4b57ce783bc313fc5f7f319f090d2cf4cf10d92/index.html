    F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛：乔治·拉塞尔在碰撞后领跑最终练习赛

    乔治·拉塞尔在紧张的第三场练习赛中与马克斯·维斯塔潘发生碰撞，但仍然领跑最终练习赛。周四的练习赛因雨中断，周五的练习赛成为关键，因为它是唯一一场干燥的练习。

    F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛：乔治·拉塞尔在碰撞后领跑最终练习赛

    梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔在周五的F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛最终练习赛中领跑，此前他与马克斯·维斯塔潘发生碰撞

    乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛的紧张第三练习赛中与马克斯·维斯塔潘发生碰撞，作为梅赛德斯F1车手，他领跑计时表

    在周四的两次练习赛都被雨水打断后，周五傍晚拉斯维加斯大道上充满了活力。唯一的剩余练习赛变得至关重要，因为它提供了进入排位赛和比赛的唯一干燥赛道，所有22辆车迅速从维修区冲出。

    在14摄氏度的寒冷气温中，周四的降雨使得围绕拉斯维加斯大道的6.2公里赛道更加难以驾驶，路面抓地力极为有限。

    维斯塔潘在侧滑惊险和转向机构问题中幸存，并在软胎上以1分35.675秒的成绩领跑，这是在寒冷条件下最受欢迎的轮胎，因为它更容易升温。

    在比赛过半后，拉塞尔以1分35.124秒的成绩接管了领先位置，这位英国人希望以高姿态结束赛季，因为他拒绝了梅赛德斯涡轮增压升级以避免发车处罚。拉塞尔进一步将自己的基准成绩降低到1分34.805秒，但在滑溜的赛道上成为多辆赛车中找到跑道的车辆之一。

    迫切需要在赛道上完成大量干净圈数的紧迫感表现得淋漓尽致，维斯塔潘和弗朗科·科拉平托在争夺干净空气的过程中几乎发生冲突。维斯塔潘的红牛车队队友伊萨克·哈达尔也为威廉姆斯车队的亚历克斯·阿隆也为处罚进行了游说。

    安诺内利对维斯塔潘强行超车感到恼火，当这位四次世界冠军随后锁死方向盘并冲入跑道区域时，兰多·诺里斯掩饰不住自己的喜悦。"即时报应，太神奇了，"他在车队无线电里说道。

    最大的冲突发生在练习课结束的时候，再次涉及维斯塔潘，他在直道上试图对拉塞尔使用类似的动作。拉塞尔毫不畏惧，将他的梅赛德斯赛车开进14号弯的内侧，再次超越红牛，在强行将维斯塔潘挤出赛道时发生了侧面接触。这一事件将由国际汽联的裁判员进行调查。"有趣的游戏，"拉塞尔在无线电里描述了这一事件。

    诺里斯在练习课的最后几分钟几乎超越了拉塞尔，以1分35.060秒的成绩排名第二。然而，拉塞尔在最后也找到了提升，以1分34.306秒和1分34.041秒的成绩在成绩单上领先诺里斯七个十分之一。

    诺里斯被他的世界冠军继承人基米·安东内利降级到第三名，而查尔斯·勒克莱尔在尝试了几个逃生区域后，滑行到第四名。

    在维特斯坦普之前，科拉平托为阿尔皮纳车队获得了第五名，这条赛道应该适合恩斯顿车队的滑溜溜的赛车，正如皮埃尔·加斯利在蒙扎的惊人杆位所证明的那样。

    小红牛车队的李安·劳森获得了第七名，随后是刘易斯·汉密尔顿、加斯利和阿尔维德·林德布拉德。

    F1 Las Vegas GP: George Russell tops final practice after Max Verstappen collision

    Mercedes' George Russell tops Friday's final practice at Formula 1's Las Vegas Grand Prix after clashing with Max Verstappen

    George Russell collided with Max Verstappen in a tense third practice session at the Las Vegas Grand Prix as the Mercedes Formula 1 driver topped the timesheets.

    After both of Thursday's practice sessions had been derailed by rain, the Las Vegas Strip was buzzing with activity at dusk on Friday. The sole remaining practice session became crucial as it presented the only dry running heading into qualifying and the race, as all 22 cars sprinted out of the pitlane in short order.

    Amid chilly 14C temperatures, the Thursday rainfall only made the 6.2km circuit around the Las Vegas Strip even more difficult to drive, with surface grip extremely limited.

    Verstappen survived a sideways scare and steering rack issues to lead the early running with a 1m35.675s on soft tyres, by far the most popular tyre in cold conditions as it was easier to fire up to temperature.

    Just after the halfway mark Russell took over top spot with a 1m35.124s, the Briton aiming to finish the season on a high as he declined a Mercedes turbo upgrade to avoid a grid penalty. Russell further lowered his own benchmark to a 1m34.805s lap, before becoming one of several cars to find the runoff area on the slippery circuit.

    The urgency to get plenty of clean laps in was on full display with Verstappen and Franco Colapinto almost coming to blows in their quest for clean air. Verstappen's Red Bull team-mate Isack Hadjar also lobbied for a penalty for Williams' Alex Albon.

    Annoyed by Verstappen forcing his way past, Lando Norris couldn't hide his glee when the four-time world champion subsequently locked up and sailed into the runoff area. "Instant karma, amazing," he said over team radio.

    The biggest clash followed right at the end of the session, again involving Verstappen, as he tried a similar move on Russell on the straight. Undeterred, Russell sent his Mercedes down the inside of Turn 14 to re-pass the Red Bull, making side-on contact as he muscled Verstappen off the track. The incident will be investigated by the FIA’s stewards. "Fun and games," Russell described the incident on the radio.

    Norris initially came closest to overhauling Russell in the closing minutes of the session, settling for second with a 1m35.060s lap. Russell found late improvements too, though, to pull out a massive seven-tenth lead on Norris at the top of the timesheets with laps of 1m34.306s and 1m34.041s.

    Norris was demoted to third by his world championship heir Kimi Antonelli, while Charles Leclerc skated his way to fourth after sampling several escape areas.

    Ahead of Verstappen, Colapinto was fifth for Alpine on a circuit that should suit the Enstone team's slippery car, as evidenced by the Pierre Gasly's shock pole back in Monza.

    Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson was seventh, followed by Lewis Hamilton, Gasly and Arvid Lindblad.
