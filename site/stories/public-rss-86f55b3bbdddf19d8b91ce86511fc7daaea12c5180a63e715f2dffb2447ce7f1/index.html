    克里斯蒂安·霍纳将红牛领导结构归咎于错失F1未来之星

    红牛赛车队的双重领导结构曾被誉为高效但无情的系统，但现在霍纳将其归咎为车队错失兰多·诺里斯、乔治·拉塞尔和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里。

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳将红牛领导结构归咎于错失F1的未来之星

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳将他的权限不足和红牛的领导层归咎于错失三名F1明星

    对于红牛的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析。

    多年来，红牛车队在车队经理克里斯蒂安·霍纳和赛车运动顾问赫尔穆特·马克奥之间的双重领导结构被赞扬为一种无情的但非常有效的系统。但现在，霍纳将这种管理风格指向正是米尔顿凯恩斯车队错失兰多·诺里斯、乔治·拉塞尔和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里的主要原因。

    在《高性能播客》中，霍纳将自己与组织的一次侦察失误区分开来。而不是简单地承认车队没有达到目标，霍纳强调他对红牛青训车队缺乏管辖权，认为公司动态意味着他对自己的未来车手阵容没有发言权。

    霍纳明确表示：“我从未负责年轻车手项目。”

    霍纳透露，诺里斯、拉塞尔和皮亚斯特里的父亲实际上是在付钱给红牛使用工厂的专业模拟器。

    霍纳解释说：“乔治·拉塞尔在付钱让我们使用我们的模拟器，兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里也是如此。他们的父亲在给他们开支票，让他们在我们的驾驶员模拟器上驾驶。”

    这些独立客户很快证明，他们比红牛车队重金培养的青训车队要优秀得多。霍纳回忆起他的模拟器工程师对他说，'克里斯蒂安，这些孩子比我们目前拥有的青训车手要强。'

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳，红牛车队，赫尔穆特·马克，红牛车队

    尽管霍纳是赢得锦标赛的运营公司的首席执行官和车队负责人，但他没有公司权力向这些青少年提供合同。他被迫在自己的工厂里充当中间人，将数据带给拥有绝对否决权的马克。

    '我记得把他们带到赫尔穆特面前，说，'看，我真的认为你应该看看这些人...'，霍纳表示。

    因为马克奥领导着红牛青训车队，他能够拒绝车队主管的建议，使得三名车手最终都能与竞争对手车队签约。

    Christian Horner blames Red Bull leadership structure for missing F1’s future stars

    Christian Horner blames his lack of jurisdiction and Red Bull's leadership for missing out on three F1 stars

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    For years, Red Bull Racing's dual-leadership structure between team principal Christian Horner and motorsport advisor Dr. Helmut Marko was praised as a ruthless but highly effective system. But Horner is now pointing to that exact management style as the primary reason the Milton Keynes squad lost out on Lando Norris, George Russell, and Oscar Piastri.

    Speaking on the High Performance podcast, Horner distanced himself from one of the organisation's scouting blunders. Rather than simply admitting the team missed the mark, Horner highlighted his lack of jurisdiction over the Red Bull Junior Team, arguing the corporate dynamic meant he had no say in his own future driver line-up.

    "I never had responsibility for the young driver programme," Horner explicitly stated.

    Horner revealed that the fathers of Norris, Russell, and Piastri were literally paying Red Bull to use the factory's professional simulator.

    "George Russell was paying us to go on our simulator, as were Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri," Horner explained. "Their dads were writing cheques for them to run on the driver simulator that we had."

    The telemetry quickly proved that these independent customers were vastly superior to Red Bull's heavily funded junior roster. Horner recalled his simulator engineers telling him, "'Christian, these kids are better than the juniors we've got at the moment.'"

    Christian Horner, Red Bull Racing, Helmut Marko, Red Bull Racing

    Yet, despite being the CEO and team principal of the championship-winning operation, Horner had no corporate authority to offer the teenagers a contract. He was forced to act as a middleman in his own factory, taking the data to Marko, who held absolute veto power over the driver pipeline.

    "I remember taking them to Helmut and going, 'Look, I really think you should look at these guys...'" Horner stated.

    Because Marko headed the Red Bull Junior Team decisions, he was able to reject the team principal's advice, allowing all three drivers to eventually sign with rival constructors.
