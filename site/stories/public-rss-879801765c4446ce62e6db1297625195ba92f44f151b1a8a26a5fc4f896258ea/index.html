    F1 Las Vegas GP 2026: start time, schedule and how to watch

    What time the 2026 F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix starts across the US, plus the full schedule and how to watch live on Apple TV

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix starts at 8 pm PT on Saturday, November 21, which is 11 pm ET. The race runs at night along the Strip, and every session is live on Apple TV.

    In Alaska, every session starts one hour earlier than in the PT column, and in Hawaii two hours earlier. This is not a sprint weekend.

    What time does the Las Vegas Grand Prix start?

    The race starts on Saturday, November 21, at 8 pm PT, 9 pm MT, 10 pm CT and 11 pm ET. In Alaska, it starts at 7 pm, and in Hawaii at 6 pm

    The race runs over 50 laps of the 3.853-mile street circuit, a total of 192.7 miles. Without interruptions, it finishes at around 9:30 pm PT, 12:30 am ET. If the race runs longer than two hours, the checkered flag falls at the two-hour mark even if not all laps are completed.

    What time is Las Vegas GP qualifying?

    Qualifying starts on Friday, November 20, at 8 pm PT, 11 pm ET, and lasts about an hour. The slowest five drivers drop out in Q1 and five more in Q2. The ten remaining drivers fight for pole position in Q3.

    The cold desert night is the main challenge. Tires struggle to reach their working temperature on the long straights, and drivers who cannot switch them on in time lose big chunks of lap time.

    How to watch the Las Vegas Grand Prix in the US

    All three Las Vegas practice sessions can be watched free on Apple TV without a subscription. Apple also makes selected race weekends available free, although it has not identified the Las Vegas Grand Prix as one of those free races.

    Max Verstappen won the 2025 Las Vegas Grand Prix. Lando Norris started from pole and crossed the line second, but both McLarens were disqualified afterward for excessive plank wear. George Russell and Kimi Antonelli completed the podium.
