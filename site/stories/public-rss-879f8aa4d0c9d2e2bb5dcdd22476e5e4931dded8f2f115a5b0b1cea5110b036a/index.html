    F1公布2027赛季启动活动落地米兰

    F1宣布2027赛季启动活动"F1 The Show"将于2月18日在米兰举行，汇集2027赛季的车手与车队。

    F1 2027：新赛季发布会将于明年2月在米兰举行，届时各车队和车手将悉数出席

    在米兰举行的"F1 The Show"将汇集2027赛季的车手和车队，于2月18日启动新赛季；该活动将在这座意大利城市举办的为期三天的车迷节中进行，为下赛季造势

    回顾2025年在伦敦O2体育馆举办的F1 75 LIVE上所有最有趣和最离奇的时刻

    一级方程式将在明年2月于米兰举行其历史上第二次赛季启动活动，为2027赛季揭幕。

    在2025年2月于伦敦The O2场馆举办F1 75 Live以纪念这项运动75周年之后，F1承诺两年后举办一场“规模更大、更具沉浸感”的活动，为新赛季拉开帷幕。

    F1 Live Milan将包括一场为期三天的车迷节，于2月18日至20日在意大利城市举行。首晚的重头戏是F1 The Show，全部11支车队的车手和领队都将亮相，此外还有音乐和娱乐表演。

    F1 2027: Season launch event to be held in Milan in February ahead of next campaign with teams, drivers present

    F1 The Show in Milan featuring the 2027 drivers and teams will launch the new season on February 18; event will form part of a three-day fan festival in Italian city to build up to next season

    Take a look back at all the funniest and most bizarre moments from the F1 75 LIVE at The O2 in 2025

    Formula 1 will stage its second-ever season launch event in Milan in February ahead of the 2027 campaign.

    After holding F1 75 Live at London's The O2 in February 2025 to mark the start of the sport's 75th anniversary year, F1 are promising an "even bigger and more immersive" event two years on to kick off the next campaign.

    F1 Live Milan will feature a three-day fan festival running in the Italian city from February 18-20. It will be headlined by F1 The Show on the first evening which will feature appearances from the drivers and principals of all 11 teams, in addition to music and entertainment acts.
