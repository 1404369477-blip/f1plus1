    安东内利和拉塞尔将就拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛问题与梅赛德斯进行讨论

    拉塞尔和安东内利将讨论Formula 1拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中因团队指令和赛道位置产生的混乱。

    基米·安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔将就拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛的困境与梅赛德斯进行会谈

    安东内利在拉斯维加斯的排位赛中速度最快，拉塞尔排名第三，但他们的策略并未按计划进行

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯、乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯

    乔治·拉塞尔和基米·安东内利将在团队指令和赛道位置出现混乱后，就一级方程式拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛的后果进行“线下”讨论。

    周五晚上的排位赛中，交通状况扮演了重要角色，车手们不愿意第一个出发，因为在这个低下压力的街道赛赛道上，被拖曳的优势很大。

    由于有五位车手需要接受发车位处罚，他们的主要任务是帮助队友，比如哈斯车队的奥利弗·贝尔曼在Q1中给拉斐尔·卡马拉提供拖曳。

    安东内利也是其中之一，因为更换新引擎被降了五位发车位，所以计划是在争夺杆位的比赛中给拉塞尔提供尾流，但很快计划就破产了。

    这是因为梅赛德斯车队的双雄在最后一圈被夹在阿尔卑斯车队和小红牛车队之间，尽管试图寻找空间，但有时却不得不进行三车并排。

    安东内利最终在5号弯道减速，而拉塞尔不情愿地成为车队的领跑者，2026年的世界冠军在排位赛中速度最快，他的队友落后0.331秒排在第三。

    当被问及之后是否希望得到安东内利的帮助时，拉塞尔回答说：“如果你有发车罚分，尽可能从更高的位置起步仍然很重要。”

    “但我们会私下讨论这个问题。我认为明天的比赛将非常接近，就像蒙扎一样——要想从车队中脱颖而出将非常具有挑战性。”

    “我本希望站在杆位，但我认为只要在顶尖的前七名位置，你就有机会竞争。”

    这种情况让梅赛德斯车队老板Toto Wolff对安东内利能最快感到难以置信，尤其是因为拉塞尔没有得到他离开维修区第二位的请求。

    “这非常危险，”拉塞尔补充道，他将与红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘一起占据前排，因为安东内利的处罚。

    “基米获得了杆位，但如果他晚出发一秒，他就无法完成这一圈了。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    “因为每个人都想要这么做，而且有一半的赛车在做准备圈，另一半的赛车没有，这也使得事情变得很困难。”

    “如果你可以选择，你会在九辆车的一组中排在最后，大约有3秒的差距，如果你能完美选择的话。”

    “但这就是每个人都追求的，所以这有点像2019年的蒙扎。”

    “尽管安东内利最终退后给了他设置最快圈速所需的空间，1分33.893秒，但这可能让他付出了代价，因为他刚好及时冲过终点线开始最后一圈。”

    “正如乔治所说，我们会讨论这件事，因为我们总是在排位赛中被派到前面，”安东内利说。

    “关于5号弯，这完全是我的责任。就像我说的，我们一开始就是三车并排，我只是说，'你知道什么？我要减速。'”，

    “这很棘手，首先，我差点错过了旗帜。是的，我确实受到了很大的牵引，但我离得太近，以至于在脏空气中等情况下绕过弯角实际上很困难。”

    “我们已经使用了低下压力套件，所以当你跟车时，你更能感觉到损失。”

    “但总的来说，这只是那种你认为你完了，但实际上却成功了的情况。”

    “在第五弯道，我决定减速。我并不真的知道我的时间安排有多紧张。我可能本会做得不同，但事后看来，结果仍然不错。”

    Kimi Antonelli and George Russell set for Mercedes talks over Las Vegas GP qualifying woes

    Antonelli was fastest in qualifying at Las Vegas with Russell third, but their strategy did not go as planned

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, George Russell, Mercedes

    George Russell and Kimi Antonelli will discuss the fallout from Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix qualifying “offline” following confusion over team orders and track position.

    Traffic played a significant role in qualifying on Friday night with drivers reluctant to go first, considering the advantage of a tow is huge around the low-downforce street circuit.

    There was the added complexity of five drivers taking a grid penalty, so their primary role was to help their team-mate like Haas’ Oliver Bearman giving Rafael Camara a tow in Q1.

    Antonelli was another one of those, taking a five-place drop for a new engine, so the plan was to give Russell the slipstream in the fight for pole but it quickly unravelled.

    That was because the Mercedes duo got caught in a pack with both Alpine and Racing Bulls cars ahead of the final lap, at times going three-wide despite trying to find space.

    Antonelli eventually backed off at Turn 5 while Russell reluctantly went first of the pack and the 2026 world champion was fastest in qualifying with his team-mate 0.331s off in third.

    So when asked afterwards if he was hoping for assistance from Antonelli, Russell replied: “It's still important, if you've got a grid penalty, to start as high up the order as possible.

    “But we'll take it offline and we can talk about it. I think tomorrow is going to be very close between everybody, similar to Monza - it'll be very challenging to pull away from the pack.

    “I would have liked to have been on pole, but I think anywhere in the top seven positions and you're in the fight.”

    The situation caused Mercedes boss Toto Wolff to express disbelief at how Antonelli was quickest, particularly because Russell didn’t receive his request of leaving the pits second.

    “It's very high risk,” added Russell, who will share the front row with Red Bull’s Max Verstappen due to Antonelli’s penalty.

    “Kimi got the pole, but if he started the lap a second later, he wouldn't have got the lap in.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    “It's very difficult to orchestrate because everybody's going for it, and also the fact that half the cars were doing prep laps and half the cars were not also made it difficult.

    “If you could choose, you would be at the back of a pack of nine cars with around a three-second gap, if you could perfectly choose.

    “But that's what everyone's chasing, so it's kind of like Monza 2019."

    Although Antonelli eventually backing off gave him the space needed to set his fastest lap, a 1m33.893s, it could have cost him as he only just crossed the line in time to start his final tour.

    “As George said, we will talk about it because we've been sent out at the front in qualifying always,” said Antonelli.

    “About Turn 5, it was fully on me. Like I said, we were three-wide, first of all, and I just said, "You know what? I'm just going to back off’."

    “It was tricky because, first of all, I almost missed the flag. Yes, I did get a big tow, but I was also so close that getting around the corner was actually difficult with the dirty air and so on.

    “We're already on a lower-downforce package, so you do feel even more the loss when following.

    “But overall, it was just one of those times where you think you're screwed, but actually it works out.

    “On Turn 5, I just decided to back off. I didn't really know how tight I was with the timing. I probably would have done it differently, but in hindsight, it still worked out.”
