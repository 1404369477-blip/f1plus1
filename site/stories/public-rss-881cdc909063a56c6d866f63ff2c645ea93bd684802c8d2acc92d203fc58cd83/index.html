    荷兰大奖赛首圈维斯塔潘严重撞车引发红旗

    荷兰大奖赛首圈，马克斯·维斯塔潘在湿滑的最后一弯打滑撞墙，赛会出示红旗中断比赛。比赛随后于当地时间下午3:33重启。

    荷兰大奖赛在仅仅一圈后因马克斯·维斯塔潘的严重撞车而被出示红旗。

    这位红牛车手在首圈结束时于倾斜的最后一弯打滑撞上护墙。

    比赛在湿滑的赛道上开始，尽管维斯塔潘和其他所有车手都选择了干胎。最后一弯仍然特别湿滑。

    当车阵抵达最后一弯时，维斯塔潘处于第七位。他似乎碰到了弯道内侧湿滑的路肩，这导致他的赛车打滑。当维斯塔潘试图纠正打滑时，他的赛车向左猛烈甩尾，并重重撞上外侧的护墙。

    红牛赛车随后停下，维斯塔潘在爬出赛车前向车队致歉。

    赛后发言时，维斯塔潘表示撞击没有可能发生的那么严重。“我想我仍然撞上了SAFER护栏，也许，有可能，”他说。“但我认为正面撞击实际上比侧面撞击要好一些，所以从这个意义上说，一切都还好。”

    他并不是唯一在湿滑的最后一弯失去对赛车控制的的车手。两辆奥迪车手在加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托于尼科·霍肯伯格面前打滑后几乎相撞。虽然博尔托莱托令人印象深刻地在没有撞墙的情况下重新控制了赛车，但霍肯伯格在避让队友时上演了小小的奇迹。两人都继续比赛。

    比赛于当地时间下午3:33重启。

    英文原文

    The Dutch Grand Prix has been red-flagged after a single lap due to a heavy crash for Max Verstappen.

    The Red Bull driver spun into the barrier at the banked final corner at the end of the first lap.

    The race began on a damp track, though Verstappen and all the other drivers opted for slick tyres. The final corner remained particularly wet.

    Verstappen was in seventh place as the field reached the final corner. He appeared to touch the damp kerb on the inside of the corner, which tipped his car into a spin. As Verstappen attempted to correct the spin his car snapped hard to the left and made heavy contact with the barrier on the outside.

    The Red Bull came to a rest shortly afterwards and Verstappen apologised to his team before climbing out.

    Speaking after the race, Verstappen said the impact was not as serious as it could have been. “I think I still hit the SAFER barrier, maybe, potentially,” he said. “But I think also more frontal impact is actually a bit better than a sideways one, so from that sense, all okay.”

    He wasn’t the only driver to lose control of his car in the damp final corner. The two Audi drivers almost collided after Gabriel Bortoleto spun in front of Nico Hulkenberg. While Bortoleto impressively regained control of his car without hitting the barrier, Hulkenberg performed a minor miracle in swerving to avoid his team mate. Both continued.

    The race restarted at 3:33pm local time.
