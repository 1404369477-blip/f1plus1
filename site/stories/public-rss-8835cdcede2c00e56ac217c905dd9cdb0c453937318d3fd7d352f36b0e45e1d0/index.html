    伊萨克·哈德贾表示，拉斯维加斯大奖赛奇特的赛车前翼故障“破坏了我的乐趣”

    伊萨克·哈德贾在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛的前半程与F1的领跑者激烈竞争，甚至一度逼近队友小红牛的马克斯·维斯塔潘。然而，在50圈比赛的半程时，一场奇特的机械故障让他的比赛变成了孤独的维修区限制。

    伊萨克·哈德贾表示，在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上发生的前翼故障让他“乐趣尽失”，因为他在颁奖台上的一圈比赛突然崩溃了

    伊萨克·哈德贾在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛的前半段与F1的领跑者展开了车轮战，甚至一度逼近红牛车队队友马克斯·维斯塔潘争夺领先。然而，在50圈比赛的半程，一场突如其来的机械故障让他的夜晚变成了孤独的损害限制战

    在发车线上第四位发车的22岁车手，在第一圈就超越了兰多·诺里斯，并在一场激烈的早阶段尾流战中强行超过了乔治·罗素，甚至通过车队无线电询问维斯塔潘能否"停止防守"，因为红牛车队以1-2的顺序行驶。

    在安全车因塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯与拉斐尔·卡马尔的碰撞而触发并在阿维德·林德布拉德的打滑后延长至第26圈后，比赛恢复时，哈达尔保持第三位。

    然而，在复赛后不久，哈达尔的赛车前翼一侧突然在直道上断裂，没有接触就散落向第5个弯道 - 部分碎片卡在他的小红牛22赛车下，红牛车队立即将他带回维修区更换前鼻翼。他重新回到第19位，但只追回到第14位，落后比赛获胜者维斯塔潘36.488秒。

    在媒体区被问及突然崩溃的原因时，哈达尔没有答案。

    “实际上，我不知道。只是前翼断了，就是这样，”哈达尔说。

    当一名记者评论说维加斯是他强劲速度没有得到回报的“最坏例子”时，哈达尔反驳道，“没错，这正是最好的例子，而不是最坏的。”

    “这甚至不是关于积分或领奖台。这只是在破坏我的乐趣，你知道的，”他继续说。“我来这里是为了和世界上最优秀的车手们比赛，结果我却排在最后，不得不独自比赛——这只是在破坏我驾驶的积极性；真是太烦人了。我想我永远不知道我可能得到什么样的结果，这真的很令人沮丧。”

    对于小红牛队来说，这是在剩下两次机会的情况下的一次昂贵打击。

    哈达尔零分完赛对红牛车队来说是最糟糕的时间。尽管维斯塔潘为胜利赢得了25分，但兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里的2-3名次为麦克拉伦赢得了33分，将红牛车队在车队积分榜上对第三名的领先优势从17分缩小到只有9分（440分对431分）。哈达尔还面临赛后 stewards 对其在红牛车队双堆栈维修区事故中涉及拉塞尔的早期安全车进站事件的调查。

    即便如此，哈达尔在车手积分榜上以122分排名第八——已经在摩纳哥、巴库和圣保罗赢得了2026年的三个领奖台——在卡塔尔和阿布扎比之前，他在小红牛22的速度中找到了一线希望。

    “当然，除了我打破赛车的那一刻之外，一切都是积极的，但这只是显示了赛车在最近几场比赛中的良好表现，本可以再次获得领奖台，”哈达尔说。“是的，这肯定是有益的。我们还有两场比赛要追赶它。”

    Isack Hadjar says freak Las Vegas front wing failure is “ruining my fun” after podium run collapses

    Isack Hadjar says a sudden Las Vegas GP front wing failure is "ruining my fun" after dropping from third to 14th

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Isack Hadjar spent the opening half of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix racing wheel-to-wheel with Formula 1’s frontrunners - even pressing Red Bull team-mate Max Verstappen for the lead. Halfway through the 50-lap race, a freak mechanical failure reduced the rest of his night to lonely damage limitation.

    Starting fourth on the grid, the 22-year-old cleared Lando Norris on Lap 1 and muscled past George Russell into second during a frantic early slipstream battle, going so far as to ask over team radio if Verstappen could "stop defending" as the Red Bulls ran 1–2.

    Hadjar held third when racing resumed on Lap 26 following the safety car triggered by Sergio Pérez’s collision with Rafael Câmara and extended by Arvid Lindblad’s spin.

    Moments into the restart, however, one side of Hadjar’s front wing abruptly broke apart on the straight without contact - failing just as the flaps closed, exiting 2026’s Straight Mode active aerodynamics. Debris scattered towards Turn 5, part of the assembly wedged beneath his RB22, and Red Bull immediately hauled him in for a replacement nose. He rejoined in 19th and clawed back only to 14th, 36.488 seconds behind race winner Verstappen.

    Asked in the media pen what caused the sudden collapse, Hadjar had no answers.

    "Actually, I don’t know. Just the front wing broke, and that’s it," Hadjar said.

    When a reporter remarked that Vegas was the "worst example" of his strong pace going unrewarded, Hadjar snapped back, "Yeah, exactly. It’s the best example, not the worst."

    "It’s not even about the points or the podium. It’s just ruining my fun, you know," he continued. "I’m here to race the best guys in the world, and I end up at the back and having to race alone - it’s just ruining my motivation to drive; it’s just so, so annoying. I guess I never know what kind of result I could have had, and that’s really frustrating."

    A costly blow for Red Bull with two shots left

    Hadjar’s zero-point finish landed at the worst possible time for Red Bull. Although Verstappen banked 25 points for victory, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri’s 2–3 haul delivered 33 points for McLaren, slicing Red Bull’s hold on third in the Constructors’ standings from 17 points to just nine (440 to 431). Hadjar also faced a post-race stewards’ look into an earlier safety car pit-entry incident involving Russell during Red Bull’s double-stack.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    Even so, sitting eighth in the Drivers’ Championship on 122 points - with three 2026 podiums already banked in Monaco, Baku, and São Paulo - Hadjar found a silver lining in the RB22’s speed ahead of Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

    "Of course everything was positive except the moment I broke the car, but it just shows how good the car is behaving the last couple of races and could have been another podium," Hadjar said. "Yeah, it’s positive for sure. We have two more races to chase it."
