    这名红牛车手因视频显示他推开一名车迷，必须前往墨西哥城大奖赛赛会干事处。

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    红牛车手伊萨克·哈贾尔因在墨西哥大奖赛周末被指行为不当而被传唤至赛会干事，F1车迷随后纷纷为他辩护。此前有视频拍到他推开一名试图与他自拍的车迷。

    周五，这段视频在社交媒体上迅速传播，画面似乎显示哈贾尔推开了一名粉丝，该粉丝正扑向这位车手，试图与他合影。在视频中，这名粉丝在哈贾尔走过围场时试图搂住他。车手用手臂将他挡开，随后在这名执着的粉丝继续纠缠时用力推了他一把。

    此事发生在周五的两节练习赛之间。第一节练习赛对哈贾尔来说很艰难，他因动力单元问题而受限，无法跑更多圈数。他最终排名第11快，但在第二节练习赛结束时，他的成绩提升到了第六位。

    虽然少数人批评哈贾尔对粉丝的反应，但也有不少人支持他。

    “所以，令人难以置信的是，当你对他礼貌、不侵犯他的空间、不触碰他时，他对粉丝其实真的很酷……能见到一位车手是一种荣幸，但你永远没有资格要求它，”一位用户在X上写道，该帖子获得了超过3000个赞。

    另一位补充道：“说实话，哈贾尔做得对，车迷们该收敛点了。个人空间是存在的，应该受到尊重。”

    一些人质疑为什么是哈贾尔面对赛事干事。

    一位车迷发帖说：“哈贾尔不该有麻烦，这个年纪更大的人不该碰他。围场是工作场所，不是表演场。车手是人，不是财产。”

    其他人同意他的看法，但觉得这种情况本可以处理得更好。

    “马克斯说得对，这简直像个动物园。人们根本不尊重车手的个人空间和边界。但这件事或许本可以处理得更好一些。安保人员应该更早介入，”另一位写道。

    并非所有人都如此宽容，一位用户问道：“我们是在假装这……没问题吗？”

    这起事件发生前几天，拉尔夫·舒马赫对德国天空体育表示，哈贾尔“需要收敛一点”，此前他在奥斯汀的无线电通讯中愤怒发言。

    哈贾尔定于当地时间周六上午10点15分面见赛事干事。违反行为不当规则的基础罚款为5000欧元，不过初犯也有可能仅受到警告。

    F1 fans rush to defend Isack Hadjar after controversial Mexican GP incident

    The Red Bull driver must see the Mexico City Grand Prix stewards after footage showed him pushing away a fan

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Formula 1 fans have jumped to the defence of Isack Hadjar after the Red Bull driver was summoned to the stewards for alleged misconduct during the Mexican Grand Prix race weekend. This comes after he was filmed pushing a fan as they tried to get a selfie with the driver.

    The video spread quickly on social media on Friday, appearing to show Hadjar pushing away a fan who was launching himself at the driver in an attempt to get a photo with him. In the footage, the fan attempts to put his arm around Hadjar as he walks through the paddock. The driver pushed him back at arm's length before giving him a forceful shove as the relentless fan continued.

    This took place between the two practice sessions on Friday. FP1 was a struggle for Hadjar, who suffered from power unit problems limiting his runtime. He finished 11th fastest, but jumped up the timesheets to sixth by the end of FP2.

    While a few have criticised Hadjar for his response to the fan, several have stood by him.

    “So, incredibly, when you’re polite to him and don’t infringe on his space and touch him, he’s actually really cool to fans… it’s a privilege to meet a driver but you’re never entitled to it,” one user wrote on X, in a post liked more than 3,000 times.

    Another added: “Good on Hadjar, honestly, fans should get a grip. Personal space is a thing and it should be respected.”

    Some questioned why Hadjar was the one facing the stewards.

    “Hadjar should not be in trouble, this OLDER man should not be touching him. Paddocks are workplaces, not show grounds. Drivers are people, not property,” one fan posted.

    Others agreed with him but felt the situation could have been handled better.

    “Max is correct, it’s like a zoo. People have no respect for drivers' personal space and boundaries. But it probably could have been handled a little better. Security should have stepped in sooner,” wrote another.

    Not everyone was so forgiving, with one user asking: “Are we pretending this is… okay?”

    The incident comes days after Ralf Schumacher told Sky Deutschland that Hadjar “needs to take it down a notch” following his angry radio messages in Austin.

    Hadjar is due to see the stewards at 10.15am local time on Saturday. A breach of the misconduct rule carries a base fine of €5,000, although a warning is also possible for a first offence.
