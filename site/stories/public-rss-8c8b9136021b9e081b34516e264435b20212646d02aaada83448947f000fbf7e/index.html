    法拉利提升F2车手成为2027赛季F1预备车手

    法拉利宣布将青训车手迪诺·贝加诺维奇提升为2027赛季的F1预备车手。贝加诺维奇曾是法拉利车手学院的一员，曾赢得意大利和瑞典卡丁车锦标赛冠军，并在WSK Euro Series中获得亚军。

    法拉利提升F2车手至F1预备车手角色

    迪诺·贝加诺维奇将离开F2，专注于与斯库德里亚的F1职责

    法拉利将青训车队车手迪诺·贝加诺维奇提升为2027赛季的F1预备车手。

    贝加诺维奇在过去七年一直是法拉利车手学院的一员，在此期间他赢得了意大利和瑞典卡丁车锦标赛冠军，并在WSK Euro Series中获得亚军。

    在他的年轻车手项目中，贝加诺维奇以单座赛车新手的身份在意大利F4中排名第三，在第二次尝试中赢得了欧洲区域锦标赛冠军，并在他的F3赛季中排名第六。目前，这位DAMS车手在2026年F2积分榜上位列第五——再次作为二年级生——在马德里赛道和巴库连续获胜。

    尽管在所有他全职参加的比赛中都赢得了比赛，贝加诺维奇还是被另一位法拉利培养的年轻车手，年轻的拉斐尔·卡马拉超越了。这位巴西人在F2锦标赛中作为新秀领跑，并与哈斯车队签订了2027年的F1合同，在那里他预计将与FDA校友奥利弗·贝尔曼搭档。

    因此，贝加诺维奇的青训生涯即将结束，他将在没有F1驾驶机会的情况下加入安东尼奥·吉奥维纳齐，进入法拉利的预备名单。这位22岁的瑞典-波斯尼亚人，在2025年和2026年参加了四次FP1练习，将承担额外的开发任务。

    尽管卡马拉和贝加诺维奇离开了F2，但明年F1的卫星系列赛仍将有一位法拉利培养的车手。图库卡·塔帕嫩在本赛季结束前排名第三，得益于在最后两轮中取得的三个领奖台位置——在此之前，他在2026年还没有取得过任何前三名的成绩。

    Ferrari promotes F2 racer to F1 reserve driver role

    Dino Beganovic will leave Formula 2 to focus on his F1 duties with the Scuderia

    Ferrari is promoting its junior driver Dino Beganovic to a Formula 1 reserve driver role for the 2027 season.

    Beganovic has been a member of the Ferrari Driver Academy for the past seven years, after he won the Italian and Swedish karting championships and took the runner-up spot in the WSK Euro Series.

    During his time in the young-driver programme, Beganovic finished third as a single-seater rookie in Italian F4, claimed the Formula Regional European Championship title at his second attempt and finished sixth in both his F3 seasons. The DAMS driver currently lies fifth in the 2026 F2 standings – again as a sophomore – with back-to-back victories in Madring and Baku.

    Despite winning races in all competitions he has raced in full-time, Beganovic has been surpassed by another Ferrari protege, the younger Rafael Camara. The Brazilian currently leads the F2 championship as a rookie and signed a 2027 F1 deal with Haas, where he is expected to partner fellow FDA graduate Oliver Bearman.

    As a consequence, Beganovic’s junior career is coming to an end without an F1 drive, and he will join Antonio Giovinazzi in Ferrari’s reserve roster. The 22-year-old Swedish-Bosnian, who has taken part in four FP1 sessions with the Scuderia across 2025 and 2026, will have additional development duties.

    Despite Camara and Beganovic moving on from F2, there will be a Ferrari protege in F1’s feeder series next year. Tuukka Taponen will graduate from F3 after finishing third this season, courtesy of a remarkable three podiums in the final two rounds – he hadn’t taken a single top-three finish prior to this in 2026.
