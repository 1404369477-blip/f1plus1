    阿塞拜疆大奖赛：乔治·拉塞尔在第二节练习赛中做出最快圈速，领先基米·安东内利，在巴库取得统治性开局

    梅赛德斯在阿塞拜疆周末开局强势，乔治·拉塞尔早早展现出飞驰状态；红牛的马克斯·维斯塔潘是唯一差距在一秒内的车手；周五巴库第三节练习赛于上午9点30分进行，排位赛下午1点开始，均在天空体育 F1直播

    阿塞拜疆大奖赛周四练习赛集锦。

    乔治·拉塞尔完成了令人印象深刻的练习赛双料最快，在阿塞拜疆大奖赛周末的统治性开局中超越了积分榜领先的队友基米·安东内利。

    梅赛德斯的拉塞尔延续了当天早些时候首节练习赛排名第一的状态，继续位居成绩榜首位，这一次在充满挑战的巴库街道赛道上以半秒优势完赛。

    安东内利在2026年车手总冠军争夺中以81分领先拉塞尔，但由于液压泄漏导致赛车在首节练习赛中途停下，他的周四并不顺利。

    这位意大利车手在更换引擎后，于第二节练习赛还剩30多分钟时重返赛道，一路升至成绩榜第二，最终落后拉塞尔0.552秒。

    “我们在FP1损失了不少圈数，错过半个FP2也绝不理想，尤其是在街道赛道上，”安东内利说。

    “总体来说，我觉得这是一次不错的恢复。我在车里感觉相当好。还有一些地方需要改进，但期待明天。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘驾驶升级版红牛赛车获得第三，为开局不错的一天收官，不过他落后领先者0.8秒。

    其他所有车手都落后超过一秒，包括法拉利和迈凯伦。夏尔·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿分列第四和第五，迈凯伦的兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里排名第六和第八。

    阿维德·林德布拉德在阿塞拜疆大奖赛P2中于狭窄的城堡减速弯撞上护栏。

    巴库会成为拉塞尔对安东内利的对决吗？

    在上一站马德里落后安东内利超过三场分站胜利的积分后，拉塞尔承认，尽管他是队友在积分上最接近的挑战者，但他并未处于现实的冠军争夺中。

    不过，拉塞尔周三表示，他感觉自己正处于能够再次赢得比赛的状态——自6月奥地利大奖赛以来他再未获胜——而他在巴库无疑为这一目标开了个好头，过去四年他在这里三次登上领奖台。

    “他开得很好，”天空体育 F1的雅克·维伦纽夫在第二节练习赛后说。

    基米·安东内利承认，在像阿塞拜疆这样的街道赛道上，P1早早退出并不“理想”，但他对第二节练习赛的恢复感到满意。

    “他拉开差距的地方都在那些需要赛车精准的狭窄路段。安东内利驾驶时转向不足更多，所以在那里要付出一些代价。

    “看起来这是两位（梅赛德斯）队友之间的直接较量。”

    Azerbaijan GP: George Russell fastest in second practice ahead of Kimi Antonelli in dominant start in Baku

    Mercedes make a commanding start to the Azerbaijan weekend, with George Russell in early flying form; Red Bull's Max Verstappen only driver within one second of Mercedes' pace; watch final practice in Baku on Friday at 9.30am, with Qualifying at 1pm, live on Sky Sports F1

    Highlights from Thursday's practice sessions at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    George Russell completed an impressive practice double as he outpaced title-leading team-mate Kimi Antonelli in a dominant start to the Azerbaijan Grand Prix weekend.

    ​​Mercedes' Russell built on the form that had seen him top the day's earlier first practice session to stay at the head of the timesheet, this time finishing with a half-second advantage around the challenging streets of Baku.

    Antonelli, who leads Russell by 81 points in the chase for 2026's Drivers' Championship, experienced a compromised Thursday owing to the hydraulic leak that stopped his car midway through the opening session.

    The Italian returned to the track with just over 30 minutes of the second session left, after an engine change, and worked his way up to second on the timesheet, eventually finishing 0.552s behind Russell.

    "We lost quite a few laps in FP1 and missing half of FP2 is never ideal, especially on a street circuit," said Antonelli.

    "Overall, I feel like it was a good recovery. I feel quite good in the car. A few things to work on but looking forward to tomorrow."

    Max Verstappen took third to end a promising opening day in an upgraded Red Bull car, although his deficit to the front was 0.8s.

    Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson was not pleased with Lewis Hamilton after a near miss during the second practice session at the Azerbaijan GP.

    Everyone else was over a second off the pace, including the Ferraris and McLarens. Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton were fourth and fifth respectively, with McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri sixth and eighth.

    Norris described McLaren's deficit to the front as "pretty pants".

    Pierre Gasly was seventh for Alpine with the returning Isack Hadjar ninth for Red Bull and Esteban Ocon, whose Haas seat appears set to go elsewhere for 2027, 10th quickest.

    Racing Bulls' Arvid Lindblad brought out Thursday's only red flag when he crashed in the narrow castle section of the challenging Baku lap.

    Arvid Lindblad hits the barriers at the tight castle chicane during P2 at the Azerbaijan GP.

    Is Baku poised to be Russell vs Antonelli?

    After falling in excess of three race wins behind Antonelli last time out in Madrid, Russell admitted he was not in a realistic fight for the championship despite being his team-mate's nearest challenger in the points.

    Russell, though, said on Wednesday that he felt he was in the form to win races again - something he has not done since the Austrian Grand Prix in June - and he has certainly made a positive start to that quest in Baku, where has finished on the podium in three of the past four years.

    "He's driving well," said Sky Sports F1's Jacques Villeneuve after the second session.

    Kimi Antonelli admits the early exit in P1 wasn't 'ideal' for a street circuit like Azerbaijan but was pleased with his recovery in the second practice session.

    "Where he makes the difference is all the narrow bits where the car needs to be precise. Antonelli drives with more understeer, so pays a bit of a price there.

    "It seems like a straight fight between the two [Mercedes] team-mates."
