    雅克·维伦纽夫表示，马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛中展现出了对乔治·拉塞尔和基米·安东内利的“驾驶技术”优势。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘顶住了梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔和基米·安东内利的压力，为红牛赢得了一场激动人心的拉斯维加斯大奖赛；本周末卡塔尔大奖赛将在天空体育直播，排位赛于周六18点进行，正赛于周日16点进行。

    安东内利已在梅赛德斯赛季初的统治性表现帮助下建立起显著优势，从而加冕2026年世界冠军，但维斯塔潘正以令人不安的状态为这一年收官，这位四届世界冠军赢下了最近六场比赛中的四场，并连续九次登上领奖台。

    Highlights from the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix

    Sky Sports F1 pundit and 1997 F1 world champion Villeneuve said after the race: "It was exciting. People will say there was a battery involved [in all the passes], but it doesn't matter.

    "We saw some race craft. Max was above and the two Mercedes drivers were lacking in that respect.

    "And what it is telling us is that unless next year Mercedes have a car that's superior, they can't win because there's something missing."

    George Russell tussles with Max Verstappen and Isack Hadajr at the start of the Las Vegas Grand Prix

    A combination of factors, most notably the extreme power of the slipstream effect, meant there were more than 350 overtakes during a breathless race at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.

    The lead changed hands 34 times, but every time Verstappen was overtaken, which on almost every occasion was by a Mercedes, the four-time world champion retook his position within a few corners.

    Russell locked up and hit a barrier as he tried to pass Antonelli for second in the closing stages, before the Italian suffered a loss of power at the start of the final lap after incorrectly lifting off the throttle as he was preparing to mount a final challenge to Verstappen.

    Max Verstappen had a choice few words for long-time foe George Russell after winning the Las Vegas Grand Prix

    Villeneuve was also impressed by the drives of seven-time world champion Lewis Hamilton and last year's title winner Lando Norris, who took advantage of the Mercedes errors to finish second ahead of his McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri.

    Hamilton could have also ended up on the podium had it not been for him spinning in a contentious collision with his Ferrari team-mate Charles Leclerc.

    "You could see that Lewis had good race craft as well as Lando," Villeneuve added.

    "We had a race where drivers could push like qualifying laps all the time and that allowed them to race. It was great fun."
