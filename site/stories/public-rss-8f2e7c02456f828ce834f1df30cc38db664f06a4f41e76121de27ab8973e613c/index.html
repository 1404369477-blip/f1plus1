    多梅尼卡利祝贺梅赛德斯夺2026车队总冠军

    F1首席执行官斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利祝贺梅赛德斯赢得2026年车队总冠军，并特别提到领队托托·沃尔夫跻身纪录册、与弗兰克·威廉姆斯爵士并列。

    F1首席执行官斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利在梅赛德斯赢得车队总冠军后发去诚挚祝贺

    F1首席执行官斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利祝贺梅赛德斯赢得2026年车队总冠军

    斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利，一级方程式集团首席执行官

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    一级方程式首席执行官斯特凡诺·多梅尼卡利祝贺梅赛德斯赢得2026年车队总冠军，并特别提到车队领队托托·沃尔夫与弗兰克·威廉姆斯爵士一同跻身纪录册。

    这支位于布拉克利的车队在巴西大奖赛上提前三站锁定冠军，当时乔治·拉塞尔和基米·安东内利分别获得第四和第五名，使目前排名第二的法拉利无法追赶。

    梅赛德斯在2026赛季占据统治地位，两位车手合计赢得12场大奖赛胜利和4场冲刺赛胜利。

    在一份声明中，多梅尼卡利希望将功劳归于整个团队。

    他写道：“我要向梅赛德斯一级方程式车队的每一位成员致以最热烈的祝贺，祝贺你们成为2026年车队世界冠军。登上F1的巅峰从来都不是一个人的成就。”

    “这是非凡天赋、不懈决心以及数百人共同信念的结果——无论是在赛道上还是在工厂里，所有人都被同一个雄心所凝聚。这支车队的每一位成员都应当为自己所取得的成就感到无比自豪。”

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯；安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯；托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    不过，他确实有话要对沃尔夫说——沃尔夫在2014年至2021年率队连续八次夺冠后，如今已带领梅赛德斯拿下九个冠军头衔。

    多梅尼卡利补充道：“这也是一个将在我们这项运动历史上占据特殊位置的时刻。”

    “凭借他的第九个车队总冠军，我的朋友托托·沃尔夫追平了伟大的弗兰克·威廉姆斯爵士——F1 真正的传奇人物之一。能在纪录册上与弗兰克并肩，是一项非凡的成就，也有力地证明了托托多年来展现出的领导力、远见以及对卓越的不懈追求。”

    梅赛德斯夺得车队总冠军的一周前，安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛上锁定了他首个车手总冠军，成为该系列赛历史上最年轻的总冠军。

    这位 F1 首席执行官继续说道：“在基米·安东内利成为史上最年轻车手世界冠军的同一个赛季赢得车队总冠军，让 2026 年对梅赛德斯和 F1 而言都真正令人难忘。”

    “这就是我们这项运动的全部意义所在：怀揣远大梦想，突破极限，并证明当优秀的人们为了一个共同的目标团结在一起时，一切皆有可能。梅赛德斯已经重返巅峰，并由此在F1的历史上写下了又一个非凡的篇章。”

    在还剩三站比赛的情况下，法拉利以512分位列车队积分榜第二，红牛以415分排名第三。

    F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali sends heartfelt message to Mercedes after constructors' title victory

    Formula 1 CEO Stefano Domenicali has congratulated Mercedes on its 2026 constructors' championship victory

    Stefano Domenicali, CEO of the Formula One Group

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Formula 1 CEO Stefano Domenicali has congratulated Mercedes on winning the 2026 constructors' championship while singling out team principal Toto Wolff for joining Sir Frank Williams in the record books.

    The Brackley outfit clinched the title with three races to spare at the Brazilian Grand Prix when George Russell and Kimi Antonelli finished fourth and fifth, leaving Ferrari, which currently sits second in the standings, with no way of catching them.

    Mercedes has dominated the 2026 season with 12 grand prix wins and four sprint victories between its two drivers.

    In a statement, Domenicali was keen to share the credit around the whole team.

    "My huge congratulations to everyone at the Mercedes Formula 1 Team on becoming 2026 constructors’ world champions," he wrote. "Reaching the summit of Formula 1 is never the achievement of one person alone.

    "It is the result of extraordinary talent, relentless determination and the shared belief of hundreds of people, at the circuit and back at the factory, all united by the same ambition. Every single member of this team should feel incredibly proud of what they have achieved."

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    He did, however, have a message for Wolff, who has now led Mercedes to nine titles after its run of eight in a row from 2014 to 2021.

    "This is also a moment that will have a special place in the history of our sport," Domenicali added.

    "With his ninth constructors’ title, my friend Toto Wolff draws level with the great Sir Frank Williams, one of Formula 1’s true legends. To stand alongside Frank in the record books is an extraordinary achievement and a powerful testament to Toto’s leadership, vision and relentless pursuit of excellence over so many years."

    Mercedes' constructors' title comes one week after Antonelli clinched his maiden drivers' championship at the Mexican Grand Prix, becoming the youngest champion in the history of the series.

    "To win the constructors’ championship in the same season that Kimi Antonelli became the youngest ever drivers’ world champion makes 2026 truly unforgettable for Mercedes and for Formula 1," the F1 CEO continued.

    "This is what our sport is all about: dreaming big, pushing beyond the limits, and proving what is possible when great people come together with one shared purpose. Mercedes have returned to the very top, and in doing so have written another remarkable chapter in the history of Formula 1."

    With three rounds remaining, Ferrari is second in the constructors' standings with 512 points, and Red Bull is third with 415.
