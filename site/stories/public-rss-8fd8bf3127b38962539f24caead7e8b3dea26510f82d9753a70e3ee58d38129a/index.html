    诺里斯：为了麦凯轮争夺第三名，投入了巨额资金

    随着梅赛德斯已经锁定2026年车队冠军，法拉利稳居第二，麦凯轮在拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔和阿布扎比的剩余目标是扭转17分差距，与红牛车队争夺第三名。

    诺里斯：“为了麦凯轮争夺第三名，投入了巨额资金”

    诺里斯强调，随着梅赛德斯已经锁定2026年车队冠军，法拉利稳居第二，麦凯轮在拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔和阿布扎比的剩余目标是扭转17分差距，与红牛车队争夺第三名。

    对于麦凯轮的粉丝来说，来自沃金克的最新消息，包括车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的赛事。

    红牛车队以415分进入拉斯维加斯大奖赛，而迈凯轮车队以398分落后。除了荣誉之外，在一级方程式商业奖金结构中，第三名和第四名之间的差距具有重大意义，《独立报》估计2026年的奖金为第三名122百万美元，而第四名为113百万美元——大约相差900万美元，这直接影响到年终员工奖金。

    在拉斯维加斯媒体日上发言时，兰多·诺里斯明确表示，随着赛季的结束，他在乎的是位于沃金工厂的人们。

    “你总是谈论积分、车队积分以及所有这些，这些都非常重要，但我认为你经常忘记额外的奖励，随之而来的奖金。并不是为我，而是为团队的所有成员，”诺里斯说。‘所以，从P4到P3的奖金数额非常可观。每天辛勤工作的所有团队成员，他们也会在年底获得奖金。所以，他们同样渴望得到这些奖金。’

    “他们可以用这些钱给孩子们买更好的礼物，或者搬进新房子，或者做其他事情，”他补充道。‘所以，这显然意义重大。这意义重大。’

    皮亚斯特里的处罚和红牛的势头

    在内华达州缩小17分的差距不会很顺利。在周末之前，麦凯轮车队确认奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里将因可靠性问题更换了本赛季第五台梅赛德斯内燃机而受到10个位置的处罚。

    "奥斯卡这周末将接受一个不错的处罚，所以他将从最后一排出发，或者希望不是最后一排，而是中游的位置，"诺里斯说。"显然，马克斯[维斯塔潘]做得很好，红牛也做得很好。伊萨克[哈德贾]上周末也做得很好。所以我们面临一场艰难的战斗，但我们准备好了。"

    即使诺里斯赢得了圣保罗大奖赛，红牛也凭借维斯塔潘获得第二名和哈德贾获得第三名，在内战中与麦凯轮车队在巴西打成了33分的平手。

    兰多·诺里斯，梅赛德斯，奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂，梅赛德斯

    诺里斯还与梅赛德斯车队的表现有着个人激励。他在车手积分榜上以246分排名第六，落后第三名的乔治·拉塞尔（262分）16分，刘易斯·汉密尔顿（261分）和查尔斯·勒克莱尔（249分）夹在他们中间。要进入前三名，需要参加12月12日在上海举行的FIA颁奖典礼——诺里斯开玩笑说，他曾经与工程师们讨论过这次长途飞行。

    “我的目标是如果我能获得车手第三名，就飞往中国，”诺里斯说。“这也许意味着我们本赛季有一个好的结尾，他们也能得到一笔不错的奖金。所以，这就是我的目标。”

    Lando Norris: “A hell of a lot of money” riding on McLaren’s fight for third

    Lando Norris highlights the millions in prize money and staff bonuses at stake as McLaren chases Red Bull for third

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    With Mercedes having already sealed the 2026 constructors' championship and Ferrari sitting clear in second, McLaren's remaining focus across Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi comes down to one battle: overturning a 17-point deficit to Red Bull Racing for third place.

    Red Bull enters the Las Vegas Grand Prix on 415 points to McLaren's 398. Beyond bragging rights, the gap between third and fourth in Formula 1's commercial payout structure carries serious weight, with The Independent estimating 2026 prize money at $122million for third compared to $113million for fourth - roughly a $9million swing that directly feeds year-end employee bonuses.

    Speaking during Las Vegas media day, Lando Norris made clear that the people back at the Woking factory are front of mind as the season wraps up.

    "You always talk about points and Constructor points and all these things, and it's very important, but I think you often do forget the extras, the bonuses that come with it. And not for me, but for all the members of the team," Norris said. "So, it's a hell of a lot of money from P4 to P3. And all the team members that work every day, they also get their bonuses at the end of the year. So, they want it just as much as anyone."

    "They can buy their kids cooler presents or whatever it might be and move into their new house or whatever it is," he added. "So, it obviously means a lot. It means a huge amount."

    Piastri's penalty and Red Bull's momentum

    Closing that 17-point margin will not be straightforward in Nevada. McLaren confirmed ahead of the weekend that Oscar Piastri will serve a 10-place grid penalty after switching to his fifth Mercedes internal combustion engine of the season due to a reliability issue.

    "Oscar's taking a nice penalty this weekend, so he's coming from the back of the grid or hopefully not even at the back, the middle of the pack," Norris said. "Obviously, Max [Verstappen] is doing a good job, and Red Bull are doing a good job. Isack [Hadjar] did a good job last weekend. So we've got a tough fight, but we're up for it."

    Even with Norris winning the São Paulo Grand Prix, Red Bull matched McLaren's 33-point haul in Brazil thanks to Verstappen finishing second and Hadjar claiming third.

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Oscar Piastri, McLaren

    Norris also has a personal incentive tied to McLaren's surge. Sitting sixth in the drivers' standings on 246 points, he trails third-placed George Russell (262) by 16 points, with Lewis Hamilton (261) and Charles Leclerc (249) wedged between them. Finishing in the top three requires attending the FIA Awards in Shanghai on 12 December - a long-haul flight Norris joked he had debated with his engineers.

    "My aim is to fly to China at the end of the season if I can finish P3 in the drivers," Norris said. "That will hopefully mean we've had a good end to the season, and they also get a good bonus. So, that's my goal."
