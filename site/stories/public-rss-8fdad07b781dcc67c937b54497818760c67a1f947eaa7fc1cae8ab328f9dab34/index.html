    托托·沃尔夫在新加坡大奖赛上佩戴两块手表的原因

    梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫在新加坡大奖赛上佩戴了两块豪华IWC手表，以便设置不同的时区。

    这不是沃尔夫第一次佩戴两块手表，得益于梅赛德斯赞助商IWC Schaffhausen的支持，这肯定不会是最后一次。

    梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫在新加坡大奖赛上吸引了众多目光，他佩戴的两块豪华IWC手表成为了焦点。

    梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫在新加坡大奖赛上佩戴了两块手表，这并不是他第一次这样做。

    沃尔夫戴了两块手表，这样他就可以将它们设置为不同的时区。一块设置为新加坡时间，另一块设置为欧洲时间。这样，他就可以在知道确切时间的情况下，应对紧张夜间比赛的压力，无论是他在布拉克莱的家人所在的地方，还是可能是在摩纳哥，他的家人也住在那里。

    沃尔夫已经这样做了很长时间。他与IWC的合作关系让他在本届新加坡大奖赛周末佩戴了IWC大飞行员系列防震表XPL Toto Wolff版。这块44毫米的计时器由Ceratanium制成，全球仅限量100块，旨在承受甚至最强烈的奥地利办公桌撞击——沃尔夫之前就因这一点而闻名。

    第二块手表看起来像是IWC的新款飞行员系列计时码表41 梅赛德斯-AMG Petronas一级方程式车队版，这是品牌为纪念今年新加坡大奖赛而制作的。

    它的紫色日出表盘灵感来源于新加坡的国花万代兰，搭配Petronas绿色的数字和标记、黑色指针以及相匹配的紫色橡胶表带。乔治·拉塞尔和基米·安东内利今年早些时候协助了该表的发布。

    它不仅仅是一种车队色彩的搭配。这款41毫米的5级钛金属表壳搭载了IWC自产的69385自动计时码表机芯，46小时的动力储备通过蓝宝石底盖清晰可见，防水深度达到100米。然而，与沃尔夫的100件大型飞行员表不同，它并非限量版。它是IWC永久收藏的一部分，售价为7700瑞士法郎。

    Why Toto Wolff wore two watches at the Singapore Grand Prix

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff turned heads by wearing two luxury IWC watches at the Singapore Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Toto Wolff arrived at the Singapore Grand Prix last weekend with not one, but two watches strapped to his left wrist. This isn't the first time he's worn two watches, and, thanks to Mercedes sponsor IWC Schaffhausen, it certainly won't be the last.

    Wolff wore two watches so he could set them to different time zones. One was set to Singapore time, and the other to European time. This way, he could juggle the pressures of the tight night race while knowing exactly what time it was back in Brackley, or potentially Monaco, where his family live.

    Wolff has been doing this for a long time. His partnership with IWC led him to don the Big Pilot's Watch Shock Absorber XPL Toto Wolff edition throughout the Singapore Grand Prix weekend. This 44 mm timepiece, built from Ceratanium and restricted to just 100 units globally, is made to withstand even the strongest of Austrian desk slams - something Wolff has previously been known for.

    The second watch appeared to be IWC’s new Pilot’s Watch Chronograph 41 Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team, which the brand created as a tribute to this year’s Singapore Grand Prix.

    Its purple sunburst dial is inspired by the Vanda Miss Joaquim orchid, Singapore’s national flower, and is paired with Petronas-green numerals and markers, black hands and a matching purple rubber strap. George Russell and Kimi Antonelli assisted with the launch earlier this year.

    It’s more than a team-colours tie-in. The 41mm grade 5 titanium case holds IWC’s in-house calibre 69,385 automatic chronograph, with a 46-hour power reserve visible through a sapphire cashback, and it’s water-resistant to 100m. Unlike Wolff’s 100-piece Big Pilot, however, it isn’t a limited edition. It’s part of IWC’s permanent collection and costs CHF 7,700.
