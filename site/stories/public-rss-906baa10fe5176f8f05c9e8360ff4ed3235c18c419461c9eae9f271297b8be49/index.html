    安东内利解释2027赛季为何不用1号

    F1世界冠军安东内利表示，他下赛季不会使用冠军专属的1号，而是继续沿用12号，因为12号对他有特殊意义，且他过去使用1号时运气不佳。

    F1世界冠军基米·安东内利解释了他为何选择下赛季不在自己的赛车上使用冠军专属的1号。

    这位意大利车手在墨西哥大奖赛创造历史，以20岁的年龄成为史上最年轻的世界冠军。

    这一成就让他有机会在下赛季选择使用车上的1号，正如2025年冠军兰多·诺里斯本赛季所做的那样。

    然而，安东内利表示他将沿用目前的12号——因为这个号码对他有个人意义，而且他过去使用1号时运气并不好。

    安东内利选择12号作为他的F1比赛号码，以纪念他的英雄艾尔顿·塞纳，塞纳在1985至1987年效力路特斯期间以及1988年随迈凯伦首次夺得世界冠军的那个赛季都使用了这个号码。

    当被问及是否会选择使用1号时，安东内利表示：“我会继续用12号，因为显然这对我来说是一个非常特别的号码，而且这也是让我保持脚踏实地、保持谦逊的一种方式。

    “我觉得保留12号让我有了一个新的起点，可以在此基础上重新构建一切，并努力提升。

    “而且说实话，我非常迷信，1号从来都不是我的幸运号码。每次我用它，结果都极其糟糕。所以这是继续用12号的又一个理由。”

    安东内利在整个单座赛车生涯中一直使用12号，除了2024年参加F2时使用的是4号。

    这个号码伴随他在2022年赢得意大利F4冠军，并在次年赢得FRECA冠军。他提到自己用1号时运气不佳，这几乎肯定与他在卡丁车时期的经历有关。

    具体来说，这可能与2020年在波尔蒂芒湿地举行的世界卡丁车锦标赛对决有关——当时使用1号车的安东内利与玛雅·韦格相撞（韦格被甩出卡丁车但未受伤），安东内利左腿和脚部骨折。

    在本周末前往巴西大奖赛之际，安东内利还透露了使用塞纳致敬头盔的计划——以帮助庆祝这位三届冠军1991年在英特拉格斯赛道的著名胜利。

    安东内利表示：“距离他当年在主场比赛中卡在第六挡夺冠，已经过去35年了。”

    “这将是一顶向巴西、向艾尔顿致敬的头盔，我认为这是我制作过的最好的头盔之一。所以希望你们大家，希望你们会喜欢它。”

    “这将是一个非常特别的周末。我非常期待，因为来自巴西的支持总体上令人难以置信，不仅是今年，从去年开始就已经如此。”

    英文原文

    Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli has explained why he is choosing not to run with the title winner's #1 on his car next season.

    The Italian made history at the Mexican Grand Prix as he became the youngest driver to ever win the crown at the age of 20.

    That feat gives him the opportunity if he chooses to run with the #1 on his car next season, as 2025 champion Lando Norris has done this season.

    However, Antonelli has said that he will stick with his current #12 - as that number has personal significance for him, plus he has not had the best of luck in the past when he has run with #1.

    Antonelli chose #12 as his F1 race number to honour his hero Ayrton Senna, who raced with it during his Lotus years from 1985-87 as well as in his first title-winning campaign with McLaren in 1988.

    Asked whether he would take up the option of running #1, Antonelli said: "I'll stay with #12 because, obviously, it's a very special number for me, but also it's a way for me to stay grounded, to stay humble.

    "I feel like by keeping #12 it allows me to have a new point where to start and build on everything again and try to improve.

    "And also, to be honest, I'm very superstitious, and number one has never been a good number for me. Whenever I put it, it went extremely badly. So one more reason to stay with #12."

    Antonelli has used #12 throughout his single seater racing career, apart from in Formula 2 in 2024 when he raced with #4.

    It is the number that he carried as he won crowns in Italian Formula 4 in 2022 and FRECA the following season. His reference to not having much luck with number one almost certainly relates to his time in karting.

    Specifically, it may tie in to the 2020 World Karting Championship showdown in the wet at Portimao - in which Antonelli, running the #1, collided with Maya Weug (who was thrown from her kart but was unhurt) and broke his left leg and foot.

    As he heads to the Brazilian GP this weekend, Antonelli has also revealed plans to run with a Senna tribute helmet - to help celebrate the three-time champion's famous 1991 success at Interlagos.

    "It's going to be 35 years since he won [stuck] in sixth gear, his home race," said Antonelli.

    "It's going to be a tribute helmet for Brazil, for Ayrton, and I think it's one of my best helmets that I've made. So hopefully you guys, everyone, hopefully you will like it.

    "It's going to be a very special weekend. I'm very much looking forward to it because the support from Brazil in general has been incredible, not only this year but already from last year."
