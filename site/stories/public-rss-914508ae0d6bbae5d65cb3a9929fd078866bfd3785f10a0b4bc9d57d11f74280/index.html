    勒克莱尔反驳阿隆索：F1全场驾驶水平非常高

    勒克莱尔拒绝接受阿隆索关于车队后段车手驾驶水平偏低的说法，强调整个F1赛场的水平“非常非常高”。

    勒克莱尔在阿隆索发表尖锐批评后为阿尔本辩护

    在阿隆索近期发表评论后，勒克莱尔为整个F1赛场的驾驶水平进行了辩护

    勒克莱尔、法拉利、阿尔本、威廉姆斯

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更远地方的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    法拉利F1车手夏尔·勒克莱尔驳斥了费尔南多·阿隆索关于发车区后段车手驾驶水平低下的说法，坚称整个赛场的水平"非常、非常高"。

    在上周末的美国大奖赛上与威廉姆斯车手亚历克斯·阿尔本交锋后，阿隆索批评了发车区后三分之一的车手，并在墨西哥城接受媒体采访时坚持了自己的言论。

    "我认为发车区后三分之一车手的质量非常低，就像我们今天在亚历克斯身上看到的那样，"阿隆索在美洲赛道首圈与阿尔本发生碰撞后说道。

    "所以我认为我们需要改进赛车，从稍微靠前的位置起步。"

    在墨西哥车手新闻发布会上被问及发车区水平时，勒克莱尔驳斥了这位西班牙人的言论。

    “我认为水平非常高。我觉得非常非常高。就像我之前说的，发车区的大多数人，我们都是在卡丁车时期一起成长起来的，”勒克莱尔说。“亚历克斯当时就在那里，实际上是我仰视的车手。他总是比我高出一两个级别，但他非常出色。”

    目前在车手积分榜上排名第五的勒克莱尔补充说，前场与后场之间的差距归根结底在于赛车。

    “今天，我确信发车区的水平超级超级高，无论是在后场、中游还是前场。唯一改变的东西显然是我们驾驶的赛车，但标准非常高，”这位摩纳哥人说。

    与勒克莱尔一同出席新闻发布会的阿尔本也不同意阿隆索的评价。

    “我非常尊重费尔南多，小时候我一直很仰慕他，显然从很多方面来说听到那些评论并不好受，”这位威廉姆斯车手说。“你正在和你仰慕的人比赛，但与此同时，我不同意。就这么简单。

    “我认为整个发车区的水平都很强。我认为10年、15年前，发车区的后半部分并不是现在这样。我们不再有付费车手了。”

    Charles Leclerc defends Alex Albon after Fernando Alonso's scathing criticism

    Charles Leclerc has defended the standard of driving across the Formula 1 grid after Fernando Alonso's recent comments

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Alexander Albon, Williams

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari Formula 1 driver Charles Leclerc has rejected Fernando Alonso's claim that the standard of driving at the back of the grid is low, insisting the level is "very, very high" across the field.

    Alonso criticised the lower third of the grid after his battle with Williams driver Alex Albon at last weekend's United States Grand Prix and stood by his comments while speaking to the media in Mexico City.

    "I think the quality of the drivers in the lower third of the grid is very low, as we saw today with Alex," Alonso said after a collision with Albon on the opening lap at the Circuit of the Americas.

    "So I think we need to improve the car and start a little bit higher up."

    Asked about the standard of the grid during the drivers' press conference in Mexico, Leclerc dismissed the Spaniard's comments.

    "I think it’s very high. I think it’s very, very high. Just like I was saying earlier, most of the grid, we grew up together in karting," Leclerc said. "Alex was up there and was actually a driver I was looking up to. He was always one or two categories above me, but he was exceptional."

    Leclerc, who is currently fifth in the drivers' standings, added that the difference between the front and the back of the field comes down to machinery.

    "Today, I’m sure that the level of the grid is super, super high, whether it’s in the back, in the middle or in the front. The only things that change is obviously the race car that we are driving, but the standards are very high," the Monegasque said.

    Albon, who joined Leclerc in the press conference, also disagreed with Alonso's assessment.

    "I have huge respect for Fernando, I looked up to him a lot growing up as a kid, and obviously it’s not nice to hear those comments in many ways," the Williams driver said. "You’re racing someone that you look up to, but at the same time, I don’t agree. It’s as simple as that.

    "I think the level is strong up and down the grid. I think 10, 15 years ago, the back half of the grid wasn’t the way it is now. We don’t have paid drivers anymore."
