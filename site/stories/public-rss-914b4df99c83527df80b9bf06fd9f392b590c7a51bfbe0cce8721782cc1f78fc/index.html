    红牛任命措洛夫为2027年双车队储备车手

    红牛宣布尼古拉·措洛夫将在2027年担任两支F1车队的官方储备车手，并为其安排专项培养计划。措洛夫此前无缘小红牛2027年正赛车手席位，劳森和林德布拉德均获续约。

    红牛宣布尼古拉·措洛夫为2027年两支F1车队的官方储备车手，并为其制定了一项“旨在确保他完全准备好以最高水平参加F1比赛”的计划。

    由于利亚姆·劳森和阿维德·林德布拉德均获续约至明年，措洛夫无缘2027年小红牛的正赛车手席位。

    但红牛明确表示，措洛夫仍是F1席位的下一位候选车手；在确认劳森和林德布拉德2027年席位的同一天，红牛也确认措洛夫将出任储备车手。

    措洛夫目前正与2027年哈斯F1车手拉斐尔·卡马拉以及亚历克斯·邓恩争夺F2年度冠军，已取得六场胜利，赛季还剩卡塔尔和阿布扎比两站。

    措洛夫在去年的第三个F3赛季中获得亚军，仅次于卡马拉。

    红牛公告写道：“在新角色中，尼古拉将参与一项广泛的培养计划，将模拟器与研发工作，同通过自由练习赛、旧车测试（TPC）、部分测试环节以及轮胎测试所获得的有价值的赛道经验结合起来。”

    “除了作为储备车手的职责外，该计划旨在确保他为以最高水平参加F1比赛做好充分准备。”

    措洛夫表示，他对红牛给予的机会感到“无比高兴和感激”。

    “我的目标很明确：我想成为一名F1车手，”他说。

    “有机会在明年为下一步做准备，这令人无比兴奋。我相信，在车队的支持与经验帮助下，我将获得尽可能最好的准备，确保当时机到来时，我已完全准备好迈上F1发车格，踏出下一步。”

    措洛夫在墨西哥站的FP1首秀中给小红牛留下了深刻印象，当时他的表现与2026年新秀林德布拉德相比毫不逊色。

    “尼科拉是一位极具天赋的年轻车手，过去几年他的成长和表现给我们留下了极为深刻的印象，”红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯说道。

    “在F3为冠军而战，如今在首个完整赛季就挑战F2年度冠军，这充分说明了他的能力和决心。

    “同样令我们印象深刻的是他的成熟、他的比赛方式，以及他不断学习和进步的意愿。

    “我们对尼科拉（Nikola）的潜力抱有极大信心，认为他是一位在F1拥有非常光明未来的车手。

    “这一任命体现了我们对他的信任，以及对他持续发展的承诺。我们在2027年的重点将是给予他所需的经验、工具和支持，让他为下赛季征战F1做好准备。”

    英文原文

    Red Bull has named Nikola Tsolov the official reserve driver of both of its Formula 1 teams in 2027 with a programme “designed to ensure he is fully prepared to race in F1 at the highest level”.

    Tsolov missed out on a 2027 Racing Bulls seat as Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad were both handed extensions into next year.

    But Red Bull has made clear Tsolov is still very much the next driver in line for an F1 seat, confirming on the same day Lawson and Lindblad were confirmed for 2027 that Tsolov would be made reserve.

    Tsolov is currently fighting for the Formula 2 title with 2027 Haas F1 driver Rafael Camara and Alex Dunne, having taken six race wins with two rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi remaining.

    Tsolov finished runner-up to Camara in his third Formula 3 campaign last year.

    Red Bull's announcement read: “In his new role, Nikola will undertake an extensive development programme, combining simulator and development work with valuable track experience through Free Practice sessions, Testing of Previous Cars (TPC), selected test sessions and tyre testing.

    “Alongside his responsibilities as a reserve driver, the programme is designed to ensure he is fully prepared to race in F1 at the highest level.”

    Tsolov said he was “incredibly happy and grateful” to Red Bull for the opportunity.

    “My ambition is clear: I want to become an F1 driver,” he said.

    “Having the opportunity to spend next year preparing for that next step is incredibly exciting. I’m confident that with the team’s support and experience, I’ll have the best possible preparation to make sure I’m fully ready to take the next step onto the F1 grid when the time comes.”

    Tsolov impressed Racing Bulls with his FP1 debut in Mexico where he compared favourably to 2026 rookie Lindblad.

    “Nikola is an exceptionally talented young driver, and we have been hugely impressed by his development and performances over the past few years,” Red Bull team boss Laurent Mekies said.

    “To fight for the championship in F3 and now challenge for the F2 title in his first full season says a great deal about his ability and determination.

    “What has impressed us just as much is his maturity, his approach to racing and his willingness to keep learning and improving.

    “We have enormous belief in Nikola’s potential and see him as a driver with a very exciting future in F1.

    “This appointment reflects our confidence in him and our commitment to his continued development. Our focus in 2027 will be on giving him the experience, tools and support he needs to be ready to race in F1 next.”
