    F1荷兰大奖赛因维斯塔潘撞车被红旗中断

    F1荷兰大奖赛在首圈结束后因主场车手马克斯·维斯塔潘的严重事故被红旗中断，维斯塔潘在赞德福特赛道最后一弯压到内侧白线后失控撞墙，随后报告自己无恙并向车队致歉。

    F1荷兰大奖赛因维斯塔潘撞车被红旗中断

    马克斯·维斯塔潘最后一场F1主场比赛在首圈后结束，红牛赛车遭遇了巨大事故

    F1荷兰大奖赛在首圈后因主场英雄马克斯·维斯塔潘的巨大事故而被红旗中断。

    在潮湿赛道的首圈末段，维斯塔潘在赞德福特赛道最后一弯——通往主直道的倾斜全油门卢因迪克弯——内侧压到白线，导致红牛赛车失控。

    维斯塔潘径直撞上外侧护墙，随后赛车横穿赛道，停在赛道内侧。

    这位荷兰车手很快报告自己无恙，并为提前结束比赛向车队致歉。

    维斯塔潘幸运地没有被其他赛车波及，阿尔派的皮埃尔·加斯利采取了避让动作，与此同时奥迪的尼科·霍肯伯格也险些遭遇巨大事故，因为他的队友加布里埃尔·博托莱托在他前方打转。

    红旗期间所有车手聚集在维修区等待重启。他们必须在进入维修区前穿过一片碎片区，但车队被允许在红旗期间为重启更换轮胎。预计重启时间为当地时间15:33。

    事发时维斯塔潘在令人失望的排位赛后排名第七。杆位得主兰多·诺里斯在混乱的起步阶段保持领先，积分榜领跑者基米·安东内利超越乔治·拉塞尔升至第二。

    法拉利的夏尔·勒克莱尔上升两个位置，占据第四，领先于第二辆迈凯伦的奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里和队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿。

    对维斯塔潘而言，这起事故为他六年的主场作战画上了不幸的句号，他曾在2021、2022和2023年赢得荷兰大奖赛恢复举办后的前三届冠军。在组织者决定不再与F1管理层续约后，该赛事将从赛历中消失。

    本周早些时候，这位四届世界冠军宣布他已将与红牛的合同延长至2030赛季末。

    F1 Dutch GP red-flagged after Verstappen crash

    Max Verstappen's final Formula 1 home race ended after one lap as the Red Bull suffered a colossal accident

    Formula 1's Dutch Grand Prix has been red-flagged after one lap following an enormous accident for home hero Max Verstappen.

    At the end of lap 1 on a damp circuit, Verstappen lost control of his Red Bull as he tagged the white line on the inside of Zandvoort's final turn - the banked, flat out Luyendyk corner leading onto the main straight.

    Verstappen veered head on into the outside wall, before his car crossed the track and came to a standstill on the inside.

    The Dutchman soon reported he was OK and apologised to his team for ending his race early.

    Verstappen was fortunate not to be collected by any other cars, with Alpine's Pierre Gasly taking avoiding action, while in the background Audi's Nico Hulkenberg also narrowly avoided a huge accident as his team-mate Gabriel Bortoleto spun in front of him.

    Under the red flag all drivers gathered in the pitlane awaiting the restart. They were having to navigate a field of debris before pit entry, but teams are allowed to change tyres under a red flag ahead of the restart. That restart is expected at 15:33 local time.

    At the time Verstappen was running seventh after a disappointing qualifying session. Polesitter Lando Norris retained his lead during a chaotic start sequence, with championship leader Kimi Antonelli passing George Russell for second.

    Ferrari's Charles Leclerc gaining two positions to hold down fourth ahead of the second McLaren of Oscar Piastri and team-mate Lewis Hamilton.

    For Verstappen, the accident marks an unfortunate end to a six-year spell racing on home soil, winning the first three editions of the Dutch Grand prix revival in 2021, 2022 and 2023. The race will disappear from the calendar after organisers decided not to pursue a contract renewal with F1 management.

    Earlier in the week, the four-time world champion announced he had extended his Red Bull contract until the end of the 2030 season.
