    2027年海尼根F1赛季票得主揭晓

    捷克粉丝卢卡斯·查拉巴拉在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上被揭晓为2027年海尼根F1赛季票的获胜者。查拉巴拉在首届获奖者布兰登·伯吉斯的见证下获得了奖品，随后四届F1冠军得主马克斯·维斯塔潘也加入了颁奖仪式。

    捷克粉丝卢卡斯·查拉巴拉在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上被揭晓为2027年海尼根F1赛季票的获胜者。

    查拉巴拉在首届获奖者布兰登·伯吉斯的见证下获得了奖品，随后四届F1冠军得主马克斯·维斯塔潘也加入了颁奖仪式。

    获胜者将有机会与一位嘉宾一起参加下赛季的每一场比赛，并获得前排看台座位，以及由F1合作伙伴海尼根提供的旅行和住宿。

    查拉巴拉在粉丝投票中击败了其他五位决赛选手获胜。他说：“我从小就一直是F1的粉丝，但从未有机会亲自体验一场比赛，所以赢得这样的奖品对我来说是一个梦想成真。”

    “知道有多少粉丝在网上为我投票，感觉非常不真实，我想确保通过带他们一起经历这段不可思议的旅程，分享尽可能多的体验来回报他们。”

    “二十四场比赛，不同的文化，无数难忘的时刻——我仍然难以置信这竟然真的发生了！”

    当前赛季球票持有者伯吉斯表示，他在“非常兴奋”地传递奖项时说，他“创造了无数难忘的记忆，这些记忆我将终身难忘”。

    维斯塔潘补充说：“喜力x F1赛季球票是我见过的最酷的机会，对于一个F1粉丝来说。”

    “有机会体验整个赛季的每一场大奖赛是非常罕见的，卢卡斯将能够看到比仅仅赛车本身更多的东西。”

    “他还将有机会探索城市，体验每个地方的文化，这是我自己在从一场比赛到下一场比赛的旅行中并不总是有机会做的事情。”

    “这是体验一级方程式赛车，无论是在赛道上还是赛道外，的一种真正特别的方式。”

    英文原文

    Czech fan Lukas Chalabala has been revealed as the winner of the Heineken x F1 Season Ticket for 2027 at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Chalabala was presented with his prize by inaugural winner Brandon Burgess, and the pair were then surprised by four-time F1 champion Max Verstappen who gatecrashed the presentation, having also presented Burgess with his prize in late-2025.

    The winner of the Heineken x F1 Season Ticket gets to attend every race on the calendar for the following season with a guest, getting access to top grandstand seats, plus travel and accommodation all covered by F1 partner Heineken.

    Chalabala, who won a fan vote against five other finalists, said: “I’ve been a fan of F1 for as long as I can remember, but I’ve never had the chance to experience a race in person, so to win something like this is a dream come true.

    “Knowing how many fans voted for me online feels surreal, and I want to make sure I give something back by taking them along on this incredible journey and sharing as much of the experience as I can.

    “Twenty-four races, different cultures and countless unforgettable moments - I still can’t quite believe it’s actually happening!”

    Current season ticket holder Burgess said he was “beyond excited” to pass the award on “having made countless memories all of which I’ll remember for the rest of my life”.

    Verstappen added: “The Heineken x F1 Season Ticket is the coolest opportunity I’ve seen for an F1 fan.

    “Having the chance to experience every grand prix across the season is incredibly rare, and Lukas will get to see so much more than just the racing.

    “He’ll also get to explore the cities and experience the culture of each place, which is something I don’t always get the chance to do myself when travelling from race to race.

    “It’s a truly special way to experience Formula 1, both on and off the track.”
