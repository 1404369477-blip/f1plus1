    2026年卡塔尔F1大奖赛首练情况

    新任F1世界冠军基米·安东内利在2026年卡塔尔大奖赛的首练中轻松领跑，尽管他在最软的轮胎配方上未能完成一个干净的快速圈。

    按照惯例，中等Pirelli轮胎在FP1的早期练习中被优先选择，安东内利的成绩从1m25.0s提升至1m24.0s，最终达到1m23.127s，这出人意料地成为了本节练习的最快圈速，尽管赛道有所改善，且所有人都使用了软胎。

    安东内利尝试了自己在软胎上的圈速，但之后因为过度转向而不得不放弃这一圈。他在本节练习结束时领先队友乔治·拉塞尔0.507秒，而拉塞尔在软胎上的最佳圈速比中等轮胎慢了0.25秒。

    伊萨克·哈吉亚继续他近期出色的表现，为小红牛车队贡献了第三快的圈速，领先于迈凯轮车队的兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，据天空体育报道，他们在本节练习中都使用了较旧的底板规格。他们之间的差距仅为0.01秒，在本节练习结束时落后于领先者超过八十分之一秒。

    他们之间只相差0.01秒，并且在这次练习赛中落后了超过八十分之一秒的速度。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在最后圈速中遭遇了交通堵塞，落后了0.916秒，排在第六位。在这场练习赛中，他还遇到了更多的红牛车队无线电问题，并抱怨说需要额外的10次点击来调整前翼角度，以克服车辆的慢性转向不足。

    法拉利车队在第七和第八位，落后了节奏，查尔斯·勒克莱尔比安东内利的基准慢了1.256秒，比队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿快了0.102秒。

    阿维德·林德布拉德的小红牛车队是中游最快的车队，比汉密尔顿的法拉利慢了十分之一秒，比皮埃尔·加斯利的阿尔卑斯快了近三十分之一秒，完成了前十名。

    英文原文

    New Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli comfortably set the pace in first practice for the 2026 Qatar Grand Prix, despite failing to complete a clean quick lap on the softest tyre compound.

    As per usual, medium Pirelli tyres were favoured for the early running in FP1 and Antonelli worked his way down from a 1m25.0s to a 1m24.0s and finally to a 1m23.127s best, which surprisingly stood as the session’s fastest time, regardless of track improvement and everyone using the soft tyre.

    Antonelli attempted a soft tyre run of his own but had to abandon the lap after an oversteer moment. He ended the session 0.507s up on Mercedes team-mate George Russell, who found only 0.25s on the softs compared to his best lap on the medium tyre.

    Isack Hadjar continued his impressive recent form by setting the third fastest time for Red Bull, ahead of McLaren duo Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri, who according to Sky F1 both ran with an older specification of floor in this session.

    They were separated by just 0.01s and ended the session more than eight tenths off the pace.

    Max Verstappen rounded out the top six, 0.916s off, after encountering traffic through the final corner on his best lap. He also suffered more Red Bull radio problems in this session and complained of needing an impossible 10 more clicks of front wing angle to counter the car’s chronic understeer.

    The Ferraris were well off the pace in seventh and eighth, Charles Leclerc 1.256s slower than Antonelli's benchmark and 0.102s clear of team-mate Lewis Hamilton.

    Arvid Lindblad’s Racing Bulls was the quickest of the midfield cars in ninth, a tenth down on Hamilton’s Ferrari and almost three tenths up on Pierre Gasly’s Alpine, which rounded out the top 10.
