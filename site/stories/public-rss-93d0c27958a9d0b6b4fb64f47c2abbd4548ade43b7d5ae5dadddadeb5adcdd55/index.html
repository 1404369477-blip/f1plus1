    伊萨克·哈德贾被墨西哥大奖赛 stewards 召集，涉嫌 F1 赛道不当行为

    红牛车手伊萨克·哈德贾因在墨西哥大奖赛赛道发生的不当行为被召唤至 stewards。他在 FP1 赛段后，因被粉丝拍照而两次被愤怒的哈德贾推开。

    伊萨克·哈德贾被墨西哥大奖赛 stewards 召集，涉嫌 F1 赛道不当行为。

    红牛车手伊萨克·哈德贾在墨西哥大奖赛赛道发生的不当行为被召唤至 stewards。

    哈德贾在 FP1 赛段后，因被粉丝拍照而两次被愤怒的哈德贾推开。

    事件发生在 Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez 赛道。

    “国际汽联国际体育章程第12.2.1.l条款的违规行为——不当行为，与今天在一级方程式维修区发生的事件有关，” stewards报告读道。

    这发生在周五两场练习赛之间，哈贾尔在首场一个小时的比赛中感到失望，仅以11名完赛，比法拉利领跑者勒克莱尔慢1.417秒。

    哈贾尔受到动力单元问题的困扰，这导致他只完成了21圈，这是第三低的总数，仅次于阿斯顿·马丁的Jak Crawford和奥迪的Freddie Slater。

    不过，FP2的情况略有改善，因为哈贾尔以第六名完赛，比再次领跑的勒克莱尔慢0.410秒，队友维斯塔潘获得第五名。

    Isack Hadjar summoned to Mexico GP stewards for "misconduct" in F1 paddock

    The Red Bull driver shoved away a fan who was attempting to take a selfie

    Isack Hadjar has been summoned to the Formula 1 Mexico Grand Prix stewards for an alleged incident of “misconduct” in the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez paddock.

    The Red Bull driver had been approached by a fan attempting to take a picture, but he was shoved away twice by a furious Hadjar after his disrupted FP1 session.

    “Alleged breach of Article 12.2.1.l of the FIA International Sporting Code - Misconduct, relating to an incident in the Formula 1 paddock today,” read the stewards report.

    It happened in between both Friday practice sessions with Hadjar suffering disappointment in the opening one-hour outing, finishing just 11th and 1.417s off Ferrari pacesetter Charles Leclerc.

    Hadjar was plagued by power unit problems, which caused him to complete just 21 laps, and that was the third-lowest total ahead of Jak Crawford (Aston Martin) and Freddie Slater (Audi).

    The situation did slightly improve in FP2 though, as Hadjar finished sixth and 0.410s behind Leclerc, who was again on top, with team-mate Max Verstappen claiming fifth.
