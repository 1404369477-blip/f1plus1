    Topps展示诺里斯可口可乐战袍补丁卡

    Topps公布了一张极为稀有的兰多·诺里斯签名卡，为2024 Dynasty系列F1卡片，嵌有两块比赛服布料，其中一块取自其迈凯伦赛车服袖口上的可口可乐标志。

    Topps展示评级为Gem Mint 10的兰多·诺里斯可口可乐战袍补丁卡

    这张2024 Dynasty签名卡在诺里斯签名下方嵌有两块比赛服布料

    致迈凯伦车迷。来自沃金的木瓜色新闻，包括车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个比赛周末的最新动态。

    Topps公布了一张极为稀有的兰多·诺里斯卡片：一张2024 Dynasty系列F1签名卡，嵌有两块比赛服布料，其中一块取自其迈凯伦赛车服袖口上的可口可乐标志。

    Topps Racing 账号周五在 Instagram 上发布了一段这张卡片的短视频，配文是“这张兰多·诺里斯补丁签名卡简直疯了！”

    这张卡片来自 Single Driver Dual Relic Autograph 系列。诺里斯的签名下方有两个红色布料窗口。左边那块布料上能看到白色可口可乐手写体标志的一部分。

    它已由美国公司 PSA 完成评级，并被封存在塑料评级盒中，获得了该公司的最高等级 Gem Mint 10。

    车迷们立刻发现了这个标志。“他们从哪儿弄到他的赛车服的？”帖子下一位评论者问道。“天哪，这太酷了，”另一个人评论道，还有人补充说：“哈哈哈可口可乐想回西班牙看看因为可口可乐瓶子引发的混乱。可口可乐，来当 LN4 Fusion 的赞助商吧！”

    在新建的马德里赛道举行的西班牙大奖赛之后，诺里斯在冷却室里的一幕让他迅速走红。当时似乎有人要求诺里斯放下他正在喝的可口可乐，但他拒绝了。“我刚跑完一场比赛，所以我有权喝我想喝的东西。非常感谢，”诺里斯当时说道。

    这一幕在网上引发了轩然大波，因为可口可乐的竞争对手百事公司是F1的官方合作伙伴。

    在得克萨斯州奥斯汀接受媒体采访时，诺里斯被进一步追问此事。“没有，之后没人再跟我说过什么。我觉得比赛结束后我们应该被允许喝任何我们想喝的东西，尤其是当你在车里待了两个小时的时候。

    “这已经是一些最炎热的比赛了，赛车也很难驾驶，所以我觉得我应该被允许喝我想喝的东西。”

    他补充道：“从车队角度来说，车队可能比我受到更多责备。因此我们用来装饮料的杯架很小，所以没有品牌标识，但我可以喝任何我能喝的东西。只是他们存在品牌方面的问题。”

    “但这不是我需要考虑、或者应该考虑的事情，所以这肯定是一些已经在其他人之间解决掉的事情。我想事情就是这样。只是电视和品牌之类的事情，但我可以喝任何我喜欢的东西。”

    Dynasty 是 Topps 生产的最昂贵的 F1 产品。每盒装有一张封装卡，总是带有纪念物的签名卡，且每张都编号为 10 张或更少。当 2024 版于 2025 年 2 月发售时，一盒售价 1,099.99 美元。

    这张诺里斯卡片的基础版本限量 10 张，上面还有更稀有的红色、黑色和金色版本。

    Topps reveals Gem Mint 10 Lando Norris card with Coca-Cola suit patch

    The signed 2024 Dynasty card features two race suit relics beneath Norris’s autograph

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Topps has shared one of its rarest Lando Norris cards: a signed 2024 Dynasty Formula 1 card with two pieces of race suit, one of them cut from the Coca-Cola logo on the sleeve of his McLaren race suit.

    The Topps Racing account posted a short video of the card on Instagram on Friday, with the caption "This Lando Norris patch auto goes CRAZY!"

    The card comes from the Single Driver Dual Relic Autograph set. Two windows of red fabric sit below Norris's signature. The left-hand piece shows part of the white Coca-Cola script.

    It has been graded by the American company PSA, which sealed it in a plastic case with its highest grade, Gem Mint 10.

    Fans spotted the logo straight away. "Where did they get his race suit?" asked one of the commenters under the post. "Omg this is so dope," someone else commented, while another added: "Hahaha Coca-Cola wants to come back to Spain and see the chaos that erupted over the Coca-Cola bottle. Coca-Cola, become a sponsor of LN4 Fusion!"

    Norris went viral after the Spanish Grand Prix at the new Madring Circuit during a moment in the cooldown room. Norris was seemingly asked to put down the Coca-Cola he was drinking, but he refused. “I just did a race, so I’m allowed to drink what I would like. Thank you so much,” Norris said at the time.

    The moment caused chaos online as PepsiCo, a rival of Coca-Cola, is an official partner of F1.

    Norris was pressed further on the situation while speaking to the media in Austin, Texas. “No, no one’s ever said anything to me since. I feel like we should be allowed to drink whatever we want after a race, especially when you’re in it for two hours.

    "It’s been some of the hottest races, cars are also difficult to drive, so I feel like I should be allowed to drink what I want.”

    He added: “From a team, the team might have been told off more than I was. And therefore we have little holders to put our drinks in, so it’s unbranded, but I’m allowed to drink whatever I can. It’s just the branding issues that they have.

    "But this is not something I need to be thinking of, or I should be thinking of, so it’s some stuff I’m sure that was cleared up between other people. That’s simply all it was I think. It’s just TV and branding and all of those things, but I can drink whatever I like.”

    Dynasty is the most expensive F1 product Topps makes. Each box holds a single encased card, always an autograph with a relic, and every one is numbered to 10 copies or fewer. When the 2024 edition went on sale in February 2025, a box cost $1,099.99.

    The base version of this Norris card is limited to 10 copies, with rarer red, black and gold versions on top.
