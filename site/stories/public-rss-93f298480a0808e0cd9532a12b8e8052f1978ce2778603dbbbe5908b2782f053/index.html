    汉密尔顿坚决否认要求更换法拉利员工

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿在社交媒体上坚决否认他曾要求法拉利更换车队员工，回应了意大利媒体的相关报道。

    法拉利F1车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿在社交媒体上坚称，他“从未要求更换我团队中的任何人”，此前意大利媒体报道称他曾提出这一要求。

    意大利报纸《晚邮报》声称，汉密尔顿曾要求对法拉利的场边人员进行调整，并报道称他与车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔的关系减弱，而瓦塞尔最初在汉密尔顿签约过程中发挥了关键作用。

    但周四，汉密尔顿在Instagram快拍中发帖，驳斥了这些谣言。

    “我看到了一些流传的报道，我想在这里澄清。我从未要求更换我团队中的任何人，”汉密尔顿写道。

    “与我共事的每个人每天都在努力工作，他们的奉献精神推动我们前进。”

    “我总是试图赋予人们力量，提升他们，让他们成为最好的自己，或者至少看到自己最好的一面。”

    “我可能并不总能做到这一点，但这是我的意图，也是我在这里要做的。”

    “我们共同努力，这将继续下去。”

    此前，法拉利经历了一段艰难时期，自7月斯帕站以来再未登上大奖赛领奖台。

    最糟糕的是蒙扎主场噩梦般的比赛，当时沮丧的汉密尔顿恳请法拉利齐心协力纠正操作失误和执行不力的问题。

    他在马德里实际上已承认争冠无望，因刹车问题退赛后，他落后积分榜领先者基米·安东内利超过100分。

    英文原文

    Ferrari Formula 1 driver Lewis Hamilton has taken to social media to insist he’s “never asked for anyone on my team to be replaced” after a report in the Italian media suggested he had.

    Italian newspaper Corriere della Sera claimed Hamilton had asked for changes to be made to trackside personnel at Ferrari and reported a weakened relationship with team boss Fred Vasseur, who had been instrumental to Hamilton signing in the first place.

    But on Thursday, Hamilton posted an Instagram story that rubbished the rumours.

    “I’ve seen some of the stories that have gone out and I want to clear it up here. I’ve never asked for anyone on my team to be replaced,” Hamilton wrote.

    “Everyone I work with works hard every day, and their dedication is what moves us forward.

    “I always try to empower and lift people to be the best versions of themselves or at least see the best in themselves.

    “I may not always achieve that but it’s my intention and that’s what I’m here to do.

    “We all work together and that will continue.”

    It follows a tricky patch for Ferrari, which hasn’t had a grand prix podium since Spa in July.

    It peaked with a nightmare home race at Monza, where an exasperated Hamilton pleaded for Ferrari to work together to correct operational errors and poor execution.

    He effectively conceded the title in Madrid as he fell over 100 points adrift of points leader Kimi Antonelli, having retired with a brake issue.
