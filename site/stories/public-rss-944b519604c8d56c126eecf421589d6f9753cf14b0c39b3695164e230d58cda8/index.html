    迈凯伦CEO布朗：2026新规削弱皮亚斯特里高速弯优势

    迈凯伦CEO扎克·布朗认为，皮亚斯特里在2026赛季的挣扎源于高速弯本是他的强项，但新规则下的赛车需要不同的驾驶技术。2026年F1动力单元更偏电气化，电池管理在比赛中扮演关键角色。

    2026年F1新规如何削弱了奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里的“真正强项”

    这位迈凯伦车手在2025年非常接近成为F1世界冠军，但2026年对他而言艰难得多。

    迈凯伦CEO扎克·布朗认为，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在2026年F1赛季的挣扎源于高速弯本是他的强项——但新赛车需要不同的驾驶技术。

    F1今年引入了最新的规则大改，动力单元电气化程度更高，使得电池管理在比赛中颇具争议地扮演了关键角色。

    这意味着赛车不能像以前那样总是全速行驶，有时需要放慢速度，为单圈中另一段高速弯路段保留电量，这就是一个典型例子。

    皮亚斯特里就是受影响的车手之一，他目前排名第七，落后迈凯伦队友兰多·诺里斯94分，本赛季未尝胜绩，仅两次登上领奖台。

    这与上赛季形成对比，当时这位澳大利亚车手大部分时间占据主导，直到赛季末崩盘，最终以九场胜利排名第三，仅落后世界冠军诺里斯13分。

    “我认为今年车手阵容有些变化，”布朗在本周末拉斯维加斯大奖赛上说，“有些人适应得比其他人更快。”

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，迈凯伦，扎克·布朗，迈凯伦

    梅赛德斯是最好的例子，去年乔治·拉塞尔以169分领先队友基米·安东内利，而安东内利在2026年扭转了这一差距，成为世界冠军。

    “这些赛车确实需要不同的驾驶技术，”布朗补充道。“我记得在澳大利亚的第一场比赛时，我们讨论过减速以提速。

    “从历史上看，这始终是关于尽可能快地踩下油门，尽可能长时间地保持，尽可能晚地刹车。

    “这些赛车需要不同的驾驶技术。看看奥斯卡，他在高速弯里非常勇敢，这是他的真正优势，而现在你有时会在无线电里听到我们牺牲其中一些弯道。”

    “所以从圈速本身来看，他一直非常非常接近兰多。我认为他已经掌握了它，所以我们给了奥斯卡很多支持。

    “去年他距离成为世界冠军只差一个领奖台，而在所有这些运动中，你都会进入势头。所以他处于一个很好的位置。

    “我们很兴奋，想要强势结束这个赛季，并为27年做好准备。”

    距离皮亚斯特里上一次夺冠——2025年荷兰大奖赛——已经过去了将近15个月，而在他于迈阿密获得第三名之后，目前正处于连续16场比赛无缘领奖台的尴尬纪录之中，与此同时诺里斯表现抢眼。

    尽管这位2025年世界冠军在积分榜上排名第六，但他仍然赢下了三场大奖赛，并在两人的排位赛直接交锋中以18比8领先皮亚斯特里（包括冲刺赛），而赛季还剩三轮。

    因此，皮亚斯特里仍有时间终结自己无缘领奖台和分站冠军的荒期，同时迈凯伦正试图在车队积分榜上超越红牛升至第三，目前仅落后17分。

    当被问及自己在2026年剩余赛程中的目标时，皮亚斯特里表示：“我认为就是努力继续学习，这是最重要的事情。”

    “这不是一个很令人兴奋的答案；我明白，但只是努力继续加深理解。我认为今年我们从赛季初试图弄清楚我需要做哪些不同的事情，到后来对需要呈现的样子有了相当好的理解。

    “但真正去实现它一直很有挑战性。在过去的几场比赛中，最近五六场，确实有更多令人鼓舞的迹象。

    “它并不是线性的，在这些比赛中有些表现更好，有些表现更差。

    “但我认为巴西站，即使结果没有体现出来，那可能是我很长一段时间以来最好的比赛节奏。

    “所以，我认为对我来说，就是要努力在此基础上继续进步。显然，作为一支车队，我们想在车队锦标赛中击败红牛。

    “对我自己来说，我很想重返领奖台，或者努力争取拿下一场胜利。但最重要的是尽可能为明年做好铺垫，希望我们能在接下来这几周里实现这一点。”

    How the F1 2026 regulations neutered Oscar Piastri's "real strength"

    The McLaren driver was very close to becoming F1 world champion in 2025, yet 2026 has been much more of a struggle

    McLaren boss Zak Brown reckons Oscar Piastri’s struggles in the 2026 Formula 1 season stem from high-speed corners being his strength - but the new cars require a different technique.

    F1 introduced its latest regulation overhaul this year with power units being more electrical, causing battery management to contentiously play a key role in the racing.

    It means cars can’t always go as flat out like before, instead sometimes going slower to preserve battery for another part of a lap with high-speed corners a prime example of this.

    Piastri is one driver to have suffered as he sits seventh in the standings and 94 points behind McLaren team-mate Lando Norris, enduring a winless campaign with only two podiums.

    That is opposed to last season which the Australian largely dominated, until a late collapse saw him finish third with nine wins and sit just 13 points behind world champion Norris.

    “I think we've seen the driver rosters get mixed up a bit this year,” said Brown at this weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix. “Some have adapted quicker than others.”

    Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Zak Brown, McLaren

    Mercedes is the best example of this, as last year George Russell finished 169 points ahead of team-mate Kimi Antonelli, who overturned that gap to become world champion in 2026.

    “These cars definitely take a different technique to drive,” added Brown. “I remember in the first race in Australia talking about slowing down to speed up.

    “Historically it's always been about putting your foot down as quickly as possible, keeping it down as long as possible, braking as late as possible.

    “These cars require a different driving technique. When you look at Oscar, he's quite brave through the high-speed corners, which is a real strength of his, and now you'll hear us on the radio sometimes compromising some of those corners.

    “So he's been very, very close to Lando when you look at the lap times themselves. I think he's gotten on top of it, so we got a lot of support for Oscar.

    “He was one podium away from being a world champion last year and in all these sports, you get into momentum. So he's in a good place.

    “We're excited and want to finish the season strong and getting ready for '27.”

    It has now been close to 15 months since Piastri’s last victory - 2025 Dutch GP - while he is on a current 16-race podiumless streak after taking third in Miami, amid Norris shining.

    Although the 2025 champion is sixth in the standings, he has still won three grands prix and leads Piastri 18-8 in their qualifying head-to-head (including sprints) with three rounds left.

    So there’s still time for Piastri to end his podium and win drought as McLaren attempts to beat Red Bull to third in the constructors’ standings, sitting only 17 points behind.

    “I think just trying to keep learning, that's the biggest thing,” said Piastri, when asked about his aim for the rest of 2026.

    “It's not a very exciting answer; I get it, but just trying to keep building understanding. I think this year we've gone from in the earlier part of the season trying to get our head around what I need to do differently, to then having a pretty good understanding of what it needs to look like.

    “But then actually going and achieving it's been challenging. There's definitely been more encouraging signs in the last few races, the last five or six.

    “It's not been linear, there's been some better races, there's been some worse ones among those.

    “But I think Brazil, even if the result didn't show, it was probably the best race pace that I've had for a long time.

    “So, I think for me just trying to keep building on that. Obviously as a team we want to beat Red Bull in the constructors' championship.

    “For myself I'd love to get back on the podium or try and snag a win. But the most important thing is to try and set up next year as best as I can and hopefully, we can achieve that in these next few weeks.”
