    劳森2026赛季表现提升的原因

    小红牛车队负责人艾伦·珀曼在采访中解释了利亚姆·劳森2026赛季表现提升的原因，劳森已于上周确认留队征战2027赛季，击退了青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫的竞争。

    速度、信任、可控的侵略性——究竟是什么成就了利亚姆·劳森挽救职业生涯的2026赛季F1征程

    在一次内容广泛的采访中，艾伦·珀曼解释了是什么让利亚姆·劳森在2026赛季表现提升，从而赢得了一份新的F1合同

    利亚姆·劳森状态的提升，让小红牛车队无法忽视他征战2027赛季F1的价值。但一如既往，这一表现回升的背后并不只有单一因素。

    就在上周，这位24岁的车手才最终确认下赛季继续留在红牛的卫星车队，击退了目前正在争夺F2年度冠军的青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫的竞争。

    在接受Motorsport.com采访时，小红牛车队领队艾伦·珀曼解释了为什么选择劳森不必违背车队长期以来的青训车手培养宗旨。

    不太明显的是，劳森如何在如此巨大的压力下找回最佳状态，因为他在帮助小红牛车队创纪录地获得制造商锦标赛第五名方面发挥了关键作用，今年为车队贡献了114分中的89分。

    劳森在20场大奖赛中15次获得积分，包括目前连续五场进入前九名，这一切都发生在最关键、他的席位压力最大的时候。

    “我认为他处理得非常好，”珀曼告诉Motorsport.com。“他能够屏蔽那些噪音。但公平地说，我一直在这整个过程中支持他。”

    “我没有跟他说过‘别担心，席位是你的’之类的话，因为在决定做出之前，决定就还没有做出。但他在车队内部一直得到了支持。”

    利亚姆·劳森，小红牛车队，艾伦·珀曼，小红牛车队

    当被问及劳森进步的根本原因时，珀曼表示，这是学会信任身边团队、磨砺比赛技巧以及收敛他标志性侵略性的综合结果。

    “我认为他在各种方面都有进步，”他补充道，“但我认为他的比赛技巧，尤其是第一圈第一个弯，现在非常出色。他很少陷入麻烦，而且经常能提升位置。

    他的比赛速度也非常非常好。离开赛车后，他给出的反馈非常清晰，而且带着一种自信。

    这种状态会以正确的方式滚雪球：他取得好成绩，离开赛车时更自信一些，这又让他在接下来的周末充满信心，从而再次表现出色。

    他带来了惊喜。这是他自己做到的，但当我觉得他需要倾听时，他也听了，并且信任我、车队和他的工程师。这是一种非常健康的比赛方式。

    尽管赛季中期他曾作为受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔的替补，为红牛出战了三场比赛，但状态的回升依然发生了。

    尽管这段经历让劳森在2025年与主力车队短暂而艰难的两场比赛后（随后被角田裕毅取代）得到了一些了结，但佩尔曼透露，劳森其实更愿意留在原处，继续他在英意车队（小红牛）积累的势头。

    “说实话，如果让他选，他并不想去那里，”佩尔曼说。

    “这不是针对红牛。不是他不想为他们开车，而是他想继续和我们一起做好正在做的事。我们几乎每天都在讨论伊萨克的手腕，我觉得他回来时是带着一些释然的。

    “我们在巴库并不很有竞争力，尽管最终取得了很好的成绩，但之后一切就突然顺畅起来了。”

    Speed, trust, controlled aggression - What really led to Liam Lawson's career-saving F1 2026

    In a wide-ranging interview, Alan Permane has explained what led to Liam Lawson's improved 2026 performances that earned him a new Formula 1 contract

    Liam Lawson's improved form made him too good for Racing Bulls to ignore for the 2027 Formula 1 campaign. But, as always, there's more than just a single factor behind that upturn in performances.

    It was only last week that the 24-year-old was finally confirmed as staying with Red Bull's satellite team for next season, fending off a challenge by junior driver Nikola Tsolov who is currently vying for the Formula 2 crown.

    Speaking to Motorsport.com, Racing Bulls team principal Alan Permane explained why a choice for Lawson doesn't have to go against the team's long-running mandate of training young drivers.

    What is less obvious is how Lawson managed to find his best form under such immense pressure, as he has played an instrumental role in earning Racing Bulls a record-setting fifth place in the constructors' championship, contributing 89 of the team's 114 points this year.

    Lawson has taken 15 points finishes from 20 grands prix, including a current streak of five top nine results, this coming when it mattered the most and pressure on his seat was at its highest.

    "I think he's handled himself excellently," Permane told Motorsport.com. "He's just been able to shut that noise out. But in fairness, I've been supporting him throughout all this.

    “I haven't been telling him: 'Don't worry, you've got the drive' or anything, because until the decisions are made, the decisions are not made. But he has had support from within the team."

    Liam Lawson, Racing Bulls, Alan Permane, Racing Bulls

    When asked about the underlying reasons for Lawson's improvements, Permane said it was a combination of learning to trust the team around him, sharpening his race craft and toning down his trademark aggression.

    "There's all sorts of areas that I believe he's improved in,” he added, “but I think his race craft, certainly his turn one, lap one is immense now. He rarely gets in trouble and often makes places up.

    "His race pace has been very, very good too. Out of the car, he is giving very clear feedback and just has a level of confidence.

    "It snowballs in the right way: he has a good result, he's a bit more confident out of the car, and that gives him the confidence going into the following weekend, where he does another great job.

    "He's been a revelation. He's done this himself, but he's also listened when I felt he needed to listen and put his trust in me, the team, and his engineers. And that's a really healthy way to go racing."

    That upturn in form came despite a three-race mid-season run with Red Bull as a substitute for the injured Isack Hadjar.

    Although it gave Lawson some closure after a brutal two-race spell with the main team in 2025 before being replaced by Yuki Tsunoda, Permane revealed Lawson would have rather stayed where he was to continue the momentum he was on with the Anglo-Italian team.

    "If I'm being really frank with you, he didn't want to go there if you'd given him the choice," Permane said.

    "That's nothing against Red Bull. It's not that he didn't want to drive for them, but he wanted to carry on the good work he was doing with us. We were having almost daily conversations about Isack's wrist, and I think there was some relief coming back.

    "We weren't very competitive in Baku despite ending up having a great result, but after that, things just clicked very well."
