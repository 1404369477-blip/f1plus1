    拉塞尔淡化维斯塔潘2024年"撞墙"威胁言论

    在重返卢赛尔国际赛道的卡塔尔大奖赛前，乔治·拉塞尔对马克斯·维斯塔潘2024年那句著名的"撞墙"威胁态度明显缓和，并澄清了相关卡丁车传闻。

    乔治·拉塞尔淡化对马克斯·维斯塔潘2024年那句著名的"撞墙"威胁的态度

    回到卡塔尔，乔治·拉塞尔缓和了对马克斯·维斯塔潘2024年"撞墙"威胁的看法，并澄清了卡丁车相关的传闻

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    两年前在卢赛尔国际赛道，一次例行的排位赛冷却圈让围场里暗涌的紧张情绪演变为公开对抗。如今，回到他与马克斯·维斯塔潘矛盾爆发的同一场地——而且就在拉斯维加斯站刚刚火上浇油几天之后——乔治·拉塞尔对那句曾促使他称这位荷兰车手为霸凌者的威胁，给出了明显更为平静的评价。

    在周日卡塔尔大奖赛之前，拉塞尔独家接受了天空体育F1的马丁·布伦德尔的采访，回顾了2024年12月那场主导了F1阿布扎比赛季收官战的余波。当时，拉塞尔震惊了整个围场，他声称维斯塔潘在卡塔尔曾威胁要“故意冲着我撞过来，把我的脑袋撞到墙上”——这一指控促使拉塞尔宣称“多年来人们一直受到马克斯的霸凌”，并坚持认为“总得有人站出来对抗一个自认为凌驾于法律之上的人”。

    维斯塔潘当时强烈否认了这一说法，他对荷兰媒体表示拉塞尔“又在夸大其词”，称他是“背后捅刀的人”和“就是个失败者”，并在离开时嘲讽了干事听证会：“也许下次，我会带上纸巾。”

    两个赛季之后，拉塞尔对那番所谓威胁的激动情绪已经大幅降温。

    “他就是这么办事的，”拉塞尔告诉布伦德尔，“我认识他够久了，所以我知道，那更多是一句玩笑话，我觉得。”

    承担 2024 年闭门会议中所发生之事的责任

    拉塞尔在赛事干事会议室内并未软化自己的说法。2024 年卡塔尔站排位赛中，维斯塔潘以 0.055 秒的优势力压拉塞尔夺得临时杆位，随后赛事干事认定这位红牛车手在冷却圈中不必要地慢速行驶并阻挡了梅赛德斯车手，因此给予他罕见的正赛发车位后退一位处罚。

    维斯塔潘在周日的正赛中依然在起步时击败拉塞尔并最终夺冠，而拉塞尔仅获第四，但他离场时怒火中烧，称自己已经对这位对手“失去了全部尊重”，并指责对方“试图如此狠毒地算计别人”。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    当布伦德尔问及拉塞尔在那场听证会上的姿态时，拉塞尔丝毫没有试图装无辜。

    “嗯，我并不是想放过他，”拉塞尔承认道。“我的意思是，我进去时并没有说，‘那没关系。’”

    这番坦率的承认恰好契合了这对竞争对手之间更广泛的模式——自拉塞尔驾驶梅赛德斯具备争胜能力的赛车以来，这种竞争每年都会爆发一次：

    2023年巴库站：阿塞拜疆冲刺赛首圈2号弯的接触损坏了维斯塔潘的底板，并在封闭停车区引发了激烈的对峙。

    2024年卡塔尔与阿布扎比：排位赛的罚退发车点燃了维斯塔潘“失去所有尊重”的炮轰，以及拉塞尔“撞墙”的反击。

    2025年巴塞罗那：在比赛尾声安全车重启时切弯，并被红牛告知交还第四名后，维斯塔潘与拉塞尔发生严重碰撞——因此被罚10秒，名次跌至第10，并被记3个罚分，累计达到11分，距离禁赛一场仅差1分，随后维斯塔潘承认这次接触“本不该发生”。

    2026年拉斯维加斯：练习赛中的接触与新的讥讽，随后维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大道赛道夺冠，而拉塞尔滑落至第九。

    揭穿青训卡丁车出身的故事

    连续四个赛季都有冲突记录在案，车迷们长期以来一直以为，两人之间的不和可以追溯到青少年卡丁车时期的竞争——就像维斯塔潘早年与夏尔·勒克莱尔或埃斯特班·奥康的较量那样。拉塞尔彻底否定了这种说法。

    “我们在卡丁车时期其实并没有那么多次碰撞，”拉塞尔解释道。“他比我大一岁，所以我们在2011年只同场竞技过一次，之后显然他就快速推进了自己的职业生涯。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队；乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    记录也支持他的说法。尽管两人都在2011年赢得了KF3青少年组的重要头衔——维斯塔潘拿下WSK欧洲系列赛冠军，拉塞尔则夺得欧洲KF3锦标赛冠军——但在维斯塔潘迅速攀升单座赛车阶梯之后，两人的道路很快分开了，直到拉塞尔加入梅赛德斯，两人才真正成为赛道上的常客。

    无论拉塞尔在镜头外提出怎样的休战，卢赛尔的泛光灯下两人很少能长时间相安无事。梅赛德斯在周五首节练习中率先确立节奏，安东内利领先拉塞尔，包揽前二，而维斯塔潘在周五晚间第二节练习前仅列第六——这为周日当地时间19:00大奖赛绿灯亮起时又一次头排之争埋下伏笔。

    George Russell softens stance on Max Verstappen’s infamous 2024 “in the wall” threat

    Back in Qatar, George Russell softens his view on Max Verstappen's 2024 "in the wall" threat and debunks karting myths

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Two years ago at the Losail International Circuit, a routine qualifying cooldown lap turned a simmering paddock tension into open warfare. Now, returning to the exact venue where his feud with Max Verstappen detonated - and days after Las Vegas poured fresh gasoline on it - George Russell is offering a strikingly calmer verdict on the threat that once pushed him to brand the Dutchman a bully.

    Speaking exclusively to Martin Brundle for Sky Sports F1 ahead of Sunday’s Qatar Grand Prix, Russell revisited the December 2024 fallout that dominated Formula 1’s season finale in Abu Dhabi. Back then, Russell stunned the paddock by alleging Verstappen had threatened in Qatar to "purposely go out of his way to crash into me, putting my f*****g head in the wall" - an accusation that prompted Russell to declare "people have been bullied by Max for years" and insist "somebody has to stand up to someone who thinks he's above the law".

    Verstappen fiercely denied the claim at the time, telling Dutch media Russell was "exaggerating again", calling him a "backstabber" and "just a loser", and mocking the stewards’ hearing with a parting shot: "Maybe next time, I'll bring tissues."

    Two seasons later, Russell’s temperature on that alleged threat has dropped considerably.

    "That's how he [Verstappen] goes about his business," Russell told Brundle. "I've known him long enough that I know, again, that was a bit more of a laugh-away comment, I thought."

    Owning what happened behind closed doors in 2024

    Where Russell did not soften his story was inside the stewards’ room itself. In Qatar 2024 qualifying, Verstappen beat Russell to provisional pole by 0.055 seconds before stewards handed the Red Bull driver a rare one-place grid penalty for driving unnecessarily slowly on a cooldown lap and impeding the Mercedes.

    Verstappen still beat Russell off the line to win Sunday’s race while Russell finished fourth, yet left fuming that he had "lost all respect" for a rival he accused of "trying to screw someone over that hard".

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    Asked by Brundle about his posture during that hearing, Russell made no attempt to play innocent.

    "Well, I wasn't trying to let him off," Russell admitted. "I mean, I didn't go in there saying, 'That's fine.'"

    That candid concession fits neatly into the broader pattern of a rivalry that has flared up once a year since Russell stepped into race-winning machinery at Mercedes:

    Baku 2023: Opening-lap Turn 2 contact in the Azerbaijan Sprint damaged Verstappen’s floor and triggered a heated parc fermé confrontation.

    Qatar & Abu Dhabi 2024: The qualifying grid drop sparked Verstappen’s "lost all respect" blast and Russell’s "in the wall" counterattack.

    Barcelona 2025: After cutting a corner during a late safety car restart and being told by Red Bull to hand fourth back, Verstappen collided heavily with Russell—picking up a 10-second penalty that dropped him to 10th and three penalty points that left him on 11, one shy of a race ban, before Verstappen later admitted the contact "shouldn't have happened".

    Las Vegas 2026: Practice contact and fresh barbs ahead of Verstappen’s Strip victory while Russell slid to ninth.

    Debunking the junior-karting origin story

    With four consecutive seasons of flashpoints on the ledger, fans have long assumed the bad blood traces back to teenage karting rivalries - much like Verstappen’s early battles with Charles Leclerc or Esteban Ocon. Russell shut that narrative down flat.

    "We actually didn't have that many comings together in karting," Russell explained. "He's a year older than me, so we only raced together once in 2011, and then obviously he fast-tracked his career."

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    The record backs him up. Although both won major titles in the KF3 junior class in 2011 - Verstappen claiming the WSK Euro Series crown while Russell captured the European KF3 Championship - their paths quickly split once Verstappen jumped swiftly up the single-seater ladder, leaving the pair to become genuine on-track regulars only after Russell joined Mercedes.

    Whatever truce Russell is offering off-camera, Losail’s floodlights rarely keep the pair apart for long. Mercedes set the early pace in Friday’s opening practice with Kimi Antonelli leading Russell in a 1-2 while Verstappen opened in sixth ahead of Friday night’s second session - setting the stage for another front-row collision course when Sunday’s Grand Prix goes green at 19:00 local time.
