    斯特拉盼迈凯伦在拉斯维加斯“终于”取得突破

    迈凯伦车队领队安德烈亚·斯特拉表示，拉斯维加斯历来是车队最艰难的一站，他希望车队这次能“终于”与这条赛道和解。诺里斯在巴西大奖赛夺冠后，斯特拉带着乐观情绪前往拉斯维加斯。

    安德烈亚·斯特拉希望迈凯伦“终于”与拉斯维加斯和解

    这位迈凯伦领队表示，拉斯维加斯大概是车队最艰难的一站，但在兰多·诺里斯于巴西夺冠后，他带着一些乐观情绪前往那里

    致迈凯伦车迷。来自沃金的木瓜色新闻，带来车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个比赛周末的最新消息。

    安德烈亚·斯特拉表示，迈凯伦正力争“终于”与历史上对车队而言颇为艰难的拉斯维加斯站和解。

    圣保罗大奖赛对迈凯伦来说是一个成功的周末，兰多·诺里斯以本赛季第三场胜利展现出统治级的表现，队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在速度上也有强劲的提升。

    拉斯维加斯对车队来说通常是截然不同的挑战，这条赛道以内华达州深夜的低温为特点。但迈凯伦很享受巴西站提供的寒冷潮湿周末，MCL40能够控制其轮胎温度。

    车队领队安德烈亚·斯特拉相信，车队重返美国可能会收获大量积分。

    “拉斯维加斯是一条非常不同的赛道布局。长直道，”他说。“我们在蒙扎见过这种情况，我们在巴库也见过，当要求是提升极速时，迈凯伦不一定是最好的赛车。

    “我们知道，这也在某种程度上与我们的传动比有关，当你需要超过某个最高速度水平时，这些传动比并不理想。但话虽如此，我认为我们带着一些乐观情绪前往拉斯维加斯，因为赛车现在更快了。”

    自夏季以来，速度提升来自多项升级，包括在巴西引入的新前翼，迈凯伦设计它是为了让赛车更可预测。斯特拉确认，更多新部件将抵达拉斯维加斯。

    “这些升级将得到一些抵达拉斯维加斯的新部件的补充，它们构成了明年赛车的基础，”他说。

    去年，诺里斯和皮亚斯特里在拉斯维加斯分别获得第二和第四，但两辆赛车都因底板下方滑块的过度磨损而被取消资格。

    他说：“我们想去那里取得好成绩，因为这在全年赛历的所有比赛中，可能是我们最挣扎的一站。即便去年我们有一场不错的比赛，我们也因为底板磨损的问题把自己的处境搞得很糟。”

    “所以我们期待去拉斯维加斯，在那里取得好成绩，最终与这条赛道和解。”

    迈凯伦在车队积分榜上排名第四，在仅剩三站的情况下落后红牛17分。这支木瓜色涂装的车队在拉斯维加斯大道霓虹灯下承受着拿大分的压力，以在漫长赛季后拿下第三名。

    Andrea Stella wants McLaren to "finally" make peace with Las Vegas

    McLaren's team principal says Las Vegas has probably been the team's toughest race, but he is heading there with some optimism after Lando Norris's win in Brazil

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Andrea Stella says McLaren is aiming to finally make "peace" with what has historically been a difficult race for the team in Las Vegas.

    The Sao Paulo Grand Prix was a successful weekend for McLaren, with Lando Norris showing a dominant level of performance with his third win of the season, and team-mate Oscar Piastri showing a strong uptick in pace.

    Las Vegas is usually a very different beast for teams, with the circuit boasting the cold temperatures of a late evening in Nevada. But McLaren enjoyed the cold and wet weekend Brazil offered, with the MCL40 being able to control its tyre temperatures.

    Team principal Andrea Stella believes the team's return to the United States could yield a strong haul of points.

    "It's a very different circuit layout, Vegas. Long straights," he said. "We have seen this in Monza, we have seen this in Baku, that when the requirement is to develop top speed, not necessarily McLaren is the best car.

    "We know that this also somehow relates to our gear ratios that are not ideal when you need to go above a certain level of top speed. But having said that, I think we approach Vegas with some optimism because the car is faster now."

    The pace has come from several upgrades since the summer, including a new front wing in Brazil that McLaren designed to make the car more predictable. Stella confirmed that more new parts would arrive in Las Vegas.

    "These upgrades, they will be complemented by some more parts that come in Vegas, and they form the foundation for what will be next year's car," he said.

    Last year, Norris and Piastri finished second and fourth in Vegas, only for both cars to be disqualified for excessive wear on the skid blocks under the floor.

    "We want to go there and have a good result because it's been probably, of all the races in the calendar, the one that we've been struggling the most," he said. "And even last year that we had a good race, we made our life bad with the problem that we had with the skid wear.

    "So we look forward to Vegas, have a good result there and finally being in peace with this venue."

    McLaren is fourth in the constructors' standings, 17 points behind Red Bull with just three races left. The papaya-coloured team is under pressure to score big under the neon lights of The Strip to finish third after what has been a long season.
