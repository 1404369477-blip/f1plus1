    安东内利解释2027赛季为何不用1号

    梅赛德斯车手安东内利表示，2027赛季他将继续使用12号，不会改用1号。他称自己有些迷信，1号常给他带来坏运气。

    安东内利解释2027赛季为何不用1号

    这位梅赛德斯车手将保留12号，因为12号对他有特殊意义。此外，这位年轻的意大利车手说自己有些迷信，1号常给他带来坏运气。

    下赛季F1发车格上将自2021年以来首次看不到1号。维斯塔潘和诺里斯在夺得世界冠军后都曾选择使用1号，但安东内利不会这样做。

    当Motorsport.com问他是否会改用1号，还是12号对他有特殊意义、他想保留时，他在墨西哥站赛后解释道：“我想我会继续用12号。”

    “我会保留12号，因为很明显，这对我来说是一个非常特别的号码，”安东内利在接替塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔成为史上最年轻的世界冠军后不久说道。

    12号是对安东内利偶像艾尔顿·塞纳的致敬，也是他在单座赛车中使用的第一个号码，那是在四级方程式时期。另一个有趣的细节是，安东内利也是自1988年塞纳之后，第一位以12号夺得F1世界冠军的车手。

    除了这层特殊意义之外，安东内利保留12号还有第二个理由，这个理由反映了他作为一个人的本色。

    “这也是让我保持脚踏实地、保持谦逊的一种方式。我觉得保留12号让我有了一个新的起点，可以在此基础上重新构建一切，并努力继续提升。”

    今年多次证明，他的谦逊是一项重要品质。即便在奥斯汀表现出色之后，当本网站问他如何在如此年轻的年纪就很好地应对压力时，安东内利尤其希望把功劳归于家人和梅赛德斯的人，而不是自己。

    除了他对12号所赋予的价值之外，安东内利还表示，他对1号并没有过好的经历，这也提供了另一个不换号码的理由。

    “说实话，我非常迷信，一号对我来说从来都不是一个好号码。每次我把它[贴上]，情况都糟糕透顶！”安东内利笑着说。

    “所以这是继续使用12号的又一个理由。”

    这种迷信在墨西哥比赛周末也对安东内利起了作用，正如他在赛后新闻发布会上透露的那样。这意味着他绝对不想认为冠军已经十拿九稳，因为他担心自己可能会因此带来霉运，影响最终结果。

    “我一遍又一遍地听到同样的话，”安东内利说道，据梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫所说，他指的是周五时许多人就已经提前祝贺他赢得世界冠军这件事。

    “而且，你知道，我在这一点上很直接：事情没完成之前，就不算完成。所以，我知道我仍然想好好完成这项工作，而当你不断听到许多人、嘉宾、车迷祝贺你夺冠时，这并不容易。

    “了解我自己，我非常迷信。我告诉你，这对我来说并不容易，尤其是因为我这么迷信！”

    不过，既然总冠军已经真正到手，安东内利现在可以尽情庆祝他的世界冠军了，他说自己主要会在这三连赛之后，也就是圣保罗大奖赛之后再好好庆祝。

    “今晚我会好好享受。我会看看自己要做什么，但肯定的是，等巴西站结束回家后，我会尽情狂欢。然后，再回到拉斯维加斯。去拉斯维加斯的时候我状态可能不会太好，但我觉得没关系，对吧？这些时刻你还是得好好享受！”

    Kimi Antonelli reveals why he won’t take number 1 for the 2027 F1 season

    The Mercedes driver will keep number 12, as it holds special meaning for him. Moreover, the young Italian says he is superstitious and number 1 has often brought him bad luck

    The number 1 will not be seen on the Formula 1 grid next season for the first time since 2021. Max Verstappen and Lando Norris have consistently decided to take the opportunity to claim number 1 after winning their world titles, but Antonelli will not do so.

    Asked by Motorsport.com whether he would switch to number 1 or whether number 12 held such special meaning for him that he wanted to keep it, he explained after the race in Mexico: “I think I'll stay with number 12.

    "I'll stay with number 12 because, obviously, it's a very special number for me”, Antonelli said shortly after replacing Sebastian Vettel as the youngest world champion of all time.

    The number 12 is a tribute to Antonelli's idol Ayrton Senna and was also the first number he used in single-seaters, in Formula 4. Another nice detail is that Antonelli is also the first driver since Senna in 1988 to clinch the F1 world title with the number 12.

    Beyond this special meaning, Antonelli has a second reason for keeping number 12, one that reflects who he is as a person.

    “It's also a way for me to stay grounded, to stay humble. I feel like keeping number 12 allows me to have a new point where to start and to build on everything again and try to improve more.”

    His modesty has proved to be an important quality on several occasions this year. Even after his impressive performance in Austin, when asked by this website how he manages to cope so well with the pressure at such a young age, Antonelli was particularly keen to give credit to his family and the people at Mercedes instead of himself.

    Besides the value he attaches to number 12, Antonelli indicated that he has not had good experiences with the number 1, providing another reason not to make the switch.

    “To be honest, I'm very superstitious and number one has never been a good number for me. Whenever I put it [on], it went extremely badly!” Antonelli laughed.

    “So that’s one more reason to stay with number 12.”

    That superstition also played a role for Antonelli during the race weekend in Mexico, as he revealed in the post-race press conference. It meant he absolutely did not want to think the title was already in the bag, as he feared he might jinx the outcome.

    “I kept hearing the same thing over and over again”, Antonelli said, referring, according to Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff, to the fact that many people had already congratulated him prematurely on winning the world title on Friday.

    “And, you know, I'm quite straightforward on this: until it's done, it's not done. So, I knew that I still wanted to finish the job well, and it's not easy when you keep hearing from many people, guests, fans, congratulations for the championship.

    “Knowing myself, I'm very superstitious. And I'm telling you, it's not easy for me, especially being that superstitious!”

    Now that the title is actually secured, however, Antonelli can fully celebrate his world championship, something he says he will do mainly after this triple-header, meaning after the Sao Paulo Grand Prix.

    “I will enjoy tonight. I'll see what I will do, but definitely when I go back home after Brazil, I will push [with partying]. And, yeah, then back to Vegas. I probably won't be in the best state going into Vegas, but I think it's fine, right? You've still got to enjoy all these moments!”
