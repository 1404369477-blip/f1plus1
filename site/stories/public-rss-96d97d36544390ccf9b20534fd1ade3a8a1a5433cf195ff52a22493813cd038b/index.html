    维斯塔潘支持伊萨克·哈德贾接任红牛F1角色

    四届F1冠军维斯塔潘支持伊萨克·哈德贾最终接替他在红牛的位置。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘支持伊萨克·哈德贾接替红牛一级方程式角色

    马克斯·维斯塔潘支持红牛队友伊萨克·哈德贾作为潜在的继任者

    伊萨克·哈德贾，红牛车队，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    对于红牛车迷来说。关注红牛车队，获取一级方程式新闻，车手更新，技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析。

    四届一级方程式冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘支持伊萨克·哈迪亚最终能在红牛车队接替他。

    哈迪亚在2026年初与维斯塔潘一同加入红牛车队，此前他在姐妹车队小红牛的新秀赛季表现出色。尽管米尔顿凯恩斯车队一段时间以来一直在寻找能够从赛车中提取出同样水平表现的队友，但哈迪亚至今已经给人留下了深刻印象。

    在2026赛季还剩六轮的情况下，维斯塔潘在车手积分榜上排名第四，积分为221分，而哈迪亚由于手腕受伤缺席了三场周末的比赛，目前排名第八，积分为97分。

    在与Canal+的访谈中，维斯塔潘讨论了他的22岁队友。

    "伊萨克，他还是一个非常年轻的小伙子。他已经加入红牛项目有一段时间了，"维斯塔潘说。"即使他当时还没有在F1中比赛，他也参加过一些比赛，倾听并关注。

    维斯塔潘, 红牛车队, 伊萨克·哈德贾, 红牛车队

    "所以，有这样一个年轻很多岁的队友，我开始慢慢感觉到自己有点老了。但这是一个正常的过程，这也是作为一个团队，你应该追求的东西，对吧？

    "我不会永远比赛下去，总有一天你需要新的天才加入团队，试图在某个时刻接管。这一直是红牛的工作方式，寻找新的天才，我认为这会持续很长时间。"

    “作为队友，他表现得非常出色。他刚刚开始，但已经非常棒了。”

    当被问及哈德杰未来能否接替他在车队中的角色时，维斯塔潘补充说：“这正是我所希望的。正如我所说，我并不是永远都在这里赛车。他比我年轻几岁，所以希望他能够多留一段时间。”

    Max Verstappen backs Isack Hadjar to take over Red Bull F1 role

    Max Verstappen has backed Red Bull team-mate Isack Hadjar as a potential successor

    Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen has backed Isack Hadjar to eventually succeed him at Red Bull.

    Hadjar joined Red Bull alongside Verstappen at the start of 2026 after impressing in his rookie season with sister outfit Racing Bulls. While the Milton Keynes outfit has struggled for some time to find a team-mate for the Dutchman who could extract the same level of performance from the car, Hadjar has impressed so far.

    With six rounds remaining in the 2026 season, Verstappen sits fourth in the drivers' championship with 221 points, while Hadjar is eighth with 97 points, having missed three race weekends due to a wrist injury.

    While speaking to Canal+, Verstappen discussed his 22-year-old team-mate.

    "Isack, he’s still a very young guy. He’s been a part of the Red Bull programme for a while now," Verstappen said. "Even when he was still not racing in Formula 1, he’s been around to some of the races, listening in and following.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    "So, I slowly start to feel a little bit old having a younger team-mate by quite a few years. But that’s a normal process, and that’s also what, as a team, you should be looking for, right?

    "I’m not going to race forever, and at one point you need new talent coming into the team to try and take over at one point. That’s always been a little bit the way that Red Bull works, finding new talent, and I think that will continue for a long time.

    "As a team-mate, he has been great. He’s just getting started, but it has been really nice."

    When asked if Hadjar could take his role within the team in the future, Verstappen added: "That’s what I hope. As I said, I’m not racing here forever. He has a few years on me, so hopefully he can stay around for a bit longer."
