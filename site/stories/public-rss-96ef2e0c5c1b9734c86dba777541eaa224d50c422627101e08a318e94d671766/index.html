    阿隆索：感觉慢了才会退出F1，不看年龄

    阿隆索表示，他会在自己感觉速度变慢时退出F1，而不是按某个特定年龄来决定，并称自己不需要像足球运动员那样体能出色才能保持竞争力。

    阿隆索：“我会在感觉自己变慢时退出F1，而不是在某个特定年龄。”

    这位45岁的车手在成为父亲之际谈到了自己的F1未来

    阿隆索表示，他会在自己感觉速度变慢时退出F1，而且他不需要像足球运动员那样体能出色才能保持竞争力。

    阿隆索是F1历史上经验最丰富的车手，从2001年到2026年共参加23个赛季；这位两届世界冠军的参赛时间已远超同龄人的退役年龄，尽管他目前处于245场未获胜的纪录中。

    “在这方面，赛车是一项非常独特的运动，”这位45岁的车手说道。“你不需要过分依赖体能来达到最高水平的发挥。你不是网球或足球运动员，需要不停地奔跑。你很大程度上依赖你的技术环境。”

    “当然，你仍然需要保持健康和动力，但归根结底，我不认为赛道上有任何人比我更优秀。我不觉得自己失去了速度，或者在比赛起步时失去了反应能力，或者失去了参加下一场大奖赛的动力。”

    “有些人说年轻一代需要在F1中占有一席之地，像我这样的人和刘易斯·汉密尔顿需要让位，因为我们在发车区待得太久了，但赛车运动不是这样运作的。我会在我觉得自己变慢时停下来，而不是当我的护照上写着我是这个或那个年龄时。”

    费尔南多·阿隆索，阿斯顿·马丁，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    阿隆索确实明确表示，当V8引擎在2030年回归时，他肯定不会还在围场里，而这或许与他作为父亲的新角色不无关系。

    “那一直是最艰难的事情，”他在谈到长时间离家在外时说道。“今年有很多个夜晚，我发现自己会想，我是不是错过了重要的时刻。

    “我也会想到比赛团队。我的一号机械师和他的伴侣三年前有了一个男孩，他们很快还会再有一个孩子。

    “在我成为父亲之前，我可能没有完全理解这对有孩子的人来说意味着什么，也没有完全理解如此频繁地离开他们有多难，但现在我完全理解了。

    “我想到他们，是因为他们比我更努力。他们在赛道待的时间比我长。他们比我提前一两天出发，比我晚一两天回家，但他们始终在努力工作，依然充满动力。

    “看到这些让我明白，我是车队中享有特权的一员，这也让我想和他们一起更加努力。”

    Fernando Alonso: ‘I will quit F1 when I feel slow, not at a specific age’

    The 45-year-old weighs in on his F1 future amid fatherhood

    Fernando Alonso says he will quit Formula 1 when he feels slow and he doesn’t need to be as fit as a football player to be competitive.

    Alonso is the most experienced driver in F1’s history, having competed in 23 seasons from 2001 to 2026, and the two-time champion has maintained his involvement well beyond his peers’ retirement age despite his current 245-race winless streak.

    “Motorsport is a very unique sport in that regard,” the 45-year-old said. “You don't rely so much on your fitness to perform at the highest level. You are not a tennis or football player where you need to run all the time. You rely a lot on your technical environment.

    “Of course, you still need to be fit and motivated, but at the end of the day I don't see anyone superior on the track. I don't feel that I've lost speed, or lost my reflexes at a race start, or lost motivation to come to the next grand prix.

    “Some people say that the younger generation need a spot in F1 and drivers like myself and Lewis [Hamilton] need to move on because we've been on the grid so long, but motorsport doesn't work like that. I will stop when I feel slow, not when my passport says I'm this age or that age.”

    Fernando Alonso, Aston Martin, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    Alonso did clarify that he definitely wouldn’t be around when V8 engines return in 2030, and this may not be unrelated to his new role as a parent.

    “That's been the toughest thing,” he said of spending so much time away from home. “Many nights this year, I've found myself wondering if I'm missing important moments.

    “I think about the race team, too. My number-one mechanic and his partner had a boy three years ago, and they will have another child soon.

    “Before I became a father, I probably didn't fully understand how much it meant to those who have children and how difficult it was to be away from them so often, but now I completely understand.

    “And I think about them because they work harder than me. They spend more hours than I do at the circuit. They travel one or two days earlier than me, they travel one or two days after me back home, but they're always working hard, still motivated.

    “Seeing that has helped me understand I'm a privileged part of the team and makes me want to work even harder together with them.”
