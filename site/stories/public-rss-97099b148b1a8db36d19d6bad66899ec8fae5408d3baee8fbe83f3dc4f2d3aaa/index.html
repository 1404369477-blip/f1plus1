    迈凯伦将在拉斯维加斯为MCL40带来升级

    迈凯伦将在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛为MCL40引入升级部件，并将其称为“明年赛车的基础”。车队此前在巴西推出了2027年前翼概念，并在英特拉格斯由兰多·诺里斯在湿地条件下夺得杆位和胜利。

    迈凯伦将在拉斯维加斯为MCL40带来升级

    迈凯伦在拉斯维加斯从未取得成功，但即将到来的升级或许会有帮助——目光已投向2027年

    迈凯伦将在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛为MCL40引入升级，新部件被称为“明年赛车的基础”。

    车队在巴西推出了2027年前翼概念，车队负责人安德烈亚·斯特拉将其描述为可驾驶性和可预测性的提升；迈凯伦在英特拉格斯重回正轨，兰多·诺里斯在湿地条件下夺得杆位和胜利。这终结了连续五轮无缘领奖台的走势，是车队自2023赛季前半段以来最差的一段表现。

    “在匈牙利站升级之后，我们能够处于争胜的位置，这令人鼓舞——我们本应赢下马德里站的，真的，”斯特拉说道，他指的是西班牙站虚拟安全车出现的不幸时机，这很可能让诺里斯丢掉了在马德里赛道的胜利。

    “但在那之后，其他人也带来了升级，我们失去了这个位置。我认为我们也为没有跑出出色的排位赛表现付出了代价。如果能看到兰多（诺里斯）在奥斯汀或墨西哥这样的比赛中从前方发车，那会很有意思；那绝对会是一场领奖台之争。

    “但正是得益于这些升级，我们现在才有条件去争夺胜利，当然也要结合具体情况。而这些升级，首先，它们将在拉斯维加斯得到更多部件的补充。它们构成了明年赛车的基础。所以，对于迈凯伦的发展轨迹来说，这令人鼓舞，因为我们似乎能够将研发转化为赛道上的表现。”

    拉斯维加斯对这支木瓜色车队来说并不是福地。早在2023年，迈凯伦已经成为稳定的领奖台争夺者，但两辆车都在第一节排位赛（Q1）中被淘汰；奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里只拿到一个积分，而兰多·诺里斯则在第三圈触底并严重撞车。

    第二年，这位最终的车队冠军车手仅排位第六和第八，正赛中平淡地跑到第六和第七，落后前五名27秒。

    上赛季，诺里斯确实拿到了杆位；他和皮亚斯特里在正赛中守住了第二和第四，而争冠对手马克斯·维斯塔潘获胜，但两辆车因MCL39最尾部的滑板块磨损过度而被取消资格。

    基于MCL40的特性，斯特拉并不一定认为内华达赛道今年会利于迈凯伦，因为近2公里的长直道部分需要全油门。

    “拉斯维加斯是非常不同的赛道布局，长直道，”这位意大利人评论道。“我们知道——我们在蒙扎见过，我们在巴库见过——当要求是提升极速时，迈凯伦不一定是最好的车。我们知道这也在某种程度上与我们[较短的]齿轮比有关，当你需要超过某个极速水平时，这些齿轮比并不理想。

    “但话虽如此，我们带着一些乐观情绪前往维加斯，因为赛车现在更快了。这场比赛将让我们——我想不只是我们，而是所有人——对如何使用轮胎、赛车在哪些地方表现好、他们的赛车或我们的赛车在哪些地方稍逊一筹有一些了解。所以也有信息可以为维加斯所用。”

    “所以我们带着一些乐观情绪看待。另外，维加斯，我们想去那里取得好成绩，因为在赛历上的所有比赛中，这可能是我们最挣扎的一站。即便去年我们有一场不错的比赛，我们也因为底板滑块的问题让自己的处境变得糟糕。我们期待维加斯，在那里取得好成绩，最终与这条赛道和平相处。”

    McLaren to upgrade MCL40 car at its bogey F1 track Las Vegas

    McLaren has never been successful in Las Vegas, but upcoming updates might help – with eyes on 2027

    McLaren will introduce upgrades to its MCL40 at Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix, with the new parts labelled as “the foundation for next year’s car”.

    The team launched its 2027 front wing concept in Brazil, with team principal Andrea Stella describing it as an improvement for driveability and predictability; McLaren returned to form at Interlagos, with Lando Norris clinching pole position and victory in wet conditions. This ended a five-round podium-less streak, the squad’s worst since the first half of the 2023 campaign.

    “After the upgrades in Hungary, it was encouraging that we were in a position to win – we should have won Madrid, really,” Stella said, referring to the unfortunate timing of the Virtual Safety Car in Spain, which likely cost Norris the victory at Madring.

    “But after that, other people took upgrades and we lost this position. I think we also paid the price of not having had great qualifying sessions. It would have been interesting to see Lando starting from the front in races like Austin or Mexico; it would have been definitely a fight for podium.

    “But it's thanks to these upgrades that we are now in condition to fight for the victory, combined with the conditions surely. And these upgrades, first of all, they will be complemented by some more parts that come in Vegas. And they form the foundation for what will be next year's car. So, it's encouraging for the trajectory of McLaren that we seem to be able to transform our development into performance on track.”

    Las Vegas has not been a happy hunting ground for the papaya team. Back in 2023, McLaren had become a consistent podium contender, but both cars were out in Q1; Oscar Piastri scored a solitary point, while Lando Norris bottomed out and heavily crashed on lap three.

    The following year, the eventual constructors’ champion qualified just sixth and eighth, uneventfully driving to sixth and seventh in the race, 27 seconds adrift of the top five.

    Last season, Norris did take pole; he and Piastri held on to second and fourth in the race as title rival Max Verstappen prevailed, but both cars were disqualified due to the MCL39s’ rearmost skid blocks being too worn.

    Based on the MCL40’s specificities, Stella doesn’t necessarily expect the Nevadan track to favour McLaren this year, with full throttle down the near-2km Strip portion.

    “It's a very different circuit layout, Vegas; long straights,” the Italian commented. “We know – we have seen this in Monza, we have seen this in Baku – that when the requirement is to develop top speed, not necessarily McLaren is the best car. We know that this also somehow relates to our [shorter] gear ratios that are not ideal when you need to go above a certain level of top speed.

    “But having said that, we approach Vegas with some optimism because the car is faster now. This race will give us – and not [just] us but everyone, I think – a bit of understanding of how to use the tyres and where the car is good, where their car or our car is less good. So there's information to be capitalised for Vegas as well.

    “So we look with some optimism. Also, Vegas, we want to go there and have a good result, because it's been probably, of all the races in the calendar, the one that we've been struggling the most. And even last year that we had a good race, we made our life bad with the problem that we had with the skid. We look forward to Vegas, have a good result there and finally be in peace with this venue.”
