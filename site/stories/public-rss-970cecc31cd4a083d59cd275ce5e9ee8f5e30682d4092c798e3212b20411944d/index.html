    乔治·拉塞尔湿滑新加坡F1冲刺赛中冲出赛道

    梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔在湿滑的新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中冲出赛道，导致第2圈出动安全车。

    乔治·拉塞尔在湿滑的新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中冲出赛道，导致第2圈出动安全车。

    拉塞尔在比赛开始时超越杆位车手马克斯·维斯塔潘取得领先。

    由于突如其来的降雨导致比赛延迟。

    拉塞尔在第2圈最后一个弯角处失控冲出赛道。

    但拉塞尔并没有享受他的领先太久，他在第二圈的最后弯角失控撞车。

    拉塞尔撞到了维修区墙壁的内侧，幸运的是，他在横穿马路时没有被来车撞上，最终在起终点直道的外侧停下。

    这一事故引发了一辆安全车的出动，在中立区期间，维斯特潘领先，而汉密尔顿从第六位出发取得了出色的成绩，以及查尔斯·勒克莱尔。

    在背景中，阿斯顿·马丁的兰斯·斯特罗尔和塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯在4号弯角相撞，导致两位车手都被淘汰出比赛。

    红牛车队的伊萨克·哈德贾和阿尔皮纳车队的弗朗哥·科拉平托也退出了比赛，哈德贾似乎遇到了动力单元问题。

    小红牛车手阿维德·林德布拉德发生打滑，排名下降至第17位，成为剩余参赛者中的最后一名。

    George Russell crashes out of lead at wet Singapore F1 sprint

    Russell overtook Max Verstappen at the start of a wet Singapore Grand Prix sprint, but crashed out on lap 2

    Mercedes driver George Russell has crashed out of a wet Singapore GP sprint, bringing out a safety car on lap 2.

    Following a delay triggered by a sudden downpour, Russell took the lead on a wet start of the race by passing polesitter Max Verstappen on the inside into Turn 1.

    But Russell didn't enjoy his lead for long, crashing in the final corner on lap two as he appeared to lose control over the white line.

    Russell crashed into the pitwall on the inside and he was lucky not to get collected by oncoming traffic as he crossed the road sideways, before coming to a stop on the outside of the start-finish straight.

    The incident triggered a safety car, with Verstappen leading during the neutralisation ahead of Lewis Hamilton, who got off to an excellent start from sixth, and Charles Leclerc.

    In the background Aston Martin's Lance Stroll and Sergio Perez came together at turn 4, eliminating both drivers from the race.

    Red Bull's Isack Hadjar and Alpine's Franco Colapinto are also out of the race with Hadjar appearing to struggle with a power unit issue.

    Racing Bulls man Arvid Lindblad suffered a spin and dropped down to 17th and last of the remaining runners.
