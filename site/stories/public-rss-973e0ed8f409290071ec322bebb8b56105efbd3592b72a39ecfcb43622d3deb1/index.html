    乔治·拉塞尔在银石推出全额资助的GR63卡丁车学院

    梅赛德斯一级方程式车手乔治·拉塞尔在银石推出了全额资助的GR63卡丁车学院，旨在帮助年轻车手克服卡丁车运动的财务障碍。

    乔治·拉塞尔在银石推出了全额资助的GR63卡丁车学院。

    乔治·拉塞尔推出GR63学院，旨在帮助25名年轻车手绕过卡丁车运动的财务障碍。

    赛车文化不仅限于赛道。车手的生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车运动超越体育本身的故事。

    赛车运动存在令人担忧的财务门槛，初级卡丁车赛季经常需要花费六位数以上的费用。梅赛德斯一级方程式车手乔治·拉塞尔正在采取直接行动来改变这一现状。

    拉塞尔正式宣布了GR63学院的启动，这是一个与银石赛道和英国赛车驾驶俱乐部合作创立的草根卡丁车计划。该计划明确旨在绕过传统付费参与的青少年赛车模式，为有才华的孩子们提供一个合法、全额资助的晋升阶梯。

    "卡丁车是我一切的开始，在我的职业生涯中扮演了至关重要的角色，" 拉塞尔在他的公告中写道。"我也知道有才华的年轻车手获得首次机会是多么困难，尤其是在预算有限的情况下。

    "通过GR63学院，我想通过发现隐藏的才华并向他们提供他们实现全部潜力的支持和指导，来回报我的运动所给予我的，" 拉塞尔继续说道。

    2027赛季的首个选拔过程完全基于实力，而非预算。该计划将在位于银石赛道卡丁车场的不同阶段进行操作：

    第一阶段（2026年12月）：开放式的“到站即赛”资格赛将从12月6日持续到12月31日。

    第二阶段（2027年1月）：从初始资格赛脱颖而出的80名最快车手将晋级到1月31日的对决。

    第三阶段（锦标赛）：在银石赛道的前两轮比赛之后，拉塞尔将选出25名年轻车手加入2027年的GR63学院。这25名孩子将在2027年3月至11月期间参加一个八轮的卡丁车系列赛。

    学院将为这25名选出的车手承担所有参赛费用，为他们提供一套完整的赛车装备，包括连体服、头盔、靴子和手套。锦标赛的每一轮比赛都将在一个不同的赛道配置上在银石赛道进行。

    在整个八轮锦标赛中，车手们将在赛车技巧、原始圈速、体能和媒体技能方面接受评估。拉塞尔将积极参与作为导师，指导年轻才俊通过严格的培养过程。

    该计划将于2027年12月结束，届时锦标赛的前五名完赛者将在银石赛道进行一场决赛。

    GR63学院的总体获胜者将获得2028年日历年度全额资助的卡丁车赛季。

    George Russell launches fully-funded GR63 Karting Academy at Silverstone

    George Russell launches the fully-funded GR63 Academy to help 25 young drivers bypass the financial barriers of karting

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Motorsport has an alarming financial barrier to entry, with seasons of junior karting frequently running deep into the six figures. Mercedes Formula 1 star George Russell is taking direct action to change that.

    Russell officially announced the launch of the GR63 Academy, a grassroots karting initiative created in partnership with Silverstone and the British Racing Drivers' Club. The programme is explicitly designed to bypass the traditional pay-to-play model of junior motorsport and provide a legitimate, fully funded ladder for talented kids.

    "Karting is where it all started for me and has played an essential role in my career," Russell wrote in his announcement. "I also know how difficult it is for talented young drivers to get their first opportunity, especially when budgets are limited."

    "With the GR63 Academy, I want to give back to my sport what it has given me by spotting hidden talent and offering them the support and guidance they need to reach their full potential," Russell continued.

    The selection process for the inaugural 2027 season is based purely on merit, not budget. The programme will operate in distinct stages based at the Silverstone karting circuit:

    Stage 1 (December 2026): Open-access "arrive-and-drive" qualifying sessions will run from 6 December to 31 December.

    Stage 2 (January 2027): The 80 fastest drivers from the initial qualifying stage will advance to a shootout on 31 January 2027.

    Stage 3 (The Championship): Following the first two rounds at Silverstone, Russell will select 25 young drivers to join the GR63 Academy in 2027. These 25 kids will compete in an eight-round karting series running from March to November 2027.

    The academy will cover all participation costs for the 25 selected drivers, providing them with a complete set of racing equipment, including overalls, a helmet, boots, and gloves. Each round of the championship will take place on a different circuit configuration at Silverstone.

    Throughout the eight-round championship, drivers will be evaluated on racecraft, raw lap times, fitness, and media skills. Russell will actively participate as a mentor, guiding the young talent through the rigorous development process.

    The programme concludes in December 2027, when the top five finishers from the championship will face off in a final race at Silverstone.

    The overall winner of the GR63 Academy will be rewarded with a fully funded karting season for the 2028 calendar year.
