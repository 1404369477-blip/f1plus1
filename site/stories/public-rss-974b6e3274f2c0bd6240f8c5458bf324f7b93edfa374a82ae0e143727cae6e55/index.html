    格拉斯利在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上错失胜利的原因

    格拉斯利认为，如果不是冷却问题影响了他的阿尔派赛车，他本有机会赢得拉斯维加斯大奖赛。他在发车第八位的情况下最终获得第五名。

    导致皮埃尔·加斯利在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛上错失潜在胜利的问题

    阿尔派车手加斯利最终排名第五，但在比赛过半时曾一度位列第三

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯、兰多·诺里斯、麦克拉伦、皮埃尔·加斯利、阿尔派

    皮埃尔·加斯利认为，如果冷却问题没有在比赛最后阶段影响他的阿尔派一级方程式赛车，他本有可能赢得拉斯维加斯大奖赛。

    法国人从第八位发车最终获得第五名，但在原定50圈中的第31圈后，加斯利排在第三位，仅次于红牛车队的最终获胜者维斯塔潘和梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔。

    然而，加斯利在接下来的四圈中突然失去了五个位置，从第八位降至兰多·诺里斯、奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里、基米·安东内利、刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔之后。

    这位30岁的车手解释说，这是由于他的A526赛车出现技术问题，最终由于拉塞尔撞上护栏以及法拉利车队的汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔相撞，他最终获得第五名。

    因此，当被问及是否对错过他职业生涯的第六个领奖台感到沮丧时，加斯利在周六晚上回应说：“我不会这么说。但我会从事实上开始，这是我们作为团队最好的比赛，我们肯定会吸取积极的方面。”

    他的2026赛季最佳成绩是在上海的第二轮比赛中获得第六名，尽管他在结果在法庭上得到解决之前，暂时被授予了摩纳哥的领奖台。

    “事实上，那是一场非常有趣的比赛，我很享受在车队前列的战斗，”加斯利补充道。“但然后，是的，在比赛开始时我们飞得很快，我设法一路冲到了P3的位置。

    “在那个时刻，我真的觉得我们可能有足够的速度去挑战胜利。当我到达P3时，我通过无线电被告知赛车过热，我不得不降低功率。

    “我们降低了45圈的功率。所以当我切换到最激进的开关来冷却赛车时，我一路跌到了5号弯。我在直道上失去了位置，所以在那一圈里我失去了两个位置，然后我就一路退到了后面。我们正在管理赛车的温度。

    “我必须打开差距。我可以在变速箱里，但我必须打开差距来冷却汽车。我真的觉得我们有机会获得领奖台，我一直要求恢复全功率，但只被允许在最后两圈这样做。”

    “我们将审查确切发生了什么，因为我看到你们所有人都穿着冬季夹克，我穿着一件冬季夹克。我真的不明白为什么汽车会过热，但除此之外，这是整个团队不可思议的努力。”

    气温低至9摄氏度，这让加斯利对问题感到困惑，尽管他认为这可能会比他保持第10名所获得的第五名更糟糕。

    “我认为我们没有预料到这一点，”他声称。“但是，是的，这些45圈，我认为在最极端的情况下，我每圈损失超过一秒，因为我们运行的是那个最慢的引擎模式。”

    “我很高兴排在第五，但确实，赛车非常快，还有更多的潜力可以挖掘。在跑极端策略的时候，我损失了很多，我以为我会比现在退得更远。”

    ““我试图保持稳定，我们在中间尝试了四五种不同的模式。最极端的一次，我想我可能使用了大约10圈，这是比赛中最令人担忧的部分，因为有了拖车增益，我在跟随其他车辆并进行尾流超车。”"

    ““但正如我说的，我们会回顾。这是赛车的一部分，但我认为这确实让我们损失了很多。”"

    The issue that cost Pierre Gasly a potential "win" at F1's Las Vegas GP

    The Alpine driver finished fifth but was as high as third over halfway through

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Lando Norris, McLaren, Pierre Gasly, Alpine

    Pierre Gasly reckons he potentially had the pace to win the Las Vegas Grand Prix, had a cooling issue not affected his Alpine Formula 1 car in the closing stages.

    The Frenchman finished fifth from eighth on the grid, but after 31 of the scheduled 50 laps, Gasly was third behind Red Bull’s eventual winner Max Verstappen and Mercedes’ George Russell.

    Yet Gasly suddenly lost five positions within the next four laps, having dropped into eighth behind Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri, Kimi Antonelli, Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc.

    The 30-year-old explained that this was down to a technical issue on his A526, which eventually took fifth due to Russell hitting the barriers plus Ferrari duo Hamilton and Leclerc colliding into each other.

    So when asked afterwards if he was frustrated to miss out on his sixth career podium, Gasly responded on Saturday night: “I'm not going to call it that way. But I'll start with the fact that it's our best race as a team, and we're definitely going to take the positives.”

    His previous best result in the 2026 F1 season was sixth at round two in Shanghai, despite him temporarily being given a Monaco podium until that result was settled in the courts.

    “Follow with the fact that it was a very fun race to be part of and I enjoyed a lot battling at the front of the pack,” added Gasly. “But then yeah, at the start we were flying, I managed to find my way all the way up to P3.

    “At that point I really felt that we had the pace to maybe even challenge for the win. And the moment I got to P3, I got told on the radio that the car was overheating and I had to turn down the power.

    “We turned down the power for 45 laps. So the moment I went to the most aggressive switch to cool it down, I lost position all the way to Turn 5. I lost the position on the back straight, so I lost two positions in a lap and then I just dropped back all the way. We were managing the temperature.

    “I had to open the gap. I could be in the gearbox, but I had to open the gap to cool down the car. I really feel we had the podium, and I kept asking to get back to full power, but I was only allowed to do it for the last two laps.

    “We'll review exactly what happened, because I can see you guys all wearing winter jackets, I am wearing a winter jacket. I don't really understand why the car was overheating, but apart from that it was an incredible effort from all the team.”

    Conditions were as low as 9C which left Gasly perplexed by the issue, though he reckons it could have been much worse than the fifth he took that keeps him 10th in the standings.

    “I don't think we saw it coming,” he claimed. “But yeah, these 45 laps, I think the most extreme I was losing over a second a lap with that slowest engine mode that we were running.

    “I'm happy to be fifth, but yeah, definitely the car was very fast, and there was more to take. At some point when I was running that extreme one, I was losing so much that I thought I was going to drop back even further than that.

    “I was trying to hold down and we had four or five different modes we were playing in between. The most extreme, I think I had it for maybe 10 laps, which were the most worrying part of the race, because with the tow gain, I was following other cars and drafting back.

    “But yeah, as I said, we'll review. It's part of racing, but I think it definitely cost us quite a bit too.”
