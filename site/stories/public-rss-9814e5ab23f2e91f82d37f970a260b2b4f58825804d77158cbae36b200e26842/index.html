    措洛夫将参加一套严格的培养计划，为升入F1做准备

    尼古拉·措洛夫被任命为红牛与小红牛2027赛季F1储备车手，此前他未能获得正式车手席位。

    这位19岁的保加利亚车手此前被传将从F2升入，于2027年在小红牛顶替利亚姆·劳森，但经过数周猜测，这支位于法恩扎的车队平息了传言，确认这位新西兰车手将继续与阿尔维德·林德布拉德搭档。

    措洛夫在他首个完整F2赛季中表现出色，斩获六场胜利，其中包括四场主赛，并创下连续三场获胜的纪录，成为该系列赛历史上首位达成此成就的车手。

    明年他将担任替补与发展车手角色，除了在紧急情况下可能顶替出战——正如角田裕毅在小红牛所做的那样，当时劳森被召去红牛替代受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔——措洛夫还将参与一项内容广泛的计划，将模拟器工作与赛道经验相结合，通过第一节自由练习和旧车测试（TPC）环节以及倍耐力轮胎测试来积累经验。

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯表示：“尼古拉是一位极具天赋的年轻车手，过去几年他的成长和表现给我们留下了深刻印象。”

    “在F3为冠军而战，如今又在首个完整赛季中挑战F2冠军，这充分说明了他的能力和决心。同样令我们印象深刻的是他的成熟、他对比赛的态度，以及他不断学习和进步的意愿。”

    “我们对尼科拉的潜力抱有极大的信心，认为他是一位在F1拥有非常令人兴奋未来的车手。

    “这一任命反映了我们对他本人的信任，以及我们对支持他持续发展的承诺。我们在2027年的重点将是给予他所需的经验、工具和支持，让他为下一赛季征战F1做好准备。

    “目前，尼科拉在F2还有一项重要任务需要完成。我们祝愿他在最后两轮比赛中一切顺利，并将一路支持他为总冠军而战。”

    措洛夫目前在F2积分榜上排名第二，在剩余两轮的情况下落后领先者拉斐尔·卡马拉七分，不过这位巴西车手如果继续随哈斯车队——他明年将为其效力——仍有可能缺席这两轮比赛；在埃斯特班·奥康离队后，他已晋升并在英特拉格斯完成了首秀。

    措洛夫补充道：“我非常高兴，也感谢红牛给我这个机会。自从加入青训车队以来，我得到了惊人的支持，作为车手和个人都成长了很多。我的职业生涯进展得非常快，我非常感谢每一个相信我并帮助我走到这一步的人。”

    “我的目标很明确：我想成为F1车手。有机会在明年为下一步做准备，这令人无比兴奋。我相信，在车队的支持和经验帮助下，我将得到最好的准备，确保当时机到来时，我完全准备好迈上F1发车格。”

    “我知道还有很多要学，但我准备好努力工作，充分利用每一个机会，为车队付出我的一切。目前，我的首要任务是尽可能强势地完成F2赛季。”

    Tsolov named Red Bull, Racing Bulls reserve driver after missing out on 2027 F1 race seat

    Tsolov will undertake a rigorous development programme to prepare for the step up to F1

    Nikola Tsolov has been named as reserve driver for both Red Bull and Racing Bulls for the 2027 Formula 1 season having missed out on a full-time drive.

    The 19-year-old Bulgarian had been linked to a step up from F2 to replace Liam Lawson at Racing Bulls in 2027, but after weeks of speculation the Faenza-based squad put rumours to rest by confirming the New Zealander would continue alongside Arvid Lindblad.

    Tsolov has impressed in his first full F2 season, claiming six victories which include four feature races and a streak that saw him become the first in series history to claim three on the bounce.

    Next year he will take on a reserve and development role that, alongside the potential to act as a substitute in case of emergency - as Yuki Tsunoda did at Racing Bulls when Lawson was called upon to replace the injured Isack Hadjar at Red Bull - Tsolov will undergo an extensive programme that will combine simulator work with on-track experience, gained through FP1 and TPC [Testing of Previous Car] sessions, as well as Pirelli tyre tests.

    “Nikola is an exceptionally talented young driver, and we have been hugely impressed by his development and performances over the past few years," said Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies.

    "To fight for the championship in F3 and now challenge for the F2 title in his first full season says a great deal about his ability and determination. What has impressed us just as much is his maturity, his approach to racing and his willingness to keep learning and improving.

    "We have enormous belief in Nikola’s potential and see him as a driver with a very exciting future in F1.

    “This appointment reflects our confidence in him and our commitment to his continued development. Our focus in 2027 will be on giving him the experience, tools and support he needs to be ready to race in F1 next.

    "For now, Nikola still has an important job to finish in F2. We wish him every success in the final two rounds and will be supporting him all the way as he fights for the championship."

    Tsolov sits second in the F2 standings and is seven points behind leader Rafael Camara with two rounds remaining, though the Brazilian could yet miss those if continuing with Haas - who he will race for next year - having stepped up to make his debut at Interlagos following Esteban Ocon's departure.

    Tsolov added: "I’m incredibly happy and grateful to Red Bull for giving me this opportunity. Since joining the junior team, I’ve received amazing support and have developed so much, both as a driver and as a person. My career has progressed incredibly quickly, and I owe a lot to everyone who has believed in me and helped me get to this point.

    "My ambition is clear: I want to become an F1 driver. Having the opportunity to spend next year preparing for that next step is incredibly exciting. I’m confident that with the team’s support and experience, I’ll have the best possible preparation to make sure I’m fully ready to take the next step onto the F1 grid when the time comes.

    "I know there is still a lot to learn, but I’m ready to work hard, make the most of every opportunity and give everything I have to the Team. For now, my immediate focus is on finishing the F2 season as strongly as possible.”
