    维斯塔潘拉斯维加斯练习赛与拉塞尔碰撞仅获警告

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛第三节练习赛末段与拉塞尔在14号弯并排时车轮相碰，赛会干事最终仅对其发出警告，未处以正赛罚退。

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛第三节练习赛与拉塞尔发生碰撞，最终仅被警告。

    维斯塔潘与拉塞尔在第三节练习赛末段于14号弯并排时离奇地车轮相碰，两人不得不在排位赛前接受赛会干事问询。

    维斯塔潘和利亚姆·劳森此前在墨西哥练习赛中发生冲突，曾被处以正赛罚退一位；但在拉斯维加斯，赛会干事最终裁定对维斯塔潘给予警告已足够。

    赛会干事解释说，“车手们讨论了关于飞驰圈准备的非正式君子协定以及在这种情况下车手之间应有的行为”，但决定“这些非正式共识对判定该事件参考价值有限”。

    “决定性考量因素是两辆赛车的实际动作及其相对位置，”干事文件继续写道。

    “因此，干事们审查了维斯塔潘的刹车和变向、拉塞尔的避让动作和位置选择，并考虑了适用的驾驶标准指南。

    “干事们认为，维斯塔潘在刹车下变向不可预测，导致了拉塞尔不得不移向内线以避免接触的局面。

    “虽然干事们认为该动作未严重到需要更严厉处罚的程度，但他们得出结论，在这些情况下刹车下变向低于预期的驾驶标准。

    “考虑到事件的具体情况，赛会干事认定对车手发出警告是适当的。”

    最终，拉斯维加斯事件被判定为一次简单的刹车区变线违规。

    在墨西哥与劳森的事件导致发车位罚退，因为用赛会干事的话说，两位车手“的行为被认为不可接受”，而且此事直接发生在对所有车队发出警告、要求避免此类练习赛事件之后。

    对于维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯三练中与弗兰科·科拉平托的阿尔派赛车发生的另一起事件，未采取进一步行动，原因是存在“减轻情节”，包括维斯塔潘在该圈早些时候遇到引擎问题，以及维斯塔潘对科拉平托超越他感到意外，因为两人都不在冲刺圈中。

    在其他地方，亚历山大·阿尔本因在同一节练习中于伊萨克·哈贾尔的红牛赛车前做出“不可预测且可能危险”的移动而受到训诫。

    英文原文

    Max Verstappen escaped with a warning for his clash with George Russell in final practice at Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Verstappen and Russell bizarrely banged wheels while going side-by-side through Turn 14 in the dying moments of FP3 and they had to see the stewards before qualifying.

    Verstappen and Liam Lawson had been given a one-place grid penalty for a practice run-in in Mexico, but in Las Vegas, the stewards ultimately ruled that a warning for Verstappen was sufficient.

    The stewards explained that “the drivers discussed the informal gentlemen's agreements concerning the preparation for a flying lap and the conduct expected between drivers in such situations" but decided "such informal understandings were of limited relevance to the assessment of the incident".

    “The decisive considerations were the actual movements of both cars and their relative positions," the stewards document continued.

    “The stewards therefore examined the braking and change of direction of Verstappen, the evasive manoeuvre and positioning of Russell and taking into account the applicable driving standards guidelines.

    “The stewards considered that Verstappen’s change of direction under braking was unpredictable and contributed to the situation in which Russell had to move to the inside to avoid contact.

    “While the stewards did not consider the manoeuvre sufficiently serious to warrant a more severe penalty, they concluded that changing direction under braking in these circumstances fell below the standard of driving expected.

    “Taking into account the circumstances of the incident, the stewards determined that a driver warning was appropriate.”

    Ultimately, the Las Vegas incident has been judged as a simple moving-under-braking offence.

    The Mexico incident with Lawson resulted in grid drops because both drivers, in the words of the stewards, “behaved in a manner that was considered unacceptable”, and it came directly after a warning to all teams to avoid such practice incidents.

    No further action was taken over Verstappen’s other FP3 Vegas incident with Franco Colapinto’s Alpine because of “mitigating elements”, including Verstappen experiencing an engine issue earlier in the lap and Verstappen being surprised that Colapinto was overtaking him, given neither was on a push lap.

    Elsewhere, Alex Albon was handed a reprimand for “unpredictable and potentially dangerous” movement in front of Isack Hadjar’s Red Bull in the same session.
