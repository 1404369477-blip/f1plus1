    安东内利因身体不适缺席墨西哥大奖赛媒体日

    梅赛德斯车队宣布，由于身体不适，安东内利将不会参加周四的墨西哥大奖赛媒体活动。

    安东内利因身体不适缺席F1墨西哥大奖赛媒体日

    安东内利获得国际汽联的许可，免于参加媒体活动

    梅赛德斯车队透露，由于身体不适，安东内利将不会参加周四的F1墨西哥大奖赛

    这家英德混血的团队发言人告诉媒体，这位车手领袖因为正在恢复的‘病毒性疾病’而感到‘身体不适’，因此国际汽联因此免除他今天的媒体活动

    “我们期待他明天回来，”声明继续说道。

    安东内利正站在首个F1冠军的边缘，这将使他成为最年轻的世界冠军，因为他将超过塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔2010年的记录超过三年。他周日只需要得到第九名，或者乔治·拉塞尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿中没有人获胜。

    这位20岁的意大利人不会是唯一缺席媒体日的车手，瓦尔特里·博塔斯也声称生病；凯迪拉克通知媒体，这位芬兰人“不适”。

    “车队已经决定让他休息一天，以支持他的恢复，”声明中这样写道。

    “这是一项预防措施，目前不会影响他在本周末剩余赛事中的参赛。我们将继续监控情况，并在必要时提供更新。”

    如果需要，谁将顶替上场？

    尽管可能性不大，但有可能召唤一名预备车手来替代任何一名车手。

    在梅赛德斯车队，这名预备车手将是2023年二级方程式亚军弗雷德里克·韦斯蒂，他从未参加过大奖赛，但本周末将在乔治·罗素的车上参加他职业生涯的第七次自由练习1。

    就凯迪拉克车队而言，周冠宇是球队的预备车手，因此这位七次积分得主将在结束他与阿尔法·罗密欧/赛百灵三年的合同后，在2024赛季结束后重返F1。

    Unwell Kimi Antonelli skips F1 Mexico GP media day

    Antonelli has been excused from media activities by the FIA

    Kimi Antonelli won’t attend Formula 1’s Mexico Grand Prix on Thursday due to feeling unwell, the Mercedes team has revealed.

    A spokesperson for the Anglo-German outfit told media that the championship leader was “feeling under the weather” due to a “viral illness” he has been recovering, with the FIA therefore excusing him from today’s media activities.

    “We expect him to be back tomorrow,” the statement continued.

    Antonelli is on the brink of a maiden F1 title, which would make him the youngest world champion as he would beat Sebastian Vettel’s 2010 record by over three years. All he needs on Sunday is ninth place, or for neither George Russell nor Lewis Hamilton to win.

    The 20-year-old Italian won’t be the only driver to skip media day, with Valtteri Bottas also calling in sick; Cadillac notified media that the Finn was “unwell”.

    “The team has taken the decision to allow him a day of rest, to support his recovery,” the statement read.

    “This is a precautionary measure and does not currently affect his participation in the remainder of the race weekend. We will continue to monitor the situation and provide updates if necessary.”

    Who will step up if necessary?

    Albeit unlikely, there is a chance of a reserve driver being called up for either racer.

    At Mercedes, that would be 2023 Formula 2 runner-up Frederik Vesti, who has never competed in a grand prix but is scheduled to make his seventh career Free Practice 1 outing this weekend in George Russell’s car.

    As far as Cadillac is concerned, Zhou Guanyu is the team’s reserve driver, so the seven-time points scorer would make his F1 return after he wound up his three-year stint with Alfa Romeo/Sauber at the end of the 2024 campaign.
