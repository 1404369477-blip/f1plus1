    前哈斯一级方程式车队负责人古恩特·施泰纳认为，法拉利车队将在刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上再次发生冲突后做出改变。

    勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯街道上与汉密尔顿争夺第六名时将汉密尔顿旋转了一圈，这发生在墨西哥大奖赛上另一次冲突后的三周。勒克莱尔被处罚，最终排名第10，汉密尔顿排名第7，车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔称这"完全不能接受"。

    "显然，这是不能接受的。首先，有一点和墨西哥一样，因为你要考虑车队，考虑在马拉内洛工作的所有人，考虑所有利益相关者，包括赞助商，"瓦瑟尔在比赛后对天空体育说。

    "我们传达了信息给查尔斯，让他不要超车，因为在这样的比赛中，正如你之前说的，你必须超越前面的车，否则就会被别人超越。这意味着你必须完成你的比赛。"

    “查尔斯，这显然是一个对车队造成很大惩罚的错误。在今天这是完全不能接受的。老实说，我非常生气，因为墨西哥站之后那个信息已经很明确了。”

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、查尔斯·勒克莱尔、法拉利

    在《红旗播客》上讨论碰撞事件时，斯特尼尔表示：“会有所改变的。”他承认自己不知道具体时间，并补充说：“你能想象昨天法拉利董事会的情况吗？”

    斯特尼尔认为，解决这种情况的责任并不完全在于车队总监。

    “我已经说过好几次了，这不仅仅是因为弗雷德。有一大群决策者，”斯特尼尔说。

    “这不仅是在两位车手和弗雷德之间讨论。这是在董事会中讨论。每个人都会对如何处理这件事有自己的看法。

    “在董事会中，真正能够做出决定的人会有不同的意见，因此，不会有结论。”

    Guenther Steiner expects Ferrari board to react after Lewis Hamilton-Charles Leclerc clash

    Charles Leclerc spun Lewis Hamilton during their fight for sixth at the Las Vegas Grand Prix

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Former Haas Formula 1 team principal Guenther Steiner believes Ferrari will make changes after Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc clashed again at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Leclerc spun Hamilton around while the pair fought over sixth place around the streets of Las Vegas, only three weeks after another clash at the Mexican Grand Prix. Leclerc was penalised and finished 10th, with Hamilton seventh, and team principal Fred Vasseur called it "completely unacceptable".

    "Clearly, it's unacceptable. First, a little bit the same message as Mexico, because you have to think about the team, about the people working in Maranello, about all the stakeholders, the sponsors," Vasseur told Sky Sports after the race.

    "We passed the message to Charles to not overtake, because in this kind of race, as you were saying before, you have to overtake the cars in front of you or you are passed by the others. It means that you have to do your race.

    "And Charles, it's clearly a mistake that is penalising the team a lot. And it's completely unacceptable today. Honestly, I'm more than upset because that message was clear after Mexico."

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari

    Discussing the clash on The Red Flags Podcast, Steiner said: "Something will change." He admitted he did not know the timing, adding: "Can you imagine in the Ferrari boardroom after yesterday?"

    Steiner argued that the responsibility for resolving the situation does not rest solely with the team chief.

    "I said it already a few times, it's not only down to Fred. There is a big group of decision-makers," Steiner said.

    "This is not only discussed between the two drivers and Fred. This is discussed in the boardroom. Every man and his dog will have an opinion about how it should be dealt with.

    "In the boardroom, the people who actually can decide will have different opinions, and therefore, there will be no conclusion."

    With two rounds remaining in the season, Ferrari sits second in the constructors' championship with 517 points. Red Bull is third on 440 points and McLaren fourth with 431 points. Mercedes claimed the title at the Brazilian Grand Prix.
