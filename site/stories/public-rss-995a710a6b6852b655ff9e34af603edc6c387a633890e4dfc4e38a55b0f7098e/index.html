    2026年F1阿塞拜疆大奖赛：巴库戏剧性周末的全面复盘

    2026年阿塞拜疆大奖赛充满戏剧性：卫冕世界冠军呼吁对另一位车手禁赛、两起队友相撞，以及2002年以来最接近的冲线差距。兰多·诺里斯认为弗兰科·科拉平托应因安全车重启时引发事故而被禁赛，但多数同行与干事并不认同，仅对其处以马来西亚站退后五位发车的处罚。

    现役世界冠军呼吁禁赛另一名车手，两起队友相撞事故，以及自2002年以来最接近的冲线差距——2026年F1阿塞拜疆大奖赛确实带来了我们早已习惯在巴库看到的那种戏剧性。

    以下是本周末我们了解到的一切……

    兰多·诺里斯的许多同行并不认同他的观点，即弗朗哥·科拉平托因在巴库安全车第一次重启时引发的事故应该被禁赛——赛事干事们也不这么认为。

    干事们认为，对这位阿尔派车手在马来西亚站罚退五个发车位（由于科拉平托未能完赛，以此代替比赛中仅罚时10秒）已经是足够合适的处罚了。

    国际汽联希望将处罚重点放在违规行为本身，而不是造成的后果。尽管如此，罚退五位还是显得有点轻描淡写，尤其是今年驾照扣分处罚制度已经形同虚设。

    国际汽联在之前曾想放宽一些因荒唐原因而扣分的情况，结果现在出现了过度矫正。如今，单纯造成一次碰撞已不再被认为值得扣分。通常来说，必须是很危险的行为或故意行为才行。

    这削弱了一个本应用于惩罚惯犯的制度的初衷——该制度规定，如果在12个月内累计扣满12分，将自动禁赛一场。很少有人能否认，如果科拉平托做出三次这样的行为（每次扣四分），禁赛处罚是应得的。

    F1处罚制度的糟糕一天还因为林布拉德被完全免责而雪上加霜。他在同一次重新发车时驾驶失误，撞上了小红牛队友劳森，运气好才没有导致一辆或多辆赛车直接退赛。

    放林德布拉德一马，被轻描淡写地解释为科拉平托在前方撞车所引发的连锁反应所致。但回看回放和车载镜头，其实没有任何证据支持这种说法。

    更可能的解释是那条老的非官方政策——干事通常不介入队友之间的碰撞。有时确实会介入，但这次没有，或许第一圈（重启后）的宽容又一次起到了作用。——斯科特·米切尔-马尔姆

    科拉平托知道国际汽联（FIA）会因这起连环撞车而如何处罚他，但巴库站留下的悬念是，他自己的车队会怎么做。

    实际上的车队老板弗拉维奥·布里亚托利对科拉平托的措辞极为严厉，而那句悬而未决的威胁——'我想花时间反思，并考虑应采取什么行动'——是被刻意放在车队官方新闻稿中的，而不是在电视采访中怒气冲冲地脱口而出。这是一个经过深思熟虑的信息，布里亚托利和阿尔卑斯希望科拉平托乃至整个F1世界都能领会——据我们的了解，布里亚托利对这起事故的愤怒，正如其新闻稿措辞所暗示的那样强烈。

    真正让人痛心的是车队总冠军之争中受到的损伤。本周末阿尔派对本站排名第五的对手小红牛（小红牛）占据上风，原本有望把双方的差距缩小到6分。可如今这个差距不但没缩小，反而扩大到了15分。

    阿尔派真如诺里斯所愿、主动替科拉平托承担禁赛的可能性极低，我们也不认为车队内部会对车手施加任何形式的经济处罚。

    更可能的情况是，这只是一次严厉的警告，也许之后会伴随某种形式的官方内部“黄牌”，并暗示如果再有下次，后果可能严重到直接停赛。

    但这可是布里亚托利，所以谁也说不准。如果车队不公开发声，下周在马来西亚站，媒体肯定会向阿尔派和科拉平托抛出一连串问题，追问幕后到底发生了什么。——马特·比尔（Matt Beer）

    梅赛德斯在巴库凭借拉塞尔的统治性表现，终结了连续四站没有杆位的尴尬，赛车上明显展现出其车身与动力单元套装在全场的领先实力。

    随着拉塞尔在城市的弯弯曲曲中如鱼得水，W17 和 2026 款梅赛德斯动力单元的全面实力得到充分展示，最后一段赛程中即便维斯塔潘拥有惊人的尾流优势以及身后超车模式的额外加成，拉塞尔也刚好守住了领先。"我们的赛车在低下压力赛道上表现得异常出色，"拉塞尔说。

    梅赛德斯的竞争对手们在阿塞拜疆的排名又一次互相易位：红牛强势崛起，法拉利有所退步，迈凯伦则度过了一个喜忧参半的周末（随后结果惨淡）。这位积分榜领跑者继续在所有地方保持强劲，甚至是最强——而且它即将再次改变竞争格局。

    梅赛德斯一套重要的空气动力学升级即将在下一站马来西亚大奖赛推出，拉塞尔表示"我们希望在与其他车队的差距上迈出更大的一步"。

    但这还不是全部。我们仍在等待梅赛德斯的升级版引擎亮相——它目前定于下个月的美国大奖赛推出，这将为拉塞尔和安东内利在赛季冲刺阶段提供一件更锋利的武器。——斯科特·米切尔-马尔姆

    虽然他的继任者尚未签约，但奥孔实际上已经知道他在哈斯的时间结束了。

    他未能说服哈斯在最初两年的合约期满后继续留用他，车队很可能选择一名速度快的新秀（F2 积分榜领先者卡马拉是最热门人选）来取代他。

    但即使这几乎已成定局，也不会阻止奥孔奋力一搏。他决心证明自己仍然配得上留在F1，即使他为2027年在其他车队找到空缺的机会看起来微乎其微。

    这就是为什么奥孔在周五排位赛后大发雷霆，他认为一次时机不当的黄旗让他失去了进入第三节排位赛（Q3）的资格；他说：“不同的比赛周末，同样的破事”。

    他在大奖赛中的表现非常出色，完成了一次精彩的追回，在重新发车时从内侧俯冲超过劳森，仅仅一个弯角之后又大胆地超过了自己的队友奥利·比尔曼。

    奥孔知道这是他取得领先、成为哈斯车队在本场比赛结束时积分最多的车手的机会。

    他的第八名只是他本赛季第三次获得积分；他说：“我可以说这是我应得的，而不是今年其余那些比赛。”

    他在赛后给书面媒体的最终回答，很能说明他如今的心态。

    "我和场上所有人都交过手，我一直都不逊色于任何人，或者说在我参加过的每个锦标赛里我都拿过冠军，"奥康说。

    "我赢过雷诺方程式的比赛，赢过卡丁车的锦标赛，赢过F3，赢过GP3，在德国房车大师赛跑了半个赛季也在前十名里竞争过，我还在F1里赢过比赛。

    "我做得到，你知道吗？我是场上最顶尖的车手之一，而且我不怕说出来。" —— 乔什·萨蒂尔

    随着法拉利在2026年最大的优势被逐渐蚕食，它仍然被自己最大的劣势所拖累。

    尽管在蒙扎引入了ADUO 2升级，法拉利的引擎仍然达不到梅赛德斯或红牛的水平，这在巴库全长2.2公里（1.4英里）的全油门直道上暴露无遗。

    车队领队瓦塞尔解释说：“以我们目前引擎的状况，我们每圈要损失一个位次，然后就失去了比赛的节奏。”

    “最大的限制在于，当你处于这种境地并想要防守时，就会陷入一种恶性循环——也许会尝试使用比最优值略低一些的下压力，因为你需要防守，而这又会让你损失圈速，这种循环非常常见。我们在蒙扎也遇到过类似的情况。”

    这意味着勒克莱尔在周五能拿到发车位的第二位，某种程度上算是超常发挥。

    正如瓦塞尔所解释的，法拉利要到2027年才能真正解决其主要劣势，而对手们已经能够削弱它在底盘上可能拥有的任何优势。

    瓦塞尔说：“底盘方面也存在某种趋同；在底盘上你可以比在引擎上进步得快得多。”

    “底盘方面的性能存在某种趋同，而引擎方面则没有这种趋同。”

    英文原文

    The reigning world champion calling for another driver to be banned, two different team-mate collisions and the closest finish since 2002 - Formula 1's 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix certainly delivered the drama we've come to expect from Baku.

    Here's everything we learned from the weekend…

    Many of Lando Norris's peers did not share his view Franco Colapinto deserved a race ban for the accident he caused at the first safety car restart in Baku - and neither did the stewards.

    A five-place grid penalty for Malaysia (in lieu of just a 10-second time penalty in the race as Colapinto did not finish) was all the stewards saw fit to give the Alpine driver.

    The FIA wants to focus on punishing the offence rather than the outcome. Still, five places is something of a light touch, especially as licence penalty points have gone out the window this year.

    There's been an overcorrection from the FIA after wanting to ease back on some of the silly things penalty points were given for previously. Now, causing a collision alone is not considered worthy of them. Generally, it must be dangerous or deliberate.

    That undermines the purpose of a system designed to punish repeat offenders with an automatic one-race ban if they get 12 penalty points in a 12 month period. Few could argue that if Colapinto did that three times (at four penalty points a pop) a suspension would be merited.

    And a bad day for F1's penalty system was compounded by Arvid Lindblad being completely exonerated for his own poor driving at the same restart, when he clattered into Racing Bulls team-mate Liam Lawson and was lucky not to wipe one or more cars out of the race.

    Letting Lindblad off the hook was meekly justified as being a result of the knock-on effect of the Colapinto crash ahead. Nothing really supports this watching back replays and onboards.

    A more likely explanation is the old, unofficial ‘the stewards don't usually get involved with team-mate-on-team-mate collisions' policy. Sometimes it happens but not here, perhaps again helped by some lap-one (restart) leniency. - Scott Mitchell-Malm

    Colapinto knows what the FIA's doing to punish him for the pile-up, but the open question coming out of Baku is what his own team will do.

    The extremely strong comments about Colapinto from de facto team boss Flavio Briatore and the hanging threat of "I want to take time to reflect on it and consider what course of action should be taken" were deliberately placed in the team's official press release rather than being blurted out mid-anger in a TV interview. This was a considered message that Briatore and Alpine wanted to make sure Colapinto, and the F1 world, took on board - and our understanding is Briatore's anger at the incident is as strong as his press release phrasing implies.

    It's the constructors' championship battle damage that hurts. Alpine had the edge on fifth-place rival Racing Bulls this weekend and was on course to trim its deficit to six points. Now that gap is up to 15 instead.

    Alpine actually fulfilling Norris's wish and giving Colapinto its own race ban is extremely unlikely, and we wouldn't anticipate any kind of internal financial penalty either.

    It feels more likely this is intended as a strong warning, perhaps one that will be followed with some form of official internal 'yellow card' and a hint that if there's a next time then the consequences could be as strong as a suspension.

    But this is Briatore, so you never know. If nothing is said publicly, expect plenty of questions to Alpine and Colapinto in Malaysia next week about what's gone on behind closed doors. - Matt Beer

    Mercedes ended a four-race run without a pole position with George Russell's dominant turn in Baku, where its car and engine package was clearly the class of the field.

    The all-round strength of the W17 and the 2026 Mercedes power unit was on full display as Russell thrived through the city's twists and turns, then had just enough to keep Max Verstappen at bay even with a prodigious tow and the extra benefit of the overtake mode behind him in the final stint. "Our car has been working exceptionally well on the low-drag circuits," Russell said.

    Mercedes' rivals traded places in the pecking order yet again in Azerbaijan as Red Bull came to the fore, Ferrari slipped back, and McLaren had a mixed weekend (then a terrible result). The championship leader continues to be strong, or strongest, everywhere - and it's about to shift the goalposts again.

    A significant Mercedes aerodynamic update is coming for the next race in Malaysia, with which Russell said "we're hoping to make a bigger step to the rest of the field".

    But that's not all. We're still waiting for Mercedes' upgraded engine to appear - and that's now due at the United States Grand Prix next month, giving Russell and Kimi Antonelli an even sharper weapon to fight with during the run-in. - Scott Mitchell-Malm

    While his replacement is yet to be signed, Esteban Ocon effectively knows his time at Haas is over.

    He's lost the battle to convince Haas to stick with him beyond their initial two-year agreement and the team's likely to opt for a fast rookie (F2 points leader Rafael Camara is the favourite) to replace him.

    But even if that is almost a foregone conclusion, it's not going to stop Ocon from going down swinging. He's determined to prove he still deserves to stay in F1, even if his chances of finding a vacancy elsewhere for 2027 look minuscule.

    It's why Ocon was furious after qualifying on Friday when he felt an ill-timed yellow flag cost him a place in Q3; "It's different race weekend, same s***".

    He drove brilliantly to recover in the grand prix, diving down the inside of Lawson on the restart, then pulling off a bold move on his team-mate Ollie Bearman just one corner later.

    Ocon knew that was his chance to get ahead and be the Haas driver with the biggest points total at the end of the race.

    His eighth place is only his third points finish of the season; "I can say I deserved that, not all the rest of this year."

    His final answer to written media after the race was telling of his mindset right now.

    "I've raced with everyone out there, and I've always been on par, or I've won every championship that I've raced in," Ocon said.

    "I've won races in Formula Renault, I've won championships in go-karts, I've won Formula 3, I've won GP3, I was racing in the top 10 in DTM for half a season, and I won in Formula 1.

    "I can do it, you know? I'm one of the best out there, and I'm not afraid to say it." - Josh Suttill

    As Ferrari's biggest 2026 advantage has been eroded away, it's still been hamstrung by its biggest disadvantage.

    Despite the ADUO 2 update introduced at Monza, Ferrari's engine is not on the level of the Mercedes or Red Bull, and that was exposed across Baku's 2.2km (1.4-mile) flat-out run.

    "With the situation that we are in with the engine, we are losing one position per lap, and then we lose the path of the race," team boss Fred Vasseur explained.

    "The biggest limitation is that when you are in this situation and you want to defend, you go in a kind of negative spiral, perhaps to try to run a bit lower downforce than is optimum, because you need to defend and you are losing laptime, and it's quite often this kind of spiral. We had a similar case in Monza."

    It means Charles Leclerc's second place on the grid was something of an overachievement on Friday.

    As Vasseur explained, Ferrari can't properly address its main deficit until 2027 while rivals have been able to take lumps out of any chassis advantage it might have.

    "There is also a kind of convergence on the chassis; you can improve much quicker than with the engine," Vasseur said.

    "There is a kind of convergence of performance on the chassis that you don't have on the engine."
