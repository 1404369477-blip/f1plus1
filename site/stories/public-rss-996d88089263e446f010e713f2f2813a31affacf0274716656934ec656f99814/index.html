    维斯塔潘赢得拉斯维加斯大奖赛，法拉利内斗

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛顶住两辆梅赛德斯的追击夺冠，法拉利的勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿发生碰撞。

    F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛：维斯塔潘赢得惊险大战，法拉利车手发生碰撞

    随着“拉锯式”争夺重回F1，维斯塔潘虽多次受到冲击，但仍坚持到最后夺冠，而勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿发生碰撞

    马克斯·维斯塔潘（红牛车队）、乔治·拉塞尔（梅赛德斯）

    维斯塔潘在一场跌宕起伏的拉斯维加斯大奖赛中夺冠，顶住了两辆梅赛德斯的追击，F1新奇的“拉锯式”争夺回归——最终领奖台上没有梅赛德斯车手。

    由于排位赛最快圈速创造者基米·安东内利因超出引擎配额被罚退五位发车，马克斯·维斯塔潘继承杆位，并在起步时守住领先位置，乔治·拉塞尔紧随其后，前五名顺序不变：兰多·诺里斯、伊萨克·哈贾尔和阿尔维德·林德布拉德跟在后面——不过哈贾尔在第一圈就迅速超越了迈凯伦车手。

    安东内利是前十名中唯一使用中性胎起步的车手，起步时被两辆阿尔派赛车超越，落到第八位，排在法拉利车手之前，而法拉利车手在第二节排位赛就被淘汰——不过弗朗哥·科拉平托很快从第七跌至第十，随后跌出积分区。

    维斯塔潘无法甩开拉塞尔，这位英国车手在第二圈短暂抢走领先位置，但荷兰人立即做出回应。拉塞尔无法再次攻击领先者，反而被红牛的另一辆赛车超越。现在轮到这两辆车交换位置……直到林德布拉德出人意料地连续超越诺里斯和拉塞尔，尽管他无法挡住梅赛德斯车手。

    与此同时，哈贾尔正在向维斯塔潘施压争夺领先位置，但不得不应对拉塞尔的攻击。“作为车队，我们不能浪费时间，”法国人被告知。“他能停止防守吗？”他询问队友的驾驶方式。哈贾尔不久后短暂取得领先，但片刻之后又跌至第三。

    第10圈时，前十名赛车之间的差距仍在四秒以内。拉塞尔短暂地（再次）夺回领先位置，皮埃尔·加斯利则奋力争夺第三名，但他没能长时间挡住哈贾尔。前四名的位置一度没有变化，但诺里斯的颓势仍在继续，最终跌出了前十。

    拉塞尔在第14圈和第18圈重新取得领先，但这同样是14号弯的超越动作，维斯塔潘在起终点直道上又反超回来。第二次时，哈贾尔悄悄从第二位溜了过去。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    新科世界冠军安东内利在第17圈升至第四，从第一圈的第八名一路追回。与此同时，法拉利车手们既要与对手缠斗，又要在内部争夺第七名。夏尔·勒克莱尔提醒队友“小心这种挤压”；刘易斯·汉密尔顿说同队赛车应该“赶紧退后”，并因离开赛道获利被罚10秒。

    第19圈安全车出动，因为塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯把拉斐尔·卡马拉转得打转并撞坏了他的前悬挂；几乎所有赛车都立即进站——红牛的双车同圈进站让拉塞尔悄悄回到第二——但刘易斯·汉密尔顿没有，因为这位七届世界冠军在队友前面的赛道位置以及他的10秒发车位罚时。为了不耽误维修区里的另一辆赛车，他在下一圈才换胎，出来后排在第14位。

    与此同时，原本第六的林德布拉德在安全车状态下莫名打转并退赛，这让诺里斯回到第六，排在勒克莱尔和加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托之前。

    第26圈绿旗挥动，哈贾尔的前翼自行断裂。这位红牛车手进站，加斯利因此继承第三，但在第31圈被复苏的诺里斯超越。与此同时，汉密尔顿只用一圈就杀回前十。

    随后拉塞尔再次夺回领先——同样非常短暂——但他不断尝试超越维斯塔潘，这让诺里斯加入战局，这位英国人此时挑战——并短暂超越——领先者。

    拉塞尔夺回了第二名，并在第37圈终于在1号弯而非14号弯找到了超越维斯塔潘的方法，但他仍然无法守住领先位置。安东内利是前十名中唯一使用软胎的车手，甚至在第38圈从队友手中抢走了第二名，并在一圈后短暂超越维斯塔潘取得领先。

    与此同时，在更靠后的位置上，勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿在争夺第六名时发生碰撞，后者被撞得打转，最终跌至第10位。勒克莱尔则掉到第八名，并被罚时10秒。

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利车队，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利车队

    到目前为止，维斯塔潘被证明只是暂时可被攻击的，但这位红牛车手担心的是比赛尾声。“你们必须关注最后一圈的能量部署，因为他们已经搞明白了，他们可以在冲线前一直压在我前面，所以我们需要看看最后一圈我们该怎么做，”他在车队无线电中说道。

    安东内利和维斯塔潘一次又一次地交换领先位置，第43圈冲线时安东内利以0.001秒的优势领先，这印证了荷兰人此前提到的担忧。然而，红牛告知其车手，最后一圈没有更好的能量部署策略可用。

    第48圈，拉塞尔试图超越安东内利争夺第二名，但锁死轮胎撞上护墙，跌至第九位，似乎让前两名车手得以争夺胜利……但安东内利在第49圈末段失去位置，掉到了两辆迈凯伦之后。

    因此维斯塔潘从杆位夺冠，领先于诺里斯和皮亚斯特里。诺里斯从第三位发车，一度跌至第11位，最终追至亚军；皮亚斯特里从第15位发车，一路奋战登上领奖台。

    安东内利守住了第四名，领先于阿尔派车手加斯利和科拉平托；而法拉利的汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔分别只获得令人失望的第七名和第十名，两人之间夹着塞恩斯和拉塞尔。

    哈贾尔的前翼断裂后，他只能排在第14位，紧随其后的是劳森。劳森原本应在发车格上排第七，但因燃油泄漏不得不从维修区起步，此后一直未能恢复。

    原本应从第14位发车的阿尔本，尽管天气只有12摄氏度，却遇到了刹车过热问题，无法参加比赛。

    F1 Las Vegas GP: Max Verstappen wins thriller as Ferraris come to blows

    As ‘yo-yo’ racing returned to Formula 1, Verstappen was knocked around but held on for victory, while Leclerc and Hamilton collided

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    Max Verstappen won an eventful Las Vegas Grand Prix, holding off both Mercedes as Formula 1’s novel ‘yo-yo’ racing returned – with no Silver Arrow on the eventual podium.

    With qualifying pacesetter Kimi Antonelli taking a five-place grid penalty for exceeding his engine allocation, Max Verstappen inherited pole position and retained the lead from George Russell at the start with an unchanged top five: Lando Norris, Isack Hadjar and Arvid Lindblad followed – though Hadjar swiftly found a way past the McLaren on lap one.

    The only top-10 driver starting on mediums, Antonelli lost two spots to the Alpines at lights out, finding himself eighth ahead of the Ferraris, who were out in Q2 – though Franco Colapinto would soon collapse from seventh to 10th, then out of the points-paying positions.

    Verstappen couldn’t break away from Russell, and the Briton briefly snatched the lead away on lap two before the Dutchman immediately responded. Russell couldn’t attack the leader again and was instead overtaken by the sister Red Bull car. Now it was these two trading places… until Lindblad remarkably overtook Norris and Russell in quick succession, even though he could not hold the Mercedes off.

    Meanwhile, Hadjar was pressuring Verstappen for the lead but had to deal with Russell’s attacks. “We need to not lose time as a team,” the Frenchman was told. “Can he stop defending?” he inquired about his team-mate’s driving. Hadjar briefly took the lead soon after, though he dropped to third moments later.

    On lap 10, the top 10 cars still lay within four seconds. Russell momentarily retook the lead (again) as Pierre Gasly fought to third, though he couldn’t hold off Hadjar for long. There were no changes among the top four for a moment, but Norris’ collapse continued as he ended up outside the top 10.

    Russell took the lead back on laps 14 and 18, but this was again Turn 14 moves with Verstappen sweeping back past on the start/finish straight. The second time, Hadjar snuck by in second.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    New world champion Antonelli climbed to fourth on lap 17, recovering from eighth on lap one. Meanwhile, the Ferraris were battling both their rivals and among themselves for seventh place. Charles Leclerc called on his team-mate to “be careful with this squeezing”; Lewis Hamilton said the sister car should “back the f**k off” – and copped a 10-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage.

    The safety car was called into action on lap 19 as Sergio Perez spun Rafael Camara around and broke his front suspension; nearly all cars pitted straightaway – Red Bull’s double-stack allowing Russell to snuck back into second – but not Lewis Hamilton, due to the seven-time world champion’s track position ahead of his team-mate and his 10-second grid penalty. In order not to delay the sister car in the pits, he changed tyres on the following lap and emerged back out in 14th.

    Meanwhile, sixth-placed Lindblad had an odd spin in safety-car conditions and retired from the race, promoting Norris back to sixth ahead of Leclerc and Gabriel Bortoleto.

    The green flag was waved on lap 26, and Hadjar’s front wing broke on its own. The Red Bull driver pitted, with Gasly inheriting third before losing out to a resurgent Norris on lap 31. Meanwhile, Hamilton needed just one lap to fight his way back into the top 10.

    Russell retook the lead then – again ever so briefly – but his incessant attempts to surpass Verstappen brought Norris into play, with the Briton now challenging – and temporarily overtaking – the leader.

    Russell recovered second place, and finally found a way past Verstappen into Turn 1 on lap 37, rather than Turn 14, but he still couldn’t hold on to the lead. Antonelli, the only driver on soft tyres in the top 10, even took second place away from his team-mate on lap 38 and briefly passed Verstappen for the lead one lap later.

    At the same time, further down the field, Leclerc and Hamilton clashed as they battled each other for sixth, with the latter being spun around and ending up down to 10th. Leclerc dropped down to eighth and copped a 10-second penalty.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    So far, Verstappen had proved to be only temporarily assailable, but the Red Bull driver was worrying about the end of the race. “You guys have to look at the deployment of the final lap because they’ve figured it out, that they can stay ahead of me until the line, so we need to see what we need to do for the final lap only,” he said on team radio.

    Antonelli and Verstappen traded the lead again and again, with Antonelli 0.001s ahead on the finish line on lap 43, vindicating the Dutchman’s aforementioned concern. However, Red Bull let its driver know that there was no better deployment strategy available for the final lap.

    On lap 48, Russell attempted to overtake Antonelli for second place but locked up and hit the wall, dropping to ninth, seemingly letting the top two drivers fight it out for the win… but Antonelli lost ground at the end of lap 49 and dropped behind the McLarens.

    Verstappen therefore triumphed from pole ahead of Norris, who had started third but dropped to 11th before driving to the runner-up spot, and Piastri, who was 15th on the grid and fought to a podium finish.

    Antonelli saved fourth ahead of Alpine duo Gasly and Colapinto, while the Ferraris of Hamilton and Leclerc were a disappointing seventh and 10th respectively, separated by Carlos Sainz and Russell.

    Hadjar couldn’t do better than 14th after his front wing broke, right ahead of Lawson, who was supposed to be seventh on the grid but had to start from the pitlane due to a fuel leak and never recovered.

    Alex Albon, who was supposed to start from 14th, encountered a brake overheating issue despite 12C weather and couldn’t take part in the race.
