    哈斯墨西哥城站换下奥康，由平川亮接替

    哈斯车队在墨西哥城大奖赛剩余赛程中由平川亮接替奥康，未说明原因，并确认奥康并非因病缺席。

    埃斯特班·奥康：哈斯在墨西哥城大奖赛周末剩余赛程中由平川亮接替这位法国车手，未提供原因

    埃斯特班·奥康将不再参加墨西哥城大奖赛余下环节，哈斯由平川亮接替这位法国车手；哈斯未说明换下奥康的原因；奥康将在本赛季结束后离开车队

    埃斯特班·奥康已在哈斯被平川亮接替，参加墨西哥城大奖赛剩余赛程。

    车队未说明换下奥康的原因，但确认这位法国车手并非患病。

    奥康在第二次练习赛后以第13名完赛，在车队无线电中发泄了自己的不满，并且将不再参加本站比赛的任何环节，因为平川亮将在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道顶替他的发车位。

    奥康质疑了车队在周五第二次练习赛中让他使用中性胎而非软胎的决定。

    这位法国车手在被赛事工程师劳拉·穆勒告知其驾驶计划有变后，恳求道“别这样对我”。

    哈斯车队最近确认，奥康将在本赛季结束后离开这支美国所有的车队，他已在队效力两年。

    车队声明写道：“TGR哈斯F1车队储备车手平川亮将在本周末墨西哥城大奖赛的剩余赛程中替换埃斯特班·奥康，时间为10月31日星期六至11月1日星期日。”

    “平川亮（32岁）曾随丰田GAZOO Racing两度赢得国际汽联世界耐力锦标赛冠军（2022年、2023年），并夺得勒芒24小时耐力赛冠军（2022年）。他于10月30日星期五在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道参加了第一节自由练习，这是他本赛季第三次驾驶VF-26赛车出战自由练习，最终位列全场第15名。”

    “这位日本车手将在星期六的第三节自由练习和排位赛以及星期日的71圈大奖赛中与队友奥利·贝尔曼搭档——这将是平川亮的F1正赛首秀。”

    Esteban Ocon: Haas replace French driver with Ryo Hirakawa for remainder of Mexico City GP weekend without providing reason

    Esteban Ocon will take no further part in the Mexico City GP, with Haas replacing French driver with Ryo Hirakawa; Haas gave no reason for Ocon's replacement; Ocon is leaving the team at the end of the season

    Esteban Ocon has been replaced by Ryo Hirakawa at Haas for the remainder of the Mexico City Grand Prix.

    The team provided no reason for replacing Ocon, but confirmed the Frenchman was not suffering from illness.

    Ocon vented his frustrations on the team radio while finishing 13th following second practice and will take no further part in the race as Hirakawa will take his place on the grid at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodríguez.

    Ocon questioned the team's decision to run him on the medium rather than soft tyre during second practice on Friday.

    The Frenchman pleaded "don't do this to me" to his race engineer Laura Mueller after being informed of a change to his run-plan.

    Haas recently confirmed that Ocon will be leaving the team at the end of this season after two years with the American-owned squad.

    A team statement read: "TGR Haas F1 Team reserve driver Ryō Hirakawa will replace Esteban Ocon for the remainder of the weekend's Mexico City Grand Prix, Saturday 31 October - Sunday 1 November.

    "Hirakawa (32), a two-time winner of the FIA World Endurance Championship (2022, 2023) and 24 Hours of Le Mans Winner (2022) with Toyota GAZOO Racing, participated in FP1 at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez on Friday 30 October - his third free practice outing of the season driving the VF-26, finishing 15th overall.

    "The Japanese driver will partner teammate Ollie Bearman through final practice and qualifying on Saturday and Sunday's 71-lap grand prix - Hirakawa's Formula 1 race debut."
