    麦凯轮指出卡塔尔大奖赛27圈规则中的关键提前进站陷阱

    Formula 1本周重返洛塞尔国际赛道，面临熟悉的策略防护栏和灯光熄灭后的微妙陷阱。在2026年卡塔尔大奖赛之前，倍耐力和国际汽联第三次对每条轮胎的严格最长行驶圈数限制进行了调整，将上限从2025年的25圈提高到周日57圈赛事中的27圈。

    麦凯轮在卡塔尔27圈规则中提出了关键的提前进站陷阱

    麦凯轮警告在卡塔尔新的27圈F1轮胎限制下存在提前进站的三个停站陷阱，因为它在追逐小红牛的P3位置

    兰多·诺里斯，麦凯轮，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，麦凯轮

    对于麦凯轮的粉丝来说。来自沃金斯的木瓜新闻，有关车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的比赛。

    一级方程式赛车本周末重返洛塞尔国际赛道，带来熟悉的战略防护栏——以及一个在熄灯后稍作调整的陷阱。

    在2026年卡塔尔大奖赛之前，倍耐力与国际汽联第三次重新实施了对每条轮胎的严格最长行驶圈数限制，将上限从2025年的25圈提高到周日57圈赛程中的27圈。

    在麦凯轮队的官方赛前预告中，赛车工程副总监阿德里安·古德温解释说，这一上限取消了传统的单站轮胎节省策略，将焦点完全转移到时机和轮胎复合材料的保持上。

    “今年的最长行驶圈数上限为27圈，比去年的25圈有所增加，因为倍耐力能够提高这一限制，得益于2026年车型的更低空气动力学负荷和轮胎结构的加强，”古德温说。

    “比赛共有57圈，而不是在一站式比赛中规划最佳续航速度，我们需要确定哪种轮胎配方能给我们带来最佳的三次冲刺续航，以及哪些轮胎配方需要保留用于比赛。”

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂，麦克拉伦，兰多·诺里斯，麦克拉伦

    尽管倍耐力的最硬三款轮胎——C1硬胎、C2中胎和C3软胎，与铃鹿和银石赛道相匹配——必须仍然在12到15弯吸收大量的横向能量，古德温指出，23到25摄氏度的凉爽环境温度和2026年更慢的转弯速度应该会减轻与过去访问相比的惩罚。即便如此，固定的圈数上限仍然存在一个无法容忍的陷阱。

    固定的续航长度实际上使轮胎比赛变得更加简单，因为我们不是试图在轮胎寿命结束时管理它们；主要的战略点是不要过早停车，否则你会被迫进行三站式比赛，”古德温警告。

    如何通过Pirelli的计分规则来缩小第3圈截止时间

    数学计算几乎没有留下任何余地。两个完整的27圈练习覆盖了54圈，这意味着第3圈是车手最早可以进站的时间，并且还能在两停策略下完成比赛——前提是剩余的两套轮胎都是全新的。在早期事故或安全车阶段在第1圈或第2圈进站，将剩下55或56圈要跑，锁定该车辆进入第三次进站。

    Pirelli的计分规则进一步缩小了这个差距。虽然侦察圈、排位圈和赛后冷却圈被排除在外，但从发车灯亮起到比赛结束的每一圈都计入，包括安全车和虚拟安全车阶段，并且圈数在整个周末累计。

    在实际练习和排位赛中，任何开始但未完成的圈数仍然算作完整的一圈，如果车队混合了不同年龄的轮胎，最高里程数的轮胎决定了整个套件的限制。即使是单一排位赛在比赛轮胎上的外圈也会削减周日的上限，并将安全的第一停站窗口推后。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂，麦凯轮，兰多·诺里斯，麦凯轮

    2026年的一项有益的改革是取消卡塔尔的冲刺赛制。去年12套冲刺轮胎的分配改为每位车手获得13套干胎——两套硬胎、四套中胎和七套软胎，这为工程师提供了更多的灵活性，以便为周日的比赛保留新鲜轮胎。

    2025年的幽灵和与红牛的九分之战

    2025年，当25圈的圈数上限使得第7圈成为两次进站窗口的精确数学起点时，尼科·胡肯伯格和皮埃尔·加斯利的碰撞引发的第7圈安全车几乎将整个车阵——包括马克斯·维斯塔潘——直接送入维修区，进行连续的两次25圈赛段。麦凯轮车队将杆位车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂和兰多·诺里斯留在了赛道上，放弃了赛道位置，让维斯塔潘连续第三次赢得洛杉矶的胜利，而皮亚斯蒂获得了第二名，诺里斯第四名。

    那堂课在2026年的倒数第二轮比赛中具有真正的分量。在诺里斯赢得圣保罗胜利以及拉斯维加斯的双料领奖台（诺里斯第二，皮亚斯特里第三）之后，梅赛德斯车队以431分排在车队积分榜第四位——仅比红牛（440分）少9分，在卡塔尔和阿布扎比还有86分可用。诺里斯与乔治·拉塞尔积分相同，264分，距离刘易斯·汉密尔顿在车手积分榜上第三位还差3分。

    在洛塞尔国际赛道流畅的、类似MotoGP的布局下，没有像拉斯维加斯那样的创纪录的352次超车，古德温指出车库面临的挑战："我们知道我们至少要完成四次有竞争力的维修区停站，我们还需要与车手们合作，以获得最佳轮胎速度。"

    在过去的三个卡塔尔大奖赛中，梅赛德斯车队都达到了领奖台，车队知道速度通常都在那里——只要保持在27圈数学计算的合理范围内。

    McLaren flags the crucial early-stop trap inside Qatar’s raised 27-lap rule

    McLaren warns of an early-stop three-stop trap under Qatar's new 27-lap F1 tyre limit as it chases Red Bull for P3

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Oscar Piastri, McLaren

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Formula 1 returns to the Losail International Circuit this weekend with a familiar strategic guardrail - and a subtly shifted trap waiting right after lights out.

    Ahead of the 2026 Qatar Grand Prix, Pirelli and the FIA have reimposed a strict maximum stint limit per tyre set for the third time in four years, raising the ceiling from 25 laps in 2025 to 27 laps across Sunday’s 57-lap distance.

    In McLaren’s official pre-race preview, associate director of car race engineering Adrian Goodwin explained that the cap strips away traditional one-stop tyre-saving, shifting the focus squarely onto timing and compound retention.

    "The maximum stint length is 27 laps this year, up from 25, as Pirelli have been able to increase the limit thanks to the lower aerodynamic load of the 2026 cars and a reinforced tyre construction," Goodwin said.

    "With a 57-lap race, rather than planning for best-stint pace in a one-stop race, we need to determine which compounds give us the best three sprint stints – and which to retain for the race."

    Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    While Pirelli’s hardest trio - C1 Hard, C2 Medium, and C3 Soft, matching Suzuka and Silverstone - must still absorb heavy lateral energy through Turns 12 to 15, Goodwin noted that cooler 23-25C ambient temperatures and 2026’s slower cornering speeds should ease the punishment compared to past visits. Even so, the fixed cap carries one unforgiving pitfall.

    "The fixed stint lengths actually make it a simpler event for tyres, as we’re not trying to manage them beyond end of life; the main strategic point is not stopping so early that you’re forced into a three-stop race," Goodwin warned.

    How Pirelli’s counting rules tighten the lap 3 cutoff

    The maths leaves almost no breathing room. Two full 27-lap stints cover 54 laps, meaning Lap 3 is the absolute earliest a driver can pit and still reach the chequered flag on a two-stop strategy - and only if both remaining sets are brand new. Pitting on Lap 1 or Lap 2 during an early incident or safety car leaves 55 or 56 laps to go, locking that car into a third pitstop.

    Pirelli’s counting regulations narrow that margin further. While reconnaissance laps, formation laps, and post-race cool-down laps are excluded, every lap from lights out to the flag counts - including safety car and virtual safety car periods - and laps accumulate across the entire weekend.

    In practice and qualifying, any lap started but not completed still counts as a full lap, and if a team mixes tyres of different ages, the highest-mileage tyre dictates the entire set’s limit. Even a single qualifying out-lap on a race set trims its Sunday ceiling and pushes the safe first-stop window later.

    Oscar Piastri, McLaren, Lando Norris, McLaren

    One helpful change for 2026 is the removal of Qatar’s Sprint format. Instead of last year’s 12-set sprint allocation, each driver receives 13 dry sets - two hard, four medium, and seven soft - giving engineers more flexibility to preserve fresh rubber for Sunday.

    Ghosts of 2025 and a nine-point fight with Red Bull

    In 2025, when the 25-lap cap made Lap 7 the exact mathematical opening of the two-stop window, a Lap 7 safety car triggered by Nico Hülkenberg and Pierre Gasly’s clash sent almost the entire grid - including Max Verstappen - straight to the pitlane for back-to-back 25-lap stints. McLaren kept polesitter Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris out, surrendering track position and handing Verstappen his third straight Losail win while Piastri settled for second and Norris fourth.

    That lesson carries real weight entering the penultimate round of 2026. Fresh off Norris’s victory in Sao Paulo and a double podium in Las Vegas (Norris second, Piastri third), McLaren sits fourth in the constructors’ standings on 431 points - just nine behind Red Bull (440) with 86 points still available across Qatar and Abu Dhabi. Norris is also tied with George Russell on 264 points, three shy of Lewis Hamilton for third in the drivers’ standings.

    With Losail’s flowing, MotoGP-built layout offering nothing like Las Vegas’s record 352 overtakes, Goodwin noted the workload awaiting the garage: "We know we will complete at least four competitive pitstops, and we’ll still need to work with the drivers to get the best pace out of the tyres."

    Having reached the podium in each of the last three Qatar Grands Prix, McLaren knows the pace is usually there - provided it stays on the right side of the 27-lap maths.
