    维斯塔潘回应里卡多勒芒车队梦之队邀请

    维斯塔潘称前队友里卡多为选择他加入勒芒车队梦之队是明智之举，并透露两人已讨论过这一可能性。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘回应丹尼尔·里卡多的勒芒梦幻车队召唤

    红牛车手透露他和里卡多已经讨论过一次性的勒芒参赛，但他表示目前没有时间好好做这件事

    丹尼尔·里卡多，红牛车队第三车手，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    对于红牛车迷来说。关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘开玩笑说，他的前队友丹尼尔·里卡多选择他是“一个聪明的人”，因为他选择了他的梦想勒芒阵容。事实上，他透露，他们两人已经讨论了这种可能性。

    在一次接受Beyond the Grid播客的采访中，这位澳大利亚人谈到了他在红牛车队的日子以及他离开一级方程式的经历。虽然他讨论了他接下来要做什么，但他表示，勒芒的参赛可能在他的名单上。当被问及他会和谁搭档时，马克斯·维斯塔潘是他名单上的第一个名字。塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔也是一个选择，他称之为一种“美好的小红牛重聚”。

    “他选择我进车是一个聪明的人，”维斯塔潘开玩笑说。“但我们还得看看。这取决于很多因素。”

    这位四次世界冠军没有隐瞒他对耐力赛的兴趣，他最近在波尔蒂芒的比赛中就表现出了这一点，他和他的车队最终排名第9。然而，他强调，他只有在充分准备的情况下才会参加勒芒赛。

    “对我来说，如果我想做这件事，我想做得很好，做好充分的准备。但目前我做不到。我没有时间——空闲时间来投入到勒芒项目中，因为我还在做其他一些除了F1之外的事情。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，祝贺杆位车手丹尼尔·里卡多，红牛车队

    里卡多的评论是他第一次公开谈论以任何真正意义上的赛车。但维斯塔潘表示，他们之间已经讨论过这个想法了。

    “这对我来说当然不是新鲜事。是的，我们讨论过，”他说。“我不认为我们总是需要分享一切。我们在幕后讨论了很多事情。”

    里卡多表示，一个完整的赛季的赛车活动并不吸引他，而维斯塔潘认为，如果他们能够真正具有竞争力，那么一次性的比赛才会值得去做。

    “他并不想像整个赛季那样回来参加一场完整的比赛。但一次性的比赛可能会引起他的兴趣。为什么不呢？

    “但再次强调，我认为如果我们做这样的事情，他肯定会同意这需要是合适的。它需要是为了赢得比赛的机会。为了与那些整个赛季都在做这件事的人竞争，你需要准备得非常充分。你只需要找到正确的时机。”

    维斯塔潘和里卡多在2016年至2018年期间是红牛车队的队友。里卡多在播客中表示，在两人2018年底分手后，一个共同的勒芒参赛可能是一种“满足人们期望”的方式。

    Max Verstappen responds to Daniel Ricciardo’s Le Mans dream team call

    Red Bull driver reveals he and Ricciardo have already discussed a one-off Le Mans entry, but says he does not currently have time to do it properly

    Daniel Ricciardo, Third Driver, Red Bull Racing and Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Max Verstappen has joked that his former team-mate Daniel Ricciardo is "a smart man" for picking him for his dream Le Mans line-up. In fact, he revealed that the pair of them had already discussed the possibility of it.

    In a recent interview with the Beyond the Grid podcast, the Australian talked about his time at Red Bull and his exit from Formula 1. While he discussed what was next for him, he said that a Le Mans entry could be on the list. When asked who he'd team up with, Max Verstappen was the first name on his list. Sebastian Vettel was also an option, making something he called a "nice little Red Bull reunion" of sorts.

    “He’s a smart man to choose me in the car," Verstappen joked. "But we have to see. It just depends on a lot of things.”

    The four-time world champion has made no secret of his interest in endurance racing with him recently competing at Portimao, where he and his team finished ninth. He did, however, stress that he would only take on Le Mans with full preparation.

    “Also for me, if I want to do it, I want to do it proper with really good prep. And at the moment I cannot do that. I don’t have the time - the free time to dedicate to a Le Mans project with all the other things that I’m also doing outside of Formula 1.”

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, congratulates pole man Daniel Ricciardo, Red Bull Racing

    Ricciardo’s comments were the first time he had publicly spoken about racing again in any real capacity. But Verstappen said the idea had already come up between them.

    “This is not new to me, of course. Yeah, we have spoken,” he said. “I don’t think we are the types that always need to share everything. We talk about a lot of things in the background.”

    Ricciardo said a full season of racing did not appeal, and Verstappen believes that a one-off would only be worthwhile if they could be seriously competitive.

    “He doesn’t see himself coming back for a whole race, like a season. But the one-offs can interest him. Why not?

    “But again, I think if we do something like that, he will for sure agree that it needs to be proper. It needs to be for a chance to win. For a chance to win against people that do it a whole season, you need to be prepared really well. You just need to find the right timings.”

    Verstappen and Ricciardo were team-mates at Red Bull from 2016 to 2018. Ricciardo said on the podcast that a shared Le Mans entry could be a way to “give the people what they want” after the pair’s split at the end of 2018.
