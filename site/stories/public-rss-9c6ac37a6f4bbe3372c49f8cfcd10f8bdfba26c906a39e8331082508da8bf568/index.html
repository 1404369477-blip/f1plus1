    勒克莱尔称墨西哥站与汉密尔顿碰撞反应被夸大

    勒克莱尔认为，外界对他与法拉利队友汉密尔顿在墨西哥大奖赛首圈碰撞的反应“被夸大了”。那次碰撞导致两人在4号弯双双冲出刹车点，胜利被维斯塔潘夺走。

    勒克莱尔称墨西哥站与汉密尔顿碰撞反应被夸大

    勒克莱尔与法拉利队友汉密尔顿在墨西哥首圈发生碰撞，两人因此将胜利拱手让给维斯塔潘

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔认为，外界对他与一级方程式队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿在墨西哥大奖赛上的碰撞反应“被夸大了”。

    三周前在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道，勒克莱尔在首圈进攻杆位起步的汉密尔顿，导致两人在4号弯双双冲出刹车点，法拉利因此错失一场本可轻松拿下的胜利。

    红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘获胜，而汉密尔顿批评了队友“要么拼要么死”的态度，随后暗示勒克莱尔对他的对抗比对他人的对抗更激烈。

    随后巴西站围绕这一戏剧性事件的讨论自然非常热烈，尤其是因为法拉利是F1最大的车队，但在本周末拉斯维加斯大奖赛之前，紧张气氛似乎已经平息。

    “一切都被夸大了，”勒克莱尔说道，他自2019年起为法拉利效力，比汉密尔顿加入早了六年。

    “为法拉利效力这么多年，我现在有点习惯了。但事情经常被夸大。”

    “目标并不总是要达成一致，而这也永远不会发生。我们可以有不同的意见，情况确实如此，例如在墨西哥。

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    “但仅仅是交谈这一事实就缓解了所有紧张情绪，我们对此完全没问题。并不是说我们非得谈上一个小时，尽管最后确实持续了一个小时，只是因为我们聊得很愉快，还开始谈到许多其他事情，试图把车队凝聚起来，帮助车队向前迈进。

    “但关于紧张关系的讨论大概只有五到十分钟，然后气氛就清朗了，就这样。”

    汉密尔顿对此表示赞同，称“我不觉得这会是未来前进道路上的问题”，并表示“我对他没有任何顾虑或担忧”，此前两人在圣保罗都与车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔进行了交谈。

    “弗雷德肯定参与了，因为他在会上，而且是他组织了这次会议，”勒克莱尔透露。

    “所以我们三个人在一起，是的，讨论进行得非常顺利。但正如我们所说，我认为非常、非常快，我们只需要谈一谈。

    “而且几句话之后，原本存在的紧张气氛就已经消失了，一切都很好。然后双方都朝着在制造商[锦标赛]中获得第二名的方向努力。”

    “车队积分榜第二的位置很重要。确实我们身后有相对较大的差距，但这并不意味着我们需要去缩小它。

    “我们仍然必须作为一个团队去努力。但现在对我来说真正重要的是，努力在赛季结束前赢下比赛，赢得一场大奖赛。”

    2026赛季现在还剩三场大奖赛，勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿今年都只有一场胜利，而法拉利领先第三名红牛95分。

    这支奥地利车队在赛季后期状态回升，很大程度上要归功于其在巴库带来的升级，维斯塔潘自那之后已经三次获胜，但红牛缩小差距并不会让勒克莱尔对墨西哥站感到后悔。

    这位九次分站冠军得主表示：“我会继续竭尽全力，努力为车队带来尽可能最好的成绩。作为车手，我只想尽可能多地赢得胜利。”

    F1 Mexico GP fallout was “taken out of proportion” - Charles Leclerc

    Leclerc and Ferrari team-mate Lewis Hamilton clashed on lap one in Mexico, causing both to lose victory to Max Verstappen

    Charles Leclerc believes the reaction to his clash with Formula 1 team-mate Lewis Hamilton at the Mexico Grand Prix was “taken out of proportion”.

    The Scuderia lost a straightforward victory at Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez three weeks ago after Leclerc attacked polesitter Hamilton on lap one, leading to both outbraking themselves at Turn 4.

    Red Bull’s Max Verstappen emerged victorious while Hamilton criticised his team-mate’s “do or die” attitude, before suggesting that Leclerc races him harder than he does with others.

    Talk surrounding the drama at the following race in Brazil was naturally high, particularly with Ferrari being F1’s biggest team, but tensions have seemingly now died ahead of this weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    “Everything has been taken out of proportion,” said Leclerc, who has driven for Ferrari since 2019, six years before Hamilton joined.

    “I'm kind of used to it now, driving for Ferrari for so many years. But things are very often taken out of proportion.

    “The goal is not always to agree and that will never happen. We can have different opinions and that was the case, for example, in Mexico.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    “But just the fact of speaking eased all tensions and we were completely fine with it. It's not that we had to speak for an hour, even though it ended up lasting an hour, just because we had a good time and we started speaking about also many other things in trying to bring the team and to help the team go forward.

    “But the discussion about the tension was like maybe five or 10 minutes and then the air was clean and that's it.”

    Hamilton echoed those sentiments claiming “I don't feel like that will be a problem moving forward”, as “I have no concerns or worries about racing him” after both held talks with team boss Fred Vasseur in Sao Paulo.

    “Fred was definitely involved as he was in the meeting and he organised the meeting,” revealed Leclerc.

    “So we were all three together and, yeah, the discussion went very well. But as we've said, I think very, very quickly, we only had to speak.

    “And after a few sentences, already the tension that was there was not there anymore and it was all fine. Then to both work in the direction of having that second place in the constructor [championship].

    “The second place in the constructor is important. It's also true that we've got a relatively big margin behind, which doesn't mean we need to minimise that.

    “We still have to work as a team. But what is really important for me now is to try and win before the end of the season and win a race.”

    There are now three grands prix remaining in 2026 with Leclerc and Hamilton both on just one victory for the year, while Ferrari is 95 points ahead of third-placed Red Bull.

    The Austrian outfit has enjoyed a late resurgence, largely thanks to its Baku upgrades with Verstappen winning three times since, but Red Bull closing the gap doesn’t make Leclerc regret Mexico.

    “I'll keep pushing as hard as I possibly can to try and bring the best possible result to the team,” said the nine-time race winner. “As a driver, I just want to win as much as possible.”
