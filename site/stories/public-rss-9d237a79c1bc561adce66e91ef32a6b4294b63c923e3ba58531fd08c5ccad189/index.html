    贝尔曼与卡马拉竞争法拉利未来席位

    贝尔曼表示法拉利席位是他的终极目标，而拉斐尔·卡马拉加入哈斯表明法拉利仍在保留选择。

    为什么现在是奥利弗·贝尔曼与拉斐尔·卡马拉争夺法拉利下一个空缺席位

    奥利弗·贝尔曼已确认法拉利席位是他的“终极目标”，但拉斐尔·卡马拉加入哈斯表明，这支车队仍在保留选择。

    继任规划是F1车队负责人较为微妙的艺术之一。所有顶级车队都设有青训车手项目，明确目的是在比赛席位空缺时培养人才。

    通常时机并不利于等待该空缺的车手。

    作为法拉利车手学院的成员，奥利弗·贝尔曼在2025年被安排到哈斯车队，为他在法拉利可能的未来做准备。他尚未被确认将在2027年留在该车队，但这被认为只是形式上的问题。但现在他在争夺法拉利席位的竞争中将会有一个对手：F2锦标赛领跑者拉斐尔·卡马拉，上周他被宣布为下赛季埃斯特班·奥孔的替代者。

    在奥斯汀美国大奖赛前接受包括Motorsport.com在内的媒体采访时，贝尔曼承认了卡马拉被任命的重要性，但坚称他已准备好继续推进并证明自己配得上一支顶级车队的机会。

    “我们同龄，”他说。“显然，拉法在F1的经验比我少。

    “但是的，我想对法拉利来说，看看他们中期想放进车里的人选也是件好事。”

    当然，我的目标是为自己争取到最有利的位置，让那个位置属于我。

    我自己是忠于哈斯车队的，合同的期限并不真正属于我的职责范围。我很高兴能留在F1，但当然，从中长期来看，我也觉得自己已经准备好为领奖台而战、在前排竞争。

    就中游车队而言，哈斯是我想要效力、并且我看到了自己未来的车队。但在某个时刻，我也觉得自己已经赢得了机会，去外面寻找一支前四的车队。

    当然，考虑到我与法拉利的联系，那将是我的终极目标。

    F2 领跑者卡马拉已根据旧车测试协议驾驶过一辆地面效应法拉利F1赛车。

    从历史上看，年轻车手的机会窗口很难恰好对齐。近来出现了一种趋势，车队将最抢手的车手锁定在长期合同中。法拉利的夏尔·勒克莱尔就是其中之一，他在本赛季摩纳哥大奖赛前宣布将现有合同延长为“多年”。

    鉴于勒克莱尔现有的安排据信将持续到2029年底，这表明他现在与法拉利车队绑定至2030年代——当然，其中会有解约条款。这样一来，就只剩下一个席位可能空缺：刘易斯·汉密尔顿的席位。

    这位七届世界冠军将在2027年为法拉利效力，他已证明自己足够有竞争力，从而触发了现有合同中的表现条款。接下来会发生什么将取决于他下赛季的表现——但鉴于汉密尔顿将在1月年满42岁，可以合理预期他会比勒克莱尔更早腾出席位。

    这对贝尔曼和卡马拉来说都是机会。但在这种情况下，对于经验更丰富的车手来说，危险在于他们在等待状态中停留的时间越长，其他人就越有可能出现并引起高层管理人员的注意。

    勒克莱尔同样是法拉利车手学院的产物，他不必面对这种风险，因为当他在2018年被安排到索伯车队时，法拉利正寻求尽快替换基米·莱科宁，觉得需要一位更年轻、更有竞争力的车手来推动塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔，他们认为维特尔需要这样的推动。

    当被问及与勒克莱尔的比较时，贝尔曼说：“这完全是时机问题，对吧？”

    “我的意思是，查尔斯在[索伯]待了一年。他度过了非常好的一年，这给了他那个机会。就我而言，事情并没有那样发展。”

    “时机看起来如何，始终是个问题。这就是一级方程式的现实。”

    Why it's now Oliver Bearman versus Rafael Camara for the next Ferrari vacancy

    Oliver Bearman has confirmed a Ferrari seat is his "ultimate goal", but the arrival of Rafael Camara at Haas shows the Scuderia is keeping its options open

    Succession planning is one of the more subtle arts of the Formula 1 team boss. All the top teams maintain junior-driver programmes with the explicit intention of developing talent for when a race seat falls vacant.

    Often the timing doesn't work in favour of the driver waiting for that vacancy.

    As a member of the Ferrari Driver Academy, Oliver Bearman was placed at Haas in 2025 to prepare him for a possible future at Ferrari. He is yet to be confirmed at the team for 2027 but this is understood to be a formality. But now he will have a rival in the race for a Ferrari seat: Formula 2 championship leader Rafael Camara, who was announced last week as the replacement for Esteban Ocon next season.

    Speaking to media including Motorsport.com ahead of the US GP in Austin, Bearman acknowledged the significance of Camara's appointment, but insisted that he is ready to push on and prove himself worthy of an opportunity with a top-tier team.

    "We're the same age," he said. "Obviously, Rafa has less experience than me in F1.

    "But yeah, I guess that for Ferrari, it's also a good thing to see who they want to put in their car in the medium term.

    "And of course, my goal is to put myself in the best position for that to be me.

    "I'm committed to Haas myself and the length of the deal is not really my remit. I'm happy to be in F1, but of course, looking into the medium-long term, I also feel like I'm ready to fight for podiums and fight at the front.

    "In terms of a midfield team, Haas is the team that I want to be with and I see my future with. But at some point as well, I feel like I've earned the opportunity to go out and look for a top-four team.

    "And of course, with my link with Ferrari, that would be my ultimate goal."

    F2 frontrunner Camara has driven a ground-effect Ferrari F1 car under Testing of Previous Cars protocols

    Historically it is difficult for the window of opportunity for young drivers to align correctly. Recently there has been a trend for teams to lock the most in-demand drivers into long-term contracts. One such is Charles Leclerc at Ferrari, who announced a "multi-year" extension to his existing contract ahead of this season's Monaco Grand Prix.

    Given Leclerc's existing arrangement is understood to run until the end of 2029, this suggests he is now tied to the Scuderia until the 2030s – although there will, of course, be break clauses. That leaves just one seat potentially up for grabs: Lewis Hamilton's.

    The seven-time world champion will race for Ferrari in 2027, having proved competitive enough to trigger performance clauses in his existing contract. What happens next will hinge on how he performs next season – but, since Hamilton turns 42 in January, it's fair to expect that he will be vacating his seat sooner than Leclerc.

    That is the opportunity for both Bearman and Camara. But the danger for the more experienced driver in this scenario is that the longer they remain in a holding pattern, the greater the likelihood of someone else coming along to catch the eye of senior management.

    Leclerc, also a product of Ferrari's Driver Academy, didn't have to face that risk because when he was placed with Sauber in 2018, Ferrari was looking to replace Kimi Raikkonen as soon as possible, feeling that it needed a younger and more competitive driver to give Sebastian Vettel the push it believed he required.

    "It's all a question of timing, right?" said Bearman when asked about the Leclerc comparison.

    "I mean, Charles was there [Sauber] for one year. He had a very good year that gave him that opportunity. In my case, things didn't work out like that.

    "It's always a question of how timing looks. That's the reality of Formula 1."
