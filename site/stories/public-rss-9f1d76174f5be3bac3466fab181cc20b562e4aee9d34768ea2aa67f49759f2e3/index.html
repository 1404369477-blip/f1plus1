    Stray Kids成员Felix分享了新加坡大奖赛到访法拉利车队的新幕后照片

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更广范围的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析与比赛反应。

    Stray Kids成员Felix分享了新加坡大奖赛期间到访法拉利的幕后照片，法拉利车队也发布了这位K-pop明星在维修区的视频。

    韩国组合Stray Kids成员Felix上周末作为法拉利一级方程式车队的嘉宾，现身滨海湾赛道。在随法拉利车队度过一段时间后，他在Instagram上发布了多张此次活动的幕后照片。

    “法拉利幕后，”Felix写道。截至10月18日，该帖子已获得超过64.8万个赞和6000条评论。

    此前他还发布了比赛日帖子，只写了：“Forza 法拉利。”该帖子获得了超过200万个赞。

    法拉利亚太区账号在比赛周末前已通过联合帖子预告了他的到访。“从赛道到舞台。因赛车而团结，因激情而驱动，”帖子写道。

    车队也发布了他们自己拍摄的这次到访视频。法拉利的短片配文写道：“跟随@yong.lixx 参观车库。”

    一位粉丝写道：“Felix 随意地把我们介绍给法拉利，就好像我们现在都是围场的一员了。我爱死了！！”

    另一位说：“Felix 真的很高兴能到那里。Forza 法拉利！”第三位则补充道：“我能感受到他对他们的真心支持。”

    有些粉丝见到车手也同样激动。一位粉丝发帖写道：“FELIUIXXXX 啊啊啊啊啊，还有我的神夏尔，我要尖叫了。”

    费利克斯的造型也引起了关注。我们在赛后整理的手表盘点中，注意到了他佩戴的愛彼皇家橡树计时码表50周年纪念款，41毫米玫瑰金材质，估值在11.2万至11.8万美元之间。

    这次到访正值这位歌手忙碌的一个月。10月初，他曾在路易威登2027春夏大秀现场发帖，还为美妆品牌赫拉出镜了一支“跟我一起准备”视频。

    法拉利在滨海湾赛道的比赛喜忧参半。夏尔·勒克莱尔落后于马克斯·维斯塔潘获得第二，而刘易斯·汉密尔顿因策略冒险失败仅列第六。

    车队在前往奥斯汀参加美国大奖赛时位列车队积分榜第二，该赛事将于10月23日至25日举行。

    Stray Kids' Felix sends fans into frenzy with behind-the-scenes Ferrari F1 photos

    Stray Kids star Felix has shared new behind-the-scenes photos from his Singapore Grand Prix visit

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Stray Kids' Felix has shared behind-the-scenes photos from his time with Ferrari at the Singapore Grand Prix, and the team has posted its own footage of the K-pop star in the garage.

    Felix, a member of the South Korean group Stray Kids, was a guest of the Ferrari Formula 1 team at Marina Bay circuit last weekend. Following his time with the Scuderia, he posted to Instagram a number of behind-the-scenes photos of the occasion.

    "Ferrari behind the scenes," Felix wrote. By 18 October the post had more than 648,000 likes and 6,000 comments.

    It followed his race day post, which simply read: "Forza Ferrari." That had more than two million likes.

    Ferrari's Asia-Pacific account had already teased his visit with a joint post before the race weekend. "From the circuit to the stage. United by racing, driven by passion," it read.

    The team also published its own video of the visit. "Following @yong.lixx around the garage," the Ferrari reel was captioned.

    Felix's fans, known as Stays, quickly filled the comments.

    "Felix casually introducing us to Ferrari like we're all part of the paddock now. I love it!!" one fan wrote.

    "Felix is so genuinely happy to be there. Forza Ferrari!" said another, while a third added: "I can feel his genuine support for them."

    Some were just as excited to see the drivers. "FELIUIXXXX AAHHHHH AND MY GOAT CHARLES IM SCREAMING," one fan posted.

    Felix's look drew attention too. Our round-up of the watches worn at the race brought attention to his Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Chronograph 50th Anniversary in 41mm rose gold, valued at between $112,000 and $118,000.

    The visit came in a busy month for the singer. Earlier in October, he posted from Louis Vuitton's Spring/Summer 2027 show, and he also appeared in a "get ready with me" video for beauty brand Hera.

    Ferrari had a mixed race at Marina Bay. Charles Leclerc finished second behind Max Verstappen, while Lewis Hamilton was sixth after a strategy gamble backfired.

    The team is second in the constructors' championship heading to the United States Grand Prix in Austin, which runs from 23 to 25 October.
