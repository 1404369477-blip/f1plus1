    FIA宣布2027赛季起F1启用全职赛事干事

    国际汽联宣布，F1将从2027赛季起首次启用全职赛事干事，并计划在2028年扩大规模，最终建立12至15名全职干事队伍。

    国际汽联宣布，F1将从2027赛季起首次启用全职赛事干事。

    国际汽联将为新赛季招募“少量”全职干事，并在2028年扩大计划，最终建立12至15名全职干事队伍。

    因此，在“可预见的未来”，将混合使用全职和兼职干事。

    现行体系下，每场比赛的干事小组来自国际汽联选定的庞大轮换名单、当地体育机构提名和一名前车手。

    国际汽联表示，此举“将为最高级别的赛事执裁带来更高水平的一致性和奉献精神”，并为其干事增加管理其他国际汽联单座锦标赛的能力。

    车手和车队负责人过去曾呼吁采取这样的举措。

    在2024年美国大奖赛上发生多起事件后，梅赛德斯的乔治·拉塞尔呼吁结束目前使用轮换志愿干事池的制度。

    “我们过去谈论过一致性，获得一致性的唯一方法就是在每个比赛周末都有相同的干事，”他争辩道。“目前，他们非常有经验，但他们几乎是作为志愿者来到这里。

    "管理机构补充说，全职干事将带来专业知识，同时提供"更强的能力来识别新兴趋势和需要监管澄清的领域，有助于为体育规则的演变提供信息。"

    国际汽联的全职干事还将通过参与其现有的高性能项目来指导未来的国际汽联官员，该项目培训干事和官员。该管理机构于2026年早些时候在英国比金山的一级方程式总部启动了新的远程赛事控制中心。

    "这是国际汽联官员工作方面的最新重大投资，反映了我们致力于在赛车运动中实现最高标准的决策、公平和诚信，"国际汽联主席穆罕默德·本·苏拉耶姆说。

    "招聘全职干事是我们长期使命中的重要一步，该使命是在国际汽联一级方程式世界锦标赛中建立一个专门的官员生态系统，这也是对我们运动未来的投资。

    “随着国际汽联远程操作中心的开业这一里程碑，以及通过国际汽联高性能计划等举措加强裁判培养，这一发展将有助于在我们的赛事干事架构中积累更丰富的专业知识和连续性，增强我们审查和改进规则、支持未来官员培养的能力，并确保我们所有锦标赛中一致的高标准执裁。”

    英文原文

    Full-time stewards will be used in Formula 1 for the first time from the 2027 F1 season, the FIA has announced.

    The FIA will recruit a "small number" of full-time stewards for the new season and ramp up its programme in 2028, eventually establishing 12-15 full-time stewards.

    A mix of full-time and part-time stewards will therefore be used for the "foreseeable future".

    Under the current system, the stewards panel at each race comes from a large rotating pool of FIA picks, nominations from local sporting authority and one ex-driver.

    The FIA says the move "will bring even greater levels of consistency and dedication to race officiating at the highest levels" and add capacity for its stewards to govern other FIA single-seater championships.

    Drivers and team bosses have called for such a move in the past.

    After multiple incidents in the 2024 United States Grand Prix, Mercedes' George Russell called for an end to the current system of using a rotating pool of volunteer stewards.

    "We've spoken about consistency in the past, the only way you can have consistency is if you have the same stewards at every single race weekend," he argued. "And at the moment, they are very experienced, but they are here as almost volunteers.

    "The governing body adds that full-time stewards will add expertise but also provide "increased capacity to identify emerging trends and areas requiring regulatory clarification, helping to inform the evolution of sporting regulations."

    FIA full-time stewards will also mentor future FIA officials by contributing to its existing High Performance Programme that trains stewards and officials.The governing body launched its new remote race control at F1's headquarters in Biggin Hill, UK, earlier in 2026.

    "This latest major investment in FIA officiating reflects our dedication to delivering the highest standards of decision-making, fairness and integrity in motorsport," said FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem.

    "The recruitment of full-time stewards is a significant step in our longstanding mission of building a dedicated officiating ecosystem in the FIA Formula 1 World Championship, it is also an investment in the future of our sport.

    "Following the milestone opening of the new FIA Remote Operations Centre and strengthening of officiating development through initiatives such as the FIA High Performance Programme, this development will help to build greater expertise and continuity within our stewarding structure, strengthening our ability to review and improve regulations, support the development of future officials and ensure the highest standards of consistent officiating across all our championships."
