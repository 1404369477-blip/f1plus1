    布德科夫斯基接替洛登出任凯迪拉克F1车队领队

    凯迪拉克F1车队任命马尔钦·布德科夫斯基为新任车队领队，接替格雷姆·洛登。49岁的布德科夫斯基曾在普罗斯特、法拉利和迈凯伦担任空气动力学专家，之后负责FIA的F1技术部门，并加入雷诺车队。

    布德科夫斯基接替洛登出任凯迪拉克F1车队领队

    马尔钦·布德科夫斯基，曾任阿尔派执行董事，已在这支F1新军接替洛登

    凯迪拉克F1车队已聘请新任车队领队，任命马尔钦·布德科夫斯基担任该职位，而非格雷姆·洛登。

    49岁的布德科夫斯基在过去25年中在世界锦标赛中担任过多种职务，曾在普罗斯特、法拉利和迈凯伦担任空气动力学专家，之后负责FIA的F1技术部门。

    在FIA任职之后，布德科夫斯基加入雷诺车队担任执行董事；他在2021赛季与达维德·布里维奥以双人架构共同分担车队领队职责，随后离开该车队，此后从事咨询和评论工作。

    “凯迪拉克F1车队拥有非凡的基础和雄心，”布德科夫斯基说。

    “这是车队面临巨大机遇的阶段，其定义将是一个明确的目标：在成本上限内最大化赛车研发，并调动每一个车队职能来参与竞争。

    “这意味着建立一个具有赢家心态的精英组织——充满动力，面对挫折不屈不挠，对细节和流程痴迷，并能在比赛日精准执行。

    “我的重点将是协调我们的人员、工具和信息流，以便快速做出正确决策，并让我们每次参赛时赛车的性能都有所提升。很荣幸能领导凯迪拉克F1车队的赛道工作，从下周末的赞德沃特开始。”

    格雷姆·洛登，凯迪拉克F1车队领队

    这位在法国接受教育的波兰人的使命是改善这支垫底车队的命运，因为新车队正在适应F1，并入驻目前正在印第安纳州最终建设的新基础设施。凯迪拉克目前位列车队积分榜末位，尚未获得首个世界锦标赛积分。

    “马尔钦的任命标志着凯迪拉克F1车队持续发展的又一重要步骤，”首席执行官丹·托里斯补充道。

    “从一开始，我们的目标就不仅仅是参与F1，而是打造一支能够在前列竞争的新一代车队。

    “随着我们进入凯迪拉克F1车队的新时代，马尔钦带来了卓越的F1经验、技术专长和战略领导力，这将加强我们的组织，并帮助我们培养长期成功所需的赢家心态、工具和流程。”

    布德科夫斯基的任命意味着此前于2024年12月被聘用的车队领队洛登将离开车队。

    “我们感谢格雷姆·洛登的领导以及他在从零开始组建凯迪拉克F1车队中所发挥的作用，”托里斯继续说道。

    “他的贡献帮助我们在建设阶段奠定了坚实的基础，我们感谢他的付出并祝愿他一切顺利。”

    Budkowski replaces Lowdon as Cadillac F1 boss

    Marcin Budkowski, previously executive director of Alpine, has replaced Lowdon at the debutant F1 outfit

    The Cadillac Formula 1 outfit has hired a new team principal, appointing Marcin Budkowski to the position instead of Graeme Lowdon.

    Budkowski, 49, has held a myriad of roles in the world championship over the past 25 years, working as an aerodynamicist at Prost, Ferrari and McLaren before heading the FIA’s F1 technical department.

    Following his FIA stint, Budkowski joined the Renault squad as an executive director; he shared the team principal duties in a dual set-up alongside Davide Brivio in the 2021 season before leaving the outfit, and has since engaged in consulting and punditry.

    “Cadillac Formula 1 Team has an extraordinary foundation and ambition,” Budkowski said.

    “This is a phase of huge opportunity for the team, one that will be defined by a clear objective: maximising car development within the cost cap and turning every team function on to compete.

    “That means building an elite organisation with a winning mindset - driven, relentless in the face of setbacks, obsessed with detail and process, and able to execute with precision on race day.

    “My focus will be on aligning our people, tools and information flow so the right decisions are made quickly, and the performance of the car improves every time we go racing. It is a privilege to be leading Cadillac Formula 1 Team’s on-track efforts, starting in Zandvoort next weekend.”

    Graeme Lowdon, Team Principal of Cadillac F1 Team

    The French-educated Pole’s mission will be to improve the backmarker’s fortunes as the new team adapts to F1 and settles into new infrastructure currently being finalised in Indiana. Cadillac currently lies last in the constructors’ standings, awaiting its maiden world championship points.

    “Marcin's appointment marks another important step in the continued evolution of the Cadillac Formula 1 Team,” CEO Dan Towriss added.

    “From the beginning, our goal has not been simply to participate in Formula 1, but to build a next-generation team capable of competing at the very front.

    “As we enter this new era for the Cadillac Formula 1 Team, Marcin brings exceptional F1 experience, technical expertise and strategic leadership that will strengthen our organisation and help us develop the winning mindset, tools and processes required for long-term success.”

    Budkowski’s appointment means previous team principal Lowdon, who had been hired in December 2024, will leave the team.

    “We’re grateful to Graeme Lowdon for his leadership and the role he played in forming the Cadillac Formula 1 Team from the ground up,” Towriss continued.

    “His contributions helped establish a strong foundation in our build-up phase, and we thank him for his commitment and wish him well.”
