    弗雷德里克·瓦瑟尔在汉密尔顿-勒克莱尔墨西哥站碰撞后呼吁尊重

    法拉利车队负责人弗雷德里克·瓦瑟尔表示，他在墨西哥大奖赛上两位车手汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔在第一圈发生碰撞，是“尊重问题”。

    弗雷德·瓦瑟尔在刘易斯·汉密尔顿-查尔斯·勒克莱尔墨西哥大奖赛碰撞后呼吁“尊重”

    法拉利车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔呼吁刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔之间保持尊重

    对于法拉利车迷来说。最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，车手更新，技术分析和从马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应

    法拉利一级方程式车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔表示，他在墨西哥大奖赛中两位车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔在第一圈发生的碰撞归结于“尊重的问题”

    勒克莱尔试图在4号弯超越从杆位起跑的汉密尔顿，他们在Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez赛道上。两位车手都出了赛道，勒克莱尔率先回到赛道上。在混乱中，汉密尔顿跌至第三，排在红牛的维斯塔潘后面。

    摩纳哥车手因离开赛道并获得优势而被处以5秒罚时。维斯塔潘继续赢得比赛，汉密尔顿获得第二名，梅赛德斯车手基米·安东内利获得第三名，勒克莱尔以第四名冲过终点。

    在比赛后接受天空体育F1采访时，瓦瑟尔被问及车手们在比赛前讨论了什么，以及这些讨论是否被遗忘。

    “我认为这只是尊重的问题，对品牌的尊重，对赞助商的尊重，对团队的尊重，对那些像地狱一样工作的2000名在家工作的人的尊重，我认为我们在性能上有所回升，我们向前迈出了良好的一步，”这位法国人解释道。

    “有了这两项升级，家中的工作人员日夜都在工作。说实话，对我来说放弃这样的胜利是很难接受的。我将有时间与他们讨论，但说实话，今天我有点沮丧。”

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利，查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利

    汉密尔顿在赛后对队友的评论毫不保留。这位七届冠军认为，马拉内洛车队需要“在幕后进行一些严肃的谈话”，并且车队在墨西哥“本可以轻松取得一、二名的成绩”。勒克莱尔补充说，他感觉汉密尔顿的驾驶“非常接近极限。”

    当被问及两位车手的评论时，瓦瑟尔更愿意私下处理。

    “我们和刘易斯讨论刘易斯的评论，和查尔斯讨论查尔斯的评论，”他说。“我所看到的是结果。结果是我们在赛场上取得了胜利。我们在墨西哥站取得了第一和第二的成绩。墨西哥站的最后一次超车是在‘72年。当你取得一、二名时，你不会想象自己会输掉比赛，而我们做到了。”

    法拉利目前在车队积分榜上排名第二，积504分，落后积分榜领跑者梅赛德斯126分。红牛以382分的成绩排名第三，而迈凯轮以365分的成绩排名第四。

    Fred Vasseur calls for "respect" after Lewis Hamilton-Charles Leclerc Mexican GP clash

    Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur has called for respect between Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari Formula 1 team principal Fred Vasseur said the first-lap clash between his drivers, Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc, at the Mexican Grand Prix comes down to "a matter of respect".

    Leclerc attempted to overtake Hamilton, who had started the race at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez from pole position, at Turn 4. Both drivers went off the track and Leclerc came out ahead. During the chaos, Hamilton dropped down to third behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

    The Monegasque driver was handed a five-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage. Verstappen went on to win, Hamilton finished second and Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli finished third. Leclerc crossed the line in fourth.

    While speaking to Sky Sports F1 following the race, Vasseur was asked what the drivers had discussed before the race and whether those discussions had been forgotten.

    "I think it’s just a matter of respect, the respect for the brand, for the sponsor, for the team, for the 2,000 people working at home like hell that I think we are coming back in terms of performance, that we did a good step forward," the Frenchman explained.

    "With both upgrades, the people at home are working night and day. And honestly, it’s difficult to digest for me to give up a win like this. I will have time to discuss with them, but honestly, today I’m a bit down."

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari

    Hamilton did not hold back in his comments about his team-mate after the race. The seven-time champion argued that the Maranello outfit needs to have "some serious talks behind the scenes", and the team could have "easily had a one-two" in Mexico. Leclerc added that he felt Hamilton's driving was "very much on the limit".

    When asked about both drivers' comments, Vasseur preferred to deal with that privately.

    "The comments of Lewis, we discuss with Lewis, and the comments of Charles, we discuss with Charles," he said. "What I see is the result. The result is that we had a win on the table. We were one-two in Mexico. The last overtaking in Mexico was in ‘72. And when you are one-two you don’t imagine that you can lose the race, and we were able to do it."

    Ferrari currently sits second in the constructors' standings with 504 points, 126 points behind championship leader Mercedes. Red Bull follows in third with 382 points and McLaren sits fourth with 365 points.
