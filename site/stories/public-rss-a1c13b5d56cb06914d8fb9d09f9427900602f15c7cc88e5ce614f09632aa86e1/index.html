    F1积分榜领跑者安东内利回顾了本赛季他希望从中吸取教训的失误

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    F1积分榜现任领跑者安东内利在接近年度冠军之际，列出了本赛季他希望从中吸取教训的失误，引来维斯塔潘的打趣回应。

    这位梅赛德斯车手在美洲赛道从杆位赢得美国大奖赛，收获本赛季第九个大奖赛胜利。他在奥斯汀的胜利意味着，他如今只需在即将到来的墨西哥大奖赛中以第九名或更好的成绩完赛，就能锁定年度总冠军。

    当被问及本赛季的高潮与低谷时，安东内利表示仍有工作要做。

    “嗯，当然，到目前为止这个赛季是个不错的惊喜，”这位意大利车手在赛后新闻发布会上解释道。“当然，赛季还没有结束。还有几场比赛，我只想好好表现，仍然努力争取胜利。

    “能以高光表现收官会很好。但回看这个赛季，确实还有需要改进的地方，需要在某些情况下调整心态，或者改变我处理一些场景的方式。

    "所以，可以肯定的是，在赛季结束时，我会有更多时间回顾事情：哪些做得好，哪些本可以更好。但到目前为止，这是一个非常稳定的赛季，我们会努力保持到最后一刻。"

    当被追问指的是哪些情况时，安东内利补充道："首先，澳大利亚第三节练习，那个失误。日本，我在起步时执行得很糟。然后迈阿密也是，冲刺赛，因为赛道限制白白丢掉了积分。是的，赛道限制对我来说是更大的问题。"

    四届冠军维斯塔潘如今已在数学上退出冠军争夺，他开玩笑说安东内利的赛道限制违规："你今天只有一次，"安东内利回应道："是的，只有一次！今天不错。但可以肯定的是，在这类情况下，以及在一些排位赛节次中，我就是没有带着正确的心态进入。所以，是的，就是要确保我改进这些。"

    Kimi Antonelli reveals biggest mistakes of 2026 as Max Verstappen takes playful dig

    Formula 1 championship leader Kimi Antonelli reflected on the mistakes he wants to learn from this season

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Current Formula 1 championship leader Kimi Antonelli has listed the mistakes he wants to learn from this season as he closes in on the title, prompting a playful dig from Max Verstappen.

    The Mercedes driver won the United States Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas from pole position, claiming his ninth grand prix victory of the season. His win in Austin means he can now clinch the championship with a top-nine finish at the upcoming Mexican Grand Prix.

    Asked about the highs and lows of his campaign, Antonelli said there is still work to be done.

    "Well, for sure, it's been a good surprise this season so far," the Italian explained during the post-race press conference. "Of course, it's still not over. There are still a few races where I just want to do well and still try to fight for wins.

    "It would be good to finish on a high. But definitely looking back at the season, there are still things to work on and improve on the mindset in certain situations or the way I handle a few scenarios.

    "So, for sure, at the end of the season, I will have more time to look back at things: what went well, what could have been better. But it's been a very consistent season so far, and we'll try to keep it that way until the end."

    Pressed on which situations he was referring to, Antonelli added: "First of all, Australia FP3, the mistake. Japan, I had a bad execution at the start. Then Miami as well, the sprint race, just throwing away points as well with track limits. Yeah, track limits has been the bigger thing for me."

    Four-time champion Verstappen, who is now mathematically out of the championship fight, joked about Antonelli's track limits infringement: "You had only one [today]," to which Antonelli responded: "Yeah, only one! It was good today. But definitely, in these kinds of situations, and also in some of the qualifying sessions, I just didn't go in with the right mindset. So, yeah, just making sure I improve those."
