    Lando Norris apologises to Franco Colapinto for critical comments made about Alpine driver following Azerbaijan GP crash

    Reigning Formula 1 world champion Lando Norris said he apologised to Franco Colapinto for the stinging comments he made after the Alpine driver caused a crash that took both of them out of the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    Colapinto triggered the accident during a Safety Car restart in Baku when he locked his brakes into the first corner and hit the side of his Alpine team-mate Pierre Gasly, who was sent straight into Norris' McLaren on his outside.

    Speaking in the immediate aftermath of the incident in Saturday's race, Norris said "there are some drivers who shouldn't be in Formula 1" and called for such incidents to result in one-race bans for the initiator.
