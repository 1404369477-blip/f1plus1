    维斯塔潘与荷兰VDK集团签任大使

    四届F1世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘成为荷兰安装与技术企业集团VDK Group的新任大使，未来数年将参与该公司的传播与营销活动。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘与荷兰技术集团VDK签任大使协议

    这位红牛车手将在未来数年参与VDK的传播与营销活动

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    四届F1世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘成为荷兰安装与技术企业集团VDK Group的新任大使，该集团正在向欧洲扩张。

    这位荷兰车手将在未来几年在该公司的国际品牌推广中发挥重要作用，并将出现在其传播和营销活动中。

    VDK 成立于 2013 年，是一家荷兰技术公司集团，如今在荷兰、德国、瑞典、奥地利、瑞士和比利时都有业务。

    维斯塔潘表示，他认为 VDK 的组织方式与 F1 车队的运作方式有相似之处。

    “VDK 的世界与一级方程式赛车之间有很多相似之处，”这位红牛车手说道。“技术发挥着巨大作用，但任何关注这项运动的人都知道，只有整个团队都达到顶尖水平，你才能发挥出最佳表现。”

    在一支一级方程式车队里，每天都有大量的人在工作，让一切变得更好、更快。每个人都有自己的专业领域和职责。我在VDK的运作方式中看到了这一点。

    它由许多技术公司和专业人士组成，他们各有专长，但团结在一起就更强大。VDK是一家创新型公司，也有着远大的国际雄心。我很高兴能在未来几年成为其中一员。

    VDK创始人兼首席执行官弗兰斯·范德科尔克表示，这位荷兰人是公司下一步发展的理想人选。

    “马克斯·维斯塔潘加入VDK集团让我无比自豪，”范德科尔克说。“马克斯多年来一直是世界上最顶尖的车手之一。但同样吸引我的是这背后的一切。仅靠天赋永远不够。你需要身边有优秀的人、相互信任、技术专长，以及每天变得更好的动力。这正是我在VDK集团所看到的。”

    “我们通过整合实力雄厚的本地家族企业，并给予创业者自由发展的空间来实现增长。这一基础不会改变。与此同时，我们正日益将目光投向国门之外。”

    “随着在荷兰、德国、瑞典、奥地利、瑞士和比利时拥有公司，我们正在形成一个日益国际化的集团。马克斯·维斯塔潘闻名世界，同时也体现了那种令人熟悉的荷兰务实态度和赢家心态。说实话，我无法想象还有谁比他更能代表我们希望带领VDK前进的方向。”

    过去两年一直与VDK合作的前F1车手罗伯特·杜恩博斯将继续担任大使和演讲嘉宾。根据公告，他在协调与维斯塔潘的合作关系中发挥了关键作用。

    “我非常高兴能与弗兰斯和VDK集团合作，”杜恩博斯解释道。“我们已经共同取得了伟大的成就，我也真正了解了VDK背后的人。如今我还能为这一下一步贡献力量，这让一切更加特别。”

    “有了马克斯，VDK 集团当然就引进了一位真正独一档的人物。我很高兴我们现在为 VDK 集团携手合作，也非常期待未来的岁月。”

    Max Verstappen signs ambassador deal with Dutch technical group VDK

    The Red Bull driver will feature in VDK’s communications and marketing campaigns over the coming years

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen has become the new ambassador of the VDK Group, a Dutch group of installation and technical companies that is expanding across Europe.

    The Dutchman will play a prominent role in the company's international branding over the coming years and will feature in its communications and marketing campaigns.

    VDK was founded in 2013 as a Dutch group of technical companies and now has businesses in the Netherlands, Germany, Sweden, Austria, Switzerland and Belgium.

    Verstappen said he saw parallels between the way VDK is organised and the way an F1 team works.

    "There are many similarities between the world of VDK and Formula 1," the Red Bull driver said. "Technology plays a huge role, but anyone who follows the sport knows that you can only perform at your best if the entire team is top-notch.

    "In a Formula 1 team, a huge number of people work every day to make everything better and faster. Everyone has their own area of specialisation and responsibility. I recognise that in the way VDK operates.

    "It’s a group of many technical companies and professionals who all have their own expertise but are stronger together. VDK is an innovative company and also has major international ambitions. I’m excited to be a part of that in the coming years."

    VDK founder and chief executive Frans van der Kolk said the Dutchman was the ideal fit for the company's next step.

    "The fact that Max Verstappen is joining the VDK Group makes me incredibly proud," van der Kolk said. "Max has been among the absolute world’s best for years. But what appeals to me just as much is what lies behind that. Talent alone is never enough. You need good people around you, mutual trust, technical expertise, and the drive to get better every day. That’s exactly what I see in the VDK Group.

    "We’ve grown by bringing together strong local and family-owned businesses and giving entrepreneurs the freedom to thrive. That foundation won’t change. At the same time, we’re looking beyond our borders with increasing focus.

    "With companies in the Netherlands, Germany, Sweden, Austria, Switzerland, and Belgium, we’re forming an increasingly international group. Max Verstappen is known worldwide and, at the same time, embodies that recognisable Dutch down-to-earth attitude and winner’s mentality. To be honest, I can’t imagine a stronger face to represent where we want to take VDK."

    Former F1 driver Robert Doornbos, who has worked with VDK for the past two years, will stay on as an ambassador and speaker. According to the announcement, he played a key role in coordinating the partnership with Verstappen.

    "I am very pleased with the collaboration with Frans and the VDK Group," Doornbos explained. "We’ve already achieved great things together, and I’ve really gotten to know the people behind VDK. The fact that I’ve now also been able to contribute to this next step makes it even more special.

    "With Max, the VDK Group is, of course, bringing in someone who is truly in a league of his own. I’m happy that we’re now working together for the VDK Group and am really looking forward to the years ahead."
