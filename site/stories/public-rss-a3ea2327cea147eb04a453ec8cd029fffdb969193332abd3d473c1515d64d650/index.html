    哈基宁警告迈凯伦勿追维斯塔潘：勿“搅局”破坏队内氛围

    两届F1世界冠军米卡·哈基宁警告老东家迈凯伦不要试图签下马克斯·维斯塔潘，建议车队保护内部团队精神，避免在赛季中期的车手市场传闻中“搅局”。

    米卡·哈基宁敦促迈凯伦不要因追逐马克斯·维斯塔潘而“打破平衡”

    米卡·哈基宁警告迈凯伦不要去追求马克斯·维斯塔潘，他认为车队应该保护其良好的内部氛围

    两届F1世界冠军米卡·哈基宁警告迈凯伦不要去追逐马克斯·维斯塔潘，建议他的老东家保护内部的团队精神，避免在赛季中的车手市场传闻中“打破平衡”。

    在与他的前迈凯伦队友大卫·库特哈德共同参加Up To Speed播客节目时，这位芬兰传奇车手谈到了F1当下持续的“转会流言季”，以及关于红牛车手维斯塔潘可能转投沃金车队（迈凯伦）的持续传闻。

    库特哈德在讨论车手流动问题时提到了这个话题。他指出哈基宁仍然非常认同迈凯伦，并问如果他自己担任迈凯伦车队领队，会如何处理这种局面。

    具体来说，库特哈德质疑把一位像四届冠军那样的车手放到兰多·诺里斯身边，是否必然会导致车队内部的复杂问题和摩擦。

    “这非常有意思，”哈基宁解释道。“当然，赛场上不只有维斯塔潘（Max），还有很多其他车手。但我所看到的迈凯伦的运作方式是，他们始终想要真正着眼于整个车队的整体组合。因为那是取得成功的唯一途径——在车队内部建立难以置信的团队精神力量。

    “如果你问我会怎么做，我会尽量把那种风险降到最低——如果车队拥有两位顶尖车手，就可能会失去这种团队精神。为什么要自找麻烦、打破现有的平衡呢？”

    诺里斯（迈凯伦）、维斯塔潘（红牛车队）

    这位芬兰车手回顾了自己在迈凯伦的经历。Hakkinen 和 Coulthard 在沃金作为队友共事了六个赛季。他认为，当车队内部存在健康的氛围时，管理层应该优先考虑保持延续性，而不是在车手市场上冒险。

    “当然，迈凯伦当时拥有一位世界冠军……而且我们是F1历史上合作时间最长的队友组合，”Hakkinen说道。“这说明如果车队内部有着良好的和谐氛围，为什么还要换别人进来呢？”

    F1将于8月21日至23日在赞德沃特重返赛场，举行荷兰大奖赛。

    Mika Hakkinen urges McLaren not to "rock the boat" with Max Verstappen move

    Mika Hakkinen has warned McLaren against pursuing Max Verstappen, arguing the team should protect its strong internal dynamics

    Two-time Formula 1 champion Mika Hakkinen has warned McLaren against pursuing Max Verstappen, advising his former team to protect its internal team spirit and avoid "rocking the boat" amid midseason driver market speculation.

    Speaking on the Up To Speed podcast alongside his former McLaren team-mate David Coulthard, the Finnish legend addressed the ongoing F1 'silly season' and persistent rumours linking Red Bull's Verstappen with a potential move to the Woking outfit.

    Coulthard raised the topic while discussing driver movements. Noting that Hakkinen remains very much a McLaren person, Coulthard asked how he would approach the scenario if he were acting as McLaren team principal.

    Specifically, Coulthard questioned whether attempting to place a driver such as the four-time champion alongside Lando Norris would inevitably lead to internal complications and friction.

    "It's very interesting," Hakkinen explained. "It's not only Max out there, of course, but there are many other drivers. But what I have seen [with] how McLaren operates, they want to really look at the overall team package all the time. Because that's the only way to achieve success — to build incredible team spirit power inside.

    "If you would ask me what I would do, I would like to minimise that risk — that way you would lose this kind of team spirit if the team has two great drivers. Why rock the boat?"

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    The Finnish driver drew upon his own tenure at McLaren. Hakkinen and Coulthard spent six seasons together as team-mates at Woking. He argued that when healthy team dynamics exist, management should prioritise continuity over driver market gambles.

    "And of course McLaren having a world champion... and we were the longest team-mates in Formula 1," Hakkinen noted. "That indicates that if there's a good harmony inside a team, why have somebody else there?"
