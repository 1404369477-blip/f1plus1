    汉密尔顿解释法拉利拉斯维加斯排位赛Q2出局原因

    法拉利两位车手在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中止步Q2，汉密尔顿将出局归因于能量部署和底板损伤。

    汉密尔顿揭示法拉利F1赛车在拉斯维加斯排位赛Q2出局后的损伤

    法拉利两位车手在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中表现不佳，均未能晋级Q3

    汉密尔顿将法拉利在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中的Q2出局归因于能量部署和底板损伤，这位七届世界冠军觉得自己已经从整体轮胎抓地力的困难中恢复过来。

    这位英国车手在第三节练习中刹车时全程挣扎，前轮特别容易锁死，导致汉密尔顿多次冲出赛道进入缓冲区。

    汉密尔顿在排位赛首节虽然底板受损，但仍闯入了Q2，不过他和夏尔·勒克莱尔都未能进入争夺杆位的前十名淘汰赛。

    汉密尔顿在Q2的飞驰圈中总体显得满意，这些圈速很少出现他在排位赛前常见的锁死轮胎现象，但法拉利动力单元的能量部署似乎比梅赛德斯和红牛动力赛车更早达到极限，抑制了直线性能。

    法拉利在动力上一直落后于这两家制造商，而拉斯维加斯大道沿线的长加速区在整个练习与排位赛节次中暴露了这一劣势。

    “我们在Q1的第一圈或第二圈损失了一块底板，赛车平衡发生了变化，我想我们损失了大约三到四成的下压力，”汉密尔顿表示。

    “而在那之后，我们更多是在打滑方面挣扎，但基本上从一开始就是这样。”

    “然后我的最后一圈在结尾时真的很好，但我用现有的条件做到了最好。但后来我们仅仅在部署上就损失了大约零点六秒。所以我认为有了那个我们本可以进入Q3，但除此之外我对自己的圈速感到满意。”

    “我们知道长直道之类的东西会很有挑战性。差距在直线上似乎比我预想的要大一点。这并非微不足道。在直道上是一个很大的部分。”

    一直有一种普遍的感觉，认为法拉利会在拉斯维加斯的低温条件下挣扎，因为该车队在整个2026赛季都难以在排位圈中保持轮胎温度。

    汉密尔顿也认为这辆赛车很难驾驭，在低速弯中频繁打滑，尤其是在损失了一部分底板之后，他只希望法拉利能在周六晚上的正赛中有所进步，因为车队的目标是锁定制造商年度亚军。

    “就抓地力而言，感觉我们并没有很强的抓地力。我们一直很难让轮胎进入工作状态，就是有点找不到感觉，”他补充道。

    “今年这些轮胎非常非常难进入工作状态，即使在高温路面上也是如此。所以这确实很困难。我对正赛没有任何期待。只是想看看自己能不能往前赶。”

    Lewis Hamilton reveals Ferrari F1 car damage after Las Vegas Q2 exit

    Both Ferraris failed to make the cut for Q3 after floundering in qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix

    Lewis Hamilton put Ferrari's Q2 exit in qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix down to energy deployment and floor damage, as the seven-time champion felt he'd rallied from difficulties with overall tyre grip.

    The Briton struggled throughout FP3 under braking, as the front tyres proved particularly lock-up prone and resulted in Hamilton enduring multiple excursions into the run-off.

    Hamilton landed in Q2 despite suffering floor damage in the opening stage of qualifying, but neither he nor Charles Leclerc were able to make their way into the top-10 shootout for pole.

    Hamilton appeared generally happy with his runs in Q2, which demonstrated few of the lock-ups characteristic of his lead-up to qualifying, but the Ferrari power unit's energy deployment seemed to hit its maximum sooner than the Mercedes and Red Bull-powered cars and inhibited straightline performance.

    Ferrari has been down on power versus those two manufacturers, and the long acceleration zone along the Strip exposed this deficit throughout the session.

    "We lost a piece of the floor on the first or second lap of Q1 and the car balance shifted, and we lost I think it was like three or four tenths of downforce we lost," Hamilton stated.

    "And then after that we were struggling more so with sliding, but it was basically from the get-go.

    "And then my last lap was really good at the end, but I did the best I could with what I had. But then we lost something like six tenths just on deployment. So I think with that we would have been in Q3, but otherwise I was happy with my lap.

    "We knew long straights and stuff was going to be challenging. The gap seems to be a little bit more than I anticipated in a straight line. It's not insignificant. It's a big chunk on the straights.

    There had been a pervading sense that Ferrari would struggle in the low-temperature conditions in Las Vegas, as the team has struggled throughout 2026 to maintain tyre temperature through a qualifying lap.

    Hamilton agreed that the car was difficult to work with amid frequent sliding through the low-speed corners, especially after losing a chunk of floor, and only hopes that Ferrari can move forward in the Saturday night race as it aims to lock out second in the constructors' championship.

    "Just grip-wise, it just didn't feel like we had a ton of grip. We were struggling to get the tyres working and we were just kind of nowhere," he added.

    "These tyres have been very, very difficult this year to get working, even on hot surfaces. So it's definitely been difficult. I don't have any expectations [for the race]. Just trying to see if I can move forward."
