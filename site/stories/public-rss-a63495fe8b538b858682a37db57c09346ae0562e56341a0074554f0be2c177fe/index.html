    里卡多考虑与维斯塔潘搭档出战勒芒

    前F1车手里卡多表示，他愿意考虑在勒芒24小时耐力赛中进行一次性参赛，并称前红牛队友维斯塔潘是他的首选搭档。

    里卡多考虑与维斯塔潘搭档出战勒芒

    里卡多透露，他会考虑在勒芒24小时耐力赛中进行一次性复出参赛

    里卡多，红牛车队第三车手，与马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    前一级方程式车手丹尼尔·里卡多透露，他会考虑在勒芒24小时耐力赛中进行一次性的亮相，并点名他的前红牛队友马克斯·维斯塔潘作为他首选的共享赛车人选。

    这位澳大利亚车手自2024年新加坡大奖赛后退出F1以来便未再参赛，但在参加《F1超越格子旗》节目时，这位八次大奖赛冠军得主表示，他希望有机会按照自己的方式结束赛车生涯。

    “一年前，我会说我再也不会戴上头盔了。我结束了。就这样。但内心深处有个声音告诉我，能按照自己的方式为赛车生涯画上句号，那会很好。

    “所以去做这样一件事，当我摘下头盔时，我是面带微笑、快乐的，也许那就是我需要的最后一点释怀。”

    丹尼尔·里卡多与马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    里卡多表示，完整的一个赛季仍然无法吸引他，但在勒芒的一次性出战是他觉得自己可以去做的事情。对这位前红牛车手而言，吸引力的一部分在于与队友共享一辆赛车，而不是像在F1中那样作为对手相互竞争。

    “与其他车手共享一辆赛车，并且真正拥有可以分享数据、互相帮助的队友，”他继续说道。“我会很享受这一点。那会是我真正喜欢的那种驾驶方式和伙伴情谊。”

    当被问及他希望在耐力赛中与谁共享一辆赛车时，里卡多补充道：“我不想给他施加压力，但显然马克斯是一个会高居名单前列的名字。”

    “我们显然在2018年谈过红牛以及‘如果会怎样’，而我当时正试图在职业生涯末期回到那支车队。所以也许那是一种方式，我们可以，是的，我说给大家他们想要的。”

    Daniel Ricciardo opens door to racing return with Max Verstappen at Le Mans

    Daniel Ricciardo has revealed he would consider a one-off return to racing at the Le Mans 24 Hours

    Daniel Ricciardo, Third Driver, Red Bull Racing and Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Former Formula 1 driver Daniel Ricciardo has revealed he would consider a one-off appearance at the Le Mans 24 Hours and named his former Red Bull team-mate Max Verstappen as his first choice to share a car with.

    The Australian driver has not raced since his F1 exit after the 2024 Singapore Grand Prix, but during an appearance on the F1 Beyond the Grid, the eight-time grand prix winner said he would like the chance to end his racing career on his own terms.

    “A year ago, I would have said I will never put a helmet on again. I’m done. That’s it. But there is something there where I’m like, it’d be nice for me to also finish a racing career a bit more on my terms.

    "So to do something that, when I take the helmet off, I’m smiling and happy, and maybe that’s the last bit of closure I need."

    Daniel Ricciardo & Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    Ricciardo said a full season still did not appeal to him, but that a one-off outing at Le Mans was something he could see himself doing. Part of the appeal for the former Red Bull driver is sharing a car with his team-mates, rather than competing against them as rivals in F1.

    "To share a car with other drivers and to actually have real team-mates to share data and help out," he continued. "I would just enjoy it. It would be the form of driving and companionship that I would really enjoy."

    Asked who he would love to share a car with in the endurance race, Ricciardo added: "I don’t want to put pressure on him, but obviously Max is a name that would be high on the list.

    "We talked about Red Bull obviously in 2018 and ‘what if’, and I was trying to get back at the team at the end of my career. So maybe that’s a way that we could, yeah, I say give the people what they want."
