    阿斯顿·马丁F1车队刚刚确认了其壁球合作伙伴关系，但这不会阻止它推出一系列文具和旅行产品，与邦德街品牌Smythson合作。价格从14英镑的生日卡片到令人咋舌的595英镑的笔记本电脑包不等。

    阿斯顿·马丁F1的最新商品系列价格令人咋舌

    阿斯顿·马丁F1推出了与英国品牌Smythson合作的奢华文具和旅行系列

    虽然该系列本月才上线，但这一合作最初是在5月的摩纳哥大奖赛之前宣布的。Smythson表示：“这一系列根植于英国传统和无与伦比的工艺，将Smythson和阿斯顿·马丁阿拉莫科F1车队结合在一起。”

    我们个人最喜欢的产品系列是Soho Circuit Journal，售价为295英镑。它作为F1赛历的指南，包含了团队幕后内容，并为所有者提供了记录整个赛季每场比赛的空间。据Smythson公司称，它是“终极赛道伴侣”

    当宣布合作时，阿斯顿·马丁许可和商品部负责人Matthew Chapman表示：“我们很自豪能够与如此标志性的英国品牌Smythson实现这一合作。”

    Smythson公司的市场营销和传播总监Samantha Dobbie补充道：“这次合作展示了将英国两大最标志性的奢侈品牌结合在一起的力量。”

    该产品系列由Smythson公司的内部设计团队与阿斯顿·马丁共同开发，旨在面向奢侈购物者和F1粉丝。

    对于本周前往奥斯汀的粉丝来说，有一本价值295英镑的日记本等待记录发生的事情。

    Aston Martin F1's latest merchandise collection comes with eye-watering prices

    Aston Martin F1 has launched a luxury stationery and travel collection with British brand Smythson

    The Aston Martin Formula 1 team may have only just confirmed its padel partnership, but that won't stop it from revealing a range of stationery and travel products alongside Bond Street brand Smythson. Prices range from £14 for a birthday card to a eye-watering £595 for a laptop case.

    While the range has only recently gone live this month, the collaboration was originally announced ahead of the Monaco Grand Prix in May. “Rooted in British heritage and unrivalled craftsmanship, this collection brings together Smythson and Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team,” Smythson said.

    Our personal favourite part of the range is the Soho Circuit Journal, priced at £295. It sits as a guide to the F1 calendar, with behind-the-scenes content from the team and space for its owner to record every race of the season. According to Smythson, it's “the ultimate trackside companion”.

    When the partnership was announced, Matthew Chapman, Aston Martin’s head of licensing and merchandise, said: “We are proud to bring this collaboration to life with such an iconic British brand like Smythson.”

    Samantha Dobbie, Smythson’s marketing and communications director, added: “This collaboration demonstrates the power of bringing together two of Britain’s most iconic luxury brands.”

    The range was developed by Smythson’s in-house design team together with Aston Martin, and is aimed at both luxury shoppers and F1 fans.

    For fans heading to Austin this week, there is a £295 journal waiting to record what happens.
