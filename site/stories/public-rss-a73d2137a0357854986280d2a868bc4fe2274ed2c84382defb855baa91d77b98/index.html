    勒克莱尔：法拉利须解决与汉密尔顿冲突

    勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛因与队友汉密尔顿发生可避免的碰撞被罚10秒，并受到车队领队瓦塞尔的训斥。

    勒克莱尔：法拉利须解决与汉密尔顿冲突后“不可接受”的内斗

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛“拉锯式”比赛节奏回归，导致法拉利两位车手发生争议性碰撞

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利，夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利

    勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛因与法拉利队友汉密尔顿发生可避免的碰撞被罚10秒，并受到车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔的训斥。

    两辆法拉利赛车在排位赛中未能组织好必要的尾流配合，因此从第10名开外起步，但随后在比赛的开局阶段取得了进展，这一阶段的特点是本赛季初以来罕见的极端“拉锯式”超车。每当勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿在赛道上近距离行驶时，争论必然随之而来，第一次摩擦始于勒克莱尔在无线电中说“他（汉密尔顿）在挤压时需要小心”，当时他们正在与迈凯伦的奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在第10名左右的位置缠斗。

    尽管汉密尔顿随后在第10圈与另一辆迈凯伦赛车（兰多·诺里斯）的较量中因离开赛道并获得优势而被罚10秒，但为清理塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯抛锚的凯迪拉克赛车而出动的安全车减少了潜在影响。尽管如此，汉密尔顿还是在无线电中对处罚本身以及法拉利决定将他的进站推迟一圈表达了不满。

    由于安全车出动时汉密尔顿领先于勒克莱尔，如果两人同时进站，勒克莱尔将不得不在维修区外等待汉密尔顿接受处罚。

    正是在这种日益增长的不满情绪背景下，他们在第38圈的第7至第9号弯复合弯发生了碰撞。汉密尔顿在比赛重启后取得了良好进展，此前他在进站时接受处罚后曾跌至第14位。他在第34圈超越勒克莱尔升至第七，当时他的队友正在寻找超越第六名皮埃尔·加斯利驾驶的阿尔派赛车的方法。

    四圈后，在两人都超越了加斯利之后，勒克莱尔在7号弯内线猛冲，并骑上弯心处的路肩，蹭到了汉密尔顿的右后轮，导致他打转。这引发了赛会干事的调查和10秒罚时，尽管车载画面显示汉密尔顿在内线并没有留出整整一个车身的宽度；国际汽联的驾驶准则要求进攻车辆完全处于控制之下，才有权获得“赛车空间”。

    勒克莱尔以第六名冲过终点线，但在罚时生效后被降至第10名。他还被指示在前往强制性的赛后媒体采访之前，先向车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔报到。

    “查尔斯，我想在比赛结束后、一切开始之前见你，”瓦塞尔在无线电中说。

    车队老板瓦塞尔在赛后召见勒克莱尔进行训斥。

    随后，勒克莱尔带着恰如其分的懊悔，对这项纪录发表了自己的感想。

    “一开始挺有意思，后来就完全不是那么回事了，”他这样评价这场比赛——正如赛季初一样，大多数超车动作都是由电能部署的差异所决定的。

    “但是，说实话，我真的不能——如果还说这很有趣，那又会显得非常不尊重，因为最终的结果是……是的，我为车队感到非常难过。

    “所以，我想这个结果是应得的。”

    勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿本赛季已在赛道上多次交锋，但在墨西哥站，两人在首圈为争夺领先而内斗，浪费了潜在的1-2名完赛机会，气氛随之升温。当时，勒克莱尔尝试超车，汉密尔顿进行防守，这让马克斯·维斯塔潘得以超越两人并最终获胜。

    在本站比赛前夕，两位车手都表示他们已经进行了富有成效的坦诚交流，但拉斯维加斯发生的情况表明，还需要更多沟通。

    “我们只需要解决不起作用的地方，”勒克莱尔说。

    “再说一次，身处这种情况很棘手，显然在你们面前谈论这些事情，某种程度上很难。所以这是不可接受的，我只能这么说……而且我觉得，每当我们同时在赛道上时，情况总是很棘手。”

    “我不认为这次超车比其他任何缠斗更接近极限，我觉得有一个位置可以过去而不用冒太大风险。我不认为也许刘易斯……我可能低估了刘易斯当时看到的视野，那个空隙确实存在，我的意思是内侧有路肩，非常棘手。”

    “再说一次，我终究不想讨论处罚，我是那个被罚10秒的人。”

    Charles Leclerc: Ferrari must find fix to 'unacceptable' feud after Lewis Hamilton clash

    The return of 'yo-yo racing' in Las Vegas Grand Prix led to a controversial collision between Ferrari's drivers

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari

    Charles Leclerc ended the Las Vegas with a 10-second penalty for an avoidable collision with Ferrari team-mate Lewis Hamilton – and a telling-off from team boss Frederic Vasseur ringing in his ears.

    Both Ferraris had started outside the top 10, having failed to orchestrate the necessary slipstream in qualifying, but then made progress in an opening phase of a race characterised by the kind of extreme 'yo-yo' overtaking rarely seen since the beginning of the season. Whenever Leclerc and Hamilton are running in close proximity on track, arguments are sure to follow, and the first rumblings began when Leclerc took to the radio to say "He [Hamilton] needs to be careful with the squeezing" as they duelled with McLaren's Oscar Piastri in the lower reaches of the top 10.

    Although Hamilton then collected a 10-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage during a lap-10 battle with the other McLaren of Lando Norris, a safety car deployment to cover the removal of Sergio Perez's stranded Cadillac reduced the potential impact. Nevertheless, Hamilton expressed frustration over the radio about the penalty itself, and Ferrari's decision to delay his stop by a lap.

    Since Hamilton was running ahead of Leclerc when the safety car was deployed, Leclerc would have had to wait outside the 'box' for Hamilton to serve the penalty if they had both pitted at the same time.

    It was against this background of growing frustration that they tangled at the Turn 7-9 complex on lap 38. Hamilton had made good progress after the restart, having dropped to 14th place through taking the penalty at his stop. He passed Leclerc for seventh on lap 34 as his team-mate sought a way past sixth-placed Pierre Gasly's Alpine.

    Four laps later, after they had both dispatched Gasly, Leclerc lunged up the inside at Turn 7 and rode over the kerb at the apex, clipping Hamilton's right-rear wheel and tipping him into a spin. That resulted in a stewards' investigation and a 10-second penalty, even though onboard footage revealed Hamilton had not left a full car's width on the inside; the FIA's driving guidelines require the attacking car to be fully under control to be entitled to 'racing room'.

    Leclerc crossed the line in sixth place but was demoted to 10th once the penalty was applied. He was also instructed to report to team principal Frederic Vasseur before moving on to the mandatory post-race media interviews.

    "Charles, I want to see you just after the race, before everything," Vasseur said on the radio.

    Team boss Vasseur summoned Leclerc for a dressing-down after the race

    It was a suitably chastened Leclerc who then gave his reflections on the record.

    "It was fun until it wasn't at all," he said of a race where, as with the start of the season, the majority of the overtaking manoeuvres were dictated by differences in electrical deployment.

    "But, yeah, honestly, I cannot really – it would be again very disrespectful to say actually it was fun because the end result is… yeah, I feel very bad for the team.

    "As a result, I guess it was deserved."

    Leclerc and Hamilton have locked horns on track several times this season but the temperature escalated in Mexico when they squandered a potential 1-2 by squabbling for the lead on the opening lap. There, Leclerc's attempted pass, and Hamilton's defence of it, enabled Max Verstappen to overtake them both and go on to win.

    In the run-up to this event, both drivers said they had concluded productive clear-the-air talks but events in Las Vegas suggest more are required.

    "We just need to fix whatever is not working," said Leclerc.

    "Again, it's a tricky situation to be in and obviously speaking about these things here in front of you, it's difficult in a way. So it's not acceptable, this is all I can say in a way… and I feel like it's always tricky whenever we find ourselves on track.

    "I don't think that this was as on the limit as any other fight, I felt there was a place to get there without taking too much risk. I don't think maybe Lewis… I probably underestimated the vision that Lewis had at that point and the gap was there, I mean with the kerb on the inside it was very tricky.

    "Again I don't want to discuss the penalty at the end of the day, I'm the one taking 10 seconds."
