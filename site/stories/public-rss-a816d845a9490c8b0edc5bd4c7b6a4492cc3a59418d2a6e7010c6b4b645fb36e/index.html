    维斯塔潘批评劳森墨西哥站二练碰撞：争席位时此举不明智

    墨西哥大奖赛第二节练习中，维斯塔潘与小红牛车手劳森在飞驰圈发生接触，维斯塔潘赛后批评劳森的行为“不太明智”，并提醒对方正为席位而战。

    墨西哥大奖赛第二节练习赛发生冲突后，马克斯·维斯塔潘向利亚姆·劳森发出直言警告

    墨西哥大奖赛练习赛中发生碰撞后，马克斯·维斯塔潘批评了利亚姆·劳森“不太聪明的行为”

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与赛事分析。

    四届一级方程式世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘在墨西哥大奖赛第二节练习赛中与小红牛车手利亚姆·劳森发生碰撞后对其提出批评，警告称对于一名正在为席位而战的车手来说，这是“不太聪明的行为”。

    在周五于罗德里格斯兄弟赛道进行的第二节自由练习中，红牛车队车手与劳森都在进行飞驰圈时发生了接触。

    当天空体育F1询问这是否只是一次不幸的事件时，维斯塔潘明确表达了自己的看法。

    “嗯，我不认为这能叫不幸。也许他当时想做的举动不太明智，”这位荷兰车手解释道。“我的意思是，归根结底这不会改变我的周末，但如果我是他，我会表现得稍微聪明一点。而且，当你在为自己的席位而战时，这种行为可不太明智。”

    劳森与红牛姊妹车队签订的合同将于2026年底到期。

    维斯塔潘在第二节练习赛结束后接受 Motorsport.com 及其他媒体采访时，对这起事件做了更详细的描述。

    他说：“我当时正在做一个快速的出场圈，因为我遇到了一些小问题。所以我推进得比较猛。他开得非常慢。于是我想，好吧，那就冲一把。但他一看到我过来，也开始加速了。”

    “我不知道他脑子里在想什么，然后就发生了那样的事，想把我挤出赛道。对我来说，以他的位置来跟我挑事，这种行为实在不太明智。”

    劳森因在第二节练习赛中驾驶行为异常而被传唤至赛会干事，但截至发稿时，赛会干事尚未公布任何决定。

    这位新西兰车手目前以77分在车手积分榜上排名第九，而他的小红牛新秀队友阿尔维德·林德布拉德以38分排名第11。维斯塔潘以236分排名第四。

    Max Verstappen sends blunt warning to Liam Lawson after Mexican GP FP2 clash

    Max Verstappen has criticised Liam Lawson's "not very smart behaviour" following their collision during Mexican GP practice

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen has criticised Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson after the pair came together in second practice for the Mexican Grand Prix, warning that it was "not very smart behaviour" from a driver fighting for his seat.

    The Red Bull driver and Lawson were both on flying laps when they made contact during Friday's FP2 session at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez.

    Asked by Sky Sports F1 whether it was simply an unlucky incident, Verstappen made his feelings clear.

    "Well, I don't think it's called unlucky. Maybe not very smart behaviour, what he was trying to do there," the Dutchman explained. "I mean, at the end of the day it's not going to change my weekend, but if I were him I would act a little bit smarter. Also, when you're fighting for your seat, it's not very smart behaviour."

    Lawson's contract with the Red Bull sister team expires at the end of 2026.

    Verstappen went into more detail about the incident while speaking to Motorsport.com and other media after the second practice session.

    "I was on a fast out-lap because I had some little issues," he said. "So I was pushing on quite a bit. He was driving really slow. So I was like, all right, let's go for it. But as soon as he saw me arrive, he started to speed up as well.

    "I don't know what was going through his head and that happened, trying to just run me off the track. For me, it's just not very smart behaviour when you're in his position to pick a fight with me."

    Lawson has been summoned to the stewards for driving erratically during FP2, but no decision from the stewards has been released at the time of writing.

    The New Zealander currently sits ninth in the drivers' standings with 77 points, while his Racing Bulls rookie team-mate Arvid Lindblad is 11th with 38 points. Verstappen is fourth with 236 points.
