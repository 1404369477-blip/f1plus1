    耐克成为F1官方服装合作伙伴

    耐克将于2027年开始取代彪马，成为F1的官方服装和鞋类供应商。耐克还将为F1学院提供装备。

    耐克将取代彪马成为F1的官方供应商，并发布F1主题系列。

    耐克作为运动服装巨头，已加入F1成为其官方合作伙伴和制服供应商，从2027年开始接替彪马。

    正如长期传闻的那样，耐克将从下个赛季开始为F1人员提供服装和鞋类，这一合作关系也扩展到了F1学院，此前该学院一直使用彪马的装备。

    这家运动公司将推出灵感来自F1的零售系列，其中第一个系列将于周五上市，以拉斯维加斯大奖赛为中心。

    “耐克将通过胶囊系列，专注于风格、性能和生活方式，包括鞋类、服装和配饰，以及性能创新，以及为下一代运动员和粉丝提供的赛车周末体验，为赛车运动的巅峰注入新的活力，”公告承诺道。

    “粉丝将在11月20日首次看到限量版的拉斯维加斯大奖赛x耐克胶囊系列，该系列将在F1 Hub和耐克拉斯维加斯门店推出。”

    耐克首席执行官埃利奥特·希尔说：“一级方程式赛车由竞争、速度和对创新的持续推动所驱动。这是一种运动员的心态，也是我们在耐克所共有的。我们一起，有一个不可思议的机会为这项运动注入新的活力，服务于它的运动员，并激励全世界的粉丝。”

    Nike to replace Puma as Formula 1's official supplier, release F1-themed collections

    Athletic apparel giant Nike has joined F1 as its official partner and uniform supplier, taking over from Puma at the start of 2027.

    As was long rumoured, Nike will start supplying F1 personnel with apparel and footwear form the start of next season, a partnership that also extends to F1 Academy, which had both been using Puma kit until this year.

    The athletic company will also come up with F1-inspired retail collections, the first of which will go on sale on Friday and be centred around the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    "Nike will bring new energy to the pinnacle of motorsport through capsule collections with a focus on style, performance, and lifestyle including footwear, apparel, and accessories, as well as performance innovations, and race-weekend experiences for the next generation of athletes and fans," the announcement promised.

    "Fans will get a first look on 20 November, when the limited Las Vegas Grand Prix x Nike capsule collection drops at the F1 Hub and Nike Las Vegas stores.

    Nike CEO Elliott Hill said: "Formula 1 is driven by competition, speed and a constant push to innovate. That’s an athlete mindset, and it’s one we share at Nike. Together, we have an incredible opportunity to bring new energy to the sport, serve its athletes and inspire fans around the world.”
