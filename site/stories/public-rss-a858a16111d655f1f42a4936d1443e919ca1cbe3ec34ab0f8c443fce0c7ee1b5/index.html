    Mexico City GP: Charles Leclerc completes Friday practice double as Ferrari team-mate Lewis Hamilton suffers technical failure

    Charles Leclerc tops both Friday practice sessions for Ferrari; Lewis Hamilton suffers technical failure; Max Verstappen collides with Liam Lawson; watch every Mexico City GP session live on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 9pm on Saturday and the race at 8pm on Sunday

    Highlights from Friday's practice sessions at the Mexico City Grand Prix

    Charles Leclerc completed a Friday practice double at the Mexico City Grand Prix but Ferrari's strong showing was dampened by Lewis Hamilton suffering a technical issue.

    Leclerc produced a time of 1:17.827s to finish just over a tenth clear of McLaren's Lando Norris and Mercedes' George Russell, with the British pair among 10 drivers who had earlier sat out the opening session to make way for rookie drivers.

    Kimi Antonelli, who only needs to finish ninth in Sunday's race to become Formula 1's youngest-ever world champion, was a tenth down on his team-mate Russell in fourth.
