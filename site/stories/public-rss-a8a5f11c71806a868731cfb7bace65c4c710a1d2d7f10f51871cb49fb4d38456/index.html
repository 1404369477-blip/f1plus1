    凯迪拉克推出佩雷斯特别版Escalade-V，庆祝其F1第300次参赛

    汽车制造商凯迪拉克推出了一款以F1车手塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯命名的特别版Escalade-V。仅生产11辆，以庆祝车手的赛车号码。

    凯迪拉克在墨西哥城大奖赛之前发布了价值222,500美元的塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯特别版Escalade-V车型，以庆祝其300次F1赛事的启动

    凯迪拉克为其F1车手塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯推出了一款独家版的682马力Escalade-V车型，以庆祝墨西哥城大奖赛

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    汽车制造商凯迪拉克为了纪念其F1车手塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯，推出了一款特别版的Escalade-V。仅生产11辆，以庆祝车手的赛车号码

    名为Escalade-V Checo Perez版的SUV现在在墨西哥的经销商处有售，售价为409.9万比索——约合22.25万美元。这比该国目前销售的普通版本高出约25万比索。

    除了在车身某处有F1车手的名字外，你还能得到什么？

    正如你所期望的，变化主要是外观上的，Escalade和V系列标志以黑色完成，加上与MAC-26 F1赛车颜色相呼应的黑白凯迪拉克标志。格栅和车门把手都带有照明，打开引擎盖将揭示一个碳纤维引擎盖。

    车轮是24英寸的磨砂石墨单元。

    内饰也进行了一些小改动，配备了运动踏板和与车队相同的单色主题装饰。B柱和中央控制台上两个编号的铭牌告诉你你购买的是哪一辆，佩雷斯签名的标志旁边提醒你为什么最初会购买这款车。

    从性能角度来看，没有任何变化。6.2升V8发动机仍然采用涡轮增压，功率为682马力，扭矩为653磅英尺，通过10速自动变速箱传递到所有四个车轮。60英里每小时可以在4.4秒内完成，虽然不如F1赛车快，但对于公路使用来说已经足够快了。

    新车发布在佩雷斯家乡赛事墨西哥城大奖赛之前，紧接着本周末在奥斯汀的赛事。这将是他第300次大奖赛发车。

    但36岁的佩雷斯暗示他的F1生涯即将结束。本周他与墨西哥广播公司Televisa交谈时表示，凯迪拉克的项目“需要比我想象的更多时间”。

    “我认为给出具体年份是困难的，但不会太久，”佩雷斯说。 “真正让我犹豫不决（继续）的是我的孩子们。”

    他补充说： “我希望在一级方程式赛车的生涯中画上一个圆满的句号，我所能说的是，我们很快就会知道（我何时退休）。

    Cadillac unveils $222,500 Sergio Perez special-edition ahead of 300th F1 start

    Cadillac has unveiled an exclusive Sergio Perez edition of its 682hp Escalade-V ahead of the Mexico City Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Car manufacturer Cadillac has launched a special edition Escalade-V in the name of its Formula 1 driver Sergio Perez. Just 11 will be made, in celebration of the driver's race number.

    Aptly named the Escalade-V Checo Perez Edition, the SUV is now on sale in dealers throughout Mexico with a purchase rice of 4,099,000 pesos - or around $222,500. This is roughly 250,000 pesos more than the standard version currently for sale in the country.

    So what do you get apart from an F1 driver's name scribbled somewhere on the bodywork?

    As you would expect, the changes are mostly cosmetic, with the Escalade and V-Series badges finished in black, plus a monochrome Cadillac logo mirroring the colours of the MAC-26 F1 car. The grille and door handles are illuminated, and lifting the bonnet will reveal a carbon fibre engine cover.

    Wheels are 24-inch Satin Graphite units.

    The interior also gets a minor makeover, with sports pedals and trim coloured in the same monochrome theme as the team. Two numbered plaques on the B-pillar and centre console tell you which of the 11 you have purchased, and Perez's signature sat next to the number reminds you why you made the purchase in the first place.

    From a performance point of view, nothing changes at all. The 6.2-litre V8 still comes supercharged to the tune of 682hp and 653lb-ft of torque, which is sent through a 10-speed automatic gearbox to all four wheels. 60mph comes and goes in 4.4 seconds - not quite as fast as an F1 car, but plenty for road use.

    The launch comes ahead of Perez’s home race, the Mexico City Grand Prix, which follows this weekend’s round in Austin. It will be his 300th grand prix start.

    But the 36-year-old has hinted that the end of his F1 career is getting closer. Speaking to Mexican broadcaster Televisa this week, he said Cadillac’s project “requires more time than I initially thought”.

    “I think giving a specific number [of years] is difficult, but it won’t be much longer,” Perez said. “What really holds me back [from continuing] is my kids.”

    He added: “I want to finish my time in Formula 1 on a high note, and all I can say is that we will know when [I retire] soon.”
