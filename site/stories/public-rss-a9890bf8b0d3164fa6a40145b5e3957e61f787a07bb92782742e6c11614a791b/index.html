    红牛正式留用哈贾尔，2027赛季搭档维斯塔潘

    红牛官方确认，伊萨克·哈贾尔将在2027赛季继续留在红牛F1车队，担任马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友。车队在阿塞拜疆大奖赛哈贾尔伤愈复出之际宣布了这一消息。

    红牛已正式确认，伊萨克·哈贾尔将在2027赛季F1中继续担任马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友。

    车队在哈贾尔于巴库举行的阿塞拜疆大奖赛伤愈复出之际宣布了这一消息，此前他因手腕骨折缺席了三站比赛。

    在那之前，他在车队的首个赛季表现非常出色，可以说是自丹尼尔·里卡多以来维斯塔潘最接近的队友，尽管这可能得益于2026年新赛车和动力单元带来的竞争力重新平衡。

    车队领队洛朗·梅基斯表示，哈贾尔“在赛道内外都表现出色，尤其是考虑到这只是他第二个F1赛季，这让续约成为容易的决定”。

    “受伤以来的这段时间对伊萨克来说很艰难，但这次续约证明了我们对他有多么信任，我们迫不及待想看到他重返赛道，”梅基斯补充道。

    哈贾尔已获得71分，维斯塔潘则获得145分（多赛三场），哈贾尔在摩纳哥登上领奖台——该成绩因皮埃尔·加斯利的罚单经历漫长的上诉程序后被取消又恢复。

    现在注意力转向红牛的第二支车队小红牛，新秀阿尔维德·林德布拉德几乎确定留队，但关于队友利亚姆·劳森的情况则更有悬念。

    红牛有F2冠军争夺者尼古拉·措洛夫等待F1首秀，但劳森在顶替哈贾尔期间表现非常出色，并且在小红牛度过了一个强劲的赛季，因此似乎是留队的热门人选。

    英文原文

    Red Bull has officially retained Isack Hadjar as Max Verstappen's team-mate for the 2027 Formula 1 season.

    The team made the announcement upon Hadjar's return from injury at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix in Baku, after he'd missed three rounds with a wrist fracture.

    He had been having a very productive first season in the team before that, arguably the closest of Verstappen's team-mates since Daniel Ricciardo, though this may have been aided by the competitive re-balancing triggered by the new-for-2026 cars and power units.

    Team boss Laurent Mekies says Hadjar "has been superb on and off track, especially considering this is only his second Formula 1 season, which made extending his contract an easy decision".

    "The period since his injury has been a difficult one for Isack, but this extension is a testament to how much we believe in him and we can't wait to see him back out on track," Mekies added.

    Hadjar has scored 71 points to Verstappen's 145 (over three more rounds) and has scored a podium in Monaco - which was taken away and then reinstated in a drawn-out appeals process over Pierre Gasly's penalty.

    Attention now turns to Red Bull's second team Racing Bulls, where rookie Arvid Lindblad is virtually certain to remain but there's more intrigue surrounding team-mate Liam Lawson.

    Red Bull has Formula 2 title contender Nikola Tsolov waiting in the wings for an F1 debut, but Lawson has acquitted himself very well standing in for Hadjar and has had a strong season at Racing Bulls so appears to be the favourite to stay on.
