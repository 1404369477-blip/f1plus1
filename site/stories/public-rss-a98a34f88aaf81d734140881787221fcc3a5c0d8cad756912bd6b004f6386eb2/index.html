    汉密尔顿谈观看自己F1拉斯维加斯看台的感受：令人孤独

    汉密尔顿在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛前的媒体日上，谈及看到粉丝为他专设的看台感到孤独。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿在看到自己的F1拉斯维加斯看台后谈到了“孤独”的感觉

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿反思F1的“孤独和寂寞”感，因为他的专属看台回归拉斯维加斯大奖赛

    对于法拉利车迷来说。最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，车手更新，技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应

    在F1生涯的第近20个年头，刘易斯·汉密尔顿仍然没有把他的粉丝规模视为理所当然。在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛前的媒体日上，这位七届世界冠军在被问及看到粉丝在自己的专属看台填满看台时是什么感觉时，罕见地透露了车手生活的情感压力。

    “我从未想过，真的，我从未想过我会有自己的看台，”汉密尔顿说。 “此外，我不知道我会拥有如此令人惊叹的支持社区，他们一直陪伴我走过这些岁月，看到他们在本周末如此全情投入地支持……他们的能量在这个有时可能会相当孤立和孤独的世界中有着巨大的差别，所以当你有机会与一大群人一起看到并分享这些经历时，那真是太棒了。”

    路易斯·汉密尔顿看台在2025年首次亮相后，连续第二年回归拉斯维加斯大道赛道。位于和谐直道附近的第17弯道处，由原西和谐区内的表演区，这个看台在2026年再次吸引了大量需求，周四和周五的单日门票已在官方赛车门户网站上售罄。组织者今年还扩大了套餐，为持票者提供进入三个粉丝区、F1学院维修区的视野以及三天购票者专属的汉密尔顿T恤。

    这种支持在一场要求极高的比赛中来得正是时候。在进入拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔和阿布扎比的赛季收官三连赛之前，汉密尔顿刚刚结束了一场充满起伏的美洲之旅，他在奥斯汀排名第四，在墨西哥城排名第二，但在圣保罗排名第九。

    “想想看，这一年过得如此漫长，所以我当然期待着结束，但我同时也只是想尝试看看我们是否能够以一个高潮结束，”汉密尔顿说。

    随着梅赛德斯已经锁定了2026年的两个冠军，汉密尔顿的主要目标是保护法拉利在车队积分榜上对红牛的95分优势（510分对415分），以获得第二的位置。个人而言，他目前在车手积分榜上以261分排在第四位——仅比梅赛德斯的乔治·拉塞尔少一分，比马克斯·维斯塔潘少18分。

    “我们的主要焦点是确保车队在车队积分榜（冠军）上稳固第二的位置，因为……这对工厂里的每个人都意义重大，尤其是对我而言，”汉密尔顿说，并补充说，强劲的成绩可以帮助他“从第四位爬升回来，并希望进入前三名”。

    在2024年获得第二名后，改善去年在拉斯维加斯的第八名成绩将取决于征服沙漠的寒冷。‘让轮胎工作，这需要团队付出很多努力，确保我们能在正确的窗口让轮胎工作，”汉密尔顿说，称这是“我们赛道上可能最大的挑战，因为我们几乎没有下压力和抓地力”

    Lewis Hamilton opens up on "isolating" feeling after seeing his own F1 Las Vegas grandstand

    Lewis Hamilton reflects on F1 feeling "isolating and lonely" as his dedicated grandstand returns to the Las Vegas GP

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Nearly two decades into his Formula 1 career, Lewis Hamilton still is not taking the scale of his following for granted. Speaking on media day ahead of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, the seven-time world champion offered a rare glimpse into the emotional toll of paddock life when asked what it meant to see fans packing his own dedicated grandstand at the circuit.

    "I never in a million years thought that, honestly, I never ever thought that I’d ever have my own grandstand," Hamilton said. "Also, I didn’t know that I would have such an amazing community of support that have kept me going all these years, and to see them here this weekend and in full force supporting... their energy makes the world of difference in a world that can sometimes be quite isolating and lonely, so it’s great when you get to see and share those experiences with a large group of people."

    The Lewis Hamilton Grandstand has returned to the Las Vegas Strip Circuit for a second consecutive season after debuting in 2025. Located near Turn 17 along the Harmon Straight inside The Performance Zone by WHOOP - formerly the West Harmon Zone - the stand has drawn heavy demand again in 2026, with Thursday and Friday single-day tickets already sold out on the official race portal. Organisers also expanded the package this year to grant ticket holders access across three fan zones, views of the F1 Academy paddock, and an exclusive Hamilton T-shirt for three-day buyers.

    That support comes at a welcome point in a demanding campaign. Entering the season-closing triple-header across Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi, Hamilton is coming off a roller coaster Americas swing that saw him finish fourth in Austin and second in Mexico City before slipping to ninth in São Paulo.

    "It’s crazy to think it’s been such a long year, so I’m definitely looking forward to the end, but I’m also just wanting to try and see if we can finish on a high," Hamilton said.

    With Mercedes having already sealed both 2026 championships, Hamilton’s main target is protecting Ferrari’s 95-point cushion over Red Bull for second in the constructors’ standings (510 points to 415). Individually, he enters Nevada fourth in the drivers’ table on 261 points—just one behind Mercedes’ George Russell and 18 adrift of Max Verstappen.

    "The main focus is making sure we solidify second in the constructors' [championship] for the team, because... it means a huge amount to everyone at the factory, but especially to me also," Hamilton said, adding that strong results could help him "climb back up from fourth and hopefully be in the top three".

    Improving on last year’s eighth-place finish in Vegas - after taking second in 2024 - will hinge on conquering the desert cold. "Getting the tyres working, that takes a lot of hard work from all the team, making sure we’ve got the tyres working in the right window," Hamilton said, calling it "probably the biggest challenge on the track where we have very little downforce and grip".
