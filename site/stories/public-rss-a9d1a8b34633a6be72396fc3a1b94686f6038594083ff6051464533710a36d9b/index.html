    克拉克森在美国大奖赛采访中谈及科拉平托与卡戴珊引热议

    杰里米·克拉克森作为阿尔派车队嘉宾出席美国大奖赛，在接受天空体育F1采访时谈到弗朗哥·科拉平托的罚退以及金·卡戴珊，引发争议。

    杰里米·克拉克森因对弗朗哥·科拉平托和金·卡戴珊的评论引发热议

    杰里米·克拉克森现身美国大奖赛，让人回想起他当年颇具争议的《Top Gear》阿根廷之行

    杰里米·克拉克森接受天空体育F1采访

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    杰里米·克拉克森只接受了天空体育F1的一次采访，却依然引发了混乱。这位《克拉克森的农场》主持人跟马丁·布伦德尔、娜奥米·希夫和西蒙·拉赞比聊了诸多话题，从弗兰科·科拉平托因“身为阿根廷人”而受到的罚退，到他对金·卡戴珊的看法。

    克拉克森是阿尔派车队在美洲赛道这个周末的嘉宾，由于他住得离位于恩斯通的车队总部很近，双方建立了牢固的关系。被问及车队的机会时，他说：

    “弗兰科因为身为阿根廷人而受到了罚退。大概吧。嗯，要我说，就凭这个我也得给他一个罚退。但不管怎样，那只是因为我们当年去那里时发生的事。”

    不出所料，这位主持人的这番话让车迷们分成了两派。克拉克森与阿根廷的渊源要追溯到十多年前，那是《最高档》历史上最具争议的几集之一。

    2014年，克拉克森、理查德·哈蒙德和詹姆斯·梅为拍摄特别节目穿越巴塔哥尼亚，克拉克森驾驶一辆车牌号为H982 FKL的保时捷928。当地人将其视为对1982年福克兰战争的影射，乌斯怀亚爆发抗议，退伍老兵在剧组下榻的酒店外示威。

    局势迅速升级。团队遭到石块袭击，最终将车辆弃置路边，随后越过边境逃往智利。

    英国广播公司坚称该车牌纯属巧合。执行制片人安迪·威尔曼表示，任何关于车辆因车牌而被选中或车牌被调换的说法都“完全不实”。

    克拉克森显然没有忘记这一事件。

    “皮埃尔（加斯利）应该能进第三节排位赛，”他继续说道。

    科拉平托因本赛季使用第四套控制电子单元，将在奥斯汀站接受发车位后退十位的处罚。加斯利从第十一位起步。

    克拉克森接着解释了他为何支持这支位于他牛津郡农场附近的车队。

    “从迈克尔（舒马赫）在的时候，到费尔南多（阿隆索），再到基米·莱科宁的那些年，现在则是皮埃尔。因为他们是我们的本地车队。我是说，农场里帮我照看兔子的那个女孩，她爸爸就在阿尔派工作。所以你会觉得自己和他们像一家人一样。”

    “那可真够牵强的，”布伦德尔微笑着回答。

    随后话题转向了布伦德尔在他著名的发车格漫步中经常遇到的明星们，克拉克森承认他很喜欢看这个环节。

    “我喜欢你面对那些完全不知道自己为什么在这里的人的样子。你知道，今天早上我还在聊99年加拿大站红旗下的埃迪·欧文，以及他如何在掉到队尾后重新登上领奖台。而你就想，你要采访的大多数人，他们连车是什么都未必清楚。所以我觉得看你不得不做这种事很有趣。我真的这么觉得。抱歉。”

    随后莱森比问布伦德尔，当他在新加坡终于采访到刘易斯·汉密尔顿的伴侣金·卡戴珊时——此前她在摩纳哥曾著名地对他视而不见——他的心是否融化了。

    “我没看那个。因为说实话，我对金·卡戴珊不太感兴趣。所以我把那段剪掉了。不，我不感兴趣。我为什么要对金·卡戴珊感兴趣？我是来看一级方程式赛车的，不是来看金·卡戴珊的。所有镜头都对着那边，‘哦，金·卡戴珊来了！’我为什么要在意？”

    克拉克森坚称自己并非锦标赛专家，说他“关于一级方程式赛车所知道的一切”都是从与他交谈的天空体育评论员那里学来的。仅凭这句话或许已经赢得了一些车迷的好感，但社交媒体上突然又热闹起来，大家纷纷对克拉克森关于科拉平托和阿根廷的尖锐言论发表看法。

    Jeremy Clarkson causes a stir with Franco Colapinto and Kim Kardashian comments

    Jeremy Clarkson's appearance at the United States Grand Prix revived memories of his controversial "Top Gear trip" to Argentina

    eremy Clarkson is interviewed on Sky Sports F1

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Jeremy Clarkson had a single interview with Sky Sports F1 and yet it still resulted in chaos. The Clarkson's Farm presenter discussed a multitude of subjects with Martin Brundle, Naomi Schiff and Simon Lazenby, from Franco Colapinto's grid penalty "for being Argentinian" to his thoughts on Kim Kardashian.

    Clarkson was Alpine's guest for the weekend at the Circuit of the Americas, having formed a strong relationship with the outfit due to his proximity to the headquarters in Enstone. Asked about the team's chances, he said:

    “Franco’s got a grid penalty for being Argentinian. Probably. Well, I’d give him one for that. But anyway, that’s just because of what happened when we went there."

    This line from the presenter, unsurprisingly, has divided fans. Clarkson’s history with Argentina goes back more than a decade, to one of the most controversial episodes in Top Gear history.

    In 2014, Clarkson, Richard Hammond and James May travelled through Patagonia for a special episode, with Clarkson driving a Porsche 928 carrying the number plate H982 FKL. Locals took it as a reference to the 1982 Falklands War, and protests broke out in Ushuaia, with war veterans demonstrating outside the crew’s hotel.

    The situation quickly escalated. The team was pelted with stones and eventually abandoned their cars at the roadside before fleeing across the border into Chile.

    The BBC insisted the plate was a coincidence. Executive producer Andy Wilman said any suggestion that the car had been chosen for its number plate, or that the plate had been swapped, was “completely untrue”.

    Clarkson has clearly not forgotten this episode.

    "Pierre [Gasly] should be good for Q3," he continued.

    Colapinto will serve a 10-place grid penalty in Austin, for using a fourth control electronics unit of the season. Gasly starts in P11.

    Clarkson went on to explain why he supports the squad, which is based near his farm in Oxfordshire.

    “Since Michael [Schumacher] was there, then Fernando [Alonso], then all through the Kimi Raikkonen years, and then now with Pierre. Because they’re our local team. I mean, the girl that looks after my rabbits at the farm, her dad works for Alpine. So you feel as though you’re part of a family with them.”

    “That’s pretty tenuous,” replied Brundle with a smile.

    The conversation then turned to the celebrities Brundle regularly meets on his famous grid walk, which Clarkson admitted he enjoys watching.

    “I love the way that you’re faced with these people who have literally no idea why they’re here. You know, I was sitting talking this morning about Eddie Irvine in Canada in ‘99 under the red flag and how he got back onto the podium having dropped to the back of the field. And you just think, most people you have to interview, they’d barely know what a car is. So I find it funny watching you having to do that. I genuinely do. Sorry.”

    Lazenby then asked whether his heart had melted when Brundle finally interviewed Kim Kardashian - Lewis Hamilton's partner - in Singapore, after she had famously blanked him in Monaco.

    “I wasn’t watching that. Because I’m sort of not interested in Kim Kardashian if I’m honest. So I edited that bit out. No, I’m not interested. Why would I be interested in Kim Kardashian? I’m here to watch Formula 1, not Kim Kardashians. And all the cameras go, ‘Oh, Kim Kardashian’s here!’ Why do I care?”

    Clarkson insisted he was no expert on the championship, saying he learns “everything I know about Formula 1” from the Sky pundits he was in conversation with. While this line alone may have won some fans over, social media is suddenly alight again with opinions on Clarkson's edgy comments on Colapinto and Argentina.
