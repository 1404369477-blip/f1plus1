    安东内利有意与维斯塔潘同战纽博格林，但有一前提

    安东内利表示愿意与维斯塔潘一同参加纽博格林耐力赛，但前提是先获得梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫的支持。

    安东内利有意与维斯塔潘同战纽博格林，但有一前提

    这位新科世界冠军表示，他很愿意加入维斯塔潘的耐力赛计划

    基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车不止于运动的故事。

    2026年一级方程式世界冠军基米·安东内利表示，他很愿意在纽博格林赛道与四届世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘同场竞速，但他也承认，自己首先必须赢得梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫的支持。

    维斯塔潘从不掩饰自己对耐力赛的热爱，并且曾在参加一级方程式赛事之余，在纽博格林驾驶GT3赛车参赛。

    在卡塔尔大奖赛前接受媒体采访时，这位意大利车手被问及是否会考虑加入维斯塔潘的耐力赛项目。

    “哦，是的，我们确实谈过这件事，而且显然首先我们需要说服大老板托托，”安东内利解释道，“所以我必须继续赢下去，才能给自己争取到最好的机会。”

    但未来我肯定非常想去做这件事，尤其是纽博格林北环赛道，因为那是我梦想中想要驾驶的赛道之一。因为我只开着公路车跑过一圈，那简直太疯狂了，这条赛道绝对疯狂。

    所以我非常想有一天能去那里比赛，而且如果能和马克斯一起在车队里，那就更好了。但除此之外，我其实非常想在那条赛道上驾驶。

    安东内利还透露了本赛季与维斯塔潘同场竞技让他学到了什么。

    他说：“当然学到了很多关于他的比赛技巧，以及他如何为自己创造最佳机会来成功完成动作，从而超越你，同时还有他防守时的做法。”

    今年有很多精彩的场景。其中最近的一个是新加坡站起步时，勒克莱尔与他并排，把他逼到了一个非常困难的境地，他几乎无法再继续踩刹车，因为首先他完全在内线，几乎没有空间，所以选择非常有限。

    而且显然，继续踩刹车对他来说会是一个非常冒险的选择。所以，仅仅看马克斯在防守或进攻时如何定位自己就非常引人入胜，因为显然你能看出他的比赛技巧非常高超，他总是试图把自己置于最佳位置，以便完成超车或完成一次漂亮的防守。

    这真的很酷，也是有很多可以向他学习的地方。除了许多其他方面之外，但这是最重要的。

    安东内利在墨西哥大奖赛上锁定了车手总冠军。

    Kimi Antonelli teases Nordschleife race with Max Verstappen, but there is one condition

    The new world champion says he would love to join Verstappen's endurance racing programme

    Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    2026 Formula 1 champion Kimi Antonelli says he would love to race alongside four-time champion Max Verstappen at the Nordschleife, but admits he would first have to win the support of Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff.

    Verstappen has made no secret of his love of endurance racing, and has raced GT3 cars at the Nurburgring alongside his F1 commitments.

    While speaking to the media ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix, the Italian driver was asked whether he would consider joining Verstappen's endurance racing programme.

    "Oh yeah, we did talk about it and obviously first we need to convince the big boss, Toto," Antonelli explained. "So I've got to keep winning to give myself the best chance to do that.

    "But definitely in the future it's something I would really love to do, especially the Nordschleife because it's one of my dream tracks to drive. Because I only did a lap with the road car and it's just insane, the track is absolutely insane.

    "So I would love to race there one day and definitely if it would be with Max together in the team, it would be even better. But apart from that I would actually love to drive at that track."

    Antonelli also revealed what he has learned from racing against Verstappen this season.

    "Well definitely a lot about his race craft and how he positioned himself to give himself the best shot to make a successful move so to overtake you but as well when he's defending," he said.

    "There's many great scenarios this year. One of them that was quite recent, Singapore at the start when Leclerc went alongside him, he put him in a very difficult position, where he couldn't really push the brake any further because first of all he was fully on the inside and he had basically very little room, so he had very little options.

    "And obviously pushing the brake would have been a very risky option for him. So just by how Max positioned himself while defending or attacking is very fascinating, because obviously you can see his race craft is very advanced and he always tries to put himself in the best position to come away with an overtake or come away with a good defence.

    "And that's really cool and something to learn a lot from him. Other than many other things but this is the most important."

    Antonelli secured the drivers' championship at the Mexican Grand Prix.
