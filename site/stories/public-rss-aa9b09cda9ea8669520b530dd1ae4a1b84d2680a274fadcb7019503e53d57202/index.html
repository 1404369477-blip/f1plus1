    红牛领队：维斯塔潘不会参与决定2027年小红牛车手阵容

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯表示，小红牛2027年的F1车手阵容将完全由小红牛自行决定，维斯塔潘不会参与其中，尽管他在墨西哥与劳森发生了接触。

    红牛领队洛朗·梅基斯坚称，维斯塔潘对小红牛2027年的车手阵容没有任何影响力。

    红牛车队领队洛朗·梅基斯表示，小红牛2027年的F1车手阵容将完全由小红牛自行决定，尽管马克斯·维斯塔潘在墨西哥与利亚姆·劳森发生了冲突。

    维斯塔潘和劳森在第二节练习中发生接触，两人均被罚在墨西哥大奖赛退后一位发车。

    红牛车队首席执行官兼领队洛朗·梅基斯解释称，2027年F1车手阵容将完全由小红牛自行决定，尽管马克斯·维斯塔潘在墨西哥与利亚姆·劳森发生冲突，但他对此没有发言权。

    维斯塔潘和劳森在周五第二节练习赛中发生接触，两人均被处以墨西哥大奖赛正赛发车位退后一位的处罚。

    这位四届世界冠军在练习赛结束后对媒体表示，劳森的行为“不太明智”。

    “嗯，我不认为这能叫运气不好。也许他当时想做的事不太明智，”这位荷兰人解释道，“我的意思是，归根结底这不会改变我的周末，但如果我是他，我会表现得稍微聪明一点。而且，当你在为自己的席位而战时，这种行为不太明智。”

    劳森与红牛姊妹车队的合同将于2026年底到期，但梅基斯坚称维斯塔潘和红牛不会对小红牛车队的车手阵容施加影响。

    “不，我认为你知道必须把两方面分开。有些在赛道上的事情非常没有必要，因此让涉及其中的车手感到沮丧，也让涉及其中的车队感到沮丧，这大概就是你们从马克斯的回答中听到的，然后还有车手决定，”梅基斯对天空体育F1说。

    “小红牛（车手阵容）由他们决定，而且只由他们决定。”

    当被要求澄清维斯塔潘是否会影响小红牛车队的车手决定时，梅基斯补充道：“马克斯对赛车运动了解得太多了，无论如何都不会被单一事件影响。我认为到目前为止我们一直处于落后。”

    “其他三支顶尖车队不知怎么地总比我们多榨取了一点性能，但周末还没结束。伙计们一直在为我们能对赛车做的最后一项修改倾尽全力。”

    “让我们看看能不能再靠近领先集团一点。今天早上情况很糟的时候，我们大约落后零点三五秒。如果我们能缩小一点这个差距，应该能让我们重新回到竞争行列中。”

    Max Verstappen will have no say in 2027 Racing Bulls driver line-up, says Red Bull boss

    Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies has insisted Max Verstappen will have no influence over Racing Bulls' 2027 driver line-up

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Liam Lawson, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Red Bull Racing CEO and team principal Laurent Mekies has explained that Racing Bulls alone will decide its Formula 1 driver line-up for 2027 and that Max Verstappen will not have a say despite his clash with Liam Lawson in Mexico.

    Both Verstappen and Lawson were handed a one-place grid penalty for the Mexican Grand Prix after making contact during the second practice session on Friday.

    The four-time champion argued it was "not very smart behaviour" from Lawson while speaking to the media after the session.

    "Well, I don't think it's called unlucky. Maybe not very smart behaviour, what he was trying to do there," the Dutchman explained. "I mean, at the end of the day it's not going to change my weekend, but if I were him I would act a little bit smarter. Also, when you're fighting for your seat, it's not very smart behaviour."

    Lawson's contract with the Red Bull sister team expires at the end of 2026, but Mekies insisted that Verstappen and Red Bull would have no sway over the Racing Bulls driver line-up.

    "No, I think you know you have to separate both aspects. You have some things that were very unnecessary on the track and therefore are frustrating for the drivers involved and frustrating for the teams involved, and this is probably what you hear with Max's answer, and then there are driver decisions," Mekies told Sky Sports F1.

    "Racing Bulls [driver line-up is] down to them and only down to them."

    When asked to clarify if Verstappen could influence Racing Bulls' driver decisions, Mekies added: "Max knows motorsport far too much to be influenced by a single episode anyway. I think so far we've been on the back foot.

    "The other three top teams have been somehow extracting a bit more than us, but the weekend is not finished. The guys have been putting the best shot at the last modification we can do to the car.

    "Let's see if we can get a bit closer to the top. When things were very bad this morning, we were about three and a half tenths away. If we can close a bit of that gap, it should put us back in the mix."
