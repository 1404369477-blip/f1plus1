    乐高1,639件拼装版红牛小红牛20赛车内置F1技术

    马克斯·维斯塔潘的2024年夺冠红牛小红牛20是乐高制作的最受欢迎的F1赛车之一，尽管其表现不如小红牛19出色，但仍然帮助马克斯·维斯塔潘赢得了他的第四个F1世界冠军头衔。

    这款由1,639个零件组成的乐高红牛小红牛20赛车充满了工作着的F1技术

    乐高1,639个零件的Technic红牛小红牛20复刻了马克斯·维斯塔潘2024年夺冠的F1赛车，拥有工作着的转向、悬挂、变速箱和DRS系统

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    这是什么？

    乐高技术系列红牛小红牛20一级方程式赛车模型是范斯特appen在2024年赢得最新世界冠军时所驾驶的赛车的大型模型。在这款模型中，他赢得了九场大奖赛。

    它由1639个零件组成，适合18岁以上人士拼装。完全组装后，它的尺寸巨大，长64厘米，宽25厘米，高15厘米。这使得它成为一件完美的展示品，对于那些想要近距离观察的人来说，细节丰富。

    它的价格是多少？

    它在英国售价为199.99英镑，在美国售价为229.99美元。它首次于2025年3月上市，但目前在LEGO.com上仍在销售。但由于其受欢迎程度，大多数销售点都已售罄。

    它有什么功能？

    技术系列赛车是真正的装备，小红牛20也不例外。它包括：

    一个带有移动活塞和旋转MGU-H的V6发动机。

    一个带有中位挡的 双速变速箱，与后差速器相连

    转向系统可以同时控制前后轮和方向盘

    前悬挂采用拉杆式，后悬挂采用推杆式，与真实赛车相同

    后翼带有可手动开启的DRS襟翼

    可拆卸的后车身，以便您展示引擎

    轮胎甚至有印刷的Pirelli细节，涂装由两块大贴纸组成。

    建造过程如何？

    套装包含10袋，每袋构建汽车的一个部分。后悬挂、引擎、车鼻甚至侧箱都建造得非常细致。不幸的是，对于细心的粉丝来说，前后轮胎的尺寸与真实汽车不同，颜色也不完全准确。但尽管如此，这是每个F1粉丝都应该关注的乐高模型。

    有没有更便宜的选择？

    是的，但它没有这款全脂Technic版本那么美味。乐高速度冠军系列版本由251个零件组成，并附带一个红牛车手迷你人偶。在美国，它售价约为27美元，对于那些寻找更经济实惠的方式来搭建地面效应时代外观最佳之一的赛车的人来说。

    This 1,639-piece LEGO Red Bull RB20 is packed with working F1 technology

    LEGO's 1,639-piece Technic Red Bull RB20 recreates Max Verstappen's 2024 championship-winning Formula 1 car with working steering, suspension, gearbox and DRS

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Max Verstappen's 2024 title-winning Red Bull is one of the most popular Formula 1 cars LEGO has ever made. While the real car was not quite as dominant as the RB19, it did propel Max Verstappen to his fourth F1 championship title. It is still on sale if you fancy yourself a bit of F1 history on your mantel.

    What is it?

    The LEGO Technic Oracle Red Bull Racing RB20 F1 Car is a large-scale model of the car Verstappen drove to his latest world championship title in 2024. In it, he won nine grands prix.

    It's made up of 1639 pieces and has an age rating of 18+. When fully built, it measures a huge 64cm long, 25cm wide and 15cm high. This makes it a perfect display piece with plenty of detail for those who have a closer peek.

    How much does it cost?

    It costs £199.99 in the UK and $229.99 in the United States. It first appeared on sale in March 2025, but is still on sale at LEGO.com. But due to its popularity, it's sold out in most outlets.

    What does it do?

    Technic cars are serious bits of kit, and the RB20 is no different. It includes:

    A V6 engine with moving pistons and a spinning MGU-H

    A two-speed gearbox with neutral, linked to a rear differential

    Steering that turns both the front wheels and the steering wheel

    Pull-rod front and push-rod rear suspension, as on the real car

    A rear wing with a DRS flap that opens by hand

    Removable rear bodywork so you can show off the engine

    The tyres even boast printed Pirelli detailing, and the livery is made up of two large sheets of stickers.

    What is the build like?

    The set comes in 10 bags with each one building a section of the car. The rear suspension, engine, nose and even sidepods are built in extreme detail. Unfortunately for eagle-eyed fans, the front and rear tyres are the same size unlike the real car, and the colours aren't quite spot on. But despite this, it's a LEGO model that every F1 fan should be eyeing up.

    Is there a cheaper option?

    Yes, but it's not quite as tasty as this full-fat Technic version. The LEGO Speed Champions version is made of 251 pieces and comes with a Red Bull driver minifigure. It sells for around $27 in the US for those looking for a more budget-friendly way to build one of the best looking cars of the ground effect era.
