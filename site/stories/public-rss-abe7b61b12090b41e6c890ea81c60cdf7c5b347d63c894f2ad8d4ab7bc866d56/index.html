    科顿·赫塔未来未定，凯迪拉克续约博塔斯

    凯迪拉克决定续约瓦尔特里·博塔斯与塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯搭档，为2026赛季的连续性和稳定性做出选择。这一决定也引发了关于科顿·赫塔未来走向的关键问题。

    决定时刻临近，科尔顿·赫塔在2027年凯迪拉克F1的拒绝后面临一个关键的职业抉择

    随着他进入一级方程式的努力遭遇阻碍，科尔顿·赫塔现在面临一个关键的职业决定

    凯迪拉克决定继续与经验丰富的老将瓦尔特里·博塔斯搭档塞吉奥·佩雷斯，这是在2026年一级方程式第11支车队首秀赛季问题缠身之后，为了连续性和稳定性做出的选择。这也引发了关于科尔顿·赫塔下一步将何去何从的关键问题

    随着凯迪拉克的大多数股东TWG对不久的将来拥有一位美国车手驾驶其赛车的前景着迷，车队不惜一切代价将赫塔带到车队，让他离开了与其姐妹车队安德里蒂全球的舒适环境，为了现在或永不的机会去尝试进入一级方程式

    Herta，一位九次赢得IndyCar赛事的车手，也是迄今为止最年轻的一位，面临着调整到欧洲赛车运动的挑战，包括掌握F2中特有的Pirelli轮胎，这些轮胎他也会在F1中遇到。

    这与他进行的私人测试相辅相成——凯迪拉克没有之前的赛车，因此从阿尔派恩租了一辆进行测试——以及多次大奖赛FP1练习，以增加他的超级驾照积分达到关键的40分。

    在巴西进行的今年第六次此类测试后，Herta终于达到了这个神奇数字。但就在那时，凯迪拉克首席执行官Dan Towriss和车队负责人Marcin Budkowski开始就保留博塔斯的想法达成一致，将Herta作为2027年的官方预备车手，准备在博塔斯无法在赛季前半段提升他的表现上限时介入。

    凯迪拉克可能没有考虑到的是，Herta对被忽视的消息的反应。据悉，这位26岁的美国人在目前拒绝了预备车手的角色，并决定不完成他在Hitech的麻烦F2赛季，放弃了在卡塔尔和阿布扎比的最后两轮比赛。

    瓦尔特里·博塔斯，凯迪拉克车队，塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯，凯迪拉克车队

    虽然这个决定可能因为他对错过机会的最初失望而变得模糊，赫塔也渴望继续在竞争性比赛中驾驶，而不是坐在场边，但这个立场多少有些令人惊讶。在37岁的博塔斯之后成为凯迪拉克的第三车手，似乎在整个车队的整个 paddock 中，无论是2027年还是2028年，都是最有机会晋升F1的位置。

    “我们给他提供了一个预备位置，”托里斯在拉斯维加斯说。“这个位置为’28年空着，所以Colton有选择。在其他运动中也有选择。我认为他已经决定了一个方向，但我们仍在讨论这个话题，并期待看到Colton想要做什么。”

    “这绝对是一个艰难的决定，与Colton的艰难讨论。我认为他感到失望。为此投入了大量的工作，大量的准备工作[已经完成]。我知道Colton非常勇敢，甚至愿意承担这个风险，进入F2并尝试在短时间内吸收所有这些知识。”

    这个角色被认为对于一名预备车手来说非常有利，而且当赛道扩展到33个参赛者时，它还可以与一次性的印第安纳波利斯500赛事相结合。

    赫塔现在面临着一项决定自己职业生涯的重大决定，因为2028年的赌注很大。如果他能在测试中留下足够好的印象以取代博塔斯，那么这将是他在F1赛场上全职参赛的最早时间。鉴于预备车手们往往很快就会失去职业势头，这也可能是他最后一次这样做的机会。

    与此同时，他的老东家也即将进行一次大规模的翻新。在超过16年后，印第安纳波利斯赛车将在2028年首次引入一款全新的赛车，Dallara IR-28，它在测试中获得了极高的评价，并配备了一台更强大的2.4升双涡轮增压V6引擎。

    在这个愚蠢的季节的这个阶段，由于在印第安纳波利斯赛车中已经没有全职的机会，如果赫塔决定放弃他的F1梦想，那么他需要尽快重新定位自己，以便在2028赛季找到一席竞争之地，无论是TWG还是其他地方。在2028年回归，当新的底盘提供了一个全新的起点时，从竞争的角度来看，这将是最理想的时间。

    无论如何，赫塔必须做出决定，因为2028年将是他成败的关键一年。要么他吞下自尊，给他的F1梦想最后一次机会，与凯迪拉克合作，要么他过早地放弃那个梦想，开始努力重返竞争激烈的印第安纳波利斯赛车座椅。

    这个过程从他的下一步行动开始——就在这里，就在现在。

    Decision time looms for Colton Herta after 2027 Cadillac F1 snub

    With his campaign to get to Formula 1 hitting a roadblock, Colton Herta now faces a crucial career decision

    Cadillac's decision to stick with veteran incumbent Valtteri Bottas to partner Sergio Perez is a choice for continuity and stability after a debut campaign plagued with issues for Formula 1's 11th team in 2026. It also raises key questions over what's next for Colton Herta.

    With Cadillac's majority owner TWG enamoured with the prospect of having an American driver in one of its cars before too long, the team spared no expense on bringing Herta over, leaving the comfort of his IndyCar seat with sister team Andretti Global for a now-or-never shot at making it to F1.

    Herta, a nine-time IndyCar race winner and the youngest to ever do so, faced a tricky challenge of adjusting to European racing in Formula 2, including mastering the peculiar Pirelli tyres that he would also find in F1.

    That was dovetailed with private testing - Cadillac has no previous cars, so it rented a test from Alpine - and a number of grand prix FP1 sessions to boost his number of superlicence points to the critical number of 40.

    After his sixth such test of the year in Brazil, Herta finally got to the magic number. But around that time, Cadillac CEO Dan Towriss and team principal Marcin Budkowski started aligning on the idea of retaining Bottas, deploying Herta as the official reserve driver for 2027, ready to step in if Bottas couldn't raise his performance ceiling over the first part of the season.

    What Cadillac perhaps wasn't factoring in, is how Herta took the news of being snubbed. It is understood that the 26-year-old American has turned down the reserve role for now and has also decided against finishing his troubled F2 campaign with Hitech, forfeiting the final two rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

    Valtteri Bottas, Cadillac Racing, Sergio Perez, Cadillac Racing

    While that decision may be clouded by his initial disappointment at missing out and Herta is desperate to keep racing competitively, rather than sit on the sidelines, it's a somewhat surprising position to hold. Being Cadillac's third driver behind 37-year-old Bottas still appears to be the position with the best odds of F1 promotion across the entire paddock, whether over the course of 2027 or in 2028.

    "We've offered him a reserve position," Towriss said in Las Vegas. "The seat's open for ’28, so there are options on the table for Colton. There are options in other sports as well. I think he's decided on a direction, but we're still in conversations on that and look forward to seeing what Colton wants to do.

    "It was definitely a tough decision, a tough discussion with Colton. I think he was disappointed. There was a lot of work, a lot of a build-up [that went] into this. I know Colton was very brave in even taking on this risk, going into F2 and trying to cram all this learning in."

    The role, which is understood to be rather lucrative for a reserve driver, could also be paired with a one-off Indy 500 outing when the grid expands to 33 entries.

    Herta is now facing a career defining decision because of what's at stake in 2028. That is now the earliest he can get onto the F1 grid full-time if he makes a good enough impression to replace Bottas. It is likely also the last opportunity to do so, given how quickly reserve drivers tend to lose career momentum.

    At the same time, his old home is also due a major renovation. For the first time in over 16 years, IndyCar will introduce an all-new car in 2028, the Dallara IR-28 which debuted to rave reviews in testing and is armed with a beefier 2.4l twin-turbo V6 engine.

    With no full-time opportunities in IndyCar left for 2027 at this stage of the silly season, if Herta is deciding to call time on his F1 dream, then he needs to reposition himself as soon as possible to land a competitive seat for IndyCar 2028's season, whether that be with TWG or elsewhere. Returning in 2028, when the new chassis offers a blank slate, would be the most desirable timing from a competitive standpoint.

    In any case, Herta has a decision to make as 2028 will be his make-or-break year. Either he swallows his pride and gives his F1 dream one last shot with Cadillac, or he abandons that dream prematurely and starts working on returning to a competitive IndyCar seat.

    That process starts with his next move - right here, right now.
