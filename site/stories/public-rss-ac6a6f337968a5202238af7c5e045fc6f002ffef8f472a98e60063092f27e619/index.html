    前哈斯一级方程式车队领队京特·施泰纳质疑刘易斯·汉密尔顿是否是能扭转法拉利局面的车手，并认为马克斯·维斯塔潘可能更适合这一角色。

    汉密尔顿在梅赛德斯度过了成功的12年之后，于2025年加入法拉利，在此期间他赢得了七个世界冠军中的六个。

    在法拉利经历了一个棘手的首个赛季后，这位41岁的车手在2026年展现出了昔日状态的迹象。他在巴塞罗那加泰罗尼亚大奖赛上为马拉内罗车队赢得了自己的首场胜利，目前在车手积分榜上排名第四，还剩三站比赛。

    但在最近的比赛周末中，由于汉密尔顿与队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔在赛道上的冲突，法拉利车队内部的动态受到了审视。

    在《红旗播客》节目中，讨论转向了一种理论，即法拉利在像迈克尔·舒马赫这样的强势车手掌控车队时最为成功。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    “但他是那个能让车队获胜的人吗？”施泰纳问道。“当强大的车手接手时，他们取得了成功，但费尔南多·阿隆索尝试过。他成功了吗？没有。你认为刘易斯是合适的人选来实现这一目标吗？”

    当被问及维斯塔潘是否更适合那个位置时，施泰纳解释道：“你知道马克斯是什么样的人。他不会停下来。他不屈不挠。那可能就是解决方案。”

    施泰纳的联合主持人布莱恩·穆勒补充道：“嗯，他们短期内得不到马克斯。所以，他们拥有的是刘易斯，如果他们不准备在拥有一位准备让法拉利再次伟大的七届世界冠军时放下一切，并且你不准备在时机到来时抓住这个机会，那么你就有更大的问题了。”

    此前，拉尔夫·舒马赫声称这位英国车手“从未学会如何领导一支车队”。

    “汉密尔顿从未学会如何领导一支车队，无论是在托托·沃尔夫手下，还是在罗恩·丹尼斯手下，”这位前车手在天空体育德国频道的《Backstage Boxengasse》播客中说道。“这本来也从来不是他的职责。他只需要坐进车里，尽可能开得快就行了。”

    “你能看出来这一点，而这也让他变得相当自我。”

    Guenther Steiner suggests Max Verstappen could be better suited to lead Ferrari

    The former Haas principal believes Max Verstappen’s relentless approach could provide Ferrari with the solution

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Former Haas Formula 1 team principal Guenther Steiner has questioned whether Lewis Hamilton is the driver to turn Ferrari's fortunes around, suggesting Max Verstappen might be better suited to the role.

    Hamilton joined Ferrari in 2025 after a successful 12-year stint with Mercedes, during which time he won six of his seven world titles.

    After a tricky first season with Ferrari, the 41-year-old has shown glimpses of his former form in 2026. He claimed his maiden win with the Maranello outfit at the Barcelona Catalunya Grand Prix and currently sits fourth in the drivers' standings with three rounds remaining.

    But in recent race weekends, the intra-team dynamics at Ferrari has come under scrutiny due to on-track clashes between Hamilton and his team-mate Charles Leclerc.

    On The Red Flags Podcast, the discussion turned to a theory that Ferrari has been most successful when a strong driver, such as Michael Schumacher, has taken charge of the team.

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    "But is he the guy who can make the team win?" Steiner asked. "When strong drivers took over, they made it a success, but Fernando Alonso tried. Did he succeed? No. Do you think Lewis is the right guy to make this happen?"

    Asked whether Verstappen would be better suited to that position, Steiner explained: "You know how Max is. He wouldn't stop. He's relentless. That could be the solution."

    Steiner's co-host Brian Muller added: "Well, they're not getting Max anytime soon. So, what they have is Lewis, and if they're not ready to drop things when you have a seven-time world champion who's ready to make Ferrari great again, and you're not ready to jump on that when it's time, then you've got bigger problems."

    It follows Ralf Schumacher's claim that the Briton "never learned how to lead a team".

    "Hamilton never learned how to lead a team, not under Toto Wolff and not under Ron Dennis," the former driver said during Sky Sports Germany's Backstage Boxengasse podcast. "That was never his job anyway. He just had to get in the car and drive as fast as possible.

    "You can see that, and it's made him quite egotistical as well."
