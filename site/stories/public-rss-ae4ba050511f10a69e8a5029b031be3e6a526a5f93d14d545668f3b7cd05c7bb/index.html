    安东内利：奥斯汀杆位夺冠胜过蒙扎第19位发车夺冠

    安东内利在意大利大奖赛从第19位发车夺冠，但他认为自己在奥斯汀从杆位起步的统治性表现更胜一筹。

    为什么蒙扎从第19位夺冠仍不是安东内利2026赛季F1统治级表现中的最佳一战

    F1准世界冠军在意大利大奖赛从后排发车夺冠，但安东内利认为他在奥斯汀从杆位起步的胜利更加出色

    安东内利在意大利大奖赛上演了一场轰动的胜利，从第19位发车夺冠，但这位F1准世界冠军认为，他在奥斯汀从杆位起步的统治性表现更加出色。

    这位梅赛德斯车手在9月的蒙扎因更换动力单元被迫从后排发车，但这位速度飞快的意大利车手依然穿越车阵拿下胜利。

    这为安东内利带来了他在2026赛季统治性表现中的第七场胜利，他的第八场胜利在下一站马德里到来，随后一直等到十月才在美洲赛道拿下第九胜。

    他在周日的胜利与蒙扎大不相同，因为他从杆位起步并执行了一次进站策略，而其他领先车手选择两次进站，最终他以13秒的优势领先亚军夏尔·勒克莱尔夺冠。

    “在蒙扎，我们显然拥有最快的赛车，”安东内利回应道。“我们就是超级快，所以当然从后方追上来感觉非常特别。

    “但那也是一条相对容易超车的赛道，而且我们太快了。毫无疑问，蒙扎是一场超级特别的比赛，也是我会永远记住的一场。

    “但这一次在执行层面可能更好，因为我们逐步积累的方式以及整个周末提升表现的方式都非常出色。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯；乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，蒙扎

    这让安东内利以123分的优势领先队友乔治·拉塞尔，后者位列第二，这意味着他只需在下周末的墨西哥站拿到两分，就能夺得首个总冠军。

    他将在赛季还剩四轮的情况下做到这一点，对于这位二年级车手来说，这是一个激动人心的赛季，而他的队友在进入新规则时代时曾是夺冠热门。

    安东内利补充道：“嗯，当然，到目前为止这个赛季一直是个不错的惊喜。当然，赛季还没有结束。还有几场比赛，我只想好好表现，仍然努力争取胜利。

    “以高光收尾会很好。但是，是的，回顾这个赛季，肯定还有需要努力和改进的地方，比如在某些情况下的心态，或者我处理一些场景的方式。

    “所以，当然，在赛季结束时，我会有更多时间回顾事情：哪些做得好，哪些本可以更好。

    “但可以肯定的是，到目前为止这是一个非常稳定的赛季，我们会努力保持到最后一刻。”

    因此，安东内利从第一年迈入第二年的这一步是巨大的，而他应对奥斯汀比赛周末的方式清楚地展示了他的进步。

    这是他首次有机会锁定冠军，但他没有被这个机会弄得慌乱，而是像平常一样对待比赛，为墨西哥站创造了很好的机会。

    “我非常幸运，因为我身边有一群非常优秀的人，”他说。“在车队里，我有我的父亲，他总是会来，还有我的表现教练。

    “我身边就是有一群很棒的人，他们帮助我更加放松，不去担心这类事情。

    “它们帮助我屏蔽外界的噪音，这也正是让我能够享受这个周末并发挥出色的原因。”

    “我觉得，显然，已经有很多讨论了。说实话，周四有很多关于总冠军之类的讨论，我只是尽量完全不去担心那些，只是尽力交出我能做到的最好成绩。”

    “今天对我来说、对车队来说都是一个非常重要的成绩。”

    Why Monza win from 19th still isn't the best of Kimi Antonelli's dominant F1 2026 season

    F1's champion-elect won from the back row at the Italian Grand Prix, but his Austin victory from pole was even better according to Antonelli

    Kimi Antonelli pulled off a sensational victory from 19th at the Italian Grand Prix, but Formula 1’s champion-elect reckons his dominant performance from pole in Austin was even better.

    The Mercedes driver was forced into a back row start at Monza in September due to a change of power unit, yet the rapid Italian still scythed through the field to take victory.

    That gave Antonelli his seventh win of a dominant 2026 campaign, with his eighth coming at the next race in Madrid before waiting until October to take his ninth at Circuit of the Americas.

    His win on Sunday was much different to Monza as he started from pole and nailed a one-stop strategy, other frontrunners opted for two, to win by 13s ahead of runner-up Charles Leclerc.

    “Monza, we clearly had the fastest car,” reacted Antonelli. “We were just super quick, so of course it was incredibly special coming from the back.

    “But it was also a track where it was relatively easy to overtake and we were so quick. Definitely, Monza was a super special one, and one that I will remember forever.

    “But this one probably, in terms of execution, was better, because the way we built up and the way we built the performance throughout the weekend was very good.”

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, George Russell, Mercedes at Monza

    It leaves Antonelli with a 123-point advantage over team-mate George Russell in second, meaning he needs just two points in Mexico next weekend to claim a maiden title.

    He would do so with four rounds remaining in what has been a thrilling campaign from the sophomore, whose stablemate headed into the new regulations as title favourite.

    “Well, for sure, it's been a good surprise, this season so far,” added Antonelli. “Of course, it's still not over. There are still a few races where I just want to do well and still try to fight for wins.

    “It would be good to finish on a high. But, yeah, definitely looking back at the season, there are still things to work on and improve, on the mindset in certain situations or the way I handle a few scenarios.

    “So, for sure, at the end of the season, I will have more time to look back at things: what went well, what could have been better.

    “But definitely, it's been a very consistent season so far, and we'll try to keep it that way until the end.”

    The step Antonelli made from year one into year two was therefore huge and the way he approached the Austin weekend was a clear demonstration of his improvement.

    It was his first chance of clinching the title and instead of being flustered by the opportunity, Antonelli just approached the race as normal to set up a great chance in Mexico.

    “I'm very lucky because I have a very good group of people around me,” he said. “In the team, I have my father, who always comes, then my performance coach.

    “I just have a great group of people around me that help me to be more relaxed and not worry about these kinds of things.

    “They help me to block out the noise, and that's also what's been allowing me to enjoy the weekend and perform.

    “I think, obviously, there's been a lot of talk. I'm not going to lie, on Thursday there was a lot of talk about the championship and stuff, and I just tried not to worry about that at all and just tried to deliver the best result I could.

    “Today was a very important result for me and for the team.”
