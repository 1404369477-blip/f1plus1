    “复仇者：末日”将亮相美国大奖赛

    威廉姆斯车队将把其漫威涂装带到2026年美国大奖赛，但亚历克斯·阿隆索和卡洛斯·塞恩斯将不会驾驶这辆赛车。车队将在奥斯汀的粉丝区展示一辆涂装赛车。

    “复仇者联盟：末日之战”即将登陆美国大奖赛

    特制涂装将于10月19日至24日在奥斯汀市中心展出

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    威廉姆斯车队将把其漫威涂装带到2026年美国大奖赛，但亚历克斯·阿隆索和卡洛斯·塞恩斯将不会在这辆车上参赛。

    威廉姆斯一级方程式车队将在奥斯汀的粉丝区展示一辆涂装的展示车。虽然这款涂装受到了粉丝的一致好评，但它仅仅是一件展示品，而不是真正能在美洲赛车场跑一圈的赛车。尽管如此，如果你喜欢与你的最爱赛车运动有好莱坞式的跨界，那么这就是你想要关注的对象。

    威廉姆斯车队今天在Instagram上发布了这条新闻，配文写道：“你在漫展上看到了它，现在来我们的奥斯汀粉丝区看看……这款特别的漫威涂装即将出现在奥斯汀粉丝区的展示车上——我们迫不及待地想要让你亲眼看到它！”

    灵感来自末日车型的赛车获得了大量的关注，这条帖子获得了近13万点赞和超过500条评论。人们对这次合作的热情显而易见。

    粉丝区在哪里？

    威廉姆斯粉丝区位于奥斯汀市中心800号国会大道。它将于10月19日至24日展出，而大奖赛将在25日举行。展示赛车将全面向粉丝展示，车手亚历克斯·阿隆索和车队负责人詹姆斯·沃尔斯也将出席。

    威廉姆斯还在运营COTA模拟器、Zoox自动驾驶体验、小松卡车模拟器，以及漫威漫画的“奥斯汀版”。

    车队涂装在本月初的纽约漫展上首次亮相。10月8日，威廉姆斯表示，他们将与漫威合作，以配合备受期待的《复仇者联盟：末日之战》的上映。

    漫威展台展示了同样的展示赛车，以及受超级英雄启发的赛车服，这些赛车服可供粉丝赢取。还有2000本限量版漫画，封面独特，也是奖品之一。这一切都是在车队和漫威通过《钢铁侠冲向末日》确认他们的合作之后发生的，这本漫画由伊森·萨克斯撰写，讲述了阿隆索、赛恩斯和沃尔斯与钢铁侠、美国队长和黑寡妇一起捍卫一级方程式锦标赛，对抗多玛姆的故事。

    复仇者联盟：末日之战将于今年12月18日上映，届时原本饰演钢铁侠的罗伯特·唐尼将扮演毁灭博士，这部影片预计将创下票房纪录。

    "Avengers: Doomsday" is coming to the United States Grand Prix

    The special livery will be displayed in downtown Austin from 19 to 24 October

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Williams is bringing its Marvel livery to the 2026 United States Grand Prix, but Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz won't be racing in it.

    The Williams F1 team will be putting a painted show car in the team's Fan Zone in Austin. While the livery received a round of compliments from fans, it will purely be a display piece rather than anything that actually drives a lap at the Circuit of the Americas. Still, if you like a Hollywood-sized crossover with your favourite motorsport, then this is the one you'll want to look out for.

    Williams revealed the news today on Instagram, with the caption reading: "You saw it at Comic Con, now see it in our Austin Fan Zone… This special Marvel livery is coming to the show car in the Austin Fan Zone – we can't wait for you to see it in person!"

    The Doom-inspired car received crazy amounts of engagement, with the post accumulating almost 130,000 likes and over 500 comments. The enthusiasm around the partnership is palpable.

    Where is the Fan Zone?

    The Williams Fan Zone can be found at 800 Congress Avenue in downtown Austin. It will be there from 19 to 24 October, while the grand prix takes place on the 25th. The show car will be on full display for fans of the championship, and both driver Alex Albon and team principal James Vowles will make an appearance.

    Williams is also running COTA simulators, a Zoox autonomous mobility experience and a Komatsu truck simulator, plus an "Austin Edition" of the Marvel comic.

    The livery debuted earlier this month at New York Comic Con. On 8 October, Williams said it was joining Marvel to coincide with the run-up to the much-awaited Avengers: Doomsday.

    The Marvel booth had the same show car on display alongside superhero-inspired race suits that were available to be won by fans. 2000 special-edition comics with an exclusive cover were also up for grabs. This all comes after the team and Marvel confirmed their partnership through Iron Man Racing Towards Doom, a comic book written by Ethan Sacks that featured Albon, Sainz and Vowles alongside Iron Man, Captain America and Black Widow as they defended the Formula 1 championship from Doctor Doom.

    Avengers: Doomsday will follow on 18 December later this year, when Robert Downey Jr, who originally played Iron Man, will take on the character Doctor Doom in what is expected to be a record-breaking release.
