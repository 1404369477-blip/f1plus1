    乔治·拉塞尔淡然应对维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯的言论：有些事情真的让我笑了

    乔治·拉塞尔抵达洛杉矶国际赛道，拒绝在F1最激烈的争议中火上浇油，尽管他明确表示不会收回任何引起争议的话。

    乔治·拉塞尔对马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯的言论一笑置之：“有些事情真的让我笑了”

    乔治·拉塞尔在卡塔尔轻松应对马克斯·维斯塔潘的嘲讽，同时在FP3练习赛中立场坚定

    马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队、乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯-奔驰

    梅赛德斯车迷们。关于银箭的最新消息，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析。

    乔治·拉塞尔周四抵达洛杉矶国际赛道，拒绝在一级方程式最激烈的驾驶员纷争中再添油加醋——尽管他明确表示他没有收回任何引起纷争的话。

    在周四的FIA新闻发布会上，在卡塔尔大奖赛之前——这是他在马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯获胜后首次公开露面——拉塞尔带着一丝苦笑轻描淡写地评论了荷兰人的言论。

    “说实话，我没什么好说的。我觉得比赛后的有些评论很有趣，”拉塞尔说。“他开了一场精彩的比赛。我想我坚持我在练习后说的话。但他是那个出去赢得比赛的人，而我没有，所以我想他可能觉得这很有趣。”

    他们之间持续不断的纷争的最新章节发生在拉斯维加斯街道的最后练习中，当时维斯塔潘在准备圈中超过了拉塞尔，在14号弯口提前刹车，并在刹车前转向，导致两车擦碰——这使得红牛车手因驾驶不当而收到了FIA的正式警告。

    在意大利天空体育的采访中，拉塞尔在排位赛后指责维斯塔潘 "每周都和许多车手玩同样的把戏"，并宣称 "有人必须站出来对抗那个人"，而不是让他 "像他们自己一样，认为自己比所有人都要大都要好"。

    一天后，维斯塔潘在赛道上和麦克风后做出了回应。在一场激烈、尾流众多的比赛中与拉塞尔交换领先之后，维斯塔潘率先冲过终点线，而拉塞尔在离线时锁定了刹车，在攻击梅赛德斯车队队友基米·安东内利时撞上了第5弯的护栏，摔倒至第九位。

    在赛后新闻发布会上，维斯塔潘将拉塞尔贬低为 "远远低于我的水平"，并将晚些时候的撞车事故称为 "报应"，承认当他在后视镜中看到发生的事情时，这 "老实说非常棒"。

    在接受荷兰广播公司Viaplay采访时，这位四次世界冠军进一步加剧了对拉塞尔2026赛季的批评，称其 "对他来说是一场彻底的灾难，尤其是在那辆车上"。

    捍卫攻击——并回到Losail的焦点之地

    在卡塔尔，拉塞尔驳回了针对他个人的挖苦，并捍卫了那场让他Vegas领奖台之梦破碎的激进举动。

    "维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯之后的评论让我笑了，但他赢了，并且比赛很棒，" 拉塞尔说。"尽管有些事情真的让我笑了，但我还是在前进。我和队友在战斗中撞到了墙；我想要为了一个好的结果而攻击。而且在那之前，我和Max的比赛也是很好和公平的。"

    即使拉塞尔想要翻篇，但环境使得紧张难以忽视。Losail是那对冤家在2024年末公开敌对的地方，当时维斯塔潘在排位赛中因妨碍而被处以一档的罚退，导致了一场在阿布扎比爆炸性的对峙。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    在周四的 车队区闲聊中，当被天空体育F1询问关于碰撞时，麦克拉伦的兰多·诺里斯发表了看法。诺里斯说：“你可以在赛道上说话，或者你可以在摄像机前说话。通常情况下，如果你在赛道上证明了自己的话，那就更有帮助。毕竟，每个人最后真正关心的是这个。马克斯显然已经向所有人证明了他能做什么，而乔治还有待证明他自己的能力。”

    最近的积分榜显示，为什么拉塞尔渴望在最后两个周末通过自己的驾驶来证明自己。虽然维斯塔潘——从2023年到2025年在卡塔尔连续三次获胜——以304分排在积分榜第二位，在过去的六场大奖赛中有四次获得杆位，但拉塞尔自巴库从杆位发车获胜以来，已经有六场比赛没有站在领奖台上。与诺里斯并列264分，仅落后第三名的刘易斯·汉密尔顿（267分）三分，在周日洛杉矶的灯光下干净利落地击败维斯塔潘，将比任何新闻发布会上的俏皮话都要有意义。

    George Russell shrugs off Max Verstappen’s Las Vegas remarks: “Certain things really made me laugh”

    George Russell laughs off Max Verstappen's Las Vegas barbs in Qatar while standing firm on their FP3 practice clash

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell arrived at the Losail International Circuit on Thursday refusing to pour fresh fuel on Formula 1’s loudest driver feud - though he made it clear he was not retracting a word of what started it.

    Speaking in Thursday’s FIA press conference ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix - his first public appearance since Max Verstappen had his say after winning in Las Vegas - Russell downplayed the Dutchman’s remarks with a wry smile.

    "I don’t really have much to say, to be honest. I found it quite amusing, some of the comments after the race," Russell said. "He drove a great race. I guess I stand by what I said after practice. But he is the one that went out and won the race, and I didn’t, so I imagine he found that amusing."

    The latest chapter in their running feud erupted during final practice on the Las Vegas Strip, when Verstappen passed Russell on a preparation lap, braked early approaching Turn 14, and shifted direction under braking before the pair banged wheels—earning the Red Bull driver a formal FIA warning for erratic driving.

    Speaking to Sky Italia after qualifying, Russell accused Verstappen of pulling the same move "week in, week out with many drivers" and declared that "somebody has got to stand up to that guy" rather than letting him "walk around like they are bigger and better than everyone else".

    One night later, Verstappen answered on track and behind the microphone. After trading the lead with Russell across an intense, slipstream-heavy race, Verstappen crossed the line first while Russell locked up off-line and clipped the Turn 5 barrier while attacking Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli, tumbling to ninth.

    In the post-race press conference, Verstappen dismissed Russell as "so far below my level" and called the late crash "karma", admitting it was "honestly fantastic when I looked in my mirror, and I saw what was happening".

    Speaking to Dutch broadcaster Viaplay, the four-time champion sharpened the knife further, branding Russell’s 2026 campaign "a complete disaster for him, especially in that car".

    Defending the attack - and returning to Losail’s flashpoint

    In Qatar, Russell brushed past the personal digs and defended the aggressive move that wrecked his Vegas podium bid.

    "Some of Verstappen’s comments after Las Vegas made me laugh, but he won and had a great race," Russell said. "I’m moving on, though certain things really made me laugh. I hit the wall while fighting with my team-mate; I wanted to attack for a good result. And up until that point, the race with Max had also been good and fair."

    Even if Russell wants to turn the page, the setting makes the tension hard to ignore. Losail is where the pair’s long-simmering friction boiled into open hostility in late 2024, when Verstappen lost pole position to Russell via a one-place grid penalty for impeding in qualifying - triggering an explosive exchange that carried straight through Abu Dhabi.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    Adding to the paddock chatter on Thursday, McLaren’s Lando Norris weighed in when asked by Sky Sports F1 about the clash. "You can either do your talking on the track, or you can do your talking in front of a camera," Norris said. "It normally always helps when you back it up on track. That's what everyone at the end of the day really cares about. Max has obviously proved to everyone what he is capable of, and George is still yet to prove what he's capable of."

    The recent form chart shows why Russell is eager to let his driving speak over the final two weekends. While Verstappen - a three-time consecutive winner in Qatar from 2023 to 2025 - arrives second in the standings on 304 points with four P1s in his last six grands prix, Russell has not stood on a podium in six races since winning from pole in Baku. Tied with Norris on 264 points and just three behind third-placed Lewis Hamilton (267), beating Verstappen cleanly under Losail’s floodlights on Sunday would do far more than any press-conference punchline.
