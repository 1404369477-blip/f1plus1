    Topps庆祝汉密尔顿400次F1赛事启动发行限量版NOW卡

    Topps为汉密尔顿在圣保罗大奖赛上完成第400次F1赛事启动发行了限量版Topps NOW交易卡。这是继阿斯顿·马丁的阿隆索之后，成为历史上第二位达到这一里程碑的车手。

    Topps 庆祝刘易斯·汉密尔顿的 400 次F1 赛事起跑，推出限量版 NOW 卡片

    法拉利车手成为第二位完成 400 场大奖赛起跑的 F1 车手

    法拉利车迷们，这里是 Scuderia 法拉利 F1 的最新消息，车手更新，技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应

    Topps 为了庆祝刘易斯·汉密尔顿上周末在圣保罗大奖赛上完成第 400 次F1 赛事起跑，推出了 Topps NOW 交易卡片

    法拉利车手成为了锦标赛历史上第二位达到这一里程碑的车手，此前的第一位是阿斯顿·马丁的费尔南多·阿隆索，他在2024年实现了这一成就。

    Topps在周五通过Instagram宣布了这张卡片，并表示："刘易斯·汉密尔顿成为了F1中第二位开始参加400场大奖赛的车手。用Topps NOW记录这一时刻。"

    这是2026年F1 Topps NOW系列的第76张卡片，在Topps网站上售价为11.99美元。

    Topps NOW卡片是按订单制作的，而不是固定批量生产。每张卡片在纪念赛事后的一段时间内出售，一旦窗口关闭，Topps才会公布印刷的副本数量。

    这是收藏家们期待已久的时刻。今年早些时候，他在6月的巴塞罗那大奖赛上以法拉利车队的颜色赢得处女冠，Topps NOW卡片成为有史以来销量最高的F1 Topps NOW卡片。Topps表示，其印刷量超过了50,000份。

    他的第400次起步令人沮丧。这位七届冠军因为动力单元处罚从最后一排发车。虽然他最初上升了10个位置，但最终落后一圈，排在第九位。

    “当你想到我们作为一个家庭从哪里开始，我爸爸和琳达投入了无数小时在我的赛车梦想中，来到这里，”汉密尔顿在比赛前的F1电视转播中说。“我记得在这里赢得我的第一个世界冠军。”

    Topps celebrates Lewis Hamilton’s 400th F1 start with limited NOW card

    The Ferrari driver became only the second Formula 1 driver to reach 400 grands prix starts

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Topps has released a Topps NOW trading card in celebration of Lewis Hamilton's 400th Formula 1 start, which came at the São Paulo Grand Prix last weekend.

    The Ferrari driver became only the second driver in the championship's history to reach the milestone, after Aston Martin's Fernando Alonso, who got there in 2024.

    Topps announced the card on Instagram on Friday, saying: "Lewis Hamilton becomes only the second driver to start 400 grands prix in F1. Mark the moment with Topps NOW."

    It is card number 76 in the 2026 Formula 1 Topps NOW series and costs $11.99 on the Topps website.

    Topps NOW cards are made to order rather than in a fixed run. Each one is on sale for a short window after the race it commemorates, and Topps only reveals how many copies were printed once that window has closed.

    That is the number collectors will be waiting for. Earlier this year, the Topps NOW card for his maiden win in Ferrari colours, at the Barcelona Grand Prix in June, became the highest-selling F1 Topps NOW card ever. Topps said its print run passed 50,000 copies.

    His 400th start was frustrating. The seven-time champion started from the back of the grid after a power unit penalty. While he initially climbed 10 places, he finished a lap down in ninth.

    "It is really crazy, when you think of where we started as a family, the countless hours that my dad and Linda put into my racing, with that dream of coming here," Hamilton said on the F1 TV broadcast ahead of the race. "I remember winning my first world championship here."
