    阿隆索：我们无法与现有的动力单元竞争

    阿隆索表示本田在F1动力单元方面落后于竞争对手，尽管如此，他对未来仍抱有希望。

    费尔南多·阿隆索：“我们无法用我们现有的动力单元进行竞争”

    阿隆索指出阿斯顿·马丁的引擎劣势，但坚持认为本田正在努力改善这种情况

    费尔南多·阿隆索表示本田在F1对手中“落后很远”，因此尽管表达了对未来的希望，但阿斯顿·马丁“无法与日本引擎竞争”

    随着F1转向包含47%电能的动力单元，本田的新动力总成在动力和可靠性方面都存在问题。制造商在荷兰大奖赛上推出的ADUO升级提高了其马力输出，但牺牲了驾驶性，这从一开始就是问题，阿隆索将其描述为“随机的降挡”。

    兰斯·斯特罗尔在巴西因ERS问题遭遇本赛季第13次退赛，情况如此糟糕，以至于本田认为在拉斯维加斯的接近双倍完成情况——阿隆索在最后一圈之前退赛——是令人鼓舞的，尽管绿色赛车在比赛结束时只领先瓦尔特里·博塔斯的凯迪拉克。

    高速赛道布局并没有帮助，因为本田在动力上仍然明显落后，AMR26比排位赛最快的赛车慢33km/h，而在比赛中直线差距为27km/h。

    “在发动机方面，我们落后很远，我们无法用我们现有的动力单元进行竞争，”阿隆索在阿斯顿·马丁网站上承认。

    “有些赛道，比如拉斯维加斯，我们在直道上就比一圈慢两秒以上，可靠性也不在需要的位置。没有人对动力单元方面的情况感到满意，但我们知道本田正在努力改善这种情况。”

    “看到幕后进行的努力和承诺，这让我对明年充满信心。”

    然而，在底盘方面，进步意味着阿隆索在蜿蜒的赞德福特赛道上获得了第九名，在吉隆坡的雨天条件下获得了第八名——在这两种情况下，动力不足的影响并不那么大。

    “‘布达佩斯的底盘升级原本应该带来一定的圈速，它确实做到了这一点，’阿隆索说。‘我们在布达佩斯具有竞争力，我们在赞德福特和马来西亚获得了积分。’"

    “‘本赛季我们只对底盘进行了一次大的更新，就进入了中游车队，这让我有信心我们知道我们正在走向何方，并且我们有信心这只是时间问题，我们将会拥有一辆非常好的赛车。’"

    Fernando Alonso: “We can't be competitive with the power unit we have”

    Alonso points out Aston Martin’s engine deficit, but insists Honda is striving to improve the situation

    Fernando Alonso says Honda is “a very long way behind” its Formula 1 rivals and Aston Martin therefore “can’t be competitive” with the Japanese engine, albeit expressing hope for the future.

    As F1 switched to power units featuring 47% electrical energy, Honda’s new powertrain has been lacking both power and reliability. The ADUO upgrade introduced by the manufacturer at the Dutch Grand Prix improved its horsepower output at the expense of driveability, which had been a problem from the start with what Alonso described as “random downshifts”.

    Lance Stroll suffered his 13th race retirement of the season in Brazil due to an ERS issue, with the situation so dire that Honda saw the near double finish in Las Vegas – Alonso retired before the last lap – as encouraging, despite the green cars only leading Valtteri Bottas’ Cadillac at the end of the race.

    The high-speed layout didn’t help as Honda remains noticeably down on power, with the AMR26 33km/h slower than the fastest car in qualifying, while its straightline deficit was 27km/h in the race.

    “On the engine side, we are a very long way behind and we can't be competitive with the power unit we have,” Alonso admitted on the Aston Martin website.

    “There are some circuits like Las Vegas where we are losing more than two seconds a lap on the straights alone, and the reliability is not where it needs to be. No one is happy with how things are going on the PU side of things, but we know Honda is working hard to improve the situation.

    “Seeing the work and commitment going on behind the scenes gives me confidence for next year.”

    On the chassis side however, progress meant Alonso managed to finish ninth on the winding Zandvoort track and eighth in Sepang’s wet conditions – two contexts in which the power deficit wasn’t as impactful.

    “The Budapest chassis upgrade was supposed to deliver a certain lap time, and it delivered that lap time,” Alonso said. “We were competitive in Budapest and we scored points in Zandvoort and Malaysia.

    “We've got into the midfield pack with only one large update to the chassis this season, so that has given me the confidence that we know the direction we're heading in and the belief that it's only a matter of time until we have a very good car.”
