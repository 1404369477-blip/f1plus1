    勒克莱尔透露他在法拉利中“隐藏”的设备

    勒克莱尔透露他在驾驶法拉利时已经佩戴了WHOOP健身追踪器，并表示在F1准备将车手心率显示在屏幕上时，他没有什么要隐藏的。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔透露他在法拉利中“隐藏”的装置

    法拉利车手表示，随着F1准备在电视转播中展示车手的心率数据，他没有‘什么要隐藏’

    对于法拉利车迷来说，这是最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻，车手更新，技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远处的比赛反应

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔透露，他在驾驶法拉利时已经佩戴了WHOOP健身追踪器，并表示随着F1准备在屏幕上展示车手的心率，他没有‘什么要隐藏’。

    F1于周三宣布，WHOOP将成为F1的官方合作伙伴，车手们将在赛车中佩戴其手环。心率等数据将被用于社交媒体图形，并可能最早在2027年作为电视直播的叠加显示。

    F1尚未说明有多少车手将参与，但法拉利今年已经开始了与WHOOP的合作，勒克莱尔、刘易斯·汉密尔顿以及整个车队已经使用了这些设备。

    在周末比赛前被问及他对自己的数据向公众展示的看法时，勒克莱尔表示他没有异议。

    “我没有特别要隐藏的东西。显然，我现在是WHOOP的代言人，我已经使用这个设备很长时间来追踪我的恢复、睡眠，以及我在车上的情况，”勒克莱尔说。

    “现在有一个经过FIA认证的实际设备，我们可以在开车时佩戴它，而不再需要隐藏了。这是一件好事。至少对我来说，它非常有用。”

    当被直接问及是否在车内佩戴它时，勒克莱尔确认说：“是的。”

    摩纳哥人表示，关于在直播中显示多少数据的这个问题仍需决定。

    “向所有人展示是另一个问题，我们肯定会讨论这个问题，但就我个人而言，我对此没有任何问题，”他说。

    追踪器可以测量心率、心率变异性，以及其他数据，如睡眠和最大摄氧量。F1希望这能帮助粉丝了解驾驶赛车对身体的要求有多大。

    "几十年来，粉丝对赛车的了解非常深入，但对比赛对车手的影响了解较少，" WHOOP首席执行官威尔·艾哈迈德在公告中表示。

    勒克莱尔在车手积分榜上排名第五，他在7月份在银石赛道获胜后，仍在寻找本赛季的第二场胜利。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛将于当地时间周六晚上举行。

    Charles Leclerc reveals the gadget he’s been "hiding" in his Ferrari

    The Ferrari driver says he has "nothing to hide" as F1 prepares to show drivers' heart rate data on its TV broadcasts

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Charles Leclerc has revealed that he has already been wearing a WHOOP fitness tracker while driving his Ferrari, and says he has "nothing to hide" as Formula 1 prepares to put drivers' heart rates on screen.

    F1 announced on Wednesday that WHOOP will become an official partner of F1, with drivers wearing its wristbands in the car. Data such as heart rate will be used in social media graphics and could even appear as live TV overlays as early as 2027.

    F1 has not said how many drivers will take part, but Ferrari already stared its own partnership with WHOOP earlier this year, and Leclerc, Lewis Hamilton and the wider team already use the devices.

    Asked in Las Vegas ahead of this weekend's race how he felt about his data being shown to the public, Leclerc said he had no objections.

    "I don't have anything to hide in particular. Obviously I'm now an ambassador for WHOOP and I've been using the device for quite a long time now to track my recovery, my sleep, but also when I'm in the car," Leclerc said.

    "And now there's an actual device that is certified by FIA that we can, without hiding it anymore, wear it while I'm driving. So it's a good thing. At least for me it's very useful."

    Asked directly whether he had been wearing it in the car, Leclerc confirmed: "Yes."

    The Monegasque said the question of how much data is shown on the broadcast still had to be decided.

    "To show it to everybody is another matter that we'll for sure discuss, but I personally don't have any problems with it," he said.

    The trackers measure heart rate and heart rate variability, as well as sleep and VO2 max among other data. F1 hopes this will help fans understand how physically demanding the cars are to drive.

    "For decades, fans have had incredible insight into the cars, but less insight into the impact that races have on the drivers," said WHOOP chief executive Will Ahmed in the announcement.

    Leclerc is fifth in the drivers' standings, and is still looking for a second win of the season after his victory at Silverstone in July.

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix takes place on Saturday night, local time.
