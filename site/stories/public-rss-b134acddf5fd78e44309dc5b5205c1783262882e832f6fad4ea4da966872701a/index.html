    Every LEGO F1 set you can buy right now: £10.99 to £199.99

    LEGO currently lists 31 Formula 1 sets across its UK and US stores

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    LEGO currently boasts a total of 31 Formula 1 sets on its UK and US stores, from a £10.99 McLaren for young children to £199.99 Technic models of championship-winning cars.

    Here is every set an F1 fan like yourself you buy right now.

    The biggest sets are 1:8 scale models for adults, each with working steering, suspension and a DRS flap, plus an engine you can see under the cover. They're not as complicated as the machinery you see racing over an F1 weekend, but they're not far off.

    McLaren MCL39 (42228): 1,675 pieces, £199.99 / $229.99. The 2025 car, with push-rod and pull-rod suspension, a gearbox, a differential and a V6 engine.

    Oracle Red Bull Racing RB20 (42206): 1,639 pieces, £199.99 / $229.99. Max Verstappen's 2024 car, with steering through a roof knob or the steering wheel, a two-speed gearbox and a V6 with moving pistons. US orders currently ship in 60 days.

    Aston Martin Aramco AMR25 (42240): 1,547 pieces, £199.99 / $229.99. Includes a two-speed gearbox, a working differential, a V6 and a simulated electric motor.

    Ferrari SF-24 (42207): 1,361 pieces, £199.99 / $229.99. Features a two-speed gearbox, printed tyres and a V6 with a spinning MGU-H.

    Mercedes-AMG F1 W14 E Performance (42171): 1,643 pieces, £189.99 / $219.99. The 2023 car is retiring soon, but is back in stock in the US.

    These display models each come with a cute little minifigure of the driver and a podium-style stand. I own a couple of these, and they're absolutely fantastic shelf fillers.

    Ferrari F2004 & Michael Schumacher (11375): 735 pieces, £79.99 / $89.99. Includes a detailed V10, working steering and a winner's trophy.

    McLaren MP4/4 & Ayrton Senna (10330): 693 pieces, £69.99 / $79.99. Senna's 1988 car, with working steering, suspension, an adjustable rear wing and a turbocharged V6.

    Williams FW14B & Nigel Mansell (10353): 799 pieces, £69.99 / $79.99. Mansell's 1992 title winner is retiring soon, and is currently reduced to $55.99 in the US.

    Ayrton Senna (43024): 931 pieces. The newest helmet, with a Senna minifigure wearing a Senninha T-shirt.

    Charles Leclerc (43014): 886 pieces.

    Lewis Hamilton (43022): 884 pieces. Includes Hamilton in his red Ferrari race suit.

    Lando Norris (43023): 793 pieces. Half papaya, celebrating McLaren's 1,000th grand prix.

    Oscar Piastri (43017): 793 pieces. Co-designed by Piastri and his team.

    Nine are based on the 2024 grid, and every one of them is retiring soon so if you want to get your mits on one, you'll want to move fast: the Red Bull RB20, McLaren MCL38, Mercedes W15, Ferrari SF-24, Williams FW46, Aston Martin AMR24, Alpine A524, Haas VF-24 and Racing Bulls VCARB 01.
