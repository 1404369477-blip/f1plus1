    科拉平托透露米兰夏季休赛期遭窃细节

    阿尔派车手弗兰科·科拉平托首次公开谈论夏季休赛期在米兰遭遇的车内盗窃事件，窃贼偷走了他的衣物和护照，他戏称这是“我人生中最糟糕的一天”。

    科拉平托 披露了 F1 夏休期期间他在米兰遭遇汽车盗窃案的细节

    科拉平托 透露了在 F1 夏休期期间，窃贼在米兰如何从他的车里偷走了他的衣服、护照和个人物品

    阿尔派 车队的 F1 车手 科拉平托 首次公开谈及他在夏休期期间在米兰遭遇的盗窃事件，并开玩笑地称这是“我一生中最糟糕的一天”，因为窃贼抢走了他的衣服和护照

    这位23岁的阿根廷车手最初在8月6日通过一条 Instagram 帖子分享了这一盗窃消息。而现在他披露了这次遭遇的更多细节。

    在接受ESPN采访时，Colapinto解释说，他在米兰外出就餐时，他的私家车被撬了。

    “他们把我的东西全偷走了，”他说。“我真希望他们只偷了马黛茶（阿根廷饮品）。全部东西。我的钱包、护照。我所有的衣服。一场彻底的灾难。我一无所有了。在米兰。”

    “我把所有东西都留在了车里。我去了一家我非常喜欢意大利餐厅吃饭。那里有最棒的培根蛋面。然后，当我回来的时候，车里什么都不剩了。什么都没有。”

    在向当地当局报案后，Colapinto回到了他在摩纳哥的住所。令人惊讶的是，在他到达后不久，情况就得到了部分解决。

    "我们报了警，"他补充道。"我们回到了摩纳哥。我刚到摩纳哥就接到了一个电话。手机上显示+39，一个意大利号码。他们（警察）告诉我，'我们找到了你的钱包和证件。'于是我又回了米兰。我们把所有东西都取了回来。我一天开了1200公里。我这辈子从来没在油费上花过这么多钱。"

    "我不知道他们把马黛茶壶和保温杯弄哪儿了。梅西的保温杯啊。那马黛茶的味道真是太棒了。我特别喜欢那壶茶。那是我一生中最糟糕的一天。简直是场灾难！"他大笑着说。

    Colapinto目前以19分位居车手总冠军榜第12位。

    Franco Colapinto reveals details of Milan car robbery during F1 summer break

    Franco Colapinto has revealed how thieves stole his clothes, passport and personal belongings from his car in Milan during the F1 summer break

    Alpine Formula 1 driver Franco Colapinto has spoken for the first time about a theft he suffered in Milan during the summer break, jokingly declaring it "the worst day of my life" after the thieves made off with his clothes and passport.

    The 23-year-old Argentine initially shared the news of the theft via an Instagram post on 6 August. But he has now revealed further details about the ordeal.

    During an interview with ESPN, Colapinto explained that his personal car was broken into while he was dining out in Milan.

    "They stole everything from me," he said. "I wish they had only stolen the mate [Argentinian drink]. Everything. My wallet, passport. All my clothes. A complete disaster. I was left with nothing. In Milan.

    "I left everything in the car. I went to eat at an Italian restaurant that I really like. The best carbonara. And then, when I came back, there was nothing left inside. Nothing."

    After reporting the incident to the local authorities, Colapinto returned to his base in Monaco. Remarkably, the situation was partially resolved shortly after his arrival.

    "We filed a police report," he added. "We went back to Monaco. I get to Monaco and I got a call. +39 showing up on the phone, an Italian number. They tell me [the police], 'We found your wallet with your documents.' I went back to Milan. We picked everything up. I drove 1,200 kilometres in one day. I’ve never spent so much money on gas in my life.

    "I don’t know what they did with the mate and the thermos. Messi’s thermos. The mate tasted amazing. I loved that mate. It was the worst day of my life. A total tragedy!" he laughed.

    Colapinto currently sits 12th in the drivers' championship with 19 points.
