    F1 Brazilian GP: Sunday schedule, weather forecast and how to watch

    What time the 2026 Formula 1 Brazilian Grand Prix starts, plus the full Interlagos schedule, weather forecast and how to watch live

    Formula 1 heads into Sunday at Interlagos with Lando Norris on pole after a rain-soaked qualifying session dramatically reshaped the grid.

    The McLaren driver beat George Russell by 0.204s in treacherous conditions, but Russell’s five-place engine penalty promotes newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli to the front row.

    Liam Lawson starts third for Racing Bulls ahead of Nico Hulkenberg, Isack Hadjar and Oliver Bearman. Russell drops to seventh, while Charles Leclerc falls down the order with a 10-place engine penalty.

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen face an even bigger recovery after power unit penalties put both at the back of the grid. With more rain possible on Sunday, another unpredictable Interlagos race could be in store.
