    麦凯伦车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里在F1和MotoGP的跨界越野赛事中与MotoGP明星车手佩德罗·阿科斯塔对决。

    麦凯伦车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里在F1和MotoGP的跨界越野赛事中征服越野赛道

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂里在Quad Lock的Young Guns 2活动中与MotoGP的佩德罗·阿科斯塔展开了一场史诗般的越野赛车对决

    赛车文化超越赛道，涉及车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车运动不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    我们知道现代F1车手都讨厌输，无论是摩纳哥大奖赛、他们在模拟器上的F1比赛，还是与MotoGP骑手的越野赛车比赛，他们都会全力以赴

    当Quad Lock决定让麦凯轮车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂和MotoGP sensation佩德罗·阿科斯塔在《Young Guns Part 2》中重聚时，那种竞争的势头表现得淋漓尽致。他们把价值数百万美元的赛车换成相同的Segway超级反派越野车，这两位全球赛车运动明星在法国里维埃拉的Monti MX赛道上，为了解决他们2024年第一次卡丁车挑战赛中的恩怨而展开对决。

    皮亚斯蒂在自己的主场沥青赛道上主导了最初的卡丁车挑战赛，但在Monti MX的土质赛道上，他面临的是完全不同的挑战。这里有巨大的跳跃、深深的凹槽和滑溜的弯道，你可能会认为阿科斯塔——一个以驾驶300马力摩托车为生的人——会有明显的优势。

    阿科斯塔显然对越野车上的安全特性比他通常使用的两轮火箭船感到更加舒适。

    “我感觉太安全了。就像，如果我翻车，我不会头朝地撞到地上，你知道吗？”这位MotoGP明星开玩笑说，并指出四点安全带和防滚架比皮夹克和安全帽有了巨大的提升。

    皮亚斯特里，另一方面，他只是很高兴自己不必平衡车辆。

    “我觉得它还能保持四轮行驶对我来说是好事。实际上它在泥土上，而我从未在泥土上开过车……这对我来说不是什么好事，”皮亚斯特里在比赛前承认道。

    由冒险骑手和电视主持人Charley Boorman主持的比赛包括短距离冲刺、速度陷阱和最后的两圈大奖赛。

    皮亚斯特里很快证明了他是一个快速学习者，赢得了短距离冲刺和速度陷阱的挑战。但真正的戏剧性事件发生在最后的Grand Prix赛事中，Acosta在试图追回地盘时用力过猛。

    “我在第一个发夹弯处犯了一个错误，然后我只是在尝试弥补我失去的时间，我差点就把自己送上了绝路，”阿科斯塔笑着回忆起一次恐怖的高速跳跃。'我真的很接近让赛车翻转到前面。“'

    皮亚斯特里展现出了那种赢得九场F1大奖赛胜利的冷静、流畅的驾驶风格，他完美地保持了冷静。他以1分53秒的成绩冲过终点线，击败了阿科斯塔的1分54秒的疯狂表现，赢得了整体年轻枪手的冠军。“

    接下来是什么？

    随着皮亚斯特里获得胜利，自然而然的问题是，这两位明星如何在潜在的年轻枪手第三部分中解决平局。“

    阿科斯塔渴望使比赛场地公平，他建议最简单获胜的方式是在下次比赛时让他们两人都骑上两轮摩托车。但皮亚斯特里立即打消了看到他骑 dirt bike 的任何希望。

    "如果我们这么做，我认为麦凯轮可能不会那么高兴，" 阿科斯塔开玩笑说，皮亚斯特里微笑着补充道，"不，我不这么认为，" 他更愿意保持自己的赛车合同完整。"我仍然开放接受四轮挑战，但我们必须继续做《年轻枪手3》来尝试看看我是否能赢得一些东西，" 阿科斯塔总结道。

    目前，比分已经确定。如果你想看到一名MotoGP车手在几乎翻车之前，在并排的越野车中能抓到多少空气，你可以在Quad Lock的YouTube频道上观看《年轻枪手2》的全场对决。

    McLaren’s Oscar Piastri conquers the dirt in F1 and MotoGP crossover

    Oscar Piastri takes on MotoGP’s Pedro Acosta in an epic dirt buggy showdown for Quad Lock's Young Guns 2

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    If there's one thing we know about modern Formula 1 drivers, it's that they hate losing. It doesn't matter if it's the Monaco Grand Prix, a round of F1 on their sim rigs, or a dirt buggy race against a MotoGP rider - they are going to absolutely send it.

    That competitive streak was on full display when Quad Lock decided to reunite McLaren's Oscar Piastri and MotoGP sensation Pedro Acosta for Young Guns Part 2. Trading in their multi-million-dollar race machines for identical Segway Super Villain off-road buggies, the two global motorsport stars hit the Monti MX track on the French Riviera to settle a score from their first go-karting challenge back in 2024.

    While Piastri dominated the original karting challenge on his home turf of asphalt, the dirt track at Monti MX presented a totally different beast. With massive jumps, deep ruts, and slippery corners, you'd think Acosta - a guy who wrestles 300-horsepower motorcycles for a living - would have a clear advantage.

    Acosta certainly felt more comfortable with the buggies' safety features compared to his usual two-wheeled rocket ships.

    "I feel too safe. Like, if I flip, I will not hit my head with the ground, you know?" the MotoGP star joked, noting that a four-point harness and a rollcage is a massive upgrade from a leather suit and a helmet.

    Piastri, on the other hand, was just glad he didn't have to balance the vehicle.

    "I feel like the fact it's still on four wheels is good for me. Fact it's on dirt, and that I've never driven on dirt... that's not in my thing," Piastri admitted before the race.

    Hosted by adventure rider and TV presenter Charley Boorman, the competition consisted of a sprint, a speed trap, and a final two-lap Grand Prix.

    Piastri quickly proved that he’s a fast learner, taking both the sprint race and the speed trap challenges. But the real drama unfolded during the final Grand Prix event, where Acosta pushed a little too hard trying to make up ground.

    "I make a mistake going down to the first hairpin, and then I was just trying to recover the time that I lost, and I was close enough to kill myself," Acosta laughed, recounting a terrifying, high-speed launch off a jump. "I was really close to flip the car to the front".

    Piastri, displaying the ice-cold, smooth driving style that has earned him nine F1 Grand Prix wins, stayed perfectly calm. He crossed the line with a 1:53 lap time, beating Acosta’s wild 1:54 run to claim the overall Young Guns crown.

    What's next?

    With Piastri taking the win, the natural question is how the two stars will settle the tiebreaker in a potential Young Guns Part 3.

    Acosta, eager to level the playing field, suggested the easiest way to win is to put them both on two wheels next time. But Piastri immediately shut down any hopes of seeing him on a dirt bike.

    "I think McLaren will not be so happy if we go for it," Acosta joked, with Piastri adding, "No, I don't think so," with a smile, preferring to keep his racing contract intact. "I still open for four-wheel challenge, but we'll have to keep doing Young Guns Part 3 to try if I see if I win something," Acosta concluded.

    For now, the score is settled. If you want to see exactly how much air a MotoGP rider can catch in a side-by-side buggy before nearly rolling it, you can catch the full Young Guns 2 showdown on Quad Lock's YouTube channel.
