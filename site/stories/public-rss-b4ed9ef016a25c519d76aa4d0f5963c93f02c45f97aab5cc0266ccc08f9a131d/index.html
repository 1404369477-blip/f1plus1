    诺里斯称迈凯伦MCL40直道表现糟糕，仍列拉斯维加斯第三

    诺里斯在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛Q3跑出1分34秒244，位列第三，落后继承杆位的维斯塔潘0.093秒、落后拉塞尔0.020秒，但他赛后称迈凯伦MCL40在直道上表现糟糕。

    诺里斯称迈凯伦MCL40直道表现糟糕，尽管他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛位列第三

    诺里斯表示，尽管在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中获得第三，迈凯伦MCL40在直道上表现糟糕

    致迈凯伦车迷。来自沃金的木瓜色新闻，带来车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个比赛周末的最新消息。

    诺里斯将在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛从第三位发车，他在Q3跑出1分34秒244，仅落后继承杆位的维斯塔潘0.093秒，落后梅赛德斯的拉塞尔0.020秒。纸面上看，锁定第二排对迈凯伦而言像是一次不错的回升。然而诺里斯走出座舱后，给出了本赛季对MCL40最尖锐的批评之一。

    诺里斯在排位赛中位列第四快，落后于被罚退的世界冠军基米·安东内利（1分33秒893），他坚称自己在Q3的最后一圈“显然是我最好的一圈”，但迈凯伦在1.9公里长直道上的极速劣势让他的努力付诸东流。

    “我们在直道上简直糟透了，直道上太可怕了，所以我的圈速已经非常好了，”诺里斯说。“跟基米相比，我们在最后一个计时段慢了零点八秒。他有一个完美的尾流，他做得非常好，但跟大多数其他人相比，我们落后太多了。”

    安东内利在后方直道上借助尾流获得了高达16公里/小时的提升，诺里斯解释说，为了追求类似的尾流，他被迫做出了严苛的妥协——在距离前车1.5秒的位置开始飞驰圈，而脏空气在每个弯角都剥夺了“至少零点零五到零点一秒”的圈速。“我们不得不弥补直道速度的严重损失，因此我们必须牺牲自己的过弯速度，”他补充道。

    当被问及拉斯维加斯漫长的全油门路段是否只是放大了现有的缺陷时，诺里斯拒绝让这条赛道背锅。

    诺里斯说：“不，我的意思是，你不能怪直道。只是我们的车不够好。我们必须在这方面大幅改进。你没法竞争。蒙扎、这里、巴库，我们表现糟糕，因为我们在直道上太差了，明年必须改进。”

    迈凯伦2026赛季的成绩单印证了他的沮丧。诺里斯本赛季的三场胜利都来自高下压力赛道——匈牙利、赞德沃特和圣保罗——在这些地方，过弯负载比低阻力更重要。在动力敏感型布局上，木瓜色赛车一直难以竞争：在蒙扎，诺里斯从第八位发车，最终以第四名完赛，落后安东内利超过19秒；而在巴库，诺里斯退赛后，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里以第13名完赛，迈凯伦一分未得。

    这种直道上的脆弱性在周六晚间50圈的比赛前显得尤为突出。在制造商锦标赛中，迈凯伦落后红牛车队17分（415比398）排名第三，还剩三轮比赛，迈凯伦看到两辆红牛——维斯塔潘杆位、伊萨克·哈贾尔第四——正好排在诺里斯周围。

    与此同时，皮亚斯特里以第7名（1分34秒899）排位，但在使用本赛季第五台内燃机后降至第17位。即便从第三位发车，诺里斯也必须奋力拼搏才能守住赛道位置，对手包括从第六位冲锋的安东内利等直道更快的车手。

    Lando Norris brands McLaren “awful in the straight” despite third on Las Vegas GP grid

    Lando Norris says McLaren's MCL40 is "awful in the straight" despite qualifying for third on the Las Vegas GP grid

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Lando Norris will launch from third on the grid for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix after setting a 1m34.244s in Q3 - just 0.093 seconds shy of inherited polesitter Max Verstappen and 0.020s behind Mercedes’ George Russell. On paper, locking down the second row looks like a tidy recovery for McLaren. Yet Norris stepped out of the cockpit delivering one of his sharpest criticisms of the MCL40 all season.

    Qualifying fourth fastest behind penalised world champion Kimi Antonelli (1m33.893s), Norris insisted his final Q3 effort was "easily my best lap," only for McLaren’s top-speed deficit to undo his work down the 1.9-kilometre Strip straight.

    "We’re just shocking in the straights, like horrendous in the straight, so my lap was very good," Norris said. "To Kimi, we’re eight tenths off in the last sector. He has a perfect tow, and he does a very good job, but comparing to most other people, we’re too far off."

    Antonelli’s slipstream down the back straight proved worth up to 16 km/h, and Norris explained that chasing a similar tow forced him into a punishing compromise - starting his lap 1.5s behind the car ahead while dirty air stripped away "at least a half a tenth to a tenth" in every corner. "We’re having to make up for a pretty terrible loss of straight-line speed, and therefore we have to harm our cornering speed," he added.

    Asked whether Las Vegas’s long flat-out run simply exaggerated an existing flaw, Norris refused to let the circuit take the blame.

    "No, I mean you can’t blame the straights. It’s just our car is not good. We have to improve that a lot," Norris said. "You cannot fight. Monza, here, Baku, we’re bad because we’re awful in the straight, and we have to improve for next year."

    McLaren’s 2026 scorecard supports his frustration. All three of Norris’s wins this season came at higher-downforce venues - Hungary, Zandvoort, and São Paulo - where cornering load matters more than low drag. On power-sensitive layouts, the papaya cars have struggled to contend: at Monza, Norris started eighth and finished fourth more than 19s behind Antonelli, while Baku ended without a single point for McLaren after Norris retired and Oscar Piastri came home 13th.

    That straight-line vulnerability looms large over Saturday night’s 50-lap race. Trailing Red Bull Racing by 17 points for third in the constructors’ championship (415 to 398) with three rounds remaining, McLaren sees both Red Bulls - Verstappen on pole and Isack Hadjar in fourth - lined up right around Norris.

    Meanwhile, Piastri qualified seventh (1m34.899s) but drops to 17th after taking his fifth internal combustion engine of the year. Even starting third, Norris will have to fight hard just to defend track position against faster straight-line rivals, including Antonelli charging from sixth.
