    奥迪巴库带来14项气动升级，比诺托称符合预期

    奥迪在阿塞拜疆大奖赛前为巴库带来14类气动升级件，领队比诺托表示数据第一印象显示套件符合预期。一练仅博尔托莱托使用新套件，二练两车分列第14、15位。

    “套件正按预期交付”——奥迪在阿塞拜疆站练习后谈新升级套件。奥迪为争取本赛季进一步提升性能，在巴库带来了14个新部件。

    奥迪领队马蒂亚·比诺托认为，车队在阿塞拜疆大奖赛前引入的重大升级套件“正按预期交付”。

    这支欣维尔车队在FIA周末前提交的气动升级清单上勾选了14个类别，其中包括大幅改动的前翼和后翼、修订版地板以及新的引擎盖布局等。

    作为相关性验证流程的一部分，一练中只有加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托使用了升级件，从而可以在巴库与尼科·霍肯伯格驾驶的旧规格赛车进行直接对比。

    “如果看数据的第一印象，可以说，这套件正按预期交付，”比诺托说。

    “这是好事，事实上这也是我们本周末的主要关注点。对我们来说，升级当然对性能很重要，对我们在锦标赛中的竞争也很重要。

    “但同样，对我们来说，更重要的是确保车队在方法论和能力方面持续发展。

    奥迪在巴库带来大量升级。“因此，从心态上的勇敢，到我们可能交付的气动概念类型，再到交期、工程、生产、制造，我们把挑战提上去了。

    “就我们在这里带来的升级而言，最终能带来多少圈速提升并不重要。

    “我认为对我们最重要的是，我们提高了挑战，再次在心态和交期等方面推动了整个系统，并希望我们能与风洞和模拟器保持正确的相关性。”

    奥迪目前位列车队积分榜第八，仅落后哈斯4分，霍肯伯格和博尔托莱托在最近六场大奖赛中合计五次进入积分区。

    博尔托莱托使用新套件在一练中位列第10，紧随其后的是霍肯伯格，随后两人在第二节练习中分列第14和第15。

    “我觉得赛车在二练中感觉并不好，”博尔托莱托说。

    “说实话，我们缺一点抓地力，还不明白原因。我对此没有答案。

    “我猜这就是巴库，低附着、灰尘很大。赛道我想已经好几年没清理过了，我们只能这样开，然后尽力而为。”

    'The package is delivering as expected' – Audi reflect on new upgrade package after practice in AzerbaijanAudi have brought 14 new components to Baku in a bid to gain further performance this season.

    Audi Team Principal Mattia Binotto believes the team's major upgrade package, which has been introduced ahead of the Azerbaijan Grand Prix, is "delivering as expected".

    The Hinwil outfit ticked 14 category boxes on the FIA’s pre-weekend submission list of aerodynamic upgrades, which includes substantially changed front and rear wings, a revised floor, and a new engine cover arrangement amongst others.

    As part of the correlation process, only Gabriel Bortoleto had the updates in FP1, allowing for a direct comparison with Nico Hulkenberg’s older spec car in Baku.

    “If you look at the first, let me say, impression of the data, the package is delivering as expected,” said Binotto.

    “Which is good, which is our main focus, as a matter of fact, for the weekend. For us, upgrading, yes, it’s important for the performance, it’s important for our fight in the championship.

    “But again, for us, what’s more important is to make sure that the team is developing in terms of methodologies and capacity.

    Audi have a big raft of upgrades in Baku"So from being brave when it comes to mindset, to the type of aero concepts we may deliver, on the lead times, engineering, production, manufacturing, we raised the challenge.

    "With the upgrade we have brought here, it doesn’t matter how much it will deliver in terms of lap time.

    "I think what matters for us the most is having raised the challenge, having again pushed the system on whatever is mindset and lead times, and hopefully we’ll have a correlation which is right compared to the wind tunnel and the sim.”

    Audi sit eighth in the teams' standings, just four points behind Haas, with Hulkenberg and Bortoleto having finished in the points at five of the last six Grands Prix between them.

    Bortoleto was able to finish P10 in FP1 with the new package, just ahead of Hulkenberg, before the pair finished P14 and P15 in the second session.

    “I think the car didn't feel amazing in FP2,” said Bortoleto.

    “If I'm very honest, we're lacking a little bit of grip, still to understand why. I don't have answers for that.

    “I guess that's Baku, low grip, super dusty. The track has not been cleaned, I think, for a few years now, and yeah, just we need to drive with this and then try our best.”
