    Fernando Alonso: Aston Martin driver to race in Dakar Rally ahead of 2027 F1 season in return to rally-raid event

    Aston Martin's F1 driver joins the marque's first foray into the hugely-challenging Dakar Rally in January; 2027 event being held in Portugal and Morocco; watch the Las Vegas GP this weekend on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

    Fernando Alonso will race in the Dakar Rally for Aston Martin in January.

    The 45-year-old will return to compete in the legendary off-road endurance event seven years on from his one previous appearance, when he finished 13th with Toyota.

    That came during Alonso's two-year stint out of Formula 1 but this time he will race in Dakar ahead of what will be a record-extending 24th campaign in the sport after he extended his deal with Aston Martin's F1 team for the 2027 season.

    The 2027 edition of the rally-raid event is being held in Portugal and Morocco on January 9-23 after being moved from Saudi Arabia due to the conflict in the Middle East. The race will feature a prologue and 12 different stages across different challenging terrains.

    Alonso's co-driver will again be compatriot Marc Coma, who is a five-time Dakar motorcycle category winner, in one of three Aston Martin Dreadnoughts being built and run by Prodrive. Sebastien Loeb, the nine-time World Rally champion, and reigning World Rally Raid champion Lucas Moraes will drive the other Aston Martin Racing entries.

    Alonso said: "After deciding to continue racing in Formula 1 with the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team, I thought carefully about Dakar.

    "In the end, the decision became simple. I wanted to embrace the challenge completely and take on both Formula 1 and the Dakar Rally in the same year.

    "In 2020, Dakar was one of the most challenging and enjoyable experiences of my career.

    "I have enormous respect for this race and the specialists who have mastered it. Having Marc alongside me again is very important, and with Aston Martin, Prodrive, Sebastien and Lucas we have an exceptional team.

    "Formula 1 remains my priority. But I am a racing driver, and challenges like this are why I love competing. I'm looking forward to seeing what we can achieve."

    The Dreadnoughts will the competition-ready version of the car originally designed in digital form as a military vehicle for the new Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 video game

    Aston Martin CEO Adrian Hallmark said: "Aston Martin Dreadnought was conceived to dominate any terrain, under the most extreme circumstances and come through it all to deliver on its mission. Now along with our engineering partner, Prodrive, we will challenge this vision in the most extreme competitive environment in global motorsport - the Dakar 2027.

    "It is in Aston Martin's nature to forge forward with pioneering concepts, and just as we have always done, explore their limits on the global stage. We do not shy away from such challenges, but embrace them, and in Fernando, Sebastien and Lucas we have three great champions with whom to mount a formidable challenge on Dakar 2027."

    The event covers a total distance of 7,824km, including 4,341km of special stages, and features all manner of challenging terrain and desert conditions

    It begins in Loule, Faro in Portugal's Algarve with a prologue on January 9 and finishes in Marrakech, Morocco on January 23.
