    安东内利2026年将如何成为首位F1世界冠军的亮点

    如果20岁的安东内利在今天晚些时候的墨西哥大奖赛上赢得2026年的世界冠军，他将不仅仅成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。他的统计成绩已经超越了过去的首次夺冠者。

    基米·安东内利如果，如看起来几乎确定的那样，在今天晚些时候的墨西哥大奖赛中赢得2026年的世界冠军头衔，他将不仅仅成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军。

    这位20岁的年轻人只需要从剩余的比赛中再获得两个积分，相当于一个第九名的成绩，就能成为冠军，而他的数学上的竞争对手乔治·拉塞尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿实际上需要赢得本赛季剩余的所有比赛。

    安东内利的赛季统计已经超越了过去首次夺冠的冠军们。

    他将成为第36位F1世界冠军，但在那些冠军中，将有20位是在最后一轮决赛中赢得他们的首个冠军。相比之下，安东内利正在轻松地赢得冠军，并有望在19轮比赛后夺冠。这将是在完成了82.6%赛季的情况下，首次夺冠的车手中最快的第五位。即使本赛季减少到22轮，他也能保持这个位置，无论你如何调整计算。

    那些领先的人中，有Nigel Mansell，他在39岁时终于赢得了他唯一的冠军，那是在1992年，当时赛季还剩下68.8%。然而，他是一位从1986年开始就参与冠军争夺的老将。年赛季的70和年赛季的72紧随其后。

    唯一一个比安东内利赢得更快冠军的司机看起来将是年赛季的75，当时世界冠军赛是在F2机械上进行的。

    安东内利有望成为过去30年中唯一三位在最后一轮之前就锁定他们第一个冠军的司机之一。这将使他加入阿隆索（2005年赛季的89.5%）和年赛季的94的杰出行列。

    所有这些冠军都是在不同的机械和不同水平的对手中赢得的，但安东内利的胜利也显得有些轻松。即使在16次没有进行到最后一场比赛的首次冠军争夺中，也有一些是真正的冠军争夺战，在赛季深入进行后才偏向于某位车手。

    例如，阿兰·普罗斯特在1985年为麦凯轮赢得的冠军是在剩下两场比赛的情况下锁定的，但法拉利的米歇尔·阿尔伯托与他并肩作战，却在最后五场比赛中未能得分。艾伦·琼斯（1980年）、巴顿、埃尔顿·塞纳（1988年）和阿隆索都是真正的战斗的一部分。

    尽管你可以争论梅赛德斯的主导作用在其中起了很大作用，但拉塞尔在第二辆车的表现表明，如果没有安东内利的出色表现，这个赛季可能看起来会完全不同。

    英文原文

    Kimi Antonelli will become more than just Formula 1's youngest-ever world champion if, as looks near-certain, he clinches the 2026 title at the Mexican Grand Prix later today.

    The 20-year-old needs just two more points, equivalent to a single ninth place, from the remaining races to be champion, while remaining mathematical rivals George Russell and Lewis Hamilton effectively need to win all of the rest of this season’s races.

    Antonelli’s campaign already stands out statistically against past first-time title winners.

    He’ll be the 36th F1 world champion, but should be in a minority in that 20 of those clinched their first title in a final-race decider. Antonelli, by comparison, is cruising to the title and set to clinch it after 19 rounds. That will be with 82.6% of the season completed, by that metric the fifth-fastest of the first-time title-winners. He retains that position even if this season drops to 22 rounds, regardless of how you adjust the calculation.

    Those ahead are led by Nigel Mansell, who finally won his only championship at the age of 39 in 1992 and sealed it with just 68.8% of the season gone. However, he was a veteran of title fights stretching back to 1986. Jim Clark (70% of the season in 1963) and Jackie Stewart (72.5% of the 1969 season) are next up.

    The only other driver who won faster than Antonelli looks set to was Alberto Ascari (75% of the 1952 season) when the world championship was run for Formula 2 machinery.

    Antonelli is poised to be one of only three drivers in the last 30 years who have sealed their first titles before the last round. That will put him in the illustrious company of Fernando Alonso (89.5% of the season in 2005) and Jenson Button (94.1% of the 2009 season).

    All of these championships were won in different machinery against different levels of opposition, but Antonelli’s also stands out as something of a walkover. Even among the 16 first-time title wins that didn’t go down to the last round, some were genuine title fights that went deep into the season before swinging in one driver’s favour.

    For example, Alain Prost’s 1985 championship for McLaren was sealed with two races to spare but Ferrari’s Michele Alboreto was right there with him before failing to score in the final five races. Alan Jones (1980), Button and Ayrton Senna (1988) and Alonso were all part of genuine battles.

    And while you can argue the dominance of Mercedes played a big part in this, Russell’s performances in the second car show that it might have looked like a profoundly different season without Antonelli’s brilliance.
