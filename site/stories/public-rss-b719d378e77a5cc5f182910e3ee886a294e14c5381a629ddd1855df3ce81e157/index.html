    勒克莱尔表示，尽管他的第一个孩子还有几周就要出生，但他预计不会缺席本赛季剩余六场一级方程式比赛中的任何一场。

    这位法拉利车手和妻子亚历山德拉在二月结婚后即将迎来他们的第一个孩子。预产期预计会落在F1赛季繁忙的收官阶段。

    剩余六场比赛包含两个三连赛。本周末的美国大奖赛将拉开第一个三连赛的序幕，随后是墨西哥大奖赛和巴西大奖赛。之后车队和车手将有一个周末的休息时间，然后迎来最后一个三连赛，包括拉斯维加斯大奖赛、卡塔尔大奖赛和阿布扎比大奖赛。

    在奥斯汀的媒体日上被问及是否会缺席某场比赛时，勒克莱尔表示他会尽一切努力在比赛间隙待在家里，即使这意味着比平时更晚抵达赛道。

    “不，我不认为我会错过一场比赛，”这位摩纳哥车手解释道。“我不认为我会错过一场比赛，但显然我会尽一切努力在这些比赛之间尽可能多地待在家里。

    “所以，我可能会稍微晚到一点。但我会参加周日的比赛。”

    勒克莱尔抵达美国大奖赛周末时，以215分在车手积分榜上排名第五，落后积分榜领先者基米·安东内利125分。

    这位29岁的车手预测，在美洲赛道，前四支车队之间的竞争将会非常激烈。

    勒克莱尔对Motorsport.com等媒体表示：“我预计前四名车队之间的竞争会非常激烈，这是肯定的。我们最近看到的情况是，只要有人带来升级，即使不是非常重大的升级，也足以从这个集团中的第四名跃升到第一名，红牛已经很好地证明了这一点。”

    “我希望我们本周末能通过带来的一些升级证明这一点。但纸面上看这并不重大，不应该改变我们在过去几场比赛中看到的格局。不过，我坚信竞争会非常激烈，就像最近几场比赛一样。”

    Charles Leclerc addresses potential F1 race absence ahead of first child's birth

    Ferrari's Charles Leclerc does not expect to miss any of the final six races of the 2026 F1 season despite the imminent birth of his first child

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari with his wife Alexandra Saint Mleux

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Charles Leclerc says he does not expect to miss any of the remaining six Formula 1 races this season, despite the birth of his first child being only weeks away.

    The Ferrari driver and his wife Alexandra are expecting their first child after tying the knot in February. The due date is expected to fall during a busy end to the F1 season.

    Six races remain with two triple-headers. The United States Grand Prix kicks off the first triple-header this weekend and is followed by the Mexican and Brazilian Grands Prix. The teams and drivers will then have one weekend off before the final triple-header, featuring the Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi Grands Prix.

    Asked during media day in Austin whether he could miss a race, Leclerc said he would do everything he could to be at home between races, even if it meant arriving at the circuit later than usual.

    "No, I don’t think I’ll miss a race," the Monegasque driver explained. "I don’t think I’ll miss a race, but obviously I’ll do absolutely everything to be at home as much as possible in between those races.

    "So, I might arrive a little bit late. But I will be doing the races on Sunday."

    Leclerc arrived at the United States Grand Prix weekend fifth in the drivers' standings with 215 points, 125 behind championship leader Kimi Antonelli.

    The 29-year-old predicted a tight field between the top four teams at the Circuit of the Americas.

    "I expect a very tight top four teams, that's for sure," Leclerc told Motorsport.com and other media. "That's what we've seen recently, is that as soon as someone brings an upgrade, even if it's not a very significant one, that's enough to go from fourth in this group to first in this group, and Red Bull have shown that very well.

    "And I hope we'll show that this weekend with a few upgrades we brought. But it's nothing significant on paper that shouldn't change the hierarchy that we've seen in the past races. But yeah, I strongly believe that it will be very tight, just like it's been in the last few races."
