    梅基耶盛赞哈贾尔复出登台「非常出色」：比赛时的冷静与情绪控制令人印象深刻

    红牛领队洛朗·梅基耶称，伊萨克·哈贾尔伤愈复出的巴库周末「非常出色，真的非常出色」：最重要的是他一上车就感觉良好、没有疼痛；巴库容错空间极小，并不容易，但他「一节接一节，非常冷静、带着很多正能量」地找回节奏，排位第四、正赛第三。

    伊萨克·哈贾尔在阿塞拜疆大奖赛上以令人印象深刻的表现重返F1。

    洛朗·梅基耶盛赞伊萨克·哈贾尔在阿塞拜疆大奖赛上的表现「非常出色」，这位红牛车手从手腕伤势中复出便登上了领奖台。

    哈贾尔在夏休期间受伤后首次亮相，尽管在巴库练习赛中稳扎稳打地建立信心，但他在排位赛中拿到了令人印象深刻的第四。

    这还意味着他排在红牛队友马克斯·维斯塔潘之前发车。虽然他在比赛开头几圈让一路追来的荷兰人超了过去，但哈贾尔一直跟住这位四届世界冠军，并在比赛末段奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在1号弯失误后升至第三。

    「我觉得伊萨克这个周末的表现非常出色。」领队梅基耶说。

    「真的非常出色。首先，最重要的是他一上车就感觉很好，没有疼痛。过去几周我们每次碰面都说，我们要等伊萨克百分之百恢复再回来，因为那是首要任务。

    「尽管如此，即使没有疼痛，这也不是一条容易回归的赛道，因为这里没有任何容错空间。而他一节接一节地建立起自己的节奏，非常冷静，充满能量——充满正能量。」

    除了复出之外，哈贾尔还被确认2027年将继续留在红牛。在第二个赛季里，这位法国车手的速度稳步提升，其中包括在摩纳哥登上领奖台。

    梅基耶承认，哈贾尔在周六巴库正赛中的冷静尤其令他印象深刻——哈贾尔当时在无线电中坚持表示，他不想和维斯塔潘缠斗，以免影响两人的比赛。

    「这说明这孩子比赛有多聪明，他在车里正在发展出多强的思考能力，以及他能做到多好的情绪控制。」梅基耶说。

    「在所有这些方面，除了跑出更快的速度之外，还有很多迹象表明他取得了进步。

    「所以，正是这样的成长轨迹，从他加入车队以来每天都让我们印象深刻。去年可能也是如此。

    「这也是我们希望为他提供尽可能好的环境、让他继续进步的原因，因为他这一步的进步确实令人印象深刻。」

    （来源：Formula 1 官网）

    英文原文

    Isack Hadjar returned to Formula 1 with an impressive performance during the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    Laurent Mekies has praised Isack Hadjar’s performance in the Azerbaijan Grand Prix as “outstanding”, after the Red Bull driver returned from a wrist injury to claim a podium.

    Hadjar made his first appearance since sustaining the injury during the summer break, and despite a steady start during practice in Baku to build up confidence, took an impressive fourth in Qualifying.

    It also included starting ahead of Red Bull team mate Max Verstappen, and although he let the charging Dutchman through in the opening laps, Hadjar stayed with the four-time World Champion, eventually moving up to third after Oscar Piastri made a mistake at Turn 1 in the closing stages.

    “I think it was outstanding what Isack has produced this weekend,” said Team Principal Mekies.

    “Really outstanding. At first, the most important thing is that he felt straight away good in the car, no pain. Every time that we met together in the last few weeks we said we will wait for Isack to come back at 100%, because that's the priority.

    “Nonetheless, even without pain, it's not an easy track to go back to, because of the no margin for error. And he has been building up his rhythms, session after session, very calmly, with a lot of energy – with a lot of positive energy.”

    As well as making his return, Hadjar was also confirmed as being retained by Red Bull for 2027 after a sophomore season where the Frenchman has steadily improved his pace, which includes a podium in Monaco.

    Mekies admits he was particularly impressed by Hadjar's calmness during Saturday's race in Baku, after insisting over the radio he didn't want to fight Verstappen and compromise both their races.

    “It shows you how cleverly the kid is racing, and how much of brain capacity he is developing while in the car, and how much emotion control he is managing to have," said Mekies.

    "And [there are] many indicators of steps he has made in all these areas, on top of producing more speed.

    “So it's exactly that trajectory that has been impressing us every day since he has joined the team. Probably last year as well.

    "And that's also there that we want to give him the best possible environment for him to continue to develop, because that step was truly impressive.”
