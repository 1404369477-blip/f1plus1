    Watch or listen to the latest episode of The Fast and the Curious to hear Williams boss James Vowles discuss the team's 2026 struggles, why he is confident a corner has been turned, and the changes he wants to see made to F1's cost cap rules

    Joining Christian Hewgil on the Fast and the Curious podcast, Williams Team Principal James Vowles responds to comments on the pressure of his position.

    Williams team principal James Vowles has admitted his squad "got carried away" with their success last season but insists a disappointing 2026 campaign is merely "a blip".

    Vowles took charge of Williams after joining from Mercedes at the start of 2023 and appeared to be having a hugely positive impact when the team finished fifth in the 2025 constructors' standings, which was their best finish since 2017.

    However, the biggest regulation change in the sport's history, which saw the introduction of both new power units and chassis, has seen Williams regress, leaving drivers Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon hugely frustrated.
