    在激烈的冠军争夺战中，克里斯蒂安·霍纳向托托·沃尔夫发送一条热情的祝贺短信的想法，对于大多数一级方程式车迷来说似乎完全是虚构的。但在基米·安东内利以梅赛德斯车队赢得历史性的2026年冠军之后，这位前红牛车队负责人向他的老对手伸出了赞誉之手。

    在接受德国出版物《 Bild》的独家采访时，沃尔夫被问及是否在最近的祝贺声中收到了霍纳的消息。

    鉴于霍纳在2025年7月从红牛车队被解雇之前一直是沃尔夫最激烈的对手，采访者质疑奥地利人是否对这一举动感到惊讶。

    “是的，但这并没有让我感到惊讶，”沃尔夫表示。“克里斯蒂安和我有着相互尊重的关系。那时就已经是这样了。”

    当沃尔夫决定用安东内利替换即将加盟法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿时，批评者们积极质疑这位青少年是否准备好承受一支冠军级车队的巨大压力。在红牛车队任职期间，霍纳曾与类似的青训车队合作，因此他联系沃尔夫，赞扬他敢于冒险。

    当被要求透露短信的确切内容时，沃尔夫分享了霍纳的私人话语。

    “恭喜你。基米今年表现得不可思议，我尊重你在如此年轻时就支持他，”霍纳写道。

    这次交流揭示了现代涡轮增压混合动力时代两位最强大的车队老板之间罕见的友谊时刻，凸显了他们在赛道上的激烈竞争最终建立在职业尊重的基础之上。

    Christian Horner’s private message to Toto Wolff revealed after Kimi Antonelli's 2026 F1 title

    Toto Wolff reveals the private congratulatory text he received from former Red Bull boss Christian Horner after the 2026 title

    Toto Wolff, Team Principal and CEO, Mercedes-AMG, Christian Horner, Team Principal, Red Bull Racing

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    At the height of their bitter championship battles, the idea of Christian Horner sending Toto Wolff a warm congratulatory text would have seemed entirely fictional to most Formula 1 fans. But following Kimi Antonelli's historic 2026 title victory with Mercedes, the former Red Bull team principal reached out to his old adversary with high praise.

    In an exclusive interview with the German publication BILD, Wolff was asked if he had heard from Horner amid the flood of recent congratulations.

    Given that Horner was Wolff's fiercest rival for years before his dismissal from Red Bull in July 2025, the interviewer questioned if the Austrian was surprised by the gesture.

    "Yes, but that didn't surprise me," Wolff stated. "Christian and I have a respectful relationship. That was already the case back then."

    When Wolff made the decision to replace Ferrari-bound Lewis Hamilton with Antonelli, critics actively questioned if the teenager was ready for the immense pressure of a championship-calibre team. Having worked with a similar junior driver pipeline during his time at Red Bull, Horner reached out to commend Wolff for taking that gamble.

    When asked to reveal the exact contents of the text message, Wolff shared Horner's private words.

    "'Congratulations. Kimi was incredible this year and respect for you giving him your back at such a young age,'" Horner wrote.

    The exchange reveals a rare moment of camaraderie between the two most dominant team bosses of the modern turbo-hybrid era, underscoring that their intense on-track warfare was ultimately underpinned by professional respect.
