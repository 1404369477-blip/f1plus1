    托托·沃尔夫详解梅赛德斯F1低调庆祝2026年双冠

    梅赛德斯在混乱的2026年圣保罗大奖赛上正式完成回归F1顶峰，成功锁定车队世界冠军头衔。然而，对于车队负责人兼CEO托托·沃尔夫来说，这一历史性里程碑并没有在巴西的夜总会举行盛大的庆祝活动。

    梅赛德斯CEO托托·沃尔夫解释了他为什么在巴西跳过胜利派对，而是在私人飞机上独自喝了一杯伏特加。

    对于梅赛德斯车迷来说，这里是所有关于银箭的最新消息，包括来自Brackley和Brixworth的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析。

    梅赛德斯在圣保罗大奖赛上成功锁定车队世界冠军头衔，这是在基米·安东内利一周前获得车手冠军后实现的。但车队负责人和CEO托托·沃尔夫并没有在巴西的夜总会举行盛大的庆祝活动。

    在最近的一次德国出版物《BILD》的采访中，沃尔夫透露了他的冠军庆祝活动实际上有多么低调。

    当被问及是否有像迈克尔·舒马赫著名的百利甜和可乐那样的庆祝饮品时，沃尔夫表示他通常完全避免饮酒。

    “我真的对此感到厌烦，”沃尔夫告诉该出版物。“我在赛季期间不喝酒，因为我需要保持100%的健康，而酒精会使我的时差反应更糟。”

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    但是，确保双冠王头衔需要在漫长的飞行回欧洲期间做出罕见的短暂例外。

    沃尔夫表示，'在巴西比赛后的周日，我在飞机上和乔治·拉塞尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿喝了一杯伏特加。'

    在私人飞机上与汉密尔顿分享胜利的饮料，这位著名车手在2025赛季前离开了梅赛德斯，为法拉利效力，这展示了两人之间持久的个人关系，尽管他们目前作为竞争对手在赛道上的竞争激烈。

    当被问及为什么梅赛德斯领导层没有留在圣保罗举办一场正式的胜利派对时，这位奥地利高管直接指出了他巨大的企业义务。

    “我不是仅仅一个车队负责人，我还是一个CEO，”沃尔夫解释道。“我无法承担一个派对假期——即使只是为了半天或一晚。”

    承认在英国布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯工厂运营的庞大劳动力，沃尔夫指出他的工作立即转向车队下一个运营目标，而不是沉浸在最近的成功中。

    乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯、安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯、托托·沃尔夫、梅赛德斯

    “在赛车队背后有超过1500名员工，他们是这个成功的一部分，”沃尔夫表示。“我必须为他们提供支持，就像我在赛道上为车队提供支持一样。尤其是我们不会因为成功和众多的祝贺而沾沾自喜。”

    随着2026年的冠军头衔正式到手，沃尔夫确认工厂的焦点已经转向卫冕他们的冠军。

    "是的，我们赢得了两个世界锦标赛，但我们希望明年再次做到这一点！"他总结道。

    Toto Wolff details subdued Mercedes F1 celebrations after securing 2026 double

    Mercedes CEO Toto Wolff explains why he skipped a victory party in Brazil to take a single vodka shot on a private jet

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes officially completed its return to the top of Formula 1 at the chaotic 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix, successfully locking down the constructors' world championship one week after Kimi Antonelli secured the drivers' title. But for team principal and CEO Toto Wolff, the historic milestone was not met with an explosive nightclub celebration in Brazil.

    In a recent interview with the German publication BILD, Wolff revealed exactly how subdued his championship celebrations actually were.

    When asked if he had a go-to celebratory drink like Michael Schumacher's famous Bacardi and Cola, Wolff said he usually avoids alcohol entirely.

    "I'm really bored with that," Wolff told the publication. "I don't drink alcohol during the season because I need to be 100% fit and alcohol makes my jet lag worse."

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    But securing a double championship warranted a rare, brief exception during the long flight back to Europe.

    "On Sunday after the race in Brazil, I treated myself to a vodka shot on the plane with George Russell and Lewis Hamilton," Wolff stated.

    Sharing a victory drink on a private jet with Hamilton, who famously departed Mercedes to drive for Ferrari ahead of the 2025 season, demonstrates the enduring personal relationship between the two men, despite their current on-track rivalry as competitors.

    When pressed on why the Mercedes leadership did not stay in São Paulo to host a proper victory party, the Austrian executive pointed directly to his massive corporate obligations.

    "No. I'm not just a team principal, I'm also a CEO," Wolff explained. "I can't afford a party holiday – even if it's only for a day or a night."

    Acknowledging the massive workforce operating at the Brackley and Brixworth factories in the United Kingdom, Wolff noted that his job immediately shifts to the team's next operational target rather than resting on its recent success.

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    "Behind the race team are over 1500 employees who are part of this success," Wolff stated. "I have to be there for them just as I am for the team at the race track. Especially since we don't rest on our laurels over success and the many congratulations."

    With the 2026 titles officially secured, Wolff confirmed that the factory's focus has already transitioned to defending their crown.

    "Yes, we won both world championships, but we want to do that again next year!" he concluded.
