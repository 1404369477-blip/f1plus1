    奥康回应哈斯车队签下卡马拉加盟F1 2027

    奥康认为卡马拉应得到F1的机会，但认为他不应在2027年没有座位。

    埃斯蒂班·奥康回应哈斯车队签下拉斐尔·卡马拉参加2027年F1赛事

    埃斯蒂班·奥康将在2027年被法拉利青训车队车手拉斐尔·卡马拉取代，并且明年没有真正的F1赛车手位置选择

    埃斯蒂班·奥康认为他的哈斯车队替代者拉斐尔·卡马拉应该得到在F1中展示自己的机会，但他觉得不应该没有2027年的赛车手位置

    卡马拉的签约在上周得到确认，作为巴西人从F2联赛晋升而来——他目前是联赛的领导者。尽管奥利弗·贝尔曼尚未被哈斯车队官方宣布留队，但美国车队希望延长他的合同。

    在2027年的赛车上没有留下任何真正的机会，除非出现任何惊喜的退出，奥康的选择非常有限，这位30岁的车手最有可能签订一份预备合同来保持他的竞技状态——而不是转战其他类别。

    奥康坚称他的表现并不是他离开的原因，但再次不愿透露哈斯车队选择不续约的具体原因。

    “我无法说太多。我可以说的是，拉法应该得到一次F1的机会。我认为他在其他类别中已经证明了自己非常出色，”他在本周末的美国大奖赛之前说。

    “但我不应该同时被排除在F1之外。就是这样。我来到这里是为了尽我所能做好工作，试图最大限度地发挥汽车的性能。”

    “正如我所说，我有资格在这里参加一级方程式赛车。有很多原因说明我明年不会在这里，但它们不是性能原因。我在每个类别中都赢得了所有的一切。”

    拉斐尔·卡马拉将加入哈斯一级方程式车队参加2027赛季

    “我在卡丁车、雷诺方程式、三级方程式、GP3、F1等所有这些类别中都赢得了比赛。仅从体育角度考虑，我有资格参加F1。我相信这不会是我F1生涯的最后一年。”

    奥康的焦点是确保他能在哈斯车队的最后几场比赛中继续表现出色，并帮助车队在 制造商锦标赛中保持第七名的位置 - 目前领先奥迪七分。

    尽管他在得知自己2026年没有赛车后得到了公众的支持，奥康表示他并不需要来自同行的私人支持——而且事实上也没有收到过。

    "当然，我在私底下会和一些家伙聊天，"这位法国人补充道。"比如，兰斯来找我谈过。但我没有收到任何人的消息。

    "他们没有给我发短信——在媒体上表示支持。这没关系。毕竟，这并不是终点。我不需要任何车手或什么的支持。那不是我所需要的。对每个人来说，这是一个艰难的世界。

    "我们都在经历同样的事情。如果你在三场比赛中表现不佳，你就是最差的。如果你在三场好比赛中表现良好，你就是最好的。所以，这就是这项运动的困难所在。"

    Esteban Ocon responds to Haas signing Rafael Camara for F1 2027

    Esteban Ocon is being replaced by Ferrari junior Rafael Camara for 2027 and has no real options for an F1 drive next year

    Esteban Ocon agrees that his Haas replacement Rafael Camara deserves his chance to be in Formula 1, but feels he does not deserve to be without a seat for 2027.

    Camara's signing was confirmed last week as the Brazilian steps up from F2 - where he is currently the championship leader. Although Oliver Bearman is yet to be officially announced by Haas as staying at the team, the American outfit is hoping to extend his deal.

    With no real opportunities left on the 2027 grid barring any surprise exits, Ocon's options are slim and the 30-year-old is most likely looking at a reserve deal to keep his hand in - rather than making a switch to a different category.

    Ocon was adamant that his performances were not the reason for his departure, but once more would not be drawn on the specific reasons why Haas chose not to extend his deal.

    "I can't say very much. What I will say is that Rafa deserves a shot in F1. I think he's shown in the categories that he's very good," he said ahead of this weekend's United States Grand Prix.

    "But I don't deserve to be out of F1 at the same time. That's it. I'm coming here to do the best job I can, trying to exploit the maximum out of the car.

    "As I said, I deserve to be here in Formula 1. There are many circumstances why I'm not here next year. But they are not the performance reasons. I've won everything in every category.

    Rafael Camara joins Haas F1 Team for 2027

    "I've done in go-karts, in Formula Renault, in Formula 3, in GP3, in F1. In all these categories I've won races. Just thinking from a sports perspective, I deserve to be in F1. I believe that this is not my last year in F1."

    Ocon's focus, then, was on ensuring that he could continue to deliver in his final races for Haas and assist the team in its fight to retain seventh in the constructors' championship - it is currently seven points clear of Audi.

    Although he'd received public support following the news that he was out of a drive for 2026, Ocon stated that he didn't need private support from his fellow drivers - and nor had he received anyway.

    "Of course I talk to some of the guys in private life," the Frenchman added. "Lance came to speak to me for example. But I didn't receive any messages from anyone.

    "They haven't texted me - supportive in the press. It's fine. It's not the end anyway. I'm not calling for any support from any drivers or whatever. That's not what I need. It's a difficult world for everybody in a way.

    "We are all living in that same thing. If you don't perform for three races, you are the worst. If you perform in three good races, you are the best. So, that's the difficulty of the sport."
