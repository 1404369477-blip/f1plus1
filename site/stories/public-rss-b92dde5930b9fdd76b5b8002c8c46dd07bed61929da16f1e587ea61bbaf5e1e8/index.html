    诺里斯在美国大奖赛前推出新服装系列

    迈凯伦车手兰多·诺里斯在美国大奖赛前推出其LN4品牌的新服装系列，灵感来自1970年代的赛车车库，并把奥斯汀一座旧加油站改造成快闪店展示新品。

    兰多·诺里斯在美国大奖赛前推出新服装系列

    兰多·诺里斯推出了一个全新的12件服装系列，灵感来自1970年代的赛车车库

    致迈凯伦车迷。来自沃金的木瓜色新闻，带来车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个比赛周末的最新消息。

    迈凯伦车手兰多·诺里斯在美国大奖赛前推出了一条新的服装线。受1970年代赛车车库启发，他把奥斯汀一座旧加油站改造成快闪店，用来展示他的新产品。

    Lando 的 Original Service Department 是他 LN4 品牌的一个 12 件单品系列，涵盖工装风格服装、T 恤和男女通用帽子。如果这让你感兴趣，而且你本周末在奥斯汀观看大奖赛，可以前往奥斯汀市中心圣哈辛托大道 1500 号，浏览现有商品。

    该店于周三开业，周四至周日营业时间为上午 9 点至晚上 7 点。

    如果这还不够，你还可以借助迈凯伦赞助商 Google Gemini 定制明信片或徽章。

    诺里斯的另一个品牌 Quadrant 本周早些时候也在奥斯汀举办了快闪活动。

    这套收藏让这位英国人在艰难的卫冕赛季中有了值得庆祝的东西。这位卫冕世界冠军以201分位列积分榜第六，落后领先者基米·安东内利139分，他驾驶的迈凯伦赛车被他形容为“精神上令人疲惫”。这支来自沃金的车队是卫冕车队冠军，目前在积分榜上排名第三，领先复苏的红牛七分。

    “每个弯道都像在刀尖上，”诺里斯在新加坡站后说道。“你必须在精确的百分比内驾驶，而且你必须以各种方式强行驾驭赛车。你不能让赛车自己去做任何工作。你不能只让前部工作或只让后部工作。

    “你必须强行控制前部，强行控制后部，你必须以一种需要高度集中和极强能力的方式驾驶它。这完全是全力以赴，而且精神上令人疲惫，因为你在方向盘后面必须付出多么大的努力，所以这实在太难了，这使得在排位赛中做出完整的圈速变得不可能，这恰恰说明了当我们拿到杆位之类的时候我们能够做得有多好，以及那种感觉有多值得。

    “但现在它不够快，就只是……太难了，你无法做出任何神奇的事情，因为你根本不够快。所以只是……你必须强行驾驭赛车，你不能让赛车自己去做任何工作，因为它不会自己去做，而这完全不是你想要的赛车。”

    Lando Norris launches new clothing collection ahead of United States Grand Prix

    Lando Norris has launched a new 12-piece clothing collection inspired by 1970s racing garages

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    McLaren driver Lando Norris has launched a new clothing line ahead of the United States Grand Prix. Inspired by 1970s racing garages, he's turned an old Austin fuel station into a pop-up to show off his new products.

    Lando's Original Service Department is a 12-piece collection from his LN4 brand, which covers workwear-style clothing, T-shirts and caps for both sexes. If that tickles your fancy and you're in Austin this weekend for the grand prix, head to 1500 San Jacinto Boulevard in downtown Austin and you will be able to browse through what's available.

    It opened on the Wednesday and is open from 9am to 7pm from Thursday to Sunday.

    If that's not enough, you can even get a custom postcard or patch made thanks to McLaren sponsor Google Gemini.

    Norris’s other brand, Quadrant, also held a pop-up in Austin earlier in the week.

    The collection gives the Briton something to celebrate during a difficult title defence. The reigning world champion is sixth in the standings on 201 points, 139 behind leader Kimi Antonelli, in a McLaren he calls "mentally draining" to drive. The Woking outfit, the reigning constructors’ champion, is third in the standings, seven points ahead of a resurgent Red Bull.

    "It's just on a knife edge on every single corner," Norris said after the Singapore race. "Just got to drive it within an exact percent and you have to force the car in every single way. You can't let the car do any work. You can't just let the front do the work or the rear do the work.

    "You have to force the front, you got to force the rear, you got to drive it in a way that takes so much concentration and so much ability. It's just full on and mentally draining because of how hard you have to work behind the wheel so it's just too difficult and it makes it impossible to put laps together in qualifying which just show how good of a job we were able to do when we were getting on pole and things like that and how rewarding that felt.

    "But with it not being quick enough now it's just... it's too difficult and you can't do anything magical because you're simply not quick enough. So it's just... you have to force the car and you can't let the car do any work because it doesn't do it on its own and that's not what you want from a car at all."
