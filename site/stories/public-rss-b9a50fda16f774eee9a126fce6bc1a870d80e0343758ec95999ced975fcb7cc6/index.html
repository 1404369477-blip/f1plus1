    沃尔夫回顾梅赛德斯无冠岁月：我们并非彻底失败者

    梅赛德斯在巴西大奖赛锁定第九个车队世界冠军，托托·沃尔夫回顾此前四年无冠时期，表示车队并非“彻底失败者”。

    托托·沃尔夫回顾梅赛德斯无冠岁月：“我们并非彻底失败者”

    在梅赛德斯锁定第九个车队世界冠军之际，托托·沃尔夫回顾了F1的地面效应时代

    2022年的刘易斯·汉密尔顿与乔治·拉塞尔

    梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫表示，车队在四年无缘F1世界冠军的时期并非“彻底失败者”。

    银箭车队周日于巴西大奖赛上夺得他们的第九个车队总冠军，而就在一周前，基米·安东内利成为F1历史上最年轻的世界车手冠军。

    随着F1在2026年改用新底盘和动力单元规则，梅赛德斯脱颖而出，但此前的地面效应时代对这支车队而言颇为艰难——2022至2025年间他们仅赢得七场胜利，而仅今年一年就有12场。

    “问题是，当你身处这些艰难时刻——就像我们所有人生活中有时也会经历的那样——坚持下去并不容易，”沃尔夫说。“因为事后回看，你会说：‘我们需要这些艰难时刻，才能作为个人成长，或作为组织成长。’但当你身处低谷时，很难有这种感觉。

    “所以回看过去，我认为那最后四年非常艰难，先是做错了，然后多少明白为什么错了，接着又意识到，不，我们并没有真正理解。那是一个巨大的反复。

    “但当我们10年或20年后回望时，看看统计数据，那四年我们并不是彻底的输家，因为我们连续获得了[第三、第二、第四、第二]。我们赢过比赛，很多比赛。

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    “所以单看数字，情况一点也不差，但感觉并不好。车队的韧性——那些在艰难时期团结在一起的人——是一个因素。

    “所以锁定这两个冠军感觉真的很好。没有多少运动队能在连续夺冠、然后经历一段困难时期、再卷土重来之后做到这一点。所以我真的很自豪，我们属于在所有体育项目中做到这一点的极少数车队之一。”

    梅赛德斯第九次夺得车队总冠军，意味着它在F1历史纪录上追平了威廉姆斯，距离迈凯伦仅差一个冠军。法拉利以16次车队冠军保持纪录。

    Toto Wolff reflects on Mercedes’ non-title years: ‘We weren’t complete losers’

    As Mercedes clinches a ninth constructors’ world championship, Toto Wolff looks back on Formula 1’s ground-effect era of Formula 1

    Lewis Hamilton and George Russell in 2022

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff says the squad wasn’t “complete losers” in its four years without a Formula 1 world title.

    The Silver Arrows claimed their ninth constructors’ championship on Sunday at the Brazilian Grand Prix, one week after Kimi Antonelli became F1’s youngest world drivers’ champion.

    Mercedes came out on top as F1 switched to new chassis and power unit regulations for 2026, but the previous ground-effect era had been tough for the outfit – it took just seven victories from 2022-2025, compared to 12 this year alone.

    “The thing is, when you are in these difficult moments, like we all are sometimes in our lives, it's not that easy to keep going,” Wolff said. “Because looking back in retrospect, you're saying, ‘We needed these tough moments in order to grow as people or to grow as an organisation’. But when you're down, that is quite difficult to feel.

    “So looking back, I think those last four years were very difficult, between getting it wrong and then kind of understanding why it was wrong and then realising, no, we didn't understand. And that was a big back and forth.

    “But we will be looking back in 10 or 20 years, and when you look at the statistics, it wasn't that we were complete losers over those four years, because we finished [third, second, fourth, second successively]. We won races, many races.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    “So it wasn't that bad at all when you look at the sheer numbers, but it felt not good. And the resilience of the team – the people that held together in those tough times – is a factor.

    “So it feels really good sealing those two titles. Not many sports teams have done that after a consecutive run of championships, then a difficult spell, and then coming back. So I'm really proud that we are part of this very small group of teams that have done that in all sports.”

    Mercedes’ ninth constructors’ title means it has equalled Williams in F1’s history books and is just one crown away from McLaren. Ferrari is the record holder with 16 championship victories.
