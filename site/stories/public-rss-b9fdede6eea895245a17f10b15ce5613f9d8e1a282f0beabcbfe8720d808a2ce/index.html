    LISA成为首位设计F1服装系列艺术家

    泰国歌手LISA成为首位设计F1服装系列艺术家，推出名为'Pretty Fast'的胶囊系列。

    LISA成为首位设计一级方程式赛车服装的艺术家

    该系列包括赛车夹克、剪裁连帽衫、婴儿T恤、托特包和限量版配饰

    超越赛道的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    LISA成为首位设计一级方程式赛车服装系列的艺术家，她的'Pretty Fast'系列今天在全球范围内上市。

    泰国歌手，在K-pop组合BLACKPINK成名后转型单飞，与创意机构Ceremony of Roses合作设计了胶囊系列。F1表示，这个系列是在LISA的创意指导下完成的，将赛车图形和复古赛车运动元素与她的个人艺术作品相结合。

    系列中包括赛车夹克、剪裁短款卫衣、婴儿T恤和超大号手提袋，以及限量版配饰。其中一些单品仅将在本周F1抵达拉斯维加斯，为赛季最后三场比赛拉开序幕的这座城市出售。

    粉丝可以通过LISA的网站和官方F1商店购买，或者在比赛周末在F1 Hub以及凯撒宫的斗兽场现场购买。

    “我一直对一级方程式赛车充满热情，参加比赛并体验整个过程。我想从我对时尚的热爱中汲取灵感，并将其与一级方程式的元素结合起来，为这个独家系列设计。每一件单品都是为了有趣、表达自我，并且是我自己会穿的衣服，”LISA说。

    Emily Prazer，一级方程式赛车的主管商业官和拉斯维加斯大奖赛的首席执行官补充说：“LISA是首位担任一级方程式赛车服装系列创意领导者的艺术家，使‘Pretty Fast’对我们来说成为了一个里程碑时刻。她是世界上最有影响力的文化声音之一，拥有塑造音乐、时尚和娱乐领域对话的惊人能力。”

    她还补充说，给予LISA“通过她自己的视角重新诠释一级方程式赛车的自由”产生了一些“新鲜、真实且与我们之前所创造的不同”的东西。

    LISA becomes first artist to design F1 clothing collection

    The collection includes racing jackets, cropped hoodies, baby tees, totes and limited-edition accessories

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    LISA has become the first artist to design a Formula 1 clothing range, with her 'Pretty Fast' collection going on sale around the world today.

    The Thai singer, who made her name in K-pop group BLACKPINK before going solo, designed the capsule with Ceremony of Roses, a creative agency that works on the commercial side of music artists' careers. F1 says the range was put together under LISA's creative direction, mixing racing graphics and vintage motorsport touches with her own artwork.

    Included in the collection are a racing jacket, a cropped hoodie, a baby tee and an oversized tote bag, alongside limited-edition accessories. A handful of pieces will be sold only in Las Vegas, where F1 has arrived this week for the start of the final three races of the season.

    Fans can buy it through LISA's website and the official F1 Store, or in person at the F1 Hub during the race weekend and at the Colosseum at Caesars Palace.

    "I've always had a passion for Formula 1, attending races and taking in the whole experience. I wanted to draw inspiration from my love for fashion and mix it with elements of Formula 1 for this exclusive collection. Each piece was designed to be fun, expressive and pieces that I actually wear," LISA said.

    Emily Prazer, F1's chief commercial officer and chief executive of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, added: "LISA is the first artist to take on creative leadership for a Formula 1 apparel collection, making Pretty Fast a landmark moment for us. She is one of the most influential cultural voices in the world, with an incredible ability to shape conversations across music, fashion, and entertainment."

    She added that giving LISA "the freedom to reinterpret Formula 1 through her own lens" had produced something "fresh, authentic, and unlike anything we've created before".
