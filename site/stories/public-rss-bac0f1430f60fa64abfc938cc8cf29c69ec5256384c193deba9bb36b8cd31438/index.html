    皮亚斯特里因与林德布拉德碰撞被罚，失去积分

    皮亚斯特里在墨西哥大奖赛首弯与林德布拉德碰撞，赛后收到10秒罚时，最终排名从第七降至第15位，失去积分。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在F1墨西哥大奖赛中因与阿尔维德·林德布拉德碰撞而受罚，失去了积分完赛的成绩。

    迈凯伦与小红牛在首弯相撞，当时皮亚斯特里试图从林德布拉德手中抢下第七位，小红牛赛车一度被顶起后撞上护墙，而皮亚斯特里仍将受损的赛车带回第七名。

    两位车手赛后都被召至干事处，皮亚斯特里被处以10秒罚时，最终排名降至第15位。

    “81号车（皮亚斯特里）从未完全与41号车（林德布拉德）并排，因此无权占据该弯角，”干事的裁决写道。

    “在1号弯，41号车手通过松开刹车来防守自己的位置，但并未将81号车手逼出赛道，在外侧留出了比赛空间——尽管他并无义务这样做。”

    “干事们认定，如果81号车手在1号弯后进一步向左移动，碰撞本可避免，即使这意味着在2号弯前驶离赛道。”

    皮亚斯特里此前坚称林德布拉德应承担责任。

    “我试图从内线切入，然后他非常非常晚地关门，所以我不得不避开他，结果跑到了外侧，”他说。

    “所以，我从那里尝试了，显然他把我推得很宽，那一刻我知道我无法完成超车，所以我试图脱身。

    “但他把我推得太宽了，我无法再往左走，我们的车轮锁在一起，不幸的是，结果就是这样。”

    林德布拉德在媒体活动中不想详细评论这一事件，直到他看到事件的录像。

    讽刺的是，皮亚斯特里受罚对林德布拉德的 小红牛 车队在与 阿尔派 争夺制造商第五名的战斗中伤害稍大，因为成绩洗牌意味着 阿尔派 的弗兰科·科拉平托和皮埃尔·加斯利现在因第七和第九名合计获得八分，而 小红牛 仅因利亚姆·劳森的第八名获得四分——尽管 小红牛 仍领先 29 分。

    皮亚斯特里的罚时也意味着奥迪获得一个积分，加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托升至第10名。

    英文原文

    Oscar Piastri has lost his points finish in Formula 1’s Mexican Grand Prix as a punishment for his collision with Arvid Lindblad.

    The McLaren and Racing Bulls came together at the first corner when Piastri tried to take seventh from Lindblad, firing the Racing Bulls briefly into the air and then into the barriers, while Piastri brought his battered car home seventh still.

    Both drivers were called to the stewards after the race, where Piastri was given a 10-second time penalty that dropped him to 15th in the final results.

    “Car 81 [Piastri] was never fully alongside car 41 [Lindblad] so was not able to claim the right to the corner,” said the stewards’ verdict.

    “Through Turn 1, the driver of car 41 defended his position by releasing the brakes, however does not force car 81 off the track, allowing racing room on the outside – which he is not required to do.

    “The stewards determined that the collision could have been avoided if the driver of car 81 had moved further to the left after Turn 1, even if this involved leaving the track prior to Turn 2.”

    Piastri had been adamant Lindblad was to blame.

    “I tried to go up the inside and then he shut the door very, very late, so I had to avoid him and end up on the outside,” he said.

    “So, I tried from there and obviously he pushed me pretty wide, and at that point I knew I wasn’t going to complete the overtake, so I was trying to get out.

    “But he pushed me so far wide, I couldn’t go any further left and we interlocked wheels and that was the end result unfortunately.”

    Lindblad didn't want to comment on the incident in detail during his media commitments until he had seen the footage of the incident.

    Ironically Piastri being punished hurts Lindblad’s Racing Bulls team slightly more in its battle with Alpine for fifth in the constructors’ championship as the results shuffle means Alpine’s Franco Colapinto and Pierre Gasly now take a combined eight points for seventh and ninth while Racing Bulls only gets four for Liam Lawson’s eighth place - though Racing Bulls is still 29 points clear.

    The Piastri penalty also means Audi gets a point as Gabriel Bortoleto moves up to 10th.
