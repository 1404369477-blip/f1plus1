    TAG Heuer发布限量版小红牛F1手表庆祝10周年合作

    TAG Heuer推出限量版F1 Solargraph手表，庆祝与小红牛车队10年合作；手表售价1900英镑（约合2250美元），美国买家于10月20日提前在线购买，为奥斯汀的美国大奖赛做准备。

    泰格豪雅推出庆祝10周年纪念版红牛一级方程式手表

    泰格豪雅推出一款特别版一级方程式太阳图形手表，以庆祝其与红牛赛车队的10年合作

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    泰格豪雅推出了一款特别版的一级方程式太阳图形手表，以庆祝其作为红牛一级方程式车队合作伙伴的10周年。

    F1 Solargraph x Oracle 红牛 Racing售价为1900英镑（约合2250美元）。美国买家于10月20日提前获得了在线购买权限，这比本周末在德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的美国大奖赛还要早。

    这款手表的颜色灵感来源于米尔顿凯恩斯车队的外观。它拥有深蓝色的太阳光刷面表盘，白色的时针和分针，以及黄色的秒针，这些都设置在一个由TAG Heuer的TH-Polylight材料制成的蓝色双向表圈中。

    TAG Heuer首席执行官贝阿特丽斯·戈亚斯格拉斯说：“十年前，TAG Heuer和Oracle 红牛 Racing基于一个简单的信念走到一起：每一秒的每一部分都很重要。从那时起，我们分享了领奖台、冠军头衔和相同的无休止的获胜动力。”

    这款特别版手表庆祝了这段旅程，同时坚定地向前看。它拥有38毫米的表壳和Solargraph机芯，将赛道的能量带给新一代的钟表爱好者，对他们来说，赛车不仅是运动，也是一种文化。这是‘专为胜利设计’所代表的一切。

    泰格豪雅自2016年起成为红牛一级方程式车队的官方计时员、官方手表和团队性能合作伙伴。

    红牛车队在美国大奖赛上以332分排在车队积分榜第四位，落后积分榜领跑者梅赛德斯246分。车队还带着在马来西亚巴林大奖赛和新加坡大奖赛上连续两次获胜的势头。

    维斯塔潘在车手积分榜上排名第四，积分为221分，他的队友伊萨克·哈德贾排名第八，积分为97分。

    美国大奖赛将于10月23日至25日在美国赛车场举行。

    TAG Heuer unveils special edition Red Bull F1 watch for 10-year celebration

    TAG Heuer has unveiled a special edition Formula 1 Solargraph watch celebrating its 10-year partnership with Red Bull Racing

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    TAG Heuer has released a special edition of its Formula 1 Solargraph watch to celebrate 10 years as a partner of the Red Bull F1 team.

    The Formula 1 Solargraph x Oracle Red Bull Racing costs £1900 (approximately $2250). Buyers based in America were given early online access on 20 October, ahead of this weekend's United States Grand Prix in Austin, Texas.

    The watch takes its colours from the Milton Keynes outfit's livery. It has a deep blue sunray-brushed dial, white hour and minute hands and a yellow seconds hand, set inside a blue bidirectional bezel made from TAG Heuer's TH-Polylight material.

    "Ten years ago, TAG Heuer and Oracle Red Bull Racing came together around a simple conviction: every fraction of a second matters. Since then, we have shared podiums, titles and the same relentless drive to win," TAG Heuer CEO Béatrice Goasglas said.

    "This special edition celebrates that journey while looking firmly ahead. With its 38 mm case and Solargraph movement, it brings the energy of the grid to a new generation of watch enthusiasts, for whom racing is as much a culture as a sport. It is everything ‘Designed to Win’ stands for."

    TAG Heuer has been a partner of the Red Bull F1 team as official timekeeper, official watch and team performance partner since 2016.

    Red Bull heads into the United States Grand Prix fourth in the constructors' standings with 332 points, 246 points behind championship leader Mercedes. It also carries the momentum of back-to-back wins with Max Verstappen at the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia and the Singapore Grand Prix.

    Verstappen sits fourth in the drivers' standings with 221 points, and his team-mate Isack Hadjar is eighth with 97 points.

    The United States Grand Prix is scheduled to take place at the Circuit of the Americas from 23-25 October.
