    哈贾尔回应墨西哥大奖赛围场推人事件

    哈贾尔为自己在墨西哥大奖赛围场推开一名试图自拍的访客辩护，称“这不是真人秀”。他因该行为被国际汽联传唤至赛会干事处，并被认定违反国际体育准则。

    哈贾尔在墨西哥大奖赛围场推人事件后回应国际汽联警告：“这不是真人秀”

    哈贾尔为在墨西哥大奖赛推开一名围场访客辩护

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术解析与比赛分析。

    哈贾尔回击了他在墨西哥大奖赛推开的那名围场访客，坚称“这不是真人秀”。

    周六上午，这位红牛车手被传唤至赛会干事处，此前社交媒体上的视频显示他在围场里推搡了一名试图与他自拍的男子。

    赛会干事认定哈贾尔违反了国际体育规则，但仅给予他正式警告，并列举了五项减轻处罚的因素。

    赛会干事表示：“这名车手是对另一人未经邀请且相当具有攻击性的行为做出的反应。”

    “这名车手当时正努力穿过拥挤区域前往第一节自由练习赛的起点，他身边没有足够的安保人员，那名嘉宾未经他同意便侵犯了他的个人空间，这也是车手们在穿行围场时普遍遇到的问题。”

    哈贾尔在墨西哥大奖赛排位赛获得第七名后，接受媒体采访时谈到了这一事件。

    当被问及是否高兴干事理解了他的观点时，哈贾尔解释道：“是的，因为他们是正确的，这说得通。我很高兴他们做出了正确的决定。我和干事们谈过，他们理解了我的观点。所以，是的，公平竞争。”

    当被要求提供更多关于当时情况的细节时，这位法国-阿尔及利亚裔车手补充道：“这也是有界限的，尤其是在那个年纪。当你还是个孩子的时候，我会去拍张照片。

    “当我不想拍照，因为你是个成年男人时，不要侵犯我的空间，因为我要去工作；这不是真人秀。我实际上是要去维修区工作。”

    哈贾尔的队友马克斯·维斯塔潘最近表示，越来越多的嘉宾让围场感觉“像动物园一样”。哈贾尔对此表示赞同。

    “是的，我同意。需要稍微冷静一下，”他说。

    Isack Hadjar defends Mexican GP paddock incident after FIA warning: "Not a reality show"

    Isack Hadjar has defended pushing away a paddock guest at the Mexican Grand Prix

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Isack Hadjar has hit back at the paddock guest he pushed away at the Mexican Grand Prix, insisting that "it's not a reality show".

    The Red Bull driver was summoned to the stewards on Saturday morning after footage on social media showed him shoving a man who was trying to take a selfie with him in the paddock.

    The stewards found that Hadjar had breached the International Sporting Code, but only handed him a formal warning, citing five mitigating factors.

    "The driver was reacting to an uninvited and rather aggressive action by another person," the stewards stated.

    "The driver was endeavouring to make his way to the start of FP1 through a congested area, the driver did not have sufficient security present, the guest was invading his personal space without his consent and the general issue drivers have experienced moving through the paddock."

    Hadjar addressed the incident while speaking to the media after qualifying seventh for the Mexican Grand Prix.

    "I am, because they were right, it makes sense," Hadjar explained when asked if he was glad the stewards understood his point of view. "I'm glad they took the right decision. I was talking to the stewards and they understood my point. So, yeah, fair play."

    When asked for more details on the situation, the French-Algerian added: "There's a limit to it, also when you're that age. When you're a kid, I'm going to take a picture.

    "When I don't want to take a picture because you're a grown man, don't invade my space, because I'm going to work; it's not a reality show. I'm actually going to work in the garage."

    Hadjar's team-mate Max Verstappen recently said the growing number of guests had made the paddock feel "like a zoo". Hadjar agreed.

    "Yeah, I agree. It needs to chill a bit," he said.
