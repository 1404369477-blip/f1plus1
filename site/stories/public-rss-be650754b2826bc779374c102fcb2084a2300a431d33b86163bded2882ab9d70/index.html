    乌克兰敦促FIA逆转对俄罗斯参赛者的政策转变

    乌克兰政府致信国际汽联，要求其撤销近期允许俄罗斯人以本国国旗参加赛车运动的决定，并指责此举是在纵容“体育洗白”。

    乌克兰政府已致信国际汽联（FIA），要求其撤销最近允许俄罗斯人在本国国旗下参加赛车运动的决定。

    FIA在2022年初俄罗斯入侵乌克兰后禁止了这种做法，尽管一些俄罗斯车手被允许以非俄罗斯执照参赛。两国仍处于战争状态。

    乌克兰青年和体育部长Matvii Bidnyi在社交媒体帖子中表示：“俄罗斯对乌克兰的战争仍在继续。

    “在国际体育中，没有体育洗白或侵略正常化的容身之地。”

    Bidnyi说，自战争开始以来，已有数百名乌克兰运动员被杀。

    “最近，国际汽车联合会（FIA）决定解除对俄罗斯和白俄罗斯代表在国际赛车运动中的限制，允许他们在没有中立身份要求的情况下参赛，并在比赛中恢复他们的国旗和国家标志，”他解释道。

    “我们要求重新考虑这一决定。我已正式向FIA提出呼吁，要求撤销允许俄罗斯和白俄罗斯代表重返国际比赛的决定。

    “制裁的理由并未消失。俄罗斯继续其武装侵略，而白俄罗斯为其提供军事支持。自入侵开始以来，已有超过770名乌克兰运动员和教练被侵略者杀害。敌人摧毁了全国925多个体育设施。

    “展示一个犯下种族灭绝罪行的国家的标志，是对国际法和公平竞赛原则的蔑视。

    “我们呼吁所有国际联合会站在正义一边。体育绝不能成为侵略正常化的工具。世界体育中不应有体育洗白的容身之地。”

    FIA在宣布政策逆转时没有解释其原因。BBC报道称，FIA表示其决定是在国际奥委会发表类似声明之后做出的。

    同一报道称，俄罗斯汽车联合会主席Boris Romanovich Rotenberg表示，该决定是在“与FIA同事进行专业对话”之后做出的。Rotenberg在俄罗斯入侵乌克兰后受到英国和美国的制裁。

    英文原文

    The government of Ukraine has written to the FIA requesting it reverses its recent decision to allow Russians to compete in motorsport under their national flag.

    The FIA banned the practice following Russia’s invasion of Ukraine early in 2022, though some Russian drivers have been allowed to participate under non-Russian licences. The two countries remain at war.

    Ukraine’s minister of youth and sports Matvii Bidnyi said in a social media post: “Russia’s war against Ukraine continues.

    “No place for sportswashing or the normalisation of aggression in international sport.”

    Hundreds of Ukrainian athletes have been killed since the war began, said Bidnyi.

    “Recently, the Federation Internationale de l’Automobile (FIA) decided to lift the restrictions on Russian and Belarusian representatives in international motor sport, allowing them to compete without the requirement of neutral status and restoring their flags and national symbols at competitions,” he explained.

    “We demand that this decision be reconsidered. I have formally appealed to the FIA, calling for the decision to allow Russian and Belarusian representatives to return to international competitions to be revoked.

    “The reasons for the sanctions have not gone away. Russia continues its armed aggression, while Belarus provides it with military support. Since the beginning of the invasion, more than 770 Ukrainian athletes and coaches have been killed by the aggressor. The enemy has destroyed more than 925 sports facilities across the country.

    “Displaying the symbols of a country committing genocide is contempt for international law and the principles of fair play.

    “We call on all international federations to remain on the side of justice. Sport must not become a tool for normalising aggression. There should be no place for sportswashing in world sport.”

    The FIA did not explain its reasons for the reversal in its policy when it was announced. The BBC reported the FIA said its decision followed a similar announcement by the International Olympic Committee.

    The same report stated the president of the Russian Automobile Federation, Boris Romanovich Rotenberg, said the decision followed “a professional dialogue with our colleagues from the FIA”. Rotenberg was sanctioned by the UK and the USA after Russia invaded Ukraine.
