    帕尔默警告拉塞尔：2026年或是最佳夺冠机会

    前F1车手乔利恩·帕尔默警告乔治·拉塞尔，必须抓住2026年的冠军争夺机会，否则可能永远不会再有更好的夺冠时机。

    Jolyon Palmer就F1总冠军向拉塞尔发出警告：'这可能是他有史以来最好的机会'

    Jolyon Palmer认为，拉塞尔必须抓住他2026年的夺冠机会，这位梅赛德斯车手正力争缩小与总冠军领跑者安东内利之间的差距

    据前F1车手Jolyon Palmer所说，拉塞尔必须充分利用他2026年的冠军争夺战，他警告这位梅赛德斯车手可能再也不会有更好的夺冠机会。

    F1本周末将在赞德沃特重启，迎来采用冲刺赛赛制的荷兰大奖赛。随着夏休期后围场重新集结，Russell落后于他的梅赛德斯队友、总冠军领跑者安东内利 59分。七届总冠军汉密尔顿在积分榜上位居这两位梅赛德斯车手之间。

    拉塞尔在2026赛季的开局相当艰难。虽然他赢得了揭幕战澳大利亚大奖赛和中国站冲刺赛，但他遭遇了一连串的霉运，而他的队友则连续五场大奖赛夺冠。赛季上半程，拉塞尔拿下两场大奖赛胜利，而安东内利则拿下了六场。

    “自墨尔本以来，他过得并不轻松，他需要重新调整，”帕尔默在F1 TV的Weekend Warm-Up节目中解释道。

    “所以，这正是完美的时机。你有几周的强制休赛期。去别的地方度过，而且心里还有一件大事，”他补充道，指的是拉塞尔向交往多年的伴侣卡门·蒙特罗·蒙德求婚一事。

    “而且他确实需要做出回应。压力不仅在于争夺冠军的基米，也在于同样争夺冠军的乔治。这是他有史以来最好的机会。这可能仍将是他在F1中改变局面的最好机会。所以现在是时候抛弃各种借口，把成绩写在纸面上了。”

    拉塞尔为荷兰大奖赛周末开了一个好头。在为冲刺赛夺得杆位后，他成功防住了迈凯伦的诺里斯和法拉利的勒克莱尔，拿下胜利，这是他本赛季的第三个冲刺赛冠军。

    Jolyon Palmer warns George Russell over F1 title: 'Might be his best-ever shot'

    Jolyon Palmer believes George Russell must capitalise on his 2026 title opportunity as the Mercedes driver looks to close the gap to championship leader Kimi Antonelli

    George Russell must capitalise on his 2026 championship fight, according to former Formula 1 driver Jolyon Palmer, who warns the Mercedes driver may never get a better title opportunity.

    F1 resumes this weekend at Zandvoort for the Dutch Grand Prix, which features the sprint format. As the paddock reconvenes following the summer break, Russell trails his Mercedes team-mate and championship leader Kimi Antonelli by 59 points. Seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton splits the two Mercedes drivers in the standings.

    Russell has endured a difficult start to his 2026 campaign. While he won the season-opening Australian Grand Prix and the Chinese sprint race, he suffered a string of bad luck while his team-mate claimed five consecutive grand prix wins. In the first half of the season, Russell secured two grand prix wins, whereas Antonelli took six.

    "It's not been easy for him since Melbourne, and he needs to reset," Palmer explained during the F1 TV Weekend Warm-Up show.

    "So, it's the perfect thing. You have a few weeks of enforced shutdown. Go and spend it away, something big on your mind as well," he added, referring to Russell's proposal to his long-term partner Carmen Montero Mundt.

    "And he does need a response. The pressure is not only on Kimi with the title; it's on George with the title as well. This is his best-ever shot. It might stay as his best-ever shot in Formula 1 to make the difference here. So now is the time that the excuses have got to go and you've got to put it on the paper."

    Russell has kicked off the Dutch Grand Prix weekend on a positive note. After securing pole position for the sprint race, he successfully defended against McLaren's Lando Norris and Ferrari's Charles Leclerc to take victory, his third sprint win of the season.
