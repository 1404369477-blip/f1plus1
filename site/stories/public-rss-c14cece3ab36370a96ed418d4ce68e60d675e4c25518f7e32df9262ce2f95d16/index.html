    小红牛F1电动自行车以维斯塔潘的铃鹿涂装亮相拉斯维加斯大道

    本周末，拉斯维加斯的红牛粉丝可以骑上一款带有维斯塔潘2025赛季涂装的官方电动自行车回家。这款电动自行车在拉斯维加斯大道上的一个临时商店开始销售。

    红牛一级方程式电动自行车以马克斯·维斯塔潘的铃鹿涂装亮相拉斯维加斯大道

    SUPER73在拉斯维加斯大道的贝拉吉奥酒店内举办了一场赛车周快闪活动，销售其Oracle红牛赛车电动自行车，该大道是拉斯维加斯大奖赛赛道的一部分

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    本周拉斯维加斯的红牛粉丝可以骑着马克斯·维斯塔潘2025赛季的一部分回家，因为该队的官方电动自行车在大道上的快闪店开始销售。

    加州电动自行车品牌SUPER73正在Podium Collective销售其限量版的Oracle 红牛 Racing车型，Podium Collective是一个由Julien Solomita的Bad Year Racing在Bellagio举办的免费快闪活动。活动从11月19日星期四开始，持续到11月22日星期日，覆盖了整个大奖赛周末。

    销售的自行车是Oracle 红牛 Racing M1D SE，该品牌于9月份以2,995美元的价格推出。它有两种颜色：Nightfall Blue基于红牛2026年的F1涂装，而Suzuka White则模仿了去年车队在日本比赛中使用的白色涂装，他们说这是对国家复古赛车文化的致敬，以及对维斯塔潘2025年胜利的致敬。

    维斯塔潘在铃鹿赛道从杆位赢得了那场比赛，这是他在那个赛季中赢得的八个胜利之一，他在最后一场比赛中仅以两分之差输给了兰多·诺里斯。

    在油漆下面，M1D SE被销售为美国第二类电动自行车，速度限制在20英里/小时。它配备了一台750瓦的电动机和一块780瓦时的电池，公司声称它可以提供25到30英里的续航里程，或者如果选择额外的电池，可以提供50到55英里的续航里程。它的重量为88磅。

    这是SUPER73和红牛第二次合作。第一次合作是在2024年10月，产生了R冒险系列，售价4995美元，限量500辆，每辆都有单独编号的自行车，以及3195美元的Z冒险系列。这两款系列现在都已售罄。

    除了自行车，快闪店还将出售F1的独家服装。

    快闪店从上午10点开放到晚上7点，周日关闭时间为下午2点。入场免费。

    Red Bull F1 e-bike in Max Verstappen's Suzuka livery hits the Las Vegas Strip

    SUPER73 is selling its Oracle Red Bull Racing e-bikes at a race-week pop-up inside the Bellagio, on the Strip that forms part of the Las Vegas Grand Prix circuit

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Red Bull fans in Las Vegas this weekend can ride home on a piece of Max Verstappen's 2025 season, with the team's official e-bike going on sale at a pop-up shop on the Strip.

    Californian electric bike brand SUPER73 is selling its limited-edition Oracle Red Bull Racing models at the Podium Collective, a free pop-up hosted by Julien Solomita's Bad Year Racing at the Bellagio. It runs from Thursday 19 November to Sunday 22 November, covering the whole Grand Prix weekend.

    The bike on sale is the Oracle Red Bull Racing M1D SE, which the brand launched in September at $2,995. It comes in two colours: Nightfall Blue is based on Red Bull's 2026 F1 livery, while Suzuka White copies the white livery the team raced in Japan last year, which they say "pays homage to the country's retro racing culture and Verstappen's 2025 victory."

    Verstappen won that race from pole position at Suzuka, one of eight victories in a season he lost to Lando Norris by just two points on the final race.

    Underneath the paint, the M1D SE is sold as a US Class 2 e-bike, limited to 20mph. It has a 750W motor and a 780Wh battery that the company claims gives 25 to 30 miles of range, or 50 to 55 miles with an optional second battery. It weighs 88lb.

    It is the second time SUPER73 and Red Bull have partnered up. The first collaboration, in October 2024, resulted in the R Adventure Series, which cost $4,995 and was limited to 500 individually numbered bikes, and the $3,195 Z Adventure Series. Both are now sold out.

    Alongside the bikes, the pop-up will sell exclusive pieces of F1 clothing.

    The pop-up is open from 10am to 7pm, and closes at 2pm on Sunday. Admission is free.
