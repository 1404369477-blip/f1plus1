    查尔斯·勒克莱尔相信他在最近的比赛中在法拉利的驾驶中“解锁了某种东西”，并对自己在2026赛季F1赛季结束前有机会赢得胜利充满信心。

    摩纳哥车手在美国大奖赛中获得了第二名，落后于冠军和车手积分榜领跑者基米·安东内利13秒，在比赛中与马克斯·维斯塔潘和兰多·诺里斯展开了激烈的竞争。

    这之后，他在新加坡也获得了第二名，勒克莱尔表示他对赛车的感受在最近几场比赛周末有所提升。

    “能否将这种感觉转化为胜利还有点难以预测，因为差距非常小，同时也取决于其他人在即将到来的比赛中升级计划如何，”勒克莱尔在赛后新闻发布会上解释道。

    “但与赛车的感受越来越好。这些赛车非常特殊且独特，我感觉在最近两三场比赛中，我在驾驶方面又解锁了一些东西，尤其是在我的驾驶设置上。”

    “我们俩在一起，我们已经向前迈出了一大步，我对这辆车的感觉也更好了。所以我希望这能很快带来一场胜利。无论是在新加坡还是在这里的奥斯汀，我们都非常接近，所以我很有信心在赛季结束前我们会有赢得比赛的机会。”

    The 美国站 开启了赛季倒数第二个三连赛，车队和车手们现在将前往墨西哥城参加下周末的墨西哥大奖赛。

    剩下五个赛车周末，假设在卡塔尔和阿布扎比的最后两场比赛按计划进行，勒克莱尔目前在车手积分榜上排名第五，但仍在争夺第二名。梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔目前排名第二，积242分。刘易斯·汉密尔顿、马克斯·维斯塔潘和勒克莱尔分别排名第三、第四和第五，第二名和第五名之间只有九分差距。

    Charles Leclerc reveals Ferrari breakthrough after United States GP podium

    Charles Leclerc believes he has made a breakthrough with his Ferrari driving and is confident of challenging for victory in 2026

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Charles Leclerc believes he has "unlocked something" in his driving with Ferrari in recent races and is confident he will have a shot at victory before the end of the 2026 Formula 1 season.

    The Monegasque driver finished second in the United States Grand Prix, 13 seconds behind race winner and championship leader Kimi Antonelli, after battling Max Verstappen and Lando Norris.

    It followed another second-place in Singapore, and Leclerc said his feeling with the car had improved over the last few race weekends.

    "Whether I can convert that into a win is a bit difficult to predict, because it's such fine margins, and it also depends on what the upgrade plans of the others are in upcoming races," Leclerc explained during the post-race press conference.

    "But the feeling with the car is getting better and better. These cars are very specific and unique, and I feel like, over the last two or three races, I've unlocked something again in terms of driving, and particularly the set-up with my driving.

    "Just both together, we've taken a step forward, and I just feel much more comfortable with the car. So I hope that this will result in a win soon. Whether it's in Singapore or here in Austin, we were quite close, so I'm confident that we'll have a shot at winning a race before the end of the season."

    The United States Grand Prix kicked off the penultimate triple-header of the season, and the teams and drivers will now head to Mexico City for the Mexican Grand Prix next weekend.

    With five race weekends remaining, assuming the final two races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi go ahead as planned, Leclerc sits fifth in the drivers' standings but is still in the fight for second. Mercedes' George Russell currently sits second with 242 points. Lewis Hamilton, Max Verstappen and Leclerc are third, fourth and fifth respectively, with only nine points separating second and fifth.
