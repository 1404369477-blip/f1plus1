    倍耐力在F1卡塔尔大奖赛重新引入单段里程上限

    倍耐力在F1卡塔尔大奖赛重新引入单段里程上限，每位车手每段最多跑27圈，以保护轮胎。57圈的比赛中，车队很可能采用两停策略。

    倍耐力在F1卡塔尔大奖赛重新引入单段里程上限

    F1下周末造访卢赛尔，任何车手每段都不得超过27圈

    F1车队在卡塔尔大奖赛将再次为每辆赛车遵守规定的单段里程上限，以保护倍耐力轮胎应对这条高磨损赛道。

    车手在卢赛尔国际赛道每段最多跑27圈，这意味着在57圈的比赛中，车队很可能选择两停策略。

    这与去年的情况类似，当时在分析2024年比赛后，将每段行驶圈数限制在25圈，因为“多条轮胎，尤其是左前轮，已达到最大磨损”。2023年的赛事也受到每段圈数限制的约束，不过那一年强制限制仅为18圈。

    此前车队曾尝试超过30圈的行驶段，这在拥有16个弯角、5.4公里长、包含各种类型弯道——尤其是中高速弯——的赛道上尤为具有挑战性。

    倍耐力首席F1工程师西蒙·贝拉在本周末拉斯维加斯大奖赛上表示：“规则方面的限制相同，但圈数将是27圈，而非25圈。

    “我们增加了两圈。从我们的室内测试中可以看出，可以说这是可接受的，没有特别的风险。”

    “但我们知道卡塔尔站的问题在于局部磨损非常高，尤其是前轮，平均跑完25、26圈后磨损就很容易达到100%。

    “这会给轮胎带来更大的压力，因为胎面胶料无法保护内部结构，再加上高负荷、高横向载荷，以及高温、高胎体温度。

    “所以，这些因素对轮胎完整性的影响更大。”

    比赛中车手完成的每一圈，包括安全车带领下的圈数，都会计入总数，任何未遵守规定的人都可能面临取消资格的处罚。

    不过这并不意味着策略选择受限，因为2025年迈凯伦就选择不在第七圈安全车下进站，以保持灵活性，避免在第32圈前再次进站。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里和兰多·诺里斯一直坚持到该段进站窗口极限结束，但这仍然适得其反，因为这意味着两位车手都将胜利输给了红牛车队的冠军竞争对手马克斯·维斯塔潘。

    尽管如此，这场大奖赛最终仍以两停为主流，并且紧随周六的19圈冲刺赛之后进行，而今年的赛事将不设冲刺赛。

    贝拉补充道：“我们将保留相同的圈数限制，只是为了促成两次进站，并避免在胎面底层上跑多圈、产生完整性问题的任何风险。”

    “我们知道，如果前轮磨损达到100%，其实并不会引发严重的性能衰退。

    “车队在长距离运行中很容易管理前轴，如果没看到性能明显下降，他们不会进站。

    “所以情况就是这样，我们会统计每节练习的圈数。今年不再是冲刺赛周末，去年才是，所以会稍微容易一些。

    “因此，连圈数统计对我们来说都会更简单，我们会在周六试跑结束后复核圈数，并公布每套轮胎剩余的可用圈数。”

    卡塔尔将作为2026赛季23站比赛中的倒数第二场大奖赛，阿布扎比将于12月4日至6日举办收官战。

    Pirelli reintroduces stint limit for F1 Qatar GP

    F1 visits Lusail next weekend and no driver can go beyond 27 laps per stint

    Formula 1 teams will again have to follow a prescribed stint limit for each car at the Qatar Grand Prix to protect the Pirelli tyres around the high-degradation track.

    Drivers must complete no more than 27 laps per stint at Lusail International Circuit, meaning teams will likely opt for a two-stop strategy in the 57-lap race.

    It is a similar situation to last year when stints were limited to 25 laps following analysis of the 2024 contest, where “several tyres, particularly the left front, had reached the maximum wear”. The 2023 event was also the subject of stint limits, though that year, the enforced limit was just 18 laps.

    That came after teams attempted stints of over 30 laps, which is especially challenging at the 16-turn, 5.4km circuit that features various types of corners - particularly medium-to-high-speed.

    Pirelli’s chief F1 engineer Simone Berra said at this weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix: “The same limitation in terms of rules, but the number of laps will be 27, not 25.

    “We increased by two laps. We have seen from our indoor testing that this is, let's say, acceptable without any particular risk.

    “But we know the issue in Qatar is that we have very high localised wear, especially on the front, with 100% wear easily reached after 25, 26 laps on average.

    “This is generating higher stress on the tyre because you don't have the thread compound protecting the structure and then you have high loads, high lateral loads there, and high temperature, high carcass temperature.

    “So, these are the factors that are influencing more the integrity resistance of the tyre.”

    Every lap a driver completes during the race, including those behind a safety car, will count towards the total and a disqualification is the likely punishment for anybody who fails to oblige.

    It doesn’t mean strategic options are limited though, as 2025 saw McLaren opt against pitting under a lap seven safety car to maintain flexibility and not have to stop again before lap 32.

    Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris stayed out until the end of the stint limit, but this still backfired as it meant both drivers lost the victory to title rival Max Verstappen at Red Bull.

    The grand prix ended as a predominantly two-stop contest nevertheless and it followed the 19-lap sprint race on the Saturday, which will not feature in this year’s event.

    Berra added: “We are going to keep the same laps limitation just to induce the two stops and avoid any risk of running on the under-thread for multiple laps and generating integrity issues.

    “We know that if you reach 100% wear on the front, then you don't really induce a big degradation.

    “The teams, they can easily manage the front axle in the long runs, they don't stop if they don't see quite a significant drop in performance.

    “So, that's the situation, and we will count the number of laps for each session. It will be a little bit easier because it's no more a sprint event this year, while it was last year.

    “So, even the lap count will be easier for us and we will review the lap count and release the remaining laps per set at the end of Saturday running.”

    Qatar will serve as the penultimate grand prix of the 23-round 2026 campaign with Abu Dhabi hosting the finale on 4-6 December.
