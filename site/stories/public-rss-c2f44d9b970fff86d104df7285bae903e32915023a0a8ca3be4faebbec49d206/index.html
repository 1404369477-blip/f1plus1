    法拉利F1车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔称，勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛中与汉密尔顿的碰撞“完全不可接受”，并补充说，车队向车手传达的信息似乎再次被无视，他对此“极为不满”。

    在拉斯维加斯街道赛道上，队友勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿为争夺第六名缠斗时，勒克莱尔导致汉密尔顿打转。汉密尔顿跌至第10位，最终追回至第7名完赛，而勒克莱尔被处以10秒罚时，最终第10名完赛。

    赛后接受天空体育采访时，瓦塞尔表示车队已重申了墨西哥站后给出的信息。

    “显然，这是不可接受的。首先，信息和墨西哥站大致相同，因为你必须为车队着想，为在马拉内罗工作的人们着想，为所有利益相关者和赞助商着想，”他说。

    这位车队负责人透露，法拉利在比赛中已与勒克莱尔沟通，解释了拉斯维加斯赛道的特性意味着车手必须关注周围的赛车。

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    “我们把消息传递给了夏尔，让他不要超车，因为在这种比赛中，正如你之前所说，你必须超越前面的车，否则就会被其他人超越。这意味着你必须跑好自己的比赛，”他继续说道。

    “而夏尔，这显然是一个让车队损失惨重的错误。今天这完全不可接受。老实说，我非常不满，因为那个消息在墨西哥之后就已经很明确了。”

    三周前在墨西哥，两人在开场圈发生冲突，勒克莱尔因在赛道外超越汉密尔顿被罚五秒。法拉利随后与两位车手进行了沟通。

    “我们在巴西一起聊过，所有人一起。今天我们传达了信息，让他们在并肩比赛时多为车队着想。结果就是这样，”瓦塞尔说道。

    “这对我们所有人来说都极其令人沮丧。”

    Fred Vasseur "more than upset" with "unacceptable" Lewis Hamilton-Charles Leclerc Las Vegas clash

    The Ferrari boss says Charles Leclerc's clash with Lewis Hamilton in Las Vegas came after the team had warned its drivers to think about the team

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Ferrari Formula 1 team principal Fred Vasseur said that Charles Leclerc's clash with Lewis Hamilton at the Las Vegas Grand Prix was "completely unacceptable", and added he was "more than upset" that the team's message to its drivers seems to have been ignored again.

    Leclerc spun Hamilton around while the team-mates fought over sixth place around the streets of Las Vegas. Hamilton dropped to 10th and recovered to finish seventh, while Leclerc was given a 10-second penalty and finished 10th.

    Speaking to Sky Sports after the race, Vasseur said the team had repeated the message it gave after Mexico.

    "Clearly, it's unacceptable. First, a little bit the same message as Mexico, because you have to think about the team, about the people working in Maranello, about all the stakeholders, the sponsors," he said.

    The team chief revealed Ferrari had spoken to Leclerc during the race, explaining that the nature of the Las Vegas track meant its drivers had to focus on the cars around them.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    "We passed the message to Charles to not overtake, because in this kind of race, as you were saying before, you have to overtake the cars in front of you or you are passed by the others. It means that you have to do your race," he continued.

    "And Charles, it's clearly a mistake that is penalising the team a lot. And it's completely unacceptable today. Honestly, I'm more than upset because that message was clear after Mexico."

    The pair clashed on the opening lap in Mexico three weeks ago, when Leclerc was given a five-second penalty for passing Hamilton off the track. Ferrari then held talks with both drivers.

    "We had a chat together, all together, in Brazil. Today we passed the message to think about the team when they were racing together. And the outcome is this one," Vasseur said.

    "It's mega frustrating for all of us."
