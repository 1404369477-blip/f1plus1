    胡肯伯格在美国大奖赛上遭队友恶作剧尴尬

    年轻的车手加布里埃尔·博尔特洛托在美国大奖赛上对队友尼科·胡肯伯格进行了恶作剧，在众多观众面前将胡肯伯格的裤子拉下。

    尼科·胡肯伯格在美国大奖赛上被加布里埃尔·博特洛托恶作剧后颜面扫地

    加布里埃尔·博特洛托在美国大奖赛期间的一次粉丝见面会上玩了一个意外的恶作剧，让他的奥迪车队队友笑得前仰后合

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    年轻的奥迪车手加布里埃尔·博特洛托在美洲赛道前与队友尼科·胡肯伯格走在一起时，对他进行了一次恶作剧。两位车手在奥斯汀观众的欢呼声中走出，巴西人拉下了队友的裤子。

    这个时刻的视频已经迅速走红，展示了胡肯伯格在周五如何走上舞台，当时博尔特洛从后面抓住他的裤子并将其拉下，引发了观众的欢呼和笑声。这位德国人对此玩笑表示宽容，一边笑着一边整理好衣服。

    这个恶作剧为博尔特洛艰难的周末带来了一些轻松。巴西人在周日美国大奖赛将从最后一排发车，因为奥迪为他提供的动力单元组件超出了分配额度。

    博尔特洛和胡肯伯格自2025年以来一直是队友，当时车队还以萨博的身份参赛，尽管车队已被德国巨头奥迪接管，但他们成为了赛场上更为轻松的搭档之一。然而，车队在2026年很难获得积分。车队在车队积分榜上排名第八，积分为23分，胡肯伯格积13分，博尔特洛积10分。

    博尔特洛在美国大奖赛周末赛前表示：“我在可靠性方面看到了很多改进。显然，我们在新加坡显然有一个问题，我们相信我们已经找到了原因。”

    “这显然不是理想的情况，但与此同时，它发生的时候我并没有在前面真正地竞争，所以这让我对它稍微不那么沮丧。但我希望我们能够在接下来的赛季中没有任何重大问题，并且能够以最佳状态完成比赛和排位赛，驾驶我们能够驾驶的最好的汽车，从中提取一切。”

    Nico Hulkenberg left red-faced after Gabriel Bortoleto prank at United States GP

    Gabriel Bortoleto left his Audi team-mate laughing after pulling an unexpected prank during a fan appearance at the United States Grand Prix

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Young Audi driver Gabriel Bortoleto played a prank on his team-mate Nico Hulkenberg as the pair walked out in front of thousands of fans at the Circuit of the Americas. With both drivers walking out to cheers from the Austin crowds, the Brazilian pulled down his team-mate's trousers.

    Videos of this moment have gone incredibly viral, showing Hulkenberg making his way onto the stage on Friday when Bortoleto grabbed his trousers from behind and pulled them down, to loud cheers and laughter from the crowd. The German took the joke in good humour, laughing as he pulled his clothes straight.

    The prank offered some light relief on a tough weekend for Bortoleto. The Brazilian will start Sunday’s United States Grand Prix from the back of the grid, after Audi took him beyond his allocation of power unit components.

    Bortoleto and Hulkenberg have been team-mates since 2025, when the team still raced as Sauber, and have formed one of the more light-hearted partnerships on the grid despite it being taken over by German giant Audi. The outfit has found points hard to come by in 2026, however. The team is eighth in the constructors’ championship on 23 points, with the experienced Hulkenberg on 13 and Bortoleto on 10.

    "I have seen lots of improvement on reliability," Bortoleto said on Thursday ahead of the United States Grand Prix race weekend. "I think we had one [issue] in Singapore obviously, that we believe we found the reason of.

    "It was obviously something not ideal to happen, but at the same time it happened when I was not really fighting at the front, so made me a little bit less upset about it. But I hope we are able to do the rest of the season without having any major issue and that we are able to just complete the races and qualifying in the best condition and the best car possible out there that we can drive and extract everything from it."
