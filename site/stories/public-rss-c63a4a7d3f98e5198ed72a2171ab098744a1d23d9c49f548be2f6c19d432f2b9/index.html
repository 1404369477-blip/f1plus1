    博塔斯通过F1头盔筹款活动为犀牛慈善机构筹集近5万欧元

    博塔斯在阿塞拜疆大奖赛上使用的犀牛头盔筹款活动结束后，揭晓了头盔的获胜者。他通过Instagram宣布了结果，并表示感谢所有人的支持。

    瓦尔特里·博塔斯通过F1头盔筹款活动为犀牛慈善机构筹集了近5万美元

    瓦尔特里·博塔斯宣布了他的比赛用犀牛头盔抽奖活动的获胜者，该活动为犀牛保护慈善机构Care for Wild筹集了资金

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    瓦尔特里·博塔斯在为南非犀牛慈善机构筹款后，揭开了他在阿塞拜疆大奖赛上使用的比赛用犀牛头盔的获胜者

    凯迪拉克车手在Instagram上公布了结果。"我们正式宣布我的比赛头盔获胜者！感谢大家为关爱野生动物的支持，"他写道。"我们将直接联系获胜者，帮助犀牛的资金很快就会上路！并且所有的卡片都已经寄出。"

    这款独一无二的头盔是由博塔斯的搭档、澳大利亚自行车手蒂芙尼·克罗姆威尔设计的。它以犀牛和它们的影子为特色，呈现出黄色和橙色的日落景象，他在9月的巴库世界犀牛日那周戴着它参赛。

    而不是简单的拍卖，粉丝可以购买一张带有一级方程式车手裸照的签名卡片。然后，在比赛结束后，随机选择一位购买者赢得头盔。

    筹集的资金将捐给关爱野生动物，这是一家总部位于南非的慈善机构，运营着世界上最大的犀牛孤儿院。它致力于保护犀牛免受猎杀，并将康复的动物放归野外。

    博塔斯之前也使用过同样的方法。他之前发布了一本裸体日历来为男性健康慈善机构Movember筹集资金和提高意识。

    博塔斯确认，这次活动为Care for Wild筹集了近5万欧元。

    这对博塔斯来说是一个艰难的赛季。在作为梅赛德斯的一号车手储备车手缺席了一年后，37岁的他签约加盟凯迪拉克。这支美国车队于2026年加入赛车网格，作为第11支车队，并一直在努力在锦标赛中确立自己的地位。

    博塔斯和他的队友塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯还没有得分，因此他们在车手锦标赛中排名最后。

    Valtteri Bottas raises almost €50,000 for rhino charity with F1 helmet fundraiser

    Valtteri Bottas has announced the winner of his race-worn rhino helmet giveaway, which raised funds for rhino conservation charity Care for Wild

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Valtteri Bottas has revealed the winner of the race-worn rhino helmet he used at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix after a fundraising drive for a South African rhino charity.

    The Cadillac driver revealed the result on Instagram. "We officially have a winner of my race-worn rhino helmet! Thank you all for your support for Care for Wild," he wrote. "We will contact the winner directly, and the funds to help the rhinos are on their way soon! And all the cards have been posted."

    The one-off helmet was designed by Bottas's partner, Australian cyclist Tiffany Cromwell. It features a yellow and orange sunset with rhinos and their shadows, and he wore it in Baku in September during the week of World Rhino Day.

    Rather than a straightforward auction, fans could buy a signed card featuring a nude photograph of the Formula 1 driver. One of the purchasers was then chosen at random to win the helmet after the race.

    The money raised goes to Care for Wild, a South African-based charity that runs what is described as the world's largest rhino orphanage. It works to protect rhinos from poaching and returns rehabilitated animals to the wild.

    Bottas has used the same approach before. He previously released a nude calendar to raise money and awareness for the men's health charity Movember.

    Bottas confirmed that the campaign has raised almost €50,000 for Care for Wild.

    This comes during a difficult season for Bottas. After spending a year out of a full-time racing seat as a reserve driver for Mercedes, the 37-year-old signed with Cadillac. The American outfit joined the grid in 2026 as the 11th team and has since worked to establish itself in the championship.

    Bottas and team-mate Sergio Perez are yet to score points and, as a result, sit at the bottom of the drivers' championship.
