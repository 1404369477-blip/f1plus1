    诺里斯新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛碰撞被施泰纳批评

    前哈斯车队负责人施泰纳批评诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中的碰撞不是世界冠军所为。

    兰多·诺里斯因"不是世界冠军的行为"被格恩特·斯特尼尔撞上

    格恩特·斯特尼尔批评兰多·诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中与队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里的碰撞

    对于麦凯轮车队的粉丝来说，来自沃金克的帕帕亚新闻，包括关于车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的每一场比赛的最新消息。

    前哈斯一级方程式车队负责人格恩特·斯特尼尔表示，2025年冠军兰多·诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛冲刺赛中与队友麦凯轮的碰撞"不是世界冠军的行为"。

    诺里斯和皮亚斯特里在冲刺赛的最后一圈发生了接触。当时诺里斯排在第四和第五位，他试图在内线超越队友，但结果撞到了皮亚斯特里，导致两人都发生了打转。

    因此，皮亚斯特里退出了比赛，而诺里斯设法继续。这位英国车手以第八名的成绩获得了积分。

    "你真的不应该这么做。这不是世界冠军级别的操作，" 斯泰纳在《红旗播客》节目中说。

    斯泰纳赞扬了麦凯轮车队负责人安德里亚·斯特拉对这一事件的处理。

    “安德烈亚在这种情况下表现得非常好，”前车队负责人补充道。 “我认为他让他觉得自己像个废物。他向他解释说这种情况不会发生。我不认为诺里斯[道歉]是出于政治原因。 [诺里斯]得出结论，他做的实际上很愚蠢……没有必要，尤其是之后的反应。”

    兰多·诺里斯，麦克拉伦，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，麦克拉伦

    “因为当你撞上你的队友时……可能会发生这种情况。但他的反应，因为那不是兰多。他意识到他有点太自大。”

    虽然诺里斯最初声称“有很大的空隙，我冲过去了，我有权那么做，”但他后来为这次事件道歉。

    “我无法尝试我之前做的事情，我一开始就不应该尝试，即使那个动作是成功的，”诺里斯在大奖赛结束后告诉F1.com。

    “我必须更多地考虑我所代表的团队，而不是我自己。这是我的错误，我让团队失望了。我也向他们所有人道歉，我欠他们一个道歉，我们下次会做得更好。”

    在冲刺赛中只获得一分后，诺里斯和皮亚斯特里在大奖赛中分别获得第四和第五名，各自获得了22分。

    Lando Norris slammed by Guenther Steiner for "not a world champion move"

    Guenther Steiner criticised Lando Norris for his collision with McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri during the Singapore GP sprint race

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Former Haas Formula 1 team principal Guenther Steiner says 2025 champion Lando Norris's crash with his McLaren team-mate during the Singapore Grand Prix sprint race was "not a world champion move".

    Norris and Piastri made contact on the last lap of the sprint race. While running in fourth and fifth, Norris went for a move down the inside of his team-mate but ended up clipping Piastri, sending both of them into a spin.

    As a result, Piastri was out of the race while Norris managed to continue. The Briton picked up one point with an eighth-place finish.

    "You really shouldn't be doing this. This was not a world champion move," Steiner said during an appearance on The Red Flags Podcast.

    Steiner praised McLaren team principal Andrea Stella's management of the situation.

    "Andrea in these situations is very good," the former team chief added. "I think he made him feel a w***er. He explained to him that this is not happening. I don't think Norris [apologised] for a political reason. [Norris] came to the conclusion that [it] was actually pretty stupid what he did... There was no need for it, especially the reaction afterwards.

    Lando Norris, McLaren, Oscar Piastri, McLaren

    "Because when you crash into your team-mate... it can happen. But the reaction from him, because that is not Lando. He realised he was a little bit too cocky."

    While Norris initially claimed "it was a big gap, I went for it, I have the right to do that," he later apologised for the incident.

    "I can't attempt what I did, I should never have attempted it in the first place, even if the move was on," Norris told F1.com after the grand prix.

    "I simply have to think more about the team that I represent more than myself. It was a mistake on my part, I let down the team. I've also apologised to all of them and I owe them all an apology and we'll do better next time."

    After only picking up one point in the sprint race, Norris and Piastri scored 22 points in the grand prix after finishing fourth and fifth, respectively.
