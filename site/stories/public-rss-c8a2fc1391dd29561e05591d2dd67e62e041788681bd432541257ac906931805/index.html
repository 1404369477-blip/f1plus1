    小红牛确认劳森2027赛季留队

    小红牛宣布2027赛季车手阵容不变，劳森将继续与林德布拉德搭档。

    小红牛宣布2027赛季F1车手阵容不变，利亚姆·劳森下赛季将继续留在车队。

    这位新西兰车手的未来此前数周一直不确定，这家红牛旗下的车队曾考虑是让他继续与阿尔维德·林德布拉德搭档，还是提拔青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫。

    不过，正如本周早些时候所报道的，劳森近期在墨西哥和巴西的强劲表现被认为让他走上了获得新合同的道路，并让小红牛确信他是最佳人选。

    劳森目前位居车手积分榜第九，是前四支车队之外的最佳车手，他的贡献对小红牛位居车队积分榜第五至关重要，放弃他本会是不合理的。

    小红牛车队领队艾伦·珀曼认为，劳森和林德布拉德的阵容在融合年轻与经验方面堪称完美。

    “两人在整个赛季中都展现了各自的能力和潜力，我们也看到了他们取得的进步，无论是个人还是作为搭档，”珀曼解释道。

    “我毫不怀疑这是对我们而言最好的车手阵容，带来了经验与雄心的强强组合。我们将携手在今年的进展基础上夯实基础，继续推动自己前进，迈出下一步，朝着在2027年实现我们目标的方向努力。”

    劳森今年在围场中因其在小红牛以及顶替受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔代表红牛出战时的出色表现而赢得赞誉。

    他感谢了小红牛（小红牛）对他的信任。

    “我们共同度过了一个出色的赛季，车队非常努力地打造了一辆快车，我很喜欢驾驶它，”他说。

    “能成为车队历史性赛季的一部分感觉很棒，我们继续为制造商锦标赛第五名而战。

    “这证明了赛道现场和工厂后方每个人付出的辛勤努力。我期待与阿维德一起将这股势头保持到2027年；我们正在建设非常积极的东西，我对我们能共同取得的成就感到兴奋。”

    小红牛（小红牛）在宣布劳森和林德布拉德留队至2027年的声明中，并未提及对措洛夫的安排，而措洛夫今年正力争拿下F2年度冠军。

    这位保加利亚车手在墨西哥为小红牛（小红牛）完成了一次新秀一练，2027年很可能在红牛承担更重要的角色。他也被传可能转战日本超级方程式。

    小红牛（小红牛）：利亚姆·劳森/阿维德·林德布拉德

    阿斯顿·马丁：费尔南多·阿隆索/兰斯·斯特罗尔

    英文原文

    Liam Lawson will remain at Racing Bulls next season after the squad announced an unchanged Formula 1 line-up for 2027.

    The New Zealander’s future had been uncertain for many weeks, with the Red Bull-owned outfit pondering whether to keep him on alongside Arvid Lindblad or promote its junior driver Nikola Tsolov.

    However, as reported earlier this week, Lawson’s recent strong performances in Mexico and Brazil were understood to have put him on course for a new deal and convinced Racing Bulls he was the best candidate.

    With Lawson currently sitting ninth in the drivers’ standings, as best of the rest behind the top four teams, and his contribution key to helping Racing Bulls sit fifth in the constructors’ championship, it would have been nonsensical to have dropped him.

    Racing Bulls team principal Alan Permane believed that the line-up of Lawson and Lindblad was the perfect one in terms of blending youth and experience.

    “Both have demonstrated their ability and potential throughout the season, and we’ve seen the progress they’ve made, both individually and as a pairing,” explained Permane.

    “I have no doubt that this is the best driver line-up for us, bringing a strong combination of experience and ambition. Together, we have a solid foundation to build on the progress made this year, continue pushing ourselves forward and take the next step towards achieving our ambitions in 2027."

    Lawson has earned praise in the paddock this year for his standout performances for both Racing Bulls and during a stand-in run at Red Bull, when he filled in for the injured Isack Hadjar.

    He thanked Racing Bulls for the belief in him.

    “We've had a brilliant season together and the team has worked very hard to produce a fast car, which I've loved driving,” he said.

    “It feels great to be part of a historic season for the team as we continue our fight for P5 in the constructors' championship.

    "It's a testament to the hard work of everyone both at track, and back at the factory. I’m looking forward to carrying this momentum forward with Arvid through to 2027; we're building something super positive and I’m excited by what we can achieve together.”

    The Racing Bulls statement that announced Lawson and Lindblad were staying for 2027 did not make any reference to plans for Tsolov, who is in the hunt to clinch the F2 crown this year.

    The Bulgarian had a rookie FP1 run for Racing Bulls in Mexico, and will likely have a wider role with Red Bull in 2027. He has also been linked with a switch to Super Formula in Japan.

    Racing Bulls: Liam Lawson/Arvid Lindblad

    Aston Martin: Fernando Alonso/Lance Stroll
