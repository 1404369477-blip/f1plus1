    凯迪拉克续约博塔斯而非赫塔的决定解读

    凯迪拉克决定续约博塔斯至2027年，尽管赫塔已获得F1超级执照资格。本文分析了这一决定。

    凯迪拉克已经结束了瓦尔特里·博塔斯F1未来不确定性的问题，尽管他们已经确保科尔顿·赫塔获得了F1超级驾照。

    这是我们对这个决定的看法。

    忘记性能差距，博塔斯有价值

    重要的是要记住博塔斯最初被签下的原因。他带来了经验，与队友塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯有良好的默契，并作为一个重要的基石，对于一个仍不是一个完全成熟的F1车队，正处于组建过程中的车队来说至关重要。

    F1车队的复杂性巨大，从外面看是看不见的。这就是为什么博塔斯的贡献远远超过了与佩雷斯相比的表现。

    佩雷斯是这个赛季在赛道上更令人印象深刻的凯迪拉克车手，这一点毫无疑问。然而，由于汽车之间的差异和目标的不同，往往无法进行公平的比较。博塔斯是一个团队球员，并且一直在完成他所被要求的工作。

    我理解为什么人们觉得这是一个无聊的选择，我很想看看赫塔在赛车座位上的表现如何，但决策应该基于车手能为车队带来什么。

    而现在，正是这种看不见的贡献让博塔斯比一个新手更实际——无论替代方案可能多么有趣。

    做理智和正确的事情。

    在这个时候放弃博塔斯不仅代价高昂，而且在我看来，现在提升赫塔——除了他是一名美国车手之外——的好处也不存在。

    尽管凯迪拉克车队仍然扎根在车尾，需要经验来推动车队前进，赫塔却有可能被这种状况吞噬。

    这样，赫塔为自己的F1超级驾照存档，并获得更多时间为未来的赛车席位做准备，而车队则继续利用博塔斯丰富的知识和经验。

    凯迪拉克已经与佩雷斯签订了新的多年合约，而博塔斯仅确认到2027年，这清楚地表明了车队的发展方向，但过早地做出决定仍然是有意义的。

    就在赫塔首次被严重怀疑将取代凯迪拉克的博塔斯的时候，在10月份，很多人认为他在F2的表现无关紧要，并指出其他车手已经证明了这一点。但这只是故事的一半。

    是的，像奥利·贝尔曼和基米·安东内利这样的车手虽然F1成绩不佳，但成功晋级，尽管对于安东内利来说，从区域方程式赛车跳到F2赛车有点像从橡皮艇的船长变成航空母舰的指挥官。

    这两位车手之所以能在F1取得成功，尽管他们在F2的表现不尽如人意，是因为他们都说服了非常强大的车队，他们有能力在F1中比赛。

    无论凯迪拉克坚持与博塔斯合作的理由是什么，赫塔并没有给他留下同样的印象，以至于他对于2027年来说是不可否认的，尽管现在已经有文件表明这成为可能。

    他仍然有机会实现这一点。诚然，鉴于F2的糟糕成绩和凯迪拉克选择目前并不令人印象深刻的博塔斯而不是他，前景并不乐观。

    但这也可能只是凯迪拉克为赫塔争取更多时间。

    这意味着他很可能在有机会获得全职F1席位时已经28岁了。这会让人感觉非常'90年代'，我对此表示支持。

    他明年能在更整齐的凯迪拉克车队中争取到在练习赛中留下深刻印象的机会，那将很不错，他可以做一些更多的性能测试来展示他的才华，而不仅仅是像这支新队在2026年所需的基础工作。

    2028年一直是赫塔的计划吗？

    现在凯迪拉克坚持了博塔斯，这可能过于受事后之明的影响，但我确实想知道赫塔/2027年是否曾是他们的计划 - 以及这真的是在权衡他是否是2028年的下一位车手。

    赫塔的超驾照故事被广泛记录，跨越了几个赛季。我记得第一次在2021年土耳其大奖赛周末写关于它，并在伊斯坦布尔的维修区追逐它！

    关于F1超级驾照的一个有趣的事情是，它非常难获得，但一旦你有了它，即使你不是一名活跃的F1车手，也很容易继续获得。

    所以，考虑到赫塔跳过和跑过的障碍和圈套，凯迪拉克认为现在是时候获得超级驾照了，因为它延长了让他进入赛车的时间窗口。当然，可能是2027年，但也可能是之后的几年。

    目前看来，仅凭表现就足以让赫塔加入的情况并不令人震惊。所以，遵守博塔斯的合同，以及他签约的原因——为2028年对他进行适当的准备和评估，知道没有监管上的胡说八道需要克服才能让他进入赛车。这仅仅关乎他是否足够优秀。

    英文原文

    Cadillac has ended the uncertainty over Valtteri Bottas’s Formula 1 future by committing to him for 2027, despite having made sure Colton Herta qualified for an F1 superlicence.

    Here’s our take on the decision.

    Forget the performance gap, Bottas has value

    It's important to remember the reasons Bottas was signed in the first place. He brings experience, good cohesion with team-mate Sergio Perez and acts as an important building block for a squad that is still not a fully-formed F1 team and in its building-up process.

    The complexity of F1 teams is enormous and invisible from the outside. That's why Bottas's contribution is measured by far more than his performances relative to Perez.

    Perez has been the more impressive Cadillac driver this season on track, there's no doubt about that. However, often a fair comparison has been impossible given differences between the cars and the objectives. Bottas is a team player and has been doing the job asked of him.

    I can understand why people find it a boring choice, and I'd be fascinated to see how Herta fares in a race seat, but the decision should be made on what the drivers bring to the team.

    And right now, it's that invisible contribution that makes Bottas a more pragmatic option than a rookie - no matter how interesting the alternative might be.

    The sensible and correct thing to do.

    Not only would it have been needlessly expensive to ditch Bottas at this point, the upside of promoting Herta now - beyond the fact he's an American driver - just isn't there, as far as I can see.

    While Cadillac remains a team rooted to the back of the grid, in need of experience to drive it forward, Herta risks getting swallowed up by that.

    This way, Herta banks his F1 superlicence and gets more time to prepare himself for a future race seat, while the team continues milking Bottas for his vast nous and experience.

    The fact Cadillac has Perez signed up to a new multi-year deal, while Bottas is only confirmed for 2027, tells you clearly the direction of travel, but it still makes sense not to pull that trigger too early.

    Right when Herta was first seriously linked to replacing Bottas at Cadillac, in October, there were plenty of people suggesting that his F2 form was irrelevant and that other drivers prove it. But that only told half the story.

    Yes, the likes of Ollie Bearman and Kimi Antonelli managed to graduate to F1 with relatively poor results, although for Antonelli, jumping from a Formula Regional car to an F2 car was a bit like going from the captain of a rubber dinghy to the commander in charge of an aircraft carrier.

    The reason both of those drivers succeeded in F1 where they'd 'failed' in F2, is that both of them convinced very big teams that they were capable of racing in F1, regardless of underwhelming F2 form.

    Whatever Cadillac's reasoning for sticking with Bottas, Herta hasn't quite made the same impression to make him undeniable for 2027, even though the paperwork now exists to make that a possibility.

    He still has a chance to make it. Admittedly, given the poor F2 results and Cadillac opting for a currently-underwhelming Bottas over him, things don't look great.

    But this also might just be Cadillac buying a bit more time for Herta.

    That does mean he's likely to be 28 by the time he has a chance at a full-time F1 seat. That would feel very '1990s' and I'm here for it.

    And it would be nice for him to get the chance to impress in practice shots in a more sorted Cadillac next year, where he can do a bit more performance running to show off his talent, rather than just the baseline work this new team needed in 2026.

    Was 2028 always the Herta plan?

    This could be too influenced by hindsight, now that Cadillac has stuck with Bottas, but I do wonder if Herta/2027 was ever the play – and if it's really about weighing up whether he's the next guy in 2028.

    Herta's superlicence saga is well documented and spans several seasons. I remember first writing about it during the 2021 Turkish Grand Prix weekend and chasing it in the Istanbul paddock!

    A funny thing about the F1 superlicence is it's very hard to get but once you have it, it's easy to keep getting one even when you're not an active F1 driver.

    So, given the hurdles and hoops Herta's been jumping over and running through, it stands to reason that Cadillac thought it was now or never to get the superlicence, knowing it extended the window to put him in the car. It could have been 2027, sure, but could be the years after.

    And right now it doesn't look like there's a stunning case to put Herta in on performance alone. So, honour Bottas's deal, and the reasons he was signed - and prepare and assess Herta properly for 2028 knowing that there's not a regulatory nonsense to overcome to put him in the car. It'll just be about whether he's good enough.
