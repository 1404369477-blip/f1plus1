    小红牛疑似2.7亿欧元合同曝光，维斯塔潘拍摄现场照片流出

    小红牛车手维斯塔潘的拍摄现场照片流出，疑似曝光了其与车队的新合同。

    红牛车队传闻的2.7亿欧元合同在泄露的马克斯·维斯塔潘照片中成形

    泄露的照片显示马克斯·维斯塔潘身穿阿迪达斯装备在小红牛22赛车上，进一步证实了每年2.7百万欧元的红牛合同传闻

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析

    一次商业拍摄意外地加剧了目前在F1车队中最大的场外传闻

    泄露的照片在社交媒体上流传，显示马克斯·维斯塔潘随意地靠在小红牛22赛车的前左轮上，身上穿着预计将发布的未上市车队装备。红牛明星的胸前和肩膀上清晰地展示着标志性的阿迪达斯标志和三条杠设计，完全取代了目前的Castore品牌。

    品牌大换血不仅限于服装。仔细观察泄露的套图可以发现，一个显眼的白色阿迪达斯标志直接印在小红牛22的侧面。

    关于德国运动巨头窃取米尔顿凯恩斯车队的第一手消息在今年初引起了广泛关注。BILD的初步报道表明，这两大巨头正在协商一份价值巨大的商品和服装合同，该合同将于2027赛季生效。

    传闻中的交易金额令人震惊。据报道，该协议每年价值约2700万欧元，将赛车队锁定在三条杠生态系统中至少三个赛季。

    接管红牛的盈利性商品运营将是阿迪达斯在赛车运动扩张中的一次重大升级。这个服装品牌已经深度渗透到赛车阵容中，并现在正打算将这家奥地利巨头添加到不断增长的F1组合中，该组合包括梅赛德斯-奔驰和奥迪。

    尽管明星车手被摄像头捕捉到穿着实物装备，但赛车服装和运动服高管们还没有发布官方新闻稿确认2027年的转变。直到公开签署，Castore仍然是官方供应商。

    Red Bull's rumoured €27million deal takes shape in leaked Max Verstappen shoot

    Leaked photos show Max Verstappen wearing Adidas gear on his RB22, fueling rumours of a €27M per year Red Bull deal

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    A commercial production shoot just accidentally poured gasoline on one of the biggest off-track rumours in the Formula 1 paddock right now.

    Leaked photographs circulating across social media show Max Verstappen casually resting on the front left wheel of his RB22 while sporting what is expected to be an unreleased team kit. Displayed crystal clear on the right side of the Red Bull star's chest and across his shoulders is the iconic Adidas logo and three-stripe design, completely replacing the current Castore branding.

    The branding overhaul wasn't limited to the apparel. A closer look at the leaked set photos reveals a prominent white Adidas logo stamped directly onto the side of the RB22.

    Whispers of the German sportswear juggernaut poaching the Milton Keynes squad first gained serious traction earlier this year. Initial reports from BILD indicated that the two powerhouses were negotiating a massive merchandise and apparel contract set to take effect for the 2027 F1 season.

    The financials of the rumoured deal is staggering. The agreement is reportedly worth approximately €27 million per year, locking the racing outfit into the three-stripe ecosystem for at least three seasons.

    Taking over Red Bull's lucrative merchandise operations would serve as a massive escalation in Adidas's aggressive motorsport expansion. The apparel brand has already heavily infiltrated the grid and is now looking to add the Austrian powerhouse to a growing F1 portfolio that includes Mercedes and Audi.

    While the star driver was caught on camera wearing the physical gear, neither the racing outfit nor the sportswear executives have issued an official press release confirming the 2027 switch. Until the ink is publicly dry, Castore remains the official supplier.
