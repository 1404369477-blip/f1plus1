    安东内利预计拉斯维加斯站将再现“溜溜球”式比赛

    F1世界冠军安东内利预测，本周末拉斯维加斯大奖赛因低下压力特性将再现“溜溜球”式比赛。2026年新规引入更偏电动的动力单元，已在部分赛道造成截然不同的比赛风格，被称为“溜溜球”。

    安东内利：溜溜球式比赛将在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛回归

    这位2026年F1世界冠军认为，本周末将见证备受争议的溜溜球式比赛回归

    F1世界冠军安东内利预测，本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛因低下压力特性将再现“溜溜球”式比赛。

    2026年新规引入更偏电动的动力单元，已在部分赛道造成截然不同的比赛风格，被称为“溜溜球”。

    这就是赛车可以借助电池的额外动力轻松超车的地方，但随后它们会因能量耗尽而无法拉开差距，甚至无法保持位置。

    这种情况大多出现在低下压力赛道上，9月的蒙扎就是一个典型例子，因为其长直道上有巨大的尾流效应，而能量回收的地点又较少。

    本周末在拉斯维加斯可能再次出现这种情况，这条街道赛道单圈有17个弯角，却拥有赛历上最长的一条直道之一，几乎没有喘息之机。

    安东内利说：“说实话，能量部署并不是最好的，尤其是在那条长长的后直道上，这并不轻松。”

    “但对每个人来说都是如此。这会在各种地方创造大量超车机会。

    “因为有很多长直道，也有很多以不同方式使用电池的机会，我们大概会看到一场像溜溜球一样来回拉锯的比赛。

    “这并不容易，对我们所有人来说都不会轻松，尤其是天气这么冷，你需要在每个地方都尽可能快，才能让轮胎建立起温度。”

    安东内利认为，拉斯维加斯这场比赛独特的条件带来了额外挑战，气温可能低至4摄氏度，这意味着轮胎性能始终至关重要。

    这应该会导致一场混乱的排位赛，车手还必须管理赛道演变，因为赛道在各节练习与排位之间重新开放通行，导致尘土重新落回沙漠表面。

    “我认为这将是那种你会看到我们做多圈暖胎圈、然后才开始飞驰圈的场合之一，”这位2026年世界冠军补充道。

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，2025年拉斯维加斯站

    “直接出场就立刻推进是不可能的，因为今年在我们去过的任何赛道上，让轮胎升温都已经更加困难了。

    “暖胎变得更困难了。我认为在这里，这方面还会更进一步。

    “所以是的，这就是为什么我认为这将是那种可能成为异常值的周末之一，因为我认为只要在开始飞驰圈时让轮胎处于正确的工作窗口，就会是关键。”

    因此，这样的条件可能有利于梅赛德斯，该车队在2026年的大部分时间里一直拥有最高效的动力单元，这在一定程度上解释了安东内利在蒙扎从第19位冲到第一名的表现。

    但红牛是状态正佳的车队，马克斯·维斯塔潘连续八次登上领奖台，其中包括三场胜利，在此期间与安东内利并列获胜次数最多。

    安东内利表示：“我认为红牛本周末会非常强劲。不过，我觉得我们是唯一一支拥有一套下压力略低、阻力也稍小的尾翼的车队。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯

    “所以这会很有意思。让我们看看我们是否会在这里使用它，但肯定会非常有趣。”

    “我认为我们的动力单元在电动方面一直非常强劲，显然在燃烧方面也是如此，但电动方面一直非常强劲。”

    “在电力和效率方面，我们可能是最高效的。不过，红牛同样拥有一套非常强劲的动力单元，他们的赛车在直道上非常高效。”

    “我认为本周末我们的主要竞争对手会是红牛，可能还有迈凯伦。我们也看看法拉利会表现如何。”

    Kimi Antonelli: Yo-yo racing to return at F1 Las Vegas GP

    The 2026 F1 world champion reckons this weekend will witness the return of the contentious yo-yo racing

    Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli has predicted the return of “yo-yo” racing at this weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix thanks to its low-downforce characteristics.

    The new-for-2026 regulations, which feature a more electrical power unit, have caused a radically different style of racing at certain circuits with it being labelled as ‘yo-yo’.

    This is where cars can easily overtake using extra power from the battery, but are subsequently unable to pull clear - or even maintain position - as they lose energy later on.

    It is mostly witnessed at low-downforce tracks with Monza in September being a particular example, thanks to the huge tow on its long straights and fewer places to harvest.

    That could be seen again this weekend in Las Vegas, which boasts one of the longest straights on the calendar with very little respite across the 17-corner lap of the street circuit.

    Antonelli said: “Deployment, it's not the best, not going to lie, and especially with the long back straight, it's not the easiest.

    “But it's like this for everyone. That will create a lot of opportunities to overtake in all kinds of places.

    “Probably we can expect a yo-yo race because of so many long straights and so many opportunities to use the battery differently.

    “It's not easy and it's not going to be straightforward for all of us and especially because it's so cold, you need as much speed as possible everywhere to build any temperature.”

    Antonelli reckons those unique conditions of the Vegas race presents an extra challenge, with temperatures potentially falling as low as 4C meaning tyre performance is ever important.

    This should cause a hectic qualifying where track evolution must also be managed, given the circuit reopens to traffic between sessions causing dust to settle back onto the desert surface.

    “I think it's going to be one of those occasions where you will see us doing multiple warm-up laps just to then start the push lap,” added the 2026 world champion.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Vegas 2025

    “Going out and push straight away is not going to be possible, because already this year has been more difficult to build temperature at any tracks we've been.

    “It's been more difficult to warm up. I think here is going to be an even step further on that side.

    “So yeah, that's why I think it's going to be one of those weekends that could be an outlier, because I think just by having the tyres in the right window when starting a push lap, it’s going to be key.”

    Such conditions could therefore favour Mercedes, which for much of 2026 has enjoyed the most-efficient power unit, partly explaining Antonelli’s charge from 19th to first in Monza.

    But Red Bull is the team in form with Max Verstappen on a podium streak of eight including three victories, equalling Antonelli for the most wins during that period.

    “I think Red Bull is going to be very strong this weekend,” said Antonelli. “However, I think we're the only one who have a set of wings that is slightly less downforce-wise, so a bit less dragging.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    “So it's going to be interesting. Let's see if we're going to use it here, but definitely it's going to be very interesting.

    “I think our power unit has been very strong on the electrical side, obviously also on the combustion, but the electrical side has been very strong.

    “We're probably the most efficient ones in terms of electrical power and efficiency. However, Red Bull as well, they have a very strong power unit and their car is very efficient on the straight.

    “I think our main competitor will be Red Bull this weekend, probably McLaren as well. Let's also see Ferrari, how they're going to do.”
