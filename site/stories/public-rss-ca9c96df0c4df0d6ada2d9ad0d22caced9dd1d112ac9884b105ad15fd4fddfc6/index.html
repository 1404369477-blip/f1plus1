    梅赛德斯F1车队负责人托托·沃尔夫驳回了前红牛赛车运动顾问赫尔穆特·马克奥的声称，乔治·拉塞尔永远不会再次获得F1冠军的机会

    拉塞尔目前在车手积分榜上落后队友、当前车手冠军领跑者安东内利123分。

    在美国赛车场举行的美国大奖赛前夕，马克在接受天空体育德国采访时表示，安东内利已经取代了Brackley车队的主车手位置。

    “拉塞尔不再是一号车手。安东内利很显然已经取代了他的位置。但拉塞尔是顶级车手之一，”马克说。 “我怀疑他是否还有机会再次获得世界冠军或者在任何地方获得一号车手的位置。”

    在奥斯汀的比赛后，沃尔夫表示马克的观点是错误的。

    “我从赫尔穆特开始，我的意思是，他是我的奥地利人，不是那么亲密的朋友，但我尊重他的意见。但我认为他在乔治的问题上是错的。乔治拥有所有的能力。我认为这辆车不适合他。”

    “他还补充说：'我认为在赛季初期，面对这种情况可能很困难，那就是我觉得这是我的年份，然后我们这边有一个天才少年开始真正地削弱我们的优势。我相信，乔治，如果我们给他一辆快速的车，新赛季是完全可能的。”

    “他拥有赢得冠军的一切所需。所以我认为说让我们忘记他在F2和F3中赢得的所有这些年份，他的新秀赛季，还有他在卡丁车中的连续欧洲锦标赛，这些都还在那里，是有点太容易了。”

    George Russell F1 title chances defended by Toto Wolff after Helmut Marko comments

    Mercedes team chief Toto Wolff has defended George Russell's Formula 1 title prospects

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes Formula 1 team chief Toto Wolff has rejected former Red Bull motorsport adviser Helmut Marko's claim that George Russell will never get another shot at the F1 title.

    Russell currently sits 123 points behind his team-mate and current championship leader Kimi Antonelli in the drivers' standings.

    While speaking to Sky Sports Deutschland ahead of the United States Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas, Marko said Antonelli had taken over as the Brackley outfit's lead driver.

    "Russell is no longer the number one driver. Antonelli has quite clearly taken that spot from him. But Russell is one of the top drivers," Marko said. "I doubt whether he’ll ever have another chance at the world title or be given number one status anywhere."

    Asked about the comments after the race in Austin, Wolff said Marko had got it wrong.

    "I start with Helmut, I mean, he’s my Austrian, not-so-buddy, but I respect his opinion. But I think he’s wrong on George. George has all the ability. I think the car doesn’t suit him."

    He added: "I think that at the beginning of the season, it is maybe difficult to cope with the situation where you’re feeling that this is my year to come, and then we have this wonderkid on the other side that starts to really chip away. And I believe that a new season, George, if we provide him with a quick car, is absolutely possible.

    "He has absolutely what it needs to win a championship. So I think it's a bit too easy to say, let's forget about all these years where he won in F2 and F3, his rookie years and the European championships back-to-back in karting, that's still all there."

    The 19th round of the 2026 season, the Mexican Grand Prix, is scheduled for 30 October to 1 November.
