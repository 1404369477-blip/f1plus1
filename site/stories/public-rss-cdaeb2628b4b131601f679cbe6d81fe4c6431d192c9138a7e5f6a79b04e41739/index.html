    安东内利如何成为墨西哥大奖赛F1世界冠军？

    安东内利在周日美国大奖赛中从杆位发车，统治了比赛，赢得了本赛季的第九场胜利，使他几乎锁定世界冠军。

    基米·安东内利如何在墨西哥大奖赛成为F1世界冠军？

    梅赛德斯车手有望赢得他个人的F1冠军

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    基米·安东内利通过在周日的美国大奖赛中从杆位发车并统治比赛，又迈出了成为一级方程式世界冠军的重要一步。

    梅赛德斯车手因此赢得了2026赛季的第九场胜利，这让他一只手已经触及了冠军奖杯——尽管这已经有一段时间了。

    那么，他什么时候能完成这一壮举，成为F1历史上最年轻的世界冠军呢？

    有多少车手还能赢得2026年的F1冠军？

    在奥斯汀赛前，有六位数学上的竞争者——安东内利、乔治·拉塞尔、刘易斯·汉密尔顿、马克斯·维斯塔潘、查尔斯·勒克莱尔和兰多·诺里斯——因为139分差距，还有150分在德克萨斯州之前的比赛中可用。

    其中三人——维斯塔潘、勒克莱尔和诺里斯——现在已经退出了竞争，因为他们分别获得了第三、第二和第五名。虽然都是稳定的表现，但这只是表明他们获得世界冠军的可能性非常小，因为这意味着他们的希望已经结束。

    安东内利要想在美洲赛道成为冠军，他需要赢得比赛，而拉塞尔排名第九或以下，汉密尔顿排名第五。但鉴于他的梅赛德斯车队队友排名第八，而他接替的银箭车队车手排名第四，比赛将继续到下个周末。

    安东内利如何在墨西哥大奖赛成为世界冠军

    在2026赛季还剩下125个积分可用，剩余五轮比赛——墨西哥、圣保罗、拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔和阿布扎比——前提是中东大奖赛在冲突中得以进行。

    拉塞尔现在落后安东内利123分，汉密尔顿落后124分，因此这位20岁的意大利人几乎可以确定将在赫曼诺斯·罗德里格斯赛道夺得冠军。在那场比赛之后，还剩下100分，但其他两位车手必须落后安东内利99分或更少，因为本赛季冠军领跑者以较大的优势赢得了最多的胜利——所以他将在积分平局中获胜。

    这意味着他们两人必须分别比安东内利多出24分和25分。因此，对于拉塞尔来说，这意味着他必须获胜，而他的队友成绩不能高于第10名，而对于汉密尔顿来说，他也必须获胜，但安东内利不能获得任何积分。

    How will Kimi Antonelli become F1 world champion at Mexico GP?

    The Mercedes driver is set to win his maiden F1 crown

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    Kimi Antonelli has taken another giant step towards becoming Formula 1 world champion by dominating the United States Grand Prix from pole on Sunday.

    The Mercedes driver consequently took his ninth victory of the 2026 season and it leaves him with one hand on the championship trophy - though it has been for a while.

    So, when will he do it and become F1’s youngest ever world champion?

    How many drivers can still win the F1 2026 title?

    There were six mathematical contenders heading into Austin - Antonelli, George Russell, Lewis Hamilton, Max Verstappen, Charles Leclerc and Lando Norris - as 139 points separated the drivers with 150 left available pre-Texas.

    Three of those - Verstappen, Leclerc and Norris - have now dropped out of contention though, due to them finishing third, second and fifth respectively. All solid results, but it just shows how unlikely world championship glory was for them as it meant their hopes had ended.

    For Antonelli to become champion at Circuit of the Americas, he needed to win with Russell finishing ninth or below and Hamilton taking fifth. But as his Mercedes team-mate took eighth and the Ferrari driver, who he succeeded at the Silver Arrows, finished fourth, the fight goes on to next weekend.

    How Antonelli becomes world champion at Mexico GP

    There are now 125 points left available in the 2026 campaign with five rounds remaining - Mexico, Sao Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi - provided the Middle Eastern grands prix go ahead amid the conflict.

    Russell is now 123 points behind Antonelli and Hamilton is 124, so the 20-year-old Italian is all-but certain to take the crown at Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez. After that race there will be 100 points left on the table, but the other two must be behind Antonelli by 99 or fewer, due to the championship leader having the most wins this season by a comfortable margin - so he would win it on countback.

    This means both must outscore Antonelli by 24 and 25 points respectively. So for Russell, that entails him winning and his team-mate finishing no higher than 10th, while for Hamilton, he must also take victory but Antonelli not take any points.
