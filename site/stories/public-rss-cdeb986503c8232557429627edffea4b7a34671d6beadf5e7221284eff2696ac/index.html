    法拉利发展车手透露已确认的F1测试计划

    萨沙·费内斯特拉兹今夏从梅赛德斯转投法拉利，担任模拟器与发展车手，他表示法拉利已承诺在2027年为他安排一次F1测试。

    法拉利发展车手透露已确认的F1测试计划

    萨沙·费内斯特拉兹透露，他从梅赛德斯转投法拉利后，法拉利已承诺在2027年为他安排一次F1测试。

    萨沙·费内斯特拉兹今夏加入法拉利，担任模拟器与发展车手。他表示，这支意大利车队已承诺在2027年为他安排一次F1测试。此前，他从梅赛德斯位于布拉克利的总部转至法拉利位于马拉内罗的总部。

    他在Super Formula富士站比赛周末接受Grande Premio采访时，确认了明年的驾驶安排。

    “事实是，能在那里工作是一个梦想，能和赛车运动里这么响亮的名字共事也是一个梦想，”他说。“工作很棒；这有点像我之前在布拉克利为梅赛德斯做的事，只不过现在我能吃到意大利的美味意面，还能在模拟器里驾驶法拉利F1赛车，明年可能还会参加几次测试，所以这真的是一个很棒的机会。

    “明年我会参加一次测试，我已经知道了，他们也确认了。我本来下周就该去那里，但因为我在奥托波利斯有超级GT比赛，所以去不了，唉，算了，总会成行的。”

    法拉利车队尚未确认此事，而这位阿根廷人表示，其目的是让他成为更好的模拟器车手。

    “我真的很期待，但显然对他们来说最重要的是让我坐进车里，以提高我在模拟器中的表现。所有模拟器车手都必须驾驶旧款赛车测试（TPC）用车，以积累F1赛车的经验，所以我只能等待那一天的到来。”

    费内斯特拉兹迄今为止在日本拥有成功的赛车生涯，曾赢得2019年全日本三级方程式锦标赛冠军，并参加过超级方程式和超级GT赛事。他还曾随日产车队征战电动方程式。他在2022年和2023年的锦标赛中均获得第16名。

    在法拉利，迪诺·贝加诺维奇将在2027年成为储备车手，与安东尼奥·吉奥维纳齐一同任职，此前他已离开该车队的车手学院。

    Ferrari development driver reveals confirmed F1 test plans

    Sacha Fenestraz has revealed that Ferrari has promised him a Formula 1 test in 2027 after he joined the Italian team from Mercedes

    After joining Ferrari as a simulator and development driver this summer, Sacha Fenestraz has said that the Italian team has promised him a Formula 1 test in 2027. This comes after the driver moved from Mercedes' Brackley headquarters to Ferrari's in Maranello.

    Speaking to Grande Premio during the Fuji race weekend for Super Formula, he confirmed his drive next year.

    “The truth is, it’s a dream to be able to work there, and it’s a dream to be with such a big name in motorsport," he said. "The work is great; it’s a bit like what I did with Mercedes in Brackley, only now I get to eat some good pasta in Italy and drive a Formula 1 Ferrari in the simulator, and potentially a couple of tests next year as well, so it’s a really great opportunity.

    “I’m going to have a test next year, I already know that and they confirmed it. I was supposed to be there next week, but I can’t make it because I have the Super GT race at Autopolis, so oh well, it’ll happen eventually."

    The Scuderia team is yet to confirm it, while the Argentine has said the purpose of it is to make him a better simulator driver.

    "I’m really looking forward to it, but obviously the most important thing for them is for me to get in the car to improve my simulator performance. All the simulator drivers have to get behind the wheel of a TPC car to gain experience in a Formula 1 car, so I’ll just have to wait for that day."

    Fenestraz has had a successful racing career so far in Japan, winning the 2019 All-Japan Formula 3 championship and racing in Super Formula and Super GT. He also spent time in Formula E with Nissan. He finished 16th in both the 2022 and 2023 championships.

    At Ferrari, Dino Beganovic will become reserve driver in 2027, alongside Antonio Giovinazzi, after leaving the team's Driver Academy.
