    小红牛为美国大奖赛推出白色赛车服，粉丝呼吁匹配涂装

    小红牛车队为即将到来的美国大奖赛推出了全白色赛车服，并暗示可能会为美洲赛道的大奖赛引入匹配的特别涂装。

    红牛车队在美国大奖赛上展示了引人注目的白色一级方程式赛车服，粉丝呼吁匹配的涂装

    红牛车队公布了为美国大奖赛特别定制的白色赛车服

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解一级方程式新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的分析与赛事分析。

    红牛车队在奥斯汀即将举行的一级方程式美国大奖赛上揭幕了全部白色的赛车服，暗示可能为美洲赛车场的赛事引入匹配的一次性涂装。

    四次冠军得主马克斯·维斯塔潘和他的队友伊萨克·哈迪亚在社交媒体上分享了一段展示新赛车服的视频。白色设计配有醒目的蓝色装饰和复古风格的缝制合作伙伴徽章。

    新的赛车服让粉丝呼吁匹配的车队涂装。一位粉丝评论道：“现在白色公牛？！？！请吗？？！！”，另一位补充说：“现在用这个做一个类似的涂装。”

    “有特别的涂装吗？”有人发帖询问，另一条评论写道：“刚刚想起今年我们还没有小红牛 22的特别涂装……COTA有什么计划？”

    其他反应包括：“哦我的，请让赛车是白色的！”和“VCARB的赛车服也是白色的；我如何在赛道上找到这些人？”

    美国大奖赛定于10月23日至25日举行，这将紧随维斯塔潘连续两次大奖赛胜利之后。他在新加坡大奖赛周末表现出色，赢得了冲刺赛的杆位，并最终夺冠，同时还赢得了大奖赛的杆位，这也是他职业生涯中第一次赢得大奖赛的杆位。

    尽管红牛车队近期战绩出色，但目前他们在车队积分榜上排名第四，积分为332分。梅赛德斯车队以578分领先，法拉利车队以444分排名第二，麦凯轮车队以339分排名第三。

    维斯塔潘在车手积分榜上排名第四，积分为221分，落后积分榜领跑者基米·安东内利119分。梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔排名第二，法拉利车队的刘易斯·汉密尔顿排名第三。哈迪尔因手腕受伤缺席了三次大奖赛周末，目前排名第八，积分为97分。

    Red Bull reveals striking white F1 race suits for US GP as fans call for matching livery

    Red Bull has unveiled special white race suits for the United States Grand Prix

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Red Bull has unveiled all-white race suits for the upcoming Formula 1 United States Grand Prix in Austin, hinting that a matching one-off livery could also be introduced for the race at the Circuit of the Americas.

    Four-time champion Max Verstappen and his team-mate Isack Hadjar showed off the new race suits in a video shared on the team's social media platforms. The white design features bold blue accents and vintage-style sewn-on partner badges.

    The new race suits have left fans calling for a matching livery. "WHITE BULL NOW?!?! PLEASE??!?!!" one fan commented, while another added: "Now do a similar livery with this."

    "There's a special livery, right?" someone else posted, and another comment read: "Just remembered we've not had a special livery of the RB 22 this year... anything cooking in COTA?"

    Further reactions included: "Oh my, please let the car be white!" and "VCARB's suits are white as well; how am I gonna find these guys on the track?"

    The United States Grand Prix, scheduled for 23-25 October, will follow back-to-back grand prix wins for Verstappen. He dominated the Singapore Grand Prix weekend, claiming pole position for the sprint race, which he won, and pole position for the grand prix, which he went on to win for the first time in his career.

    Despite its recent run of wins, Red Bull currently sits fourth in the constructors' championship with 332 points. Mercedes leads with 578 points, and Ferrari and McLaren follow in second and third with 444 and 339 points, respectively.

    Verstappen is fourth in the drivers' standings with 221 points, 119 points behind championship leader Kimi Antonelli. Mercedes' George Russell is second and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton is third. Hadjar is eighth with 97 points, having missed three grand prix weekends due to a wrist injury.
