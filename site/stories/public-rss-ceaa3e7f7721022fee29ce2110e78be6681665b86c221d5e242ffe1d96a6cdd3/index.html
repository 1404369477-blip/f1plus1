    皮亚斯特里因阻挡劳森被罚退三位

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里因在F1意大利大奖赛排位赛中阻挡利亚姆·劳森，被罚退三位，失去第三位发车位。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里因在排位赛中阻挡利亚姆·劳森，失去了F1意大利大奖赛正赛的第三位发车顺位。

    在Q2接近尾声时，这位迈凯伦车手刚刚驶出维修站进行飞驰圈，恰逢马克斯·维斯塔潘和利亚姆·劳森这对红牛车手组合开始他们的最后一圈冲刺。

    红牛车手与其他车手的节奏并不同步，因为劳森最初为维斯塔潘提供了尾流牵引，而维斯塔潘也试图通过回礼帮助劳森晋级Q3。

    皮亚斯特里设法避开了维斯塔潘，但当他在第一个减速弯转弯时阻挡了劳森，导致劳森损失了宝贵的时间，而他在该圈余下部分已无法弥补。

    皮亚斯特里在排位赛后被传唤至干事处，他解释说，虽然他曾收到红牛双车正在逼近的警告，但他并不知道他们正在做飞驰圈。

    当他意识到维斯塔潘确实是在刷圈速时，他尽可能地让出了赛道。

    然而，由于他所处的位置，他别无选择，只能继续滑行进入减速弯，在那里他阻挡了劳森。

    尽管干事们承认皮亚斯特里当时处于一个棘手的境地，但他们认为除了因阻挡对手而对他处以处罚之外别无选择。

    国际汽联（FIA）发布的一份声明中表示：“干事们承认，鉴于车手所掌握的信息以及他所处的境况，81号车车手在这一阶段没有其他可选的办法，因此，无意间也不必要地阻碍了30号车。

    “因此，处以后退3个发车位的标准处罚。”

    这项处罚使皮亚斯特里（Piastri）从第三位降至第六位，这意味着法拉利车队的夏尔·勒克莱尔和刘易斯·汉密尔顿将升至第二排，排在皮埃尔·加斯利和乔治·拉塞尔之后。

    劳森未能进入Q3，使得维斯塔潘只能独自去寻找尾流，车队原本希望劳森能够提供支援，帮助给他的队友一个拖曳加速，但由于更换引擎的处罚，他将从周日的比赛发车位末尾起步。

    英文原文

    Oscar Piastri has lost third-place on the grid for Formula 1’s Italian Grand Prix after impeding Liam Lawson in qualifying.

    Towards the end of Q2, the McLaren driver exited the pits on an out-lap just as the Red Bull duo of Max Verstappen and Liam Lawson were beginning their final push laps.

    The Red Bulls were running out of sync with the field as Lawson had towed Verstappen initially and Verstappen was trying to help Lawson get through to Q3 by returning the favour.

    Piastri managed to get out of the way of Verstappen, but as Piastri turned into the first chicane he baulked Lawson, who lost valuable time that he was unable to recover later in the lap.

    Piastri was summoned to see the stewards after qualifying, and explained that while had been warned about the Red Bull duo approaching, he was not aware they were on push laps.

    When he realised that Verstappen was indeed going for a time, he got out of the way as best he could.

    However, due to his positioning, he had no choice but to continue to roll into the chicane where he impeded Lawson.

    Although the stewards accepted that Piastri had been left in a tricky spot, they felt that there was no option but to serve him with a penalty for blocking a rival.

    A statement issued by the FIA said: “The Stewards accept that with the information he had, and the situation he was placed in, the driver of Car 81 had, at this stage, no other options available and hence, inadvertently, and also unnecessarily, impeded Car 30.

    “Therefore the standard penalty of a drop of 3 grid positions is imposed.”

    The penalty drops Piastri from third down to sixth, and means that Ferrari duo Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton will move up to the second row behind Pierre Gasly and George Russell.

    Lawson’s failure to get through to Q3 meant that Verstappen was left alone to try to find a slipstream, with the team having hoped that Lawson could act as support in helping give his team-mate a tow thanks to the engine change penalty that puts him to the back of the grid for Sunday's race.
