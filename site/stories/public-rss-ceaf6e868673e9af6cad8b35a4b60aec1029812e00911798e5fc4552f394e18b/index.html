    F1 Grand Prix distances set to be reduced for 2027 season along with introduction of 'hard stop' time on race days

    Grand Prix distances will be reduced from 305km to 290km in 2027; the change is required due to the alteration of technical regulations for next season; watch every session of the Azerbaijan GP from Thursday live on Sky Sports F1, with the race on Saturday at 12pm

    There is set to be a slight reduction in the length of Formula 1 races for the 2027 season, the FIA has confirmed.

    At a meeting of the F1 Commission in London on Monday, the sport's decision makers agreed to reduce Grand Prix distances from 305km to 290km, which corresponds to a reduction of two to three laps depending on the circuit.

    The change is being made because fuel flow rates are being increased next season as part of measures the FIA are introducing to optimise the all-new regulations brought in at the start of 2026.
