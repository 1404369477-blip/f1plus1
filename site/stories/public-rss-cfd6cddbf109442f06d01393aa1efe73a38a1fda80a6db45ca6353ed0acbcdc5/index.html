    汉密尔顿失去最具市场价值运动员称号，勒克莱尔超越

    汉密尔顿在体育市场价值排名中失去榜首位置，被自己的法拉利队友勒克莱尔超越。

    汉密尔顿失去最具市场价值运动员称号，勒克莱尔超越

    SportsPro的2026年排名中，汉密尔顿从第一降至第二十二位，而他的法拉利队友现在成为了排名最高的F1车手，位列第九。

    对于法拉利车迷来说，这里是Scuderia 法拉利 F1的最新消息，包括车手更新、技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远地方的赛事反应。

    七次世界冠军去年位居SportsPro年度50位最具市场价值运动员榜首，但在2026年版中降至第22位，该榜单是根据NorthStar Solutions Group的数据制作的。

    白俄罗斯网球明星阿莉娜·萨巴伦卡位居榜首，领先自由式滑雪运动员谷爱凌、篮球明星扬尼斯·安特托昆博、足球运动员埃尔林·哈兰德和网球运动员卡洛斯·阿尔卡拉斯。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔现在是排名最高的F1车手，从去年的第25位上升到第9位。汉密尔顿的得分从去年的96.03降至66.63，落后于勒克莱尔的71.12。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘是唯一一位进入前50名的另一位F1车手，他从第81位攀升至第48位。

    超越前50名，看到前100名，名单上出现了一些令人惊讶的名字。奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂排名56位，有趣的是，他超过了他的麦凯轮车队队友、2025年冠军兰多·诺里斯，诺里斯的排名从第41位下降到了第78位。基米·安东内利，F1的新世界冠军，勉强挤进了前150名，排名第126位。

    正如你所想象的那样，这些分数是由品牌价值、势头和商业增长空间构建的。它们涵盖了从2025年7月1日到2026年6月30日的这段时间。这意味着本月早些时候安东内利在墨西哥的冠军头衔根本不包括在内。

    这也意味着排名涵盖了汉密尔顿在法拉利艰难的第一个赛季的大部分时间。在那个时间段内，他未能完成大奖赛领奖台。他今年的赛季则是一个完全不同的故事，他在许多早期比赛中都坐在车手积分榜的第二位。在还剩下三场比赛的情况下，他目前排名第四，仍然在争夺第二名的位置。

    这位英国人仍然排在大多数车手之上，他的品牌影响力远不止赛车，从时尚到电影。

    Lewis Hamilton loses most marketable athlete crown as Charles Leclerc moves ahead

    SportsPro's 2026 rankings have Hamilton down from first to 22nd, with his Ferrari team-mate now the highest-placed F1 driver in ninth

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton has lost his place at the top of sport's marketability rankings, and has slipped behind his own Ferrari team-mate too.

    The seven-time world champion topped SportsPro's annual list of the 50 Most Marketable Athletes last year, but has dropped to 22nd in the 2026 edition, which was created with data from NorthStar Solutions Group.

    Aryna Sabalenka, the Belarusian tennis star, sits at the very top of the list ahead of freestyle skier Eileen Gu, basketball star Giannis Antetokounmpo, footballer Erling Haaland and tennis player Carlos Alcaraz.

    Charles Leclerc is now the highest-ranked F1 driver, in ninth after he sat at 25th last year. Hamilton's score has dropped to 66.63 out of 100, from 96.03 last year, leaving him behind Leclerc on 71.12.

    Max Verstappen is the only other F1 driver in the top 50, having climbed from 81st to 48th.

    Looking beyond the top 50 to the top 100 adds some surprising names to the list. Oscar Piastri is 56th, interestingly ahead of McLaren team-mate and 2025 champion Lando Norris, who has dropped from 41st to 78th. Kimi Antonelli, F1's new world champion, only scrapes into the top 150 in 126th.

    As you would imagine, these scores are built from brand equity, momentum and room for commercial growth. They cover the period from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. That means Antonelli's title in Mexico earlier this month is not included at all.

    It also means the rankings cover much of Hamilton's difficult first season at Ferrari. In that time period, he failed to finish on the podium for a grand prix. His season this year has been a very different story, with him sat second in the drivers' standings for many of the early races. With three races to go, he sits fourth and still in the fight for second place.

    The Briton still ranks well above most of the grid, and his brand stretches far beyond just the racing, from fashion to film.
