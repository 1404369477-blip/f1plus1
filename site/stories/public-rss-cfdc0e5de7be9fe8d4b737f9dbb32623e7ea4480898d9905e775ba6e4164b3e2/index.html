    Zak Brown排除2020年F1赛事麦凯轮车队自产引擎计划

    Zak Brown表示，由于对与梅赛德斯合作的满意度，麦凯轮车队不会在2030年的F1赛事中推出自产引擎计划。

    Zak Brown排除了麦凯轮为F1 2030提供的引擎计划

    英国品牌目前是梅赛德斯的车队客户

    安东内利, 梅赛德斯, 兰多·诺里斯, 麦凯轮

    Zak Brown已经排除了为2030年一级方程式赛车赛季提供工厂引擎计划，因为麦凯轮对与梅赛德斯的合作关系“非常满意”。

    英国品牌自2021年以来一直是梅赛德斯客户，并在这一时期取得了巨大成功，赢得了两个车队冠军（2024年、2025年）、一个车手冠军（2025年）和24场大奖赛。

    但F1将在2030年迎来下一次引擎变革，通过引入涡轮增压、2.1升V8动力单元来取代今年才出现的现有引擎，但这些引擎已经证明存在争议。

    布朗在上周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛上表示：“这是我们过去考虑过的事情，但我们非常满意HPP。

    “我认为我们已经证明我们可以用它们赢得冠军，所以我们没有打算在我们的车后面放任何除了梅赛德斯之外的东西。”

    这个问题是由麦凯轮车队在本赛季与梅赛德斯引擎的麻烦引起的，一度导致两家制造商之间的关系紧张。

    这发生在工厂和客户车队之间在更复杂的引擎上存在知识差距之后，麦凯轮车队难以充分利用其梅赛德斯动力单元的最大潜力。

    因此，英国车队至少探索了成为自己制造商的想法，尤其是在转向更便宜的公式更加可行的时候，但麦凯轮车队在2026年下半年表现强劲，赢得了三场比赛。

    我认为，对于整个车手阵容来说，这是一个充满挑战的季节，这些动力单元带来的挑战，”麦凯轮赛车首席执行官补充道，他的车队在积分榜上排名第四，落后冠军梅赛德斯235分。

    “所以我们已经和梅赛德斯合作了一段时间。他们是一对极好的合作伙伴。”

    “我们在赛道上遇到过一些问题，他们也在赛道上遇到过一些问题。我认为每个车队在赛道上都会遇到问题，但我们有一个很好的工作关系。”

    “我们非常有信心我们得到的是相同的规格。这就是规则，你知道，我们在赢得比赛。我们上周末赢了，所以是的，我们很高兴。”

    布朗的评论是在Motorsport.com透露F1计划从2030年开始转向每个制造商提供两个动力单元的计划之前——包括工厂车队。

    所以，每个引擎将只剩下一个小车队，考虑到迈凯轮、阿尔派和威廉姆斯都是梅赛德斯的车队客户，这可能会在赛道内引起政治上的阴谋诡计。

    考虑到法拉利也将有一个客户车队（哈斯），加上红牛（小红牛），这留下了奥迪、本田和通用汽车凯迪拉克的潜在选择。

    然而，布朗对2030年的引擎更换感到“非常、非常兴奋”，这一变化是在F1、其制造商和FIA的共同努力下提前一年的。

    “我认为现在是要把所有的细节都处理好，”他补充道。“如果你看看FIA所做的事情，他们已经宣布了全职裁判。”

    “所以他们非常积极主动，行动迅速，说到做到。我认为这项运动从未如此健康。我认为竞争非常精彩。”

    “因此，我对这项运动未来的发展充满信心。现在的状态已经很好了。我对新引擎感到兴奋，我认为每个人都会这样。”

    “但我认为还有更多的事情正在进行，以继续推动这项运动的发展。所以，我认为在FIA和一级方程式之间，他们正在做一件了不起的工作。”

    Zak Brown rules out McLaren engine programme for F1 2030

    The British marque is currently a Mercedes customer squad

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Lando Norris, McLaren

    Zak Brown has ruled out a factory engine programme for the 2030 Formula 1 campaign as McLaren is “very happy” with its Mercedes partnership.

    The British marque has been a Mercedes customer since 2021 and has enjoyed vast success in that period, winning two constructors’ titles (2024, 2025), a drivers’ championship (2025) and 24 grands prix.

    But F1 is set for its next engine change in 2030 by introducing turbocharged, 2.1-litre V8 power units to replace the current ones that only came in this year, yet have proven to be contentious.

    Brown said at last weekend’s Las Vegas Grand Prix: “It's something we've looked at in the past, but we're very happy with HPP.

    “I think we've proven that we can win championships with them, so we don't have any intention to have anything in the back of our car other than a Mercedes.”

    The question was prompted by McLaren’s struggles with its Mercedes engine this season that at one point caused the relationship between both manufacturers to come under strain.

    It came after there was a knowledge gap on the more complex engine between the factory and customer outfit, with McLaren struggling to exploit the maximum from its Mercedes power unit.

    So the British outfit did at least explore the idea of becoming its own manufacturer, especially when a move to a cheaper formula is more feasible, but McLaren has enjoyed a stronger second half of 2026 with three wins.

    "It's been a challenging season, I think, for the entire grid with these power units,” added the McLaren Racing CEO, whose squad is fourth in the standings and 235 points behind champions Mercedes.

    “So we've been working with Mercedes for quite some time. They've been an awesome partner.

    “We've had some issues on track, they've had some issues on track. I think every team has had issues on track, but we've got a great working relationship.

    “Very confident we get the same specification. That's what the regulations are, and, you know, we're winning races. We won last weekend, so yeah, we're happy.”

    Brown’s comments did, however, come before Motorsport.com revealed F1’s plans to shift towards two power unit supplies per manufacturer come 2030 - including the factory team.

    So that would leave just one customer squad per engine and with McLaren, Alpine and Williams all Mercedes customers, it will potentially cause political intrigue throughout the paddock.

    Considering Ferrari will also have a customer team for then (Haas), plus Red Bull (Racing Bulls), it leaves potential options of Audi, Honda and Cadillac’s General Motors.

    Nevertheless, Brown is “very, very excited” for the engine change in 2030, which has been brought forward by a year following joint efforts from F1, its manufacturers and the FIA.

    “I think now it's about kind of dotting the i's and crossing the t's,” he added. “If you look at what the FIA has done, they've announced full-time stewards.

    “So, they are very proactive, they move quick, they do what they say they're going to do. I think the sport's never been healthier. I think the competition is amazing.

    “So, I've got a lot of confidence in the future of the sport. Where it is today is great. I'm excited for the new engines, I think everyone will be.

    “But I think there's a lot more also going on to continue to evolve the sport. So, I think between the FIA and Formula 1, they're doing a great job.”
