    2026年巴林大奖赛移师马来西亚雪邦，周末赛程一览

    由于中东局势，2026年F1巴林大奖赛将改在马来西亚雪邦赛道举行，远离其传统的巴林国际赛道主场。雪邦上一次举办大奖赛是在2017年，如今车队和车手几乎将其视为一条全新赛道。

    千万别错过本周末这场注定成为2026年一级方程式赛季最新颖的比赛，锦标赛将前往马来西亚，迎来一届与此前21届截然不同的巴林大奖赛。

    没错——中东的持续局势意味着今年的巴林比赛做出了特别安排，比赛将在雪邦赛道举行，距离其传统的巴林国际赛道主场超过3700英里。

    雪邦曾在1999年至2017年间承办大奖赛，在公布为替代场地时也是广受欢迎的选择，但自F1上次到访以来很多情况已经改变——尤其是今年的赛车——以至于车队和车手基本上把它当作一条全新赛道来对待。

    仅此一点就应该会造就一个引人入胜的周末，但如果你还需要更多守在屏幕前的理由，那就是比赛周末后期有降雨的威胁——降雨向来必定会在雪邦制造混乱——同时还能立刻检验争夺冠军的较量中是否出现了真正的势头转变，此前拉塞尔在阿塞拜疆夺冠，而安东内利在排位赛撞车后于正赛 recovery 到第五名。

    Be sure not to miss what is set to be the most novel event of the 2026 Formula 1® season this weekend as the championship heads to Malaysia for a very different Bahrain Grand Prix to the previous 21 editions.

    That's right - the ongoing situation in the Middle East means a special arrangement has been made for this year's Bahrain race, which will take place at the Sepang circuit, more than 3700 miles away from its traditional home at the Bahrain International Circuit.

    Sepang previously hosted grands prix from 1999 to 2017 and was a popular choice of replacement venue when it was announced, but so much has changed in the time since F1 last visited - particularly with this year's cars - that teams and drivers are essentially treating it as a brand-new track.

    That in itself should make for an intriguing weekend, but if you needed any more encouragement to tune in there's the threat of rain further into the race weekend - always guaranteed to cause chaos at Sepang - and an immediate chance to see whether there's been any real swing in momentum in the title fight, after George Russell's win in Azerbaijan and Kimi Antonelli's recovery to fifth following a crash in qualifying.
