    维斯塔潘回应沃尔夫关于"无情"F1车手时代的结束的说法

    四次F1冠军维斯塔潘回应了梅赛德斯车队负责人沃尔夫关于"无情"F1车手时代的结束的说法。

    维斯塔潘为自己的方法和频繁的无线电投诉进行了辩护。

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛赛车，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和比赛分析。

    在梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利上周末在墨西哥大奖赛上赢得了他的首个世界冠军后，沃尔夫将这位20岁的年轻人作为一个新类型冠军的例子。

    "这种不惜一切代价获胜、自私、机会主义、只为自己利益而冷酷无情的赛车手模式已经死亡，" 沃尔夫说。

    "我们现在有一个代表谦逊、正直、忠诚、尊重、真实自我的新冠军。他应该是许多年轻车手在青训车队中的榜样。他也应该是F1中那些有特权的少数人的榜样，那些在无线电中不断抱怨、抱怨、撞车的人。"

    沃尔夫没有点名任何车手，但在Interlagos的驾驶员新闻发布会上，维斯塔潘和两届冠军费尔南多·阿隆索被问到了他们的看法。

    “我的意思是，每个人都有权表达自己的观点，对吧？我真的没什么别的要说的，”维斯塔潘说。‘每个人都是不同的。我认为这对一级方程式赛车来说是个好事，因为如果每个人都有相同的个性，那就太无聊了。’

    阿隆索被邀请分享他对沃尔夫评论的看法，但他拒绝了。

    当话题转向维斯塔潘自己的无线电信息时，他谈得更加详细，这些信息经常包含对本赛季汽车的不满。

    “关于你听到的我抱怨的任何变化或 whatever，那只是因为我从不满意，因为我认为总有一些事情可以做得更好，我总是尝试微调或找到改进的地方，”这位荷兰人解释道。

    “所以如果你听到我在抱怨或者什么的，那更多的是为了确保车队总是在关注细节，试图找到更多的圈速，因为我知道还有更多的圈速可以挖掘。”

    维斯塔潘在巴西大奖赛开始前在车手积分榜上排名第二。

    Max Verstappen responds to Toto Wolff’s claim that era of "ruthless" F1 drivers is over

    Max Verstappen defended his own approach and frequent radio complaints

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen has responded to Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff's claim that the era of the "ruthless" F1 driver is over.

    After Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli clinched his maiden world championship at the Mexican Grand Prix last weekend, Wolff used the 20-year-old as an example of a new kind of champion.

    "This notion of win at all costs, selfish, opportunistic, ruthless for your own benefit, that model of racing driver has died," Wolff said.

    "We have a new champion that represents values like humility, like integrity, loyalty, respect, being himself. Should be a role model for many other young drivers in junior formulas. And should be a role model for the few entitled ones in Formula 1, the endless moaners on the radio, the ones that complain, the ones that crash."

    Wolff did not name any drivers, but Verstappen and two-time champion Fernando Alonso were asked for their views during the drivers' press conference at Interlagos.

    "I mean, everyone is entitled to their opinion, right? I have nothing else to say, really," Verstappen said. "Every person is different anyway. I think that’s what is good for Formula 1, because if everyone has the same personality, it’s very boring."

    Alonso was invited to share his thoughts on Wolff's comments, but he refused.

    Verstappen was more expansive when the subject turned to his own radio messages, which have often featured complaints about the car this season.

    "With shifts or whatever that you hear me complain about, it’s just because I’m never satisfied, because I think always certain things can be better, and I always try to fine-tune or find improvements," the Dutchman explained.

    "So if you hear it as complaining or whatever, it’s more for me to make sure that the team is always looking into details to try and find more lap time because I know there is more lap time in it."

    Verstappen sits second in the drivers' standings heading into the Brazilian Grand Prix.
