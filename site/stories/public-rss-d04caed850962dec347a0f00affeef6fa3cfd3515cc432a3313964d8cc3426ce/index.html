    蒙托亚解析红牛拉斯维加斯战术胜梅赛德斯

    蒙托亚解释了红牛如何在拉斯维加斯站凭借能量部署策略战胜梅赛德斯。安东内利排位最快但因引擎罚退五位，正赛经历35次领跑易主后红牛夺冠。

    胡安·巴勃罗·蒙托亚解释了红牛如何在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛中智胜梅赛德斯。

    胡安·巴勃罗·蒙托亚解释了红牛的能量部署陷阱如何在拉斯维加斯这场出现352次超车的激战中智胜梅赛德斯。

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    梅赛德斯为拉斯维加斯大道带来了最快的单圈套件——新加冕的2026年世界冠军基米·安东内利在排位赛中排名第一，但五位的引擎罚退使他降至第六，而乔治·拉塞尔与杆位得主马克斯·维斯塔潘同排发车。

    然而在50圈混乱的比赛和35次领先变化之后，红牛捧起了奖杯，而两辆银箭赛车均未登上领奖台。

    七届大奖赛冠军胡安·巴勃罗·蒙托亚在F1电视台赛后转播中指出了红牛智胜对手的原因。

    “不仅是马克斯，红牛的战略也非常出色，”蒙托亚说。“梅赛德斯采用了追求最快圈速的策略，他们认为这是在正常比赛中绕行此地的最快方式。我不认为他们预料到我们会每圈都互相超越。”

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    在这场以352次超车打破一级方程式赛车超车纪录的比赛中，如果在计时线前赛车耗尽电能，那么直道上的原始速度就几乎毫无意义。蒙托亚解释道：“红牛总会在直道上牺牲一点速度，他总会被超越，但马克斯总能在终点线前追回来。”

    排位赛的数据凸显了这一冒险策略：维斯塔潘在测速点仅排第18位，他把电能留到了最后一个弯道的出弯处，一直冲向终点。蒙托亚在为西班牙语电视台解说比赛时看到这一模式反复出现，他知道梅赛德斯没有简单的答案。“他们唯一能击败马克斯的办法，就是如果能有两辆车插在中间，也就是如果有两个人能在长直道上超越他，那么他们才有机会。如果只有一辆车，马克斯就会赢。而他们做得超出了预期。”

    在比赛工程师汤姆·哈特的墙边指导下——哈特在替代詹皮耶罗·兰比亚塞时庆祝了自己的首场胜利——维斯塔潘确认他不断与红牛沟通，以保护终点线前的冲刺。

    维斯塔潘对F1电视台说：“只是人们在改变电能部署，我想确保车队专注于此，确保我们在终点线前有尽可能最好的电能部署，当然不会在其他地方牺牲太多，但要确保如果终点线前有战斗，我们处于正确的电能部署状态。”

    蒙托亚仍然特别指出拉塞尔是“唯一一个稍微摸清了维斯塔潘套路的人”，并指出他能量管理得足够好，即便两次跑宽，仍能在进入1号弯时领先。拉塞尔后来承认，在直道上最多赚到1.3秒让他在中段暴露无遗——而在第48圈，他在脏线上攻击安东内利时于5号弯锁死，滑入护栏，最终第九名完赛。

    片刻之后，安东内利在背直道上收油再给油，试图管理身后维斯塔潘的位置，结果完全耗尽电池，从第二跌至第四，落在两辆迈凯伦之后——让托托·沃尔夫计划中的最后400米冲刺未能用上。

    维斯塔潘2026年的第四个第一名——也是生涯第75个——使他的积分升至304分，在车手积分榜上落后安东内利（402分）排名第二，领先刘易斯·汉密尔顿（267分）37分，领先拉塞尔（264分）40分。梅赛德斯或许把最快单圈赛车带到了内华达，但红牛把这场棋局读得透彻得多。

    Juan Pablo Montoya explains how Red Bull outsmarted Mercedes at F1 Las Vegas GP

    Juan Pablo Montoya explains how Red Bull's deployment trap outsmarted Mercedes in Las Vegas's 352-overtake thriller

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Mercedes brought the fastest single-lap package to the Las Vegas Strip - newly crowned 2026 world champion Kimi Antonelli topped qualifying before a five-place engine penalty dropped him to sixth, while George Russell lined up alongside polesitter Max Verstappen.

    Yet after 50 chaotic laps and 35 lead changes, Red Bull collected the trophy while neither Silver Arrow reached the podium.

    Speaking on F1 TV’s post-race broadcast, seven-time grand prix winner Juan Pablo Montoya pinpointed why Red Bull outfoxed its rival.

    "Not only Max, but Red Bull's strategy was so good," Montoya said. "Mercedes went with the strategy for the fastest lap and what they thought would be the fastest way around here for a normal race. I don't think they were expecting that we're going to be passing each other every lap."

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    In a contest that shattered Formula 1’s overtaking record with 352 passes, raw velocity down the Strip counted for little if a car ran dry before the timing beam. "Red Bull would always sacrifice a little bit on the Strip, and he could always get passed, but Max always came back at the line," Montoya explained.

    Qualifying numbers underscored that gamble: Verstappen ranked just 18th through the speed trap, saving electrical punch for the exit of the final corner toward the flag. Watching the pattern repeat while calling the race for Spanish-language television, Montoya knew Mercedes had no simple answer. "The only way they could beat Max, if they could put two cars in between, is if two people could pass him in the long straight, then they would have had a shot. If it was one car, Max was going to win. And they outdid themselves."

    Guided on the pitwall by race engineer Tom Hart - who celebrated his maiden victory substituting for Gianpiero Lambiase - Verstappen confirmed he constantly checked with Red Bull to protect that finish-line kick.

    "It's just people changing their deployment, and I wanted to make sure that the team was on it, that we had the best possible deployment to the line without, of course, compromising too much other places, but to make sure that if there was a fight to the line that we were on the right deployment," Verstappen told F1 TV.

    Montoya still singled out Russell as "the only guy that figured out a little bit of Max," noting he managed energy well enough to lead into Turn 1 even after running wide twice. Russell later admitted gaining up to 1.3 seconds down the Strip left him exposed in the middle sector—and on Lap 48, he locked up on the dirty line attacking Antonelli at Turn 5, sliding into the barrier and finishing ninth.

    Moments later, Antonelli lifted and reapplied throttle down the back straight trying to manage position behind Verstappen, completely draining his battery and tumbling from second to fourth behind both McLarens - leaving Toto Wolff’s planned final-400-metre push unused.

    Verstappen’s fourth P1 of 2026 - and 75th overall - lifted him to 304 points, moving 37 clear of Lewis Hamilton (267) and 40 ahead of Russell (264) for second in the drivers’ championship behind Antonelli (402). Mercedes may have brought the quickest one-lap car to Nevada, but Red Bull read the chess match far better.
