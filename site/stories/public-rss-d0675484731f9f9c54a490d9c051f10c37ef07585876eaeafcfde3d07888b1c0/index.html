    安东内利会见意大利总统马塔雷拉

    新科F1世界冠军安东内利会见意大利总统马塔雷拉，庆祝他在墨西哥大奖赛赢得世界冠军，结束意大利车手自1953年以来的世界冠军荒。

    基米·安东内利会见了意大利总统塞尔吉奥·马塔雷拉

    这位新的F1世界冠军受到了意大利总统的接待，向塞尔吉奥·马塔雷拉总统赠送了一顶头盔，头盔的护目镜上写着致词

    “致马塔雷拉总统，怀着崇高的敬意，”基米·安东内利签名道。

    新的F1世界冠军正处于一场风暴之中，这是他两周前在墨西哥大奖赛上赢得的世界冠军，这场胜利结束了自1953年以来意大利车手赢得世界冠军的干旱局面。

    安东内利，和他的父母Marco和Veronica以及他的妹妹Maggie一起，于周五受到了意大利共和国总统Sergio Mattarella的接见。

    会面在罗马的奎里纳莱宫举行，安东内利向总统赠送了他的一顶带有题词的官方头盔。

    安东内利与意大利国家最高领导人进行了交谈，向他解释了在比赛中佩戴头盔的感受，以及驾驶一级方程式赛车从身体角度所需要承受的挑战。

    "你大概会失去两公斤体重，"他说。"我们穿着防火服，这也非常热。我们训练以尽可能做好准备，并能够承受驾驶时感受到的热量。但然后在训练结束后，你会跳进一个装满冷水的浴缸中..."

    总统问道：“那么在二级方程式赛车和卡丁车比赛中也是这样吗？”

    安东内利回答道：“在卡丁车中稍微少一些。你更暴露在外。在单座赛车中，你是封闭的。但在二级方程式赛车中，比赛时间稍短，也更不激烈。一级方程式赛车的比赛平均持续一个半小时。但明年你会来看一些比赛吗？也许在蒙扎？有你和我们一起真是太好了。”

    马塔雷拉微笑着回答：“我真的希望如此。现在一级方程式赛车对我有很大的吸引力！上次还是几年前。总是很复杂，因为我的活动很复杂……但这个新的情况[指安东内利的竞争力]极大地鼓舞了我。”

    与总统的会面只是安东内利众多活动中的最新一次。几个小时前，安东内利观看了意大利男子国家队与法国队的比赛，意大利以3-1获胜，而前一天他在他目前居住的圣马力诺停留，以庆祝世界冠军。

    安东内利将飞回美国，这次前往内华达州，参加2026年一级方程式赛季倒数第三轮比赛，地点在拉斯维加斯。

    Kimi Antonelli meets Italian president Sergio Mattarella

    The new Formula 1 world champion was received by the president of the Italy, presenting Sergio Mattarella with one of his helmets bearing a dedication written on the visor

    "To President Mattarella, with great admiration," signed Kimi Antonelli.

    The new Formula 1 world champion is in the middle of a whirlwind of meetings, events and celebrations for the world title he won two weeks ago at the Mexico Grand Prix, with which he ended a title drought for an Italian driver to win the world championship dating back to 1953.

    Antonelli, together with his parents Marco and Veronica and his sister Maggie, was received on Friday by the president of the Italian Republic, Sergio Mattarella.

    The meeting took place at the Quirinale in Rome, with Antonelli giving the president one of his official helmets with a dedication written on the visor.

    Antonelli spoke with the highest office of the Italian state, explaining to him the sensations of wearing a helmet during a race, but also what driving a Formula 1 single-seater entails from a physical point of view.

    "You lose roughly a couple of kilograms," he said. "We have the fireproof suit, which is also hot. We train to be as ready as we can and to be able to withstand the heat we experience when we drive. But then after the session you jump into a tub of cold water..."

    The president then asked: "But was it like that in Formula 2 as well? And in karts?"

    "In karts a little less so," Antonelli replied. "You are much more out in the open. In a single-seater you are enclosed. But in F2 the races last a little less and are less physical. In Formula 1 the race lasts an average of an hour and a half. But will you come and see some races next year? Maybe at Monza? It would be very nice to have you with us."

    Mattarella, with a smile, replied: "I really hope so. Now Formula 1 attracts me a lot! The last time was several years ago. It is always complicated, because my movements are complex... But this new situation [referring to Antonelli's competitiveness] greatly encourages me."

    The meeting with the president was only the latest of Antonelli's engagements. A few hours earlier, Antonelli had attended the match between the Italian men's national football team and the French side, which Italy won 3-1, while the previous day he had stopped in San Marino - where he currently lives - to celebrate the world title.

    Antonelli will fly back to the United States, this time to Nevada, to contest the third-to-last round of the 2026 Formula 1 season in Las Vegas.
