    拉尔夫·舒马赫质疑汉密尔顿领导力并警告维斯塔潘

    前F1车手拉尔夫·舒马赫认为，维斯塔潘若转投梅赛德斯会“非常困难”，并批评汉密尔顿从未学会如何带领一支车队。

    拉尔夫·舒马赫质疑汉密尔顿的领导力，并对维斯塔潘发出警告

    拉尔夫·舒马赫认为，维斯塔潘在梅赛德斯会难以适应，并批评了汉密尔顿带领车队的能力

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    前一级方程式车手拉尔夫·舒马赫认为，马克斯·维斯塔潘会觉得在梅赛德斯的生活“非常艰难”，并辩称刘易斯·汉密尔顿从未学会如何管理一支车队。

    在天空体育德国台的《Backstage Boxengasse》播客节目中，舒马赫表示，维斯塔潘深度参与红牛技术层面的事务，这种模式很可能不容易移植到梅赛德斯这样的另一支车队。

    “我认为那会非常困难，”这位德国人说道。“马克斯习惯了（一些不同的东西）……他对技术如此敏锐，他想确切知道发生了什么。

    “他基本上坐进赛车就想立刻知道变速箱、差速器的情况。或者比如减震器是如何工作的。无论如何，他都会觉得深入探究这些很有意思。”

    舒马赫将这一点归因于维斯塔潘与父亲、前F1车手乔斯·维斯塔潘共同成长的经历，并将其与自己与哥哥、七届世界冠军迈克尔·舒马赫一起度过的童年进行了比较。

    “而且马克斯也属于与父亲一起成长起来的那一代人，他父亲亲自参与赛车调校，就像我和我哥哥那样，”他补充道，“我认为这就是最大的区别。”

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利；刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    他怀疑梅赛德斯不会欢迎那种影响力，并认为法拉利会更适合。

    “他毕竟是和谁一起成长起来的？是和红牛一起。梅赛德斯已经是一支大车队了。如果像马克斯这样的人现在去那里，我不认为他们会太热衷。”

    “在法拉利，我认为他们习惯了车手能够提出要求，但在我看来，这仍然源自迈克尔[舒马赫]，源自他在那里留下的东西。”

    法拉利的车手阵容动态在最近几场比赛中受到了审视。舒马赫对汉密尔顿在马拉内罗车队的表现毫不留情地做出了评价。

    “汉密尔顿从未学会如何领导一支车队，无论是在托托·沃尔夫手下还是在罗恩·丹尼斯手下，”这位前车手说。“那本来也从来不是他的工作。他只需要坐进车里，尽可能快地驾驶。”

    “我觉得你能看出来，而且我认为这也让他变得相当自负。”

    Ralf Schumacher questions Lewis Hamilton's leadership as he issues Max Verstappen warning

    Ralf Schumacher believes Max Verstappen would struggle to fit in at Mercedes and has criticised Lewis Hamilton's ability to lead a team

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Former Formula 1 driver Ralf Schumacher believes Max Verstappen would find life at Mercedes "very difficult" and has argued that Lewis Hamilton has never learned to run a team.

    On Sky Sports Germany's Backstage Boxengasse podcast, Schumacher said that Verstappen's deep involvement in the technical side of Red Bull would likely not carry over easily to another team such as Mercedes.

    "I think that would be very difficult," the German said. "Max is used to [something different]... He's so technically switched on, he wants to know exactly what's going on.

    "He basically gets in the car and wants to know straight away what's happening with the gearbox, with the differential. Or how a damper works, for example. He'd find it interesting to dig into that regardless."

    Schumacher put that down to Verstappen's upbringing with his father, former F1 driver Jos Verstappen, comparing it to his own childhood alongside his older brother and seven-time champion Michael Schumacher.

    "And Max is also from the generation that grew up with his father, who worked on cars himself, just like it was with me and my brother," he added. "I think that's the big difference."

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    He doubts Mercedes would welcome that kind of influence, and thinks Ferrari would be better suited.

    "Who did he grow up with, after all? Together with Red Bull. Mercedes is already a big team. If someone like Max went there now, I don't think they'd be too keen on that.

    "At Ferrari, I think they're used to a driver being able to make demands, but in my opinion that still comes from Michael [Schumacher], from what he left behind there."

    Ferrari's driver line-up dynamics have come under scrutiny in recent races. Schumacher did not hold back in his assessment of Hamilton at the Maranello outfit.

    "Hamilton never learned how to lead a team, not under Toto Wolff and not under Ron Dennis," the former driver said. "That was never his job anyway. He just had to get in the car and drive as fast as possible.

    "I think you can see that, and I think it's made him quite egotistical as well."
