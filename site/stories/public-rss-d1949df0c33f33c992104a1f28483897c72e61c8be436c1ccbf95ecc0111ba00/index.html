    Charles Leclerc was left "angry" while Lewis Hamilton said he was "devastated" after both Ferrari drivers suffered shock early exits from Las Vegas Grand Prix Qualifying.

    The Italian team had arrived at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit wary of the challenges the long straights and cold temperatures could pose them, but Friday night's session went far worse than either driver had anticipated.

    Both drivers were eliminated in Q2 as Leclerc took 11th with Hamilton a place behind him. They will each move a position forward on the grid due to a penalty for McLaren's Oscar Piastri.

    'Devastated' Hamilton reveals damage and deployment issues

    Hamilton was lacking pace throughout the session, which he suggested was caused by damage he had done to his car in the early stages.

    "I'm devastated," Hamilton told Sky Sports F1. "We work hard to do a good job.
