    博塔斯墨西哥站撞墙退赛 接受预防性医疗检查

    博塔斯在墨西哥大奖赛第45圈右前悬挂失效撞墙，赛会出示红旗，他随后被送往医疗中心接受预防性检查。

    博塔斯在一场导致F1墨西哥大奖赛出示红旗的事故后被送往接受预防性医疗检查。

    在71圈比赛的第45圈，博塔斯驾驶的凯迪拉克赛车右前悬挂在接近1号弯时突然失效，赛车打转后倒着撞上护栏。

    比赛因需要修复护栏而中断，医疗团队也在座舱旁与博塔斯交谈了很长时间，最终他自行爬出赛车并步行离开。

    凯迪拉克的一份声明称，博塔斯在车队无线电中“有回应”，将前往医疗中心接受“进一步的预防性医疗评估”。

    在撞车发生时，车队无线电询问博塔斯是否还好，他回答说：“不。操！”

    护栏修复完成后，比赛将恢复，马克斯·维斯塔潘将领先查尔斯·勒克莱尔。此前，这位红牛车手利用了勒克莱尔与杆位发车的法拉利队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿在第一圈颇具争议的事故。勒克莱尔因该事故受罚，将早期领先位置拱手让给了维斯塔潘。

    汉密尔顿起初在博塔斯撞车引发安全车时进站，将第三名输给了很可能成为世界冠军的基米·安东内利，但比赛随后被红旗中断。不过，重启顺序按前一圈计算，这意味着汉密尔顿保住了第三名。

    英文原文

    Valtteri Bottas has been taken for precautionary medical checks after a crash that caused Formula 1's Mexican Grand Prix to be red-flagged.

    The right front suspension on Bottas's Cadillac dramatically failed as he approached Turn 1 on lap 45 of the 71-lap race, sending him spinning backwards into the barriers.

    The race was stopped for the barriers to be repaired, with the medical team also spending a long time talking to Bottas in the cockpit before he eventually emerged and walked away unaided.

    A Cadillac statement said Bottas was "responsive" on team radio and was going to the medical centre for "further medical assessments as a precaution".

    Asked on team radio if he was OK as the crash unfolded, Bottas replied "no. F**k!".

    Once barrier repairs are complete, the race will resume with Max Verstappen leading from Charles Leclerc after the Red Bull driver took advantage of a contentious first-lap incident between Leclerc and polesitting Ferrari team-mate Lewis Hamilton. A penalty for Leclerc over that incident cost him his early lead to Verstappen.

    Hamilton initially lost third to likely world champion Kimi Antonelli when he pitted as the safety car initially came out for Bottas's crash before the race was red-flagged instead. But the restart order has been counted from the lap before, meaning Hamilton keeps third.
