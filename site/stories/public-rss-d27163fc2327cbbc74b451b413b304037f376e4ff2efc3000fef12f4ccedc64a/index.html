    凯迪拉克取消V6 F1引擎，推迟至2030年进入

    凯迪拉克将推迟其内部F1动力单元的引入，推迟到2030年。原计划在2029年使用通用汽车的动力单元首次参加锦标赛。

    凯迪拉克放弃V6一级方程式引擎，推迟进入作为动力单元制造商

    首席执行官丹·托里斯表示，凯迪拉克将作为法拉利的客户车队再保持一年。通用汽车推迟其自身一级方程式动力单元的引入，直到2030年V8引擎的到来

    凯迪拉克计划将其内部一级方程式动力单元的引入推迟一年，至2030年

    美国车队最初的计划是在2029年首次使用通用汽车的动力单元参加锦标赛。然而，这是基于当前法规周期将完整运行的假设，以及下一代动力单元将不会在2031年引入的想法

    这种情况已经发生了变化。在拉斯维加斯大奖赛之前，车队和制造商原则上达成了一致，提前恢复V8引擎的回归，正如麦凯轮首席执行官Zak Brown在周四的团队负责人新闻发布会上公开确认的那样。

    阿尔卑斯车队负责人Flavio Briatore补充说，签名尚未落笔，但已经就2030年采用2.1升涡轮增压V8引擎并减少电力比例达成一致。

    这一发展对通用汽车的计划产生了影响。如果美国制造商打算最早在2029年将凯迪拉克转变为官方车队，通用汽车将不得不开发符合当前规格的动力单元，仅用于一个赛季。这将是一项昂贵的举措。

    当Motorsport.com询问是否因此继续作为客户车队再待一个赛季更有意义时，丹·托里斯CEO在拉斯维加斯确认说：“是的，我们将继续在2029年成为法拉利的客户，并在2030年使用V8引擎推出凯迪拉克动力单元。”

    托里斯认为V8的到来对这项运动是一个积极的进步，也是凯迪拉克支持的方向。

    “是的，我的意思是，我们很高兴看到这个系列赛在FIA的领导下真正团结起来，为2030年的V8设定方向。我们对此感到非常高兴，”托里斯说。

    他还补充说，凯迪拉克和通用汽车正接近另一个里程碑。不到一个月的时间，他们在北卡罗来纳州夏洛特的专用动力单元设施将开业。

    “是的，我们的新动力单元设施将于12月18日开业。作为一个新进入这项运动的制造商，有这个方向显然非常有帮助。我们对这个方向感到兴奋，并且我们的团队正在努力准备。”

    除了预期会更能吸引粉丝外，V8引擎还旨在更加简单和便宜，这对新来者来说也是好消息。

    当设施开始运营时，凯迪拉克最初仍然会在V6和V8设计上同时工作，尽管当前动力单元的2029年版本将不再需要完全开发。

    “嗯，在一段时间内，我们同时在开发两者。所以他们已经有一个V6的基础可以在此基础上构建，然后我们再向V8努力，”托里斯解释道。

    现在有多个设计正在进行中，所以我们有很好的基础来构建我们正在尝试的东西，以及我们前进的方向。

    这应该会导致凯迪拉克在2030年推出其首个内部动力单元，正式将这位美国新来者打造成一支工厂车队。这个长期目标对于确保其F1参赛资格的批准也至关重要，因为其他车队认为这是整个F1增加价值的主要来源。

    Cadillac scraps V6 F1 engine, delays entry as PU manufacturer

    CEO Dan Towriss says Cadillac will remain a Ferrari customer team for another year. General Motors has postponed the introduction of its own F1 power unit until the arrival of the V8 in 2030

    Cadillac is set to delay the introduction of its in-house Formula 1 power unit by a year to 2030.

    The American squad's initial plan was to compete in the championship with its own General Motors power unit for the first time in 2029. However, that was based on the idea that the current regulations cycle would run its full course and that the next generation of power units would not be introduced until 2031.

    That situation has since changed. Ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, the teams and manufacturers reached an agreement in principle on bringing forward the return of V8 engines, as the McLaren CEO Zak Brown also publicly confirmed during the team principals' press conference on Thursday.

    The signatures have not yet been put on paper, as Alpine team principal Flavio Briatore added, but an agreement has been reached on a 2.1-litre turbocharged V8 with a reduced proportion of electrical power from 2030.

    This development has implications for General Motors' plans. If the American manufacturer were to press ahead with its intention to turn Cadillac into a works team as early as 2029, GM would have to develop a power unit to the current specifications for just a single season. That would be a costly undertaking.

    Asked by Motorsport.com whether it would therefore make more sense to continue as a customer team for an extra season, CEO Dan Towriss confirmed in Las Vegas: "Yes, we would remain a customer of Ferrari through 2029 and bring the Cadillac power unit online in 2030 with the V8."

    Towriss sees the arrival of the V8 as a positive development for the sport, and one that Cadillac supports.

    "Yeah, I mean, I think we're glad to see really the series coming together under the leadership of the FIA to set that direction for a V8 in 2030. We're super happy with that," Towriss said.

    He added that Cadillac and General Motors are approaching another milestone. In less than a month's time, their dedicated power unit facility in Charlotte, North Carolina, will open.

    "Yes, our new power unit facility opens up on December 18th. So as a new manufacturer coming into the sport, having this direction is obviously very helpful. We're excited about the direction and the crew's hard at work getting ready."

    Besides being expected to appeal more to fans, the V8 is also intended to be simpler and cheaper, which is likewise good news for a newcomer.

    When the facility begins operations, Cadillac will initially still be working on both V6 and V8 designs, although the current generation of power units will no longer need to be fully developed for 2029.

    "Well, for a period of time, we're working on both. So they've already got a V6 base to build off of and then we’re working towards the V8," Towriss explained.

    "There are a number of designs that are in motion now, so there's a lot of good foundation for what we're trying to build and the direction we're headed in."

    This should lead to the introduction of Cadillac's first in-house power unit in 2030, officially making the American newcomer a works team. That long-term ambition was also important in securing approval for its F1 entry, as the other teams saw it as the main source of added value for F1 as a whole.
