    胡安·巴勃罗·蒙托亚呼吁法拉利在刘易斯·汉密尔顿和夏尔·勒克莱尔于墨西哥首圈发生冲突后对他们施加后果

    前一级方程式车手、F1 TV评论员胡安·巴勃罗·蒙托亚认为，刘易斯·汉密尔顿和夏尔·勒克莱尔在首圈的争夺很可能让法拉利丢掉了墨西哥大奖赛的胜利，因此必须让他们承担“后果”。

    当勒克莱尔试图超越从杆位发车的汉密尔顿时，两位车手都跑宽了。勒克莱尔取得领先，而汉密尔顿被红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘超越，丢掉了一个位置。这位摩纳哥车手因该事件被处以五秒罚时，维斯塔潘最终赢得了比赛。

    蒙托亚在天空体育（天空体育）的F1电视赛后节目中发言，敦促法拉利对其车手施加后果。

    “必须进行适当的对话，也必须实施处罚，”这位前车手解释道。“他们需要知道自己的行为会带来后果，因为他们今天本应该赢的。”

    前印地赛车系列赛车手詹姆斯·欣奇克利夫与蒙托亚一同参与转播，他补充道：“马克斯太不可思议了。红牛执行得非常漂亮。但这不是红牛赢得的比赛，这是法拉利输掉的比赛。如果你看看接下来的赛道，除非出现奇迹，我认为这是他们今年剩余时间里赢得胜利的最佳机会。他们配得上胜利。他们排位赛表现很好，但他们的车手今天让他们失望了。”

    夏尔·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    法拉利车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔在事故发生后呼吁他的车手们保持尊重。

    “我认为这只是一个尊重的问题，对品牌的尊重，对赞助商的尊重，对车队的尊重，对在家里拼命工作的2000名员工的尊重，我认为我们在性能方面正在回归，我们确实向前迈出了良好的一步，”这位法国人对天空体育F1说。

    “两次升级，家里的人们日夜工作。老实说，对我来说，像这样放弃一场胜利很难消化。我会有时间和他们讨论，但老实说，今天我有点低落。”

    法拉利现在将展望本周的巴西大奖赛。

    Juan Pablo Montoya demands "consequences" for Ferrari drivers after Mexican GP disaster

    Juan Pablo Montoya has called for Ferrari to impose consequences on Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc after their first-lap clash in Mexico

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Former Formula 1 driver and F1 TV pundit Juan Pablo Montoya has argued that there must be "consequences" for Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc after their first-lap battle likely cost Ferrari the win at the Mexican Grand Prix.

    Both drivers ran wide when Leclerc attempted to overtake Hamilton, who had started from pole position. Leclerc emerged in the lead, while Hamilton lost a position to Red Bull's Max Verstappen. The Monegasque driver was handed a five-second penalty for the incident, and Verstappen ultimately won the race.

    Speaking on F1 TV's post-race show, Montoya urged Ferrari to impose consequences on its drivers.

    "There have to be proper conversations, and there have to be penalties," the former driver explained. "They need to know there’s going to be consequences for what they’re doing, because they should have won today."

    Former IndyCar Series driver James Hinchcliffe, who joined Montoya on the broadcast, added: "Max is incredible. Red Bull executed beautifully. But this was not a race that Red Bull won. This was a race that Ferrari lost. And if you look at the tracks coming up, short of a miracle, I think this was their best chance for the rest of the year to get a win. They deserved it. They qualified well and their drivers let them down today."

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur called for respect from his drivers after the incident.

    "I think it’s just a matter of respect, the respect for the brand, for the sponsor, for the team, for the 2,000 people working at home like hell that I think we are coming back in terms of performance, that we did a good step forward," the Frenchman told Sky Sports F1.

    "With both upgrades, the people at home are working night and day. And honestly, it’s difficult to digest for me to give up a win like this. I will have time to discuss with them, but honestly, today I’m a bit down."

    Ferrari will now look ahead to the Brazilian Grand Prix this week.
