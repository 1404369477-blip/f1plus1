    这位梅赛德斯车手是在接受奥地利广播机构ORF Sport采访时说这番话的，该机构向他展示了去年他在滨海湾街道赛道与伴侣卡门·蒙特罗·蒙德特庆祝夺冠的照片。

    “我的意思是，那是一个非常特别的周末，因为幕后有很多不为众人所知的事情在发生。”

    当采访者请他暗示一下所指何事时，这位英国车手拒绝了：“不，总有一天吧。”

    他接着解释了为什么这些照片对他意义重大。

    “心理上，我的大脑相当忙碌，”拉塞尔补充道。“然后出场，拿下杆位，赢得比赛，那真是一个非常特别的时刻，而且卡门也在那里。”

    “为什么这些照片和这个时刻对我如此重要，是因为在我应对赛道外那些时刻的过程中，她一直陪伴在我身边，所以这是人们看不到的部分。”

    2025年新加坡大奖赛的胜利是他那个赛季的第二场胜利，也弥补了此前在滨海湾的失望。2023年，他在最后一圈跑在第三位时撞车，丢掉了一个几乎到手的领奖台。

    本赛季，拉塞尔以238分位居车手积分榜第二，落后梅赛德斯队友安东内利102分。法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿以229分排名第三。

    “我会继续每场比赛都全力拼搏，直到数学上不再可能为止，”拉塞尔在阿塞拜疆大奖赛周末期间对媒体表示。

    “这不会改变我对待工作的方式。我想努力赢下每一场比赛，并在每一场比赛中夺得杆位。

    “这就是我的目标。这就是我每个周末都在努力追求的东西，之后会发生什么就顺其自然。显然存在巨大的积分差距，但现在这甚至不在考虑范围内。表现才是最大的优先事项。

    “我不认为之前我从争冠的角度给自己施加了压力。我是从表现的角度给自己施加了压力。我目前在积分榜上处于这个位置，是因为表现还不够强劲。

    “一段时间以来，压力一直来自努力提升性能，而不是想着我需要为这个冠军而战，因为只有当你具备性能时，你才能为冠军而战。”

    George Russell hints at hidden struggles behind 2025 Singapore GP victory

    George Russell has revealed he faced undisclosed challenges behind the scenes during his 2025 Singapore Grand Prix victory

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell has revealed he was dealing with "a lot" behind the scenes during the weekend of his 2025 Singapore Grand Prix victory, saying "not many people know" what was happening away from the track.

    The Mercedes driver was speaking to Austrian broadcaster ORF Sport, which showed him photographs of himself celebrating last year's win at the Marina Bay Street Circuit with his partner, Carmen Montero Mundt.

    "I mean, it was a very special weekend because there was a lot going on behind the scenes that not many people know about."

    When the interviewer asked him to give a hint about what he meant, the Briton declined: "No, one day."

    He went on to explain why the images meant so much to him.

    "Psychologically, my brain was quite busy," Russell added. "Then going out, putting it on pole, winning the race was a really special moment and having Carmen there as well.

    "Why these photos and this moment were so important to me is because she was there through those moments off the track that I was dealing with, so this is the part that people don't see."

    The 2025 Singapore Grand Prix win was his second victory of that season and made up for previous disappointments at Marina Bay. In 2023, he crashed on the final lap while running third, throwing away a likely podium.

    This season, Russell sits second in the drivers' standings with 238 points, 102 points behind his Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli. Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton is third with 229 points.

    "I'm going to keep fighting every single race until it's mathematically impossible," Russell told the media during the Azerbaijan Grand Prix weekend.

    "It doesn't change how I go about my job. I want to try and win every single race and put it on pole every single race.

    "That's my goal. That's what I'm working towards every single weekend and what happens thereafter will be. There's obviously a huge deficit, but it's not even a consideration right now. The performance is the biggest priority.

    "I don't think I was really putting pressure on myself before from a championship standpoint. I was putting pressure on myself from a performance perspective. I'm in this position in the championship because the performance hasn't been strong enough.

    "It's been the case for a while where the pressure has come from trying to improve the performance, rather than thinking I need to fight for this championship because you can only fight for a championship if you've got the performance."
