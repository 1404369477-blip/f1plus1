    克里斯蒂安·霍纳承认在获得6700万英镑的赔偿后无需回归F1

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳离开红牛车队后，由于获得了巨额赔偿金，他无需在赛道上寻找新角色。

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳承认，在获得6700万英镑的雷诺车队赔偿后，他不需要回到F1

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳在一次揭露性的新采访中谈到了他在F1的未来以及他的6700万英镑的离职赔偿金

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析

    当一位高调的F1高管被解雇时，谣言机器立即转向他们下一个可能落脚的地方。但就克里斯蒂安·霍纳而言，他在2025年离开红牛车队时，得到了如此巨大的金降落伞，以至于完全改写了他的职业轨迹。

    而不是急于在维修区争取一个新的角色，这位52岁的老将正在为自己的F1生涯结束后作为一位高度流动的自由球员的生活进行规划。在最近的一次澳大利亚60分钟的节目中，焦点从导致他下台的职场政治转向了对可能回归的经济分析。由于提前终止合同，他的银行账户得到了大幅增强，霍纳正从绝对有利的位置计算着他的下一步行动。

    当红牛GmbH终止了他的雇佣关系时，霍纳的合同还有四年多。解约的财务损失最近被揭露。根据红牛技术有限公司2025年的财务报告，霍纳获得了一笔惊人的6700万英镑（约合8850万美元）的赔偿金，一些车队的消息人士估计，一旦解决了额外的合同费用，总金额可能接近1亿美元。

    这样的财富水平从根本上改变了霍纳对下一步职业发展的看法。

    霍纳在直播中解释说：“我认为这给了我时间上的奢侈，我处于一个不需要回到一级方程式的有利位置。我认为生活中最宝贵的东西就是时间。”

    尽管霍纳在英格兰乡村庄园享受着宁静的生活，并且目前正为英国美洲杯帆船队提供咨询，但他迅速确认自己的竞技动力并未消退。

    “我仍然是个速度狂热者，”霍纳承认。“所以无论是四轮、两轮、四条腿……用马。给我看看赛道。”

    车库里普遍认为，霍纳作为传统、拿薪水的雇员的日子已经结束。在直播中，记者塔拉·布朗指出，尽管关于他回归一级方程式的传言仍在流传，但霍纳只有在“他拥有车队的一部分所有权”的情况下才会回来。

    在红牛的20年统治期间，霍纳是车队成功的终极建筑师，但他从未拥有过运营的股份。现在，霍纳拥有九位数净财富，这种动态已经完全改变。

    如果一个陷入困境的中场车队或即将到来的制造商想要利用霍纳的冠军级专业知识来重建其项目，它必须提供董事会席位，而不仅仅是维修区。购买车队的一小部分股份立刻将霍纳从一名可替换的员工提升为特许经营伙伴。

    无论他决定花钱重返赛道，还是选择退休专注于自己的赛马，这位前红牛智囊已经确保任何未来的谈判都将完全按照他自己的条款进行。

    Christian Horner admits he doesn't need F1 return after £67 million Red Bull payout

    Christian Horner addresses his future in Formula 1 and his £67million severance package in a revealing new interview

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    When a high-profile Formula 1 executive is shown the door, the rumour mill immediately shifts to where they will land next. But in the case of Christian Horner, his departure from Red Bull Racing in 2025 came with a golden parachute so immense that it completely rewrote his career trajectory.

    Rather than scrambling to secure a new role on the pitwall, the 52-year-old is navigating his post-F1 reality as a highly liquid free agent. During a recent feature on 60 Minutes Australia, the focus pivoted away from the corporate politics that caused his downfall and honed in on the pure economics of a potential comeback. With a bank account heavily reinforced by an early contract termination, Horner is calculating his next move from a position of absolute leverage.

    When Red Bull GmbH terminated his employment, Horner still had over four years remaining on his contract. The financial toll of severing that agreement was recently laid bare. According to Red Bull Technology Limited's 2025 financial reports, Horner received a staggering payout of £67million (approximately $88.5million), with some paddock sources estimating the total package closer to $100million once additional contractual fees were settled.

    That level of generational wealth has fundamentally shifted how Horner approaches his next career move.

    "I think it gives you the luxury of time, that I'm in a privileged position that I don't need to go back to Formula 1," Horner explained during the broadcast. "The most precious thing I think in life is time."

    Despite embracing a quieter life on his English country estate and currently consulting for the British America's Cup sailing team, Horner was quick to confirm that his competitive drive has not faded.

    "I'm still a speed junkie," Horner admitted. "So whether it's on four wheels, two wheels, four legs... with horses. Show me the racing line."

    The consensus within the paddock is that Horner’s days of being a traditional, salaried employee are over. During the broadcast, reporter Tara Brown noted that while rumours of a Formula 1 return continue to swirl, Horner will only come back "if he has some ownership of a team".

    Throughout his 20-year reign at Red Bull, Horner was the ultimate architect of the team's success, yet he never possessed an equity stake in the operation. Now that Horner is armed with a nine-figure net worth, the dynamic has completely changed.

    If a struggling midfield constructor or an incoming manufacturer wants to leverage Horner's championship-winning expertise to rebuild its programme, it will have to offer a seat at the boardroom table, not just the pitwall. Purchasing a minority stake in a team instantly elevates Horner from a disposable employee to a franchise partner.

    Whether he decides to buy his way back onto the grid or stays in retirement focusing on his racehorses, the former Red Bull mastermind has ensured that any future negotiations will be conducted entirely on his own terms.
