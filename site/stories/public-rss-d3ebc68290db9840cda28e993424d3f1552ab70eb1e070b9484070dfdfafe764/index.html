    维斯塔潘透露考虑2027年戴通纳24小时耐力赛参赛

    在拉斯维加斯大奖赛之前，维斯塔潘访问了戴通纳。 荷兰人表示他主要是去与红牛拍摄内容，但这次访问也引发了他是否认真考虑参加在戴通纳国际赛道举行的耐力赛——Rolex 24的问题。

    维斯塔潘透露他在考虑参加2027年戴通纳24小时耐力赛

    维斯塔潘表示他目前正在探索是否有可能参加明年的戴通纳24小时耐力赛。如果他参加，将会驾驶GT3赛车

    在Formula 1拉斯维加斯大奖赛之前，维斯塔潘首先访问了戴通纳

    这位荷兰人说他主要是去和红牛拍摄内容，但这次访问也引发了是否他真的在认真考虑参加戴通纳国际赛道举行的耐力赛——Rolex 24的疑问。

    当Motorsport.com在拉斯维加斯的荷兰媒体会上问他这个问题时，维斯塔潘透露，下个冬天出现在发车线上并不是不可能的。

    “我们正在研究这是否可能，但到目前为止，这次访问更多的是关于和红牛拍摄一些东西。”

    德托纳是四次F1世界冠军愿望清单上的一项耐力赛。维斯塔潘确认，“那肯定是我想要参加的比赛。”

    这也是维斯塔潘在他的F1生涯中已经可以参加的比赛之一，因为这项赛事发生在冬季休赛期。2027年1月28日至31日的第65届德托纳24小时赛计划在这个周末举行。

    “是的，那场比赛在我的F1生涯中肯定已经可能了，所以我希望如此。但我们还得再等一会儿[消息]。我们还有时间。”

    维斯塔潘明确表示，他将驾驶GT3赛车参加那场比赛，在德托纳意味着参加GTD Pro组别。

    在纽博格林北环的冒险之旅以及他在波尔蒂马奥为自己的赛车队做客演出之后，这将标志着维斯塔潘在美国土地上首次参加GT赛车比赛。

    至于勒芒24小时耐力赛，维斯塔潘已经表示他明年不会在那里参赛。这位荷兰人只想在勒芒赛道上参加比赛，但需要非常充分的准备，但他解释说，德托纳的情况不同。

    “我真的很想在一辆hypercar中参加勒芒。然后你必须仔细考虑你想要如何接近它。你是想自己参加，还是想和一支工厂车队一起参加？我还有时间来解决这个问题。”

    “如果我想在德托纳或诺德舍夫勒赛道上比赛，那将会是在GT3赛车中。我不想说这更容易，但相比于hypercar，组织起来要容易一些。”

    “当有人指出，维斯塔潘至少会在F1的冬季休赛期给赛车迷们带来一些期待——就像费尔南多·阿隆索将带着达喀尔拉力赛一样——他笑了：“这很好，不是吗？我总是喜欢看到车手们在F1之外做其他事情，并且从其他赛车系列中也能获得乐趣。”"

    “当然，F1赛季已经够长了，但如果我能把它与带着家人一起参加活动结合起来，例如，我仍然会尝试参加其他赛事。”"

    这很可能也适用于2027年的纽博格林24小时耐力赛，尽管准备阶段的NLS赛事与F1大奖赛相冲突。然而，维斯塔潘笑了，他说：'幸运的是，我已经知道那条赛道！'

    他仍在考虑安排一个测试日，作为在艾菲尔地区24小时耐力赛准备的一部分。

    Max Verstappen reveals he is considering 2027 Daytona 24 Hours entry

    Verstappen says he is currently exploring whether it would be possible to compete in the Daytona 24 Hours next year. If he does, it would be in a GT3 car

    Ahead of the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, Max Verstappen first paid a visit to Daytona.

    The Dutchman said he was primarily there to film content with Red Bull, but the visit also raises the question of whether he is seriously considering taking part in the Rolex 24, the endurance race held on the road course of Daytona International Speedway.

    When Motorsport.com put that question to him during the Dutch media session in Las Vegas, Verstappen revealed that an appearance on the grid next winter has not been ruled out.

    "We're looking into it, whether that's possible, but for now the visit was more about filming things with Red Bull."

    Daytona is one of the endurance races high on the four-time F1 world champion's wish list. "That's definitely one that I want to do, yes," Verstappen confirmed.

    It is also one of the races Verstappen could already contest during his F1 career, as the event takes place during the winter break. The 65th edition of the Daytona 24 Hours is scheduled for the weekend of 28-31 January 2027.

    "Yes, that race is definitely possible already during my F1 career, so I hope so. But we'll have to wait a little longer [for news]. We still have some time for that as well."

    Verstappen made it clear that he would contest that race in a GT3 car, which at Daytona would mean competing in the GTD Pro category.

    Following his adventures on the Nürburgring Nordschleife and the guest appearance for his own racing team at Portimao, it would mark Verstappen's first GT racing outing on American soil.

    As for the Le Mans 24 Hours, Verstappen has already indicated that he will not compete there next year. The Dutchman only wants to race at the Circuit de la Sarthe with very thorough preparation, but he explained that Daytona is a different story.

    "I really only want to do Le Mans in a hypercar. And then you have to think carefully about how you want to approach it. Do you want to do it yourself, or do you want to do it with a factory team? I still have time to figure that out as well.

    "If I want to race at Daytona, or on the Nordschleife, that would be in a GT3 car. I don't want to say that's easier, but it is a little easier to organise than with a hypercar."

    When it was pointed out that Verstappen would at least be giving motorsport fans something to look forward to during the F1 winter break – just as Fernando Alonso will do with the Dakar Rally – he laughed: "That's nice, isn't it? I always enjoy seeing drivers doing other things outside F1 and also getting enjoyment out of other racing series.

    "The F1 season is already long enough, of course, but if I can combine it with bringing the family along, for example, then I'll still try to take part in other events."

    That will most likely also apply to the Nürburgring 24 Hours in 2027, although the preparatory NLS races clash with F1 Grands Prix. Verstappen laughed, however: "Luckily, I already know that track!"

    He is still considering arranging a test day with his team as part of the preparations for the 24-hour race in the Eifel region.

    "Yes, but of course you also have to look at that with the track, to see which days are possible. But we're going to see whether we can schedule another test day, because that's what every good team competing there does. So yes, that's definitely something we're going to look into as well."
