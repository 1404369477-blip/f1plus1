    汉密尔顿：拉斯维加斯大奖赛处罚"实在太过分"，领奖台机会溜走

    汉密尔顿表示，他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛中的10秒处罚"实在太过分"，因为在混乱的比赛中，他的领奖台机会从手中溜走。

    汉密尔顿：拉斯维加斯大奖赛处罚"实在太过分"，领奖台机会溜走

    法拉利车手表示，尽管受到10秒处罚并被队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔（勒克莱尔）旋转，但他本可以完成领奖台

    汉密尔顿、法拉利、兰多·诺里斯、麦克拉伦

    为法拉利车迷。最新的法拉利F1新闻、车手更新、技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远处的赛事反应

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿表示，他在拉斯维加斯大奖赛中的10秒罚时“非常严厉”，因为他在混乱的比赛中失去了登上领奖台的机会。

    汉密尔顿在Q2中两辆法拉利都被淘汰后，从第11位起步。他迅速攀升，但因为在与麦克拉伦车队的兰多·诺里斯的比赛中，在7-8弯道处离开赛道并获得优势而被罚。

    然后，当他在争夺第六名时，队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔将他旋转了一圈，他失去了之前追回的很多位置，降至第10位。汉密尔顿奋力反击，最终排名第7，而勒克莱尔被罚，排名第10。

    “我们知道这个周末我们面临着巨大的挑战。我们没有意识到直线上的限制会如此严重，”汉密尔顿对天空体育说。

    “显然，资格赛很困难，但在比赛中我感觉很好，我真的进步很大。”

    他没有掩饰自己对处罚的感受。

    “然后避免与一辆麦拉伦相撞让我得到了10秒的处罚，我认为这非常严厉，这很令人沮丧。但我只是把头低下，继续追赶，并设法重新回到了比赛中，”他说。

    “然后我又被击退了。但我从未放弃，我只是试图取得最好的成绩。我真的认为我们今天本可以拿到领奖台的。”

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    汉密尔顿在比赛中也没有掩饰自己的情绪。在一个没有在世界转播中播出的信息中，他暗示了勒克莱尔在墨西哥因类似场外超车而受到的五秒罚时，说：“他们给我10秒是为了避免事故。”

    法拉利车队负责人弗雷德·瓦瑟尔同意这个处罚过于严厉，说：“说实话，我对10秒的处罚感到惊讶。”

    汉密尔顿不愿就与勒克莱尔的碰撞发表评论，说这是“你需要与法拉利讨论的事情。”

    在墨西哥站之后，有人问他是否是时候为两位法拉利车手制定适当的规则了，他说："我已经尽我所能，我说了一切，甚至更多。"

    "我能做的只是出现并尽我所能，希望他们在幕后最终会明白这一切。"

    这个结果让汉密尔顿在267分的积分榜上上升到第三位，领先乔治·拉塞尔和兰多·诺里斯三分，还有两场比赛要进行。

    Lewis Hamilton: Las Vegas penalty "really harsh" as podium chance slipped

    The Ferrari driver says he could have finished on the podium, despite a 10-second penalty and being spun around by team-mate Charles Leclerc

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Lando Norris, McLaren

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton says his 10-second penalty at the Las Vegas Grand Prix was "really harsh" as his chance of a podium slipped from his fingers during a chaotic race.

    Hamilton started 11th after both Ferraris were knocked out in Q2. He climbed quickly through the field, but was penalised for leaving the track and gaining an advantage at the Turn 7-8 chicane while battling McLaren's Lando Norris.

    He then lost even more of the ground he'd made up when team-mate Charles Leclerc spun him around while they fought over sixth, dropping him to 10th. Hamilton fought back to finish seventh, while Leclerc was penalised and finished 10th.

    "We knew that we were faced with massive challenges this weekend. We didn't realise there would be such a limitation on the straights," Hamilton told Sky Sports.

    "The qualifying, obviously, was difficult, but in the race I felt great, and I really was progressing well."

    He didn't hide how he felt about the penalty.

    "Then avoiding collision with one of the McLarens got me a 10-second penalty, which was, I think, really harsh, which was frustrating. But I just got my head back down and just kept chasing and managed to get back in the fight," he said.

    "Then again, I got knocked back again. But I never gave up, and I was just trying to get the best results. I really think we could have had a podium today."

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    Hamilton didn't hide his feelings during the race, either. In a message not broadcast on the world feed, he hinted at the five-second penalty Leclerc was given for a similar off-track pass in Mexico, saying: "They give me 10 for avoiding an incident."

    Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur agreed the punishment was too harsh, saying: "Honestly, I was surprised with the 10 seconds."

    Hamilton would not be drawn on the collision with Leclerc, saying it was "something for you to speak to Ferrari about".

    Asked whether it was time for proper rules between the two Ferrari drivers after Mexico, he said: "I've done all I can, and I've said everything and more."

    "All I can do is just show up and give it everything I can, and hopefully in the background they'll eventually figure it out."

    The result moves Hamilton up to third in the drivers' standings on 267 points, three ahead of George Russell and Lando Norris, with two races to go.
