    法拉利勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿的矛盾远未解决

    法拉利车手勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿在巴西站期间经车队领队瓦塞尔调解后表面和睦，但文章认为两人之间的紧张关系远未真正解决。

    别被法拉利F1车手在巴西的和睦表现所迷惑；查尔斯·勒克莱尔与刘易斯·汉密尔顿之间仍然存在非常真实的紧张关系。

    在汉密尔顿于墨西哥站因碰撞错失胜利后对勒克莱尔提出非同寻常的批评之后，两位车手与车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔在巴西聚在一起试图沟通解决问题。

    那次会议被证明是有建设性的，勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿在巴西大奖赛期间表现得非常规矩，甚至遵守了车队指令并给彼此留出了充足的空间，与墨西哥站不同。

    汉密尔顿的总结是：“一切都好。弗雷德把我们聚到一起。弗雷德在会议上很棒。然后他就让我和查尔斯各自表达观点。”

    “我们双方进行了一次非常好、非常坦诚的讨论。现在一切都好了。我们只需继续向前。”

    但墨西哥和巴西的情况不能相提并论，因此瓦塞尔没有费心去寻求那种本可轻易得到的赞许。

    “这两种情况无法比较，”瓦塞尔在谈到勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿在英特拉格斯赛道上表现出的友好时说道。

    “老实说，我们在周五进行了一次很好的讨论。我们把情况说清楚了。我们在心态上做了一次很好的调整。

    “他们一起开着玩笑离开了我的房间。这很好。我希望这能持续下去，保持这样。”

    瓦塞尔目前能做的只有“希望”这能持续下去，因为巴西那场争斗只可能是在积分区靠后的位置上展开，而不是争夺分站冠军或领奖台。

    所以完全有理由相信，当法拉利赛车重新具备争胜实力时——无论是在拉斯维加斯、卡塔尔还是阿布扎比——汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔都会重新拼尽全力地较量。

    毕竟，勒克莱尔在会后承认：“当然会有紧张气氛，当然我们都想跑在对方前面，有时我们也会做错事，有时在对事情的看法上存在分歧也没关系。”

    “但与此同时，讨论这些内容会有很大帮助。”

    鉴于勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿之间的紧张关系，勒克莱尔在法拉利的前队友卡洛斯·塞恩斯被问及他与勒克莱尔共事的那段时光。

    毕竟，汉密尔顿曾指出勒克莱尔与塞恩斯和塞巴斯蒂安·维特尔共事的经历，以此作为他对队友竞争更激烈的证据。

    “我对与查尔斯共事的时光以及我们之间的较量有着相对美好的回忆，”塞恩斯在巴西说道。

    "确实，当你在为胜利而战时，压力总是更大，后续的事情也更多。所以你会自然而然地比在中游为第八或第九名而战时更自私一些。"

    "也许更难完全遵循车队希望你遵循的理想情况，因为前方有胜利在望。当时前方也有胜利，但我一直尊重查尔斯。他也一直尊重我。我们从未有过接触或类似的事情。我们只是有过激烈的争斗。你还记得蒙扎、拉斯维加斯。那一年有很多激烈的时刻。"

    "更困难的是车迷群体。当时公关和车迷群体真的被点燃了，在查尔斯和我之间非常两极分化。我记得有点受到查尔斯非常铁杆车迷的辱骂或诅咒的困扰。"

    "也许一些意大利媒体也说我在需要的时候不够二号车手，反之亦然，每当查尔斯不得不这样做的时候。"

    所以，更多的是围绕法拉利的压力，涉及法拉利，而不是我和查尔斯之间实际存在的问题，那些问题总是在当晚我们用更冷静的头脑互相打电话时就解决了，我们总能找到解决方案和前进的方向。

    塞恩斯觉得，同一支车队里有两个平等的车手，这是一个不可避免的问题。

    “我不记得有哪支一级方程式车队拥有两位优秀车手在前方争夺，却在为胜利而战时没有出现过问题，”塞恩斯补充道。

    “你可以选择让一位二号车手担任第二车手，或者你可以拥有两位优秀车手，而他们会制造那些问题。但在我看来，你不能只要其一而不要其二。而且你永远不可能只要其一而不要其二。

    “也许在法拉利，涉及法拉利的一切会让情况变得有些加剧，但我想不起有哪一刻没发生过这种事。梅赛德斯发生过。过去几年迈凯伦发生过。法拉利也发生过。”

    “所以我不知道什么时候没发生过。这是F1的一部分。如果你是车队领队，而且你知道自己将拥有两位伟大的车手，你就总会遇到这个问题。”

    “我不认为你能让那两个人百分之百冷静下来，还指望他们不为分站冠军发起进攻。”

    这与2019年的紧张局势有某些相似之处，当时勒克莱尔以21岁的年纪成功从四届世界冠军维特尔手中夺走了法拉利的头号地位。Mark Hughes的这篇文章至今仍是关于那段往事的出色记述。

    那时，车手们可以自由竞争，车队领队（马蒂亚·比诺托）试图公平行事，车手们在表现上足够接近，经常在赛道上彼此靠近，并且有一场激烈的霸权争夺战。

    勒克莱尔最终令人信服地赢得了那场战斗，塞恩斯被引入以取代维特尔，但从未与勒克莱尔平起平坐。（这也不意味着他是一个尽职的二号车手。他太快了，不适合那样。）

    勒克莱尔和塞恩斯有过摩擦点，但最终事情从未失控地爆发，原因很简单：大约三分之二的时间里，勒克莱尔都领先于塞恩斯。

    因此，动态非常不同：勒克莱尔甚至最终帮助塞恩斯在2023年新加坡站取得了唯一一场非维斯塔潘的胜利。这证明了勒克莱尔在放长线钓大鱼，他知道在那里做出小小的牺牲只会在他一旦拥有法拉利争冠赛车时加强他的地位。

    最终，两人之间的性能差距足够大，因此从未产生过太多针锋相对。勒克莱尔并不把塞恩斯视为对自己法拉利领军地位的威胁。他是对的，因为在那场新加坡胜利仅几个月后，法拉利就选择用汉密尔顿取代了塞恩斯。

    而在2025年，勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿之间存在明显的性能差距，这意味着两人之间没有太多针锋相对，这并非巧合。

    突然间，他们在2026年实力非常接近，勒克莱尔-维特尔时代的紧张关系立刻重新暴露出来，因为现在勒克莱尔的位置受到了威胁。勒克莱尔此前相当从容，就像维特尔在2015-18年作为车队核心时那样，队友基米·莱科宁很大程度上只是辅助角色。

    现在，队内主导权之争已经打响。如果法拉利明年改进其动力单元并拥有争冠赛车，汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔都希望成为引领车队冲锋的那个人。

    这就是为什么认为法拉利在巴西的会议能给勒克莱尔与汉密尔顿之间的问题画上句号的想法纯属无稽之谈。

    这种情况不会改变，直到勒克莱尔像当年击败维特尔那样再次击溃另一位世界冠军，或者直到他自己被队友击溃。

    英文原文

    Don't be fooled by Ferrari's Formula 1 drivers playing nicely in Brazil; there are still very real tensions between Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton.

    The drivers and team principal Fred Vasseur got together in Brazil to try to talk things through after Hamilton's extraordinary criticism of Leclerc following their victory-losing Mexico run-in.

    That meeting proved constructive, and Leclerc and Hamilton were very well-behaved during the Brazilian Grand Prix, even following team orders and leaving each other plenty of room, unlike in Mexico.

    Hamilton's summary was: "Everything's good. Fred brought us together. Fred was great in the meeting. And then he just left me and Charles to just give our points.

    "We both had a really good, open discussion. And it's all good now. We'll just move on."

    But you can't compare Mexico and Brazil, so Vasseur didn't bother reaching for what would have been an easy pat-on-the-back.

    "You can't compare the two situations," Vasseur said, in respect of Leclerc and Hamilton's cordiality on-track at Interlagos.

    "Honestly, we had a good discussion on Friday. We cleared the situation. We did a good reset on the mindset.

    "They left my room joking together. It's good. I hope that it will last, to stay like this."

    All Vasseur can do for now is "hope" it lasts because that Brazil fight was only ever going to be for minor points-paying positions, not a race win or a podium.

    So there's every reason to believe that when the Ferrari is back fighting for wins, be it in Las Vegas or Qatar or Abu Dhabi, Hamilton and Leclerc are going to be back fighting tooth and nail.

    After all, Leclerc admitted post-meeting: "Of course there is tension, of course we want to finish ahead of each other, and sometimes we are not going to do things right, and it's OK sometimes to disagree on the way you see things.

    "But at the same time discussing about those helps a whole lot."

    In light of the Leclerc-Hamilton tensions, Leclerc's ex-team-mate at Ferrari Carlos Sainz was asked about his time with Leclerc.

    Hamilton had, after all, pointed to Leclerc's time alongside Sainz and Sebastian Vettel as evidence that he races his team-mates harder.

    "I have relatively good memories of my time with Charles and the fights we had," Sainz said in Brazil.

    "It is true that when you're fighting for wins, there's always more pressure, more things down the line. So you become naturally a bit more selfish than when you are fighting in the midfield for a P8 or a P9.

    "It's more difficult maybe to follow exactly the ideal situation that the team would like you to follow, because there's a win down the line. There was wins down the line back then, but I always respected Charles. He always respected me. We never had contacts or anything like this. We just had strong fights. You remember Monza, Vegas. There was plenty of hot moments during the year.

    "What was difficult more was the fanbase. The PR and the fanbase was really ignited back then, and it was very polarised between Charles and me. And I remember suffering a bit from the very hardcore fans of Charles insulting or wishing me bad.

    "Maybe some Italian media also saying that I was not being enough of a number two when I needed to be, and vice-versa whenever Charles had to do it.

    "So it's more the pressure surrounding Ferrari, that involves Ferrari, than the actual issues that I had with Charles, which were always resolved that same night when we called each other with a more cold head and we always managed to find the solution and the way forward."

    Sainz felt it is an inevitable problem with having two equals in the same team.

    "I cannot remember no Formula 1 team that has had two good drivers fighting at the front that haven't had issues when fighting for wins," Sainz added.

    "You can choose whether to have a number two as a second driver, or you can have two good drivers that will create those issues. But you cannot have one without the other, in my opinion. And you will never have one without the other.

    "Maybe in Ferrari it gets a bit exacerbated with everything that involves Ferrari, but I cannot remember a moment where this hasn't happened. It happened in Mercedes. It happened in McLaren the last few years. It happened in Ferrari.

    "So I don’t know when it hasn't happened. It is part of Formula 1. And if you are a team principal and you know you're going to have two great drivers, you're always going to have that issue.

    "I don't think you can ever calm those two guys 100% down and expect them not to go for a move for a race win."

    There are some parallels to the tensions in 2019 when Leclerc managed to steal Ferrari away from four-time world champion Sebastian Vettel as a 21-year-old. This piece by Mark Hughes still stands as an excellent account of that.

    Back then, the drivers were 'free to race', the team principal (Mattia Binotto) tried to play things fairly, the drivers were close enough in performance to routinely be near each other on track, and there was a huge battle for supremacy.

    Leclerc ultimately won that battle convincingly, with Sainz brought into replace Vettel, but never as an equal to Leclerc. (That didn't mean he was a dutiful number two either. He was too quick for that.)

    Leclerc and Sainz had their flashpoints, but ultimately things never uncontrollably spilt over for one good reason: Leclerc was ahead of Sainz around two-thirds of the time.

    The dynamic therefore was very different: Leclerc even ended up helping Sainz to the only non-Verstappen victory of 2023 in Singapore. That was evidence of Leclerc playing the long game, knowing a small sacrifice there would only strengthen his position once Ferrari had a title-challenging car.

    There was ultimately enough of a performance gap for there never to be too much needle between them. Leclerc didn't see Sainz as a threat to his position as Ferrari's spearhead. He was right, as Ferrari chose to replace Sainz with Hamilton just a few months after that Singapore win.

    And it's no coincidence that in 2025, there being a clear performance gap between Leclerc and Hamilton meant there wasn't much needle between them.

    Suddenly they're very closely matched in 2026, and the Leclerc-Vettel era tension is immediately re-exposed because now there is a threat to Leclerc's position. Leclerc was previously fairly comfortable, like Vettel was in 2015-18 as the focal point of the team, with team-mate Kimi Raikkonen very much a support act.

    Now there is an active battle for supremacy. If Ferrari improves its power unit and has a title-challenging car next year, both Hamilton and Leclerc want to be the one leading that charge.

    It's why the prospect of Ferrari's Brazil meeting being any kind of full stop on its Leclerc-Hamilton problem is absolute nonsense.

    That's not going to change until Leclerc has destroyed another world champion - as he did to Vettel - or until he's the one destroyed by a team-mate.
