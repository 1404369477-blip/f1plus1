    伊萨克·哈贾尔因伤缺席F1荷兰大奖赛，红牛确认劳森替补

    红牛车队正式确认，利亚姆·劳森将在本周末的F1荷兰大奖赛中替补受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔。哈贾尔因在健身房训练时手腕受伤，将缺席赞德福特站的比赛。

    伊萨克·哈德贾尔将缺席F1荷兰大奖赛，红牛替补车手已确认

    哈德贾尔受伤，将缺席本周末的大奖赛

    红牛官方确认，利亚姆·劳森将代替受伤的伊萨克·哈德贾尔参加本周末的F1荷兰大奖赛。

    周三上午有消息称，哈德贾尔在一次健身房训练中手腕受伤，因此将缺席赞德沃特的比赛，正式决定即将公布。

    这意味着哈贾尔将无法参加这条赛道——目前为止他唯一一次登上F1领奖台正是在这里取得的，那是在2025年他令人印象深刻的新秀赛季中，他为小红牛（小红牛）拿下第三名。

    车队在向Motorsport.com提供的一份声明中确认：“甲骨文红牛车队车手伊萨克·哈贾尔在夏休期期间手腕受伤，将无法参加2026年荷兰大奖赛的比赛。

    “我们将迎回利亚姆·劳森，他将在本周末驾驶小红牛22取代他的位置。

    “车队祝愿伊萨克早日康复，并感谢利亚姆在短时间内临危受命。”

    劳森现在将再次获得机会，成为马克斯·维斯塔潘的车队队友，此前他在2025赛季前两站曾与维斯塔潘搭档，随后被降级回小红牛（小红牛）。

    但这位24岁的车手如今更加成熟，本赛季在红牛姊妹车队表现出色，劳森在11站比赛后排名第九，今年至今只有三站大奖赛未拿到积分。

    "由于车手变动，替补车手角田裕毅将于本周末在赞德沃特为小红牛（小红牛）出战，与阿维德·林德布拉德搭档，"小红牛（小红牛）在发给Motorsport.com的声明中补充道。

    "我们感谢利亚姆和角田裕毅在车队备战本周末比赛时所展现的适应能力，并祝愿伊萨克早日康复。"

    这条消息也意味着角田裕毅将重返F1，他是劳森在红牛的继任者，但在去年与维斯塔潘搭档时表现十分挣扎，因而在2026年赛场上失去了席位。

    这位拥有111场大奖赛起步经历的26岁车手成为了红牛的储备车手，因此将与新秀阿维德·林德布拉德搭档，代表老东家小红牛出战它在赛历上的最后一站——荷兰大奖赛。

    据了解，角田裕毅正在与迈耶·尚克车队（Meyer Shank 比赛）洽谈2027年的印地赛车（IndyCar）席位。

    Isack Hadjar to miss F1 Dutch GP, Red Bull replacement confirmed

    Hadjar has sustained an injury so will miss this weekend's grand prix

    Red Bull has officially confirmed that Liam Lawson will replace the injured Isack Hadjar at this weekend’s Formula 1 Dutch Grand Prix.

    News emerged on Wednesday morning that Hadjar had sustained a wrist injury from a gym session and was therefore set to skip Zandvoort, with a formal decision impending.

    It means Hadjar is unable to compete at the track which has so far given him his only F1 podium, after taking third for Racing Bulls during his impressive rookie campaign in 2025.

    "Oracle Red Bull Racing driver, Isack Hadjar, has sustained a wrist injury during summer shutdown that will prevent him from racing at the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix," confirmed the team in a statement shared with Motorsport.com.

    "We will be welcoming back Liam Lawson who will replace him in RB22 this weekend.

    "The team wishes Isack a speedy recovery and thanks Liam for stepping in on short notice."

    Lawson will now receive the opportunity to once again be Max Verstappen’s team-mate, after doing so for the first two rounds of 2025 before being demoted back to Racing Bulls.

    But the 24-year-old is now more mature and has impressed with Red Bull’s sister squad this season, as Lawson sits ninth after 11 rounds with points in all but three grands prix so far this year.

    "As a result of the driver change, reserve driver Yuki Tsunoda, will race for Racing Bulls in Zandvoort this weekend, alongside Arvid Lindblad," added Racing Bulls in a statement shared with Motorsport.com.

    "We thank both Liam and Yuki for their adaptability as the teams prepare for the weekend and we wish Isack a speedy recovery."

    This news also means an F1 return for Lawson’s Red Bull successor Yuki Tsunoda, who was left without a seat on the 2026 grid after seriously struggling alongside Verstappen last year.

    The 26-year-old with 111 grand prix starts became a Red Bull reserve, so will partner rookie Arvid Lindblad at former squad Racing Bulls for the final Dutch GP before it drops off the calendar.

    Tsunoda is understood to be in talks for an IndyCar drive at Meyer Shank Racing in 2027.
