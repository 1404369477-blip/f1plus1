    梅赛德斯一级方程式车队负责人Toto Wolff表示，Brackley车队在墨西哥大奖赛的发车问题让乔治·拉塞尔感到失望。

    拉塞尔在发车位置上从第二位起步，仅以0.009秒的差距输给了七冠王刘易斯·汉密尔顿，但在发车时失去了位置。随后，他因为第一圈与红牛车队的伊萨克·哈德贾发生碰撞而被处以10秒罚时。

    尽管拉塞尔的队友基米·安东内利获得了第三名并赢得了他的首个冠军头衔，但拉塞尔却排在第六位。

    “我们今天又一次让他失望了，”Wolff在比赛后对天空体育F1说。 “在那个阶段，我们不知道发生了什么。我相信他是在这里为胜利而战，然后出现了这种能量不足的情况，毁了这场比赛。”

    “我们有两个车手，但我们没有。这辆车今年对他来说根本不起作用。然后像今天这样的不幸，我们只能为他解决这个问题。”

    “这对他是非常困难的一刻；非常、非常、非常困难的一刻。我的目标是提供给他所需的设备和支持，让他为冠军而战。”

    “拉塞尔获得第六名意味着他从第二名下滑到第四名，在车手积分榜上。这位英国人现在落后第二名维斯塔潘11分，落后第三名汉密尔顿9分。”

    Toto Wolff apologises to George Russell after Mercedes "let him down" at Mexican GP

    Toto Wolff admitted the team had let George Russell down after an energy deployment issue hampered his Mexican GP

    Toto Wolff, Executive Director of Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team, George Russell, Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes Formula 1 team principal Toto Wolff claimed the Brackley outfit "let down" George Russell after a deployment issue spoiled his start at the Mexican Grand Prix.

    Russell started from second on the grid after missing out on pole position to seven-time champion Lewis Hamilton by just 0.009 seconds, but he lost ground at the start. He was then given a 10-second penalty for a first-lap collision with Red Bull's Isack Hadjar.

    While Russell's team-mate Kimi Antonelli finished third and claimed his maiden championship title, Russell was left down in sixth.

    "We completely let him down today again," Wolff told Sky Sports F1 after the race. "At that stage, we don't know what happened. I believe he was racing for the victory here and then came this lack of energy and ruined his race.

    "We have two drivers, we don't have one. The car just didn't work for him this year. And then misfortune like today, we just have to sort it out for him.

    "This is a very difficult moment for him; very, very, very difficult moment. My aim is to provide him with the equipment and the support he needs to fight for a championship."

    Russell's sixth-place finish meant he slipped from second to fourth in the drivers' standings. The Briton now sits 11 points behind second-placed Max Verstappen and nine points behind third-placed Hamilton.

    Footage of Wolff apologising to Russell after the race quickly gained traction on social media. "I have gained a lot of respect for George. It’s gotten away from him, but he’s handled it with a lot of grace, honestly. Better man than me," one fan commented on Reddit.

    "I've lost count of the number of times Toto has had to apologise to George this season," another fan posted, while someone else commented: "I really hope they sort it out and he comes back next year fighting."
