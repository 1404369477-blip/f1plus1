    耐克回归F1，24年后再次成为官方合作伙伴

    耐克将重返F1，成为官方合作伙伴，并在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛期间推出首个合作项目。耐克将取代德国品牌彪马，成为F1的官方服装和制服供应商，并与粉丝共享官方耐克F1商品。

    耐克将重返F1，成为官方合作伙伴，并在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛期间推出首个合作项目。

    美国时尚和运动品牌耐克将取代德国品牌彪马，成为F1的官方服装和制服供应商，并与粉丝共享官方耐克F1商品。

    首个F1 x 耐克系列将于11月20日推出，限量版拉斯维加斯大奖赛品牌系列将在城市的威尼斯人酒店F1中心以及耐克拉斯维加斯门店上市。

    耐克还将取代彪马，成为F1学院的官方合作伙伴，通过车手服装和粉丝活动来实现。

    艾利奥特·希尔，耐克总裁兼首席执行官表示：“一级方程式赛车运动由竞争、速度和对创新的持续推动所驱动。这是一种运动员的心态，也是我们在耐克所共有的。我们一起拥有一个不可思议的机会，为这项运动带来新的活力，服务于其运动员，并激励全球的粉丝。”

    The品牌还为2026年迈阿密大奖赛与凯迪拉克F1车队合作推出了一款鞋款——仅生产了两双，分别赠予首席执行官丹·托里斯和首席品牌顾问卡西迪·托里斯。

    阿尔法塔ウリ（AlphaTauri）——之前为F1提供制服——将继续作为FIA的服装供应商，至少到2027年底。

    一级方程式赛车运动首席执行官兼总裁斯蒂法诺·多梅尼卡利表示：“一级方程式赛车运动和耐克是世界上最标志性的品牌之一，它们由对创新、最高水平的性能以及激励人们超越可能的不断追求所联合。”

    “一级方程式赛车正经历前所未有的全球增长，吸引了数亿粉丝，并参与其中最热情、多元和充满活力的体育社区之一。”

    他还补充说，这项合作是“两个塑造文化、挑战传统并设定新标准卓越的品牌汇聚在一起。”

    “耐克在体育、文化和灵感交汇的独特能力使他们在我们进入下一个篇章时成为理想的合作伙伴。”

    英文原文

    Nike will become an official partner to Formula 1 after 24 years away from the motorsport and launch its first collaboration during the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    The American fashion and sportswear brand will replace German brand Puma to become F1's official apparel and uniform supplier in a long-term partnership that also offers fans the chance to buy official Nike F1 merchandise.

    The first Formula 1 x Nike collection will be launched on November 20 when a limited-edition Las Vegas GP-branded collection lands at the F1 Hub in the city's Venetian Hotel, plus Nike's Las Vegas stores.

    Nike will also replace Puma to become F1 Academy's official partner through driver apparel and fan activations.

    Elliott Hill, president and CEO of Nike, said: “Formula 1 is driven by competition, speed and a constant push to innovate. That’s an athlete mindset, and it’s one we share at Nike. Together, we have an incredible opportunity to bring new energy to the sport, serve its athletes and inspire fans around the world.”

    The brand also created a shoe with the Cadillac F1 team for the 2026 Miami GP - producing just two pairs that were gifted to CEO Dan Towriss and chief brand advisor Cassidy Towriss.

    AlphaTauri - which previously supplied uniform to F1 - continues as the FIA's apparel supplier until at least the end of 2027.

    Stefano Domenicali, F1 CEO and president, said: “Formula 1 and Nike are two of the world’s most iconic brands, united by a relentless drive to innovate, perform at the highest level, and inspire people to push beyond what is possible.

    "Formula 1 is experiencing unprecedented global growth, reaching hundreds of millions of fans and engaging one of the most passionate, diverse and dynamic communities in sport.

    "Nike’s unique ability to sit at the intersection of sport, culture and inspiration makes them the ideal partner as we enter our next chapter.”

    He added that the partnership is "the coming together of two brands that shape culture, challenge convention and set new standards of excellence."
