    勒克莱尔在巴西大奖赛上佩戴价值125,000美元的腕表引关注

    法拉利一队车手勒克莱尔在巴西大奖赛前佩戴了一款价值125,000美元的Richard Mille RM 33-02腕表。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔在F1巴西大奖赛上佩戴价值125,000美元的Richard Mille手表引人注目

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔在迎接他的第一个孩子后的几天，戴着一块价值125,000美元的理查德·米勒手表抵达巴西大奖赛

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    法拉利一级方程式车手查尔斯·勒克莱尔在巴西大奖赛之前抵达Interlagos，手腕上戴着一块六位数的手表。

    根据名人手表追踪账号Watches of Celebrities的报道，这位摩纳哥车手在抵达维修区时佩戴了一款理查德·米勒RM 33-02碳纤维TPT和玫瑰金款手表。该账号估计其市场价为125,000美元。

    RM 33-02在发布时限量140件，当时报道的售价为145,000美元。

    该设计偏离了瑞士品牌典型的独特外观。理查德·米勒最出名的是其桶形表壳，但RM 33-02采用由多层碳纤维制成的圆形表壳，并配备铂金摆轮。

    勒克莱尔是理查德·米勒的代言人，多年来一直被看到佩戴该品牌的许多手表，包括他自己的RM 67-02 勒克莱尔。

    “自从他在卡丁车时代的早期，我就一直密切关注查尔斯的进步，”米勒谈到法拉利车手时说。

    “以他的成绩和快速的预选赛而闻名，这位车手吸引了我的注意和热情，立即说服了品牌支持他初露头角的天赋。”

    勒克莱尔在妻子亚历山德拉在比赛周末前几天生下他们的第一个孩子比安卡后，比以往晚些时候到达圣保罗。

    “对我来说，这是一周非常、非常特别的日子，”他在周六预选赛结束后对媒体说。“这可能是我一生中最好的几周之一，显然。有职业生活，也有家庭生活。在那个时刻，家庭生活才是唯一重要的事情。而这周对我来说是极其特别的。”

    当被问及是否在赛道上集中注意力很困难时，勒克莱尔补充道："作为一名车手，当你关闭风挡时，你会有种重置的感觉。在我的生活中，我曾有过其他一些非常困难的时刻。而这次，事情完全相反，一切都非常不可思议。"

    "但在我的私人生活中，无论是这两种极端情况，我认为一旦你关闭风挡，你就会非常专注于唯一的一件事。"

    Charles Leclerc turns heads with $125,000 watch at F1 Brazilian GP

    Charles Leclerc arrived at the Brazilian Grand Prix wearing a Richard Mille watch valued at $125,000, days after welcoming his first child

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Ferrari Formula 1 driver Charles Leclerc arrived at Interlagos ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix wearing a six-figure watch on his wrist.

    According to watch-spotting account Watches of Celebrities, the Monegasque driver wore a Richard Mille RM 33-02 in Carbon TPT and rose gold when he arrived at the paddock. The account puts its market price at $125,000.

    The RM 33-02 was limited to 140 pieces when it launched and was reported to cost $145,000 at the time.

    The design strays from the distinctive look typical of the Swiss brand. Richard Mille is best known for its barrel-shaped cases, but the RM 33-02 has a round case made from layers of carbon fibre and features a platinum rotor.

    Leclerc is a Richard Mille ambassador and has been seen wearing plenty of the brand's watches over the years, including his own RM 67-02 Charles Leclerc.

    "Since his early days in karting, I have closely followed Charles’ progress," Mille said of the Ferrari driver.

    "Noted for his times, and his speedy qualifiers, the driver attracted my attention and enthusiasm, immediately convincing the brand to support his budding talent."

    Leclerc arrived in São Paulo later than usual after his wife, Alexandra, gave birth to their first child, Bianca, a few days before the race weekend.

    "It's been a very, very special week for me," he told the media after qualifying on Saturday. "Probably one of the best weeks of my life, obviously. There's the professional life, and there's also the life that is at home. And the life at home in those moments is the only thing that matters. And this week was incredibly special."

    When asked if it was difficult to concentrate on track, Leclerc added: "When you close the visor as a driver, you kind of reset. I've had other moments where in my life things were very difficult. And this time it's on the other side of the spectrum where things are incredible.

    "But in both those extremes in my private life, I think once you close the visor, you are really very much focused on one thing and one thing only."
