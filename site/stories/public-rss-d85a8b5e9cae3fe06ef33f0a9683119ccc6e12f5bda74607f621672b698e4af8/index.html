    拉斯维加斯大奖赛：混乱非凡之战的赢家与输家

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛上演了一场载入史册的比赛，全场共出现35次领先位置变化，队友之间发生冲突，情绪一度升温。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛的赢家与输家

    F1在拉斯维加斯奉献了一场载入史册的比赛，队友之间发生冲突，情绪一度升温

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队；乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    多辆赛车被压缩在数秒之内，前三名车手轮流占据领先位置，全场共出现35次领先位置变化，车手们在最后一圈努力寻找最佳位置，以便在冲线时处于领先；拉斯维加斯大奖赛感觉像是印地500大赛那种光鲜亮丽、由电池助力的街道赛道版本。

    在美国这座人造娱乐之都，F1上演了一场颇具娱乐性、 albeit 有些刻意的表演，这倒也相得益彰。它或许不是每个人的菜，但你很难找到任何到场的人不觉得自己辛苦赚来的钱花得值。

    在拉斯维加斯大道上夺冠需要什么？胆识和赛车技巧，当然。但还需要脑力。找出正确的能量部署策略以保持在前列，然后调整该策略，以便在最后一圈冲线时领先，而不是在1号弯前领先。

    维斯塔潘通过与红牛的沟通，就这一主题透露了一些他的想法。“你们必须关注最后一圈的能量部署，因为他们已经弄明白了。他们能一直领先我到冲线，所以我们需要看看仅针对最后一圈我们需要做什么。”

    最终，他的计划奏效了，他挡住了兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里。但在此之前，他还嘲讽了乔治·拉塞尔对基米·安东内利未果的超越——就在一天前，他在排位赛中与这位梅赛德斯车手发生了冲突。

    “他在赛道边说了很多话，老实说，这并不真的困扰我，因为他远低于我的水平，所以对我来说，试图去挑战这一点根本没有任何意义，你明白吗？今天，因果报应真是讽刺。老实说，当我看向后视镜，看到正在发生的事情时，那感觉真是棒极了，所以我想，‘太棒了，我这一天只会越来越好。’”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在六场比赛中第四次获胜，红牛正为2027年冠军争夺积累势头

    维斯塔潘的言论有些不得体且没有必要，但这是最新的例子，表明他或许并不是拉塞尔试图打心理战的合适对象。

    无论如何，这起事件与拉塞尔在此处的入选无关。那是因为拉塞尔滑出了正常路线，在赛道脏污的内侧对安东内利尝试了一次雄心勃勃的冲刺，最终与护栏发生了碰撞。

    拉塞尔在早期阶段一直处理得很好，看起来是夺冠的大威胁，但就像在排位赛中一样，他未能达到预期，最终远远落后于队友基米·安东内利，而后者正带着发车位罚退在比赛。如果这位英国车手想在经历了一段艰难赛季后积累一些势头，他剩下的圈数已经不多了。

    梅赛德斯的问题——安东内利在后期因车手失误退出了争冠行列——为迈凯伦打开了大门，尽管排位赛成绩不佳，兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里还是为车队拿下了双领奖台。

    单独来看，迈凯伦在直线速度上的不足太过明显，无法在单圈中具备竞争力。然而在车阵中比赛时，得益于增压模式，诺里斯全程都在争夺前列，皮亚斯特里则从发车位末尾完成了惊人的逆转，与他一同登上领奖台。值得注意的是，这只是这位澳大利亚车手自迈阿密以来的首个领奖台，所以这是一个令人欣慰的景象。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里表现出色，一路杀到了车阵前方。

    如果弗雷德·瓦塞尔对法拉利车手在墨西哥的互斗感到不满，那么仅仅两场比赛后同样的事情再次发生时，这位法国人则是恼怒、无力且彻底愤怒了。

    此前刘易斯·汉密尔顿曾呼吁查尔斯·勒克莱尔不要再挤压他，而是合作，考虑到直道上尾流的重要性，第38圈一次本可避免的碰撞让汉密尔顿打转，勒克莱尔被罚10秒。

    “查尔斯，比赛结束后我要见你，先于其他任何事情，”瓦塞尔在冷却圈通过车队无线电说道。

    当被问及那次谈话是什么样子时，瓦塞尔意味深长地回答道：“那不是谈话。是我在说。”

    “这是不可接受的。这是对车队、对赞助商的不尊重，和墨西哥站之后说的话一模一样。我认为我们在圣保罗已经有过明确的讨论。今天这表现不够职业。”

    除非让勒克莱尔坐冷板凳——而这不会发生——否则并不完全清楚瓦塞尔还能做什么，来让这位最近才续约到2029年的车手守规矩。但勒克莱尔在与队友轮对轮竞速时就是有这种难以控制的攻击性，今年汉密尔顿与他实力相当之后，这一点更加明显。

    勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯与汉密尔顿干净竞速的挣扎仍在继续，这让车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔十分沮丧

    本赛季大部分时间都被小红牛压制的阿尔派，确实需要一场强劲的成绩。在类似的赛道上，阿尔派那辆低阻力的2026年赛车已经凭借皮埃尔·加斯利在蒙扎的杆位震惊了世界，并且在巴库看起来也有望取得强劲成绩，直到弗朗哥·科拉平托把自己的队友撞了出去。

    这一次两位车手都充分利用了赛车的性能，加斯利甚至在早期给领跑者制造了麻烦，之后才落后于争夺领先位置的多车混战。正如上文所述，法拉利的一点协助完成了剩余的工作，阿尔派在当天积分超过梅赛德斯。

    本赛季无可争议的中场领跑者迅速崩盘。利亚姆·劳森未能赶上发车格，因为他的机械师们面临着与时间赛跑修复燃油泄漏的挑战，而从维修区出发的劳森从未成为影响因素。

    阿尔维德·林德布拉德的情况则不能这么说，他早期就坐在了成年人的餐桌上，先后超越了诺里斯，并——极其短暂地——超越了拉塞尔，一度升至第三位，随后在安全车下打滑退赛。

    “我仍在努力弄清楚到底发生了什么，”林德布拉德说。“安全车出动时我正在暖胎，一踩油门赛车就打转了。归根结底，这是我的错，我真诚地向车队道歉。”

    威廉姆斯凭借复古夹克和卡洛斯·塞恩斯拿到了积分。

    自九月阿塞拜疆站以来，威廉姆斯一直未能得分，而自六月摩纳哥站以来更是只有一次得分。在灾难性赛季的尾声，卡洛斯·塞恩斯的四分对这位西班牙人和车队辛勤工作的员工来说是一份不错的回报。

    比赛重启后，塞恩斯得以跟上领先集团，而他身后的奥迪赛车逐渐掉队，随后一点外部帮助让他升至第八，排在拉塞尔和勒克莱尔之前。

    第八名并不能改变车队2026年灾难的叙事，也无法改变眼前的任务。威廉姆斯今年已经消耗了大量信誉，必须在2027年显著提升竞争力。

    Winners and losers from F1's chaotically extraordinary Las Vegas GP

    Formula 1 delivered a race for the ages in Las Vegas as team-mates clashed and tempers flared

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    A pack of cars covered by seconds, the top three trading first position as part of 35 lead changes, and drivers trying to figure out the best positioning to be ahead at the finish line on the final lap; The Las Vegas Grand Prix felt like the glitzy, battery-assisted street course version of the Indy 500.

    It was quite fitting to see how in America's artificial entertainment capital, F1 put on an entertaining, if somewhat artificial, spectacle. It may not have been everyone's cup of tea, but you'll be hard-pressed to find anyone in attendance that didn't feel like they got their hard-earned money's worth.

    What did it take to triumph on the Las Vegas Strip? Guts and racecraft, sure. But also brainpower. Figuring out the right energy deployment strategy to stay at the front, and then tweaking that strategy to be ahead crossing the line on the final lap rather than into Turn 1.

    Verstappen provided some insight into his thinking on that very subject through his communication with Red Bull. "You guys have to look at the deployment of the final lap because they’ve figured it out. They can stay ahead of me until the line, so we need to see what we need to do for the final lap only."

    In the end, his plan worked as he held off Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri. But not before mocking George Russell's unsuccessful move on Kimi Antonelli, a day after he clashed with the Mercedes driver in qualifying.

    "He speaks a lot next to the track, and honestly, it doesn't really bother me because he's so far below my level that it doesn't really make any sense for me anyway to try and challenge that, you know? Today, karma is a bitch. It was honestly fantastic when I looked in my mirror and I saw what was happening, so I was like, "Fantastic, my day is only getting better."

    Max Verstappen took his fourth win in six races as Red Bull looks to build momentum for a 2027 title campaign

    Verstappen's comments were a bit unseemly and unnecessary, but it's the latest example that he is perhaps not the right man for Russell to try and play mind games with.

    In any case, the incident is unrelated to Russell's inclusion here. That's down to Russell skating off-piste, trying an ambitious lunge on Antonelli on the dirty inside of the track that ended with a tap with the barriers.

    Russell had been playing the game well in the early stages and looked like a big threat to win, but just like in qualifying he came up short and finished well behind team-mate Kimi Antonelli, who was nursing a grid penalty. If the Briton wants to build up a bit of momentum following a bruising campaign, he's running out of laps to do so.

    Mercedes' issues - Antonelli dropped out of winning contention late on with driver error - opened the door for McLaren to take a double podium with Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri despite a poor qualifying result.

    In isolation, McLaren struggled too much with a lack of straightline speed to be competitive over one lap. Racing in the pack, though, with the benefit of boost mode, Norris was in the hunt throughout and Piastri completed a remarkable comeback from the back of the grid to join him on the podium. Remarkably, it was only the Australian's first podium since Miami, so it was a welcome sight.

    Oscar Piastri produced an excellent display to carve his way to the front of the field.

    If Fred Vasseur was unimpressed with his Ferrari drivers locking horns in Mexico, then the Frenchman was exasperated, powerless and downright furious after it happened again just two races later.

    Following earlier calls from Lewis Hamilton for Charles Leclerc to stop squeezing him and instead work together, given the importance of slipstreaming on the straight, an avoidable lap 38 clash sent Hamilton into a spin and Leclerc copped a 10-second penalty.

    "Charles, I want to see you at the end of the race, before anything else," Vasseur said on the team radio during the cooldown lap.

    When asked what that discussion was like, Vasseur tellingly replied: "It was not a discussion. I spoke.

    "It's unacceptable. It's a lack of respect for the team, for the sponsors, the same speech as after Mexico. I think we had a clear discussion in Sao Paulo. Today it wasn't professional."

    Short of benching Leclerc, which isn't going to happen, it is not entirely clear what's left for Vasseur to do to get his driver, who's only recently re-signed until 2029, in line. But there's just this aggression racing his team-mates wheel-to-wheel that Leclerc is somehow struggling to contain, more so this year now Hamilton is on par with him.

    Charles Leclerc's struggles to race Lewis Hamilton cleanly continued in Las Vegas, much to the dismay of team boss Fred Vasseur

    After being battered by Racing Bulls for the majority of this season, Alpine really needed a strong result. On similar circuits, Alpine's slippery 2026 car had already shocked the world with Pierre Gasly's pole at Monza, and looked set for a strong result in Baku until Franco Colapinto torpedoed his team-mate.

    This time both drivers capitalised on the performance of their car, with Gasly even troubling the frontrunners early on before falling behind the multi-car battle for the lead. A little assistance from Ferrari, as described above, did the rest as Alpine outscored Mercedes on the day.

    Things unravelled quickly for this season's undisputed midfield leader. Liam Lawson didn't make it to the starting grid as his mechanics faced a race against time to fix a fuel leak, and from the pitlane Lawson was never a factor.

    The same can't be said of Arvid Lindblad, who was sitting at the grown-ups' table early on as he picked off Norris and - ever so briefly - Russell to run as high as third, before spinning out of the race under the safety car.

    "I’m still struggling to understand exactly what happened," Lindblad said. "I was warming up the tyres when the Safety Car was out and as soon as I got on the throttle, the car just spun. Ultimately, it was my fault and I sincerely apologise to the team."

    Williams scored points with its retro jackets, and with Carlos Sainz.

    Williams hadn't scored a point since Azerbaijan in September, and more than one since Monaco in June. Coming at the end of a calamitous campaign, Carlos Sainz's four points were a nice reward for the Spaniard and the team's hardworking staff.

    After the restart, Sainz was able to stay with the lead pack as the Audi's behind him lost touch, and a little bit of external help then saw him move up to eighth ahead of Russell and Leclerc.

    Eighth doesn't change the narrative of the team's 2026 disaster, nor does it change the task at hand. Williams has used up a lot of credit this year and must be significantly more competitive in 2027.
