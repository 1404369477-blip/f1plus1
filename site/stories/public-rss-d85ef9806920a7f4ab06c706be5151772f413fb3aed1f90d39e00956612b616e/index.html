    皮亚斯特里坦言怀念F1争冠压力

    迈凯伦车手皮亚斯特里表示，在艰难的2026赛季中，他怀念上赛季争夺F1世界冠军时的那种压力。

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里承认，他“怀念”F1争冠战带来的压力。

    这位迈凯伦车手去年曾是冠军争夺者，但如今他的重心是帮助车队在车队积分榜上击败红牛、拿到第三名。

    致迈凯伦车迷。来自沃金的木瓜色新闻，包含车手、赛车、车队策略以及每个比赛周末的最新动态。

    迈凯伦的奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里表示，在艰难的2026赛季中，他怀念争夺F1世界冠军时的那种压力。

    这位澳大利亚车手去年大部分时间都领跑车手积分榜，最终落后于队友兰多·诺里斯和红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘获得第三名。尽管去年错失了总冠军，但对皮亚斯特里来说，这是地面效应规则时代的成功收官。

    然而今年并不属于他。目前他仅剩三场比赛，在积分榜上排名第七，且尚未赢得任何一场比赛。当被问及缺乏争冠机会是否对他造成打击时，他更愿意关注本赛季迄今为止的积极面。

    “是也不是，”他说。“如果可能的话，我希望每年都能参与总冠军争夺，但今年确实轻松了一些。显然，我们并不想处于这个位置，但压力没有那么大。

    “但争冠时的那种压力正是你想要的感觉，因为这意味着你在为你的目标而战。今年我肯定怀念那种感觉。”

    迈凯伦一直难以匹配其在过去两个赛季F1新规则下所展现的速度，皮亚斯特里表示这辆车从一开始就很难驾驭。

    “这辆车开起来并不那么容易，即使到现在也仍然不容易，”他说。“我们花了一段时间才准确理解需要改变什么，而真正去改变它又很困难。但我们正在慢慢取得进展。”

    巴西站有一些迹象。周日，诺里斯在英特拉格斯获胜，迈凯伦为赛车升级了新的前翼，旨在让赛车更可预测，而皮亚斯特里在Q2被淘汰后追至第六。

    皮亚斯特里现在有了另一个目标。迈凯伦在车队积分榜上排名第四，落后红牛17分，他在赛后表示，缩小这一差距是他本赛季剩余时间的首要任务。

    他说：“继续学习，最终努力在车队锦标赛中击败红牛。这是最重要的事情。这肯定不是一件容易的事，但我认为这是可能的。我认为赛车这个周末向前迈进了一步，所以希望这能继续保持下去。”

    皮亚斯特里还在周一确认，他已经与经纪人马克·韦伯分道扬镳。韦伯是前红牛车手，从皮亚斯特里的青训时期起就一直指导他的职业生涯。

    他将在下周的拉斯维加斯获得下一次缩小与红牛差距的机会。

    Oscar Piastri admits he is "missing" the stress of an F1 title fight

    The McLaren driver was a championship contender last year, but is now focused on helping his team beat Red Bull to third in the constructors' standings

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    McLaren's Oscar Piastri is missing the pressure of fighting for the Formula 1 world championship amid what has been a difficult 2026 season.

    The Australian led the drivers' standings for a lot of last year before he eventually finished third behind team-mate Lando Norris and Red Bull's Max Verstappen. Although he missed out on the championship last year, it was a successful end to the ground effect regulations for Piastri.

    This year hasn't been his year, however. He currently sits seventh in the standings with just three races left and is yet to win a race. Asked whether his lack of title contention has been a blow to him, he's keen to focus on the upside of his year so far.

    "Yes and no," he said. "I want to be in a championship fight every year if I can be, but also, it's definitely a bit more relaxed this year. Obviously, we're not wanting to be in this position, but there's not quite the same stress.

    "But that stress that you have in a championship fight is the feeling you want, because it means you're fighting for something that is what your goal is. I am missing that feeling this year for sure."

    McLaren has struggled to match the pace it showed in the last two seasons under F1's new rules, and Piastri said the car had been tricky from the start.

    "It wasn't that easy to drive, and it's still not been easy to drive, even now," he said. "It took us a while to understand exactly what needed to change, and then trying to actually change it is tough. But we're making progress with it slowly."

    There were hints of that in Brazil. Norris won at Interlagos on Sunday with a car that McLaren had upgraded with a new front wing designed to make it more predictable, while Piastri recovered to sixth after being knocked out in Q2.

    Piastri now has something else to aim for. McLaren sits fourth in the constructors' championship, 17 points behind Red Bull, and he said after the race that closing that gap was his priority for the rest of the season.

    "Just keep learning and ultimately try and beat Red Bull in the constructors' championship. That's the big thing," he said. "It's not going to be an easy task for sure, but I think it's possible. I think the car took a step forward this weekend, so hopefully that keeps going."

    Piastri also confirmed on Monday that he had split from his manager Mark Webber, the former Red Bull driver who had guided his career since his junior days.

    He will get his next chance to close the gap to Red Bull in Las Vegas next week.
