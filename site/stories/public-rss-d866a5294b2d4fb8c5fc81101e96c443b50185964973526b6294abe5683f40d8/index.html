    金·卡戴珊重燃与梅赛德斯车手安东内利的搞笑毛巾风波

    金·卡戴珊在新加坡大奖赛期间与梅赛德斯车手安东内利发生搞笑毛巾事件，并誓言不再触碰他的毛巾。

    金·卡戴珊在新加坡大奖赛期间与梅赛德斯车手安东内利发生搞笑毛巾风波

    金·卡戴珊誓言永远不会再次触碰梅赛德斯车手安东内利的毛巾，此前她在摩纳哥大奖赛期间不小心偷走了他的冷却毛巾

    金·卡戴珊站在发车线上

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    一级方程式赛车场的看台是高调名人的巨大磁铁，但很少有A级名人的亮相能像金·卡戴珊与梅赛德斯-奔驰的安德烈亚·基米·安东内利之间的持续故事那样，引发如此多的搞笑病毒性争议。

    在参加2026年新加坡大奖赛以支持法拉利车队的小汉密尔顿时，这位真人秀亿万富翁在新加坡航空的红毯上遇到了这位年轻的车手冠军，瞬间复活了本赛季最有趣的场外故事。

    在即将到来的艰苦的滨海湾夜赛中，卡戴珊撞到了安东内利，她立刻提到了他们共同的病毒性历史。在朋友们的陪伴下，卡戴珊以防御性的、开玩笑的姿态举起双手，被听到告诉这位梅赛德斯车手，"不，基米，这次我可不敢碰你的毛巾了。"

    这场搞笑的交流成为了被称为"毛巾门"的整个赛季友好看台玩笑的最新章节。

    这个内部笑话可以追溯到六月初的摩纳哥大奖赛。在安东内利在蒙特卡洛街道上取得压倒性胜利后，正在访问公国以支持汉密尔顿的卡戴珊被相机捕捉到意外地拿走了梅赛德斯车手的官方颁奖台冷却毛巾。病毒式视频显示她天真地用获胜者的毛巾擦去额头和太阳镜，然后随意地带着毛巾离开。

    意识到这是一个巨大的社交媒体机会，梅赛德斯数字团队和安东内利迅速发布了一段搞笑视频，展示了这位意大利青少年在摩纳哥维修区四处走动，询问包括乔治·拉塞尔在内的队友们是否看到了他丢失的毛巾。

    几周后，卡戴珊证明她完全参与了这场玩笑。在巴塞罗那-加泰罗尼亚大奖赛之前，她正式弥补了错误，给安东内利送上一条全新的、定制的刺绣毛巾，引发了年轻梅赛德斯车手制作的备受瞩目的“感谢”视频。

    虽然红毯互动都是笑容和玩笑，但新加坡大奖赛对卡戴珊的F1圈子中的车手们产生了截然不同的结果。

    安东内利设法在极端高温、严重的轮胎磨损以及一场关于赛道的重大争议中幸存下来，艰难地赢得了第三名的领奖台，同时保持了他在车手总冠军中的巨大领先优势。与此同时，汉密尔顿——在马丁·布兰德利的赛前网格采访后，卡戴珊在法拉利维修区积极为他加油——在比赛后期犯下了策略错误，并因错过维修区入口而付出了高昂的代价，这使他排名大幅下降，最终以令人沮丧的第六名结束比赛。

    随着安东内利在即将到来的美国大奖赛（在奥斯汀举行）中在数学上几乎可以锁定世界车手总冠军，人们仍然不知道卡戴珊是否会前往德克萨斯州，以确保这位年轻明星的车手总冠军毛巾安全无恙。

    Kim Kardashian revives hilarious F1 towel drama with Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli

    Kim Kardashian vows never to touch Kimi Antonelli's towels again after she accidentally stole his cool-down towel in Monaco

    Kim Kardashian on the grid.

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    The Formula 1 paddock is a massive magnet for high-profile celebrities, but few A-list appearances have generated as much hilarious viral drama as Kim Kardashian's ongoing saga with Mercedes' Andrea Kimi Antonelli.

    While attending the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix to support Scuderia Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, the reality TV billionaire ran into the young championship leader on the Singapore Airlines red carpet, instantly reviving the funniest off-track storyline of the season.

    As Kardashian bumped into Antonelli ahead of the gruelling Marina Bay night race, she immediately referenced their shared viral history. Throwing her hands up in a defensive, joking posture alongside friends, Kardashian was heard telling the Mercedes driver, "Nooo, Kimi, I don't dare to touch your towel this time."

    The hilarious exchange serves as the latest chapter in a friendly, season-long paddock joke dubbed "Towel-Gate".

    The inside joke dates back to the Monaco Grand Prix in early June. After Antonelli secured a dominant victory around the streets of Monte Carlo, Kardashian - who was visiting the Principality to support Hamilton - was caught on camera accidentally swiping the Mercedes driver's official podium cool-down towel. Viral footage showed her innocently using the race winner's towel to wipe her brow and sunglasses before casually walking away with it.

    Recognising a massive social media opportunity, the Mercedes digital team and Antonelli quickly released a hilarious video of the Italian teenager wandering around the Monaco paddock asking team-mates, including George Russell, if they had seen his missing towel.

    Kardashian proved she was fully in on the joke just a couple of weeks later. Ahead of the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix, she officially made amends by surprising Antonelli with a brand-new, custom-embroidered replacement towel, sparking a highly publicised "thank you" video from the young Mercedes driver.

    While the red carpet interaction was all smiles and jokes, the actual Singapore Grand Prix yielded very different results for the drivers involved in Kardashian's F1 orbit.

    Antonelli managed to survive the extreme heat, severe tyre degradation, and a major track limits controversy to secure a hard-fought third-place podium finish, keeping his massive championship lead intact. Meanwhile, Hamilton - whom Kardashian was actively cheering for from the Ferrari garage following a pre-race grid interview with Martin Brundle - suffered a late-race strategy blunder and a costly missed pit entry, plummeting him down the order to a deeply frustrating sixth-place finish.

    With Antonelli mathematically on the verge of clinching the World Drivers' Championship at the upcoming United States Grand Prix in Austin, it remains to be seen if Kardashian will make the trip to Texas to ensure the young star's championship towels stay safe.
