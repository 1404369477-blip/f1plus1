    阿尔本与威廉姆斯续约至2027年

    威廉姆斯车队宣布，亚历山大·阿尔本已签署新合同，将继续为车队效力至2027年。

    亚历山大·阿尔本已签署新合同，将继续留在威廉姆斯车队，因此他将在2027年继续为威廉姆斯效力。

    明年将是阿尔本在威廉姆斯的第六个赛季。他去年为车队获得年度第八，但由于威廉姆斯在2026赛季开局不佳，他在夏休期仅列积分榜第16位。

    不过阿尔本表示，他仍然相信车队领队詹姆斯·沃尔斯让车队重返前列的愿景。

    “这支车队所经历的转变难以用言语形容，”阿尔本说。“一个艰难的赛季并不能说明全部，这只会给我更多动力，与工厂和我们的车迷一起继续建设、共同推动，去实现伟大的成就。”

    沃尔斯表示，阿尔本“从第一天起就是这项转型项目的领导者，我们还有很多未竟的事业，我们决心去实现。

    “亚历克斯是围场里最好的车手之一，他比任何人都更清楚我们近年来取得的巨大进步——与他续约表明了我们双方对前进方向的信心。”

    这位30岁的车手于2022年加入威廉姆斯，上一次续约是在2024年5月。三年前，他在红牛二队（现为小红牛）完成F1首秀，并在赛季中期被提拔至红牛。在与马克斯·维斯塔潘搭档的艰难202赛季后，他失去了比赛席位。

    英文原文

    Alexander Albon will continue to drive for Williams in 2027 having signed a new deal to remain at the team.

    Next year will be Albon’s sixth at Williams. He finished eighth in the championship for the team last year but is only 16th on the standings at the summer break following Williams’ poor start to 2026.

    However Albon said he is keeping in the faith in team principal James Vowles’ vision to bring the team back to the front of the grid.

    “The transformation this team has made is difficult to put into words,” said Albon. “One tough year doesn’t tell the full picture and it only gives me more motivation to keep building and pushing together with the factory and our fans to achieve great things.”

    Vowles said Albon “as been a leader of this transformation project since day one and we have plenty of unfinished business that we are determined to achieve.

    “Alex is one of the best drivers on the grid and knows better than anyone the enormous improvements we have made in recent years – re-signing him shows the belief we both have in the direction we are heading.”

    The 30-year-old joined Williams in 2022 and last renewed his contract in May 2024. He made his Formula 1 debut three years earlier at Toro Rosso, now Racing Bulls, and was promoted to Red Bull at mid-season. He lost his race seat after a difficult 202 alongside Max Verstappen.
