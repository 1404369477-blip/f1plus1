    F1车队预计10月中旬决定卡塔尔与阿布扎比大奖赛是否如期举行

    F1各车队了解到，他们将在10月中旬获知卡塔尔和阿布扎比两场收官战能否按计划举行。

    一级方程式车队预计，关于2026赛季收官阶段卡塔尔和阿布扎比大奖赛是否举办的最终决定将在十月中旬公布。

    哈斯车队的Ayao Komatsu和奥迪车队的Mattia Binotto表示，车队本周早些时候被告知，将在十月中旬之前得知中东的赛季收官站比赛是否会如期举行；请于周五上午9点30分收看阿塞拜疆的最终练习赛，下午1点为排位赛，天空体育 F1将进行现场直播。

    天空体育 News的Craig Slater带来2026年F1赛历中收官站——卡塔尔和阿布扎比大奖赛的最新消息。

    F1的车队明白，他们将在10月中旬得知卡塔尔和阿布扎比的赛季末比赛是否会按计划举行。

    2026赛季原计划以背靠背的两场比赛收官——卡塔尔（11月27-29日）和阿布扎比（12月4-6日），但由于今年2月开始的中东冲突，这几场比赛在过去几个月里一直显得不确定。

    这两场比赛目前仍保留在赛历上，赛历目前共有23场比赛。据天空体育 News了解，目前的意图是让这两场比赛如期举行。

    据认为，如果中东站的比赛被认为无法进行，意大利的伊莫拉赛道是收官站的备用选项，F1 在做出最终决定前还有更多时间。

    F1委员会于周一召开了一次会议，而在周四阿塞拜疆大奖赛的新闻发布会上，各车队领队表示，他们已经清楚了解到何时会做出决定。

    "我相信伊莫拉正在做好准备，以备我们不得不在那里进行（比赛）。

    关于卡塔尔和阿布扎比大奖赛有什么最新消息？

    天空体育的F1记者克雷格·斯莱特（Craig Slater）：

    “我从F1管理层消息人士那里得到的最新信息是，这两站比赛都在赛历上，意向是要照常举行。”

    “我被告知，就目前该地区的稳定程度而言，如果那些赛事安排在接下来的几周内举行，局势将足够稳定，比赛可以照常进行。情况将是，除非局势再次恶化，否则那些比赛将会如期举行。”

    “伊莫拉是一个备选方案，但他们不必为了它而放弃最后两站比赛。所以，如果他们无法举办最后两站比赛，伊莫拉将作为备选方案，但如果伊莫拉为此做准备而产生了任何经济损失，他们将予以补偿。”

    “车队的理解是，十月中旬会就此问题得到某种形式的澄清，所以在一定程度上我们只能拭目以待。”

    Formula 1 teams expect final call on Qatar, Abu Dhabi Grands Prix by middle of October on closing 2026 season races

    Haas' Ayao Komatsu and Audi's Mattia Binotto say teams told earlier this week that they would know by mid-October whether the season-ending Middle East races go ahead; watch final practice in Azerbaijan on Friday at 9.30am, with Qualifying at 1pm, live on Sky Sports F1

    Sky Sports News' Craig Slater with the latest update on the 2026 F1 calendar's season-ending races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

    F1's teams understand they will learn in mid-October whether the season-ending races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi will take place as scheduled.

    ​The 2026's season is due to finish with a back-to-back races in Qatar (November 27-29) and Abu Dhabi (December 4-6) but those events have appeared uncertain for a number of months owing to the conflict this year in the Middle East, which started in February.

    The two events remain on the calendar, which currently runs to 23 races, with Sky Sports News understanding that the intention is for them to take place.

    Italy's Imola circuit is thought to be the contingency option to close the season if it is deemed that the Middle Eastern rounds cannot go ahead, with F1 having more time before a final decision is made.

    An F1 Commission meeting took pace on Monday and, speaking in Thursday's press conference at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix, team chiefs said they had been given clarity on when a decision would be taken by.

    Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu said: "I believe that Stefano [Domenicali, F1's president] is targeting to tell us in mid-October so we all know what the situation is.

    "I believe that Imola is getting ready in case we have to do it [there].

    "But hopefully things can stay as a baseline and we can go to Qatar and Abu Dhabi. That'd be really good."

    Audi counterpart Mattia Binotto added: "We discussed it during the F1 Commission last Monday. Mid-October is the last call for such a decision."

    What's the latest on Qatar and Abu Dhabi GPs?

    Sky Sports' F1 correspondent Craig Slater:

    "The latest information I have from leadership sources at Formula 1 itself is that they are both on the calendar, the intention is to go.

    "I have been informed that, as things stand in terms of the level of stability in the region, should those events have been happening in the next couple of weeks, it would be stable enough for them to go ahead. The situation would be that unless there is a deterioration again then those races would be going ahead.

    "There is a contingency of Imola but they do not have to commit to that to the exclusion of those last two races. So Imola will be a contingency situation if they can't run the last two races, but they will compensate Imola for any financial loss they make in getting ready for that.

    "Mid-October is the teams' understanding that they will get some kind of clarification on that matter, so we will have to watch this space to a degree."

    'I do hope that we get to go there' - Hamilton keen on Middle East return

    Speaking in Baku on Wednesday, Ferrari driver Lewis Hamilton said he hoped the sport was able to return to the Middle East as scheduled at the end of November.

    "I personally want to go. I love going to the Middle East," said Hamilton.

    "From what I've read, I think things seem OK out there. But I don't have all the information so I don't know what the risk levels are. But I trust in Stefano to make the right decision for the sport, but I do hope that we get to go there.

    "Finishing in winter in Imola might not be the best weather-wise, and also it's a beautiful track, but it's not a track that we're going to be doing any overtaking in and the more races, the better."
