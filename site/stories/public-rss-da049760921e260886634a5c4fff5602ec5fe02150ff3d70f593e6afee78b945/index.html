    霍纳称维斯塔潘团队要求红牛2022年研发转向

    前红牛车队负责人克里斯蒂安·霍纳透露，马克斯·维斯塔潘的管理团队曾要求车队将2022年赛车研发方向从塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯身上转移开。

    克里斯蒂安·霍纳揭示马克斯·维斯塔潘的管理团队如何改变了红牛2022年的研发方向

    前红牛负责人克里斯蒂安·霍纳透露，马克斯·维斯塔潘的管理团队要求车队将2022年赛车研发方向从塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯身上转移开

    马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队、塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯、红牛车队

    致红牛车迷。关注红牛车队，获取来自米尔顿凯恩斯的F1新闻、车手动态、技术洞察与比赛分析。

    关于塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯在红牛车队效力期间的叙述，往往集中在他无法持续展现出与队友马克斯·维斯塔潘相同的原始速度上。但红牛车队前首席执行官兼领队克里斯蒂安·霍纳最近揭开了幕后工程之争的帷幕，这场争斗改变了佩雷斯2022赛季的轨迹。

    霍纳在《高性能》播客中谈到他在米尔顿凯恩斯车队执掌二十年的经历时透露，2022年的初始底盘小红牛18并非天生按照维斯塔潘的极端偏好打造。相反，这辆早期地面效应赛车是2021年全年由当时的储备车手亚历克斯·阿尔本在模拟器中开发的，结果是一台更平衡、更可预测的机器。

    “亚历克斯·阿尔本在幕后开发22款赛车方面做了了不起的工作……那非常符合他的驾驶风格，这是一种更经典的驾驶方式，可能更接近塞尔吉奥或丹尼尔（里卡多），”霍纳解释道。

    由于2022年的初始赛车拥有可预测的车头，并且缺乏维斯塔潘偏好的那种极端、突然的旋转，佩雷斯一上来就表现出色。

    霍纳指出：“当我们开始2022赛季时，如果你还记得，马克斯讨厌那辆车，车有太多转向不足，而塞尔吉奥凭借那种可预测性……突然间他们各自赢得了一场比赛，”他指的是年初两人激烈的竞争。

    但内部均势是短暂的。意识到（当时的）卫冕世界冠军难以最大化一辆为传统驾驶风格调校的赛车，维斯塔潘的管理团队介入，迫使进行重大技术转向。

    霍纳确认，在2022年阿塞拜疆大奖赛周末期间发生了一次直接对峙。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯，红牛车队

    霍纳透露：“我记得那年阿塞拜疆站前后，我和马克斯的经纪团队坐下来谈，他们对我说：‘你看，你是围绕佩雷兹来开发这辆车的；你需要去顺应马克斯的需求。’”

    面对这位当家车手阵营发出的最后通牒，红牛的工程部门决定调整研发方向。霍纳承认，车队优先考虑了这位“核心资产”的需求，因为维斯塔潘终究拥有更高的性能上限。

    霍纳承认：“马克斯是核心资产，他是你最有希望用来赢下车手的车手……当然，任何工程团队都会自然而然地倾向于他们最快的车手。”

    随着红牛对小红牛18进行研发，消除转向不足并引入更锐利、响应更灵敏的车头，维斯塔潘的速度爆发了。反过来，这条研发路径也剥夺了佩雷兹所习惯的可预测性，导致两位车手之间出现了巨大的性能差距。

    Christian Horner reveals how Max Verstappen’s management reshaped 2022 Red Bull development

    Former Red Bull boss Christian Horner reveals Max Verstappen's management demanded the team shift its 2022 car development away from Sergio Perez

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Sergio Perez, Red Bull Racing

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    The narrative surrounding Sergio Perez's tenure at Red Bull Racing often centres on his inability to consistently extract the same raw pace as his team-mate Max Verstappen. But former Red Bull Racing CEO and team principal Christian Horner recently pulled back the curtain on a behind-the-scenes engineering battle that altered the trajectory of Perez's 2022 season.

    Speaking on the High Performance podcast about his two-decade stint at the helm of the Milton Keynes squad, Horner revealed that the initial 2022 chassis, the RB18, was not inherently built to Verstappen's extreme preferences. Instead, the early ground-effect car was developed in the simulator by then-reserve driver Alex Albon throughout 2021, resulting in a more balanced, predictable machine.

    "Alex Albon did a hell of a job in the background developing the '22 car... it was very much around his style of driving, which [is a] much more classic way of driving that was probably more akin to a Sergio or a Daniel [Ricciardo]," Horner explained.

    Because the initial 2022 car possessed a predictable front end and lacked the extreme, snappy rotation that Verstappen prefers, Perez thrived right out of the gate.

    "When we started the '22 season, if you remember that Max hated the car, it had too much understeer in the car, and Sergio with that predictability... suddenly they were winning a race apiece," Horner noted, referencing their close fight at the start of the year.

    But the internal parity was short-lived. Recognising that the (then) reigning world champion was struggling to maximise a car geared toward a traditional driving style, Verstappen's management team stepped in to force a major technical pivot.

    Horner confirmed that a direct confrontation occurred during the 2022 Azerbaijan Grand Prix weekend.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Sergio Perez, Red Bull Racing

    "I remember sitting down with Max's management around Azerbaijan that year and them saying, 'Look, you've developed this car around Checo; you need to be following what Max needs,'" Horner revealed.

    Faced with an ultimatum from the camp of its franchise driver, Red Bull's engineering department made the decision to shift its development path. Horner admitted that the team prioritised the demands of its "prime asset" because Verstappen ultimately possessed the higher performance ceiling.

    "Max is the prime asset, he's the driver you're most likely going to win with... of course any engineering team will gravitate just naturally towards their fastest driver," Horner admitted.

    As Red Bull developed the RB18 to strip away the understeer and introduce a sharper, more responsive front end, Verstappen's pace exploded. Conversely, the development path stripped away the predictability that Perez was used to, leading to a massive performance delta between the two drivers.
