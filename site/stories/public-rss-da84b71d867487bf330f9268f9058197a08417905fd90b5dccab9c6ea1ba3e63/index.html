    迈凯伦领队斯特拉为皮亚斯特里拉斯维加斯爆粗辩护

    迈凯伦领队安德烈亚·斯特拉为奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里在拉斯维加斯大奖赛中的爆粗回应辩护，称这正是他想要看到的性格。皮亚斯特里从第17位发车，最终以第三名完赛。

    “我其实挺喜欢”——迈凯伦领队为皮亚斯特里在拉斯维加斯大奖赛上的爆粗辩护

    奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里对“小心点”的指令给出了简短尖锐的回应，该指令是针对队友兰多·诺里斯的。安德烈亚·斯特拉认为：“这正是你想看到的性格。”

    在兰多·诺里斯和奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里经历了一场麻烦的排位赛之后，在拉斯维加斯跟在获胜的马克斯·维斯塔潘身后登上领奖台，大概不在迈凯伦的预料之中。考虑到皮亚斯特里因使用超出配额的引擎部件被罚，正赛从第17位发车，他最终获得第三名显得尤其不可能。

    赛后，迈凯伦领队安德烈亚·斯特拉将皮亚斯特里的心态比作他作为工程师时共事过的世界冠军：迈克尔·舒马赫、费尔南多·阿隆索和基米·莱科宁。他的这番话是在回应提问：第41圈皮亚斯特里排在兰多·诺里斯和乔治·拉塞尔身后、位居第五时，比赛工程师汤姆·斯托拉德要求他“小心队友”，皮亚斯特里对此给出了尖锐的回应。

    “别他妈再叫我小心点，”这是皮亚斯特里的回应。

    在一场以长串赛车要么紧咬彼此尾流、要么进行短暂‘悠悠球’式超车为特征的比赛中，皮亚斯特里从低微的发车位迅速推进，在开场圈就提升了五个位置。当第19圈安全车出动以清理塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯抛锚的凯迪拉克时，皮亚斯特里已升至第九位，紧跟在诺里斯身后。

    安全车期间在维修区双车叠停让皮亚斯特里损失了赛道位置，但他再次穿越车阵，当来自指挥台的指令传来时，他又一次跟在了队友身后。

    “我其实挺喜欢的，”斯特拉赛后对包括Motorsport.com在内的媒体说。

    “我喜欢这样。这就是你期望从一名赛车手身上看到的性格。这不像迈克尔·舒马赫、费尔南多·阿隆索、基米·莱科宁——那些我以前共事过的人，他们也没有更容易对付。他们是冠军。”

    “这就是你想看到的性格。这也是你在维修区指挥台上必须做的事。你需要确保车手脑子里有一个清晰的目标。而且你要一直这样做，直到车手让你感觉到：‘我明白了。不用再往前了。’

    “这正是你从驾驶舱外想要达到的状态。所以实际上，维修区指挥台做得很好，奥斯卡在这种情况下的处理也很好。这正是我们希望团队与车手之间互动所呈现的状态。也是我们通过经历过的一些经验教训所学到的。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，迈凯伦

    迈凯伦队友之间的纪律性并不总是很强，但他们在拉斯维加斯保持了这一点，而法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔组合在棘手条件下公开发生冲突。诺里斯和皮亚斯特里不得不克服相对于其他赛车的直线速度劣势，小心判断尾流来穿过车阵。

    像英特拉格斯和拉斯维加斯这样的低抓地力赛道过去曾让皮亚斯特里吃亏，但斯特拉强调他最近取得了多大进步——甚至在这场比赛和上一个周末之间，他在穿越车流时损失了太多位置。

    “在这里更清楚的是，除了动力单元使用和低抓地力驾驶之外，我认为奥斯卡带着信心和果断驾驶，”斯特拉说。

    “这意味着他现在某种程度上与赛车合拍了。他可以开始使用他的超能力——也就是这种比赛能力、这种专注。”

    “这对赛季末来说非常令人鼓舞，也是我们带入下个赛季的东西。”

    "I actually liked it" – McLaren boss defends Piastri's four-letter outburst in Las Vegas GP

    Oscar Piastri responded curtly to an instruction to "be careful" around team-mate Lando Norris. "That's the character you want to see" reckons Andrea Stella

    Completing the podium behind a victorious Max Verstappen in Las Vegas probably wasn't on McLaren's bingo card after a troubled qualifying session for Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri. Finishing third would have seemed particularly unlikely for Piastri given that penalties for fitting out-of-quota engine components dropped him to 17th on the grid.

    Afterwards, McLaren team principal Andrea Stella likened Piastri's mindset to that of world champions with whom he worked as an engineer: Michael Schumacher, Fernando Alonso and Kimi Raikkonen. His remark was a response to being asked about the sharp retort Piastri delivered to race engineer Tom Stallard when asked to "be careful around our team-mate" while running in fifth place behind Lando Norris and George Russell on lap 41.

    "Stop telling me to fucking be careful," was Piastri's response.

    In a race characterised by long trains of cars either locked in each other's slipstream, or engaging in short-lived ‘yo-yo' overtaking moves, Piastri had made rapid progress from his lowly grid spot, gaining five places on the opening lap. By the time the safety car was deployed on lap 19 to cover the removal of Sergio Perez's stricken Cadillac, Piastri was running in ninth place, right behind Norris.

    Double-stacking in the pits during the safety car period cost Piastri track position, but he cut through the field again to be running behind his team-mate once more when the call came from the pitwall.

    "I actually liked it," Stella told media including Motorsport.com after the race.

    "I like it. That's the character that you expect from a racer. It's not like Michael Schumacher, Fernando Alonso, Kimi Räikkönen, who I worked with before, they were any easier. They were champions.

    "That's the character you want to see. And that's also what you have to do from the pitwall. You need to make sure that the driver has a clear objective in his head. And you want to do it up until the point the driver makes you feel, ‘I got it. Don't go beyond.'

    "That's exactly where you want to be from outside the cockpit. So actually well done to the pit wall and well done to Oscar in relation to this situation. That's exactly where we want to position the interaction between the team and the driver. And what we have learned through some of the experiences we have gone through."

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, Oscar Piastri, McLaren

    Discipline between the McLaren team-mates hasn't always been strong but they maintained it in Las Vegas as the Ferrari pairing of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc publicly came to blows in tricky conditions. Norris and Piastri had to overcome a straightline speed deficit relative to other cars to make their way through the pack, judging the slipstream carefully.

    Low-grip circuits such as Interlagos and Las Vegas have proved Piastri's undoing in the past, but Stella highlighted how much progress he has made recently – even between this race and the previous weekend, where he lost too much ground making his way through traffic.

    "Here it was much clearer that in addition to power unit utilisation and driving at low grip, I think Oscar drove with confidence and assertiveness," said Stella.

    "Which means that he is now kind of in tune with the car. He can start using his superpower – which is this racing ability, this focus.

    "That's very promising for the end of the season, and what we carry into the next one."
