    Alexa Rendell has joined the Sky Sports F1 team ahead of this weekend's Sao Paulo Grand Prix in Brazil; watch Friday's practice sessions for the Sao Paulo GP from 3.30pm and 7pm, live on Sky Sports F1

    Sky Sports has strengthened its award-winning Formula 1 team with the appointment of Alexa Rendell as F1 Multimedia Journalist, as the broadcaster looks to bring even more fans closer to the sport.

    In a newly created role, Alexa will work across Sky Sports' social channels, live Formula 1 programming and Sky Sports News, bringing fans closer to the personalities, stories, and moments that make Formula 1 one of the world's most compelling sports.

    Her appointment comes as Formula 1 continues to attract a broader and more diverse audience, with younger fans and women playing an increasingly important part in the sport's growth. Since becoming the home of Formula 1 in the UK and Ireland in 2012, Sky Sports has seen viewing among under-35s increase by 120 per cent, while its female audience has more than doubled. The 2025 season was also the most-watched in Sky Sports' history.
