    阿尔派2026赛季复兴获科维亚特助力

    阿尔派车队披露，前红牛车手丹尼尔·科维亚特今年以模拟器车手身份低调回归，成为车队本赛季F1复兴的意外助力。

    阿尔派车队披露了本赛季支持其F1复兴的一个意外资产——前红牛车手、领奖台获得者丹尼尔·科维亚特。

    科维亚特在2014年至2020年间共参加了110场大奖赛，今年低调重返恩斯通车队，担任模拟器车手。

    他曾在2021年短暂效力阿尔派担任储备车手，其F1生涯中多次为红牛旗下车队效力，中间在2018年休赛一年，当时他担任法拉利的模拟器车手。

    科维亚特的角色一直未公开，他与阿尔派储备车手保罗·阿隆和库什·马伊尼分担模拟器工作。

    这包括研发工作，也包括当阿隆需要前往海外分站现场时的比赛周末支持任务。

    比赛周末期间，科维亚特与阿尔派正赛车手皮埃尔·加斯利保持联系，分享直接反馈；两人曾在红牛青年队做过队友，关系良好。

    据信科维亚特在阿尔派日本站和加拿大站的周末逆转中发挥了重要作用。

    阿尔派本赛季在F1中表现大幅提升，新规则以及改用梅赛德斯引擎使其重返中游车队前列。

    离开F1后，科维亚特曾参加和/或测试NASCAR、顶级跑车和电动方程式赛车，并曾在阿布扎比担任与自动驾驶车辆竞速的参考车手。

    他今年参加日本Super GT系列赛GT300组别，与老将高木虎之介搭档。两人目前在积分榜上排名第16，组别最佳成绩为第六名。

    英文原文

    Alpine has revealed a surprise asset supporting its Formula 1 revival this season - ex-Red Bull driver and podium finisher Daniil Kvyat.

    Kvyat, who started 110 grands prix from 2014 to 2020, quietly rejoined the Enstone team this year as a simulator driver.

    He had a stint at Alpine in 2021 as its reserve driver, having spent his F1 career racing for Red Bull's teams across multiple stints split by a year on the sidelines in 2018, when he was a simulator driver for Ferrari.

    Kvyat's role has been kept under wraps as he shares simulator duties with Alpine reserve drivers Paul Aron and Kush Maini.

    This includes development work but also race weekend support duties when Aron is required to be on-site at flyaway races.

    During race weekends, Kvyat keeps in contact with Alpine race driver Pierre Gasly, who he was team-mate to at Red Bull's junior team and has a good relationship with, to share direct feedback.

    Kvyat is understood to have played a significant role in Alpine's in-weekend turnarounds in Japan and Canada.

    Alpine is enjoying a much improved season in F1 as the new rules and its switch to Mercedes engines have launched it back to the head of the midfield.

    Kvyat has raced and/or tested NASCAR, top-level sportscar and Formula E machinery since his F1 exit, and had a role as a reference driver racing against an autonomous vehicle in Abu Dhabi.

    He is competing in Japan's Super GT series this year in the GT300 category, alongside veteran driver Takashi Kogure. The pair are 16th in the points with a best class finish of sixth.
