    梅赛德斯在巴西大奖赛将穿新赛车服致敬传奇时代

    梅赛德斯车队将在本周末的圣保罗大奖赛中穿着紫色赛车服，世界冠军安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔将换下他们常用的黑色和蓝绿色赛车服。这一设计在周二于圣保罗的Nubank公园揭晓，这是车队与巴西银行Nubank合作的一部分。

    梅赛德斯将在巴西大奖赛中穿着新赛车服，向传奇时代致敬。

    世界冠军安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔将在整个周末的Interlagos赛事中穿着这款特殊设计的赛车服，这是本赛季车队第二次穿上紫色赛车服。

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·安东内利，梅赛德斯。

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里有最新的银箭消息，包括来自Brackley和Brixworth的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和赛事分析。

    梅赛德斯车队在本周末的圣保罗大奖赛上看起来会有一些不同，世界冠军基米·安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔将换上紫色的赛车服，而不是他们通常的黑色和蓝色赛车服。

    这款设计于周二在圣保罗的Nubank公园揭晓，这是帕尔梅拉斯足球俱乐部的主场，作为车队与巴西银行Nubank合作的一部分。两位车手将在整个周末的各节练习与排位中穿着这套赛车服。

    “受过去启发，为未来打造。欢迎来到新纪元，”安东内利在Instagram上写道。“这个周末的新形象！”

    这套赛车服由梅赛德斯设计，灵感来源于20世纪80年代和90年代，那是纳尔逊·皮克特和埃尔顿·塞纳赢得他们冠军头衔的时期。这次活动的口号是“受黄金时代启发，为新时代打造”。

    两种色调都来自银行。深色基调取自其高端卡Ultravioleta，而较浅的紫色代表其最大的客户群。

    这个揭晓伴随着一部介绍两位车手的电影，由在Netflix关于三届冠军的系列剧中扮演塞纳的加布里埃尔·莱昂担任旁白。其配乐是Tema da Vitoria，这是当巴西车手在F1中获胜时，TV Globo播放的音乐，与塞纳的职业生涯紧密相连。

    这是梅赛德斯在2026年的第二次这样做。在5月的迈阿密大奖赛上，车队使用了三种不同的紫色西装，每种设计都与Nubank的扩张有关，安东内利随后赢得了那场比赛。

    梅赛德斯市场运营主管维多利亚·约翰逊表示，对那些西装的反应让车队兴奋地想在圣保罗更进一步。

    “与Nu的合作基于寻找大胆而有意义的方式将粉丝更紧密地与我们团队联系在一起。紫色的赛车服是这种雄心的绝佳表达，将Nu的独特身份与我们自己的身份相结合，同时庆祝巴西对一级方程式的无限热情。在今年早些时候在迈阿密的紫色赛车服反应之后，我们很兴奋在圣保罗更进一步地实现这一概念，为赛季中最具激情的周末之一创造一些独特的东西。”

    Nubank联合创始人克里斯蒂娜·茹涅拉补充说：“圣保罗大奖赛对巴西人、对Nubank、对梅赛德斯-AMG PETRONAS F1车队车手来说都是一场特别的比赛，因为它发生在该银行成立的城市。”

    这些赛车服也将在Nubank House展出，这是一场为该银行客户举办的免费展览，在体育场进行，直到周日。展览还包括一辆W17的复制品和2021年刘易斯·汉密尔顿穿过的赛车服。

    Nubank表示，巴西对一级方程式的兴趣正在迅速增长。它使用Ibope数据表示，2026年的前10场比赛在该国吸引了5280万人观看，已经超过了2025年整个赛季的观众人数。”

    梅赛德斯车队如果周日一切顺利，将不仅仅因为新形象而庆祝，因为它可以在英特拉戈斯赛道结束车队 constructors’ championship 的争夺。

    Mercedes to race in new suits at Brazil GP in nod to legendary era

    Kimi Antonelli and George Russell will wear the special design all weekend at Interlagos, the second time this season the team has gone purple

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    The Mercedes team will look a little different at this weekend’s Sao Paulo Grand Prix, with world champion Kimi Antonelli and George Russell switching to purple race suits instead of their usual black and turquoise.

    The design was revealed on Tuesday at the Nubank Parque in Sao Paulo, the stadium of football club Palmeiras, as part of the team’s partnership with Brazilian bank Nubank. Both drivers will wear the suits in all sessions of the race weekend.

    “Inspired by the past. Built for the New. Welcome, to the Nu Era,” Antonelli wrote on Instagram. “New look for this weekend!”

    Designed by Mercedes, the suits borrow from those worn in the 1980s and 1990s, the period when Nelson Piquet and Ayrton Senna won their titles. The campaign’s slogan is “Inspired by the Golden Era, Built for the New Era”.

    The two shades both come from the bank. The dark base is taken from Ultravioleta, its premium card, and the lighter purple represents its largest customer base.

    The reveal came with a film featuring both drivers, narrated by Gabriel Leone, who played Senna in Netflix’s series about the three-time champion. Its soundtrack is the Tema da Vitoria, the music that TV Globo played when Brazilian drivers won in F1, and which is closely tied to Senna’s career.

    It is the second time Mercedes has done this in 2026. At the Miami Grand Prix in May, the team used three different purple suits, one for each day of the weekend, with each design carrying coordinates connected to Nubank’s expansion. Antonelli went on to win that race.

    Victoria Johnson, Mercedes’ marketing operations chief, said the reaction to those suits made the team excited to take the idea further in Sao Paulo.

    “The partnership with Nu is based on finding bold and meaningful ways to bring fans closer to our team. The purple race suits are an excellent expression of that ambition, uniting Nu’s unmistakable identity with our own while celebrating the incredible passion Brazil has for Formula 1. After the reaction to our purple suits in Miami earlier this year, it is exciting to take this concept even further in Sao Paulo and create something unique for one of the most passionate race weekends of the season.”

    Nubank co-founder Cristina Junqueira added: “The Sao Paulo Grand Prix is a special round for Brazilians, for Nubank, because it takes place in the city where it was founded, and for the Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 Team drivers.”

    The suits will also be on show at the Nubank House, a free exhibition for the bank’s customers running at the stadium until Sunday. It also includes a replica W17 and a suit worn by Lewis Hamilton in 2021.

    Nubank says interest in F1 is growing quickly in Brazil. Using Ibope data, it said the first 10 races of 2026 reached 52.8 million people in the country, already more than the whole of the 2025 season.

    Mercedes will have more to celebrate than a new look if things go its way on Sunday, as it can wrap up the constructors’ championship at Interlagos.
