    阿利森承认梅赛德斯W17“第18版升级”存在缺陷

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森承认，赛季后期推出的“第18版升级”引入了意外缺陷，使W17变得难以驾驭。

    詹姆斯·阿利森承认存在缺陷的“第18版升级”让梅赛德斯W17变得“难以驾驭”。

    詹姆斯·阿利森承认，梅赛德斯赛季后期的“第18版升级”引入了意外缺陷，使W17变得难以驾驭。

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    梅赛德斯在2026赛季开局强势，迅速建立起统治性领先优势，最终拿下车手和车队双料冠军。但随着赛季接近尾声，W17与其余车阵之间的性能差距明显缩小。

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·艾利森表示，这种差距缩小并非对手追赶的自然结果。艾利森在NU Silver Arrows电台节目中坦言，梅赛德斯自身的研发路径在赛季后期主动损害了赛车的驾驶性。

    在讨论与竞争对手不断缩小的差距时，艾利森指出了车队最新的重大空气动力学套件。

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯，安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯

    “我们最后一步的性能提升，即所谓的‘第18版升级’，我们在雪邦赛道带来的东西，随后在美国又跟进了前翼……它给了我们更多下压力，但也让赛车更难驾驭，”艾利森承认。

    新部件并未在进站圈时间上带来纯粹的提升，反而引入了不可预测的副作用。

    “它在某些方面削减了一点我们未曾预料到的性能，”艾利森解释道。

    艾利森关于‘第18版升级’的承认，也揭示了W17在赛季初几轮为何显得如此不可撼动。

    针对2026年技术规则，国际汽联要求全围场大幅减重30公斤，将最低底盘重量降至仅768公斤。当其他制造商还在为减重苦苦挣扎时，梅赛德斯立即达成了这一工程目标。

    “我们在年初可能也是唯一一辆达到重量限制的赛车，”艾利森透露。“我们在锦标赛中竞争的所有对手都还有一些重量可以削减，而那是他们能够获得的圈速优势，我们却无法获得，因为我们已经很轻，处于重量极限。”

    随着赛季推进，竞争对手车队最终去除了多余重量，赛场自然被压缩——梅赛德斯有缺陷的‘更新18’加剧了这一效应，使W17明显更难榨取速度。

    James Allison admits flawed “Update 18” made the Mercedes W17 a “handful”

    James Allison admits Mercedes' late-season "Update 18" upgrade introduced unexpected flaws that made the W17 a handful

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes stormed out of the gates in 2026, quickly establishing a dominant lead that ultimately secured both the drivers' and constructors' championships. But as the season nears its conclusion, the performance gap between the W17 and the rest of the grid has visibly shrunk.

    According to Mercedes technical director James Allison, this convergence was not just a natural result of rivals catching up. Speaking on the NU Silver Arrows Radio Show, Allison confessed that Mercedes' own development path actively hampered the car's driveability late in the year.

    While discussing the shrinking gap to the competition, Allison pointed to the team's latest major aerodynamic package.

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    "Our final step of performance, our so-called 'Update 18', the thing we brought to the Sepang track and then followed up with the front wing in America... it has given us more downforce, but it has made the car more of a handful," Allison admitted.

    Rather than delivering a pure step forward in-lap time, the new parts introduced unpredictable side effects.

    "It's got some areas where it has subtracted a bit of performance that we were not expecting," Allison explained.

    Allison's admission regarding 'Update 18' also shed light on why the W17 looked so untouchable during the opening rounds of the championship.

    For the 2026 regulations, the FIA mandated a sweeping 30-kilogram weight reduction across the grid, dropping the minimum chassis weight to just 768 kilograms. While other constructors struggled to shed the mass, Mercedes nailed the engineering target immediately.

    "We were also probably the only car on the weight limit at the start of the year," Allison revealed. "All the folk we were competing with in the championship had a bit of timber to shift, and that was lap time that came to them that wasn't available to us because we were already skinny and on the limit with the weight."

    As rival teams eventually lost their excess weight as the season progressed, the field naturally compressed – an effect exacerbated by Mercedes' flawed 'Update 18', making the W17 significantly harder to extract pace from.
