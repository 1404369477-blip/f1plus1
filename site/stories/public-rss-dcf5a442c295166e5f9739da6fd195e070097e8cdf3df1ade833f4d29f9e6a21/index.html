    红牛称维斯塔潘抱怨与巴西站相同，正查找根源

    红牛技术总监皮埃尔·瓦谢表示，拉斯维加斯周四练习赛后车队再次面对平衡问题，与巴西站情况相同，正试图找出维斯塔潘抱怨的根源。由于下雨，车队尚未测试小幅升级，维斯塔潘直到最后五分钟才做出首个计时圈。

    “和巴西站一样”——红牛“试图理解”马克斯·维斯塔潘抱怨的根源

    皮埃尔·瓦谢在首节练习赛后说明，红牛再次面对平衡问题。由于下雨，车队尚未在拉斯维加斯测试其小幅升级

    拉斯维加斯周四练习赛后，红牛和所有F1车队一样，几乎没有得到答案。伊萨克·哈贾尔最终在计时榜上排名第四，但考虑到周末剩余时间不再有雨，这一成绩意义不大。

    因此练习日的条件不具代表性，这正是马克斯·维斯塔潘长时间留在车库的原因。直到最后五分钟、后期红旗之后，这位荷兰车手才做出首个计时圈。实在没有太多可学的东西。

    尽管如此，维斯塔潘在赛道上短暂亮相期间对小红牛22并不满意。

    事情始于一个疑似动力单元问题，维斯塔潘在出场圈报告动力不足。红牛回应称这主要与低温有关，并且会自行改善，不过维斯塔潘回答说：“这不正常！”

    本节练习结束后，技术总监皮埃尔·瓦谢解释了这些抱怨背后的原因，维斯塔潘的队友哈贾尔也提出了同样的抱怨。

    “我认为这主要是能量管理和MGU-K在单圈中的部署问题。在这方面做了一些微调，以确保为单圈优化，”瓦谢说。

    “在这种条件下，抓地力如此之低，能量管理的适应对我们来说需要一些时间。”

    然而，这并不是维斯塔潘唯一的抱怨。这位四届世界冠军还在车队无线电中说：“这辆车不好，伙计们，和巴西一样的问题。”

    瓦谢强调，这与DM01动力单元无关，也与发动机制动无关——这是今年经常困扰维斯塔潘的另一个方面——而纯粹是赛车的平衡问题。

    “不，那与动力单元无关。这是关于赛车平衡的问题，他对赛车的行为方式不满意。我们正试图弄清楚问题出在哪里。”

    在圣保罗，维斯塔潘表示，在使用中性胎的第一段比赛后，赛车的平衡完全改变了。据他说，这不能完全用换上干地胎来解释，因为在最后几圈使用中性胎时，赛车的感觉也与开场阶段截然不同。

    在拉斯维加斯的练习赛中，维斯塔潘在赛车尾部遇到了同样的平衡问题，红牛将在排位赛前对此进行调查。

    尽管如此，哈贾尔表示他对这条街道赛道有“良好的感觉”，不过瓦谢还不准备完全认同这种乐观情绪。

    “我从不乐观。我们有很多工作要做，但不知道我们处在什么位置。我认为每个人都在带来某种升级。有些车队甚至带来了引擎升级。”

    “我们看看自己处在什么位置。很难说。目前，马克斯对赛车不太满意，而伊萨克挺满意。我们得在这上面下功夫，看看如何把它推演到明天的条件下。”

    虽然瓦谢提到了升级，但红牛也为内华达沙漠带来了一处小更新。它涉及前翼端板的一处小改动，哈贾尔说这值“不到十分之一秒”。

    然而，由于条件棘手，红牛尚未能测试这个新部件，将不得不等到第三节练习再试。

    “是的，我们也带来了一些更新，主要想在这里测试它。原本计划在第一节练习测试，但我们没能做到，第二节练习也一样。我们必须在干地上跑它，希望明天早上能够做到。”

    "Same as Brazil" – Red Bull "trying to understand" the root cause of Max Verstappen's complaints

    Pierre Waché clarifies after the opening practice sessions that Red Bull is dealing with a balance issue again. The team has not yet been able to test its minor upgrade in Las Vegas due to the rain

    After Thursday's practice sessions in Las Vegas, Red Bull – like all Formula 1 teams – has been left with very few answers. Isack Hadjar eventually finished fourth on the timesheets, but that means very little given that no more rain is forecast for the remainder of the weekend.

    The conditions were therefore not representative during the practice day, and that was precisely why Max Verstappen stayed in the garage for so long. It was only in the final five minutes, following the late red flag, that the Dutchman set his first timed lap. There was simply very little to learn.

    Nevertheless, Verstappen was not happy with the RB22 during his brief appearance on track.

    It started with a suspected power unit issue, with Verstappen reporting a lack of power on his out-lap. Red Bull responded by explaining that it was mainly related to the low temperatures and would improve on its own, although Verstappen replied: "This is not normal!"

    After the session, technical director Pierre Waché explained the background to these complaints, which were also heard from Verstappen's team-mate Hadjar.

    "I think it's mainly energy management and the MGU-K deployment around the lap. There was a little bit of fine-tuning on that aspect to make sure that it's optimised for the lap," Waché said.

    "When the grip is so low in these conditions, the adaptation of the energy management takes some time on our side."

    However, that was not Verstappen's only complaint. The four-time world champion also said over the team radio: "This car is not good guys, same problem as Brazil."

    Waché stressed that this was not related to the DM01 power unit, nor to engine braking – another aspect that has frequently troubled Verstappen this year – but purely to the car's balance.

    "No, that's not about the power unit. It’s about the car balance, he was not happy with how the car behaved. We are trying to understand where it's coming from."

    In Sao Paulo, Verstappen said that the balance had changed completely after the first stint on intermediates. According to him, this could not be fully explained by the switch to slick tyres, as the car also felt very different during the final laps on inters compared to the opening phase.

    During practice in Las Vegas, Verstappen experienced the same balance problems at the rear of the car, something Red Bull will look into ahead of qualifying.

    Nevertheless, Hadjar said he has "a good feeling" about this street circuit, although Waché is not yet prepared to fully share that optimism.

    "I'm never optimistic. We have a lot of work to do, but it's unknown where we are. I think everybody is bringing some kind of update. Some teams are even bringing some engine updates.

    "We will see where we are. It's difficult to say. At the moment, Max is not very happy with the car, and Isack is. We have to work on it and see how we can extrapolate it to tomorrow’s conditions."

    While Waché mentioned upgrades, Red Bull has also brought a minor update to the Nevada desert. It involves a small modification to the front wing endplate, which Hadjar has said is worth "less than a tenth".

    However, due to the tricky conditions, Red Bull has not yet been able to test the new part and will have to wait until the third practice session to do so.

    "Yeah, we also brought some update, we mainly want to test it here. It was planned to test it in FP1, which we were not able to do, and the same for FP2. We have to run it in the dry and we will hope to be able to do it tomorrow morning."
