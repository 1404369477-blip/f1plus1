    阿隆索2027年代表阿斯顿·马丁出战达喀尔

    两届F1世界冠军费尔南多·阿隆索确认将于2027年代表阿斯顿·马丁厂队参加达喀尔拉力赛，与塞巴斯蒂安·勒布和卢卡斯·莫赖斯组成阵容。他将驾驶Prodrive打造的阿斯顿·马丁Dreadnought出战第49届达喀尔。

    费尔南多·阿隆索将于2027年代表阿斯顿·马丁重返达喀尔拉力赛。

    经过数月猜测，现已确认阿隆索将于明年参加达喀尔，作为Prodrive运营的阿斯顿·马丁车队的一员。

    两届F1世界冠军费尔南多·阿隆索将作为阿斯顿·马丁厂队参加2027年达喀尔拉力赛的核心人物，与塞巴斯蒂安·勒布和卢卡斯·莫赖斯一同组成星光熠熠的阵容。

    阿隆索将驾驶Prodrive打造的阿斯顿·马丁Dreadnought出战第49届达喀尔拉力赛。该赛事在从原定举办地沙特阿拉伯迁出后，将于2027年1月9日至23日在葡萄牙和摩洛哥举行。

    这将标志着这位西班牙人自2020年随丰田完成令人印象深刻的达喀尔首秀以来，首次重返这项全球最受欢迎的拉力越野赛事，当时他获得总成绩第13名，并在第8赛段取得第二名。

    这位45岁的车手将再次与马克·科马搭档，后者曾五次获得达喀尔摩托车组冠军，将在这为期两周的马拉松赛事中担任他的领航员。

    阿隆索表示：“在决定继续代表阿斯顿·马丁征战一级方程式后，我仔细考虑了达喀尔的事。最终，决定变得很简单。我想全身心迎接挑战，在同一年同时参加一级方程式和达喀尔拉力赛。”

    “2020年，达喀尔是我职业生涯中最具挑战性也最令人愉快的经历之一。我对这项赛事以及那些精通它的专家们怀有极大的敬意。

    “马克再次与我并肩作战非常重要，而与阿斯顿·马丁、Prodrive、塞巴斯蒂安和卢卡斯一起，我们拥有一支非凡的车队。”

    “一级方程式仍然是我的优先事项。但我是一名赛车手，正是这样的挑战让我热爱竞争。我期待看到我们能取得怎样的成就。”

    #310 丰田Gazoo Racing车队：费尔南多·阿隆索

    阿隆索长期以来一直怀有重返达喀尔的愿望，即便他仍将主要精力放在留在F1上。在他于9月与阿斯顿·马丁续约后，关于他参加2027年赛事的猜测愈演愈烈。

    据了解，这位西班牙人还于10月在摩洛哥测试了一辆原型车，此前这个非洲国家被确认为2027年达喀尔拉力赛的主要主办地。拉力赛车手经常在摩洛哥进行测试，为即将到来的赛季做准备，那里广袤的沙漠是达喀尔拉力赛的理想测试场地。

    阿隆索参加达喀尔拉力赛的消息于周二得到确认，阿斯顿·马丁宣布将作为厂队历史上首次参加这项世界上最受欢迎的拉力赛。

    这家英国制造商将与赛车运动巨头Prodrive一起争夺总冠军荣誉，延续双方已经涵盖GT赛车和此前原型车项目的成功合作关系。

    阿斯顿·马丁将参赛的赛车被命名为无畏舰，该车最初是为热门《使命召唤》游戏系列构思的一款纯数字军用车辆。

    据了解，Dreadnought 是此前以 Dacia Sandrider 名义参赛的原型车的最新迭代版本，直到雷诺集团在今年与纳赛尔·阿提亚一起赢得胜利后退出越野拉力赛。

    Prodrive 也曾运营达契亚的厂商项目，它在夏天与多家制造商就接管 Sandrider 项目进行了讨论，之后与阿斯顿·马丁达成了协议。

    阿斯顿·马丁首席执行官阿德里安·霍尔马克表示：“阿斯顿·马丁 Dreadnought 的构想是在最极端的环境下主宰任何地形，并经受住一切考验以完成其使命。”

    “现在，我们将与我们的工程合作伙伴 Prodrive 一起，在全球赛车运动中最极端的竞争环境——2027 年达喀尔拉力赛中——挑战这一愿景。”

    “开拓先锋理念是阿斯顿·马丁的本性，正如我们一贯所做的那样，在全球舞台上探索它们的极限。”

    “我们不回避这样的挑战，而是拥抱它们，有了费尔南多、塞巴斯蒂安和卢卡斯，我们拥有三位伟大的冠军，可以与之一同向2027年达喀尔发起强有力的挑战。”

    #219 达契亚 Sandriders 达契亚：塞巴斯蒂安·勒布

    阿斯顿·马丁还将倚重九届世界拉力锦标赛冠军勒布的经验，他自2021年起便加入Prodrive的体系，先是Bahrain Raid Xtreme，随后是达契亚。

    在与包括福特和丰田在内的多家制造商进行洽谈后，这位法国人将代表阿斯顿·马丁参加他的第11次达喀尔拉力赛，继续追逐他的首个冠军。

    “赢得达喀尔对我来说非常重要，”勒布说道。“我们曾多次接近胜利，而继续努力直到实现目标的动力一直都在。与阿斯顿·马丁和Dreadnought一起迎接这一挑战是一个绝佳的机会。这是一个拥有特殊赛车运动故事的品牌，若能帮助它首次赢得达喀尔，我会感到非常自豪。

    “这场比赛已多次证明，它需要的远不止速度。你必须应对艰难的日子，照顾好赛车，并保持专注直到终点。有法比安在我身边，还有费尔南多和卢卡斯，我们将把所有的经验带到团队的项目准备和开发中。面对一辆新车有很多东西要学，而我很享受与工程师们一起做这项工作。目标是到达起点时已准备好为胜利而战。”

    阿斯顿的达喀尔阵容将由莫赖斯补全，他曾在2025年随丰田赢得世界拉力-越野锦标赛冠军，之后于今年转投达契亚。

    在雷诺集团退出后，勒布和莫赖斯都留在Prodrive，而阿提亚和克里斯蒂娜·古铁雷斯成为仅有的两位在2027年尚无席位的达契亚车手。前者如今是史上最成功的拉力赛车手之一，拥有六次达喀尔冠军，但尚未宣布下赛季的计划。

    Fernando Alonso to return to Dakar Rally with Aston Martin in 2027

    Following months of speculation, it has been confirmed that Alonso will race in the Dakar next year as part of the Prodrive-run Aston Martin squad

    Two-time Formula 1 champion Fernando Alonso will headline Aston Martin’s factory entry into the Dakar Rally in 2027, joining Sebastien Loeb and Lucas Moraes as part of a star-studded line-up.

    Alonso will drive a Prodrive-built Aston Martin Dreadnought in the 49th edition of Dakar, which will take place in Portugal and Morocco from 9-23 January 2027 after being relocated from its original location in Saudi Arabia.

    This will mark the Spaniard’s first appearance in the world’s most popular rally-raid since his impressive debut with Toyota in 2020, when he finished 13th overall and recorded a second-place finish on Stage 8.

    The 45-year-old will reprise his partnership with Marc Coma, a five-time Dakar winner on motorcycles, who will serve as his navigator for the two-week marathon.

    “After deciding to continue racing in Formula 1 with Aston Martin, I thought carefully about Dakar,” said Alonso. “In the end, the decision became simple. I wanted to embrace the challenge completely and take on both Formula 1 and the Dakar Rally in the same year.

    “In 2020, Dakar was one of the most challenging and enjoyable experiences of my career. I have enormous respect for this race and the specialists who have mastered it.

    “Having Marc alongside me again is very important, and with Aston Martin, Prodrive, Sebastien and Lucas we have an exceptional team.

    “Formula 1 remains my priority. But I am a racing driver, and challenges like this are why I love competing. I’m looking forward to seeing what we can achieve.”

    #310 Toyota Gazoo Racing: Fernando Alonso

    Alonso had long harboured aspirations of returning to the Dakar, even as he continued to put his primary focus on remaining in F1. Speculation about a 2027 entry intensified after he extended his contract with Aston Martin in September.

    The Spaniard is also understood to have tested a prototype version of the car in Morocco in October, before the African nation was confirmed as the primary host for the 2027 Dakar. Rally drivers regularly test in Morocco to prepare for the forthcoming season, with its vast deserts serving as an ideal testing ground for the Dakar.

    Alonso’s participation in the Dakar was confirmed on Tuesday, as Aston Martin announced that it will be entering the world’s most popular rally-raid as a factory team for the first time in history.

    The British manufacturer will compete for outright honours with motorsport powerhouse Prodrive, extending a successful partnership that already encompasses GT racing and previous prototype ventures.

    The car that Aston Martin will enter has been badged as Dreadnought, which was first conceived as a digital-only military vehicle for the popular Call of Duty game series.

    Dreadnought is understood to be the latest iteration of the prototype that previously competed as the Dacia Sandrider until the Renault Group pulled out of cross-country rallying following its victory with Nasser Al-Attiyah this year.

    Prodrive, which also ran the factory Dacia programme, held discussions with multiple manufacturers about taking over the Sandrider project in the summer before reaching an agreement with Aston Martin.

    “Aston Martin Dreadnought was conceived to dominate any terrain, under the most extreme circumstances, and come through it all to deliver on its mission,” said Aston Martin CEO Adrian Hallmark.

    “Now along with our engineering partner, Prodrive, we will challenge this vision in the most extreme competitive environment in global motorsport – the Dakar 2027.

    “It is in Aston Martin’s nature to forge forward with pioneering concepts, and just as we have always done, explore their limits on the global stage.

    “We do not shy away from such challenges, but embrace them, and in Fernando, Sebastien and Lucas we have three great champions with whom to mount a formidable challenge on Dakar 2027.”

    #219 The Dacia Sandriders Dacia: Sebastien Loeb

    Aston Martin will also count on the experience of nine-time World Rally champion Loeb, who has been part of Prodrive’s set-up since 2021, first as Bahrain Raid Xtreme and subsequently Dacia.

    After holding talks with several manufacturers, including Ford and Toyota, the Frenchman will contest his 11th Dakar with Aston Martin as he continues his pursuit of a maiden win.

    “Winning the Dakar is very important to me,” said Loeb. “We have come close several times, and the motivation is there to keep working until we achieve it. To take on this challenge with Aston Martin and Dreadnought is a great opportunity. It is a brand with a special motorsport story, and I would feel very proud to help it win the Dakar for the first time.

    “This race has proved many times that it demands much more than speed. You have to manage the difficult days, look after the car and stay focused until the finish. With Fabian alongside me, and with Fernando and Lucas, we will bring all our experience to the preparation and development of the programme as a team. There is a lot to learn with a new car, and I enjoy that work with the engineers. The objective is to arrive at the start ready to fight for victory.”

    Aston’s Dakar line-up will be completed by Moraes, who won the 2025 World Rally-Raid Championship with Toyota before moving to Dacia this year.

    With both Loeb and Moraes remaining with Prodrive following Renault Group’s exit, Al-Attiyah and Cristina Gutierrez remain the only Dacia drivers left without a seat for 2027. The former, now one of the most successful rally drivers of all time with six Dakar wins, has yet to announce his plans for next season.
