    F1拉斯维加斯站：降雨影响一练

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛第一节自由练习因持续降雨导致赛道湿滑，没有完成有意义的测试。小红牛车手劳森此前在巴西试用雨胎后表示，在拉斯维加斯这样的赛道遇到降雨会陷入麻烦。

    F1拉斯维加斯站：降雨影响一练

    持续降雨使拉斯维加斯大奖赛赛道变得像溜冰场一样滑，一级方程式第一节自由练习中没有完成有意义的测试。

    “如果我们在像拉斯维加斯这样的赛道上遇到降雨，说实话，我认为我们会陷入麻烦，”小红牛车手利亚姆·劳森在巴西试用今年的雨胎后预测道。

    他的评论反映了围场内的普遍情绪，因为倍耐力轮胎难以升温，尤其是在这条位于赌城的6公里街道赛道这样低速弯多且温度极低的情况下。

    劳森的话被证明是有先见之明的，因为周四上午内华达州的天空间歇性地放晴，而当地时间下午4点30分第一次练习赛开始前又下起了更多的雨。

    由于周末剩余时间预计几乎没有降雨，这自然导致了一节缺乏动作的练习赛，让花了大价钱购买赛事高得令人咋舌的门票的车迷们只能转而去二十一点赌桌上寻找刺激。

    在低抓地力的赛道上冒险驶出，面对近在咫尺的护墙，感觉也像是一场赌博。但仿佛至少有意给主场车迷一些交代，美国拥有的凯迪拉克车队的塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯率先勇敢地使用全雨胎完成了一个探索性单圈。随后哈斯车队的拉斐尔·卡马拉和平川亮也加入其中，后者代替奥利弗·贝尔曼完成了新秀第一次练习赛。

    第二辆凯迪拉克——瓦尔特利·博塔斯的赛车也探索了这恶劣的条件，在他唯一的一圈中用一个巨大的甩尾庆祝了自己2027年的确认，表明这两支美国拥有的车队当然不能被指责没有出力。

    当电视观众和那些热衷于分析圈速与长距离表现的人同样感到不满足时，第一次自由练习赛实际上成了全年最具画面感的时段之一。拉斯维加斯大道本就上镜，但雨水让整条长街更沉浸于霓虹色的倒影之中，那种色调亨特·S·汤普森大概也会认可。

    “太棒了，这条长街真漂亮，”法拉利替补车手迪诺·贝加诺维奇在代替刘易斯·汉密尔顿出战该节练习时惊叹道。

    “好好观光吧……不过这一圈我们要进维修区了，谢谢，”法拉利那边传来例行公事般的回复。

    贝加诺维奇倒不妨好好享受，因为对这位瑞典裔波黑车手来说，一开始留在车库里根本不是选项。车队的四次强制性新秀第一次自由练习赛要计入，车手至少必须离开车库一次。

    这解释了为什么三位新秀车手都退出了比赛，其中包括在阿斯顿·马丁车队顶替费尔南多·阿隆索出战的杰克·克劳福德，尽管他们会为这次难得的机会未能提供实际里程数而感到遗憾。

    正如世界冠军基米·安东内利报告了水滑现象一样，几位更有经验的车手也险些撞墙。亚历克斯·阿尔本在长街尽头的11号弯直接冲了出去，而奥迪车队的尼科·霍肯伯格也在湿透的左弯锁死了轮胎。

    “这几乎就像溜冰场一样，”红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘报告说，这位荷兰人在最好的时候也是街道赛道的冷静崇拜者。

    最终，除了五名车手外，所有人都体验了这些条件，迈凯伦和小红牛车队的四辆赛车认为不值得一试，阿斯顿·马丁车队的兰斯·斯特罗尔也感觉不是特别有动力。

    卡马拉以及阿尔派车队的皮埃尔·加斯利和弗兰科·科拉平托是仅有的三位两次驶出维修区的车手，其中这位阿根廷车手在直道上短暂失控，几乎重现了他在2024年代表威廉姆斯车队高速排位赛撞车时的场景。

    为避免疑问，本次未记录任何圈速。

    F1 Las Vegas GP: Rain impacts FP1 running

    With steady rainfall turning the Las Vegas Grand Prix track into an ice rink, no meaningful running was completed during the first session of Formula 1 free practice.

    "If we're on a track like Vegas and it rains, honestly, I think we're in trouble," Racing Bulls driver Liam Lawson predicted after sampling this year's rain tyres in Brazil.

    His comments reflected the general mood in the paddock, as the Pirelli tyres are difficult to bring up to temperature, especially at a circuit with low cornering speeds and glacial temperatures like the 6km street circuit in the gambling haven.

    Lawson's comments proved relevant as the heavens opened intermittently in Nevada on Thursday morning, and more rain arrived right before the start of FP1 at 4:30 pm local time.

    With little rain expected for the remainder of the weekend, it naturally led to an action-starved session, leaving fans forking out the event's eye-watering ticket prices to look for action at the blackjack tables instead.

    Venturing out on the low-grip surface, with the walls looming large, felt like a gamble, too. But as if intent on at least offering the home fans something, Sergio Perez of US-owned team Cadillac was first to brave the conditions on full wet tyres for an exploratory lap. He was later joined by the Haas duo of Rafael Camara and Ryo Hirakawa, the latter completing a rookie FP1 session in place of Oliver Bearman.

    The second Cadillac of Valtteri Bottas also explored the inclement conditions, celebrating his 2027 confirmation with a huge tank-slapper on his one lap, showing the two US-owned teams certainly could not be accused of failing to chip in.

    While TV audiences and punters intent on analysing lap times and long runs were also left wanting, FP1 actually became one of the most picturesque sessions of the year. Las Vegas Boulevard is photogenic at the best of times, but the rain further drowned the Strip in neon-tinted reflections, hues Hunter S. Thompson would have approved of.

    "Amazing, it's a lovely Strip," gawked Ferrari reserve driver Dino Beganovic as he replaced Lewis Hamilton for the session.

    "Enjoy the sightseeing... but we'll box this lap, please," came the perfunctory reply from Ferrari.

    Beganovic may as well have enjoyed it, as staying tucked inside the garage was not an option to begin with for the Swedish-Bosnian driver. For any of the teams' four mandatory rookie FP1 sessions to count, drivers must at least leave the garage once.

    It explains why all three rookie runners went out, including Jak Crawford, who deputised for Fernando Alonso at Aston Martin, though they will be ruing the lack of actual mileage the rare opportunity was able to provide.

    As world champion Kimi Antonelli reported aquaplaning, several more experienced drivers had close shaves with the walls. Alex Albon went straight on at Turn 11 at the end of the Strip, while Audi's Nico Hulkenberg also locked up at the drenched left-hander.

    "It's almost like an ice rink," reported Red Bull's Max Verstappen, the Dutchman a rather cool admirer of street circuits at the best of times.

    Eventually, all but five drivers sampled the conditions, with the four cars from McLaren and Racing Bulls thinking better of it, and Aston's Lance Stroll also not feeling particularly motivated.

    Camara and the Alpine duo of Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto were the only three drivers to venture out twice, with the Argentine nearly reliving the scenes of his high-speed qualifying shunt in 2024 for Williams as he momentarily lost control of his car on the straight.

    For the avoidance of doubt, no lap times were recorded.
