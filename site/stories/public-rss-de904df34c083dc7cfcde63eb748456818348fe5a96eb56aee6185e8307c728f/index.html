    赫塔提前结束艰难的F2赛季

    科尔顿·赫塔在2026赛季还剩两轮的情况下提前离开了F2席位，未能获得2027年凯迪拉克F1的驾驶机会。

    科顿·赫塔在2026赛季还剩两轮的情况下放弃了他的F2赛车席位，因为他错过了2027年凯迪拉克F1赛车的驾驶机会。

    赫塔通过多次参加凯迪拉克FP1赛事获得了必要的超级驾照积分，但他无法说服凯迪拉克在下一年取代瓦尔特里·博塔。

    他F1任务的一部分是做出大胆的赌注，放弃赢得比赛的IndyCar赛车生涯，转而在2026年F1系列赛F2中参赛，但赫塔在那里表现不佳，他在车手锦标赛中排名第19，只有一次进入前五名的成绩。

    赫塔在适应他毫无经验的Pirelli轮胎以及F2所需的非常不同的驾驶风格方面遇到了困难，加上周末练习时间有限（在排位赛之前只有一次45分钟的练习时间）和周末之外的最小测试，所有这些都意味着赫塔今年难以取得显著进步。

    他不会参加在卡塔尔和阿布扎比的F2赛季最后两轮比赛，他将由2027年Hitech F2车手Roman Bilinski取代，他将提前为车队亮相。

    由于巴库和卡塔尔/阿布扎比轮次之间有两个月的间隔，因此赛季末的F2车手变动很常见，这通常可以为2027年的计划提供准备时间。

    Herta明年不会留在F2的第二年，这一点早已确认，因此提前在2026年退出一直是选项之一。

    “我想感谢Colton在他与Hitech共事期间的表现和决心，尽管这是一个充满挑战的季节，”Hitech F2赛车工程主管Jan Sumann说。

    “我们祝愿他未来一切顺利。”

    在卡塔尔和阿布扎比，F2车手发生了多次变动，塞巴斯蒂安·蒙特亚奥离开Prema车队，填补比林斯基在DAMS车队的空缺，尼古拉·拉科尔特取代蒙特亚奥加入Prema车队。

    即使F2积分领跑者拉斐尔·卡马拉也不会在卡塔尔和阿布扎比参加F2比赛，因为他正在与他的2027年雇主哈斯车队一起完成F1赛季。

    预计他将由奥迪F1青训车队车手弗雷迪·斯莱特在Invicta车队接替，他将在2027年为该车队参赛。

    至于赫塔，这位26岁的车手2027年的计划尚未公布——包括这其中包括他是否会接受凯迪拉克提供的预备车手角色。

    这仍然可能让赫塔在2028年为凯迪拉克的F1赛车席位保持竞争力，但他需要说服车队这次值得为了他而让博塔斯让位。

    赫塔在2027年全职回归IndyCar赛车似乎几乎不可能，但他可能会在明年重返印第安纳波利斯500赛事。

    英文原文

    Colton Herta has vacated his Formula 2 seat with two rounds of the 2026 season to go, having missed out on a 2027 Cadillac Formula 1 drive.

    Herta earned the necessary superlicence points through multiple Cadillac FP1 outings, but he couldn't convince Cadillac to replace Valtteri Bottas next year.

    Part of his F1 mission had been making the audacious gamble of leaving behind a race-winning IndyCar career to race in F1 feeder series F2 in 2026, but Herta's struggled massively there, as he's only 19th in the drivers' championship with just one top-five finish.

    Struggles adapting to the Pirelli tyres Herta has no experience of, as well as the very different driving style F2 requires, plus limited in-weekend track time (there's only a single 45 minutes of practice before qualifying) and minimal testing outside of race weekends, have all meant Herta's struggled to make significant progress this year.

    He won't compete in the final two rounds of the F2 season in Qatar and Abu Dhabi with his Hitech team and will instead be replaced by 2027 Hitech F2 driver Roman Bilinski, who will make an early debut with the team.

    Late-season F2 driver changes are common given there's a two-month gap between Baku and the Qatar/Abu Dhabi rounds, which can often provide preparation for 2027 programmes.

    Herta not staying for a second year in F2 next year has long been confirmed, so an early 2026 exit was always an option.

    "I would like to thank Colton for his commitment and determination throughout his time with Hitech, despite a challenging season," Hitech's F2 head of race engineering Jan Sumann said.

    "We wish him the very best for the future."

    There are multiple F2 driver changes for Qatar and Abu Dhabi, with Sebastian Montoya leaving Prema to fill Bilinski's DAMS drive, and Nicola Lacorte replacing Montoya at Prema.

    Even F2 points leader Rafael Camara won't be racing in F2 in Qatar and Abu Dhabi as he's seeing out the F1 season with his 2027 employer Haas.

    He's expected to be replaced at Invicta by Audi's F1 junior Freddie Slater, who will race for that team in 2027.

    As for Herta, the 26-year-old's 2027 plans are yet to be revealed - including whether that will include the reserve driver role Cadillac has offered him.

    That could still keep Herta in play for a 2028 F1 seat with Cadillac, but he'd need to convince the team that it was worth moving Bottas aside for him this time.

    Herta returning to racing in IndyCar in 2027 full-time would appear nearly impossible, but he may return to the Indianapolis 500 next year.
