    佩尔曼称劳森2026年进步是"惊喜"，获2027年小红牛席位

    小红牛车队负责人艾伦·佩尔曼表示，利亚姆·劳森2026年的进步堪称"惊喜"，车队最终选择他而非红牛青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫，与阿尔维德·林德布拉德搭档出战2027赛季。

    利亚姆·劳森堪称"惊喜"，他因此获得2027年小红牛F1席位——艾伦·佩尔曼

    小红牛车队负责人艾伦·佩尔曼首次就他选择支持利亚姆·劳森而非尼古拉·措洛夫出战2027赛季一事接受采访

    小红牛F1负责人艾伦·佩尔曼表示，利亚姆·劳森2026年状态的提升堪称"惊喜"，并回应了有关红牛内部愿景存在分歧的说法。

    在接受Motorsport.com的一次广泛采访中，佩尔曼谈到了为何在现任车手劳森与红牛青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫之间选择与阿尔维德·林德布拉德搭档的决定耗时如此之久，以及为何支持劳森并不一定违背车队培养年轻人才的宗旨。

    “有利亚姆在，对我们车队来说非常棒，这一点毫无疑问。他一直表现得极为出色，”佩尔曼说道。“可以说，他凭一己之力击败了阿尔派。但这不仅仅是关于这一点。这关乎培养年轻人才。老实说，如果没有一个基准，要做到这一点是极其困难的。

    “我知道有关于让措洛夫上车的讨论，但我们留下利亚姆的原因之一，首先毫无疑问是因为他的表现，但这也是为了未来的发展。我们对阿尔维德评价非常、非常高，这将使他能够成长，也将使我们能够看到进展如何。

    艾伦·佩尔曼，小红牛，利亚姆·劳森，小红牛

    “利亚姆无疑有了巨大进步，而阿尔维德有这一切可以观察。我们在赛车之间共享所有这些数据，以及他在做什么、他如何在比赛中最大限度地发挥赛车性能。我认为这对阿尔维德来说绝对是无价的。”

    有强烈迹象表明，这一延迟做出的决定是由于参与决策的各方利益相关者之间存在愿景分歧，其中不仅包括佩尔曼，还包括更广泛的红牛公司，包括佩尔曼的上级奥利弗·明茨拉夫以及红牛车队负责人洛朗·梅基斯。但佩尔曼与梅基斯口径一致，坚称这一决定是由小红牛做出的。

    “洛朗在媒体上已经非常明确地表示，这不是他的决定，”佩尔曼坚称。“这是小红牛的决定，我在小红牛负责体育事务。这一点很清楚。不过，我有上级奥利弗·明茨拉夫，我们还有股东。”

    “如此重要的决定不是我一个人做出的。这是我和我的上级以及股东共同做出的，确保他们满意。而这件事比你们希望的时间稍长一些，说实话，原因只是我在花时间确保我们做出正确的决定。”

    Liam Lawson has been a "revelation" as he earned 2027 Racing Bulls F1 deal - Alan Permane

    Racing Bulls team principal Alan Permane has given his first interview on his decision to back Liam Lawson for 2027 over Nikola Tsolov

    Racing Bulls Formula 1 chief Alan Permane says Liam Lawson's improved 2026 form has been a "revelation", and addressed suggestions of a difference in vision within Red Bull.

    In a wide-ranging interview with Motorsport.com, Permane addressed why the decision between incumbent Lawson and Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov to partner Arvid Lindblad had taken so long, and why backing Lawson doesn't have to go against the team's manta to bring up young talent.

    "Having Liam is great for our team, there's no doubt. He's been immense," Permane said. "He's beaten Alpine on his own, if you like. But it's not just about that. It is about developing young talent. And honestly, that is incredibly difficult to do without having a benchmark.

    "I know there was talk about putting Tsolov in the car, but one of the reasons we have kept Liam is firstly because of how he's performed, no doubt, but it's also about developing for the future. We rate Arvid very, very highly, and this will enable him to develop and it will enable us to see how that's going.

    Alan Permane, Racing Bulls, Liam Lawson, Racing Bulls

    "Liam has undoubtedly massively improved and Arvid has got all that to look at. We share all that data across cars and what's he doing and how is he getting the most out of the car in the race. And I think that's absolutely invaluable for Arvid."

    There are strong suggestions the delayed decision was prompted by a difference in vision between the various stakeholders involved in the decision, which not only includes Permane but also the wider Red Bull company, including Permane's boss Oliver Mintzlaff as well as Red Bull Racing boss Laurent Mekies. But Permane echoed Mekies in insisting the call was made by Racing Bulls.

    "Laurent has been very clear in the press that the decision is not his," Permane insisted. "It's a Racing Bulls decision and I'm responsible for sporting [matters] at Racing Bulls. That's clear. However, I have a boss, Oliver Mintzlaff, and we have shareholders as well.

    "A decision as important as this isn't just made by myself. It's made by myself in conjunction with my boss and the shareholders, making sure they're happy. And the reason it's taken a little bit longer than you guys would like, honestly, is that I was just taking my time and making sure we got to the right decision."
