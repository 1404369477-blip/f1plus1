    未播出的拉斯维加斯电台透露，红牛考虑放弃维斯塔潘的领先位置

    未播出的拉斯维加斯电台消息显示，红牛车队在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛期间考虑让维斯塔潘在最后一圈放弃领先位置。

    未播出的拉斯维加斯电台透露，红牛考虑放弃维斯塔潘的领先位置

    红牛电台消息显示，汤姆·哈特询问维斯塔潘是否考虑在拉斯维加斯大奖赛最后一圈让基米·安东内利超过他

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛赛车，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和比赛分析是至关重要的

    在几乎任何其他F1比赛中，提出让领跑者在最后一圈故意放弃第一名的想法听起来都是不可想象的。但在2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛中，红牛的维修区确实提出了这个想法

    车队无线电信息揭示了范斯特appen和赛车工程师汤姆·哈特如何激烈地计算周六晚上35次领先变化惊心动魄的最后一阶段。早在第39圈，范斯特appen就感觉到梅赛德斯已经破解了他的在赛道上的部署模式，并要求维修区制定新的计划。

    “你们必须看看最后一圈的计划，因为他们已经想明白了，他们仍然可以[不清楚]这条线，”范斯特appen通过无线电说道。“所以我们需要看看我们最后一圈需要做什么。”

    哈特回答说策略小组正在“讨论”中，然后在第46圈回来时给出了直截了当的结论：“在部署方面，我们没有比你现在正在做的策略更好的了。所以最后一圈，你已经使用了最好的策略。”

    梅赛德斯的车手安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，范斯特appen，红牛车队

    一圈之后，哈特警告说前五名赛车之间的差距只有一秒。然后是第48圈，乔治·拉塞尔在与队友基米·安东内利争夺时锁定了第5弯的护栏——维斯塔潘讽刺地说了句‘干得好，我的天啊’，因为他的对手滑出了赛道。

    这使2026年世界冠军安东内利成为维斯塔潘在最后一圈的唯一威胁。在警告了第5弯的碎片并更新了间隔后，哈特在第49圈中途给出了当晚最大胆的战略提示。

    “安东内利领先1.2秒，皮亚斯特里领先1.9秒。想想看，你想要在检测时成为领跑车，马克斯。这是最后一圈了，”哈特对他说。

    哈特的逻辑集中在拉斯维加斯独特的2026年规则上。由于赛道的唯一超车模式检测点位于第16弯之后，任何在那里落后一秒的赛车在整个最后一圈将获得额外的0.5兆焦耳电力部署——搭配1.9公里长直道的强大尾流。领跑过那条线可能会让维斯塔潘在终点线前变成一个靶子。

    然而，维斯塔潘从未需要冒险让安东内利通过——因为梅赛德斯的小伙子正试图用同样的手段反其道而行。

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    安东内利试图在16号弯的检测线上保持在维斯塔潘后面，并在第49圈时发动梅赛德斯最后的冲刺，他在第14弯接近时松开油门，然后轻轻重新施加动力以保持动力。这个意外的踏板序列打乱了车辆的能量回收软件，导致他出弯时电池耗尽，在直道上损失了近两秒。

    在最后一圈开始之前，两辆麦拉伦就冲了过去，将哈特的第50圈简报变成了胜利之旅。'最后一圈，车在后面，诺里斯，' 哈特告诉维斯塔潘。'诺里斯1.2，皮亚斯特里2.4。我们可以使用14号弯的加速退出。'

    维斯塔潘在最后一段比赛中以1分36.450秒的干净成绩击败了兰多·诺里斯，领先0.639秒，赢得了他2026年的第四次胜利——以及总成绩的第75次胜利——在车手积分榜上领先刘易斯·汉密尔顿37分，同时在车队积分榜上领先迈凯轮9分，进入卡塔尔站。

    Unaired Las Vegas radio reveals Red Bull considered giving up Max Verstappen's lead

    Red Bull radio reveals Tom Hart asked Max Verstappen to consider letting Kimi Antonelli pass before Vegas's final lap

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    In almost any other Formula 1 race, suggesting a race leader deliberately surrender first place with one lap left would sound unthinkable. At the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, Red Bull’s pitwall genuinely floated the idea to Max Verstappen.

    Team radio messages revealed just how intensely Verstappen and race engineer Tom Hart calculated the closing stages of Saturday night’s 35-lead-change thriller. As early as Lap 39, Verstappen sensed Mercedes had deciphered his deployment pattern down the Strip and pressed his pitwall for a fresh plan.

    "You guys have to look at the plan for the final lap, because they have figured it out, that they can still [unclear] the line," Verstappen radioed. "So we need to see what we have to do for the final lap only."

    Hart replied that the strategy group had it "under discussion", before returning seven laps later on Lap 46 with a blunt verdict: "Deployment-wise, we don’t have anything better than the strategy you are doing at the moment. So final lap, you are already using the best strategy."

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    One lap later, Hart warned that the top five were stacked within a single second. Then came Lap 48, when George Russell locked up into the Turn 5 barrier while fighting team-mate Kimi Antonelli - drawing a sarcastic "Well done. My god," from Verstappen as his rival skidded off.

    That left 2026 world champion Antonelli as Verstappen’s sole remaining threat entering the penultimate tour. After warning of Turn 5 debris and updating the intervals, Hart delivered the boldest tactical prompt of the night midway through Lap 49.

    "Antonelli at 1.2, Piastri at 1.9. Just consider whether you want to be the lead car at the detection, Max. This is the penultimate lap," Hart told him.

    Hart’s logic centred on Las Vegas’s unique 2026 ruleset. With the circuit’s lone Overtake Mode detection point positioned immediately after the Turn 16 chicane, any driver trailing within one second there gained an extra 0.5 MJ of electrical deployment for the entire final lap - paired with a potent slipstream down the 1.9-kilometre Strip. Leading across that line risked turning Verstappen into a sitting duck before the chequered flag.

    Yet Verstappen never had to gamble on letting Antonelli through - because Mercedes’ teenager was trying to pull the exact same bluff in reverse.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    Aiming to stay behind Verstappen at the Turn 16 detection line and unleash a Mercedes final-lap battery push, Antonelli lifted off the throttle approaching Turn 14 on Lap 49 and gently reapplied power to maintain momentum. That unexpected pedal sequence disrupted his car’s energy-harvesting software, leaving him exiting the corner with zero battery and costing nearly two seconds down the straight.

    Both McLarens surged past before the final lap even began, transforming Hart’s Lap 50 briefing into a victory cruise. "Final lap, car behind, Norris," Hart informed Verstappen. "Norris 1.2, Piastri 2.4. And we can use boost exit Turn 14 regardless."

    Verstappen fired in a clean 1m36.450s final lap to beat Lando Norris by 0.639 seconds for his fourth win of 2026 - and 75th overall - stretching 37 points clear of Lewis Hamilton for second in the drivers’ championship while keeping Red Bull nine points ahead of McLaren for third in the constructors’ race entering Qatar.
