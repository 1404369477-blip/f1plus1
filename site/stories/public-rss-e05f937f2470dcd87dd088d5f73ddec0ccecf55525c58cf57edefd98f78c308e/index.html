    Isack Hadjar: Red Bull driver to return to action at Azerbaijan GP after missing three races with wrist injury

    Isack Hadjar missed the first three races following the summer break after sustaining a wrist injury while doing fitness training; watch every session of the Azerbaijan GP from Thursday live on Sky Sports F1, with the race on Saturday at 12pm

    Red Bull driver Isack Hadjar will return to action at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix after missing three races with a wrist injury.

    Hadjar, who has fared reasonably well in his first season as Max Verstappen's team-mate, was unable to drive at races in the Netherlands, Italy and Spain after sustaining the injury while doing fitness training during Formula 1's summer break in August.

    The 21-year-old Frenchman's return to the cockpit will come during Thursday's two practice sessions in Baku, with this weekend's schedule moved a day earlier to avoid a clash with Azerbaijan Remembrance Day.
