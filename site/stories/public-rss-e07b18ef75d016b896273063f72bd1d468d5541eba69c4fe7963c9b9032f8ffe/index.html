    红牛对劳森在荷兰大奖赛的表现印象深刻：'他做到了他能做的'

    红牛F1车队负责人劳伦特·梅基斯称赞利亚姆·劳森在荷兰大奖赛中最大限度发挥了赛车潜力。这位24岁车手从第八位发车，以第七名完赛。

    红牛对劳森在荷兰大奖赛的表现印象深刻：'他做到了他能做的'

    Lawson在周末的荷兰大奖赛上顶替受伤的哈贾尔出战，并最大化发挥了他的红牛赛车性能

    红牛一级方程式车队负责人Laurent Mekies对这位超级替补劳森印象深刻，因为他在荷兰大奖赛上发挥出了最大潜力。

    这位24岁的车手在排位赛第八起步后最终排名第七，落后于三大强队——迈凯伦、梅赛德斯和法拉利，并且由于驾驶另一台小红牛22的维斯塔潘撞车而提升了一个名次。

    这发生在一场更为艰难的冲刺赛之后，Lawson 在那场比赛中以第 11 位发车并以第 11 位完赛。这个周末，由于 哈贾尔 手腕受伤，他从 小红牛 临时转到了 红牛。

    Mekies 告诉 天空体育：“如今要从一个车队跳槽到另一个车队实在太难了，所以我认为 Liam 已经做到了他能做的。”

    “他显然在昨天的排位赛中做得非常出色，只比 Max 慢了十分之一秒，今天他基本上是一个人在比赛。”

    “我们缺少 Max 这个参照，所以你知道的，对他的节奏不作评判，但他拿到了积分，拿到了第七名的积分，这大概就是他今天能做到的最好成绩了。”

    尽管劳森在第55圈于3号弯因伊斯特班·奥孔在前一弯停车后，未能在单黄旗情况下减速而被罚10秒，他的成绩依然如此。

    这位新西兰车手确认他会“从中吸取教训”，但考虑到他领先排名第八的尼科·霍肯伯格一整圈完成比赛，这对周日他在赞德沃特的比赛几乎没有影响。

    “今天很艰难，说实话，”目前在车手积分榜排名第九的劳森告诉天空体育。

    “第一圈相当有趣，但从那之后，说实话，我们的速度就跟不上前面那几位车手。”

    “但我觉得经过昨天之后，我就知道这会是一场艰难的比赛，我们俩都落在他们后面，而且我觉得我昨天跑了一记相当不错的单圈，所以我早就知道会很难。

    “可能没那么难，比如我们确实很难跟上前排的车队，但我确信自己能从中学到一些东西。周末结束后我显然会试着去理解一下。”

    赞德沃特是Lawson自2025年初两次令人失望的比赛之后首次为红牛出战，目前尚不清楚他是否会回到姐妹车队小红牛参加接下来的蒙扎站。

    Red Bull impressed by Liam Lawson at Dutch GP: 'He did what he could'

    Lawson replaced the injured Isack Hadjar at the Dutch Grand Prix over the weekend and maximised his Red Bull package

    Red Bull’s Formula 1 boss Laurent Mekies was impressed by its super-sub Liam Lawson after he maximised what was possible at the Dutch Grand Prix.

    The 24-year-old finished seventh behind the top three teams – McLaren, Mercedes and Ferrari – after starting eighth but gaining a place due to Max Verstappen’s crash in the sister RB22.

    It came after a tougher sprint race where Lawson started and finished in 11th, on the weekend he temporarily moved from Racing Bulls to Red Bull due to Isack Hadjar’s wrist injury.

    Mekies told Sky Sports: “It's so difficult nowadays to jump from a team to another, so I think Liam did what he could.

    “He obviously did a very good job in qualifying yesterday being only a tenth away from Max, today was a bit of a race on his own.

    “We are missing the reference from Max so, you know, no judgment on the pace but he took the points, he took the points of seventh place and that's probably the best he could have done today.”

    Lawson’s result came despite him receiving a 10-second penalty for failing to slow down for a single yellow flag at Turn 3 on lap 55, after Esteban Ocon had stopped at the following corner.

    The New Zealander confirmed he’ll “learn from that”, but considering he finished a lap ahead of eighth-placed Nico Hulkenberg, it made little difference to his race at Zandvoort on Sunday.

    “Today was hard, honestly,” Lawson, who is ninth in the championship, told Sky Sports.

    “The first lap was quite fun, but from then, honestly, we just struggled for pace to match the front guys.

    “But I feel like after yesterday I knew it was going to be a hard race, we were both behind them and I feel like I did a pretty good lap yesterday, so I knew it was gonna be hard.

    “Probably not that hard, like we really struggled to keep up with the front teams, but I'm sure there's stuff I can learn from. I'll obviously try and have an understanding after the weekend.”

    Zandvoort was Lawson’s first race for Red Bull since his two disappointing outings at the start of 2025, and it is not yet clear if he will return to sister squad Racing Bulls for Monza up next.
