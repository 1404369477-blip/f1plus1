    汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘呼吁新加坡F1赛道改动

    汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘希望对滨海湾街道赛道进行改动，他们认为需要更长的直道或一个合适的发夹弯，以使新加坡成为伟大的赛车赛道。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿、法拉利、马克斯·维斯塔潘、红牛车队

    法拉利车迷们。最新的Scuderia 法拉利 F1新闻、车手更新、技术分析和来自马拉内洛及更远处的比赛反应。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿和马克斯·维斯塔潘希望对滨海湾街道赛道进行改动，他们认为需要一条更长的直道或一个合适的弯道，以便让新加坡成为一条伟大的赛车赛道。

    七届冠军解释说，他喜欢在街道赛道上比赛，但他补充说，他和维斯塔潘一直在讨论对赛道布局的潜在改动。

    当被问及赛道是否会在赛后冲刺新闻发布会上举办冲刺赛时，汉密尔顿表示：“我不知道。我的意思是，我真的很喜欢这里的比赛，”

    我只是对马克斯说，我认为他们需要改变赛道，可能是在某个地方增加一条更长的直道，以便让它真正成为一条伟大的赛车赛道。

    “因为我认为它有潜力成为一个伟大的赛车赛道。这是一条在排位赛中单圈驾驶的好赛道，但事实上并没有很多……我想可能只有一个地方，真的，你可以足够接近以完成超越。”

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利，马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队

    “但在其他地方，比如[转弯]16，赛道中间的凸起处，会因为赛道不是很平坦而出现火花。我不知道是因为下雨还是其他什么原因，但我认为可以做一些调整，让这个赛道成为100%的伟大赛道。但它们是否会这么做，我不知道。”

    维斯塔潘同意，滨海湾街道赛道将受益于更长的直道或一个‘真正的发夹弯’。

    “我完全同意我们需要更长的直道或者更多的合适发夹弯来超车。但即便如此，我们还需要在直道上更大的差距，”红牛车手说。

    维斯塔潘在新加坡的冲刺赛中获胜，随后是法拉利的汉密尔顿和查尔斯·勒克莱尔。荷兰人随后也赢得了大奖赛，勒克莱尔在第二位，梅赛德斯车手基米·安东内利在第三位登上领奖台。

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen call for Singapore F1 track changes

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen have called for changes to Singapore's Marina Bay Street Circuit

    Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen want changes to the Marina Bay Street Circuit, saying a longer straight or a proper hairpin is needed to make Singapore a great racing track.

    While the seven-time champion explained that he loves racing at the street circuit, he added that he and Verstappen had been discussing potential changes to the layout.

    "I don’t know. I mean, I love the race here. I really do love the race," Hamilton said when he was asked about the circuit hosting a sprint race during the post-sprint press conference.

    "I was just saying to Max, I think they need to change the track to maybe a longer straight somewhere in order to allow for it to be truly a great racing circuit.

    "Because I think it has the potential to be a great racing circuit. It’s a great track to drive over a single lap in qualifying, but there literally just aren’t many… There’s like one place, I guess, really, that you could potentially get close enough to overtake.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing

    "But then in other places, like [Turn] 16, the crest in the middle of the track, sparks come up because the track isn’t particularly flat. I don’t know if it’s for the rain or whatever it is, but I think there’s some adjustments that can be made to make this 100% a great circuit. But whether they’re going to do that, I don’t know."

    Verstappen agreed that the Marina Bay Street Circuit would benefit from a longer straight or a "proper hairpin".

    "I fully agree we need to have a longer straight or more of a proper hairpin to overtake. But even with that, also we need a bigger delta on the straight," the Red Bull driver said.

    Verstappen won the sprint race in Singapore, followed by Ferrari's Hamilton and Charles Leclerc. The Dutchman went on to also claim victory in the grand prix and was joined on the podium by Leclerc in second and Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli in third.
