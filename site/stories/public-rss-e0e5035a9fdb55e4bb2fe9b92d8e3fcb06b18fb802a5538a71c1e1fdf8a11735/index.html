    薪资分歧拖延贝尔曼与哈斯新合同

    Motorsport.com获悉，财务条款是贝尔曼2027年合同迟迟未获确认的原因，法拉利已行使对这位英国车手的选项。贝尔曼目前是F1发车阵容中唯一没有2027年合同的车手。

    薪资分歧拖延奥利·贝尔曼与哈斯F1车队的新合同

    Motorsport.com获悉，财务条款是贝尔曼2027年合同迟迟未获确认的原因，法拉利已行使对这位英国车手的选项。

    法拉利青训车手奥利弗·贝尔曼仍是F1发车阵容中唯一没有2027年合同的车手。考虑到自去年夏天以来，他留在哈斯一直被视作理所当然，这种情况在几周前还难以想象。

    在巴西大奖赛前宣布拉斐尔·卡马拉为贝尔曼2027年的队友后，外界原本预计很快会公布这位英国队友的续约。然而沉默一直延续，甚至成为话题，还引发了贝尔曼明年转投其他车队的传闻。矛盾之处在于，贝尔曼与哈斯都有充分理由达成协议。

    贝尔曼已排除2027年转投其他车队参赛的可能性，而哈斯车队长期以来一直被预计会留用他，但谈判拖延的时间远超最初预期。

    最近几周，外界对僵局原因有诸多猜测，其中一些与贝尔曼和法拉利之间的合同关系以及这位21岁英国车手的未来前景有关。

    然而，根据Motorsport.com获得的信息，阻碍签约的问题并不涉及车手的长期未来，而是与哈斯车队合同中的财务条款有关。

    法拉利已经行使了选择权，允许其将贝尔曼保留在车手培养计划中。鉴于自2021年贝尔曼加入车手学院以来，马拉内罗方面一直在这位年轻英国车手身上投入资金资源，允许贝尔曼免费转会离开将是对所有这些努力的一大浪费。

    拉斐尔·卡马拉将为哈斯车队完成2026赛季，同时为2027年与奥利弗·贝尔曼搭档的完整首秀做准备。

    如果贝尔曼在2027年底前仍未收到加入厂队的征召，他可能会处于离开法拉利的位置，但这一情形并不构成与哈斯续约的障碍，也无需在短期内敲定他与法拉利的关系——尤其是考虑到刘易斯·汉密尔顿正处于职业生涯的暮年。法拉利车队并未排除任何长期选项。

    因此，眼前的谈判集中在两方：哈斯和贝尔曼的团队。症结在于美国车队提供的薪水，车手及其团队认为这一数字与他们的期望不相称。

    这导致了长时间的僵局，尽管双方都清楚达成协议的必要性。在拉斯维加斯大奖赛前夕，贝尔曼毫不掩饰自己对这一已拖延数月局面的失望。“没有什么新消息可说。说实话，在赛季第20站时发现自己还没有明年的合同，我相当失望。这是个奇怪的局面，但我们正在推进。”

    这位英国车手承认，这种不确定性正变得难以应对，尤其是在他自认为已经证明了自己作为车队头号车手价值的这个赛季之后。

    不过，贝尔曼断然排除了2027年转投其他车队的可能性。当被问及是否还有哪怕一丝可能去别处参赛——在已没有更多席位可供真正讨论的情况下——他的回答毫不含糊：“不，零可能。”

    他补充道：“我2027年的目标非常明确。我认为我们在这里还有未竟的事业。然后再往更远的未来看，那其实并不完全掌握在我手中，这么说吧。”

    贝尔曼一直公开表达自己渴望有朝一日能去马拉内罗，但他也清楚，关于法拉利车队未来的决定并不只取决于他的表现。

    “当然，我与法拉利有合同。法拉利给了我这次不可思议的机会，让我第一次和他们一起进入F1，”他承认道。“我仍然是法拉利的青训车手，我的终极梦想是为法拉利比赛。首先，他们是最具标志性的车队，也是第一个在我小时候就相信我的车队。”

    “这仍然是我的终极梦想，但毫无疑问，展望未来，那里可能会有一些挑战。不可否认，这非常清楚。我在这件事上真的没有太多发言权。我们只需要看看事情随着时间的推移如何发展。”

    眼前的合同情况更为直接。法拉利已经澄清了其合同立场，并不是阻碍贝尔曼确认的一方。正在进行的谈判仅涉及车手与哈斯之间协议的条款。根据Motorsport.com收集的信息，此事预计将在阿布扎比的最后一个比赛周末之前解决，很可能通过在财务方面不可避免的妥协来实现。

    但这并不能让贝尔曼在11月底仍未达成协议的事实变得不那么令人沮丧。“在还剩两场比赛的情况下坐在这里，事情还没有解决，这种感觉并不好，”他说。“尽管我知道我们差不多已经到了那一步，但事实是我们并没有。老实说，在没有签署合同的情况下坐在这里，没有任何借口。”

    这些言论表明，整场拉锯战对这位车手造成了多大的心理负担。除非谈判出现意外破裂，否则贝尔曼预计将在2027年继续留在哈斯车队，与卡马拉组成围场中最年轻的车手组合之一。

    Salary dispute holding up Ollie Bearman’s new Haas F1 deal

    Motorsport.com understands financial terms are behind the delay in confirming Ollie Bearman for 2027, with Ferrari having already exercised its option on the British driver

    Ferrari protege Oliver Bearman remains the only driver on the Formula 1 grid without a contract for 2027. It is a situation that would have been hard to imagine just a few weeks ago, given that his retention at Haas had been taken for granted since last summer.

    The announcement of Rafael Camara as Bearman's 2027 team-mate, which came ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix, was expected to be swiftly followed by that of his British team-mate. Instead, the silence has dragged on to the point of becoming a talking point, even prompting rumours of Bearman driving elsewhere next year. The paradox is that Bearman and Haas have strong reasons to reach an agreement.

    Bearman has ruled out racing elsewhere in 2027, while Haas has long been expected to retain him, yet the negotiations have dragged on far longer than initially anticipated.

    In recent weeks, there has been speculation about the reasons for the deadlock, some linked to the contractual relationship between Bearman and Ferrari and to the future prospects of the 21-year-old Briton.

    According to information obtained by Motorsport.com, however, the issue holding up the signing does not concern the driver’s long-term future, but rather the financial terms of the deal with Haas.

    Ferrari has already exercised the option allowing it to retain Bearman within its driver programme. Given that Maranello has invested financial resources in the young Briton ever since he joined the Driver Academy in 2021, allowing Bearman to leave on a free transfer would be a big waste of all those efforts.

    Rafael Camara will finish the 2026 season for Haas as he prepares for his full 2027 debut alongside Oliver Bearman.

    Bearman could be in a position to leave Ferrari at the end of 2027 if he has not received a call-up to join the works team, but this scenario does not represent an obstacle to renewing with Haas, and there is no need to finalise his relationship with Ferrari in the short term – especially given that Lewis Hamilton is in the twilight of his career. The Scuderia has not ruled out any of its longer-term options.

    The immediate negotiations therefore centre on two parties: Haas and Bearman’s entourage. The sticking point is the salary offered by the American team, a figure that the driver and his staff do not consider commensurate with their expectations.

    This has led to a prolonged deadlock, despite both sides being aware of the need to reach an agreement. On the eve of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, Bearman made no secret of his disappointment at a situation that has now been dragging on for months. “There’s nothing new to say. To be honest, I’m rather disappointed to find myself here, at the twentieth round of the season, without a signed contract for next year. It’s a strange situation, but we’re getting there.”

    The Briton admitted that the uncertainty is becoming difficult to cope with, especially after a season in which he believes he has proved his worth as the team's lead driver.

    Bearman categorically ruled out the possibility of moving to another team in 2027, though. When asked if there was even the slightest chance of racing elsewhere, with no more seats up for real discussion, his answer was unequivocal: "No, zero chance."

    He added: "My goal for 2027 is very clear. I think we have unfinished business here. Then looking further into the future, it's not really in my hands, let's say it like that."

    Bearman has been open about his ambition to make it to Maranello, but he is aware that decisions regarding the Scuderia’s future do not depend solely on his performance.

    "Of course I'm under contract with Ferrari. Ferrari have given me this incredible opportunity to step into F1 with them for the first time," he acknowledged. "I'm still a Ferrari junior driver and my ultimate dream is to race for Ferrari. First of all, they're the most iconic team and they're the first team that believed in me as a kid.

    "It's still my ultimate dream, but there's no doubt that looking into the future, there may be some challenges there. There's no denying that, it's very clear. I don't really have much say in the matter. We just need to see how things develop over time."

    The immediate contract situation is more straightforward. Ferrari has already clarified its contractual position and is not the party holding up Bearman’s confirmation. The ongoing negotiations concern only the terms of the agreement between the driver and Haas. According to information gathered by Motorsport.com, the matter is expected to be resolved before the final weekend in Abu Dhabi, in all likelihood through an inevitable compromise on the financial front.

    That doesn't make the lack of a deal by the end of November any less frustrating for Bearman. "It's not a good feeling to be sitting here with two races to go without things sorted out," he said. "Even though I know that we're pretty much there, the fact is that we're not. There's no excuse for being here without a contract signed, quite honestly."

    Those comments show just how much this whole tug-of-war has weighed on the driver. Barring an unexpected breakdown in negotiations, Bearman is expected to remain with Haas in 2027 as well, forming one of the youngest driver pairings on the grid alongside Camara.
