    巴西大奖赛获得五年延期

    F1巴西大奖赛获得五年延期，至少持续到2030年代中期。

    一级方程式巴西大奖赛获得了五年延期，使其至少保持在2030年代中期之前的赛历上。

    巴西之前的合同原定于2030年到期，但在2026年周末的周六，F1宣布了一项新的五年延期。

    Interlagos在1973年首次出现在赛历上，自1990年以来一直独家举办巴西F1赛事。

    F1声称巴西有580万粉丝，从2024年到2025年同比增长6.5% - 其中44%为女性，41%年龄在35岁以下。

    去年周末的观众人数记录为303,627人。

    “巴西是F1历史的重要组成部分，也是我们的未来，我很高兴这个五年的延期将使我们继续在圣保罗赛车，直到2035年，”F1总裁兼首席执行官斯蒂法诺·多梅尼卡利表示。

    “我们巴西粉丝的热情是非凡的，每次我们回到英特拉戈斯，我们都能在看台上和圣保罗市感受到他们的能量。”

    大奖赛首席执行官艾伦·阿德勒将延期称为“巨大的骄傲来源”，并表示它“代表着对基于奉献、投资和长期愿景所做工作的认可。”

    英文原文

    Formula 1's Brazilian Grand Prix has earned a five-year extension, keeping it on the calendar until at least the mid-2030s.

    Brazil's previous contract was set to expire in 2030, but on the Saturday of the 2026 weekend, a new five-year extension was announced by F1.

    Interlagos made its F1 debut on the calendar in 1973 and has hosted the Brazil F1 race exclusively since 1990.

    F1 claims there's a fanbase in Brazil of "5.8 million, a 6.5% year-on-year increase from 2024 to 2025 - 44% of whom are female and 41% of whom are under 35".

    Last year's weekend attendance was recorded as 303,627.

    “Brazil is an important part of Formula 1’s history and our future, and I am delighted that this five-year extension will see us continue racing in Sao Paulo until 2035," president and CEO of Formula 1 Stefano Domenicali said.

    "The passion of our Brazilian fans is extraordinary, and every time we return to Interlagos we feel their energy in the grandstands and in the city of Sao Paulo."

    The CEO of the grand prix, Alan Adler, called the extension a "source of immense pride", and said it "represents recognition of work built on dedication, investment and a long-term vision".
