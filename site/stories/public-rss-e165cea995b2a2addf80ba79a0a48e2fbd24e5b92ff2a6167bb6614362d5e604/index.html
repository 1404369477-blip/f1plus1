    Toto Wolff透露：两名赞助商因基米·安东内利离开了梅赛德斯

    对于梅赛德斯粉丝来说。来自银箭的最新消息，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析。

    梅赛德斯车队负责人Toto Wolff在发表大胆言论方面并不陌生，但对他对基米·安东内利的受欢迎程度的最新评估可能会让忠实的法拉利粉丝感到震惊。

    在安东内利在墨西哥城赢得历史性的2026年车手锦标赛胜利后，Wolff接受了德国出版物BILD的独家采访。当对话转向这位20岁天才新星日益增长的星光时，Wolff被问及他的年轻车手是否注定在意大利比七届世界冠军迈克尔·舒马赫更受欢迎。

    "绝对是这样！他是意大利人，"Wolff直率地回答。

    几十年来，迈克尔·舒马赫在意大利被广泛视为不可触及的神，为斯库德利亚赢得了五个连续的世界冠军。相比之下，安东内利目前为梅赛德斯车队效力，这个车队在历史上被视为法拉利的头号竞争对手。

    Wolff 认为，尽管安东内利不是法拉利的车手，但他的意大利血统和真实个性使他超越了团队忠诚。

    “是的，但基米凭借他自然的方式和成功所取得的成就，是意大利当然仍然对他的国家队，法拉利——但同时也向这个小意大利敞开了心扉，”Wolff 解释道。

    为了证明他的观点，Wolff 直接指出了2026年意大利大奖赛在蒙扎的气氛。根据梅赛德斯老板的说法，传奇的tifosi 主动庆祝了安东内利在他们家乡制造商的成功。

    “现在tifosi 穿着法拉利的球衣，戴着梅赛德斯帽子，”Wolff 断言。“蒙扎是完美的例子。观众实际上很伤心，两位红色赛车都没有赢得第一，但他们很高兴，他们的一个基米，站在了领奖台的最顶端。”

    安东内利成功锁定2026年冠军，并巩固了自己自1953年阿尔贝托·阿斯卡里以来首位意大利一级方程式世界冠军的地位，他在国内的受欢迎程度预计只会增长——即使他继续在终点线前击败法拉利。

    Toto Wolff predicts Kimi Antonelli will become more popular in Italy than Michael Schumacher

    Mercedes boss Toto Wolff claims Italian F1 fans love Kimi Antonelli more than they loved Michael Schumacher at Ferrari

    Toto Wolff verrät: Zwei Sponsoren verließen Mercedes wegen Kimi Antonelli

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff is no stranger to making bold statements, but his latest assessment of Kimi Antonelli's popularity might actually shock the dedicated Ferrari fanbase.

    Following Antonelli's historic 2026 drivers' championship victory in Mexico City, Wolff sat down for an exclusive interview with the German publication BILD. When the conversation shifted to the 20-year-old prodigy's exploding star power, Wolff was asked if his young driver was destined to become more popular in Italy than seven-time world champion Michael Schumacher.

    "Absolutely! He is Italian," Wolff bluntly replied.

    For decades, Michael Schumacher has been widely considered an untouchable deity in Italy, having secured five consecutive world championships for the Scuderia. In contrast, Antonelli currently races for Mercedes, a team historically viewed as one of Ferrari's primary rivals.

    Wolff argued that, although Antonelli is not a Ferrari driver, his Italian heritage and authentic personality have allowed him to transcend team loyalty.

    "Yes, but what Kimi has achieved through his natural manner and success is that Italy is, of course, still feverish for his national team, Ferrari - but has also opened its heart and soul to this little Italian," Wolff explained.

    To prove his point, Wolff pointed directly to the atmosphere at the 2026 Italian Grand Prix in Monza. According to the Mercedes boss, the legendary Tifosi actively celebrated Antonelli's success over their own hometown constructor.

    "The Tifosi are now wearing the Ferrari shirt with a Mercedes cap," Wolff claimed. "Monza was the perfect example. The spectators were actually sad that neither of the two red racers came in first, but they were pleased that Kimi, one of their own, was at the very top of the podium."

    With Antonelli successfully locking down the 2026 championship and cementing himself as the first Italian F1 world champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953, his popularity at home is only expected to grow - even if he continues to beat the Ferraris to the finish line.
