    汉密尔顿推出外星主题Area 44系列服装，为拉斯维加斯大奖赛预热

    七届F1冠军汉密尔顿的时尚品牌+44回归拉斯维加斯，推出外星主题系列，为本周的大奖赛预热。

    法拉利车手汉密尔顿的时尚品牌推出了以不明飞行物为主题的系列，包括品牌的首款针织服装。

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    +44官网和Wynn拉斯维加斯恩科勒广场的临时摊位上有售，直到周日结束。

    这位英国人通过Instagram向他的粉丝展示了这款产品。'AREA 44 - 线上收藏现已开启。灵感来源于宇宙、拉斯维加斯以及所有未知的事物。希望你们喜欢，'他在帖子中写道，截至写作时，该帖子已有305,000个赞。

    这个名称是对内华达沙漠中城市北部的秘密美国空军基地51区的戏谑，这个主题贯穿于每一件产品。产品名称有如地面勤务、夜班、近距离接触、首次接触和已删除等，其中一些产品的+44标志中心有一个外星人头像。

    最贵的产品是地面勤务飞行员夹克，售价250美元，背部有刺绣的外星人补丁和印有拉斯维加斯区域+44的衬里。夜班风衣售价175美元，背部有+44标志，用发光的墨水印刷。

    T恤起价65美元，袜子、帽子、帆布袋和钥匙扣也都在这个系列中。

    最大的亮点是实验实验室针织衫，售价130美元的尼瓦沙色或黑色羊毛和羊绒混纺开衫。这是该品牌首次制作的针织服装。

    汉密尔顿说：“拉斯维加斯的赛车周在其他地方是独一无二的，因此为它创造一些东西感觉是正确的。Area +44在这个神秘和未知的感觉上玩弄，但它的核心是关于你可以真正穿的衣服，这些单品可以层层叠叠，带你从赛道到城市，从白天到夜晚。”

    “我们探索了新材料、细节和轮廓，我很自豪我们首次将针织服装引入+44。”

    汉密尔顿目前在车手积分榜上争夺第二名。拉斯维加斯大奖赛开启了赛季末的三连赛，包括卡塔尔和阿布扎比大奖赛。41岁的他目前排名第四，积261分，落后于第三名的梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔一分，落后第二名红牛车手马克斯·维斯塔潘18分。

    Lewis Hamilton launches alien-themed Area 44 collection for Las Vegas GP

    The Ferrari driver's fashion label has released a UFO-themed range for race week, including the brand's first knitwear

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    Seven-time Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton's fashion label +44 has returned to Las Vegas with Area 44, an alien-themed collection released ahead of this weekend's grand prix.

    The range is on sale through the +44 website and at a pop-up on the Encore Esplanade at Wynn Las Vegas, which runs until Sunday.

    The Briton showed it off to his followers on Instagram. "AREA 44 - Collection online now. Inspired by the universe, Las Vegas, and all things unknown. Hope you enjoy," he wrote, in a post that had passed 305,000 likes at the time of writing.

    The name is a play on Area 51, the secretive US Air Force base in the Nevada desert north of the city, and the theme runs through every piece. Items carry names such as Ground Crew, Night Shift, Close Encounter, First Contact and Redacted, and an alien head sits at the centre of the +44 cross on several of them.

    The most expensive item is the Ground Crew bomber jacket at $250, which has an embroidered alien patch on the back and a printed Vegas Area +44 lining. The Night Shift windbreaker, at $175, has a +44 logo across its back, printed in ink that glows in the dark.

    T-shirts start at $65, with socks, caps, tote bags and keyrings also in the collection.

    The headline addition is the Experimental Lab Knit, a $130 wool-and-cashmere-blend jumper in Nevada Sand or Blackout. It is the first knitwear the label has made.

    "Race week in Las Vegas is like nowhere else, so it felt right to create something just for it. Area +44 plays with that sense of mystery and the unknown, but at its heart it's about clothes you can really live in, pieces that layer up and take you from the track to the city, day to night," Hamilton said.

    "We've explored new materials, details and silhouettes with this collection, and I'm really proud that we're introducing knitwear to +44 for the first time."

    Hamilton is currently in the fight for second in the drivers' championship. The Las Vegas Grand Prix kicks off the season-ending triple-header, which includes the Qatar and Abu Dhabi Grands Prix. The 41-year-old sits fourth in the standings with 261 points, one point behind Mercedes' George Russell in third and 18 points behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen in second.
