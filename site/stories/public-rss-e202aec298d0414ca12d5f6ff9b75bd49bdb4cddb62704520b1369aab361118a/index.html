    维斯塔潘因处罚从后位起跑巴西大奖赛

    四届F1冠军维斯塔潘将在巴西大奖赛从后位起跑，红牛车队负责人劳伦特·梅基斯确认。

    维斯塔潘将在巴西大奖赛从后位起跑，红牛确认他将接受引擎处罚。

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛赛车，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和比赛分析。

    四届F1冠军维斯塔潘将在巴西大奖赛从后位起跑，红牛车队负责人劳伦特·梅基斯确认了这一消息。

    红牛车队策略总监汉娜·施密茨在圣保罗赛道第二次练习赛结束后解释说，米尔顿凯恩斯的团队正在为马克斯的赛车考虑几个改动。有一些事情他不太满意，但我们知道是什么，所以我们可以着手解决它们。

    在第三次练习赛之前接受天空体育F1采访时，梅克斯确认了荷兰人的处罚。

    “我们将不得不为马克斯承受一个引擎处罚。他将从最后一位发车——这是他在过去两年中非常熟悉的位置，”梅克斯说。

    “他在这里从后发车已经完成了一些奇迹，我们相信他今年还会完成更多。”

    尽管维斯塔潘将承受处罚，梅基斯补充说，在第一和第二次练习赛中，车的速度“相当不错”。

    “星期五总是很辛苦的工作。一点也不糟糕，”他解释道。

    “车的速度相当不错。是的，你听到了很多无线电通讯，但对我们来说，我不确定对其他人来说是否如此，我们在星期五有很多事情要追求，以优化圈速，挖掘出所有潜力。”

    “在许多方面，我们今年取得的进步是因为我们追求一切的精神。昨天也不例外，希望我们今天能把车调整得更好。”

    上周末赢得墨西哥大奖赛后，维斯塔潘在车手积分榜上攀升至第二位。梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利在墨西哥城夺得冠军，但第二名的争夺战仍在继续。维斯塔潘和第五位的勒克莱尔之间只有16分之差。

    刘易斯·汉密尔顿和乔治·拉塞尔分别位列第三和第四，两人都将受到处罚。拉塞尔将受到五位的发车位置处罚，而汉密尔顿处罚的具体情况尚未确定。

    Max Verstappen to start Brazilian GP from back of grid as Red Bull confirms penalty

    Max Verstappen will start the Brazilian Grand Prix from the back of the grid after Red Bull confirmed he will take an engine penalty

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen will start the Brazilian Grand Prix from the back of the grid, Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies has confirmed.

    Red Bull's head of strategy Hannah Schmitz explained after the second practice session at Interlagos that the Milton Keynes outfit was "looking into several changes for Max's car. There were things he was unhappy with, but we know what they are so can work on them."

    Speaking with Sky Sports F1 ahead of the third practice session, Mekies confirmed the penalty for the Dutchman.

    "We will have to take an engine penalty with Max. He will start from the back - a position he knows very well here after the last two years," Mekies said.

    "He's done a few miracles in the past starting from the back here and we're sure he will do a few more this year."

    While Verstappen will take the penalty, Mekies added that the pace of the car was "decent" during the first two practice sessions.

    "It's always hard work on Friday. It wasn't bad at all," he explained.

    "The pace of the car was decent. Yes, you heard a lot of radio communication, but certainly for us, I'm not sure for the other guys, there are a number of things we are chasing on a Friday to optimise lap time to extract everything.

    "In many respects, the progress we've had this year is because of this spirit we have of chasing everything. That was no different yesterday and hopefully we have the car in a better place today."

    After winning the Mexican Grand Prix last weekend, Verstappen moved up to second in the drivers' championship. Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli claimed the title in Mexico City, but the fight for second continues. Only 16 points separate Verstappen in second place and Charles Leclerc in fifth.

    Lewis Hamilton and George Russell sit third and fourth, respectively, and both will also be taking penalties. Russell will receive a five-place grid penalty, while the extent of Hamilton's penalty is yet to be confirmed.
