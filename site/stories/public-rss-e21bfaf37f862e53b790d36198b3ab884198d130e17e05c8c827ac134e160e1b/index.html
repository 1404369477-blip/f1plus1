    汉密尔顿：法拉利在拉斯维加斯直道速度劣势“残酷”

    汉密尔顿表示，法拉利SF-26在拉斯维加斯长直道上的极速劣势比他预想的更加“残酷”。他与勒克莱尔均在Q2被淘汰，正赛中各自被罚10秒，最终分别以第七和第十名完赛。

    汉密尔顿：法拉利在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛的极速劣势“残酷”

    汉密尔顿对SF-26在内华达街道赛道直道上缺乏速度感到震惊

    法拉利F1车手刘易斯·汉密尔顿表示，SF-26在拉斯维加斯长直道上的极速劣势暴露得比他预想的更加“残酷”。

    汉密尔顿和夏尔·勒克莱尔均在Q2被淘汰，正赛最终分别以第七和第十名完赛，他们各自的10秒罚时浪费了潜在的成绩——其中勒克莱尔因与队友相撞被罚，这让情况更加不利。

    法拉利的实际速度比排位赛中更好——他们在比赛的大部分时间里都保持在距离领先者五秒以内——但汉密尔顿认为，随着F1中“拉锯式”的竞争回归，他们缺乏直线速度的代价很大。

    “我们知道我们在直道上存在速度劣势，但我们没想到在这里这些长直道上会如此残酷，”这位英国车手评论道。

    “始终确保你跟在车后很重要，而试图向前推进时，你总是必须确保自己在尾流中。否则，你就只是周围人的活靶子。

    “梅赛德斯和红牛，他们在直线上的动力令人印象深刻。真的非常令人印象深刻。

    “我的引擎已经到极限了，没有更多动力可输出了。我拼命按按钮，想完成超车。”

    汉密尔顿在比赛中的最高速度为316.7公里/小时，有趣的是，这比领跑的勒克莱尔的330.1公里/小时慢了13.4公里/小时，而梅赛德斯和红牛的最高速度分别为320.6公里/小时和314.3公里/小时。

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    “我已经拼尽全力，为拿到这些积分付出了一切。我们不断遭遇挑战。我只是保持专注，”这位法拉利车手补充道，随后回应了他因离开赛道并获得优势而受到的处罚。

    “十秒罚时太离谱了，而且非常非常严厉，”他这样认为。“我当时是在避免撞上迈凯伦。他们本可以直接让我把位置还回去。十秒的停车罚时简直疯了。

    “但我回来了，重新投入了战斗，我能看到前面的马克斯。”法拉利的速度就是这么不错，但在他的车手再次相撞之后，车队领队弗雷德·瓦塞尔却很难看到任何积极之处。

    “我不想说速度好还是怎样，因为对我来说今天这无关紧要，”这位法国人严厉地评论道。“当然，当我们回来并超越了迈凯伦等等，我有一种积极的感觉。但老实说，这不是我桌上要讨论的话题。

    补充报道来自吉汉吉尔·佩尔佩里克和罗纳德·沃丁。

    Lewis Hamilton: Ferrari’s top-speed deficit was “brutal” at F1 Las Vegas GP

    Hamilton was shocked by the SF-26’s lack of velocity on straights at the Nevadan street circuit

    Ferrari Formula 1 driver Lewis Hamilton says the SF-26’s top-speed deficit was exposed as even more “brutal” than he expected on Las Vegas’ long straights.

    Hamilton and Charles Leclerc were both eliminated in Q2 and finished the race down in seventh and 10th, with their potential squandered by a 10-second penalty for each – including one inflicted on Leclerc for colliding with his team-mate, which didn’t help matters.

    Ferrari’s actual pace was better than in qualifying – they stayed within five seconds of the race leader for most of the race – but Hamilton felt their lack of straightline speed was costly as ‘yo-yo’ racing returned to F1.

    “We knew that we have a pace deficit on the straights, but we weren't expecting it to be so brutal here with these long straights,” the Briton commented.

    “It was important always to make sure you're behind the car, and trying to move forwards you always have to make sure you're in a tow. Otherwise, you're just a sitting duck to people around.

    “The Mercedes and the Red Bull, their power on a straight line is impressive. Really impressive.

    “My engine was on the limit, there was nothing else coming out. I was pressing the button as hard as I could to make moves.”

    Hamilton’s top speed in the race was 316.7km/h, interestingly 13.4km/h down on pacesetting Leclerc’s 330.1km/h, while the top Mercedes and Red Bull were on 320.6km/h and 314.3km/h respectively.

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    “I've fought as hard as I can and given everything I could to get those points today. We were just constantly hit with challenges. I just kept my head down,” the Ferrari driver added, before reacting to his penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage.

    “Ten seconds was insane and very, very harsh,” he reckoned. “I was avoiding colliding with the McLaren. They could have just told me to give the place back. Ten seconds to stop and go is nuts.

    “But I came back and I got back into the fight and I could see Max up ahead.” That’s how decent Ferrari’s pace was, but team principal Fred Vasseur was struggling to see any positives after his drivers collided yet again.

    “I don't want to say that the pace was good or whatever, because for me today it’s anecdotic,” the Frenchman sternly commented. “For sure, when we came back and we overtook McLaren and so, I had a positive feeling. But honestly, it's not the topic on my desk.”

    Additional reporting by Cihangir Perperik and Ronald Vording
