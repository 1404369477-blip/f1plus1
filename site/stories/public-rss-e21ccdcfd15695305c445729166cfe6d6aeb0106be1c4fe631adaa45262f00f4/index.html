    梅赛德斯计划仅在安东内利赛车上首秀ADUO升级

    梅赛德斯计划在本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛上，在其车手安东内利的F1赛车上引入期待已久的ADUO辅助涡轮升级。梅赛德斯在2026年F1基准红牛引擎上落后2%至4%，根据F1的ADUO引擎追赶系统，允许其在2026年和2027年各进行一次升级。

    梅赛德斯计划在本周末的拉斯维加斯大奖赛上，在其车手安东内利的F1赛车上引入期待已久的ADUO辅助涡轮升级。

    梅赛德斯在2026年F1基准红牛引擎上落后2%至4%，根据F1的ADUO引擎追赶系统，允许其在2026年和2027年各进行一次升级。

    升级是一款经过修改的涡轮，梅赛德斯已经为此准备了数月。

    考虑到梅赛德斯在墨西哥和巴西周末的比赛中已经提前锁定了两个冠军头衔，还有三场比赛尚未进行，因此它几乎没有必要急于进行升级。

    尽管如此，梅赛德斯在首秀这款发动机仍然具有价值，因为其发展将融入其2027年的套件。

    这款发动机计划仅出现在安东内利的赛车在拉斯维加斯的比赛中。他不需要接受发车线处罚，因为他今年只使用了四个允许的涡轮增压器中的三个。

    队友乔治·拉塞尔已经使用了五个，由于在新加坡接受了发车线处罚，所以如果他再在拉斯维加斯接受处罚，他将面临五位的发车线处罚。

    “从本周末开始，我们将有ADUO启用的涡轮增压器规格可供使用，”梅赛德斯车队负责人托托·沃尔夫确认。

    “这主要是为了证明在赛车周末条件下的开发，预期的性能提升相对较小。”

    “我们预计会与基米一起运行，而与乔治一起，我们的首要任务是保护他的发车位置，因为性能提升可能过于有限，不足以证明接受发车罚分的合理性。”

    “拉塞尔目前正在为争夺车手锦标赛第二名而战。他目前落后于马克斯·维斯塔潘17分，沃尔夫不排除使用车队指令来帮助拉塞尔获得第二名。”

    “我还没有考虑过车手锦标赛，但显然我们绝对希望他获得最高分，并希望他最终获得第二名，我们需要考虑我们将如何做。”沃尔夫在巴西周日表示。

    “但是，我们可能会全力以赴。”

    在拉斯维加斯的预览引语中，沃尔夫清楚地提出了2026年剩余时间的目标。

    “帮助乔治在车手锦标赛中尽可能取得高分，以势头结束这一年，并继续挖掘汽车的性能，”沃尔夫说。

    “在这方面的努力还有更多，最后三场比赛给我们提供了三个学习和改进的机会。”

    英文原文

    Mercedes is planning to introduce its long-awaited Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities-assisted turbo on Kimi Antonelli's Formula 1 car in Las Vegas this weekend.

    Mercedes has been judged to be between 2% and 4% adrift of F1 2026's benchmark Red Bull engine, meaning it is allowed one additional upgrade during 2026 and another for 2027 under F1's ADUO engine catch-up system.

    The upgrade is a modified turbo, which Mercedes has been preparing for months.

    It has felt little need to rush the upgrade, given it wrapped up both championship titles across the Mexico and Brazil weekends with three races still to go.

    There is still value in Mercedes debuting it, however, as the development will feed into its 2027 package.

    It is only planned to appear on Antonelli's car in Las Vegas. He does not need to take a grid penalty because he has only used three of the four permitted turbos this year.

    Team-mate George Russell has already used five of them, having taken a grid penalty in Singapore, so he would face a five-place grid penalty had he taken another in Las Vegas.

    "We will have the ADUO-enabled turbo specification available from this weekend," Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff confirmed.

    "This is primarily about proving out the development in race-weekend conditions, with the expected performance benefit relatively small.

    "We anticipate running it with Kimi, whilst with George our priority is protecting his grid position with the performance benefit likely too limited to justify taking a grid penalty."

    Russell is currently fighting to secure second place in the drivers' championship. He is 17 points adrift of Max Verstappen right now, with Wolff not ruling out using team orders to help Russell secure it.

    "I haven't thought about the drivers' championship yet, but clearly we absolutely want him to score the maximum and hopefully finish second, and we need to think about what we're going to do about it," Wolff said on Sunday in Brazil.

    "But it could be a possibility that we're giving it all the force."

    In his Las Vegas preview quotes, Wolff made the targets for the rest of 2026 clear.

    "Helping George finish as high as possible in the drivers' championship, ending the year with momentum, and continuing to unlock more performance from the car," Wolff said.

    "There is more work to do on that front, and the final three races give us three more opportunities to learn and improve."
