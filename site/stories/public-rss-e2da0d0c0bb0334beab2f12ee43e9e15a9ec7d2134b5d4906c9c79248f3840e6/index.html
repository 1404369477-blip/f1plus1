    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析，来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃斯

    乔治·拉塞尔在墨西哥大奖赛后缺席媒体活动

    这位英国车手在他的社交媒体上向粉丝发表了一篇长文，承认“2026年本不该是我的年份”，这是在他梅赛德斯车队的队友基米·安东内利在阿特奥多莫·赫尔曼诺斯·罗德里格斯赛道赢得首个车手总冠军之后。

    拉塞尔以夺冠热门的身份进入了2026赛季。但在他赢得赛季揭幕战澳大利亚大奖赛和中国短道赛之后，他眼睁睁地看着安东内利在日本站领跑积分榜，并在车手总冠军中不断扩大领先优势。

    这位28岁的车手缺席了赛后媒体见面会，这似乎促使他向粉丝发表了一则信息。他在文中写道：“现在感觉是一个从心底说话的合适时机。”

    “这个赛季一开始就正如我所希望的那样，但很快因为不幸和糟糕的表现而变得支离破碎。这是我职业生涯中第一个真正意义上的赛季，我的坏日子比好日子多。”

    “我所知道的就是我已经全力以赴了，2026年并不是我的年份。我衷心祝贺基米，他的赛季绝对令人难以置信。你真正值得这个冠军。”

    “感谢车队制造出本赛季最好的赛车和动力单元；看到大家在这四年的艰难中团结一致，把梅赛德斯带回顶峰，这令人鼓舞。”

    “现在，我所能承诺的就是，在这个赛季的剩余时间和下一个赛季，我会全力以赴，重新找回我在职业生涯的大部分时间里能够产生的表现，并希望一点运气也能回来。谢谢大家。”

    “拉塞尔仍然在为车手锦标赛的第二名而战。尽管他在墨西哥的第六名成绩让他从第二名降至第四名，但第二名维斯塔潘和第五名勒克莱尔之间只有16分。”

    George Russell breaks silence with heartfelt message after Kimi Antonelli's F1 title win

    George Russell has reflected on his difficult 2026 Formula 1 season in a heartfelt message to fans

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell has shared a heartfelt message after missing media duties following the Mexican Grand Prix.

    The Briton admitted that "2026 was not meant to be my year" in a long message to his fans on social media after his Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli claimed his maiden championship at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez.

    Russell entered the 2026 season as a clear favourite to win the title. But after he won the season-opening Australian Grand Prix and the Chinese sprint race, he watched on as Antonelli took the lead of the standings in Japan and continued to extend his advantage in the championship.

    The 28-year-old's absence from the post-race media sessions appears to have prompted a message to his fans. "Now feels like a pretty suitable moment to speak from the heart," he wrote.

    "This season started off exactly how I had hoped but it very quickly unravelled with tough luck and poor performances. It’s been the first proper season of my career where I’ve had more bad days than good.

    "All I know is that I’ve given it my all and 2026 was not meant to be my year. I want to sincerely congratulate Kimi on what has been an absolutely incredible season. You truly deserve the championship.

    "Thank you to the team for producing the best car and power unit this season; it’s been inspiring to see everyone coming together over four tough years to bring Mercedes back to the top.

    "Now, all I can promise is that for the rest of this season, and into the next one, I will be giving my absolute all to rediscover the performance I have been capable of producing for the majority of my career and fingers crossed a little bit of luck returns. Thanks all."

    Russell is still in the fight for second in the drivers' championship. While his sixth-place finish in Mexico dropped him from second to fourth, there are still only 16 points between Max Verstappen in second and Charles Leclerc in fifth.
