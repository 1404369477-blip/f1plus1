    维斯塔潘透露红牛在拉斯维加斯无法解决的隐忧

    安东内利的发车格罚退让维斯塔潘将从拉斯维加斯大奖赛杆位起步，但他表示赛车驾驶感受仍不理想，并暗示红牛今年可能无法解决这一敏感问题。

    维斯塔潘透露红牛在拉斯维加斯无法解决的神秘问题

    与巴西站一样，小红牛22给维斯塔潘的感觉并不对。这位荷兰车手无法透露敏感信息，但暗示红牛今年可能无法解决这一问题

    由于安东内利的发车格罚退，维斯塔潘将从F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛杆位起步，但这未必是这位荷兰车手感到满意的理由。

    是的，红牛在能量匮乏型赛道上动力单元取得了明显进步，并且在练习赛中出现的平衡问题之后，车队也成功做出了有效的调校调整，但驾驶座舱内的感受仍不是维斯塔潘认为应有的样子。

    维斯塔潘表示：“其实从巴西站开始，赛车就出现了一些奇怪的问题，这个周末依然存在。我只能努力想办法在剩余的周末里应对它。”

    “这让第三次练习变得不太轻松，但我认为我们在排位赛中设法让它运转起来了。”

    当维斯塔潘在新闻发布会上被问及能否详细说明那些“奇怪的问题”时，他坚定地回答：“不能。”

    尽管如此，这个话题在他随后于围场接受荷兰媒体采访时自然又被提起。维斯塔潘仍然无法详细说明，但透露了更多情况。

    “我可以告诉你，这不是平衡问题。赛车就是有地方不对劲，但我们现在没法解决，”这位红牛车手在被 Motorsport.com 问及该问题时说道。

    “我们知道问题出在哪里，但解决不了。这已经是连续第二个周末出现这种情况了，但我不能多说。”

    表面上看，这个问题似乎是在巴西站正赛期间出现的。毕竟，维斯塔潘曾报告说，赛车在第二和第三阶段（stint）的感觉与开局阶段截然不同，但这位荷兰车手澄清说，这个问题实际上困扰他的时间更长。

    “是的，它是在巴西突然出现的，但那已经是在正赛之前了。整个周末我在那里都有问题。”

    这位四届世界冠军澄清，这与他此前在蒙扎等赛道抱怨的“赛车性能衰退”不同。当时，赛车会在一次练习或排位的过程中逐渐性能下降，而这次的问题更为根本。据维斯塔潘所说，这主要与方向盘传来的手感有关。

    “这和赛车性能衰退完全是两回事，但手感就是达不到我想要的状态。我没法再多说什么了，否则就要开始解释一些我不想透露的东西了。”

    在红牛于巴库引入升级之后，维斯塔潘曾表示他对赛车更有信心，因此能更激进地攻弯，但由于这个问题，这变得更困难了。

    “在某些地方现在确实没那么容易了，是的。这和弯道类型无关，问题就是到处都存在。我只能努力去适应，但这并不容易。”

    最后，维斯塔潘明确表示，队友伊萨克·哈贾尔并没有遇到同样的问题。

    “不，显然只有我这边有问题。这不危险，但开起来就是不舒服。我们只能设法解决它。很长一段时间里它都运转良好，但到了巴西突然就不行了，在这里也一样。”

    Max Verstappen reveals mysterious Red Bull issue that cannot be fixed in Las Vegas

    Just as in Brazil, the RB22 did not feel right for Verstappen. The Dutchman cannot reveal sensitive information, but suggests Red Bull may not be able to resolve the issue this year

    Thanks to Kimi Antonelli's grid penalty, Max Verstappen will start Formula 1's Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole position, but that is not necessarily a reason for the Dutchman to be satisfied.

    Yes, Red Bull has made significant progress with its power unit on energy-poor circuits and, following the balance problems during free practice, the team also managed to make effective set-up changes, but the feeling behind the wheel was still not what Verstappen believes it should be.

    “Already since Brazil I have some weird issues in the car and that continued this weekend. I'm just trying to get my head around how to deal with that for the remainder of the weekend,” Verstappen said.

    "That didn't make things easy in FP3, but I think in qualifying we managed to make it work."

    When Verstappen was asked during the press conference whether he could elaborate on those “weird issues”, he responded firmly: "No."

    Nevertheless, the subject naturally came up again during his subsequent Dutch media session in the paddock. Verstappen was still unable to go into detail, but did shed a little more light on the situation.

    "It's not a balance problem, I can tell you that. There's simply something wrong with the car, but we can't fix it right now," the Red Bull driver said when asked about the issue by Motorsport.com.

    "We know where the problem is coming from, but we can't solve it. It's now the second weekend in a row that it's been like this, but I can't say too much about it."

    On the face of it, the issue appeared to emerge during the race in Brazil. After all, Verstappen reported that the car felt very different during the second and third stints compared to the opening stint, but the Dutchman clarified that the problem had actually been troubling him for longer.

    "It suddenly appeared in Brazil, yes, but that was already before the race. I had problems there throughout the entire weekend."

    The four-time world champion clarified that it is different from the 'car degradation' he complained about at circuits including Monza. Back then, the car would deteriorate over the course of a session, whereas this time the issue is more fundamental. According to Verstappen, it is mainly related to the feeling through the steering wheel.

    "It's something completely different from car degradation, but the feeling is simply not how I want it to be. I can't say much more about it, because otherwise I'll start explaining things that I don't want to give away."

    Following the introduction of Red Bull's Baku upgrade, Verstappen said he had gained more confidence in the car and could therefore attack the corners more aggressively, but that has become more difficult because of this issue.

    "In certain places that's less the case now, yes. It doesn't matter what type of corner it is, either. The problem is simply there everywhere. I just have to try to adapt, but that's not easy."

    Finally, Verstappen made it clear that team-mate Isack Hadjar is not experiencing the same problem.

    "No, apparently it's only on my side. It's not dangerous, but it's just not pleasant for me to drive. We just have to try to solve it. It had been working well for a very long time, but suddenly it wasn't in Brazil, and it isn't here either."
