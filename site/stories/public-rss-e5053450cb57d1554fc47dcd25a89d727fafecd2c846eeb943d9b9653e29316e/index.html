    梅赛德斯技术总监透露F1表现不佳的心理压力

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森详细描述了车队在F1低谷期间经历的"自我厌恶"和痛苦。

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森详细描述了F1表现不佳的心理压力

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森详细描述了车队在F1低谷期间经历的"自我厌恶"和痛苦

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析

    在F1中，经过公关培训的执行人员通常会把失去赛季描述为"学习机会"，并承诺车队将迅速反弹。但梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森最近打破了这种光鲜的叙述，揭示了冠军荒的心理现实

    在连续八次获得车队冠军的历史性纪录之后，梅赛德斯经历了四个表现不佳的艰难赛季。现在，在梅赛德斯于2026年正式夺回冠军之后，艾里森（Allison）公开谈到了在这段间隔年份里布拉克利工厂内部的氛围。

    乔治·拉塞尔（拉塞尔）、梅赛德斯、安德烈亚·基米·安东内利（安东内利）、梅赛德斯

    在NU银箭广播节目中，梅赛德斯老板承认，通过锦标赛或粉丝的角度来看待2026年的冠军很困难。相反，他的关注点完全集中在同事们的情感救援上。

    “我发现很难从我们现在的混乱中抽身出来，去考虑体育和粉丝，”艾里森承认。“我只是想着我的朋友们和队友们，他们在过去几个赛季中经历了表现不佳，以及随之而来的所有自我怀疑，甚至是极端的自我厌恶。”

    从主导到中场的竞争，对负责设计和操作汽车的工作人员的打击很大。阿隆索解释说，修复之前挑战者如W13和W14的空气动力学缺陷，不仅是一个技术挑战，也是工程人员个人的、情感上的战斗。

    当被问及他将如何记住2026年的胜利赛季时，阿隆索直接指向了他员工在多年质疑自己能力后的情感救赎。

    乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯、安德烈亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯、托托·沃尔夫、梅赛德斯

    “现在我们得到了一个赛季的回报，我们说了我们要做什么，我们告诉彼此我们要做什么，我们互相要求的是什么非常艰难，然后我们做到了，”阿隆索表示。“我知道我们集体做到了这一点，我内心深处的满足感……实际上，中间季节的所有相对痛苦都使我们现在感受到的更加真实。”

    随着梅赛德斯庆祝其第九个车队 constructors' championship 和车手基米·安东内利的创纪录冠军，艾莉森解释说，工厂的心理恢复是 2026 赛季真正的胜利。

    "这就是我将记住这一年直到我太老无法再想其他事情的方式，" 艾莉森总结道。

    Mercedes technical director details the psychological toll of F1 underperformance

    Mercedes technical director James Allison details the "self-loathing" and misery the team endured during its F1 slump

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    In Formula 1, PR-trained executives routinely frame losing seasons as "learning opportunities" and promise that the team will quickly bounce back. But Mercedes technical director James Allison recently stripped away that polished narrative to reveal the psychological reality of a championship drought.

    Following a historic run of eight consecutive constructors' championships, Mercedes endured four difficult seasons of underperformance. Now, after Mercedes officially reclaimed the title in 2026, Allison opened up about the internal atmosphere inside the Brackley factory during those intervening years.

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    Speaking on the NU Silver Arrows Radio Show, the Merc boss admitted it is hard to view the 2026 title through the lens of the championship or the fans. Instead, his focus remains entirely on the emotional rescue of his colleagues.

    "I find it very hard to lift myself out of the melee we're in and to think about the sport and the fans," Allison confessed. "I just think about my friends and team-mates that have lived the last seasons of underperformance and all of the self-doubt and, at the extreme, self-loathing that accompanies that."

    The drop from domination to fighting in the midfield took a toll on the workforce responsible for designing and operating the cars. Allison explained that fixing the aerodynamic flaws of previous challengers such as the W13 and W14 was not just a technical challenge, but a personal, emotional battle for the engineering staff.

    When asked how he will remember the triumphant 2026 season, Allison pointed directly to the emotional redemption of his staff after years of questioning their own abilities.

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    "To have the payoff now of a season where we said what we were going to do, we told each other what we were going to do, and what we asked of each other was extremely tough, and then we've done it," Allison stated. "The bone-deep feeling of contentment that I have in knowing that collectively that's what we have done together... actually all of the relative misery of the intervening seasons makes more valid what we're feeling now."

    As Mercedes celebrates its ninth constructors' championship and driver Kimi Antonelli's historic title, Allison explained that the psychological recovery of the factory is the true triumph of the 2026 campaign.

    "That's how I will remember this year until I'm too old to think of anything else," Allison concluded.
