    诺里斯谈《GTA VI》：小时候不允许玩

    诺里斯承认在F1赛季最后的三连赛期间不会玩《GTA VI》，并透露小时候不允许玩这款流行的游戏系列。

    兰多·诺里斯关于《GTA VI》表示：“在我年轻的时候，我不被允许玩这款游戏”

    麦凯轮车手表示他以前在学校旅行时借用朋友的游戏机来玩《侠盗猎车手》，但不会在F1赛季最后的三连赛期间玩新游戏。

    对于麦凯轮车迷来说，来自沃金斯的最新消息，包括车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的赛事。

    兰多·诺里斯承认，在F1赛季最后的三连赛期间，他不会玩《侠盗猎车手VI》，同时透露当他还是孩子的时候，不允许他玩这个流行的游戏系列。

    Rockstar Games备受期待的《侠盗猎车手VI》将于周四在PlayStation 5和Xbox Series X|S上发售，距离上一款游戏《侠盗猎车手V》在2013年上市已有十多年。

    诺里斯，以在赛车周末之间进行直播和成为知名游戏玩家而闻名，他表示这款现在已成为传奇的系列游戏并不是他成长过程中的部分。

    “不，我不这么认为，”当被问及他是否会在赛季最后几场比赛之间玩这款游戏时，他说，“我从未真正玩过《侠盗猎车手V》，可能就几次。我的意思是，这是一款很酷的游戏，我很喜欢。我观看其他人玩游戏，诸如此类的东西。但是的，这不是我的菜，”诺里斯在拉斯维加斯大奖赛前的车手新闻发布会上说。

    这位麦克拉伦车手解释说，他的父母在他成长过程中阻止他玩这款游戏，这可能是由于其高年龄评级。

    “我非常想，但在我年轻的时候，我不被允许玩它。我不被允许。所以当我们去学校旅行，我能够偷别人的PSP或者其他东西时，那是我一生中最美好的旅行，因为我终于可以玩它了，”他说。

    “但我在家里从未真正长大玩它。我长大后从未真正玩过它，因此。所以它从未是我的事情。但我知道世界上有很多人正在玩它。所以也许我的想法会改变。但现在，答案是不了。”

    并不是每个车手都持有他的观点。阿斯顿·马丁的拉塞尔·斯特罗尔表示他“肯定会在某个时候打开它”，而凯迪拉克的瓦尔特里·博塔斯说他有其他计划。”

    “这不是我的计划。我认为外面有更好做的事情。你知道，外面的世界非常美丽。走出去是非常好的，”博塔斯说。

    诺里斯在接下来的几周里几乎没有时间玩游戏。F1的最后一站三连赛将从本周末的拉斯维加斯开始，然后前往卡塔尔和阿布扎比。

    这位27岁的车手有很多要争取的。在车队制造商锦标赛中，迈凯轮落后红牛17分，争夺第三名，诺里斯在圣保罗的胜利后抵达拉斯维加斯。

    他在车手积分榜上排名第六，距离第三名有16分的差距，在一场五人争夺亚军的位置的比赛中，落后世界冠军基米·安东内利。

    Lando Norris on "GTA VI": "I wasn't allowed to play it when I was younger"

    The McLaren driver says he used to borrow friends' consoles on school trips to play Grand Theft Auto, but won't be playing the new game during F1's final triple-header.

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Lando Norris admits he won't be playing Grand Theft Auto VI during Formula 1's final triple-header of the season while also revealing that he wasn't allowed to play the popular franchise when he was a child.

    Rockstar Games' long-awaited Grand Theft Auto VI launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on Thursday, after more than a decade of waiting since the last game, Grand Theft Auto V, came to market in 2013.

    Norris, known for streaming and being a prominent gamer between race weekends, has said the now legendary series wasn't part of his life growing up.

    "No, I don't think so," he said on being asked if he'll be checking out the game between the last few races of the season. "I never really ever played GTA V, maybe a few times. I mean, it's a cool game and I enjoy it. I watch other people playing and stuff like that. But yeah, not my cup of tea," Norris said in the drivers' press conference ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    The McLaren driver explained that his parents had kept him from playing the game when he was growing up, likely due to its high age rating.

    "I would love to, but I wasn't allowed to play it when I was younger. I wasn't allowed. So when we would go on a school trip and I could nick someone else's PSP or something, it was the best trip of my life because I could finally get to play it," he said.

    "But I never grew up playing it when I was at home. I never really played it when I was older, therefore. So it's never been my thing. But I know a lot of people around the world are playing it right now. So maybe my mind changes. But for now, it's a no."

    Not every driver shares his view. Aston Martin's Lance Stroll said he would "probably fire it up at some point for sure", while Cadillac's Valtteri Bottas said he had other plans.

    "Not my plan. I think there's better things to do out there. You know, the outside world is quite beautiful. It's quite nice going outside," Bottas said.

    Norris will have little time for gaming over the coming weeks anyway. F1's final triple-header takes him from Las Vegas this weekend to Qatar and then Abu Dhabi.

    The 27-year-old has plenty to race for. McLaren is 17 points behind Red Bull in the fight for third in the constructors' championship, and Norris arrives in Las Vegas off the back of victory in Sao Paulo.

    He is sixth in the drivers' standings, 16 points off third place, in a five-way fight for runner-up behind world champion Kimi Antonelli.
