    "在听证会的开始和结束时，双方当事人都被邀请就诉讼程序或法庭组成提出任何问题。双方均未提出。在听证会期间，也没有人对法庭询问证人或主持诉讼程序的方式提出任何异议。

    "国际汽联上诉法院（ICA）强调由来自不同文化和地域背景的评委组成合议庭的重要性。这有助于确保审议和讨论反映多元的法律传统、观点和方法，进一步增强程序的公正性、独立性和可信度，以及获得公平审判的机会。

    "法庭的裁决在法律依据方面可能留有多种解释和评论的空间；这是公平的，并为所有评委所接受。法庭对诉讼程序的稳妥性和评委的公正廉正充满信心。国际汽联法庭将继续致力于维护评委的独立性与公正性，以及司法程序的完整性。

    Andrea Stella has accused Alpine chief Flavio Briatore of "insulting" McLaren by questioning the relationship between the reigning constructors' champions and a judge at the FIA's International Court of Appeal.

    The court announced on Friday that penalties given to Alpine driver Pierre Gasly during June's Monaco Grand Prix would be reinstated, following a successful appeal by McLaren and Red Bull.

    Gasly took the chequered flag in third but was demoted when two five-second penalties for speeding in the pit lane were applied at that point. However, a right of review appeal from Alpine saw the penalties withdrawn five days later.

    In an explosive press conference at this weekend's Italian Grand Prix, which coincidentally featured Alpine executive advisor Briatore and McLaren team principal Stella, the pair engaged in a remarkable clash.

    Alpine had put out a statement moments after the decision was announced expressing "disappointment" with the outcome but also said that the team "acknowledges the decision of the judging panel from the recent hearing in Paris".

    Briatore stunned the room by striking a different tone in the press conference just a couple of hours later as he complained that one of the four judges at the hearing had a "working relationship" with McLaren.

    "It's very unfair, firstly, but we accept the decision," Briatore said. "But what is very strange was the third judge in the panel, one of the judges acted like a prosecutor.

    "We have a nice picture of this guy at the speech for McLaren in 2018," Briatore said.

    Briatore added that the judge in question was "very nasty" to Alpine's representatives during the hearing, and said that his team were "thinking about" taking legal action.

    ICA 'confident in the soundness of the proceedings and the integrity of the judges'

    The International Court of Appeal issued a statement on Saturday morning, saying it was "aware of public comments questioning the independence and impartiality of the judges of the panel".

    The statement continued: "The FIA Courts wish to make clear that the appointment and participation of the judges in that case, like for any other case, were conducted in accordance with the applicable FIA judicial procedures and usual practice, which are inspired by the highest standards like the IBA Guidelines on Conflicts of Interest in International Arbitration.

    "All judges are elected by the FIA General Assemblies, some of them upon proposition of a group of at least five F1 teams in accordance with the FIA Statutes. They are all subject to the independence and confidentiality requirements contained in the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, make annual disclosures of interests to the FIA Compliance Officer, and sign a specific declaration of independence for each particular case in consideration of the issue at stake and the parties involved, which is served to the parties.

    "At both the beginning and conclusion of the hearing, the parties were invited to raise any issue concerning the proceedings or the composition of the Court. None did so. No objection was raised during the hearing concerning the manner in which the Court questioned the witness or conducted the proceedings.

    "The ICA emphasises the importance of constituting panels with judges from different cultural and geographical backgrounds. This helps ensure that the deliberations and discussions reflect a diversity of legal traditions, perspectives and approaches, further strengthening the fairness, independence, credibility of the process and access to a fair trial.

    "A Court's decision can leave room for various interpretations and comments as for its legal merits; that is fair and accepted by all the judges. The Court is confident in the soundness of the proceedings and the integrity of the judges. The FIA Courts remain committed to the independence and impartiality of their judges and to the integrity of the judicial process.
