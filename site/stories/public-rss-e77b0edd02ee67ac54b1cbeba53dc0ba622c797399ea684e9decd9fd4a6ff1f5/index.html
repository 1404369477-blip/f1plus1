    施泰纳认为韦伯过度干预皮亚斯蒂职业生涯

    前哈斯车队负责人施泰纳认为，马克·韦伯可能在2025年下半年过度干预了奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂的职业生涯，在麦克拉伦车手决定‘我要自己来做’之前。

    对于麦凯轮车队的粉丝来说，来自沃金克的最新消息，包括车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的比赛情况

    前哈斯一级方程式车队负责人格恩特·施泰纳认为，在马克·韦伯决定‘我要自己来做这件事’之前，他可能在2025年下半年过于介入了奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂的职业生涯。

    皮亚斯特里和Webber在圣保罗大奖赛之后宣布，他们已经相互同意结束自2019年开始的管理协议，在这期间澳大利亚人从三级方程式和二级方程式一路晋升至麦凯轮的赛车席位。

    Webber今年已经缩减了自己的角色，并且不再与皮亚斯特里一同参加比赛。

    在《红旗播客》上，Steiner表示这个消息并不让他感到惊讶。

    “实际上我并不太惊讶，因为正如你所说，Mark并不在身边。今年我可能见过Mark一两次，不多。他没有在身边。而且很明显，他在年初就说过了，他不会再像以前那样陪伴Oscar了。”

    Steiner随后指向了2025年的后半部分，当时皮亚斯特里对迈凯伦车队队友诺里斯的冠军挑战逐渐减弱，这是关系开始改变的时刻。

    去年赛季的后半段，当奥斯卡没有赢得世界冠军时，也许马克也介入得有点过多。然后在某个阶段，奥斯卡说，'好吧，我现在更有经验了。我想自己来做这件事。'

    Steiner表示，后退并不是Webber的天然选择。

    一开始，你说，'嘿，让我们少参与一些。'但马克不是那种坐在人行道上看着我们表现不佳的人。显然，奥斯卡可能想与马克切断联系，但他欠他很多。在我看来，他的一些成功要归功于Mark Webber。

    Guenther Steiner suggests Mark Webber became too involved in Oscar Piastri’s career

    The former Haas team principal believes Oscar Piastri wanted greater independence after a difficult second half of 2025

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Former Haas Formula 1 team principal Guenther Steiner believes Mark Webber may have become too involved in Oscar Piastri’s career in the second half of 2025, before the McLaren driver decided he wanted to “do this on my own”.

    Piastri and Webber announced after the São Paulo Grand Prix that they had mutually agreed to end their management agreement, which began in 2019 and saw the Australian rise through Formula 3 and Formula 2 to a McLaren race seat.

    Webber had already scaled back his role this year and stopped attending races alongside Piastri.

    Speaking on The Red Flags Podcast, Steiner said the news did not come as a shock.

    “I’m actually not too surprised because, as you said, Mark was not around. I’ve seen Mark this year maybe once or twice, not more,” Steiner said. “He was not around. And obviously, he said at the beginning of the year that he would not be beside Oscar anymore as he was in the good old days.”

    Steiner then pointed to the latter part of 2025, when Piastri’s title challenge against his McLaren team-mate Lando Norris faded, as the moment the relationship began to change.

    “The second half of the season last year, when Oscar, after he didn’t win the world championship, maybe Mark got a little bit involved too much. And then at some stage, Oscar said, ‘OK, I’m now more experienced. I want to do this on my own.’”

    Steiner suggested that stepping back did not come naturally to Webber.

    “In the beginning, you say, ‘Hey, let’s be less around.’ But Mark is not the guy who is like, ‘OK, I’m just sitting on the sidewalk seeing how we are not performing.’ And then, obviously, Oscar maybe wants to cut the ties with Mark, but he owes him a lot. Some of his success is owed to Mark Webber, in my opinion.”

    With three rounds remaining in the 2026 season, Piastri currently sits seventh in the drivers' championship with 152 points.
