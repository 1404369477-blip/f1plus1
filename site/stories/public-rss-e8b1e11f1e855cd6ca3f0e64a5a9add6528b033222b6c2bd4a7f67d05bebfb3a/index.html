    “不，我的意思是，我对这没有问题，因为最终，如果他们彼此不喜欢，我为什么要关心呢？”斯泰纳说。

    “我尊重乔治作为一名赛车手，但最终，马克斯在心理上更胜一筹。在如何处理心理问题上，他比你强得多，不要去招惹熊，在我看来，”他说。

    Guenther Steiner warns George Russell: Do not "poke the bear" Max Verstappen

    The former Haas boss says Russell picked a fight he cannot win, after the Mercedes driver's war of words with Verstappen in Las Vegas

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Former Haas Formula 1 team principal Guenther Steiner has urged Mercedes driver George Russell not to "poke the bear" with Max Verstappen, after their war of words at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    Russell hit out at Verstappen after the pair clashed in final practice, saying the Red Bull driver walks around "like they're bigger and better than everyone else" and that "somebody's got to stand up to that guy".

    The pair then swapped the lead repeatedly during the race on the Las Vegas Strip, until the Briton locked up and hit the wall on lap 48 while trying to pass team-mate Kimi Antonelli for second. Verstappen won, and afterwards said Russell was "so far below my level".

    Speaking on The Red Flags Podcast, Steiner said he did not mind the two drivers disliking each other, but questioned why Russell had chosen to take Verstappen on.

    "No, I mean, I haven't got a problem with it, because in the end, if they don't like each other, what do I care?" Steiner said.

    "This has been going on for a long time between them, and obviously, George coming out to say, 'Oh, this guy behaves very arrogantly', or whatever he said about Max, and 'Somebody needs to stand up to him.' Yeah, good luck with that one, buddy. You see how that worked out."

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    The former team chief said he respected Russell as a driver, but felt he had underestimated Verstappen's mental strength.

    "I respect George as a race car driver, but in the end, Max is just mentally superior. On how to manage mental things, he's so superior to you, do not poke the bear, in my opinion," he said.

    "There is no need for it, because you don't do yourself any favours. Get over it. Try to win races instead of picking a battle with the best driver out there, the strongest mental guy out there, because guess what? You're going to lose.

    "It's like there's nobody sitting down with him and saying, 'George, sometimes less is more. Just say nothing, do your own job,'" Steiner said, adding that the Mercedes driver should focus on winning races.

    "And then when you have got something to say because you beat the guy, pick your battle then. But as I said, if you cannot beat them, join them, and in the moment just shut up and get on with it."

    Verstappen's win moved him further clear in second place in the drivers' standings on 304 points. Russell, who finished ninth, is fourth on 264, behind Lewis Hamilton.
