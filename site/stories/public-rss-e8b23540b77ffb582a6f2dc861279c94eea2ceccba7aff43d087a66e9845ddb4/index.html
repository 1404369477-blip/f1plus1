    维斯塔潘为美国大奖赛揭晓特制德克萨斯主题头盔

    四次F1冠军维斯塔潘为美国大奖赛揭晓了一款特制的德克萨斯主题头盔，在美洲赛车场大赛前对其常用设计进行了奥斯汀，德克萨斯风格的改造。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘为美国大奖赛揭晓了特别设计的德克萨斯主题头盔

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说。关注红牛车队，获取F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯赛道上的比赛分析。

    四次F1世界冠军马克斯·维斯塔潘为美国大奖赛揭幕了一款特别头盔，在美洲赛道比赛前，对他的常规设计进行了奥斯汀，德克萨斯州的改造。

    荷兰人已在社交媒体上揭幕了新设计。"即将前往德克萨斯州。我将带着这顶特别的头盔来到奥斯汀！"他发布了新头盔的详细照片。

    The主要的变化是在头盔顶部的狮子，现在它戴着一顶牛仔帽。狮子在白色背景上以蓝色突出，整个头盔都保留了红、白、蓝的主题。

    截至写作时，维斯塔潘的帖子已经获得了超过747,000个赞。一位粉丝评论道："10分满分 - 戴着牛仔帽的狮子太棒了，"另一位补充说："戴着牛仔帽的狮子太棒了哈哈哈。

    其他反应包括："哦，这太美了，"和"头盔真是太可爱了。"

    为了配合新的头盔，维斯塔潘和他的红牛车队队友伊萨克·哈德贾也将穿着特别的全白色复古风格的赛车服。新的设计在粉丝中激起了希望，希望米尔顿凯恩斯车队能在奥斯汀的比赛中引入全白色的涂装，但这尚未得到确认。

    维斯塔潘在马来西亚巴林大奖赛和新加坡大奖赛连续获胜后，将前往奥斯汀。他目前在车手积分榜上排名第四，积分为221分，落后积分榜领跑者基米·安东内利119分。

    但大多数车手在经历了紧张的连续三站赛事后都会有一周的休息时间，而维斯塔潘本周将在波尔蒂马奥的GT世界挑战赛欧洲决赛中驾驶维斯塔潘赛车队的#3号奔驰-AMG赛车，与克里斯·卢尔汉和本·巴尼科特一起参赛。

    美国大奖赛将于10月23日至25日在美国赛车场举行，并开启了锦标赛下个三站赛事，紧接着是墨西哥和巴西大奖赛。

    Max Verstappen unveils special Texas-themed helmet for F1 United States GP

    Max Verstappen has unveiled a special Texas-themed helmet for the United States Grand Prix

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Four-time Formula 1 champion Max Verstappen has revealed a special helmet for the United States Grand Prix, giving his usual design an Austin, Texas, makeover ahead of the race at the Circuit of the Americas.

    The Dutchman unveiled the new design on social media. "Texas bound. I’ll be coming to Austin with this special helmet!" he posted alongside detailed photos of the new helmet.

    The main change is to the lion on the top of the helmet, which now wears a cowboy hat. The lion stands out in blue on a white background and the red, white and blue theme has been retained throughout the whole helmet.

    At the time of writing, Verstappen's post had already received more than 747,000 likes. "10s across the board - the lion with the cowboy hat is incredible," one fan commented, while another added: "The lion in the cowboy hat is brilliant haha."

    Further reactions included: "Oh this is gorgeous", and "Simply lovely that helmet."

    To complement the new helmet, Verstappen and his Red Bull team-mate Isack Hadjar will also be wearing special all-white, vintage-style race suits. The new designs have sparked hope among fans that the Milton Keynes outfit could introduce an all-white livery for the race in Austin, but this is yet to be confirmed.

    Verstappen heads to Austin after back-to-back wins at the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia and the Singapore Grand Prix. He currently sits fourth in the drivers' standings with 221 points, 119 points behind championship leader Kimi Antonelli.

    But while most drivers have a weekend off from racing after an intense triple-header, Verstappen is racing in the GT World Challenge Europe finale at Portimao this weekend in the Verstappen Racing #3 Mercedes-AMG with Chris Lulham and Ben Barnicoat.

    The United States Grand Prix takes place at the Circuit of the Americas from 23-25 October and kicks off the next triple-header in the championship, with the Mexican and Brazilian Grands Prix in the two weeks following.
