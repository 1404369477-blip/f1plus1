    梅赛德斯技术总监：车队不会对2027年下压力削减坐视不管

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·艾利森警告，F1工程师将对国际汽联2027年削减下压力的计划予以反击。国际汽联正推动在2027年再次强制降低下压力，有早期报道称目标削减幅度为20至50点下压力。

    梅赛德斯技术总监警告，F1车队不会对2027年下压力削减坐视不管

    梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·艾利森警告，F1工程师将对国际汽联2027年的下压力削减予以反击

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    F1向2026年新动力单元与底盘规则的过渡彻底打乱了竞争格局，但规则手册远未最终确定。随着车队为2027年新规下的第二个赛季做准备，国际汽联正推动再次强制降低下压力。

    早期报告表明，管理机构的目标是在2027赛季将下压力减少20至50个点。为了实现这一目标，国际汽联计划禁止若干关键的后部空气动力学部件，以改善混合动力能量管理，并减少阻碍赛车紧密跟车的湍流“脏空气”。

    但梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·艾利森最近警告说，制定规则来减慢一级方程式赛车的速度，很少能阻止围场顶尖工程师找到绕过它的办法。

    在NU Silver Arrows Radio Show播客节目中，艾利森确认了国际汽联拟议的空气动力学限制范围，同时向即将到来的研发战发出了挑战。

    “我所知道的是，新规则会让赛车损失一些下压力，不止一点，”艾利森解释道。

    乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯车队；安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯车队

    从历史上看，国际汽联与一级方程式车队之间的关系始终是一场无休止的猫鼠游戏。管理机构出于安全和竞赛考量修改规则手册以限制过弯速度，而车队则立即在风洞中投入数百万美元，以恢复国际汽联试图削去的下压力。

    据艾利森表示，2027年的规则周期也不会有任何不同。

    “他们已经做出了不错的尝试，试图将下压力拉回到2026时代初期的水平，”艾利森指出。

    他补充道：“看看各车队是会选择妥协接受，还是会全力夺回全部性能，这将非常有意思。”

    如果梅赛德斯在2026年的巨大飞跃能说明什么，那就是阿利森在布拉克利的工程部门完全没有妥协的打算。2027赛季的技术战线已经正式划定。

    Mercedes technical director warns F1 teams won’t “lie down” over 2027 downforce cuts

    Mercedes technical director James Allison warns F1 engineers will fight back against the FIA's 2027 downforce cuts

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    Formula 1's transition to the new 2026 engine and chassis regulations completely reshuffled the competitive order, but the rulebook is far from finalised. As teams prepare for their second year under the new formula in 2027, the FIA is pushing to mandate another drop in downforce.

    Early reports indicate the governing body is targeting a reduction of 20 to 50 points of downforce for the 2027 season. To achieve this, the FIA plans to ban several key rear aerodynamic elements in a bid to improve hybrid energy management and reduce the turbulent "dirty air" that prevents cars from following each other closely.

    But Mercedes technical director James Allison recently cautioned that writing a rule to slow down a Formula 1 car rarely stops the grid's top engineers from finding a way around it.

    Speaking on the NU Silver Arrows Radio Show podcast, Allison confirmed the scope of the FIA's proposed aerodynamic restrictions while simultaneously throwing down the gauntlet for the upcoming development war.

    "All I know is that the cars are going to be losing a bit of downforce from the new regs, more than a bit," Allison explained.

    George Russell, Mercedes, Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes

    Historically, the relationship between the FIA and Formula 1 constructors is a perpetual game of cat and mouse. The governing body alters the rulebook to limit cornering speeds on safety and racing grounds, and the teams immediately spend millions of dollars in the wind tunnel to recover the exact downforce the FIA attempted to take away.

    According to Allison, the 2027 regulation cycle will be no different.

    "They've had a good go at trying to pull the downforce back to [the] start of the 2026 era," Allison noted.

    "It'll be interesting to see whether the teams lie down and take that or whether we recover it all or not," he added.

    If Mercedes' massive leap forward in 2026 is any indication, Allison's engineering department in Brackley has zero intention of lying down. The technical battle lines for the 2027 season have officially been drawn.
