    F1将于2027年2月在米兰举办赛季启动活动

    据The 比赛报道，F1将于2027年2月18日在米兰举办赛季启动活动“F1 The Show”，这是继两年前在O2体育馆举办F1 75 Live之后再次举办此类活动。

    一级方程式将于明年二月在米兰举行2027赛季发布活动，这距离在O2体育馆举办的F1 75 Live活动已有两年。

    由喜剧演员Jack Whitehall主持的F1 75 Live活动对F1来说是第一次，它将全部10支车队和20位车手齐聚一堂，并仅在一晚之内展示所有2025年的涂装。

    F1一直希望重办这一活动，但在繁忙的2026年季前准备期间，由于新规出台以及三次独立的季前测试，该活动未能举行，而发布活动将以扩展的形式于2027年重返米兰。

    这场为期一晚的发布活动“F1 The Show”将于2月18日在一个尚未确认的“标志性城市场地”举行，届时全部11支车队的车手和车队负责人都将到场，还将有“音乐、娱乐节目以及更多内容”。

    2月18日至20日，市内还将举办为期三天的车迷节。

    这两项活动的更多细节将于日后公布。

    门票将于今年晚些时候开售，车迷现在可以预先登记对这项活动的兴趣。与F1 75 Live一样，该活动将在全球范围内播出。

    本周早些时候，在意大利大奖赛之前的周三，米兰举办了多场法拉利活动，刘易斯·汉密尔顿和夏尔·勒克莱尔的亮相吸引了大量人群聚集在城市街道上。

    英文原文

    Formula 1 will hold a 2027 season launch event in Milan next February, two years on from its F1 75 Live event at the O2 arena.

    The F1 75 Live event hosted by comedian Jack Whitehall was a first for F1, bringing together all 10 teams and 20 drivers and showcasing every 2025 livery for one night only.

    F1 has been keen to repeat it, and while it wasn’t held amid a busy 2026 pre-season with new regulations and three separate pre-season tests, the launch event will return in 2027 in Milan in an expanded form.

    The one-night launch event ‘F1 The Show’ will be held at a yet-to-be-confirmed “iconic city venue” on February 18, with drivers and team principals from all 11 teams and “music, entertainment, and more”.

    There’s also a three-day fan festival in the city taking place across February 18-20.

    More details of both events will be released at a later date.

    Tickets will go on sale later this year and fans can pre-register their interest in the event now. As with F1 75 Live, the event will be broadcast around the world.

    Milan hosted multiple Ferrari events on the Wednesday prior to the Italian GP, with Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc’s appearances attracting huge crowds on the streets of the city earlier this week.
