    BBC可能失去F1广播权，talkSPORT考虑竞标

    talkSPORT考虑竞标英国F1广播权，可能结束BBC长期垄断。

    BBC可能将在32年后失去一级方程式的广播权，因为竞争对手正在考虑投标

    talkSPORT正在探索对一级方程式英国广播权的潜在投标，这给BBC长期以来的报道带来了压力

    费尔南多·阿隆索、阿斯顿·马丁赛车、伊萨克·哈吉亚、红牛车队

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计以及那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事。

    talkSPORT正在考虑竞标英国一级方程式赛车的广播权，这可能会结束BBC在该国作为赛车广播主场的长期统治。

    该电台负责人Liam Fisher在接受《每日电讯报》采访时表示，一级方程式赛车适合talkSPORT的听众，如Crash.net报道。自1994年以来，BBC一直在广播一级方程式赛车，因此这可能对英国的一级方程式车迷如何跟上他们最喜欢的车手产生重大变化。

    "这是一套非常棒的权益。如果我不积极探索是否有机会，我就没有在做好我的工作," Fisher说。

    "我们认为观众与talkSPORT的观众非常契合。我们认为我们有很好的机会为它提供真正的放大效果。"

    talkSPORT最著名的节目是深度足球报道，但这位英国人表示，该电台希望通过扩大组织的覆盖范围进入新的细分市场来吸引新的听众。

    "足球仍然是我们的主食，但我们确实在基本理解的基础上寻求扩展，即使足球是你的第一、第二和第三运动，你很可能还对其他事物感兴趣，"他说。

    "因此，我们的策略是挑选出我们认为有潜在观众的事件，这些事件与我们提供的内容相得益彰。"

    该电台今年已经进入了赛车运动。5月份，它获得了夏季六场MotoGP赛事的转播权，由TNT Sports的解说团队在talkSPORT2上播出。

    BBC的当前合同即将到期

    BBC与一级方程式的当前合同从2025年到2027年给予Radio 5 Live、5 Sports Extra和BBC Sounds在英国的独家音频版权。

    其报道团队包括1996年世界冠军达蒙·希尔、一级方程式车手山姆·伯德、前W系列车手爱丽丝·鲍威尔和前F1机械师马克·普里斯特利。

    据《每日电讯报》报道，BBC目前正在尝试削减成本，这样的变化并不令人意外。尤其是在它最近还取消了赛船赛和英联邦运动会田径比赛之后。

    BBC could lose F1 radio rights after 32 years as rival broadcaster considers bid

    talkSPORT is exploring a potential bid for Formula 1's UK radio rights, putting the BBC's long-running coverage under pressure

    Fernando Alonso, Aston Martin Racing, Isack Hadjar, Red Bull Racing

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    talkSPORT is considering a bid for the UK radio rights to Formula 1, which could end the BBC's long run as the sport's home on radio in the country.

    The station's head, Liam Fisher, told The Telegraph that F1 would suit talkSPORT's listeners, as reported by Crash.net. The BBC has broadcast F1 on radio since 1994, so this could be a massive change to how UK-based F1 fans keep up with their favourite drivers.

    "It's another brilliant set of rights. I wouldn't be doing my job if I wasn't actively exploring whether there's an opportunity," Fisher said.

    "We think the audience goes quite well with the talkSPORT audience. We think there's an opportunity for us to deliver really good amplification for it as well."

    talkSPORT is best known for its in-depth football coverage, but the Briton said the station wants to reach new listeners by extending the organisation's reach into new niches.

    "Football still is our bread and butter, but we've definitely looked to extend it on the basic understanding that, even if football is your first, second, and third sport, you are probably into something else as well," he said.

    "So our strategy has been to pick off the events where we believe there is an audience potential that sits well with what we're providing."

    The station has already moved into motorsport this year. In May it secured the rights to six MotoGP races over the summer, broadcast on talkSPORT2 with the TNT Sports' commentary team.

    The BBC's current deal is coming to an end

    The BBC's current deal with F1 gives Radio 5 Live, 5 Sports Extra and BBC Sounds exclusive UK audio rights from 2025 to 2027.

    Its coverage team includes 1996 world champion Damon Hill, Formula E driver Sam Bird, former W Series driver Alice Powell and ex-F1 mechanic Marc Priestley.

    With The Telegraph reporting that the BBC is currently trying to cut costs, this change wouldn't come as such a surprise. Especially after it also recently dropped the Boat Race and the Commonwealth Games athletics.
