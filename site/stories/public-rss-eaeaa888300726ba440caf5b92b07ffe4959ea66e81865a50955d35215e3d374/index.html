    乐高将推出2026赛季全部F1赛车与车手积木套装

    乐高宣布将把2026赛季全部F1赛车和22名车手做成Speed Champions套装与人仔，并于2027年3月发售。套装将提供可自定义选项，供车迷混搭喜爱的车手与车队组合。

    乐高公布2026赛季全部F1车手与车队的Speed Champions套装

    乐高宣布了与F1合作的下一个阶段，将把2026赛季全部赛车和车手制作为Speed Champions积木套装与人仔

    玩具巨头乐高宣布，将把2026赛季全部F1赛车和22名车手制作为人仔，同时其在拉斯维加斯举办了最新一届展览赛。

    继2025年首次启动、今年进一步扩大的合作之后，乐高将于2027年3月推出完整的F1 Speed Champions系列，这意味着每一辆2026赛季赛车和每一位车手都将面向车迷发售。套装将提供可自定义选项，供车迷混搭自己喜爱的车手与车队组合。

    乐高首席产品与营销官朱莉娅·戈尔丁认为，让该系列粉丝能够获得每一位车手，对品牌而言至关重要。“一级方程式车迷不只关注车队和赛车。他们与车手之间建立了极其牢固的情感联结，”她说。

    “首次将全部22位车手带入乐高小人仔世界，为粉丝提供了一种充满趣味的新方式，来庆祝这些车手个性、竞争关系以及比赛日的精彩瞬间。”

    这些套装将于明年3月1日起发售，现已开启预售。

    在拉斯维加斯的发布会上，乐高还宣布，其乐高迷你赛车比赛将在银石赛道混乱的巡游圈首秀之后回归。每支车队将有一辆赛车参加周六赛前仪式期间的一圈比赛，乐高将在赛事周期间通过社交媒体陆续揭晓车手身份。

    LEGO reveals Speed Champions sets for all 2026 F1 drivers and teams

    LEGO has announced the next stage of its Formula 1 partnership, producing all 2026 cars and drivers as Speed Champions builds and minifigures

    Toy giant LEGO has announced it will produce all 2026 F1 cars and 22 drivers in minifigure form, as it hosts its latest exhibition race in Las Vegas.

    Off the back of its partnership first launched in 2025 and expanded this year, LEGO will introduce an entire range of F1 Speed Champions in March 2027, which means every single 2026 car and driver will be available to fans. The sets will come with customisable options for fans to mix and match their favourite driver and team combinations.

    Julia Goldin, LEGO's Chief Product & Marketing Officer, felt it was crucial for the brand to make every single driver available to fans of the series. "Formula 1 fans do not only follow teams and cars. They form incredibly strong connections with the drivers," she said.

    "Bringing all 22 drivers into the LEGO minifigure world for the first time gives fans a playful new way to celebrate those personalities, rivalries and race-day moments."

    The sets will be available from 1 March next year, with pre-orders opening now.

    At its Las Vegas presentation, LEGO also announced its LEGO minicar race would return following its chaotic parade lap debut in Silverstone. One car per team will take part in a one-lap race during Saturday's pre-race ceremony, with LEGO revealing the identity of the drivers throughout the event week on social media.
