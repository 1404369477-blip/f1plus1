    F1卡塔尔大奖赛：乔治·拉塞尔击败安东内利夺得杆位

    乔治·拉塞尔在F1卡塔尔大奖赛中夺得杆位，击败队友安东内利，梅赛德斯车队在多哈的比赛中全面领先。

    F1卡塔尔大奖赛：乔治·拉塞尔击败安东内利夺得杆位

    乔治·拉塞尔在2026年第六次夺得杆位，梅赛德斯车队在F1卡塔尔大奖赛的预赛中全面领先。

    在卢塞尔赛道的高下压力赛道上，梅赛德斯车队在周末的每一节练习中都证明了自己比竞争对手更胜一筹，并通过利用其转向优势锁定了前两排。

    拉塞尔在决定性的Q3冲刺赛中，在第一轮和第二轮中分别跑出1m21.695s的成绩，领先他的世界冠军队友近两十分，并且明显领先其他竞争者。

    安东内利质疑自己为何在最后一轮中排在第一位，这位意大利少年在第二次尝试中找不到任何改进，最终排名第二。拉塞尔几乎复制了他的第一次成绩，但无需改进就赢得了他本赛季第六个杆位，这对于他的成长之旅意义重大。

    迈凯轮的兰多·诺里斯在第二轮中提高了十分，以第三名的成绩领先队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，而伊萨克·哈吉亚在排位赛中击败了他的红牛队友马克斯·维斯塔潘，获得了第五个发车位。

    法拉利在多哈的糟糕周末继续，整个练习赛中都在为抓地力和速度而挣扎。查尔斯·勒克莱尔只能排在第七位，距离杆位有1.260秒的差距。青训车队小红牛的菜鸟车手阿维德·林德布拉德击败了刘易斯·汉密尔顿的第二辆法拉利，利亚姆·劳森以第十名的成绩结束了前十。

    红牛车队在Q2的表现与梅赛德斯车队相当接近，哈德贾的成绩为1m22.344s，比领跑者拉塞尔慢不到三十分之一，拉塞尔以1m22.079s的成绩领先安东内利。

    在这两支车队和梅赛德斯车队之后，法拉利再次陷入困境，勒克莱尔落后整整一秒，他们试图与小红牛车队抗衡。

    尽管如此，两队仍有足够的余地进入Q3。对于前十名的争夺，哈斯、阿尔派和奥迪之间的竞争非常激烈，但最终这三支车队都没有足够的实力去威胁排名第十的劳森。

    奥利弗·贝尔曼最接近，只差0.240s未能晋级。皮埃尔·加斯利从FP2中失去的时间中恢复过来，在发车位上获得第十二名，随后是加布里埃尔·博托莱托和他的队友弗朗哥·科拉平托。哈斯车队的新人拉斐尔·卡马拉和经验丰富的尼科·胡肯伯格将在周日的第八排发车。

    梅赛德斯和红牛是唯一两支在Q1中只完成了一圈计时圈速的车队，拉塞尔的1分22.557秒领先，排在安东内利和维斯塔潘之前。其他领跑者虽然也在磨损的轮胎上完成了一圈，但同样也为稍后保留了关键的软胎。

    第一轮练习结束后，卡洛斯·塞恩斯和亚历克斯·阿隆诺在圈外观望，威廉姆斯车手在第二轮练习后仍保持在这个位置。这对科拉平托来说是个安慰，他凭借0.1秒的优势从第16位惊险晋级Q1。与此同时，哈斯车队的新人卡马拉也晋级到第14位，但他为此不得不使用第二套软胎。

    费尔南多·阿隆索和兰斯·斯特罗尔分别以第19位和第20位为陷入困境的阿斯顿·马丁车队挣扎，凯迪拉克车队的瓦尔特里·博塔斯和塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯完成了周日的排位赛。

    F1 Qatar GP: George Russell beats Kimi Antonelli to pole

    George Russell takes sixth pole of 2026 as Mercedes dominates qualifying at Formula 1's Qatar Grand Prix

    George Russell has grabbed an emphatic pole for Formula 1's Qatar Grand Prix, defeating team-mate Kimi Antonelli as Mercedes blitzed the competition in Doha.

    On the demanding, high-downforce circuit of Lusail, Mercedes proved a step ahead of the competition in every session over the weekend, and the Silver Arrows capitalised on their cornering advantage by locking out the front row.

    Russell led from Antonelli after the first of two runs in the decisive Q3 shootout with a 1m21.695s lap, nearly two tenths up on his world champion team-mate and well clear of the competition.

    As Antonelli questioned being out first in the queue for the final runs, the Italian teenager struggled to find any improvement on his second attempt and settled for second. Russell almost matched his first run, but did not need to improve to claim his sixth pole of a character-building campaign.

    McLaren's Lando Norris found a tenth on his second lap to claim third ahead of team-mate Oscar Piastri, with Isack Hadjar out-qualifying his Red Bull team-mate Max Verstappen for fifth on the grid.

    Ferrari continued a woeful weekend in Doha, having been struggling for grip and pace throughout practice. Charles Leclerc could only manage seventh on the grid, a shocking 1.260s off pole. Racing Bulls rookie Arvid Lindblad beat the second Ferrari of Lewis Hamilton to eighth, with Liam Lawson rounding out the top 10.

    Red Bull, rather than McLaren, looked somewhat close to Mercedes in Q2, with Hadjar's 1m22.344s less than three tenths behind pacesetter Russell, who had the edge on Antonelli with a 1m22.079s lap.

    Behind those two teams and McLaren, Ferrari struggled again, with Leclerc a full second behind as they looked to fight with Racing Bulls.

    There was still a big enough cushion for both teams to make it into Q3, though. The battle for the top-10 was a tight fight between Haas, Alpine and Audi, but ultimately none of the three teams had enough in the tank to bother 10th-placed Lawson.

    Oliver Bearman came closest, missing the cut by 0.240s. Pierre Gasly recovered from lost track time in FP2 to take 12th on the grid, followed by Gabriel Bortoleto and team-mate Franco Colapinto. Haas rookie Rafael Camara and veteran Nico Hulkenberg will start from the eighth row on Sunday.

    Mercedes and Red Bull were the only two teams to just set one timed lap in Q1, with Russell's 1m22.557s leading the way ahead of Antonelli and Verstappen. The other frontrunners set a second time on scrubbed tyres, though, equally saving crucial sets of softs for later on.

    After the first set of runs, Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon were on the outside looking in and the Williams drivers stayed in those positions after the second effort. That was a relief for Colapinto, who escaped Q1 by a tenth of a second in 16th. Meanwhile, Haas rookie Camara also advanced in 14th, but had to deploy a second set of softs to do so in 14th.

    Fernando Alonso and Lance Stroll were 19th and 20th for struggling Aston Martin, with Cadillac duo Valtteri Bottas and Sergio Perez completing Sunday's grid.
