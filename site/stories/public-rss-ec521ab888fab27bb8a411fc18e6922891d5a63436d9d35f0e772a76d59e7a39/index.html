  1. 车手近况

    维斯塔潘：「能用的办法都用上了」，巴库仅差十分之一秒无缘赛季首胜

    因排位出问题从第八位起步的维斯塔潘说，第一段中性胎「抓地远不如软胎，只能想办法活下来」；安全车来得正是时候，换上软胎后「快了一大截」，他也在第一次重启时超越皮亚斯特里升至第二。收官阶段他始终在拉塞尔身后约一秒，最终仅以十分之一秒之差落败。「我把能用的办法都用上了。」（来源：Formula 1）

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在阿塞拜疆大奖赛上离赛季首胜只有一步之遥。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘承认，为了在巴库街道上拿下胜利，他「把所有办法都试过了」，但这位红牛车手最终仅以十分之一秒之差不敌乔治·拉塞尔。

    由于排位出现问题，这位荷兰车手只能懊恼地从第八位出发参加周六的51圈比赛，但他用中性胎很快就开始追赶。

    升至第三后，维斯塔潘在两次安全车重启中的第一次超越了奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里的迈凯伦，升到第二，从而得以在收官阶段挑战领跑的拉塞尔。

    「从第八位起步，我知道不会那么简单，但我们相当顺利地一路追进了前三，只是第一段非常棘手。」这位四届世界冠军说。

    「那套中性胎就是不太行，没有我觉得软胎所拥有的抓地力，所以那一段我只是在努力活下来，尽量跟住那些用软胎的车手。

    「我觉得那次安全车来得正是时候，大家都能进站换上软胎。一换上软胎，感觉就好多了，简直快了一大截。

    「我觉得这一点也体现在，乔治和我换上软胎后，我们把其他所有人甩开了很多。」

    最后几圈，维斯塔潘在主直道上始终保持在拉塞尔身后约一秒的距离，红牛在这场比赛中拥有直线速度优势。

    瓦尔特利·博塔斯撞车后15号弯出现黄旗，让维斯塔潘在最后一次冲向终点时获得了更强的尾流。但这位荷兰车手最终仅差十分之一秒，这意味着他在2026赛季仍未取得分站冠军。

    「我把所有能用的办法都用上了。」维斯塔潘谈到超越拉塞尔的尝试时说，其间他还数次在16号弯入口擦到护墙。

    「当然，最后一圈因为有黄旗，我觉得乔治出现了一点助推迟滞，所以他出15号弯时没能拉开，这给了我多一点机会，这也是为什么冲线会那么接近。我们一直只差一秒，彼此非常接近。」

    （来源：Formula 1 官网）

    英文原文

    Max Verstappen came close to taking his first victory of the season at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix.

    Max Verstappen admits he "was trying everything" to claim victory on the streets of Baku, the Red Bull driver eventually falling short by just one tenth against George Russell.

    The Dutchman was left a frustrated eighth on the grid for Saturday's 51-lap race after issues in Qualifying, but Verstappen quickly began to make progress with the medium tyre.

    Moving into third, Verstappen demoted Oscar Piastri's McLaren for second at the first of two Safety car restarts, allowing him to challenge race leader Russell in the closing stages.

    "Starting in P8 I knew it wasn't going to be that straightforward but we worked our way quite nicely up into the top three but that first stint was very tricky," said the four-time World Champion.

    "That medium tyre was just not very good, not having the grip that I think the soft tyres had so was just trying to survive on that stint to try and stay with the guys on the soft.

    "I think that Safety Car came at the right time for everyone to box onto that soft, because once we were on that soft tyre, it felt a lot better, just miles faster.

    "I think that also showed once George and I were on the soft, we were pulling away quite a lot over everyone else."

    Verstappen stayed on the cusp of a one second gap to Russell over the final laps down the main straight, with Red Bull enjoying straight-line speed during the race.

    A yellow flag at Turn 15 after Valtteri Bottas crashed gifted Verstappen the chance to gain an even stronger slipstream on the run to the line for the final time. But the Dutchman ultimately fell short by just one tenth, meaning he has still yet to record a victory during the 2026 season.

    "I was trying everything that I have available," said Verstappen in his quest to pass Russell, which included glancing the wall on the entry to Turn 16 several times.

    "Of course on the last lap because there was a yellow, I think George picked up a bit of boost lag, so he didn't pull out of Turn 15 and that gave me a little bit more of an opportunity and that's why it was so close to the line. We were constantly one second, so we were very close to each other."
