    F1专家称，乔治·拉塞尔比任何车手都更能激怒维斯塔潘

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛周末再次点燃了红牛与梅赛德斯车手维斯塔潘和乔治·拉塞尔之间的长期紧张关系。

    维斯塔潘和乔治·拉塞尔的竞争对赛车运动来说“真的很酷”，F1评论员如是说

    天空体育评论员杰米·查德威克认为乔治·拉塞尔比任何其他车手更能挑衅维斯塔潘

    维斯塔潘、红牛车队、乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说。关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的比赛分析。

    拉斯维加斯大奖赛周末再次点燃了红牛车队和梅赛德斯车队车手，马克斯·维斯塔潘和乔治·罗素之间长期存在的紧张关系，这成为了本周天空体育F1秀播客的讨论焦点。

    三届W系列冠军、天空体育评论员杰米·查德威克在播客中讨论了这场重燃的竞争，声称没有其他车手能像罗素那样让维斯塔潘如此烦恼。

    这场争执始于最后的练习赛，并在比赛后继续，当时维斯塔潘获胜，而罗素在试图超越他的梅赛德斯车队队友时撞上了墙壁。罗素曾对Sky Italia表示，“得有人站出来对抗那个人”。维斯塔潘的回应是，罗素“远远低于我的水平”，这并不让他烦恼。

    查德威克表示她非常了解罗素，而对维斯塔潘了解较少，并给出了她的观点。她说：“这仅仅是在某种程度上个性的极端冲突。我认为没有其他车手能像乔治那样让马克斯如此烦恼。我们在他们之间多次比赛、多种情况和场景中看到了这一点。”

    马克斯·维斯塔潘，红牛车队，乔治·拉塞尔，梅赛德斯

    “一方面，对于这项运动来说，有这种竞争真的很酷，因为它让每个人都开始讨论。在某种程度上，它有点像是过去的赛车手时代。但同样，也有点沮丧。”

    “她补充说：'马克斯是目前我们这一代中最优秀的车手，他在比赛中证明了这一点。难以置信的天赋。’

    “他需要说这样的评论吗？他需要让它影响自己，让自己达到那个水平吗？显然，这对他是种释放，但对我来说，作为一个体育人看到这些，总是感到沮丧，因为他在赛道上取得的每一项成就，我都不觉得他需要把自己拉低到赛道之外的水平。”

    这对车手将在本周末的卡塔尔大奖赛再次相遇，该赛事在阿布扎比的赛季决赛前一周举行。

    Max Verstappen and George Russell rivalry "really cool for the sport", says F1 pundit

    Sky Sports pundit Jamie Chadwick believes George Russell provokes Max Verstappen more than any other driver

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    The Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend reignited the long-running tension between Red Bull and Mercedes drivers, Max Verstappen and George Russell, and it was the talking point of Sky's F1 Show podcast this week.

    Three-time W Series champion and Sky Sports pundit Jamie Chadwick discussed the reignited rivalry on the podcast, claiming that no other driver gets under Verstappen's skin as much as Russell.

    The row began in final practice and continued after the race, which Verstappen won while Russell hit the wall trying to pass his Mercedes team-mate. Russell had told Sky Italia that "someone's got to stand up to that guy". Verstappen's response was that Russell was "so far below my level" that it did not bother him.

    Chadwick said she knows Russell very well and Verstappen less so, and gave her view of what is driving it. "It is just an extreme clash of personalities in a way. I don't think any other driver gets beneath Max's skin as much as George does. We've seen that across multiple races and multiple situations and scenarios between them.

    Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing, George Russell, Mercedes

    "On one side it's really cool for the sport to have this kind of rivalry because it gets everyone talking. It's a little bit of times of old, in a way, of drivers of the past. But equally, there's a bit of frustration."

    She added: "Max is the best driver of our generation at the moment, proved that in the race. Unbelievable talent.

    "Does he need to say comments like that? Does he need to let it get under his skin and let himself go to that level? Clearly it relieves something for him, but for me as a sports person seeing it, it's always frustrating, because everything he achieves on track, I just don't feel like he needs to drag himself down to that off track."

    The pair meet again at this weekend's Qatar Grand Prix, which arrives one week before the season finale in Abu Dhabi.
