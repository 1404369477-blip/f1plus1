    卡马拉在F2争冠与哈斯F1出场之间两难

    法拉利青训车手拉斐尔·卡马拉正面临抉择：是完成他争冠的F2赛季，还是代表哈斯车队跑完2026赛季剩余的F1比赛。

    拉斐尔·卡马拉在F2争冠与代表哈斯参加更多F1比赛之间两难

    卡马拉、哈斯和法拉利需要就他在卡塔尔和阿布扎比的赛程做出关键决定

    拉斐尔·卡马拉正面临抉择：是完成他争冠的F2赛季，还是代表哈斯车队跑完2026赛季剩余的F1比赛。

    这位法拉利青训车手是卫冕F3冠军，作为新秀效力Invicta Racing并领跑F2积分榜，凭借本赛季的出色表现，他已拿下五个杆位、七次主赛领奖台，其中包括三场胜利。

    埃斯特班·奥康与哈斯车队的决裂为卡马拉提供了在巴西大奖赛完成F1首秀的机会，而本周末在拉斯维加斯他将获得更多经验，为2027年随队完整的新秀赛季做准备。

    然而，F2赛季最后两轮将与F1在卡塔尔和阿布扎比同期举行，因此卡马拉、哈斯和法拉利现在必须做出艰难抉择——这位年轻车手表示，“多方人士”将参与决策。

    当被问及这一情况时，这位21岁的车手回答道：“嗯，这是个很好的问题。两者对我都非常重要。在F2，我觉得你全年都在为此努力，当然希望以高光收尾。而且，如果没有今年在F2与Invicta车队发生的一切，我也不会处于这个位置或拥有这些机会。所以，这肯定会非常特别。

    “归根结底，我都会做我热爱的事，无论做哪一项我都会很开心。但对我而言，这肯定会是一个艰难的决定，因为一方面，你拥有F1，这是你一生都梦想成为和从事的事业；另一方面，你拥有整个锦标赛，你全年都在努力以圆满结束。”

    “让我们看看。我现在会努力专注于这个周末，以好的方式结束。然后希望我们能解决问题，之后会发生什么再看。”

    有趣的是，国际汽联（FIA）的规定意味着没有车手能在同一个周末同时参加F1和F2；如果卡马拉确实要参加这个青训系列赛，哈斯可以求助于储备车手平川亮，他在奥康突然被解雇后于墨西哥意外完成了大奖赛首秀。

    尽管他目前领跑F2积分榜，这位Invicta车手仅领先红牛青训车手尼古拉·措洛夫七分，后者已无缘2027年随小红牛（小红牛）晋升F1。阿尔派青训成员亚历克斯·邓恩和加布里埃莱·米尼分别落后18分和44分。

    当被Motorsport.com问及F2冠军对他的履历和职业生涯有多大价值时，卡马拉评论道：“是的，我认为那会很好，完成F2赛季。当然，当你回顾你在青训组别中取得的成就时，我认为那是非常美好的事情，拥有F3和F2冠军，作为新秀获胜。不仅如此，获胜和做事的感觉会非常棒。”

    “但我仍然不知道会发生什么，也不知道我会怎么做。就像我说的，我只想先完成这个周末，之后肯定会有很多事情要讨论。”

    哈斯的新车手在第二场大奖赛前感觉自信多了，他在因特拉戈斯赛道棘手的湿地条件下获得了关键经验，最终仅列第20名。

    “主要的收获是与车队合作以及赛车表现方面的经验，”卡马拉在谈到自己作为赛车手的首次出场时说。“我认为这让你在本周末开始时就平静得多，因为某种程度上你知道会发生什么。

    “这有助于你在周末开始时脑子里装的事情更少，这样你可以某种程度上更专注于细节，更专注于其他事情。可能在巴西时，你只是想同时学习所有东西，你的大脑开始有点过热。这肯定有帮助。”

    Rafael Camara torn between his F2 title bid and more F1 races with Haas

    Camara, Haas and Ferrari have a crucial decision to make for his schedule in Qatar and Abu Dhabi

    Rafael Camara is torn between completing his title-contending Formula 2 campaign and seeing out the 2026 season at Haas in Formula 1.

    The Ferrari protege is the reigning F3 champion and leads the F2 standings as a rookie with Invicta Racing, courtesy of a starring campaign in which he has taken five pole positions and seven feature race podiums including three victories.

    Esteban Ocon’s fallout with Haas has provided Camara with an opportunity to make his F1 debut at the Brazilian Grand Prix, and he will gain more experience this weekend in Las Vegas ahead of his full rookie campaign with the team in 2027.

    However, the last two rounds of the F2 season are taking place alongside F1 in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, so Camara, Haas and Ferrari now have a tough call to make – with the youngster indicating “a mix of people” will be involved in the decision-making.

    Asked about the situation, the 21-year-old replied: “Well, that's a very good question. Both of them will be very important for me. In F2, I feel like the whole year you're working for that and for sure you want to finish in a high. And also, I wouldn't be in this position or having the opportunities if it didn't happen, what happened this year in F2 with Invicta. So, for sure, it would be very special.

    “At the end of the day, I'll be doing what I love to do, and I'll be happy in doing [either] of them. But it will definitely be a difficult decision to do on my side, because one side, you have Formula 1, something you've always been dreaming of being and doing in your life; and the other side, you have the whole championship and you're working the whole year to close that.

    “Let's see. I'll try now to focus on this weekend and finish in a good way. And then hopefully we can sort it out, what will happen after that.”

    Interestingly, FIA regulations mean no driver can compete in both F1 and F2 on the same weekend; if Camara does race in the feeder series, Haas can resort to reserve driver Ryo Hirakawa, who made an unexpected grand prix debut in Mexico after Ocon was suddenly dismissed.

    Though he currently leads the F2 standings, the Invicta racer is just seven points ahead of Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov, who has missed out on a 2027 F1 graduation with Racing Bulls. Alpine academy members Alex Dunne and Gabriele Mini are respectively 18 and 44 points adrift.

    Asked by Motorsport.com how valuable the F2 title would be to his CV and career, Camara commented: “Yeah, I think that would be nice, finishing the F2 season. And for sure, when you [look back on] what you achieved on the junior categories, I think it's something very nice, having the F3 and F2 championship, winning as a rookie. Not only that, but the feeling of winning and doing things will be very nice.

    “But I still don't know what will happen and I don't know how I will do it. Like I said, I'm just trying to finish this weekend and then for sure there will be a lot of things to discuss after.”

    Haas’ new driver is feeling much more confident ahead of his second grand prix, having gained key experience in tricky wet conditions on his way to a lowly 20th at Interlagos.

    “The main takeaways were working with the team and also with how the car behaves,” Camara said of his maiden outing as a race driver. “I think it makes you already starting this weekend much calmer, because in a way you know what to expect.

    “It helps starting the weekend with less things in your head, so you can, in a way, focus more into details, more in other things. Probably in Brazil, you're just trying to learn everything at the same time and your brain starts to overheat a bit. For sure it helps.”
