    乔治·拉塞尔开玩笑称带基米·安东内利喝酒可能是阻止他赢得F1冠军的最佳方法

    乔治·拉塞尔开玩笑称带基米·安东内利喝酒可能是阻止他赢得F1冠军的最佳方法；安东内利在积分榜上领先拉塞尔102分，有机会在美国大奖赛上赢得首个F1冠军。

    乔治·拉塞尔开玩笑说，带基米·安东内利去喝酒可能是他阻止意大利人赢得F1冠军的最佳机会

    安德里亚·基米·安东内利、梅赛德斯、乔治·拉塞尔、梅赛德斯

    对于梅赛德斯车迷来说。来自Brackley和Brixworth的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和比赛分析。

    乔治·拉塞尔提出了一项幽默的计划，以阻止他的梅赛德斯车队队友基米·安东内利在美国大奖赛上夺得F1冠军：带他去喝酒。

    安东内利有机会在美洲赛道上获得他个人的首个F1冠军，因为他以102分的领先优势进入比赛，在积分榜上排在第二位的拉塞尔之后。为了确保冠军，这位意大利车手需要在美国大奖赛中获胜，而拉塞尔需要排名第八名之外，刘易斯·汉密尔顿需要排名第四名之外。

    在比赛周末之前，两位梅赛德斯车手出现在一个粉丝论坛上，拉塞尔开玩笑说，在奥斯汀的夜晚可能是他缩小与安东内利差距的最佳机会。

    拉塞尔在社交媒体上广泛分享的一段视频中告诉安东内利：“我可以带你去喝酒。实际上，这可能是一个在锦标赛中击败你的好方法，让你在周日晚上被捕。”

    这个笑话让观众笑了出来，特别是这个20岁的年轻人还没有达到美国法定饮酒年龄。

    "我真的很喜欢拉塞尔的心态。他承受了太多的仇恨，但他已经接受了今年他做得不够，他只想熬过这一年," 一位粉丝在Reddit上评论道，另一位补充说: "这是个玩笑，除非..."

    "现在乔治开始有了正确的想法！实际上，我真的很钦佩他乐天知命，对此开玩笑," 另一个人发帖说。

    进一步的反应包括: "你可以看到他脸上的表情；就像'你知道什么，这是个不错的主意。一不做二不休，'" 和 "乔治在自言自语。"

    美国大奖赛标志着赛季倒数第二个三连赛的开始，将于10月25日星期日举行。

    George Russell's tongue-in-cheek Kimi Antonelli F1 title threat: "Get you arrested"

    George Russell joked that taking Kimi Antonelli drinking could be his best chance of preventing the Italian from clinching the F1 championship

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, George Russell, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    George Russell has come up with a tongue-in-cheek plan to stop Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli from clinching the Formula 1 championship at the United States Grand Prix: take him out drinking.

    Antonelli has the chance to claim his maiden F1 title as he heads into the race at the Circuit of the Americas with a 102-point lead over second-placed Russell in the standings. To secure the title, the Italian driver would need to win the United States Grand Prix, with Russell finishing outside of the top eight and Lewis Hamilton outside of the top four.

    The two Mercedes drivers appeared together at a fan forum ahead of the race weekend, where Russell joked that a night out in Austin could be his best chance of closing the gap to Antonelli.

    "I could take you drinking," Russell told Antonelli in a clip widely shared on social media. "Actually, this could be a good way to beat you in the championship, to get you arrested on Sunday night."

    The joke drew laughs from the crowd, especially as the 20-year-old has not yet reached the legal drinking age in the US.

    "I really like Russell’s mentality. He gets way too much hate, but he’s accepted that this year he’s not done enough, and he just wants to get through it," one fan commented on Reddit, while another added: "It's a joke, unless..."

    "Now George is starting to get the right idea! Really, though, I admire him being good-natured and joking about it," someone else posted.

    Further reactions included: "You can see the look on his face afterwards; it’s like 'You know what, that’s not a bad idea. In for a penny, in for a pound,'" and "George thinking out loud."

    The United States Grand Prix kicks off the penultimate triple-header of the season and will take place on Sunday 25 October.
