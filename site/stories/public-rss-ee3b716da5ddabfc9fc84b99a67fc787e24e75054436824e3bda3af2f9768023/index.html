    奥迪上诉角田裕毅被驳回，意大利站第十名确认

    国际汽联国际上诉法院驳回了奥迪针对蒙扎站重启程序的上诉，角田裕毅保住意大利大奖赛第十名。奥迪认为角田在勒克莱尔严重事故后的静态重启中存在违规，应被处以时间处罚。

    奥迪针对角田裕毅的上诉被驳回——原因如下

    在奥迪就国际汽联国际上诉法院的重启程序提出质疑后，角田裕毅保住了意大利大奖赛第十名。

    蒙扎的F1比赛结束两个多月后，结果终于得到确认。奥迪认为，小红牛车手角田裕毅——在伊萨克·哈贾尔手腕受伤、利亚姆·劳森随后被提拔后获得替补出场机会——因潜在的重启违规应被处以时间处罚。

    在夏尔·勒克莱尔严重撞车后，速度神殿赛道进行了静态重启，角田裕毅起初驶向发车格左侧，因此是错误发车位。在较晚时刻调整回自己的实际发车位后，比赛增加了一圈编队圈。

    体育规则规定，造成额外编队圈的车手必须从维修区出口处发车。若未遵守，该违规行为可能被处以停车再起步（stop-and-go）处罚。在角田裕毅的情况下，他像往常一样从发车格起步，但赛后干事决定不再就该事件采取进一步行动。

    这足以让奥迪向国际上诉法院（ICA）提出申诉。在蒙扎，加布里埃尔·博尔托莱托和尼科·霍肯伯格分别以第11名和第12名完赛，排在角田裕毅之后；考虑到与哈斯争夺制造商锦标赛第七名，任何额外积分对这家四环品牌来说都将是受欢迎的。

    然而，奥迪肯定无法从蒙扎站获得那额外的一分。在卡塔尔比赛周末期间，国际上诉法院宣布，虽然奥迪最初的申诉请求被认定为“可受理”，但申诉本身随后被“以实体理由驳回”。

    对这一决定的完整解释需要更长时间才能公布。为求效率，国际上诉法院总是先传达其决定，完整理由仅在稍后阶段才会发布。

    国际上诉法院只是回溯国际汽联的原始裁决（第69号文件），该裁决仍被视为有效。在该文件中，赛会干事接受了小红牛的辩护，并补充说角田当时不可能知道自己被视为额外编队圈的原因：

    “此外，第B5.9.4条规定，如果造成额外编队圈的车手随后能够参加额外编队圈，他必须在该圈结束时进入维修区并从维修区起步。只有在此之后，如果车手未能这样做，才会施加强制性的停车再走处罚。

    “换言之，处罚是因为未能进入维修区并从那里起步。处罚不是因为造成额外编队圈，即使他确实是造成额外编队圈的原因。

    “这里出现了一个关键问题：为了遵守进入维修区并从维修区起步的要求，车手需要知道自己就是额外编队圈的原因。车队代表引用了因摄影师位置等外部情况而启动额外编队圈的案例。在本案中，没有向车队或车手发送任何消息，告知他们自己是额外编队圈的原因。

    “该车队基于赛车已按规定停在其发车格内这一事实，在我们看来，没有理由指示其车手进入维修区并从那里起步。

    “因此，无论该车手是否直接导致了额外的编队圈，该车队和车手都不知道他们被视为原因，所以我们认定在此案中无法施加停车再走处罚，并决定不再采取进一步行动。”

    Audi appeal against Yuki Tsunoda dismissed – here’s why

    Yuki Tsunoda retains his 10th place in the Italian Grand Prix after Audi challenged the restart procedure at the FIA’s International Court of Appeal

    More than two months after the Formula 1 race at Monza, the result has finally been confirmed. Audi believed that Racing Bulls driver Yuki Tsunoda – who was given the opportunity to stand in following Isack Hadjar's wrist injury and Liam Lawson's subsequent promotion – deserved a time penalty for a potential restart infringement.

    Following Charles Leclerc's heavy crash, there was a standing restart at the Temple of Speed, with Tsunoda initially heading towards the left side of the grid and therefore the wrong grid spot. After a late manoeuvre towards his actual grid spot, an extra formation lap followed.

    The sporting regulations stipulate that a driver who caused an extra formation lap must start from the end of the pitlane. Failure to do so could result in the offence being punished with a stop-and-go penalty. In Tsunoda's case, he started from the grid as normal, but the stewards decided to take no further action over the incident after the race.

    It was enough for Audi to challenge the matter at the International Court of Appeal (ICA). Gabriel Bortoleto and Nico Hulkenberg finished 11th and 12th behind Tsunoda at Monza, and given the battle with Haas for seventh place in the constructors' championship, any additional point would have been welcome for the brand with the four rings.

    However, Audi will definitely not gain that extra point from the Monza race. During the race weekend in Qatar, the International Court of Appeal announced that while Audi's initial request to appeal was deemed “admissible”, the appeal itself was subsequently “dismissed on the merits”.

    The full explanation of this decision will take longer to arrive. In the interests of expediency, the ICA always communicates its decision first, with the full reasoning only being released at a later stage.

    The ICA simply refers back to the FIA's original decision (document 69), which is still deemed valid. In that document, the stewards accepted Racing Bulls' defence and added that Tsunoda could not have known at the time that he was being considered the cause of the extra formation lap:

    “Further, Article B5.9.4 goes on to specify that if a driver who causes the extra formation lap and is then able to start the extra formation lap, he must enter the Pit Lane at the end of the lap and start from the Pit Lane. It is only then, if the driver fails to do this, that the mandatory penalty of a Stop and Go penalty is imposed.

    “In other words, the penalty is for failing to come into the Pit Lane and start from there. The penalty is not for causing the extra formation lap, if indeed, he caused the extra formation lap in the first place.

    “A critical issue emerges here, in that in order to comply with the requirement to enter and start from the Pit Lane, a driver would need to know that he was the cause of the extra formation lap. The team representative quoted situations where extra formation laps have been commenced due to extraneous circumstances such as the position of a photographer etc. In this case, no message was sent to the team or the driver, advising them that they were the cause of the extra formation lap.

    “The team, based on the fact that the car was positioned within its starting grid box in compliance with the regulations, had, in our determination, no reason to instruct its driver to enter and start from the Pit Lane.

    “Accordingly, irrespective of whether or not the driver directly caused the extra formation lap, the team and driver were unaware that they were being considered as the cause, therefore we determine that we are unable to impose a Stop and Go penalty in this case, and decide to take no further action.”
