    沃尔夫批评F1车手“自视应得”与无线电“无休止抱怨”

    梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫在年轻车手安东内利提前四站夺得世界冠军后，批评F1中“自视应得”的车手和无线电里“无休止的抱怨者”，认为新科世界冠军应成为他们的榜样。

    沃尔夫批评F1车手“自视应得”与无线电“无休止抱怨”

    这位梅赛德斯老板表示，新科世界冠军基米·安东内利应当成为榜样

    安德烈亚·基米·安东内利，梅赛德斯；托托·沃尔夫，梅赛德斯

    致梅赛德斯车迷。来自银箭车队的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手动态、赛车研发以及来自布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    在他的年轻车手基米·安东内利赢得一场令人惊叹的总冠军后，梅赛德斯车队领队托托·沃尔夫批评了一级方程式赛车中那些“自以为是”的车手以及“在无线电里无休止抱怨的人”。他认为今年的世界冠军应当成为他们的榜样。

    这位意大利车手在墨西哥城大奖赛上获得第三名，但这已足以让他在还剩四场比赛的情况下赢得总冠军，成为历史上最年轻的总冠军。

    赛后，沃尔夫在罗德里格斯兄弟赛道对媒体发言时，在发布会即将结束时要求补充最后一点，并借此论证安东内利的成功展示了一种不同的获胜方式。

    这位梅赛德斯老板没有点名他所指的任何车手。

    “既然大家都在这里，我再多说一句，因为我和那边的某个人讨论过这件事——实际上是和穆罕默德[本·苏拉耶姆]。这种不惜一切代价取胜、自私、投机、为自身利益冷酷无情的观念，那种赛车手模式已经消亡了。

    “我们有一位新的世界冠军，他代表着谦逊、正直、忠诚、尊重、做自己这样的价值观。他应该成为许多青训方程式年轻车手的榜样。也应该成为F1中少数自视甚高者的榜样——那些在无线电里没完没了抱怨的人、那些诉苦的人、那些撞车的人。

    “看看这个20岁的孩子，然后想想你能做些什么来提升自己。这就是给那些年轻人的信息。这就是国际汽联、穆罕默德、斯特凡诺[多梅尼卡利]——这就是我希望未来看到的赛车手的样子。这就是现代F1。基米代表着现代F1车手，因为那些价值观，而不仅仅因为他的天赋。天赋人人都有，但你也必须是个好人。

    “他已经证明，做一个好人，你同样能击败所有人。”

    沃尔夫还透露了他在比赛前夕对安东内利说过的话，谈及他未来的职业生涯，并引用了拉波·埃尔坎关于其祖父、已故菲亚特老板吉安尼·阿涅利的一句评论。

    “你知道我昨天对他说的话，是拉波·埃尔坎对我说的关于他祖父的事，说他能够一生都保持影响力。我认为这是一句很棒的话。

    “我对他说的是：我们必须一起努力，让你一生都保持影响力。你还有25年要走，因为你会比任何人都开得更久。我希望在这么多年后回顾时能说，你一生都保持了影响力。这不是昙花一现，而是我们共同努力，帮助他在能提升的地方进步，让他变得更好。

    “没有多少人能保持影响力。就连世界冠军也有不再保持影响力的，如今没人再找他们自拍了。我认为这就是我想和你一起实现的目标。”

    安东内利在第二个F1赛季中已赢得九场比赛，此前梅赛德斯在2025年提拔他接替刘易斯·汉密尔顿。

    Toto Wolff hits out at F1’s “entitled” drivers and “endless moaners on the radio”

    The Mercedes boss says new world champion Kimi Antonelli should be a role model

    Andrea Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, Toto Wolff, Mercedes

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    After a stunning championship win from his young driver Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff has criticised the "entitled" drivers and "endless moaners on the radio" in Formula 1. He believes this year's world champion should be a role model for them.

    The Italian finished third at the Mexico City Grand Prix, but that was enough for him to win the title with four races to spare, making him the youngest champion in history.

    Speaking to the media at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez after the race, Wolff asked to add one last point as the session was being brought to a close, and used it to argue that Antonelli’s success showed a different way of winning.

    The Mercedes boss did not name any of the drivers he was referring to.

    “Let me say one more word now that I have you all here, because I discussed it with someone else out there - with Mohammed [Ben Sulayem] actually. This notion of win at all costs, selfish, opportunistic, ruthless for your own benefit, that model of racing driver has died.

    "We have a new champion that represents values like humility, like integrity, loyalty, respect, being himself. Should be a role model for many other young drivers in junior formulas. And should be a role model for the few entitled ones in Formula 1, the endless moaners on the radio, the ones that complain, the ones that crash.

    "Look at this kid of 20 years and then think what you can do in order to improve. That’s the message to these kids. That is how the FIA, that Mohammed, that Stefano [Domenicali], that’s how I would want to see racing drivers in the future. That is modern Formula 1. Kimi represents the modern Formula 1 driver because of those values, not only because of his talent. That comes, but the talent is there with everyone, but you got to be a good person too.

    "He has demonstrated that you can beat everyone out there by being a good person.”

    Wolff also revealed what he had told Antonelli on the eve of the race about the career ahead of him, using a comment made by Lapo Elkann about his grandfather, the late Fiat boss Gianni Agnelli.

    “You know what I said to him yesterday is Lapo Elkann said to me about his granddad that he was able to stay relevant his whole life. And I think that’s a great thing to say.

    "And what I said to him is: together we have to try for you to stay relevant all life. You have 25 years to go because you’re going to drive longer than anybody else did before. And I want to look back in these many years and say, you stayed relevant all your life. It wasn’t a one-time wonder, but we all worked together in a way that we helped him to improve where he can improve, get him better.

    "There’s not many that stay relevant. Also world champions that's not stayed relevant that today, nobody’s taking a selfie of anymore. And I think this is what I would want to achieve with you.”

    Antonelli has won nine races in his second F1 season, having been promoted by Mercedes to replace Lewis Hamilton in 2025.
