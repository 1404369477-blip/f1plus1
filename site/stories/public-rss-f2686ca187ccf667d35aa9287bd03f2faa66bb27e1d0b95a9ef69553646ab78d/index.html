    勒克莱尔不满法拉利拉斯维加斯直道速度损失

    法拉利在拉斯维加斯大奖赛排位赛中两车均止步Q2，勒克莱尔对车队表现“非常失望”，并指出直道上损失了一秒。

    “我们在直道上损失了一秒”——查尔斯·勒克莱尔对法拉利在拉斯维加斯的“疯狂”短板感到不满

    勒克莱尔未能在内华达州的排位赛中进入Q3——他将原因归结为多种因素的叠加

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔对法拉利在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛上的表现“非常失望”，因为两辆赛车均在Q2被淘汰。

    勒克莱尔与队友刘易斯·汉密尔顿在第三次练习赛中分别排名第四和第八——那是唯一一节干地自由练习——但随后在排位赛中经历了更艰难的局面。

    这位摩纳哥车手在Q1中确实取得了第三名，而汉密尔顿在底板受损后仅列第13位，但在Q2中两人分别只能排在第11和第12位，这是法拉利自2021年比利时大奖赛以来并列最差的结果。

    法拉利因发动机功率低于对手而缺乏极速，这是SF-26表现不佳的一个突出因素，但勒克莱尔也提到了他在7号弯的锁死，以及总体上车队执行不力的问题。

    “整体上我非常失望，”这位摩纳哥车手说。“当然，Q2中的失误让我们付出了代价，但我认为这是在管理一切、交通和其他事情的方式上犯了很多错误后积累的许多挫败感所致。

    “这没有帮助，尤其是在这样一条一切都关乎轮胎的赛道上，你必须把轮胎带入正确的工作窗口。我更多是在看后视镜，试图自己理解交通状况；这让做任何事情都变得非常困难。”

    “我们在直道上损失了一秒，这太疯狂了；这是意料之中的，而这使得在最后阶段很难追回任何东西。这是本赛季对动力最敏感的赛道，所以我们预料到会很艰难，但我原本期望能进入Q3的。”

    尽管他澄清自己确实设法让轮胎进入了正确的工作窗口，勒克莱尔承认这对法拉利来说又是一个问题——也是他和汉密尔顿没有互相拉尾流的原因。

    “没有讨论过（拉尾流），”勒克莱尔说。“对我们来说，保持轮胎温度是最重要的，我们知道这是我们的弱点；即使在冷却圈，我们也必须推进以保持轮胎高温。

    “我不知道其他人是否面临和我们一样的问题，但当你遇到那个问题时，就很难等待两辆车并把握好时机，因为那要求你开得比你能做到的更慢，而对我们来说，让那些轮胎保持在正确温度是非常非常棘手的。”

    由于奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里因超出动力单元配额而被罚退10位，加之其他多位车手同样受罚，勒克莱尔和汉密尔顿将在周六的正赛中分别从第10位和第11位起步。

    “We lost one second down the straight” – Charles Leclerc upset with Ferrari’s “crazy” Las Vegas deficiency

    Leclerc didn’t make it to Q3 in the Nevadan qualifying session – and blamed a combination of factors

    Charles Leclerc is “very disappointed” with Ferrari’s performance at Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix as both cars were eliminated in Q2.

    Leclerc and team-mate Lewis Hamilton were fourth and eighth in FP3 - the only dry-weather free practice session - but later endured a tougher qualifying.

    The Monegasque did take third position in Q1 while Hamilton struggled to 13th after picking up floor damage, but in Q2 neither could do any better than 11th and 12th, marking Ferrari’s joint-worst result since the 2021 Belgian Grand Prix.

    Ferrari’s lack of top speed due to lower engine power than its rivals was a prominent factor in the SF-26’s poor performance, but Leclerc also mentioned his Turn 7 lock-up and, overall, poor execution from the Scuderia.

    “I'm very disappointed as a whole,” the Monegasque said. “Of course there's the mistake in Q2 that we paid the price of, but I think that is following a lot of frustration building up with a lot of mistakes with the way we managed everything, traffic and other things.

    “That doesn't help, particularly on a track like this where it's all about tyres, you’ve got to put tyres in the right window. I was more looking in my mirrors and trying to understand the traffic myself; that makes it very difficult to do anything.

    “We lost one second down the straight, which is crazy; it's expected, and that makes it very difficult to recover anything at the end. It's the most power-sensitive track of the season so we expected to have a hard time, but I was expecting to go through Q3, though.”

    Although he clarified that he did manage to put his tyres in the right window, Leclerc admitted that this had been another issue for Ferrari – and a reason why he and Hamilton did not tow one another.

    “[The tow] was not discussed,” Leclerc said. “Keeping just the temperature in the tyres was the most important for us, we know it's our weakness; even in the cool-down laps we've got to push to keep the tyres high.

    “I don't know if the others are facing the same issues as we do, but when you have that problem then it's very difficult to wait for the two cars and to time it well, because that requires you to go slower than what you can, and it's very, very tricky for us to keep those tyres in the right temperatures.”

    Leclerc and Hamilton will start the race from 10th and 11th on Saturday, courtesy of Oscar Piastri’s 10-place penalty for exceeding his power unit allocation amid many other grid drops.
