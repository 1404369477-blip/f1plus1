    '这是他的重大机会'——拉塞尔在蒙扎取得了理想的开局

    虽然拉塞尔肯定不会因为创造了周五的最快成绩而得意忘形，但显然他为本周末开了一个好头，此前他已经连续五站比赛未能夺得分站杆位和冠军，上一次还要追溯到奥地利大奖赛。

    天空体育F1的马丁·布伦德尔说：“他看起来状态很好。这是他在本周末缩小积分差距的大好机会，也是证明自己完全配得上一座世界冠军的机会。”

    “他最不希望看到的是，几站比赛之后被迫退居次要位置，去保护基米·安东内利，尤其是当迈凯伦或法拉利对他的积分领先优势发起猛攻的时候。”

    “现在这些比赛对乔治·拉塞尔来说至关重要。”

    梅赛德斯的安东内利在法拉利于意大利大奖赛练习赛中首次亮相新引擎后，谈了他对法拉利的看法。

    与此同时，安东内利知道自己的周末已经受到影响，因为他很可能会从周日22辆车的发车区的最后一排起步。因此，他在周五的训练安排更多地侧重于长距离节奏。

    “今天和平时相比有些不同，”安东内利说。

    “今天更专注于长距离测试，所以说实话感觉有点奇怪，但总的来说，今天是不错的一天。我们收集了大量数据，现在期待接下来几天的练习。”

    蒙扎的长直道提供了超车机会，安东内利表示他跟在其他赛车后面学到了很多。

    “这是一条可以做很多事情的赛道，”他补充道。

    “今天长距离测试中跟在其他赛车后面很有意思，可以看到他们有什么不同的做法，以及他们相比我们在哪里使用（能量或动力输出，deploy）。所以非常有趣。我们收集了大量数据，现在期待明天。”

    Italian GP: George Russell fastest in Practice Two as Mercedes edge ahead of fast-starting Ferrari at Monza

    George Russell starts a big weekend in which Kimi Antonelli takes a grid penalty by setting Friday's fastest time at The Temple of Speed; Charles Leclerc leads Ferrari in second; watch final practice at 11.30am on Saturday ahead of Qualifying at 3pm, live on Sky Sports F1

    Highlights from Friday's practice sessions at the Italian Grand Prix.

    George Russell beat Charles Leclerc to the fastest time of second practice at the Italian Grand Prix as Mercedes responded to Ferrari's early pace at Monza.

    At an event in which championship-leading team-mate Kimi Antonelli will start at the back of Sunday's grid owing to engine-change penalties, Russell has a big chance to eat into the Italian's 59-point advantage if he can deliver a strong race weekend.

    Ferrari had finished first and second quickest in opening practice as they debuted an upgraded engine but Russell was the driver to beat in the second session amid continued sweltering temperatures in northern Italy.

    The Briton set a quickest lap of 1:22.559 to outpace Leclerc - who had a late spin - by 0.120s and Antonelli by 0.141s.

    Charles Leclerc spins on the track during the second practice session at the Italian Grand Prix in Monza.

    Lewis Hamilton, second fastest in the earlier running, slipped to fifth behind McLaren's Lando Norris during a session which included the Ferrari running straight on at the first chicane.

    Norris has won the last two races but McLaren are expecting Monza's straights and slow chicanes to prove more challenging for their car. Fourth-fastest Norris was 0.384s off the second-practice pace with Oscar Piastri a further tenth behind in sixth.

    The world champion was angered by what he perceived to be "dangerous" driving by Aston Martin's Lance Stroll midway through the session when he was forced off track at the first chicane. That incident was placed under a post-session stewards' investigation.

    Lando Norris almost makes contact with Lance Stroll during P2 at the Italian GP.

    Max Verstappen returned after sitting out the first session for Red Bull rookie Ayumu Iwasa but the Dutchman was unhappy with his RB22's balance and was only able to set the ninth-fastest time. Stand-in team-mate Liam Lawson was 12th.

    Racing Bulls' Arvid Lindblad was the leading Red Bull-owned car in seventh place, with Haas' Oliver Bearman eighth in an upgraded VF-26 car.

    A downbeat Lando Norris reflects on McLaren’s frustrating lack of pace in Friday Practice.

    'This is his big chance' - Russell makes ideal start at Monza

    While he certainly will not be getting carried away by setting the Friday pace, Russell has clearly got his weekend off to a positive start ahead of his bids for a first Grand Prix pole and win since the Austrian Grand Prix five events ago.

    Sky Sports F1's Martin Brundle said: "He looked good. This is his big chance to close that deficit this weekend and show that he is very worthy of a world championship.

    "What he desperately doesn't want to have to do is fall into line a few races down the road and protect Kimi Antonelli if there's an onslaught coming from McLaren or Ferrari at his championship lead.

    "These are key races now for George Russell."

    Mercedes Kimi Antonelli gives his thoughts on Ferrari after they debuted a new engine at the Italian GP practice sessions.

    Antonelli, meanwhile, knows his weekend is already compromised given he will start from likely the back row of Sunday's 22-car grid. His programme on Friday was therefore more heavily weighted towards long-run pace.

    "Today a bit of a different day compared to usual," said Antonelli.

    "A lot more focused on long runs so it was a bit weird - not going to lie - but overall, it was a good day. We gathered a lot of data and now looking forward to the next few days."

    Monza's long straights offer overtaking opportunities and Antonelli said he learnt lots from following other cars.

    "It's a track where you can do so many different things," he added.

    "It was interesting in the long run today running behind other cars to see what they were doing differently, where they were deploying compared to us. So it was very interesting. We have gathered a lot of data and now we look forward to tomorrow."
