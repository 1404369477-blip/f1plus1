    从摩纳哥“被抢”到惊险杆位——加斯利能否赢下意大利大奖赛？

    皮埃尔·加斯利在失去摩纳哥大奖赛领奖台仅一天后，出人意料地在意大利大奖赛排位赛中夺得杆位。他将于周日与乔治·拉塞尔并排发车，向蒙扎的胜利发起冲击。

    皮埃尔·加斯利在失去摩纳哥大奖赛领奖台仅一天后出人意料地夺得杆位；加斯利将与乔治·拉塞尔（拉塞尔）并肩发车，这可能是一场激动人心的比赛；周日下午2点通过天空体育 F1观看意大利大奖赛直播，下午12点30分开始赛前预热节目

    2026年意大利大奖赛排位赛集锦

    加斯利 在周五感到自己被“抢走了”一些东西，因为他失去了6月摩纳哥大奖赛的领奖台，但随后他以最惊人的方式实现了逆转，为意大利大奖赛夺得了杆位。

    就在FIA国际上诉法院恢复了Gasly因维修区超速处罚的第二天，这位阿尔派车手以他的首个F1杆位强势回击。

    这是F1历史上最大的冷门之一，而且在距离他赢得迄今唯一一场胜利（同样是在蒙扎）六年之后，Gasly现在有机会为他认为周五遭受的错误判罚讨回公道。

    "我在F1已经九年了，一直努力在每个周末都做到最好，但并不是每次我们都能有一辆足以争夺杆位的车,"加斯利说道。

    “今天就是那个机会了，是的，我真的非常、非常开心，非常自豪能抓住这个机会并把它变成现实。那可真是了不起的一圈。”

    是“额外的动力”助加斯利拿下杆位吗？

    加斯利在车内车外一向情绪外露，但在得知迈凯伦和红牛对摩纳哥比赛结果提出的上诉导致他的两次因超速被判的五秒时间处罚被恢复后，他勃然大怒。

    今年六月，阿尔派曾成功赢得了复审权，处罚被撤销，因为他们证明加斯利并非超速，原因是维修区通道距离的测量有误。

    加斯利对Canal+表示“我们被抢了”，并说“我认为这事不会就这么结束”，而阿尔派领队弗拉维奥·布里亚托利则质疑了卫冕车队冠军与国际汽联国际上诉法院（ICA）一名法官之间的关系。

    ICA于周六回应了布里亚托利的言论，并表示他们“对程序的严密性和法官的公正性充满信心”。

    迈凯伦车队领队安德烈亚·斯特拉对弗拉维奥·布里亚托利的反应感到不满，该裁决意味着在两次时间处罚被重新执行后，阿尔派 车队的 加斯利 失去了摩纳哥大奖赛第三名的成绩

    尽管如此，这无疑激起了 Gasly 的斗志，他做出了其九年 F1 生涯中最出色的单圈之一，并承认关于上诉的情况“不令人愉快”，但他试图将其加以利用

    “我试着把一切都能利用的东西转化为积极的东西，转化为额外的动力，但我觉得这个周末如果没有这件事会更好。我只想说这么多，”他说。

    阿尔派车队常务董事史蒂夫·尼尔森在接受天空体育F1采访时补充道："想想我们在收到摩纳哥站的比赛结果时车队里的气氛，说实话这件事会让我懊恼一辈子，而用杆位来强势回归真是太好了。这很美妙。"

    一起来看看蒙扎排位赛的收官阶段，皮埃尔·加斯利夺得了杆位

    多位车手向加斯利的杆位表示祝贺，其中包括乔治·拉塞尔，他将在周日的正赛中从头排发车。

    拉塞尔透露，排位赛前他正在和加斯利下国际象棋，结果这位阿尔派车手在那盘棋里也赢了他。

    “排位赛开始前大约15分钟我们刚结束一盘棋，他赢了我，我当时想，‘好吧，我想排位赛里我会赢你，’结果现在，他又排在我前面了，”他说。

    “所以，是的，向他表示巨大的祝贺。皮埃尔和阿尔派从Q1开始就一直非常快。”

    皮埃尔·加斯利对在意大利大奖赛上意外获得个人首个杆位作出反应

    加斯利目前在F1中唯一的分站胜利来自2020年意大利大奖赛——那是在他挚友安托万·于贝尔去世一年后，而现在他又迎来了一个冲击第二场胜利的意外机会。

    阿尔派赛车在蒙扎的直道上速度极快，这得益于梅赛德斯动力单元的优势，但车队还拥有一辆空气动力学性能出色、低阻力的赛车。

    蒙扎是一条对能量需求极高的赛道，因此预计会出现不同的动力单元部署策略，这将导致我们今年早些时候看到的那种“悠悠球”式的攻防超车场面。

    这可能会让加斯利有机会把拉塞尔挡在身后，或者如果这位梅赛德斯车手在前面，就紧咬住他。

    梅赛德斯车手乔治·拉塞尔对在意大利大奖赛排位赛中以微弱差距不敌皮埃尔·加斯利、错失杆位作出反应

    Italian GP: Can Pierre Gasly win Monza race for Alpine just days after losing Monaco podium?

    Pierre Gasly claimed a shock pole position just one day after losing his Monaco Grand Prix podium; Gasly starts alongside George Russell for what could be a thrilling race; watch the Italian Grand Prix live on Sunday at 2pm on Sky Sports F1, with build-up from 12.30pm

    Highlights from qualifying for the 2026 Italian Grand Prix

    Pierre Gasly felt he was "robbed" on Friday after losing his podium from June's Monaco Grand Prix but turned it around in the most remarkable fashion with pole position for the Italian Grand Prix.

    Just one day after the FIA International Court of Appeal reinstated Gasly's pit lane speeding penalties, the Alpine driver hit back with his maiden Formula 1 pole.

    It is one of the biggest shocks in the history of F1 and, six years after winning his only race to date, also coming at Monza, Gasly has the opportunity to avenge, what he feels, was a wrong judgement call on Friday.

    "It's been nine years I'm in Formula 1, trying to, give my best absolutely every single weekend and it's not every time we get a car to fight for pole position," said Gasly.

    "Today it was the case and, yeah, I'm really, really happy, really proud to have seized this chance and to make it work. It was a heck of a lap."

    Mercedes driver George Russell reacts to narrowly missing out on pole position to Pierre Gasly during qualifying at the Italian Grand Prix

    Did 'extra motivation' power Gasly to pole?

    Gasly has always shown plenty of emotion in and out of the car but was furious after finding out McLaren and Red Bull's appeals of the Monaco race result led to the return of his two five-second time penalties for speeding.

    In June, Alpine had successfully won a right of review that saw the penalties removed as they proved Gasly was not speeding due to an incorrect measurement of the pit lane distance.

    Gasly told Canal+ that "we're being robbed" and "I don't think this is going to end here" while Alpine chief Flavio Briatore questioned the relationship between the reigning constructors' champions and a judge at the FIA's International Court of Appeal (ICA).

    The ICA responded to Briatore's comments on Saturday and stated they are "confident in the soundness of the proceedings and the integrity of the judges".

    McLaren's Team Principal Andrea Stella was not happy with Flavio Briatore's reaction to the ruling which meant Alpine's Pierre Gasly lost his Monaco GP third place finish after his two time penalties were reapplied

    Nevertheless it certainly fired Gasly up and he produced one of the best laps in his nine-year F1 career, admitting the situation regarding the appeal was "not nice" but something he tried to use.

    "I try to use everything I can into something positive and, extra motivation, but I think I could have dealt without it this weekend. That's all I'll say," he said.

    Alpine managing director Steve Nielsen added to Sky Sports F1: "When you think what the mood was in the camp when we got the result from Monaco, which honestly is going to annoy me for the rest of my life, it's a nice way to bounce back with pole position. It's lovely."

    Take a look at closing stages of the qualifying at Monza as Pierre Gasly took pole

    Several drivers congratulated Gasly on his pole, including George Russell who will start on the front row in Sunday's race.

    Russell revealed he was playing chess with Gasly before qualifying and the Alpine driver also beat him in their game.

    "We were actually finishing a game of chess about 15 minutes before qualifying and he beat me there, and I thought, 'Well, I think I'll beat you in qualifying,' and here we are, he's ahead of me again," he said.

    "So, yeah, huge congrats to him. Pierre and Alpine have been super-fast even from Q1."

    Pierre Gasly reacts to a shock first pole position at Italian GP

    Gasly's only win so far in F1 came at the 2020 Italian Grand Prix - one year after his close friend Anthoine Hubert died and now he has another unexpected chance for a second victory.

    The Alpine has been very quick in the straights in Monza thanks to the benefit of a Mercedes power unit, but the team also have a low-drag car which is strong aerodynamically.

    Monza is an energy-rich track, so there is expected to be different power unit deployment strategies at play which will lead to the yoyo style of racing we saw earlier this year.

    It may give Gasly a chance to keep Russell behind, or stick with him if the Mercedes driver is ahead.
