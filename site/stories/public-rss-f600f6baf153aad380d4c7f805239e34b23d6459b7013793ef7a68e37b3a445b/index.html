    拉尔夫·舒马赫：汉密尔顿对勒克莱尔玩心理战

    拉尔夫·舒马赫认为，拉斯维加斯大奖赛队友碰撞后，汉密尔顿试图在心理上击垮勒克莱尔，让人想起当年汉密尔顿与罗斯伯格之间的“心理战”。

    拉尔夫·舒马赫认为，拉斯维加斯大奖赛之后，汉密尔顿对勒克莱尔的举动让人想起当年汉密尔顿与罗斯伯格之间的“心理战”。

    拉尔夫·舒马赫指责汉密尔顿在拉斯维加斯大奖赛的碰撞后试图在心理上击垮勒克莱尔。

    致法拉利车迷。来自马拉内罗及更广范围的最新法拉利F1新闻、车手动态、技术分析和比赛反应。

    赛事干事和法拉利车队领队弗雷德里克·瓦塞尔将法拉利最近这次队内碰撞的责任归于夏尔·勒克莱尔。但前F1车手拉尔夫·舒马赫认为，马拉内罗真正的权力博弈来自车库的另一侧。

    在接受德国天空体育《Backstage Boxengasse》播客采访时（据德国媒体sport.de报道），这位51岁的评论员对刘易斯·汉密尔顿和勒克莱尔在拉斯维加斯大奖赛7号弯末段纠缠后不断升级的摩擦给出了直率的评判。

    “刘易斯·汉密尔顿完全是在压制查尔斯·勒克莱尔，”舒马赫说。“他想不惜一切代价夺取一号车手的权力。他想让勒克莱尔受到打击——他想在心理上摧毁他。”

    从纸面上看，拉斯维加斯的情况似乎一目了然。勒克莱尔在7号弯从内线冲向汉密尔顿，撞到队友导致其打转，并因此被罚10秒，名次跌至第10，而汉密尔顿则恢复到第七——这促使瓦塞尔在赛后立即召见了这位摩纳哥车手。

    查尔斯·勒克莱尔，法拉利，刘易斯·汉密尔顿，法拉利

    尽管舒马赫同意勒克莱尔犯了主要错误，但他认为汉密尔顿在让碰撞发生方面扮演了精心算计的角色。

    “仔细观察可以发现，汉密尔顿没有让行，还假装没注意到勒克莱尔，”舒马赫说。“我认为那是故意的，因为比赛对他来说已经结束了。”

    舒马赫还看到了汉密尔顿与尼科·罗斯伯格在梅赛德斯争冠时的影子——在德国被称为“星球大战”时代——那在2014年斯帕以及2016年西班牙和奥地利再度升级为碰撞。

    “尼科·罗斯伯格至今也乐此不疲地谈论这些心理战，”舒马赫补充道。“这说明今年对他们两人造成了什么影响。”

    为何法拉利2026年的转变引发了关于车队内部层级关系的争论

    舒马赫的警告发出之际，围场内正日益激烈地争论：在墨西哥城和拉斯维加斯的三轮比赛中车手两次发生冲突后，法拉利是否必须确立明确的队内顺位。1996年世界冠军达蒙·希尔在社交媒体上敦促马拉内罗公开支持汉密尔顿作为其头号车手后，汉密尔顿在内华达州直接予以反驳。

    “我从未要求过成为一号车手。我真的从来没有提过，”汉密尔顿对记者说。不过，他承认在2026年早些时候——当时他在巴塞罗那-加泰罗尼亚大奖赛的胜利使他在积分上领先勒克莱尔40分，成为最接近安东内利的挑战者——在赞德沃特等分站获得车队支持本可以帮助法拉利对抗梅赛德斯，并补充说队友相撞“本就不该发生”。

    法拉利内部竞争力的变化有助于解释为何紧张情绪加剧。在2025年身披红色战袍的首个赛季中大幅落后于勒克莱尔（156分对242分）之后，汉密尔顿在第二个法拉利赛季的最后两轮比赛前领先队友17分（267分对250分）。

    两位车手在2026年各获得一场大奖赛胜利——汉密尔顿在巴塞罗那，勒克莱尔在银石——汉密尔顿以七比六的领奖台数略占优势，并在墨西哥城为法拉利拿下唯一一个杆位，而勒克莱尔在21个共同参赛的分站中仍以13比8领先排位赛交锋。

    勒克莱尔对他在拉斯维加斯的罚时承担全部责任，并承认"为车队感到相当尴尬"，而汉密尔顿坚称他一直只专注于追赶迈凯伦，而不是与队友缠斗。

    随着卡塔尔大奖赛于周五（11月27日至29日）开赛，下周将在阿布扎比（12月4日至6日）迎来收官战，法拉利几乎没有时间证明其明星车手组合能够共存而不互相毁掉彼此的比赛。

    Ralf Schumacher sees echoes of Lewis Hamilton's Nico Rosberg "mind games" after Las Vegas

    Ralf Schumacher accuses Lewis Hamilton of trying to mentally break Charles Leclerc after their Las Vegas GP clash

    For Ferrari fans. The latest Scuderia Ferrari F1 news, driver updates, technical analysis and race reaction from Maranello and beyond.

    Stewards and Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur placed the blame for the Scuderia’s latest intra-team collision on Charles Leclerc. Former Formula 1 driver Ralf Schumacher, however, believes the real power play at Maranello is coming from the other side of the garage.

    Speaking on Sky Sports Germany’s Backstage Boxengasse podcast, as reported by German outlet sport.de, the 51-year-old analyst delivered a blunt verdict on the escalating friction between Lewis Hamilton and Leclerc following their late Turn 7 tangle at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

    "Lewis Hamilton is completely showing up Charles Leclerc," Schumacher said. "He wants to seize number one power by any means necessary. He wants Leclerc to take a hit - he wants to mentally destroy him."

    On paper, Las Vegas looked cut-and-dried. Leclerc lunged down Hamilton’s inside at Turn 7, clipped his team-mate into a spin, and took a 10-second time penalty that dropped him to 10th while Hamilton recovered to seventh - prompting Vasseur to summon the Monegasque driver immediately afterwards.

    Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari

    While Schumacher agreed that Leclerc committed the main mistake, he argued Hamilton played a calculated role in letting the contact happen.

    "Upon closer inspection, you can see that Hamilton did not give way and pretended not to have noticed Leclerc," Schumacher said. "I assume that was intentional, because the race was already over for him."

    Schumacher also saw echoes of Hamilton’s Mercedes championship battles with Nico Rosberg - known in Germany as the "Star Wars" era - which boiled over into collisions at Spa in 2014 and again in Spain and Austria in 2016.

    "Nico Rosberg also never tires of talking about these mind games today," Schumacher added. "That shows what this year has done to the two of them."

    Why Ferrari’s 2026 turnaround has fuelled the hierarchy debate

    Schumacher’s warning lands amid growing paddock debate over whether Ferrari must establish a clear pecking order after its drivers clashed twice in three rounds across Mexico City and Las Vegas. After 1996 World Champion Damon Hill urged Maranello on social media to back Hamilton outright as its lead driver, Hamilton pushed back directly in Nevada.

    "I've never asked to be number one. I genuinely haven't ever asked," Hamilton told reporters. Still, he conceded that earlier in 2026 - when his victory at the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix put him 40 points ahead of Leclerc as Kimi Antonelli’s closest challenger - team support at rounds such as Zandvoort could have helped Ferrari take on Mercedes, adding that team-mates colliding "should just never happen".

    The competitive swing inside Ferrari helps explain why nerves are frayed. After trailing Leclerc heavily in his debut red campaign in 2025 (156 points to 242), Hamilton enters the final two rounds of his second Ferrari season 17 points ahead of his team-mate (267 to 250).

    Both drivers hold one grand prix win apiece in 2026 - Hamilton in Barcelona and Leclerc at Silverstone - with Hamilton edging podiums seven to six and claiming Ferrari’s lone pole position in Mexico City, while Leclerc retains a 13–eight qualifying edge across 21 shared rounds.

    Leclerc accepted full responsibility for his Las Vegas penalty and admitted feeling "quite embarrassed for the team", while Hamilton maintained he had been focused solely on catching McLaren rather than fighting the sister car.

    With the Qatar Grand Prix getting under way on Friday (27-29 November) ahead of next week’s finale in Abu Dhabi (4-6 December), Ferrari has little time left to prove its star pairing can coexist without tearing each other’s races apart.
