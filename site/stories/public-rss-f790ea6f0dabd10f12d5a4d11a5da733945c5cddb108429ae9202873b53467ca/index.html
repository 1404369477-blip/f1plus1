    小红牛车队确认利亚姆·劳森将在2027年F1赛季继续留队，与阿尔维德·林德布拉德搭档第二年。

    劳森和林德布拉德今年组成了强大的搭档，推动小红牛车队在制造商锦标赛中升至第五名，这将成为这支英意车队有史以来的最佳成绩。

    但尽管贡献了89分，是新人林德布拉德的两倍，劳森的未来仍被蒙上阴影，因为小红牛在短期表现需求与为母队红牛车队培养年轻车手的明确目标之间进行权衡。

    红牛拥有令人兴奋的保加利亚年轻新秀尼古拉·措洛夫，他是F2的领跑者，正在等待机会，红牛已给予他私人测试机会以及在墨西哥的一节新秀FP1练习赛。

    但随着劳森近几周挺身而出，在持续压力下交出一些他最强劲的比赛，这位24岁的新西兰人表现太过出色，让车队无法忽视。消息人士在巴西大奖赛周末向Motorsport.com透露，在公司高层大力推动支持措洛夫的背景下，随着劳森在美洲三连赛中的表现，天平又摆回了有利于劳森的一边。

    劳森现已签下一份新合同，将在2027年继续留在车队，而林德布拉德在经历了一个充满希望的新秀赛季后获得第二个赛季的机会。

    车队领队艾伦·佩尔曼表示，他“毫不怀疑”车队下赛季将派出尽可能最强的车手阵容。

    艾伦·佩尔曼，小红牛，利亚姆·劳森，小红牛

    “我们很高兴能在2027年继续与利亚姆和阿尔维德合作，”佩尔曼在周四上午宣布。“两人在整个赛季中都展现了各自的能力和潜力，我们也看到了他们取得的进步，无论是个人的还是作为搭档的。我毫不怀疑这是我们最好的车手阵容，带来了经验与雄心的强有力结合。我们将携手在今年的进步基础上夯实基础，继续推动自己向前，迈出下一步，朝着在2027年实现我们目标的方向前进。”

    劳森补充道：“我非常高兴明年能继续留在 Visa Cash App 小红牛 车队。我们一起度过了一个出色的赛季，车队非常努力地打造了一辆快车，我很喜欢驾驶它。能成为车队这个历史性赛季的一部分，感觉棒极了，我们正在继续为车队积分榜第五名而战。这证明了赛道现场和工厂里每个人付出的辛勤努力。”

    “我期待与阿尔维德一起把这种势头延续到 2027 年；我们正在打造非常积极的东西，我对我们能共同取得的成就感到兴奋。”

    林德布拉德表示，他的首个 F1 赛季感觉“非常特别”，他希望在 2027 年在此基础上继续发展。

    这位英国车手说：“回顾我在一级方程式的第一个赛季，这是真正特别的一年，从第一次季前测试开始就充满了新的经历和收获。我迫不及待想开始明年的比赛，在新秀赛季学到的一切基础上继续前进。这将是令人兴奋的一年，我期待继续与利亚姆并肩驾驶。”

    Liam Lawson retained by Racing Bulls for 2027 alongside Arvid Lindblad

    There had been speculation surrounding Lawson's future with the team, with F2 star Nikola Tsolov tipped as a replacement.

    Arvid Lindblad, Racing Bulls, Liam Lawson, Racing Bulls

    Racing Bulls has confirmed Liam Lawson will stay with the team for the 2027 Formula 1 season, partnering Arvid Lindblad for a second year.

    Lawson and Lindblad formed a formidable duo this year as they propelled Racing Bulls to fifth in the constructors' championship, which is set to be the Anglo-Italian team's best-ever result.

    But despite accounting for 89 points, twice the number rookie Lindblad managed, Lawson's future had been thrown into doubt as Racing Bulls weighed up its need for short-term performance with its explicit goal of training young drivers for its parent team Red Bull Racing.

    Red Bull has the exciting young Bulgarian prospect Nikola Tsolov, a frontrunner in F2, waiting in the wings, having handed him private testing opportunities and a rookie FP1 session in Mexico.

    But with Lawson rising to the occasion in recent weeks, delivering some of his strongest races under constant pressure, the 24-year-old New Zealander has been too good for the team to ignore. Sources indicated to Motorsport.com over the Brazilian Grand Prix weekend that amid a big push for the company's top leadership to back Tsolov, the pendulum swung back towards Lawson's favour with his performances over the Americas triple-header.

    Lawson has now signed a fresh deal that will keep him at the team for 2027, with Lindblad afforded a second season after a promising rookie campaign.

    Team boss Alan Permane said he had "no doubt" the team will field the strongest possible line-up next season.

    Alan Permane, Racing Bulls, Liam Lawson, Racing Bulls

    "We’re delighted to be continuing with Liam and Arvid for 2027," Permane announced on Thursday morning. "Both have demonstrated their ability and potential throughout the season, and we’ve seen the progress they’ve made, both individually and as a pairing. I have no doubt that this is the best driver line-up for us, bringing a strong combination of experience and ambition. Together, we have a solid foundation to build on the progress made this year, continue pushing ourselves forward and take the next step towards achieving our ambitions in 2027."

    Lawson added: "I’m very happy to be continuing with Visa Cash App Racing Bulls next year. We’ve had a brilliant season together and the team has worked very hard to produce a fast car, which I’ve loved driving. It feels great to be part of a historic season for the team as we continue our fight for P5 in the Constructors' Championship. It's a testament to the hard work of everyone both at track, and back at the factory.

    "I’m looking forward to carrying this momentum forward with Arvid through to 2027; we’re building something super positive and I’m excited by what we can achieve together.”

    Lindblad said his maiden F1 campaign felt "truly special" as he aims to build on a solid foundation in 2027.

    "Looking back on my first season in Formula 1, it's been a truly special year, full of new experiences and learnings since the very first pre-season test," the Briton said. "I can't wait to get started next year, building on everything I've learned during my rookie season. It's set to be an exciting year, and I look forward to continuing to drive alongside Liam."
