    梅赛德斯维修区承认：法拉利的糟糕表现缓解了圣保罗站的冠军压力

    梅赛德斯领导层承认，法拉利在巴西的糟糕表现缓解了确保车队冠军的所有压力

    对于梅赛德斯车队的粉丝来说，这里是关于银箭的最新消息，包括F1新闻、车手更新、赛车发展和从布拉克利和布里克斯沃思的赛事分析。

    当在英特拉戈斯下雨时，通常会有混乱。对于梅赛德斯来说，2026年圣保罗大奖赛是一场混乱的比赛。这个位于布拉克利的车队来到巴西，需要执行一场干净的赛事来正式结束车队冠军争夺，但很快发现自己在一个难以预测的湿滑赛道上作斗争。

    乔治·拉塞尔和刚刚获得车手冠军的基米·安东内利在比赛中都遭遇了打滑，这导致驾驶舱内环境变得紧张。但梅赛德斯维修区墙上，对冠军头衔的推测却出奇地清晰。

    在NU银箭广播秀播客节目中，梅赛德斯技术总监詹姆斯·阿利森和副车队总监布拉德利·洛德透露，车队从未感到有压力要保守地关注积分。原因很简单：他们最接近的对手法拉利在车队排名下游的表现灾难性。

    当被问及维修区是否曾不得不将注意力严格转向锦标赛数学时，阿利森打断了对马拉内洛车队速度的直率评价。

    “此外，法拉利这场比赛表现糟糕；即使我们面前的戏剧性场面也没有在任何阶段让红色赛车看起来会给我们带来麻烦，”阿利森表示。

    虽然刘易斯·汉密尔顿在他的法拉利车上从第20位发车，实现了精彩的起步，但车队在复杂条件下速度很快下降。来自马拉内洛的持续威胁不足让梅赛德斯车队可以完全专注于自己的恢复赛程。

    洛德立即支持了阿利森的评估，指出梅赛德斯策略师从未真正担心失去对制造商冠军的控制。

    “是的，刘易斯有那个惊人的起步……[但是]他们似乎在进步然后又退步，你看到他们超车然后又被超车，”洛德解释说。“所以我认为在整个比赛中，我们实际上在任何时候都不会失去冠军，因为我们始终在赛道上领先法拉利。”

    最终的排名完全证实了维修区轻松的态度。虽然兰多·诺里斯为麦克拉伦赢得了胜利，但梅赛德斯车队成功确保了2026年的制造商冠军，罗素以第四名结束比赛，安东内利恢复到第五名。

    与此同时，法拉利只收获了微薄的积分。查尔斯·勒克莱尔以第八名的成绩冲过终点线，比第九名的汉密尔顿高一位。斯库德里亚未能充分利用梅赛德斯混乱的赛事，最终让托托·沃尔夫的队伍在剩余三轮比赛中锁定了冠军。

    Mercedes pitwall admits floundering Ferraris relieved title pressure in São Paulo

    Mercedes leadership admits Ferrari's "rubbish race" in Brazil relieved all the pressure of securing the Constructors' title

    For Mercedes fans. All the latest from the Silver Arrows, with F1 news, driver updates, car development and race analysis from Brackley and Brixworth.

    When the rain falls at Interlagos, chaos usually follows. For Mercedes, the 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix was a turbulent affair. The Brackley-based squad arrived in Brazil needing to execute a clean race to officially wrap up the constructors' championship, but quickly found themselves battling a highly unpredictable wet-dry track.

    Both George Russell and newly crowned drivers' champion Kimi Antonelli suffered spins during the race, leading to a stressful environment inside the cockpits. But on the Mercedes pitwall, the focus remained surprisingly clear of title permutations.

    Speaking on the NU Silver Arrows Radio Show podcast, Mercedes technical director James Allison and deputy team principal Bradley Lord revealed that the team never felt pressured to conservatively points-watch. The reason was simple: their closest rival, Ferrari, was putting in a disastrous performance further down the order.

    When asked if the pitwall ever had to shift its focus strictly to the championship maths, Allison interjected with a blunt assessment of the Maranello outfit's pace.

    "Also, Ferrari were having such a rubbish race; even our dramas in front of them were not really making it look like, at any stage, the red cars were going to trouble us," Allison stated.

    While Lewis Hamilton managed a brilliant initial launch from 20th on the grid in his Ferrari, the team's pace quickly evaporated in the tricky conditions. The lack of a sustained threat from Maranello allowed Mercedes to focus entirely on its own recovery drives.

    Lord immediately backed up Allison's assessment, noting that the Mercedes strategists were never genuinely concerned about losing their grip on the constructors' crown.

    "Yeah, Lewis had that amazing start... [but] they were sort of making progress and then falling back, and you saw them overtaking and then being passed again," Lord explained. "So I don't think there was any point in the race where we actually wouldn't have taken the championship regardless, because we were always ahead of the Ferraris on the road."

    The final classification completely validated the pitwall's relaxed stance. While Lando Norris took the victory for McLaren, Mercedes successfully secured the 2026 constructors' championship with Russell finishing fourth and Antonelli recovering to fifth.

    Meanwhile, Ferrari walked away with a meagre points haul. Charles Leclerc crossed the line in eighth place, just one position ahead of Hamilton in ninth. The Scuderia's inability to capitalise on Mercedes' scrappy race ultimately allowed Toto Wolff's squad to seal the title with three rounds to spare.
