    法拉利与万宝龙推出4300美元限量钢笔

    法拉利与万宝龙合作推出Meisterstuck Great Masters 法拉利 Tailor Made Inedita特别版钢笔，在美国售价4300美元，灵感来自法拉利的定制超级跑车。

    法拉利发布售价4300美元、灵感来自其定制超级跑车的奢华钢笔

    Meisterstuck Great Masters 法拉利 Tailor Made Inedita的价格几乎是普通万宝龙大班系列钢笔的四倍。

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    你不需要买一辆法拉利才能为跃马花大钱。万宝龙与马拉内罗的最新合作以一支钢笔的形式呈现，它的价格比一些二手车还贵。

    这款万宝龙大班系列大师杰作法拉利Tailor Made Inedita特别版在美国售价为4300美元。如果你不想处理这种钢笔麻烦的重新上墨问题，还有一款4000美元的走珠笔，而法拉利自己的商店出售一款4000美元的圆珠笔。

    作为对比，万宝龙经典镀金大班149钢笔——该品牌最受欢迎的产品之一——在其美国网站上标价为1105美元。

    那么多出的约3000美元能给你带来什么？答案是碳纤维。

    这款笔的灵感来自法拉利Tailor Made，这是一个个性化定制项目，让车主选择自己汽车的颜色、材料和饰面。它分为三个主题：Classica、Inedita和Scuderia，其中Inedita是三者中最具实验性的，采用不寻常的材料组合打造。

    这就解释了碳纤维笔帽和笔杆的材质，其中织入了蓝色线材。金属配件采用亮黑色PVD涂层处理，笔杆上饰有一枚精美的铝制跃马标志，笔夹则采用特殊造型，取代了通常的大班系列设计。

    笔尖由实心18K金手工打造，并覆有钌涂层，而笔帽顶部的万宝龙标志则由Azzurro Dino树脂制成。据法拉利介绍，这种蓝色调是1969年法拉利Dino 246的颜色，该车型以恩佐·法拉利的儿子阿尔弗雷多·“迪诺”·法拉利命名。

    如果你想要与这支笔配套的完整套装，那就得再多掏些钱。法拉利还出售配套的Inedita记事本，售价185美元，以及笔盒，售价595美元。把这些与钢笔和圆珠笔加在一起，整套系列的总价略高于9000美元。

    Inedita是与法拉利设计工作室及Tailor Made部门共同开发的第二款笔。第一款是Classica，于2025年推出，其设计灵感源自法拉利20世纪50年代和60年代的grand touring跑车。它的售价同样是4300美元。

    但即便是 Inedita，也不是万宝龙打造过的最贵法拉利钢笔。钛金属材质的 Stilema SP3 钢笔灵感来自查尔斯·勒克莱尔和卡洛斯·塞恩斯都拥有的 Daytona SP3 超级跑车，限量仅 599 支，目前在法拉利官方商店以 27,500 美元出售。

    那支笔的笔帽、笔尖和上墨机构采用白金材质，并配有一片 Rosso Corsa 红色树脂。

    相比之下，4,300 美元听起来几乎算合理了。几乎。

    Ferrari reveals luxury $4,300 fountain pen inspired by its bespoke supercars

    The Meisterstuck Great Masters Ferrari Tailor Made Inedita costs almost four times as much as a regular Montblanc Meisterstuck.

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    You do not need to buy a Ferrari to spend serious money on the Prancing Horse. Montblanc’s latest collaboration with Maranello has come in the shape of a fountain pen, and it costs more than some used cars.

    The Meisterstuck Great Masters Ferrari Tailor Made Inedita Special Edition is for sale for $4,300 in the United States. If you would rather not deal with the messy reloading of such a pen, there is also a $4,000 rollerball, and Ferrari’s own store sells a $4,000 ballpoint.

    For comparison, Montblanc’s classic gold-coated Meisterstuck 149 fountain pen, one of the most popular products from the brand, is listed at $1,105 on its US website.

    So what does the extra $3,000 or so get you? The answer is carbon fibre.

    The pen is inspired by Ferrari Tailor Made, the personalisation programme that lets owners choose the colours, materials and finishes of their cars. It is split into three themes, Classica, Inedita and Scuderia, with Inedita the most experimental of the three, built with unusual combinations of materials.

    That explains the carbon fibre cap and barrel, which have blue thread woven through them. The metal fittings are finished in glossy black PVD, with a beautiful aluminium Prancing Horse on the barrel and a specially shaped clip replacing the usual Meisterstuck design.

    The nib of the pen is handcrafted from solid 18-carat gold and coated in ruthenium, while the Montblanc emblem on top of the cap is made from Azzurro Dino resin. According to Ferrari, the blue shade is the colour of the 1969 Ferrari Dino 246, named after Enzo Ferrari’s son, Alfredo “Dino” Ferrari.

    If you want the full set to go with the pen, open your wallet wider. Ferrari also sells a matching Inedita notepad at $185 and a pen case at $595. Add those to the fountain pen and ballpoint, and you are looking at just over $9,000 for the whole collection.

    The Inedita is the second pen developed with the Ferrari Design Studio alongside Tailor Made. The first, the Classica, came in 2025 and channels the designs of Ferrari’s grand touring cars of the 1950s and 1960s. It costs the same $4,300.

    But even the Inedita is not the most expensive Ferrari pen Montblanc has made. The titanium Stilema SP3 fountain pen, inspired by the incredible Daytona SP3 hypercar owned by both Charles Leclerc and Carlos Sainz and limited to just 599 pieces, is for sale on Ferrari’s store at $27,500.

    That pen features white gold on the cap, nib and filling mechanism, along with a blade of Rosso Corsa resin.

    Compared with that, $4,300 almost sounds reasonable. Almost.
