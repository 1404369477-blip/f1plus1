    皮亚斯特里悼念澳大利亚F1传奇阿兰·琼斯

    1980年F1世界冠军、澳大利亚车手阿兰·琼斯在短暂患病后于80岁去世，迈凯伦车手皮亚斯特里向其致哀。

    皮亚斯特里悼念澳大利亚F1传奇阿兰·琼斯

    1980年F1世界冠军阿兰·琼斯在80岁去世后，迈凯伦车手皮亚斯特里向其致哀。

    赛道之外的赛车文化。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计，以及让赛车超越运动本身的故事。

    迈凯伦车手皮亚斯特里在澳大利亚1980年F1世界冠军阿兰·琼斯去世后向其致哀。

    琼斯于2026年11月5日因病短暂治疗后去世，享年80岁。他赢得过12场大奖赛胜利，24次登上领奖台，并为威廉姆斯车队赢得了首个车手总冠军。他至今仍是最后一位在1980年于卡尔德公园赢得澳大利亚大奖赛的澳大利亚车手。

    琼斯在1975年至1981年、1983年以及1985年至1986年期间参加锦标赛。除了威廉姆斯车队，他还曾为赫斯基思、希尔、瑟蒂斯、影子、飞箭和哈斯-洛拉车队效力。

    皮亚斯特里在巴西大奖赛周末前于英特拉格斯对F1.com表示：“这显然是非常悲伤的一天。他的一生非常值得庆祝。我认为他显然是一位在赛道上取得巨大成功的车手，也是澳大利亚最近的一位世界冠军。

    “但我认为他这个人本身，你知道，非常直率，直奔主题。在个人层面上，他也经常对我说些好话，这很好。”

    弗兰克·德尼、弗兰克·威廉姆斯和艾伦·琼斯

    这位澳大利亚车手透露，他曾驾驶过琼斯赢得世界冠军的那辆威廉姆斯赛车。

    “我也有幸驾驶过他那辆赢得世界冠军的赛车，所以能体验那种感觉，并以某种方式与他产生联系，这非常酷。这是悲伤的一天，但也应该是庆祝一段伟大人生的一天。

    “显然，他比我早了很多很多年，我和艾伦从来没有太多交流。但我觉得在很多方面，他体现了澳大利亚精神——非常坚韧，实话实说，想什么说什么，我认为这大概是最重要的一点。

    “任何在F1赢得世界冠军的人，显然都是凭借天赋、努力以及其他各种因素取得这一成就的，所以我认为这大概已经说明了一切。”

    Oscar Piastri pays tribute to Australian F1 legend Alan Jones following his death

    McLaren's Oscar Piastri has paid tribute to 1980 Formula 1 world champion Alan Jones following his death at the age of 80

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    McLaren's Oscar Piastri has paid tribute to Alan Jones following the death of Australia's 1980 Formula 1 world champion.

    Jones died at the age of 80 on 5 November 2026, following a short illness. He won 12 grands prix, celebrated 24 podium finishes and gave Williams its first drivers' title. He also remains the last Australian to have won the Australian Grand Prix at Calder Park in 1980.

    Jones competed in the championship from 1975 to 1981, in 1983 and again from 1985 to 1986. In addition to Williams, he also drove for Hesketh, Hill, Surtees, Shadow, Arrows and Haas-Lola.

    Speaking to F1.com at Interlagos ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix weekend, Piastri said: "It's obviously a very sad day. A life that should be very well celebrated. I think [he was] a guy who obviously had a lot of success on the racetrack and was Australia’s most recent world champion.

    "But I think also the person he was, you know, a very straightforward person, straight to the point. On a personal level as well, he often had nice things to say about me, which was nice."

    Frank Dernie, Frank Williams and Alan Jones

    The Australian driver revealed he had driven the Williams in which Jones won his title.

    "I’ve had the honour to drive his world championship-winning car as well, so [it was] very cool to be able to experience that and connect with him in some kind of way. A sad day, but a day that should celebrate a great life.

    "Obviously, he was a long, long time before me, and I never had much interaction with Alan. But I think in a lot of ways, he kind of embodied the Australian spirit – very tough, saying it how it is, saying what he thinks, and I think that’s probably the biggest thing.

    "Anyone who’s won a world championship in F1 has clearly achieved it through talent, hard work, all kinds of other things, so I think that probably says enough."
