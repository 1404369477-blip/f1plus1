    维斯塔潘回应拉斯尔在拉斯维加斯碰撞事件

    在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛的最后练习赛中，维斯塔潘与拉斯尔再次发生碰撞。荷兰车手超越梅赛德斯车手后减速，据裁判所述，他多次改变方向。

    维斯塔潘回应乔治·拉塞尔在拉斯维加斯碰撞事件

    维斯塔潘表示，他没有打算在拉斯维加斯F1第三场练习赛中浪费时间去处理与拉塞尔的这次事件

    在F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛的最后一场练习赛中，维斯塔潘和乔治·拉塞尔再次相遇。荷兰人超越了梅赛德斯车手，但随后在拉塞尔面前减速，据裁判长所说，他还多次改变方向

    随后，拉塞尔从四次世界冠军的内侧超车，导致两人在14号弯发生接触。这使两位对手都受到了裁判长的调查，尽管他们最终通过发出警告来处理了这次事件。与斥责不同，警告没有任何后果

    当被问及在排位赛后的新闻发布会上发生的意外时，维斯塔潘对讨论此事兴趣不大。当被问及发生了什么，他简单地回答道：“没什么。什么都没发生。”

    然而，拉塞尔随后在媒体区对红牛车手发起了猛烈的抨击。在接受天空体育意大利采访时，拉塞尔表示，其中之一是：

    “我不会接受有人这样背叛我，然后像他们比所有人都大更好一样四处走动。有人必须站出来对抗那个人。”

    维斯塔潘在很大程度上不为所动。当Motorsport.com在拉斯维加斯赛道区的荷兰媒体会上问他关于碰撞时，他回答道：

    “我甚至不想在这方面浪费时间。这对我的意义根本不大。”

    当有人指出 stewards 在 FIA 文档中提到了准备圈中的绅士协议，即在开始飞驰圈之前，车手们应保持自己的位置并避免相互干扰，维斯塔潘回应说：“我一生中从未见过这样的协议。”

    stewards 还明确表示，他们在做出决定时对 拉塞尔 提到的绅士协议重视程度不高，而是专注于两位车手的行动。

    stewards 记录了这些解释，但认为这种非正式的理解对评估事件的相关性有限。决定性因素是两辆车的实际移动和它们相对的位置。

    特别值得注意的是，由于基米·安东内利的发车罚分，两位车手将在周六晚上的拉斯维加斯赛道占据发车区的第一排，但维斯塔潘坚称这次碰撞并没有给他任何额外的动力来保持对拉塞尔的领先。

    不，那绝对不是我获得动力的地方。

    Max Verstappen responds to George Russell after Vegas clash

    Verstappen says he has no intention of wasting his time on the incident with Russell during F1’s third practice session in Las Vegas

    During the final practice session for the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, Max Verstappen and George Russell crossed paths once again. The Dutchman overtook the Mercedes driver, but then slowed down in front of him and, according to the stewards, changed direction several times.

    Russell subsequently went down the inside of the four-time world champion, leading to contact between the two at Turn 14. It earned both rivals a trip to the stewards, although they ultimately dealt with the incident by issuing a warning. Unlike a reprimand, a warning carries no consequences at all.

    When Verstappen was asked about the incident during the press conference after qualifying, the Dutchman had little interest in discussing it. Asked what had happened, he simply replied: "Nothing. Nothing happened."

    Russell, however, subsequently launched a scathing attack on the Red Bull driver in the media pen. Speaking to Sky Sports Italy, Russell said, among other things:

    "I’m not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they’re bigger and better than everyone else. Somebody has got to stand up to that guy."

    Verstappen was largely unmoved. When Motorsport.com asked him about the clash during the Dutch media session that followed in the Las Vegas paddock, he replied:

    "I don't even want to waste my time on that. It's not worth it to me at all."

    When it was pointed out that the stewards had referred in the FIA document to a gentlemen's agreement during preparation laps, under which drivers are expected to maintain their positions and avoid interfering with one another before starting a flying lap, Verstappen responded: "I've never seen such an agreement in my life."

    The stewards also made clear that they had attached little weight to the gentlemen's agreement mentioned by Russell in reaching their decision, instead focusing on the actions of both drivers.

    "The Stewards noted these explanations but considered that such informal understandings were of limited relevance to the assessment of the incident. The decisive considerations were the actual movements of both cars and their relative positions."

    It is particularly notable that the two drivers will share the front row of the grid in Vegas on Saturday night following Kimi Antonelli's grid penalty, but Verstappen insists the clash has not given him any extra motivation to keep Russell behind.

    "No, that's definitely not where I get my motivation from."
