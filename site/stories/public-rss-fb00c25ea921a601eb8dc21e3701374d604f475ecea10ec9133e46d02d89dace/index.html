    诺里斯在社交视频中被指嘲讽斯特罗尔引发争议

    社交媒体上一段视频显示诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛夜宴中似乎嘲讽了斯特罗尔，该视频在X上观看次数超过三百万。

    兰多·诺里斯在病毒视频中出现，似乎在嘲笑拉恩斯·斯特罗尔引发争议

    麦凯轮车手兰多·诺里斯在一段病毒视频中似乎在拿拉恩斯·斯特罗尔开玩笑，引发了F1粉丝的讨论

    赛车文化超越赛道。车手生活方式、时尚、旅行、设计和那些让赛车不仅仅是一项运动的故事

    一段视频在社交媒体上流传，似乎显示兰多·诺里斯在新加坡大奖赛后的一个夜晚嘲笑拉恩斯·斯特罗尔。这段视频在X上已经观看超过三百万次。

    视频是由粉丝账号MV3daily在比赛之夜发布的。视频中，可以看到迈凯轮车手与哈斯一级方程式车手Ollie 贝尔曼一起外出，人群中有人举着一个写着"给最佳车手"的牌子。

    视频中，诺里斯大声喊道"兰斯在哪里，兰斯在哪里？"，许多F1粉丝认为这是对阿斯顿·马丁车手的讽刺。

    斯特罗尔本赛季还没有得分，今年成为首位达到100次Q1淘汰的车手。

    分享视频的账号在其帖子中对诺里斯进行了批评。MV3daily写道："兰斯可能不是赛道上最好的车手，但至少要有一些尊严。"

    在撰写本文时，该帖子已有330万次观看，约19,000个赞和564条回复。其中一条回复获得了超过5,000个赞，开玩笑说：“兄弟们的公关经理正在为他们的生命而战” [原文如此]。

    辩论变得非常激烈，以至于后来账号要求粉丝冷静下来。

    “我们能不能都放松一点，这里每个人都太过于激进，这都表现出来了，”MV3daily在第二天发布了以下内容。

    诺里斯和斯特罗尔都没有对这段视频做出回应。

    视频出现在世界冠军艰难周末的结尾。在周六的冲刺赛中，诺里斯和他的队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂在最后一圈的5号弯道发生碰撞，争夺第四名。皮亚斯蒂退赛，而诺里斯恢复到第八名，因此麦凯轮车队在红牛车队逼近车队 constructors' championship 第三名的时候只得到了一分。

    诺里斯在随后的周日承认说：“这是我的错误，我让团队失望了。我也向他们所有人道歉，我欠他们一个道歉，我下次会做得更好。”

    诺里斯以第四名的成绩完成了大奖赛，落后获胜者马克斯·维斯塔潘40.5秒。诺里斯现在在积分榜上排名第六，积分为201分，而麦凯轮车队在车队 constructors' championship 中领先红牛车队七分，前往德克萨斯州。

    下一场比赛是10月23日至25日在奥斯汀举行的美国大奖赛。

    Lando Norris sparks controversy after appearing to mock Lance Stroll in viral video

    The McLaren driver has sparked debate among F1 fans after a viral video shows what appeared to be him making a joke at Lance Stroll's expense

    Motorsport culture beyond the track. Driver lifestyle, fashion, travel, design and the stories that make racing more than a sport.

    A video is doing the rounds on social media, appearing to show Lando Norris mocking Lance Stroll on a night out after the Singapore Grand Prix. It's now been viewed more than three million times on X.

    The clip was posted by the fan account MV3daily on the night of the race. In it, the McLaren driver is seen out with Haas Formula 1 driver Ollie Bearman while someone in the crowd raises a sign saying "To the best driver".

    In the footage, Norris shouts "Where's Lance, where's Lance?", which many F1 fans took as a dig at the Aston Martin driver.

    Stroll has yet to score a point this season and this year became the first driver to reach 100 Q1 eliminations.

    The account that shared the video was critical of Norris in its post. "Lance may not be the best driver on the grid but have some decency atleast," MV3daily wrote.

    At the time of writing, the post had 3.3 million views, around 19,000 likes and 564 replies. One reply, with more than 5,000 likes, joked: "bros PR manager is fighting for their life" [sic].

    The debate became heated enough that the account later asked followers to calm down.

    "Can we all relax a little bit, everyone is a little bit too aggressive on here and it's showing," MV3daily posted the following day.

    Neither Norris nor Stroll has responded to the clip.

    The video came at the end of a difficult weekend for the reigning world champion. In Saturday's sprint, Norris and team-mate Oscar Piastri were fighting for fourth when they collided at Turn 5 on the last lap. Piastri retired while Norris recovered to eighth, so McLaren left with one point at a time when Red Bull is closing in on third place in the constructors' championship.

    "It was a mistake on my part, and I let down the team. I've also apologised to all of them, I owe them all an apology, and I will do better next time," Norris conceded on the following Sunday.

    Norris finished the grand prix fourth, 40.5 seconds behind winner Max Verstappen. Norris now sits sixth in the standings on 201 points, and McLaren heads to Texas just seven points ahead of Red Bull in the constructors' championship.

    The next race is the United States Grand Prix in Austin, from 23 to 25 October.
