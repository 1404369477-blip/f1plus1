    2026年F1西班牙大奖赛完整周末赛程公布

    The 比赛 Team发布了2026年F1西班牙大奖赛的完整周末赛程，比赛将在马德里郊外的新赛道马德里赛道首次举行。

    随着F1®本赛季首次来到一条全新赛道，气氛令人振奋——位于西班牙首都郊外的马德里赛道将承办西班牙大奖赛。

    该赛道围绕IFEMA展览中心而建，全长3.365英里，共有22个弯角，其中包括La Monumental——一个巨大的右弯，预计将成为F1中最长的倾斜弯角。

    安东内利在进入本站比赛时领跑积分榜，他在蒙扎从队尾发车并史诗级夺冠，为其表现提供了有力支撑，那么其他争冠车手能否重整旗鼓？还是说Antonelli已经证明自己高人一档？

    西班牙本土车手将备受关注，因为车迷们可以在威廉姆斯车队的塞恩斯和阿斯顿·马丁车队的阿隆索之间尽情选择。

    塞恩斯与这条赛道以及比赛主办方都有合作，他说：“按照他们设置护栏的方式和赛道的设计来看，这将是近年来一级方程式最大的挑战之一。

    “这条赛道让我想起了很多吉达，让我想起了很多巴库，在某些方面甚至让我有点想起澳门。所以要在F1里用上1000马力去跑这样的赛道，将会是非常有趣的挑战。”

    这会是阿隆索的最后一届西班牙大奖赛吗？决定尚未做出，但这种不确定性为整个赛事增添了一层额外的悬念。

    英文原文

    The excitement is palpable as Formula 1® heads to a new track for the first time this year, with the Madring, just outside the Spanish capital, taking on the Spanish Grand Prix title.

    The circuit, based around the IFEMA Exhibition Centre, is 3.365 miles long with 22 corners, including La Monumental, a huge right-hander which is set to become the longest banked corner in F1.

    Kimi Antonelli leads the championship heading in, backed up by an epic win from the back of the grid in Monza, so can the rest of the title challengers regroup? Or has Antonelli proven he's on another level?

    There will be plenty of attention on the home Spanish drivers, as the fans will be spoiled for choice between Williams's Carlos Sainz and Fernando Alonso of Aston Martin.

    Sainz is involved with the track and organiser of the race, and said: "With the way they've put the barriers and the design of the track is going to be one of the biggest challenges in Formula 1 recently.

    "The track reminds me a lot of Jeddah, reminds me a lot of Baku, even reminds me a bit of Macau in some ways. So having that in an F1 with a 1000bhp is going to be quite an interesting challenge."

    Will it be Alonso's final Spanish Grand Prix? The decision isn't made, but that uncertainty adds an extra layer of intrigue to proceedings.
