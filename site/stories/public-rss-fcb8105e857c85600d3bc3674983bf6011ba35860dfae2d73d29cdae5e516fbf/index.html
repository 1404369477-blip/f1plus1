    劳森顶替受伤的哈贾尔，角田裕毅重返F1

    利亚姆·劳森将在赞德福特举行的荷兰大奖赛重返红牛F1车队，顶替手腕受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔，角田裕毅则被征召回归小红牛车队参赛。

    利亚姆·劳森将在本周末的荷兰大奖赛上回归红牛F1车队，顶替受伤的伊萨克·哈贾尔，而角田裕毅则相应地被召入姊妹车队小红牛参赛。

    据荷兰媒体《电讯报》率先报道，哈贾尔在F1夏休期期间手腕受伤，伤势严重到将缺席本周末在赞德沃特举行的比赛。去年他正是在这里为小红牛拿到了自己的第一个F1领奖台。

    因此，劳森将第三次为红牛一线车队出战，从小红牛被提拔上来。

    他最初被选中在2025赛季接替塞尔吉奥·佩雷斯，但仅参加了两站比赛就被毫不客气地弃用——回到小红牛，而角田裕毅则被提拔为马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友。

    Lawson 从那次降级中很好地反弹过来，在那场赛事的剩余部分中七次拿到积分，最好成绩为第五名，这种状态的提升帮助他保住了2026年的车手席位，而代价是 Tsunoda——他同样未能在红牛的第二席上留下深刻印象。

    Lawson 已经令人鼓舞地开启了2026赛季；他以43分位居车手积分榜第九，这一积分也帮助 小红牛 升至车队积分榜第五位。

    正是由于对2026年这些截然不同且复杂的赛车的经验，这位24岁车手——三年前在赞德沃特获得F1首秀机会，当时 Daniel Ricciardo 在二练撞车导致手部骨折而由他替补出战——才被选中与 Verstappen 搭档出战，而不是 Tsunoda。

    Tsunoda 目前在2025年底失去正式比赛席位后，担任红牛的储备车手。

    本周，他与Meyer Shank本田车队的IndyCar前景联系在一起，可能取代印地500冠军Felix Rosenqvist，因为Rosenqvist将于2027年回归迈凯伦的IndyCar车队。

    赞德沃特的这次机会将标志着角田裕毅为这支他于2021年首次效力的小红牛车队出战的第90场比赛。

    红牛尚未宣布Hadjar是否需要错过之后的任何分站。

    在过去五年里，荷兰大奖赛都在蒙扎意大利大奖赛前一周举行，但今年两站之间有两周的间隔，为恢复提供了更长的时间。

    英文原文

    Liam Lawson will make a return to the Red Bull Formula 1 team at this weekend's Dutch Grand Prix in place of an injured Isack Hadjar, with Yuki Tsunoda in turn getting a call-up to race for the sister Racing Bulls team.

    Hadjar injured his wrist during the F1 summer break - as first reported by Dutch publication De Telegraaf - to such an extent that he will be sidelined for this weekend's race at Zandvoort, where last year he scored his first F1 podium for Racing Bulls.

    Lawson will therefore make his third start for the senior team, stepping up from Racing Bulls.

    He was originally picked as Sergio Perez's replacement for the 2025 season, but lasted just two rounds before being unceremoniously dropped - returning to Racing Bulls while Tsunoda was promoted to partner Max Verstappen.

    Lawson rebounded well from that demotion, scoring points seven times with a best finish of fifth over the remainder of that campaign, and that uptick in form helped him retain a drive for 2026 at the expense of Tsunoda, who also struggled to make an impression in the second Red Bull seat.

    And Lawson has begun the 2026 campaign encouragingly; he sits ninth in the drivers' championship standings with 43 points, a tally that has also helped elevate Racing Bulls into fifth in the constructors' standings.

    That experience of these vastly different and complex 2026 cars is likely why the 24-year-old - who got his F1 break at Zandvoort three years ago, deputising for Daniel Ricciardo when he broke his hand in an FP2 crash - has been picked over Tsunoda to race alongside Verstappen.

    Tsunoda is currently serving as Red Bull's reserve driver following his exit from a race seat at the end of 2025.

    This week, he has been linked to an IndyCar future at the Meyer Shank Honda team in place of Indianapolis 500 winner Felix Rosenqvist, as Rosenqvist returns to McLaren’s IndyCar team for 2027.

    The Zandvoort opportunity will mark Tsunoda's 90th start for the junior team that he first raced for in 2021.

    Red Bull has not announced whether Hadjar will need to miss any subsequent rounds.

    For the past five years the Dutch GP has taken place one week before the Italian GP at Monza, but this year there is a two-week gap between them, providing longer for recovery.
