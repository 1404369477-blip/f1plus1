    维斯塔潘赢得F1本月超车王奖项，反应平淡

    维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯大奖赛粉丝论坛上获得本月超车王奖项，他的反应非常平淡。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘对赢得F1本月超越奖的平淡反应

    马克斯·维斯塔潘在拉斯维加斯舞台上赢得F1十月超越奖后给出了平淡的反应

    对于红牛车队的粉丝来说，关注红牛车队，了解F1新闻、车手更新、技术洞察和米尔顿凯恩斯的分析与比赛解析。

    马克斯·维斯塔潘从不擅长做作的表现，F1拉斯维加斯大奖赛的粉丝论坛又给了我们一个提醒。

    出现在红牛车队在拉斯维加斯的浅色队服上，与队友伊萨克·哈德贾以及哈斯车队的奥利弗·贝尔曼和拉斐尔·卡马拉一起站在舞台上，这位四次世界冠军获得了一块额外的奖牌——并以极致的冷静回应。

    “你可能知道接下来会发生什么，马克斯，”舞台主持人调侃着，观众欢呼。‘我不知道，’维斯塔潘平淡地回答。

    当她宣布他赢得了Formula 1的‘本月超越奖’并递给他一个清晰的圆形奖杯供投票粉丝签名时，维斯塔潘只是发出了一声低沉的‘哦’，然后回头看向巨幕重放的获胜动作：他在马来西亚巴林大奖赛上超越梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利。

    即使是维斯塔潘也对粉丝投票表示怀疑。

    “那不可能赢，”他通过麦克风说，同时回头看着重放。站在他左边，贝尔曼 立即插话：“这是这个月吗？那感觉像是两个月前。”

    维斯塔潘 持着签名的奖杯向主持人问道：“这是哪个月的？”

    “十月，”她回答道。

    “十月。好吧，”维斯塔潘 嘟囔着，停顿了一下，然后带着完全严肃的表情半心半意地将奖杯举到空中，并单调地说：“是的！”观众爆发出欢呼和笑声，主持人开玩笑说：“我们接受这个，Max！我们会接受的。”

    Sepang赛道——以及拉斯维加斯的超越讽刺

    贝尔曼的时间线俏皮话并不离谱。移师至马来西亚雪邦国际赛道的巴林大奖赛在10月4日举行，比原定时间提前了近七周，这是自2017年赛事从中东移出后，一级方程式赛车首次重返该场地。

    尽管维斯塔潘的反应平淡，但这次变动本身对红牛车队本赛季来说至关重要。由于大雨和排位赛软件故障，比赛开始被推迟了超过90分钟，发车时杆位车手维斯塔潘在起跑线上落后于安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔。

    在瓦尔特里·博塔斯引发的第一次安全车出动后——当时红牛车队在梅赛德斯车队的中胎轮胎上选择了软胎——维斯塔潘在复赛后，在第一弯道外侧无情地超越了安东内利。他一路领先，以2.307秒的优势击败安东内利，这不仅为他赢得了2026年的首个胜利，也为红牛福特动力系统赢得了突破性的处女冠。

    粉丝投票让维斯塔潘在6月和7月之前赢得的第三个月度超越奖杯后，成为了赛季领先每月获胜者，进入年终超越奖评选。

    更令人印象深刻的是，周六晚上的喜剧舞台瞬间迅速过时。维斯塔潘在一场混乱的50圈比赛中赢得了2026年拉斯维加斯大奖赛，这场比赛创下了352次超越和35次领先变化的新纪录——然而他却称电池驱动的滑流战斗"不是F1"。如果粉丝再给他11月的奖杯，预计他的反应会更平淡。

    Max Verstappen’s deadpan reaction to winning F1’s Overtake of the Month award

    Max Verstappen gives a deadpan reaction on the Las Vegas stage after winning F1's October Overtake of the Month award

    For Red Bull fans. Keeping watch on Red Bull Racing, with F1 news, driver updates, technical insight and race analysis from Milton Keynes.

    Max Verstappen has never been one for forced showmanship, and Formula 1’s Las Vegas Grand Prix Fan Forum offered another reminder.

    Appearing on stage in Red Bull’s tan Las Vegas teamwear alongside team-mate Isack Hadjar and Haas drivers Oliver Bearman and Rafael Camara, the four-time world champion was awarded an extra piece of silverware - and responded with peak deadpan restraint.

    "You may know what’s coming, Max," the stage host teased as the crowd cheered. "I don’t know," Verstappen replied flatly.

    When she announced he had won Formula 1’s Overtake of the Month award and handed him the clear circular trophy to sign for a voting fan, Verstappen offered only a muted, unbothered "Ooh" before glancing behind him at the giant screen replaying the winning move: his pass on Mercedes’ Kimi Antonelli at the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia.

    Even Verstappen seemed sceptical of the fan vote.

    "There’s no way that that won," he said into the microphone while looking back at the replay. Standing to his left, Bearman immediately chimed in: "Was that this month? That was like two months ago."

    Holding the signed trophy toward the host, Verstappen asked, "For what month is this?"

    "October," she answered.

    "October. All right," Verstappen muttered, pausing for a beat before hoisting the trophy half-heartedly into the air with a completely straight face and adding a monotone, "Yes!" The crowd erupted into cheers and laughter, with the host joking, "We’ll take that, Max! We will take that."

    The Sepang pass - and Vegas’s overtaking irony

    Bearman’s timeline quip was not far off. The relocated Bahrain Grand Prix at Malaysia’s Sepang International Circuit took place nearly seven weeks earlier on 4 October, marking Formula 1’s first visit to the venue since 2017, after the race moved out of the Middle East.

    Despite Verstappen’s dry reaction, the move itself proved important to Red Bull’s season. After heavy rain and formation-lap software glitches delayed the start by more than 90 minutes, polesitter Verstappen slipped behind Antonelli and George Russell off the line.

    Following an early safety car triggered by Valtteri Bottas - when Red Bull switched to soft slicks against Mercedes’ mediums - Verstappen swept ruthlessly around the outside of Antonelli at Turn 1 on the restart. He never looked back, beating Antonelli by 2.307 seconds to deliver both his first victory of 2026 and the breakthrough maiden win for Red Bull Ford Powertrains.

    The fan vote gave Verstappen his third Overtake of the Month trophy of the 2026 campaign after earlier wins in June and July, making him the season’s leading monthly winner heading into the year-end Overtake of the Year poll.

    Better still, the comedic stage moment aged quickly on Saturday night. Verstappen won the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix in a chaotic 50-lap race featuring a record 352 overtakes and 35 lead changes - only to label the battery-driven slipstream battles "not F1". If fans hand him November’s trophy too, expect an even flatter reaction.
