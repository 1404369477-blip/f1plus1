    Kimi安东内利完成排位赛练习全胜，梅赛德斯在卡塔尔大奖赛占据巨大优势

    Kimi安东内利在卡塔尔大奖赛练习赛中全胜，梅赛德斯车队以巨大优势进入排位赛。

    F1卡塔尔大奖赛：Kimi安东内利完成排位赛练习全胜，梅赛德斯占据巨大优势

    Kimi安东内利和乔治·拉塞尔在F1卡塔尔大奖赛的最后一场练习赛中展示了梅赛德斯车队的强大实力

    Kimi安东内利为梅赛德斯车队在卡塔尔大奖赛的练习赛中赢得了全胜，银箭车队带着巨大优势进入排位赛

    乔治·拉塞尔在半程之前速度最快，各车队开始进行软胎测试，但安东内利在下半场再次缩短了圈速

    不同于周五两次红旗的FP2练习，第三场练习没有发生任何事故，这在很大程度上要归功于一个平淡的开局，早期几乎没有动作。一开始，皮埃尔·加斯利独自一人绕赛道行驶，这是在周五第二场练习中他的阿尔派赛车在中途翻车后，弥补失去的地盘。

    加斯利最终被队友弗朗哥·科拉平托和凯迪拉克的瓦尔特里·博塔加入，而其他人则在练习的前三分之一时间里待在维修区。

    但在20分钟之前，情况突然发生了变化，重量级的赛车手们开始了软胎的练习——这种轮胎预计只对排位赛有用。比赛预计将在中胎和硬胎的Pirelli轮胎上完成，这些轮胎在卢塞尔赛道上面临最严峻的周末。随着排位赛模拟的开始，即将离任的世界冠军兰多·诺里斯在遭遇剧烈的过度转向后，驶向了14号弯的逃生道路。

    正如周五两次练习的情况一样，梅赛德斯车队立即掌控了局面，乔治·拉塞尔以1分22.983秒的成绩领先队友基米·安东内利。这比麦克拉伦的奥斯卡·皮亚斯蒂和马克斯·维斯塔潘能做出的成绩快了半秒以上，后者驾驶的雷诺红牛小红牛22赛车在更艰难的自由练习后需要重新平衡。

    虽然拉塞尔随后在5号弯道漂移过宽，安东内利的第二次中期练习跑出的成绩几乎与队友相同，仅慢了0.017秒。

    在还剩15分钟的时候，维斯塔潘短暂地占据了领先位置，但安东内利立即反击，并以1分22.400秒的成绩为梅赛德斯车队恢复了半秒的领先优势。拉塞尔对此速度的爆发毫无应对之策，落后了0.249秒。

    维斯塔潘仍然排在第三位，作为第一辆非梅赛德斯车队赛车，落后0.501秒，其后是他的队友伊萨克·哈德贾。

    在查尔斯·勒克莱尔将其描述为“非常困难”的周五之后，法拉利车队几乎没有取得进步，勒克莱尔排名第五，落后安东内利八十分之一秒。迈凯轮车队的表现也超过了预期，诺里斯排名第六，皮亚斯特里排名第七。

    汉密尔顿在最后的练习圈中被交通堵塞，表现不如预期，最终在第九位完成了比赛，分开了小红牛的中场领跑者阿维德·林德布拉德和利亚姆·劳森。

    F1 Qatar GP: Kimi Antonelli completes practice clean sweep as Mercedes dominates

    Kimi Antonelli and George Russell showcase Mercedes' dominant form in final practice for Formula 1's Qatar Grand Prix

    Kimi Antonelli has handed Mercedes a clean sweep of Qatar GP practice sessions as the Silver Arrows head to qualifying with a huge advantage.

    Russell went quickest before halfway as teams embarked on soft-tyre runs with Mercedes continuing its ominous form on the high-downforce motorcycle circuit of Lusail, only for Antonelli to take another chunk out of that laptime in the second half.

    Unlike Friday's twice red-flagged FP2 session, third practice took place without incident, which was partly thanks to a tepid start with little to no action early on. At the start, Pierre Gasly circulated by himself, making up for lost ground after beaching his Alpine in the gravel midway through Friday's second practice.

    Gasly was eventually joined by team-mate Franco Colapinto and Cadillac's Valtteri Bottas, while the others stayed in the garage for the first third of the session.

    That all changed suddenly before the 20-minute mark as the heavy hitters embarked on soft-tyre runs - a compound projected to be only useful for qualifying. The race is expected to be completed on medium and hard Pirelli tyres, which are facing their most demanding weekend on the Lusail loop. As qualifying simulations got underway, outgoing world champion Lando Norris headed to the Turn 14 escape road after catching a violent snap of oversteer.

    As was the case during both of Friday's practice endeavours, Mercedes immediately took charge with George Russell's 1m22.983s leading team-mate Kimi Antonelli. That was over half a second better than what McLaren's Oscar Piastri and Max Verstappen could muster, the latter driving a Red Bull RB22 which required rebalancing after a tougher free practice outing.

    While Russell then drifted wide in Turn 5, Antonelli's second mid-session run yielded a time almost identical to his team-mate's, just 0.017s off.

    With 15 minutes to go, Verstappen briefly claimed top before Antonelli immediately retaliated and restored Mercedes' comfortable half-second cushion with a 1m22.400s effort. Russell had no answer for that flash of pace, conceding 0.249s.

    Verstappen remained third, a sobering 0.501s in arrears as the first non-Mercedes car, followed by team-mate Isack Hadjar.

    After a Friday which Charles Leclerc described as "very difficult", there was little progress for Ferrari as Leclerc took fifth, eight tenths behind Antonelli. McLaren also struggled more than was expected with sixth for Norris and seventh for Piastri.

    Lewis Hamilton fared worse after being held up by traffic on his final hotlap, splitting Racing Bull's midfield leaders Arvid Lindblad and Liam Lawson in ninth.
