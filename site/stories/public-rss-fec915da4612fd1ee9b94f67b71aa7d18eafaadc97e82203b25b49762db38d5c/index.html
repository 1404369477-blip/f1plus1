    马尔克斯就手腕伤势向哈贾尔提出建议

    伊萨克·哈贾尔因上周在健身房训练时手腕受伤，将缺席本周末的荷兰大奖赛；红牛与七届MotoGP世界冠军马克·马尔克斯都建议他不要冒险，安心恢复。

    马尔克斯就手腕伤势给哈贾尔的建议

    伊萨克·哈贾尔渴望直接回到赛车中参加荷兰大奖赛，但红牛和马克·马尔克斯都劝他不要冒任何风险

    伊萨克·哈贾尔因上周在健身房训练时手腕受伤，将缺席本周末的荷兰大奖赛。据小红牛首席执行官彼得·拜耳透露，红牛姊妹车队于周五得知可能更换车手的消息，情况在周二进一步进展。

    当时，角田裕毅被召入——他接到通知时正在希腊的海滩上——而利亚姆·劳森则在2025年中国大奖赛后被下放后，于红牛获得又一次机会。

    虽然这一情况自然为两位车手带来了机会，但对哈贾尔来说却极为沮丧，不过他还是在周五上午出现在了赞德沃特围场。

    哈贾尔去年在荷兰首次登上F1领奖台，原本计划在今年以一款特别头盔设计俏皮地致敬这一成就。

    红牛与马尔克斯意见一致：慢慢恢复

    哈贾尔——正如任何运动员都会有的反应——想立刻重新参赛，但他被两个不同的方面建议不要冒任何风险，缺席荷兰大奖赛。

    首先，这一信息来自红牛。劳伦特·梅基斯确认，他的车队不想对马克斯·维斯塔潘的队友冒任何风险，后者今年表现得出人意料地好。

    哈贾尔原本为这个周末设计了一款特别头盔，灵感来自代尔夫特蓝陶和他去年破损的奖杯

    “伊萨克没事，”梅基斯告诉F1官方网站。“我们没有任何理由对他冒险。他本赛季开局非常出色，每次他把这辆车驶出车库，一节接一节、一个周末接一个周末，他都越来越强。”

    “我们零风险，我们让他这个周末处理好恢复，希望我们能非常、非常快地让他回到车里。”

    建议他跳过赞德沃特的还有另一个更令人意外的来源：七届MotoGP世界冠军马克·马尔克斯。

    这位杜卡迪车手与哈贾尔共用同一位理疗师卡洛斯·J·加西亚。哈贾尔在手腕受伤后致电马尔克斯，这位西班牙人建议他不要急于复出。

    这位目前在MotoGP积分榜上排名第四的车手还敦促哈贾尔不要急于接受某些医疗程序，因为这位法国人还很年轻，前方还有漫长的F1生涯。

    因此马尔克斯认为，慢慢来并妥善恢复对哈贾尔来说是最明智的做法，而这正是红牛已经告诉这位年轻人的。

    马尔克斯本人在伤病方面有大量亲身经历。这位MotoGP偶像除其他伤病外，曾右臂和右手拇指骨折、双肩脱臼，并患有复视。

    马尔克斯以伤后快速复出而闻名，但他建议哈贾尔反其道而行之

    当然，关键问题是哈贾尔何时能够重返小红牛22的驾驶舱。

    当被Autosport问及时，红牛表示现在提供官方更新还为时过早，不过计划是让他在两周后的意大利大奖赛上回到车中。

    “现在肯定说还为时过早，但我们的目标是让他在蒙扎回归，”梅基斯告诉德国天空体育。

    意大利大奖赛将于9月4日至6日的周末举行。尽管蒙扎公园内这条标志性赛道当然不是赛历上对身体要求最高的赛道，但一周后紧接着就是在新马德里赛道举行的西班牙大奖赛。

    The advice Marquez gave Hadjar over his wrist injury

    Isack Hadjar was eager to get straight back in the car for the Dutch GP, but both Red Bull and Marc Marquez urged him not to take any risks

    Isack Hadjar misses this weekend's Dutch Grand Prix after sustaining a wrist injury during a gym session last week. According to Racing Bulls CEO Peter Bayer, Red Bull’s sister team was informed on Friday of a potential driver change, with the situation developing further on Tuesday.

    That was when Yuki Tsunoda was called up – having been on a beach in Greece when he was asked to step in – while Liam Lawson was handed another opportunity at Red Bull following his demotion after the 2025 Chinese Grand Prix.

    While the situation naturally presents opportunities for both drivers, it is highly frustrating for Hadjar, who nevertheless made an appearance in the Zandvoort paddock on Friday morning.

    Hadjar claimed his maiden F1 podium in the Netherlands last year and had planned to reference that achievement with a playful nod in a special helmet design this year.

    Red Bull and Marquez in agreement: Take your time to recover

    Hadjar – as you might expect from any athlete – wanted to go racing again straight away, but he has been advised by two different parties not to take any risks and to sit out the Dutch GP.

    First of all, that message came from Red Bull. Laurent Mekies confirmed that his team did not want to take any risks with Max Verstappen's team-mate, who has performed surprisingly well this year.

    Hadjar had planned a special helmet design for this weekend, inspired by Delft blue pottery and his broken trophy from last year

    "Isack is alright," Mekies told the official F1 website. "We have no reason to take any risk with him. He has done a fantastic start of the season, he has been stronger and stronger every time he has been driving this car out of the garage, session after session, weekend after weekend.

    "We take zero risk, we let him sort out the recovery this weekend, and hopefully we’ll have him very, very quickly [back] in the car."

    The advice to skip Zandvoort also came from another, more surprising source: seven-time MotoGP world champion Marc Marquez.

    The Ducati rider and Hadjar share the same physiotherapist, Carlos J. Garcia. Hadjar called Marquez after sustaining the wrist injury, with the Spaniard advising him not to rush his return.

    The rider currently fourth in the MotoGP standings also urged Hadjar not to rush into certain medical procedures, given that the Frenchman is still young and has a long F1 career ahead of him.

    Marquez therefore felt that taking his time and recovering properly was the most sensible approach for Hadjar, which is exactly what Red Bull had already told the youngster.

    Marquez himself has plenty of first-hand experience when it comes to injuries. The MotoGP icon has, among other things, broken his right upper arm and right thumb, dislocated both shoulders and suffered from diplopia.

    Marquez is known for returning quickly after injury, but advised Hadjar to do the opposite

    The key question, of course, is when Hadjar will be able to return behind the wheel of the RB22.

    When asked by Autosport, Red Bull said it is still too early to provide an official update on that, although the plan is to have him back in the car at Monza for the Italian GP in two weeks' time.

    "It's too early to say that definitely, but it’s our target to have him back in Monza," Mekies told Sky Sports Germany.

    The Italian GP will take place on the weekend of 4-6 September. Although the iconic circuit in Monza's park is certainly not the most physically demanding track on the calendar, the Spanish GP at the new Madring follows just one week later.
