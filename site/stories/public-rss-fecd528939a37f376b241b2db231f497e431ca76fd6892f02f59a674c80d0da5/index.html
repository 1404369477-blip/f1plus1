    罗斯伯格：安东内利与自己的心理较量将决定F1总冠军归属

    前F1世界冠军尼科·罗斯伯格认为，基米·安东内利在本赛季总冠军争夺收官阶段面临的最大障碍是他自己。

    尼科·罗斯伯格表示，基米·安东内利与自己的心理较量将决定2026年一级方程式总冠军之争，这番话是在阿塞拜疆大奖赛之前说的

    在天空体育F1节目《The F1 Show》的最新一期中，尼科·罗斯伯格讨论了基米·安东内利要想成为世界冠军需要克服什么；从本周四开始，在天空体育F1频道直播阿塞拜疆大奖赛的每一节练习赛和排位赛，正赛将于周六中午12点播出

    尼科·罗斯伯格认为，唯一能阻止基米·安东内利夺得世界冠军的就是他自己

    前一级方程式世界冠军尼科·罗斯伯格认为，在本赛季冠军争夺战的收官阶段，基米·安东内利最大的障碍将是他自己。

    安东内利有望在20岁时成为一级方程式历史上最年轻的世界冠军，目前他领先梅赛德斯队友拉塞尔81分，领先法拉利的刘易斯·汉密尔顿101分，领先迈凯伦的兰多·诺里斯106分。

    今年只剩下九轮比赛，首先是本周的阿塞拜疆大奖赛，比赛将于周六中午12点举行，由天空体育F1频道和天空体育主频道进行直播。

    在天空体育《F1秀》最新一期节目中，罗斯伯格谈到了自己2016年夺冠的经历，当时他经历了一场激烈的心理斗争，而安东内利可能也需要克服同样的挑战。

    罗斯伯格说：“我知道当我成为最大热门时，我的大脑是怎么开始和我玩心理游戏的。”

    “当你真正成为最大热门时，就像他现在的处境一样，那种感觉就是：‘天哪，这冠军实际上唾手可得。我最好别搞砸，最好别犯愚蠢的、不必要的错误，因为世界冠军就在眼前’。”

    “一旦这类想法冒出来，你就会开始害怕，真的开始恐惧自己会完全没必要地把事情搞砸，因为毕竟驾驶一级方程式赛车并保持在车阵前列，当然是世界上最难做到的事情之一。”

    "要不出错并一直保持状态，一次又一次地做出不错的排位成绩、又不撞上护墙，实在是太难了。犯错太容易了，因为这本身就难到了极点。然后你的脑子开始混乱，就会夺走你专注于开好车所需的精力。"

    尼科称梅赛德斯车手安东内利为一代天才

    安东内利没有表现出任何紧张的迹象，他赢得了最近两场比赛，包括在蒙扎的主场赛事中从第十九位发车一路惊人地追到第一名，随后一周又在马德里的全新赛道上夺冠。

    2016年，罗斯伯格与汉密尔顿的冠军争夺战要激烈得多，但当时他的处境是并不需要赢下最后四场比赛，他可以接受拿下两个第二和一个第三。

    他继续说道："当我争夺世界冠军时，最后四场比赛就是我脑子里真正开始疯狂的时候，在那最后四场比赛里，我完全没有可能拿到杆位或者再赢一场比赛，因为我用于真正把车开好的脑力大约少了百分之三十。"

    安东内利表示，F1车手总冠军之争还没有结束

    “通常情况下，我可以说是百分之百专注于把车开好，而这次只有百分之七十是在把车开好，另外百分之三十完全是害怕犯下愚蠢的错误，从而失去我赢得一级方程式世界冠军的机会，这个机会实在是太重大了。”

    “这也是基米·安东内利从八岁起就一直努力追逐的一个巨大梦想。所以这是他12年来对生活全身心的投入，这占据了他人生的一大半，只为了实现有朝一日成为F1世界冠军的梦想。这个梦想太大了。”

    “这是我们需要重点关注的大问题。现在真的是他和自己的心理在较量，我们看看情况会怎样，但他目前的表现非常出色。”

    罗斯伯格：如果迈凯伦逐渐拉开差距，将是最糟糕的情况

    安东内利在赛季的大部分时间里基本上都压制住了队友拉塞尔，所以除非这种情况突然改变，他最大的竞争威胁可能来自法拉利或迈凯伦在研发进度上超过梅赛德斯。

    诺里斯本有可能在过去四场比赛中赢下三场，要不是上一站马德里比赛中一次不合时宜的虚拟安全车，所以他尽管目前在车手积分榜上排名第四，看起来仍是安东内利最大的威胁。

    迈凯伦为本周末在巴库的比赛带来了新部件，而梅赛德斯则将在下周在马来西亚的比赛中带来一套重要升级包。

    兰多·诺里斯对摩纳哥大奖赛将于2027年举办冲刺赛的消息作出反应。

    “最坏的情况是迈凯伦突然开始逐渐拉开差距，也许法拉利也会有所进步之类，而基米开始更多地掉回车阵中，”罗斯伯格说。

    “这又和我夺冠那年的情况类似，因为当时我的驾驶状态不佳，在最后四场比赛中我开始掉回车阵。”

    看看安东内利如何从第19位一路赶超到第一位，在意大利大奖赛上夺得一场轰动性的胜利。

    “甚至在墨西哥排位赛中直到最后一刻，我在第三节排位赛里其实一直只排在第六。在那几场比赛里，我一度只排在第三而不是第二，而且马克斯·维斯塔潘几乎每场比赛都在撞击我。”

    “如果你稍微往后掉进车群之中，风险就会再次增加，你会开始变得越来越慌乱。”

    Kimi Antonelli versus his mind will decide 2026 Formula 1 title race, says Nico Rosberg ahead of Azerbaijan Grand Prix

    Nico Rosberg believes the only thing that can stop Kimi Antonelli winning the world title is himself

    Former Formula 1 world champion Nico Rosberg believes Kimi Antonelli's biggest obstacle will be himself in this season's title race run-in.

    Antonelli is on course to become F1's youngest world champion at 20 years old as he leads Mercedes team-mate George Russell by 81 points, Ferrari's Lewis Hamiton by 101 points and McLaren's Lando Norris by 106 points.

    Just nine rounds remain this year, starting with this week's Azerbaijan Grand Prix which will see the race take place on Saturday at 12pm, live on Sky Sports F1 and Sky Sports Main Event.

    Speaking on the latest episode of Sky Sports' The F1 Show, Rosberg brought up his own title-winning experience in 2016 where he faced a big mental battle which Antonelli may also need to overcome.

    "I know how my head started to play games with me when I was the big favourite," said Rosberg.

    "When you're really the big favourite, like he is now, it's like, 'oh my God, this is this is actually mine for the taking. I better not mess this up, I better not make these make stupid mistakes and unnecessary mistakes now because the World Championship is there for the taking'.

    "As soon as those kind of thoughts come in, you start to get scared and you really start to get fear that you're going to mess something up completely unnecessarily because it remains that driving an F1 car and being at the front of the pack, of course, is one of the hardest things in the world to do.

    "It is so damn difficult to not make mistakes and to be there, qualify time and time again and do good laps and not put it on the wall. It is easy to make mistakes because it's just so damn hard. Then your head starts to go, and it just takes capacity away from your concentration to drive well."

    Nico described Mercedes driver Antonelli as a generational talent

    Antonelli has shown no signs of any nerves as he has won the last two races, including an incredible drive from 19th to first at his home event at Monza, then at a new track in Madrid a week later.

    In 2016, Rosberg was in a much tighter title fight against Hamilton but was in a position where he did not need to win the last four races and could afford to finish second, with one third.

    He continued: "When I was going for my World Championship, the last four races, that's when it really went crazy in my head, and there was no chance in the world for me to do a pole position or to win another race in those last four races because I had like 30 per cent less capacity in my mind to actually drive well.

    Antonelli says the F1 Drivers' Championship is not over yet

    "Usually, it's like 100 per cent to drive well, and now it was 70 per cent to drive well, and 30 per cent was just absolutely terrified to make some stupid mistake and lose my opportunity to win the Formula 1 World Championship, which is just so extremely big.

    "It was a massive dream that also Kimi has been working towards since he's eight years old. So it's 12 years of dedicating his life, which is more than half of his life, towards the dream of maybe being F1 world champion. It's so big.

    "That's the big one that we need to keep an eye out. It's really him against his mind now, so we see how that goes, but he's doing incredibly well."

    Rosberg: Worst-case scenario if McLaren edge away

    Antonelli has largely had the upper hand on team-mate Russell for much of the season, so unless that suddenly changes, his biggest competition could come from Ferrari or McLaren outdeveloping Mercedes.

    Norris could have won three of the last four races, had it not been for an untimely virtual safety car last time out in Madrid, so he appears to be Antonelli's biggest threat despite being fourth in the Drivers' Championship.

    McLaren have new parts for this weekend's race in Baku, before Mercedes bring a major upgrade package for next week's race in Malaysia.

    "That again is similar to where I was in my championship year, because I was not driving as well, I started to drop back into the pack in those last four races.

    "If you were to drop back into the pack ever so slightly, again, the risk just comes up more and you start to get more and more panicked about things."
