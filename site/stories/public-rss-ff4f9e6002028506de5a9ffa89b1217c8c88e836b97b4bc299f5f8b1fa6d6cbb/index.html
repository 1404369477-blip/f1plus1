    奥康就哈斯弃用发声明，否认与车队负责人有肢体冲突传闻

    被哈斯车队弃用、无缘墨西哥大奖赛的奥康发表声明，否认法国媒体关于他与车队负责人小松礼雄发生肢体冲突的传闻。

    埃斯特班·奥康在哈斯车队被按在替补席后发表声明，否认了肢体冲突的传闻

    埃斯特班·奥康发表了首份声明，回应了在F1墨西哥城大奖赛上与哈斯车队闹翻相关的媒体传闻

    被哈斯F1车队弃用的车手埃斯特班·奥康否认了法国媒体关于他与车队领队小松礼雄发生肢体冲突的传闻，并就缺席本周末墨西哥大奖赛作出回应。

    奥康在墨西哥被按在替补席上，最可能的原因是在自由练习期间与他的比赛工程师发生了公开分歧。这导致周五晚上与车队进行了一次讨论，这位法国车手与车队之间的关系已变得日益紧张。

    由于周五事件的结果，其细节仍然稀少，哈斯决定在周末剩余时间里放弃奥康，由储备车手兼丰田WEC车手平川亮从第三次练习赛开始接管他的赛车。

    但法国媒体对奥康与小松之间争执的确切性质猜测纷纷，包括两人之间可能发生肢体冲突的传闻，奥康的团队被迫通过他的社交媒体账户否认这些未经证实的谣言。

    奥康的账户发布声明称：“在哈斯F1车队最近做出决定确认我将不参加本周末的墨西哥城大奖赛后，我希望正式回应一些围绕此事的媒体报道和猜测。”

    “昨晚，观点分歧导致了与相关方的讨论。我承认这确实发生了，但我坚决否认任何报道或指控，称使用了任何形式的肢体暴力来解决任何性质的任何分歧。”

    他补充道：“我强烈谴责一切形式的暴力，并坚信分歧应始终以专业精神、尊重和相互理解来处理。我恳请大家在事件处理期间避免猜测。后续进展将在适当时候通报。”

    目前尚不清楚奥康是否会在下周末的巴西大奖赛重返赛车，或者他是否会随这支美国所有的车队完成本赛季。

    这位30岁的大奖赛分站冠军得主正在为自己的F1未来而战，他因2027年席位被法拉利新秀拉法·卡马拉取代而遭弃用，留在F1的选择正迅速减少。

    Esteban Ocon issues statement after Haas benching, denies physical altercation rumours

    Esteban Ocon has issued his first statement addressing media rumours around his falling out with Haas at Formula 1's Mexico City Grand Prix

    Dropped Haas Formula 1 driver Esteban Ocon has denied French media rumours of a physical altercation with team boss Ayao Komatsu as he issued his response to missing this weekend's Mexico Grand Prix.

    Ocon was benched in Mexico, most likely connected to a public disagreement with his race engineer during free practice. That led to a discussion with the team on Friday night as the relationship between the Frenchman and his team has become increasingly strained.

    As a result of Friday's events, details of which remain sparse, Haas made the decision to drop Ocon for the remainder of the weekend, with reserve driver and Toyota WEC driver Ryo Hirakawa taking over his car from FP3 onwards.

    But with speculation rife in French media outlets over the exact nature of Ocon's spat with Komatsu, including rumours of a possible physical altercation between the two, Ocon's camp was forced to deny those unsubstantiated rumours via his social media accounts.

    "Following the recent decision from Haas F1 Team confirming that I will not be participating in this weekend’s Mexico City Grand Prix, I wish to formally address some media reports and speculation surrounding the situation," Ocon's accounts posted.

    "Last night, a difference in viewpoints led to a discussion with the parties involved. Whilst I acknowledge that this took place, I categorically reject any reports or allegations suggesting that any form of physical violence was used to resolve any differences of any nature."

    He added: "I strongly condemn violence in all its forms and firmly believe that disagreements should always be handled with professionalism, respect, and mutual understanding. I kindly ask that speculation be avoided while the matter is being addressed. Further updates will be communicated in due course."

    At this point it isn't yet clear if Ocon will return to the car at next weekend's Brazilian Grand Prix or if he will finish the season with the US-owned team at all.

    The 30-year-old grand prix winner is fighting for his F1 future after being dropped for 2027 for Ferrari rookie Rafa Camara, with options to remain in F1 dwindling rapidly.
