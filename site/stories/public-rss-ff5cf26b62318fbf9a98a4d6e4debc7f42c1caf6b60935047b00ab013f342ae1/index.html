    诺里斯对中东赛季最后两站赛事信心满满

    2025年F1冠军诺里斯表示，他对赛季最后两站中东赛事没有担忧，强调锦标赛不会让车手和车队处于风险之中。

    兰多·诺里斯自信F1不会在中东“让我们处于危险之中”

    这位麦凯轮车手表示，他对在卡塔尔和阿布扎比赛车没有个人担忧

    对于麦凯轮车迷来说，来自沃金克的最新消息，包括车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的赛事

    2025年F1冠军兰多·诺里斯表示，他对赛季最后两场中东赛事没有担忧，坚称锦标赛不会让其车手和车队处于危险之中。

    在本周末的拉斯维加斯赛事之后，锦标赛将于11月27日至29日移师卡塔尔，然后于12月4日至6日前往阿布扎比。

    由于该地区的情况，今年一级方程式不得不调整了赛历，巴林大奖赛被移至马来西亚，沙特阿拉伯大奖赛被取消。如果卡塔尔和阿布扎比的最后两场比赛无法进行，伊莫拉曾被考虑作为替代场地，但现在这一意大利场地已被排除在外。

    在周四的拉斯维加斯车手新闻发布会上，当被问及他对前往该地区的担忧时，诺里斯表示他期待这次旅行。

    “我只是期待这次旅行。从我的角度来看，我很好。我想问题是，我们是一个大车队。所以更多的是，我的队友们怎么想？我很好，”诺里斯说。

    麦克拉伦车手透露，他在赛车周末开始之前就已经前往该地区。

    "我下周在巴林有朋友的婚礼。所以目前我计划先去那里，然后去卡塔尔和阿布扎比。所以对我来说，没有问题，"他说。

    "我只是不相信这项运动会在这种情况下让我们处于危险之中。MotoGP也去过那里。所以对我来说，没有顾虑。"

    MotoGP在本月初在卡塔尔赛车，没有出现任何问题。

    诺里斯强调，这个决定影响到的人远不止车手们。

    “但是，是的，这不仅仅是我个人的事。我想这涉及到更多的人。是粉丝，是车队人员，”他补充道。

    同样出席新闻发布会的凯迪拉克车手瓦尔特里·博塔斯也表示同意。

    “我肯定会去那里参赛。没有顾虑，因为，就像兰多说的，如果一级方程式决定我们去那里，他们不会去如果那里没有明确的计划或者不知道那里是否安全，”博塔斯说。

    Lando Norris confident F1 would not "put us at risk" in the Middle East

    The McLaren driver says he has no personal concerns about racing in Qatar and Abu Dhabi

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    2025 Formula 1 champion Lando Norris says he has no concerns about the final two races of the season in the Middle East, insisting the championship would not put its drivers and teams at risk.

    After this weekend's race in Las Vegas, the championship heads to Qatar from 27-29 November and then Abu Dhabi from 4-6 December.

    F1 has had to adapt its calendar this year because of the situation in the region, with the Bahrain Grand Prix moved to Malaysia and the Saudi Arabian Grand Prix cancelled. Imola had been considered as a replacement venue if the final two races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi were unable to go ahead, but the Italian venue has now been ruled out.

    Asked in the drivers' press conference in Las Vegas on Thursday whether he had any reservations about travelling to the region, Norris said he was looking forward to it.

    "I'm just looking forward to it. From my side, I'm fine. I guess the thing is, it's a big team that we travel with. So it's more, what do the rest of my team think? I'm fine," Norris said.

    The McLaren driver revealed that he is heading to the region even before the race weekends begin.

    "I've my friend's wedding in Bahrain next week. So at the moment I was planning on going there, and then going on to Qatar and then Abu Dhabi. So from my side, not a concern," he said.

    "I just don't believe the sport would put us at risk in this kind of situation. MotoGP have also been there. So from my side, no concerns."

    MotoGP raced in Qatar earlier this month without any issues.

    Norris stressed that the decision affected far more people than the drivers.

    "But yeah, it's not about me. I guess it's a lot more people. It's the fans, it's the team personnel," he added.

    Cadillac's Valtteri Bottas, who was also in the press conference, agreed.

    "I'm going to be racing there for sure. No concerns because, like Lando said, if Formula 1 decides that we're going there, they wouldn't go there if there's not a clear plan or if they didn't know that it's going to be safe," Bottas said.
