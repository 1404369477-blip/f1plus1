    对于麦凯轮车队的粉丝来说，来自沃金克的木瓜新闻，有关车手、赛车、团队策略以及每个周末的赛事

    兰多·诺里斯在巴西大奖赛领奖台上的意外事件后，在社交媒体上走红

    麦克拉伦车手在伊纳卡罗斯赛道上控制了几乎所有的事情，直到他站在领奖台上。

    2025年的冠军在领取奖杯后，又玩起了他常用的把戏，蹲下身来，将香槟瓶底猛地砸在地上，让里面的泡沫从瓶口喷涌而出。但这次，瓶子在撞击时裂开了，摔在了地上。然后他向前迈步，看起来被领奖台上的烟花击中了脸部。

    诺里斯似乎并没有受到这次事件的伤害，他被看到和红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘和伊萨克·哈德贾一起笑着，他们分别获得了第二和第三名。

    这一幕很快在社交媒体上走红。一位Reddit上的粉丝评论说：“打破瓶子，走进烟花。这不是他会轻易忘记的胜利。”另一位粉丝补充说：“下一场比赛，他们会给他一张印在塑料纸上的参赛证书。”

    “另一个Lando经典时刻在领奖台上，”有人评论道，另一个人写道：“用一种相当壮观的砸瓶方式破坏了香槟...然后将自己的头伸进流动的烟花中。这至少值一分或更多哈哈。”

    进一步的反应包括：“经典的Lando领奖台上的恶作剧”和“这太有趣了。打破香槟，将烟花喷到脸上，然后立即被他们三个喷得满身都是，尴尬得要命。”

    诺里斯自从他的青训赛车时代以来就是这样庆祝的，他在去年的F1 Explains播客中解释了这种方法。“不是摇香槟然后喷它，而是把它砸在地上，”他说。“这里的技巧是不要砸它。而要做到这一点，就是要有一个非常非常厚、非常结实的瓶子。”

    这次意外并没有减少诺里斯下午的光芒。他在变化多端的情况下以14.602秒的优势击败了维斯塔潘，赢得了本赛季的第三个胜利，哈吉尔完成了第三名的领奖台。

    Lando Norris goes viral after Brazilian GP podium celebration goes wrong

    Lando Norris went viral after smashing his champagne bottle and appearing to get caught in the face by fireworks during his Brazilian GP podium celebrations

    For McLaren fans. Papaya news from Woking, with the latest on the drivers, the car, team strategy and every race weekend.

    Lando Norris has gone viral on social media after his Brazilian Grand Prix podium mishap.

    The McLaren driver controlled almost everything that came his way at Interlagos, right up until he got to the podium.

    The 2025 champion went for his usual party trick after collecting his trophy, crouching down and slamming the base of the champagne bottle onto the ground to send the contents fizzing out the top. But this time, the bottle gave way on impact and smashed on the floor. He then stepped forward and appeared to be hit in the face by the podium fireworks.

    Norris did not seem to be hurt by the incident and was seen laughing it off with Red Bull drivers Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar, who had finished second and third, respectively.

    The moment quickly went viral on social media. "Broke the bottle, walked into the fireworks. Not a win he'll forget easily," one fan commented on Reddit, while another added: "Next race they'll hand him a participation certificate printed on laminated paper."

    "Another Lando classic moment on the podium," someone else posted, while another wrote: "Destroyed the bottle with a pretty majestic slam... and then put his head inside a flowing firework stream. That's gotta be worth at least one extra point or something haha."

    Further reactions included: "Classic Lando on the podium shenanigans" and "This was hilarious. Breaks the bottle, takes the pyrotechnics to the face, and is then immediately sprayed by all three of them while dying of embarrassment."

    Norris has celebrated this way since his junior racing days, and he explained how it works on the F1 Explains podcast last year. "Instead of shaking the bottle and then spraying it, you smash it against the ground," he said. "The trick here is to not smash it. And the trick to that is by having a very, very thick, strong bottle."

    The mishap did not take much of the shine off Norris's afternoon. He beat Verstappen by 14.602 seconds in changing conditions to take his third win of the season, with Hadjar completing the podium in third.
