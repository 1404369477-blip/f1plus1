    诺里斯承认在新加坡大奖赛中对阵皮亚斯特里失误

    诺里斯承认在新加坡大奖赛中与队友皮亚斯特里发生碰撞的责任，并表示自己应该更多地考虑车队。

    兰多·诺里斯现在已经接受了在新加坡短道赛与迈凯轮一级方程式车队队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里发生碰撞的责任，在大奖赛之后分享了他的立场调整。

    诺里斯和皮亚斯特里在短道赛的最后一圈争夺第四和第五名时发生碰撞，诺里斯在5号弯道内侧超车皮亚斯特里并发生接触。

    这使得车队从比赛中只得到一分——诺里斯的第八名——并且引起了车队负责人安德烈亚·斯特拉的不满，他称这次碰撞“不可接受”。

    裁判官认为两位车手在碰撞中都没有主要责任，他们在周六接受媒体采访时都没有承担责任，诺里斯告诉Viaplay：“有很大的差距，所以我冲了出去，我有权这么做。”

    那个Viaplay采访的开头在社交媒体上走红，诺里斯问记者：“比赛的标题是什么？冲刺...冲刺之后是什么？”

    当记者回答“比赛”时，诺里斯回答说：“哇，干得好。是的，是比赛！”

    斯特拉承诺将进行全面的回顾，两位车手一起，因为这对车队来说是严重的，因为损失是实质性的。”,

    周日，在大奖赛中获得第四名后，诺里斯提出了一个修订后的立场。

    “我无法尝试我之前做的事情。我一开始就不应该尝试，即使那个动作是正确的，”诺里斯说。

    “我必须更多地考虑我所代表的团队，而不是我自己。这是我的错误，我让团队失望了。

    “因此，我首先向他们所有人道歉。我欠他们一个道歉，下次我会做得更好。”

    英文原文

    Lando Norris has now accepted blame for clashing with McLaren Formula 1 team-mate Oscar Piastri in the Singapore sprint race, sharing his revised stance after the grand prix.

    Norris and Piastri clashed on the final lap of the sprint while fighting for fourth and fifth place, with Norris going down the inside of Piastri at Turn 5 and making contact.

    That left the team with just one point from the race - Norris's for eighth place - and drew ire from team principal Andrea Stella, who called the clash “unacceptable”.

    The stewards ruled neither driver was predominantly to blame for the clash, and neither took responsibility for causing it when speaking to the media on Saturday, with Norris telling Viaplay: “It was a big gap so I went for it, I have the right to do that.”

    The start of that Viaplay interview went viral on social media, with Norris asking the journalist: “What's the title of the race? Sprint ... What's after sprint?"

    When the journalist replied, “race”, Norris replied "Wow, good job. Yeah, it's race!"

    Stella promised a full review with the two drivers as “it is serious because the damage for the team is material”.

    And on Sunday, having finished fourth in the grand prix, Norris presented a revised stance.

    “I can't attempt what I did. I should never have attempted it in the first place, even if the move was on,” Norris said.

    “I’ve simply got to think more of the team that I represent rather than myself. It was a mistake on my part and I let down the team.

    “So I first of all apologise to all of them. I owe them all an apology, and I'll do better next time.”
